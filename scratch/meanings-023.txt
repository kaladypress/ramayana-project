1: Having spoken thus to Maithilī and given his commands to all the Rākṣasīs, King Rāvaṇa, the scourge of his foes, strode out.
2: As soon as the king of Rākṣasas departed and retired to his palace, those hideous Rākṣasīs charged straight at Sītā.
3: Closing in on Sītā and seething with fury, the Rākṣasīs snarled at Vaidehī with vicious words:
4: "Listen, Sītā! Don't you think it's a great honor to become the wife of the mighty ten-headed Rāvaṇa, foremost of Pulastya's line?"
5: Then a Rākṣasī named Ekajaṭā, her eyes crimson with rage, confronted slender Sītā:
6: "Among the six Prajāpatis, the fourth is the mind-born son of Brahmā, famed as Pulastya.
7: Pulastya's mind-born son was the radiant sage named Viśravas, whose splendor equaled that of a Prajāpati himself.
8: Wide-eyed girl, his son is Rāvaṇa, the terror of his enemies! You ought to be the wife of that king of Rākṣasas; why don't you agree to what I tell you, beautiful one?"
9: Then a Rākṣasī named Harijaṭā glared with furious, cat-like eyes and barked:
10: "He conquered the thirty-three gods along with the king of gods himself! You ought to be proud to become the wife of that king of Rākṣasas!
11: Why won't you accept being the wife of this mighty hero, brimming with prowess and unyielding in battle?"
12-13: Then a Rākṣasī named Praghasā, boiling with rage, hurled fierce threats at her: "The mighty King Rāvaṇa is willing to turn away from his cherished, highly honored queen, foremost among all women, just to come to you!
14: Leaving behind his palace filled with thousands of women and glittering with every jewel, Rāvaṇa wants to be with you!
15: He who has repeatedly routed the gods, Nāgas, Gandharvas, and Dānavas in battle has now come courting you!
16: Wretch! Why don't you want to be the wife of the mighty Rāvaṇa, the king of Rākṣasas, who possesses every conceivable luxury?"
17: "Out of fear of him, the sun dares not burn too hot and the wind dares not blow too hard; wide-eyed fool, why won't you submit to him?
18: Out of fear of him, the trees shower down blossoms, and the mountains and clouds yield water whenever he desires, fair-browed one!
19: Why don't you make up your mind to be the wife of that king of the Nairr̥tas, the king of kings?
20: We are telling you this for your own good, lady! Listen to our advice, bright-smiling one, or you won't live to see another day!"
