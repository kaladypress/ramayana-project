1: Reaching Mount Prasravaṇa with its picturesque forests, they bowed their heads to Rāma and the mighty Lakṣmaṇa.
2: Placing crown prince Aṅgada in front and saluting Sugrīva, they began to report the news of Sītā.
3: They told of her confinement in Rāvaṇa's palace, the threats of the Rākṣasīs, her deep love for Rāma, and the time limit that had been set.
4: As all those Haris recounted this in Rāma's presence, hearing that Vaidehī was unharmed, Rāma spoke in reply:
5: "Where is Devī Sītā living, and what are her feelings toward me? Vānaras, tell me everything about Vaidehī!"
6: Hearing Rāma's words, the Haris present before Rāma urged Hanumān forward, for he knew the full account of Sītā.
7: Hearing their words, Hanumān, the son of Māruta and master of speech, bowed his head in the direction of Devī Sītā and spoke, describing how he had found her.
8: Handing Rāma that divine golden jewel blazing with its own luster, Hanumān stood with joined palms and said:
9: "Leaping across the ocean a hundred yojanas wide, I went searching for Jānakī Sītā, longing to see her.
10: There lies the city called Laṅkā, belonging to the wicked Rāvaṇa,
11: situated on the southern shore of the southern sea. There I saw the virtuous Sītā in Rāvaṇa's palace grounds.
12: Rāma, that lovely lady lives only by centering all her thoughts on you; I saw her in the midst of the Rākṣasīs, constantly threatened by them.
13: Guarded by hideous Rākṣasīs in the pleasure grove, your chaste devī, who never deserved any suffering, is undergoing great grief.
14: Confined in Rāvaṇa's palace grounds and closely watched by Rākṣasīs, she wears her hair in a single braid, sorrowful and entirely absorbed in thoughts of you.
15: Sleeping on the bare ground, pale and withered like a lotus pond at the onset of winter frost, she spurns Rāvaṇa completely and is resolved to die.
16: Faultless Kākutstha, keeping her mind on you, that devī was found by me with great effort as I softly sang the renowned praises of the Ikṣvāku dynasty.
17: Tiger among men, I won her confidence, and then spoke with the devī and explained the entire situation to her.
18: Hearing of the friendship between Rāma and Sugrīva, she was overjoyed. Her conduct remains steadfast, and her devotion to you is unwavering.
19: Foremost of men, thus did I see the illustrious daughter of Janaka, engaged in severe austerity and wholly devoted to you.
20: Wise Rāghava, she gave me a token of recognition — the incident with the crow that happened in your presence at Citrakūṭa.
21: Jānakī said to me: 'Son of Vāyu, you must report everything you have seen here to Rāma, that tiger among men.'
22: And while speaking these words within Sugrīva's hearing, she added: 'This jewel, preserved with great care, must be given to him.'
23: She said, 'This splendid crest jewel has been carefully guarded by me for you. And remember the tilaka of red arsenic paste you drew on my forehead.'
24: 'Faultless one, this splendid gem born of the sea is now sent to you by me. In my sorrow, seeing it used to bring me joy, as if I were seeing you.'
25: 'Son of Daśaratha, I will hold on to my life for one month more; fallen into the clutches of the Rākṣasas, I cannot survive beyond a month.'
26: So Sītā spoke to me — slender, walking the path of dharma, confined in Rāvaṇa's palace grounds, her wide eyes like those of a frightened doe.
27: Rāghava, I have reported all this to you exactly as it took place. Now, by all means, let arrangements be made to cross the waters of the ocean."
28: Seeing that the two princes were comforted, and having handed that token of recognition to Rāghava, the son of Vāyu related in full speech everything the devī had said, in exact sequence.
