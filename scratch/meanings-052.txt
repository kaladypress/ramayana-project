1: Seeing the āśrama, the mighty and valiant Viśvāmitra was overjoyed and bowed in humility before Vasiṣṭha, the foremost of those who recite mantras.
2: "Welcome to you," said the great-souled Vasiṣṭha, and the revered Vasiṣṭha offered him a seat.
3: When the wise Viśvāmitra had taken his seat, the foremost of munis offered him fruits and roots in the customary manner.
4: Accepting that honor from Vasiṣṭha, the finest of kings inquired whether all was well with his tapas, his agnihotra fire, and his disciples.
5: The deeply radiant Viśvāmitra also asked after the trees of the grove, and Vasiṣṭha told the finest of kings that all was well everywhere.
6: While King Viśvāmitra sat comfortably at ease, the great ascetic Vasiṣṭha, son of Brahmā and foremost of those who recite mantras, asked him:
7: "'Righteous king, is all well with you? Are you gladdening your people through dharma and protecting them in accordance with the duties of a king?
8: "'Destroyer of foes, are your servants well supported, do they obey your commands, and have all your enemies been conquered?
9: "'Foremost of men, blameless scorcher of foes, is all well with your army, your treasury, your allies, and your sons and grandsons?'"
10: The deeply radiant King Viśvāmitra respectfully replied to Vasiṣṭha that all was well everywhere.
11: After those two supremely righteous men had conversed for a long time on auspicious topics, they were filled with the highest joy and delighted in each other's company.
12: "Raghunandana, at the close of their conversation the revered Vasiṣṭha smiled and spoke these words to Viśvāmitra:
13: "'Mighty king, I wish to offer hospitality to this army of yours as well as to you, whose greatness is immeasurable; please accept it from me as befits you.
14: "'King, you are the foremost of guests and must be honored with every effort; please accept this hospitality that I am ready to offer.'
15: "Addressed thus by Vasiṣṭha, the wise King Viśvāmitra replied: 'By your kind words of welcome alone, you have already honored me.
16: "'Revered one, with the fruits and roots found in your āśrama, with water for my feet and for sipping, and with your very darśana,
17: "'Sage of great wisdom, I have been thoroughly honored in every way by you who are yourself worthy of all honor; I will now take my leave—salutations to you, and please look upon me with a friendly eye.'
18: "Even as the king spoke thus, the righteous, generous-minded Vasiṣṭha pressed his invitation again and again.
19: "The son of Gādhi replied to Vasiṣṭha: 'So be it! Foremost of munis, let it be as you wish, revered one.'
20: "At these words, the deeply radiant Vasiṣṭha, foremost of those who recite mantras and free from all taint, gladly called for his dappled cow:
21: "'Come, Śabalā, come quickly and hear my words! I have resolved to host this rājarṣi together with his entire army.
22: "'Arrange a reception for me with a lavish feast, providing each man, according to his wish, with whatever he desires among the six tastes.
23: "'Divine wish-fulfilling cow, for my sake rain down all of it—quickly, Śabalā, bring forth whole heaps of every kind of food, complete with juices, solid dishes, drinks, and delicacies to be licked and sucked!'"
