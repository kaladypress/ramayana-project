1-2: When he heard those words, Vāli's son Aṅgada said: "Vānaras, we have seen the queen, but it isn't right for us to go to the noble Rāghava without her. We have heard that Laṅkā was burned by the strength of Vāyu's son alone. All of you are famous for your courage. I don't think it's right to go there and report, 'We saw the queen, but we didn't bring her back.'
3: "You finest of the Haris, in all the worlds, including the devatās and the Daityas, no one equals you in leaping, and no one equals you in valor.
4: "Let's conquer Laṅkā with its hordes of Rākṣasas, kill that Rāvaṇa in battle, and take Sītā with us. Then we can go back with our mission accomplished and our hearts full of joy.
5: "Hanumān has already killed the Rākṣasa heroes. What else is there to do here? Let's take Jānakī and go.
6: "We'll place Janaka's daughter between Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa. Why trouble all those other Vānaras, those bulls among the Vānaras, back home?
7: "We ourselves should go, kill those leading Rākṣasas, and then go to see Rāghava, Sugrīva, and Lakṣmaṇa."
8: Aṅgada had made up his mind. Jāmbavān, the finest of the Haris, who understood what really mattered, was very pleased with him, but he replied with words full of sense.
9: "You are very wise, great Kapi, but what you're suggesting isn't the right course. We were ordered to search the great southern direction.
10: "We were not ordered to bring her back, either by the king of the Kapis or by the wise Rāma. Rāma would not be pleased with a Sītā somehow won back by us.
11: "Rāghava, a tiger among kings, has himself publicly vowed, in the name of his own lineage, that he will win Sītā back,
12: "and he made that vow in front of all the chief Kapis. How could he let it turn out false? Our work would be wasted, and he would not be satisfied. The valor we displayed would also be for nothing, you bulls among the Vānaras.
13: "So let all of us go to where Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa are, and the brilliant Sugrīva, and report what we have done.
14: "Prince, this way of seeing things is not my own opinion, as you might think. Look at the success of this task the way Rāma has set his mind on it."
