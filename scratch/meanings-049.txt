1: "Then the emasculated Śakra, his face full of dread, spoke to the devatās led by Agni, together with the hosts of r̥ṣis and Cāraṇas:
2: "'By obstructing the tapas of the great-souled Gautama and provoking his wrath, I have accomplished this task for the suras.
3: "'In his anger he has emasculated me and cast her off as well; by unleashing this great curse, I have drained him of his tapas.
4: "'Therefore, foremost of the suras, hosts of r̥ṣis, and Cāraṇas, all of you ought to restore my manhood, for I acted in service of the suras.'
5: "Hearing the words of Śatakratu, the devatās led by Agni approached the Pitr̥devas together with all the hosts of Maruts and said:
6: "'Here is a ram with testicles, whereas Śakra has been left without his; take the ram's testicles and give them quickly to Śakra.
7: "'A castrated ram will bring you the highest satisfaction, and you will bestow rewards upon the humans who offer them to delight you.'
8: "Hearing Agni's words, the assembled Pitr̥devas pulled out the ram's testicles and attached them to the thousand-eyed Indra.
9: "Kākutstha, from that time onward the assembled Pitr̥devas partake of castrated rams and reward those who offer them with the fruits of their offerings.
10: "And from that day on, Rāghava, Indra has borne the testicles of a ram, through the power of the great-souled Gautama and his tapas.
11: "So come, radiant prince, enter the āśrama of the sage of holy deeds and deliver the blessed Ahalyā, whose form is like that of a devatā."
12: Hearing Viśvāmitra's words, Rāghava together with Lakṣmaṇa entered the āśrama, placing Viśvāmitra in front.
13: There he saw that blessed lady, her radiance kindled by tapas—impossible even for the worlds, or for the suras and asuras together, to gaze upon directly.
14: Fashioned with great care by Dhātā as though she were a divine being woven of māyā, she looked like a blazing flame shrouded in smoke,
15: like the radiance of the full moon veiled by mist and clouds, or like the dazzling brilliance of the sun shining upon the midst of the waters.
16: For by Gautama's word she had remained invisible to all the three worlds until she beheld Rāma.
17: Then the two Rāghavas clasped her feet, and remembering Gautama's words, she welcomed them both.
18: With deep composure she offered them pādya, arghya, and full hospitality, and Kākutstha accepted it in accordance with traditional rites.
19: A great shower of flowers fell from the sky to the sound of celestial dundubhi drums, and a grand gathering of Gandharvas and Apsarases took place.
20: Exclaiming, "Wonderful! Wonderful!", the devatās honored Ahalyā, whose whole being was purified by the power of her tapas and who was completely devoted to Gautama's will.
21: The deeply radiant great ascetic Gautama too, reunited happily with Ahalyā, honored Rāma in accordance with the rites and resumed his tapas.
22: And Rāma, after receiving the highest honor from the great muni Gautama in accordance with the rites, proceeded from there toward Mithilā.
