1: In the center of that palace complex, eager to see Sītā, the Vānara explored bower houses, picture galleries, and night chambers, but he could not find that lovely lady.
2: Not finding the beloved of the joy of Raghu, the great Kapi thought in distress: "Surely Sītā must be dead, since Maithilī is nowhere to be seen despite my searching.
3: Devoted to guarding her virtue and steadfast on the noble path, that chaste young woman has surely been killed by this villainous king of Rākṣasas.
4: Or perhaps the daughter of King Janaka died of sheer terror upon seeing the wives of the Rākṣasa king — misshapen, deformed, pale, huge-faced, and grotesque in appearance.
5: Without finding Sītā, failing in this feat of courage, and having spent so much time away with the Vānaras, I cannot return to Sugrīva; that powerful Vānara king enforces fierce punishment.
6: I have searched the entire inner palace and seen all of Rāvaṇa's wives, but the virtuous Sītā is nowhere to be found. All my toil has been in vain!
7: What will all the gathered Vānaras say to me when I return? 'Hero, tell us, what did you accomplish when you went there?'
8: If I have not seen the daughter of Janaka, what will I tell them? With the time limit expired, they will surely resolve to fast unto death.
9: What will the aged Jāmbavān say, and what will Aṅgada say, and all the gathered Vānaras, once I have crossed back over the ocean?"
10: "Perseverance is the root of all success; non-despondency is the highest happiness. I will search once more in places I have not yet looked!
11: Relentless determination drives every purposeful effort; it brings to fruition whatever action a living being undertakes.
12: Therefore, shaking off all despair, I will make the utmost effort and scour every unseen corner of the realms ruled by Rāvaṇa."
13: "The banquet halls were searched, as were the flower pavilions; the picture galleries were examined, and so were the pleasure houses.
14: The paths between inner gardens and the aerial chambers were searched on every side." Reflecting thus, he set out to search yet again.
15: Leaping up and dropping down, stopping at times and moving on, he searched underground cellars, sanctuary halls, and detached inner mansions.
16: Opening doorways, pushing past door leaves, entering and exiting, springing down and leaping high,
17: the great Kapi roamed through every available space. There was not even four finger-breadths of space in Rāvaṇa's inner palace that the Kapi did not visit.
18: The avenues between inner walls, the platforms around shrines, underground pits, and lotus ponds — everything was scrutinized by him.
19: Rākṣasīs of diverse forms, deformed and grotesque, were seen there by Hanumān, but not the daughter of Janaka.
20: Vidyādhara women of peerless beauty on earth were seen there by Hanumān, but not the joy of Rāghava.
21: Nāga maidens of graceful bearing, with faces like the full moon, were seen there by Hanumān, but not the slender-waisted Sītā.
22: Nāga maidens overpowered and abducted by force by the king of Rākṣasas were seen there by Hanumān, but not the joy of Janaka.
23: Failing to find Sītā while seeing all those other noble women, the mighty-armed Hanumān, son of Māruta, fell into dejection.
24: Seeing the great efforts of the Vānara chiefs and his own leap across the ocean coming to nothing, the son of Anila was once again plunged into anxiety.
25: Descending from the aerial palace, Hanumān, the son of Māruta, was weighed down with sorrow, his mind reeling with anxious thoughts.
