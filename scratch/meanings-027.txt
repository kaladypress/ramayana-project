1: Having spent that night there, the illustrious Viśvāmitra smiled and spoke these sweet-toned words to Rāghava:
2: "Illustrious prince, I am deeply pleased with you—may all blessings be yours! Filled with the highest affection, I now give you all these astras,
3: "with which you will overpower and conquer any enemies in battle—even hosts of devatās, asuras, gandharvas, and uragas.
4: "Blessings on you—I give you all those divine astras. Rāghava, I will give you the great divine Daṇḍacakra,
5: "and then, hero, the Dharmacakra, the Kālacakra, the fierce Viṣṇucakra, and the Aindra Cakra;
6: "the Vajra astra, best of men, Śiva's supreme trident, the Brahmaśiras astra, and the Aiṣīka astra as well, Rāghava.
7: "Mighty-armed Kākutstha, I give you the supreme Brāhma astra, and also the two maces, Modakī and Śikharī—
8: "both blazing with radiance, tiger among men, prince—I bestow them upon you, along with the Dharmapāśa, Rāma, and the Kālapāśa,
9: "and the supreme Vāruṇa-pāśa astra. Raghunandana, I also give you the two thunderbolts, Śuṣkā and Ārdrā.
10: "I give you the Paināka astra, the Nārāyaṇa astra, and the beloved Āgneya astra known by the name Śikhara.
11: "Rāghava, I give you the Vāyavya astra called Prathama, the astra named Hayaśiras, the Krauñca astra, and the two Śaktis, blameless Kākutstha.
12: "I also give you all those weapons wielded by the asuras—the Kaṅkāla, the dreadful Musala, the Kāpāla, and the Kaṅkaṇa.
13: "Mighty-armed prince, I give you the great Vaidyādhara astra known as Nandana, and the jewel among swords, Asiratna.
14: "Rāghava, I give you the beloved Gāndharva astra named Mānava, the Prasvāpana and Praśamana astras, and the Saura astra;
15: "the Varṣaṇa, Śoṣaṇa, Santāpana, and Vilāpana astras, as well as the irresistible Madana astra, dear to Kandarpa;
16: "and the favorite Paiśāca astra named Mohana—receive them, illustrious prince, tiger among men!
17: "Receive too, tiger among men, prince, the Tāmasa, the mighty Saumana, the irresistible Saṁvarta, and the Mausala astras;
18: "the Satya astra, mighty-armed one, the supreme Māyādhara, and the dreadful Tejaḥprabha, which drains away the energy of enemies;
19: "the Soma astra called Śiśira, Tvaṣṭā's astra called Sudāmana, the terrible astra of Bhaga, and the Śīteṣu and Mānava astras.
20: "Mighty-armed, noble-hearted prince, quickly receive these mighty, shape-shifting astras!"
21: Standing facing the east, purified and self-controlled, the deeply pleased sage then imparted to Rāma that supreme collection of mantras.
22: That brāhmaṇa then handed over to Rāghava all those astras, whose complete mastery is hard to attain even for the devatās.
23: As the wise sage Viśvāmitra chanted the mantras, all those precious astras appeared in person before Rāghava.
24: Joyful and with palms joined in reverence, they all said to Rāma, "Most noble Rāghava, here we are, your servants!"
25: Accepting them and touching them gently with his hand, Kākutstha instructed them, "Come to me whenever I call you to mind."
26: Then, glad at heart, the radiant Rāma bowed in reverence to the great sage Viśvāmitra and prepared to set out.
