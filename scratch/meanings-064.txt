1: Addressed thus by Sugrīva, the Kapi Dadhimukha rejoiced and bowed in salutation to Rāghava, Lakṣmaṇa, and Sugrīva.
2: Bowing to Sugrīva and the two mighty descendants of Raghu, he leapt straight into the sky along with the heroic Vānara guards.
3: Returning just as swiftly as he had come, he dropped down from the sky to the earth and entered that forest.
4: Entering Madhuvana, he saw the Hari troop leaders — sobered from intoxication, brimming with high spirits, discharging the honey-water they had drunk.
5: Approaching them, that hero joined his hands in reverence and addressed Aṅgada with these courteous and cheerful words:
6: "Gentle prince, do not be angry that these guards tried to stop you; out of ignorance and hot temper, they stood in your way.
7: You arrived exhausted from afar — enjoy your own honey! Mighty prince, you are the crown prince and master of this forest.
8: You should forgive the mistake made earlier out of foolishness. For just as your father was formerly the ruler of the Hari host,
9: so too are you, and so is Sugrīva — there is no difference, best among Haris. Faultless one, I went and reported everything to your uncle,
10: telling him of the arrival here of all these forest-dwellers. When he heard of your arrival along with these Hari troop leaders,
11: your uncle Sugrīva, king of the Vānaras, was not angry at all to hear that the forest was plundered; instead, he was overjoyed, and in his joy
12: the king told me, 'Send them all here at once!' Hearing these courteous words from Dadhimukha, Aṅgada,
13: the best among Haris and skilled in speech, said to them: 'Hari troop leaders, I believe Rāma has heard of our news.
14: And this joy displayed by the king makes that clear to me. Therefore, scorchers of foes, now that our mission is accomplished, it is not fitting for us to linger here.
15: You forest-dwellers have drunk honey to your hearts' content and rested; what remains now but to go to where my elder Sugrīva is?
16: Whatever all you Hari troop leaders tell me together, that I will do as our duty; in all matters I am guided by you.
17: Even though I am the crown prince, I have no authority to order you about. Now that you have accomplished the mission, it would be wrong of me to impose upon you.'
18: Hearing these modest and noble words spoken by Aṅgada, the forest-dwellers were delighted at heart and replied:
19: 'Bull among Vānaras, what master speaks like this? For anyone drunk with the pride of power thinks only of himself!
20: Such speech is worthy of you alone, and of no one else; your humility clearly foretells your future greatness and prosperity.
21: All of us are fully prepared to go where Sugrīva, the steadfast master of the Hari heroes, resides.
22: Foremost of Haris, without your word the Haris cannot move a single step anywhere — we tell you the plain truth.'
23: As they spoke thus, Aṅgada replied, 'Yes, let us go,' and leapt up from the ground.
24: As he leapt, all those Hari troop leaders leapt up after him, crowding the sky like flames shooting up from a sacrificial fire.
25: Soaring into the sky with great speed, those Plavaṅgamas roared loudly like clouds driven by the wind.
26: Even before Aṅgada arrived, Sugrīva, the sovereign of the Vānaras,
27: spoke to lotus-eyed Rāma, who was stricken with grief: 'Take heart, may all be well with you! The devī has been found, without doubt.
28: They could never have returned here after the time limit had expired, and from Aṅgada's great joy I know that she has been seen.
29: If the mission had failed, the mighty-armed crown prince Aṅgada, foremost among the leaping hosts, would never have come into my presence.
30: Even if those who failed could behave in this way, he would have had a gloomy face and an anxious, unsettled mind.
31: Madhuvana is ancestral property, protected by my forefathers; that leader of the Plavagas would never have ravaged it unless he were celebrating.'
32: 'Rāma of noble vows, son of Kausalyā, take heart! The devī has been found, without doubt, and by none other than Hanumān.
33: For there is no one else capable of achieving such an undertaking. In Hanumān, foremost among the wise, reside capability, sharp intellect, steadfast resolve, and valor, as surely as radiance resides in the sun.
34: Where Jāmbavān is the guide, Aṅgada commands the force, and Hanumān oversees the task, its success cannot be otherwise.
35: Warrior of boundless valor, do not worry now. For when the forest-dwellers have banded together with such proud exuberance,
36: they would never behave this way if their work were unfinished. From their destruction of the grove and their consuming of the honey, I know it.'
37: Then in the sky close by, he heard the joyful clamor of shouts and cheers.
38: The forest-dwellers, proud of Hanumān's achievement, roared aloud as they approached Kiṣkindhā, as if announcing their triumph.
39: Hearing that roar of the Kapis, Sugrīva, foremost among Kapis, lifted his long tail in excitement and rejoiced at heart.
40: Eager to see Rāma, those Haris arrived, placing Aṅgada and the Vānara Hanumān in the lead.
41: Those heroes led by Aṅgada, overjoyed and filled with delight, landed near the king of Haris and Rāghava.
42: Then the mighty-armed Hanumān bowed his head and reported to Rāghava that the devī was steadfast in her devotion and unharmed.
43: Hearing those nectar-like words 'The devī has been seen' from Hanumān's lips, Rāma, together with Lakṣmaṇa, experienced boundless joy.
44: Seeing that the son of Pavana had confirmed the certainty of the mission, the delighted Lakṣmaṇa looked at the joyful Sugrīva with deep respect.
45: And Rāghava, slayer of enemy heroes, filled with joy and affection, gazed upon Hanumān with immense esteem and admiration.
