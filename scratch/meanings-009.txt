1: Hearing this, Sumantra, the king's charioteer and minister, spoke to him in private: "The course the priests have advised—I once heard an ancient account about this very matter."
2: "Long ago, in the presence of the sages, the revered Sanatkumāra related an account, foretelling how you would be blessed with sons, great king."
3: "Kāśyapa has a son known as Vibhāṇḍaka, and to him will be born a son renowned as Ṛśyaśṛṅga."
4: "That sage, best of brahmins, will grow up in the forest and always move about in the woods. Because he always obeys his father, he will know no one else."
5: "O king, the great soul's brahmacarya will be of two kinds, which people in the worlds know well and which brahmins have always spoken of."
6: "While he lived like this, tending the sacred fire and his renowned father, time went by."
7: "At that very time, the mighty and glorious Lomapāda will become a famous king among the people of Aṅga."
8: "Because of that king's transgression, a terrible and dreadful drought will come, bringing fear to all beings."
9: "When the drought has set in, the king, overcome with sorrow, will gather brahmins who are elders learned in the Vedas and say:"
10: "'You know the dharma from the śāstras and you understand how the world works. Please prescribe a vow and a penance for setting this right.'"
11: "Those brahmins, masters of the Vedas, will tell the king: 'O king, bring Vibhāṇḍaka's son here by every means.'"
12: "'O king, once you have brought Ṛśyaśṛṅga and honored him well, give him your daughter Śāntā in marriage, with full attention to the rite.'"
13: "Hearing their words, the powerful king will fall into thought: 'How can he be brought here?'"
14: "Then the wise king, after settling on a plan with his ministers, will send his priest and his counselors, treating them with honor."
15: "Hearing the king's words, they will be troubled and, with faces bowed, say, 'We won't go, we are afraid of the ṛṣi.' Then they will try to win the king over."
16: "After thinking it over, they will tell him of suitable means: 'We will bring the brahmin here, and no fault will come of it.'"
17: "In this way, through courtesans, the king of Aṅga brought the ṛṣi's son. The devatā sent rain, and Śāntā was given to him."
18: "Ṛśyaśṛṅga, as son-in-law, will give you sons. This is all that I have told you, as Sanatkumāra related it."
19: "Then Daśaratha, delighted, said to Sumantra, 'Tell me in detail how Ṛśyaśṛṅga was brought here.'"
