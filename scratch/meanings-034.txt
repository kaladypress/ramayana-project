1: "Once Brahmadatta was married and had departed, Rāghava, Kuśanābha—who had no son—arranged a sacred yajña to obtain a son.
2: "While the yajña was underway, Kuśa, the son of Brahmā, deeply pleased, spoke to King Kuśanābha:
3: "'My son, you will have a deeply righteous son just like yourself. You will be blessed with Gādhi, and through him you will gain lasting renown in the world.'
4: "After saying this to King Kuśanābha, Rāma, Kuśa rose into the sky and returned to the eternal Brahmaloka.
5: "After some time, a supremely righteous son named Gādhi was born to the wise Kuśanābha.
6: "That supremely righteous Gādhi is my father, Kākutstha. Born in Kuśa's lineage, I am Kauśika, Raghunandana.
7: "I also had an elder sister of steadfast vows, Rāghava, named Satyavatī, who was given in marriage to Ṛcīka.
8: "Ever devoted to her husband, she ascended to svarga in her physical body, and that most noble woman flowed forth on earth as the great river Kauśikī.
9: "Divine, lovely, and filled with sacred waters, my sister flows along the slopes of Himavat for the good of the entire world.
10: "That is why I live happily along the slopes of Himavat, disciplined in my vows and bound by deep affection for my sister Kauśikī, Raghunandana.
11: "Pure, steadfast in truth and dharma, a devoted wife and supremely blessed—that Satyavatī is Kauśikī, foremost among rivers.
12: "Leaving her side to observe my vows, Rāma, I came here; and having reached Siddhāśrama, I have attained my goal through your power.
13: "This, Rāma, is the account of my birth, my lineage, and this region that you asked me about, mighty-armed prince.
14: "Midnight has already passed as I told these stories, Kākutstha. Go to sleep—blessings upon you—so that nothing hinders our journey ahead.
15: "All the trees stand motionless, the animals and birds have tucked themselves away to rest, and the darkness of night fills every direction, Raghunandana.
16: "The evening twilight has gently faded, and the sky—thick with stars and constellations—shines with their lights as though covered with countless eyes.
17: "Now the cool-rayed moon is rising, dispelling the world's darkness and soothing the hearts of all living beings with its soft radiance, mighty prince.
18: "All creatures of the night are now prowling here and there—bands of Yakṣas and Rākṣasas, and fierce flesh-eating spirits."
19: Having spoken thus, the radiant great sage fell silent, and all the assembled munis honored him, exclaiming, "Well spoken! Well spoken!"
20: "This lineage of the Kuśikas is great and always devoted to dharma; the descendants of Kuśa are noble souls equal to Brahmā, noblest of men.
21: "Most of all, you yourself, illustrious Viśvāmitra, and Kauśikī, foremost among rivers, bring radiant glory to your family."
22: Praised by those delighted foremost sages, the glorious son of Kuśa went to sleep, looking like the sun sinking to rest.
23: Rāma too, along with Saumitri, filled with quiet wonder, praised that foremost among munis and drifted off to sleep.
