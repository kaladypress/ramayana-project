1: While Vasiṣṭha was still speaking, King Daśaratha, his face glowing with joy, called his son Rāma, along with Lakṣmaṇa.
2: Rāma's mother and his father Daśaratha had performed the blessing rites for his safe journey. The family priest Vasiṣṭha also consecrated him with auspicious mantras.
3: King Daśaratha kissed his dear son on the head. With a heart full of joy, he handed him over to the son of Kuśika.
4: Then, seeing the lotus-eyed Rāma go with Viśvāmitra, a gentle breeze began to blow, soft to the touch and free of dust.
5: A great shower of flowers fell, and the sound of divine drums rang out. Conches and drums sounded as the great-souled sage set out.
6: Viśvāmitra walked in front. Behind him came the renowned Rāma, bow in hand, with his hair in side-locks, and Saumitri followed after him.
7: Both carrying quivers and bows in hand, lighting up all ten directions, those two noble princes followed the great-souled Viśvāmitra. They were like two three-headed serpents, or like the two Aśvins following Pitāmaha.
8: Wearing finger-guards of lizard skin and carrying swords, the two radiant brothers walked on. They looked like the two sons of Pāvaka, the Kumāras, following the unfathomable god Sthāṇu.
9: After they had gone about a yojana and a half and reached the southern bank of the Sarayū, Viśvāmitra spoke to Rāma in a sweet voice.
10: Take water, child, and let no time be lost. Receive from me the collection of mantras, along with Balā and Atibalā.
11: You won't feel tired or feverish, and your form will never change. The Rākṣasas will never overpower you, whether you are asleep or careless.
12: There is no one on earth equal to you in the strength of your arms, Rāma, and no one in the three worlds will be your equal.
13: No one in the world will be your equal in good fortune, in graciousness, in knowledge, or in firmness of judgment, blameless one. And in answering questions you will never be matched.
14: Once you have gained these two vidyās, there will be no one like you. Balā and Atibalā are the mothers of all knowledge.
15: Hunger and thirst will never trouble you, Rāma, best of men, on the road. Rāghava, as you study and recite Balā and Atibalā, these two vidyās will also bring you unmatched fame on earth.
16: These two vidyās are the daughters of Pitāmaha, full of radiance. Kākutstha, you are truly righteous and worthy of receiving them.
17: You have all good qualities in plenty, and there is no doubt about that. These two vidyās, nurtured through tapas, will take many forms for you.
18: Then Rāma touched the water, pure and with a joyful face, and received the two vidyās from the sage, whose mind was fully refined. Equipped with these vidyās, the very powerful Rāma shone brilliantly.
19: After carrying out all the duties owed to their guru, the three of them, with Kuśika's son, spent that night very comfortably there on the Sarayū.
