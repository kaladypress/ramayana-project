1: Standing in the center of that palace complex, the heroic Kapi, son of Pavana, saw the immense aerial chariot, adorned with gems and diamonds and fashioned with latticework of refined gold.
2: Wrought with immeasurable and matchless skill by Viśvakarmā himself, who deemed it his masterpiece, it was capable of soaring through heaven along the wind's course, shining like a jewel along the path of the sun.
3: There was nothing in it that was not crafted with exquisite care, nothing that was not set with priceless gems, nothing that did not possess supreme distinction — features not found fixed even among the devatās.
4: Won through intense austerity and prowess, it moved wherever the mind directed it. Constructed with countless distinct chambers, it presented an equally marvelous appearance in every part.
5: Moving at tremendous speed at the mere focus of the mind, irresistible and swift as the wind, it seemed like the supreme abode of joy reserved for glorious, great-souled beings of immense merit and prosperity.
6: Supported by extraordinary design and standing in unique grandeur, adorned with numerous wonderful spires, it was delightful to the heart, spotless as the autumn moon, resembling the multi-peaked summit of Mount Citrakūṭa.
7: It was borne by thousands of sky-roaming night-stalkers and swift spirits with huge appetites, their faces adorned with earrings and their wide, rolling eyes ablaze.
8: More delightful to look upon than a profusion of spring blossoms, lovelier even than the season of spring itself, that supreme aerial chariot, the Puṣpaka, was seen there by that best among Vānara heroes.
