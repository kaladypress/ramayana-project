1: Daśaratha was a king of such majesty, a knower of dharma and a great soul. Yet he grieved for want of a son, because he had no son to carry on his line.
2: As he brooded this way, the great king had a thought: "Why don't I perform the aśvamedha sacrifice for the sake of a son?"
3: The wise and righteous king made up his mind that he must offer the sacrifice. He then consulted all his ministers, who were self-disciplined and accomplished.
4: Then the king said to Sumantra, the best of his ministers, "Quickly bring all my gurus here, along with the priests."
5: Sumantra, swift in his movements, went at once. He brought back all those brāhmaṇas, who were masters of the Vedas.
6: They were Suyajña, Vāmadeva, Jābāli and Kāśyapa, the priest Vasiṣṭha, and other eminent brāhmaṇas.
7: The righteous King Daśaratha honored them and then spoke gentle, fitting words that served dharma and purpose.
8: "I grieve deeply for want of a son, and I have no peace of mind. For that reason, I have resolved to perform the Aśvamedha sacrifice."
9: "So I wish to perform it by the rite laid down in the śāstras. Think it over, brāhmaṇas, and tell me how I can achieve it."
10: The brāhmaṇas, with Vasiṣṭha at their head, all praised the king's words as they fell from his lips, saying, "Well said!"
11: Greatly delighted, they all said to Daśaratha, "Let the materials be gathered, and let the horse be set free."
12: "King, you will surely obtain the sons you desire, since your mind has turned to dharma in this way, for the sake of sons."
13: The king was pleased to hear what the brāhmaṇas said. With a joyful heart he spoke to his ministers.
14: "On the gurus' word, let the materials be gathered here. Let the horse also be set free, guarded by a capable protector."
15: "Prepare the sacrificial ground on the north bank of the Sarayū. Let the rites of peace be carried out fully, in the proper manner and by the prescribed rules."
16: "Any king would perform this sacrifice, if only there were no risk of making a disastrous mistake in this supreme rite."
17: "Learned brahmarākṣasas look for any flaw in it. And when a sacrifice is spoiled, the one who performs it is destroyed at once."
18: "So make arrangements so that this rite is completed according to the rules. You are capable of arranging the means."
19: All the ministers, who had been honored, said, "So it will be." They listened to the king's words, just as before.
20: The righteous brāhmaṇas offered their blessings to the king and, taking his leave, departed for their homes.
21: After sending the brāhmaṇas away, the king said to his ministers, "Let the sacrifice be carried out properly, as the priests direct."
22: After saying this to the ministers standing by, the wise tiger among kings dismissed them and went into his own quarters.
23: Then the king went to his wives, who were dear to his heart. He said, "Prepare for the initiation. I am going to perform a sacrifice for the sake of sons."
24: At those words, spoken so tenderly, the radiant faces of the queens shone, like lotuses when the cold season ends.
