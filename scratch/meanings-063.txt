1: Seeing the Vānara fallen prostrate with his head at his feet, the bull among Vānaras became anxious at heart and spoke these words:
2: "Rise, rise! Why have you fallen at my feet? Hero, you need not fear anything — tell me the plain truth!
3: Why are you so distressed and agitated? Tell me what you have to say. Vānara, is all well with Madhuvana? I want to know."
4: Reassured by the great-souled Sugrīva, the highly intelligent Dadhimukha stood up and said:
5: "King, that forest was never thrown open by R̥kṣarajas, nor by Vāli, nor by you. Yet the Vānaras have pillaged it!
6: When the forest guards and I tried to stop them, they paid no attention to me; in their drunken glee, they kept eating the fruits and drinking the honey.
7: Though held back by these forest guards, they attacked them, disregarded them completely, and kept devouring the honey and eating the fruits.
8: Some eat the honey and fling away what is left, while others gorge themselves. When we try to stop them, they knit their eyebrows in anger at us.
9: When these guards tried to keep them out of that forest, those enraged bulls among Vānaras grew even more violent and attacked them fiercely.
10: Then, bull among Vānaras, those many heroic Vānaras, their eyes blood-red with rage, drove our Haris away.
11: Some were struck with fists, some were kicked with knees, dragged about at will, and tossed up into the sky, the path of the devatās.
12: Even while you rule as our master, these brave guards have been beaten this way, and they are devouring all of Madhuvana as they please."
13: As Sugrīva, the bull among Vānaras, was being informed in this manner, the wise Lakṣmaṇa, slayer of enemy heroes, asked him:
14: "King, why has this Vānara forest guard come here? What matter is he referring to, and why is he speaking in such distress?"
15: Addressed thus by the great-souled Lakṣmaṇa, Sugrīva, skilled in speech, replied to Lakṣmaṇa:
16: "Noble Lakṣmaṇa, the heroic Kapi Dadhimukha reports that the heroic Vānaras, led by Aṅgada, have consumed the honey.
17: Those bulls among Haris have returned after searching the southern direction. If their mission had failed, they would never behave like this!
18: The guards who came to stop them were struck down and beaten with knees, and even this powerful Kapi Dadhimukha was completely disregarded.
19: The devī has been found, without a doubt, and by none other than Hanumān — for no one else could have accomplished it, and Dadhimukha was placed as guardian of that forest by ourselves.
20: Indeed, no one other than Hanumān could be the instrument for achieving this feat. In Hanumān reside success in action, sharp intellect, bull among Haris,
21: as well as steadfast resolve, prowess, and sacred learning. Where Jāmbavān is the guide and Aṅgada is commander of the force,
22: and Hanumān oversees the task, its success cannot be otherwise. It is reported that the heroes led by Aṅgada have ransacked Madhuvana.
23: Having searched the southern quarter, those bulls among Haris returned, and as soon as they arrived they burst into Madhuvana.
24: The entire forest was plundered and enjoyed by the Vānaras; the guards who tried to hold them back were attacked together and struck with knees.
25: For this reason, this sweet-spoken Hari of renowned valor, named Dadhimukha, has come here to report what happened.
26: Mighty-armed Saumitri, understand the truth: Sītā has been found! See how all the Vānaras gathered and drank the honey without any fear.
27: Bull among men, if those renowned forest-dwellers had not seen Vaidehī, they would never have dared to ravage this divine forest, protected by a boon."
28: Hearing these words, so sweet to the ear, flowing from Sugrīva's lips, the righteous Lakṣmaṇa and Rāghava were overjoyed.
29: Rāma and the illustrious Lakṣmaṇa rejoiced exceedingly. Having heard Dadhimukha's report, Sugrīva too was delighted, and
30: Sugrīva spoke once more to the forest guard: "Gentle one, I am glad that those heroes who accomplished their mission have enjoyed the forest.
31: The actions of those who have fulfilled their purpose are forgiven and indeed ought to be forgiven. Go quickly, protect Madhuvana yourself, and send all those Kapis led by Hanumān here at once!
32: Along with both descendants of Raghu, I wish to see those branch-roaming Kapis at once — proud as lions with Hanumān at their head, triumphant in their goal — and to hear of their efforts in finding Sītā."
33: Seeing the two princes, their eyes wide with joy and delight at the success of their quest, the king of the Vānaras felt his body thrill with exhilaration; knowing that the mission was fulfilled and within their grasp, he rejoiced exceedingly.
