1: Hearing those words, choked with fatherly affection, Viśvāmitra grew angry and answered the king.
2: Having first promised to grant my request, you now wish to abandon your vow. Such a reversal is unworthy of the Raghu lineage.
3: If this seems right to you, King, I will go back the way I came. Live happily with your kinsmen, Kākutstha, as a man who broke his word!
4: As the wise Viśvāmitra was gripped by wrath, the entire earth trembled, and fear seized the devatās.
5: Seeing the whole world struck with terror, the great sage Vasiṣṭha, steadfast in his vows and calm in mind, spoke to the king.
6: Born in the Ikṣvāku line, you are like dharma itself in person — resolute, faithful to your vows, and illustrious. You must not abandon dharma.
7: Across all three worlds, a descendant of Raghu is renowned as a soul of dharma. Uphold your own dharma — you must not take on adharma.
8: Rāghava, if you promise, "I will do this," and then fail to keep your word, all the merit of your sacrifices and charitable deeds will be lost. Therefore, send Rāma with me.
9: Whether he has mastered astras or not, the Rākṣasas cannot touch him when he is guarded by the son of Kuśika — any more than they could touch amr̥ta surrounded by fire.
10: He is dharma embodied; he is the foremost among the mighty. He surpasses the world in wisdom, and he is the supreme refuge of tapas.
11: He knows every kind of astra in all the three worlds, moving and unmoving. No one else knows them as he does, nor will anyone ever know them.
12: Neither the devatās, nor any sages, nor Asuras, nor Rākṣasas, nor the foremost Gandharvas and Yakṣas, nor Kinnaras and great Nāgas know them.
13: All those astras, the supremely righteous sons of Kr̥śāśva, were given to Kauśika long ago when he ruled his kingdom.
14: Those sons of Kr̥śāśva were born to the daughters of Prajāpati Dakṣa; they take many forms, are immensely powerful, radiant, and bringers of victory.
15: Slender-waisted Jayā and Suprabhā, daughters of Dakṣa, gave birth to a hundred supremely radiant astras and weapons.
16: Long ago, Jayā bore fifty choice sons, immeasurable in power and able to take any form, to destroy the armies of the Asuras.
17: And Suprabhā gave birth to another fifty sons called the Saṁhāras — irresistible, unassailable, and mighty.
18: This son of Kuśika knows all those astras completely, and this knower of dharma is even capable of creating new, unprecedented ones.
19: Such is the power, brilliance, and great tapas of Viśvāmitra. King, you should harbor no doubt about letting Rāma go.
20: The son of Kuśika is fully capable of subduing those Rākṣasas himself; he has come to you and asked for Rāma only for your son's own good.
21: Reassured by the sage's words, that foremost of kings and bull among the Raghus grew glad at heart, and the famed ruler willingly agreed to send Rāghava with the son of Kuśika.
