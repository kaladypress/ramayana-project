1.
निर्वृत्ते तु क्रतौ तस्मिन् हयमेधे महात्मनः |
प्रतिगृह्य सुरा भागान् प्रतिजग्मुर्यथागतम् ||

2.
समाप्तदीक्षानियमः पत्नीगणसमन्वितः |
प्रविवेश पुरीं राजा सभृत्यबलवाहनः ||

3.
यथार्हं पूजितास्तेन राज्ञा च पृथिवीश्वराः |
मुदिताः प्रययुर्देशान् प्रणम्य मुनिपुंगवम् ||

4.
श्रीमतां गच्छतां तेषां स्वगृहाणि पुरात्ततः |
बलानि राज्ञां शुभ्राणि प्रहृष्टानि चकाशिरे ||

5.
गतेषु पृथिवीशेषु राजा दशरथस्तदा |
प्रविवेश पुरीं श्रीमान् पुरस्कृत्य द्विजोत्तमान् ||

6.
शांतया प्रययौ सार्धमृष्यशृङ्गः सुपूजितः |
अनुगम्यमानो राज्ञा च सानुयात्रेण धीमता ||

7.
एवं विसृज्य तान् सर्वान् राजा संपूर्णमानसः |
उवास सुखितस्तत्र पुत्रोत्पत्तिं विचिंतयन् ||

8.
ततो यज्ञे समाप्ते तु ऋतूनां षट् समत्ययुः |
ततश्च द्वादशे मासे चैत्रे नावमिके तिथौ ||

12.
कौसल्या शुशुभे तेन पुत्रेणामिततेजसा |
यथा वरेण देवानामदितिर्वज्रपाणिना ||

13.
भरतो नाम कैकेय्यां जज्ञे सत्यपराक्रमः |
साक्षाद्विष्णोश्चतुर्भागः सर्वैः समुदितो गुणैः ||

15.
अथ लक्ष्मणशत्रुघ्नौ सुमित्राजनयत् सुतौ |
वीरौ सर्वास्त्रकुशलौ विष्णोरर्धसमन्वितौ | १-१८-१४</p>

<p class="pratipada">
14. <em>atha</em> = then; <em>sumitraa</em> = Queen Sumitra; <em>viirau</em> = valiant ones; <em>sarvaastrakushalau</em> 
= experts in all weaponry; <em>viSNoH</em> = Vishnu&#39;s; <em>ardhasamanvitau</em> = those that are embodied with essence 
epitome; <em>lakShmaNashatrughnau</em> = Lakshmana and Shatrughna;
<em>sutau ajanayat</em> = gave birth to sons.</p>

<p class="tat">
Queen Sumitra then gave birth to two sons who are the embodied epitomes of Vishnu, namely 
Lakshmana, and Shatrughna, who are valiant ones and experts in all kinds of weaponry. [1-18-14]</p>

<p class="pratipada">
<em>24b, 25a. teSaam jyeSThaH raamaH</em> = among them the princes Rama is eldest one;
<em>ketuH iva</em> = flagstaff like; <em>pituH bhuyaH ratikaraH</em> = very 
much delightful one to his father; <em>svayambhuuH iva bhuutaanaam sammataH babhuuva</em> = he became acceptable to all beings like
self-emerged [god, Brahma].</p>

<p class="tat">
Among those princes the eldest one Rama is like a flagstaff and a delight of his father Dasharatha, 
and he became acceptable to all beings like the self-created Brahma. [1-18-24b, 25a]</p>

Among them the great resplendent Rama, whose valour itself is his truthfulness, is the dear

Right from childhood Lakshmana, the enhancer of prosperity, is always amiable towards his

प्रणष्टस्य यथा लाभो यथा हर्षो महोदये ||

53.
कं च ते परमं कामं करोमि किमु हर्षितः || १-१८-५२
पात्रभूतोऽसि मे ब्रह्मन् दिष्ट्या प्राप्तोऽसि मानद |<br>
अद्य मे सफलं जन्म जीवितं च सुजीवितम् ||

55.
पूर्वं राजर्षिशब्देन तपसा द्योतितप्रभः || १-१८-५४
ब्रह्मर्षित्वमनुप्राप्तः पूज्योऽसि बहुधा मया |</p>

<p class="pratipada">
54b, 55a. <em>puurvam raajarShishabdena</em>= earlier - originally, 
by kingly-saint title; <em> dyotitaprabhaH</em>= indicated - explicit, is your glory – such as 
you are; <em>tapasaa brahmarShitwam anupraaptaH</em>= you attained Absolute 
sainthood by ascesis; <em>mayaa bahudhaa puujyaH asi</em> = you are venerable in many ways by me.</p>

<p class="tat">
&quot;Originally your glory was explicit by your title kingly-sage, and subsequently you 
attained the Absolute-sainthood by your ascesis, and you are venerable to me, in many 
ways.&quot; [1-18-54b, 55a]</p>
