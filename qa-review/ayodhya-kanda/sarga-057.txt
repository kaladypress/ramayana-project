=== Sarga 57 ===

---
1.
pratibuddho muhur tena śokopahatacetanaḥ |
atha rājā daśarathaḥ sa cintām abhyapadyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
rāmalakṣmaṇayoś caiva vivāsād vāsavopamam |
āviveśopasargas taṁ tamaḥ sūryam ivāsuram ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sa rājā rajanīṁ ṣaṣṭhīṁ rāme pravrajite vanam |
ardharātre daśarathaḥ saṁsmaran duṣkr̥taṁ kr̥tam |
kausalyāṁ putraśokārtām idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
yad ācarati kalyāṇi śubhaṁ vā yadi vāśubham |
tad eva labhate bhadre kartā karmajam ātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
guru lāghavam arthānām ārambhe karmaṇāṁ phalam |
doṣaṁ vā yo na jānāti sa bāla iti hocyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
kaś cid āmravaṇaṁ chittvā palāśāṁś ca niṣiñcati |
puṣpaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā phale gr̥dhnuḥ sa śocati phalāgame ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
so ’ham āmravaṇaṁ chittvā palāśāṁś ca nyaṣecayam |
rāmaṁ phalāgame tyaktvā paścāc chocāmi durmatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
labdhaśabdena kausalye kumāreṇa dhanuṣmatā |
kumāraḥ śabdavedhīti mayā pāpam idaṁ kr̥tam |
tad idaṁ me ’nusaṁprāptaṁ devi duḥkhaṁ svayaṁ kr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
saṁmohād iha bālena yathā syād bhakṣitaṁ viṣam |
evaṁ mamāpy avijñātaṁ śabdavedhyamayaṁ phalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
devy anūḍhā tvam abhavo yuvarājo bhavāmy aham |
tataḥ prāvr̥ḍ anuprāptā madakāmavivardhinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
upāsyahi rasān bhaumāṁs taptvā ca jagad aṁśubhiḥ |
paretācaritāṁ bhīmāṁ ravir āviśate diśam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
uṣṇam antardadhe sadyaḥ snigdhā dadr̥śire ghanāḥ |
tato jahr̥ṣire sarve bhekasāraṅgabarhiṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
patitenāmbhasā channaḥ patamānena cāsakr̥t |
ābabhau mattasāraṅgas toyarāśir ivācalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tasminn atisukhe kāle dhanuṣmān iṣumān rathī |
vyāyāma kr̥tasaṁkalpaḥ sarayūm anvagāṁ nadīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
nipāne mahiṣaṁ rātrau gajaṁ vābhyāgataṁ nadīm |
anyaṁ vā śvāpadaṁ kaṁ cij jighāṁsur ajitendriyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
athāndhakāre tv aśrauṣaṁ jale kumbhasya paryataḥ |
acakṣur viṣaye ghoṣaṁ vāraṇasyeva nardataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tato ’haṁ śaram uddhr̥tya dīptam āśīviṣopamam |
amuñcaṁ niśitaṁ bāṇam aham āśīviṣopamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tatra vāg uṣasi vyaktā prādurāsīd vanaukasaḥ |
hā heti patatas toye vāg abhūt tatra mānuṣī |
katham asmadvidhe śastraṁ nipatet tu tapasvini ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
praviviktāṁ nadīṁ rātrāv udāhāro ’ham āgataḥ |
iṣuṇābhihataḥ kena kasya vā kiṁ kr̥taṁ mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
r̥ṣer hi nyasta daṇḍasya vane vanyena jīvataḥ |
kathaṁ nu śastreṇa vadho madvidhasya vidhīyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
jaṭābhāradharasyaiva valkalājinavāsasaḥ |
ko vadhena mamārthī syāt kiṁ vāsyāpakr̥taṁ mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
evaṁ niṣphalam ārabdhaṁ kevalānarthasaṁhitam |
na kaś cit sādhu manyeta yathaiva gurutalpagam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
nemaṁ tathānuśocāmi jīvitakṣayam ātmanaḥ |
mātaraṁ pitaraṁ cobhāv anuśocāmi madvidhe ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tad etān mithunaṁ vr̥ddhaṁ cirakālabhr̥taṁ mayā |
mayi pañcatvam āpanne kāṁ vr̥ttiṁ vartayiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
vr̥ddhau ca mātāpitarāv ahaṁ caikeṣuṇā hataḥ |
kena sma nihatāḥ sarve subālenākr̥tātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
taṁ giraṁ karuṇāṁ śrutvā mama dharmānukāṅkṣiṇaḥ |
karābhyāṁ saśaraṁ cāpaṁ vyathitasyāpatad bhuvi ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
taṁ deśam aham āgamya dīnasattvaḥ sudurmanāḥ |
apaśyam iṣuṇā tīre sarayvās tāpasaṁ hatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
sa mām udvīkṣya netrābhyāṁ trastam asvasthacetasam |
ity uvāca vacaḥ krūraṁ didhakṣann iva tejasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
kiṁ tavāpakr̥taṁ rājan vane nivasatā mayā |
jihīrṣur ambho gurvarthaṁ yad ahaṁ tāḍitas tvayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
ekena khalu bāṇena marmaṇy abhihate mayi |
dvāv andhau nihatau vr̥ddhau mātā janayitā ca me ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
tau nūnaṁ durbalāv andhau matpratīkṣau pipāsitau |
ciram āśākr̥tāṁ tr̥ṣṇāṁ kaṣṭāṁ saṁdhārayiṣyataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
na nūnaṁ tapaso vāsti phalayogaḥ śrutasya vā |
pitā yan māṁ na jānāti śayānaṁ patitaṁ bhuvi ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
jānann api ca kiṁ kuryād aśaktir aparikramaḥ |
bhidyamānam ivāśaktas trātum anyo nago nagam ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
pitus tvam eva me gatvā śīghram ācakṣva rāghava |
na tvām anudahet kruddho vanaṁ vahnir ivaidhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
iyam ekapadī rājan yato me pitur āśramaḥ |
taṁ prasādaya gatvā tvaṁ na tvāṁ sa kupitaḥ śapet ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
viśalyaṁ kuru māṁ rājan marma me niśitaḥ śaraḥ |
ruṇaddhi mr̥du sotsedhaṁ tīram amburayo yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
na dvijātir ahaṁ rājan mā bhūt te manaso vyathā |
śūdrāyām asmi vaiśyena jāto janapadādhipa ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
itīva vadataḥ kr̥cchrād bāṇābhihatamarmaṇaḥ |
tasya tv ānamyamānasya taṁ bāṇam aham uddharam ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
jalārdragātraṁ tu vilapya kr̥cchān; marmavraṇaṁ saṁtatam ucchasantam |
tataḥ sarayvāṁ tam ahaṁ śayānaṁ; samīkṣya bhadre subhr̥śaṁ viṣaṇṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
