=== Sarga 6 ===

---
1.
gate purohite rāmaḥ snāto niyatamānasaḥ |
saha patnyā viśālākṣyā nārāyaṇam upāgamat ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
pragr̥hya śirasā pātrīṁ haviṣo vidhivat tadā |
mahate daivatāyājyaṁ juhāva jvalite ’nale ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
śeṣaṁ ca haviṣas tasya prāśyāśāsyātmanaḥ priyam |
dhyāyan nārāyaṇaṁ devaṁ svāstīrṇe kuśasaṁstare ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
vāgyataḥ saha vaidehyā bhūtvā niyatamānasaḥ |
śrīmaty āyatane viṣṇoḥ śiśye naravarātmajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
ekayāmāvaśiṣṭāyāṁ rātryāṁ prativibudhya saḥ |
alaṁkāravidhiṁ kr̥tsnaṁ kārayām āsa veśmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tatra śr̥ṇvan sukhā vācaḥ sūtamāgadhabandinām |
pūrvāṁ saṁdhyām upāsīno jajāpa yatamānasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tuṣṭāva praṇataś caiva śirasā madhusūdanam |
vimalakṣaumasaṁvīto vācayām āsa ca dvijān ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
teṣāṁ puṇyāhaghoṣo ’tha gambhīramadhuras tadā |
ayodhyāṁ pūrayām āsa tūryaghoṣānunāditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
kr̥topavāsaṁ tu tadā vaidehyā saha rāghavam |
ayodhyā nilayaḥ śrutvā sarvaḥ pramudito janaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tataḥ paurajanaḥ sarvaḥ śrutvā rāmābhiṣecanam |
prabhātāṁ rajanīṁ dr̥ṣṭvā cakre śobhāṁ parāṁ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sitābhraśikharābheṣu devatāyataneṣu ca |
catuṣpatheṣu rathyāsu caityeṣv aṭṭālakeṣu ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
nānāpaṇyasamr̥ddheṣu vaṇijām āpaṇeṣu ca |
kuṭumbināṁ samr̥ddheṣu śrīmatsu bhavaneṣu ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sabhāsu caiva sarvāsu vr̥kṣeṣv ālakṣiteṣu ca |
dhvajāḥ samucchritāś citrāḥ patākāś cābhavaṁs tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
naṭanartakasaṁghānāṁ gāyakānāṁ ca gāyatām |
manaḥkarṇasukhā vācaḥ śuśruvuś ca tatas tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
rāmābhiṣekayuktāś ca kathāś cakrur mitho janāḥ |
rāmābhiṣeke saṁprāpte catvareṣu gr̥heṣu ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
bālā api krīḍamānā gr̥hadvāreṣu saṁghaśaḥ |
rāmābhiṣekasaṁyuktāś cakrur eva mithaḥ kathāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
kr̥tapuṣpopahāraś ca dhūpagandhādhivāsitaḥ |
rājamārgaḥ kr̥taḥ śrīmān paurai rāmābhiṣecane ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
prakāśīkaraṇārthaṁ ca niśāgamanaśaṅkayā |
dīpavr̥kṣāṁs tathā cakrur anu rathyāsu sarvaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
alaṁkāraṁ purasyaivaṁ kr̥tvā tat puravāsinaḥ |
ākāṅkṣamāṇā rāmasya yauvarājyābhiṣecanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
sametya saṁghaśaḥ sarve catvareṣu sabhāsu ca |
kathayanto mithas tatra praśaśaṁsur janādhipam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
aho mahātmā rājāyam ikṣvākukulanandanaḥ |
jñātvā yo vr̥ddham ātmānaṁ rāmaṁ rājye ’bhiṣekṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
sarve hy anugr̥hītāḥ sma yan no rāmo mahīpatiḥ |
cirāya bhavitā goptā dr̥ṣṭalokaparāvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
anuddhatamanā vidvān dharmātmā bhrātr̥vatsalaḥ |
yathā ca bhrātr̥ṣu snigdhas tathāsmāsv api rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
ciraṁ jīvatu dharmātmā rājā daśaratho ’naghaḥ |
yatprasādenābhiṣiktaṁ rāmaṁ drakṣyāmahe vayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
evaṁvidhaṁ kathayatāṁ paurāṇāṁ śuśruvus tadā |
digbhyo ’pi śrutavr̥ttāntāḥ prāptā jānapadā janāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
te tu digbhyaḥ purīṁ prāptā draṣṭuṁ rāmābhiṣecanam |
rāmasya pūrayām āsuḥ purīṁ jānapadā janāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
janaughais tair visarpadbhiḥ śuśruve tatra niḥsvanaḥ |
parvasūdīrṇavegasya sāgarasyeva niḥsvanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tatas tad indrakṣayasaṁnibhaṁ puraṁ; didr̥kṣubhir jānapadair upāgataiḥ |
samantataḥ sasvanam ākulaṁ babhau; samudrayādobhir ivārṇavodakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
