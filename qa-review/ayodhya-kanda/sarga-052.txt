=== Sarga 52 ===

---
1.
pratyāśvasto yadā rājā mohāt pratyāgataḥ punaḥ |
athājuhāva taṁ sūtaṁ rāmavr̥ttāntakāraṇāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
vr̥ddhaṁ paramasaṁtaptaṁ navagraham iva dvipam |
viniḥśvasantaṁ dhyāyantam asvastham iva kuñjaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
rājā tu rajasā sūtaṁ dhvastāṅgaṁ samupasthitam |
aśrupūrṇamukhaṁ dīnam uvāca paramārtavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
kva nu vatsyati dharmātmā vr̥kṣamūlam upāśritaḥ |
so ’tyantasukhitaḥ sūta kim aśiṣyati rāghavaḥ |
bhūmipālātmajo bhūmau śete katham anāthavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
yaṁ yāntam anuyānti sma padātirathakuñjarāḥ |
sa vatsyati kathaṁ rāmo vijanaṁ vanam āśritaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
vyālair mr̥gair ācaritaṁ kr̥ṣṇasarpaniṣevitam |
kathaṁ kumārau vaidehyā sārdhaṁ vanam upasthitau ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sukumāryā tapasvinyā sumantra saha sītayā |
rājaputrau kathaṁ pādair avaruhya rathād gatau ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
siddhārthaḥ khalu sūta tvaṁ yena dr̥ṣṭau mamātmajau |
vanāntaṁ praviśantau tāv aśvināv iva mandaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
kim uvāca vaco rāmaḥ kim uvāca ca lakṣmaṇaḥ |
sumantra vanam āsādya kim uvāca ca maithilī |
āsitaṁ śayitaṁ bhuktaṁ sūta rāmasya kīrtaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
iti sūto narendreṇa coditaḥ sajjamānayā |
uvāca vācā rājānaṁ sabāṣpaparirabdhayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
abravīn māṁ mahārāja dharmam evānupālayan |
añjaliṁ rāghavaḥ kr̥tvā śirasābhipraṇamya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sūta madvacanāt tasya tātasya viditātmanaḥ |
śirasā vandanīyasya vandyau pādau mahātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sarvam antaḥpuraṁ vācyaṁ sūta madvacanāt tvayā |
ārogyam aviśeṣeṇa yathārhaṁ cābhivādanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
mātā ca mama kausalyā kuśalaṁ cābhivādanam |
devi devasya pādau ca devavat paripālaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
bharataḥ kuśalaṁ vācyo vācyo madvacanena ca |
sarvāsv eva yathānyāyaṁ vr̥ttiṁ vartasva mātr̥ṣu ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
vaktavyaś ca mahābāhur ikṣvākukulanandanaḥ |
pitaraṁ yauvarājyastho rājyastham anupālaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
ity evaṁ māṁ mahārāja bruvann eva mahāyaśāḥ |
rāmo rājīvatāmrākṣo bhr̥śam aśrūṇy avartayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
lakṣmaṇas tu susaṁkruddho niḥśvasan vākyam abravīt |
kenāyam aparādhena rājaputro vivāsitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
yadi pravrājito rāmo lobhakāraṇakāritam |
varadānanimittaṁ vā sarvathā duṣkr̥taṁ kr̥tam |
rāmasya tu parityāge na hetum upalakṣaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
asamīkṣya samārabdhaṁ viruddhaṁ buddhilāghavāt |
janayiṣyati saṁkrośaṁ rāghavasya vivāsanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
ahaṁ tāvan mahārāje pitr̥tvaṁ nopalakṣaye |
bhrātā bhartā ca bandhuś ca pitā ca mama rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
sarvalokapriyaṁ tyaktvā sarvalokahite ratam |
sarvaloko ’nurajyeta kathaṁ tvānena karmaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
jānakī tu mahārāja niḥśvasantī tapasvinī |
bhūtopahatacitteva viṣṭhitā viṣmr̥tā sthitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
adr̥ṣṭapūrvavyasanā rājaputrī yaśasvinī |
tena duḥkhena rudatī naiva māṁ kiṁ cid abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
udvīkṣamāṇā bhartāraṁ mukhena pariśuṣyatā |
mumoca sahasā bāṣpaṁ māṁ prayāntam udīkṣya sā ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tathaiva rāmo ’śrumukhaḥ kr̥tāñjaliḥ; sthito ’bhaval lakṣmaṇabāhupālitaḥ |
tathaiva sītā rudatī tapasvinī; nirīkṣate rājarathaṁ tathaiva mām ||

=== meaning ===

---
