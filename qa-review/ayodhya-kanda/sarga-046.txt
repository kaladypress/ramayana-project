=== Sarga 46 ===

---
1.
prabhātāyāṁ tu śarvaryāṁ pr̥thu vr̥kṣā mahāyaśāḥ |
uvāca rāmaḥ saumitriṁ lakṣmaṇaṁ śubhalakṣaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
bhāskarodayakālo ’yaṁ gatā bhagavatī niśā |
asau sukr̥ṣṇo vihagaḥ kokilas tāta kūjati ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
barhiṇānāṁ ca nirghoṣaḥ śrūyate nadatāṁ vane |
tarāma jāhnavīṁ saumya śīghragāṁ sāgaraṁgamām ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
vijñāya rāmasya vacaḥ saumitrir mitranandanaḥ |
guham āmantrya sūtaṁ ca so ’tiṣṭhad bhrātur agrataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tataḥ kalāpān saṁnahya khaḍgau baddhvā ca dhanvinau |
jagmatur yena tau gaṅgāṁ sītayā saha rāghavau ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
rāmam eva tu dharmajñam upagamya vinītavat |
kim ahaṁ karavāṇīti sūtaḥ prāñjalir abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
nivartasvety uvācainam etāvad dhi kr̥taṁ mama |
yānaṁ vihāya padbhyāṁ tu gamiṣyāmo mahāvanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
ātmānaṁ tv abhyanujñātam avekṣyārtaḥ sa sārathiḥ |
sumantraḥ puruṣavyāghram aikṣvākam idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
nātikrāntam idaṁ loke puruṣeṇeha kena cit |
tava sabhrātr̥bhāryasya vāsaḥ prākr̥tavad vane ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
na manye brahmacarye ’sti svadhīte vā phalodayaḥ |
mārdavārjavayor vāpi tvāṁ ced vyasanam āgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
saha rāghava vaidehyā bhrātrā caiva vane vasan |
tvaṁ gatiṁ prāpsyase vīra trīm̐l lokāṁs tu jayann iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
vayaṁ khalu hatā rāma ye tayāpy upavañcitāḥ |
kaikeyyā vaśam eṣyāmaḥ pāpāyā duḥkhabhāginaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
iti bruvann ātma samaṁ sumantraḥ sārathis tadā |
dr̥ṣṭvā dura gataṁ rāmaṁ duḥkhārto rurude ciram ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tatas tu vigate bāṣpe sūtaṁ spr̥ṣṭodakaṁ śucim |
rāmas tu madhuraṁ vākyaṁ punaḥ punar uvāca tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
ikṣvākūṇāṁ tvayā tulyaṁ suhr̥daṁ nopalakṣaye |
yathā daśaratho rājā māṁ na śocet tathā kuru ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
śokopahata cetāś ca vr̥ddhaś ca jagatīpatiḥ |
kāma bhārāvasannaś ca tasmād etad bravīmi te ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
yad yad ājñāpayet kiṁ cit sa mahātmā mahīpatiḥ |
kaikeyyāḥ priyakāmārthaṁ kāryaṁ tad avikāṅkṣayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
etadarthaṁ hi rājyāni praśāsati nareśvarāḥ |
yad eṣāṁ sarvakr̥tyeṣu mano na pratihanyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tad yathā sa mahārājo nālīkam adhigacchati |
na ca tāmyati duḥkhena sumantra kuru tat tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
adr̥ṣṭaduḥkhaṁ rājānaṁ vr̥ddham āryaṁ jitendriyam |
brūyās tvam abhivādyaiva mama hetor idaṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
naivāham anuśocāmi lakṣmaṇo na ca maithilī |
ayodhyāyāś cyutāś ceti vane vatsyāmaheti vā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
caturdaśasu varṣeṣu nivr̥tteṣu punaḥ punaḥ |
lakṣmaṇaṁ māṁ ca sītāṁ ca drakṣyasi kṣipram āgatān ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
evam uktvā tu rājānaṁ mātaraṁ ca sumantra me |
anyāś ca devīḥ sahitāḥ kaikeyīṁ ca punaḥ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
ārogyaṁ brūhi kausalyām atha pādābhivandanam |
sītāyā mama cāryasya vacanāl lakṣmaṇasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
brūyāś ca hi mahārājaṁ bharataṁ kṣipram ānaya |
āgataś cāpi bharataḥ sthāpyo nr̥pamate pade ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
bharataṁ ca pariṣvajya yauvarājye ’bhiṣicya ca |
asmatsaṁtāpajaṁ duḥkhaṁ na tvām abhibhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
bharataś cāpi vaktavyo yathā rājani vartase |
tathā mātr̥ṣu vartethāḥ sarvāsv evāviśeṣataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
yathā ca tava kaikeyī sumitrā cāviśeṣataḥ |
tathaiva devī kausalyā mama mātā viśeṣataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
nivartyamāno rāmeṇa sumantraḥ śokakarśitaḥ |
tat sarvaṁ vacanaṁ śrutvā snehāt kākutstham abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
yad ahaṁ nopacāreṇa brūyāṁ snehād aviklavaḥ |
bhaktimān iti tat tāvad vākyaṁ tvaṁ kṣantum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
kathaṁ hi tvadvihīno ’haṁ pratiyāsyāmi tāṁ purīm |
tava tāta viyogena putraśokākulām iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
sarāmam api tāvan me rathaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā tadā janaḥ |
vinā rāmaṁ rathaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā vidīryetāpi sā purī ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
dainyaṁ hi nagarī gacched dr̥ṣṭvā śūnyam imaṁ ratham |
sūtāvaśeṣaṁ svaṁ sainyaṁ hatavīram ivāhave ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
dūre ’pi nivasantaṁ tvāṁ mānasenāgrataḥ sthitam |
cintayantyo ’dya nūnaṁ tvāṁ nirāhārāḥ kr̥tāḥ prajāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
ārtanādo hi yaḥ paurair muktas tadvipravāsane |
rathasthaṁ māṁ niśāmyaiva kuryuḥ śataguṇaṁ tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
ahaṁ kiṁ cāpi vakṣyāmi devīṁ tava suto mayā |
nīto ’sau mātulakulaṁ saṁtāpaṁ mā kr̥thā iti ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
asatyam api naivāhaṁ brūyāṁ vacanam īdr̥śam |
katham apriyam evāhaṁ brūyāṁ satyam idaṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
mama tāvan niyogasthās tvadbandhujanavāhinaḥ |
kathaṁ rathaṁ tvayā hīnaṁ pravakṣyanti hayottamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
yadi me yācamānasya tyāgam eva kariṣyasi |
saratho ’gniṁ pravekṣyāmi tyakta mātra iha tvayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
bhaviṣyanti vane yāni tapovighnakarāṇi te |
rathena pratibādhiṣye tāni sattvāni rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
tat kr̥tena mayā prāptaṁ ratha caryā kr̥taṁ sukham |
āśaṁse tvatkr̥tenāhaṁ vanavāsakr̥taṁ sukham ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
prasīdecchāmi te ’raṇye bhavituṁ pratyanantaraḥ |
prītyābhihitam icchāmi bhava me patyanantaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
tava śuśrūṣaṇaṁ mūrdhnā kariṣyāmi vane vasan |
ayodhyāṁ devalokaṁ vā sarvathā prajahāmy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
na hi śakyā praveṣṭuṁ sā mayāyodhyā tvayā vinā |
rājadhānī mahendrasya yathā duṣkr̥takarmaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
ime cāpi hayā vīra yadi te vanavāsinaḥ |
paricaryāṁ kariṣyanti prāpsyanti paramāṁ gatim ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
vanavāse kṣayaṁ prāpte mamaiṣa hi manorathaḥ |
yad anena rathenaiva tvāṁ vaheyaṁ purīṁ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
caturdaśa hi varṣāṇi sahitasya tvayā vane |
kṣaṇabhūtāni yāsyanti śataśas tu tato ’nyathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
bhr̥tyavatsala tiṣṭhantaṁ bhartr̥putragate pathi |
bhaktaṁ bhr̥tyaṁ sthitaṁ sthityāṁ tvaṁ na māṁ hātum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
evaṁ bahuvidhaṁ dīnaṁ yācamānaṁ punaḥ punaḥ |
rāmo bhr̥tyānukampī tu sumantram idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
jānāmi paramāṁ bhaktiṁ mayi te bhartr̥vatsala |
śr̥ṇu cāpi yadarthaṁ tvāṁ preṣayāmi purīm itaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
nagarīṁ tvāṁ gataṁ dr̥ṣṭvā jananī me yavīyasī |
kaikeyī pratyayaṁ gacched iti rāmo vanaṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
52.
parituṣṭā hi sā devi vanavāsaṁ gate mayi |
rājānaṁ nātiśaṅketa mithyāvādīti dhārmikam ||

=== meaning ===

---
53.
eṣa me prathamaḥ kalpo yad ambā me yavīyasī |
bharatārakṣitaṁ sphītaṁ putrarājyam avāpnuyāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
54.
mama priyārthaṁ rājñaś ca sarathas tvaṁ purīṁ vraja |
saṁdiṣṭaś cāsi yān arthāṁs tāṁs tān brūyās tathātathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
55.
ity uktvā vacanaṁ sūtaṁ sāntvayitvā punaḥ punaḥ |
guhaṁ vacanam aklībaṁ rāmo hetumad abravīt |
jaṭāḥ kr̥tvā gamiṣyāmi nyagrodhakṣīram ānaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
56.
tat kṣīraṁ rājaputrāya guhaḥ kṣipram upāharat |
lakṣmaṇasyātmanaś caiva rāmas tenākaroj jaṭāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
57.
tau tadā cīravasanau jaṭāmaṇḍaladhāriṇau |
aśobhetām r̥ṣisamau bhrātarau rāmalakṣmaṇau ||

=== meaning ===

---
58.
tato vaikhānasaṁ mārgam āsthitaḥ sahalakṣmaṇaḥ |
vratam ādiṣṭavān rāmaḥ sahāyaṁ guham abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
59.
apramatto bale kośe durge janapade tathā |
bhavethā guha rājyaṁ hi durārakṣatamaṁ matam ||

=== meaning ===

---
60.
tatas taṁ samanujñāya guham ikṣvākunandanaḥ |
jagāma tūrṇam avyagraḥ sabhāryaḥ sahalakṣmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
61.
sa tu dr̥ṣṭvā nadītīre nāvam ikṣvākunandanaḥ |
titīrṣuḥ śīghragāṁ gaṅgām idaṁ lakṣmaṇam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
62.
āroha tvaṁ nara vyāghra sthitāṁ nāvam imāṁ śanaiḥ |
sītāṁ cāropayānvakṣaṁ parigr̥hya manasvinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
63.
sa bhrātuḥ śāsanaṁ śrutvā sarvam apratikūlayan |
āropya maithilīṁ pūrvam ārurohātmavāṁs tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
64.
athāruroha tejasvī svayaṁ lakṣmaṇapūrvajaḥ |
tato niṣādādhipatir guho jñātīn acodayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
65.
anujñāya sumantraṁ ca sabalaṁ caiva taṁ guham |
āsthāya nāvaṁ rāmas tu codayām āsa nāvikān ||

=== meaning ===

---
66.
tatas taiś coditā sā nauḥ karṇadhārasamāhitā |
śubhasphyavegābhihatā śīghraṁ salilam atyagāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
67.
madhyaṁ tu samanuprāpya bhāgīrathyās tv aninditā |
vaidehī prāñjalir bhūtvā tāṁ nadīm idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
68.
putro daśarathasyāyaṁ mahārājasya dhīmataḥ |
nideśaṁ pālayatv enaṁ gaṅge tvadabhirakṣitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
69.
caturdaśa hi varṣāṇi samagrāṇy uṣya kānane |
bhrātrā saha mayā caiva punaḥ pratyāgamiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
70.
tatas tvāṁ devi subhage kṣemeṇa punar āgatā |
yakṣye pramuditā gaṅge sarvakāmasamr̥ddhaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
71.
tvaṁ hi tripathagā devi brahma lokaṁ samīkṣase |
bhāryā codadhirājasya loke ’smin saṁpradr̥śyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
72.
sā tvāṁ devi namasyāmi praśaṁsāmi ca śobhane |
prāpta rājye naravyāghra śivena punar āgate ||

=== meaning ===

---
73.
gavāṁ śatasahasrāṇi vastrāṇy annaṁ ca peśalam |
brāhmaṇebhyaḥ pradāsyāmi tava priyacikīrṣayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
74.
tathā saṁbhāṣamāṇā sā sītā gaṅgām aninditā |
dakṣiṇā dakṣiṇaṁ tīraṁ kṣipram evābhyupāgamat ||

=== meaning ===

---
75.
tīraṁ tu samanuprāpya nāvaṁ hitvā nararṣabhaḥ |
prātiṣṭhata saha bhrātrā vaidehyā ca paraṁtapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
76.
athābravīn mahābāhuḥ sumitrānandavardhanam |
agrato gaccha saumitre sītā tvām anugacchatu ||

=== meaning ===

---
77.
pr̥ṣṭhato ’haṁ gamiṣyāmi tvāṁ ca sītāṁ ca pālayan |
adya duḥkhaṁ tu vaidehī vanavāsasya vetsyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
78.
gataṁ tu gaṅgāparapāram āśu; rāmaṁ sumantraḥ pratataṁ nirīkṣya |
adhvaprakarṣād vinivr̥ttadr̥ṣṭir; mumoca bāṣpaṁ vyathitas tapasvī ||

=== meaning ===

---
79.
tau tatra hatvā caturo mahāmr̥gān; varāham r̥śyaṁ pr̥ṣataṁ mahārurum |
ādāya medhyaṁ tvaritaṁ bubhukṣitau; vāsāya kāle yayatur vanaspatim ||

=== meaning ===

---
