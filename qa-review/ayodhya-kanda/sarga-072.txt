=== Sarga 72 ===

---
1.
atra yātrāṁ samīhantaṁ śatrughno lakṣmaṇānujaḥ |
bharataṁ śokasaṁtaptam idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
gatir yaḥ sarvabhūtānāṁ duḥkhe kiṁ punar ātmanaḥ |
sa rāmaḥ sattva saṁpannaḥ striyā pravrājito vanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
balavān vīrya saṁpanno lakṣmaṇo nāma yo ’py asau |
kiṁ na mocayate rāmaṁ kr̥tvāpi pitr̥nigraham ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
pūrvam eva tu nigrāhyaḥ samavekṣya nayānayau |
utpathaṁ yaḥ samārūḍho nāryā rājā vaśaṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
iti saṁbhāṣamāṇe tu śatrughne lakṣmaṇānuje |
prāgdvāre ’bhūt tadā kubjā sarvābharaṇabhūṣitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
liptā candanasāreṇa rājavastrāṇi bibhratī |
mekhalā dāmabhiś citrai rajjubaddheva vānarī ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tāṁ samīkṣya tadā dvāḥstho bhr̥śaṁ pāpasya kāriṇīm |
gr̥hītvākaruṇaṁ kubjāṁ śatrughnāya nyavedayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
yasyāḥ kr̥te vane rāmo nyastadehaś ca vaḥ pitā |
seyaṁ pāpā nr̥śaṁsā ca tasyāḥ kuru yathāmati ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
śatrughnaś ca tad ājñāya vacanaṁ bhr̥śaduḥkhitaḥ |
antaḥpuracarān sarvān ity uvāca dhr̥tavrataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tīvram utpāditaṁ duḥkhaṁ bhrātr̥̄ṇāṁ me tathā pituḥ |
yayā seyaṁ nr̥śaṁsasya karmaṇaḥ phalam aśnutām ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
evam uktā ca tenāśu sakhī janasamāvr̥tā |
gr̥hītā balavat kubjā sā tadgr̥ham anādayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tataḥ subhr̥śa saṁtaptas tasyāḥ sarvaḥ sakhījanaḥ |
kruddham ājñāya śatrughnaṁ vyapalāyata sarvaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
amantrayata kr̥tsnaś ca tasyāḥ sarvasakhījanaḥ |
yathāyaṁ samupakrānto niḥśeṣaṁ naḥ kariṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sānukrośāṁ vadānyāṁ ca dharmajñāṁ ca yaśasvinīm |
kausalyāṁ śaraṇaṁ yāmaḥ sā hi no ’stu dhruvā gatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa ca roṣeṇa tāmrākṣaḥ śatrughnaḥ śatrutāpanaḥ |
vicakarṣa tadā kubjāṁ krośantīṁ pr̥thivītale ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tasyā hy ākr̥ṣyamāṇāyā mantharāyās tatas tataḥ |
citraṁ bahuvidhaṁ bhāṇḍaṁ pr̥thivyāṁ tad vyaśīryata ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tena bhāṇḍena saṁkīrṇaṁ śrīmadrājaniveśanam |
aśobhata tadā bhūyaḥ śāradaṁ gaganaṁ yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
sa balī balavat krodhād gr̥hītvā puruṣarṣabhaḥ |
kaikeyīm abhinirbhartsya babhāṣe paruṣaṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tair vākyaiḥ paruṣair duḥkhaiḥ kaikeyī bhr̥śaduḥkhitā |
śatrughna bhayasaṁtrastā putraṁ śaraṇam āgatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tāṁ prekṣya bharataḥ kruddhaṁ śatrughnam idam abravīt |
avadhyāḥ sarvabhūtānāṁ pramadāḥ kṣamyatām iti ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
hanyām aham imāṁ pāpāṁ kaikeyīṁ duṣṭacāriṇīm |
yadi māṁ dhārmiko rāmo nāsūyen mātr̥ghātakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
imām api hatāṁ kubjāṁ yadi jānāti rāghavaḥ |
tvāṁ ca māṁ caiva dharmātmā nābhibhāṣiṣyate dhruvam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
bharatasya vacaḥ śrutvā śatrughno lakṣmaṇānujaḥ |
nyavartata tato roṣāt tāṁ mumoca ca mantharām ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
sā pādamūle kaikeyyā mantharā nipapāta ha |
niḥśvasantī suduḥkhārtā kr̥paṇaṁ vilalāpa ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
śatrughnavikṣepavimūḍhasaṁjñāṁ; samīkṣya kubjāṁ bharatasya mātā |
śanaiḥ samāśvāsayad ārtarūpāṁ; krauñcīṁ vilagnām iva vīkṣamāṇām ||

=== meaning ===

---
