=== Sarga 51 ===

---
1.
kathayitvā suduḥkhārtaḥ sumantreṇa ciraṁ saha |
rāme dakṣiṇa kūlasthe jagāma svagr̥haṁ guhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
anujñātaḥ sumantro ’tha yojayitvā hayottamān |
ayodhyām eva nagarīṁ prayayau gāḍhadurmanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sa vanāni sugandhīni saritaś ca sarāṁsi ca |
paśyann atiyayau śīghraṁ grāmāṇi nagarāṇi ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tataḥ sāyāhnasamaye tr̥tīye ’hani sārathiḥ |
ayodhyāṁ samanuprāpya nirānandāṁ dadarśa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
sa śūnyām iva niḥśabdāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā paramadurmanāḥ |
sumantraś cintayām āsa śokavegasamāhataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
kaccin na sagajā sāśvā sajanā sajanādhipā |
rāmasaṁtāpaduḥkhena dagdhā śokāgninā purī |
iti cintāparaḥ sūtas tvaritaḥ praviveśa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sumantram abhiyāntaṁ taṁ śataśo ’tha sahasraśaḥ |
kva rāma iti pr̥cchantaḥ sūtam abhyadravan narāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
teṣāṁ śaśaṁsa gaṅgāyām aham āpr̥cchya rāghavam |
anujñāto nivr̥tto ’smi dhārmikeṇa mahātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
te tīrṇā iti vijñāya bāṣpapūrṇamukhā janāḥ |
aho dhig iti niḥśvasya hā rāmeti ca cukruśuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
śuśrāva ca vacas teṣāṁ vr̥ndaṁ vr̥ndaṁ ca tiṣṭhatām |
hatāḥ sma khalu ye neha paśyāma iti rāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
dānayajñavivāheṣu samājeṣu mahatsu ca |
na drakṣyāmaḥ punar jātu dhārmikaṁ rāmam antarā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
kiṁ samarthaṁ janasyāsya kiṁ priyaṁ kiṁ sukhāvaham |
iti rāmeṇa nagaraṁ pitr̥vat paripālitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
vātāyanagatānāṁ ca strīṇām anvantarāpaṇam |
rāmaśokābhitaptānāṁ śuśrāva paridevanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sa rājamārgamadhyena sumantraḥ pihitānanaḥ |
yatra rājā daśarathas tad evopayayau gr̥ham ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
so ’vatīrya rathāc chīghraṁ rājaveśma praviśya ca |
kakṣyāḥ saptābhicakrāma mahājanasamākulāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tato daśarathastrīṇāṁ prāsādebhyas tatas tataḥ |
rāmaśokābhitaptānāṁ mandaṁ śuśrāva jalpitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
saha rāmeṇa niryāto vinā rāmam ihāgataḥ |
sūtaḥ kiṁ nāma kausalyāṁ śocantīṁ prativakṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
yathā ca manye durjīvam evaṁ na sukaraṁ dhruvam |
ācchidya putre niryāte kausalyā yatra jīvati ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
satya rūpaṁ tu tadvākyaṁ rājñaḥ strīṇāṁ niśāmayan |
pradīptam iva śokena viveśa sahasā gr̥ham ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
sa praviśyāṣṭamīṁ kakṣyāṁ rājānaṁ dīnam ātulam |
putraśokaparidyūnam apaśyat pāṇḍare gr̥he ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
abhigamya tam āsīnaṁ narendram abhivādya ca |
sumantro rāmavacanaṁ yathoktaṁ pratyavedayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
sa tūṣṇīm eva tac chrutvā rājā vibhrānta cetanaḥ |
mūrchito nyapatad bhūmau rāmaśokābhipīḍitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tato ’ntaḥpuram āviddhaṁ mūrchite pr̥thivīpatau |
uddhr̥tya bāhū cukrośa nr̥patau patite kṣitau ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
sumitrayā tu sahitā kausalyā patitaṁ patim |
utthāpayām āsa tadā vacanaṁ cedam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
imaṁ tasya mahābhāga dūtaṁ duṣkarakāriṇaḥ |
vanavāsād anuprāptaṁ kasmān na pratibhāṣase ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
adyemam anayaṁ kr̥tvā vyapatrapasi rāghava |
uttiṣṭha sukr̥taṁ te ’stu śoke na syāt sahāyatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
deva yasyā bhayād rāmaṁ nānupr̥cchasi sārathim |
neha tiṣṭhati kaikeyī viśrabdhaṁ pratibhāṣyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
sā tathoktvā mahārājaṁ kausalyā śokalālasā |
dharaṇyāṁ nipapātāśu bāṣpaviplutabhāṣiṇī ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
evaṁ vilapatīṁ dr̥ṣṭvā kausalyāṁ patitāṁ bhuvi |
patiṁ cāvekṣya tāḥ sarvāḥ sasvaraṁ ruruduḥ striyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
tatas tam antaḥpuranādam utthitaṁ; samīkṣya vr̥ddhās taruṇāś ca mānavāḥ |
striyaś ca sarvā ruruduḥ samantataḥ; puraṁ tadāsīt punar eva saṁkulam ||

=== meaning ===

---
