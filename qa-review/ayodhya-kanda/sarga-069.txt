=== Sarga 69 ===

---
1.
tathaiva krośatas tasya bharatasya mahātmanaḥ |
kausalyā śabdam ājñāya sumitrām idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
āgataḥ krūrakāryāyāḥ kaikeyyā bharataḥ sutaḥ |
tam ahaṁ draṣṭum icchāmi bharataṁ dīrghadarśinam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
evam uktvā sumitrāṁ sā vivarṇā malināmbarā |
pratasthe bharato yatra vepamānā vicetanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
sa tu rāmānujaś cāpi śatrughnasahitas tadā |
pratasthe bharato yatra kausalyāyā niveśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tataḥ śatrughnabharatau kausalyāṁ prekṣya duḥkhitau |
paryaṣvajetāṁ duḥkhārtāṁ patitāṁ naṣṭacetanām ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
bharataṁ pratyuvācedaṁ kausalyā bhr̥śaduḥkhitā |
idaṁ te rājyakāmasya rājyaṁ prāptam akaṇṭakam |
saṁprāptaṁ bata kaikeyyā śīghraṁ krūreṇa karmaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
prasthāpya cīravasanaṁ putraṁ me vanavāsinam |
kaikeyī kaṁ guṇaṁ tatra paśyati krūradarśinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
kṣipraṁ mām api kaikeyī prasthāpayitum arhati |
hiraṇyanābho yatrāste suto me sumahāyaśāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
atha vā svayam evāhaṁ sumitrānucarā sukham |
agnihotraṁ puraskr̥tya prasthāsye yatra rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
kāmaṁ vā svayam evādya tatra māṁ netum arhasi |
yatrāsau puruṣavyāghras tapyate me tapaḥ sutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
idaṁ hi tava vistīrṇaṁ dhanadhānyasamācitam |
hastyaśvarathasaṁpūrṇaṁ rājyaṁ niryātitaṁ tayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
evaṁ vilapamānāṁ tāṁ bharataḥ prāñjalis tadā |
kausalyāṁ pratyuvācedaṁ śokair bahubhir āvr̥tām ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
ārye kasmād ajānantaṁ garhase mām akilbiṣam |
vipulāṁ ca mama prītiṁ sthirāṁ jānāsi rāghave ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
kr̥tā śāstrānugā buddhir mā bhūt tasya kadā cana |
satyasaṁdhaḥ satāṁ śreṣṭho yasyāryo ’numate gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
praiṣyaṁ pāpīyasāṁ yātu sūryaṁ ca prati mehatu |
hantu pādena gāṁ suptāṁ yasyāryo ’numate gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
kārayitvā mahat karma bhartā bhr̥tyam anarthakam |
adharmo yo ’sya so ’syās tu yasyāryo ’numate gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
paripālayamānasya rājño bhūtāni putravat |
tatas tu druhyatāṁ pāpaṁ yasyāryo ’numate gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
baliṣaḍbhāgam uddhr̥tya nr̥pasyārakṣataḥ prajāḥ |
adharmo yo ’sya so ’syāstu yasyāryo ’numate gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
saṁśrutya ca tapasvibhyaḥ satre vai yajñadakṣiṇām |
tāṁ vipralapatāṁ pāpaṁ yasyāryo ’numate gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
hastyaśvarathasaṁbādhe yuddhe śastrasamākule |
mā sma kārṣīt satāṁ dharmaṁ yasyāryo ’numate gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
upadiṣṭaṁ susūkṣmārthaṁ śāstraṁ yatnena dhīmatā |
sa nāśayatu duṣṭātmā yasyāryo ’numate gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
pāyasaṁ kr̥saraṁ chāgaṁ vr̥thā so ’śnātu nirghr̥ṇaḥ |
gurūṁś cāpy avajānātu yasyāryo ’numate gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
putrair dāraiś ca bhr̥tyaiś ca svagr̥he parivāritaḥ |
sa eko mr̥ṣṭam aśnātu yasyāryo ’numate gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
rājastrībālavr̥ddhānāṁ vadhe yat pāpam ucyate |
bhr̥tyatyāge ca yat pāpaṁ tat pāpaṁ pratipadyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
ubhe saṁdhye śayānasya yat pāpaṁ parikalpyate |
tac ca pāpaṁ bhavet tasya yasyāryo ’numate gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
yad agnidāyake pāpaṁ yat pāpaṁ gurutalpage |
mitradrohe ca yat pāpaṁ tat pāpaṁ pratipadyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
devatānāṁ pitr̥̄ṇāṁ ca mātā pitros tathaiva ca |
mā sma kārṣīt sa śuśrūṣāṁ yasyāryo ’numate gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
satāṁ lokāt satāṁ kīrtyāḥ sajjuṣṭāt karmaṇas tathā |
bhraśyatu kṣipram adyaiva yasyāryo ’numate gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
vihīnāṁ patiputrābhyāṁ kausalyāṁ pārthivātmajaḥ |
evam āśvasayann eva duḥkhārto nipapāta ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
tathā tu śapathaiḥ kaṣṭaiḥ śapamānam acetanam |
bharataṁ śokasaṁtaptaṁ kausalyā vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
mama duḥkham idaṁ putra bhūyaḥ samupajāyate |
śapathaiḥ śapamāno hi prāṇān uparuṇatsi me ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
diṣṭyā na calito dharmād ātmā te sahalakṣmaṇaḥ |
vatsa satyapratijño me satāṁ lokān avāpsyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
evaṁ vilapamānasya duḥkhārtasya mahātmanaḥ |
mohāc ca śokasaṁrodhād babhūva lulitaṁ manaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
lālapyamānasya vicetanasya; pranaṣṭabuddheḥ patitasya bhūmau |
muhur muhur niḥśvasataś ca dīrghaṁ; sā tasya śokena jagāma rātriḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
