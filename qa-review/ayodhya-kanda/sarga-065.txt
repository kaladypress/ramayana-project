=== Sarga 65 ===

---
1.
sa prāṅmukho rājagr̥hād abhiniryāya vīryavān |
hrādinīṁ dūrapārāṁ ca pratyaksrotas taraṅgiṇīm |
śatadrūm atarac chrīmān nadīm ikṣvākunandanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
eladhāne nadīṁ tīrtvā prāpya cāparaparpaṭān |
śilām ākurvatīṁ tīrtvā āgneyaṁ śalyakartanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
satyasaṁdhaḥ śuciḥ śrīmān prekṣamāṇaḥ śilāvahām |
atyayāt sa mahāśailān vanaṁ caitrarathaṁ prati ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
veginīṁ ca kuliṅgākhyāṁ hrādinīṁ parvatāvr̥tām |
yamunāṁ prāpya saṁtīrṇo balam āśvāsayat tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
śītīkr̥tya tu gātrāṇi klāntān āśvāsya vājinaḥ |
tatra snātvā ca pītvā ca prāyād ādāya codakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
rājaputro mahāraṇyam anabhīkṣṇopasevitam |
bhadro bhadreṇa yānena mārutaḥ kham ivātyayāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
toraṇaṁ dakṣiṇārdhena jambūprastham upāgamat |
varūthaṁ ca yayau ramyaṁ grāmaṁ daśarathātmajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tatra ramye vane vāsaṁ kr̥tvāsau prāṅmukho yayau |
udyānam ujjihānāyāḥ priyakā yatra pādapāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sālāṁs tu priyakān prāpya śīghrān āsthāya vājinaḥ |
anujñāpyātha bharato vāhinīṁ tvarito yayau ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
vāsaṁ kr̥tvā sarvatīrthe tīrtvā cottānakāṁ nadīm |
anyā nadīś ca vividhāḥ pārvatīyais turaṁgamaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
hastipr̥ṣṭhakam āsādya kuṭikām atyavartata |
tatāra ca naravyāghro lauhitye sa kapīvatīm |
ekasāle sthāṇumatīṁ vinate gomatīṁ nadīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
kaliṅga nagare cāpi prāpya sālavanaṁ tadā |
bharataḥ kṣipram āgacchat supariśrāntavāhanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
vanaṁ ca samatītyāśu śarvaryām aruṇodaye |
ayodhyāṁ manunā rājñā nirmitāṁ sa dadarśa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tāṁ purīṁ puruṣavyāghraḥ saptarātroṣiṭaḥ pathi |
ayodhyām agrato dr̥ṣṭvā rathe sārathim abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
eṣā nātipratītā me puṇyodyānā yaśasvinī |
ayodhyā dr̥śyate dūrāt sārathe pāṇḍumr̥ttikā ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
yajvabhir guṇasaṁpannair brāhmaṇair vedapāragaiḥ |
bhūyiṣṭham r̥ddhair ākīrṇā rājarṣivarapālitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
ayodhyāyāṁ purāśabdaḥ śrūyate tumulo mahān |
samantān naranārīṇāṁ tam adya na śr̥ṇomy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
udyānāni hi sāyāhne krīḍitvoparatair naraiḥ |
samantād vipradhāvadbhiḥ prakāśante mamānyadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tāny adyānurudantīva parityaktāni kāmibhiḥ |
araṇyabhūteva purī sārathe pratibhāti me ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
na hy atra yānair dr̥śyante na gajair na ca vājibhiḥ |
niryānto vābhiyānto vā naramukhyā yathāpuram ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
aniṣṭāni ca pāpāni paśyāmi vividhāni ca |
nimittāny amanojñāni tena sīdati te manaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
dvāreṇa vaijayantena prāviśac chrāntavāhanaḥ |
dvāḥsthair utthāya vijayaṁ pr̥ṣṭas taiḥ sahito yayau ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
sa tv anekāgrahr̥dayo dvāḥsthaṁ pratyarcya taṁ janam |
sūtam aśvapateḥ klāntam abravīt tatra rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
śrutā no yādr̥śāḥ pūrvaṁ nr̥patīnāṁ vināśane |
ākārās tān ahaṁ sarvān iha paśyāmi sārathe ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
malinaṁ cāśrupūrṇākṣaṁ dīnaṁ dhyānaparaṁ kr̥śam |
sastrī puṁsaṁ ca paśyāmi janam utkaṇṭhitaṁ pure ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
ity evam uktvā bharataḥ sūtaṁ taṁ dīnamānasaḥ |
tāny aniṣṭāny ayodhyāyāṁ prekṣya rājagr̥haṁ yayau ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tāṁ śūnyaśr̥ṅgāṭakaveśmarathyāṁ; rajoruṇadvārakapāṭayantrām |
dr̥ṣṭvā purīm indrapurī prakāśāṁ; duḥkhena saṁpūrṇataro babhūva ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
bahūni paśyan manaso ’priyāṇi; yāny annyadā nāsya pure babhūvuḥ |
avākśirā dīnamanā nahr̥ṣṭaḥ; pitur mahātmā praviveśa veśma ||

=== meaning ===

---
