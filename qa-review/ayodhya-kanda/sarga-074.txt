=== Sarga 74 ===

---
1.
atha bhūmipradeśajñāḥ sūtrakarmaviśāradāḥ |
svakarmābhiratāḥ śūrāḥ khanakā yantrakās tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
karmāntikāḥ sthapatayaḥ puruṣā yantrakovidāḥ |
tathā vardhakayaś caiva mārgiṇo vr̥kṣatakṣakāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
kūpakārāḥ sudhākārā vaṁśakarmakr̥tas tathā |
samarthā ye ca draṣṭāraḥ puratas te pratasthire ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
sa tu harṣāt tam uddeśaṁ janaugho vipulaḥ prayān |
aśobhata mahāvegaḥ sāgarasyeva parvaṇi ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
te svavāraṁ samāsthāya vartmakarmāṇi kovidāḥ |
karaṇair vividhopetaiḥ purastāt saṁpratasthire ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
latāvallīś ca gulmāṁś ca sthāṇūn aśmana eva ca |
janās te cakrire mārgaṁ chindanto vividhān drumān ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
avr̥kṣeṣu ca deśeṣu ke cid vr̥kṣān aropayan |
ke cit kuṭhāraiṣ ṭaṅkaiś ca dātraiś chindan kva cit kva cit ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
apare vīraṇastambān balino balavattarāḥ |
vidhamanti sma durgāṇi sthalāni ca tatas tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
apare ’pūrayan kūpān pāṁsubhiḥ śvabhram āyatam |
nimnabhāgāṁs tathā ke cit samāṁś cakruḥ samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
babandhur bandhanīyāṁś ca kṣodyān saṁcukṣudus tadā |
bibhidur bhedanīyāṁś ca tāṁs tān deśān narās tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
acireṇaiva kālena parivāhān bahūdakān |
cakrur bahuvidhākārān sāgarapratimān bahūn |
udapānān bahuvidhān vedikā parimaṇḍitān ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sasudhākuṭṭimatalaḥ prapuṣpitamahīruhaḥ |
mattodghuṣṭadvijagaṇaḥ patākābhir alaṁkr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
candanodakasaṁsikto nānākusumabhūṣitaḥ |
bahv aśobhata senāyāḥ panthāḥ svargapathopamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
ājñāpyātha yathājñapti yuktās te ’dhikr̥tā narāḥ |
ramaṇīyeṣu deśeṣu bahusvāduphaleṣu ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
yo niveśas tv abhipreto bharatasya mahātmanaḥ |
bhūyas taṁ śobhayām āsur bhūṣābhir bhūṣaṇopamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
nakṣatreṣu praśasteṣu muhūrteṣu ca tadvidaḥ |
niveśaṁ sthāpayām āsur bharatasya mahātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
bahupāṁsucayāś cāpi parikhāparivāritāḥ |
tatrendrakīlapratimāḥ pratolīvaraśobhitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
prāsādamālāsaṁyuktāḥ saudhaprākārasaṁvr̥tāḥ |
patākā śobhitāḥ sarve sunirmitamahāpathāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
visarpadbhir ivākāśe viṭaṅkāgravimānakaiḥ |
samucchritair niveśās te babhuḥ śakrapuropamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
jāhnavīṁ tu samāsādya vividhadruma kānanām |
śītalāmalapānīyāṁ mahāmīnasamākulām ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
sacandratārāgaṇamaṇḍitaṁ yathā; nabhaḥkṣapāyām amalaṁ virājate |
narendramārgaḥ sa tathā vyarājata; krameṇa ramyaḥ śubhaśilpinirmitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
