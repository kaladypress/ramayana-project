=== Sarga 42 ===

---
1.
anugamya nivr̥ttānāṁ rāmaṁ nagaravāsinām |
udgatānīva sattvāni babhūvur amanasvinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
svaṁ svaṁ nilayam āgamya putradāraiḥ samāvr̥tāḥ |
aśrūṇi mumucuḥ sarve bāṣpeṇa pihitānanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
na cāhr̥ṣyan na cāmodan vaṇijo na prasārayan |
na cāśobhanta paṇyāni nāpacan gr̥hamedhinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
naṣṭaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā nābhyanandan vipulaṁ vā dhanāgamam |
putraṁ prathamajaṁ labdhvā jananī nābhyanandata ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
gr̥he gr̥he rudantyaś ca bhartāraṁ gr̥ham āgatam |
vyagarhayanto duḥkhārtā vāgbhis totrair iva dvipān ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
kiṁ nu teṣāṁ gr̥haiḥ kāryaṁ kiṁ dāraiḥ kiṁ dhanena vā |
putrair vā kiṁ sukhair vāpi ye na paśyanti rāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
ekaḥ satpuruṣo loke lakṣmaṇaḥ saha sītayā |
yo ’nugacchati kākutsthaṁ rāmaṁ paricaran vane ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
āpagāḥ kr̥tapuṇyās tāḥ padminyaś ca sarāṁsi ca |
yeṣu snāsyati kākutstho vigāhya salilaṁ śuci ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
śobhayiṣyanti kākutstham aṭavyo ramyakānanāḥ |
āpagāś ca mahānūpāḥ sānumantaś ca parvatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
kānanaṁ vāpi śailaṁ vā yaṁ rāmo ’bhigamiṣyati |
priyātithim iva prāptaṁ nainaṁ śakṣyanty anarcitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
vicitrakusumāpīḍā bahumañjaridhāriṇaḥ |
akāle cāpi mukhyāni puṣpāṇi ca phalāni ca |
darśayiṣyanty anukrośād girayo rāmam āgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
vidarśayanto vividhān bhūyaś citrāṁś ca nirjharān |
pādapāḥ parvatāgreṣu ramayiṣyanti rāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
yatra rāmo bhayaṁ nātra nāsti tatra parābhavaḥ |
sa hi śūro mahābāhuḥ putro daśarathasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
purā bhavati no dūrād anugacchāma rāghavam |
pādacchāyā sukhā bhartus tādr̥śasya mahātmanaḥ |
sa hi nātho janasyāsya sa gatiḥ sa parāyaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
vayaṁ paricariṣyāmaḥ sītāṁ yūyaṁ tu rāghavam |
iti paurastriyo bhartr̥̄n duḥkhārtās tat tad abruvan ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
yuṣmākaṁ rāghavo ’raṇye yogakṣemaṁ vidhāsyati |
sītā nārījanasyāsya yogakṣemaṁ kariṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
ko nv anenāpratītena sotkaṇṭhitajanena ca |
saṁprīyetāmanojñena vāsena hr̥tacetasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
kaikeyyā yadi ced rājyaṁ syād adharmyam anāthavat |
na hi no jīvitenārthaḥ kutaḥ putraiḥ kuto dhanaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
yayā putraś ca bhartā ca tyaktāv aiśvaryakāraṇāt |
kaṁ sā parihared anyaṁ kaikeyī kulapāṁsanī ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
kaikeyyā na vayaṁ rājye bhr̥takā nivasemahi |
jīvantyā jātu jīvantyaḥ putrair api śapāmahe ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
yā putraṁ pārthivendrasya pravāsayati nirghr̥ṇā |
kas tāṁ prāpya sukhaṁ jīved adharmyāṁ duṣṭacāriṇīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
na hi pravrajite rāme jīviṣyati mahīpatiḥ |
mr̥te daśarathe vyaktaṁ vilopas tadanantaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
te viṣaṁ pibatāloḍya kṣīṇapuṇyāḥ sudurgatāḥ |
rāghavaṁ vānugacchadhvam aśrutiṁ vāpi gacchata ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
mithyā pravrājito rāmaḥ sabhāryaḥ sahalakṣmaṇaḥ |
bharate saṁniṣr̥ṣṭāḥ smaḥ saunike paśavo yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tās tathā vilapantyas tu nagare nāgarastriyaḥ |
cukruśur bhr̥śasaṁtaptā mr̥tyor iva bhayāgame ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tathā striyo rāmanimittam āturā; yathā sute bhrātari vā vivāsite |
vilapya dīnā rurudur vicetasaḥ; sutair hi tāsām adhiko hi so ’bhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
