=== Sarga 24 ===

---
1.
evam uktā tu vaidehī priyārhā priyavādinī |
praṇayād eva saṁkruddhā bhartāram idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
āryaputra pitā mātā bhrātā putras tathā snuṣā |
svāni puṇyāni bhuñjānāḥ svaṁ svaṁ bhāgyam upāsate ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
bhartur bhāgyaṁ tu bhāryaikā prāpnoti puruṣarṣabha |
ataś caivāham ādiṣṭā vane vastavyam ity api ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
na pitā nātmajo nātmā na mātā na sakhījanaḥ |
iha pretya ca nārīṇāṁ patir eko gatiḥ sadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
yadi tvaṁ prasthito durgaṁ vanam adyaiva rāghava |
agratas te gamiṣyāmi mr̥dnantī kuśakaṇṭakān ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
īrṣyāroṣau bahiṣkr̥tya bhuktaśeṣam ivodakam |
naya māṁ vīra viśrabdhaḥ pāpaṁ mayi na vidyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
prāsādāgrair vimānair vā vaihāyasagatena vā |
sarvāvasthāgatā bhartuḥ pādacchāyā viśiṣyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
anuśiṣṭāsmi mātrā ca pitrā ca vividhāśrayam |
nāsmi saṁprati vaktavyā vartitavyaṁ yathā mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sukhaṁ vane nivatsyāmi yathaiva bhavane pituḥ |
acintayantī trīm̐l lokāṁś cintayantī pativratam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
śuśrūṣamāṇā te nityaṁ niyatā brahmacāriṇī |
saha raṁsye tvayā vīra vaneṣu madhugandhiṣu ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tvaṁ hi kartuṁ vane śakto rāma saṁparipālanam |
anyasyāpi janasyeha kiṁ punar mama mānada ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
phalamūlāśanā nityaṁ bhaviṣyāmi na saṁśayaḥ |
na te duḥkhaṁ kariṣyāmi nivasantī saha tvayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
icchāmi saritaḥ śailān palvalāni vanāni ca |
draṣṭuṁ sarvatra nirbhītā tvayā nāthena dhīmatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
haṁsakāraṇḍavākīrṇāḥ padminīḥ sādhupuṣpitāḥ |
iccheyaṁ sukhinī draṣṭuṁ tvayā vīreṇa saṁgatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
saha tvayā viśālākṣa raṁsye paramanandinī |
evaṁ varṣasahasrāṇāṁ śataṁ vāhaṁ tvayā saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
svarge ’pi ca vinā vāso bhavitā yadi rāghava |
tvayā mama naravyāghra nāhaṁ tam api rocaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
ahaṁ gamiṣyāmi vanaṁ sudurgamaṁ; mr̥gāyutaṁ vānaravāraṇair yutam |
vane nivatsyāmi yathā pitur gr̥he; tavaiva pādāv upagr̥hya saṁmatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
ananyabhāvām anuraktacetasaṁ; tvayā viyuktāṁ maraṇāya niścitām |
nayasva māṁ sādhu kuruṣva yācanāṁ; na te mayāto gurutā bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tathā bruvāṇām api dharmavatsalo; na ca sma sītāṁ nr̥varo ninīṣati |
uvāca caināṁ bahu saṁnivartane; vane nivāsasya ca duḥkhitāṁ prati ||

=== meaning ===

---
