=== Sarga 109 ===

---
1.
rāghavas tv apayāteṣu tapasviṣu vicintayan |
na tatrārocayad vāsaṁ kāraṇair bahubhis tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
iha me bharato dr̥ṣṭo mātaraś ca sanāgarāḥ |
sā ca me smr̥tir anveti tān nityam anuśocataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
skandhāvāraniveśena tena tasya mahātmanaḥ |
hayahastikarīṣaiś ca upamardaḥ kr̥to bhr̥śam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tasmād anyatra gacchāma iti saṁcintya rāghavaḥ |
prātiṣṭhata sa vaidehyā lakṣmaṇena ca saṁgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
so ’trer āśramam āsādya taṁ vavande mahāyaśāḥ |
taṁ cāpi bhagavān atriḥ putravat pratyapadyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
svayam ātithyam ādiśya sarvam asya susatkr̥tam |
saumitriṁ ca mahābhāgāṁ sītāṁ ca samasāntvayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
patnīṁ ca tam anuprāptāṁ vr̥ddhām āmantrya satkr̥tām |
sāntvayām āsa dharmajñaḥ sarvabhūtahite rataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
anasūyāṁ mahābhāgāṁ tāpasīṁ dharmacāriṇīm |
pratigr̥hṇīṣva vaidehīm abravīd r̥ṣisattamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
rāmāya cācacakṣe tāṁ tāpasīṁ dharmacāriṇīm |
daśa varṣāṇy anāvr̥ṣṭyā dagdhe loke nirantaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
yayā mūlaphale sr̥ṣṭe jāhnavī ca pravartitā |
ugreṇa tapasā yuktā niyamaiś cāpy alaṁkr̥tā ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
daśavarṣasahasrāṇi yayā taptaṁ mahat tapaḥ |
anasūyāvratais tāta pratyūhāś ca nibarhitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
devakāryanimittaṁ ca yayā saṁtvaramāṇayā |
daśarātraṁ kr̥tvā rātriḥ seyaṁ māteva te ’nagha ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tām imāṁ sarvabhūtānāṁ namaskāryāṁ yaśasvinīm |
abhigacchatu vaidehī vr̥ddhām akrodhanāṁ sadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
evaṁ bruvāṇaṁ tam r̥ṣiṁ tathety uktvā sa rāghavaḥ |
sītām uvāca dharmajñām idaṁ vacanam uttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
rājaputri śrutaṁ tv etan muner asya samīritam |
śreyo ’rtham ātmanaḥ śīghram abhigaccha tapasvinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
anasūyeti yā loke karmabhiḥ kyātim āgatā |
tāṁ śīghram abhigaccha tvam abhigamyāṁ tapasvinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
sītā tv etad vacaḥ śrutvā rāghavasya hitaiṣiṇī |
tām atripatnīṁ dharmajñām abhicakrāma maithilī ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
śithilāṁ valitāṁ vr̥ddhāṁ jarāpāṇḍuramūrdhajām |
satataṁ vepamānāṅgīṁ pravāte kadalī yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tāṁ tu sītā mahābhāgām anasūyāṁ pativratām |
abhyavādayad avyagrā svaṁ nāma samudāharat ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
abhivādya ca vaidehī tāpasīṁ tām aninditām |
baddhāñjalipuṭā hr̥ṣṭā paryapr̥cchad anāmayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tataḥ sītāṁ mahābhāgāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā tāṁ dharmacāriṇīm |
sāntvayanty abravīd dhr̥ṣṭā diṣṭyā dharmam avekṣase ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tyaktvā jñātijanaṁ sīte mānam r̥ddhiṁ ca mānini |
avaruddhaṁ vane rāmaṁ diṣṭyā tvam anugacchasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
nagarastho vanastho vā pāpo vā yadi vāśubhaḥ |
yāsāṁ strīṇāṁ priyo bhartā tāsāṁ lokā mahodayāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
duḥśīlaḥ kāmavr̥tto vā dhanair vā parivarjitaḥ |
strīṇām ārya svabhāvānāṁ paramaṁ daivataṁ patiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
nāto viśiṣṭaṁ paśyāmi bāndhavaṁ vimr̥śanty aham |
sarvatra yogyaṁ vaidehi tapaḥ kr̥tam ivāvyayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
na tv evam avagacchanti guṇa doṣam asat striyaḥ |
kāmavaktavyahr̥dayā bhartr̥nāthāś caranti yāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
prāpnuvanty ayaśaś caiva dharmabhraṁśaṁ ca maithili |
akārya vaśam āpannāḥ striyo yāḥ khalu tad vidhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tvadvidhās tu guṇair yuktā dr̥ṣṭalokaparāvarāḥ |
striyaḥ svarge cariṣyanti yathā puṇyakr̥tas tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
