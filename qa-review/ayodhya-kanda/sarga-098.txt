=== Sarga 98 ===

---
1.
tataḥ puruṣasiṁhānāṁ vr̥tānāṁ taiḥ suhr̥dgaṇaiḥ |
śocatām eva rajanī duḥkhena vyatyavartata ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
rajanyāṁ suprabhātāyāṁ bhrātaras te suhr̥dvr̥tāḥ |
mandākinyāṁ hutaṁ japyaṁ kr̥tvā rāmam upāgaman ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tūṣṇīṁ te samupāsīnā na kaś cit kiṁ cid abravīt |
bharatas tu suhr̥nmadhye rāmavacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
sāntvitā māmikā mātā dattaṁ rājyam idaṁ mama |
tad dadāmi tavaivāhaṁ bhuṅkṣva rājyam akaṇṭakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
mahatevāmbuvegena bhinnaḥ setur jalāgame |
durāvāraṁ tvadanyena rājyakhaṇḍam idaṁ mahat ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
gatiṁ khara ivāśvasya tārkṣyasyeva patatriṇaḥ |
anugantuṁ na śaktir me gatiṁ tava mahīpate ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sujīvaṁ nityaśas tasya yaḥ parair upajīvyate |
rāma tena tu durjīvaṁ yaḥ parān upajīvati ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
yathā tu ropito vr̥kṣaḥ puruṣeṇa vivardhitaḥ |
hrasvakena durāroho rūḍhaskandho mahādrumaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sa yadā puṣpito bhūtvā phalāni na vidarśayet |
sa tāṁ nānubhavet prītiṁ yasya hetoḥ prabhāvitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
eṣopamā mahābāho tvam arthaṁ vettum arhasi |
yadi tvam asmān r̥ṣabho bhartā bhr̥tyān na śādhi hi ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
śreṇayas tvāṁ mahārāja paśyantv agryāś ca sarvaśaḥ |
pratapantam ivādityaṁ rājye sthitam ariṁdamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tavānuyāne kākutṣṭha mattā nardantu kuñjarāḥ |
antaḥpura gatā nāryo nandantu susamāhitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tasya sādhv ity amanyanta nāgarā vividhā janāḥ |
bharatasya vacaḥ śrutvā rāmaṁ pratyanuyācataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tam evaṁ duḥkhitaṁ prekṣya vilapantaṁ yaśasvinam |
rāmaḥ kr̥tātmā bharataṁ samāśvāsayad ātmavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
nātmanaḥ kāmakāro ’sti puruṣo ’yam anīśvaraḥ |
itaś cetarataś cainaṁ kr̥tāntaḥ parikarṣati ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sarve kṣayāntā nicayāḥ patanāntāḥ samucchrayāḥ |
saṁyogā viprayogāntā maraṇāntaṁ ca jīvitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
yathā phalānaṁ pakvānāṁ nānyatra patanād bhayam |
evaṁ narasya jātasya nānyatra maraṇād bhayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
yathāgāraṁ dr̥ḍhasthūṇaṁ jīrṇaṁ bhūtvāvasīdati |
tathāvasīdanti narā jarāmr̥tyuvaśaṁ gatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
ahorātrāṇi gacchanti sarveṣāṁ prāṇinām iha |
āyūṁṣi kṣapayanty āśu grīṣme jalam ivāṁśavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
ātmānam anuśoca tvaṁ kim anyam anuśocasi |
āyus te hīyate yasya sthitasya ca gatasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
sahaiva mr̥tyur vrajati saha mr̥tyur niṣīdati |
gatvā sudīrgham adhvānaṁ saha mr̥tyur nivartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
gātreṣu valayaḥ prāptāḥ śvetāś caiva śiroruhāḥ |
jarayā puruṣo jīrṇaḥ kiṁ hi kr̥tvā prabhāvayet ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
nandanty udita āditye nandanty astam ite ravau |
ātmano nāvabudhyante manuṣyā jīvitakṣayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
hr̥ṣyanty r̥tumukhaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā navaṁ navam ihāgatam |
r̥tūnāṁ parivartena prāṇināṁ prāṇasaṁkṣayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
yathā kāṣṭhaṁ ca kāṣṭhaṁ ca sameyātāṁ mahārṇave |
sametya ca vyapeyātāṁ kālam āsādya kaṁ cana ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
evaṁ bhāryāś ca putrāś ca jñātayaś ca vasūni ca |
sametya vyavadhāvanti dhruvo hy eṣāṁ vinābhavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
nātra kaś cid yathā bhāvaṁ prāṇī samabhivartate |
tena tasmin na sāmarthyaṁ pretasyāsty anuśocataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
yathā hi sārthaṁ gacchantaṁ brūyāt kaś cit pathi sthitaḥ |
aham apy āgamiṣyāmi pr̥ṣṭhato bhavatām iti ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
evaṁ pūrvair gato mārgaḥ pitr̥paitāmaho dhruvaḥ |
tam āpannaḥ kathaṁ śoced yasya nāsti vyatikramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
vayasaḥ patamānasya srotaso vānivartinaḥ |
ātmā sukhe niyoktavyaḥ sukhabhājaḥ prajāḥ smr̥tāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
dharmātmā sa śubhaiḥ kr̥tsnaiḥ kratubhiś cāptadakṣiṇaiḥ |
dhūtapāpo gataḥ svargaṁ pitā naḥ pr̥thivīpatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
bhr̥tyānāṁ bharaṇāt samyak prajānāṁ paripālanāt |
arthādānāc ca dhārmeṇa pitā nas tridivaṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
iṣṭvā bahuvidhair yajñair bhogāṁś cāvāpya puṣkalān |
uttamaṁ cāyur āsādya svar gataḥ pr̥thivīpatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
sa jīrṇaṁ mānuṣaṁ dehaṁ parityajya pitā hi naḥ |
daivīm r̥ddhim anuprāpto brahmalokavihāriṇīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
taṁ tu naivaṁ vidhaḥ kaś cit prājñaḥ śocitum arhati |
tvadvidho yadvidhaś cāpi śrutavān buddhimattaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
ete bahuvidhāḥ śokā vilāpa rudite tathā |
varjanīyā hi dhīreṇa sarvāvasthāsu dhīmatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
sa svastho bhava mā śoco yātvā cāvasa tāṁ purīm |
tathā pitrā niyukto ’si vaśinā vadatāmv vara ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
yatrāham api tenaiva niyuktaḥ puṇyakarmaṇā |
tatraivāhaṁ kariṣyāmi pitur āryasya śāsanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
na mayā śāsanaṁ tasya tyaktuṁ nyāyyam ariṁdama |
tat tvayāpi sadā mānyaṁ sa vai bandhuḥ sa naḥ pitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
evam uktvā tu virate rāme vacanam arthavat |
uvāca bharataś citraṁ dhārmiko dhārmikaṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
ko hi syād īdr̥śo loke yādr̥śas tvam ariṁdama |
na tvāṁ pravyathayed duḥkhaṁ prītir vā na praharṣayet ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
saṁmataś cāsi vr̥ddhānāṁ tāṁś ca pr̥cchasi saṁśayān |
yathā mr̥tas tathā jīvan yathāsati tathā sati ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
yasyaiṣa buddhilābhaḥ syāt paritapyeta kena saḥ |
sa evaṁ vyasanaṁ prāpya na viṣīditum arhati ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
amaropamasattvas tvaṁ mahātmā satyasaṁgaraḥ |
sarvajñaḥ sarvadarśī ca buddhimāṁś cāsi rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
na tvām evaṁ guṇair yuktaṁ prabhavābhavakovidam |
aviṣahyatamaṁ duḥkham āsādayitum arhati ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
proṣite mayi yat pāpaṁ mātrā matkāraṇāt kr̥tam |
kṣudrayā tad aniṣṭaṁ me prasīdatu bhavān mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
dharmabandhena baddho ’smi tenemāṁ neha mātaram |
hanmi tīvreṇa daṇḍena daṇḍārhāṁ pāpakāriṇīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
kathaṁ daśarathāj jātaḥ śuddhābhijanakarmaṇaḥ |
jānan dharmam adharmiṣṭhaṁ kuryāṁ karma jugupsitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
guruḥ kriyāvān vr̥ddhaś ca rājā pretaḥ piteti ca |
tātaṁ na parigarheyaṁ daivataṁ ceti saṁsadi ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
ko hi dharmārthayor hīnam īdr̥śaṁ karma kilbiṣam |
striyāḥ priyacikīrṣuḥ san kuryād dharmajña dharmavit ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
antakāle hi bhūtāni muhyantīti purāśrutiḥ |
rājñaivaṁ kurvatā loke pratyakṣā sā śrutiḥ kr̥tā ||

=== meaning ===

---
52.
sādhv artham abhisaṁdhāya krodhān mohāc ca sāhasāt |
tātasya yad atikrāntaṁ pratyāharatu tad bhavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
53.
pitur hi samatikrāntaṁ putro yaḥ sādhu manyate |
tad apatyaṁ mataṁ loke viparītam ato ’nyathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
54.
tad apatyaṁ bhavān astu mā bhavān duṣkr̥taṁ pituḥ |
abhipat tat kr̥taṁ karma loke dhīravigarhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
55.
kaikeyīṁ māṁ ca tātaṁ ca suhr̥do bāndhavāṁś ca naḥ |
paurajānapadān sarvāṁs trātu sarvam idaṁ bhavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
56.
kva cāraṇyaṁ kva ca kṣātraṁ kva jaṭāḥ kva ca pālanam |
īdr̥śaṁ vyāhataṁ karma na bhavān kartum arhati ||

=== meaning ===

---
57.
atha kleśajam eva tvaṁ dharmaṁ caritum icchasi |
dharmeṇa caturo varṇān pālayan kleśam āpnuhi ||

=== meaning ===

---
58.
caturṇām āśramāṇāṁ hi gārhasthyaṁ śreṣṭham āśramam |
āhur dharmajña dharmajñās taṁ kathaṁ tyaktum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
59.
śrutena bālaḥ sthānena janmanā bhavato hy aham |
sa kathaṁ pālayiṣyāmi bhūmiṁ bhavati tiṣṭhati ||

=== meaning ===

---
60.
hīnabuddhiguṇo bālo hīnaḥ sthānena cāpy aham |
bhavatā ca vinā bhūto na vartayitum utsahe ||

=== meaning ===

---
61.
idaṁ nikhilam avyagraṁ pitryaṁ rājyam akaṇṭakam |
anuśādhi svadharmeṇa dharmajña saha bāndhavaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
62.
ihaiva tvābhiṣiñcantu dharmajña saha bāndhavaiḥ |
r̥tvijaḥ savasiṣṭhāś ca mantravan mantrakovidāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
63.
abhiṣiktas tvam asmābhir ayodhyāṁ pālane vraja |
vijitya tarasā lokān marudbhir iva vāsavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
64.
r̥ṇāni trīṇy apākurvan durhr̥daḥ sādhu nirdahan |
suhr̥das tarpayan kāmais tvam evātrānuśādhi mām ||

=== meaning ===

---
65.
adyārya muditāḥ santu suhr̥das te ’bhiṣecane |
adya bhītāḥ pālayantāṁ durhr̥das te diśo daśa ||

=== meaning ===

---
66.
ākrośaṁ mama mātuś ca pramr̥jya puruṣarṣabha |
adya tatra bhavantaṁ ca pitaraṁ rakṣa kilbiṣāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
67.
śirasā tvābhiyāce ’haṁ kuruṣva karuṇāṁ mayi |
bāndhaveṣu ca sarveṣu bhūteṣv iva maheśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
68.
atha vā pr̥ṣṭhataḥ kr̥tvā vanam eva bhavān itaḥ |
gamiṣyati gamiṣyāmi bhavatā sārdham apy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
69.
tathāpi rāmo bharatena tāmyata; prasādyamānaḥ śirasā mahīpatiḥ |
na caiva cakre gamanāya sattvavān; matiṁ pitus tadvacane pratiṣṭhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
70.
tad adbhutaṁ sthairyam avekṣya rāghave; samaṁ jano harṣam avāpa duḥkhitaḥ |
na yāty ayodhyām iti duḥkhito ’bhavat; sthirapratijñatvam avekṣya harṣitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
71.
tam r̥tvijo naigamayūthavallabhās; tathā visaṁjñāśrukalāś ca mātaraḥ |
tathā bruvāṇaṁ bharataṁ pratuṣṭuvuḥ; praṇamya rāmaṁ ca yayācire saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
