=== Sarga 75 ===

---
1.
tato nāndīmukhīṁ rātriṁ bharataṁ sūtamāgadhāḥ |
tuṣṭuvur vāgviśeṣajñāḥ stavair maṅgalasaṁhitaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
suvarṇakoṇābhihataḥ prāṇadad yāmadundubhiḥ |
dadhmuḥ śaṅkhāṁś ca śataśo vādyāṁś coccāvacasvarān ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sa tūrya ghoṣaḥ sumahān divam āpūrayann iva |
bharataṁ śokasaṁtaptaṁ bhūyaḥ śokair arandhrayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tato prabuddho bharatas taṁ ghoṣaṁ saṁnivartya ca |
nāhaṁ rājeti cāpy uktvā śatrughnam idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
paśya śatrughna kaikeyyā lokasyāpakr̥taṁ mahat |
visr̥jya mayi duḥkhāni rājā daśaratho gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tasyaiṣā dharmarājasya dharmamūlā mahātmanaḥ |
paribhramati rājaśrīr naur ivākarṇikā jale ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
ity evaṁ bharataṁ prekṣya vilapantaṁ vicetanam |
kr̥paṇaṁ ruruduḥ sarvāḥ sasvaraṁ yoṣitas tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tathā tasmin vilapati vasiṣṭho rājadharmavit |
sabhām ikṣvākunāthasya praviveśa mahāyaśāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
śāta kumbhamayīṁ ramyāṁ maṇiratnasamākulām |
sudharmām iva dharmātmā sagaṇaḥ pratyapadyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
sa kāñcanamayaṁ pīṭhaṁ parārdhyāstaraṇāvr̥tam |
adhyāsta sarvavedajño dūtān anuśaśāsa ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
brāhmaṇān kṣatriyān yodhān amātyān gaṇaballabhān |
kṣipram ānayatāvyagrāḥ kr̥tyam ātyayikaṁ hi naḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tato halahalāśabdo mahān samudapadyata |
rathair aśvair gajaiś cāpi janānām upagacchatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tato bharatam āyāntaṁ śatakratum ivāmarāḥ |
pratyanandan prakr̥tayo yathā daśarathaṁ tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
hrada iva timināgasaṁvr̥taḥ; stimitajalo maṇiśaṅkhaśarkaraḥ |
daśarathasutaśobhitā sabhā; sadaśaratheva babhau yathā purā ||

=== meaning ===

---
