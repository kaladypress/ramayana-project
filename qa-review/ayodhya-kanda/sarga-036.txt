=== Sarga 36 ===

---
1.
tasmiṁs tu puruṣavyāghre niṣkrāmati kr̥tāñjalau |
ārtaśabdo hi saṁjajñe strīṇām antaḥpure mahān ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
anāthasya janasyāsya durbalasya tapasvinaḥ |
yo gatiṁ śaraṇaṁ cāsīt sa nāthaḥ kva nu gacchati ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
na krudhyaty abhiśasto ’pi krodhanīyāni varjayan |
kruddhān prasādayan sarvān samaduḥkhaḥ kva gacchati ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
kausalyāyāṁ mahātejā yathā mātari vartate |
tathā yo vartate ’smāsu mahātmā kva nu gacchati ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
kaikeyyā kliśyamānena rājñā saṁcodito vanam |
paritrātā janasyāsya jagataḥ kva nu gacchati ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
aho niścetano rājā jīvalokasya saṁpriyam |
dharmyaṁ satyavrataṁ rāmaṁ vanavāso pravatsyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
iti sarvā mahiṣyas tā vivatsā iva dhenavaḥ |
ruruduś caiva duḥkhārtāḥ sasvaraṁ ca vicukruśuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sa tam antaḥpure ghoram ārtaśabdaṁ mahīpatiḥ |
putraśokābhisaṁtaptaḥ śrutvā cāsīt suduḥkhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
nāgnihotrāṇy ahūyanta sūryaś cāntaradhīyata |
vyasr̥jan kavalān nāgā gāvo vatsān na pāyayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
triśaṅkur lohitāṅgaś ca br̥haspatibudhāv api |
dāruṇāḥ somam abhyetya grahāḥ sarve vyavasthitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
nakṣatrāṇi gatārcīṁṣi grahāś ca gatatejasaḥ |
viśākhāś ca sadhūmāś ca nabhasi pracakāśire ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
akasmān nāgaraḥ sarvo jano dainyam upāgamat |
āhāre vā vihāre vā na kaś cid akaron manaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
bāṣpaparyākulamukho rājamārgagato janaḥ |
na hr̥ṣṭo lakṣyate kaś cit sarvaḥ śokaparāyaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
na vāti pavanaḥ śīto na śaśī saumyadarśanaḥ |
na sūryas tapate lokaṁ sarvaṁ paryākulaṁ jagat ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
anarthinaḥ sutāḥ strīṇāṁ bhartāro bhrātaras tathā |
sarve sarvaṁ parityajya rāmam evānvacintayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
ye tu rāmasya suhr̥daḥ sarve te mūḍhacetasaḥ |
śokabhāreṇa cākrāntāḥ śayanaṁ na juhus tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tatas tv ayodhyā rahitā mahātmanā; puraṁdareṇeva mahī saparvatā |
cacāla ghoraṁ bhayabhārapīḍitā; sanāgayodhāśvagaṇā nanāda ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
