=== Sarga 56 ===

---
1.
evaṁ tu kruddhayā rājā rāmamātrā saśokayā |
śrāvitaḥ paruṣaṁ vākyaṁ cintayām āsa duḥkhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tasya cintayamānasya pratyabhāt karma duṣkr̥tam |
yad anena kr̥taṁ pūrvam ajñānāc chabdavedhinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
amanās tena śokena rāmaśokena ca prabhuḥ |
dahyamānas tu śokābhyāṁ kausalyām āha bhūpatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
prasādaye tvāṁ kausalye racito ’yaṁ mayāñjaliḥ |
vatsalā cānr̥śaṁsā ca tvaṁ hi nityaṁ pareṣv api ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
bhartā tu khalu nārīṇāṁ guṇavān nirguṇo ’pi vā |
dharmaṁ vimr̥śamānānāṁ pratyakṣaṁ devi daivatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
sā tvaṁ dharmaparā nityaṁ dr̥ṣṭalokaparāvara |
nārhase vipriyaṁ vaktuṁ duḥkhitāpi suduḥkhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tad vākyaṁ karuṇaṁ rājñaḥ śrutvā dīnasya bhāṣitam |
kausalyā vyasr̥jad bāṣpaṁ praṇālīva navodakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sa mūdrhṇi baddhvā rudatī rājñaḥ padmam ivāñjalim |
saṁbhramād abravīt trastā tvaramāṇākṣaraṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
prasīda śirasā yāce bhūmau nitatitāsmi te |
yācitāsmi hatā deva hantavyāhaṁ na hi tvayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
naiṣā hi sā strī bhavati ślāghanīyena dhīmatā |
ubhayor lokayor vīra patyā yā saṁprasādyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
jānāmi dharmaṁ dharmajña tvāṁ jāne satyavādinam |
putraśokārtayā tat tu mayā kim api bhāṣitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
śoko nāśayate dhairyaṁ śoko nāśayate śrutam |
śoko nāśayate sarvaṁ nāsti śokasamo ripuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
śayam āpatitaḥ soḍhuṁ praharo ripuhastataḥ |
soḍhum āpatitaḥ śokaḥ susūkṣmo ’pi na śakyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
vanavāsāya rāmasya pañcarātro ’dya gaṇyate |
yaḥ śokahataharṣāyāḥ pañcavarṣopamo mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
taṁ hi cintayamānāyāḥ śoko ’yaṁ hr̥di vardhate |
adīnām iva vegena samudrasalilaṁ mahat ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
evaṁ hi kathayantyās tu kausalyāyāḥ śubhaṁ vacaḥ |
mandaraśmir abhūt suryo rajanī cābhyavartata ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
atha prahlādito vākyair devyā kausalyayā nr̥paḥ |
śokena ca samākrānto nidrāyā vaśam eyivān ||

=== meaning ===

---
