=== Sarga 108 ===

---
1.
pratiprayāte bharate vasan rāmas tapovane |
lakṣayām āsa sodvegam athautsukyaṁ tapasvinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
ye tatra citrakūṭasya purastāt tāpasāśrame |
rāmam āśritya niratās tān alakṣayad utsukān ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
nayanair bhr̥kuṭībhiś ca rāmaṁ nirdiśya śaṅkitāḥ |
anyonyam upajalpantaḥ śanaiś cakrur mithaḥ kathāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
teṣām autsukyam ālakṣya rāmas tv ātmani śaṅkitaḥ |
kr̥tāñjalir uvācedam r̥ṣiṁ kulapatiṁ tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
na kaccid bhagavan kiṁ cit pūrvavr̥ttam idaṁ mayi |
dr̥śyate vikr̥taṁ yena vikriyante tapasvinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
pramādāc caritaṁ kaccit kiṁ cin nāvarajasya me |
lakṣmaṇasyarṣibhir dr̥ṣṭaṁ nānurūpam ivātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
kaccic chuśrūṣamāṇā vaḥ śuśrūṣaṇaparā mayi |
pramadābhyucitāṁ vr̥ttiṁ sītā yuktaṁ na vartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
atharṣir jarayā vr̥ddhas tapasā ca jarāṁ gataḥ |
vepamāna ivovāca rāmaṁ bhūtadayāparam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
kutaḥ kalyāṇasattvāyāḥ kalyāṇābhirates tathā |
calanaṁ tāta vaidehyās tapasviṣu viśeṣataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tvannimittam idaṁ tāvat tāpasān prati vartate |
rakṣobhyas tena saṁvignāḥ kathayanti mithaḥ kathāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
rāvaṇāvarajaḥ kaś cit kharo nāmeha rākṣasaḥ |
utpāṭya tāpasān sarvāñ janasthānaniketanān ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
dhr̥ṣṭaś ca jitakāśī ca nr̥śaṁsaḥ puruṣādakaḥ |
avaliptaś ca pāpaś ca tvāṁ ca tāta na mr̥ṣyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tvaṁ yadā prabhr̥ti hy asminn āśrame tāta vartase |
tadā prabhr̥ti rakṣāṁsi viprakurvanti tāpasān ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
darśayanti hi bībhatsaiḥ krūrair bhīṣaṇakair api |
nānā rūpair virūpaiś ca rūpair asukhadarśanaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
apraśastair aśucibhiḥ saṁprayojya ca tāpasān |
pratighnanty aparān kṣipram anāryāḥ purataḥ sthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
teṣu teṣv āśramasthāneṣv abuddham avalīya ca |
ramante tāpasāṁs tatra nāśayanto ’lpacetasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
apakṣipanti srugbhāṇḍān agnīn siñcanti vāriṇā |
kalaśāṁś ca pramr̥dnanti havane samupasthite ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tair durātmabhir āviṣṭān āśramān prajihāsavaḥ |
gamanāyānyadeśasya codayanty r̥ṣayo ’dya mām ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tat purā rāma śārīrām upahiṁsāṁ tapasviṣu |
darśayati hi duṣṭās te tyakṣyāma imam āśramam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
bahumūlaphalaṁ citram avidūrād ito vanam |
purāṇāśramam evāhaṁ śrayiṣye sagaṇaḥ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
kharas tvayy api cāyuktaṁ purā tāta pravartate |
sahāsmābhir ito gaccha yadi buddhiḥ pravartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
sakalatrasya saṁdeho nityaṁ yat tasya rāghava |
samarthasyāpi hi sato vāso duḥkha ihādya te ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
ity uktavantaṁ rāmas taṁ rājaputras tapasvinam |
na śaśākottarair vākyair avaroddhuṁ samutsukam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
abhinandya samāpr̥cchya samādhāya ca rāghavam |
sa jagāmāśramaṁ tyaktvā kulaiḥ kulapatiḥ saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
rāmaḥ saṁsādhya tv r̥ṣigaṇam anugamanād; deśāt tasmāccit kulapatim abhivādyarṣim |
samyakprītais tair anumata upadiṣṭārthaḥ; puṇyaṁ vāsāya svanilayam upasaṁpede ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
āśramaṁ tv r̥ṣivirahitaṁ prabhuḥ; kṣaṇam api na jahau sa rāghavaḥ |
rāghavaṁ hi satatam anugatās; tāpasāś carṣicaritadhr̥taguṇāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
