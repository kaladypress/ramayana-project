=== Sarga 55 ===

---
1.
vanaṁ gate dharmapare rāme ramayatāṁ vare |
kausalyā rudatī svārtā bhartāram idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
yady apitriṣu lokeṣu prathitaṁ te mayad yaśaḥ |
sānukrośo vadānyaś ca priyavādī ca rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
kathaṁ naravaraśreṣṭha putrau tau saha sītayā |
duḥkhitau sukhasaṁvr̥ddhau vane duḥkhaṁ sahiṣyataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
sā nūnaṁ taruṇī śyāmā sukumārī sukhocitā |
katham uṣṇaṁ ca śītaṁ ca maithilī prasahiṣyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
bhuktvāśanaṁ viśālākṣī sūpadaṁśānvitaṁ śubham |
vanyaṁ naivāram āhāraṁ kathaṁ sītopabhokṣyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
gītavāditranirghoṣaṁ śrutvā śubham aninditā |
kathaṁ kravyādasiṁhānāṁ śabdaṁ śroṣyaty aśobhanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
mahendradhvajasaṁkāśaḥ kva nu śete mahābhujaḥ |
bhujaṁ parighasaṁkāśam upadhāya mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
padmavarṇaṁ sukeśāntaṁ padmaniḥśvāsam uttamam |
kadā drakṣyāmi rāmasya vadanaṁ puṣkarekṣaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
vajrasāramayaṁ nūnaṁ hr̥dayaṁ me na saṁśayaḥ |
apaśyantyā na taṁ yad vai phalatīdaṁ sahasradhā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
yadi pañcadaśe varṣe rāghavaḥ punar eṣyati |
jahyād rājyaṁ ca kośaṁ ca bharatenopabhokṣyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
evaṁ kanīyasā bhrātrā bhuktaṁ rājyaṁ viśāṁ pate |
bhrātā jyeṣṭhā variṣṭhāś ca kimarthaṁ nāvamaṁsyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
na pareṇāhr̥taṁ bhakṣyaṁ vyāghraḥ khāditum icchati |
evam eva naravyāghraḥ paralīḍhaṁ na maṁsyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
havir ājyaṁ puroḍāśāḥ kuśā yūpāś ca khādirāḥ |
naitāni yātayāmāni kurvanti punar adhvare ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tathā hy āttam idaṁ rājyaṁ hr̥tasārāṁ surām iva |
nābhimantum alaṁ rāmo naṣṭasomam ivādhvaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
naivaṁvidham asatkāraṁ rāghavo marṣayiṣyati |
balavān iva śārdūlo bāladher abhimarśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sa tādr̥śaḥ siṁhabalo vr̥ṣabhākṣo nararṣabhaḥ |
svayam eva hataḥ pitrā jalajenātmajo yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
dvijāti carito dharmaḥ śāstradr̥ṣṭaḥ sanātanaḥ |
yadi te dharmanirate tvayā putre vivāsite ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
gatir evāk patir nāryā dvitīyā gatir ātmajaḥ |
tr̥tīyā jñātayo rājaṁś caturthī neha vidyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tatra tvaṁ caiva me nāsti rāmaś ca vanam āśritaḥ |
na vanaṁ gantum icchāmi sarvathā hi hatā tvayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
hataṁ tvayā rājyam idaṁ sarāṣṭraṁ; hatas tathātmā saha mantribhiś ca |
hatā saputrāsmi hatāś ca paurāḥ; sutaś ca bhāryā ca tava prahr̥ṣṭau ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
imāṁ giraṁ dāruṇaśabdasaṁśritāṁ; niśamya rājāpi mumoha duḥkhitaḥ |
tataḥ sa śokaṁ praviveśa pārthivaḥ; svaduṣkr̥taṁ cāpi punas tadāsmarat ||

=== meaning ===

---
