=== Sarga 84 ===

---
1.
bharadvājāśramaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā krośād eva nararṣabhaḥ |
balaṁ sarvam avasthāpya jagāma saha mantribhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
padbhyām eva hi dharmajño nyastaśastraparicchadaḥ |
vasāno vāsasī kṣaume purodhāya purohitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tataḥ saṁdarśane tasya bharadvājasya rāghavaḥ |
mantriṇas tān avasthāpya jagāmānu purohitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
vasiṣṭham atha dr̥ṣṭvaiva bharadvājo mahātapāḥ |
saṁcacālāsanāt tūrṇaṁ śiṣyān arghyam iti bruvan ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
samāgamya vasiṣṭhena bharatenābhivāditaḥ |
abudhyata mahātejāḥ sutaṁ daśarathasya tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tābhyām arghyaṁ ca pādyaṁ ca dattvā paścāt phalāni ca |
ānupūrvyāc ca dharmajñaḥ papraccha kuśalaṁ kule ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
ayodhyāyāṁ bale kośe mitreṣv api ca mantriṣu |
jānan daśarathaṁ vr̥ttaṁ na rājānam udāharat ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
vasiṣṭho bharataś cainaṁ papracchatur anāmayam |
śarīre ’gniṣu vr̥kṣeṣu śiṣyeṣu mr̥gapakṣiṣu ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tatheti ca pratijñāya bharadvājo mahātapāḥ |
bharataṁ pratyuvācedaṁ rāghavasnehabandhanāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
kim ihāgamane kāryaṁ tava rājyaṁ praśāsataḥ |
etad ācakṣva me sarvaṁ na hi me śudhyate manaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
suṣuve yama mitraghnaṁ kausalyānandavardhanam |
bhrātrā saha sabhāryo yaś ciraṁ pravrājito vanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
niyuktaḥ strīniyuktena pitrā yo ’sau mahāyaśāḥ |
vanavāsī bhavetīha samāḥ kila caturdaśa ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
kaccin na tasyāpāpasya pāpaṁ kartum ihecchasi |
akaṇṭakaṁ bhoktumanā rājyaṁ tasyānujasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
evam ukto bharadvājaṁ bharataḥ pratyuvāca ha |
paryaśru nayano duḥkhād vācā saṁsajjamānayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
hato ’smi yadi mām evaṁ bhagavān api manyate |
matto na doṣam āśaṅker naivaṁ mām anuśādhi hi ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
na caitad iṣṭaṁ mātā me yad avocan madantare |
nāham etena tuṣṭaś ca na tad vacanam ādade ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
ahaṁ tu taṁ naravyāghram upayātaḥ prasādakaḥ |
pratinetum ayodhyāṁ ca pādau tasyābhivanditum ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tvaṁ mām evaṁgataṁ matvā prasādaṁ kartum arhasi |
śaṁsa me bhagavan rāmaḥ kva saṁprati mahīpatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
uvāca taṁ bharadvājaḥ prasādād bharataṁ vacaḥ |
tvayy etat puruṣavyāghra yuktaṁ rāghavavaṁśaje |
guruvr̥ttir damaś caiva sādhūnāṁ cānuyāyitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
jāne caitan manaḥsthaṁ te dr̥ḍhīkaraṇam astv iti |
apr̥cchaṁ tvāṁ tavātyarthaṁ kīrtiṁ samabhivardhayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
asau vasati te bhrātā citrakūṭe mahāgirau |
śvas tu gantāsi taṁ deśaṁ vasādya saha mantribhiḥ |
etaṁ me kuru suprājña kāmaṁ kāmārthakovida ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tatas tathety evam udāradarśanaḥ; pratītarūpo bharato ’bravīd vacaḥ |
cakāra buddhiṁ ca tadā mahāśrame; niśānivāsāya narādhipātmajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
