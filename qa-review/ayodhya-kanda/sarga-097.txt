=== Sarga 97 ===

---
1.
taṁ tu rāmaḥ samāśvāsya bhrātaraṁ guruvatsalam |
lakṣmaṇena saha bhrātrā praṣṭuṁ samupacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
kim etad iccheyam ahaṁ śrotuṁ pravyāhr̥taṁ tvayā |
yasmāt tvam āgato deśam imaṁ cīrajaṭājinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
yannimittam imaṁ deśaṁ kr̥ṣṇājinajaṭādharaḥ |
hitvā rājyaṁ praviṣṭas tvaṁ tat sarvaṁ vaktum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
ity uktaḥ kekayīputraḥ kākutsthena mahātmanā |
pragr̥hya balavad bhūyaḥ prāñjalir vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
āryaṁ tātaḥ parityajya kr̥tvā karma suduṣkaram |
gataḥ svargaṁ mahābāhuḥ putraśokābhipīḍitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
striyā niyuktaḥ kaikeyyā mama mātrā paraṁtapa |
cakāra sumahat pāpam idam ātmayaśoharam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sā rājyaphalam aprāpya vidhavā śokakarśitā |
patiṣyati mahāghore niraye jananī mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tasya me dāsabhūtasya prasādaṁ kartum arhasi |
abhiṣiñcasva cādyaiva rājyena maghavān iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
imāḥ prakr̥tayaḥ sarvā vidhavā māturaś ca yāḥ |
tvat sakāśam anuprāptāḥ prasādaṁ kartum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tadānupūrvyā yuktaṁ ca yuktaṁ cātmani mānada |
rājyaṁ prāpnuhi dharmeṇa sakāmān suhr̥daḥ kuru ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
bhavatv avidhavā bhūmiḥ samagrā patinā tvayā |
śaśinā vimaleneva śāradī rajanī yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
ebhiś ca sacivaiḥ sārdhaṁ śirasā yācito mayā |
bhrātuḥ śiṣyasya dāsasya prasādaṁ kartum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tad idaṁ śāśvataṁ pitryaṁ sarvaṁ sacivamaṇḍalam |
pūjitaṁ puruṣavyāghra nātikramitum utsahe ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
evam uktvā mahābāhuḥ sabāṣpaḥ kekayīsutaḥ |
rāmasya śirasā pādau jagrāha bharataḥ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
taṁ mattam iva mātaṅgaṁ niḥśvasantaṁ punaḥ punaḥ |
bhrātaraṁ bharataṁ rāmaḥ pariṣvajyedam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
kulīnaḥ sattvasaṁpannas tejasvī caritavrataḥ |
rājyahetoḥ kathaṁ pāpam ācaret tvadvidho janaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
na doṣaṁ tvayi paśyāmi sūkṣmam apy ari sūdana |
na cāpi jananīṁ bālyāt tvaṁ vigarhitum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
yāvat pitari dharmajña gauravaṁ lokasatkr̥te |
tāvad dharmabhr̥tāṁ śreṣṭha jananyām api gauravam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
etābhyāṁ dharmaśīlābhyāṁ vanaṁ gaccheti rāghava |
mātā pitr̥bhyām ukto ’haṁ katham anyat samācare ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tvayā rājyam ayodhyāyāṁ prāptavyaṁ lokasatkr̥tam |
vastavyaṁ daṇḍakāraṇye mayā valkalavāsasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
evaṁ kr̥tvā mahārājo vibhāgaṁ lokasaṁnidhau |
vyādiśya ca mahātejā divaṁ daśaratho gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
sa ca pramāṇaṁ dharmātmā rājā lokagurus tava |
pitrā dattaṁ yathābhāgam upabhoktuṁ tvam arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
caturdaśa samāḥ saumya daṇḍakāraṇyam āśritaḥ |
upabhokṣye tv ahaṁ dattaṁ bhāgaṁ pitrā mahātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
yad abravīn māṁ naralokasatkr̥taḥ; pitā mahātmā vibudhādhipopamaḥ |
tad eva manye paramātmano hitaṁ; na sarvalokeśvarabhāvam avyayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
