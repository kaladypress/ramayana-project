=== Sarga 9 ===

---
1.
evam uktā tu kaikeyī krodhena jvalitānanā |
dīrgham uṣṇaṁ viniḥśvasya mantharām idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
adya rāmam itaḥ kṣipraṁ vanaṁ prasthāpayāmy aham |
yauvarājyena bharataṁ kṣipram evābhiṣecaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
idaṁ tv idānīṁ saṁpaśya kenopāyena manthare |
bharataḥ prāpnuyād rājyaṁ na tu rāmaḥ kathaṁ cana ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
evam uktā tayā devyā mantharā pāpadarśinī |
rāmārtham upahiṁsantī kaikeyīm idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
hantedānīṁ pravakṣyāmi kaikeyi śrūyatāṁ ca me |
yathā te bharato rājyaṁ putraḥ prāpsyati kevalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
śrutvaivaṁ vacanaṁ tasyā mantharāyās tu kaikayī |
kiṁ cid utthāya śayanāt svāstīrṇād idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
kathaya tvaṁ mamopāyaṁ kenopāyena manthare |
bharataḥ prāpnuyād rājyaṁ na tu rāmaḥ kathaṁ cana ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
evam uktā tayā devyā mantharā pāpadarśinī |
rāmārtham upahiṁsantī kubjā vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tava devāsure yuddhe saha rājarṣibhiḥ patiḥ |
agacchat tvām upādāya devarājasya sāhyakr̥t ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
diśam āsthāya kaikeyi dakṣiṇāṁ daṇḍakān prati |
vaijayantam iti khyātaṁ puraṁ yatra timidhvajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sa śambara iti khyātaḥ śatamāyo mahāsuraḥ |
dadau śakrasya saṁgrāmaṁ devasaṁghair anirjitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tasmin mahati saṁgrāme rājā daśarathas tadā |
apavāhya tvayā devi saṁgrāmān naṣṭacetanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tatrāpi vikṣataḥ śastraiḥ patis te rakṣitas tvayā |
tuṣṭena tena dattau te dvau varau śubhadarśane ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sa tvayoktaḥ patir devi yadeccheyaṁ tadā varau |
gr̥hṇīyām iti tat tena tathety uktaṁ mahātmanā |
anabhijñā hy ahaṁ devi tvayaiva kathitaṁ purā ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tau varau yāca bhartāraṁ bharatasyābhiṣecanam |
pravrājanaṁ ca rāmasya tvaṁ varṣāṇi caturdaśa ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
krodhāgāraṁ praviśyādya kruddhevāśvapateḥ sute |
śeṣvānantarhitāyāṁ tvaṁ bhūmau malinavāsinī |
mā smainaṁ pratyudīkṣethā mā cainam abhibhāṣathāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
dayitā tvaṁ sadā bhartur atra me nāsti saṁśayaḥ |
tvatkr̥te ca mahārājo viśed api hutāśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
na tvāṁ krodhayituṁ śakto na kruddhāṁ pratyudīkṣitum |
tava priyārthaṁ rājā hi prāṇān api parityajet ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
na hy atikramituṁ śaktas tava vākyaṁ mahīpatiḥ |
mandasvabhāve budhyasva saubhāgyabalam ātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
maṇimuktāsuvarṇāni ratnāni vividhāni ca |
dadyād daśaratho rājā mā sma teṣu manaḥ kr̥thāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
yau tau devāsure yuddhe varau daśaratho ’dadāt |
tau smāraya mahābhāge so ’rtho mā tvām atikramet ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
yadā tu te varaṁ dadyāt svayam utthāpya rāghavaḥ |
vyavasthāpya mahārājaṁ tvam imaṁ vr̥ṇuyā varam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
rāmaṁ pravrājayāraṇye nava varṣāṇi pañca ca |
bharataḥ kriyatāṁ rājā pr̥thivyāṁ pārthivarṣabhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
evaṁ pravrājitaś caiva rāmo ’rāmo bhaviṣyati |
bharataś ca hatāmitras tava rājā bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
yena kālena rāmaś ca vanāt pratyāgamiṣyati |
tena kālena putras te kr̥tamūlo bhaviṣyati |
saṁgr̥hītamanuṣyaś ca suhr̥dbhiḥ sārdham ātmavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
prāptakālaṁ tu te manye rājānaṁ vītasādhvasā |
rāmābhiṣekasaṁkalpān nigr̥hya vinivartaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
anartham artharūpeṇa grāhitā sā tatas tayā |
hr̥ṣṭā pratītā kaikeyī mantharām idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
kubje tvāṁ nābhijānāmi śreṣṭhāṁ śreṣṭhābhidhāyinīm |
pr̥thivyām asi kubjānām uttamā buddhiniścaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
tvam eva tu mamārtheṣu nityayuktā hitaiṣiṇī |
nāhaṁ samavabudhyeyaṁ kubje rājñaś cikīrṣitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
santi duḥsaṁsthitāḥ kubjā vakrāḥ paramapāpikāḥ |
tvaṁ padmam iva vātena saṁnatā priyadarśanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
uras te ’bhiniviṣṭaṁ vai yāvat skandhāt samunnatam |
adhastāc codaraṁ śāntaṁ sunābham iva lajjitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
jaghanaṁ tava nirghuṣṭaṁ raśanādāmaśobhitam |
jaṅghe bhr̥śam upanyaste pādau cāpy āyatāv ubhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
tvam āyatābhyāṁ sakthibhyāṁ manthare kṣaumavāsini |
agrato mama gacchantī rājahaṁsīva rājase ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
tavedaṁ sthagu yad dīrghaṁ rathaghoṇam ivāyatam |
matayaḥ kṣatravidyāś ca māyāś cātra vasanti te ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
atra te pratimokṣyāmi mālāṁ kubje hiraṇmayīm |
abhiṣikte ca bharate rāghave ca vanaṁ gate ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
jātyena ca suvarṇena suniṣṭaptena sundari |
labdhārthā ca pratītā ca lepayiṣyāmi te sthagu ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
mukhe ca tilakaṁ citraṁ jātarūpamayaṁ śubham |
kārayiṣyāmi te kubje śubhāny ābharaṇāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
paridhāya śubhe vastre devateva cariṣyasi |
candram āhvayamānena mukhenāpratimānanā |
gamiṣyasi gatiṁ mukhyāṁ garvayantī dviṣajjanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
tavāpi kubjāḥ kubjāyāḥ sarvābharaṇabhūṣitāḥ |
pādau paricariṣyanti yathaiva tvaṁ sadā mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
iti praśasyamānā sā kaikeyīm idam abravīt |
śayānāṁ śayane śubhre vedyām agniśikhām iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
gatodake setubandho na kalyāṇi vidhīyate |
uttiṣṭha kuru kalyāṇaṁ rājānam anudarśaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
tathā protsāhitā devī gatvā mantharayā saha |
krodhāgāraṁ viśālākṣī saubhāgyamadagarvitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
anekaśatasāhasraṁ muktāhāraṁ varāṅganā |
avamucya varārhāṇi śubhāny ābharaṇāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
tato hemopamā tatra kubjā vākyaṁ vaśaṁ gatā |
saṁviśya bhūmau kaikeyī mantharām idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
iha vā māṁ mr̥tāṁ kubje nr̥pāyāvedayiṣyasi |
vanaṁ tu rāghave prāpte bharataḥ prāpsyati kṣitim ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
athaitad uktvā vacanaṁ sudāruṇaṁ; nidhāya sarvābharaṇāni bhāminī |
asaṁvr̥tām āstaraṇena medinīṁ; tadādhiśiśye patiteva kinnarī ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
udīrṇasaṁrambhatamovr̥tānanā; tathāvamuktottamamālyabhūṣaṇā |
narendrapatnī vimanā babhūva sā; tamovr̥tā dyaur iva magnatārakā ||

=== meaning ===

---
