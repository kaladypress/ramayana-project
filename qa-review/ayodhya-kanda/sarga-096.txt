=== Sarga 96 ===

---
1.
vasiṣṭhaḥ purataḥ kr̥tvā dārān daśarathasya ca |
abhicakrāma taṁ deśaṁ rāmadarśanatarṣitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
rājapatnyaś ca gacchantyo mandaṁ mandākinīṁ prati |
dadr̥śus tatra tat tīrthaṁ rāmalakṣmaṇasevitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
kausalyā bāṣpapūrṇena mukhena pariśuṣyatā |
sumitrām abravīd dīnā yāś cānyā rājayoṣitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
idaṁ teṣām anāthānāṁ kliṣṭam akliṣṭa karmaṇām |
vane prāk kevalaṁ tīrthaṁ ye te nirviṣayī kr̥tāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
itaḥ sumitre putras te sadā jalam atandritaḥ |
svayaṁ harati saumitrir mama putrasya kāraṇāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
dakṣiṇāgreṣu darbheṣu sā dadarśa mahītale |
pitur iṅgudipiṇyākaṁ nyastam āyatalocanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
taṁ bhūmau pitur ārtena nyastaṁ rāmeṇa vīkṣya sā |
uvāca devī kausalyā sarvā daśarathastriyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
idam ikṣvākunāthasya rāghavasya mahātmanaḥ |
rāghaveṇa pitur dattaṁ paśyataitad yathāvidhi ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tasya devasamānasya pārthivasya mahātmanaḥ |
naitad aupayikaṁ manye bhuktabhogasya bhojanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
caturantāṁ mahīṁ bhuktvā mahendra sadr̥śo bhuvi |
katham iṅgudipiṇyākaṁ sa bhuṅkte vasudhādhipaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
ato duḥkhataraṁ loke na kiṁ cit pratibhāti mā |
yatra rāmaḥ pitur dadyād iṅgudīkṣodam r̥ddhimān ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
rāmeṇeṅgudipiṇyākaṁ pitur dattaṁ samīkṣya me |
kathaṁ duḥkhena hr̥dayaṁ na sphoṭati sahasradhā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
evam ārtāṁ sapatnyas tā jagmur āśvāsya tāṁ tadā |
dadr̥śuś cāśrame rāmaṁ svargāc cyutam ivāmaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sarvabhogaiḥ parityaktaṁ rāma saṁprekṣya mātaraḥ |
ārtā mumucur aśrūṇi sasvaraṁ śokakarśitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tāsāṁ rāmaḥ samutthāya jagrāha caraṇāñ śubhān |
mātr̥̄ṇāṁ manujavyāghraḥ sarvāsāṁ satyasaṁgaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tāḥ pāṇibhiḥ sukhasparśair mr̥dvaṅgulitalaiḥ śubhaiḥ |
pramamārjū rajaḥ pr̥ṣṭhād rāmasyāyatalocanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
saumitrir api tāḥ sarvā mātr̥̄ḥ saṁprekṣya duḥkhitaḥ |
abhyavādayatāsaktaṁ śanai rāmād anantaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
yathā rāme tathā tasmin sarvā vavr̥tire striyaḥ |
vr̥ttiṁ daśarathāj jāte lakṣmaṇe śubhalakṣaṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
sītāpi caraṇāṁs tāsām upasaṁgr̥hya duḥkhitā |
śvaśrūṇām aśrupūrṇākṣī sā babhūvāgrataḥ sthitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tāṁ pariṣvajya duḥkhārtāṁ mātā duhitaraṁ yathā |
vanavāsakr̥śāṁ dīnāṁ kausalyā vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
videharājasya sutā snuṣā daśarathasya ca |
rāmapatnī kathaṁ duḥkhaṁ saṁprāptā nirjane vane ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
padmam ātapasaṁtaptaṁ parikliṣṭam ivotpalam |
kāñcanaṁ rajasā dhvastaṁ kliṣṭaṁ candram ivāmbudaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
mukhaṁ te prekṣya māṁ śoko dahaty agnir ivāśrayam |
bhr̥śaṁ manasi vaidehi vyasanāraṇisaṁbhavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
bruvantyām evam ārtāyāṁ jananyāṁ bharatāgrajaḥ |
pādāv āsādya jagrāha vasiṣṭhasya sa rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
purohitasyāgnisamasya tasya vai; br̥haspater indra ivāmarādhipaḥ |
pragr̥hya pādau susamr̥ddhatejasaḥ; sahaiva tenopaviveśa rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tato jaghanyaṁ sahitaiḥ sa mantribhiḥ; purapradhānaiś ca sahaiva sainikaiḥ |
janena dharmajñatamena dharmavān; upopaviṣṭo bharatas tadāgrajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
upopaviṣṭas tu tadā sa vīryavāṁs; tapasviveṣeṇa samīkṣya rāghavam |
śriyā jvalantaṁ bharataḥ kr̥tāñjalir; yathā mahendraḥ prayataḥ prajāpatim ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
kim eṣa vākyaṁ bharato ’dya rāghavaṁ; praṇamya satkr̥tya ca sādhu vakṣyati |
itīva tasyāryajanasya tattvato; babhūva kautūhalam uttamaṁ tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
sa rāghavaḥ satyadhr̥tiś ca lakṣmaṇo; mahānubhāvo bharataś ca dhārmikaḥ |
vr̥tāḥ suhr̥dbhiś ca virejur adhvare; yathā sadasyaiḥ sahitās trayo ’gnayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
