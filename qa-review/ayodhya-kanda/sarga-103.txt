=== Sarga 103 ===

---
1.
vasiṣṭhas tu tadā rāmam uktvā rājapurohitaḥ |
abravīd dharmasaṁyuktaṁ punar evāparaṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
puruṣasyeha jātasya bhavanti guravas trayaḥ |
ācāryaś caiva kākutstha pitā mātā ca rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
pitā hy enaṁ janayati puruṣaṁ puruṣarṣabha |
prajñāṁ dadāti cācāryas tasmāt sa gurur ucyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
sa te ’haṁ pitur ācāryas tava caiva paraṁtapa |
mama tvaṁ vacanaṁ kurvan nātivarteḥ satāṁ gatim ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
imā hi te pariṣadaḥ śreṇayaś ca samāgatāḥ |
eṣu tāta caran dharmaṁ nātivarteḥ satāṁ gatim ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
vr̥ddhāyā dharmaśīlāyā mātur nārhasy avartitum |
asyās tu vacanaṁ kurvan nātivarteḥ satāṁ gatim ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
bharatasya vacaḥ kurvan yācamānasya rāghava |
ātmānaṁ nātivartes tvaṁ satyadharmaparākrama ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
evaṁ madhuram uktas tu guruṇā rāghavaḥ svayam |
pratyuvāca samāsīnaṁ vasiṣṭhaṁ puruṣarṣabhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
yan mātāpitarau vr̥ttaṁ tanaye kurutaḥ sadā |
na supratikaraṁ tat tu mātrā pitrā ca yat kr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
yathāśakti pradānena snāpanāc chādanena ca |
nityaṁ ca priyavādena tathā saṁvardhanena ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sa hi rājā janayitā pitā daśaratho mama |
ājñātaṁ yan mayā tasya na tan mithyā bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
evam uktas tu rāmeṇa bharataḥ pratyanantaram |
uvāca paramodāraḥ sūtaṁ paramadurmanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
iha me sthaṇḍile śīghraṁ kuśān āstara sārathe |
āryaṁ pratyupavekṣyāmi yāvan me na prasīdati ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
anāhāro nirāloko dhanahīno yathā dvijaḥ |
śeṣye purastāc chālāyā yāvan na pratiyāsyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa tu rāmam avekṣantaṁ sumantraṁ prekṣya durmanāḥ |
kuśottaram upasthāpya bhūmāv evāstarat svayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tam uvāca mahātejā rāmo rājarṣisattamāḥ |
kiṁ māṁ bharata kurvāṇaṁ tāta pratyupavekṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
brāhmaṇo hy ekapārśvena narān roddhum ihārhati |
na tu mūrdhāvasiktānāṁ vidhiḥ pratyupaveśane ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
uttiṣṭha naraśārdūla hitvaitad dāruṇaṁ vratam |
puravaryām itaḥ kṣipram ayodhyāṁ yāhi rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
āsīnas tv eva bharataḥ paurajānapadaṁ janam |
uvāca sarvataḥ prekṣya kim āryaṁ nānuśāsatha ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
te tam ūcur mahātmānaṁ paurajānapadā janāḥ |
kākutstham abhijānīmaḥ samyag vadati rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
eṣo ’pi hi mahābhāgaḥ pitur vacasi tiṣṭhati |
ata eva na śaktāḥ smo vyāvartayitum añjasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
teṣām ājñāya vacanaṁ rāmo vacanam abravīt |
evaṁ nibodha vacanaṁ suhr̥dāṁ dharmacakṣuṣām ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
etac caivobhayaṁ śrutvā samyak saṁpaśya rāghava |
uttiṣṭha tvaṁ mahābāho māṁ ca spr̥śa tathodakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
athotthāya jalaṁ spr̥ṣṭvā bharato vākyam abravīt |
śr̥ṇvantu me pariṣado mantriṇaḥ śreṇayas tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
na yāce pitaraṁ rājyaṁ nānuśāsāmi mātaram |
āryaṁ paramadharmajñam abhijānāmi rāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
yadi tv avaśyaṁ vastavyaṁ kartavyaṁ ca pitur vacaḥ |
aham eva nivatsyāmi caturdaśa vane samāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
dharmātmā tasya tathyena bhrātur vākyena vismitaḥ |
uvāca rāmaḥ saṁprekṣya paurajānapadaṁ janam ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
vikrītam āhitaṁ krītaṁ yat pitrā jīvatā mama |
na tal lopayituṁ śakyaṁ mayā vā bharatena vā ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
upadhir na mayā kāryo vanavāse jugupsitaḥ |
yuktam uktaṁ ca kaikeyyā pitrā me sukr̥taṁ kr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
jānāmi bharataṁ kṣāntaṁ gurusatkārakāriṇam |
sarvam evātra kalyāṇaṁ satyasaṁdhe mahātmani ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
anena dharmaśīlena vanāt pratyāgataḥ punaḥ |
bhrātrā saha bhaviṣyāmi pr̥thivyāḥ patir uttamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
vr̥to rājā hi kaikeyyā mayā tad vacanaṁ kr̥tam |
anr̥tān mocayānena pitaraṁ taṁ mahīpatim ||

=== meaning ===

---
