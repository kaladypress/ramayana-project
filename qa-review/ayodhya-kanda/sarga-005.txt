=== Sarga 5 ===

---
1.
saṁdiśya rāmaṁ nr̥patiḥ śvobhāviny abhiṣecane |
purohitaṁ samāhūya vasiṣṭham idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
gacchopavāsaṁ kākutsthaṁ kārayādya tapodhana |
śrīyaśorājyalābhāya vadhvā saha yatavratam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tatheti ca sa rājānam uktvā vedavidāṁ varaḥ |
svayaṁ vasiṣṭho bhagavān yayau rāmaniveśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
sa rāmabhavanaṁ prāpya pāṇḍurābhraghanaprabham |
tisraḥ kakṣyā rathenaiva viveśa munisattamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tam āgatam r̥ṣiṁ rāmas tvarann iva sasaṁbhramaḥ |
mānayiṣyan sa mānārhaṁ niścakrāma niveśanāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
abhyetya tvaramāṇaś ca rathābhyāśaṁ manīṣiṇaḥ |
tato ’vatārayām āsa parigr̥hya rathāt svayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sa cainaṁ praśritaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā saṁbhāṣyābhiprasādya ca |
priyārhaṁ harṣayan rāmam ity uvāca purohitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
prasannas te pitā rāma yauvarājyam avāpsyasi |
upavāsaṁ bhavān adya karotu saha sītayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
prātas tvām abhiṣektā hi yauvarājye narādhipaḥ |
pitā daśarathaḥ prītyā yayātiṁ nahuṣo yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
ity uktvā sa tadā rāmam upavāsaṁ yatavratam |
mantravat kārayām āsa vaidehyā sahitaṁ muniḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tato yathāvad rāmeṇa sa rājño gurur arcitaḥ |
abhyanujñāpya kākutsthaṁ yayau rāmaniveśanāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
suhr̥dbhis tatra rāmo ’pi tān anujñāpya sarvaśaḥ |
sabhājito viveśātha tān anujñāpya sarvaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
hr̥ṣṭanārī narayutaṁ rāmaveśma tadā babhau |
yathā mattadvijagaṇaṁ praphullanalinaṁ saraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sa rājabhavanaprakhyāt tasmād rāmaniveśanāt |
nirgatya dadr̥śe mārgaṁ vasiṣṭho janasaṁvr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
vr̥ndavr̥ndair ayodhyāyāṁ rājamārgāḥ samantataḥ |
babhūvur abhisaṁbādhāḥ kutūhalajanair vr̥tāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
janavr̥ndormisaṁgharṣaharṣasvanavatas tadā |
babhūva rājamārgasya sāgarasyeva nisvanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
siktasaṁmr̥ṣṭarathyā hi tad ahar vanamālinī |
āsīd ayodhyā nagarī samucchritagr̥hadhvajā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tadā hy ayodhyā nilayaḥ sastrībālābalo janaḥ |
rāmābhiṣekam ākāṅkṣann ākāṅkṣann udayaṁ raveḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
prajālaṁkārabhūtaṁ ca janasyānandavardhanam |
utsuko ’bhūj jano draṣṭuṁ tam ayodhyā mahotsavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
evaṁ taṁ janasaṁbādhaṁ rājamārgaṁ purohitaḥ |
vyūhann iva janaughaṁ taṁ śanai rāja kulaṁ yayau ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
sitābhraśikharaprakhyaṁ prāsadam adhiruhya saḥ |
samiyāya narendreṇa śakreṇeva br̥haspatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tam āgatam abhiprekṣya hitvā rājāsanaṁ nr̥paḥ |
papraccha sa ca tasmai tat kr̥tam ity abhyavedayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
guruṇā tv abhyanujñāto manujaughaṁ visr̥jya tam |
viveśāntaḥpuraṁ rājā siṁho giriguhām iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tad agryaveṣapramadājanākulaṁ; mahendraveśmapratimaṁ niveśanam |
vyadīpayaṁś cāru viveśa pārthivaḥ; śaśīva tārāgaṇasaṁkulaṁ nabhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
