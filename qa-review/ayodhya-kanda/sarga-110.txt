=== Sarga 110 ===

---
1.
sā tv evam uktā vaidehī anasūyān asūyayā |
pratipūjya vaco mandaṁ pravaktum upacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
naitad āścaryam āryāyā yan māṁ tvam anubhāṣase |
viditaṁ tu mamāpy etad yathā nāryāḥ patir guruḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
yady apy eṣa bhaved bhartā mamārye vr̥ttavarjitaḥ |
advaidham upavartavyas tathāpy eṣa mayā bhavet ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
kiṁ punar yo guṇaślāghyaḥ sānukrośo jitendriyaḥ |
sthirānurāgo dharmātmā mātr̥vartī pitr̥ priyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
yāṁ vr̥ttiṁ vartate rāmaḥ kausalyāyāṁ mahābalaḥ |
tām eva nr̥panārīṇām anyāsām api vartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
sakr̥d dr̥ṣṭāsv api strīṣu nr̥peṇa nr̥pavatsalaḥ |
mātr̥vad vartate vīro mānam utsr̥jya dharmavit ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
āgacchantyāś ca vijanaṁ vanam evaṁ bhayāvaham |
samāhitaṁ hi me śvaśrvā hr̥daye yat sthitaṁ mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
prāṇipradānakāle ca yat purā tv agnisaṁnidhau |
anuśiṣṭā jananyāsmi vākyaṁ tad api me dhr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
navīkr̥taṁ tu tat sarvaṁ vākyais te dharmacāriṇi |
patiśuśrūṣaṇān nāryās tapo nānyad vidhīyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
sāvitrī patiśuśrūṣāṁ kr̥tvā svarge mahīyate |
tathā vr̥ttiś ca yātā tvaṁ patiśuśrūṣayā divam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
variṣṭhā sarvanārīṇām eṣā ca divi devatā |
rohiṇī ca vinā candraṁ muhūrtam api dr̥śyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
evaṁvidhāś ca pravarāḥ striyo bhartr̥dr̥ḍhavratāḥ |
devaloke mahīyante puṇyena svena karmaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tato ’nasūyā saṁhr̥ṣṭā śrutvoktaṁ sītayā vacaḥ |
śirasy āghrāya covāca maithilīṁ harṣayanty uta ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
niyamair vividhair āptaṁ tapo hi mahad asti me |
tat saṁśritya balaṁ sīte chandaye tvāṁ śucivrate ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
upapannaṁ ca yuktaṁ ca vacanaṁ tava maithili |
prītā cāsmy ucitaṁ kiṁ te karavāṇi bravīhi me |
kr̥tam ity abravīt sītā tapobalasamanvitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sā tv evam uktā dharmajñā tayā prītatarābhavat |
saphalaṁ ca praharṣaṁ te hanta sīte karomy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
idaṁ divyaṁ varaṁ mālyaṁ vastram ābharaṇāni ca |
aṅgarāgaṁ ca vaidehi mahārham anulepanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
mayā dattam idaṁ sīte tava gātrāṇi śobhayet |
anurūpam asaṁkliṣṭaṁ nityam eva bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
aṅgarāgeṇa divyena liptāṅgī janakātmaje |
śobhayiṣyāmi bhartāraṁ yathā śrīr viṣṇum avyayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
sā vastram aṅgarāgaṁ ca bhūṣaṇāni srajas tathā |
maithilī pratijagrāha prītidānam anuttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
pratigr̥hya ca tat sītā prītidānaṁ yaśasvinī |
śliṣṭāñjalipuṭā dhīrā samupāsta tapodhanām ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tathā sītām upāsīnām anasūyā dr̥ḍhavratā |
vacanaṁ praṣṭum ārebhe kathāṁ kāṁ cid anupriyām ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
svayaṁvare kila prāptā tvam anena yaśasvinā |
rāghaveṇeti me sīte kathā śrutim upāgatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tāṁ kathāṁ śrotum icchāmi vistareṇa ca maithili |
yathānubhūtaṁ kārtsnyena tan me tvaṁ vaktum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
evam uktā tu sā sītā tāṁ tato dharmacāriṇīm |
śrūyatām iti coktvā vai kathayām āsa tāṁ kathām ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
mithilādhipatir vīro janako nāma dharmavit |
kṣatradharmaṇy abhirato nyāyataḥ śāsti medinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tasya lāṅgalahastasya karṣataḥ kṣetramaṇḍalam |
ahaṁ kilotthitā bhittvā jagatīṁ nr̥pateḥ sutā ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
sa māṁ dr̥ṣṭvā narapatir muṣṭivikṣepatatparaḥ |
pāṁśu guṇṭhita sarvāṅgīṁ vismito janako ’bhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
anapatyena ca snehād aṅkam āropya ca svayam |
mameyaṁ tanayety uktvā sneho mayi nipātitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
antarikṣe ca vāg uktāpratimā mānuṣī kila |
evam etan narapate dharmeṇa tanayā tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
tataḥ prahr̥ṣṭo dharmātmā pitā me mithilādhipaḥ |
avāpto vipulām r̥ddhiṁ mām avāpya narādhipaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
dattvā cāsmīṣṭavad devyai jyeṣṭhāyai puṇyakarmaṇā |
tayā saṁbhāvitā cāsmi snigdhayā mātr̥sauhr̥dāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
patisaṁyogasulabhaṁ vayo dr̥ṣṭvā tu me pitā |
cintām abhyagamad dīno vittanāśād ivādhanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
sadr̥śāc cāpakr̥ṣṭāc ca loke kanyāpitā janāt |
pradharṣaṇām avāpnoti śakreṇāpi samo bhuvi ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
tāṁ dharṣaṇām adūrasthāṁ saṁdr̥śyātmani pārthivaḥ |
cinntārṇavagataḥ pāraṁ nāsasādāplavo yatha ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
ayonijāṁ hi māṁ jñātvā nādhyagacchat sa cintayan |
sadr̥śaṁ cānurūpaṁ ca mahīpālaḥ patiṁ mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
tasya buddhir iyaṁ jātā cintayānasya saṁtatam |
svayaṁ varaṁ tanūjāyāḥ kariṣyāmīti dhīmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
mahāyajñe tadā tasya varuṇena mahātmanā |
dattaṁ dhanurvaraṁ prītyā tūṇī cākṣayya sāyakau ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
asaṁcālyaṁ manuṣyaiś ca yatnenāpi ca gauravāt |
tan na śaktā namayituṁ svapneṣv api narādhipāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
tad dhanuḥ prāpya me pitrā vyāhr̥taṁ satyavādinā |
samavāye narendrāṇāṁ pūrvam āmantrya pārthivān ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
idaṁ ca dhanur udyamya sajyaṁ yaḥ kurute naraḥ |
tasya me duhitā bhāryā bhaviṣyati na saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
tac ca dr̥ṣṭvā dhanuḥśreṣṭhaṁ gauravād girisaṁnibham |
abhivādya nr̥pā jagmur aśaktās tasya tolane ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
sudīrghasya tu kālasya rāghavo ’yaṁ mahādyutiḥ |
viśvāmitreṇa sahito yajñaṁ draṣṭuṁ samāgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
lakṣmaṇena saha bhrātrā rāmaḥ satyaparākramaḥ |
viśvāmitras tu dharmātmā mama pitrā supūjitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
provāca pitaraṁ tatra rāghavo rāmalakṣmaṇau |
sutau daśarathasyemau dhanurdarśanakāṅkṣiṇau |
ity uktas tena vipreṇa tad dhanuḥ samupānayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
nimeṣāntaramātreṇa tad ānamya sa vīryavān |
jyāṁ samāropya jhaṭiti pūrayām āsa vīryavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
tena pūrayatā vegān madhye bhagnaṁ dvidhā dhanuḥ |
tasya śabdo ’bhavad bhīmaḥ patitasyāśaner iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
tato ’haṁ tatra rāmāya pitrā satyābhisaṁdhinā |
udyatā dātum udyamya jalabhājanam uttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
dīyamānāṁ na tu tadā pratijagrāha rāghavaḥ |
avijñāya pituś chandam ayodhyādhipateḥ prabhoḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
tataḥ śvaśuram āmantrya vr̥ddhaṁ daśarathaṁ nr̥pam |
mama pitrā ahaṁ dattā rāmāya viditātmane ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
mama caivānujā sādhvī ūrmilā priyadarśanā |
bhāryārthe lakṣmaṇasyāpi dattā pitrā mama svayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
52.
evaṁ dattāsmi rāmāya tadā tasmin svayaṁ vare |
anuraktā ca dharmeṇa patiṁ vīryavatāṁ varam ||

=== meaning ===

---
