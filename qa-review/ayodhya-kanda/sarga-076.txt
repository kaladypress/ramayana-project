=== Sarga 76 ===

---
1.
tām āryagaṇasaṁpūrṇāṁ bharataḥ pragrahāṁ sabhām |
dadarśa buddhisaṁpannaḥ pūrṇacandrāṁ niśām iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
āsanāni yathānyāyam āryāṇāṁ viśatāṁ tadā |
adr̥śyata ghanāpāye pūrṇacandreva śarvarī ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
rājñas tu prakr̥tīḥ sarvāḥ samagrāḥ prekṣya dharmavit |
idaṁ purohito vākyaṁ bharataṁ mr̥du cābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tāta rājā daśarathaḥ svargato dharmam ācaran |
dhana dhānyavatīṁ sphītāṁ pradāya pr̥thivīṁ tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
rāmas tathā satyadhr̥tiḥ satāṁ dharmam anusmaran |
nājahāt pitur ādeśaṁ śaśī jyotsnām ivoditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
pitrā bhrātrā ca te dattaṁ rājyaṁ nihatakaṇṭakam |
tad bhuṅkṣva muditāmātyaḥ kṣipram evābhiṣecaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
udīcyāś ca pratīcyāś ca dākṣiṇātyāś ca kevalāḥ |
koṭyāparāntāḥ sāmudrā ratnāny abhiharantu te ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tac chrutvā bharato vākyaṁ śokenābhipariplutaḥ |
jagāma manasā rāmaṁ dharmajño dharmakāṅkṣayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sa bāṣpakalayā vācā kalahaṁsasvaro yuvā |
vilalāpa sabhāmadhye jagarhe ca purohitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
caritabrahmacaryasya vidyā snātasya dhīmataḥ |
dharme prayatamānasya ko rājyaṁ madvidho haret ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
kathaṁ daśarathāj jāto bhaved rājyāpahārakaḥ |
rājyaṁ cāhaṁ ca rāmasya dharmaṁ vaktum ihārhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
jyeṣṭhaḥ śreṣṭhaś ca dharmātmā dilīpanahuṣopamaḥ |
labdhum arhati kākutstho rājyaṁ daśaratho yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
anāryajuṣṭam asvargyaṁ kuryāṁ pāpam ahaṁ yadi |
ikṣvākūṇām ahaṁ loke bhaveyaṁ kulapāṁsanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
yad dhi mātrā kr̥taṁ pāpaṁ nāhaṁ tad abhirocaye |
ihastho vanadurgasthaṁ namasyāmi kr̥tāñjaliḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
rāmam evānugacchāmi sa rājā dvipadāṁ varaḥ |
trayāṇām api lokānāṁ rāghavo rājyam arhati ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tad vākyaṁ dharmasaṁyuktaṁ śrutvā sarve sabhāsadaḥ |
harṣān mumucur aśrūṇi rāme nihitacetasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
yadi tv āryaṁ na śakṣyāmi vinivartayituṁ vanāt |
vane tatraiva vatsyāmi yathāryo lakṣmaṇas tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
sarvopāyaṁ tu vartiṣye vinivartayituṁ balāt |
samakṣam ārya miśrāṇāṁ sādhūnāṁ guṇavartinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
evam uktvā tu dharmātmā bharato bhrātr̥vatsalaḥ |
samīpastham uvācedaṁ sumantraṁ mantrakovidam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tūrṇam utthāya gaccha tvaṁ sumantra mama śāsanāt |
yātrām ājñāpaya kṣipraṁ balaṁ caiva samānaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
evam uktaḥ sumantras tu bharatena mahātmanā |
prahr̥ṣṭaḥ so ’diśat sarvaṁ yathā saṁdiṣṭam iṣṭavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tāḥ prahr̥ṣṭāḥ prakr̥tayo balādhyakṣā balasya ca |
śrutvā yātrāṁ samājñaptāṁ rāghavasya nivartane ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tato yodhāṅganāḥ sarvā bhartr̥̄n sarvān gr̥hegr̥he |
yātrā gamanam ājñāya tvarayanti sma harṣitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
te hayair gorathaiḥ śīghraiḥ syandanaiś ca manojavaiḥ |
saha yodhair balādhyakṣā balaṁ sarvam acodayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
sajjaṁ tu tad balaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā bharato gurusaṁnidhau |
rathaṁ me tvarayasveti sumantraṁ pārśvato ’bravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
bharatasya tu tasyājñāṁ pratigr̥hya praharṣitaḥ |
rathaṁ gr̥hītvā prayayau yuktaṁ paramavājibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
sa rāghavaḥ satyadhr̥tiḥ pratāpavān; bruvan suyuktaṁ dr̥ḍhasatyavikramaḥ |
guruṁ mahāraṇyagataṁ yaśasvinaṁ; prasādayiṣyan bharato ’bravīt tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tūrṇaṁ samutthāya sumantra gaccha; balasya yogāya balapradhānān |
ānetum icchāmi hi taṁ vanasthaṁ; prasādya rāmaṁ jagato hitāya ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
sa sūtaputro bharatena samyag; ājñāpitaḥ saṁparipūrṇakāmaḥ |
śaśāsa sarvān prakr̥tipradhānān; balasya mukhyāṁś ca suhr̥jjanaṁ ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
tataḥ samutthāya kule kule te; rājanyavaiśyā vr̥ṣalāś ca viprāḥ |
ayūyujann uṣṭrarathān kharāṁś ca; nāgān hayāṁś caiva kulaprasūtān ||

=== meaning ===

---
