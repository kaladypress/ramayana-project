=== Sarga 8 ===

---
1.
mantharā tv abhyasūyyainām utsr̥jyābharaṇaṁ ca tat |
uvācedaṁ tato vākyaṁ kopaduḥkhasamanvitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
harṣaṁ kim idam asthāne kr̥tavaty asi bāliśe |
śokasāgaramadhyastham ātmānaṁ nāvabudhyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
subhagā khalu kausalyā yasyāḥ putro ’bhiṣekṣyate |
yauvarājyena mahatā śvaḥ puṣyeṇa dvijottamaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
prāptāṁ sumahatīṁ prītiṁ pratītāṁ tāṁ hatadviṣam |
upasthāsyasi kausalyāṁ dāsīva tvaṁ kr̥tāñjaliḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
hr̥ṣṭāḥ khalu bhaviṣyanti rāmasya paramāḥ striyaḥ |
aprahr̥ṣṭā bhaviṣyanti snuṣās te bharatakṣaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā paramaprītāṁ bruvantīṁ mantharāṁ tataḥ |
rāmasyaiva guṇān devī kaikeyī praśaśaṁsa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
dharmajño gurubhir dāntaḥ kr̥tajñaḥ satyavāk śuciḥ |
rāmo rājñaḥ suto jyeṣṭho yauvarājyam ato ’rhati ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
bhrātr̥̄n bhr̥tyāṁś ca dīrghāyuḥ pitr̥vat pālayiṣyati |
saṁtapyase kathaṁ kubje śrutvā rāmābhiṣecanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
bharataś cāpi rāmasya dhruvaṁ varṣaśatāt param |
pitr̥paitāmahaṁ rājyam avāpsyati nararṣabhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
sā tvam abhyudaye prāpte vartamāne ca manthare |
bhaviṣyati ca kalyāṇe kimarthaṁ paritapyase |
kausalyāto ’tiriktaṁ ca sa tu śuśrūṣate hi mām ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
kaikeyyā vacanaṁ śrutvā mantharā bhr̥śaduḥkhitā |
dīrgham uṣṇaṁ viniḥśvasya kaikeyīm idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
anarthadarśinī maurkhyān nātmānam avabudhyase |
śokavyasanavistīrṇe majjantī duḥkhasāgare ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
bhavitā rāghavo rājā rāghavasya ca yaḥ sutaḥ |
rājavaṁśāt tu bharataḥ kaikeyi parihāsyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
na hi rājñaḥ sutāḥ sarve rājye tiṣṭhanti bhāmini |
sthāpyamāneṣu sarveṣu sumahān anayo bhavet ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tasmāj jyeṣṭhe hi kaikeyi rājyatantrāṇi pārthivāḥ |
sthāpayanty anavadyāṅgi guṇavatsv itareṣv api ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
asāv atyantanirbhagnas tava putro bhaviṣyati |
anāthavat sukhebhyaś ca rājavaṁśāc ca vatsale ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
sāhaṁ tvadarthe saṁprāptā tvaṁ tu māṁ nāvabudhyase |
sapatnivr̥ddhau yā me tvaṁ pradeyaṁ dātum icchasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
dhruvaṁ tu bharataṁ rāmaḥ prāpya rājyam akaṇṭakam |
deśāntaraṁ nāyayitā lokāntaram athāpi vā ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
bāla eva hi mātulyaṁ bharato nāyitas tvayā |
saṁnikarṣāc ca sauhārdaṁ jāyate sthāvareṣv api ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
goptā hi rāmaṁ saumitrir lakṣmaṇaṁ cāpi rāghavaḥ |
aśvinor iva saubhrātraṁ tayor lokeṣu viśrutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tasmān na lakṣmaṇe rāmaḥ pāpaṁ kiṁ cit kariṣyati |
rāmas tu bharate pāpaṁ kuryād iti na saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tasmād rājagr̥hād eva vanaṁ gacchatu te sutaḥ |
etad dhi rocate mahyaṁ bhr̥śaṁ cāpi hitaṁ tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
evaṁ te jñātipakṣasya śreyaś caiva bhaviṣyati |
yadi ced bharato dharmāt pitryaṁ rājyam avāpsyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
sa te sukhocito bālo rāmasya sahajo ripuḥ |
samr̥ddhārthasya naṣṭārtho jīviṣyati kathaṁ vaśe ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
abhidrutam ivāraṇye siṁhena gajayūthapam |
pracchādyamānaṁ rāmeṇa bharataṁ trātum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
darpān nirākr̥tā pūrvaṁ tvayā saubhāgyavattayā |
rāmamātā sapatnī te kathaṁ vairaṁ na yātayet ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
yadā hi rāmaḥ pr̥thivīm avāpsyati; dhruvaṁ pranaṣṭo bharato bhaviṣyati |
ato hi saṁcintaya rājyam ātmaje; parasya cādyaiva vivāsakāraṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
