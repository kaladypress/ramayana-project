=== Sarga 32 ===

---
1.
tataḥ sumantram aikṣvākaḥ pīḍito ’tra pratijñayā |
sabāṣpam atiniḥśvasya jagādedaṁ punaḥ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sūta ratnasusaṁpūrṇā caturvidhabalā camūḥ |
rāgavasyānuyātrārthaṁ kṣipraṁ pratividhīyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
rūpājīvā ca śālinyo vaṇijaś ca mahādhanāḥ |
śobhayantu kumārasya vāhinīṁ suprasāritāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
ye cainam upajīvanti ramate yaiś ca vīryataḥ |
teṣāṁ bahuvidhaṁ dattvā tān apy atra niyojaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
nighnan mr̥gān kuñjarāṁś ca pibaṁś cāraṇyakaṁ madhu |
nadīś ca vividhāḥ paśyan na rājyaṁ saṁsmariṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
dhānyakośaś ca yaḥ kaś cid dhanakośaś ca māmakaḥ |
tau rāmam anugacchetāṁ vasantaṁ nirjane vane ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
yajan puṇyeṣu deśeṣu visr̥jaṁś cāptadakṣiṇāḥ |
r̥ṣibhiś ca samāgamya pravatsyati sukhaṁ vane ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
bharataś ca mahābāhur ayodhyāṁ pālayiṣyati |
sarvakāmaiḥ punaḥ śrīmān rāmaḥ saṁsādhyatām iti ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
evaṁ bruvati kākutsthe kaikeyyā bhayam āgatam |
mukhaṁ cāpy agamāc choṣaṁ svaraś cāpi nyarudhyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
sā viṣaṇṇā ca saṁtrastā kaikeyī vākyam abravīt |
rājyaṁ gatajanaṁ sādho pītamaṇḍāṁ surām iva |
nirāsvādyatamaṁ śūnyaṁ bharato nābhipatsyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
kaikeyyāṁ muktalajjāyāṁ vadantyām atidāruṇam |
rājā daśaratho vākyam uvācāyatalocanām |
vahantaṁ kiṁ tudasi māṁ niyujya dhuri māhite ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
kaikeyī dviguṇaṁ kruddhā rājānam idam abravīt |
tavaiva vaṁśe sagaro jyeṣṭhaṁ putram upārudhat |
asamañja iti khyātaṁ tathāyaṁ gantum arhati ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
evam ukto dhig ity eva rājā daśaratho ’bravīt |
vrīḍitaś ca janaḥ sarvaḥ sā ca tan nāvabudhyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tatra vr̥ddho mahāmātraḥ siddhārtho nāma nāmataḥ |
śucir bahumato rājñaḥ kaikeyīm idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
asamañjo gr̥hītvā tu krīḍitaḥ pathi dārakān |
sarayvāḥ prakṣipann apsu ramate tena durmatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā nāgaraḥ sarve kruddhā rājānam abruvan |
asamañjaṁ vr̥ṣīṇvaikam asmān vā rāṣṭravardhana ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tān uvāca tato rājā kiṁnimittam idaṁ bhayam |
tāś cāpi rājñā saṁpr̥ṣṭā vākyaṁ prakr̥tayo ’bruvan ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
krīḍitas tv eṣa naḥ putrān bālān udbhrāntacetanaḥ |
sarayvāṁ prakṣipan maurkhyād atulāṁ prītim aśnute ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
sa tāsāṁ vacanaṁ śrutvā prakr̥tīnāṁ narādhipa |
taṁ tatyājāhitaṁ putraṁ tāsāṁ priyacikīrṣayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
ity evam atyajad rājā sagaro vai sudhārmikaḥ |
rāmaḥ kim akarot pāpaṁ yenaivam uparudhyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
śrutvā tu siddhārthavaco rājā śrāntatarasvanaḥ |
śokopahatayā vācā kaikeyīm idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
anuvrajiṣyāmy aham adya rāmaṁ; rājyaṁ parityajya sukhaṁ dhanaṁ ca |
sahaiva rājñā bharatena ca tvaṁ; yathā sukhaṁ bhuṅkṣva cirāya rājyam ||

=== meaning ===

---
