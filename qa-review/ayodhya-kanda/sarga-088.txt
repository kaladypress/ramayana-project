=== Sarga 88 ===

---
1.
dīrghakāloṣitas tasmin girau girivanapriyaḥ |
videhyāḥ priyamākāṅkṣan svaṁ ca cittaṁ vilobhayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
atha dāśarathiś citraṁ citrakūṭam adarśayat |
bhāryām amarasaṁkāśaḥ śacīm iva puraṁdaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
na rājyād bhraṁśanaṁ bhadre na suhr̥dbhir vinābhavaḥ |
mano me bādhate dr̥ṣṭvā ramaṇīyam imaṁ girim ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
paśyemam acalaṁ bhadre nānādvijagaṇāyutam |
śikharaiḥ kham ivodviddhair dhātumadbhir vibhūṣitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
ke cid rajatasaṁkāśāḥ ke cit kṣatajasaṁnibhāḥ |
pītamāñjiṣṭhavarṇāś ca ke cin maṇivaraprabhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
puṣyārkaketukābhāś ca ke cij jyotī rasaprabhāḥ |
virājante ’calendrasya deśā dhātuvibhūṣitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
nānāmr̥gagaṇadvīpitarakṣvr̥kṣagaṇair vr̥taḥ |
aduṣṭair bhāty ayaṁ śailo bahupakṣisamākulaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
āmrajambvasanair lodhraiḥ priyālaiḥ panasair dhavaiḥ |
aṅkolair bhavyatiniśair bilvatindukaveṇubhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
kāśmaryariṣṭavaraṇair madhūkais tilakais tathā |
badaryāmalakair nīpair vetradhanvanabījakaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
puṣpavadbhiḥ phalopetaiś chāyāvadbhir manoramaiḥ |
evamādibhir ākīrṇaḥ śriyaṁ puṣyaty ayaṁ giriḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
śailaprastheṣu ramyeṣu paśyemān kāmaharṣaṇān |
kinnarān dvaṁdvaśo bhadre ramamāṇān manasvinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
śākhāvasaktān khaḍgāṁś ca pravarāṇy ambarāṇi ca |
paśya vidyādharastrīṇāṁ krīḍed deśān manoramān ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
jalaprapātair udbhedair niṣyandaiś ca kva cit kva cit |
sravadbhir bhāty ayaṁ śailaḥ sravan mada iva dvipaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
guhāsamīraṇo gandhān nānāpuṣpabhavān vahan |
ghrāṇatarpaṇam abhyetya kaṁ naraṁ na praharṣayet ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
yadīha śarado ’nekās tvayā sārdham anindite |
lakṣmaṇena ca vatsyāmi na māṁ śokaḥ pradhakṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
bahupuṣpaphale ramye nānādvijagaṇāyute |
vicitraśikhare hy asmin ratavān asmi bhāmini ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
anena vanavāsena mayā prāptaṁ phaladvayam |
pituś cānr̥ṇatā dharme bharatasya priyaṁ tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
vaidehi ramase kaccic citrakūṭe mayā saha |
paśyantī vividhān bhāvān manovākkāyasaṁyatān ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
idam evāmr̥taṁ prāhū rājñāṁ rājarṣayaḥ pare |
vanavāsaṁ bhavārthāya pretya me prapitāmahāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
śilāḥ śailasya śobhante viśālāḥ śataśo ’bhitaḥ |
bahulā bahulair varṇair nīlapītasitāruṇaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
niśi bhānty acalendrasya hutāśanaśikhā iva |
oṣadhyaḥ svaprabhā lakṣmyā bhrājamānāḥ sahasraśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
ke cit kṣayanibhā deśāḥ ke cid udyānasaṁnibhāḥ |
ke cid ekaśilā bhānti parvatasyāsya bhāmini ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
bhittveva vasudhāṁ bhāti citrakūṭaḥ samutthitaḥ |
citrakūṭasya kūṭo ’sau dr̥śyate sarvataḥ śivaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
kuṣṭhapuṁnāgatagarabhūrjapatrottaracchadān |
kāmināṁ svāstarān paśya kuśeśayadalāyutān ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
mr̥ditāś cāpaviddhāś ca dr̥śyante kamalasrajaḥ |
kāmibhir vanite paśya phalāni vividhāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
vasvaukasārāṁ nalinīm atyetīvottarān kurūn |
parvataś citrakūṭo ’sau bahumūlaphalodakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
imaṁ tu kālaṁ vanite vijahrivāṁs; tvayā ca sīte saha lakṣmaṇena ca |
ratiṁ prapatsye kuladharmavardhinīṁ; satāṁ pathi svair niyamaiḥ paraiḥ sthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
