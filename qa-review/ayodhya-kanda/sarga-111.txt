=== Sarga 111 ===

---
1.
anasūyā tu dharmajñā śrutvā tāṁ mahatīṁ kathām |
paryaṣvajata bāhubhyāṁ śirasy āghrāya maithilīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
vyaktākṣarapadaṁ citraṁ bhāṣitaṁ madhuraṁ tvayā |
yathā svayaṁvaraṁ vr̥ttaṁ tat sarvaṁ hi śrutaṁ mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
rame ’haṁ kathayā te tu dr̥ḍhaṁ madhurabhāṣiṇi |
ravir astaṁ gataḥ śrīmān upohya rajanīṁ śivām ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
divasaṁ prati kīrṇānām āhārārthaṁ patatriṇām |
saṁdhyākāle nilīnānāṁ nidrārthaṁ śrūyate dhvaniḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
ete cāpy abhiṣekārdrā munayaḥ phalaśodhanāḥ |
sahitā upavartante salilāplutavalkalāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
r̥ṣīṇām agnihotreṣu huteṣu vidhipurvakam |
kapotāṅgāruṇo dhūmo dr̥śyate pavanoddhataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
alpaparṇā hi taravo ghanībhūtāḥ samantataḥ |
viprakr̥ṣṭe ’pi ye deśe na prakāśanti vai diśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
rajanī rasasattvāni pracaranti samantataḥ |
tapovanamr̥gā hy ete veditīrtheṣu śerate ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
saṁpravr̥ttā niśā sīte nakṣatrasamalaṁkr̥tā |
jyotsnā prāvaraṇaś candro dr̥śyate ’bhyudito ’mbare ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
gamyatām anujānāmi rāmasyānucarī bhava |
kathayantyā hi madhuraṁ tvayāhaṁ paritoṣitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
alaṁkuru ca tāvat tvaṁ pratyakṣaṁ mama maithili |
prītiṁ janaya me vatsa divyālaṁkāraśobhinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sā tadā samalaṁkr̥tya sītā surasutopamā |
praṇamya śirasā tasyai rāmaṁ tv abhimukhī yayau ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tathā tu bhūṣitāṁ sītāṁ dadarśa vadatāṁ varaḥ |
rāghavaḥ prītidānena tapasvinyā jaharṣa ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
nyavedayat tataḥ sarvaṁ sītā rāmāya maithilī |
prītidānaṁ tapasvinyā vasanābharaṇasrajām ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
prahr̥ṣṭas tv abhavad rāmo lakṣmaṇaś ca mahārathaḥ |
maithilyāḥ satkriyāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā mānuṣeṣu sudurlabhām ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tatas tāṁ sarvarīṁ prītaḥ puṇyāṁ śaśinibhānanaḥ |
arcitas tāpasaiḥ siddhair uvāsa raghunandanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tasyāṁ rātryāṁ vyatītāyām abhiṣicya hutāgnikān |
āpr̥cchetāṁ naravyāghrau tāpasān vanagocarān ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tāv ūcus te vanacarās tāpasā dharmacāriṇaḥ |
vanasya tasya saṁcāraṁ rākṣasaiḥ samabhiplutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
eṣa panthā maharṣīṇāṁ phalāny āharatāṁ vane |
anena tu vanaṁ durgaṁ gantuṁ rāghava te kṣamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
itīva taiḥ prāñjalibhis tapasvibhir; dvijaiḥ kr̥tasvastyayanaḥ paraṁtapaḥ |
vanaṁ sabhāryaḥ praviveśa rāghavaḥ; salakṣmaṇaḥ sūrya ivābhramaṇḍalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
