=== Sarga 4 ===

---
1.
gateṣv atha nr̥po bhūyaḥ paureṣu saha mantribhiḥ |
mantrayitvā tataś cakre niścayajñaḥ sa niścayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
śva eva puṣyo bhavitā śvo ’bhiṣecyeta me sutaḥ |
rāmo rājīvatāmrākṣo yauvarājya iti prabhuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
athāntargr̥ham āviśya rājā daśarathas tadā |
sūtam ājñāpayām āsa rāmaṁ punar ihānaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
pratigr̥hya sa tadvākyaṁ sūtaḥ punar upāyayau |
rāmasya bhavanaṁ śīghraṁ rāmam ānayituṁ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
dvāḥsthair āveditaṁ tasya rāmāyāgamanaṁ punaḥ |
śrutvaiva cāpi rāmas taṁ prāptaṁ śaṅkānvito ’bhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
praveśya cainaṁ tvaritaṁ rāmo vacanam abravīt |
yad āgamanakr̥tyaṁ te bhūyas tad brūhy aśeṣataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tam uvāca tataḥ sūto rājā tvāṁ draṣṭum icchati |
śrutvā pramāṇam atra tvaṁ gamanāyetarāya vā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
iti sūtavacaḥ śrutvā rāmo ’tha tvarayānvitaḥ |
prayayau rājabhavanaṁ punar draṣṭuṁ nareśvaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
taṁ śrutvā samanuprāptaṁ rāmaṁ daśaratho nr̥paḥ |
praveśayām āsa gr̥haṁ vivikṣuḥ priyam uttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
praviśann eva ca śrīmān rāghavo bhavanaṁ pituḥ |
dadarśa pitaraṁ dūrāt praṇipatya kr̥tāñjaliḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
praṇamantaṁ samutthāpya taṁ pariṣvajya bhūmipaḥ |
pradiśya cāsmai ruciram āsanaṁ punar abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
rāma vr̥ddho ’smi dīrghāyur bhuktā bhogā mayepsitāḥ |
annavadbhiḥ kratuśatais tatheṣṭaṁ bhūridakṣiṇaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
jātam iṣṭam apatyaṁ me tvam adyānupamaṁ bhuvi |
dattam iṣṭam adhītaṁ ca mayā puruṣasattama ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
anubhūtāni ceṣṭāni mayā vīra sukhāni ca |
devarṣi pitr̥viprāṇām anr̥ṇo ’smi tathātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
na kiṁ cin mama kartavyaṁ tavānyatrābhiṣecanāt |
ato yat tvām ahaṁ brūyāṁ tan me tvaṁ kartum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
adya prakr̥tayaḥ sarvās tvām icchanti narādhipam |
atas tvāṁ yuvarājānam abhiṣekṣyāmi putraka ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
api cādyāśubhān rāma svapnān paśyāmi dāruṇān |
sanirghātā maholkāś ca patantīha mahāsvanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
avaṣṭabdhaṁ ca me rāma nakṣatraṁ dāruṇair grahaiḥ |
āvedayanti daivajñāḥ sūryāṅgārakarāhubhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
prāyeṇa hi nimittānām īdr̥śānāṁ samudbhave |
rājā vā mr̥tyum āpnoti ghorāṁ vāpadam r̥cchati ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tad yāvad eva me ceto na vimuhyati rāghava |
tāvad evābhiṣiñcasva calā hi prāṇināṁ matiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
adya candro ’bhyupagataḥ puṣyāt pūrvaṁ punar vasum |
śvaḥ puṣya yogaṁ niyataṁ vakṣyante daivacintakāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tatra puṣye ’bhiṣiñcasva manas tvarayatīva mām |
śvas tvāham abhiṣekṣyāmi yauvarājye paraṁtapa ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tasmāt tvayādya vratinā niśeyaṁ niyatātmanā |
saha vadhvopavastavyā darbhaprastaraśāyinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
suhr̥daś cāpramattās tvāṁ rakṣantv adya samantataḥ |
bhavanti bahuvighnāni kāryāṇy evaṁvidhāni hi ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
viproṣitaś ca bharato yāvad eva purād itaḥ |
tāvad evābhiṣekas te prāptakālo mato mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
kāmaṁ khalu satāṁ vr̥tte bhrātā te bharataḥ sthitaḥ |
jyeṣṭhānuvartī dharmātmā sānukrośo jitendriyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
kiṁ tu cittaṁ manuṣyāṇām anityam iti me matiḥ |
satāṁ ca dharmanityānāṁ kr̥taśobhi ca rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
ity uktaḥ so ’bhyanujñātaḥ śvobhāviny abhiṣecane |
vrajeti rāmaḥ pitaram abhivādyābhyayād gr̥ham ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
praviśya cātmano veśma rājñoddiṣṭe ’bhiṣecane |
tasmin kṣaṇe vinirgatya mātur antaḥpuraṁ yayau ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
tatra tāṁ pravaṇām eva mātaraṁ kṣaumavāsinīm |
vāgyatāṁ devatāgāre dadarśa yācatīṁ śriyam ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
prāg eva cāgatā tatra sumitrā lakṣmaṇas tathā |
sītā cānāyitā śrutvā priyaṁ rāmābhiṣecanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
tasmin kāle hi kausalyā tasthāv āmīlitekṣaṇā |
sumitrayānvāsyamānā sītayā lakṣmaṇena ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
śrutvā puṣyeṇa putrasya yauvarājyābhiṣecanam |
prāṇāyāmena puruṣaṁ dhyāyamānā janārdanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
tathā saniyamām eva so ’bhigamyābhivādya ca |
uvāca vacanaṁ rāmo harṣayaṁs tām idaṁ tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
amba pitrā niyukto ’smi prajāpālanakarmaṇi |
bhavitā śvo ’bhiṣeko me yathā me śāsanaṁ pituḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
sītayāpy upavastavyā rajanīyaṁ mayā saha |
evam r̥tvigupādhyāyaiḥ saha mām uktavān pitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
yāni yāny atra yogyāni śvobhāviny abhiṣecane |
tāni me maṅgalāny adya vaidehyāś caiva kāraya ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
etac chrutvā tu kausalyā cirakālābhikāṅkṣitam |
harṣabāṣpakalaṁ vākyam idaṁ rāmam abhāṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
vatsa rāma ciraṁ jīva hatās te paripanthinaḥ |
jñātīn me tvaṁ śriyā yuktaḥ sumitrāyāś ca nandaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
kalyāṇe bata nakṣatre mayi jāto ’si putraka |
yena tvayā daśaratho guṇair ārādhitaḥ pitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
amoghaṁ bata me kṣāntaṁ puruṣe puṣkarekṣaṇe |
yeyam ikṣvākurājyaśrīḥ putra tvāṁ saṁśrayiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
ity evam ukto mātredaṁ rāmo bhāratam abravīt |
prāñjaliṁ prahvam āsīnam abhivīkṣya smayann iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
lakṣmaṇemāṁ mayā sārdhaṁ praśādhi tvaṁ vasuṁdharām |
dvitīyaṁ me ’ntarātmānaṁ tvām iyaṁ śrīr upasthitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
saumitre bhuṅkṣva bhogāṁs tvam iṣṭān rājyaphalāni ca |
jīvitaṁ ca hi rājyaṁ ca tvadartham abhikāmaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
ity uktvā lakṣmaṇaṁ rāmo mātarāv abhivādya ca |
abhyanujñāpya sītāṁ ca jagāma svaṁ niveśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
