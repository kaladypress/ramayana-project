=== Sarga 47 ===

---
1.
sa taṁ vr̥kṣaṁ samāsādya saṁdhyām anvāsya paścimām |
rāmo ramayatāṁ śreṣṭha iti hovāca lakṣmaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
adyeyaṁ prathamā rātrir yātā janapadād bahiḥ |
yā sumantreṇa rahitā tāṁ notkaṇṭhitum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
jāgartavyam atandribhyām adya prabhr̥ti rātriṣu |
yogakṣemo hi sītāyā vartate lakṣmaṇāvayoḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
rātriṁ kathaṁ cid evemāṁ saumitre vartayāmahe |
upāvartāmahe bhūmāv āstīrya svayam ārjitaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
sa tu saṁviśya medinyāṁ mahārhaśayanocitaḥ |
imāḥ saumitraye rāmo vyājahāra kathāḥ śubhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
dhruvam adya mahārājo duḥkhaṁ svapiti lakṣmaṇa |
kr̥takāmā tu kaikeyī tuṣṭā bhavitum arhati ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sā hi devī mahārājaṁ kaikeyī rājyakāraṇāt |
api na cyāvayet prāṇān dr̥ṣṭvā bharatam āgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
anāthaś caiva vr̥ddhaś ca mayā caiva vinākr̥taḥ |
kiṁ kariṣyati kāmātmā kaikeyyā vaśam āgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
idaṁ vyasanam ālokya rājñaś ca mativibhramam |
kāma evārdhadharmābhyāṁ garīyān iti me matiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
ko hy avidvān api pumān pramadāyāḥ kr̥te tyajet |
chandānuvartinaṁ putraṁ tāto mām iva lakṣmaṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sukhī bata sabhāryaś ca bharataḥ kekayīsutaḥ |
muditān kosalān eko yo bhokṣyaty adhirājavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sa hi sarvasya rājyasya mukham ekaṁ bhaviṣyati |
tāte ca vayasātīte mayi cāraṇyam āśrite ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
arthadharmau parityajya yaḥ kāmam anuvartate |
evam āpadyate kṣipraṁ rājā daśaratho yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
manye daśarathāntāya mama pravrājanāya ca |
kaikeyī saumya saṁprāptā rājyāya bharatasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
apīdānīṁ na kaikeyī saubhāgyamadamohitā |
kausalyāṁ ca sumitrāṁ ca saṁprabādheta matkr̥te ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
mā sma matkāraṇād devī sumitrā duḥkham āvaset |
ayodhyām ita eva tvaṁ kāle praviśa lakṣmaṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
aham eko gamiṣyāmi sītayā saha daṇḍakān |
anāthāyā hi nāthas tvaṁ kausalyāyā bhaviṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
kṣudrakarmā hi kaikeyī dveṣād anyāyyam ācaret |
paridadyā hi dharmajñe bharate mama mātaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
nūnaṁ jātyantare kasmiṁ striyaḥ putrair viyojitāḥ |
jananyā mama saumitre tad apy etad upasthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
mayā hi cirapuṣṭena duḥkhasaṁvardhitena ca |
viprāyujyata kausalyā phalakāle dhig astu mām ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
mā sma sīmantinī kā cij janayet putram īdr̥śam |
saumitre yo ’ham ambāyā dadmi śokam anantakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
manye prītiviśiṣṭā sā matto lakṣmaṇasārikā |
yasyās tac chrūyate vākyaṁ śuka pādam arer daśa ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
śocantyāś cālpabhāgyāyā na kiṁ cid upakurvatā |
purtreṇa kim aputrāyā mayā kāryam ariṁdama ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
alpabhāgyā hi me mātā kausalyā rahitā mayā |
śete paramaduḥkhārtā patitā śokasāgare ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
eko hy aham ayodhyāṁ ca pr̥thivīṁ cāpi lakṣmaṇa |
tareyam iṣubhiḥ kruddho nanu vīryam akāraṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
adharmabhaya bhītaś ca paralokasya cānagha |
tena lakṣmaṇa nādyāham ātmānam abhiṣecaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
etad anyac ca karuṇaṁ vilapya vijane bahu |
aśrupūrṇamukho rāmo niśi tūṣṇīm upāviśat ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
vilapyoparataṁ rāmaṁ gatārciṣam ivānalam |
samudram iva nirvegam āśvāsayata lakṣmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
dhruvam adya purī rāma ayodhyā yudhināṁ vara |
niṣprabhā tvayi niṣkrānte gatacandreva śarvarī ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
naitad aupayikaṁ rāma yad idaṁ paritapyase |
viṣādayasi sītāṁ ca māṁ caiva puruṣarṣabha ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
na ca sītā tvayā hīnā na cāham api rāghava |
muhūrtam api jīvāvo jalān matsyāv ivoddhr̥tau ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
na hi tātaṁ na śatrughnaṁ na sumitrāṁ paraṁtapa |
draṣṭum iccheyam adyāhaṁ svargaṁ vāpi tvayā vinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
sa lakṣmaṇasyottama puṣkalaṁ vaco; niśamya caivaṁ vanavāsam ādarāt |
samāḥ samastā vidadhe paraṁtapaḥ; prapadya dharmaṁ sucirāya rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
