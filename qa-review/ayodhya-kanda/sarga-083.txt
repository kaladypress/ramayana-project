=== Sarga 83 ===

---
1.
vyuṣya rātriṁ tu tatraiva gaṅgākūle sa rāghavaḥ |
bharataḥ kālyam utthāya śatrughnam idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
śatrughottiṣṭha kiṁ śeṣe niṣādādhipatiṁ guham |
śīghram ānaya bhadraṁ te tārayiṣyati vāhinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
jāgarmi nāhaṁ svapimi tathaivāryaṁ vicintayan |
ity evam abravīd bhrātrā śatrughno ’pi pracoditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
iti saṁvadator evam anyonyaṁ narasiṁhayoḥ |
āgamya prāñjaliḥ kāle guho bharatam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
kaccit sukhaṁ nadītīre ’vātsīḥ kākutstha śarvarīm |
kaccic ca saha sainyasya tava sarvam anāmayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
guhasya tat tu vacanaṁ śrutvā snehād udīritam |
rāmasyānuvaśo vākyaṁ bharato ’pīdam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sukhā naḥ śarvarī rājan pūjitāś cāpi te vayam |
gaṅgāṁ tu naubhir bahvībhir dāśāḥ saṁtārayantu naḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tato guhaḥ saṁtvaritaḥ śrutvā bharataśāsanam |
pratipraviśya nagaraṁ taṁ jñātijanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
uttiṣṭhata prabudhyadhvaṁ bhadram astu hi vaḥ sadā |
nāvaḥ samanukarṣadhvaṁ tārayiṣyāma vāhinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
te tathoktāḥ samutthāya tvaritā rājaśāsanāt |
pañca nāvāṁ śatāny eva samāninyuḥ samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
anyāḥ svastikavijñeyā mahāghaṇḍā dharā varāḥ |
śobhamānāḥ patākinyo yuktavātāḥ susaṁhatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tataḥ svastikavijñeyāṁ pāṇḍukambalasaṁvr̥tām |
sanandighoṣāṁ kalyāṇīṁ guho nāvam upāharat ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tām āruroha bharataḥ śatrughnaś ca mahābalaḥ |
kausalyā ca sumitrā ca yāś cānyā rājayoṣitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
purohitaś ca tat pūrvaṁ gurave brāhmaṇāś ca ye |
anantaraṁ rājadārās tathaiva śakaṭāpaṇāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
āvāsam ādīpayatāṁ tīrthaṁ cāpy avagāhatām |
bhāṇḍāni cādadānānāṁ ghoṣas tridivam aspr̥śat ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
patākinyas tu tā nāvaḥ svayaṁ dāśair adhiṣṭhitāḥ |
vahantyo janam ārūḍhaṁ tadā saṁpetur āśugāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
nārīṇām abhipūrṇās tu kāś cit kāś cit tu vājinām |
kaś cit tatra vahanti sma yānayugyaṁ mahādhanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tāḥ sma gatvā paraṁ tīram avaropya ca taṁ janam |
nivr̥ttāḥ kāṇḍacitrāṇi kriyante dāśabandhubhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
savaijayantās tu gajā gajārohaiḥ pracoditāḥ |
tarantaḥ sma prakāśante sadhvajā iva parvatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
nāvaś cāruruhus tv anye plavais terus tathāpare |
anye kumbhaghaṭais terur anye teruś ca bāhubhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
sā puṇyā dhvajinī gaṅgāṁ dāśaiḥ saṁtāritā svayam |
maitre muhūrte prayayau prayāgavanam uttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
āśvāsayitvā ca camūṁ mahātmā; niveśayitvā ca yathopajoṣam |
draṣṭuṁ bharadvājam r̥ṣipravaryam; r̥tvig vr̥taḥ san bharataḥ pratasthe ||

=== meaning ===

---
