=== Sarga 7 ===

---
1.
jñātidāsī yato jātā kaikeyyās tu sahoṣitā |
prāsādaṁ candrasaṁkāśam āruroha yadr̥cchayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
siktarājapathāṁ kr̥tsnāṁ prakīrṇakamalotpalām |
ayodhyāṁ mantharā tasmāt prāsādād anvavaikṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
patākābhir varārhābhir dhvajaiś ca samalaṁkr̥tām |
siktāṁ candanatoyaiś ca śiraḥsnātajanair vr̥tām ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
avidūre sthitāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā dhātrīṁ papraccha mantharā |
uttamenābhisaṁyuktā harṣeṇārthaparā satī ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
rāmamātā dhanaṁ kiṁ nu janebhyaḥ saṁprayacchati |
atimātraṁ praharṣo ’yaṁ kiṁ janasya ca śaṁsa me |
kārayiṣyati kiṁ vāpi saṁprahr̥ṣṭo mahīpatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
vidīryamāṇā harṣeṇa dhātrī paramayā mudā |
ācacakṣe ’tha kubjāyai bhūyasīṁ rāghave śriyam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
śvaḥ puṣyeṇa jitakrodhaṁ yauvarājyena rāghavam |
rājā daśaratho rāmam abhiṣecayitānagham ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
dhātryās tu vacanaṁ śrutvā kubjā kṣipram amarṣitā |
kailāsa śikharākārāt prāsādād avarohata ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sā dahyamānā kopena mantharā pāpadarśinī |
śayānām etya kaikeyīm idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
uttiṣṭha mūḍhe kiṁ śeṣe bhayaṁ tvām abhivartate |
upaplutamahaughena kim ātmānaṁ na budhyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
aniṣṭe subhagākāre saubhāgyena vikatthase |
calaṁ hi tava saubhāgyaṁ nadyaḥ srota ivoṣṇage ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
evam uktā tu kaikeyī ruṣṭayā paruṣaṁ vacaḥ |
kubjayā pāpadarśinyā viṣādam agamat param ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
kaikeyī tv abravīt kubjāṁ kaccit kṣemaṁ na manthare |
viṣaṇṇavadanāṁ hi tvāṁ lakṣaye bhr̥śaduḥkhitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
mantharā tu vacaḥ śrutvā kaikeyyā madhurākṣaram |
uvāca krodhasaṁyuktā vākyaṁ vākyaviśāradā ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sā viṣaṇṇatarā bhūtvā kubjā tasyā hitaiṣiṇī |
viṣādayantī provāca bhedayantī ca rāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
akṣemaṁ sumahad devi pravr̥ttaṁ tvadvināśanam |
rāmaṁ daśaratho rājā yauvarājye ’bhiṣekṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
sāsmy agādhe bhaye magnā duḥkhaśokasamanvitā |
dahyamānānaleneva tvaddhitārtham ihāgatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tava duḥkhena kaikeyi mama duḥkhaṁ mahad bhavet |
tvadvr̥ddhau mama vr̥ddhiś ca bhaved atra na saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
narādhipakule jātā mahiṣī tvaṁ mahīpateḥ |
ugratvaṁ rājadharmāṇāṁ kathaṁ devi na budhyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
dharmavādī śaṭho bhartā ślakṣṇavādī ca dāruṇaḥ |
śuddhabhāve na jānīṣe tenaivam atisaṁdhitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
upasthitaṁ prayuñjānas tvayi sāntvam anarthakam |
arthenaivādya te bhartā kausalyāṁ yojayiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
apavāhya sa duṣṭātmā bharataṁ tava bandhuṣu |
kālyaṁ sthāpayitā rāmaṁ rājye nihatakaṇṭake ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
śatruḥ patipravādena mātreva hitakāmyayā |
āśīviṣa ivāṅkena bāle paridhr̥tas tvayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
yathā hi kuryāt sarpo vā śatrur vā pratyupekṣitaḥ |
rājñā daśarathenādya saputrā tvaṁ tathā kr̥tā ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
pāpenānr̥tasāntvena bāle nityaṁ sukhocite |
rāmaṁ sthāpayatā rājye sānubandhā hatā hy asi ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
sā prāptakālaṁ kaikeyi kṣipraṁ kuru hitaṁ tava |
trāyasva putram ātmānaṁ māṁ ca vismayadarśane ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
mantharāyā vacaḥ śrutvā śayanāt sā śubhānanā |
ekam ābharaṇaṁ tasyai kubjāyai pradadau śubham ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
dattvā tv ābharaṇaṁ tasyai kubjāyai pramadottamā |
kaikeyī mantharāṁ hr̥ṣṭā punar evābravīd idam ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
idaṁ tu manthare mahyam ākhyāsi paramaṁ priyam |
etan me priyam ākhyātuḥ kiṁ vā bhūyaḥ karomi te ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
rāme vā bharate vāhaṁ viśeṣaṁ nopalakṣaye |
tasmāt tuṣṭāsmi yad rājā rāmaṁ rājye ’bhiṣekṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
na me paraṁ kiṁ cid itas tvayā punaḥ; priyaṁ priyārhe suvacaṁ vaco varam |
tathā hy avocas tvam ataḥ priyottaraṁ; varaṁ paraṁ te pradadāmi taṁ vr̥ṇu ||

=== meaning ===

---
