=== Sarga 3 ===

---
1.
teṣām ajñalipadmāni pragr̥hītāni sarvaśaḥ |
pratigr̥hyābravīd rājā tebhyaḥ priyahitaṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
aho ’smi paramaprītaḥ prabhāvaś cātulo mama |
yan me jyeṣṭhaṁ priyaṁ putraṁ yauvarājyastham icchatha ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
iti pratyarcya tān rājā brāhmaṇān idam abravīt |
vasiṣṭhaṁ vāmadevaṁ ca teṣām evopaśr̥ṇvatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
caitraḥ śrīmān ayaṁ māsaḥ puṇyaḥ puṣpitakānanaḥ |
yauvarājyāya rāmasya sarvam evopakalpyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
kr̥tam ity eva cābrūtām abhigamya jagatpatim |
yathoktavacanaṁ prītau harṣayuktau dvijarṣabhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tataḥ sumantraṁ dyutimān rājā vacanam abravīt |
rāmaḥ kr̥tātmā bhavatā śīghram ānīyatām iti ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sa tatheti pratijñāya sumantro rājaśāsanāt |
rāmaṁ tatrānayāṁ cakre rathena rathināṁ varam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
atha tatra samāsīnās tadā daśarathaṁ nr̥pam |
prācyodīcyāḥ pratīcyāś ca dākṣiṇātyāś ca bhūmipāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
mlecchāś cāryāś ca ye cānye vanaśailāntavāsinaḥ |
upāsāṁ cakrire sarve taṁ devā iva vāsavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
teṣāṁ madhye sa rājarṣir marutām iva vāsavaḥ |
prāsādastho rathagataṁ dadarśāyāntam ātmajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
gandharvarājapratimaṁ loke vikhyātapauruṣam |
dīrghabāhuṁ mahāsattvaṁ mattamātaṅgagāminam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
candrakāntānanaṁ rāmam atīva priyadarśanam |
rūpaudāryaguṇaiḥ puṁsāṁ dr̥ṣṭicittāpahāriṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
gharmābhitaptāḥ parjanyaṁ hlādayantam iva prajāḥ |
na tatarpa samāyāntaṁ paśyamāno narādhipaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
avatārya sumantras taṁ rāghavaṁ syandanottamāt |
pituḥ samīpaṁ gacchantaṁ prāñjaliḥ pr̥ṣṭhato ’nvagāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa taṁ kailāsaśr̥ṅgābhaṁ prāsādaṁ narapuṁgavaḥ |
āruroha nr̥paṁ draṣṭuṁ saha sūtena rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sa prāñjalir abhipretya praṇataḥ pitur antike |
nāma svaṁ śrāvayan rāmo vavande caraṇau pituḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā praṇataṁ pārśve kr̥tāñjalipuṭaṁ nr̥paḥ |
gr̥hyāñjalau samākr̥ṣya sasvaje priyam ātmajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tasmai cābhyudyataṁ śrīmān maṇikāñcanabhūṣitam |
dideśa rājā ruciraṁ rāmāya paramāsanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tad āsanavaraṁ prāpya vyadīpayata rāghavaḥ |
svayeva prabhayā merum udaye vimalo raviḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tena vibhrājitā tatra sā sabhābhivyarocata |
vimalagrahanakṣatrā śāradī dyaur ivendunā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
taṁ paśyamāno nr̥patis tutoṣa priyam ātmajam |
alaṁkr̥tam ivātmānam ādarśatalasaṁsthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
sa taṁ sasmitam ābhāṣya putraṁ putravatāṁ varaḥ |
uvācedaṁ vaco rājā devendram iva kaśyapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
jyeṣṭhāyām asi me patnyāṁ sadr̥śyāṁ sadr̥śaḥ sutaḥ |
utpannas tvaṁ guṇaśreṣṭho mama rāmātmajaḥ priyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tvayā yataḥ prajāś cemāḥ svaguṇair anurañjitāḥ |
tasmāt tvaṁ puṣyayogena yauvarājyam avāpnuhi ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
kāmatas tvaṁ prakr̥tyaiva vinīto guṇavān asi |
guṇavaty api tu snehāt putra vakṣyāmi te hitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
bhūyo vinayam āsthāya bhava nityaṁ jitendriyaḥ |
kāmakrodhasamutthāni tyajethā vyasanāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
parokṣayā vartamāno vr̥ttyā pratyakṣayā tathā |
amātyaprabhr̥tīḥ sarvāḥ prakr̥tīś cānurañjaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tuṣṭānuraktaprakr̥tir yaḥ pālayati medinīm |
tasya nandanti mitrāṇi labdhvāmr̥tam ivāmarāḥ |
tasmāt putra tvam ātmānaṁ niyamyaiva samācara ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
tac chrutvā suhr̥das tasya rāmasya priyakāriṇaḥ |
tvaritāḥ śīghram abhyetya kausalyāyai nyavedayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
sā hiraṇyaṁ ca gāś caiva ratnāni vividhāni ca |
vyādideśa priyākhyebhyaḥ kausalyā pramadottamā ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
athābhivādya rājānaṁ ratham āruhya rāghavaḥ |
yayau svaṁ dyutimad veśma janaughaiḥ pratipūjitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
te cāpi paurā nr̥pater vacas tac; chrutvā tadā lābham iveṣṭam āpya |
narendram āmantya gr̥hāṇi gatvā; devān samānarcur atīva hr̥ṣṭāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
