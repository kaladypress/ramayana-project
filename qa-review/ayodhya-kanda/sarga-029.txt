=== Sarga 29 ===

---
1.
tataḥ śāsanam ājñāya bhrātuḥ śubhataraṁ priyam |
gatvā sa praviveśāśu suyajñasya niveśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
taṁ vipram agnyagārasthaṁ vanditvā lakṣmaṇo ’bravīt |
sakhe ’bhyāgaccha paśya tvaṁ veśma duṣkarakāriṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tataḥ saṁdhyām upāsyāśu gatvā saumitriṇā saha |
juṣṭaṁ tat prāviśal lakṣmyā ramyaṁ rāmaniveśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tam āgataṁ vedavidaṁ prāñjaliḥ sītayā saha |
suyajñam abhicakrāma rāghavo ’gnim ivārcitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
jātarūpamayair mukhyair aṅgadaiḥ kuṇḍalaiḥ śubhaiḥ |
sahema sūtrair maṇibhiḥ keyūrair valayair api ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
anyaiś ca ratnair bahubhiḥ kākutsthaḥ pratyapūjayat |
suyajñaṁ sa tadovāca rāmaḥ sītāpracoditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
hāraṁ ca hemasūtraṁ ca bhāryāyai saumya hāraya |
raśanāṁ cādhunā sītā dātum icchati te sakhe ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
paryaṅkam agryāstaraṇaṁ nānāratnavibhūṣitam |
tam apīcchati vaidehī pratiṣṭhāpayituṁ tvayi ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
nāgaḥ śatruṁ jayo nāma mātulo yaṁ dadau mama |
taṁ te gajasahasreṇa dadāmi dvijapuṁgava ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
ity uktaḥ sa hi rāmeṇa suyajñaḥ pratigr̥hya tat |
rāmalakṣmaṇasītānāṁ prayuyojāśiṣaḥ śivāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
atha bhrātaram avyagraṁ priyaṁ rāmaḥ priyaṁvadaḥ |
saumitriṁ tam uvācedaṁ brahmeva tridaśeśvaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
agastyaṁ kauśikaṁ caiva tāv ubhau brāhmaṇottamau |
arcayāhūya saumitre ratnaiḥ sasyam ivāmbubhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
kausalyāṁ ca ya āśīrbhir bhaktaḥ paryupatiṣṭhati |
ācāryas taittirīyāṇām abhirūpaś ca vedavit ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tasya yānaṁ ca dāsīś ca saumitre saṁpradāpaya |
kauśeyāni ca vastrāṇi yāvat tuṣyati sa dvijaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sūtaś citrarathaś cāryaḥ sacivaḥ suciroṣitaḥ |
toṣayainaṁ mahārhaiś ca ratnair vastrair dhanais tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
śālivāhasahasraṁ ca dve śate bhadrakāṁs tathā |
vyañjanārthaṁ ca saumitre gosahasram upākuru ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tataḥ sa puruṣavyāghras tad dhanaṁ lakṣmaṇaḥ svayam |
yathoktaṁ brāhmaṇendrāṇām adadād dhanado yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
athābravīd bāṣpakalāṁs tiṣṭhataś copajīvinaḥ |
saṁpradāya bahu dravyam ekaikasyopajīvinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
lakṣmaṇasya ca yad veśma gr̥haṁ ca yad idaṁ mama |
aśūnyaṁ kāryam ekaikaṁ yāvadāgamanaṁ mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
ity uktvā duḥkhitaṁ sarvaṁ janaṁ tam upajīvinam |
uvācedaṁ dhanadhyakṣaṁ dhanam ānīyatām iti |
tato ’sya dhanam ājahruḥ sarvam evopajīvinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tataḥ sa puruṣavyāghras tad dhanaṁ sahalakṣmaṇaḥ |
dvijebhyo bālavr̥ddhebhyaḥ kr̥paṇebhyo ’bhyadāpayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tatrāsīt piṅgalo gārgyas trijaṭo nāma vai dvijaḥ |
ā pañcamāyāḥ kakṣyāyā nainaṁ kaś cid avārayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
sa rājaputram āsādya trijaṭo vākyam abravīt |
nirdhano bahuputro ’smi rājaputra mahāyaśaḥ |
uñchavr̥ttir vane nityaṁ pratyavekṣasva mām iti ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tam uvāca tato rāmaḥ parihāsasamanvitam |
gavāṁ sahasram apy ekaṁ na tu viśrāṇitaṁ mayā |
parikṣipasi daṇḍena yāvat tāvad avāpsyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
sa śāṭīṁ tvaritaḥ kaṭyāṁ saṁbhrāntaḥ pariveṣṭya tām |
āvidhya daṇḍaṁ cikṣepa sarvaprāṇena vegitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
uvāca ca tato rāmas taṁ gārgyam abhisāntvayan |
manyur na khalu kartavyaḥ parihāso hy ayaṁ mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tataḥ sabhāryas trijaṭo mahāmunir; gavām anīkaṁ pratigr̥hya moditaḥ |
yaśobalaprītisukhopabr̥ṁhiṇīs; tad āśiṣaḥ pratyavadan mahātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
