=== Sarga 45 ===

---
1.
taṁ jāgratam adambhena bhrātur arthāya lakṣmaṇam |
guhaḥ saṁtāpasaṁtapto rāghavaṁ vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
iyaṁ tāta sukhā śayyā tvadartham upakalpitā |
pratyāśvasihi sādhv asyāṁ rājaputra yathāsukham ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
ucito ’yaṁ janaḥ sarvaḥ kleśānāṁ tvaṁ sukhocitaḥ |
guptyarthaṁ jāgariṣyāmaḥ kākutsthasya vayaṁ niśām ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
na hi rāmāt priyataro mamāsti bhuvi kaś cana |
bravīmy etad ahaṁ satyaṁ satyenaiva ca te śape ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
asya prasādād āśaṁse loke ’smin sumahad yaśaḥ |
dharmāvāptiṁ ca vipulām arthāvāptiṁ ca kevalām ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
so ’haṁ priyasakhaṁ rāmaṁ śayānaṁ saha sītayā |
rakṣiṣyāmi dhanuṣpāṇiḥ sarvato jñātibhiḥ saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
na hi me ’viditaṁ kiṁ cid vane ’smiṁś carataḥ sadā |
caturaṅgaṁ hy api balaṁ sumahat prasahemahi ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
lakṣmaṇas taṁ tadovāca rakṣyamāṇās tvayānagha |
nātra bhītā vayaṁ sarve dharmam evānupaśyatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
kathaṁ dāśarathau bhūmau śayāne saha sītayā |
śakyā nidrā mayā labdhuṁ jīvitaṁ vā sukhāni vā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
yo na devāsuraiḥ sarvaiḥ śakyaḥ prasahituṁ yudhi |
taṁ paśya sukhasaṁviṣṭaṁ tr̥ṇeṣu saha sītayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
yo mantra tapasā labdho vividhaiś ca pariśramaiḥ |
eko daśarathasyaiṣa putraḥ sadr̥śalakṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
asmin pravrajito rājā na ciraṁ vartayiṣyati |
vidhavā medinī nūnaṁ kṣipram eva bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
vinadya sumahānādaṁ śrameṇoparatāḥ striyaḥ |
nirghoṣoparataṁ tāta manye rājaniveśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
kausalyā caiva rājā ca tathaiva jananī mama |
nāśaṁse yadi jīvanti sarve te śarvarīm imām ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
jīved api hi me mātā śatrughnasyānvavekṣayā |
tad duḥkhaṁ yat tu kausalyā vīrasūr vinaśiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
anuraktajanākīrṇā sukhālokapriyāvahā |
rājavyasanasaṁsr̥ṣṭā sā purī vinaśiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
atikrāntam atikrāntam anavāpya manoratham |
rājye rāmam anikṣipya pitā me vinaśiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
siddhārthāḥ pitaraṁ vr̥ttaṁ tasmin kāle hy upasthite |
pretakāryeṣu sarveṣu saṁskariṣyanti bhūmipam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
ramyacatvarasaṁsthānāṁ suvibhaktamahāpathām |
harmyaprāsādasaṁpannāṁ gaṇikāvaraśobhitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
rathāśvagajasaṁbādhāṁ tūryanādavināditām |
sarvakalyāṇasaṁpūrṇāṁ hr̥ṣṭapuṣṭajanākulām ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
ārāmodyānasaṁpannāṁ samājotsavaśālinīm |
sukhitā vicariṣyanti rājadhānīṁ pitur mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
api satyapratijñena sārdhaṁ kuśalinā vayam |
nivr̥tte vanavāse ’sminn ayodhyāṁ praviśemahi ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
paridevayamānasya duḥkhārtasya mahātmanaḥ |
tiṣṭhato rājaputrasya śarvarī sātyavartata ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tathā hi satyaṁ bruvati prajāhite; narendraputre gurusauhr̥dād guhaḥ |
mumoca bāṣpaṁ vyasanābhipīḍito; jvarāturo nāga iva vyathāturaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
