=== Sarga 20 ===

---
1.
iti bruvati rāme tu lakṣmaṇo ’dhaḥśirā muhuḥ |
śrutvā madhyaṁ jagāmeva manasā duḥkhaharṣayoḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tadā tu baddhvā bhrukuṭīṁ bhruvor madhye nararṣabha |
niśaśvāsa mahāsarpo bilastha iva roṣitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tasya duṣprativīkṣyaṁ tad bhrukuṭīsahitaṁ tadā |
babhau kruddhasya siṁhasya mukhasya sadr̥śaṁ mukham ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
agrahas taṁ vidhunvaṁs tu hastī hastam ivātmanaḥ |
tiryag ūrdhvaṁ śarīre ca pātayitvā śirodharām ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
agrākṣṇā vīkṣamāṇas tu tiryag bhrātaram abravīt |
asthāne saṁbhramo yasya jāto vai sumahān ayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
dharmadoṣaprasaṅgena lokasyānatiśaṅkayā |
kathaṁ hy etad asaṁbhrāntas tvadvidho vaktum arhati ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
yathā daivam aśauṇḍīraṁ śauṇḍīraḥ kṣatriyarṣabhaḥ |
kiṁ nāma kr̥paṇaṁ daivam aśaktam abhiśaṁsasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
pāpayos te kathaṁ nāma tayoḥ śaṅkā na vidyate |
santi dharmopadhāḥ ślakṣṇā dharmātman kiṁ na budhyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
lokavidviṣṭam ārabdhaṁ tvadanyasyābhiṣecanam |
yeneyam āgatā dvaidhaṁ tava buddhir mahīpate |
sa hi dharmo mama dveṣyaḥ prasaṅgād yasya muhyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
yady api pratipattis te daivī cāpi tayor matam |
tathāpy upekṣaṇīyaṁ te na me tad api rocate ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
viklavo vīryahīno yaḥ sa daivam anuvartate |
vīrāḥ saṁbhāvitātmāno na daivaṁ paryupāsate ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
daivaṁ puruṣakāreṇa yaḥ samarthaḥ prabādhitum |
na daivena vipannārthaḥ puruṣaḥ so ’vasīdati ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
drakṣyanti tv adya daivasya pauruṣaṁ puruṣasya ca |
daivamānuṣayor adya vyaktā vyaktir bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
adya matpauruṣahataṁ daivaṁ drakṣyanti vai janāḥ |
yad daivād āhataṁ te ’dya dr̥ṣṭaṁ rājyābhiṣecanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
atyaṅkuśam ivoddāmaṁ gajaṁ madabaloddhatam |
pradhāvitam ahaṁ daivaṁ pauruṣeṇa nivartaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
lokapālāḥ samastās te nādya rāmābhiṣecanam |
na ca kr̥tsnās trayo lokā vihanyuḥ kiṁ punaḥ pitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
yair vivāsas tavāraṇye mitho rājan samarthitaḥ |
araṇye te vivatsyanti caturdaśa samās tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
ahaṁ tadāśāṁ chetsyāmi pitus tasyāś ca yā tava |
abhiṣekavighātena putrarājyāya vartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
madbalena viruddhāya na syād daivabalaṁ tathā |
prabhaviṣyati duḥkhāya yathograṁ pauruṣaṁ mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
ūrdhvaṁ varṣasahasrānte prajāpālyam anantaram |
āryaputrāḥ kariṣyanti vanavāsaṁ gate tvayi ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
pūrvarājarṣivr̥ttyā hi vanavāso vidhīyate |
prajā nikṣipya putreṣu putravat paripālane ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
sa ced rājany anekāgre rājyavibhramaśaṅkayā |
naivam icchasi dharmātman rājyaṁ rāma tvam ātmani ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
pratijāne ca te vīra mā bhūvaṁ vīralokabhāk |
rājyaṁ ca tava rakṣeyam ahaṁ veleva sāgaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
maṅgalair abhiṣiñcasva tatra tvaṁ vyāpr̥to bhava |
aham eko mahīpālān alaṁ vārayituṁ balāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
na śobhārthāv imau bāhū na dhanur bhūṣaṇāya me |
nāsirābandhanārthāya na śarāḥ stambhahetavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
amitradamanārthaṁ me sarvam etac catuṣṭayam |
na cāhaṁ kāmaye ’tyarthaṁ yaḥ syāc chatrur mato mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
asinā tīkṣṇadhāreṇa vidyuccalitavarcasā |
pragr̥hītena vai śatruṁ vajriṇaṁ vā na kalpaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
khaḍganiṣpeṣaniṣpiṣṭair gahanā duścarā ca me |
hastyaśvanarahastoruśirobhir bhavitā mahī ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
khaḍgadhārāhatā me ’dya dīpyamānā ivādrayaḥ |
patiṣyanti dvipā bhūmau meghā iva savidyutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
baddhagodhāṅgulitrāṇe pragr̥hītaśarāsane |
kathaṁ puruṣamānī syāt puruṣāṇāṁ mayi sthite ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
bahubhiś caikam atyasyann ekena ca bahūñ janān |
viniyokṣyāmy ahaṁ bāṇān nr̥vājigajamarmasu ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
adya me ’straprabhāvasya prabhāvaḥ prabhaviṣyati |
rājñaś cāprabhutāṁ kartuṁ prabhutvaṁ ca tava prabho ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
adya candanasārasya keyūrāmokṣaṇasya ca |
vasūnāṁ ca vimokṣasya suhr̥dāṁ pālanasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
anurūpāv imau bāhū rāma karma kariṣyataḥ |
abhiṣecanavighnasya kartr̥̄ṇāṁ te nivāraṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
bravīhi ko ’dyaiva mayā viyujyatāṁ; tavāsuhr̥t prāṇayaśaḥ suhr̥jjanaiḥ |
yathā taveyaṁ vasudhā vaśe bhavet; tathaiva māṁ śādhi tavāsmi kiṁkaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
vimr̥jya bāṣpaṁ parisāntvya cāsakr̥t; sa lakṣmaṇaṁ rāghavavaṁśavardhanaḥ |
uvāca pitrye vacane vyavasthitaṁ; nibodha mām eṣa hi saumya satpathaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
