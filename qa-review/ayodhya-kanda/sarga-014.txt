=== Sarga 14 ===

---
1.
sa tad antaḥpuradvāraṁ samatītya janākulam |
praviviktāṁ tataḥ kakṣyām āsasāda purāṇavit ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
prāsakārmukabibhradbhir yuvabhir mr̥ṣṭakuṇḍalaiḥ |
apramādibhir ekāgraiḥ svanuraktair adhiṣṭhitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tatra kāṣāyiṇo vr̥ddhān vetrapāṇīn svalaṁkr̥tān |
dadarśa viṣṭhitān dvāri stryadhyakṣān susamāhitān ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
te samīkṣya samāyāntaṁ rāmapriyacikīrṣavaḥ |
sahabhāryāya rāmāya kṣipram evācacakṣire ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
prativeditam ājñāya sūtam abhyantaraṁ pituḥ |
tatraivānāyayām āsa rāghavaḥ priyakāmyayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
taṁ vaiśravaṇasaṁkāśam upaviṣṭaṁ svalaṁkr̥tam |
dādarśa sūtaḥ paryaṅke sauvaṇo sottaracchade ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
varāharudhirābheṇa śucinā ca sugandhinā |
anuliptaṁ parārdhyena candanena paraṁtapam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sthitayā pārśvataś cāpi vālavyajanahastayā |
upetaṁ sītayā bhūyaś citrayā śaśinaṁ yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
taṁ tapantam ivādityam upapannaṁ svatejasā |
vavande varadaṁ bandī niyamajño vinītavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
prāñjalis tu sukhaṁ pr̥ṣṭvā vihāraśayanāsane |
rājaputram uvācedaṁ sumantro rājasatkr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
kausalyā suprabhā deva pitā tvaṁ draṣṭum icchati |
mahiṣyā saha kaikeyyā gamyatāṁ tatra māciram ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
evam uktas tu saṁhr̥ṣṭo narasiṁho mahādyutiḥ |
tataḥ saṁmānayām āsa sītām idam uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
devi devaś ca devī ca samāgamya madantare |
mantreyete dhruvaṁ kiṁ cid abhiṣecanasaṁhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
lakṣayitvā hy abhiprāyaṁ priyakāmā sudakṣiṇā |
saṁcodayati rājānaṁ madarthaṁ madirekṣaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
yādr̥śī pariṣat tatra tādr̥śo dūta āgataḥ |
dhruvam adyaiva māṁ rājā yauvarājye ’bhiṣekṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
hanta śīghram ito gatvā drakṣyāmi ca mahīpatiḥ |
saha tvaṁ parivāreṇa sukham āssva ramasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
patisaṁmānitā sītā bhartāram asitekṣaṇā |
ādvāram anuvavrāja maṅgalāny abhidadhyuṣī ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
sa sarvān arthino dr̥ṣṭvā sametya pratinandya ca |
tataḥ pāvakasaṁkāśam āruroha rathottamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
muṣṇantam iva cakṣūṁṣi prabhayā hemavarcasam |
kareṇuśiśukalpaiś ca yuktaṁ paramavājibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
hariyuktaṁ sahasrākṣo ratham indra ivāśugam |
prayayau tūrṇam āsthāya rāghavo jvalitaḥ śriyā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
sa parjanya ivākāśe svanavān abhinādayan |
niketān niryayau śrīmān mahābhrād iva candramāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
chatracāmarapāṇis tu lakṣmaṇo rāghavānujaḥ |
jugopa bhrātaraṁ bhrātā ratham āsthāya pr̥ṣṭhataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tato halahalāśabdas tumulaḥ samajāyata |
tasya niṣkramamāṇasya janaughasya samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
sa rāghavas tatra kathāpralāpaṁ; śuśrāva lokasya samāgatasya |
ātmādhikārā vividhāś ca vācaḥ; prahr̥ṣṭarūpasya pure janasya ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
eṣa śriyaṁ gacchati rāghavo ’dya; rājaprasādād vipulāṁ gamiṣyan |
ete vayaṁ sarvasamr̥ddhakāmā; yeṣām ayaṁ no bhavitā praśāstā |
lābho janasyāsya yad eṣa sarvaṁ; prapatsyate rāṣṭram idaṁ cirāya ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
sa ghoṣavadbhiś ca hayaiḥ sanāgaiḥ; puraḥsaraiḥ svastikasūtamāgadhaiḥ |
mahīyamānaḥ pravaraiś ca vādakair; abhiṣṭuto vaiśravaṇo yathā yayau ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
kareṇumātaṅgarathāśvasaṁkulaṁ; mahājanaughaiḥ paripūrṇacatvaram |
prabhūtaratnaṁ bahupaṇyasaṁcayaṁ; dadarśa rāmo ruciraṁ mahāpatham ||

=== meaning ===

---
