=== Sarga 38 ===

---
1.
tataḥ samīkṣya śayane sannaṁ śokena pārthivam |
kausalyā putraśokārtā tam uvāca mahīpatim ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
rāghavo naraśārdūla viṣam uptvā dvijihvavat |
vicariṣyati kaikeyī nirmukteva hi pannagī ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
vivāsya rāmaṁ subhagā labdhakāmā samāhitā |
trāsayiṣyati māṁ bhūyo duṣṭāhir iva veśmani ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
atha sma nagare rāmaś caran bhaikṣaṁ gr̥he vaset |
kāmakāro varaṁ dātum api dāsaṁ mamātmajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
pātayitvā tu kaikeyyā rāmaṁ sthānād yatheṣṭataḥ |
pradiṣṭo rakṣasāṁ bhāgaḥ parvaṇīvāhitāgninā ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
gajarājagatir vīro mahābāhur dhanurdharaḥ |
vanam āviśate nūnaṁ sabhāryaḥ sahalakṣmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
vane tv adr̥ṣṭaduḥkhānāṁ kaikeyyānumate tvayā |
tyaktānāṁ vanavāsāya kā nv avasthā bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
te ratnahīnās taruṇāḥ phalakāle vivāsitāḥ |
kathaṁ vatsyanti kr̥paṇāḥ phalamūlaiḥ kr̥tāśanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
apīdānīṁ sa kālaḥ syān mama śokakṣayaḥ śivaḥ |
sabhāryaṁ yat saha bhrātrā paśyeyam iha rāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
śrutvaivopasthitau vīrau kadāyodhyā bhaviṣyati |
yaśasvinī hr̥ṣṭajanā sūcchritadhvajamālinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
kadā prekṣya naravyāghrāv araṇyāt punarāgatau |
nandiṣyati purī hr̥ṣṭā samudra iva parvaṇi ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
kadāyodhyāṁ mahābāhuḥ purīṁ vīraḥ pravekṣyati |
puraskr̥tya rathe sītāṁ vr̥ṣabho govadhūm iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
kadā prāṇisahasrāṇi rājamārge mamātmajau |
lājair avakariṣyanti praviśantāv ariṁdamau ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
kadā sumanasaḥ kanyā dvijātīnāṁ phalāni ca |
pradiśantyaḥ purīṁ hr̥ṣṭāḥ kariṣyanti pradakṣiṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
kadā pariṇato buddhyā vayasā cāmaraprabhaḥ |
abhyupaiṣyati dharmajñas trivarṣa iva māṁ lalan ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
niḥsaṁśayaṁ mayā manye purā vīra kadaryayā |
pātu kāmeṣu vatseṣu mātr̥̄ṇāṁ śātitāḥ stanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
sāhaṁ gaur iva siṁhena vivatsā vatsalā kr̥tā |
kaikeyyā puruṣavyāghra bālavatseva gaur balāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
na hi tāvad guṇair juṣṭaṁ sarvaśāstraviśāradam |
ekaputrā vinā putram ahaṁ jīvitum utsahe ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
na hi me jīvite kiṁ cit sāmartham iha kalpyate |
apaśyantyāḥ priyaṁ putraṁ mahābāhuṁ mahābalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
ayaṁ hi māṁ dīpayate samutthitas; tanūjaśokaprabhavo hutāśanaḥ |
mahīm imāṁ raśmibhir uttamaprabho; yathā nidāghe bhagavān divākaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
