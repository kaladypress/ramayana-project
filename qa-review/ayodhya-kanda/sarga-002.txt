=== Sarga 2 ===

---
1.
tataḥ pariṣadaṁ sarvām āmantrya vasudhādhipaḥ |
hitam uddharṣaṇaṁ cedam uvācāpratimaṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
dundubhisvanakalpena gambhīreṇānunādinā |
svareṇa mahatā rājā jīgmūta iva nādayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
so ’ham ikṣvākubhiḥ pūrvair narendraiḥ paripālitam |
śreyasā yoktukāmo ’smi sukhārham akhilaṁ jagat ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
mayāpy ācaritaṁ pūrvaiḥ panthānam anugacchatā |
prajā nityam atandreṇa yathāśakty abhirakṣatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
idaṁ śarīraṁ kr̥tsnasya lokasya caratā hitam |
pāṇḍur asyātapatrasyac chāyāyāṁ jaritaṁ mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
prāpya varṣasahasrāṇi bahūny āyūṁṣi jīvitaḥ |
jīrṇasyāsya śarīrasya viśrāntim abhirocaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
rājaprabhāvajuṣṭāṁ hi durvahām ajitendriyaiḥ |
pariśrānto ’smi lokasya gurvīṁ dharmadhuraṁ vahan ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
so ’haṁ viśramam icchāmi putraṁ kr̥tvā prajāhite |
saṁnikr̥ṣṭān imān sarvān anumānya dvijarṣabhān ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
anujāto hi me sarvair guṇair jyeṣṭho mamātmajaḥ |
puraṁdarasamo vīrye rāmaḥ parapuraṁjayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
taṁ candram iva puṣyeṇa yuktaṁ dharmabhr̥tāṁ varam |
yauvarājyena yoktāsmi prītaḥ puruṣapuṁgavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
anurūpaḥ sa vo nātho lakṣmīvām̐l lakṣmaṇāgrajaḥ |
trailokyam api nāthena yena syān nāthavattaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
anena śreyasā sadyaḥ saṁyojyāham imāṁ mahīm |
gatakleśo bhaviṣyāmi sute tasmin niveśya vai ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
iti bruvantaṁ muditāḥ pratyanandan nr̥pā nr̥pam |
vr̥ṣṭimantaṁ mahāmeghaṁ nardantam iva barhiṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tasya dharmārthaviduṣo bhāvam ājñāya sarvaśaḥ |
ūcuś ca manasā jñātvā vr̥ddhaṁ daśarathaṁ nr̥pam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
anekavarṣasāhasro vr̥ddhas tvam asi pārthiva |
sa rāmaṁ yuvarājānam abhiṣiñcasva pārthivam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
iti tad vacanaṁ śrutvā rājā teṣāṁ manaḥpriyam |
ajānann iva jijñāsur idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
kathaṁ nu mayi dharmeṇa pr̥thivīm anuśāsati |
bhavanto draṣṭum icchanti yuvarājaṁ mamātmajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
te tam ūcur mahātmānaṁ paurajānapadaiḥ saha |
bahavo nr̥pa kalyāṇā guṇāḥ putrasya santi te ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
divyair guṇaiḥ śakrasamo rāmaḥ satyaparākramaḥ |
ikṣvākubhyo hi sarvebhyo ’py atirakto viśāmpate ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
rāmaḥ satpuruṣo loke satyadharmaparāyaṇaḥ |
dharmajñaḥ satyasaṁdhaś ca śīlavān anasūyakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
kṣāntaḥ sāntvayitā ślakṣṇaḥ kr̥tajño vijitendriyaḥ |
mr̥duś ca sthiracittaś ca sadā bhavyo ’nasūyakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
priyavādī ca bhūtānāṁ satyavādī ca rāghavaḥ |
bahuśrutānāṁ vr̥ddhānāṁ brāhmaṇānām upāsitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tenāsyehātulā kīrtir yaśas tejaś ca vardhate |
devāsuramanuṣyāṇāṁ sarvāstreṣu viśāradaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
yadā vrajati saṁgrāmaṁ grāmārthe nagarasya vā |
gatvā saumitrisahito nāvijitya nivartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
saṁgrāmāt punar āgamya kuñjareṇa rathena vā |
paurān svajanavan nityaṁ kuśalaṁ paripr̥cchati ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
putreṣv agniṣu dāreṣu preṣyaśiṣyagaṇeṣu ca |
nikhilenānupūrvyā ca pitā putrān ivaurasān ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
śuśrūṣante ca vaḥ śiṣyāḥ kaccit karmasu daṁśitāḥ |
iti naḥ puruṣavyāghraḥ sadā rāmo ’bhibhāṣate ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
vyasaneṣu manuṣyāṇāṁ bhr̥śaṁ bhavati duḥkhitaḥ |
utsaveṣu ca sarveṣu piteva parituṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
satyavādī maheṣvāso vr̥ddhasevī jitendriyaḥ |
vatsaḥ śreyasi jātas te diṣṭyāsau tava rāghavaḥ |
diṣṭyā putraguṇair yukto mārīca iva kaśyapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
balam ārogyam āyuś ca rāmasya viditātmanaḥ |
āśaṁsate janaḥ sarvo rāṣṭre puravare tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
abhyantaraś ca bāhyaś ca paurajānapado janaḥ |
striyo vr̥ddhās taruṇyaś ca sāyaṁprātaḥ samāhitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
sarvān devān namasyanti rāmasyārthe yaśasvinaḥ |
teṣām āyācitaṁ deva tvatprasādāt samr̥dhyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
rāmam indīvaraśyāmaṁ sarvaśatrunibarhaṇam |
paśyāmo yauvarājyasthaṁ tava rājottamātmajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
taṁ devadevopamam ātmajaṁ te; sarvasya lokasya hite niviṣṭam |
hitāya naḥ kṣipram udārajuṣṭaṁ; mudābhiṣektuṁ varada tvam arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
