=== Sarga 102 ===

---
1.
kruddham ājñāya rāmaṁ tu vasiṣṭhaḥ pratyuvāca ha |
jābālir api jānīte lokasyāsya gatāgatim |
nivartayitukāmas tu tvām etad vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
imāṁ lokasamutpattiṁ lokanātha nibodha me |
sarvaṁ salilam evāsīt pr̥thivī yatra nirmitā |
tataḥ samabhavad brahmā svayambhūr daivataiḥ saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sa varāhas tato bhūtvā projjahāra vasuṁdharām |
asr̥jac ca jagat sarvaṁ saha putraiḥ kr̥tātmabhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
ākāśaprabhavo brahmā śāśvato nitya avyayaḥ |
tasmān marīciḥ saṁjajñe marīceḥ kaśyapaḥ sutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
vivasvān kaśyapāj jajñe manur vaivasvataḥ smr̥taḥ |
sa tu prajāpatiḥ pūrvam ikṣvākus tu manoḥ sutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
yasyeyaṁ prathamaṁ dattā samr̥ddhā manunā mahī |
tam ikṣvākum ayodhyāyāṁ rājānaṁ viddhi pūrvakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
ikṣvākos tu sutaḥ śrīmān kukṣir eveti viśrutaḥ |
kukṣer athātmajo vīro vikukṣir udapadyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
vikukṣes tu mahātejā bāṇaḥ putraḥ pratāpavān |
bāṇasya tu mahābāhur anaraṇyo mahāyaśāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
nānāvr̥ṣṭir babhūvāsmin na durbhikṣaṁ satāṁ vare |
anaraṇye mahārāje taskaro vāpi kaś cana ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
anaraṇyān mahābāhuḥ pr̥thū rājā babhūva ha |
tasmāt pr̥thor mahārājas triśaṅkur udapadyata |
sa satyavacanād vīraḥ saśarīro divaṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
triśaṅkor abhavat sūnur dhundhumāro mahāyaśāḥ |
dhundhumārān mahātejā yuvanāśvo vyajāyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
yuvanāśvasutaḥ śrīmān māndhātā samapadyata |
māndhātus tu mahātejāḥ susaṁdhir udapadyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
susaṁdher api putrau dvau dhruvasaṁdhiḥ prasenajit |
yaśasvī dhruvasaṁdhes tu bharato ripusūdanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
bharatāt tu mahābāhor asito nāma jāyata |
yasyaite pratirājāna udapadyanta śatravaḥ |
haihayās tālajaṅghāś ca śūrāś ca śaśabindavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tāṁs tu sarvān prativyūhya yuddhe rājā pravāsitaḥ |
sa ca śailavare ramye babhūvābhirato muniḥ |
dve cāsya bhārye garbhiṇyau babhūvatur iti śrutiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
bhārgavaś cyavano nāma himavantam upāśritaḥ |
tam r̥ṣiṁ samupāgamya kālindī tv abhyavādayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
sa tām abhyavadad vipro varepsuṁ putrajanmani |
tataḥ sā gr̥ham āgamya devī putraṁ vyajāyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
sapatnyā tu garas tasyai datto garbhajighāṁsayā |
gareṇa saha tenaiva jātaḥ sa sagaro ’bhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
sa rājā sagaro nāma yaḥ samudram akhānayat |
iṣṭvā parvaṇi vegena trāsayantam imāḥ prajāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
asamañjas tu putro ’bhūt sagarasyeti naḥ śrutam |
jīvann eva sa pitrā tu nirastaḥ pāpakarmakr̥t ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
aṁśumān iti putro ’bhūd asamañjasya vīryavān |
dilīpo ’ṁśumataḥ putro dilīpasya bhagīrathaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
bhagīrathāt kakutsthas tu kākutsthā yena tu smr̥tāḥ |
kakutsthasya tu putro ’bhūd raghur yena tu rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
raghos tu putras tejasvī pravr̥ddhaḥ puruṣādakaḥ |
kalmāṣapādaḥ saudāsa ity evaṁ prathito bhuvi ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
kalmāṣapādaputro ’bhūc chaṅkhaṇas tv iti viśrutaḥ |
yas tu tad vīryam āsādya sahaseno vyanīnaśat ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
śaṅkhaṇasya tu putro ’bhūc chūraḥ śrīmān sudarśanaḥ |
sudarśanasyāgnivarṇa agnivarṣasya śīghragaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
śīghragasya maruḥ putro maroḥ putraḥ praśuśrukaḥ |
praśuśrukasya putro ’bhūd ambarīṣo mahādyutiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
ambarīṣasya putro ’bhūn nahuṣaḥ satyavikramaḥ |
nahuṣasya ca nābhāgaḥ putraḥ paramadhārmikaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
ajaś ca suvrataś caiva nābhāgasya sutāv ubhau |
ajasya caiva dharmātmā rājā daśarathaḥ sutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
tasya jyeṣṭho ’si dāyādo rāma ity abhiviśrutaḥ |
tad gr̥hāṇa svakaṁ rājyam avekṣasva jagan nr̥pa ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
ikṣvākūṇāṁ hi sarveṣāṁ rājā bhavati pūrvajaḥ |
pūrvaje nāvaraḥ putro jyeṣṭho rājye ’bhiṣicyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
sa rāghavāṇāṁ kuladharmam ātmanaḥ; sanātanaṁ nādya vihātum arhasi |
prabhūtaratnām anuśādhi medinīṁ; prabhūtarāṣṭrāṁ pitr̥van mahāyaśāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
