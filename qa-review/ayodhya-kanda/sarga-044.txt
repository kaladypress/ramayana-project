=== Sarga 44 ===

---
1.
viśālān kosalān ramyān yātvā lakṣmaṇapūrvajaḥ |
āsasāda mahābāhuḥ śr̥ṅgaverapuraṁ prati ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tatra tripathagāṁ divyāṁ śivatoyām aśaivalām |
dadarśa rāghavo gaṅgāṁ puṇyām r̥ṣinisevitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
haṁsasārasasaṁghuṣṭāṁ cakravākopakūjitām |
śiṁśumaraiś ca nakraiś ca bhujaṁgaiś ca niṣevitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tām ūrmikalilāvartām anvavekṣya mahārathaḥ |
sumantram abravīt sūtam ihaivādya vasāmahe ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
avidūrād ayaṁ nadyā bahupuṣpapravālavān |
sumahān iṅgudīvr̥kṣo vasāmo ’traiva sārathe ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
lakṣaṇaś ca sumantraś ca bāḍham ity eva rāghavam |
uktvā tam iṅgudīvr̥kṣaṁ tadopayayatur hayaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
rāmo ’bhiyāya taṁ ramyaṁ vr̥kṣam ikṣvākunandanaḥ |
rathād avātarat tasmāt sabhāryaḥ sahalakṣmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sumantro ’py avatīryaiva mocayitvā hayottamān |
vr̥kṣamūlagataṁ rāmam upatasthe kr̥tāñjaliḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tatra rājā guho nāma rāmasyātmasamaḥ sakhā |
niṣādajātyo balavān sthapatiś ceti viśrutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
sa śrutvā puruṣavyāghraṁ rāmaṁ viṣayam āgatam |
vr̥ddhaiḥ parivr̥to ’mātyair jñātibhiś cāpy upāgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tato niṣādādhipatiṁ dr̥ṣṭvā dūrād avasthitam |
saha saumitriṇā rāmaḥ samāgacchad guhena saḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tam ārtaḥ saṁpariṣvajya guho rāghavam abravīt |
yathāyodhyā tathedaṁ te rāma kiṁ karavāṇi te ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tato guṇavadannādyam upādāya pr̥thagvidham |
arghyaṁ copānayat kṣipraṁ vākyaṁ cedam uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
svāgataṁ te mahābāho taveyam akhilā mahī |
vayaṁ preṣyā bhavān bhartā sādhu rājyaṁ praśādhi naḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
bhakṣyaṁ bhojyaṁ ca peyaṁ ca lehyaṁ cedam upasthitam |
śayanāni ca mukhyāni vājināṁ khādanaṁ ca te ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
guham eva bruvāṇaṁ taṁ rāghavaḥ pratyuvāca ha |
arcitāś caiva hr̥ṣṭāś ca bhavatā sarvathā vayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
padbhyām abhigamāc caiva snehasaṁdarśanena ca |
bhujābhyāṁ sādhuvr̥ttābhyāṁ pīḍayan vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
diṣṭyā tvāṁ guha paśyāmi arogaṁ saha bāndhavaiḥ |
api te kūśalaṁ rāṣṭre mitreṣu ca dhaneṣu ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
yat tv idaṁ bhavatā kiṁ cit prītyā samupakalpitam |
sarvaṁ tad anujānāmi na hi varte pratigrahe ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
kuśacīrājinadharaṁ phalamūlāśanaṁ ca mām |
viddhi praṇihitaṁ dharme tāpasaṁ vanagocaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
aśvānāṁ khādanenāham arthī nānyena kena cit |
etāvatātrabhavatā bhaviṣyāmi supūjitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
ete hi dayitā rājñaḥ pitur daśarathasya me |
etaiḥ suvihitair aśvair bhaviṣyāmy aham arcitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
aśvānāṁ pratipānaṁ ca khādanaṁ caiva so ’nvaśāt |
guhas tatraiva puruṣāṁs tvaritaṁ dīyatām iti ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tataś cīrottarāsaṅgaḥ saṁdhyām anvāsya paścimām |
jalam evādade bhojyaṁ lakṣmaṇenāhr̥taṁ svayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tasya bhūmau śayānasya pādau prakṣālya lakṣmaṇaḥ |
sabhāryasya tato ’bhyetya tasthau vr̥kṣam upāśritaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
guho ’pi saha sūtena saumitrim anubhāṣayan |
anvajāgrat tato rāmam apramatto dhanurdharaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tathā śayānasya tato ’sya dhīmato; yaśasvino dāśarather mahātmanaḥ |
adr̥ṣṭaduḥkhasya sukhocitasya sā; tadā vyatīyāya cireṇa śarvarī ||

=== meaning ===

---
