=== Sarga 21 ===

---
1.
taṁ samīkṣya tv avahitaṁ pitur nirdeśapālane |
kausalyā bāṣpasaṁruddhā vaco dharmiṣṭham abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
adr̥ṣṭaduḥkho dharmātmā sarvabhūtapriyaṁvadaḥ |
mayi jāto daśarathāt katham uñchena vartayet ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
yasya bhr̥tyāś ca dāsāś ca mr̥ṣṭāny annāni bhuñjate |
kathaṁ sa bhokṣyate nātho vane mūlaphalāny ayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
ka etac chraddadhec chrutvā kasya vā na bhaved bhayam |
guṇavān dayito rājño rāghavo yad vivāsyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tvayā vihīnām iha māṁ śokāgnir atulo mahān |
pradhakṣyati yathā kakṣaṁ citrabhānur himātyaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
kathaṁ hi dhenuḥ svaṁ vatsaṁ gacchantaṁ nānugacchati |
ahaṁ tvānugamiṣyāmi yatra putra gamiṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tathā nigaditaṁ mātrā tad vākyaṁ puruṣarṣabhaḥ |
śrutvā rāmo ’bravīd vākyaṁ mātaraṁ bhr̥śaduḥkhitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
kaikeyyā vañcito rājā mayi cāraṇyam āśrite |
bhavatyā ca parityakto na nūnaṁ vartayiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
bhartuḥ kila parityāgo nr̥śaṁsaḥ kevalaṁ striyāḥ |
sa bhavatyā na kartavyo manasāpi vigarhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
yāvaj jīvati kākutsthaḥ pitā me jagatīpatiḥ |
śuśrūṣā kriyatāṁ tāvat sa hi dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
evam uktā tu rāmeṇa kausalyā śubha darśanā |
tathety uvāca suprītā rāmam akliṣṭakāriṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
evam uktas tu vacanaṁ rāmo dharmabhr̥tāṁ varaḥ |
bhūyas tām abravīd vākyaṁ mātaraṁ bhr̥śaduḥkhitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
mayā caiva bhavatyā ca kartavyaṁ vacanaṁ pituḥ |
rājā bhartā guruḥ śreṣṭhaḥ sarveṣām īśvaraḥ prabhuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
imāni tu mahāraṇye vihr̥tya nava pañca ca |
varṣāṇi paramaprītaḥ sthāsyāmi vacane tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
evam uktā priyaṁ putraṁ bāṣpapūrṇānanā tadā |
uvāca paramārtā tu kausalyā putravatsalā ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
āsāṁ rāma sapatnīnāṁ vastuṁ madhye na me kṣamam |
naya mām api kākutstha vanaṁ vanyaṁ mr̥gīṁ yathā |
yadi te gamane buddhiḥ kr̥tā pitur apekṣayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tāṁ tathā rudatīṁ rāmo rudan vacanam abravīt |
jīvantyā hi striyā bhartā daivataṁ prabhur eva ca |
bhavatyā mama caivādya rājā prabhavati prabhuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
bharataś cāpi dharmātmā sarvabhūtapriyaṁvadaḥ |
bhavatīm anuvarteta sa hi dharmarataḥ sadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
yathā mayi tu niṣkrānte putraśokena pārthivaḥ |
śramaṁ nāvāpnuyāt kiṁ cid apramattā tathā kuru ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
vratopavāsaniratā yā nārī paramottamā |
bhartāraṁ nānuvarteta sā ca pāpagatir bhavet ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
śuśrūṣam eva kurvīta bhartuḥ priyahite ratā |
eṣa dharmaḥ purā dr̥ṣṭo loke vede śrutaḥ smr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
pūjyās te matkr̥te devi brāhmaṇāś caiva suvratāḥ |
evaṁ kālaṁ pratīkṣasva mamāgamanakāṅkṣiṇī ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
prāpsyase paramaṁ kāmaṁ mayi pratyāgate sati |
yadi dharmabhr̥tāṁ śreṣṭho dhārayiṣyati jīvitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
evam uktā tu rāmeṇa bāṣpaparyākulekṣaṇā |
kausalyā putraśokārtā rāmaṁ vacanam abravīt |
gaccha putra tvam ekāgro bhadraṁ te ’stu sadā vibho ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tathā hi rāmaṁ vanavāsaniścitaṁ; samīkṣya devī parameṇa cetasā |
uvāca rāmaṁ śubhalakṣaṇaṁ vaco; babhūva ca svastyayanābhikāṅkṣiṇī ||

=== meaning ===

---
