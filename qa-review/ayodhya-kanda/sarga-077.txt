=== Sarga 77 ===

---
1.
tataḥ samutthitaḥ kālyam āsthāya syandanottamam |
prayayau bharataḥ śīghraṁ rāmadarśanakāṅkṣayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
agrataḥ prayayus tasya sarve mantripurodhasaḥ |
adhiruhya hayair yuktān rathān sūryarathopamān ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
navanāgasahasrāṇi kalpitāni yathāvidhi |
anvayur bharataṁ yāntam ikṣvāku kulanandanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
ṣaṣṭhī rathasahasrāṇi dhanvino vividhāyudhāḥ |
anvayur bharataṁ yāntaṁ rājaputraṁ yaśasvinam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
śataṁ sahasrāṇy aśvānāṁ samārūḍhāni rāghavam |
anvayur bharataṁ yāntaṁ rājaputraṁ yaśasvinam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
kaikeyī ca sumitrā ca kausalyā ca yaśasvinī |
rāmānayanasaṁhr̥ṣṭā yayur yānena bhāsvatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
prayātāś cāryasaṁghātā rāmaṁ draṣṭuṁ salakṣmaṇam |
tasyaiva ca kathāś citrāḥ kurvāṇā hr̥ṣṭamānasāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
meghaśyāmaṁ mahābāhuṁ sthirasattvaṁ dr̥ḍhavratam |
kadā drakṣyāmahe rāmaṁ jagataḥ śokanāśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
dr̥ṣṭa eva hi naḥ śokam apaneṣyati rāghavaḥ |
tamaḥ sarvasya lokasya samudyann iva bhāskaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
ity evaṁ kathayantas te saṁprahr̥ṣṭāḥ kathāḥ śubhāḥ |
pariṣvajānāś cānyonyaṁ yayur nāgarikās tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
ye ca tatrāpare sarve saṁmatā ye ca naigamāḥ |
rāmaṁ prati yayur hr̥ṣṭāḥ sarvāḥ prakr̥tayas tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
maṇi kārāś ca ye ke cit kumbhakārāś ca śobhanāḥ |
sūtrakarmakr̥taś caiva ye ca śastropajīvinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
māyūrakāḥ krākacikā rocakā vedhakās tathā |
dantakārāḥ sudhākārās tathā gandhopajīvinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
suvarṇakārāḥ prakhyātās tathā kambaladhāvakāḥ |
snāpakācchādakā vaidyā dhūpakāḥ śauṇḍikās tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
rajakās tunnavāyāś ca grāmaghoṣamahattarāḥ |
śailūṣāś ca saha strībhir yānti kaivartakās tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
samāhitā vedavido brāhmaṇā vr̥ttasaṁmatāḥ |
gorathair bharataṁ yāntam anujagmuḥ sahasraśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
suveṣāḥ śuddhavasanās tāmramr̥ṣṭānulepanāḥ |
sarve te vividhair yānaiḥ śanair bharatam anvayuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
prahr̥ṣṭamuditā senā sānvayāt kaikayīsutam |
vyavatiṣṭhata sā senā bharatasyānuyāyinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
nirīkṣyānugatāṁ senāṁ tāṁ ca gaṅgāṁ śivodakām |
bharataḥ sacivān sarvān abravīd vākyakovidaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
niveśayata me sainyam abhiprāyeṇa sarvaśaḥ |
viśrāntaḥ pratariṣyāmaḥ śva idānīṁ mahānadīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
dātuṁ ca tāvad icchāmi svar gatasya mahīpateḥ |
aurdhvadeha nimittārtham avatīryodakaṁ nadīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tasyaivaṁ bruvato ’mātyās tathety uktvā samāhitāḥ |
nyaveśayaṁs tāṁś chandena svena svena pr̥thakpr̥thak ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
niveśya gaṅgām anu tāṁ mahānadīṁ; camūṁ vidhānaiḥ paribarha śobhinīm |
uvāsa rāmasya tadā mahātmano; vicintayāno bharato nivartanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
