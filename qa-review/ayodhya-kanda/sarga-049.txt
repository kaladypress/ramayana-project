=== Sarga 49 ===

---
1.
uṣitvā rajanīṁ tatra rājaputrāv ariṁdamau |
maharṣim abhivādyātha jagmatus taṁ giriṁ prati ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
prasthitāṁś caiva tān prekṣya pitā putrān ivānvagāt |
tataḥ pracakrame vaktuṁ vacanaṁ sa mahāmuniḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
athāsādya tu kālindīṁ śīghrasrotasamāpagām |
tatra yūyaṁ plavaṁ kr̥tvā taratāṁśumatīṁ nadīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tato nyagrodham āsādya mahāntaṁ haritacchadam |
vivr̥ddhaṁ bahubhir vr̥kṣaiḥ śyāmaṁ siddhopasevitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
krośamātraṁ tato gatvā nīlaṁ drakṣyatha kānanam |
palāśabadarīmiśraṁ rāma vaṁśaiś ca yāmunaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
sa panthāś citrakūṭasya gataḥ subahuśo mayā |
ramyo mārdavayuktaś ca vanadāvair vivarjitaḥ |
iti panthānam āvedya maharṣiḥ sa nyavartata ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
upāvr̥tte munau tasmin rāmo lakṣmaṇam abravīt |
kr̥tapuṇyāḥ sma saumitre munir yan no ’nukampate ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
iti tau puruṣavyāghrau mantrayitvā manasvinau |
sītām evāgrataḥ kr̥tvā kālindīṁ jagmatur nadīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tau kāṣṭhasaṁghāṭam atho cakratuḥ sumahāplavam |
cakāra lakṣmaṇaś chittvā sītāyāḥ sukhamānasam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tatra śriyam ivācintyāṁ rāmo dāśarathiḥ priyām |
īṣatsaṁlajjamānāṁ tām adhyāropayata plavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tataḥ plavenāṁśumatīṁ śīghragām ūrmimālinīm |
tīrajair bahubhir vr̥kṣaiḥ saṁterur yamunāṁ nadīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
te tīrṇāḥ plavam utsr̥jya prasthāya yamunāvanāt |
śyāmaṁ nyagrodham āseduḥ śītalaṁ haritacchadam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
kausalyāṁ caiva paśyeyaṁ sumitrāṁ ca yaśasvinīm |
iti sītāñjaliṁ kr̥tvā paryagachad vanaspatim ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
krośamātraṁ tato gatvā bhrātarau rāmalakṣmaṇau |
bahūn medhyān mr̥gān hatvā ceratur yamunāvane ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
vihr̥tya te barhiṇapūganādite; śubhe vane vāraṇavānarāyute |
samaṁ nadīvapram upetya saṁmataṁ; nivāsam ājagmur adīnadarśanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
