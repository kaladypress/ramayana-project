=== Sarga 1 ===

---
1.
kasya cit tv atha kālasya rājā daśarathaḥ sutam |
bharataṁ kekayīputram abravīd raghunandanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
ayaṁ kekayarājasya putro vasati putraka |
tvāṁ netum āgato vīra yudhājin mātulas tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
śrutvā daśarathasyaitad bharataḥ kekayīsutaḥ |
gamanāyābhicakrāma śatrughnasahitas tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
āpr̥cchya pitaraṁ śūro rāmaṁ cākliṣṭakāriṇam |
mātr̥̄ṁś cāpi naraśreṣṭhaḥ śatrughnasahito yayau ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
yudhājit prāpya bharataṁ saśatrughnaṁ praharṣitaḥ |
svapuraṁ prāviśad vīraḥ pitā tasya tutoṣa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
sa tatra nyavasad bhrātrā saha satkārasatkr̥taḥ |
mātulenāśvapatinā putrasnehena lālitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tatrāpi nivasantau tau tarpyamāṇau ca kāmataḥ |
bhrātarau smaratāṁ vīrau vr̥ddhaṁ daśarathaṁ nr̥pam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
rājāpi tau mahātejāḥ sasmāra proṣitau sutau |
ubhau bharataśatrughnau mahendravaruṇopamau ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sarva eva tu tasyeṣṭāś catvāraḥ puruṣarṣabhāḥ |
svaśarīrād vinirvr̥ttāś catvāra iva bāhavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
teṣām api mahātejā rāmo ratikaraḥ pituḥ |
svayambhūr iva bhūtānāṁ babhūva guṇavattaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
gate ca bharate rāmo lakṣmaṇaś ca mahābalaḥ |
pitaraṁ devasaṁkāśaṁ pūjayām āsatus tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
pitur ājñāṁ puraskr̥tya paurakāryāṇi sarvaśaḥ |
cakāra rāmo dharmātmā priyāṇi ca hitāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
mātr̥bhyo mātr̥kāryāṇi kr̥tvā paramayantritaḥ |
gurūṇāṁ gurukāryāṇi kāle kāle ’nvavaikṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
evaṁ daśarathaḥ prīto brāhmaṇā naigamās tathā |
rāmasya śīlavr̥ttena sarve viṣayavāsinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa hi nityaṁ praśāntātmā mr̥dupūrvaṁ ca bhāṣate |
ucyamāno ’pi paruṣaṁ nottaraṁ pratipadyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
kathaṁ cid upakāreṇa kr̥tenaikena tuṣyati |
na smaraty apakārāṇāṁ śatam apy ātmavattayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
śīlavr̥ddhair jñānavr̥ddhair vayovr̥ddhaiś ca sajjanaiḥ |
kathayann āsta vai nityam astrayogyāntareṣv api ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
kalyāṇābhijanaḥ sādhur adīnaḥ satyavāg r̥juḥ |
vr̥ddhair abhivinītaś ca dvijair dharmārthadarśibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
dharmārthakāmatattvajñaḥ smr̥timān pratibhāvanān |
laukike samayācare kr̥takalpo viśāradaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
śāstrajñaś ca kr̥tajñaś ca puruṣāntarakovidaḥ |
yaḥ pragrahānugrahayor yathānyāyaṁ vicakṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
āyakarmaṇy upāyajñaḥ saṁdr̥ṣṭavyayakarmavit |
śraiṣṭhyaṁ śāstrasamūheṣu prāpto vyāmiśrakeṣv api ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
arthadharmau ca saṁgr̥hya sukhatantro na cālasaḥ |
vaihārikāṇāṁ śilpānāṁ vijñātārthavibhāgavit ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
ārohe vinaye caiva yukto vāraṇavājinām |
dhanurvedavidāṁ śreṣṭho loke ’tirathasaṁmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
abhiyātā prahartā ca senānayaviśāradaḥ |
apradhr̥ṣyaś ca saṁgrāme kruddhair api surāsuraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
anasūyo jitakrodho na dr̥pto na ca matsarī |
na cāvamantā bhūtānāṁ na ca kālavaśānugaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
evaṁ śreṣṭhair guṇair yuktaḥ prajānāṁ pārthivātmajaḥ |
saṁmatas triṣu lokeṣu vasudhāyāḥ kṣamāguṇaiḥ |
buddhyā br̥haspates tulyo vīryeṇāpi śacīpateḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tathā sarvaprajākāntaiḥ prītisaṁjananaiḥ pituḥ |
guṇair viruruce rāmo dīptaḥ sūrya ivāṁśubhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tam evaṁvr̥ttasaṁpannam apradhr̥ṣya parākramam |
lokapālopamaṁ nātham akāmayata medinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
etais tu bahubhir yuktaṁ guṇair anupamaiḥ sutam |
dr̥ṣṭvā daśaratho rājā cakre cintāṁ paraṁtapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
eṣā hy asya parā prītir hr̥di saṁparivartate |
kadā nāma sutaṁ drakṣyāmy abhiṣiktam ahaṁ priyam ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
vr̥ddhikāmo hi lokasya sarvabhūtānukampanaḥ |
mattaḥ priyataro loke parjanya iva vr̥ṣṭimān ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
yamaśakrasamo vīrye br̥haspatisamo matau |
mahīdharasamo dhr̥tyāṁ mattaś ca guṇavattaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
mahīm aham imāṁ kr̥tsnām adhitiṣṭhantam ātmajam |
anena vayasā dr̥ṣṭvā yathā svargam avāpnuyām ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
taṁ samīkṣya mahārājo yuktaṁ samuditair guṇaiḥ |
niścitya sacivaiḥ sārdhaṁ yuvarājam amanyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
nānānagaravāstavyān pr̥thagjānapadān api |
samānināya medinyāḥ pradhānān pr̥thivīpatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
atha rājavitīrṇeṣu vividheṣv āsaneṣu ca |
rājānam evābhimukhā niṣedur niyatā nr̥pāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
sa labdhamānair vinayānvitair nr̥paiḥ; purālayair jānapadaiś ca mānavaiḥ |
upopaviṣṭair nr̥patir vr̥to babhau; sahasracakṣur bhagavān ivāmaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
