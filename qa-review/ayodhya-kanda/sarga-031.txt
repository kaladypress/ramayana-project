=== Sarga 31 ===

---
1.
sa rāmapreṣitaḥ kṣipraṁ saṁtāpakaluṣendriyaḥ |
praviśya nr̥patiṁ sūto niḥśvasantaṁ dadarśa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
ālokya tu mahāprājñaḥ paramākula cetasam |
rāmam evānuśocantaṁ sūtaḥ prāñjalir āsadat ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
ayaṁ sa puruṣavyāghra dvāri tiṣṭhati te sutaḥ |
brāhmaṇebhyo dhanaṁ dattvā sarvaṁ caivopajīvinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
sa tvā paśyatu bhadraṁ te rāmaḥ satyaparākramaḥ |
sarvān suhr̥da āpr̥cchya tvām idānīṁ didr̥kṣate ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
gamiṣyati mahāraṇyaṁ taṁ paśya jagatīpate |
vr̥taṁ rājaguṇaiḥ sarvair ādityam iva raśmibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
sa satyavādī dharmātmā gāmbhīryāt sāgaropamaḥ |
ākāśa iva niṣpaṅko narendraḥ pratyuvāca tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sumantrānaya me dārān ye ke cid iha māmakāḥ |
dāraiḥ parivr̥taḥ sarvair draṣṭum icchāmi rāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
so ’ntaḥpuram atītyaiva striyas tā vākyam abravīt |
āryo hvayati vo rājā gamyatāṁ tatra māciram ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
evam uktāḥ striyaḥ sarvāḥ sumantreṇa nr̥pājñayā |
pracakramus tad bhavanaṁ bhartur ājñāya śāsanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
ardhasaptaśatās tās tu pramadās tāmralocanāḥ |
kausalyāṁ parivāryātha śanair jagmur dhr̥tavratāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
āgateṣu ca dāreṣu samavekṣya mahīpatiḥ |
uvāca rājā taṁ sūtaṁ sumantrānaya me sutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sa sūto rāmam ādāya lakṣmaṇaṁ maithilīṁ tadā |
jagāmābhimukhas tūrṇaṁ sakāśaṁ jagatīpateḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sa rājā putram āyāntaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā dūrāt kr̥tāñjalim |
utpapātāsanāt tūrṇam ārtaḥ strījanasaṁvr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
so ’bhidudrāva vegena rāmaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā viśāṁ patiḥ |
tam asaṁprāpya duḥkhārtaḥ papāta bhuvi mūrchitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
taṁ rāmo ’bhyapātat kṣipraṁ lakṣmaṇaś ca mahārathaḥ |
visaṁjñam iva duḥkhena saśokaṁ nr̥patiṁ tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
strīsahasraninādaś ca saṁjajñe rājaveśmani |
hāhā rāmeti sahasā bhūṣaṇadhvanimūrchitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
taṁ pariṣvajya bāhubhyāṁ tāv ubhau rāmalakṣmaṇau |
paryaṅke sītayā sārdhaṁ rudantaḥ samaveśayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
atha rāmo muhūrtena labdhasaṁjñaṁ mahīpatim |
uvāca prāñjalir bhūtvā śokārṇavapariplutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
āpr̥cche tvāṁ mahārāja sarveṣām īśvaro ’si naḥ |
prasthitaṁ daṇḍakāraṇyaṁ paśya tvaṁ kuśalena mām ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
lakṣmaṇaṁ cānujānīhi sītā cānveti māṁ vanam |
kāraṇair bahubhis tathyair vāryamāṇau na cecchataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
anujānīhi sarvān naḥ śokam utsr̥jya mānada |
lakṣmaṇaṁ māṁ ca sītāṁ ca prajāpatir iva prajāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
pratīkṣamāṇam avyagram anujñāṁ jagatīpateḥ |
uvāca rarjā saṁprekṣya vanavāsāya rāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
ahaṁ rāghava kaikeyyā varadānena mohitaḥ |
ayodhyāyās tvam evādya bhava rājā nigr̥hya mām ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
evam ukto nr̥patinā rāmo dharmabhr̥tāṁ varaḥ |
pratyuvācāñjaliṁ kr̥tvā pitaraṁ vākyakovidaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
bhavān varṣasahasrāya pr̥thivyā nr̥pate patiḥ |
ahaṁ tv araṇye vatsyāmi na me kāryaṁ tvayānr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
śreyase vr̥ddhaye tāta punarāgamanāya ca |
gacchasvāriṣṭam avyagraḥ panthānam akutobhayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
adya tv idānīṁ rajanīṁ putra mā gaccha sarvathā |
mātaraṁ māṁ ca saṁpaśyan vasemām adya śarvarīm |
tarpitaḥ sarvakāmais tvaṁ śvaḥkāle sādhayiṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
atha rāmas tathā śrutvā pitur ārtasya bhāṣitam |
lakṣmaṇena saha bhrātrā dīno vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
prāpsyāmi yān adya guṇān ko me śvastān pradāsyati |
apakramaṇam evātaḥ sarvakāmair ahaṁ vr̥ṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
iyaṁ sarāṣṭrā sajanā dhanadhānyasamākulā |
mayā visr̥ṣṭā vasudhā bharatāya pradīyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
apagacchatu te duḥkhaṁ mā bhūr bāṣpapariplutaḥ |
na hi kṣubhyati durdharṣaḥ samudraḥ saritāṁ patiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
naivāhaṁ rājyam icchāmi na sukhaṁ na ca maithilīm |
tvām ahaṁ satyam icchāmi nānr̥taṁ puruṣarṣabha ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
puraṁ ca rāṣṭraṁ ca mahī ca kevalā; mayā nisr̥ṣṭā bharatāya dīyatām |
ahaṁ nideśaṁ bhavato ’nupālayan; vanaṁ gamiṣyāmi cirāya sevitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
mayā nisr̥ṣṭāṁ bharato mahīm imāṁ; saśailakhaṇḍāṁ sapurāṁ sakānanām |
śivāṁ susīmām anuśāstu kevalaṁ; tvayā yad uktaṁ nr̥pate yathāstu tat ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
na me tathā pārthiva dhīyate mano; mahatsu kāmeṣu na cātmanaḥ priye |
yathā nideśe tava śiṣṭasaṁmate; vyapaitu duḥkhaṁ tava matkr̥te ’nagha ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
tad adya naivānagha rājyam avyayaṁ; na sarvakāmān na sukhaṁ na maithilīm |
na jīvitaṁ tvām anr̥tena yojayan; vr̥ṇīya satyaṁ vratam astu te tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
phalāni mūlāni ca bhakṣayan vane; girīṁś ca paśyan saritaḥ sarāṁsi ca |
vanaṁ praviśyaiva vicitrapādapaṁ; sukhī bhaviṣyāmi tavāstu nirvr̥tiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
