=== Sarga 41 ===

---
1.
tatas tu tamasā tīraṁ ramyam āśritya rāghavaḥ |
sītām udvīkṣya saumitrim idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
iyam adya niśā pūrvā saumitre prasthitā vanam |
vanavāsasya bhadraṁ te sa notkaṇṭhitum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
paśya śūnyāny araṇyāni rudantīva samantataḥ |
yathānilayam āyadbhir nilīnāni mr̥gadvijaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
adyāyodhyā tu nagarī rājadhānī pitur mama |
sastrīpuṁsā gatān asmāñ śociṣyati na saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
bharataḥ khalu dharmātmā pitaraṁ mātaraṁ ca me |
dharmārthakāmasahitair vākyair āśvāsayiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
bharatasyānr̥śaṁsatvaṁ saṁcintyāhaṁ punaḥ punaḥ |
nānuśocāmi pitaraṁ mātaraṁ cāpi lakṣmaṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tvayā kāryaṁ naravyāghra mām anuvrajatā kr̥tam |
anveṣṭavyā hi vaidehyā rakṣaṇārthe sahāyatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
adbhir eva tu saumitre vatsyāmy adya niśām imām |
etad dhi rocate mahyaṁ vanye ’pi vividhe sati ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
evam uktvā tu saumitraṁ sumantram api rāghavaḥ |
apramattas tvam aśveṣu bhava saumyety uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
so ’śvān sumantraḥ saṁyamya sūrye ’staṁ samupāgate |
prabhūtayavasān kr̥tvā babhūva pratyanantaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
upāsyatu śivāṁ saṁdhyāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rātrim upasthitām |
rāmasya śayanaṁ cakre sūtaḥ saumitriṇā saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tāṁ śayyāṁ tamasātīre vīkṣya vr̥kṣadalaiḥ kr̥tām |
rāmaḥ saumitriṇāṁ sārdhaṁ sabhāryaḥ saṁviveśa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sabhāryaṁ saṁprasuptaṁ taṁ bhrātaraṁ vīkṣya lakṣmaṇaḥ |
kathayām āsa sūtāya rāmasya vividhān guṇān ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
jāgrato hy eva tāṁ rātriṁ saumitrer udito raviḥ |
sūtasya tamasātīre rāmasya bruvato guṇān ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
gokulākulatīrāyās tamasāyā vidūrataḥ |
avasat tatra tāṁ rātriṁ rāmaḥ prakr̥tibhiḥ saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
utthāya tu mahātejāḥ prakr̥tīs tā niśāmya ca |
abravīd bhrātaraṁ rāmo lakṣmaṇaṁ puṇyalakṣaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
asmadvyapekṣān saumitre nirapekṣān gr̥heṣv api |
vr̥kṣamūleṣu saṁsuptān paśya lakṣmaṇa sāmpratam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
yathaite niyamaṁ paurāḥ kurvanty asmannivartane |
api prāṇān asiṣyanti na tu tyakṣyanti niścayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
yāvad eva tu saṁsuptās tāvad eva vayaṁ laghu |
ratham āruhya gacchāmaḥ panthānam akutobhayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
ato bhūyo ’pi nedānīm ikṣvākupuravāsinaḥ |
svapeyur anuraktā māṁ vr̥kṣamūlāni saṁśritāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
paurā hy ātmakr̥tād duḥkhād vipramocyā nr̥pātmajaiḥ |
na tu khalv ātmanā yojyā duḥkhena puravāsinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
abravīl lakṣmaṇo rāmaṁ sākṣād dharmam iva sthitam |
rocate me mahāprājña kṣipram āruhyatām iti ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
sūtas tataḥ saṁtvaritaḥ syandanaṁ tair hayottamaiḥ |
yojayitvātha rāmāya prāñjaliḥ pratyavedayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
mohanārthaṁ tu paurāṇāṁ sūtaṁ rāmo ’bravīd vacaḥ |
udaṅmukhaḥ prayāhi tvaṁ ratham āsthāya sārathe ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
muhūrtaṁ tvaritaṁ gatvā nirgataya rathaṁ punaḥ |
yathā na vidyuḥ paurā māṁ tathā kuru samāhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
rāmasya vacanaṁ śrutvā tathā cakre sa sārathiḥ |
pratyāgamya ca rāmasya syandanaṁ pratyavedayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
taṁ syandanam adhiṣṭhāya rāghavaḥ saparicchadaḥ |
śīghragām ākulāvartāṁ tamasām ataran nadīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
sa saṁtīrya mahābāhuḥ śrīmāñ śivam akaṇṭakam |
prāpadyata mahāmārgam abhayaṁ bhayadarśinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
prabhātāyāṁ tu śarvaryāṁ paurās te rāghavo vinā |
śokopahataniśceṣṭā babhūvur hatacetasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
śokajāśruparidyūnā vīkṣamāṇās tatas tataḥ |
ālokam api rāmasya na paśyanti sma duḥkhitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
tato mārgānusāreṇa gatvā kiṁ cit kṣaṇaṁ punaḥ |
mārganāśād viṣādena mahatā samabhiplutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
rathasya mārganāśena nyavartanta manasvinaḥ |
kim idaṁ kiṁ kariṣyāmo daivenopahatā iti ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
tato yathāgatenaiva mārgeṇa klāntacetasaḥ |
ayodhyām agaman sarve purīṁ vyathitasajjanām ||

=== meaning ===

---
