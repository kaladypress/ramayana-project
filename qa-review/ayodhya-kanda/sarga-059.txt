=== Sarga 59 ===

---
1.
atha rātryāṁ vyatītāyāṁ prātar evāpare ’hani |
bandinaḥ paryupātiṣṭhaṁs tat pārthivaniveśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tataḥ śucisamācārāḥ paryupasthāna kovidaḥ |
strīvarṣavarabhūyiṣṭhā upatasthur yathāpuram ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
haricandanasaṁpr̥ktam udakaṁ kāñcanair ghaṭaiḥ |
āninyuḥ snānaśikṣājñā yathākālaṁ yathāvidhi ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
maṅgalālambhanīyāni prāśanīyān upaskarān |
upaninyus tathāpy anyāḥ kumārī bahulāḥ striyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
atha yāḥ kosalendrasya śayanaṁ pratyanantarāḥ |
tāḥ striyas tu samāgamya bhartāraṁ pratyabodhayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tā vepathuparītāś ca rājñaḥ prāṇeṣu śaṅkitāḥ |
pratisrotas tr̥ṇāgrāṇāṁ sadr̥śaṁ saṁcakampire ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
atha saṁvepamanānāṁ strīṇāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā ca pārthivam |
yat tad āśaṅkitaṁ pāpaṁ tasya jajñe viniścayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tataḥ pracukruśur dīnāḥ sasvaraṁ tā varāṅganāḥ |
kareṇava ivāraṇye sthānapracyutayūthapāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tāsām ākranda śabdena sahasodgatacetane |
kausalyā ca sumitrāca tyaktanidre babhūvatuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
kausalyā ca sumitrā ca dr̥ṣṭvā spr̥ṣṭvā ca pārthivam |
hā nātheti parikruśya petatur dharaṇītale ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sā kosalendraduhitā veṣṭamānā mahītale |
na babhrāja rajodhvastā tāreva gaganacyutā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tat samuttrastasaṁbhrāntaṁ paryutsukajanākulam |
sarvatas tumulākrandaṁ paritāpārtabāndhavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sadyo nipatitānandaṁ dīnaviklavadarśanam |
babhūva naradevasya sadma diṣṭāntam īyuṣaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
atītam ājñāya tu pārthivarṣabhaṁ; yaśasvinaṁ saṁparivārya patnayaḥ |
bhr̥śaṁ rudantyaḥ karuṇaṁ suduḥkhitāḥ; pragr̥hya bāhū vyalapann anāthavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
