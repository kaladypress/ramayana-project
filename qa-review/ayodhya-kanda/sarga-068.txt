=== Sarga 68 ===

---
1.
tāṁ tathā garhayitvā tu mātaraṁ bharatas tadā |
roṣeṇa mahatāviṣṭaḥ punar evābravīd vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
rājyād bhraṁśasva kaikeyi nr̥śaṁse duṣṭacāriṇi |
parityaktā ca dharmeṇa mā mr̥taṁ rudatī bhava ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
kiṁ nu te ’dūṣayad rājā rāmo vā bhr̥śadhārmikaḥ |
yayor mr̥tyur vivāsaś ca tvatkr̥te tulyam āgatau ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
bhrūṇahatyām asi prāptā kulasyāsya vināśanāt |
kaikeyi narakaṁ gaccha mā ca bhartuḥ salokatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
yat tvayā hīdr̥śaṁ pāpaṁ kr̥taṁ ghoreṇa karmaṇā |
sarvalokapriyaṁ hitvā mamāpy āpāditaṁ bhayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tvatkr̥te me pitā vr̥tto rāmaś cāraṇyam āśritaḥ |
ayaśo jīvaloke ca tvayāhaṁ pratipāditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
mātr̥rūpe mamāmitre nr̥śaṁse rājyakāmuke |
na te ’ham abhibhāṣyo ’smi durvr̥tte patighātini ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
kausalyā ca sumitrā ca yāś cānyā mama mātaraḥ |
duḥkhena mahatāviṣṭās tvāṁ prāpya kuladūṣiṇīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
na tvam aśvapateḥ kanyā dharmarājasya dhīmataḥ |
rākṣasī tatra jātāsi kulapradhvaṁsinī pituḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
yat tvayā dhārmiko rāmo nityaṁ satyaparāyaṇaḥ |
vanaṁ prasthāpito duḥkhāt pitā ca tridivaṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
yat pradhānāsi tat pāpaṁ mayi pitrā vinākr̥te |
bhrātr̥bhyāṁ ca parityakte sarvalokasya cāpriye ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
kausalyāṁ dharmasaṁyuktāṁ viyuktāṁ pāpaniścaye |
kr̥tvā kaṁ prāpsyase tv adya lokaṁ nirayagāminī ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
kiṁ nāvabudhyase krūre niyataṁ bandhusaṁśrayam |
jyeṣṭhaṁ pitr̥samaṁ rāmaṁ kausalyāyātmasaṁbhavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
aṅgapratyaṅgajaḥ putro hr̥dayāc cāpi jāyate |
tasmāt priyataro mātuḥ priyatvān na tu bāndhavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
anyadā kila dharmajñā surabhiḥ surasaṁmatā |
vahamānau dadarśorvyāṁ putrau vigatacetasau ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tāv ardhadivase śrāntau dr̥ṣṭvā putrau mahītale |
ruroda putra śokena bāṣpaparyākulekṣaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
adhastād vrajatas tasyāḥ surarājño mahātmanaḥ |
bindavaḥ patitā gātre sūkṣmāḥ surabhigandhinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā śokasaṁtaptāṁ vajrapāṇir yaśasvinīm |
indraḥ prāñjalir udvignaḥ surarājo ’bravīd vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
bhayaṁ kaccin na cāsmāsu kutaś cid vidyate mahat |
kuto nimittaḥ śokas te brūhi sarvahitaiṣiṇi ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
evam uktā tu surabhiḥ surarājena dhīmatā |
patyuvāca tato dhīrā vākyaṁ vākyaviśāradā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
śāntaṁ pātaṁ na vaḥ kiṁ cit kutaś cid amarādhipa |
ahaṁ tu magnau śocāmi svaputrau viṣame sthitau ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
etau dr̥ṣṭvā kr̥ṣau dīnau sūryaraśmipratāpinau |
vadhyamānau balīvardau karṣakeṇa surādhipa ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
mama kāyāt prasūtau hi duḥkhitau bhāra pīḍitau |
yau dr̥ṣṭvā paritapye ’haṁ nāsti putrasamaḥ priyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
yasyāḥ putra sahasrāṇi sāpi śocati kāmadhuk |
kiṁ punar yā vinā rāmaṁ kausalyā vartayiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
ekaputrā ca sādhvī ca vivatseyaṁ tvayā kr̥tā |
tasmāt tvaṁ satataṁ duḥkhaṁ pretya ceha ca lapsyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
ahaṁ hy apacitiṁ bhrātuḥ pituś ca sakalām imām |
vardhanaṁ yaśasaś cāpi kariṣyāmi na saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
ānāyayitvā tanayaṁ kausalyāyā mahādyutim |
svayam eva pravekṣyāmi vanaṁ muniniṣevitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
iti nāga ivāraṇye tomarāṅkuśacoditaḥ |
papāta bhuvi saṁkruddho niḥśvasann iva pannagaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
saṁraktanetraḥ śithilāmbaras tadā; vidhūtasarvābharaṇaḥ paraṁtapaḥ |
babhūva bhūmau patito nr̥pātmajaḥ; śacīpateḥ ketur ivotsavakṣaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
