=== Sarga 101 ===

---
1.
jābāles tu vacaḥ śrutvā rāmaḥ satyātmanāṁ varaḥ |
uvāca parayā yuktyā svabuddhyā cāvipannayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
bhavān me priyakāmārthaṁ vacanaṁ yad ihoktavān |
akāryaṁ kāryasaṁkāśam apathyaṁ pathyasaṁmitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
nirmaryādas tu puruṣaḥ pāpācārasamanvitaḥ |
mānaṁ na labhate satsu bhinnacāritradarśanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
kulīnam akulīnaṁ vā vīraṁ puruṣamāninam |
cāritram eva vyākhyāti śuciṁ vā yadi vāśucim ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
anāryas tv āryasaṁkāśaḥ śaucād dhīnas tathā śuciḥ |
lakṣaṇyavad alakṣaṇyo duḥśīlaḥ śīlavān iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
adharmaṁ dharmaveṣeṇa yadīmaṁ lokasaṁkaram |
abhipatsye śubhaṁ hitvā kriyāvidhivivarjitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
kaś cetayānaḥ puruṣaḥ kāryākāryavicakṣaṇaḥ |
bahu maṁsyati māṁ loke durvr̥ttaṁ lokadūṣaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
kasya yāsyāmy ahaṁ vr̥ttaṁ kena vā svargam āpnuyām |
anayā vartamāno ’haṁ vr̥ttyā hīnapratijñayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
kāmavr̥ttas tv ayaṁ lokaḥ kr̥tsnaḥ samupavartate |
yadvr̥ttāḥ santi rājānas tadvr̥ttāḥ santi hi prajāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
satyam evānr̥śaṁsyaṁ ca rājavr̥ttaṁ sanātanam |
tasmāt satyātmakaṁ rājyaṁ satye lokaḥ pratiṣṭhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
r̥ṣayaś caiva devāś ca satyam eva hi menire |
satyavādī hi loke ’smin paramaṁ gacchati kṣayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
udvijante yathā sarpān narād anr̥tavādinaḥ |
dharmaḥ satyaṁ paro loke mūlaṁ svargasya cocyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
satyam eveśvaro loke satyaṁ padmā samāśritā |
satyamūlāni sarvāṇi satyān nāsti paraṁ padam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
dattam iṣṭaṁ hutaṁ caiva taptāni ca tapāṁsi ca |
vedāḥ satyapratiṣṭhānās tasmāt satyaparo bhavet ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
ekaḥ pālayate lokam ekaḥ pālayate kulam |
majjaty eko hi niraya ekaḥ svarge mahīyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
so ’haṁ pitur nideśaṁ tu kimarthaṁ nānupālaye |
satyapratiśravaḥ satyaṁ satyena samayīkr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
naiva lobhān na mohād vā na cājñānāt tamonvitaḥ |
setuṁ satyasya bhetsyāmi guroḥ satyapratiśravaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
asatyasaṁdhasya sataś calasyāsthiracetasaḥ |
naiva devā na pitaraḥ pratīcchantīti naḥ śrutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
pratyagātmam imaṁ dharmaṁ satyaṁ paśyāmy ahaṁ svayam |
bhāraḥ satpuruṣācīrṇas tad artham abhinandyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
kṣātraṁ dharmam ahaṁ tyakṣye hy adharmaṁ dharmasaṁhitam |
kṣudraur nr̥śaṁsair lubdhaiś ca sevitaṁ pāpakarmabhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
kāyena kurute pāpaṁ manasā saṁpradhārya ca |
anr̥taṁ jihvayā cāha trividhaṁ karma pātakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
bhūmiḥ kīrtir yaśo lakṣmīḥ puruṣaṁ prārthayanti hi |
svargasthaṁ cānubadhnanti satyam eva bhajeta tat ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
śreṣṭhaṁ hy anāryam eva syād yad bhavān avadhārya mām |
āha yuktikarair vākyair idaṁ bhadraṁ kuruṣva ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
kathaṁ hy ahaṁ pratijñāya vanavāsam imaṁ guroḥ |
bharatasya kariṣyāmi vaco hitvā guror vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
sthirā mayā pratijñātā pratijñā gurusaṁnidhau |
prahr̥ṣṭamānasā devī kaikeyī cābhavat tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
vanavāsaṁ vasann evaṁ śucir niyatabhojanaḥ |
mūlaiḥ puṣpaiḥ phalaiḥ puṇyaiḥ pitr̥̄n devāṁś ca tarpayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
saṁtuṣṭapañcavargo ’haṁ lokayātrāṁ pravartaye |
akuhaḥ śraddadhānaḥ san kāryākāryavicakṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
karmabhūmim imāṁ prāpya kartavyaṁ karma yac chubham |
agnir vāyuś ca somaś ca karmaṇāṁ phalabhāginaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
śataṁ kratūnām āhr̥tya devarāṭ tridivaṁ gataḥ |
tapāṁsy ugrāṇi cāsthāya divaṁ yātā maharṣayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
satyaṁ ca dharmaṁ ca parākramaṁ ca; bhūtānukampāṁ priyavāditāṁ ca |
dvijātidevātithipūjanaṁ ca; panthānam āhus tridivasya santaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
dharme ratāḥ satpuruṣaiḥ sametās; tejasvino dānaguṇapradhānāḥ |
ahiṁsakā vītamalāś ca loke; bhavanti pūjyā munayaḥ pradhānāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
