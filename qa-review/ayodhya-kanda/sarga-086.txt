=== Sarga 86 ===

---
1.
tatas tāṁ rajanīm uṣya bharataḥ saparicchadaḥ |
kr̥tātithyo bharadvājaṁ kāmād abhijagāma ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tam r̥ṣiḥ puruṣavyāghraṁ prekṣya prāñjalim āgatam |
hutāgnihotro bharataṁ bharadvājo ’bhyabhāṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
kaccid atra sukhā rātris tavāsmadviṣaye gatā |
samagras te janaḥ kaccid ātithye śaṁsa me ’nagha ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tam uvācāñjaliṁ kr̥tvā bharato ’bhipraṇamya ca |
āśramād abhiniṣkrantam r̥ṣim uttama tejasam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
sukhoṣito ’smi bhagavan samagrabalavāhanaḥ |
tarpitaḥ sarvakāmaiś ca sāmātyo balavat tvayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
apetaklamasaṁtāpāḥ subhakṣyāḥ supratiśrayāḥ |
api preṣyān upādāya sarve sma susukhoṣitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
āmantraye ’haṁ bhagavan kāmaṁ tvām r̥ṣisattama |
samīpaṁ prasthitaṁ bhrātur maireṇekṣasva cakṣuṣā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
āśramaṁ tasya dharmajña dhārmikasya mahātmanaḥ |
ācakṣva katamo mārgaḥ kiyān iti ca śaṁsa me ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
iti pr̥ṣṭas tu bharataṁ bhrātr̥darśanalālasam |
pratyuvāca mahātejā bharadvājo mahātapāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
bharatārdhatr̥tīyeṣu yojaneṣv ajane vane |
citrakūṭo giris tatra ramyanirdarakānanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
uttaraṁ pārśvam āsādya tasya mandākinī nadī |
puṣpitadrumasaṁchannā ramyapuṣpitakānanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
anantaraṁ tat saritaś citrakūṭaś ca parvataḥ |
tato parṇakuṭī tāta tatra tau vasato dhruvam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
dakṣiṇenaiva mārgeṇa savyadakṣiṇam eva ca |
gajavājirathākīrṇāṁ vāhinīṁ vāhinīpate |
vāhayasva mahābhāga tato drakṣyasi rāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
prayāṇam iti ca śrutvā rājarājasya yoṣitaḥ |
hitvā yānāni yānārhā brāhmaṇaṁ paryavārayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
vepamānā kr̥śā dīnā saha devyā sumantriyā |
kausalyā tatra jagrāha karābhyāṁ caraṇau muneḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
asamr̥ddhena kāmena sarvalokasya garhitā |
kaikeyī tasya jagrāha caraṇau savyapatrapā ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
taṁ pradakṣiṇam āgamya bhagavantaṁ mahāmunim |
adūrād bharatasyaiva tasthau dīnamanās tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tataḥ papraccha bharataṁ bharadvājo dr̥ḍhavrataḥ |
viśeṣaṁ jñātum icchāmi mātr̥̄ṇāṁ tava rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
evam uktas tu bharato bharadvājena dhārmikaḥ |
uvāca prāñjalir bhūtvā vākyaṁ vacanakovidaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
yām imāṁ bhagavan dīnāṁ śokān aśanakarśitām |
pitur hi mahiṣīṁ devīṁ devatām iva paśyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
eṣā taṁ puruṣavyāghraṁ siṁhavikrāntagāminam |
kausalyā suṣuve rāmaṁ dhātāram aditir yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
asyā vāmabhujaṁ śliṣṭā yaiṣā tiṣṭhati durmanāḥ |
karṇikārasya śākheva śīrṇapuṣpā vanāntare ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
etasyās tau sutau devyāḥ kumārau devavarṇinau |
ubhau lakṣmaṇaśatrughnau vīrau satyaparākramau ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
yasyāḥ kr̥te naravyāghrau jīvanāśam ito gatau |
rājā putravihīnaś ca svargaṁ daśaratho gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
aiśvaryakāmāṁ kaikeyīm anāryām āryarūpiṇīm |
mamaitāṁ mātaraṁ viddhi nr̥śaṁsāṁ pāpaniścayām |
yatomūlaṁ hi paśyāmi vyasanaṁ mahad ātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
ity uktvā naraśārdūlo bāṣpagadgadayā girā |
sa niśaśvāsa tāmrākṣo kruddho nāga ivāsakr̥t ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
bharadvājo maharṣis taṁ bruvantaṁ bharataṁ tadā |
pratyuvāca mahābuddhir idaṁ vacanam arthavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
na doṣeṇāvagantavyā kaikeyī bharata tvayā |
rāmapravrājanaṁ hy etat sukhodarkaṁ bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
abhivādya tu saṁsiddhaḥ kr̥tvā cainaṁ pradakṣiṇam |
āmantrya bharataḥ sainyaṁ yujyatām ity acodayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
tato vājirathān yuktvā divyān hemapariṣkritān |
adhyārohat prayāṇārthī bahūn bahuvidho janaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
gajakanyāgajāś caiva hemakakṣyāḥ patākinaḥ |
jīmūtā iva gharmānte saghoṣāḥ saṁpratasthire ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
vividhāny api yānāni mahāni ca laghūni ca |
prayayuḥ sumahārhāṇi pādair eva padātayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
atha yānapravekais tu kausalyāpramukhāḥ striyaḥ |
rāmadarśanakāṅkṣiṇyaḥ prayayur muditās tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
sa cārkataruṇābhāsāṁ niyuktāṁ śibikāṁ śubhām |
āsthāya prayayau śrīmān bharataḥ saparicchadaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
sā prayātā mahāsenā gajavājirathākulā |
dakṣiṇāṁ diśam āvr̥tya mahāmegha ivotthitaḥ |
vanāni tu vyatikramya juṣṭāni mr̥gapakṣibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
sā saṁprahr̥ṣṭadvipavājiyodhā; vitrāsayantī mr̥gapakṣisaṁghān |
mahad vanaṁ tat pravigāhamānā; rarāja senā bharatasya tatra ||

=== meaning ===

---
