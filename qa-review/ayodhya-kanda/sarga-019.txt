=== Sarga 19 ===

---
1.
atha taṁ vyathayā dīnaṁ saviśeṣam amarṣitam |
śvasantam iva nāgendraṁ roṣavisphāritekṣaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
āsādya rāmaḥ saumitriṁ suhr̥daṁ bhrātaraṁ priyam |
uvācedaṁ sa dhairyeṇa dhārayan sattvam ātmavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
saumitre yo ’bhiṣekārthe mama saṁbhārasaṁbhramaḥ |
abhiṣekanivr̥ttyarthe so ’stu saṁbhārasaṁbhramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
yasyā madabhiṣekārthaṁ mānasaṁ paritapyate |
mātā naḥ sā yathā na syāt saviśaṅkā tathā kuru ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tasyāḥ śaṅkāmayaṁ duḥkhaṁ muhūrtam api notsahe |
manasi pratisaṁjātaṁ saumitre ’ham upekṣitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
na buddhipūrvaṁ nābuddhaṁ smarāmīha kadā cana |
mātr̥̄ṇāṁ vā pitur vāhaṁ kr̥tam alpaṁ ca vipriyam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
satyaḥ satyābhisaṁdhaś ca nityaṁ satyaparākramaḥ |
paralokabhayād bhīto nirbhayo ’stu pitā mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tasyāpi hi bhaved asmin karmaṇy apratisaṁhr̥te |
satyaṁ neti manastāpas tasya tāpas tapec ca mām ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
abhiṣekavidhānaṁ tu tasmāt saṁhr̥tya lakṣmaṇa |
anvag evāham icchāmi vanaṁ gantum itaḥ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
mama pravrājanād adya kr̥takr̥tyā nr̥pātmajā |
sutaṁ bharatam avyagram abhiṣecayitā tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
mayi cīrājinadhare jaṭāmaṇḍaladhāriṇi |
gate ’raṇyaṁ ca kaikeyyā bhaviṣyati manaḥsukham ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
buddhiḥ praṇītā yeneyaṁ manaś ca susamāhitam |
tat tu nārhāmi saṁkleṣṭuṁ pravrajiṣyāmi māciram ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
kr̥tāntas tv eva saumitre draṣṭavyo matpravāsane |
rājyasya ca vitīrṇasya punar eva nivartane ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
kaikeyyāḥ pratipattir hi kathaṁ syān mama pīḍane |
yadi bhāvo na daivo ’yaṁ kr̥tāntavihito bhavet ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
jānāsi hi yathā saumya na mātr̥ṣu mamāntaram |
bhūtapūrvaṁ viśeṣo vā tasyā mayi sute ’pi vā ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
so ’bhiṣekanivr̥ttyarthaiḥ pravāsārthaiś ca durvacaiḥ |
ugrair vākyair ahaṁ tasyā nānyad daivāt samarthaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
kathaṁ prakr̥tisaṁpannā rājaputrī tathāguṇā |
brūyāt sā prākr̥teva strī matpīḍāṁ bhartr̥saṁnidhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
yad acintyaṁ tu tad daivaṁ bhūteṣv api na hanyate |
vyaktaṁ mayi ca tasyāṁ ca patito hi viparyayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
kaś cid daivena saumitre yoddhum utsahate pumān |
yasya na grahaṇaṁ kiṁ cit karmaṇo ’nyatra dr̥śyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
sukhaduḥkhe bhayakrodhau lābhālābhau bhavābhavau |
yasya kiṁ cit tathābhūtaṁ nanu daivasya karma tat ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
vyāhate ’py abhiṣeke me paritāpo na vidyate |
tasmād aparitāpaḥ saṁs tvam apy anuvidhāya mām |
pratisaṁhāraya kṣipram ābhiṣecanikīṁ kriyām ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
na lakṣmaṇāsmin mama rājyavighne; mātā yavīyasy atiśaṅkanīyā |
daivābhipannā hi vadanty aniṣṭaṁ; jānāsi daivaṁ ca tathāprabhāvam ||

=== meaning ===

---
