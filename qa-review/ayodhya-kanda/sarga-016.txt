=== Sarga 16 ===

---
1.
sa dadarśāsane rāmo niṣaṇṇaṁ pitaraṁ śubhe |
kaikeyīsahitaṁ dīnaṁ mukhena pariśuṣyatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sa pituś caraṇau pūrvam abhivādya vinītavat |
tato vavande caraṇau kaikeyyāḥ susamāhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
rāmety uktvā ca vacanaṁ vāṣpaparyākulekṣaṇaḥ |
śaśāka nr̥patir dīno nekṣituṁ nābhibhāṣitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tad apūrvaṁ narapater dr̥ṣṭvā rūpaṁ bhayāvaham |
rāmo ’pi bhayam āpannaḥ padā spr̥ṣṭveva pannagam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
indriyair aprahr̥ṣṭais taṁ śokasaṁtāpakarśitam |
niḥśvasantaṁ mahārājaṁ vyathitākulacetasam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
ūrmi mālinam akṣobhyaṁ kṣubhyantam iva sāgaram |
upaplutam ivādityam uktānr̥tam r̥ṣiṁ yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
acintyakalpaṁ hi pitus taṁ śokam upadhārayan |
babhūva saṁrabdhataraḥ samudra iva parvaṇi ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
cintayām āsa ca tadā rāmaḥ pitr̥hite rataḥ |
kiṁsvid adyaiva nr̥patir na māṁ pratyabhinandati ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
anyadā māṁ pitā dr̥ṣṭvā kupito ’pi prasīdati |
tasya mām adya saṁprekṣya kimāyāsaḥ pravartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
sa dīna iva śokārto viṣaṇṇavadanadyutiḥ |
kaikeyīm abhivādyaiva rāmo vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
kaccin mayā nāparāddham ajñānād yena me pitā |
kupitas tan mamācakṣva tvaṁ caivainaṁ prasādaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
vivarṇavadano dīno na hi mām abhibhāṣate |
śārīro mānaso vāpi kaccid enaṁ na bādhate |
saṁtāpo vābhitāpo vā durlabhaṁ hi sadā sukham ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
kaccin na kiṁ cid bharate kumāre priyadarśane |
śatrughne vā mahāsattve mātr̥̄ṇāṁ vā mamāśubham ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
atoṣayan mahārājam akurvan vā pitur vacaḥ |
muhūrtam api neccheyaṁ jīvituṁ kupite nr̥pe ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
yatomūlaṁ naraḥ paśyet prādurbhāvam ihātmanaḥ |
kathaṁ tasmin na varteta pratyakṣe sati daivate ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
kaccit te paruṣaṁ kiṁ cid abhimānāt pitā mama |
ukto bhavatyā kopena yatrāsya lulitaṁ manaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
etad ācakṣva me devi tattvena paripr̥cchataḥ |
kiṁnimittam apūrvo ’yaṁ vikāro manujādhipe ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
ahaṁ hi vacanād rājñaḥ pateyam api pāvake |
bhakṣayeyaṁ viṣaṁ tīkṣṇaṁ majjeyam api cārṇave |
niyukto guruṇā pitrā nr̥peṇa ca hitena ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tad brūhi vacanaṁ devi rājño yad abhikāṅkṣitam |
kariṣye pratijāne ca rāmo dvir nābhibhāṣate ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tam ārjavasamāyuktam anāryā satyavādinam |
uvāca rāmaṁ kaikeyī vacanaṁ bhr̥śadāruṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
purā devāsure yuddhe pitrā te mama rāghava |
rakṣitena varau dattau saśalyena mahāraṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tatra me yācito rājā bharatasyābhiṣecanam |
gamanaṁ daṇḍakāraṇye tava cādyaiva rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
yadi satyapratijñaṁ tvaṁ pitaraṁ kartum icchasi |
ātmānaṁ ca narareṣṭha mama vākyam idaṁ śr̥ṇu ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
sa nideśe pitus tiṣṭha yathā tena pratiśrutam |
tvayāraṇyaṁ praveṣṭavyaṁ nava varṣāṇi pañca ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
sapta sapta ca varṣāṇi daṇḍakāraṇyam āśritaḥ |
abhiṣekam imaṁ tyaktvā jaṭācīradharo vasa ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
bharataḥ kosalapure praśāstu vasudhām imām |
nānāratnasamākīrṇāṁ savājirathakuñjarām ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tad apriyam amitraghno vacanaṁ maraṇopamam |
śrutvā na vivyathe rāmaḥ kaikeyīṁ cedam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
evam astu gamiṣyāmi vanaṁ vastum ahaṁ tv ataḥ |
jaṭācīradharo rājñaḥ pratijñām anupālayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
idaṁ tu jñātum icchāmi kimarthaṁ māṁ mahīpatiḥ |
nābhinandati durdharṣo yathāpuram ariṁdamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
manyur na ca tvayā kāryo devi brūhi tavāgrataḥ |
yāsyāmi bhava suprītā vanaṁ cīrajaṭādharaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
hitena guruṇā pitrā kr̥tajñena nr̥peṇa ca |
niyujyamāno viśrabdhaṁ kiṁ na kuryād ahaṁ priyam ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
alīkaṁ mānasaṁ tv ekaṁ hr̥dayaṁ dahatīva me |
svayaṁ yan nāha māṁ rājā bharatasyābhiṣecanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
ahaṁ hi sītāṁ rājyaṁ ca prāṇān iṣṭān dhanāni ca |
hr̥ṣṭo bhrātre svayaṁ dadyāṁ bharatāyāpracoditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
kiṁ punar manujendreṇa svayaṁ pitrā pracoditaḥ |
tava ca priyakāmārthaṁ pratijñām anupālayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
tad āśvāsaya hīmaṁ tvaṁ kiṁ nv idaṁ yan mahīpatiḥ |
vasudhāsaktanayano mandam aśrūṇi muñcati ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
gacchantu caivānayituṁ dūtāḥ śīghrajavair hayaiḥ |
bharataṁ mātulakulād adyaiva nr̥paśāsanāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
daṇḍakāraṇyam eṣo ’ham ito gacchāmi satvaraḥ |
avicārya pitur vākyaṁ samāvastuṁ caturdaśa ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
sā hr̥ṣṭā tasya tadvākyaṁ śrutvā rāmasya kaikayī |
prasthānaṁ śraddadhānā hi tvarayām āsa rāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
evaṁ bhavatu yāsyanti dūtāḥ śīghrajavair hayaiḥ |
bharataṁ mātulakulād upāvartayituṁ narāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
tava tv ahaṁ kṣamaṁ manye notsukasya vilambanam |
rāma tasmād itaḥ śīghraṁ vanaṁ tvaṁ gantum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
vrīḍānvitaḥ svayaṁ yac ca nr̥pas tvāṁ nābhibhāṣate |
naitat kiṁ cin naraśreṣṭha manyur eṣo ’panīyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
yāvat tvaṁ na vanaṁ yātaḥ purād asmād abhitvaran |
pitā tāvan na te rāma snāsyate bhokṣyate ’pi vā ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
dhik kaṣṭam iti niḥśvasya rājā śokapariplutaḥ |
mūrchito nyapatat tasmin paryaṅke hemabhūṣite ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
rāmo ’py utthāpya rājānaṁ kaikeyyābhipracoditaḥ |
kaśayevāhato vājī vanaṁ gantuṁ kr̥tatvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
tad apriyam anāryāyā vacanaṁ dāruṇodaram |
śrutvā gatavyatho rāmaḥ kaikeyīṁ vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
nāham arthaparo devi lokam āvastum utsahe |
viddhi mām r̥ṣibhis tulyaṁ kevalaṁ dharmam āsthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
yad atrabhavataḥ kiṁ cic chakyaṁ kartuṁ priyaṁ mayā |
prāṇān api parityajya sarvathā kr̥tam eva tat ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
na hy ato dharmacaraṇaṁ kiṁ cid asti mahattaram |
yathā pitari śuśrūṣā tasya vā vacanakriyā ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
anukto ’py atrabhavatā bhavatyā vacanād aham |
vane vatsyāmi vijane varṣāṇīha caturdaśa ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
na nūnaṁ mayi kaikeyi kiṁ cid āśaṁsase guṇam |
yad rājānam avocas tvaṁ mameśvaratarā satī ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
yāvan mātaram āpr̥cche sītāṁ cānunayāmy aham |
tato ’dyaiva gamiṣyāmi daṇḍakānāṁ mahad vanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
52.
bharataḥ pālayed rājyaṁ śuśrūṣec ca pitur yathā |
tathā bhavatyā kartavyaṁ sa hi dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
53.
sa rāmasya vacaḥ śrutvā bhr̥śaṁ duḥkhahataḥ pitā |
śokād aśaknuvan bāṣpaṁ praruroda mahāsvanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
54.
vanditvā caraṇau rāmo visaṁjñasya pitus tadā |
kaikeyyāś cāpy anāryāyā niṣpapāta mahādyutiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
55.
sa rāmaḥ pitaraṁ kr̥tvā kaikeyīṁ ca pradakṣiṇam |
niṣkramyāntaḥpurāt tasmāt svaṁ dadarśa suhr̥jjanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
56.
taṁ bāṣpaparipūrṇākṣaḥ pr̥ṣṭhato ’nujagāma ha |
lakṣmaṇaḥ paramakruddhaḥ sumitrānandavardhanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
57.
ābhiṣecanikaṁ bhāṇḍaṁ kr̥tvā rāmaḥ pradakṣiṇam |
śanair jagāma sāpekṣo dr̥ṣṭiṁ tatrāvicālayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
58.
na cāsya mahatīṁ lakṣmīṁ rājyanāśo ’pakarṣati |
lokakāntasya kāntatvaṁ śītaraśmer iva kṣapā ||

=== meaning ===

---
59.
na vanaṁ gantukāmasya tyajataś ca vasuṁdharām |
sarvalokātigasyeva lakṣyate cittavikriyā ||

=== meaning ===

---
60.
dhārayan manasā duḥkham indriyāṇi nigr̥hya ca |
praviveśātmavān veśma māturapriyaśaṁsivān ||

=== meaning ===

---
61.
praviśya veśmātibhr̥śaṁ mudānvitaṁ; samīkṣya tāṁ cārthavipattim āgatām |
na caiva rāmo ’tra jagāma vikriyāṁ; suhr̥jjanasyātmavipattiśaṅkayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
