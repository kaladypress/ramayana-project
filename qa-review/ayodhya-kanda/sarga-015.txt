=== Sarga 15 ===

---
1.
sa rāmo ratham āsthāya saṁprahr̥ṣṭasuhr̥jjanaḥ |
apaśyan nagaraṁ śrīmān nānājanasamākulam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sa gr̥hair abhrasaṁkāśaiḥ pāṇḍurair upaśobhitam |
rājamārgaṁ yayau rāmo madhyenāgarudhūpitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
śobhamānam asaṁbādhaṁ taṁ rājapatham uttamam |
saṁvr̥taṁ vividhaiḥ paṇyair bhakṣyair uccāvacair api ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
āśīrvādān bahūñ śr̥ṇvan suhr̥dbhiḥ samudīritān |
yathārhaṁ cāpi saṁpūjya sarvān eva narān yayau ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
pitāmahair ācaritaṁ tathaiva prapitāmahaiḥ |
adyopādāya taṁ mārgam abhiṣikto ’nupālaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
yathā sma lālitāḥ pitrā yathā pūrvaiḥ pitāmahaiḥ |
tataḥ sukhataraṁ sarve rāme vatsyāma rājani ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
alam adya hi bhuktena paramārthair alaṁ ca naḥ |
yathā paśyāma niryāntaṁ rāmaṁ rājye pratiṣṭhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
ato hi na priyataraṁ nānyat kiṁ cid bhaviṣyati |
yathābhiṣeko rāmasya rājyenāmitatejasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
etāś cānyāś ca suhr̥dām udāsīnaḥ kathāḥ śubhāḥ |
ātmasaṁpūjanīḥ śr̥ṇvan yayau rāmo mahāpatham ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
na hi tasmān manaḥ kaś cic cakṣuṣī vā narottamāt |
naraḥ śaknoty apākraṣṭum atikrānte ’pi rāghave ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sarveṣāṁ sa hi dharmātmā varṇānāṁ kurute dayām |
caturṇāṁ hi vayaḥsthānāṁ tena te tam anuvratāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sa rājakulam āsādya mahendrabhavanopamam |
rājaputraḥ pitur veśma praviveśa śriyā jvalan ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sa sarvāḥ samatikramya kakṣyā daśarathātmajaḥ |
saṁnivartya janaṁ sarvaṁ śuddhāntaḥpuram abhyagāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tataḥ praviṣṭe pitur antikaṁ tadā; janaḥ sa sarvo mudito nr̥pātmaje |
pratīkṣate tasya punaḥ sma nirgamaṁ; yathodayaṁ candramasaḥ saritpatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
