=== Sarga 27 ===

---
1.
sāntvyamānā tu rāmeṇa maithilī janakātmajā |
vanavāsanimittāya bhartāram idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sā tam uttamasaṁvignā sītā vipulavakṣasam |
praṇayāc cābhimānāc ca paricikṣepa rāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
kiṁ tvāmanyata vaidehaḥ pitā me mithilādhipaḥ |
rāma jāmātaraṁ prāpya striyaṁ puruṣavigraham ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
anr̥taṁ balaloko ’yam ajñānād yad dhi vakṣyati |
tejo nāsti paraṁ rāme tapatīva divākare ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
kiṁ hi kr̥tvā viṣaṇṇas tvaṁ kuto vā bhayam asti te |
yat parityaktukāmas tvaṁ mām ananyaparāyaṇām ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
dyumatsenasutaṁ vīra satyavantam anuvratām |
sāvitrīm iva māṁ viddhi tvam ātmavaśavartinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
na tv ahaṁ manasāpy anyaṁ draṣṭāsmi tvadr̥te ’nagha |
tvayā rāghava gaccheyaṁ yathānyā kulapāṁsanī ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
svayaṁ tu bhāryāṁ kaumārīṁ ciram adhyuṣitāṁ satīm |
śailūṣa iva māṁ rāma parebhyo dātum icchasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sa mām anādāya vanaṁ na tvaṁ prasthātum arhasi |
tapo vā yadi vāraṇyaṁ svargo vā syāt saha tvayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
na ca me bhavitā tatra kaś cit pathi pariśramaḥ |
pr̥ṣṭhatas tava gacchantyā vihāraśayaneṣv api ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
kuśakāśaśareṣīkā ye ca kaṇṭakino drumāḥ |
tūlājinasamasparśā mārge mama saha tvayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
mahāvātasamuddhūtaṁ yan mām avakariṣyati |
rajo ramaṇa tan manye parārdhyam iva candanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
śādvaleṣu yad āsiṣye vanānte vanagocarā |
kuthāstaraṇatalpeṣu kiṁ syāt sukhataraṁ tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
patraṁ mūlaṁ phalaṁ yat tvam alpaṁ vā yadi vā bahu |
dāsyasi svayam āhr̥tya tan me ’mr̥tarasopamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
na mātur na pitus tatra smariṣyāmi na veśmanaḥ |
ārtavāny upabhuñjānā puṣpāṇi ca phalāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
na ca tatra gataḥ kiṁ cid draṣṭum arhasi vipriyam |
matkr̥te na ca te śoko na bhaviṣyāmi durbharā ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
yas tvayā saha sa svargo nirayo yas tvayā vinā |
iti jānan parāṁ prītiṁ gaccha rāma mayā saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
atha mām evam avyagrāṁ vanaṁ naiva nayiṣyasi |
viṣam adyaiva pāsyāmi mā viśaṁ dviṣatāṁ vaśam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
paścād api hi duḥkhena mama naivāsti jīvitam |
ujjhitāyās tvayā nātha tadaiva maraṇaṁ varam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
idaṁ hi sahituṁ śokaṁ muhūrtam api notsahe |
kiṁ punar daśavarṣāṇi trīṇi caikaṁ ca duḥkhitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
iti sā śokasaṁtaptā vilapya karuṇaṁ bahu |
cukrośa patim āyastā bhr̥śam āliṅgya sasvaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
sā viddhā bahubhir vākyair digdhair iva gajāṅganā |
cira saṁniyataṁ bāṣpaṁ mumocāgnim ivāraṇiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tasyāḥ sphaṭikasaṁkāśaṁ vāri saṁtāpasaṁbhavam |
netrābhyāṁ parisusrāva paṅkajābhyām ivodakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tāṁ pariṣvajya bāhubhyāṁ visaṁjñām iva duḥkhitām |
uvāca vacanaṁ rāmaḥ pariviśvāsayaṁs tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
na devi tava duḥkhena svargam apy abhirocaye |
na hi me ’sti bhayaṁ kiṁ cit svayambhor iva sarvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tava sarvam abhiprāyam avijñāya śubhānane |
vāsaṁ na rocaye ’raṇye śaktimān api rakṣaṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
yat sr̥ṣṭāsi mayā sārdhaṁ vanavāsāya maithili |
na vihātuṁ mayā śakyā kīrtir ātmavatā yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
dharmas tu gajanāsoru sadbhir ācaritaḥ purā |
taṁ cāham anuvarte ’dya yathā sūryaṁ suvarcalā ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
eṣa dharmas tu suśroṇi pitur mātuś ca vaśyatā |
ataś cājñāṁ vyatikramya nāhaṁ jīvitum utsahe ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
sa māṁ pitā yathā śāsti satyadharmapathe sthitaḥ |
tathā vartitum icchāmi sa hi dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ |
anugacchasva māṁ bhīru sahadharmacarī bhava ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
brāhmaṇebhyaś ca ratnāni bhikṣukebhyaś ca bhojanam |
dehi cāśaṁsamānebhyaḥ saṁtvarasva ca māciram ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
anukūlaṁ tu sā bhartur jñātvā gamanam ātmanaḥ |
kṣipraṁ pramuditā devī dātum evopacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
tataḥ prahr̥ṣṭā paripūrṇamānasā; yaśasvinī bhartur avekṣya bhāṣitam |
dhanāni ratnāni ca dātum aṅganā; pracakrame dharmabhr̥tāṁ manasvinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
