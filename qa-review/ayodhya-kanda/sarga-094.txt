=== Sarga 94 ===

---
1.
āghrāya rāmas taṁ mūrdhni pariṣvajya ca rāghavaḥ |
aṅke bharatam āropya paryapr̥cchat samāhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
kva nu te ’bhūt pitā tāta yad araṇyaṁ tvam āgataḥ |
na hi tvaṁ jīvatas tasya vanam āgantum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
cirasya bata paśyāmi dūrād bharatam āgatam |
duṣpratīkam araṇye ’smin kiṁ tāta vanam āgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
kaccid daśaratho rājā kuśalī satyasaṁgaraḥ |
rājasūyāśvamedhānām āhartā dharmaniścayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
sa kaccid brāhmaṇo vidvān dharmanityo mahādyutiḥ |
ikṣvākūṇām upādhyāyo yathāvat tāta pūjyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tāta kaccic ca kausalyā sumitrā ca prajāvatī |
sukhinī kaccid āryā ca devī nandati kaikayī ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
kaccid vinaya saṁpannaḥ kulaputro bahuśrutaḥ |
anasūyur anudraṣṭā satkr̥tas te purohitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
kaccid agniṣu te yukto vidhijño matimān r̥juḥ |
hutaṁ ca hoṣyamāṇaṁ ca kāle vedayate sadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
iṣvastravarasaṁpannam arthaśāstraviśāradam |
sudhanvānam upādhyāyaṁ kaccit tvaṁ tāta manyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
kaccid ātma samāḥ śūrāḥ śrutavanto jitendriyāḥ |
kulīnāś ceṅgitajñāś ca kr̥tās te tāta mantriṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
mantro vijayamūlaṁ hi rājñāṁ bhavati rāghava |
susaṁvr̥to mantradharair amātyaiḥ śāstrakovidaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
kaccin nidrāvaśaṁ naiṣi kaccit kāle vibudhyase |
kac ciṁś cāpararātriṣu cintayasy arthanaipuṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
kaccin mantrayase naikaḥ kaccin na bahubhiḥ saha |
kaccit te mantrito mantro rāṣṭraṁ na paridhāvati ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
kaccid arthaṁ viniścitya laghumūlaṁ mahodayam |
kṣipram ārabhase kartuṁ na dīrghayasi rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
kaccit tu sukr̥tāny eva kr̥tarūpāṇi vā punaḥ |
vidus te sarvakāryāṇi na kartavyāni pārthivāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
kaccin na tarkair yuktvā vā ye cāpy aparikīrtitāḥ |
tvayā vā tava vāmātyair budhyate tāta mantritam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
kaccit sahasrān mūrkhāṇām ekam icchasi paṇḍitam |
paṇḍito hy arthakr̥cchreṣu kuryān niḥśreyasaṁ mahat ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
sahasrāṇy api mūrkhāṇāṁ yady upāste mahīpatiḥ |
atha vāpy ayutāny eva nāsti teṣu sahāyatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
eko ’py amātyo medhāvī śūro dakṣo vicakṣaṇaḥ |
rājānaṁ rājamātraṁ vā prāpayen mahatīṁ śriyam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
kaccin mukhyā mahatsv eva madhyameṣu ca madhyamāḥ |
jaghanyāś ca jaghanyeṣu bhr̥tyāḥ karmasu yojitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
amātyān upadhātītān pitr̥paitāmahāñ śucīn |
śreṣṭhāñ śreṣṭheṣu kaccit tvaṁ niyojayasi karmasu ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
kaccit tvāṁ nāvajānanti yājakāḥ patitaṁ yathā |
ugrapratigrahītāraṁ kāmayānam iva striyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
upāyakuśalaṁ vaidyaṁ bhr̥tyasaṁdūṣaṇe ratam |
śūram aiśvaryakāmaṁ ca yo na hanti sa vadhyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
kaccid dhr̥ṣṭaś ca śūraś ca dhr̥timān matimāñ śuciḥ |
kulīnaś cānuraktaś ca dakṣaḥ senāpatiḥ kr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
balavantaś ca kaccit te mukhyā yuddhaviśāradāḥ |
dr̥ṣṭāpadānā vikrāntās tvayā satkr̥tya mānitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
ka cid balasya bhaktaṁ ca vetanaṁ ca yathocitam |
saṁprāptakālaṁ dātavyaṁ dadāsi na vilambase ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
kālātikramaṇe hy eva bhakta vetanayor bhr̥tāḥ |
bhartuḥ kupyanti duṣyanti so ’narthaḥ sumahān smr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
kaccit sarve ’nuraktās tvāṁ kulaputrāḥ pradhānataḥ |
kaccit prāṇāṁs tavārtheṣu saṁtyajanti samāhitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
kaccij jānapado vidvān dakṣiṇaḥ pratibhānavān |
yathoktavādī dūtas te kr̥to bharata paṇḍitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
kaccid aṣṭādaśāny eṣu svapakṣe daśa pañca ca |
tribhis tribhir avijñātair vetsi tīrthāni cārakaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
kaccid vyapāstān ahitān pratiyātāṁś ca sarvadā |
durbalān anavajñāya vartase ripusūdana ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
kaccin na lokāyatikān brāhmaṇāṁs tāta sevase |
anartha kuśalā hy ete bālāḥ paṇḍitamāninaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
dharmaśāstreṣu mukhyeṣu vidyamāneṣu durbudhāḥ |
buddhimān vīkṣikīṁ prāpya nirarthaṁ pravadanti te ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
vīrair adhyuṣitāṁ pūrvam asmākaṁ tāta pūrvakaiḥ |
satyanāmāṁ dr̥ḍhadvārāṁ hastyaśvarathasaṁkulām ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
brāhmaṇaiḥ kṣatriyair vaiśyaiḥ svakarmanirataiḥ sadā |
jitendriyair mahotsāhair vr̥tāmātyaiḥ sahasraśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
prāsādair vividhākārair vr̥tāṁ vaidyajanākulām |
kaccit samuditāṁ sphītām ayodhyāṁ parirakṣasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
kaccic caityaśatair juṣṭaḥ suniviṣṭajanākulaḥ |
devasthānaiḥ prapābhiś ca taḍāgaiś copaśobhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
prahr̥ṣṭanaranārīkaḥ samājotsavaśobhitaḥ |
sukr̥ṣṭasīmā paśumān hiṁsābhir abhivarjitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
adevamātr̥ko ramyaḥ śvāpadaiḥ parivarjitaḥ |
kaccij janapadaḥ sphītaḥ sukhaṁ vasati rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
kaccit te dayitāḥ sarve kr̥ṣigorakṣajīvinaḥ |
vārtāyāṁ saṁśritas tāta loko hi sukham edhate ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
teṣāṁ guptiparīhāraiḥ kaccit te bharaṇaṁ kr̥tam |
rakṣyā hi rājñā dharmeṇa sarve viṣayavāsinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
kaccit striyaḥ sāntvayasi kaccit tāś ca surakṣitāḥ |
kaccin na śraddadhāsyāsāṁ kaccid guhyaṁ na bhāṣase ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
kaccin nāgavanaṁ guptaṁ kuñjarāṇāṁ ca tr̥pyasi |
kaccid darśayase nityaṁ manuṣyāṇāṁ vibhūṣitam |
utthāyotthāya pūrvāhṇe rājaputro mahāpathe ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
kaccit sarvāṇi durgāṇi dhanadhānyāyudhodakaiḥ |
yantraiś ca paripūrṇāni tathā śilpidhanurdharaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
āyas te vipulaḥ kaccit kaccid alpataro vyayaḥ |
apātreṣu na te kaccit kośo gacchati rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
devatārthe ca pitrarthe brāhmaṇābhyāgateṣu ca |
yodheṣu mitravargeṣu kaccid gacchati te vyayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
kaccid āryo viśuddhātmā kṣāritaś corakarmaṇā |
apr̥ṣṭaḥ śāstrakuśalair na lobhād badhyate śuciḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
gr̥hītaś caiva pr̥ṣṭaś ca kāle dr̥ṣṭaḥ sakāraṇaḥ |
kaccin na mucyate coro dhanalobhān nararṣabha ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
vyasane kaccid āḍhyasya dugatasya ca rāghava |
arthaṁ virāgāḥ paśyanti tavāmātyā bahuśrutāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
yāni mithyābhiśastānāṁ patanty asrāṇi rāghava |
tāni putrapaśūn ghnanti prītyartham anuśāsataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
kaccid vr̥dhāṁś ca bālāṁś ca vaidyamukhyāṁś ca rāghava |
dānena manasā vācā tribhir etair bubhūṣase ||

=== meaning ===

---
52.
kaccid gurūṁś ca vr̥ddhāṁś ca tāpasān devatātithīn |
caityāṁś ca sarvān siddhārthān brāhmaṇāṁś ca namasyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
53.
kaccid arthena vā dharmaṁ dharmaṁ dharmeṇa vā punaḥ |
ubhau vā prītilobhena kāmena na vibādhase ||

=== meaning ===

---
54.
kaccid arthaṁ ca dharmaṁ ca kāmaṁ ca jayatāṁ vara |
vibhajya kāle kālajña sarvān bharata sevase ||

=== meaning ===

---
55.
kaccit te brāhmaṇāḥ śarma sarvaśāstrārthakovidaḥ |
āśaṁsante mahāprājña paurajānapadaiḥ saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
56.
nāstikyam anr̥taṁ krodhaṁ pramādaṁ dīrghasūtratām |
adarśanaṁ jñānavatām ālasyaṁ pañcavr̥ttitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
57.
ekacintanam arthānām anarthajñaiś ca mantraṇam |
niścitānām anārambhaṁ mantrasyāparilakṣaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
58.
maṅgalasyāprayogaṁ ca pratyutthānaṁ ca sarvaśaḥ |
kaccit tvaṁ varjayasy etān rājadoṣāṁś caturdaśa ||

=== meaning ===

---
59.
kaccit svādukr̥taṁ bhojyam eko nāśnāsi rāghava |
kaccid āśaṁsamānebhyo mitrebhyaḥ saṁprayacchasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
