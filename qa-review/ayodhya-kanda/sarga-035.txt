=== Sarga 35 ===

---
1.
atha rāmaś ca sītā ca lakṣmaṇaś ca kr̥tāñjaliḥ |
upasaṁgr̥hya rājānaṁ cakrur dīnāḥ pradakṣiṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
taṁ cāpi samanujñāpya dharmajñaḥ sītayā saha |
rāghavaḥ śokasaṁmūḍho jananīm abhyavādayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
anvakṣaṁ lakṣmaṇo bhrātuḥ kausalyām abhyavādayat |
atha mātuḥ sumitrāyā jagrāha caraṇau punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
taṁ vandamānaṁ rudatī mātā saumitrim abravīt |
hitakāmā mahābāhuṁ mūrdhny upāghrāya lakṣmaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
sr̥ṣṭas tvaṁ vanavāsāya svanuraktaḥ suhr̥jjane |
rāme pramādaṁ mā kārṣīḥ putra bhrātari gacchati ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
vyasanī vā samr̥ddho vā gatir eṣa tavānagha |
eṣa loke satāṁ dharmo yaj jyeṣṭhavaśago bhavet ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
idaṁ hi vr̥ttam ucitaṁ kulasyāsya sanātanam |
dānaṁ dīkṣā ca yajñeṣu tanutyāgo mr̥dheṣu ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
rāmaṁ daśarathaṁ viddhi māṁ viddhi janakātmajām |
ayodhyām aṭavīṁ viddhi gaccha tāta yathāsukham ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tataḥ sumantraḥ kākutsthaṁ prāñjalir vākyam abravīt |
vinīto vinayajñaś ca mātalir vāsavaṁ yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
ratham āroha bhadraṁ te rājaputra mahāyaśaḥ |
kṣipraṁ tvāṁ prāpayiṣyāmi yatra māṁ rāma vakṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
caturdaśa hi varṣāṇi vastavyāni vane tvayā |
tāny upakramitavyāni yāni devyāsi coditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
taṁ rathaṁ sūryasaṁkāśaṁ sītā hr̥ṣṭena cetasā |
āruroha varārohā kr̥tvālaṁkāram ātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tathaivāyudhajātāni bhrātr̥bhyāṁ kavacāni ca |
rathopasthe pratinyasya sacarmakaṭhinaṁ ca tat ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sītātr̥tīyān ārūḍhān dr̥ṣṭvā dhr̥ṣṭam acodayat |
sumantraḥ saṁmatān aśvān vāyuvegasamāñ jave ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
prayāte tu mahāraṇyaṁ cirarātrāya rāghave |
babhūva nagare mūrcchā balamūrcchā janasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tat samākulasaṁbhrāntaṁ mattasaṁkupita dvipam |
hayaśiñjitanirghoṣaṁ puram āsīn mahāsvanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tataḥ sabālavr̥ddhā sā purī paramapīḍitā |
rāmam evābhidudrāva gharmārtaḥ salilaṁ yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
pārśvataḥ pr̥ṣṭhataś cāpi lambamānās tadunmukhāḥ |
bāṣpapūrṇamukhāḥ sarve tam ūcur bhr̥śaduḥkhitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
saṁyaccha vājināṁ raśmīn sūta yāhi śanaiḥ śanaiḥ |
mukhaṁ drakṣyāmi rāmasya durdarśaṁ no bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
āyasaṁ hr̥dayaṁ nūnaṁ rāmamātur asaṁśayam |
yad devagarbhapratime vanaṁ yāti na bhidyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
kr̥takr̥tyā hi vaidehī chāyevānugatā patim |
na jahāti ratā dharme merum arkaprabhā yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
aho lakṣmaṇa siddhārthaḥ satatāṁ priyavādinam |
bhrātaraṁ devasaṁkāśaṁ yas tvaṁ paricariṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
mahaty eṣā hi te siddhir eṣa cābhyudayo mahān |
eṣa svargasya mārgaś ca yad enam anugacchasi |
evaṁ vadantas te soḍhuṁ na śekur bāṣpam āgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
atha rājā vr̥taḥ strībhir dīnābhir dīnacetanaḥ |
nirjagāma priyaṁ putraṁ drakṣyāmīti bruvan gr̥hāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
śuśruve cāgrataḥ strīṇāṁ rudantīnāṁ mahāsvanaḥ |
yathā nādaḥ kareṇūnāṁ baddhe mahati kuñjare ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
pitā ca rājā kākutsthaḥ śrīmān sannas tadā babhau |
paripūrṇaḥ śaśī kāle graheṇopapluto yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tato halahalāśabdo jajñe rāmasya pr̥ṣṭhataḥ |
narāṇāṁ prekṣya rājānaṁ sīdantaṁ bhr̥śaduḥkhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
hā rāmeti janāḥ ke cid rāmamāteti cāpare |
antaḥpuraṁ samr̥ddhaṁ ca krośantaṁ paryadevayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
anvīkṣamāṇo rāmas tu viṣaṇṇaṁ bhrāntacetasam |
rājānaṁ mātaraṁ caiva dadarśānugatau pathi |
dharmapāśena saṁkṣiptaḥ prakāśaṁ nābhyudaikṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
padātinau ca yānārhāv aduḥkhārhau sukhocitau |
dr̥ṣṭvā saṁcodayām āsa śīghraṁ yāhīti sārathim ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
na hi tat puruṣavyāghro duḥkhadaṁ darśanaṁ pituḥ |
mātuś ca sahituṁ śaktas totrārdita iva dvipaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
tathā rudantīṁ kausalyāṁ rathaṁ tam anudhāvatīm |
krośantīṁ rāma rāmeti hā sīte lakṣmaṇeti ca |
asakr̥t praikṣata tadā nr̥tyantīm iva mātaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
tiṣṭheti rājā cukroṣa yāhi yāhīti rāghavaḥ |
sumantrasya babhūvātmā cakrayor iva cāntarā ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
nāśrauṣam iti rājānam upālabdho ’pi vakṣyasi |
ciraṁ duḥkhasya pāpiṣṭham iti rāmas tam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
rāmasya sa vacaḥ kurvann anujñāpya ca taṁ janam |
vrajato ’pi hayāñ śīghraṁ codayām āsa sārathiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
nyavartata jano rājño rāmaṁ kr̥tvā pradakṣiṇam |
manasāpy aśruvegaiś ca na nyavartata mānuṣam ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
yam icchet punar āyāntaṁ nainaṁ dūram anuvrajet |
ity amātyā mahārājam ūcur daśarathaṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
teṣāṁ vacaḥ sarvaguṇopapannaṁ; prasvinnagātraḥ praviṣaṇṇarūpaḥ |
niśamya rājā kr̥paṇaḥ sabhāryo; vyavasthitas taṁ sutam īkṣamāṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
