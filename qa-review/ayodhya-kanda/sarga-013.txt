=== Sarga 13 ===

---
1.
te tu tāṁ rajanīm uṣya brāhmaṇā vedapāragāḥ |
upatasthur upasthānaṁ saharājapurohitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
amātyā balamukhyāś ca mukhyā ye nigamasya ca |
rāghavasyābhiṣekārthe prīyamāṇās tu saṁgatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
udite vimale sūrye puṣye cābhyāgate ’hani |
abhiṣekāya rāmasya dvijendrair upakalpitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
kāñcanā jalakumbhāś ca bhadrapīṭhaṁ svalaṁkr̥tam |
rāmaś ca samyagāstīrṇo bhāsvarā vyāghracarmaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
gaṅgāyamunayoḥ puṇyāt saṁgamād āhr̥taṁ jalam |
yāś cānyāḥ saritaḥ puṇyā hradāḥ kūpāḥ sarāṁsi ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
prāgvāhāś cordhvavāhāś ca tiryagvāhāḥ samāhitāḥ |
tābhyaś caivāhr̥taṁ toyaṁ samudrebhyaś ca sarvaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
kṣaudraṁ dadhi ghr̥taṁ lājā darbhāḥ sumanasaḥ payaḥ |
salājāḥ kṣīribhiś channā ghaṭāḥ kāñcanarājatāḥ |
padmotpalayutā bhānti pūrṇāḥ paramavāriṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
candrāṁśuvikacaprakhyaṁ pāṇḍuraṁ ratnabhūṣitam |
sajjaṁ tiṣṭhati rāmasya vālavyajanam uttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
candramaṇḍalasaṁkāśam ātapatraṁ ca pāṇḍuram |
sajjaṁ dyutikaraṁ śrīmad abhiṣekapuraskr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
pāṇḍuraś ca vr̥ṣaḥ sajjaḥ pāṇḍurāśvaś ca susthitaḥ |
prasrutaś ca gajaḥ śrīmān aupavāhyaḥ pratīkṣate ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
aṣṭau kanyāś ca maṅgalyāḥ sarvābharaṇabhūṣitāḥ |
vāditrāṇi ca sarvāṇi bandinaś ca tathāpare ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
ikṣvākūṇāṁ yathā rājye saṁbhriyetābhiṣecanam |
tathā jātīyām ādāya rājaputrābhiṣecanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
te rājavacanāt tatra samavetā mahīpatim |
apaśyanto ’bruvan ko nu rājño naḥ prativedayet ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
na paśyāmaś ca rājānam uditaś ca divākaraḥ |
yauvarājyābhiṣekaś ca sajjo rāmasya dhīmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
iti teṣu bruvāṇeṣu sārvabhaumān mahīpatīn |
abravīt tān idaṁ sarvān sumantro rājasatkr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
ayaṁ pr̥cchāmi vacanāt sukham āyuṣmatām aham |
rājñaḥ saṁpratibuddhasya yac cāgamanakāraṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
ity uktvāntaḥpuradvāram ājagāma purāṇavit |
āśīrbhir guṇayuktābhir abhituṣṭāva rāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
gatā bhagavatī rātrirahaḥ śivam upasthitam |
budhyasva nr̥paśārdūla kuru kāryam anantaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
brāhmaṇā balamukhyāś ca naigamāś cāgatā nr̥pa |
darśanaṁ pratikāṅkṣante pratibudhyasva rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
stuvantaṁ taṁ tadā sūtaṁ sumantraṁ mantrakovidam |
pratibudhya tato rājā idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
na caiva saṁprasuto ’ham ānayed āśu rāghavam |
iti rājā daśarathaḥ sūtaṁ tatrānvaśāt punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
sa rājavacanaṁ śrutvā śirasā pratipūjya tam |
nirjagāma nr̥pāvāsān manyamānaḥ priyaṁ mahat ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
prapanno rājamārgaṁ ca patākā dhvajaśobhitam |
sa sūtas tatra śuśrāva rāmādhikaraṇāḥ kathāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tato dadarśa ruciraṁ kailāsasadr̥śaprabham |
rāmaveśma sumantras tu śakraveśmasamaprabham ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
mahākapāṭapihitaṁ vitardiśataśobhitam |
kāñcanapratimaikāgraṁ maṇividrumatoraṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
śāradābhraghanaprakhyaṁ dīptaṁ meruguhopamam |
dāmabhir varamālyānāṁ sumahadbhir alaṁkr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
sa vājiyuktena rathena sārathir; narākulaṁ rājakulaṁ vilokayan |
tataḥ samāsādya mahādhanaṁ mahat; prahr̥ṣṭaromā sa babhūva sārathiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tad adrikūṭācalameghasaṁnibhaṁ; mahāvimānottamaveśmasaṁghavat |
avāryamāṇaḥ praviveśa sārathiḥ; prabhūtaratnaṁ makaro yathārṇavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
