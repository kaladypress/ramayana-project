=== Sarga 62 ===

---
1.
teṣāṁ tad vacanaṁ śrutvā vasiṣṭhaḥ pratyuvāca ha |
mitrāmātyagaṇān sarvān brāhmaṇāṁs tān idaṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
yad asau mātulakule pure rājagr̥he sukhī |
bharato vasati bhrātrā śatrughnena samanvitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tac chīghraṁ javanā dūtā gacchantu tvaritair hayaiḥ |
ānetuṁ bhrātarau vīrau kiṁ samīkṣāmahe vayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
gacchantv iti tataḥ sarve vasiṣṭhaṁ vākyam abruvan |
teṣāṁ tad vacanaṁ śrutvā vasiṣṭho vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
ehi siddhārtha vijaya jayantāśokanandana |
śrūyatām itikartavyaṁ sarvān eva bravīmi vaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
puraṁ rājagr̥haṁ gatvā śīghraṁ śīghrajavair hayaiḥ |
tyaktaśokair idaṁ vācyaḥ śāsanād bharato mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
purohitas tvāṁ kuśalaṁ prāha sarve ca mantriṇaḥ |
tvaramāṇaś ca niryāhi kr̥tyam ātyayikaṁ tvayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
mā cāsmai proṣitaṁ rāmaṁ mā cāsmai pitaraṁ mr̥tam |
bhavantaḥ śaṁsiṣur gatvā rāghavāṇām imaṁ kṣayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
kauśeyāni ca vastrāṇi bhūṣaṇāni varāṇi ca |
kṣipram ādāya rājñaś ca bharatasya ca gacchata |
vasiṣṭhenābhyanujñātā dūtāḥ saṁtvaritā yayuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
te hastina pure gaṅgāṁ tīrtvā pratyaṅmukhā yayuḥ |
pāñcāladeśam āsādya madhyena kurujāṅgalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
te prasannodakāṁ divyāṁ nānāvihagasevitām |
upātijagmur vegena śaradaṇḍāṁ janākulām ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
nikūlavr̥kṣam āsādya divyaṁ satyopayācanam |
abhigamyābhivādyaṁ taṁ kuliṅgāṁ prāviśan purīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
abhikālaṁ tataḥ prāpya tejobhibhavanāc cyutāḥ |
yayur madhyena bāhlīkān sudāmānaṁ ca parvatam |
viṣṇoḥ padaṁ prekṣamāṇā vipāśāṁ cāpi śālmalīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
te śrāntavāhanā dūtā vikr̥ṣṭena satā pathā |
giri vrajaṁ pura varaṁ śīghram āsedur añjasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
bhartuḥ priyārthaṁ kularakṣaṇārthaṁ; bhartuś ca vaṁśasya parigrahārtham |
aheḍamānās tvarayā sma dūtā; rātryāṁ tu te tat puram eva yātāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
