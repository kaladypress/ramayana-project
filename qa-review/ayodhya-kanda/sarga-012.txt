=== Sarga 12 ===

---
1.
putraśokārditaṁ pāpā visaṁjñaṁ patitaṁ bhuvi |
viveṣṭamānam udīkṣya saikṣvākam idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
pāpaṁ kr̥tveva kim idaṁ mama saṁśrutya saṁśravam |
śeṣe kṣititale sannaḥ sthityāṁ sthātuṁ tvam arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
āhuḥ satyaṁ hi paramaṁ dharmaṁ dharmavido janāḥ |
satyam āśritya hi mayā tvaṁ ca dharmaṁ pracoditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
saṁśrutya śaibyaḥ śyenāya svāṁ tanuṁ jagatīpatiḥ |
pradāya pakṣiṇo rājañ jagāma gatim uttamām ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tatha hy alarkas tejasvī brāhmaṇe vedapārage |
yācamāne svake netre uddhr̥tyāvimanā dadau ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
saritāṁ tu patiḥ svalpāṁ maryādāṁ satyam anvitaḥ |
satyānurodhāt samaye velāṁ khāṁ nātivartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
samayaṁ ca mamāryemaṁ yadi tvaṁ na kariṣyasi |
agratas te parityaktā parityakṣyāmi jīvitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
evaṁ pracodito rājā kaikeyyā nirviśaṅkayā |
nāśakat pāśam unmoktuṁ balir indrakr̥taṁ yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
udbhrāntahr̥dayaś cāpi vivarṇavanado ’bhavat |
sa dhuryo vai parispandan yugacakrāntaraṁ yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
vihvalābhyāṁ ca netrābhyām apaśyann iva bhūmipaḥ |
kr̥cchrād dhairyeṇa saṁstabhya kaikeyīm idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
yas te mantrakr̥taḥ pāṇir agnau pāpe mayā dhr̥taḥ |
taṁ tyajāmi svajaṁ caiva tava putraṁ saha tvayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tataḥ pāpasamācārā kaikeyī pārthivaṁ punaḥ |
uvāca paruṣaṁ vākyaṁ vākyajñā roṣamūrchitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
kim idaṁ bhāṣase rājan vākyaṁ gararujopamam |
ānāyayitum akliṣṭaṁ putraṁ rāmam ihārhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sthāpya rājye mama sutaṁ kr̥tvā rāmaṁ vanecaram |
niḥsapatnāṁ ca māṁ kr̥tvā kr̥takr̥tyo bhaviṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa nunna iva tīkṣeṇa pratodena hayottamaḥ |
rājā pradocito ’bhīkṣṇaṁ kaikeyīm idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
dharmabandhena baddho ’smi naṣṭā ca mama cetanā |
jyeṣṭhaṁ putraṁ priyaṁ rāmaṁ draṣṭum icchāmi dhārmikam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
iti rājño vacaḥ śrutvā kaikeyī tadanantaram |
svayam evābravīt sūtaṁ gaccha tvaṁ rāmam ānaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tataḥ sa rājā taṁ sūtaṁ sannaharṣaḥ sutaṁ prati |
śokāraktekṣaṇaḥ śrīmān udvīkṣyovāca dhārmikaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
sumantraḥ karuṇaṁ śrutvā dr̥ṣṭvā dīnaṁ ca pārthivam |
pragr̥hītāñjaliḥ kiṁ cit tasmād deśād apākraman ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
yadā vaktuṁ svayaṁ dainyān na śaśāka mahīpatiḥ |
tadā sumantraṁ mantrajñā kaikeyī pratyuvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
sumantra rāmaṁ drakṣyāmi śīghram ānaya sundaram |
sa manyamānaḥ kalyāṇaṁ hr̥dayena nananda ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
sumantraś cintayām āsa tvaritaṁ coditas tayā |
vyaktaṁ rāmo ’bhiṣekārtham ihāyāsyati dharmavit ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
iti sūto matiṁ kr̥tvā harṣeṇa mahatā punaḥ |
nirjagāma mahātejā rāghavasya didr̥kṣayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tataḥ purastāt sahasā vinirgato; mahīpatīn dvāragatān vilokayan |
dadarśa paurān vividhān mahādhanān; upasthitān dvāram upetya viṣṭhitān ||

=== meaning ===

---
