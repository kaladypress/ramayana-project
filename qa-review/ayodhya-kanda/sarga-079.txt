=== Sarga 79 ===

---
1.
evam uktas tu bharato niṣādādhipatiṁ guham |
pratyuvāca mahāprājño vākyaṁ hetvarthasaṁhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
ūrjitaḥ khalu te kāmaḥ kr̥to mama guroḥ sakhe |
yo me tvam īdr̥śīṁ senām eko ’bhyarcitum icchasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
ity uktvā tu mahātejā guhaṁ vacanam uttamam |
abravīd bharataḥ śrīmān niṣādādhipatiṁ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
katareṇa gamiṣyāmi bharadvājāśramaṁ guha |
gahano ’yaṁ bhr̥śaṁ deśo gaṅgānūpo duratyayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā rājaputrasya dhīmataḥ |
abravīt prāñjalir vākyaṁ guho gahanagocaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
dāśās tv anugamiṣyanti dhanvinaḥ susamāhitāḥ |
ahaṁ cānugamiṣyāmi rājaputra mahāyaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
kaccin na duṣṭo vrajasi rāmasyākliṣṭakarmaṇaḥ |
iyaṁ te mahatī senā śaṅkāṁ janayatīva me ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tam evam abhibhāṣantam ākāśa iva nirmalaḥ |
bharataḥ ślakṣṇayā vācā guhaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
mā bhūt sa kālo yat kaṣṭaṁ na māṁ śaṅkitum arhasi |
rāghavaḥ sa hi me bhrātā jyeṣṭhaḥ pitr̥samo mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
taṁ nivartayituṁ yāmi kākutsthaṁ vanavāsinam |
buddhir anyā na te kāryā guha satyaṁ bravīmi te ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sa tu saṁhr̥ṣṭavadanaḥ śrutvā bharatabhāṣitam |
punar evābravīd vākyaṁ bharataṁ prati harṣitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
dhanyas tvaṁ na tvayā tulyaṁ paśyāmi jagatītale |
ayatnād āgataṁ rājyaṁ yas tvaṁ tyaktum ihecchasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
śāśvatī khalu te kīrtir lokān anucariṣyati |
yas tvaṁ kr̥cchragataṁ rāmaṁ pratyānayitum icchasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
evaṁ saṁbhāṣamāṇasya guhasya bharataṁ tadā |
babhau naṣṭaprabhaḥ sūryo rajanī cābhyavartata ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
saṁniveśya sa tāṁ senāṁ guhena paritoṣitaḥ |
śatrughnena saha śrīmāñ śayanaṁ punar āgamat ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
rāmacintāmayaḥ śoko bharatasya mahātmanaḥ |
upasthito hy anarhasya dharmaprekṣasya tādr̥śaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
antardāhena dahanaḥ saṁtāpayati rāghavam |
vanadāhābhisaṁtaptaṁ gūḍho ’gnir iva pādapam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
prasrutaḥ sarvagātrebhyaḥ svedaḥ śokāgnisaṁbhavaḥ |
yathā sūryāṁśusaṁtapto himavān prasruto himam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
dhyānanirdaraśailena viniḥśvasitadhātunā |
dainyapādapasaṁghena śokāyāsādhiśr̥ṅgiṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
pramohānantasattvena saṁtāpauṣadhiveṇunā |
ākrānto duḥkhaśailena mahatā kaikayīsutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
guhena sārdhaṁ bharataḥ samāgato; mahānubhāvaḥ sajanaḥ samāhitaḥ |
sudurmanās taṁ bharataṁ tadā punar; guhaḥ samāśvāsayad agrajaṁ prati ||

=== meaning ===

---
