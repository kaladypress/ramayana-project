=== Sarga 50 ===

---
1.
atha rātryāṁ vyatītāyām avasuptam anantaram |
prabodhayām āsa śanair lakṣmaṇaṁ raghunandanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
saumitre śr̥ṇu vanyānāṁ valgu vyāharatāṁ svanam |
saṁpratiṣṭhāmahe kālaḥ prasthānasya paraṁtapa ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sa suptaḥ samaye bhrātrā lakṣmaṇaḥ pratibodhitaḥ |
jahau nidrāṁ ca tandrīṁ ca prasaktaṁ ca pathi śramam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tata utthāya te sarve spr̥ṣṭvā nadyāḥ śivaṁ jalam |
panthānam r̥ṣiṇoddiṣṭaṁ citrakūṭasya taṁ yayuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tataḥ saṁprasthitaḥ kāle rāmaḥ saumitriṇā saha |
sītāṁ kamalapatrākṣīm idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
ādīptān iva vaidehi sarvataḥ puṣpitān nagān |
svaiḥ puṣpaiḥ kiṁśukān paśya mālinaḥ śiśirātyaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
paśya bhallātakān phullān narair anupasevitān |
phalapatrair avanatān nūnaṁ śakṣyāmi jīvitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
paśya droṇapramāṇāni lambamānāni lakṣmaṇa |
madhūni madhukārībhiḥ saṁbhr̥tāni nage nage ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
eṣa krośati natyūhas taṁ śikhī pratikūjati |
ramaṇīye vanoddeśe puṣpasaṁstarasaṁkaṭe ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
mātaṁgayūthānusr̥taṁ pakṣisaṁghānunāditam |
citrakūṭam imaṁ paśya pravr̥ddhaśikharaṁ girim ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tatas tau pādacāreṇa gacchantau saha sītayā |
ramyam āsedatuḥ śailaṁ citrakūṭaṁ manoramam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
taṁ tu parvatam āsādya nānāpakṣigaṇāyutam |
ayaṁ vāso bhavet tāvad atra saumya ramemahi ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
lakṣmaṇānaya dārūṇi dr̥ḍhāni ca varāṇi ca |
kuruṣvāvasathaṁ saumya vāse me ’bhirataṁ manaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā saumitrir vividhān drumān |
ājahāra tataś cakre parṇa śālām ariṁ dama ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
śuśrūṣamāṇam ekāgram idaṁ vacanam abravīt |
aiṇeyaṁ māṁsam āhr̥tya śālāṁ yakṣyāmahe vayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sa lakṣmaṇaḥ kr̥ṣṇamr̥gaṁ hatvā medhyaṁ patāpavān |
atha cikṣepa saumitriḥ samiddhe jātavedasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
taṁ tu pakvaṁ samājñāya niṣṭaptaṁ chinnaśoṇitam |
lakṣmaṇaḥ puruṣavyāghram atha rāghavam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
ayaṁ kr̥ṣṇaḥ samāptāṅgaḥ śr̥taḥ kr̥ṣṇa mr̥go yathā |
devatā devasaṁkāśa yajasva kuśalo hy asi ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
rāmaḥ snātvā tu niyato guṇavāñ japyakovidaḥ |
pāpasaṁśamanaṁ rāmaś cakāra balim uttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tāṁ vr̥kṣaparṇac chadanāṁ manojñāṁ; yathāpradeśaṁ sukr̥tāṁ nivātām |
vāsāya sarve viviśuḥ sametāḥ; sabhāṁ yathā deva gaṇāḥ sudharmām ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
anekanānāmr̥gapakṣisaṁkule; vicitrapuṣpastabalair drumair yute |
vanottame vyālamr̥gānunādite; tathā vijahruḥ susukhaṁ jitendriyāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
suramyam āsādya tu citrakūṭaṁ; nadīṁ ca tāṁ mālyavatīṁ sutīrthām |
nananda hr̥ṣṭo mr̥gapakṣijuṣṭāṁ; jahau ca duḥkhaṁ puravipravāsāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
