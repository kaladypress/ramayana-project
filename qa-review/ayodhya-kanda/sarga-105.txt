=== Sarga 105 ===

---
1.
tataḥ śirasi kr̥tvā tu pāduke bharatas tadā |
āruroha rathaṁ hr̥ṣṭaḥ śatrughnena samanvitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
vasiṣṭho vāmadevaś ca jābāliś ca dr̥ḍhavrataḥ |
agrataḥ prayayuḥ sarve mantriṇo mantrapūjitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
mandākinīṁ nadīṁ ramyāṁ prāṅmukhās te yayus tadā |
pradakṣiṇaṁ ca kurvāṇāś citrakūṭaṁ mahāgirim ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
paśyan dhātusahasrāṇi ramyāṇi vividhāni ca |
prayayau tasya pārśvena sasainyo bharatas tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
adūrāc citrakūṭasya dadarśa bharatas tadā |
āśramaṁ yatra sa munir bharadvājaḥ kr̥tālayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
sa tam āśramam āgamya bharadvājasya buddhimān |
avatīrya rathāt pādau vavande kulanandanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tato hr̥ṣṭo bharadvājo bharataṁ vākyam abravīt |
api kr̥tyaṁ kr̥taṁ tāta rāmeṇa ca samāgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
evam uktas tu bharato bharadvājena dhīmatā |
pratyuvāca bharadvājaṁ bharato dharmavatsalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sa yācyamāno guruṇā mayā ca dr̥ḍhavikramaḥ |
rāghavaḥ paramaprīto vasiṣṭhaṁ vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
pituḥ pratijñāṁ tām eva pālayiṣyāmi tattvataḥ |
caturdaśa hi varṣāṇi ya pratijñā pitur mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
evam ukto mahāprājño vasiṣṭhaḥ pratyuvāca ha |
vākyajño vākyakuśalaṁ rāghavaṁ vacanaṁ mahat ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
ete prayaccha saṁhr̥ṣṭaḥ pāduke hemabhūṣite |
ayodhyāyāṁ mahāprājña yogakṣemakare tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
evam ukto vasiṣṭhena rāghavaḥ prāṅmukhaḥ sthitaḥ |
pāduke hemavikr̥te mama rājyāya te dadau ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
nivr̥tto ’ham anujñāto rāmeṇa sumahātmanā |
ayodhyām eva gacchāmi gr̥hītvā pāduke śubhe ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
etac chrutvā śubhaṁ vākyaṁ bharatasya mahātmanaḥ |
bharadvājaḥ śubhataraṁ munir vākyam udāharat ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
naitac citraṁ naravyāghra śīlavr̥ttavatāṁ vara |
yad āryaṁ tvayi tiṣṭhet tu nimne vr̥ṣṭim ivodakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
amr̥taḥ sa mahābāhuḥ pitā daśarathas tava |
yasya tvam īdr̥śaḥ putro dharmātmā dharmavatsalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tam r̥ṣiṁ tu mahātmānam uktavākyaṁ kr̥tāñjaliḥ |
āmantrayitum ārebhe caraṇāv upagr̥hya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tataḥ pradakṣiṇaṁ kr̥tvā bharadvājaṁ punaḥ punaḥ |
bharatas tu yayau śrīmān ayodhyāṁ saha mantribhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
yānaiś ca śakaṭaiś caiva hayaiś nāgaiś ca sā camūḥ |
punar nivr̥ttā vistīrṇā bharatasyānuyāyinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tatas te yamunāṁ divyāṁ nadīṁ tīrtvormimālinīm |
dadr̥śus tāṁ punaḥ sarve gaṅgāṁ śivajalāṁ nadīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tāṁ ramyajalasaṁpūrṇāṁ saṁtīrya saha bāndhavaḥ |
śr̥ṅgaverapuraṁ ramyaṁ praviveśa sasainikaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
śr̥ṅgaverapurād bhūya ayodhyāṁ saṁdadarśa ha |
bharato duḥkhasaṁtaptaḥ sārathiṁ cedam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
sārathe paśya vidhvastā ayodhyā na prakāśate |
nirākārā nirānandā dīnā pratihatasvanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
