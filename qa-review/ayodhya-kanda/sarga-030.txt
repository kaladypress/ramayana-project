=== Sarga 30 ===

---
1.
dattvā tu saha vaidehyā brāhmaṇebhyo dhanaṁ bahu |
jagmatuḥ pitaraṁ draṣṭuṁ sītayā saha rāghavau ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tato gr̥hīte duṣprekṣye aśobhetāṁ tadāyudhe |
mālādāmabhir āsakte sītayā samalaṁkr̥te ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tataḥ prāsādaharmyāṇi vimānaśikharāṇi ca |
adhiruhya janaḥ śrīmān udāsīno vyalokayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
na hi rathyāḥ sma śakyante gantuṁ bahujanākulāḥ |
āruhya tasmāt prāsādān dīnāḥ paśyanti rāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
padātiṁ varjitacchatraṁ rāmaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā tadā janāḥ |
ūcur bahuvidhā vācaḥ śokopahatacetasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
yaṁ yāntam anuyāti sma caturaṅgabalaṁ mahat |
tam ekaṁ sītayā sārdham anuyāti sma lakṣmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
aiśvaryasya rasajñaḥ san kāmināṁ caiva kāmadaḥ |
necchaty evānr̥taṁ kartuṁ pitaraṁ dharmagauravāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
yā na śakyā purā draṣṭuṁ bhūtair ākāśagair api |
tām adya sītāṁ paśyanti rājamārgagatā janāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
aṅgarāgocitāṁ sītāṁ raktacandana sevinīm |
varṣam uṣṇaṁ ca śītaṁ ca neṣyaty āśu vivarṇatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
adya nūnaṁ daśarathaḥ sattvam āviśya bhāṣate |
na hi rājā priyaṁ putraṁ vivāsayitum arhati ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
nirguṇasyāpi putrasyā kāthaṁ syād vipravāsanam |
kiṁ punar yasya loko ’yaṁ jito vr̥ttena kevalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
ānr̥śaṁsyam anukrośaḥ śrutaṁ śīlaṁ damaḥ śamaḥ |
rāghavaṁ śobhayanty ete ṣaḍguṇāḥ puruṣottamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tasmāt tasyopaghātena prajāḥ paramapīḍitāḥ |
audakānīva sattvāni grīṣme salilasaṁkṣayāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
pīḍayā pīḍitaṁ sarvaṁ jagad asya jagatpateḥ |
mūlasyevopaghātena vr̥kṣaḥ puṣpaphalopagaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
te lakṣmaṇa iva kṣipraṁ sapatnyaḥ sahabāndhavāḥ |
gacchantam anugacchāmo yena gacchati rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
udyānāni parityajya kṣetrāṇi ca gr̥hāṇi ca |
ekaduḥkhasukhā rāmam anugacchāma dhārmikam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
samuddhr̥tanidhānāni paridhvastājirāṇi ca |
upāttadhanadhānyāni hr̥tasārāṇi sarvaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
rajasābhyavakīrṇāni parityaktāni daivataiḥ |
asmattyaktāni veśmāni kaikeyī pratipadyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
vanaṁ nagaram evāstu yena gacchati rāghavaḥ |
asmābhiś ca parityaktaṁ puraṁ saṁpadyatāṁ vanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
bilāni daṁṣṭriṇaḥ sarve sānūni mr̥gapakṣiṇaḥ |
asmattyaktaṁ prapadyantāṁ sevyamānaṁ tyajantu ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
ity evaṁ vividhā vāco nānājanasamīritāḥ |
śuśrāva rāmaḥ śrutvā ca na vicakre ’sya mānasam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
pratīkṣamāṇo ’bhijanaṁ tadārtam; anārtarūpaḥ prahasann ivātha |
jagāma rāmaḥ pitaraṁ didr̥kṣuḥ; pitur nideśaṁ vidhivac cikīrṣuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tat pūrvam aikṣvākasuto mahātmā; rāmo gamiṣyan vanam ārtarūpam |
vyatiṣṭhata prekṣya tadā sumantraṁ; pitur mahātmā pratihāraṇārtham ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
pitur nideśena tu dharmavatsalo; vanapraveśe kr̥tabuddhiniścayaḥ |
sa rāghavaḥ prekṣya sumantram abravīn; nivedayasvāgamanaṁ nr̥pāya me ||

=== meaning ===

---
