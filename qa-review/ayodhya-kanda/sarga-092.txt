=== Sarga 92 ===

---
1.
niveśya senāṁ tu vibhuḥ padbhyāṁ pādavatāṁ varaḥ |
abhigantuṁ sa kākutstham iyeṣa guruvartakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
niviṣṭa mātre sainye tu yathoddeśaṁ vinītavat |
bharato bhrātaraṁ vākyaṁ śatrughnam idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
kṣipraṁ vanam idaṁ saumya narasaṁghaiḥ samantataḥ |
lubdhaiś ca sahitair ebhis tvam anveṣitum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
yāvan na rāmaṁ drakṣyāmi lakṣmaṇaṁ vā mahābalam |
vaidehīṁ vā mahābhāgāṁ na me śāntir bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
yāvan na candrasaṁkāśaṁ drakṣyāmi śubham ānanam |
bhrātuḥ padmapalāśākṣaṁ na me śāntir bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
yāvan na caraṇau bhrātuḥ pārthiva vyañjanānvitau |
śirasā dhārayiṣyāmi na me śāntir bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
yāvan na rājye rājyārhaḥ pitr̥paitāmahe sthitaḥ |
abhiṣekajalaklinno na me śāntir bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
kr̥takr̥tyā mahābhāgā vaidehī janakātmajā |
bhartāraṁ sāgarāntāyāḥ pr̥thivyā yānugacchati ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
subhagaś citrakūṭo ’sau girirājopamo giriḥ |
yasmin vasati kākutsthaḥ kubera ivanandane ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
kr̥takāryam idaṁ durgaṁ vanaṁ vyālaniṣevitam |
yad adhyāste mahātejā rāmaḥ śastrabhr̥tāṁ varaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
evam uktvā mahātejā bharataḥ puruṣarṣabhaḥ |
padbhyām eva mahātejāḥ praviveśa mahad vanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sa tāni drumajālāni jātāni girisānuṣu |
puṣpitāgrāṇi madhyena jagāma vadatāṁ varaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sa gireś citrakūṭasya sālam āsādya puṣpitam |
rāmāśramagatasyāgner dadarśa dhvajam ucchritam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā bharataḥ śrīmān mumoda sahabāndhavaḥ |
atra rāma iti jñātvā gataḥ pāram ivāmbhasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa citrakūṭe tu girau niśāmya; rāmāśramaṁ puṇyajanopapannam |
guhena sārdhaṁ tvarito jagāma; punar niveśyaiva camūṁ mahātmā ||

=== meaning ===

---
