=== Sarga 66 ===

---
1.
apaśyaṁs tu tatas tatra pitaraṁ pitur ālaye |
jagāma bharato draṣṭuṁ mātaraṁ mātur ālaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
anuprāptaṁ tu taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā kaikeyī proṣitaṁ sutam |
utpapāta tadā hr̥ṣṭā tyaktvā sauvarṇamānasam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sa praviśyaiva dharmātmā svagr̥haṁ śrīvivarjitam |
bharataḥ prekṣya jagrāha jananyāś caraṇau śubhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
taṁ mūrdhni samupāghrāya pariṣvajya yaśasvinam |
aṅke bharatam āropya praṣṭuṁ samupacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
adya te kati cid rātryaś cyutasyāryakaveśmanaḥ |
api nādhvaśramaḥ śīghraṁ rathenāpatatas tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
āryakas te sukuśalo yudhājin mātulas tava |
pravāsāc ca sukhaṁ putra sarvaṁ me vaktum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
evaṁ pr̥ṣṭhas tu kaikeyyā priyaṁ pārthivanandanaḥ |
ācaṣṭa bharataḥ sarvaṁ mātre rājīvalocanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
adya me saptamī rātriś cyutasyāryakaveśmanaḥ |
ambāyāḥ kuśalī tāto yudhājin mātulaś ca me ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
yan me dhanaṁ ca ratnaṁ ca dadau rājā paraṁtapaḥ |
pariśrāntaṁ pathy abhavat tato ’haṁ pūrvam āgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
rājavākyaharair dūtais tvaryamāṇo ’ham āgataḥ |
yad ahaṁ praṣṭum icchāmi tad ambā vaktum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
śūnyo ’yaṁ śayanīyas te paryaṅko hemabhūṣitaḥ |
na cāyam ikṣvākujanaḥ prahr̥ṣṭaḥ pratibhāti me ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
rājā bhavati bhūyiṣṭham ihāmbāyā niveśane |
tam ahaṁ nādya paśyāmi draṣṭum icchann ihāgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
pitur grahīṣye caraṇau taṁ mamākhyāhi pr̥cchataḥ |
āhosvid amba jyeṣṭhāyāḥ kausalyāyā niveśane ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
taṁ pratyuvāca kaikeyī priyavad ghoram apriyam |
ajānantaṁ prajānantī rājyalobhena mohitā |
yā gatiḥ sarvabhūtānāṁ tāṁ gatiṁ te pitā gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tac chrutvā bharato vākyaṁ dharmābhijanavāñ śuciḥ |
papāta sahasā bhūmau pitr̥śokabalārditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tataḥ śokena saṁvītaḥ pitur maraṇaduḥkhitaḥ |
vilalāpa mahātejā bhrāntākulitacetanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
etat suruciraṁ bhāti pitur me śayanaṁ purā |
tad idaṁ na vibhāty adya vihīnaṁ tena dhīmatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tam ārtaṁ devasaṁkāśaṁ samīkṣya patitaṁ bhuvi |
utthāpayitvā śokārtaṁ vacanaṁ cedam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
uttiṣṭhottiṣṭha kiṁ śeṣe rājaputra mahāyaśaḥ |
tvadvidhā na hi śocanti santaḥ sadasi saṁmatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
sa rudatyā ciraṁ kālaṁ bhūmau viparivr̥tya ca |
jananīṁ pratyuvācedaṁ śokair bahubhir āvr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
abhiṣekṣyati rāmaṁ tu rājā yajñaṁ nu yakṣyati |
ity ahaṁ kr̥tasaṁkalpo hr̥ṣṭo yātrām ayāsiṣam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tad idaṁ hy anyathā bhūtaṁ vyavadīrṇaṁ mano mama |
pitaraṁ yo na paśyāmi nityaṁ priyahite ratam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
amba kenātyagād rājā vyādhinā mayy anāgate |
dhanyā rāmādayaḥ sarve yaiḥ pitā saṁskr̥taḥ svayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
na nūnaṁ māṁ mahārājaḥ prāptaṁ jānāti kīrtimān |
upajighred dhi māṁ mūrdhni tātaḥ saṁnamya satvaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
kva sa pāṇiḥ sukhasparśas tātasyākliṣṭakarmaṇaḥ |
yena māṁ rajasā dhvastam abhīkṣṇaṁ parimārjati ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
yo me bhrātā pitā bandhur yasya dāso ’smi dhīmataḥ |
tasya māṁ śīghram ākhyāhi rāmasyākliṣṭa karmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
pitā hi bhavati jyeṣṭho dharmam āryasya jānataḥ |
tasya pādau grahīṣyāmi sa hīdānīṁ gatir mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
ārye kim abravīd rājā pitā me satyavikramaḥ |
paścimaṁ sādhu saṁdeśam icchāmi śrotum ātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
iti pr̥ṣṭā yathātattvaṁ kaikeyī vākyam abravīt |
rāmeti rājā vilapan hā sīte lakṣmaṇeti ca |
sa mahātmā paraṁ lokaṁ gato gatimatāṁ varaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
imāṁ tu paścimāṁ vācaṁ vyājahāra pitā tava |
kāla dharmaparikṣiptaḥ pāśair iva mahāgajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
siddhārthās tu narā rāmam āgataṁ sītayā saha |
lakṣmaṇaṁ ca mahābāhuṁ drakṣyanti punar āgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
tac chrutvā viṣasādaiva dvitīyā priyaśaṁsanāt |
viṣaṇṇavadano bhūtvā bhūyaḥ papraccha mātaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
kva cedānīṁ sa dharmātmā kausalyānandavardhanaḥ |
lakṣmaṇena saha bhrātrā sītayā ca samaṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
tathā pr̥ṣṭā yathātattvam ākhyātum upacakrame |
mātāsya yugapad vākyaṁ vipriyaṁ priyaśaṅkayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
sa hi rājasutaḥ putra cīravāsā mahāvanam |
daṇḍakān saha vaidehyā lakṣmaṇānucaro gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
tac chrutvā bharatas trasto bhrātuś cāritraśaṅkayā |
svasya vaṁśasya māhātmyāt praṣṭuṁ samupacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
kaccin na brāhmaṇavadhaṁ hr̥taṁ rāmeṇa kasya cit |
kaccin nāḍhyo daridro vā tenāpāpo vihiṁsitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
kaccin na paradārān vā rājaputro ’bhimanyate |
kasmāt sa daṇḍakāraṇye bhrūṇaheva vivāsitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
athāsya capalā mātā tat svakarma yathātatham |
tenaiva strīsvabhāvena vyāhartum upacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
na brāhmaṇadhanaṁ kiṁ cid dhr̥taṁ rāmeṇa kasya cit |
kaś cin nāḍhyo daridro vā tenāpāpo vihiṁsitaḥ |
na rāmaḥ paradārāṁś ca cakṣurbhyām api paśyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
mayā tu putra śrutvaiva rāmasyaivābhiṣecanam |
yācitas te pitā rājyaṁ rāmasya ca vivāsanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
sa svavr̥ttiṁ samāsthāya pitā te tat tathākarot |
rāmaś ca sahasaumitriḥ preṣitaḥ saha sītayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
tam apaśyan priyaṁ putraṁ mahīpālo mahāyaśāḥ |
putraśokaparidyūnaḥ pañcatvam upapedivān ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
tvayā tv idānīṁ dharmajña rājatvam avalambyatām |
tvatkr̥te hi mayā sarvam idam evaṁvidhaṁ kr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
tat putra śīghraṁ vidhinā vidhijñair; vasiṣṭhamukhyaiḥ sahito dvijendraiḥ |
saṁkālya rājānam adīnasattvam; ātmānam urvyām abhiṣecayasva ||

=== meaning ===

---
