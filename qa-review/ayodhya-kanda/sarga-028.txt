=== Sarga 28 ===

---
1.
tato ’bravīn mahātejā rāmo lakṣmaṇam agrataḥ |
sthitaṁ prāggāminaṁ vīraṁ yācamānaṁ kr̥tāñjalim ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
mayādya saha saumitre tvayi gacchati tad vanam |
ko bhariṣyati kausalyāṁ sumitrāṁ vā yaśasvinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
abhivarṣati kāmair yaḥ parjanyaḥ pr̥thivīm iva |
sa kāmapāśaparyasto mahātejā mahīpatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
sā hi rājyam idaṁ prāpya nr̥pasyāśvapateḥ sutā |
duḥkhitānāṁ sapatnīnāṁ na kariṣyati śobhanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
evam uktas tu rāmeṇa lakṣmaṇaḥ ślakṣṇayā girā |
pratyuvāca tadā rāmaṁ vākyajño vākyakovidam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tavaiva tejasā vīra bharataḥ pūjayiṣyati |
kausalyāṁ ca sumitrāṁ ca prayato nātra saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
kausalyā bibhr̥yād āryā sahasram api madvidhān |
yasyāḥ sahasraṁ grāmāṇāṁ saṁprāptam upajīvanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
dhanur ādāya saśaraṁ khanitrapiṭakādharaḥ |
agratas te gamiṣyāmi panthānam anudarśayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
āhariṣyāmi te nityaṁ mūlāni ca phalāni ca |
vanyāni yāni cānyāni svāhārāṇi tapasvinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
bhavāṁs tu saha vaidehyā girisānuṣu raṁsyate |
ahaṁ sarvaṁ kariṣyāmi jāgrataḥ svapataś ca te ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
rāmas tv anena vākyena suprītaḥ pratyuvāca tam |
vrajāpr̥cchasva saumitre sarvam eva suhr̥jjanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
ye ca rājño dadau divye mahātmā varuṇaḥ svayam |
janakasya mahāyajñe dhanuṣī raudradarśane ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
abhedyakavace divye tūṇī cākṣayasāyakau |
ādityavimalau cobhau khaḍgau hemapariṣkr̥tau ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
satkr̥tya nihitaṁ sarvam etad ācāryasadmani |
sa tvam āyudham ādāya kṣipram āvraja lakṣmaṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa suhr̥jjanam āmantrya vanavāsāya niścitaḥ |
ikṣvākugurum āmantrya jagrāhāyudham uttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tad divyaṁ rājaśārdūlaḥ satkr̥taṁ mālyabhūṣitam |
rāmāya darśayām āsa saumitriḥ sarvam āyudham ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tam uvācātmavān rāmaḥ prītyā lakṣmaṇam āgatam |
kāle tvam āgataḥ saumya kāṅkṣite mama lakṣmaṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
ahaṁ pradātum icchāmi yad idaṁ māmakaṁ dhanam |
brāhmaṇebhyas tapasvibhyas tvayā saha paraṁtapa ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
vasantīha dr̥ḍhaṁ bhaktyā guruṣu dvijasattamāḥ |
teṣām api ca me bhūyaḥ sarveṣāṁ copajīvinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
vasiṣṭhaputraṁ tu suyajñam āryaṁ; tvam ānayāśu pravaraṁ dvijānām |
abhiprayāsyāmi vanaṁ samastān; abhyarcya śiṣṭān aparān dvijātīn ||

=== meaning ===

---
