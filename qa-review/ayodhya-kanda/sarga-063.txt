=== Sarga 63 ===

---
1.
yām eva rātriṁ te dūtāḥ praviśanti sma tāṁ purīm |
bharatenāpi tāṁ rātriṁ svapno dr̥ṣṭo ’yam apriyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
vyuṣṭām eva tu tāṁ rātriṁ dr̥ṣṭvā taṁ svapnam apriyam |
putro rājādhirājasya subhr̥śaṁ paryatapyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tapyamānaṁ samājñāya vayasyāḥ priyavādinaḥ |
āyāsaṁ hi vineṣyantaḥ sabhāyāṁ cakrire kathāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
vādayanti tathā śāntiṁ lāsayanty api cāpare |
nāṭakāny apare prāhur hāsyāni vividhāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
sa tair mahātmā bharataḥ sakhibhiḥ priya vādibhiḥ |
goṣṭhīhāsyāni kurvadbhir na prāhr̥ṣyata rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tam abravīt priyasakho bharataṁ sakhibhir vr̥tam |
suhr̥dbhiḥ paryupāsīnaḥ kiṁ sakhe nānumodase ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
evaṁ bruvāṇaṁ suhr̥daṁ bharataḥ pratyuvāca ha |
śr̥ṇu tvaṁ yan nimittaṁme dainyam etad upāgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
svapne pitaram adrākṣaṁ malinaṁ muktamūrdhajam |
patantam adriśikharāt kaluṣe gomaye hrade ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
plavamānaś ca me dr̥ṣṭaḥ sa tasmin gomayahrade |
pibann añjalinā tailaṁ hasann iva muhur muhuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tatas tilodanaṁ bhuktvā punaḥ punar adhaḥśirāḥ |
tailenābhyaktasarvāṅgas tailam evāvagāhata ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
svapne ’pi sāgaraṁ śuṣkaṁ candraṁ ca patitaṁ bhuvi |
sahasā cāpi saṁśantaṁ jvalitaṁ jātavedasam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
avadīrṇāṁ ca pr̥thivīṁ śuṣkāṁś ca vividhān drumān |
ahaṁ paśyāmi vidhvastān sadhūmāṁś caiva pārvatān ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
pīṭhe kārṣṇāyase cainaṁ niṣaṇṇaṁ kr̥ṣṇavāsasam |
prahasanti sma rājānaṁ pramadāḥ kr̥ṣṇapiṅgalāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tvaramāṇaś ca dharmātmā raktamālyānulepanaḥ |
rathena kharayuktena prayāto dakṣiṇāmukhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
evam etan mayā dr̥ṣṭam imāṁ rātriṁ bhayāvahām |
ahaṁ rāmo ’tha vā rājā lakṣmaṇo vā mariṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
naro yānena yaḥ svapne kharayuktena yāti hi |
acirāt tasya dhūmāgraṁ citāyāṁ saṁpradr̥śyate |
etannimittaṁ dīno ’haṁ tan na vaḥ pratipūjaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
śuṣyatīva ca me kaṇṭho na svastham iva me manaḥ |
jugupsann iva cātmānaṁ na ca paśyāmi kāraṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
imāṁ hi duḥsvapnagatiṁ niśāmya tām; anekarūpām avitarkitāṁ purā |
bhayaṁ mahat tad dhr̥dayān na yāti me; vicintya rājānam acintyadarśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
