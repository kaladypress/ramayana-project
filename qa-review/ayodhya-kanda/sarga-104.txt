=== Sarga 104 ===

---
1.
tam apratimatejobhyāṁ bhrātr̥bhyāṁ romaharṣaṇam |
vismitāḥ saṁgamaṁ prekṣya samavetā maharṣayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
antarhitās tv r̥ṣigaṇāḥ siddhāś ca paramarṣayaḥ |
tau bhrātarau mahātmānau kākutsthau praśaśaṁsire ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sa dhanyo yasya putrau dvau dharmajñau dharmavikramau |
śrutvā vayaṁ hi saṁbhāṣām ubhayoḥ spr̥hayāmahe ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tatas tv r̥ṣigaṇāḥ kṣipraṁ daśagrīvavadhaiṣiṇaḥ |
bharataṁ rājaśārdūlam ity ūcuḥ saṁgatā vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
kule jāta mahāprājña mahāvr̥tta mahāyaśaḥ |
grāhyaṁ rāmasya vākyaṁ te pitaraṁ yady avekṣase ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
sadānr̥ṇam imaṁ rāmaṁ vayam icchāmahe pituḥ |
anr̥ṇatvāc ca kaikeyyāḥ svargaṁ daśaratho gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
etāvad uktvā vacanaṁ gandharvāḥ samaharṣayaḥ |
rājarṣayaś caiva tathā sarve svāṁ svāṁ gatiṁ gatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
hlāditas tena vākyena śubhena śubhadarśanaḥ |
rāmaḥ saṁhr̥ṣṭavadanas tān r̥ṣīn abhyapūjayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
srastagātras tu bharataḥ sa vācā sajjamānayā |
kr̥tāñjalir idaṁ vākyaṁ rāghavaṁ punar abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
rājadharmam anuprekṣya kuladharmānusaṁtatim |
kartum arhasi kākutstha mama mātuś ca yācanām ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
rakṣituṁ sumahad rājyam aham ekas tu notsahe |
paurajānapadāṁś cāpi raktān rañjayituṁ tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
jñātayaś ca hi yodhāś ca mitrāṇi suhr̥daś ca naḥ |
tvām eva pratikāṅkṣante parjanyam iva karṣakāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
idaṁ rājyaṁ mahāprājña sthāpaya pratipadya hi |
śaktimān asi kākutstha lokasya paripālane ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
ity uktvā nyapatad bhrātuḥ pādayor bharatas tadā |
bhr̥śaṁ saṁprārthayām āsa rāmam evaṁ priyaṁ vadaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tam aṅke bhrātaraṁ kr̥tvā rāmo vacanam abravīt |
śyāmaṁ nalinapatrākṣaṁ mattahaṁsasvaraḥ svayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
āgatā tvām iyaṁ buddhiḥ svajā vainayikī ca yā |
bhr̥śam utsahase tāta rakṣituṁ pr̥thivīm api ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
amātyaiś ca suhr̥dbhiś ca buddhimadbhiś ca mantribhiḥ |
sarvakāryāṇi saṁmantrya sumahānty api kāraya ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
lakṣmīś candrād apeyād vā himavān vā himaṁ tyajet |
atīyāt sāgaro velāṁ na pratijñām ahaṁ pituḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
kāmād vā tāta lobhād vā mātrā tubhyam idaṁ kr̥tam |
na tan manasi kartavyaṁ vartitavyaṁ ca mātr̥vat ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
evaṁ bruvāṇaṁ bharataḥ kausalyāsutam abravīt |
tejasādityasaṁkāśaṁ pratipaccandradarśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
adhirohārya pādābhyāṁ pāduke hemabhūṣite |
ete hi sarvalokasya yogakṣemaṁ vidhāsyataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
so ’dhiruhya naravyāghraḥ pāduke hy avaruhya ca |
prāyacchat sumahātejā bharatāya mahātmane ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
sa pāduke te bharataḥ pratāpavān; svalaṁkr̥te saṁparigr̥hya dharmavit |
pradakṣiṇaṁ caiva cakāra rāghavaṁ; cakāra caivottamanāgamūrdhani ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
athānupūrvyāt pratipūjya taṁ janaṁ; gurūṁś ca mantriprakr̥tīs tathānujau |
vyasarjayad rāghavavaṁśavardhanaḥ; sthitaḥ svadharme himavān ivācalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
taṁ mātaro bāṣpagr̥hītakaṇṭho; duḥkhena nāmantrayituṁ hi śekuḥ |
sa tv eva mātr̥̄r abhivādya sarvā; rudan kuṭīṁ svāṁ praviveśa rāmaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
