=== Sarga 33 ===

---
1.
mahāmātravacaḥ śrutvā rāmo daśarathaṁ tadā |
anvabhāṣata vākyaṁ tu vinayajño vinītavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tyaktabhogasya me rājan vane vanyena jīvataḥ |
kiṁ kāryam anuyātreṇa tyaktasaṅgasya sarvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
yo hi dattvā dvipaśreṣṭhaṁ kakṣyāyāṁ kurute manaḥ |
rajjusnehena kiṁ tasya tyajataḥ kuñjarottamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tathā mama satāṁ śreṣṭha kiṁ dhvajinyā jagatpate |
sarvāṇy evānujānāmi cīrāṇy evānayantu me ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
khanitrapiṭake cobhe mamānayata gacchataḥ |
caturdaśa vane vāsaṁ varṣāṇi vasato mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
atha cīrāṇi kaikeyī svayam āhr̥tya rāghavam |
uvāca paridhatsveti janaughe nirapatrapā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sa cīre puruṣavyāghraḥ kaikeyyāḥ pratigr̥hya te |
sūkṣmavastram avakṣipya munivastrāṇy avasta ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
lakṣmaṇaś cāpi tatraiva vihāya vasane śubhe |
tāpasāc chādane caiva jagrāha pitur agrataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
athātmaparidhānārthaṁ sītā kauśeyavāsinī |
samīkṣya cīraṁ saṁtrastā pr̥ṣatī vāgurām iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
sā vyapatrapamāṇeva pratigr̥hya ca durmanāḥ |
gandharvarājapratimaṁ bhartāram idam abravīt |
kathaṁ nu cīraṁ badhnanti munayo vanavāsinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
kr̥tvā kaṇṭhe ca sā cīram ekam ādāya pāṇinā |
tasthau hy akuṣalā tatra vrīḍitā janakātmaja ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tasyās tat kṣipram āgamya rāmo dharmabhr̥tāṁ varaḥ |
cīraṁ babandha sītāyāḥ kauśeyasyopari svayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tasyāṁ cīraṁ vasānāyāṁ nāthavatyām anāthavat |
pracukrośa janaḥ sarvo dhik tvāṁ daśarathaṁ tv iti ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sa niḥśvasyoṣṇam aikṣvākas tāṁ bhāryām idam abravīt |
kaikeyi kuśacīreṇa na sītā gantum arhati ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
nanu paryāptam etat te pāpe rāmavivāsanam |
kim ebhiḥ kr̥paṇair bhūyaḥ pātakair api te kr̥taiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
evaṁ bruvantaṁ pitaraṁ rāmaḥ saṁprasthito vanam |
avākśirasam āsīnam idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
iyaṁ dhārmika kausalyā mama mātā yaśasvinī |
vr̥ddhā cākṣudraśīlā ca na ca tvāṁ devagarhite ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
mayā vihīnāṁ varada prapannāṁ śokasāgaram |
adr̥ṣṭapūrvavyasanāṁ bhūyaḥ saṁmantum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
imāṁ mahendropamajātagarbhiṇīṁ; tathā vidhātuṁ janamīṁ mamārhasi |
yathā vanasthe mayi śokakarśitā; na jīvitaṁ nyasya yamakṣayaṁ vrajet ||

=== meaning ===

---
