=== Sarga 22 ===

---
1.
sāpanīya tam āyāsam upaspr̥śya jalaṁ śuci |
cakāra mātā rāmasya maṅgalāni manasvinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
svasti sādhyāś ca viśve ca marutaś ca maharṣayaḥ |
svasti dhātā vidhātā ca svasti pūṣā bhago ’ryamā ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
r̥tavaś caiva pakṣāś ca māsāḥ saṁvatsarāḥ kṣapāḥ |
dināni ca muhūrtāś ca svasti kurvantu te sadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
smr̥tir dhr̥tiś ca dharmaś ca pāntu tvāṁ putra sarvataḥ |
skandaś ca bhagavān devaḥ somaś ca sabr̥haspatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
saptarṣayo nāradaś ca te tvāṁ rakṣantu sarvataḥ |
nakṣatrāṇi ca sarvāṇi grahāś ca sahadevatāḥ |
mahāvanāni carato muniveṣasya dhīmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
plavagā vr̥ścikā daṁśā maśakāś caiva kānane |
sarīsr̥pāś ca kīṭāś ca mā bhūvan gahane tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
mahādvipāś ca siṁhāś ca vyāghrā r̥kṣāś ca daṁṣṭriṇaḥ |
mahiṣāḥ śr̥ṅgiṇo raudrā na te druhyantu putraka ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
nr̥māṁsabhojanā raudrā ye cānye sattvajātayaḥ |
mā ca tvāṁ hiṁsiṣuḥ putra mayā saṁpūjitās tv iha ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
āgamās te śivāḥ santu sidhyantu ca parākramāḥ |
sarvasaṁpattayo rāma svastimān gaccha putraka ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
svasti te ’stv āntarikṣebhyaḥ pārthivebhyaḥ punaḥ punaḥ |
sarvebhyaś caiva devebhyo ye ca te paripanthinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sarvalokaprabhur brahmā bhūtabhartā tatharṣayaḥ |
ye ca śeṣāḥ surās te tvāṁ rakṣantu vanavāsinam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
iti mālyaiḥ suragaṇān gandhaiś cāpi yaśasvinī |
stutibhiś cānurūpābhir ānarcāyatalocanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
yan maṅgalaṁ sahasrākṣe sarvadevanamaskr̥te |
vr̥tranāśe samabhavat tat te bhavatu maṅgalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
yan maṅgalaṁ suparṇasya vinatākalpayat purā |
amr̥taṁ prārthayānasya tat te bhavatu maṅgalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
oṣadhīṁ cāpi siddhārthāṁ viśalyakaraṇīṁ śubhām |
cakāra rakṣāṁ kausalyā mantrair abhijajāpa ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
ānamya mūrdhni cāghrāya pariṣvajya yaśasvinī |
avadat putra siddhārtho gaccha rāma yathāsukham ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
arogaṁ sarvasiddhārtham ayodhyāṁ punar āgatam |
paśyāmi tvāṁ sukhaṁ vatsa susthitaṁ rājaveśmani ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
mayārcitā devagaṇāḥ śivādayo; maharṣayo bhūtamahāsuroragāḥ |
abhiprayātasya vanaṁ cirāya te; hitāni kāṅkṣantu diśaś ca rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
itīva cāśrupratipūrṇalocanā; samāpya ca svastyayanaṁ yathāvidhi |
pradakṣiṇaṁ caiva cakāra rāghavaṁ; punaḥ punaś cāpi nipīḍya sasvaje ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tathā tu devyā sa kr̥tapradakṣiṇo; nipīḍya mātuś caraṇau punaḥ punaḥ |
jagāma sītānilayaṁ mahāyaśāḥ; sa rāghavaḥ prajvalitaḥ svayā śriyā ||

=== meaning ===

---
