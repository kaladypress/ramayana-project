=== Sarga 43 ===

---
1.
rāmo ’pi rātriśeṣeṇa tenaiva mahad antaram |
jagāma puruṣavyāghraḥ pitur ājñām anusmaran ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tathaiva gacchatas tasya vyapāyād rajanī śivā |
upāsya sa śivāṁ saṁdhyāṁ viṣayāntaṁ vyagāhata ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
grāmān vikr̥ṣṭasīmāṁs tān puṣpitāni vanāni ca |
paśyann atiyayau śīghraṁ śarair iva hayottamaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
śr̥ṇvan vāco manuṣyāṇāṁ grāmasaṁvāsavāsinām |
rājānaṁ dhig daśarathaṁ kāmasya vaśam āgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
hā nr̥śaṁsādya kaikeyī pāpā pāpānubandhinī |
tīkṣṇā saṁbhinnamaryādā tīkṣṇe karmaṇi vartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
yā putram īdr̥śaṁ rājñaḥ pravāsayati dhārmikam |
vana vāse mahāprājñaṁ sānukrośam atandritam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
etā vāco manuṣyāṇāṁ grāmasaṁvāsavāsinām |
śr̥ṇvann atiyayau vīraḥ kosalān kosaleśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tato vedaśrutiṁ nāma śivavārivahāṁ nadīm |
uttīryābhimukhaḥ prāyād agastyādhyuṣitāṁ diśam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
gatvā tu suciraṁ kālaṁ tataḥ śītajalāṁ nadīm |
gomatīṁ goyutānūpām atarat sāgaraṁgamām ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
gomatīṁ cāpy atikramya rāghavaḥ śīghragair hayaiḥ |
mayūrahaṁsābhirutāṁ tatāra syandikāṁ nadīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sa mahīṁ manunā rājñā dattām ikṣvākave purā |
sphītāṁ rāṣṭrāvr̥tāṁ rāmo vaidehīm anvadarśayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sūta ity eva cābhāṣya sārathiṁ tam abhīkṣṇaśaḥ |
haṁsamattasvaraḥ śrīmān uvāca puruṣarṣabhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
kadāhaṁ punar āgamya sarayvāḥ puṣpite vane |
mr̥gayāṁ paryāṭaṣyāmi mātrā pitrā ca saṁgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
nātyartham abhikāṅkṣāmi mr̥gayāṁ sarayūvane |
ratir hy eṣātulā loke rājarṣigaṇasaṁmatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa tam adhvānam aikṣvākaḥ sūtaṁ madhurayā girā |
taṁ tam artham abhipretya yayauvākyam udīrayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
