=== Sarga 81 ===

---
1.
guhasya vacanaṁ śrutvā bharato bhr̥śam apriyam |
dhyānaṁ jagāma tatraiva yatra tac chrutam apriyam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sukumāro mahāsattvaḥ siṁhaskandho mahābhujaḥ |
puṇḍarīka viśālākṣas taruṇaḥ priyadarśanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
pratyāśvasya muhūrtaṁ tu kālaṁ paramadurmanāḥ |
papāta sahasā totrair hr̥di viddha iva dvipaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tadavasthaṁ tu bharataṁ śatrughno ’nantara sthitaḥ |
pariṣvajya rurodoccair visaṁjñaḥ śokakarśitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tataḥ sarvāḥ samāpetur mātaro bharatasya tāḥ |
upavāsa kr̥śā dīnā bhartr̥vyasanakarśitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tāś ca taṁ patitaṁ bhūmau rudantyaḥ paryavārayan |
kausalyā tv anusr̥tyainaṁ durmanāḥ pariṣasvaje ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
vatsalā svaṁ yathā vatsam upagūhya tapasvinī |
paripapraccha bharataṁ rudantī śokalālasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
putravyādhir na te kaccic charīraṁ paribādhate |
adya rājakulasyāsya tvadadhīnaṁ hi jīvitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tvāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā putra jīvāmi rāme sabhrātr̥ke gate |
vr̥tte daśarathe rājñi nātha ekas tvam adya naḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
kaccin na lakṣmaṇe putra śrutaṁ te kiṁ cid apriyam |
putra vā hy ekaputrāyāḥ sahabhārye vanaṁ gate ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sa muhūrtaṁ samāśvasya rudann eva mahāyaśāḥ |
kausalyāṁ parisāntvyedaṁ guhaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
bhrātā me kvāvasad rātriṁ kva sītā kva ca lakṣmaṇaḥ |
asvapac chayane kasmin kiṁ bhuktvā guha śaṁsa me ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
so ’bravīd bharataṁ pr̥ṣṭo niṣādādhipatir guhaḥ |
yad vidhaṁ pratipede ca rāme priyahite ’tithau ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
annam uccāvacaṁ bhakṣyāḥ phalāni vividhāni ca |
rāmāyābhyavahārārthaṁ bahucopahr̥taṁ mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tat sarvaṁ pratyanujñāsīd rāmaḥ satyaparākramaḥ |
na hi tat pratyagr̥hṇāt sa kṣatradharmam anusmaran ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
na hy asmābhiḥ pratigrāhyaṁ sakhe deyaṁ tu sarvadā |
iti tena vayaṁ rājann anunītā mahātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
lakṣmaṇena samānītaṁ pītvā vāri mahāyaśāḥ |
aupavāsyaṁ tadākārṣīd rāghavaḥ saha sītayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tatas tu jalaśeṣeṇa lakṣmaṇo ’py akarot tadā |
vāg yatās te trayaḥ saṁdhyām upāsata samāhitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
saumitris tu tataḥ paścād akarot svāstaraṁ śubham |
svayam ānīya barhīṁṣi kṣipraṁ rāghava kāraṇāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tasmin samāviśad rāmaḥ svāstare saha sītayā |
prakṣālya ca tayoḥ pādāv apacakrāma lakṣmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
etat tad iṅgudīmūlam idam eva ca tat tr̥ṇam |
yasmin rāmaś ca sītā ca rātriṁ tāṁ śayitāv ubhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
niyamya pr̥ṣṭhe tu talāṅgulitravāñ; śaraiḥ supūrṇāv iṣudhī paraṁtapaḥ |
mahad dhanuḥ sajyam upohya lakṣmaṇo; niśām atiṣṭhat parito ’sya kevalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tatas tv ahaṁ cottamabāṇacāpadhr̥k; sthito ’bhavaṁ tatra sa yatra lakṣmaṇaḥ |
atandribhir jñātibhir āttakārmukair; mahendrakalpaṁ paripālayaṁs tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
