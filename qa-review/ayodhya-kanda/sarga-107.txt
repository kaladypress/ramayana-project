=== Sarga 107 ===

---
1.
tato nikṣipya mātr̥̄ḥ sa ayodhyāyāṁ dr̥ḍhavrataḥ |
bharataḥ śokasaṁtapto gurūn idam athābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
nandigrāmaṁ gamiṣyāmi sarvān āmantraye ’dya vaḥ |
tatra duḥkham idaṁ sarvaṁ sahiṣye rāghavaṁ vinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
gataś ca hi divaṁ rājā vanasthaś ca gurur mama |
rāmaṁ pratīkṣe rājyāya sa hi rājā mahāyaśāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
etac chrutvā śubhaṁ vākyaṁ bharatasya mahātmanaḥ |
abruvan mantriṇaḥ sarve vasiṣṭhaś ca purohitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
sadr̥śaṁ ślāghanīyaṁ ca yad uktaṁ bharata tvayā |
vacanaṁ bhrātr̥vātsalyād anurūpaṁ tavaiva tat ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
nityaṁ te bandhulubdhasya tiṣṭhato bhrātr̥sauhr̥de |
āryamārgaṁ prapannasya nānumanyeta kaḥ pumān ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
mantriṇāṁ vacanaṁ śrutvā yathābhilaṣitaṁ priyam |
abravīt sārathiṁ vākyaṁ ratho me yujyatām iti ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
prahr̥ṣṭavadanaḥ sarvā mātr̥̄ḥ samabhivādya saḥ |
āruroha rathaṁ śrīmāñ śatrughnena samanvitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
āruhya tu rathaṁ śīghraṁ śatrughnabharatāv ubhau |
yayatuḥ paramaprītau vr̥tau mantripurohitaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
agrato puravas tatra vasiṣṭha pramukhā dvijāḥ |
prayayuḥ prāṅmukhāḥ sarve nandigrāmo yato ’bhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
balaṁ ca tad anāhūtaṁ gajāśvarathasaṁkulam |
prayayau bharate yāte sarve ca puravāsinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
rathasthaḥ sa tu dharmātmā bharato bhrātr̥vatsalaḥ |
nandigrāmaṁ yayau tūrṇaṁ śirasy ādhāya pāduke ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tatas tu bharataḥ kṣipraṁ nandigrāmaṁ praviśya saḥ |
avatīrya rathāt tūrṇaṁ gurūn idam uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
etad rājyaṁ mama bhrātrā dattaṁ saṁnyāsavat svayam |
yogakṣemavahe ceme pāduke hemabhūṣite |
tam imaṁ pālayiṣyāmi rāghavāgamanaṁ prati ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
kṣipraṁ saṁyojayitvā tu rāghavasya punaḥ svayam |
caraṇau tau tu rāmasya drakṣyāmi sahapādukau ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tato nikṣiptabhāro ’haṁ rāghaveṇa samāgataḥ |
nivedya gurave rājyaṁ bhajiṣye guruvr̥ttitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
rāghavāya ca saṁnyāsaṁ dattveme varapāduke |
rājyaṁ cedam ayodhyāṁ ca dhūtapāpo bhavāmi ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
abhiṣikte tu kākutsthe prahr̥ṣṭamudite jane |
prītir mama yaśaś caiva bhaved rājyāc caturguṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
evaṁ tu vilapan dīno bharataḥ sa mahāyaśāḥ |
nandigrāme ’karod rājyaṁ duḥkhito mantribhiḥ saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
sa valkalajaṭādhārī muniveṣadharaḥ prabhuḥ |
nandigrāme ’vasad vīraḥ sasainyo bharatas tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
rāmāgamanam ākāṅkṣan bharato bhrātr̥vatsalaḥ |
bhrātur vacanakārī ca pratijñāpāragas tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
pāduke tv abhiṣicyātha nandigrāme ’vasat tadā |
bharataḥ śāsanaṁ sarvaṁ pādukābhyāṁ nyavedayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
