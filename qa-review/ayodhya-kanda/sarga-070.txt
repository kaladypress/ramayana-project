=== Sarga 70 ===

---
1.
tam evaṁ śokasaṁtaptaṁ bharataṁ kekayīsutam |
uvāca vadatāṁ śreṣṭho vasiṣṭhaḥ śreṣṭhavāg r̥ṣiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
alaṁ śokena bhadraṁ te rājaputra mahāyaśaḥ |
prāptakālaṁ narapateḥ kuru saṁyānam uttaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
vasiṣṭhasya vacaḥ śrutvā bharato dhāraṇāṁ gataḥ |
pretakāryāṇi sarvāṇi kārayām āsa dharmavit ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
uddhr̥taṁ tailasaṁkledāt sa tu bhūmau niveśitam |
āpītavarṇavadanaṁ prasuptam iva bhūmipam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
niveśya śayane cāgrye nānāratnapariṣkr̥te |
tato daśarathaṁ putro vilalāpa suduḥkhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
kiṁ te vyavasitaṁ rājan proṣite mayy anāgate |
vivāsya rāmaṁ dharmajñaṁ lakṣmaṇaṁ ca mahābalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
kva yāsyasi mahārāja hitvemaṁ duḥkhitaṁ janam |
hīnaṁ puruṣasiṁhena rāmeṇākliṣṭakarmaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
yogakṣemaṁ tu te rājan ko ’smin kalpayitā pure |
tvayi prayāte svas tāta rāme ca vanam āśrite ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
vidhavā pr̥thivī rājaṁs tvayā hīnā na rājate |
hīnacandreva rajanī nagarī pratibhāti mām ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
evaṁ vilapamānaṁ taṁ bharataṁ dīnamānasam |
abravīd vacanaṁ bhūyo vasiṣṭhas tu mahān r̥ṣiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
pretakāryāṇi yāny asya kartavyāni viśāmpateḥ |
tāny avyagraṁ mahābāho kriyatām avicāritam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tatheti bharato vākyaṁ vasiṣṭhasyābhipūjya tat |
r̥tvikpurohitācāryāṁs tvarayām āsa sarvaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
ye tv agrato narendrasya agny agārād bahiṣkr̥tāḥ |
r̥tvigbhir yājakaiś caiva te hriyante yathāvidhi ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
śibilāyām athāropya rājānaṁ gatacetanam |
bāṣpakaṇṭhā vimanasas tam ūhuḥ paricārakāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
hiraṇyaṁ ca suvarṇaṁ ca vāsāṁsi vividhāni ca |
prakiranto janā mārgaṁ nr̥pater agrato yayuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
candanāguruniryāsān saralaṁ padmakaṁ tathā |
devadārūṇi cāhr̥tya citāṁ cakrus tathāpare ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
gandhān uccāvacāṁś cānyāṁs tatra dattvātha bhūmipam |
tataḥ saṁveśayām āsuś citāmadhye tam r̥tvijaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tathā hutāśanaṁ hutvā jepus tasya tadartvijaḥ |
jaguś ca te yathāśāstraṁ tatra sāmāni sāmagāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
śibikābhiś ca yānaiś ca yathārhaṁ tasya yoṣitaḥ |
nagarān niryayus tatra vr̥ddhaiḥ parivr̥tās tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
prasavyaṁ cāpi taṁ cakrur r̥tvijo ’gnicitaṁ nr̥pam |
striyaś ca śokasaṁtaptāḥ kausalyā pramukhās tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
krauñcīnām iva nārīṇāṁ ninādas tatra śuśruve |
ārtānāṁ karuṇaṁ kāle krośantīnāṁ sahasraśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tato rudantyo vivaśā vilapya ca punaḥ punaḥ |
yānebhyaḥ sarayūtīram avaterur varāṅganāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
kr̥todakaṁ te bharatena sārdhaṁ; nr̥pāṅganā mantripurohitāś ca |
puraṁ praviśyāśruparītanetrā; bhūmau daśāhaṁ vyanayanta duḥkham ||

=== meaning ===

---
