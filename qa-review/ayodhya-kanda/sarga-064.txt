=== Sarga 64 ===

---
1.
bharate bruvati svapnaṁ dūtās te klāntavāhanāḥ |
praviśyāsahyaparikhaṁ ramyaṁ rājagr̥haṁ puram ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
samāgamya tu rājñā ca rājaputreṇa cārcitāḥ |
rājñaḥ pādau gr̥hītvā tu tam ūcur bharataṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
purohitas tvā kuśalaṁ prāha sarve ca mantriṇaḥ |
tvaramāṇaś ca niryāhi kr̥tyam ātyayikaṁ tvayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
atra viṁśatikoṭyas tu nr̥pater mātulasya te |
daśakoṭyas tu saṁpūrṇās tathaiva ca nr̥pātmaja ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
pratigr̥hya ca tat sarvaṁ svanuraktaḥ suhr̥jjane |
dūtān uvāca bharataḥ kāmaiḥ saṁpratipūjya tān ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
kaccit sukuśalī rājā pitā daśaratho mama |
kaccic cārāgatā rāme lakṣmaṇe vā mahātmani ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
āryā ca dharmaniratā dharmajñā dharmadarśinī |
arogā cāpi kausalyā mātā rāmasya dhīmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
kaccit sumitrā dharmajñā jananī lakṣmaṇasya yā |
śatrughnasya ca vīrasya sārogā cāpi madhyamā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
ātmakāmā sadā caṇḍī krodhanā prājñamāninī |
arogā cāpi kaikeyī mātā me kim uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
evam uktās tu te dūtā bharatena mahātmanā |
ūcuḥ saṁpraśritaṁ vākyam idaṁ taṁ bharataṁ tadā |
kuśalās te naravyāghra yeṣāṁ kuśalam icchasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
bharataś cāpi tān dūtān evam ukto ’bhyabhāṣata |
āpr̥cche ’haṁ mahārājaṁ dūtāḥ saṁtvarayanti mām ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
evam uktvā tu tān dūtān bharataḥ pārthivātmajaḥ |
dūtaiḥ saṁcodito vākyaṁ mātāmaham uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
rājan pitur gamiṣyāmi sakāśaṁ dūtacoditaḥ |
punar apy aham eṣyāmi yadā me tvaṁ smariṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
bharatenaivam uktas tu nr̥po mātāmahas tadā |
tam uvāca śubhaṁ vākyaṁ śirasy āghrāya rāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
gaccha tātānujāne tvāṁ kaikeyī suprajās tvayā |
mātaraṁ kuśalaṁ brūyāḥ pitaraṁ ca paraṁtapa ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
purohitaṁ ca kuśalaṁ ye cānye dvijasattamāḥ |
tau ca tāta maheṣvāsau bhrātaru rāmalakṣmaṇau ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tasmai hastyuttamāṁś citrān kambalān ajināni ca |
abhisatkr̥tya kaikeyo bharatāya dhanaṁ dadau ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
rukma niṣkasahasre dve ṣoḍaśāśvaśatāni ca |
satkr̥tya kaikeyī putraṁ kekayo dhanam ādiśat ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tathāmātyān abhipretān viśvāsyāṁś ca guṇānvitān |
dadāv aśvapatiḥ śīghraṁ bharatāyānuyāyinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
airāvatān aindraśirān nāgān vai priyadarśanān |
kharāñ śīghrān susaṁyuktān mātulo ’smai dhanaṁ dadau ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
antaḥpure ’tisaṁvr̥ddhān vyāghravīryabalānvitān |
daṁṣṭrāyudhān mahākāyāñ śunaś copāyanaṁ dadau ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
sa mātāmaham āpr̥cchya mātulaṁ ca yudhājitam |
ratham āruhya bharataḥ śatrughnasahito yayau ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
rathān maṇḍalacakrāṁś ca yojayitvā paraḥśatam |
uṣṭragośvakharair bhr̥tyā bharataṁ yāntam anvayuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
balena gupto bharato mahātmā; sahāryakasyātmasamair amātyaiḥ |
ādāya śatrughnam apetaśatrur; gr̥hād yayau siddha ivendralokāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
