=== Sarga 26 ===

---
1.
etat tu vacanaṁ śrutvā sītā rāmasya duḥkhitā |
prasaktāśrumukhī mandam idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
ye tvayā kīrtitā doṣā vane vastavyatāṁ prati |
guṇān ity eva tān viddhi tava snehapuraskr̥tān ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tvayā ca saha gantavyaṁ mayā gurujanājñayā |
tvadviyogena me rāma tyaktavyam iha jīvitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
na ca māṁ tvatsamīpastham api śaknoti rāghava |
surāṇām īśvaraḥ śakraḥ pradharṣayitum ojasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
patihīnā tu yā nārī na sā śakṣyati jīvitum |
kāmam evaṁvidhaṁ rāma tvayā mama vidarśitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
atha cāpi mahāprājña brāhmaṇānāṁ mayā śrutam |
purā pitr̥gr̥he satyaṁ vastavyaṁ kila me vane ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
lakṣaṇibhyo dvijātibhyaḥ śrutvāhaṁ vacanaṁ gr̥he |
vanavāsakr̥totsāhā nityam eva mahābala ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
ādeśo vanavāsasya prāptavyaḥ sa mayā kila |
sā tvayā saha tatrāhaṁ yāsyāmi priya nānyathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
kr̥tādeśā bhaviṣyāmi gamiṣyāmi saha tvayā |
kālaś cāyaṁ samutpannaḥ satyavāg bhavatu dvijaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
vanavāse hi jānāmi duḥkhāni bahudhā kila |
prāpyante niyataṁ vīra puruṣair akr̥tātmabhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
kanyayā ca pitur gehe vanavāsaḥ śruto mayā |
bhikṣiṇyāḥ sādhuvr̥ttāyā mama mātur ihāgrataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
prasāditaś ca vai pūrvaṁ tvaṁ vai bahuvidhaṁ prabho |
gamanaṁ vanavāsasya kāṅkṣitaṁ hi saha tvayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
kr̥takṣaṇāhaṁ bhadraṁ te gamanaṁ prati rāghava |
vanavāsasya śūrasya caryā hi mama rocate ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
śuddhātman premabhāvād dhi bhaviṣyāmi vikalmaṣā |
bhartāram anugacchantī bhartā hi mama daivatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
pretyabhāve ’pi kalyāṇaḥ saṁgamo me saha tvayā |
śrutir hi śrūyate puṇyā brāhmaṇānāṁ yaśasvinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
iha loke ca pitr̥bhir yā strī yasya mahāmate |
adbhir dattā svadharmeṇa pretyabhāve ’pi tasya sā ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
evam asmāt svakāṁ nārīṁ suvr̥ttāṁ hi pativratām |
nābhirocayase netuṁ tvaṁ māṁ keneha hetunā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
bhaktāṁ pativratāṁ dīnāṁ māṁ samāṁ sukhaduḥkhayoḥ |
netum arhasi kākutstha samānasukhaduḥkhinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
yadi māṁ duḥkhitām evaṁ vanaṁ netuṁ na cecchasi |
viṣam agniṁ jalaṁ vāham āsthāsye mr̥tyukāraṇāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
evaṁ bahuvidhaṁ taṁ sā yācate gamanaṁ prati |
nānumene mahābāhus tāṁ netuṁ vijanaṁ vanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
evam uktā tu sā cintāṁ maithilī samupāgatā |
snāpayantīva gām uṣṇair aśrubhir nayanacyutaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
cintayantīṁ tathā tāṁ tu nivartayitum ātmavān |
krodhāviṣṭāṁ tu vaidehīṁ kākutstho bahv asāntvayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
