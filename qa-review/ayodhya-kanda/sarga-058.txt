=== Sarga 58 ===

---
1.
tad ajñānān mahat pāpaṁ kr̥tvā saṁkulitendriyaḥ |
ekas tv acintayaṁ buddhyā kathaṁ nu sukr̥taṁ bhavet ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tatas taṁ ghaṭam ādaya pūrṇaṁ paramavāriṇā |
āśramaṁ tam ahaṁ prāpya yathākhyātapathaṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tatrāhaṁ durbalāv andhau vr̥ddhāv apariṇāyakau |
apaśyaṁ tasya pitarau lūnapakṣāv iva dvijau ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tannimittābhir āsīnau kathābhir aparikramau |
tām āśāṁ matkr̥te hīnāv udāsīnāv anāthavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
padaśabdaṁ tu me śrutvā munir vākyam abhāṣata |
kiṁ cirāyasi me putra pānīyaṁ kṣipram ānaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
yannimittam idaṁ tāta salile krīḍitaṁ tvayā |
utkaṇṭhitā te māteyaṁ praviśa kṣipram āśramam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
yad vyalīkaṁ kr̥taṁ putra mātrā te yadi vā mayā |
na tan manasi kartavyaṁ tvayā tāta tapasvinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tvaṁ gatis tv agatīnāṁ ca cakṣus tvaṁ hīnacakṣuṣām |
samāsaktās tvayi prāṇāḥ kiṁ cin nau nābhibhāṣase ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
munim avyaktayā vācā tam ahaṁ sajjamānayā |
hīnavyañjanayā prekṣya bhīto bhīta ivābruvam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
manasaḥ karma ceṣṭābhir abhisaṁstabhya vāgbalam |
ācacakṣe tv ahaṁ tasmai putravyasanajaṁ bhayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
kṣatriyo ’haṁ daśaratho nāhaṁ putro mahātmanaḥ |
sajjanāvamataṁ duḥkham idaṁ prāptaṁ svakarmajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
bhagavaṁś cāpahasto ’haṁ sarayūtīram āgataḥ |
jighāṁsuḥ śvāpadaṁ kiṁ cin nipāne vāgataṁ gajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tatra śruto mayā śabdo jale kumbhasya pūryataḥ |
dvipo ’yam iti matvā hi bāṇenābhihato mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
gatvā nadyās tatas tīram apaśyam iṣuṇā hr̥di |
vinirbhinnaṁ gataprāṇaṁ śayānaṁ bhuvi tāpasam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
bhagavañ śabdam ālakṣya mayā gajajighāṁsunā |
visr̥ṣṭo ’mbhasi nārācas tena te nihataḥ sutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sa coddhr̥tena bāṇena tatraiva svargam āsthitaḥ |
bhagavantāv ubhau śocann andhāv iti vilapya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
ajñānād bhavataḥ putraḥ sahasābhihato mayā |
śeṣam evaṁgate yat syāt tat prasīdatu me muniḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
sa tac chrutvā vacaḥ krūraṁ niḥśvasañ śokakarśitaḥ |
mām uvāca mahātejāḥ kr̥tāñjalim upasthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
yady etad aśubhaṁ karma na sma me kathayeḥ svayam |
phalen mūrdhā sma te rājan sadyaḥ śatasahasradhā ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
kṣatriyeṇa vadho rājan vānaprasthe viśeṣataḥ |
jñānapūrvaṁ kr̥taḥ sthānāc cyāvayed api vajriṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
ajñānād dhi kr̥taṁ yasmād idaṁ tenaiva jīvasi |
api hy adya kulaṁ nasyād rāghavāṇāṁ kuto bhavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
naya nau nr̥pa taṁ deśam iti māṁ cābhyabhāṣata |
adya taṁ draṣṭum icchāvaḥ putraṁ paścimadarśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
rudhireṇāvasitāṅgaṁ prakīrṇājinavāsasam |
śayānaṁ bhuvi niḥsaṁjñaṁ dharmarājavaśaṁ gatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
athāham ekas taṁ deśaṁ nītvā tau bhr̥śaduḥkhitau |
asparśayam ahaṁ putraṁ taṁ muniṁ saha bhāryayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tau putram ātmanaḥ spr̥ṣṭvā tam āsādya tapasvinau |
nipetatuḥ śarīre ’sya pitā cāsyedam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
na nv ahaṁ te priyaḥ putra mātaraṁ paśya dhārmika |
kiṁ nu nāliṅgase putra sukumāra vaco vada ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
kasya vāpararātre ’haṁ śroṣyāmi hr̥dayaṁgamam |
adhīyānasya madhuraṁ śāstraṁ vānyad viśeṣataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
ko māṁ saṁdhyām upāsyaiva snātvā hutahutāśanaḥ |
ślāghayiṣyaty upāsīnaḥ putraśokabhayārditam ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
kandamūlaphalaṁ hr̥tvā ko māṁ priyam ivātithim |
bhojayiṣyaty akarmaṇyam apragraham anāyakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
imām andhāṁ ca vr̥ddhāṁ ca mātaraṁ te tapasvinīm |
kathaṁ putra bhariṣyāmi kr̥paṇāṁ putragardhinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
tiṣṭha mā mā gamaḥ putra yamasya sadanaṁ prati |
śvo mayā saha gantāsi jananyā ca samedhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
ubhāv api ca śokārtāv anāthau kr̥paṇau vane |
kṣipram eva gamiṣyāvas tvayā hīnau yamakṣayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
tato vaivasvataṁ dr̥ṣṭvā taṁ pravakṣyāmi bhāratīm |
kṣamatāṁ dharmarājo me bibhr̥yāt pitarāv ayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
apāpo ’si yathā putra nihataḥ pāpakarmaṇā |
tena satyena gacchāśu ye lokāḥ śastrayodhinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
yānti śūrā gatiṁ yāṁ ca saṁgrāmeṣv anivartinaḥ |
hatās tv abhimukhāḥ putra gatiṁ tāṁ paramāṁ vraja ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
yāṁ gatiṁ sagaraḥ śaibyo dilīpo janamejayaḥ |
nahuṣo dhundhumāraś ca prāptās tāṁ gaccha putraka ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
yā gatiḥ sarvasādhūnāṁ svādhyāyāt patasaś ca yā |
bhūmidasyāhitāgneś ca ekapatnīvratasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
gosahasrapradātr̥̄ṇāṁ yā yā gurubhr̥tām api |
dehanyāsakr̥tāṁ yā ca tāṁ gatiṁ gaccha putraka |
na hi tv asmin kule jāto gacchaty akuśalāṁ gatim ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
evaṁ sa kr̥paṇaṁ tatra paryadevayatāsakr̥t |
tato ’smai kartum udakaṁ pravr̥ttaḥ saha bhāryayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
sa tu divyena rūpeṇa muniputraḥ svakarmabhiḥ |
āśvāsya ca muhūrtaṁ tu pitarau vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
sthānam asmi mahat prāpto bhavatoḥ paricāraṇāt |
bhavantāv api ca kṣipraṁ mama mūlam upaiṣyataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
evam uktvā tu divyena vimānena vapuṣmatā |
āruroha divaṁ kṣipraṁ muniputro jitendriyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
sa kr̥tvā tūdakaṁ tūrṇaṁ tāpasaḥ saha bhāryayā |
mām uvāca mahātejāḥ kr̥tāñjalim upasthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
adyaiva jahi māṁ rājan maraṇe nāsti me vyathā |
yac chareṇaikaputraṁ māṁ tvam akārṣīr aputrakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
tvayā tu yad avijñānān nihato me sutaḥ śuciḥ |
tena tvām abhiśapsyāmi suduḥkham atidāruṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
putravyasanajaṁ duḥkhaṁ yad etan mama sāmpratam |
evaṁ tvaṁ putraśokena rājan kālaṁ kariṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
tasmān mām āgataṁ bhadre tasyodārasya tad vacaḥ |
yad ahaṁ putraśokena saṁtyakṣyāmy adya jīvitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
yadi māṁ saṁspr̥śed rāmaḥ sakr̥d adyālabheta vā |
na tan me sadr̥śaṁ devi yan mayā rāghave kr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
cakṣuṣā tvāṁ na paśyāmi smr̥tir mama vilupyate |
dūtā vaivasvatasyaite kausalye tvarayanti mām ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
atas tu kiṁ duḥkhataraṁ yad ahaṁ jīvitakṣaye |
na hi paśyāmi dharmajñaṁ rāmaṁ satyaparākyamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
na te manuṣyā devās te ye cāruśubhakuṇḍalam |
mukhaṁ drakṣyanti rāmasya varṣe pañcadaśe punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
52.
padmapatrekṣaṇaṁ subhru sudaṁṣṭraṁ cārunāsikam |
dhanyā drakṣyanti rāmasya tārādhipanibhaṁ mukham ||

=== meaning ===

---
53.
sadr̥śaṁ śāradasyendoḥ phullasya kamalasya ca |
sugandhi mama nāthasya dhanyā drakṣyanti tanmukham ||

=== meaning ===

---
54.
nivr̥ttavanavāsaṁ tam ayodhyāṁ punar āgatam |
drakṣyanti sukhino rāmaṁ śukraṁ mārgagataṁ yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
55.
ayam ātmabhavaḥ śoko mām anātham acetanam |
saṁsādayati vegena yathā kūlaṁ nadīrayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
56.
hā rāghava mahābāho hā mamāyāsa nāśana |
rājā daśarathaḥ śocañ jīvitāntam upāgamat ||

=== meaning ===

---
57.
tathā tu dīnaṁ kathayan narādhipaḥ; priyasya putrasya vivāsanāturaḥ |
gate ’rdharātre bhr̥śaduḥkhapīḍitas; tadā jahau prāṇam udāradarśanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
