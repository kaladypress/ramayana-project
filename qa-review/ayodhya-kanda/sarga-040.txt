=== Sarga 40 ===

---
1.
anuraktā mahātmānaṁ rāmaṁ satyaparakramam |
anujagmuḥ prayāntaṁ taṁ vanavāsāya mānavāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
nivartite ’pi ca balāt suhr̥dvarge ca rājini |
naiva te saṁnyavartanta rāmasyānugatā ratham ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
ayodhyānilayānāṁ hi puruṣāṇāṁ mahāyaśāḥ |
babhūva guṇasaṁpannaḥ pūrṇacandra iva priyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
sa yācyamānaḥ kākutsthaḥ svābhiḥ prakr̥tibhis tadā |
kurvāṇaḥ pitaraṁ satyaṁ vanam evānvapadyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
avekṣamāṇaḥ sasnehaṁ cakṣuṣā prapibann iva |
uvāca rāmaḥ snehena tāḥ prajāḥ svāḥ prajā iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
yā prītir bahumānaś ca mayy ayodhyānivāsinām |
matpriyārthaṁ viśeṣeṇa bharate sā niveśyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sa hi kalyāṇa cāritraḥ kaikeyyānandavardhanaḥ |
kariṣyati yathāvad vaḥ priyāṇi ca hitāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
jñānavr̥ddho vayobālo mr̥dur vīryaguṇānvitaḥ |
anurūpaḥ sa vo bhartā bhaviṣyati bhayāpahaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sa hi rājaguṇair yukto yuvarājaḥ samīkṣitaḥ |
api cāpi mayā śiṣṭaiḥ kāryaṁ vo bhartr̥śāsanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
na ca tapyed yathā cāsau vanavāsaṁ gate mayi |
mahārājas tathā kāryo mama priyacikīrṣayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
yathā yathā dāśarathir dharmam evāsthito ’bhavat |
tathā tathā prakr̥tayo rāmaṁ patim akāmayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
bāṣpeṇa pihitaṁ dīnaṁ rāmaḥ saumitriṇā saha |
cakarṣeva guṇair baddhvā janaṁ punar ivāsanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
te dvijās trividhaṁ vr̥ddhā jñānena vayasaujasā |
vayaḥprakampaśiraso dūrād ūcur idaṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
vahanto javanā rāmaṁ bho bho jātyās turaṁgamāḥ |
nivartadhvaṁ na gantavyaṁ hitā bhavata bhartari |
upavāhyas tu vo bhartā nāpavāhyaḥ purād vanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
evam ārtapralāpāṁs tān vr̥ddhān pralapato dvijān |
avekṣya sahasā rāmo rathād avatatāra ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
padbhyām eva jagāmātha sasītaḥ sahalakṣmaṇaḥ |
saṁnikr̥ṣṭapadanyāso rāmo vanaparāyaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
dvijātīṁs tu padātīṁs tān rāmaś cāritravatsalaḥ |
na śaśāka ghr̥ṇācakṣuḥ parimoktuṁ rathena saḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
gacchantam eva taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rāmaṁ saṁbhrāntamānasāḥ |
ūcuḥ paramasaṁtaptā rāmaṁ vākyam idaṁ dvijāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
brāhmaṇyaṁ kr̥tsnam etat tvāṁ brahmaṇyam anugacchati |
dvijaskandhādhirūḍhās tvām agnayo ’py anuyānty amī ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
vājapeyasamutthāni chatrāṇy etāni paśya naḥ |
pr̥ṣṭhato ’nuprayātāni haṁsān iva jalātyaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
anavāptātapatrasya raśmisaṁtāpitasya te |
ebhiś chāyāṁ kariṣyāmaḥ svaiś chatrair vājapeyikaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
yā hi naḥ satataṁ buddhir vedamantrānusāriṇī |
tvatkr̥te sā kr̥tā vatsa vanavāsānusāriṇī ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
hr̥dayeṣv avatiṣṭhante vedā ye naḥ paraṁ dhanam |
vatsyanty api gr̥heṣv eva dārāś cāritrarakṣitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
na punar niścayaḥ kāryas tvadgatau sukr̥tā matiḥ |
tvayi dharmavyapekṣe tu kiṁ syād dharmam avekṣitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
yācito no nivartasva haṁsaśuklaśiroruhaiḥ |
śirobhir nibhr̥tācāra mahīpatanapāṁśulaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
bahūnāṁ vitatā yajñā dvijānāṁ ya ihāgatāḥ |
teṣāṁ samāptir āyattā tava vatsa nivartane ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
bhaktimanti hi bhūtāni jaṁgamājaṁgamāni ca |
yācamāneṣu teṣu tvaṁ bhaktiṁ bhakteṣu darśaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
anugaṁtum aśaktās tvāṁ mūlair uddhr̥tavegibhiḥ |
unnatā vāyuvegena vikrośantīva pādapāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
niśceṣṭāhārasaṁcārā vr̥kṣaikasthānaviṣṭhitāḥ |
pakṣiṇo ’pi prayācante sarvabhūtānukampinam ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
evaṁ vikrośatāṁ teṣāṁ dvijātīnāṁ nivartane |
dadr̥śe tamasā tatra vārayantīva rāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
