=== Sarga 82 ===

---
1.
tac chrutvā nipuṇaṁ sarvaṁ bharataḥ saha mantribhiḥ |
iṅgudīmūlam āgamya rāmaśayyām avekṣya tām ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
abravīj jananīḥ sarvā iha tena mahātmanā |
śarvarī śayitā bhūmāv idam asya vimarditam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
mahābhāgakulīnena mahābhāgena dhīmatā |
jāto daśarathenorvyāṁ na rāmaḥ svaptum arhati ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
ajinottarasaṁstīrṇe varāstaraṇasaṁcaye |
śayitvā puruṣavyāghraḥ kathaṁ śete mahītale ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
prāsādāgra vimāneṣu valabhīṣu ca sarvadā |
haimarājatabhaumeṣu varāstaraṇaśāliṣu ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
puṣpasaṁcayacitreṣu candanāgarugandhiṣu |
pāṇḍurābhraprakāśeṣu śukasaṁgharuteṣu ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
gītavāditranirghoṣair varābharaṇaniḥsvanaiḥ |
mr̥daṅgavaraśabdaiś ca satataṁ pratibodhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
bandibhir vanditaḥ kāle bahubhiḥ sūtamāgadhaiḥ |
gāthābhir anurūpābhiḥ stutibhiś ca paraṁtapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
aśraddheyam idaṁ loke na satyaṁ pratibhāti mā |
muhyate khalu me bhāvaḥ svapno ’yam iti me matiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
na nūnaṁ daivataṁ kiṁ cit kālena balavattaram |
yatra dāśarathī rāmo bhūmāv evaṁ śayīta saḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
videharājasya sutā sītā ca priyadarśanā |
dayitā śayitā bhūmau snuṣā daśarathasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
iyaṁ śayyā mama bhrātur idaṁ hi parivartitam |
sthaṇḍile kaṭhine sarvaṁ gātrair vimr̥ditaṁ tr̥ṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
manye sābharaṇā suptā sītāsmiñ śayane tadā |
tatra tatra hi dr̥śyante saktāḥ kanakabindavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
uttarīyam ihāsaktaṁ suvyaktaṁ sītayā tadā |
tathā hy ete prakāśante saktāḥ kauśeyatantavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
manye bhartuḥ sukhā śayyā yena bālā tapasvinī |
sukumārī satī duḥkhaṁ na vijānāti maithilī ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sārvabhauma kule jātaḥ sarvalokasukhāvahaḥ |
sarvalokapriyas tyaktvā rājyaṁ priyam anuttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
katham indīvaraśyāmo raktākṣaḥ priyadarśanaḥ |
sukhabhāgī ca duḥkhārhaḥ śayito bhuvi rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
siddhārthā khalu vaidehī patiṁ yānugatā vanam |
vayaṁ saṁśayitāḥ sarve hīnās tena mahātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
akarṇadhārā pr̥thivī śūnyeva pratibhāti mā |
gate daśarathe svarge rāme cāraṇyam āśrite ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
na ca prārthayate kaś cin manasāpi vasuṁdharām |
vane ’pi vasatas tasya bāhuvīryābhirakṣitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
śūnyasaṁvaraṇārakṣām ayantritahayadvipām |
apāvr̥tapuradvārāṁ rājadhānīm arakṣitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
aprahr̥ṣṭabalāṁ nyūnāṁ viṣamasthām anāvr̥tām |
śatravo nābhimanyante bhakṣyān viṣakr̥tān iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
adya prabhr̥ti bhūmau tu śayiṣye ’haṁ tr̥ṇeṣu vā |
phalamūlāśano nityaṁ jaṭācīrāṇi dhārayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tasyārtham uttaraṁ kālaṁ nivatsyāmi sukhaṁ vane |
taṁ pratiśravam āmucya nāsya mithyā bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
vasantaṁ bhrātur arthāya śatrughno mānuvatsyati |
lakṣmaṇena saha tv āryo ayodhyāṁ pālayiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
abhiṣekṣyanti kākutstham ayodhyāyāṁ dvijātayaḥ |
api me devatāḥ kuryur imaṁ satyaṁ manoratham ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
prasādyamānaḥ śirasā mayā svayaṁ; bahuprakāraṁ yadi na prapatsyate |
tato ’nuvatsyāmi cirāya rāghavaṁ; vane vasan nārhati mām upekṣitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
