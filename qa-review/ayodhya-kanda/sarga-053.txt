=== Sarga 53 ===

---
1.
mama tv aśvā nivr̥ttasya na prāvartanta vartmani |
uṣṇam aśru vimuñcanto rāme saṁprasthite vanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
ubhābhyāṁ rājaputrābhyām atha kr̥tvāham ajñalim |
prasthito ratham āsthāya tad duḥkham api dhārayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
guheva sārdhaṁ tatraiva sthito ’smi divasān bahūn |
āśayā yadi māṁ rāmaḥ punaḥ śabdāpayed iti ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
viṣaye te mahārāja rāmavyasanakarśitāḥ |
api vr̥kṣāḥ parimlānaḥ sapuṣpāṅkurakorakāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
na ca sarpanti sattvāni vyālā na prasaranti ca |
rāmaśokābhibhūtaṁ tan niṣkūjam abhavad vanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
līnapuṣkarapatrāś ca narendra kaluṣodakāḥ |
saṁtaptapadmāḥ padminyo līnamīnavihaṁgamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
jalajāni ca puṣpāṇi mālyāni sthalajāni ca |
nādya bhānty alpagandhīni phalāni ca yathā puram ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
praviśantam ayodhyāṁ māṁ na kaś cid abhinandati |
narā rāmam apaśyanto niḥśvasanti muhur muhuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
harmyair vimānaiḥ prāsādair avekṣya ratham āgatam |
hāhākārakr̥tā nāryo rāmādarśanakarśitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
āyatair vimalair netrair aśruvegapariplutaiḥ |
anyonyam abhivīkṣante vyaktam ārtatarāḥ striyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
nāmitrāṇāṁ na mitrāṇām udāsīnajanasya ca |
aham ārtatayā kaṁ cid viśeṣaṁ nopalakṣaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
aprahr̥ṣṭamanuṣyā ca dīnanāgaturaṁgamā |
ārtasvaraparimlānā viniḥśvasitaniḥsvanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
nirānandā mahārāja rāmapravrājanāturā |
kausalyā putrahīneva ayodhyā pratibhāti mā ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sūtasya vacanaṁ śrutvā vācā paramadīnayā |
bāṣpopahatayā rājā taṁ sūtam idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
kaikeyyā viniyuktena pāpābhijanabhāvayā |
mayā na mantrakuśalair vr̥ddhaiḥ saha samarthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
na suhr̥dbhir na cāmātyair mantrayitvā na naigamaiḥ |
mayāyam arthaḥ saṁmohāt strīhetoḥ sahasā kr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
bhavitavyatayā nūnam idaṁ vā vyasanaṁ mahat |
kulasyāsya vināśāya prāptaṁ sūta yadr̥cchayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
sūta yady asti te kiṁ cin mayāpi sukr̥taṁ kr̥tam |
tvaṁ prāpayāśu māṁ rāmaṁ prāṇāḥ saṁtvarayanti mām ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
yad yad yāpi mamaivājñā nivartayatu rāghavam |
na śakṣyāmi vinā rāma muhūrtam api jīvitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
atha vāpi mahābāhur gato dūraṁ bhaviṣyati |
mām eva ratham āropya śīghraṁ rāmāya darśaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
vr̥ttadaṁṣṭro maheṣvāsaḥ kvāsau lakṣmaṇapūrvajaḥ |
yadi jīvāmi sādhv enaṁ paśyeyaṁ saha sītayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
lohitākṣaṁ mahābāhum āmuktamaṇikuṇḍalam |
rāmaṁ yadi na paśyāmi gamiṣyāmi yamakṣayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
ato nu kiṁ duḥkhataraṁ yo ’ham ikṣvākunandanam |
imām avasthām āpanno neha paśyāmi rāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
hā rāma rāmānuja hā hā vaidehi tapasvinī |
na māṁ jānīta duḥkhena mriyamāṇam anāthavat |
dustaro jīvatā devi mayāyaṁ śokasāgaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
aśobhanaṁ yo ’ham ihādya rāghavaṁ; didr̥kṣamāṇo na labhe salakṣmaṇam |
itīva rājā vilapan mahāyaśāḥ; papāta tūrṇaṁ śayane sa mūrchitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
iti vilapati pārthive pranaṣṭe; karuṇataraṁ dviguṇaṁ ca rāmahetoḥ |
vacanam anuniśamya tasya devī; bhayam agamat punar eva rāmamātā ||

=== meaning ===

---
