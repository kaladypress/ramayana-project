=== Sarga 100 ===

---
1.
āśvāsayantaṁ bharataṁ jābālir brāhmaṇottamaḥ |
uvāca rāmaṁ dharmajñaṁ dharmāpetam idaṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sādhu rāghava mā bhūt te buddhir evaṁ nirarthakā |
prākr̥tasya narasyeva ārya buddhes tapasvinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
kaḥ kasya puruṣo bandhuḥ kim āpyaṁ kasya kena cit |
yad eko jāyate jantur eka eva vinaśyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tasmān mātā pitā ceti rāma sajjeta yo naraḥ |
unmatta iva sa jñeyo nāsti kā cid dhi kasya cit ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
yathā grāmāntaraṁ gacchan naraḥ kaś cit kva cid vaset |
utsr̥jya ca tam āvāsaṁ pratiṣṭhetāpare ’hani ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
evam eva manuṣyāṇāṁ pitā mātā gr̥haṁ vasu |
āvāsamātraṁ kākutstha sajjante nātra sajjanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
pitryaṁ rājyaṁ samutsr̥jya sa nārhati narottama |
āsthātuṁ kāpathaṁ duḥkhaṁ viṣamaṁ bahukaṇṭakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
samr̥ddhāyām ayodhyāyām ātmānam abhiṣecaya |
ekaveṇīdharā hi tvāṁ nagarī saṁpratīkṣate ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
rājabhogān anubhavan mahārhān pārthivātmaja |
vihara tvam ayodhyāyāṁ yathā śakras triviṣṭape ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
na te kaś cid daśarataḥs tvaṁ ca tasya na kaś cana |
anyo rājā tvam anyaś ca tasmāt kuru yad ucyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
gataḥ sa nr̥patis tatra gantavyaṁ yatra tena vai |
pravr̥ttir eṣā martyānāṁ tvaṁ tu mithyā vihanyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
arthadharmaparā ye ye tāṁs tāñ śocāmi netarān |
te hi duḥkham iha prāpya vināśaṁ pretya bhejire ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
aṣṭakā pitr̥daivatyam ity ayaṁ prasr̥to janaḥ |
annasyopadravaṁ paśya mr̥to hi kim aśiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
yadi bhuktam ihānyena deham anyasya gacchati |
dadyāt pravasataḥ śrāddhaṁ na tat pathy aśanaṁ bhavet ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
dānasaṁvananā hy ete granthā medhāvibhiḥ kr̥tāḥ |
yajasva dehi dīkṣasva tapas tapyasva saṁtyaja ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sa nāsti param ity eva kuru buddhiṁ mahāmate |
pratyakṣaṁ yat tad ātiṣṭha parokṣaṁ pr̥ṣṭhataḥ kuru ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
satāṁ buddhiṁ puraskr̥tya sarvalokanidarśinīm |
rājyaṁ tvaṁ pratigr̥hṇīṣva bharatena prasāditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
