=== Sarga 54 ===

---
1.
tato bhūtopasr̥ṣṭeva vepamānā punaḥ punaḥ |
dharaṇyāṁ gatasattveva kausalyā sūtam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
naya māṁ yatra kākutsthaḥ sītā yatra ca lakṣmaṇaḥ |
tān vinā kṣaṇam apy atra jīvituṁ notsahe hy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
nivartaya rathaṁ śīghraṁ daṇḍakān naya mām api |
atha tān nānugacchāmi gamiṣyāmi yamakṣayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
bāṣpavegaupahatayā sa vācā sajjamānayā |
idam āśvāsayan devīṁ sūtaḥ prāñjalir abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tyaja śokaṁ ca mohaṁ ca saṁbhramaṁ duḥkhajaṁ tathā |
vyavadhūya ca saṁtāpaṁ vane vatsyati rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
lakṣmaṇaś cāpi rāmasya pādau paricaran vane |
ārādhayati dharmajñaḥ paralokaṁ jitendriyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
vijane ’pi vane sītā vāsaṁ prāpya gr̥heṣv iva |
visrambhaṁ labhate ’bhītā rāme saṁnyastamānasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
nāsyā dainyaṁ kr̥taṁ kiṁ cit susūkṣmam api lakṣaye |
uciteva pravāsānāṁ vaidehī pratibhāti mā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
nagaropavanaṁ gatvā yathā sma ramate purā |
tathaiva ramate sītā nirjaneṣu vaneṣv api ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
bāleva ramate sītā bālacandranibhānanā |
rāmā rāme hy adīnātmā vijane ’pi vane satī ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tadgataṁ hr̥dayaṁ hy asyās tad adhīnaṁ ca jīvitam |
ayodhyāpi bhavet tasyā rāmahīnā tathā vanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
pathi pr̥cchati vaidehī grāmāṁś ca nagarāṇi ca |
gatiṁ dr̥ṣṭvā nadīnāṁ ca pādapān vividhān api ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
adhvanā vātavegena saṁbhrameṇātapena ca |
na hi gacchati vaidehyāś candrāṁśusadr̥śī prabhā ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sadr̥śaṁ śatapatrasya pūrṇacandropamaprabham |
vadanaṁ tadvadānyāyā vaidehyā na vikampate ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
alaktarasaraktābhāv alaktarasavarjitau |
adyāpi caraṇau tasyāḥ padmakośasamaprabhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
nūpurodghuṣṭaheleva khelaṁ gacchati bhāminī |
idānīm api vaidehī tadrāgā nyastabhūṣaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
gajaṁ vā vīkṣya siṁhaṁ vā vyāghraṁ vā vanam āśritā |
nāhārayati saṁtrāsaṁ bāhū rāmasya saṁśritā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
na śocyās te na cātmā te śocyo nāpi janādhipaḥ |
idaṁ hi caritaṁ loke pratiṣṭhāsyati śāśvatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
vidhūya śokaṁ parihr̥ṣṭamānasā; maharṣiyāte pathi suvyavasthitāḥ |
vane ratā vanyaphalāśanāḥ pituḥ; śubhāṁ pratijñāṁ paripālayanti te ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tathāpi sūtena suyuktavādinā; nivāryamāṇā sutaśokakarśitā |
na caiva devī virarāma kūjitāt; priyeti putreti ca rāghaveti ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
