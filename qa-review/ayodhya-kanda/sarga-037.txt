=== Sarga 37 ===

---
1.
yāvat tu niryatas tasya rajorūpam adr̥śyata |
naivekṣvākuvaras tāvat saṁjahārātmacakṣuṣī ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
yāvad rājā priyaṁ putraṁ paśyaty atyantadhārmikam |
tāvad vyavardhatevāsya dharaṇyāṁ putradarśane ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
na paśyati rajo ’py asya yadā rāmasya bhūmipaḥ |
tadārtaś ca viṣaṇṇaś ca papāta dharaṇītale ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tasya dakṣiṇam anvagāt kausalyā bāhum aṅganā |
vāmaṁ cāsyānvagāt pārśvaṁ kaikeyī bharatapriyā ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tāṁ nayena ca saṁpanno dharmeṇa nivayena ca |
uvāca rājā kaikeyīṁ samīkṣya vyathitendriyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
kaikeyi mā mamāṅgāni sprākṣīs tvaṁ duṣṭacāriṇī |
na hi tvāṁ draṣṭum icchāmi na bhāryā na ca bāndhavī ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
ye ca tvām upajīvanti nāhaṁ teṣāṁ na te mama |
kevalārthaparāṁ hi tvāṁ tyaktadharmāṁ tyajāmy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
agr̥hṇāṁ yac ca te pāṇim agniṁ paryaṇayaṁ ca yat |
anujānāmi tat sarvam asmim̐l loke paratra ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
bharataś cet pratītaḥ syād rājyaṁ prāpyedam avyayam |
yan me sa dadyāt pitrarthaṁ mā mā tad dattam āgamat ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
atha reṇusamudhvastaṁ tam utthāpya narādhipam |
nyavartata tadā devī kausalyā śokakarśitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
hatveva brāhmaṇaṁ kāmāt spr̥ṣṭvāgnim iva pāṇinā |
anvatapyata dharmātmā putraṁ saṁcintya tāpasam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
nivr̥tyaiva nivr̥tyaiva sīdato rathavartmasu |
rājño nātibabhau rūpaṁ grastasyāṁśumato yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
vilalāpa ca duḥkhārtaḥ priyaṁ putram anusmaran |
nagarāntam anuprāptaṁ buddhvā putram athābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
vāhanānāṁ ca mukhyānāṁ vahatāṁ taṁ mamātmajam |
padāni pathi dr̥śyante sa mahātmā na dr̥śyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa nūnaṁ kva cid evādya vr̥kṣamūlam upāśritaḥ |
kāṣṭhaṁ vā yadi vāśmānam upadhāya śayiṣyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
utthāsyati ca medinyāḥ kr̥paṇaḥ pāṁśuguṇṭhitaḥ |
viniḥśvasan prasravaṇāt kareṇūnām ivarṣabhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
drakṣyanti nūnaṁ puruṣā dīrghabāhuṁ vanecarāḥ |
rāmam utthāya gacchantaṁ lokanātham anāthavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
sakāmā bhava kaikeyi vidhavā rājyam āvasa |
na hi taṁ puruṣavyāghraṁ vinā jīvitum utsahe ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
ity evaṁ vilapan rājā janaughenābhisaṁvr̥taḥ |
apasnāta ivāriṣṭaṁ praviveśa purottamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
śūnyacatvaraveśmāntāṁ saṁvr̥tāpaṇadevatām |
klāntadurbaladuḥkhārtāṁ nātyākīrṇamahāpathām ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tām avekṣya purīṁ sarvāṁ rāmam evānucintayan |
vilapan prāviśad rājā gr̥haṁ sūrya ivāmbudam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
mahāhradam ivākṣobhyaṁ suparṇena hr̥toragam |
rāmeṇa rahitaṁ veśma vaidehyā lakṣmaṇena ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
kausalyāyā gr̥haṁ śīghraṁ rāma mātur nayantu mām |
iti bruvantaṁ rājānam anayan dvāradarśitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tatas tatra praviṣṭasya kausalyāyā niveśanam |
adhiruhyāpi śayanaṁ babhūva lulitaṁ manaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tac ca dr̥ṣṭvā mahārājo bhujam udyamya vīryavān |
uccaiḥ svareṇa cukrośa hā rāghava jahāsi mām ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
sukhitā bata taṁ kālaṁ jīviṣyanti narottamāḥ |
pariṣvajanto ye rāmaṁ drakṣyanti punar āgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
na tvāṁ paśyāmi kausalye sādhu māṁ pāṇinā spr̥śa |
rāmaṁ me ’nugatā dr̥ṣṭir adyāpi na nivartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
taṁ rāmam evānuvicintayantaṁ; samīkṣya devī śayane narendram |
upopaviśyādhikam ārtarūpā; viniḥśvasantī vilalāpa kr̥cchram ||

=== meaning ===

---
