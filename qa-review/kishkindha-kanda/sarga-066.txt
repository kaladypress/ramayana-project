=== Sarga 66 ===

---
1.
saṁstūyamāno hanumān vyavardhata mahābalaḥ |
samāvidhya ca lāṅgūlaṁ harṣāc ca balam eyivān ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tasya saṁstūyamānasya sarvair vānarapuṁgavaiḥ |
tejasāpūryamāṇasya rūpam āsīd anuttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
yathā vijr̥mbhate siṁho vivr̥ddho girigahvare |
mārutasyaurasaḥ putras tathā saṁprati jr̥mbhate ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
aśobhata mukhaṁ tasya jr̥mbhamāṇasya dhīmataḥ |
ambarīṣopamaṁ dīptaṁ vidhūma iva pāvakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
harīṇām utthito madhyāt saṁprahr̥ṣṭatanūruhaḥ |
abhivādya harīn vr̥ddhān hanumān idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
arujan parvatāgrāṇi hutāśanasakho ’nilaḥ |
balavān aprameyaś ca vāyur ākāśagocaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tasyāhaṁ śīghravegasya śīghragasya mahātmanaḥ |
mārutasyaurasaḥ putraḥ plavane nāsti me samaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
utsaheyaṁ hi vistīrṇam ālikhantam ivāmbaram |
meruṁ girim asaṁgena parigantuṁ sahasraśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
bāhuvegapraṇunnena sāgareṇāham utsahe |
samāplāvayituṁ lokaṁ saparvatanadīhradam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
mamorujaṅghāvegena bhaviṣyati samutthitaḥ |
saṁmūrchitamahāgrāhaḥ samudro varuṇālayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
pannagāśanam ākāśe patantaṁ pakṣisevitam |
vainateyam ahaṁ śaktaḥ parigantuṁ sahasraśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
udayāt prasthitaṁ vāpi jvalantaṁ raśmimālinam |
anastamitam ādityam abhigantuṁ samutsahe ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tato bhūmim asaṁspr̥śya punar āgantum utsahe |
pravegenaiva mahatā bhīmena plavagarṣabhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
utsaheyam atikrāntuṁ sarvān ākāśagocarān |
sāgaraṁ kṣobhayiṣyāmi dārayiṣyāmi medinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
parvatān kampayiṣyāmi plavamānaḥ plavaṁgamāḥ |
hariṣye coruvegena plavamāno mahārṇavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
latānāṁ vīrudhāṁ puṣpaṁ pādapānāṁ ca sarvaśaḥ |
anuyāsyati mām adya plavamānaṁ vihāyasā |
bhaviṣyati hi me panthāḥ svāteḥ panthā ivāmbare ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
carantaṁ ghoram ākāśam utpatiṣyantam eva ca |
drakṣyanti nipatantaṁ ca sarvabhūtāni vānarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
mahāmerupratīkāśaṁ māṁ drakṣyadhvaṁ plavaṁgamāḥ |
divam āvr̥tya gacchantaṁ grasamānam ivāmbaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
vidhamiṣyāmi jīmūtān kampayiṣyāmi parvatān |
sāgaraṁ kṣobhayiṣyāmi plavamānaḥ samāhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
vainateyasya vā śaktir mama vā mārutasya vā |
r̥te suparṇarājānaṁ mārutaṁ vā mahābalam |
na hi bhūtaṁ prapaśyāmi yo māṁ plutam anuvrajet ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
nimeṣāntaramātreṇa nirālambhanam ambaram |
sahasā nipatiṣyāmi ghanād vidyud ivotthitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
bhaviṣyati hi me rūpaṁ plavamānasya sāgaram |
viṣṇoḥ prakramamāṇasya tadā trīn vikramān iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
buddhyā cāhaṁ prapaśyāmi manaś ceṣṭā ca me tathā |
ahaṁ drakṣyāmi vaidehīṁ pramodadhvaṁ plavaṁgamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
mārutasya samo vege garuḍasya samo jave |
ayutaṁ yojanānāṁ tu gamiṣyāmīti me matiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
vāsavasya savajrasya brahmaṇo vā svayambhuvaḥ |
vikramya sahasā hastād amr̥taṁ tad ihānaye |
laṅkāṁ vāpi samutkṣipya gaccheyam iti me matiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tam evaṁ vānaraśreṣṭhaṁ garjantam amitaujasaṁ |
uvāca parisaṁhr̥ṣṭo jāmbavān harisattamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
vīra kesariṇaḥ putra vegavan mārutātmaja |
jñātīnāṁ vipulaṁ śokas tvayā tāta praṇāśitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tava kalyāṇarucayaḥ kapimukhyāḥ samāgatāḥ |
maṅgalaṁ kāryasiddhyarthaṁ kariṣyanti samāhitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
r̥ṣīṇāṁ ca prasādena kapivr̥ddhamatena ca |
gurūṇāṁ ca prasādena plavasva tvaṁ mahārṇavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
sthāsyāmaś caikapādena yāvadāgamanaṁ tava |
tvadgatāni ca sarveṣāṁ jīvitāni vanaukasām ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
tatas tu hariśārdūlas tān uvāca vanaukasaḥ |
neyaṁ mama mahī vegaṁ plavane dhārayiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
etāni hi nagasyāsya śilāsaṁkaṭaśālinaḥ |
śikharāṇi mahendrasya sthirāṇi ca mahānti ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
etāni mama niṣpeṣaṁ pādayoḥ patatāṁ varāḥ |
plavato dhārayiṣyanti yojanānām itaḥ śatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
tatas tu mārutaprakhyaḥ sa harir mārutātmajaḥ |
āruroha nagaśreṣṭhaṁ mahendram arimardanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
vr̥taṁ nānāvidhair vr̥kṣair mr̥gasevitaśādvalam |
latākusumasaṁbādhaṁ nityapuṣpaphaladrumam ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
siṁhaśārdūlacaritaṁ mattamātaṅgasevitam |
mattadvijagaṇodghuṣṭaṁ salilotpīḍasaṁkulam ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
mahadbhir ucchritaṁ śr̥ṅgair mahendraṁ sa mahābalaḥ |
vicacāra hariśreṣṭho mahendrasamavikramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
pādābhyāṁ pīḍitas tena mahāśailo mahātmanā |
rarāsa siṁhābhihato mahān matta iva dvipaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
mumoca salilotpīḍān viprakīrṇaśiloccayaḥ |
vitrastamr̥gamātaṅgaḥ prakampitamahādrumaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
nānāgandharvamithunaiḥ pānasaṁsargakarkaśaiḥ |
utpatadbhir vihaṁgaiś ca vidyādharagaṇair api ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
tyajyamānamahāsānuḥ saṁnilīnamahoragaḥ |
śailaśr̥ṅgaśilodghātas tadābhūt sa mahāgiriḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
niḥśvasadbhis tadā tais tu bhujagair ardhaniḥsr̥taiḥ |
sapatāka ivābhāti sa tadā dharaṇīdharaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
r̥ṣibhis trāsa saṁbhrāntais tyajyamānaḥ śiloccayaḥ |
sīdan mahati kāntāre sārthahīna ivādhvagaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
sa vegavān vegasamāhitātmā; haripravīraḥ paravīrahantā |
manaḥ samādhāya mahānubhāvo; jagāma laṅkāṁ manasā manasvī ||

=== meaning ===

---
