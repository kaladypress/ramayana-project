=== Sarga 56 ===

---
1.
śokād bhraṣṭasvaram api śrutvā te hariyūthapāḥ |
śraddadhur naiva tad vākyaṁ karmaṇā tasya śaṅkitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
te prāyam upaviṣṭās tu dr̥ṣṭvā gr̥dhraṁ plavaṁgamāḥ |
cakrur buddhiṁ tadā raudrāṁ sarvān no bhakṣayiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sarvathā prāyam āsīnān yadi no bhakṣayiṣyati |
kr̥takr̥tyā bhaviṣyāmaḥ kṣipraṁ siddhim ito gatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
etāṁ buddhiṁ tataś cakruḥ sarve te vānararṣabhāḥ |
avatārya gireḥ śr̥ṅgād gr̥dhram āhāṅgadas tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
babhūvur kṣarajo nāma vānarendraḥ pratāpavān |
mamāryaḥ pārthivaḥ pakṣin dhārmikau tasya cātmajau ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
sugrīvaś caiva valī ca putrāv oghabalāv ubhau |
loke viśrutakarmābhūd rājā vālī pitā mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
rājā kr̥tsnasya jagata ikṣvākūṇāṁ mahārathaḥ |
rāmo dāśarathiḥ śrīmān praviṣṭo daṇḍakāvanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
lakṣmaṇena saha bhrātrā vaidehyā cāpi bhāryayā |
pitur nideśanirato dharmyaṁ panthānam āśritaḥ |
tasya bhāryā janasthānād rāvaṇena hr̥tā balāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
rāmasya ca pitur mitraṁ jaṭāyur nāma gr̥dhrarāṭ |
dadarśa sītāṁ vaidehīṁ hriyamāṇāṁ vihāyasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
rāvaṇaṁ virathaṁ kr̥tvā sthāpayitvā ca maithilīm |
pariśrāntaś ca vr̥ddhaś ca rāvaṇena hato raṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
evaṁ gr̥dhro hatas tena rāvaṇena bahīyasā |
saṁskr̥taś cāpi rāmeṇa gataś ca gatim uttamām ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tato mama pitr̥vyeṇa sugrīveṇa mahātmanā |
cakāra rāghavaḥ sakhyaṁ so ’vadhīt pitaraṁ mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
māma pitrā viruddho hi sugrīvaḥ sacivaiḥ saha |
nihatya vālinaṁ rāmas tatas tam abhiṣecayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sa rājye sthāpitas tena sugrīvo vānareśvaraḥ |
rājā vānaramukhyānāṁ yena prasthāpitā vayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
evaṁ rāmaprayuktās tu mārgamāṇās tatas tataḥ |
vaidehīṁ nādhigacchāmo rātrau sūryaprabhām iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
te vayaṁ daṇdakāraṇyaṁ vicitya susamāhitāḥ |
ajñānāt tu praviṣṭāḥ sma dharaṇyā vivr̥taṁ bilam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
mayasya māyā vihitaṁ tad bilaṁ ca vicinvatām |
vyatītas tatra no māso yo rājñā sāmayaḥ kr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
te vayaṁ kapirājasya sarve vacanakāriṇaḥ |
kr̥tāṁ saṁsthām atikrāntā bhayāt prāyam upāsmahe ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
kruddhe tasmiṁs tu kākutsthe sugrīve ca salakṣmaṇe |
gatānām api sarveṣāṁ tatra no nāsti jīvitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
