=== Sarga 53 ===

---
1.
tathā bruvati tāre tu tārādhipativarcasi |
atha mene hr̥taṁ rājyaṁ hanumān aṅgadena tat ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
buddhyā hy aṣṭāṅgayā yuktaṁ caturbalasamanvitam |
caturdaśaguṇaṁ mene hanumān vālinaḥ sutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
āpūryamāṇaṁ śaśvac ca tejobalaparākramaiḥ |
śaśinaṁ śuklapakṣādau vardhamānam iva śriyā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
br̥haspatisamaṁ buddhyā vikrame sadr̥śaṁ pituḥ |
śuśrūṣamāṇaṁ tārasya śukrasyeva puraṁdaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
bhartur arthe pariśrāntaṁ sarvaśāstraviśāradam |
abhisaṁdhātum ārebhe hanumān aṅgadaṁ tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
sa caturṇām upāyānāṁ tr̥tīyam upavarṇayan |
bhedayām āsa tān sarvān vānarān vākyasaṁpadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
teṣu sarveṣu bhinneṣu tato ’bhīṣayad aṅgadam |
bhīṣaṇair bahubhir vākyaiḥ kopopāyasamanvitaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tvaṁ samarthataraḥ pitrā yuddhe tāreya vai dhuram |
dr̥ḍhaṁ dhārayituṁ śaktaḥ kapirājyaṁ yathā pitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
nityam asthiracittā hi kapayo haripuṁgava |
nājñāpyaṁ viṣahiṣyanti putradārān vinā tvayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tvāṁ naite hy anuyuñjeyuḥ pratyakṣaṁ pravadāmi te |
yathāyaṁ jāmbavān nīlaḥ suhotraś ca mahākapiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
na hy ahaṁ ta ime sarve sāmadānādibhir guṇaiḥ |
daṇḍena na tvayā śakyāḥ sugrīvād apakarṣitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
vigr̥hyāsanam apy āhur durbalena balīyasaḥ |
ātmarakṣākaras tasmān na vigr̥hṇīta durbalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
yāṁ cemāṁ manyase dhātrīm etad bilam iti śrutam |
etal lakṣmaṇabāṇānām īṣatkāryaṁ vidāraṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
svalpaṁ hi kr̥tam indreṇa kṣipatā hy aśaniṁ purā |
lakṣmaṇo niśitair bāṇair bhindyāt patrapuṭaṁ yathā |
lakṣmaṇasya ca nārācā bahavaḥ santi tadvidhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
avasthāne yadaiva tvam āsiṣyasi paraṁtapa |
tadaiva harayaḥ sarve tyakṣyanti kr̥taniścayāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
smarantaḥ putradārāṇāṁ nityodvignā bubhukṣitāḥ |
kheditā duḥkhaśayyābhis tvāṁ kariṣyanti pr̥ṣṭhataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
sa tvaṁ hīnaḥ suhr̥dbhiś ca hitakāmaiś ca bandhubhiḥ |
tr̥ṇād api bhr̥śodvignaḥ spandamānād bhaviṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
na ca jātu na hiṁsyus tvāṁ ghorā lakṣmaṇasāyakāḥ |
apavr̥ttaṁ jighāṁsanto mahāvegā durāsadāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
asmābhis tu gataṁ sārdhaṁ vinītavad upasthitam |
ānupūrvyāt tu sugrīvo rājye tvāṁ sthāpayiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
dharmakāmaḥ pitr̥vyas te prītikāmo dr̥ḍhavrataḥ |
śuciḥ satyapratijñaś ca nā tvāṁ jātu jighāṁsati ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
priyakāmaś ca te mātus tadarthaṁ cāsya jīvitam |
tasyāpatyaṁ ca nāsty anyat tasmād aṅgada gamyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
