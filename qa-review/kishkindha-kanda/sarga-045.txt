=== Sarga 45 ===

---
1.
gateṣu vānarendreṣu rāmaḥ sugrīvam abravīt |
kathaṁ bhavān vinājīte sarvaṁ vai maṇḍalaṁ bhuvaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sugrīvas tu tato rāmam uvāca praṇatātmavān |
śrūyatāṁ sarvam ākhyāsye vistareṇa nararṣabha ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
yadā tu dundubhiṁ nāma dānavaṁ mahiṣākr̥tim |
parikālayate vālī malayaṁ prati parvatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tadā viveśa mahiṣo malayasya guhāṁ prati |
viveśa vālī tatrāpi malayaṁ tajjighāṁsayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tato ’haṁ tatra nikṣipto guhād vārivinītavat |
na ca niṣkramate vālī tadā saṁvatsare gate ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tataḥ kṣatajavegena āpupūre tadā bilam |
tad ahaṁ vismito dr̥ṣṭvā bhrātr̥śokaviṣārditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
athāhaṁ kr̥tabuddhis tu suvyaktaṁ nihato guruḥ |
śilāparvatasaṁkāśā biladvāri mayā kr̥tā |
aśaknuvan niṣkramituṁ mahiṣo vinaśed iti ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tato ’ham āgāṁ kiṣkindhāṁ nirāśas tasya jīvite |
rājyaṁ ca sumahat prāptaṁ tārā ca rumayā saha |
mitraiś ca sahitas tatra vasāmi vigatajvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
ājagāma tato vālī hatvā taṁ dānavarṣabham |
tato ’ham adadāṁ rājyaṁ gauravād bhayayantritaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
sa māṁ jighāṁsur duṣṭātmā vālī pravyathitendriyaḥ |
parilākayate krodhād dhāvantaṁ sacivaiḥ saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tato ’haṁ vālinā tena sānubandhaḥ pradhāvitaḥ |
nadīś ca vividhāḥ paśyan vanāni nagarāṇi ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
ādarśatalasaṁkāśā tato vai pr̥thivī mayā |
alātacakrapratimā dr̥ṣṭā goṣpadavat tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tataḥ pūrvam ahaṁ gatvā dakṣiṇām aham āśritaḥ |
diśaṁ ca paścimāṁ bhūyo gato ’smi bhayaśaṅkitaḥ |
uttarāṁ tu diśaṁ yāntaṁ hanumān mām athābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
idānīṁ me smr̥taṁ rājan yathā vālī harīśvaraḥ |
mataṅgena tadā śapto hy asminn āśramamaṇḍale ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
praviśed yadi vā vālī mūrdhāsya śatadhā bhavet |
tatra vāsaḥ sukho ’smākaṁ nirudvigno bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tataḥ parvatam āsādya r̥śyamūkaṁ nr̥pātmaja |
na viveśa tadā vālī mataṅgasya bhayāt tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
evaṁ mayā tadā rājan pratyakṣam upalakṣitam |
pr̥thivīmaṇḍalaṁ kr̥tsnaṁ guhām asmy āgatas tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
