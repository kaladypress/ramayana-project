=== Sarga 37 ===

---
1.
pratigr̥hya ca tat sarvam upānayam upāhr̥tam |
vānarān sāntvayitvā ca sarvān eva vyasarjayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
visarjayitvā sa harīñ śūrāṁs tān kr̥takarmaṇaḥ |
mene kr̥tārtham ātmānaṁ rāghavaṁ ca mahābalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sa lakṣmaṇo bhīmabalaṁ sarvavānarasattamam |
abravīt praśritaṁ vākyaṁ sugrīvaṁ saṁpraharṣayan |
kiṣkindhāyā viniṣkrāma yadi te saumya rocate ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā lakṣmaṇasya subhāṣitam |
sugrīvaḥ paramaprīto vākyam etad uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
evaṁ bhavatu gacchāmaḥ stheyaṁ tvacchāsane mayā |
tam evam uktvā sugrīvo lakṣmaṇaṁ śubhalakṣmaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
visarjayām āsa tadā tārām anyāś ca yoṣitaḥ |
etety uccair harivarān sugrīvaḥ samudāharat ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā harayaḥ śīghram āyayuḥ |
baddhāñjalipuṭāḥ sarve ye syuḥ strīdarśanakṣamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tān uvāca tataḥ prāptān rājārkasadr̥śaprabhaḥ |
upasthāpayata kṣipraṁ śibikāṁ mama vānarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
śrutvā tu vacanaṁ tasya harayaḥ śīghravikramāḥ |
samupasthāpayām āsuḥ śibikāṁ priyadarśanām ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tām upasthāpitāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā śibikāṁ vānarādhipaḥ |
lakṣmaṇāruhyatāṁ śīghram iti saumitrim abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
ity uktvā kāñcanaṁ yānaṁ sugrīvaḥ sūryasaṁnibham |
br̥hadbhir haribhir yuktam āruroha salakṣmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
pāṇḍureṇātapatreṇa dhriyamāṇena mūrdhani |
śuklaiś ca bālavyajanair dhūyamānaiḥ samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
śaṅkhabherīninādaiś ca bandibhiś cābhivanditaḥ |
niryayau prāpya sugrīvo rājyaśriyam anuttamām ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sa vānaraśatais tīṣkṇair bahubhiḥ śastrapāṇibhiḥ |
parikīrṇo yayau tatra yatra rāmo vyavasthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa taṁ deśam anuprāpya śreṣṭhaṁ rāmaniṣevitam |
avātaran mahātejāḥ śibikāyāḥ salakṣmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
āsādya ca tato rāmaṁ kr̥tāñjalipuṭo ’bhavat |
kr̥tāñjalau sthite tasmin vānarāś cabhavaṁs tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
taṭākam iva tad dr̥ṣṭvā rāmaḥ kuḍmalapaṅkajam |
vānarāṇāṁ mahat sainyaṁ sugrīve prītimān abhūt ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
pādayoḥ patitaṁ mūrdhnā tam utthāpya harīśvaram |
preṁṇā ca bahumānāc ca rāghavaḥ pariṣasvaje ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
pariṣvajya ca dharmātmā niṣīdeti tato ’bravīt |
taṁ niṣaṇṇaṁ tato dr̥ṣṭvā kṣitau rāmo ’bravīd vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
dharmam arthaṁ ca kāmaṁ ca kāle yas tu niṣevate |
vibhajya satataṁ vīra sa rājā harisattama ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
hitvā dharmaṁ tathārthaṁ ca kāmaṁ yas tu niṣevate |
sa vr̥kṣāgre yathā suptaḥ patitaḥ pratibudhyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
amitrāṇāṁ vadhe yukto mitrāṇāṁ saṁgrahe rataḥ |
trivargaphalabhoktā tu rājā dharmeṇa yujyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
udyogasamayas tv eṣa prāptaḥ śatruvināśana |
saṁcintyatāṁ hi piṅgeśa haribhiḥ saha mantribhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
evam uktas tu sugrīvo rāmaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
pranaṣṭā śrīś ca kīrtiś ca kapirājyaṁ ca śāśvatam |
tvatprasādān mahābāho punaḥ prāptam idaṁ mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tava devaprasadāc ca bhrātuś ca jayatāṁ vara |
kr̥taṁ na pratikuryād yaḥ puruṣāṇāṁ sa dūṣakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
ete vānaramukhyāś ca śataśaḥ śatrusūdana |
prāptāś cādāya balinaḥ pr̥thivyāṁ sarvavānarān ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
r̥kṣāś cāvahitāḥ śūrā golāṅgūlāś ca rāghava |
kāntāra vanadurgāṇām abhijñā ghoradarśanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
devagandharvaputrāś ca vānarāḥ kāmarūpiṇaḥ |
svaiḥ svaiḥ parivr̥tāḥ sainyair vartante pathi rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
śataiḥ śatasahasraiś ca koṭibhiś ca plavaṁgamāḥ |
ayutaiś cāvr̥tā vīrā śaṅkubhiś ca paraṁtapa ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
arbudair arbudaśatair madhyaiś cāntaiś ca vānarāḥ |
samudraiś ca parārdhaiś ca harayo hariyūthapāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
āgamiṣyanti te rājan mahendrasamavikramāḥ |
merumandarasaṁkāśā vindhyamerukr̥tālayāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
te tvām abhigamiṣyanti rākṣasaṁ ye sabāndhavam |
nihatya rāvaṇaṁ saṁkhye hy ānayiṣyanti maithilīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
tatas tam udyogam avekṣya buddhimān; haripravīrasya nideśavartinaḥ |
babhūva harṣād vasudhādhipātmajaḥ; prabuddhanīlotpalatulyadarśanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
