=== Sarga 33 ===

---
1.
tam apratihataṁ kruddhaṁ praviṣṭaṁ puruṣarṣabham |
sugrīvo lakṣmaṇaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā babhūva vyathitendriyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
kruddhaṁ niḥśvasamānaṁ taṁ pradīptam iva tejasā |
bhrātur vyasanasaṁtaptaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā daśarathātmajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
utpapāta hariśreṣṭho hitvā sauvarṇam āsanam |
mahān mahendrasya yathā svalaṁkr̥ta iva dhvajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
utpatantam anūtpetū rumāprabhr̥tayaḥ striyaḥ |
sugrīvaṁ gagane pūrṇaṁ candraṁ tārāgaṇā iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
saṁraktanayanaḥ śrīmān vicacāla kr̥tāñjaliḥ |
babhūvāvasthitas tatra kalpavr̥kṣo mahān iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
rumā dvitīyaṁ sugrīvaṁ nārīmadhyagataṁ sthitam |
abravīl lakṣmaṇaḥ kruddhaḥ satāraṁ śaśinaṁ yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sattvābhijanasaṁpannaḥ sānukrośo jitendriyaḥ |
kr̥tajñaḥ satyavādī ca rājā loke mahīyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
yas tu rājā sthito ’dharme mitrāṇām upakāriṇām |
mithyāpratijñāṁ kurute ko nr̥śaṁsataras tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
śatam aśvānr̥te hanti sahasraṁ tu gavānr̥te |
ātmānaṁ svajanaṁ hanti puruṣaḥ puruṣānr̥te ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
pūrvaṁ kr̥tārtho mitrāṇāṁ na tat pratikaroti yaḥ |
kr̥taghnaḥ sarvabhūtānāṁ sa vadhyaḥ plavageśvara ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
gīto ’yaṁ brahmaṇā ślokaḥ sarvalokanamaskr̥taḥ |
dr̥ṣṭvā kr̥taghnaṁ kruddhena taṁ nibodha plavaṁgama ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
brahmaghne ca surāpe ca core bhagnavrate tathā |
niṣkr̥tir vihitā sadbhiḥ kr̥taghne nāsti niṣkr̥tiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
anāryas tvaṁ kr̥taghnaś ca mithyāvādī ca vānara |
pūrvaṁ kr̥tārtho rāmasya na tat pratikaroṣi yat ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
nanu nāma kr̥tārthena tvayā rāmasya vānara |
sītāyā mārgaṇe yatnaḥ kartavyaḥ kr̥tam icchatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa tvaṁ grāmyeṣu bhogeṣu sakto mithyā pratiśravaḥ |
na tvāṁ rāmo vijānīte sarpaṁ maṇḍūkarāviṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
mahābhāgena rāmeṇa pāpaḥ karuṇavedinā |
harīṇāṁ prāpito rājyaṁ tvaṁ durātmā mahātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
kr̥taṁ cen nābhijānīṣe rāmasyākliṣṭakarmaṇaḥ |
sadyas tvaṁ niśitair bāṇair hato drakṣyasi vālinam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
na ca saṁkucitaḥ panthā yena vālī hato gataḥ |
samaye tiṣṭha sugrīva mā vālipatham anvagāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
na nūnam ikṣvākuvarasya kārmukāc; cyutāñ śarān paśyasi vajrasaṁnibhān |
tataḥ sukhaṁ nāma niṣevase sukhī; na rāmakāryaṁ manasāpy avekṣase ||

=== meaning ===

---
