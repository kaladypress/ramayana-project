=== Sarga 62 ===

---
1.
etair anyaiś ca bahubhir vākyair vākyaviśāradaḥ |
māṁ praśasyābhyanujñāpya praviṣṭaḥ sa svam āśramam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
kandarāt tu visarpitvā parvatasya śanaiḥ śanaiḥ |
ahaṁ vindhyaṁ samāruhya bhavataḥ pratipālaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
adya tv etasya kālasya sāgraṁ varṣaśataṁ gatam |
deśakālapratīkṣo ’smi hr̥di kr̥tvā muner vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
mahāprasthānam āsādya svargate tu niśākare |
māṁ nirdahati saṁtāpo vitarkair bahubhir vr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
utthitāṁ maraṇe buddhiṁ muni vākyair nivartaye |
buddhir yā tena me dattā prāṇasaṁrakṣaṇāya tu |
sā me ’panayate duḥkhaṁ dīptevāgniśikhā tamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
budhyatā ca mayā vīryaṁ rāvaṇasya durātmanaḥ |
putraḥ saṁtarjito vāgbhir na trātā maithilī katham ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tasyā vilapitaṁ śrutvā tau ca sītā vinākr̥tau |
na me daśarathasnehāt putreṇotpāditaṁ priyam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tasya tv evaṁ bruvāṇasya saṁpāter vānaraiḥ saha |
utpetatus tadā pakṣau samakṣaṁ vanacāriṇām ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sa dr̥ṣṭvā svāṁ tanuṁ pakṣair udgatair aruṇacchadaiḥ |
praharṣam atulaṁ lebhe vānarāṁś cedam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
niśākarasya maharṣeḥ prabhāvād amitātmanaḥ |
ādityaraśminirdagdhau pakṣau me punar utthitau ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
yauvane vartamānasya mamāsīd yaḥ parākramaḥ |
tam evādyāvagacchāmi balaṁ pauruṣam eva ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sarvathā kriyatāṁ yatnaḥ sītām adhigamiṣyatha |
pakṣalābho mamāyaṁ vaḥ siddhipratyaya kārakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
ity uktvā tān harīn sarvān saṁpātiḥ patatāṁ varaḥ |
utpapāta gireḥ śr̥ṅgāj jijñāsuḥ khagamo gatim ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā prītisaṁhr̥ṣṭamānasāḥ |
babhūvur hariśārdūlā vikramābhyudayonmukhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
atha pavanasamānavikramāḥ; plavagavarāḥ pratilabdha pauruṣāḥ |
abhijidabhimukhāṁ diśaṁ yayur; janakasutā parimārgaṇonmukhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
