=== Sarga 14 ===

---
1.
sarve te tvaritaṁ gatvā kiṣkindhāṁ vālipālitām |
vr̥kṣair ātmānam āvr̥tya vyatiṣṭhan gahane vane ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
vicārya sarvato dr̥ṣṭiṁ kānane kānanapriyaḥ |
sugrīvo vipulagrīvaḥ krodham āhārayad bhr̥śam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tataḥ sa ninadaṁ ghoraṁ kr̥tvā yuddhāya cāhvayat |
parivāraiḥ parivr̥to nādair bhindann ivāmbaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
atha bālārkasadr̥śo dr̥ptasiṁhagatis tadā |
dr̥ṣṭvā rāmaṁ kriyādakṣaṁ sugrīvo vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
harivāgurayā vyāptaṁ taptakāñcanatoraṇām |
prāptāḥ sma dhvajayantrāḍhyāṁ kiṣkindhāṁ vālinaḥ purīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
pratijñā yā tvayā vīra kr̥tā vālivadhe purā |
saphalāṁ tāṁ kuru kṣipraṁ latāṁ kāla ivāgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
evam uktas tu dharmātmā sugrīveṇa sa rāghavaḥ |
tam athovāca sugrīvaṁ vacanaṁ śatrusūdanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
kr̥tābhijñāna cihnas tvam anayā gajasāhvayā |
viparīta ivākāśe sūryo nakṣatra mālayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
adya vālisamutthaṁ te bhayaṁ vairaṁ ca vānara |
ekenāhaṁ pramokṣyāmi bāṇamokṣeṇa saṁyuge ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
mama darśaya sugrīvavairiṇaṁ bhrātr̥rūpiṇam |
vālī vinihato yāvad vane pāṁsuṣu veṣṭate ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
yadi dr̥ṣṭipathaṁ prāpto jīvan sa vinivartate |
tato doṣeṇa mā gacchet sadyo garhec ca mā bhavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
pratyakṣaṁ sapta te sālā mayā bāṇena dāritāḥ |
tato vetsi balenādya bālinaṁ nihataṁ mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
anr̥taṁ noktapūrvaṁ me vīra kr̥cchre ’pi tiṣṭhatā |
dharmalobhaparītena na ca vakṣye kathaṁ cana ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
saphalāṁ ca kariṣyāmi pratijñāṁ jahi saṁbhramam |
prasūtaṁ kalamaṁ kṣetre varṣeṇeva śatakratuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tadāhvānanimittaṁ tvaṁ vālino hemamālinaḥ |
sugrīva kuru taṁ śabdaṁ niṣpated yena vānaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
jitakāśī jayaślāghī tvayā cādharṣitaḥ purāt |
niṣpatiṣyaty asaṁgena vālī sa priyasaṁyugaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
ripūṇāṁ dharṣaṇaṁ śūrā marṣayanti na saṁyuge |
jānantas tu svakaṁ vīryaṁ strīsamakṣaṁ viśeṣataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
sa tu rāmavacaḥ śrutvā sugrīvo hemapiṅgalaḥ |
nanarda krūranādena vinirbhindann ivāmbaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tasya śabdena vitrastā gāvo yānti hataprabhāḥ |
rājadoṣaparāmr̥ṣṭāḥ kulastriya ivākulāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
dravanti ca mr̥gāḥ śīghraṁ bhagnā iva raṇe hayāḥ |
patanti ca khagā bhūmau kṣīṇapuṇyā iva grahāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tataḥ sa jīmūtagaṇapraṇādo; nādaṁ vyamuñcat tvarayā pratītaḥ |
sūryātmajaḥ śauryavivr̥ddhatejāḥ; saritpatir vānilacañcalormiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
