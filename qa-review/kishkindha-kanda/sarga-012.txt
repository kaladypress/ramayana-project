=== Sarga 12 ===

---
1.
etac ca vacanaṁ śrutvā sugrīveṇa subhāṣitam |
pratyayārthaṁ mahātejā rāmo jagrāha kārmukam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sa gr̥hītvā dhanur ghoraṁ śaram ekaṁ ca mānadaḥ |
sālān uddiśya cikṣepa jyāsvanaiḥ pūrayan diśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sa visr̥ṣṭo balavatā bāṇaḥ svarṇapariṣkr̥taḥ |
bhittvā sālān giriprasthe sapta bhūmiṁ viveśa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
praviṣṭas tu muhūrtena rasāṁ bhittvā mahājavaḥ |
niṣpatya ca punas tūrṇaṁ svatūṇīṁ praviveśa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tān dr̥ṣṭvā sapta nirbhinnān sālān vānarapuṁgavaḥ |
rāmasya śaravegena vismayaṁ paramaṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
sa mūrdhnā nyapatad bhūmau pralambīkr̥tabhūṣaṇaḥ |
sugrīvaḥ paramaprīto rāghavāya kr̥tāñjaliḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
idaṁ covāca dharmajñaṁ karmaṇā tena harṣitaḥ |
rāmaṁ sarvāstraviduṣāṁ śreṣṭhaṁ śūram avasthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sendrān api surān sarvāṁs tvaṁ bāṇaiḥ puruṣarṣabha |
samarthaḥ samare hantuṁ kiṁ punar vālinaṁ prabho ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
yena sapta mahāsālā girir bhūmiś ca dāritāḥ |
bāṇenaikena kākutstha sthātā te ko raṇāgrataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
adya me vigataḥ śokaḥ prītir adya parā mama |
suhr̥daṁ tvāṁ samāsādya mahendravaruṇopamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tam adyaiva priyārthaṁ me vairiṇaṁ bhrātr̥rūpiṇam |
vālinaṁ jahi kākutstha mayā baddho ’yam añjaliḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tato rāmaḥ pariṣvajya sugrīvaṁ priyadarśanam |
pratyuvāca mahāprājño lakṣmaṇānumataṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
asmād gacchāma kiṣkindhāṁ kṣipraṁ gaccha tvam agrataḥ |
gatvā cāhvaya sugrīva vālinaṁ bhrātr̥gandhinam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sarve te tvaritaṁ gatvā kiṣkindhāṁ vālinaḥ purīm |
vr̥kṣair ātmānam āvr̥tya vyatiṣṭhan gahane vane ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sugrīvo vyanadad ghoraṁ vālino hvānakāraṇāt |
gāḍhaṁ parihito vegān nādair bhindann ivāmbaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
taṁ śrutvā ninadaṁ bhrātuḥ kruddho vālī mahābalaḥ |
niṣpapāta susaṁrabdho bhāskaro ’stataṭād iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tataḥ sutumulaṁ yuddhaṁ vālisugrīvayor abhūt |
gagane grahayor ghoraṁ budhāṅgārakayor iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
talair aśanikalpaiś ca vajrakalpaiś ca muṣṭibhiḥ |
jaghnatuḥ samare ’nyonyaṁ bhrātarau krodhamūrchitau ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tato rāmo dhanuṣpāṇis tāv ubhau samudīkṣya tu |
anyonyasadr̥śau vīrāv ubhau devāv ivāśvinau ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
yan nāvagacchat sugrīvaṁ vālinaṁ vāpi rāghavaḥ |
tato na kr̥tavān buddhiṁ moktum antakaraṁ śaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
etasminn antare bhagnaḥ sugrīvas tena vālinā |
apaśyan rāghavaṁ nātham r̥śyamūkaṁ pradudruve ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
klānto rudhirasiktāṅgaḥ prahārair jarjarīkr̥taḥ |
vālinābhidrutaḥ krodhāt praviveśa mahāvanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
taṁ praviṣṭaṁ vanaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā vālī śāpabhayāt tataḥ |
mukto hy asi tvam ity uktvā sa nivr̥tto mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
rāghavo ’pi saha bhrātrā saha caiva hanūmatā |
tad eva vanam āgacchat sugrīvo yatra vānaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
taṁ samīkṣyāgataṁ rāmaṁ sugrīvaḥ sahalakṣmaṇam |
hrīmān dīnam uvācedaṁ vasudhām avalokayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
āhvayasveti mām uktvā darśayitvā ca vikramam |
vairiṇā ghātayitvā ca kim idānīṁ tvayā kr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tām eva velāṁ vaktavyaṁ tvayā rāghava tattvataḥ |
vālinaṁ na nihanmīti tato nāham ito vraje ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tasya caivaṁ bruvāṇasya sugrīvasya mahātmanaḥ |
karuṇaṁ dīnayā vācā rāghavaḥ punar abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
sugrīva śrūyatāṁ tātaḥ krodhaś ca vyapanīyatām |
kāraṇaṁ yena bāṇo ’yaṁ na mayā sa visarjitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
alaṁkāreṇa veṣeṇa pramāṇena gatena ca |
tvaṁ ca sugrīva vālī ca sadr̥śau sthaḥ parasparam ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
svareṇa varcasā caiva prekṣitena ca vānara |
vikrameṇa ca vākyaiś ca vyaktiṁ vāṁ nopalakṣaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
tato ’haṁ rūpasādr̥śyān mohito vānarottama |
notsr̥jāmi mahāvegaṁ śaraṁ śatrunibarhaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
etanmuhūrte tu mayā paśya vālinam āhave |
nirastam iṣuṇaikena veṣṭamānaṁ mahītale ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
abhijñānaṁ kuruṣva tvam ātmano vānareśvara |
yena tvām abhijānīyāṁ dvandvayuddham upāgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
gajapuṣpīm imāṁ phullām utpāṭya śubhalakṣaṇām |
kuru lakṣmaṇa kaṇṭhe ’sya sugrīvasya mahātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
tato giritaṭe jātām utpāṭya kusumāyutām |
lakṣmaṇo gajapuṣpīṁ tāṁ tasya kaṇṭhe vyasarjayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
sa tathā śuśubhe śrīmām̐l latayā kaṇṭhasaktayā |
mālayeva balākānāṁ sasaṁdhya iva toyadaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
vibhrājamāno vapuṣā rāmavākyasamāhitaḥ |
jagāma saha rāmeṇa kiṣkindhāṁ vālipālitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
