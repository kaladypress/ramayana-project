=== Sarga 16 ===

---
1.
tām evaṁ bruvatīṁ tārāṁ tārādhipanibhānanām |
vālī nirbhartsayām āsa vacanaṁ cedam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
garjato ’sya ca saṁrambhaṁ bhrātuḥ śatror viśeṣataḥ |
marṣayiṣyāmy ahaṁ kena kāraṇena varānane ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
adharṣitānāṁ śūrāṇāṁ samareṣv anivartinām |
dharṣaṇāmarṣaṇaṁ bhīru maraṇād atiricyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
soḍhuṁ na ca samartho ’haṁ yuddhakāmasya saṁyuge |
sugrīvasya ca saṁrambhaṁ hīnagrīvasya garjataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
na ca kāryo viṣādas te rāghavaṁ prati matkr̥te |
dharmajñaś ca kr̥tajñaś ca kathaṁ pāpaṁ kariṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
nivartasva saha strībhiḥ kathaṁ bhūyo ’nugacchasi |
sauhr̥daṁ darśitaṁ tāre mayi bhaktiḥ kr̥tā tvayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
pratiyotsyāmy ahaṁ gatvā sugrīvaṁ jahi saṁbhramam |
darpaṁ cāsya vineṣyāmi na ca prāṇair vimokṣyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
śāpitāsi mama prāṇair nivartasva jayena ca |
ahaṁ jitvā nivartiṣye tam alaṁ bhrātaraṁ raṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
taṁ tu tārā pariṣvajya vālinaṁ priyavādinī |
cakāra rudatī mandaṁ dakṣiṇā sā pradakṣiṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tataḥ svastyayanaṁ kr̥tvā mantravad vijayaiṣiṇī |
antaḥpuraṁ saha strībhiḥ praviṣṭā śokamohitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
praviṣṭāyāṁ tu tārāyāṁ saha strībhiḥ svam ālayam |
nagarān niryayau kruddho mahāsarpa iva śvasan ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sa niḥśvasya mahāvego vālī paramaroṣaṇaḥ |
sarvataś cārayan dr̥ṣṭiṁ śatrudarśanakāṅkṣayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sa dadarśa tataḥ śrīmān sugrīvaṁ hemapiṅgalam |
susaṁvītam avaṣṭabdhaṁ dīpyamānam ivānalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sa taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā mahāvīryaṁ sugrīvaṁ paryavasthitam |
gāḍhaṁ paridadhe vāso vālī paramaroṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa vālī gāḍhasaṁvīto muṣṭim udyamya vīryavān |
sugrīvam evābhimukho yayau yoddhuṁ kr̥takṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
śliṣṭamuṣṭiṁ samudyamya saṁrabdhataram āgataḥ |
sugrīvo ’pi samuddiśya vālinaṁ hemamālinam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
taṁ vālī krodhatāmrākṣaḥ sugrīvaṁ raṇapaṇḍitam |
āpatantaṁ mahāvegam idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
eṣa muṣṭir mayā baddho gāḍhaḥ sunihitāṅguliḥ |
mayā vegavimuktas te prāṇān ādāya yāsyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
evam uktas tu sugrīvaḥ kruddho vālinam abravīt |
tavaiva ca haran prāṇān muṣṭiḥ patatu mūrdhani ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tāḍitas tena saṁkruddhaḥ samabhikramya vegataḥ |
abhavac choṇitodgārī sotpīḍa iva parvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
sugrīveṇa tu niḥsaṁgaṁ sālam utpāṭya tejasā |
gātreṣv abhihato vālī vajreṇeva mahāgiriḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
sa tu vālī pracaritaḥ sālatāḍanavihvalaḥ |
gurubhārasamākrāntā sāgare naur ivābhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tau bhīmabalavikrāntau suparṇasamaveginau |
pravr̥ddhau ghoravapuṣau candrasūryāv ivāmbare ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
vālinā bhagnadarpas tu sugrīvo mandavikramaḥ |
vālinaṁ prati sāmarṣo darśayām āsa lāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tato dhanuṣi saṁdhāya śaram āśīviṣopamam |
rāghaveṇa mahābāṇo vālivakṣasi pātitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
vegenābhihato vālī nipapāta mahītale ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
athokṣitaḥ śoṇitatoyavisravaiḥ; supuṣpitāśoka ivāniloddhataḥ |
vicetano vāsavasūnur āhave; prabhraṁśitendradhvajavat kṣitiṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
