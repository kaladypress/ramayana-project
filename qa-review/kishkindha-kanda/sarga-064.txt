=== Sarga 64 ===

---
1.
tato ’ṅgadavacaḥ śrutvā sarve te vānarottamāḥ |
svaṁ svaṁ gatau samutsāham āhus tatra yathākramam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
gajo gavākṣo gavayaḥ śarabho gandhamādanaḥ |
maindaś ca dvividaś caiva suṣeṇo jāmbavāṁs tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
ābabhāṣe gajas tatra plaveyaṁ daśayojanam |
gavākṣo yojanāny āha gamiṣyāmīti viṁśatim ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
gavayo vānaras tatra vānarāṁs tān uvāca ha |
triṁśataṁ tu gamiṣyāmi yojanānāṁ plavaṁgamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
śarabho vānaras tatra vānarāṁs tān uvāca ha |
catvāriṁśad gamiṣyāmi yojanānāṁ na saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
vānarāṁs tu mahātejā abravīd gandhamādanaḥ |
yojanānāṁ gamiṣyāmi pañcāśat tu na saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
maindas tu vānaras tatra vānarāṁs tān uvāca ha |
yojanānāṁ paraṁ ṣaṣṭim ahaṁ plavitum utsahe ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tatas tatra mahātejā dvividaḥ pratyabhāṣata |
gamiṣyāmi na saṁdehaḥ saptatiṁ yojanāny aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
suṣeṇas tu hariśreṣṭhaḥ proktavān kapisattamān |
aśītiṁ yojanānāṁ tu plaveyaṁ plavagarṣabhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
teṣāṁ kathayatāṁ tatra sarvāṁs tān anumānya ca |
tato vr̥ddhatamas teṣāṁ jāmbavān pratyabhāṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
pūrvam asmākam apy āsīt kaś cid gatiparākramaḥ |
te vayaṁ vayasaḥ pāram anuprāptāḥ sma sāmpratam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
kiṁ tu naivaṁ gate śakyam idaṁ kāryam upekṣitum |
yad arthaṁ kapirājaś ca rāmaś ca kr̥taniścayau ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sāmprataṁ kālabhedena yā gatis tāṁ nibodhata |
navatiṁ yojanānāṁ tu gamiṣyāmi na saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tāṁś ca sarvān hariśreṣṭhāñ jāmbavān punar abravīt |
na khalv etāvad evāsīd gamane me parākramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
mayā mahābalaiś caiva yajñe viṣṇuḥ sanātanaḥ |
pradakṣiṇīkr̥taḥ pūrvaṁ kramamāṇas trivikramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sa idānīm ahaṁ vr̥ddhaḥ plavane mandavikramaḥ |
yauvane ca tadāsīn me balam apratimaṁ paraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
saṁpraty etāvatīṁ śaktiṁ gamane tarkayāmy aham |
naitāvatā ca saṁsiddhiḥ kāryasyāsya bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
athottaram udārārtham abravīd aṅgadas tadā |
anumānya mahāprājño jāmbavantaṁ mahākapim ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
aham etad gamiṣyāmi yojanānāṁ śataṁ mahat |
nivartane tu me śaktiḥ syān na veti na niścitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tam uvāca hariśreṣṭho jāmbavān vākyakovidaḥ |
jñāyate gamane śaktis tava haryr̥kṣasattama ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
kāmaṁ śatasahasraṁ vā na hy eṣa vidhir ucyate |
yojanānāṁ bhavāñ śakto gantuṁ pratinivartitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
na hi preṣayitā tata svāmī preṣyaḥ kathaṁ cana |
bhavatāyaṁ janaḥ sarvaḥ preṣyaḥ plavagasattama ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
bhavān kalatram asmākaṁ svāmibhāve vyavasthitaḥ |
svāmī kalatraṁ sainyasya gatir eṣā paraṁtapa ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tasmāt kalatravat tāta pratipālyaḥ sadā bhavān |
api caitasya kāryasya bhavān mūlam ariṁdama ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
mūlam arthasya saṁrakṣyam eṣa kāryavidāṁ nayaḥ |
mūle hi sati sidhyanti guṇāḥ puṣpaphalādayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tad bhavān asyā kāryasya sādhane satyavikramaḥ |
buddhivikramasaṁpanno hetur atra paraṁtapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
guruś ca guruputraś ca tvaṁ hi naḥ kapisattama |
bhavantam āśritya vayaṁ samarthā hy arthasādhane ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
uktavākyaṁ mahāprājñaṁ jāmbavantaṁ mahākapiḥ |
pratyuvācottaraṁ vākyaṁ vālisūnur athāṅgadaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
yadi nāhaṁ gamiṣyāmi nānyo vānarapuṁgavaḥ |
punaḥ khalv idam asmābhiḥ kāryaṁ prāyopaveśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
na hy akr̥tvā haripateḥ saṁdeśaṁ tasya dhīmataḥ |
tatrāpi gatvā prāṇānāṁ paśyāmi parirakṣaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
sa hi prasāde cātyarthaṁ kope ca harir īśvaraḥ |
atītya tasya saṁdeśaṁ vināśo gamane bhavet ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
tad yathā hy asya kāryasya na bhavaty anyathā gatiḥ |
tad bhavān eva dr̥ṣṭārthaḥ saṁcintayitum arhati ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
so ’ṅgadena tadā vīraḥ pratyuktaḥ plavagarṣabhaḥ |
jāmbavān uttaraṁ vākyaṁ provācedaṁ tato ’ṅgadam ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
asya te vīra kāryasya na kiṁ cit parihīyate |
eṣa saṁcodayāmy enaṁ yaḥ kāryaṁ sādhayiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
tataḥ pratītaṁ plavatāṁ variṣṭham; ekāntam āśritya sukhopaviṣṭam |
saṁcodayām āsa haripravīro; haripravīraṁ hanumantam eva ||

=== meaning ===

---
