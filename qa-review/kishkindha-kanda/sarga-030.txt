=== Sarga 30 ===

---
1.
sa kāminaṁ dīnam adīnasattvaḥ; śokābhipannaṁ samudīrṇakopam |
narendrasūnur naradevaputraṁ; rāmānujaḥ pūrvajam ity uvāca ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
na vānaraḥ sthāsyati sādhuvr̥tte; na maṁsyate kāryaphalānuṣaṅgān |
na bhakṣyate vānararājyalakṣmīṁ; tathā hi nābhikramate ’sya buddhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
matikṣayād grāmyasukheṣu saktas; tava prasādāpratikārabuddhiḥ |
hato ’grajaṁ paśyatu vālinaṁ sa; na rājyam evaṁ viguṇasya deyam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
na dhāraye kopam udīrṇavegaṁ; nihanmi sugrīvam asatyam adya |
haripravīraiḥ saha vāliputro; narendrapatnyā vicayaṁ karotu ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tam āttabāṇāsanam utpatantaṁ; niveditārthaṁ raṇacaṇḍakopam |
uvaca rāmaḥ paravīrahantā; svavekṣitaṁ sānunayaṁ ca vākyam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
na hi vai tvadvidho loke pāpam evaṁ samācaret |
pāpam āryeṇa yo hanti sa vīraḥ puruṣottamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
nedam adya tvayā grāhyaṁ sādhuvr̥ttena lakṣmaṇa |
tāṁ prītim anuvartasva pūrvavr̥ttaṁ ca saṁgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sāmopahitayā vācā rūkṣāṇi parivarjayan |
vaktum arhasi sugrīvaṁ vyatītaṁ kālaparyaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
so’ grajenānuśiṣṭārtho yathāvat puruṣarṣabhaḥ |
praviveśa purīṁ vīro lakṣmaṇaḥ paravīrahā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tataḥ śubhamatiḥ prājño bhrātuḥ priyahite rataḥ |
lakṣmaṇaḥ pratisaṁrabdho jagāma bhavanaṁ kapeḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
śakrabāṇāsanaprakhyaṁ dhanuḥ kālāntakopamaḥ |
pragr̥hya giriśr̥ṅgābhaṁ mandaraḥ sānumān iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
yathoktakārī vacanam uttaraṁ caiva sottaram |
br̥haspatisamo buddhyā mattvā rāmānujas tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
kāmakrodhasamutthena bhrātuḥ kopāgninā vr̥taḥ |
prabhañjana ivāprītaḥ prayayau lakṣmaṇas tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sālatālāśvakarṇāṁś ca tarasā pātayan bahūn |
paryasyan girikūṭāni drumān anyāṁś ca vegataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
śilāś ca śakalīkurvan padbhyāṁ gaja ivāśugaḥ |
dūram ekapadaṁ tyaktvā yayau kāryavaśād drutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tām apaśyad balākīrṇāṁ harirājamahāpurīm |
durgām ikṣvākuśārdūlaḥ kiṣkindhāṁ girisaṁkaṭe ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
roṣāt prasphuramāṇauṣṭhaḥ sugrīvaṁ prati kalṣmaṇaḥ |
dadarśa vānarān bhīmān kiṣkindhāyā bahiścarān ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
śailaśr̥ṅgāṇi śataśaḥ pravr̥ddhāṁś ca mahīruhān |
jagr̥huḥ kuñjaraprakhyā vānarāḥ parvatāntare ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tān gr̥hītapraharaṇān harīn dr̥ṣṭvā tu lakṣmaṇaḥ |
babhūva dviguṇaṁ kruddho bahvindhana ivānalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
taṁ te bhayaparītāṅgāḥ kruddhaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā plavaṁgamāḥ |
kālamr̥tyuyugāntābhaṁ śataśo vidrutā diśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tataḥ sugrīvabhavanaṁ praviśya haripuṁgavāḥ |
krodham āgamanaṁ caiva lakṣmaṇasya nyavedayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tārayā sahitaḥ kāmī saktaḥ kapivr̥ṣo rahaḥ |
na teṣāṁ kapivīrāṇāṁ śuśrāva vacanaṁ tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tataḥ sacivasaṁdiṣṭā harayo romaharṣaṇāḥ |
girikuñjarameghābhā nagaryā niryayus tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
nakhadaṁṣṭrāyudhā ghorāḥ sarve vikr̥tadarśanāḥ |
sarve śārdūladarpāś ca sarve ca vikr̥tānanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
daśanāgabalāḥ ke cit ke cid daśaguṇottarāḥ |
ke cin nāgasahasrasya babhūvus tulyavikramāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
kr̥tsnāṁ hi kapibhir vyāptāṁ drumahastair mahābalaiḥ |
apaśyal lakṣmaṇaḥ kruddhaḥ kiṣkindhāṁ tāṁ durāsadam ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tatas te harayaḥ sarve prākāraparikhāntarāt |
niṣkramyodagrasattvās tu tasthur āviṣkr̥taṁ tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
sugrīvasya pramādaṁ ca pūrvajaṁ cārtam ātmavān |
buddhvā kopavaśaṁ vīraḥ punar eva jagāma saḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
sa dīrghoṣṇamahocchvāsaḥ kopasaṁraktalocanaḥ |
babhūva naraśārdūlasadhūma iva pāvakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
bāṇaśalyasphurajjihvaḥ sāyakāsanabhogavān |
svatejoviṣasaṁghātaḥ pañcāsya iva pannagaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
taṁ dīptam iva kālāgniṁ nāgendram iva kopitam |
samāsādyāṅgadas trāsād viṣādam agamad bhr̥śam ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
so ’ṅgadaṁ roṣatāmrākṣaḥ saṁdideśa mahāyaśāḥ |
sugrīvaḥ kathyatāṁ vatsa mamāgamanam ity uta ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
eṣa rāmānujaḥ prāptas tvatsakāśam ariṁdamaḥ |
bhrātur vyasanasaṁtapto dvāri tiṣṭhati lakṣmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
lakṣmaṇasya vacaḥ śrutvā śokāviṣṭo ’ṅgado ’bravīt |
pituḥ samīpam āgamya saumitrir ayam āgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
te mahaughanibhaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā vajrāśanisamasvanam |
siṁhanādaṁ samaṁ cakrur lakṣmaṇasya samīpataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
tena śabdena mahatā pratyabudhyata vānaraḥ |
madavihvalatāmrākṣo vyākulasragvibhūṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
athāṅgadavacaḥ śrutvā tenaiva ca samāgatau |
mantriṇo vānarendrasya saṁmatodāradarśinau ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
plakṣaś caiva prabhāvaś ca mantriṇāv arthadharmayoḥ |
vaktum uccāvacaṁ prāptaṁ lakṣmaṇaṁ tau śaśaṁsatuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
prasādayitvā sugrīvaṁ vacanaiḥ sāmaniścitaiḥ |
āsīnaṁ paryupāsīnau yathā śakraṁ marutpatim ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
satyasaṁdhau mahābhāgau bhrātarau rāmalakṣmaṇau |
vayasya bhāvaṁ saṁprāptau rājyārhau rājyadāyinau ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
tayor eko dhanuṣpāṇir dvāri tiṣṭhati lakṣmaṇaḥ |
yasya bhītāḥ pravepante nādān muñcanti vānarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
sa eṣa rāghavabhrātā lakṣmaṇo vākyasārathiḥ |
vyavasāya rathaḥ prāptas tasya rāmasya śāsanāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
tasya mūrdhnā praṇamya tvaṁ saputraḥ saha bandhubhiḥ |
rājaṁs tiṣṭha svasamaye bhava satyapratiśravaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
