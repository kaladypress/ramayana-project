=== Sarga 39 ===

---
1.
atha rājā samr̥ddhārthaḥ sugrīvaḥ plavageśvaraḥ |
uvāca naraśārdūlaṁ rāmaṁ parabalārdanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
āgatā viniviṣṭāś ca balinaḥ kāmarūpiṇaḥ |
vānarendrā mahendrābhā ye madviṣayavāsinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
ta ime bahusāhasrair haribhir bhīmavikramaiḥ |
āgatā vānarā ghorā daityadānavasaṁnibhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
khyātakarmāpadānāś ca balavanto jitaklamāḥ |
parākrameṣu vikhyātā vyavasāyeṣu cottamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
pr̥thivyambucarā rāma nānānaganivāsinaḥ |
koṭyagraśa ime prāptā vānarās tava kiṁkarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
nideśavartinaḥ sarve sarve guruhite ratāḥ |
abhipretam anuṣṭhātuṁ tava śakṣyanty ariṁdama ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
yan manyase naravyāghra prāptakālaṁ tad ucyatām |
tat sainyaṁ tvadvaśe yuktam ājñāpayitum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
kāmam eṣām idaṁ kāryaṁ viditaṁ mama tattvataḥ |
tathāpi tu yathā tattvam ājñāpayitum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tathā bruvāṇaṁ sugrīvaṁ rāmo daśarathātmajaḥ |
bāhubhyāṁ saṁpariṣvajya idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
jñāyatāṁ saumya vaidehī yadi jīvati vā na vā |
sa ca deśo mahāprājña yasmin vasati rāvaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
adhigamya ca vaidehīṁ nilayaṁ rāvaṇasya ca |
prāptakālaṁ vidhāsyāmi tasmin kāle saha tvayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
nāham asmin prabhuḥ kārye vānareśa na lakṣmaṇaḥ |
tvam asya hetuḥ kāryasya prabhuś ca plavageśvara ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tvam evājñāpaya vibho mama kāryaviniścayam |
tvaṁ hi jānāsi yat kāryaṁ mama vīra na saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
suhr̥ddvitīyo vikrāntaḥ prājñaḥ kālaviśeṣavit |
bhavān asmaddhite yuktaḥ sukr̥tārtho ’rthavittamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
evam uktas tu sugrīvo vinataṁ nāma yūthapam |
abravīd rāma sāmnidhye lakṣmaṇasya ca dhīmataḥ |
śailābhaṁ meghanirghoṣam ūrjitaṁ plavageśvaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
somasūryātmajaiḥ sārdhaṁ vānarair vānarottama |
deśakālanayair yuktaḥ kāryākāryaviniścaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
vr̥taḥ śatasahasreṇa vānarāṇāṁ tarasvinām |
adhigaccha diśaṁ pūrvāṁ saśailavanakānanām ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tatra sītāṁ ca vaidehīṁ nilayaṁ rāvaṇasya ca |
mārgadhvaṁ giridurgeṣu vaneṣu ca nadīṣu ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
nadīṁ bhāgīrathīṁ ramyāṁ sarayūṁ kauśikīṁ tathā |
kālindīṁ yamunāṁ ramyāṁ yāmunaṁ ca mahāgirim ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
sarasvatīṁ ca sindhuṁ ca śoṇaṁ maṇinibhodakam |
mahīṁ kālamahīṁ caiva śailakānanaśobhitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
brahmamālān videhāṁś ca mālavān kāśikosalān |
māgadhāṁś ca mahāgrāmān puṇḍrān vaṅgāṁs tathaiva ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
pattanaṁ kośakārāṇāṁ bhūmiṁ ca rajatākarām |
sarvam etad vicetavyaṁ mr̥gayadbhir tatas tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
rāmasya dayitāṁ bhāryāṁ sītāṁ daśarataḥ snuṣām |
samudram avagāḍhāṁś ca parvatān pattanāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
mandarasya ca ye koṭiṁ saṁśritāḥ ke cid āyatām |
karṇaprāvaraṇāś caiva tathā cāpy oṣṭhakarṇakāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
ghorā lohamukhāś caiva javanāś caikapādakāḥ |
akṣayā balavantaś ca puruṣāḥ puruṣādakāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
kirātāḥ karṇacūḍāś ca hemāṅgāḥ priyadarśanāḥ |
āmamīnāśanās tatra kirātā dvīpavāsinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
antarjalacarā ghorā naravyāghrā iti śrutāḥ |
eteṣām ālayāḥ sarve viceyāḥ kānanaukasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
giribhir ye ca gamyante plavanena plavena ca |
ratnavantaṁ yavadvīpaṁ saptarājyopaśobhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
suvarṇarūpyakaṁ caiva suvarṇākaramaṇḍitam |
yavadvīpam atikramya śiśiro nāma parvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
divaṁ spr̥śati śr̥ṅgeṇa devadānavasevitaḥ |
eteṣāṁ giridurgeṣu pratāpeṣu vaneṣu ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
rāvaṇaḥ saha vaidehyā mārgitavyas tatas tataḥ |
tataḥ samudradvīpāṁś ca subhīmān draṣṭum arhatha ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
tatrāsurā mahākāyāś chāyāṁ gr̥hṇanti nityaśaḥ |
brahmaṇā samanujñātā dīrghakālaṁ bubhukṣitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
taṁ kālameghapratimaṁ mahoraganiṣevitam |
abhigamya mahānādaṁ tīrthenaiva mahodadhim ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
tato raktajalaṁ bhīmaṁ lohitaṁ nāma sāgaram |
gatā drakṣyatha tāṁ caiva br̥hatīṁ kūṭaśālmalīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
gr̥haṁ ca vainateyasya nānāratnavibhūṣitam |
tatra kailāsasaṁkāśaṁ vihitaṁ viśvakarmaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
tatra śailanibhā bhīmā mandehā nāma rākṣasāḥ |
śailaśr̥ṅgeṣu lambante nānārūpā bhayāvahāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
te patanti jale nityaṁ sūryasyodayanaṁ prati |
abhitaptāś ca sūryeṇa lambante sma punaḥ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
tataḥ pāṇḍurameghābhaṁ kṣīraudaṁ nāma sāgaram |
gatā drakṣyatha durdharṣā mukhā hāram ivormibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
tasya madhye mahāśveta r̥ṣabho nāma parvataḥ |
divyagandhaiḥ kusumitai rajataiś ca nagair vr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
saraś ca rājataiḥ padmair jvalitair hemakesaraiḥ |
nāmnā sudarśanaṁ nāma rājahaṁsaiḥ samākulam ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
vibudhāś cāraṇā yakṣāḥ kiṁnarāḥ sāpsarogaṇāḥ |
hr̥ṣṭāḥ samabhigacchanti nalinīṁ tāṁ riraṁsavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
kṣīrodaṁ samatikramya tato drakṣyatha vānarāḥ |
jalodaṁ sāgaraśreṣṭhaṁ sarvabhūtabhayāvaham ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
tatra tat kopajaṁ tejaḥ kr̥taṁ hayamukhaṁ mahat |
asyāhus tan mahāvegam odanaṁ sacarācaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
tatra vikrośatāṁ nādo bhūtānāṁ sāgaraukasām |
śrūyate cāsamarthānāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā tad vaḍavāmukham ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
svādūdasyottare deśe yojanāni trayodaśa |
jātarūpaśilo nāma mahān kanakaparvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
āsīnaṁ parvatasyāgre sarvabhūtanamaskr̥tam |
sahasraśirasaṁ devam anantaṁ nīlavāsasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
triśirāḥ kāñcanaḥ ketus tālas tasya mahātmanaḥ |
sthāpitaḥ parvatasyāgre virājati savedikaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
pūrvasyāṁ diśi nirmāṇaṁ kr̥taṁ tat tridaśeśvaraiḥ |
tataḥ paraṁ hemamayaḥ śrīmān udayaparvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
tasya koṭir divaṁ spr̥ṣṭvā śatayojanam āyatā |
jātarūpamayī divyā virājati savedikā ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
sālais tālais tamālaiś ca karṇikāraiś ca puṣpitaiḥ |
jātarūpamayair divyaiḥ śobhate sūryasaṁnibhaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
tatra yojanavistāram ucchritaṁ daśayojanam |
śr̥ṅgaṁ saumanasaṁ nāma jātarūpamayaṁ dhruvam ||

=== meaning ===

---
52.
tatra pūrvaṁ padaṁ kr̥tvā purā viṣṇus trivikrame |
dvitīyaṁ śikharaṁ meroś cakāra puruṣottamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
53.
uttareṇa parikramya jambūdvīpaṁ divākaraḥ |
dr̥śyo bhavati bhūyiṣṭhaṁ śikharaṁ tan mahocchrayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
54.
tatra vaikhānasā nāma vālakhilyā maharṣayaḥ |
prakāśamānā dr̥śyante sūryavarṇās tapasvinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
55.
ayaṁ sudarśano dvīpaḥ puro yasya prakāśate |
yasmiṁs tejaś ca cakṣuś ca sarvaprānabhr̥tām api ||

=== meaning ===

---
56.
śailasya tasya kuñjeṣu kandareṣu vaneṣu ca |
rāvaṇaḥ saha vaidehyā mārgitavyas tatas tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
57.
kāñcanasya ca śailasya sūryasya ca mahātmanaḥ |
āviṣṭā tejasā saṁdhyā pūrvā raktā prakāśate ||

=== meaning ===

---
58.
tataḥ paramagamyā syād dik pūrvā tridaśāvr̥tā |
rahitā candrasūryābhyām adr̥śyā timirāvr̥tā ||

=== meaning ===

---
59.
śaileṣu teṣu sarveṣu kandareṣu vaneṣu ca |
ye ca noktā mayā deśā viceyā teṣu jānakī ||

=== meaning ===

---
60.
etāvad vānaraiḥ śakyaṁ gantuṁ vānarapuṁgavāḥ |
abhāskaram amaryādaṁ na jānīmas tataḥ param ||

=== meaning ===

---
61.
adhigamya tu vaidehīṁ nilayaṁ rāvaṇasya ca |
māse pūrṇe nivartadhvam udayaṁ prāpya parvatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
62.
ūrdhvaṁ māsān na vastavyaṁ vasan vadhyo bhaven mama |
siddhārthāḥ saṁnivartadhvam adhigamya ca maithilīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
63.
mahendrakāntāṁ vanaṣaṇḍa maṇḍitāṁ; diśaṁ caritvā nipuṇena vānarāḥ |
avāpya sītāṁ raghuvaṁśajapriyāṁ; tato nivr̥ttāḥ sukhito bhaviṣyatha ||

=== meaning ===

---
