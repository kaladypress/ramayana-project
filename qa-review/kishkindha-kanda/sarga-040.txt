=== Sarga 40 ===

---
1.
tataḥ prasthāpya sugrīvas tan mahad vānaraṁ balam |
dakṣiṇāṁ preṣayām āsa vānarān abhilakṣitān ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
nīlam agnisutaṁ caiva hanumantaṁ ca vānaram |
pitāmahasutaṁ caiva jāmbavantaṁ mahākapim ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
suhotraṁ ca śarīraṁ ca śaragulmaṁ tathaiva ca |
gajaṁ gavākṣaṁ gavayaṁ suṣeṇam r̥ṣabhaṁ tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
maindaṁ ca dvividaṁ caiva vijayaṁ gandhamādanam |
ulkāmukham asaṅgaṁ ca hutāśana sutāv ubhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
aṅgadapramukhān vīrān vīraḥ kapigaṇeśvaraḥ |
vegavikramasaṁpannān saṁdideśa viśeṣavit ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
teṣām agresaraṁ caiva mahad balam asaṁgagam |
vidhāya harivīrāṇām ādiśad dakṣiṇāṁ diśam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
ye ke cana samuddeśās tasyāṁ diśi sudurgamāḥ |
kapīśaḥ kapimukhyānāṁ sa teṣāṁ tān udāharat ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sahasraśirasaṁ vindhyaṁ nānādrumalatāvr̥tam |
narmadāṁ ca nadīṁ durgāṁ mahoraganiṣevitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tato godāvarīṁ ramyāṁ kr̥ṣṇāveṇīṁ mahānadīm |
varadāṁ ca mahābhāgāṁ mahoraganiṣevitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
mekhalān utkalāṁś caiva daśārṇanagarāṇy api |
avantīm abhravantīṁ ca sarvam evānupaśyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
vidarbhān r̥ṣikāṁś caiva ramyān māhiṣakān api |
tathā baṅgān kaliṅgāṁś ca kauśikāṁś ca samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
anvīkṣya daṇḍakāraṇyaṁ saparvatanadīguham |
nadīṁ godāvarīṁ caiva sarvam evānupaśyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tathaivāndhrāṁś ca puṇḍrāṁś ca colān pāṇḍyān sakeralān |
ayomukhaś ca gantavyaḥ parvato dhātumaṇḍitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
vicitraśikharaḥ śrīmāṁś citrapuṣpitakānanaḥ |
sacandanavanoddeśo mārgitavyo mahāgiriḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tatas tām āpagāṁ divyāṁ prasannasalilāṁ śivām |
tatra drakṣyatha kāverīṁ vihr̥tām apsarogaṇaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tasyāsīnaṁ nagasyāgre malayasya mahaujasaṁ |
drakṣyathādityasaṁkāśam agastyam r̥ṣisattamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tatas tenābhyanujñātāḥ prasannena mahātmanā |
tāmraparṇīṁ grāhajuṣṭāṁ tariṣyatha mahānadīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
sā candanavanair divyaiḥ pracchannā dvīpa śālinī |
kānteva yuvatiḥ kāntaṁ samudram avagāhate ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tato hemamayaṁ divyaṁ muktāmaṇivibhūṣitam |
yuktaṁ kavāṭaṁ pāṇḍyānāṁ gatā drakṣyatha vānarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tataḥ samudram āsādya saṁpradhāryārthaniścayam |
agastyenāntare tatra sāgare viniveśitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
citranānānagaḥ śrīmān mahendraḥ parvatottamaḥ |
jātarūpamayaḥ śrīmān avagāḍho mahārṇavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
nānāvidhair nagaiḥ phullair latābhiś copaśobhitam |
devarṣiyakṣapravarair apsarobhiś ca sevitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
siddhacāraṇasaṁghaiś ca prakīrṇaṁ sumanoharam |
tam upaiti sahasrākṣaḥ sadā parvasu parvasu ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
dvīpas tasyāpare pāre śatayojanam āyataḥ |
agamyo mānuṣair dīptas taṁ mārgadhvaṁ samantataḥ |
tatra sarvātmanā sītā mārgitavyā viśeṣataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
sa hi deśas tu vadhyasya rāvaṇasya durātmanaḥ |
rākṣasādhipater vāsaḥ sahasrākṣasamadyuteḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
dakṣiṇasya samudrasya madhye tasya tu rākṣasī |
aṅgāraketi vikhyātā chāyām ākṣipya bhojinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tam atikramya lakṣmīvān samudre śatayojane |
giriḥ puṣpitako nāma siddhacāraṇasevitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
candrasūryāṁśusaṁkāśaḥ sāgarāmbusamāvr̥taḥ |
bhrājate vipulaiḥ śr̥ṅgair ambaraṁ vilikhann iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
tasyaikaṁ kāñcanaṁ śr̥ṅgaṁ sevate yaṁ divākaraḥ |
śvetaṁ rājatam ekaṁ ca sevate yaṁ niśākaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
na taṁ kr̥taghnāḥ paśyanti na nr̥śaṁsā na nāstikāḥ |
praṇamya śirasā śailaṁ taṁ vimārgata vānarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
tam atikramya durdharṣāḥ sūryavān nāma parvataḥ |
adhvanā durvigāhena yojanāni caturdaśa ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
tatas tam apy atikramya vaidyuto nāma parvataḥ |
sarvakāmaphalair vr̥kṣaiḥ sarvakālamanoharaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
tatra bhuktvā varārhāṇi mūlāni ca phalāni ca |
madhūni pītvā mukhyāni paraṁ gacchata vānarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
tatra netramanaḥkāntaḥ kuñjaro nāma parvataḥ |
agastyabhavanaṁ yatra nirmitaṁ viśvakarmaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
tatra yojanavistāram ucchritaṁ daśayojanam |
śaraṇaṁ kāñcanaṁ divyaṁ nānāratnavibhūṣitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
tatra bhogavatī nāma sarpāṇām ālayaḥ purī |
viśālarathyā durdharṣā sarvataḥ parirakṣitā |
rakṣitā pannagair ghorais tīkṣṇadaṁṣṭrair mahāviṣaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
sarparājo mahāghoro yasyāṁ vasati vāsukiḥ |
niryāya mārgitavyā ca sā ca bhogavatī purī ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
taṁ ca deśam atikramya mahān r̥ṣabhasaṁsthitaḥ |
sarvaratnamayaḥ śrīmān r̥ṣabho nāma parvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
gośīrṣakaṁ padmakaṁ ca hariśyāmaṁ ca candanam |
divyam utpadyate yatra tac caivāgnisamaprabham ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
na tu tac candanaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā spraṣṭavyaṁ ca kadā cana |
rohitā nāma gandharvā ghorā rakṣanti tad vanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
tatra gandharvapatayaḥ pañcasūryasamaprabhāḥ |
śailūṣo grāmaṇīr bhikṣuḥ śubhro babhrus tathaiva ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
ante pr̥thivyā durdharṣās tatra svargajitaḥ sthitāḥ |
tataḥ paraṁ na vaḥ sevyaḥ pitr̥lokaḥ sudāruṇaḥ |
rājadhānī yamasyaiṣā kaṣṭena tamasāvr̥tā ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
etāvad eva yuṣmābhir vīrā vānarapuṁgavāḥ |
śakyaṁ vicetuṁ gantuṁ vā nāto gatimatāṁ gatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
sarvam etat samālokya yac cānyad api dr̥śyate |
gatiṁ viditvā vaidehyāḥ saṁnivartitam arhatha ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
yas tu māsān nivr̥tto ’gre dr̥ṣṭā sīteti vakṣyati |
mattulyavibhavo bhogaiḥ sukhaṁ sa vihariṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
tataḥ priyataro nāsti mama prāṇād viśeṣataḥ |
kr̥tāparādho bahuśo mama bandhur bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
amitabalaparākramā bhavanto; vipulaguṇeṣu kuleṣu ca prasūtāḥ |
manujapatisutāṁ yathā labhadhvaṁ; tad adhiguṇaṁ puruṣārtham ārabhadhvam ||

=== meaning ===

---
