=== Sarga 22 ===

---
1.
vīkṣamāṇas tu mandāsuḥ sarvato mandam ucchvasan |
ādāv eva tu sugrīvaṁ dadarśa tv ātmajāgrataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
taṁ prāptavijayaṁ vālī sugrīvaṁ plavageśvaram |
ābhāṣya vyaktayā vācā sasneham idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sugrīvadoṣeṇa na māṁ gantum arhasi kilbiṣāt |
kr̥ṣyamāṇaṁ bhaviṣyeṇa buddhimohena māṁ balāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
yugapadvihitaṁ tāta na manye sukham āvayoḥ |
sauhārdaṁ bhrātr̥yuktaṁ hi tad idaṁ jātam anyathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
pratipadya tvam adyaiva rājyam eṣāṁ vanaukasām |
mām apy adyaiva gacchantaṁ viddhi vaivasvatakṣayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
jīvitaṁ ca hi rājyaṁ ca śriyaṁ ca vipulām imām |
prajahāmy eṣa vai tūrṇaṁ mahac cāgarhitaṁ yaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
asyāṁ tv aham avasthāyāṁ vīra vakṣyāmi yad vacaḥ |
yady apy asukaraṁ rājan kartum eva tad arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sukhārhaṁ sukhasaṁvr̥ddhaṁ bālam enam abāliśam |
bāṣpapūrṇamukhaṁ paśya bhūmau patitam aṅgadam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
mama prāṇaiḥ priyataraṁ putraṁ putram ivaurasaṁ |
mayā hīnam ahīnārthaṁ sarvataḥ paripālaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tvam apy asya hi dātā ca paritrātā ca sarvataḥ |
bhayeṣv abhayadaś caiva yathāhaṁ plavageśvara ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
eṣa tārātmajaḥ śrīmāṁs tvayā tulyaparākramaḥ |
rakṣasāṁ tu vadhe teṣām agratas te bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
anurūpāṇi karmāṇi vikramya balavān raṇe |
kariṣyaty eṣa tāreyas tarasvī taruṇo ’ṅgadaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
suṣeṇaduhitā ceyam arthasūkṣmaviniścaye |
autpātike ca vividhe sarvataḥ pariniṣṭhitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
yad eṣā sādhv iti brūyāt kāryaṁ tan muktasaṁśayam |
na hi tārāmataṁ kiṁ cid anyathā parivartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
rāghavasya ca te kāryaṁ kartavyam aviśaṅkayā |
syād adharmo hy akaraṇe tvāṁ ca hiṁsyād vimānitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
imāṁ ca mālām ādhatsva divyāṁ sugrīvakāñcanīm |
udārā śrīḥ sthitā hy asyāṁ saṁprajahyān mr̥te mayi ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
ity evam uktaḥ sugrīvo vālinā bhrātr̥sauhr̥dāt |
harṣaṁ tyaktvā punar dīno grahagrasta ivoḍurāṭ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tad vālivacanāc chāntaḥ kurvan yuktam atandritaḥ |
jagrāha so ’bhyanujñāto mālāṁ tāṁ caiva kāñcanīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tāṁ mālāṁ kāñcanīṁ dattvā vālī dr̥ṣṭvātmajaṁ sthitam |
saṁsiddhaḥ pretya bhāvāya snehād aṅgadam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
deśakālau bhajasvādya kṣamamāṇaḥ priyāpriye |
sukhaduḥkhasahaḥ kāle sugrīvavaśago bhava ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
yathā hi tvaṁ mahābāho lālitaḥ satataṁ mayā |
na tathā vartamānaṁ tvāṁ sugrīvo bahu maṁsyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
māsyāmitrair gataṁ gaccher mā śatrubhir ariṁdama |
bhartur arthaparo dāntaḥ sugrīvavaśago bhava ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
na cātipraṇayaḥ kāryaḥ kartavyo ’praṇayaś ca te |
ubhayaṁ hi mahādoṣaṁ tasmād antaradr̥g bhava ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
ity uktvātha vivr̥ttākṣaḥ śarasaṁpīḍito bhr̥śam |
vivr̥tair daśanair bhīmair babhūvotkrāntajīvitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
hate tu vīre plavagādhipe tadā; plavaṁgamās tatra na śarma lebhire |
vanecarāḥ siṁhayute mahāvane; yathā hi gāvo nihate gavāṁ patau ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tatas tu tārā vyasanārṇava plutā; mr̥tasyā bhartur vadanaṁ samīkṣya sā |
jagāma bhūmiṁ parirabhya vālinaṁ; mahādrumaṁ chinnam ivāśritā latā ||

=== meaning ===

---
