=== Sarga 46 ===

---
1.
darśanārthaṁ tu vaidehyāḥ sarvataḥ kapiyūthapāḥ |
vyādiṣṭāḥ kapirājena yathoktaṁ jagmur añjasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sarāṁsi saritaḥ kakṣān ākāśaṁ nagarāṇi ca |
nadīdurgāṁs tathā śailān vicinvanti samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sugrīveṇa samākhyātān sarve vānarayūthapāḥ |
pradeśān pravicinvanti saśailavanakānanān ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
vicintya divasaṁ sarve sītādhigamane dhr̥tāḥ |
samāyānti sma medinyāṁ niśākāleśu vānarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
sarvartukāṁś ca deśeṣu vānarāḥ saphalān drumān |
āsādya rajanīṁ śayyāṁ cakruḥ sarveṣv ahaḥsu te ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tad ahaḥ prathamaṁ kr̥tvā māse prasravaṇaṁ gatāḥ |
kapirājena saṁgamya nirāśāḥ kapiyūthapāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
vicitya tu diśaṁ pūrvāṁ yathoktāṁ sacivaiḥ saha |
adr̥ṣṭvā vinataḥ sītām ājagāma mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
uttarāṁ tu diśaṁ sarvāṁ vicitya sa mahākapiḥ |
āgataḥ saha sainyena vīraḥ śatabalis tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
suṣeṇaḥ paścimām āśāṁ vicitya saha vānaraiḥ |
sametya māse saṁpūrṇe sugrīvam upacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
taṁ prasravaṇapr̥ṣṭhasthaṁ samāsādyābhivādya ca |
āsīnaṁ saha rāmeṇa sugrīvam idam abruvan ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
vicitāḥ parvatāḥ sarve vanāni nagarāṇi ca |
nimnagāḥ sāgarāntāś ca sarve janapadās tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
guhāś ca vicitāḥ sarvā yās tvayā parikīrtitāḥ |
vicitāś ca mahāgulmā latāvitatasaṁtatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
gahaneṣu ca deśeṣu durgeṣu viṣameṣu ca |
sattvāny atipramāṇāni vicitāni hatāni ca |
ye caiva gahanā deśā vicitās te punaḥ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
udārasattvābhijano mahātmā; sa maithilīṁ drakṣyati vānarendraḥ |
diśaṁ tu yām eva gatā tu sītā; tām āsthito vāyusuto hanūmān ||

=== meaning ===

---
