=== Sarga 5 ===

---
1.
r̥śyamūkāt tu hanumān gatvā taṁ malayaṁ giram |
ācacakṣe tadā vīrau kapirājāya rāghavau ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
ayaṁ rāmo mahāprājñaḥ saṁprāpto dr̥ḍhavikramaḥ |
lakṣmaṇena saha bhrātrā rāmo ’yaṁ satyavikramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
ikṣvākūṇāṁ kule jāto rāmo daśarathātmajaḥ |
dharme nigaditaś caiva pitur nirdeśapālakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tasyāsya vasato ’raṇye niyatasya mahātmanaḥ |
rakṣasāpahr̥tā bhāryā sa tvāṁ śaraṇam āgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
rājasūyāśvamedhaiś ca vahnir yenābhitarpitaḥ |
dakṣiṇāś ca tathotsr̥ṣṭā gāvaḥ śatasahasraśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tapasā satyavākyena vasudhā yena pālitā |
strīhetos tasya putro ’yaṁ rāmas tvāṁ śaraṇaṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
bhavatā sakhyakāmau tau bhrātarau rāmalakṣmaṇau |
pratigr̥hyārcayasvemau pūjanīyatamāv ubhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
śrutvā hanumato vākyaṁ sugrīvo hr̥ṣṭamānasaḥ |
bhayaṁ sa rāghavād ghoraṁ prajahau vigatajvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sa kr̥tvā mānuṣaṁ rūpaṁ sugrīvaḥ plavagādhipaḥ |
darśanīyatamo bhūtvā prītyā provāca rāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
bhavān dharmavinītaś ca vikrāntaḥ sarvavatsalaḥ |
ākhyātā vāyuputreṇa tattvato me bhavadguṇāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tan mamaivaiṣa satkāro lābhaś caivottamaḥ prabho |
yat tvam icchasi sauhārdaṁ vānareṇa mayā saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
rocate yadi vā sakhyaṁ bāhur eṣa prasāritaḥ |
gr̥hyatāṁ pāṇinā pāṇir maryādā vadhyatāṁ dhruvā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
etat tu vacanaṁ śrutvā sugrīvasya subhāṣitam |
saṁprahr̥ṣṭamanā hastaṁ pīḍayām āsa pāṇinā |
hr̥dyaṁ sauhr̥dam ālambya paryaṣvajata pīḍitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tato hanūmān saṁtyajya bhikṣurūpam ariṁdamaḥ |
kāṣṭhayoḥ svena rūpeṇa janayām āsa pāvakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
dīpyamānaṁ tato vahniṁ puṣpair abhyarcya satkr̥tam |
tayor madhye tu suprīto nidadhe susamāhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tato ’gniṁ dīpyamānaṁ tau cakratuś ca pradakṣiṇam |
sugrīvo rāghavaś caiva vayasyatvam upāgatau ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tataḥ suprīta manasau tāv ubhau harirāghavau |
anyonyam abhivīkṣantau na tr̥ptim upajagmatuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tataḥ sarvārthavidvāṁsaṁ rāmaṁ daśarathātmajam |
sugrīvaḥ prāha tejasvī vākyam ekamanās tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
