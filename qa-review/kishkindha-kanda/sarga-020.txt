=== Sarga 20 ===

---
1.
rāmacāpavisr̥ṣṭena śareṇāntakareṇa tam |
dr̥ṣṭvā vinihataṁ bhūmau tārā tārādhipānanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sā samāsādya bhartāraṁ paryaṣvajata bhāminī |
iṣuṇābhihataṁ dr̥ṣṭvā vālinaṁ kuñjaropamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
vānarendraṁ mahendrābhaṁ śokasaṁtaptamānasā |
tārā tarum ivonmūlaṁ paryadevayad āturā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
raṇe dāruṇavikrānta pravīra plavatāṁ vara |
kiṁ dīnām apurobhāgām adya tvaṁ nābhibhāṣase ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
uttiṣṭha hariśārdūla bhajasva śayanottamam |
naivaṁvidhāḥ śerate hi bhūmau nr̥patisattamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
atīva khalu te kāntā vasudhā vasudhādhipa |
gatāsur api yāṁ gātrair māṁ vihāya niṣevase ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
vyaktam anyā tvayā vīra dharmataḥ saṁpravartatā |
kiṣkindheva purī ramyā svargamārge vinirmitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
yāny asmābhis tvayā sārdhaṁ vaneṣu madhugandhiṣu |
vihr̥tāni tvayā kāle teṣām uparamaḥ kr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
nirānandā nirāśāhaṁ nimagnā śokasāgare |
tvayi pañcatvam āpanne mahāyūthapayūthape ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
hr̥dayaṁ susthiraṁ mahyaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā vinihataṁ bhuvi |
yan na śokābhisaṁtaptaṁ sphuṭate ’dya sahasradhā ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sugrīvasya tvayā bhāryā hr̥tā sa ca vivāsitaḥ |
yat tat tasya tvayā vyuṣṭiḥ prāpteyaṁ plavagādhipa ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
niḥśreyasaparā mohāt tvayā cāhaṁ vigarhitā |
yaiṣābruvaṁ hitaṁ vākyaṁ vānarendrahitaiṣiṇī ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
kālo niḥsaṁśayo nūnaṁ jīvitāntakaras tava |
balād yenāvapanno ’si sugrīvasyāvaśo vaśam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
vaidhavyaṁ śokasaṁtāpaṁ kr̥paṇaṁ kr̥paṇā satī |
aduḥkhopacitā pūrvaṁ vartayiṣyāmy anāthavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
lālitaś cāṅgado vīraḥ sukumāraḥ sukhocitaḥ |
vatsyate kām avasthāṁ me pitr̥vye krodhamūrchite ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
kuruṣva pitaraṁ putra sudr̥ṣṭaṁ dharmavatsalam |
durlabhaṁ darśanaṁ tv asya tava vatsa bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
samāśvāsaya putraṁ tvaṁ saṁdeśaṁ saṁdiśasva ca |
mūrdhni cainaṁ samāghrāya pravāsaṁ prasthito hy asi ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
rāmeṇa hi mahat karma kr̥taṁ tvām abhinighnatā |
ānr̥ṇyaṁ tu gataṁ tasya sugrīvasya pratiśrave ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
sakāmo bhava sugrīva rumāṁ tvaṁ pratipatsyase |
bhuṅkṣva rājyam anudvignaḥ śasto bhrātā ripus tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
kiṁ mām evaṁ vilapatīṁ preṁṇā tvaṁ nābhibhāṣase |
imāḥ paśya varā bahvīr bhāryās te vānareśvara ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tasyā vilapitaṁ śrutvā vānaryaḥ sarvataś ca tāḥ |
parigr̥hyāṅgadaṁ dīnaṁ duḥkhārtāḥ paricukruśuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
kim aṅgadaṁ sāṅgada vīra bāho; vihāya yāsy adya cirapravāsaṁ |
na yuktam evaṁ guṇasaṁnikr̥ṣṭaṁ; vihāya putraṁ priyaputra gantum ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
kim apriyaṁ te priyacāruveṣa; kr̥taṁ mayā nātha sutena vā te |
sahāyinīm adya vihāya vīra; yamakṣayaṁ gacchasi durvinītam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
yady apriyaṁ kiṁ cid asaṁpradhārya; kr̥taṁ mayā syāt tava dīrghabāho |
kṣamasva me tad dharivaṁśa nātha; vrajāmi mūrdhnā tava vīra pādau ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tathā tu tārā karuṇaṁ rudantī; bhartuḥ samīpe saha vānarībhiḥ |
vyavasyata prāyam anindyavarṇā; upopaveṣṭuṁ bhuvi yatra vālī ||

=== meaning ===

---
