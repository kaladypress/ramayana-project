=== Sarga 49 ===

---
1.
saha tārāṅgadābhyāṁ tu saṁgamya hanumān kapiḥ |
vicinoti sma vindhyasya guhāś ca gahanāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
siṁhaśārdūlajuṣṭāś ca guhāś ca paritas tathā |
viṣameṣu nagendrasya mahāprasravaṇeṣu ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
teṣāṁ tatraiva vasatāṁ sa kālo vyatyavartata ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
sa hi deśo duranveṣo guhā gahanavān mahān |
tatra vāyusutaḥ sarvaṁ vicinoti sma parvatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
paraspareṇa rahitā anyonyasyāvidūrataḥ |
gajo gavākṣo gavayaḥ śarabho gandhamādanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
maindaś ca dvividaś caiva hanumāñ jāmbavān api |
aṅgado yuvarājaś ca tāraś ca vanagocaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
girijālāvr̥tān deśān mārgitvā dakṣiṇāṁ diśam |
kṣutpipāsā parītāś ca śrāntāś ca salilārthinaḥ |
avakīrṇaṁ latāvr̥kṣair dadr̥śus te mahābilam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tataḥ krauñcāś ca haṁsāś ca sārasāś cāpi niṣkraman |
jalārdrāś cakravākāś ca raktāṅgāḥ padmareṇubhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tatas tad bilam āsādya sugandhi duratikramam |
vismayavyagramanaso babhūvur vānararṣabhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
saṁjātapariśaṅkās te tad bilaṁ plavagottamāḥ |
abhyapadyanta saṁhr̥ṣṭās tejovanto mahābalāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tataḥ parvatakūṭābho hanumān mārutātmajaḥ |
abravīd vānarān sarvān kāntāra vanakovidaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
girijālāvr̥tān deśān mārgitvā dakṣiṇāṁ diśam |
vayaṁ sarve pariśrāntā na ca paśyāmi maithilīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
asmāc cāpi bilād dhaṁsāḥ krauñcāś ca saha sārasaiḥ |
jalārdrāś cakravākāś ca niṣpatanti sma sarvaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
nūnaṁ salilavān atra kūpo vā yadi vā hradaḥ |
tathā ceme biladvāre snigdhās tiṣṭhanti pādapāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
ity uktās tad bilaṁ sarve viviśus timirāvr̥tam |
acandrasūryaṁ harayo dadr̥śū romaharṣaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tatas tasmin bile durge nānāpādapasaṁkule |
anyonyaṁ saṁpariṣvajya jagmur yojanam antaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
te naṣṭasaṁjñās tr̥ṣitāḥ saṁbhrāntāḥ salilārthinaḥ |
paripetur bile tasmin kaṁ cit kālam atandritāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
te kr̥śā dīnavadanāḥ pariśrāntāḥ plavaṁgamāḥ |
ālokaṁ dadr̥śur vīrā nirāśā jīvite tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tatas taṁ deśam āgamya saumyaṁ vitimiraṁ vanam |
dadr̥śuḥ kāñcanān vr̥kṣān dīptavaiśvānaraprabhān ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
sālāṁs tālāṁś ca puṁnāgān kakubhān vañjulān dhavān |
campakān nāgavr̥kṣāṁś ca karṇikārāṁś ca puṣpitān ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
taruṇādityasaṁkāśān vaidūryamayavedikān |
nīlavaidūryavarṇāś ca padminīḥ patagāvr̥tāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
mahadbhiḥ kāñcanair vr̥kṣair vr̥taṁ bālārka saṁnibhaiḥ |
jātarūpamayair matsyair mahadbhiś ca sakacchapaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
nalinīs tatra dadr̥śuḥ prasannasalilāyutāḥ |
kāñcanāni vimānāni rājatāni tathaiva ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tapanīyagavākṣāṇi muktājālāvr̥tāni ca |
haimarājatabhaumāni vaidūryamaṇimanti ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
dadr̥śus tatra harayo gr̥hamukhyāni sarvaśaḥ |
puṣpitān phalino vr̥kṣān pravālamaṇisaṁnibhān ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
kāñcanabhramarāṁś caiva madhūni ca samantataḥ |
maṇikāñcanacitrāṇi śayanāny āsanāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
mahārhāṇi ca yānāni dadr̥śus te samantataḥ |
haimarājatakāṁsyānāṁ bhājanānāṁ ca saṁcayān ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
agarūṇāṁ ca divyānāṁ candanānāṁ ca saṁcayān |
śucīny abhyavahāryāṇi mūlāni ca phalāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
mahārhāṇi ca pānāni madhūni rasavanti ca |
divyānām ambarāṇāṁ ca mahārhāṇāṁ ca saṁcayān |
kambalānāṁ ca citrāṇām ajinānāṁ ca saṁcayān ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
tatra tatra vicinvanto bile tatra mahāprabhāḥ |
dadr̥śur vānarāḥ śūrāḥ striyaṁ kāṁ cid adūrataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
tāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā bhr̥śasaṁtrastāś cīrakr̥ṣṇājināmbarām |
tāpasīṁ niyatāhārāṁ jvalantīm iva tejasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
tato hanūmān girisaṁnikāśaḥ; kr̥tāñjalis tām abhivādya vr̥ddhām |
papraccha kā tvaṁ bhavanaṁ bilaṁ ca; ratnāni cemāni vadasva kasya ||

=== meaning ===

---
