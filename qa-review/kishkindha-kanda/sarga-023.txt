=== Sarga 23 ===

---
1.
tataḥ samupajighrantī kapirājasya tanmukham |
patiṁ lokāc cyutaṁ tārā mr̥taṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
śeṣe tvaṁ viṣame duḥkham akr̥tvā vacanaṁ mama |
upalopacite vīra suduḥkhe vasudhātale ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
mattaḥ priyatarā nūnaṁ vānarendra mahī tava |
śeṣe hi tāṁ pariṣvajya māṁ ca na pratibhāṣase ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
sugrīva eva vikrānto vīra sāhasika priya |
r̥kṣavānaramukhyās tvāṁ balinaṁ paryupāsate ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
eṣāṁ vilapitaṁ kr̥cchram aṅgadasya ca śocataḥ |
mama cemāṁ giraṁ śrutvā kiṁ tvaṁ na pratibudhyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
idaṁ tac chūraśayanaṁ yatra śeṣe hato yudhi |
śāyitā nihatā yatra tvayaiva ripavaḥ purā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
viśuddhasattvābhijana priyayuddha mama priya |
mām anāthāṁ vihāyaikāṁ gatas tvam asi mānada ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
śūrāya na pradātavyā kanyā khalu vipaścitā |
śūrabhāryāṁ hatāṁ paśya sadyo māṁ vidhavāṁ kr̥tām ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
avabhagnaś ca me māno bhagnā me śāśvatī gatiḥ |
agādhe ca nimagnāsmi vipule śokasāgare ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
aśmasāramayaṁ nūnam idaṁ me hr̥dayaṁ dr̥ḍham |
bhartāraṁ nihataṁ dr̥ṣṭvā yan nādya śatadhā gatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
suhr̥c caiva hi bhartā ca prakr̥tyā ca mama priyaḥ |
āhave ca parākrāntaḥ śūraḥ pañcatvam āgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
patihīnā tu yā nārī kāmaṁ bhavatu putriṇī |
dhanadhānyaiḥ supūrṇāpi vidhavety ucyate budhaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
svagātraprabhave vīra śeṣe rudhiramaṇḍale |
kr̥mirāgaparistome tvam evaṁ śayane yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
reṇuśoṇitasaṁvītaṁ gātraṁ tava samantataḥ |
parirabdhuṁ na śaknomi bhujābhyāṁ plavagarṣabha ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
kr̥takr̥tyo ’dya sugrīvo vaire ’sminn atidāruṇe |
yasya rāmavimuktena hr̥tam ekeṣuṇā bhayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
śareṇa hr̥di lagnena gātrasaṁsparśane tava |
vāryāmi tvāṁ nirīkṣantī tvayi pañcatvam āgate ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
udbabarha śaraṁ nīlas tasya gātragataṁ tadā |
girigahvarasaṁlīnaṁ dīptam āśīviṣaṁ yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tasya niṣkr̥ṣyamāṇasya bāṇasya ca babhau dyutiḥ |
astamastakasaṁruddho raśmir dinakarād iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
petuḥ kṣatajadhārās tu vraṇebhyas tasya sarvaśaḥ |
tāmragairikasaṁpr̥ktā dhārā iva dharādharāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
avakīrṇaṁ vimārjantī bhartāraṁ raṇareṇunā |
asrair nayanajaiḥ śūraṁ siṣecāstrasamāhatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
rudhirokṣitasarvāṅgaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā vinihataṁ patim |
uvāca tārā piṅgākṣaṁ putram aṅgadam aṅganā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
avasthāṁ paścimāṁ paśya pituḥ putra sudāruṇām |
saṁprasaktasya vairasya gato ’ntaḥ pāpakarmaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
bālasūryodayatanuṁ prayāntaṁ yamasādanam |
abhivādaya rājānaṁ pitaraṁ putra mānadam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
evam uktaḥ samutthāya jagrāha caraṇau pituḥ |
bhujābhyāṁ pīnavr̥tābhyām aṅgado ’ham iti bruvan ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
abhivādayamānaṁ tvām aṅgadaṁ tvaṁ yathāpurā |
dīrghāyur bhava putreti kimarthaṁ nābhibhāṣase ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
ahaṁ putrasahāyā tvām upāse gatacetanam |
siṁhena nihataṁ sadyo gauḥ savatseva govr̥ṣam ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
iṣṭvā saṁgrāmayajñena nānāpraharaṇāmbhasā |
asminn avabhr̥the snātaḥ kathaṁ patnyā mayā vinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
yā dattā devarājena tava tuṣṭena saṁyuge |
śātakumbhamayīṁ mālāṁ tāṁ te paśyāmi neha kim ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
rājaśrīr na jahāti tvāṁ gatāsum api mānada |
sūryasyāvartamānasya śailarājam iva prabhā ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
na me vacaḥ pathyam idaṁ tvayā kr̥taṁ; na cāsmi śaktā hi nivāraṇe tava |
hatā saputrāsmi hatena saṁyuge; saha tvayā śrīr vijahāti mām iha ||

=== meaning ===

---
