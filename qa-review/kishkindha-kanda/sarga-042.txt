=== Sarga 42 ===

---
1.
tataḥ saṁdiśya sugrīvaḥ śvaśuraṁ paścimāṁ diśam |
vīraṁ śatabaliṁ nāma vānaraṁ vānararṣabhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
uvāca rājā mantrajñaḥ sarvavānarasaṁmatam |
vākyam ātmahitaṁ caiva rāmasya ca hitaṁ tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
vr̥taḥ śatasahasreṇa tvadvidhānāṁ vanaukasām |
vaivasvata sutaiḥ sārdhaṁ pratiṣṭhasva svamantribhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
diśaṁ hy udīcīṁ vikrāntāṁ himaśailāvataṁsakām |
sarvataḥ parimārgadhvaṁ rāmapatnīm aninditām ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
asmin kārye vinivr̥tte kr̥te dāśaratheḥ priye |
r̥ṇān muktā bhaviṣyāmaḥ kr̥tārthārthavidāṁ varāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
kr̥taṁ hi priyam asmākaṁ rāghaveṇa mahātmanā |
tasya cet pratikāro ’sti saphalaṁ jīvitaṁ bhavet ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
etāṁ buddhiṁ samāsthāya dr̥śyate jānakī yathā |
tathā bhavadbhiḥ kartavyam asmatpriyahitaiṣibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
ayaṁ hi sarvabhūtānāṁ mānyas tu narasattamaḥ |
asmāsu cāgataprītī rāmaḥ parapuraṁjayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
imāni vanadurgāṇi nadyaḥ śailāntarāṇi ca |
bhavantaḥ parimārgaṁs tu buddhivikramasaṁpadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tatra mlecchān pulindāṁś ca śūrasenāṁs tathaiva ca |
prasthālān bharatāṁś caiva kurūṁś ca saha madrakaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
kāmbojān yavanāṁś caiva śakān āraṭṭakān api |
bāhlīkān r̥ṣikāṁś caiva pauravān atha ṭaṅkaṇān ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
cīnān paramacīnāṁś ca nīhārāṁś ca punaḥ punaḥ |
anviṣya daradāṁś caiva himavantaṁ vicinvatha ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
lodhrapadmakaṣaṇḍeṣu devadāruvaneṣu ca |
rāvaṇaḥ saha vaidehya mārgitavyas tatas tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tataḥ somāśramaṁ gatvā devagandharvasevitam |
kālaṁ nāma mahāsānuṁ parvataṁ taṁ gamiṣyatha ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
mahatsu tasya śr̥ṅgeṣu nirdareṣu guhāsu ca |
vicinudhvaṁ mahābhāgāṁ rāmapatnīṁ yaśasvinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tam atikramya śailendraṁ hemavargaṁ mahāgirim |
tataḥ sudarśanaṁ nāma parvataṁ gantum arhatha ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tasya kānanaṣaṇḍeṣu nirdareṣu guhāsu ca |
rāvaṇaḥ saha vaidehyā mārgitavyas tatas tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tam atikramya cākāśaṁ sarvataḥ śatayojanam |
aparvatanadī vr̥kṣaṁ sarvasattvavivarjitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
taṁ tu śīghram atikramya kāntāraṁ romaharṣaṇam |
kailāsaṁ pāṇḍuraṁ śailaṁ prāpya hr̥ṣṭā bhaviṣyatha ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tatra pāṇḍurameghābhaṁ jāmbūnadapariṣkr̥tam |
kuberabhavanaṁ divyaṁ nirmitaṁ viśvakarmaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
viśālā nalinī yatra prabhūtakamalotpalā |
haṁsakāraṇḍavākīrṇā apsarogaṇasevitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tatra vaiśravaṇo rājā sarvabhūtanamaskr̥taḥ |
dhanado ramate śrīmān guhyakaiḥ saha yakṣarāṭ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tasya candranikaśeṣu parvateṣu guhāsu ca |
rāvaṇaḥ saha vaidehyā mārgitavyas tatas tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
krauñcaṁ tu girim āsādya bilaṁ tasya sudurgamam |
apramattaiḥ praveṣṭavyaṁ duṣpraveśaṁ hi tat smr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
vasanti hi mahātmānas tatra sūryasamaprabhāḥ |
devair apy arcitāḥ samyag devarūpā maharṣayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
krauñcasya tu guhāś cānyāḥ sānūni śikharāṇi ca |
nirdarāś ca nitambāś ca vicetavyās tatas tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
krauñcasya śikharaṁ cāpi nirīkṣya ca tatas tataḥ |
avr̥kṣaṁ kāmaśailaṁ ca mānasaṁ vihagālayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
na gatis tatra bhūtānāṁ devadānavarakṣasām |
sa ca sarvair vicetavyaḥ sasānuprasthabhūdharaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
krauñcaṁ girim atikramya maināko nāma parvataḥ |
mayasya bhavanaṁ tatra dānavasya svayaṁ kr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
mainākas tu vicetavyaḥ sasānuprasthakandaraḥ |
strīṇām aśvamukhīnāṁ ca niketās tatra tatra tu ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
taṁ deśaṁ samatikramya āśramaṁ siddhasevitam |
siddhā vaikhānasās tatra vālakhilyāś ca tāpasāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
vandyās te tu tapaḥsiddhās tāpasā vītakalmaṣāḥ |
praṣṭavyāś cāpi sītāyāḥ pravr̥ttaṁ vinayānvitaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
hemapuṣkarasaṁchannaṁ tatra vaikhānasaṁ saraḥ |
taruṇādityasaṁkāśair haṁsair vicaritaṁ śubhaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
aupavāhyaḥ kuberasya sarvabhauma iti smr̥taḥ |
gajaḥ paryeti taṁ deśaṁ sadā saha kareṇubhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
tat sāraḥ samatikramya naṣṭacandradivākaram |
anakṣatragaṇaṁ vyoma niṣpayodam anādimat ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
gabhastibhir ivārkasya sa tu deśaḥ prakāśate |
viśrāmyadbhis tapaḥ siddhair devakalpaiḥ svayamprabhaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
taṁ tu deśam atikramya śailodā nāma nimnagā |
ubhayos tīrayor yasyāḥ kīcakā nāma veṇavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
te nayanti paraṁ tīraṁ siddhān pratyānayanti ca |
uttarāḥ kuravas tatra kr̥tapuṇyapratiśriyāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
tataḥ kāñcanapadmābhiḥ padminībhiḥ kr̥todakāḥ |
nīlavaidūryapatrāḍhyā nadyas tatra sahasraśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
raktotpalavanaiś cātra maṇḍitāś ca hiraṇmayaiḥ |
taruṇādityasadr̥śair bhānti tatra jalāśayāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
mahārhamaṇipatraiś ca kāñcanaprabha kesaraiḥ |
nīlotpalavanaiś citraiḥ sa deśaḥ sarvatovr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
nistulābhiś ca muktābhir maṇibhiś ca mahādhanaiḥ |
udbhūtapulinās tatra jātarūpaiś ca nimnagāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
sarvaratnamayaiś citrair avagāḍhā nagottamaiḥ |
jātarūpamayaiś cāpi hutāśanasamaprabhaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
nityapuṣpaphalāś cātra nagāḥ patrarathākulāḥ |
divyagandharasasparśāḥ sarvakāmān sravanti ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
nānākārāṇi vāsāṁsi phalanty anye nagottamāḥ |
muktāvaidūryacitrāṇi bhūṣaṇāni tathaiva ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
strīṇāṁ yāny anurūpāṇi puruṣāṇāṁ tathaiva ca |
sarvartusukhasevyāni phalanty anye nagottamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
mahārhāṇi vicitrāṇi haimāny anye nagottamāḥ |
śayanāni prasūyante citrāstāraṇavanti ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
manaḥkāntāni mālyāni phalanty atrāpare drumāḥ |
pānāni ca mahārhāṇi bhakṣyāṇi vividhāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
striyaś ca guṇasaṁpannā rūpayauvanalakṣitāḥ |
gandharvāḥ kiṁnarā siddhā nāgā vidyādharās tathā |
ramante sahitās tatra nārībhir bhāskaraprabhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
sarve sukr̥takarmāṇaḥ sarve ratiparāyaṇāḥ |
sarve kāmārthasahitā vasanti saha yoṣitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
gītavāditranirghoṣaḥ sotkr̥ṣṭahasitasvanaḥ |
śrūyate satataṁ tatra sarvabhūtamanoharaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
52.
tatra nāmuditaḥ kaś cin nāsti kaś cid asatpriyaḥ |
ahany ahani vardhante guṇās tatra manoramāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
53.
samatikramya taṁ deśam uttaras toyasāṁ nidhiḥ |
tatra somagirir nāma madhye hemamayo mahān ||

=== meaning ===

---
54.
indralokagatā ye ca brahmalokagatāś ca ye |
devās taṁ samavekṣante girirājaṁ divaṁ gatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
55.
sa tu deśo visūryo ’pi tasya bhāsā prakāśate |
sūryalakṣmyābhivijñeyas tapaseva vivasvatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
56.
bhagavān api viśvātmā śambhur ekādaśātmakaḥ |
brahmā vasati deveśo brahmarṣiparivāritaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
57.
na kathaṁ cana gantavyaṁ kurūṇām uttareṇa vaḥ |
anyeṣām api bhūtānāṁ nātikrāmati vai gatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
58.
sā hi somagirir nāma devānām api durgamaḥ |
tam ālokya tataḥ kṣipram upāvartitum arhatha ||

=== meaning ===

---
59.
etāvad vānaraiḥ śakyaṁ gantuṁ vānarapuṁgavāḥ |
abhāskaram amaryādaṁ na jānīmas tataḥ param ||

=== meaning ===

---
60.
sarvam etad vicetavyaṁ yan mayā parikīrtitam |
yad anyad api noktaṁ ca tatrāpi kriyatāṁ matiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
61.
tataḥ kr̥taṁ dāśarather mahat priyaṁ; mahattaraṁ cāpi tato mama priyam |
kr̥taṁ bhaviṣyaty anilānalopamā; videhajā darśanajena karmaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
62.
tataḥ kr̥tārthāḥ sahitāḥ sabāndhavā; mayārcitāḥ sarvaguṇair manoramaiḥ |
cariṣyathorvīṁ pratiśāntaśatravaḥ; sahapriyā bhūtadharāḥ plavaṁgamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
