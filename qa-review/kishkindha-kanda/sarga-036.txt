=== Sarga 36 ===

---
1.
evam uktas tu sugrīvo lakṣmaṇena mahātmanā |
hanumantaṁ sthitaṁ pārśve sacivaṁ vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
mahendrahimavadvindhyakailāsaśikhareṣu ca |
mandare pāṇḍuśikhare pañcaśaileṣu ye sthitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
taruṇādityavarṇeṣu bhrājamāneṣu sarvaśaḥ |
parvateṣu samudrānte paścimasyāṁ tu ye diśi ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
ādityabhavane caiva girau saṁdhyābhrasaṁnibhe |
padmatālavanaṁ bhīmaṁ saṁśritā haripuṁgavāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
añjanāmbudasaṁkāśāḥ kuñjarapratimaujasaḥ |
añjane parate caiva ye vasanti plavaṁgamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
manaḥśilā guhāvāsā vānarāḥ kanakaprabhāḥ |
merupārśvagatāś caiva ye ca dhūmragiriṁ śritāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
taruṇādityavarṇāś ca parvate ye mahāruṇe |
pibanto madhumaireyaṁ bhīmavegāḥ plavaṁgamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
vaneṣu ca suramyeṣu sugandhiṣu mahatsu ca |
tāpasānāṁ ca ramyeṣu vanānteṣu samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tāṁs tāṁs tvam ānaya kṣipraṁ pr̥thivyāṁ sarvavānarān |
sāmadānādibhiḥ kalpair āśu preṣaya vānarān ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
preṣitāḥ prathamaṁ ye ca mayā dūtā mahājavāḥ |
tvaraṇārthaṁ tu bhūyas tvaṁ harīn saṁpreṣayāparān ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
ye prasaktāś ca kāmeṣu dīrghasūtrāś ca vānarāḥ |
ihānayasva tān sarvāñ śīghraṁ tu mama śāsanāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
ahobhir daśabhir ye ca nāgacchanti mamājñayā |
hantavyās te durātmāno rājaśāsanadūṣakāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
śatāny atha sahasrāṇi koṭyaś ca mama śāsanāt |
prayāntu kapisiṁhānāṁ diśo mama mate sthitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
meghaparvatasaṁkāśāś chādayanta ivāmbaram |
ghorarūpāḥ kapiśreṣṭhā yāntu macchāsanād itaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
te gatijñā gatiṁ gatvā pr̥thivyāṁ sarvavānarāḥ |
ānayantu harīn sarvāṁs tvaritāḥ śāsanān mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tasya vānararājasya śrutvā vāyusuto vacaḥ |
dikṣu sarvāsu vikrāntān preṣayām āsa vānarān ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
te padaṁ viṣṇuvikrāntaṁ patatrijyotiradhvagāḥ |
prayātāḥ prahitā rājñā harayas tatkṣaṇena vai ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
te samudreṣu giriṣu vaneṣu ca saritsu ca |
vānarā vānarān sarvān rāmahetor acodayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
mr̥tyukālopamasyājñāṁ rājarājasya vānarāḥ |
sugrīvasyāyayuḥ śrutvā sugrīvabhayadarśinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tatas te ’ñjanasaṁkāśā gires tasmān mahājavāḥ |
tisraḥ koṭyaḥ plavaṁgānāṁ niryayur yatra rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
astaṁ gacchati yatrārkas tasmin girivare ratāḥ |
taptahemasamābhāsās tasmāt koṭyo daśacyutāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
kailāsa śikharebhyaś ca siṁhakesaravarcasām |
tataḥ koṭisahasrāṇi vānarāṇām upāgaman ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
phalamūlena jīvanto himavantam upāśritāḥ |
teṣāṁ koṭisahasrāṇāṁ sahasraṁ samavartata ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
aṅgāraka samānānāṁ bhīmānāṁ bhīmakarmaṇām |
vindhyād vānarakoṭīnāṁ sahasrāṇy apatan drutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
kṣīrodavelānilayās tamālavanavāsinaḥ |
nārikelāśanāś caiva teṣāṁ saṁkhyā na vidyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
vanebhyo gahvarebhyaś ca saridbhyaś ca mahājavāḥ |
āgacchad vānarī senā pibantīva divākaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
ye tu tvarayituṁ yātā vānarāḥ sarvavānarān |
te vīrā himavac chailaṁ dadr̥śus taṁ mahādrumam ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tasmin girivare ramye yajño maheśvaraḥ purā |
sarvadevamanastoṣo babhau divyo manoharaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
annaviṣyandajātāni mūlāni ca phalāni ca |
amr̥tasvādukalpāni dadr̥śus tatra vānarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
tad anna saṁbhavaṁ divyaṁ phalaṁ mūlaṁ manoharam |
yaḥ kaś cit sakr̥d aśnāti māsaṁ bhavati tarpitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
tāni mūlāni divyāni phalāni ca phalāśanāḥ |
auṣadhāni ca divyāni jagr̥hur hariyūthapāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
tasmāc ca yajñāyatanāt puṣpāṇi surabhīṇi ca |
āninyur vānarā gatvā sugrīvapriyakāraṇāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
te tu sarve harivarāḥ pr̥thivyāṁ sarvavānarān |
saṁcodayitvā tvaritaṁ yūthānāṁ jagmur agrataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
te tu tena muhūrtena yūthapāḥ śīghrakāriṇaḥ |
kiṣkindhāṁ tvarayā prāptāḥ sugrīvo yatra vānaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
te gr̥hītvauṣadhīḥ sarvāḥ phalaṁ mūlaṁ ca vānarāḥ |
taṁ pratigrāhayām āsur vacanaṁ cedam abruvan ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
sarve parigatāḥ śailāḥ samudrāś ca vanāni ca |
pr̥thivyāṁ vānarāḥ sarve śāsanād upayānti te ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
evaṁ śrutvā tato hr̥ṣṭaḥ sugrīvaḥ plavagādhipaḥ |
pratijagrāha ca prītas teṣāṁ sarvam upāyanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
