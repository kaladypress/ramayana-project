=== Sarga 29 ===

---
1.
guhāṁ praviṣṭe sugrīve vimukte gagane ghanaiḥ |
varṣarātroṣito rāmaḥ kāmaśokābhipīḍitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
pāṇḍuraṁ gaganaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā vimalaṁ candramaṇḍalam |
śāradīṁ rajanīṁ caiva dr̥ṣṭvā jyotsnānulepanām ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
kāmavr̥ttaṁ ca sugrīvaṁ naṣṭāṁ ca janakātmajām |
buddhvā kālam atītaṁ ca mumoha paramāturaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
sa tu saṁjñām upāgamya muhūrtān matimān punaḥ |
manaḥsthām api vaidehīṁ cintayām āsa rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
āsīnaḥ parvatasyāgre hemadhātuvibhūṣite |
śāradaṁ gaganaṁ dr̥ṣṭva jagāma manasā priyām ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
dr̥ṣṭvā ca vimalaṁ vyoma gatavidyudbalāhakam |
sārasāravasaṁghuṣṭaṁ vilalāpārtayā girā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sārasāravasaṁnādaiḥ sārasāravanādinī |
yāśrame ramate bālā sādya me ramate katham ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
puṣpitāṁś cāsanān dr̥ṣṭvā kāñcanān iva nirmalān |
kathaṁ sa ramate bālā paśyantī mām apaśyatī ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
yā purā kalahaṁsānāṁ svareṇa kalabhāṣiṇī |
budhyate cārusarvāṅgī sādya me budhyate katham ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
niḥsvanaṁ cakravākānāṁ niśamya sahacāriṇām |
puṇḍarīkaviśālākṣī katham eṣā bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sarāṁsi sarito vāpīḥ kānanāni vanāni ca |
tāṁ vinā mr̥gaśāvākṣīṁ caran nādya sukhaṁ labhe ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
api tāṁ madviyogāc ca saukumāryāc ca bhāminīm |
na dūraṁ pīḍayet kāmaḥ śaradguṇanirantaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
evamādi naraśreṣṭho vilalāpa nr̥pātmajaḥ |
vihaṁga iva sāraṅgaḥ salilaṁ tridaśeśvarāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tataś cañcūrya ramyeṣu phalārthī girisānuṣu |
dadarśa paryupāvr̥tto lakṣmīvām̐l lakṣmaṇo ’grajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
taṁ cintayā duḥsahayā parītaṁ; visaṁjñam ekaṁ vijane manasvī |
bhrātur viṣādāt paritāpadīnaḥ; samīkṣya saumitrir uvāca rāmam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
kim ārya kāmasya vaśaṁgatena; kim ātmapauruṣyaparābhavena |
ayaṁ sadā saṁhr̥iyate samādhiḥ; kim atra yogena nivartitena ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
kriyābhiyogaṁ manasaḥ prasādaṁ; samādhiyogānugataṁ ca kālam |
sahāyasāmarthyam adīnasattva; svakarmahetuṁ ca kuruṣva hetum ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
na jānakī mānavavaṁśanātha; tvayā sanāthā sulabhā pareṇa |
na cāgnicūḍāṁ jvalitām upetya; na dahyate vīravarārha kaś cit ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
salakṣmaṇaṁ lakṣmaṇam apradhr̥ṣyaṁ; svabhāvajaṁ vākyam uvāca rāmaḥ |
hitaṁ ca pathyaṁ ca nayaprasaktaṁ; sasāmadharmārthasamāhitaṁ ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
niḥsaṁśayaṁ kāryam avekṣitavyaṁ; kriyāviśeṣo hy anuvartitavyaḥ |
nanu pravr̥ttasya durāsadasya; kumārakāryasya phalaṁ na cintyam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
atha padmapalāśākṣīṁ maithilīm anucintayan |
uvāca lakṣmaṇaṁ rāmo mukhena pariśuṣyatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tarpayitvā sahasrākṣaḥ salilena vasuṁdharām |
nirvartayitvā sasyāni kr̥takarmā vyavasthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
snigdhagambhīranirghoṣāḥ śailadrumapurogamāḥ |
visr̥jya salilaṁ meghāḥ pariśrāntā nr̥pātmaja ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
nīlotpaladalaśyāmaḥ śyāmīkr̥tvā diśo daśa |
vimadā iva mātaṅgāḥ śāntavegāḥ payodharāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
jalagarbhā mahāvegāḥ kuṭajārjunagandhinaḥ |
caritvā viratāḥ saumya vr̥ṣṭivātāḥ samudyatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
ghanānāṁ vāraṇānāṁ ca mayūrāṇāṁ ca lakṣmaṇa |
nādaḥ prasravaṇānāṁ ca praśāntaḥ sahasānagha ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
abhivr̥ṣṭā mahāmeghair nirmalāś citrasānavaḥ |
anuliptā ivābhānti girayaś candraraśmibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
darśayanti śarannadyaḥ pulināni śanaiḥ śanaiḥ |
navasaṁgamasavrīḍā jaghanānīva yoṣitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
prasannasalilāḥ saumya kurarībhir vināditāḥ |
cakravākagaṇākīrṇā vibhānti salilāśayāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
anyonyabaddhavairāṇāṁ jigīṣūṇāṁ nr̥pātmaja |
udyogasamayaḥ saumya pārthivānām upasthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
iyaṁ sā prathamā yātrā pārthivānāṁ nr̥pātmaja |
na ca paśyāmi sugrīvam udyogaṁ vā tathāvidham ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
catvāro vārṣikā māsā gatā varṣaśatopamāḥ |
mama śokābhitaptasya saumya sītām apaśyataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
priyāvihīne duḥkhārte hr̥tarājye vivāsite |
kr̥pāṁ na kurute rājā sugrīvo mayi lakṣmaṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
anātho hr̥tarājyo ’yaṁ rāvaṇena ca dharṣitaḥ |
dīno dūragr̥haḥ kāmī māṁ caiva śaraṇaṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
ity etaiḥ kāraṇaiḥ saumya sugrīvasya durātmanaḥ |
ahaṁ vānararājasya paribhūtaḥ paraṁtapa ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
sa kālaṁ parisaṁkhyāya sītāyāḥ parimārgaṇe |
kr̥tārthaḥ samayaṁ kr̥tvā durmatir nāvabudhyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
tvaṁ praviśya ca kiṣkindhāṁ brūhi vānarapuṁgavam |
mūrkhaṁ grāmya sukhe saktaṁ sugrīvaṁ vacanān mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
arthinām upapannānāṁ pūrvaṁ cāpy upakāriṇām |
āśāṁ saṁśrutya yo hanti sa loke puruṣādhamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
śubhaṁ vā yadi vā pāpaṁ yo hi vākyam udīritam |
satyena parigr̥hṇāti sa vīraḥ puruṣottamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
kr̥tārthā hy akr̥tārthānāṁ mitrāṇāṁ na bhavanti ye |
tān mr̥tān api kravyādaḥ kr̥taghnān nopabhuñjate ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
nūnaṁ kāñcanapr̥ṣṭhasya vikr̥ṣṭasya mayā raṇe |
draṣṭum icchanti cāpasya rūpaṁ vidyudgaṇopamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
ghoraṁ jyātalanirghoṣaṁ kruddhasya mama saṁyuge |
nirghoṣam iva vajrasya punaḥ saṁśrotum icchati ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
kāmam evaṁgate ’py asya parijñāte parākrame |
tvatsahāyasya me vīra na cintā syān nr̥pātmaja ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
yadartham ayam ārambhaḥ kr̥taḥ parapuraṁjaya |
samayaṁ nābhijānāti kr̥tārthaḥ plavageśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
varṣāsamayakālaṁ tu pratijñāya harīśvaraḥ |
vyatītāṁś caturo māsān viharan nāvabudhyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
sāmātyapariṣat krīḍan pānam evopasevate |
śokadīneṣu nāsmāsu sugrīvaḥ kurute dayām ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
ucyatāṁ gaccha sugrīvas tvayā vatsa mahābala |
mama roṣasya yadrūpaṁ brūyāś cainam idaṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
na ca saṁkucitaḥ panthā yena vālī hato gataḥ |
samaye tiṣṭha sugrīvamā vālipatham anvagāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
eka eva raṇe vālī śareṇa nihato mayā |
tvāṁ tu satyād atikrāntaṁ haniṣyāmi sabāndhavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
tad evaṁ vihite kārye yad dhitaṁ puruṣarṣabha |
tat tad brūhi naraśreṣṭha tvara kālavyatikramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
kuruṣva satyaṁ mayi vānareśvara; pratiśrutaṁ dharmam avekṣya śāśvatam |
mā vālinaṁ pretya gato yamakṣayaṁ; tvam adya paśyer mama coditaiḥ śaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
52.
sa pūrvajaṁ tīvravivr̥ddhakopaṁ; lālapyamānaṁ prasamīkṣya dīnam |
cakāra tīvrāṁ matim ugratejā; harīśvaramānavavaṁśanāthaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
