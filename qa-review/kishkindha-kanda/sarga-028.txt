=== Sarga 28 ===

---
1.
samīkṣya vimalaṁ vyoma gatavidyudbalāhakam |
sārasāravasaṁghuṣṭaṁ ramyajyotsnānulepanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
samr̥ddhārthaṁ ca sugrīvaṁ mandadharmārthasaṁgraham |
atyartham asatāṁ mārgam ekāntagatamānasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
nivr̥ttakāryaṁ siddhārthaṁ pramadābhirataṁ sadā |
prāptavantam abhipretān sarvān eva manorathān ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
svāṁ ca pātnīm abhipretāṁ tārāṁ cāpi samīpsitām |
viharantam ahorātraṁ kr̥tārthaṁ vigatajvalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
krīḍantam iva deveśaṁ nandane ’psarasāṁ gaṇaiḥ |
mantriṣu nyastakāryaṁ ca mantriṇām anavekṣakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
utsannarājyasaṁdeśaṁ kāmavr̥ttam avasthitam |
niścitārtho ’rthatattvajñaḥ kāladharmaviśeṣavit ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
prasādya vākyair madhurair hetumadbhir manoramaiḥ |
vākyavid vākyatattvajñaṁ harīśaṁ mārutātmajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
hitaṁ tathyaṁ ca pathyaṁ ca sāmadharmārthanītimat |
praṇayaprītisaṁyuktaṁ viśvāsakr̥taniścayam |
harīśvaram upāgamya hanumān vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
rājyaṁ prāptaṁ yaśaś caiva kaulī śrīr abhivarthitā |
mitrāṇāṁ saṁgrahaḥ śeṣas tad bhavān kartum arhati ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
yo hi mitreṣu kālajñaḥ satataṁ sādhu vartate |
tasya rājyaṁ ca kīrtiś ca pratāpaś cābhivardhate ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
yasya kośaś ca daṇḍaś ca mitrāṇy ātmā ca bhūmipa |
samavetāni sarvāṇi sa rājyaṁ mahad aśnute ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tad bhavān vr̥ttasaṁpannaḥ sthitaḥ pathi niratyaye |
mitrārtham abhinītārthaṁ yathāvat kartum arhati ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
yas tu kālavyatīteṣu mitrakāryeṣu vartate |
sa kr̥tvā mahato ’py arthān na mitrārthena yujyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
kriyatāṁ rāghavasyaitad vaidehyāḥ parimārgaṇam |
tad idaṁ vīra kāryaṁ te kālātītam ariṁdama ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
na ca kālam atītaṁ te nivedayati kālavit |
tvaramāṇo ’pi san prājñas tava rājan vaśānugaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
kulasya ketuḥ sphītasya dīrghabandhuś ca rāghavaḥ |
aprameyaprabhāvaś ca svayaṁ cāpratimo guṇaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tasya tvaṁ kuru vai kāryaṁ pūrvaṁ tena kr̥taṁ tava |
harīśvara hariśreṣṭhān ājñāpayitum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
na hi tāvad bhavet kālo vyatītaś codanād r̥te |
coditasya hi kāryasya bhavet kālavyatikramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
akartur api kāryasya bhavān kartā harīśvara |
kiṁ punaḥ pratikartus te rājyena ca dhanena ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
śaktimān asi vikrānto vānararṣka gaṇeśvara |
kartuṁ dāśaratheḥ prītim ājñāyāṁ kiṁ nu sajjase ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
kāmaṁ khalu śarair śaktaḥ surāsuramahoragān |
vaśe dāśarathiḥ kartuṁ tvatpratijñāṁ tu kāṅkṣate ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
prāṇatyāgāviśaṅkena kr̥taṁ tena tava priyam |
tasya mārgāma vaidehīṁ pr̥thivyām api cāmbare ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
na devā na ca gandharvā nāsurā na marudgaṇāḥ |
na ca yakṣā bhayaṁ tasya kuryuḥ kim uta rākṣasāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tad evaṁ śaktiyuktasya pūrvaṁ priyakr̥tas tathā |
rāmasyārhasi piṅgeśa kartuṁ sarvātmanā priyam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
nādhastād avanau nāpsu gatir nopari cāmbare |
kasya cit sajjate ’smākaṁ kapīśvara tavājñayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tad ājñāpaya kaḥ kiṁ te kr̥te vasatu kutra cit |
harayo hy apradhr̥ṣyās te santi koṭyagrato ’nagha ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā kāle sādhuniveditam |
sugrīvaḥ sattvasaṁpannaś cakāra matim uttamām ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
sa saṁdideśābhimataṁ nīlaṁ nityakr̥todyamam |
dikṣu sarvāsu sarveṣāṁ sainyānām upasaṁgrahe ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
yathā senā samagrā me yūthapālāś ca sarvaśaḥ |
samāgacchanty asaṁgena senāgrāṇi tathā kuru ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
ye tv antapālāḥ plavagāḥ śīghragā vyavasāyinaḥ |
samānayantu te sainyaṁ tvaritāḥ śāsanān mama |
svayaṁ cānantaraṁ sainyaṁ bhavān evānupaśyatu ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
tripañcarātrād ūrdhvaṁ yaḥ prāpnuyān neha vānaraḥ |
tasya prāṇāntiko daṇḍo nātra kāryā vicāraṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
harīṁś ca vr̥ddhān upayātu sāṅgado; bhavān mamājñām adhikr̥tya niścitām |
iti vyavasthāṁ haripuṁgaveśvaro; vidhāya veśma praviveśa vīryavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
