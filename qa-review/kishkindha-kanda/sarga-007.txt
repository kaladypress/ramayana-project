=== Sarga 7 ===

---
1.
evam uktas tu sugrīvo rāmeṇārtena vānaraḥ |
abravīt prāñjalir vākyaṁ sabāṣpaṁ bāṣpagadgadaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
na jāne nilayaṁ tasya sarvathā pāparakṣasaḥ |
sāmarthyaṁ vikramaṁ vāpi dauṣkuleyasya vā kulam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
satyaṁ tu pratijānāmi tyaja śokam ariṁdama |
kariṣyāmi tathā yatnaṁ yathā prāpsyasi maithilīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
rāvaṇaṁ sagaṇaṁ hatvā paritoṣyātmapauruṣam |
tathāsmi kartā nacirād yathā prīto bhaviṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
alaṁ vaiklavyam ālambya dhairyam ātmagataṁ smara |
tvadvidhānāṁ na sadr̥śam īdr̥śaṁ buddhilāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
mayāpi vyasanaṁ prāptaṁ bhāryā haraṇajaṁ mahat |
na cāham evaṁ śocāmi na ca dhairyaṁ parityaje ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
nāhaṁ tām anuśocāmi prākr̥to vānaro ’pi san |
mahātmā ca vinītaś cā kiṁ punar dhr̥timān bhavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
bāṣpam āpatitaṁ dhairyān nigrahītuṁ tvam arhasi |
maryādāṁ sattvayuktānāṁ dhr̥tiṁ notsraṣṭum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
vyasane vārtha kr̥cchre vā bhaye vā jīvitāntage |
vimr̥śan vai svayā buddhyā dhr̥timān nāvasīdati ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
bāliśas tu naro nityaṁ vaiklavyaṁ yo ’nuvartate |
sa majjaty avaśaḥ śoke bhārākrānteva naur jale ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
eṣo ’ñjalir mayā baddhaḥ praṇayāt tvāṁ prasādaye |
pauruṣaṁ śraya śokasya nāntaraṁ dātum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
ye śokam anuvartante na teṣāṁ vidyate sukham |
tejaś ca kṣīyate teṣāṁ na tvaṁ śocitum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
hitaṁ vayasya bhāvena brūhi nopadiśāmi te |
vayasyatāṁ pūjayan me na tvaṁ śocitum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
madhuraṁ sāntvitas tena sugrīveṇa sa rāghavaḥ |
mukham aśrupariklinnaṁ vastrāntena pramārjayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
prakr̥tiṣṭhas tu kākutsthaḥ sugrīvavacanāt prabhuḥ |
saṁpariṣvajya sugrīvam idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
kartavyaṁ yad vayasyena snigdhena ca hitena ca |
anurūpaṁ ca yuktaṁ ca kr̥taṁ sugrīva tat tvayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
eṣa ca prakr̥tiṣṭho ’ham anunītas tvayā sakhe |
durlabho hīdr̥śo bandhur asmin kāle viśeṣataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
kiṁ tu yatnas tvayā kāryo maithilyāḥ parimārgaṇe |
rākṣasasya ca raudrasya rāvaṇasya durātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
mayā ca yad anuṣṭheyaṁ visrabdhena tad ucyatām |
varṣāsv iva ca sukṣetre sarvaṁ saṁpadyate tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
mayā ca yad idaṁ vākyam abhimānāt samīritam |
tat tvayā hariśārdūla tattvam ity upadhāryatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
anr̥taṁ noktapūrvaṁ me na ca vakṣye kadā cana |
etat te pratijānāmi satyenaiva śapāmi te ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tataḥ prahr̥ṣṭaḥ sugrīvo vānaraiḥ sacivaiḥ saha |
rāghavasya vacaḥ śrutvā pratijñātaṁ viśeṣataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
mahānubhāvasya vaco niśamya; harir narāṇām r̥ṣabhasya tasya |
kr̥taṁ sa mene harivīra mukhyas; tadā svakāryaṁ hr̥dayena vidvān ||

=== meaning ===

---
