=== Sarga 60 ===

---
1.
tatas tad dāruṇaṁ karma duṣkaraṁ sāhasāt kr̥tam |
ācacakṣe muneḥ sarvaṁ sūryānugamanaṁ tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
bhagavan vraṇayuktatvāl lajjayā cākulendriyaḥ |
pariśrānto na śaknomi vacanaṁ paribhāṣitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
ahaṁ caiva jaṭāyuś ca saṁgharṣād darpamohitau |
ākāśaṁ patitau vīrau jighāsantau parākramam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
kailāsaśikhare baddhvā munīnām agrataḥ paṇam |
raviḥ syād anuyātavyo yāvad astaṁ mahāgirim ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
athāvāṁ yugapat prāptāv apaśyāva mahītale |
rathacakrapramāṇāni nagarāṇi pr̥thak pr̥thak ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
kva cid vāditraghoṣāṁś ca brahmaghoṣāṁś ca śuśruva |
gāyantīś cāṅganā bahvīḥ paśyāvo raktavāsasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tūrṇam utpatya cākāśam ādityapatham āsthitau |
āvām ālokayāvas tad vanaṁ śādvalasaṁsthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
upalair iva saṁchannā dr̥śyate bhūḥ śiloccayaiḥ |
āpagābhiś ca saṁvītā sūtrair iva vasuṁdharā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
himavāṁś caiva vindhyaś ca meruś ca sumahān nagaḥ |
bhūtale saṁprakāśante nāgā iva jalāśaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tīvrasvedaś ca khedaś ca bhayaṁ cāsīt tadāvayoḥ |
samāviśata mohaś ca mohān mūrchā ca dāruṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
na dig vijñāyate yāmyā nāgenyā na ca vāruṇī |
yugānte niyato loko hato dagdha ivāgninā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
yatnena mahatā bhūyo raviḥ samavalokitaḥ |
tulyaḥ pr̥thvīpramāṇena bhāskaraḥ pratibhāti nau ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
jaṭāyur mām anāpr̥cchya nipapāta mahīṁ tataḥ |
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā tūrṇam ākāśād ātmānaṁ muktavān aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
pakṣibhyāṁ ca mayā gupto jaṭāyur na pradahyata |
pramādāt tatra nirdagdhaḥ patan vāyupathād aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
āśaṅke taṁ nipatitaṁ janasthāne jaṭāyuṣam |
ahaṁ tu patito vindhye dagdhapakṣo jaḍīkr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
rājyena hīno bhrātrā ca pakṣābhyāṁ vikrameṇa ca |
sarvathā martum evecchan patiṣye śikharād gireḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
