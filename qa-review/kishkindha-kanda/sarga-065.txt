=== Sarga 65 ===

---
1.
anekaśatasāhasrīṁ viṣaṇṇāṁ harivāhinīm |
jāmbavān samudīkṣyaivaṁ hanumantam athābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
vīra vānaralokasya sarvaśāstravidāṁ vara |
tūṣṇīm ekāntam āśritya hanuman kiṁ na jalpasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
hanuman harirājasya sugrīvasya samo hy asi |
rāmalakṣmaṇayoś cāpi tejasā ca balena ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
ariṣṭaneminaḥ putrau vainateyo mahābalaḥ |
garutmān iva vikhyāta uttamaḥ sarvapakṣiṇām ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
bahuśo hi mayā dr̥ṣṭaḥ sāgare sa mahābalaḥ |
bhujagān uddharan pakṣī mahāvego mahāyaśāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
pakṣayor yad balaṁ tasya tāvad bhujabalaṁ tava |
vikramaś cāpi vegaś ca na te tenāpahīyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
balaṁ buddhiś ca tejaś ca sattvaṁ ca harisattama |
viśiṣṭaṁ sarvabhūteṣu kim ātmānaṁ na budhyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
apsarāpsarasāṁ śreṣṭhā vikhyātā puñjikasthalā |
ajñaneti parikhyātā patnī kesariṇo hareḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
abhiśāpād abhūt tāta vānarī kāmarūpiṇī |
duhitā vānarendrasya kuñjarasya mahātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
kapitve cārusarvāṅgī kadā cit kāmarūpiṇī |
mānuṣaṁ vigrahaṁ kr̥tvā yauvanottamaśālinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
acarat parvatasyāgre prāvr̥ḍambudasaṁnibhe |
vicitramālyābharaṇā mahārhakṣaumavāsinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tasyā vastraṁ viśālākṣyāḥ pītaṁ raktadaśaṁ śubham |
sthitāyāḥ parvatasyāgre māruto ’paharac chanaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sa dadarśa tatas tasyā vr̥ttāv ūrū susaṁhatau |
stanau ca pīnau sahitau sujātaṁ cāru cānanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tāṁ viśālāyataśroṇīṁ tanumadhyāṁ yaśasvinīm |
dr̥ṣṭvaiva śubhasarvāgnīṁ pavanaḥ kāmamohitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa tāṁ bhujābhyāṁ pīnābhyāṁ paryaṣvajata mārutaḥ |
manmathāviṣṭasarvāṅgo gatātmā tām aninditām ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sā tu tatraiva saṁbhrāntā suvr̥ttā vākyam abravīt |
ekapatnīvratam idaṁ ko nāśayitum icchati ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
añjanāyā vacaḥ śrutvā mārutaḥ pratyabhāṣata |
na tvāṁ hiṁsāmi suśroṇi mā bhūt te subhage bhayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
manasāsmi gato yat tvāṁ pariṣvajya yaśasvini |
vīryavān buddhisaṁpannaḥ putras tava bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
abhyutthitaṁ tataḥ sūryaṁ bālo dr̥ṣṭvā mahāvane |
phalaṁ ceti jighr̥kṣus tvam utplutyābhyapato divam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
śatāni trīṇi gatvātha yojanānāṁ mahākape |
tejasā tasya nirdhūto na viṣādaṁ tato gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tāvad āpatatas tūrṇam antarikṣaṁ mahākape |
kṣiptam indreṇa te vajraṁ krodhāviṣṭena dhīmatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tataḥ śailāgraśikhare vāmo hanur abhajyata |
tato hi nāmadheyaṁ te hanumān iti kīrtyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tatas tvāṁ nihataṁ dr̥ṣṭvā vāyur gandhavahaḥ svayam |
trailokye bhr̥śasaṁkruddho na vavau vai prabhañjanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
saṁbhrāntāś ca surāḥ sarve trailokye kṣubhite sati |
prasādayanti saṁkruddhaṁ mārutaṁ bhuvaneśvarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
prasādite ca pavane brahmā tubhyaṁ varaṁ dadau |
aśastravadhyatāṁ tāta samare satyavikrama ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
vajrasya ca nipātena virujaṁ tvāṁ samīkṣya ca |
sahasranetraḥ prītātmā dadau te varam uttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
svacchandataś ca maraṇaṁ te bhūyād iti vai prabho |
sa tvaṁ kesariṇaḥ putraḥ kṣetrajo bhīmavikramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
mārutasyaurasaḥ putras tejasā cāpi tatsamaḥ |
tvaṁ hi vāyusuto vatsa plavane cāpi tatsamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
vayam adya gataprāṇā bhavān asmāsu sāmpratam |
dākṣyavikramasaṁpannaḥ pakṣirāja ivāparaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
trivikrame mayā tāta saśailavanakānanā |
triḥ saptakr̥tvaḥ pr̥thivī parikrāntā pradakṣiṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
tadā cauṣadhayo ’smābhiḥ saṁcitā devaśāsanāt |
niṣpannam amr̥taṁ yābhis tadāsīn no mahad balam ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
sa idānīm ahaṁ vr̥ddhaḥ parihīnaparākramaḥ |
sāmprataṁ kālam asmākaṁ bhavān sarvaguṇānvitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
tad vijr̥mbhasva vikrāntaḥ plavatām uttamo hy asi |
tvadvīryaṁ draṣṭukāmeyaṁ sarvā vānaravāhinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
uttiṣṭha hariśārdūla laṅghayasva mahārṇavam |
parā hi sarvabhūtānāṁ hanuman yā gatis tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
viṣāṇṇā harayaḥ sarve hanuman kim upekṣase |
vikramasva mahāvego viṣṇus trīn vikramān iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
tatas tu vai jāmbavatābhicoditaḥ; pratītavegaḥ pavanātmajaḥ kapiḥ |
praharṣayaṁs tāṁ harivīra vāhinīṁ; cakāra rūpaṁ mahad ātmanas tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
