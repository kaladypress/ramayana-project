=== Sarga 8 ===

---
1.
parituṣṭas tu sugrīvas tena vākyena vānaraḥ |
lakṣmaṇasyāgrajaṁ rāmam idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sarvathāham anugrāhyo devatānām asaṁśayaḥ |
upapannaguṇopetaḥ sakhā yasya bhavān mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
śakyaṁ khalu bhaved rāma sahāyena tvayānagha |
surarājyam api prāptuṁ svarājyaṁ kiṁ punaḥ prabho ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
so ’haṁ sabhājyo bandhūnāṁ suhr̥dāṁ caiva rāghava |
yasyāgnisākṣikaṁ mitraṁ labdhaṁ rāghavavaṁśajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
aham apy anurūpas te vayasyo jñāsyase śanaiḥ |
na tu vaktuṁ samartho ’haṁ svayam ātmagatān guṇān ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
mahātmanāṁ tu bhūyiṣṭhaṁ tvadvidhānāṁ kr̥tātmanām |
niścalā bhavati prītir dhairyam ātmavatām iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
rajataṁ vā suvarṇaṁ vā vastrāṇy ābharaṇāni vā |
avibhaktāni sādhūnām avagacchanti sādhavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
āḍhyo vāpi daridro vā duḥkhitaḥ sukhito ’pi vā |
nirdoṣo vā sadoṣo vā vayasyaḥ paramā gatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
dhanatyāgaḥ sukhatyāgo dehatyāgo ’pi vā punaḥ |
vayasyārthe pravartante snehaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā tathāvidham ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tat tathety abravīd rāmaḥ sugrīvaṁ priyavādinam |
lakṣmaṇasyāgrato lakṣmyā vāsavasyeva dhīmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tato rāmaṁ sthitaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā lakṣmaṇaṁ ca mahābalam |
sugrīvaḥ sarvataś cakṣur vane lolam apātayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sa dadarśa tataḥ sālam avidūre harīśvaraḥ |
supuṣpam īṣatpatrāḍhyaṁ bhramarair upaśobhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tasyaikāṁ parṇabahulāṁ bhaṅktvā śākhāṁ supuṣpitām |
sālasyāstīrya sugrīvo niṣasāda sarāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tāv āsīnau tato dr̥ṣṭvā hanūmān api lakṣmaṇam |
sālaśākhāṁ samutpāṭya vinītam upaveśayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tataḥ prahr̥ṣṭaḥ sugrīvaḥ ślakṣṇaṁ madhurayā girā |
uvāca praṇayād rāmaṁ harṣavyākulitākṣaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
ahaṁ vinikr̥to bhrātrā carāmy eṣa bhayārditaḥ |
r̥śyamūkaṁ girivaraṁ hr̥tabhāryaḥ suduḥkhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
so ’haṁ trasto bhaye magno vasāmy udbhrāntacetanaḥ |
vālinā nikr̥to bhrātrā kr̥tavairaś ca rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
vālino me bhayārtasya sarvalokābhayaṁkara |
mamāpi tvam anāthasya prasādaṁ kartum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
evam uktas tu tejasvī dharmajño dharmavatsalaḥ |
pratyuvāca sa kākutsthaḥ sugrīvaṁ prahasann iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
upakāraphalaṁ mitram apakāro ’rilakṣaṇam |
adyaiva taṁ haniṣyāmi tava bhāryāpahāriṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
ime hi me mahāvegāḥ patriṇas tigmatejasaḥ |
kārtikeyavanodbhūtāḥ śarā hemavibhūṣitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
kaṅkapatrapraticchannā mahendrāśanisaṁnibhāḥ |
suparvāṇaḥ sutīkṣṇāgrā saroṣā bhujagā iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
bhrātr̥saṁjñam amitraṁ te vālinaṁ kr̥takilbiṣam |
śarair vinihataṁ paśya vikīrṇam iva parvatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
rāghavasya vacaḥ śrutvā sugrīvo vāhinīpatiḥ |
praharṣam atulaṁ lebhe sādhu sādhv iti cābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
rāmaśokābhibhūto ’haṁ śokārtānāṁ bhavān gatiḥ |
vayasya iti kr̥tvā hi tvayy ahaṁ paridevaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tvaṁ hi pāṇipradānena vayasyo so ’gnisākṣikaḥ |
kr̥taḥ prāṇair bahumataḥ satyenāpi śapāmy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
vayasya iti kr̥tvā ca visrabdhaṁ pravadāmy aham |
duḥkham antargataṁ yan me mano dahati nityaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
etāvad uktvā vacanaṁ bāṣpadūṣitalocanaḥ |
bāṣpopahatayā vācā noccaiḥ śaknoti bhāṣitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
bāṣpavegaṁ tu sahasā nadīvegam ivāgatam |
dhārayām āsa dhairyeṇa sugrīvo rāmasaṁnidhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
saṁnigr̥hya tu taṁ bāṣpaṁ pramr̥jya nayane śubhe |
viniḥśvasya ca tejasvī rāghavaṁ punar abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
purāhaṁ valinā rāma rājyāt svād avaropitaḥ |
paruṣāṇi ca saṁśrāvya nirdhūto ’smi balīyasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
hr̥tā bhāryā ca me tena prāṇebhyo ’pi garīyasī |
suhr̥daś ca madīyā ye saṁyatā bandhaneṣu te ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
yatnavāṁś ca suduṣṭātmā mad vināśāya rāghava |
bahuśas tat prayuktāś ca vānarā nihatā mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
śaṅkayā tv etayā cāhaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā tvām api rāghava |
nopasarpāmy ahaṁ bhīto bhaye sarve hi bibhyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
kevalaṁ hi sahāyā me hanumat pramukhās tv ime |
ato ’haṁ dhārayāmy adya prāṇān kr̥cchra gato ’pi san ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
ete hi kapayaḥ snigdhā māṁ rakṣanti samantataḥ |
saha gacchanti gantavye nityaṁ tiṣṭhanti ca sthite ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
saṁkṣepas tv eṣa me rāma kim uktvā vistaraṁ hi te |
sa me jyeṣṭho ripur bhrātā vālī viśrutapauruṣaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
tadvināśād dhi me duḥkhaṁ pranaṣṭaṁ syād anantaram |
sukhaṁ me jīvitaṁ caiva tadvināśanibandhanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
eṣa me rāma śokāntaḥ śokārtena niveditaḥ |
duḥkhito ’duḥkhito vāpi sakhyur nityaṁ sakhā gatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
śrutvaitac ca vaco rāmaḥ sugrīvam idam abravīt |
kiṁnimittam abhūd vairaṁ śrotum icchāmi tattvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
sukhaṁ hi kāraṇaṁ śrutvā vairasya tava vānara |
ānantaryaṁ vidhāsyāmi saṁpradhārya balābalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
balavān hi mamāmarṣaḥ śrutvā tvām avamānitam |
vardhate hr̥dayotkampī prāvr̥ḍvega ivāmbhasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
hr̥ṣṭaḥ kathaya visrabdho yāvad āropyate dhanuḥ |
sr̥ṣṭaś ca hi mayā bāṇo nirastaś ca ripus tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
evam uktas tu sugrīvaḥ kākutsthena mahātmanā |
praharṣam atulaṁ lebhe caturbhiḥ saha vānaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
tataḥ prahr̥ṣṭavadanaḥ sugrīvo lakṣmaṇāgraje |
vairasya kāraṇaṁ tattvam ākhyātum upacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
