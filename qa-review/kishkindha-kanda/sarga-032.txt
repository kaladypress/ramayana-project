=== Sarga 32 ===

---
1.
atha pratisamādiṣṭo lakṣmaṇaḥ paravīrahā |
praviveśa guhāṁ ghorāṁ kiṣkindhāṁ rāmaśāsanāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
dvārasthā harayas tatra mahākāyā mahābalāḥ |
babhūvur lakṣmaṇaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā sarve prāñjalayaḥ sthitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
niḥśvasantaṁ tu taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā kruddhaṁ daśarathātmajam |
babhūvur harayas trastā na cainaṁ paryavārayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
sa taṁ ratnamayīṁ śrīmān divyāṁ puṣpitakānanām |
ramyāṁ ratnasamākīrṇāṁ dadarśa mahatīṁ guhām ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
harmyaprāsādasaṁbādhāṁ nānāpaṇyopaśobhitām |
sarvakāmaphalair vr̥kṣaiḥ puṣpitair upaśobhitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
devagandharvaputraiś ca vānaraiḥ kāmarūpibhiḥ |
divya mālyāmbaradhāraiḥ śobhitāṁ priyadarśanaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
candanāgarupadmānāṁ gandhaiḥ surabhigandhinām |
maireyāṇāṁ madhūnāṁ ca saṁmoditamahāpathām ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
vindhyamerugiriprasthaiḥ prāsādair naikabhūmibhiḥ |
dadarśa girinadyaś ca vimalās tatra rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
aṅgadasya gr̥haṁ ramyaṁ maindasya dvividasya ca |
gavayasya gavākṣasya gajasya śarabhasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
vidyunmāleś ca saṁpāteḥ sūryākṣasya hanūmataḥ |
vīrabāhoḥ subāhoś ca nalasya ca mahātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
kumudasya suṣeṇasya tārajāmbavatos tathā |
dadhivaktrasya nīlasya supāṭalasunetrayoḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
eteṣāṁ kapimukhyānāṁ rājamārge mahātmanām |
dadarśa gr̥hamukhyāni mahāsārāṇi lakṣmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
pāṇḍurābhraprakāśāni divyamālyayutāni ca |
prabhūtadhanadhānyāni strīratnaiḥ śobhitāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
pāṇḍureṇa tu śailena parikṣiptaṁ durāsadam |
vānarendragr̥haṁ ramyaṁ mahendrasadanopamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
śulkaiḥ prāsādaśikharaiḥ kailāsaśikharopamaiḥ |
sarvakāmaphalair vr̥kṣaiḥ puṣṭitair upaśobhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
mahendradattaiḥ śrīmadbhir nīlajīmūtasaṁnibhaiḥ |
divyapuṣpaphalair vr̥kṣaiḥ śītacchāyair manoramaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
haribhiḥ saṁvr̥tadvāraṁ balibhiḥ śastrapāṇibhiḥ |
divyamālyāvr̥taṁ śubhraṁ taptakāñcanatoraṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
sugrīvasya gr̥haṁ ramyaṁ praviveśa mahābalaḥ |
avāryamāṇaḥ saumitrir mahābhram iva bhāskaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
sa sapta kakṣyā dharmātmā yānāsanasamāvr̥tāḥ |
praviśya sumahad guptaṁ dadarśāntaḥpuraṁ mahat ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
haimarājataparyaṅkair bahubhiś ca varāsanaiḥ |
mahārhāstaraṇopetais tatra tatropaśobhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
praviśann eva satataṁ śuśrāva madhurasvaram |
tantrīgītasamākīrṇaṁ samagītapadākṣaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
bahvīś ca vividhākārā rūpayauvanagarvitāḥ |
striyaḥ sugrīvabhavane dadarśa sa mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
dr̥ṣṭvābhijanasaṁpannāś citramālyakr̥tasrajaḥ |
varamālyakr̥tavyagrā bhūṣaṇottamabhūṣitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
nātr̥ptān nāti ca vyagrān nānudāttaparicchadān |
sugrīvānucarāṁś cāpi lakṣayām āsa lakṣmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tataḥ sugrīvam āsīnaṁ kāñcane paramāsane |
mahārhāstaraṇopete dadarśādityasaṁnibham ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
divyābharaṇacitrāṅgaṁ divyarūpaṁ yaśasvinam |
divyamālyāmbaradharaṁ mahendram iva durjayam |
divyābharaṇamālyābhiḥ pramadābhiḥ samāvr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
rumāṁ tu vīraḥ parirabhya gāḍhaṁ; varāsanastho varahemavarṇaḥ |
dadarśa saumitrim adīnasattvaṁ; viśālanetraḥ suviśālanetram ||

=== meaning ===

---
