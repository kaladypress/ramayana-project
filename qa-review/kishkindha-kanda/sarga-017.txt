=== Sarga 17 ===

---
1.
tataḥ śareṇābhihato rāmeṇa raṇakarkaśaḥ |
papāta sahasā vālī nikr̥tta iva pādapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sa bhūmau nyastasarvāṅgas taptakāñcanabhūṣaṇaḥ |
apatad devarājasya muktaraśmir iva dhvajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tasmin nipatite bhūmau haryr̥ṣāṇāṁ gaṇeśvare |
naṣṭacandram iva vyoma na vyarājata bhūtalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
bhūmau nipatitasyāpi tasya dehaṁ mahātmanaḥ |
na śrīr jahāti na prāṇā na tejo na parākramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
śakradattā varā mālā kāñcanī ratnabhūṣitā |
dadhāra harimukhyasya prāṇāṁs tejaḥ śriyaṁ ca sā ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
sa tayā mālayā vīro haimayā hariyūthapaḥ |
saṁdhyānugataparyantaḥ payodhara ivābhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tasya mālā ca dehaś ca marmaghātī ca yaḥ śaraḥ |
tridheva racitā lakṣmīḥ patitasyāpi śobhate ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tad astraṁ tasya vīrasya svargamārgaprabhāvanam |
rāmabāṇāsanakṣiptam āvahat paramāṁ gatim ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
taṁ tathā patitaṁ saṁkhye gatārciṣam ivānalam |
yayātim iva puṇyānte devalokāt paricyutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
ādityam iva kālena yugānte bhuvi pātitam |
mahendram iva durdharṣaṁ mahendram iva duḥsaham ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
mahendraputraṁ patitaṁ vālinaṁ hemamālinam |
siṁhoraskaṁ mahābāhuṁ dīptāsyaṁ harilocanam |
lakṣmaṇānugato rāmo dadarśopasasarpa ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sa dr̥ṣṭvā rāghavaṁ vālī lakṣmaṇaṁ ca mahābalam |
abravīt praśritaṁ vākyaṁ paruṣaṁ dharmasaṁhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
parāṅmukhavadhaṁ kr̥tvā ko nu prāptas tvayā guṇaḥ |
yad ahaṁ yuddhasaṁrabdhas tvatkr̥te nidhanaṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
kulīnaḥ sattvasaṁpannas tejasvī caritavrataḥ |
rāmaḥ karuṇavedī ca prajānāṁ ca hite rataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sānukrośo mahotsāhaḥ samayajño dr̥ḍhavrataḥ |
iti te sarvabhūtāni kathayanti yaśo bhuvi ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tān guṇān saṁpradhāryāham agryaṁ cābhijanaṁ tava |
tārayā pratiṣiddhaḥ san sugrīveṇa samāgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
na mām anyena saṁrabdhaṁ pramattaṁ veddhum arhasi |
iti me buddhir utpannā babhūvādarśane tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
na tvāṁ vinihatātmānaṁ dharmadhvajam adhārmikam |
jāne pāpasamācāraṁ tr̥ṇaiḥ kūpam ivāvr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
satāṁ veṣadharaṁ pāpaṁ pracchannam iva pāvakam |
nāhaṁ tvām abhijānāni dharmacchadmābhisaṁvr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
viṣaye vā pure vā te yadā nāpakaromy aham |
na ca tvāṁ pratijāne ’haṁ kasmāt tvaṁ haṁsy akilbiṣam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
phalamūlāśanaṁ nityaṁ vānaraṁ vanagocaram |
mām ihāpratiyudhyantam anyena ca samāgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tvaṁ narādhipateḥ putraḥ pratītaḥ priyadarśanaḥ |
liṅgam apy asti te rājan dr̥śyate dharmasaṁhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
kaḥ kṣatriyakule jātaḥ śrutavān naṣṭasaṁśayaḥ |
dharmaliṅga praticchannaḥ krūraṁ karma samācaret ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
rāma rājakule jāto dharmavān iti viśrutaḥ |
abhavyo bhavyarūpeṇa kimarthaṁ paridhāvasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
sāma dānaṁ kṣamā dharmaḥ satyaṁ dhr̥tiparākramau |
pārthivānāṁ guṇā rājan daṇḍaś cāpy apakāriṣu ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
vayaṁ vanacarā rāma mr̥gā mūlaphalāśanāḥ |
eṣā prakr̥tir asmākaṁ puruṣas tvaṁ nareśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
bhūmir hiraṇyaṁ rūpyaṁ ca nigrahe kāraṇāni ca |
tatra kas te vane lobho madīyeṣu phaleṣu vā ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
nayaś ca vinayaś cobhau nigrahānugrahāv api |
rājavr̥ttir asaṁkīrṇā na nr̥pāḥ kāmavr̥ttayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
tvaṁ tu kāmapradhānaś ca kopanaś cānavasthitaḥ |
rājavr̥ttaiś ca saṁkīrṇaḥ śarāsanaparāyaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
na te ’sty apacitir dharme nārthe buddhir avasthitā |
indriyaiḥ kāmavr̥ttaḥ san kr̥ṣyase manujeśvara ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
hatvā bāṇena kākutstha mām ihānaparādhinam |
kiṁ vakṣyasi satāṁ madhye karma kr̥tvā jugupsitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
rājahā brahmahā goghnaś coraḥ prāṇivadhe rataḥ |
nāstikaḥ parivettā ca sarve nirayagāminaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
adhāryaṁ carma me sadbhī romāṇy asthi ca varjitam |
abhakṣyāṇi ca māṁsāni tvadvidhair dharmacāribhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
pañca pañcanakhā bhakṣyā brahmakṣatreṇa rāghava |
śalyakaḥ śvāvidho godhā śaśaḥ kūrmaś ca pañcamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
carma cāsthi ca me rājan na spr̥śanti manīṣiṇaḥ |
abhakṣyāṇi ca māṁsāni so ’haṁ pañcanakho hataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
tvayā nāthena kākutstha na sanāthā vasuṁdharā |
pramadā śīlasaṁpannā dhūrtena patitā yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
śaṭho naikr̥tikaḥ kṣudro mithyā praśritamānasaḥ |
kathaṁ daśarathena tvaṁ jātaḥ pāpo mahātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
chinnacāritryakakṣyeṇa satāṁ dharmātivartinā |
tyaktadharmāṅkuśenāhaṁ nihato rāmahastinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
dr̥śyamānas tu yudhyethā mayā yudhi nr̥pātmaja |
adya vaivasvataṁ devaṁ paśyes tvaṁ nihato mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
tvayādr̥śyena tu raṇe nihato ’haṁ durāsadaḥ |
prasuptaḥ pannageneva naraḥ pānavaśaṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
sugrīvapriyakāmena yad ahaṁ nihatas tvayā |
kaṇṭhe baddhvā pradadyāṁ te ’nihataṁ rāvaṇaṁ raṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
nyastāṁ sāgaratoye vā pātāle vāpi maithilīm |
jānayeyaṁ tavādeśāc chvetām aśvatarīm iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
yuktaṁ yat prapnuyād rājyaṁ sugrīvaḥ svargate mayi |
ayuktaṁ yad adharmeṇa tvayāhaṁ nihato raṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
kāmam evaṁvidhaṁ lokaḥ kālena viniyujyate |
kṣamaṁ ced bhavatā prāptam uttaraṁ sādhu cintyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
ity evam uktvā pariśuṣkavaktraḥ; śarābhighātād vyathito mahātmā |
samīkṣya rāmaṁ ravisaṁnikāśaṁ; tūṣṇīṁ babhūvāmararājasūnuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
