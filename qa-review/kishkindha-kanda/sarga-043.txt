=== Sarga 43 ===

---
1.
viśeṣeṇa tu sugrīvo hanumatyartham uktavān |
sa hi tasmin hariśreṣṭhe niścitārtho ’rthasādhane ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
na bhūmau nāntarikṣe vā nāmbare nāmarālaye |
nāpsu vā gatisaṁgaṁ te paśyāmi haripuṁgava ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sāsurāḥ sahagandharvāḥ sanāganaradevatāḥ |
viditāḥ sarvalokās te sasāgaradharādharāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
gatir vegaś ca tejaś ca lāghavaṁ ca mahākape |
pitus te sadr̥śaṁ vīra mārutasya mahaujasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tejasā vāpi te bhūtaṁ samaṁ bhuvi na vidyate |
tad yathā labhyate sītā tattvam evopapādaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tvayy eva hanumann asti balaṁ buddhiḥ parākramaḥ |
deśakālānuvr̥ttaś ca nayaś ca nayapaṇḍita ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tataḥ kāryasamāsaṁgam avagamya hanūmati |
viditvā hanumantaṁ ca cintayām āsa rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sarvathā niścitārtho ’yaṁ hanūmati harīśvaraḥ |
niścitārthataraś cāpi hanūmān kāryasādhane ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tad evaṁ prasthitasyāsya parijñātasya karmabhiḥ |
bhartrā parigr̥hītasya dhruvaḥ kāryaphalodayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
taṁ samīkṣya mahātejā vyavasāyottaraṁ harim |
kr̥tārtha iva saṁvr̥ttaḥ prahr̥ṣṭendriyamānasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
dadau tasya tataḥ prītaḥ svanāmāṅkopaśobhitam |
aṅgulīyam abhijñānaṁ rājaputryāḥ paraṁtapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
anena tvāṁ hariśreṣṭha cihnena janakātmajā |
matsakāśād anuprāptam anudvignānupaśyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
vyavasāyaś ca te vīra sattvayuktaś ca vikramaḥ |
sugrīvasya ca saṁdeśaḥ siddhiṁ kathayatīva me ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sa tad gr̥hya hariśreṣṭhaḥ sthāpya mūrdhni kr̥tāñjaliḥ |
vanditvā caraṇau caiva prasthitaḥ plavagottamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa tat prakarṣan hariṇāṁ balaṁ mahad; babhūva vīraḥ pavanātmajaḥ kapi |
gatāmbude vyomni viśuddhamaṇḍalaḥ; śaśīva nakṣatragaṇopaśobhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
atibalabalam āśritas tavāhaṁ; harivaravikramavikramair analpaiḥ |
pavanasuta yathābhigamyate sā; janakasutā hanumaṁs tathā kuruṣva ||

=== meaning ===

---
