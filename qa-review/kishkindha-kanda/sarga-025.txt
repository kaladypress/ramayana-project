=== Sarga 25 ===

---
1.
tataḥ śokābhisaṁtaptaṁ sugrīvaṁ klinnavāsanam |
śākhāmr̥gamahāmātrāḥ parivāryopatasthire ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
abhigamya mahābāhuṁ rāmam akliṣṭakāriṇam |
sthitāḥ prāñjalayaḥ sarve pitāmaham ivarṣayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tataḥ kāñcanaśailābhas taruṇārkanibhānanaḥ |
abravīt prāñjalir vākyaṁ hanumān mārutātmajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
bhavatprasādāt sugrīvaḥ pitr̥paitāmahaṁ mahat |
vānarāṇāṁ suduṣprāpaṁ prāpto rājyam idaṁ prabho ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
bhavatā samanujñātaḥ praviśya nagaraṁ śubham |
saṁvidhāsyati kāryāṇi sarvāṇi sasuhr̥jjanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
snāto ’yaṁ vividhair gandhair auṣadhaiś ca yathāvidhi |
arcayiṣyati ratnaiś ca mālyaiś ca tvāṁ viśeṣataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
imāṁ giriguhāṁ ramyām abhigantum ito ’rhasi |
kuruṣva svāmi saṁbandhaṁ vānarān saṁpraharṣayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
evam ukto hanumatā rāghavaḥ paravīrahā |
pratyuvāca hanūmantaṁ buddhimān vākyakovidaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
caturdaśasamāḥ saumya grāmaṁ vā yadi vā puram |
na pravekṣyāmi hanuman pitur nirdeśapālakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
susamr̥ddhāṁ guhāṁ divyāṁ sugrīvo vānararṣabhaḥ |
praviṣṭo vidhivad vīraḥ kṣipraṁ rājye ’bhiṣicyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
evam uktvā hanūmantaṁ rāmaḥ sugrīvam abravīt |
imam apy aṅgadaṁ vīra yauvarājye ’bhiṣecaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
pūrvo ’yaṁ vārṣiko māsaḥ śrāvaṇaḥ salilāgamaḥ |
pravr̥ttāḥ saumya catvāro māsā vārṣikasaṁjñitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
nāyam udyogasamayaḥ praviśa tvaṁ purīṁ śubhām |
asmin vatsyāmy ahaṁ saumya parvate sahalakṣmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
iyaṁ giriguhā ramyā viśālā yuktamārutā |
prabhūtasalilā saumya prabhūtakamalotpalā ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
kārtike samanuprāpte tvaṁ rāvaṇavadhe yata |
eṣa naḥ samayaḥ saumya praviśa tvaṁ svam ālayam |
abhiṣiñcasva rājye ca suhr̥daḥ saṁpraharṣaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
iti rāmābhyanujñātaḥ sugrīvo vānararṣabhaḥ |
praviveśa purīṁ ramyāṁ kiṣkindhāṁ vālipālitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
taṁ vānarasahasrāṇi praviṣṭaṁ vānareśvaram |
abhivādya prahr̥ṣṭāni sarvataḥ paryavārayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tataḥ prakr̥tayaḥ sarvā dr̥ṣṭvā harigaṇeśvaram |
praṇamya mūrdhnā patitā vasudhāyāṁ samāhitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
sugrīvaḥ prakr̥tīḥ sarvāḥ saṁbhāṣyotthāpya vīryavān |
bhrātur antaḥpuraṁ saumyaṁ praviveśa mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
praviśya tv abhiniṣkrāntaṁ sugrīvaṁ vānararṣabham |
abhyaṣiñcanta suhr̥daḥ sahasrākṣam ivāmarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tasya pāṇḍuram ājahruś chatraṁ hemapariṣkr̥tam |
śukle ca bālavyajane hemadaṇḍe yaśaskare ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tathā sarvāṇi ratnāni sarvabījauṣadhāni ca |
sakṣīrāṇāṁ ca vr̥kṣāṇāṁ prarohān kusumāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
śuklāni caiva vastrāṇi śvetaṁ caivānulepanam |
sugandhīni ca mālyāni sthalajāny ambujāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
candanāni ca divyāni gandhāṁś ca vividhān bahūn |
akṣataṁ jātarūpaṁ ca priyaṅgumadhusarpiṣī ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
dadhicarma ca vaiyāghraṁ vārāhī cāpy upānahau |
samālambhanam ādāya rocanāṁ samanaḥśilām |
ājagmus tatra muditā varāḥ kanyās tu ṣoḍaśa ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tatas te vānaraśreṣṭhaṁ yathākālaṁ yathāvidhi |
ratnair vastraiś ca bhakṣyaiś ca toṣayitvā dvijarṣabhān ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tataḥ kuśaparistīrṇaṁ samiddhaṁ jātavedasaṁ |
mantrapūtena haviṣā hutvā mantravido janāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tato hemapratiṣṭhāne varāstaraṇasaṁvr̥te |
prāsādaśikhare ramye citramālyopaśobhite ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
prāṅmukhaṁ vividhair mantraiḥ sthāpayitvā varāsane |
nadīnadebhyaḥ saṁhr̥tya tīrthebhyaś ca samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
āhr̥tya ca samudrebhyaḥ sarvebhyo vānararṣabhāḥ |
apaḥ kanakakumbheṣu nidhāya vimalāḥ śubhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
śubhair vr̥ṣabhaśr̥ṅgaiś ca kalaśaiś cāpi kāñcanaiḥ |
śāstradr̥ṣṭena vidhinā maharṣivihitena ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
gajo gavākṣo gavayaḥ śarabho gandhamādanaḥ |
maindaś ca dvividaś caiva hanūmāñ jāmbavān nalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
abhyaṣiñcanta sugrīvaṁ prasannena sugandhinā |
salilena sahasrākṣaṁ vasavo vāsavaṁ yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
abhiṣikte tu sugrīve sarve vānarapuṁgavāḥ |
pracukruśur mahātmāno hr̥ṣṭās tatra sahasraśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
rāmasya tu vacaḥ kurvan sugrīvo haripuṁgavaḥ |
aṅgadaṁ saṁpariṣvajya yauvarājye ’bhiṣecayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
aṅgade cābhiṣikte tu sānukrośāḥ plavaṁgamāḥ |
sādhu sādhv iti sugrīvaṁ mahātmāno ’bhyapūjayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
hr̥ṣṭapuṣṭajanākīrṇā patākādhvajaśobhitā |
babhūva nagarī ramyā kṣikindhā girigahvare ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
nivedya rāmāya tadā mahātmane; mahābhiṣekaṁ kapivāhinīpatiḥ |
rumāṁ ca bhāryāṁ pratilabhya vīryavān; avāpa rājyaṁ tridaśādhipo yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
