=== Sarga 27 ===

---
1.
sa tadā vālinaṁ hatvāh sugrīvam abhiṣicya ca |
vasan mālyavataḥ pr̥ṣṭe rāmo lakṣmaṇam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
ayaṁ sa kālaḥ saṁprāptaḥ samayo ’dya jalāgamaḥ |
saṁpaśya tvaṁ nabho meghaiḥ saṁvr̥taṁ girisaṁnibhaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
nava māsa dhr̥taṁ garbhaṁ bhāskārasya gabhastibhiḥ |
pītvā rasaṁ samudrāṇāṁ dyauḥ prasūte rasāyanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
śakyam ambaram āruhya meghasopānapaṅktibhiḥ |
kuṭajārjunamālābhir alaṁkartuṁ divākaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
saṁdhyārāgotthitais tāmrair anteṣv adhikapāṇḍuraiḥ |
snigdhair abhrapaṭacchadair baddhavraṇam ivāmbaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
mandamārutaniḥśvāsaṁ saṁdhyācandanarañjitam |
āpāṇḍujaladaṁ bhāti kāmāturam ivāmbaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
eṣā dharmaparikliṣṭā navavāripariplutā |
sīteva śokasaṁtaptā mahī bāṣpaṁ vimuñcati ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
meghodaravinirmuktāḥ kahlārasukhaśītalāḥ |
śakyam añjalibhiḥ pātuṁ vātāḥ ketakigandhinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
eṣa phullārjunaḥ śailaḥ ketakair adhivāsitaḥ |
sugrīva iva śāntārir dhārābhir abhiṣicyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
meghakr̥ṣṇājinadharā dhārāyajñopavītinaḥ |
mārutāpūritaguhāḥ prādhītā iva parvatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
kaśābhir iva haimībhir vidyudbhir iva tāḍitam |
antaḥstanitanirghoṣaṁ savedanam ivāmbaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
nīlameghāśritā vidyut sphurantī pratibhāti me |
sphurantī rāvaṇasyāṅke vaidehīva tapasvinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
imās tā manmathavatāṁ hitāḥ pratihatā diśaḥ |
anuliptā iva ghanair naṣṭagrahaniśākarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
kva cid bāṣpābhisaṁruddhān varṣāgamasamutsukān |
kuṭajān paśya saumitre puṣṭitān girisānuṣu |
mama śokābhibhūtasya kāmasaṁdīpanān sthitān ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
rajaḥ praśāntaṁ sahimo ’dya vāyur; nidāghadoṣaprasarāḥ praśāntāḥ |
sthitā hi yātrā vasudhādhipānāṁ; pravāsino yānti narāḥ svadeśān ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
saṁprasthitā mānasavāsalubdhāḥ; priyānvitāḥ saṁprati cakravākaḥ |
abhīkṣṇavarṣodakavikṣateṣu; yānāni mārgeṣu na saṁpatanti ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
kva cit prakāśaṁ kva cid aprakāśaṁ; nabhaḥ prakīrṇāmbudharaṁ vibhāti |
kva cit kva cit parvatasaṁniruddhaṁ; rūpaṁ yathā śāntamahārṇavasya ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
vyāmiśritaṁ sarjakadambapuṣpair; navaṁ jalaṁ parvatadhātutāmram |
mayūrakekābhir anuprayātaṁ; śailāpagāḥ śīghrataraṁ vahanti ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
rasākulaṁ ṣaṭpadasaṁnikāśaṁ; prabhujyate jambuphalaṁ prakāmam |
anekavarṇaṁ pavanāvadhūtaṁ; bhūmau pataty āmraphalaṁ vipakvam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
vidyutpatākāḥ sabalāka mālāḥ; śailendrakūṭākr̥tisaṁnikāśāḥ |
garjanti meghāḥ samudīrṇanādā; mattagajendrā iva saṁyugasthaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
meghābhikāmī parisaṁpatantī; saṁmoditā bhāti balākapaṅktiḥ |
vātāvadhūtā varapauṇḍarīkī; lambeva mālā racitāmbarasya ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
nidrā śanaiḥ keśavam abhyupaiti; drutaṁ nadī sāgaram abhyupaiti |
hr̥ṣṭā balākā ghanam abhyupaiti; kāntā sakāmā priyam abhyupaiti ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
jātā vanāntāḥ śikhisupranr̥ttā; jātāḥ kadambāḥ sakadambaśākhāḥ |
jātā vr̥ṣā goṣu samānakāmā; jātā mahī sasyavanābhirāmā ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
vahanti varṣanti nadanti bhānti; dhyāyanti nr̥tyanti samāśvasanti |
nadyo ghanā mattagajā vanāntāḥ; priyāvinīhāḥ śikhinaḥ plavaṁgāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
praharṣitāḥ ketakapuṣpagandham; āghrāya hr̥ṣṭā vananirjhareṣu |
prapāta śabdākulitā gajendrāḥ; sārdhaṁ mayūraiḥ samadā nadanti ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
dhārānipātair abhihanyamānāḥ; kadambaśākhāsu vilambamānāḥ |
kṣaṇārjitaṁ puṣparasāvagāḍhaṁ; śanair madaṁ ṣaṭcaraṇās tyajanti ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
aṅgāracūrṇotkarasaṁnikāśaiḥ; phalaiḥ suparyāpta rasaiḥ samr̥ddhaiḥ |
jambūdrumāṇāṁ pravibhānti śākhā; nilīyamānā iva ṣaṭpadaughaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
taḍitpatākābhir alaṁkr̥tānām; udīrṇagambhīramahāravāṇām |
vibhānti rūpāṇi balāhakānāṁ; raṇodyatānām iva vāraṇānām ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
mārgānugaḥ śailavanānusārī; saṁprasthito megharavaṁ niśamya |
yuddhābhikāmaḥ pratināgaśaṅkī; matto gajendraḥ pratisaṁnivr̥ttaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
muktāsakāśaṁ salilaṁ patad vai; sunirmalaṁ patrapuṭeṣu lagnam |
hr̥ṣṭā vivarṇacchadanā vihaṁgāḥ; surendradattaṁ tr̥ṣitāḥ pibanti ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
nīleṣu nīlā navavāripūrṇā; megheṣu meghāḥ pravibhānti saktāḥ |
davāgnidagdheṣu davāgnidagdhāḥ; śaileṣu śailā iva baddhamūlāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
mattā gajendrā muditā gavendrā; vaneṣu viśrāntatarā mr̥gendrāḥ |
ramyā nagendrā nibhr̥tā nagendrāḥ; prakrīḍito vāridharaiḥ surendraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
vr̥ttā yātrā narendrāṇāṁ senā pratinivartate |
vairāṇi caiva mārgāś ca salilena samīkr̥tāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
māsi prauṣṭhapade brahma brāhmaṇānāṁ vivakṣatām |
ayam adhyāyasamayaḥ sāmagānām upasthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
nivr̥ttakarmāyatano nūnaṁ saṁcitasaṁcayaḥ |
āṣāḍhīm abhyupagato bharataḥ koṣakādhipaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
nūnam āpūryamāṇāyāḥ sarayvā vadhate rayaḥ |
māṁ samīkṣya samāyāntam ayodhyāyā iva svanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
imāḥ sphītaguṇā varṣāḥ sugrīvaḥ sukham aśnute |
vijitāriḥ sadāraś ca rājye mahati ca sthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
ahaṁ tu hr̥tadāraś ca rājyāc ca mahataś cyutaḥ |
nadīkūlam iva klinnam avasīdāmi lakṣmaṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
śokaś ca mama vistīrṇo varṣāś ca bhr̥śadurgamāḥ |
rāvaṇaś ca mahāñ śatrur apāraṁ pratibhāti me ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
ayātrāṁ caiva dr̥ṣṭvemāṁ mārgāṁś ca bhr̥śadurgamān |
praṇate caiva sugrīve na mayā kiṁ cid īritam ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
api cātiparikliṣṭaṁ cirād dāraiḥ samāgatam |
ātmakāryagarīyastvād vaktuṁ necchāmi vānaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
svayam eva hi viśramya jñātvā kālam upāgatam |
upakāraṁ ca sugrīvo vetsyate nātra saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
tasmāt kālapratīkṣo ’haṁ sthito ’smi śubhalakṣaṇa |
sugrīvasya nadīnāṁ ca prasādam anupālayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
upakāreṇa vīro hi pratikāreṇa yujyate |
akr̥tajño ’pratikr̥to hanti sattvavatāṁ manaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
athaivam uktaḥ praṇidhāya lakṣmaṇaḥ; kr̥tāñjalis tat pratipūjya bhāṣitam |
uvāca rāmaṁ svabhirāma darśanaṁ; pradarśayan darśanam ātmanaḥ śubham ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
yathoktam etat tava sarvam īpsitaṁ; narendra kartā nacirād dharīśvaraḥ |
śaratpratīkṣaḥ kṣamatām imaṁ bhavāñ; jalaprapātaṁ ripunigrahe dhr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
