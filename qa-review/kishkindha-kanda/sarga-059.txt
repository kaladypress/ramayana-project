=== Sarga 59 ===

---
1.
tataḥ kr̥todakaṁ snātaṁ taṁ gr̥dhraṁ hariyūthapāḥ |
upaviṣṭā girau durge parivārya samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tam aṅgadam upāsīnaṁ taiḥ sarvair haribhir vr̥tam |
janitapratyayo harṣāt saṁpātiḥ punar abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
kr̥tvā niḥśabdam ekāgrāḥ śr̥ṇvantu harayo mama |
tattvaṁ saṁkīrtayiṣyāmi yathā jānāmi maithilīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
asya vindhyasya śikhare patito ’smi purā vane |
sūryātapaparītāṅgo nirdagdhaḥ sūryaraśmibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
labdhasaṁjñas tu ṣaḍrātrād vivaśo vihvalann iva |
vīkṣamāṇo diśaḥ sarvā nābhijānāmi kiṁ cana ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tatas tu sāgarāñ śailān nadīḥ sarvāḥ sarāṁsi ca |
vanāny aṭavideśāṁś ca samīkṣya matir āgamat ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
hr̥ṣṭapakṣigaṇākīrṇaḥ kandarāntarakūṭavān |
dakṣiṇasyodadhes tīre vindhyo ’yam iti niścitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
āsīc cātrāśramaṁ puṇyaṁ surair api supūjitam |
r̥ṣir niśākaro nāma yasminn ugratapābhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
aṣṭau varṣasahasrāṇi tenāsminn r̥ṣiṇā vinā |
vasato mama dharmajñāḥ svargate tu niśākare ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
avatīrya ca vindhyāgrāt kr̥cchreṇa viṣamāc chanaiḥ |
tīkṣṇadarbhāṁ vasumatīṁ duḥkhena punar āgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tam r̥ṣiṁ draṣṭu kāmo ’smi duḥkhenābhyāgato bhr̥śam |
jaṭāyuṣā mayā caiva bahuśo ’bhigato hi saḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tasyāśramapadābhyāśe vavur vātāḥ sugandhinaḥ |
vr̥kṣo nāpuṣpitaḥ kaś cid aphalo vā na dr̥śyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
upetya cāśramaṁ puṇyaṁ vr̥kṣamūlam upāśritaḥ |
draṣṭukāmaḥ pratīkṣe ca bhagavantaṁ niśākaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
athāpaśyam adūrastham r̥ṣiṁ jvalitatejasaṁ |
kr̥tābhiṣekaṁ durdharṣam upāvr̥ttam udaṅmukham ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tam r̥kṣāḥ sr̥marā vyāghrāḥ siṁhā nāgāḥ sarīsr̥pāḥ |
parivāryopagacchanti dātāraṁ prāṇino yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tataḥ prāptam r̥ṣiṁ jñātvā tāni sattvāni vai yayuḥ |
praviṣṭe rājani yathā sarvaṁ sāmātyakaṁ balam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
r̥ṣis tu dr̥ṣṭvā māṁ tuṣṭaḥ praviṣṭaś cāśramaṁ punaḥ |
muhūrtamātrān niṣkramya tataḥ kāryam apr̥cchata ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
saumya vaikalyatāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā roṁṇāṁ te nāvagamyate |
agnidagdhāv imau pakṣau tvak caiva vraṇitā tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
dvau gr̥dhrau dr̥ṣṭapūrvau me mātariśvasamau jave |
gr̥dhrāṇāṁ caiva rājānau bhrātarau kāmarūpiṇau ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
jyeṣṭhas tvaṁ tu ca saṁpātir jaṭāyur anujas tava |
mānuṣaṁ rūpam āsthāya gr̥hṇītāṁ caraṇau mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
kiṁ te vyādhisamutthānaṁ pakṣayoḥ patanaṁ katham |
daṇḍo vāyaṁ dhr̥taḥ kena sarvam ākhyāhi pr̥cchataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
