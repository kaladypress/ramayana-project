=== Sarga 18 ===

---
1.
ity uktaḥ praśritaṁ vākyaṁ dharmārthasahitaṁ hitam |
paruṣaṁ vālinā rāmo nihatena vicetasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
taṁ niṣprabham ivādityaṁ muktatoyam ivāmbudam |
uktavākyaṁ hariśreṣṭham upaśāntam ivānalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
dharmārthaguṇasaṁpannaṁ harīśvaram anuttamam |
adhikṣiptas tadā rāmaḥ paścād vālinam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
dharmam arthaṁ ca kāmaṁ ca samayaṁ cāpi laukikam |
avijñāya kathaṁ bālyān mām ihādya vigarhase ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
apr̥ṣṭvā buddhisaṁpannān vr̥ddhān ācāryasaṁmatān |
saumya vānaracāpalyāt tvaṁ māṁ vaktum ihecchasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
ikṣvākūṇām iyaṁ bhūmiḥ saśailavanakānanā |
mr̥gapakṣimanuṣyāṇāṁ nigrahānugrahāv api ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tāṁ pālayati dharmātmā bharataḥ satyavāg r̥juḥ |
dharmakāmārthatattvajño nigrahānugrahe rataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
nayaś ca vinayaś cobhau yasmin satyaṁ ca susthitam |
vikramaś ca yathā dr̥ṣṭaḥ sa rājā deśakālavit ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tasya dharmakr̥tādeśā vayam anye ca pārthivaḥ |
carāmo vasudhāṁ kr̥tsnāṁ dharmasaṁtānam icchavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tasmin nr̥patiśārdūla bharate dharmavatsale |
pālayaty akhilāṁ bhūmiṁ kaś cared dharmanigraham ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
te vayaṁ mārgavibhraṣṭaṁ svadharme parame sthitāḥ |
bharatājñāṁ puraskr̥tya nigr̥hṇīmo yathāvidhi ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tvaṁ tu saṁkliṣṭadharmā ca karmaṇā ca vigarhitaḥ |
kāmatantrapradhānaś ca na sthito rājavartmani ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
jyeṣṭho bhrātā pitā caiva yaś ca vidyāṁ prayacchati |
trayas te pitaro jñeyā dharme ca pathi vartinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
yavīyān ātmanaḥ putraḥ śiṣyaś cāpi guṇoditaḥ |
putravat te trayaś cintyā dharmaś ced atra kāraṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sūkṣmaḥ paramadurjñeyaḥ satāṁ dharmaḥ plavaṁgama |
hr̥disthaḥ sarvabhūtānām ātmā veda śubhāśubham ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
capalaś capalaiḥ sārdhaṁ vānarair akr̥tātmabhiḥ |
jātyandha iva jātyandhair mantrayan drakṣyase nu kim ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
ahaṁ tu vyaktatām asya vacanasya bravīmi te |
na hi māṁ kevalaṁ roṣāt tvaṁ vigarhitum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tad etat kāraṇaṁ paśya yadarthaṁ tvaṁ mayā hataḥ |
bhrātur vartasi bhāryāyāṁ tyaktvā dharmaṁ sanātanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
asya tvaṁ dharamāṇasya sugrīvasya mahātmanaḥ |
rumāyāṁ vartase kāmāt snuṣāyāṁ pāpakarmakr̥t ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tad vyatītasya te dharmāt kāmavr̥ttasya vānara |
bhrātr̥bhāryābhimarśe ’smin daṇḍo ’yaṁ pratipāditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
na hi dharmaviruddhasya lokavr̥ttād apeyuṣaḥ |
daṇḍād anyatra paśyāmi nigrahaṁ hariyūthapa ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
aurasīṁ bhaginīṁ vāpi bhāryāṁ vāpy anujasya yaḥ |
pracareta naraḥ kāmāt tasya daṇḍo vadhaḥ smr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
bharatas tu mahīpālo vayaṁ tv ādeśavartinaḥ |
tvaṁ ca dharmād atikrāntaḥ kathaṁ śakyam upekṣitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
gurudharmavyatikrāntaṁ prājño dharmeṇa pālayan |
bharataḥ kāmavr̥ttānāṁ nigrahe paryavasthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
vayaṁ tu bharatādeśaṁ vidhiṁ kr̥tvā harīśvara |
tvadvidhān bhinnamaryādān niyantuṁ paryavasthitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
sugrīveṇa ca me sakhyaṁ lakṣmaṇena yathā tathā |
dārarājyanimittaṁ ca niḥśreyasi rataḥ sa me ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
pratijñā ca mayā dattā tadā vānarasaṁnidhau |
pratijñā ca kathaṁ śakyā madvidhenānavekṣitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tad ebhiḥ kāraṇaiḥ sarvair mahadbhir dharmasaṁhitaiḥ |
śāsanaṁ tava yad yuktaṁ tad bhavān anumanyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
sarvathā dharma ity eva draṣṭavyas tava nigrahaḥ |
vayasyasyopakartavyaṁ dharmam evānupaśyatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
rājabhir dhr̥tadaṇḍās tu kr̥tvā pāpāni mānavāḥ |
nirmalāḥ svargam āyānti santaḥ sukr̥tino yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
āryeṇa mama māndhātrā vyasanaṁ ghoram īpsitam |
śramaṇena kr̥te pāpe yathā pāpaṁ kr̥taṁ tvayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
anyair api kr̥taṁ pāpaṁ pramattair vasudhādhipaiḥ |
prāyaścittaṁ ca kurvanti tena tac chāmyate rajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
tad alaṁ paritāpena dharmataḥ parikalpitaḥ |
vadho vānaraśārdūla na vayaṁ svavaśe sthitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
vāgurābhiś ca pāśaiś ca kūṭaiś ca vividhair narāḥ |
praticchannāś ca dr̥śyāś ca gr̥hṇanti subahūn mr̥gān |
pradhāvitān vā vitrastān visrabdhān ativiṣṭhitān ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
pramattān apramattān vā narā māṁsārthino bhr̥śam |
vidhyanti vimukhāṁś cāpi na ca doṣo ’tra vidyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
yānti rājarṣayaś cātra mr̥gayāṁ dharmakovidāḥ |
tasmāt tvaṁ nihato yuddhe mayā bāṇena vānara |
ayudhyan pratiyudhyan vā yasmāc chākhāmr̥go hy asi ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
durlabhasya ca dharmasya jīvitasya śubhasya ca |
rājāno vānaraśreṣṭha pradātāro na saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
tān na hiṁsyān na cākrośen nākṣipen nāpriyaṁ vadet |
devā mānuṣarūpeṇa caranty ete mahītale ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
tvaṁ tu dharmam avijñāya kevalaṁ roṣam āsthitaḥ |
pradūṣayasi māṁ dharme pitr̥paitāmahe sthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
evam uktas tu rāmeṇa vālī pravyathito bhr̥śam |
pratyuvāca tato rāmaṁ prāñjalir vānareśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
yat tvam āttha naraśreṣṭha tad evaṁ nātra saṁśayaḥ |
prativaktuṁ prakr̥ṣṭe hi nāpakr̥ṣṭas tu śaknuyāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
yad ayuktaṁ mayā pūrvaṁ pramādād vākyam apriyam |
tatrāpi khalu me doṣaṁ kartuṁ nārhasi rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
tvaṁ hi dr̥ṣṭārthatattvajñaḥ prajānāṁ ca hite rataḥ |
kāryakāraṇasiddhau te prasannā buddhir avyayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
mām apy avagataṁ dharmād vyatikrāntapuraskr̥tam |
dharmasaṁhitayā vācā dharmajña paripālaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
bāṣpasaṁruddhakaṇṭhas tu vālī sārtaravaḥ śanaiḥ |
uvāca rāmaṁ saṁprekṣya paṅkalagna iva dvipaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
na tv ātmānam ahaṁ śoce na tārāṁ nāpi bāndhavān |
yathā putraṁ guṇaśreṣṭham aṅgadaṁ kanakāṅgadam ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
sa mamādarśanād dīno bālyāt prabhr̥ti lālitaḥ |
taṭāka iva pītāmbur upaśoṣaṁ gamiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
sugrīve cāṅgade caiva vidhatsva matim uttamām |
tvaṁ hi śāstā ca goptā ca kāryākāryavidhau sthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
yā te narapate vr̥ttir bharate lakṣmaṇe ca yā |
sugrīve cāṅgade rājaṁs tāṁ cintayitum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
maddoṣakr̥tadoṣāṁ tāṁ yathā tārāṁ tapasvinīm |
sugrīvo nāvamanyeta tathāvasthātum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
tvayā hy anugr̥hītena śakyaṁ rājyam upāsitum |
tvadvaśe vartamānena tava cittānuvartinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
52.
sa tam āśvāsayad rāmo vālinaṁ vyaktadarśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
53.
na vayaṁ bhavatā cintyā nāpy ātmā harisattama |
vayaṁ bhavadviśeṣeṇa dharmataḥ kr̥taniścayāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
54.
daṇḍye yaḥ pātayed daṇḍaṁ daṇḍyo yaś cāpi daṇḍyate |
kāryakāraṇasiddhārthāv ubhau tau nāvasīdataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
55.
tad bhavān daṇḍasaṁyogād asmād vigatakalmaṣaḥ |
gataḥ svāṁ prakr̥tiṁ dharmyāṁ dharmadr̥ṣṭtena vartmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
56.
sa tasya vākyaṁ madhuraṁ mahātmanaḥ; samāhitaṁ dharmapathānuvartinaḥ |
niśamya rāmasya raṇāvamardino; vacaḥ suyuktaṁ nijagāda vānaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
57.
śarābhitaptena vicetasā mayā; pradūṣitas tvaṁ yad ajānatā prabho |
idaṁ mahendropamabhīmavikrama; prasāditas tvaṁ kṣama me mahīśvara ||

=== meaning ===

---
