=== Sarga 38 ===

---
1.
iti bruvāṇaṁ sugrīvaṁ rāmo dharmabhr̥tāṁ varaḥ |
bāhubhyāṁ saṁpariṣvajya pratyuvāca kr̥tāñjalim ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
yad indro varṣate varṣaṁ na tac citraṁ bhaved bhuvi |
ādityo vā sahasrāṁśuḥ kuryād vitimiraṁ nabhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
candramā raśmibhiḥ kuryāt pr̥thivīṁ saumya nirmalām |
tvadvidho vāpi mitrāṇāṁ pratikuryāt paraṁtapa ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
evaṁ tvayi na tac citraṁ bhaved yat saumya śobhanam |
jānāmy ahaṁ tvāṁ sugrīva satataṁ priyavādinam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tvatsanāthaḥ sakhe saṁkhye jetāsmi sakalān arīn |
tvam eva me suhr̥n mitraṁ sāhāyyaṁ kartum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
jahārātmavināśāya vaidehīṁ rākṣasādhamaḥ |
vañcayitvā tu paulomīm anuhlādo yathā śacīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
nacirāt taṁ haniṣyāmi rāvaṇaṁ niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ |
paulomyāḥ pitaraṁ dr̥ptaṁ śatakratur ivārihā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
etasminn antare caiva rajaḥ samabhivartata |
uṣṇāṁ tīvrāṁ sahasrāṁśoś chādayad gagane prabhām ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
diśaḥ paryākulāś cāsan rajasā tena mūrchitāḥ |
cacāla ca mahī sarvā saśailavanakānanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tato nagendrasaṁkāśais tīkṣṇa daṁṣṭrair mahābalaiḥ |
kr̥tsnā saṁchāditā bhūmir asaṁkhyeyaiḥ plavaṁgamaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
nimeṣāntaramātreṇa tatas tair hariyūthapaiḥ |
koṭīśataparīvāraiḥ kāmarūpibhir āvr̥tā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
nādeyaiḥ pārvatīyaiś ca sāmudraiś ca mahābalaiḥ |
haribhir meghanirhrādair anyaiś ca vanacāribhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
taruṇādityavarṇaiś ca śaśigauraiś ca vānaraiḥ |
padmakesaravarṇaiś ca śvetair merukr̥tālayaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
koṭīsahasrair daśabhiḥ śrīmān parivr̥tas tadā |
vīraḥ śatabalir nāma vānaraḥ pratyadr̥śyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tataḥ kāñcanaśailābhas tārāyā vīryavān pitā |
anekair daśasāhasraiḥ koṭibhiḥ pratyadr̥śyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
padmakesarasaṁkāśas taruṇārkanibhānanaḥ |
buddhimān vānaraśreṣṭhaḥ sarvavānarasattamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
anīkair bahusāhasrair vānarāṇāṁ samanvitaḥ |
pitā hanumataḥ śrīmān kesarī pratyadr̥śyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
golāṅgūlamahārājo gavākṣo bhīmavikramaḥ |
vr̥taḥ koṭisahasreṇa vānarāṇām adr̥śyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
r̥kṣāṇāṁ bhīmavegānāṁ dhūmraḥ śatrunibarhaṇaḥ |
vr̥taḥ koṭisahasrābhyāṁ dvābhyāṁ samabhivartata ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
mahācalanibhair ghoraiḥ panaso nāma yūthapaḥ |
ājagāma mahāvīryas tisr̥bhiḥ koṭibhir vr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
nīlāñjanacayākāro nīlo nāmātha yūthapaḥ |
adr̥śyata mahākāyaḥ koṭibhir daśabhir vr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
darīmukhaś ca balavān yūthapo ’bhyāyayau tadā |
vr̥taḥ koṭisahasreṇa sugrīvaṁ samupasthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
maindaś ca dvividaś cobhāv aśviputrau mahāvalau |
koṭikoṭisahasreṇa vānarāṇām adr̥śyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tataḥ koṭisahasrāṇāṁ sahasreṇa śatena ca |
pr̥ṣṭhato ’nugataḥ prāpto haribhir gandhamādanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tataḥ padmasahasreṇa vr̥taḥ śaṅkuśatena ca |
yuvarājo ’ṅgadaḥ prāptaḥ pitr̥tulyaparākramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tatas tārādyutis tāro harir bhīmaparākramaḥ |
pañcabhir harikoṭībhir dūrataḥ pratyadr̥śyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
indrajānuḥ kapir vīro yūthapaḥ pratyadr̥śyata |
ekādaśānāṁ koṭīnām īśvaras taiś ca saṁvr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tato rambhas tv anuprāptas taruṇādityasaṁnibhaḥ |
ayutena vr̥taś caiva sahasreṇa śatena ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
tato yūthapatir vīro durmukho nāma vānaraḥ |
pratyadr̥śyata koṭibhyāṁ dvābhyāṁ parivr̥to balī ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
kailāsaśikharākārair vānarair bhīmavikramaiḥ |
vr̥taḥ koṭisahasreṇa hanumān pratyadr̥śyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
nalaś cāpi mahāvīryaḥ saṁvr̥to drumavāsibhiḥ |
koṭīśatena saṁprāptaḥ sahasreṇa śatena ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
śarabhaḥ kumudo vahnir vānaro rambha eva ca |
ete cānye ca bahavo vānarāḥ kāmarūpiṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
āvr̥tya pr̥thivīṁ sarvāṁ parvatāṁś ca vanāni ca |
āplavantaḥ plavantaś ca garjantaś ca plavaṁgamāḥ |
abhyavartanta sugrīvaṁ sūryam abhragaṇā iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
kurvāṇā bahuśabdāṁś ca prahr̥ṣṭā balaśālinaḥ |
śirobhir vānarendrāya sugrīvāya nyavedayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
apare vānaraśreṣṭhāḥ saṁgamya ca yathocitam |
sugrīveṇa samāgamya sthitāḥ prāñjalayas tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
sugrīvas tvarito rāme sarvāṁs tān vānararṣabhān |
nivedayitvā dharmajñaḥ sthitaḥ prāñjalir abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
yathā sukhaṁ parvatanirjhareṣu; vaneṣu sarveṣu ca vānarendrāḥ |
niveśayitvā vidhivad balāni; balaṁ balajñaḥ pratipattum īṣṭe ||

=== meaning ===

---
