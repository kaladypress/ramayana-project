=== Sarga 19 ===

---
1.
sa vānaramahārājaḥ śayānaḥ śaravikṣataḥ |
pratyukto hetumadvākyair nottaraṁ pratyapadyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
aśmabhiḥ paribhinnāṅgaḥ pādapair āhato bhr̥śam |
rāmabāṇena cākrānto jīvitānte mumoha saḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
taṁ bhāryābāṇamokṣeṇa rāmadattena saṁyuge |
hataṁ plavagaśārdūlaṁ tārā śuśrāva vālinam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
sā saputrāpriyaṁ śrutvā vadhaṁ bhartuḥ sudāruṇam |
niṣpapāta bhr̥śaṁ trastā vividhād girigahvarāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
ye tv aṅgadaparīvārā vānarā hi mahābalāḥ |
te sakārmukam ālokya rāmaṁ trastāḥ pradudruvuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
sā dadarśa tatas trastān harīn āpatato drutam |
yūthād iva paribhraṣṭān mr̥gān nihatayūthapān ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tān uvāca samāsādya duḥkhitān duḥkhitā satī |
rāma vitrāsitān sarvān anubaddhān iveṣubhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
vānarā rājasiṁhasya yasya yūyaṁ puraḥsarāḥ |
taṁ vihāya suvitrastāḥ kasmād dravata durgatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
rājyahetoḥ sa ced bhrātā bhrātā raudreṇa pātitaḥ |
rāmeṇa prasr̥tair dūrān mārgaṇair dūra pātibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
kapipatnyā vacaḥ śrutvā kapayaḥ kāmarūpiṇaḥ |
prāptakālam aviśliṣṭam ūcur vacanam aṅganām ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
jīva putre nivartasya putraṁ rakṣasva cāndagam |
antako rāma rūpeṇa hatvā nayati vālinam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
kṣiptān vr̥kṣān samāvidhya vipulāś ca śilās tathā |
vālī vajrasamair bāṇair vajreṇeva nipātitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
abhidrutam idaṁ sarvaṁ vidrutaṁ prasr̥taṁ balam |
asmin plavagaśārdūle hate śakrasamaprabhe ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
rakṣyatāṁ nagaraṁ śūrair aṅgadaś cābhiṣicyatām |
padasthaṁ vālinaḥ putraṁ bhajiṣyanti plavaṁgamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
atha vā ruciraṁ sthānam iha te rucirānane |
āviśanti hi durgāṇi kṣipram adyaiva vānarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
abhāryāḥ saha bhāryāś ca santy atra vanacāriṇaḥ |
lubdhebhyo viprayuktebhyaḥ svebhyo nas tumulaṁ bhayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
alpāntaragatānāṁ tu śrutvā vacanam aṅganā |
ātmanaḥ pratirūpaṁ sā babhāṣe cāruhāsinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
putreṇa mama kiṁ kāryaṁ kiṁ rājyena kim ātmanā |
kapisiṁhe mahābhāge tasmin bhartari naśyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
pādamūlaṁ gamiṣyāmi tasyaivāhaṁ mahātmanaḥ |
yo ’sau rāmaprayuktena śareṇa vinipātitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
evam uktvā pradudrāva rudatī śokakarśitā |
śiraś coraś ca bāhubhyāṁ duḥkhena samabhighnatī ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
āvrajantī dadarśātha patiṁ nipatitaṁ bhuvi |
hantāraṁ dānavendrāṇāṁ samareṣv anivartinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
kṣeptāraṁ parvatendrāṇāṁ vajrāṇām iva vāsavam |
mahāvātasamāviṣṭaṁ mahāmeghaughaniḥsvanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
śakratulyaparākrāntaṁ vr̥ṣṭvevoparataṁ ghanam |
nardantaṁ nardatāṁ bhīmaṁ śūraṁ śūreṇa pātitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
śārdūlenāmiṣasyārthe mr̥garājaṁ yathā hatam |
arcitaṁ sarvalokasya sapatākaṁ savedikam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
nāgahetoḥ suparṇena caityam unmathitaṁ yathā |
avaṣṭabhyāvatiṣṭhantaṁ dadarśa dhanur ūrjitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
rāmaṁ rāmānujaṁ caiva bhartuś caivānujaṁ śubhā |
tān atītya samāsādya bhartāraṁ nihataṁ raṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
samīkṣya vyathitā bhūmau saṁbhrāntā nipapāta ha |
supteva punar utthāya āryaputreti krośatī ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
ruroda sā patiṁ dr̥ṣṭvā saṁditaṁ mr̥tyudāmabhiḥ |
tām avekṣya tu sugrīvaḥ krośantīṁ kurarīm iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
viṣādam agamat kaṣṭaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā cāṅgadam āgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
