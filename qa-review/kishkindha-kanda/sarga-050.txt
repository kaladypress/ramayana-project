=== Sarga 50 ===

---
1.
ity uktvā hanumāṁs tatra punaḥ kr̥ṣṇājināmbarām |
abravīt tāṁ mahābhāgāṁ tāpasīṁ dharmacāriṇīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
idaṁ praviṣṭāḥ sahasā bilaṁ timirasaṁvr̥tam |
kṣutpipāsā pariśrāntāḥ parikhinnāś ca sarvaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
mahad dhiraṇyā vivaraṁ praviṣṭāḥ sma pipāsitāḥ |
imāṁs tv evaṁ vidhān bhāvān vividhān adbhutopamān |
dr̥ṣṭvā vayaṁ pravyathitāḥ saṁbhrāntā naṣṭacetasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
kasyeme kāñcanā vr̥kṣās taruṇādityasaṁnibhāḥ |
śucīny abhyavahāryāṇi mūlāni ca phalāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
kāñcanāni vimānāni rājatāni gr̥hāṇi ca |
tapanīya gavākṣāṇi maṇijālāvr̥tāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
puṣpitāḥ phālavantaś ca puṇyāḥ surabhigandhinaḥ |
ime jāmbūnadamayāḥ pādapāḥ kasya tejasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
kāñcanāni ca padmāni jātāni vimale jale |
kathaṁ matsyāś ca sauvarṇā caranti saha kacchapaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
ātmānam anubhāvaṁ ca kasya caitat tapobalam |
ajānatāṁ naḥ sarveṣāṁ sarvam ākhyātum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
evam uktā hanumatā tāpasī dharmacāriṇī |
pratyuvāca hanūmantaṁ sarvabhūtahite ratā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
mayo nāma mahātejā māyāvī dānavarṣabhaḥ |
tenedaṁ nirmitaṁ sarvaṁ māyayā kāñcanaṁ vanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
purā dānavamukhyānāṁ viśvakarmā babhūva ha |
yenedaṁ kāñcanaṁ divyaṁ nirmitaṁ bhavanottamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sa tu varṣasahasrāṇi tapas taptvā mahāvane |
pitāmahād varaṁ lebhe sarvam auśasanaṁ dhanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
vidhāya sarvaṁ balavān sarvakāmeśvaras tadā |
uvāsa sukhitaḥ kālaṁ kaṁ cid asmin mahāvane ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tam apsarasi hemāyāṁ saktaṁ dānavapuṁgavam |
vikramyaivāśaniṁ gr̥hya jaghāneśaḥ puraṁdaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
idaṁ ca brahmaṇā dattaṁ hemāyai vanam uttamam |
śāśvataḥ kāmabhogaś ca gr̥haṁ cedaṁ hiraṇmayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
duhitā merusāvarṇer ahaṁ tasyāḥ svayaṁ prabhā |
idaṁ rakṣāmi bhavanaṁ hemāyā vānarottama ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
mama priyasakhī hemā nr̥ttagītaviśāradā |
tayā dattavarā cāsmi rakṣāmi bhavanottamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
kiṁ kāryaṁ kasya vā hetoḥ kāntārāṇi prapadyatha |
kathaṁ cedaṁ vanaṁ durgaṁ yuṣmābhir upalakṣitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
imāny abhyavahāryāṇi mūlāni ca phalāni ca |
bhuktvā pītvā ca pānīyaṁ sarvaṁ me vaktum arhatha ||

=== meaning ===

---
