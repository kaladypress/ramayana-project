=== Sarga 10 ===

---
1.
tataḥ krodhasamāviṣṭaṁ saṁrabdhaṁ tam upāgatam |
ahaṁ prasādayāṁ cakre bhrātaraṁ priyakāmyayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
diṣṭyāsi kuśalī prāpto nihataś ca tvayā ripuḥ |
anāthasya hi me nāthas tvam eko ’nāthanandanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
idaṁ bahuśalākaṁ te pūrṇacandram ivoditam |
chatraṁ savālavyajanaṁ pratīcchasva mayodyatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tvam eva rājā mānārhaḥ sadā cāhaṁ yathāpurā |
nyāsabhūtam idaṁ rājyaṁ tava niryātayāmy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
mā ca roṣaṁ kr̥thāḥ saumya mayi śatrunibarhaṇa |
yāce tvāṁ śirasā rājan mayā baddho ’yam añjaliḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
balād asmi samāgamya mantribhiḥ puravāsibhiḥ |
rājabhāve niyukto ’haṁ śūnyadeśajigīṣayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
snigdham evaṁ bruvāṇaṁ māṁ sa tu nirbhartsya vānaraḥ |
dhik tvām iti ca mām uktvā bahu tat tad uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
prakr̥tīś ca samānīya mantriṇaś caiva saṁmatān |
mām āha suhr̥dāṁ madhye vākyaṁ paramagarhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
viditaṁ vo yathā rātrau māyāvī sa mahāsuraḥ |
māṁ samāhvayata krūro yuddhākāṅkṣī sudurmatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tasya tad garjitaṁ śrutvā niḥsr̥to ’haṁ nr̥pālayāt |
anuyātaś ca māṁ tūrṇam ayaṁ bhrātā sudāruṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sa tu dr̥ṣṭvaiva māṁ rātrau sadvitīyaṁ mahābalaḥ |
prādravad bhayasaṁtrasto vīkṣyāvāṁ tam anudrutau |
anudrutas tu vegena praviveśa mahābilam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
taṁ praviṣṭaṁ viditvā tu sughoraṁ sumahad bilam |
ayam ukto ’tha me bhrātā mayā tu krūradarśanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
ahatvā nāsti me śaktiḥ pratigantum itaḥ purīm |
biladvāri pratīkṣa tvaṁ yāvad enaṁ nihanmy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sthito ’yam iti matvā tu praviṣṭo ’haṁ durāsadam |
taṁ ca me mārgamāṇasya gataḥ saṁvatsaras tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa tu dr̥ṣṭo mayā śatrur anirvedād bhayāvahaḥ |
nihataś ca mayā tatra so ’suro bandhubhiḥ saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tasyāsyāt tu pravr̥ttena rudhiraugheṇa tad bilam |
pūrṇam āsīd durākrāmaṁ stanatas tasya bhūtale ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
sūdayitvā tu taṁ śatruṁ vikrāntaṁ dundubheḥ sutam |
niṣkrāmann eva paśyāmi bilasya pihitaṁ mukham ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
vikrośamānasya tu me sugrīveti punaḥ punaḥ |
yadā prativaco nāsti tato ’haṁ bhr̥śaduḥkhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
pādaprahārais tu mayā bahuśas tad vidāritam |
tato ’haṁ tena niṣkramya yathā punar upāgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tatrānenāsmi saṁruddho rājyaṁ mārgayatātmanaḥ |
sugrīveṇa nr̥śaṁsena vismr̥tya bhrātr̥sauhr̥dam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
evam uktvā tu māṁ tatra vastreṇaikena vānaraḥ |
tadā nirvāsayām āsa vālī vigatasādhvasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tenāham apaviddhaś ca hr̥tadāraś ca rāghava |
tadbhayāc ca mahīkr̥tsnā krānteyaṁ savanārṇavā ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
r̥śyamūkaṁ girivaraṁ bhāryāharaṇaduḥkhitaḥ |
praviṣṭo ’smi durādharṣaṁ vālinaḥ kāraṇāntare ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
etat te sarvam ākhyātaṁ vairānukathanaṁ mahat |
anāgasā mayā prāptaṁ vyasanaṁ paśya rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
vālinas tu bhayārtasya sarvalokābhayaṁkara |
kartum arhasi me vīra prasādaṁ tasya nigrahāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
evam uktaḥ sa tejasvī dharmajño dharmasaṁhitam |
vacanaṁ vaktum ārebhe sugrīvaṁ prahasann iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
amoghāḥ sūryasaṁkāśā mameme niśitāḥ śarāḥ |
tasmin vālini durvr̥tte patiṣyanti ruṣānvitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
yāvat taṁ na hi paśyeyaṁ tava bhāryāpahāriṇam |
tāvat sa jīvet pāpātmā vālī cāritradūṣakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
ātmānumānāt paśyāmi magnaṁ tvāṁ śokasāgare |
tvām ahaṁ tārayiṣyāmi kāmaṁ prāpsyasi puṣkalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
