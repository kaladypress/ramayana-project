=== Sarga 2 ===

---
1.
tau tu dr̥ṣṭvā mahātmānau bhrātarau rāmalakṣmaṇau |
varāyudhadharau vīrau sugrīvaḥ śaṅkito ’bhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
udvignahr̥dayaḥ sarvā diśaḥ samavalokayan |
na vyatiṣṭhata kasmiṁś cid deśe vānarapuṁgavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
naiva cakre manaḥ sthāne vīkṣamāṇo mahābalau |
kapeḥ paramabhītasya cittaṁ vyavasasāda ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
cintayitvā sa dharmātmā vimr̥śya gurulāghavam |
sugrīvaḥ paramodvignaḥ sarvair anucaraiḥ saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tataḥ sa sacivebhyas tu sugrīvaḥ plavagādhipaḥ |
śaśaṁsa paramodvignaḥ paśyaṁs tau rāmalakṣmaṇau ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
etau vanam idaṁ durgaṁ vālipraṇihitau dhruvam |
chadmanā cīravasanau pracarantāv ihāgatau ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tataḥ sugrīvasacivā dr̥ṣṭvā paramadhanvinau |
jagmur giritaṭāt tasmād anyac chikharam uttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
te kṣipram abhigamyātha yūthapā yūthaparṣabham |
harayo vānaraśreṣṭhaṁ parivāryopatasthire ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
ekam ekāyanagatāḥ plavamānā girer girim |
prakampayanto vegena girīṇāṁ śikharāṇi ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tataḥ śākhāmr̥gāḥ sarve plavamānā mahābalāḥ |
babhañjuś ca nagāṁs tatra puṣpitān durgasaṁśritān ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
āplavanto harivarāḥ sarvatas taṁ mahāgirim |
mr̥gamārjāraśārdūlāṁs trāsayanto yayus tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tataḥ sugrīvasacivāḥ parvatendraṁ samāśritāḥ |
saṁgamya kapimukhyena sarve prāñjalayaḥ sthitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tatas taṁ bhayasaṁtrastaṁ vālikilbiṣaśaṅkitam |
uvāca hanumān vākyaṁ sugrīvaṁ vākyakovidaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
yasmād udvignacetās tvaṁ pradruto haripuṁgava |
taṁ krūradarśanaṁ krūraṁ neha paśyāmi vālinam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
yasmāt tava bhayaṁ saumya pūrvajāt pāpakarmaṇaḥ |
sa neha vālī duṣṭātmā na te paśyāmy ahaṁ bhayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
aho śākhāmr̥gatvaṁ te vyaktam eva plavaṁgama |
laghucittatayātmānaṁ na sthāpayasi yo matau ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
buddhivijñānasaṁpanna iṅgitaiḥ sarvam ācara |
na hy abuddhiṁ gato rājā sarvabhūtāni śāsti hi ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
sugrīvas tu śubhaṁ vākyaṁ śrutvā sarvaṁ hanūmataḥ |
tataḥ śubhataraṁ vākyaṁ hanūmantam uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
dīrghabāhū viśālākṣau śaracāpāsidhāriṇau |
kasya na syād bhayaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā etau surasutopamau ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
vālipraṇihitāv etau śaṅke ’haṁ puruṣottamau |
rājāno bahumitrāś ca viśvāso nātra hi kṣamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
arayaś ca manuṣyeṇa vijñeyāś channacāriṇaḥ |
viśvastānām aviśvastāś chidreṣu praharanti hi ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
kr̥tyeṣu vālī medhāvī rājāno bahudarśanāḥ |
bhavanti parahantāras te jñeyāḥ prākr̥tair naraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tau tvayā prākr̥tenaiva gatvā jñeyau plavaṁgama |
śaṅkitānāṁ prakāraiś ca rūpavyābhāṣaṇena ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
lakṣayasva tayor bhāvaṁ prahr̥ṣṭamanasau yadi |
viśvāsayan praśaṁsābhir iṅgitaiś ca punaḥ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
mamaivābhimukhaṁ sthitvā pr̥ccha tvaṁ haripuṁgava |
prayojanaṁ praveśasya vanasyāsya dhanurdharau ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
śuddhātmānau yadi tv etau jānīhi tvaṁ plavaṁgama |
vyābhāṣitair vā rūpair vā vijñeyā duṣṭatānayoḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
ity evaṁ kapirājena saṁdiṣṭo mārutātmajaḥ |
cakāra gamane buddhiṁ yatra tau rāmalakṣmaṇau ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tatheti saṁpūjya vacas tu tasya; kapeḥ subhītasya durāsadasya |
mahānubhāvo hanumān yayau tadā; sa yatra rāmo ’tibalaś ca lakṣmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
