=== Sarga 41 ===

---
1.
tataḥ prasthāpya sugrīvas tān harīn dakṣiṇāṁ diśam |
buddhivikramasaṁpannān vāyuvegasamāñjave ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
athāhūya mahātejāḥ suṣeṇaṁ nāma yūthapam |
tārāyāḥ pitaraṁ rājā śvaśurabhīmavikramam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
abravīt prāñjalir vākyam abhigamya praṇamya ca |
sāhāyyaṁ kuru rāmasya kr̥tye ’smin samupasthite ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
vr̥taḥ śatasahasreṇa vānarāṇāṁ tarasvinām |
abhigaccha diśaṁ saumya paścimāṁ vāruṇīṁ prabho ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
surāṣṭrān saha bāhlīkāñ śūrābhīrāṁs tathaiva ca |
sphītāñjanapadān ramyān vipulāni purāṇi ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
puṁnāgagahanaṁ kukṣiṁ bahuloddālakākulam |
tathā ketakaṣaṇḍāṁś ca mārgadhvaṁ hariyūthapāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
pratyak srotogamāś caiva nadyaḥ śītajalāḥ śivāḥ |
tāpasānām araṇyāni kāntārā girayaś ca ye ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
girijālāvr̥tāṁ durgāṁ mārgitvā paścimāṁ diśam |
tataḥ paścimam āsādya samudraṁ draṣṭum arhatha |
timi nakrāyuta jalam akṣobhyam atha vānaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tataḥ ketakaṣaṇḍeṣu tamālagahaneṣu ca |
kapayo vihariṣyanti nārikelavaneṣu ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tatra sītāṁ ca mārgadhvaṁ nilayaṁ rāvaṇasya ca |
marīcipattanaṁ caiva ramyaṁ caiva jaṭīpuram ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
avantīm aṅgalopāṁ ca tathā cālakṣitaṁ vanam |
rāṣṭrāṇi ca viśālāni pattanāni tatas tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sindhusāgarayoś caiva saṁgame tatra parvataḥ |
mahān hemagirir nāma śataśr̥ṅgo mahādrumaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tasya prastheṣu ramyeṣu siṁhāḥ pakṣagamāḥ sthitāḥ |
timimatsyagajāṁś caiva nīḍāny āropayanti te ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tāni nīḍāni siṁhānāṁ giriśr̥ṅgagatāś ca ye |
dr̥ptās tr̥ptāś ca mātaṅgās toyadasvananiḥsvanāḥ |
vicaranti viśāle ’smiṁs toyapūrṇe samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tasya śr̥ṅgaṁ divasparśaṁ kāñcanaṁ citrapādapam |
sarvam āśu vicetavyaṁ kapibhiḥ kāmarūpibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
koṭiṁ tatra samudre tu kāñcanīṁ śatayojanam |
durdarśāṁ pariyātrasya gatā drakṣyatha vānarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
koṭyas tatra caturviṁśad gandharvāṇāṁ tarasvinām |
vasanty agninikāśānāṁ ghorāṇāṁ kāmarūpiṇām ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
nātyāsādayitavyās te vānarair bhīmavikramaiḥ |
nādeyaṁ ca phalaṁ tasmād deśāt kiṁ cit plavaṁgamaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
durāsadā hi te vīrāḥ sattvavanto mahābalāḥ |
phalamūlāni te tatra rakṣante bhīmavikramāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tatra yatnaś ca kartavyo mārgitavyā ca jānakī |
na hi tebhyo bhayaṁ kiṁ cit kapitvam anuvartatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
caturbhāge samudrasya cakravān nāma parvataḥ |
tatra cakraṁ sahasrāraṁ nirmitaṁ viśvakarmaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tatra pañcajanaṁ hatvā hayagrīvaṁ ca dānavam |
ājahāra tataś cakraṁ śaṅkhaṁ ca puruṣottamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tasya sānuṣu citreṣu viśālāsu guhāsu ca |
rāvaṇaḥ saha vaidehyā mārgitavyas tatas tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
yojanāni catuḥṣaṣṭir varāho nāma parvataḥ |
suvarṇaśr̥ṅgaḥ suśrīmān agādhe varuṇālaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tatra prāgjyotiṣaṁ nāma jātarūpamayaṁ puram |
yasmin vasti duṣṭātmā narako nāma guhāsu ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tasya sānuṣu citreṣu viśālāsu guhāsu ca |
rāvaṇaḥ saha vaidehyā mārgitavyas tatas tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tam atikramya śailendraṁ kāñcanāntaranirdaraḥ |
parvataḥ sarvasauvarṇo dhārā prasravaṇāyutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
taṁ gajāś ca varāhāś ca siṁhā vyāghrāś ca sarvataḥ |
abhigarjanti satataṁ tena śabdena darpitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
tasmin harihayaḥ śrīmān mahendraḥ pākaśāsanaḥ |
abhiṣiktaḥ surai rājā meghavān nāma parvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
tam atikramya śailendraṁ mahendraparipālitam |
ṣaṣṭiṁ girisahasrāṇi kāñcanāni gamiṣyatha ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
taruṇādityavarṇāni bhrājamānāni sarvataḥ |
jātarūpamayair vr̥kṣaiḥ śobhitāni supuṣpitaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
teṣāṁ madhye sthito rājā merur uttamaparvataḥ |
ādityena prasannena śailo dattavaraḥ purā ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
tenaivam uktaḥ śailendraḥ sarva eva tvadāśrayāḥ |
matprasādād bhaviṣyanti divārātrau ca kāñcanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
tvayi ye cāpi vatsyanti devagandharvadānavāḥ |
te bhaviṣyanti raktāś ca prabhayā kāñcanaprabhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
ādityā vasavo rudrā marutaś ca divaukasaḥ |
āgamya paścimāṁ saṁdhyāṁ merum uttamaparvatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
ādityam upatiṣṭhanti taiś ca sūryo ’bhipūjitaḥ |
adr̥śyaḥ sarvabhūtānām astaṁ gacchati parvatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
yojanānāṁ sahasrāṇi daśatāni divākaraḥ |
muhūrtārdhena taṁ śīghram abhiyāti śiloccayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
śr̥ṅge tasya mahad divyaṁ bhavanaṁ sūryasaṁnibham |
prāsādaguṇasaṁbādhaṁ vihitaṁ viśvakarmaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
śobhitaṁ tarubhiś citrair nānāpakṣisamākulaiḥ |
niketaṁ pāśahastasya varuṇasya mahātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
antarā merum astaṁ ca tālo daśaśirā mahān |
jātarūpamayaḥ śrīmān bhrājate citravedikaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
teṣu sarveṣu durgeṣu saraḥsu ca saritsu ca |
rāvaṇaḥ saha vaidehyā mārgitavyas tatas tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
yatra tiṣṭhati dharmātmā tapasā svena bhāvitaḥ |
merusāvarṇir ity eva khyāto vai brahmaṇā samaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
praṣṭavyo merusāvarṇir maharṣiḥ sūryasaṁnibhaḥ |
praṇamya śirasā bhūmau pravr̥ttiṁ maithilīṁ prati ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
etāvaj jīvalokasya bhāskaro rajanīkṣaye |
kr̥tvā vitimiraṁ sarvam astaṁ gacchati parvatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
etāvad vānaraiḥ śakyaṁ gantuṁ vānarapuṁgavāḥ |
abhāskaram amaryādaṁ na jānīmas tataḥ param ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
adhigamya tu vaidehīṁ nilayaṁ rāvaṇasya ca |
astaṁ parvatam āsādya pūrṇe māse nivartata ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
ūrdhvaṁ māsān na vastavyaṁ vasan vadhyo bhaven mama |
sahaiva śūro yuṣmābhiḥ śvaśuro me gamiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
śrotavyaṁ sarvam etasya bhavadbhir diṣṭa kāribhiḥ |
gurur eṣa mahābāhuḥ śvaśuro me mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
bhavantaś cāpi vikrāntāḥ pramāṇaṁ sarvakarmasu |
pramāṇam enaṁ saṁsthāpya paśyadhvaṁ paścimāṁ diśam ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
dr̥ṣṭāyāṁ tu narendrasyā patnyām amitatejasaḥ |
kr̥takr̥tyā bhaviṣyāmaḥ kr̥tasya pratikarmaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
ato ’nyad api yat kiṁ cit kāryasyāsya hitaṁ bhavet |
saṁpradhārya bhavadbhiś ca deśakālārthasaṁhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
52.
tataḥ suṣeṇa pramukhāḥ plavaṁgamāḥ; sugrīvavākyaṁ nipuṇaṁ niśamya |
āmantrya sarve plavagādhipaṁ te; jagmur diśaṁ tāṁ varuṇābhiguptām ||

=== meaning ===

---
