=== Sarga 11 ===

---
1.
rāmasya vacanaṁ śrutvā harṣapauruṣavardhanam |
sugrīvaḥ pūjayāṁ cakre rāghavaṁ praśaśaṁsa ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
asaṁśayaṁ prajvalitais tīkṣṇair marmātigaiḥ śaraiḥ |
tvaṁ daheḥ kupito lokān yugānta iva bhāskaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
vālinaḥ pauruṣaṁ yat tad yac ca vīryaṁ dhr̥tiś ca yā |
tan mamaikamanāḥ śrutvā vidhatsva yadanantaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
samudrāt paścimāt pūrvaṁ dakṣiṇād api cottaram |
krāmaty anudite sūrye vālī vyapagataklamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
agrāṇy āruhya śailānāṁ śikharāṇi mahānty api |
ūrdhvam utkṣipya tarasā pratigr̥hṇāti vīryavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
bahavaḥ sāravantaś ca vaneṣu vividhā drumāḥ |
vālinā tarasā bhagnā balaṁ prathayatātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
mahiṣo dundubhir nāma kailāsaśikharaprabhaḥ |
balaṁ nāgasahasrasya dhārayām āsa vīryavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
vīryotsekena duṣṭātmā varadānāc ca mohitaḥ |
jagāma sa mahākāyaḥ samudraṁ saritāṁ patim ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
ūrmimantam atikramya sāgaraṁ ratnasaṁcayam |
mama yuddhaṁ prayaccheti tam uvāca mahārṇavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tataḥ samudro dharmātmā samutthāya mahābalaḥ |
abravīd vacanaṁ rājann asuraṁ kālacoditam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
samartho nāsmi te dātuṁ yuddhaṁ yuddhaviśārada |
śrūyatām abhidhāsyāmi yas te yuddhaṁ pradāsyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
śailarājo mahāraṇye tapasviśaraṇaṁ param |
śaṁkaraśvaśuro nāmnā himavān iti viśrutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
guhā prasravaṇopeto bahukandaranirjharaḥ |
sa samarthas tava prītim atulāṁ kartum āhave ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
taṁ bhītam iti vijñāya samudram asurottamaḥ |
himavadvanam āgacchac charaś cāpād iva cyutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tatas tasya gireḥ śvetā gajendravipulāḥ śilāḥ |
cikṣepa bahudhā bhūmau dundubhir vinanāda ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tataḥ śvetāmbudākāraḥ saumyaḥ prītikarākr̥tiḥ |
himavān abravīd vākyaṁ sva eva śikhare sthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
kleṣṭum arhasi māṁ na tvaṁ dundubhe dharmavatsala |
raṇakarmasv akuśalas tapasviśaraṇaṁ hy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā girirājasya dhīmataḥ |
uvāca dundubhir vākyaṁ krodhāt saṁraktalocanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
yadi yuddhe ’samarthas tvaṁ madbhayād vā nirudyamaḥ |
tam ācakṣva pradadyān me yo ’dya yuddhaṁ yuyutsataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
himavān abravīd vākyaṁ śrutvā vākyaviśāradaḥ |
anuktapūrvaṁ dharmātmā krodhāt tam asurottamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
vālī nāma mahāprājñaḥ śakratulyaparākramaḥ |
adhyāste vānaraḥ śrīmān kiṣkindhām atulaprabhām ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
sa samartho mahāprājñas tava yuddhaviśāradaḥ |
dvandvayuddhaṁ mahad dātuṁ namucer iva vāsavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
taṁ śīghram abhigaccha tvaṁ yadi yuddham ihecchasi |
sa hi durdharṣaṇo nityaṁ śūraḥ samarakarmaṇi ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
śrutvā himavato vākyaṁ krodhāviṣṭaḥ sa dundubhiḥ |
jagāma tāṁ purīṁ tasya kiṣkindhāṁ vālinas tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
dhārayan māhiṣaṁ rūpaṁ tīkṣṇaśr̥ṅgo bhayāvahaḥ |
prāvr̥ṣīva mahāmeghas toyapūrṇo nabhastale ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tatas tu dvāram āgamya kiṣkindhāyā mahābalaḥ |
nanarda kampayan bhūmiṁ dundubhir dundubhir yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
samīpajān drumān bhañjan vasudhāṁ dārayan khuraiḥ |
viṣāṇenollekhan darpāt taddvāraṁ dvirado yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
antaḥpuragato vālī śrutvā śabdam amarṣaṇaḥ |
niṣpapāta saha strībhis tārābhir iva candramāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
mitaṁ vyaktākṣarapadaṁ tam uvāca sa dundubhim |
harīṇām īśvaro vālī sarveṣāṁ vanacāriṇām ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
kimarthaṁ nagaradvāram idaṁ ruddhvā vinardasi |
dundubhe vidito me ’si rakṣa prāṇān mahābala ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā vānarendrasya dhīmataḥ |
uvāca dundubhir vākyaṁ krodhāt saṁraktalocanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
na tvaṁ strīsaṁnidhau vīra vacanaṁ vaktum arhasi |
mama yuddhaṁ prayaccha tvaṁ tato jñāsyāmi te balam ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
atha vā dhārayiṣyāmi krodham adya niśām imām |
gr̥hyatām udayaḥ svairaṁ kāmabhogeṣu vānara ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
yo hi mattaṁ pramattaṁ vā suptaṁ vā rahitaṁ bhr̥śam |
hanyāt sa bhrūṇahā loke tvadvidhaṁ madamohitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
sa prahasyābravīn mandaṁ krodhāt tam asurottamam |
visr̥jya tāḥ striyaḥ sarvās tārāprabhr̥tikās tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
matto ’yam iti mā maṁsthā yady abhīto ’si saṁyuge |
mado ’yaṁ saṁprahāre ’smin vīrapānaṁ samarthyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
tam evam uktvā saṁkruddho mālām utkṣipya kāñcanīm |
pitrā dattāṁ mahendreṇa yuddhāya vyavatiṣṭhata ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
viṣāṇayor gr̥hītvā taṁ dundubhiṁ girisaṁnibham |
vālī vyāpātayāṁ cakre nanarda ca mahāsvanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
yuddhe prāṇahare tasmin niṣpiṣṭo dundubhis tadā |
śrotrābhyām atha raktaṁ tu tasya susrāva pātyataḥ |
papāta ca mahākāyaḥ kṣitau pañcatvam āgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
taṁ tolayitvā bāhubhyāṁ gatasattvam acetanam |
cikṣepa vegavān vālī vegenaikena yojanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
tasya vegapraviddhasya vaktrāt kṣatajabindavaḥ |
prapetur mārutotkṣiptā mataṅgasyāśramaṁ prati ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
tān dr̥ṣṭvā patitāṁs tatra muniḥ śoṇitavipruṣaḥ |
utsasarja mahāśāpaṁ kṣeptāraṁ vālinaṁ prati |
iha tenāpraveṣṭavyaṁ praviṣṭasya badho bhavet ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
sa maharṣiṁ samāsādya yācate sma kr̥tāñjaliḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
tataḥ śāpabhayād bhīta r̥śyamūkaṁ mahāgirim |
praveṣṭuṁ necchati harir draṣṭuṁ vāpi nareśvara ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
tasyāpraveśaṁ jñātvāham idaṁ rāma mahāvanam |
vicarāmi sahāmātyo viṣādena vivarjitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
eṣo ’sthinicayas tasya dundubheḥ saṁprakāśate |
vīryotsekān nirastasya girikūṭanibho mahān ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
ime ca vipulāḥ sālāḥ sapta śākhāvalambinaḥ |
yatraikaṁ ghaṭate vālī niṣpatrayitum ojasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
etad asyāsamaṁ vīryaṁ mayā rāma prakāśitam |
kathaṁ taṁ vālinaṁ hantuṁ samare śakṣyase nr̥pa ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
yadi bhindyād bhavān sālān imāṁs tv ekeṣuṇā tataḥ |
jānīyāṁ tvāṁ mahābāho samarthaṁ vālino vadhe ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā sugrīvasya mahātmanaḥ |
rāghavo dundubheḥ kāyaṁ pādāṅguṣṭhena līlayā |
tolayitvā mahābāhuś cikṣepa daśayojanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
kṣiptaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā tataḥ kāyaṁ sugrīvaḥ punar abravīt |
lakṣmaṇasyāgrato rāmam idaṁ vacanam arthavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
52.
ārdraḥ samāṁsapratyagraḥ kṣiptaḥ kāyaḥ purā sakhe |
laghuḥ saṁprati nirmāṁsas tr̥ṇabhūtaś ca rāghava |
nātra śakyaṁ balaṁ jñātuṁ tava vā tasya vādhikam ||

=== meaning ===

---
