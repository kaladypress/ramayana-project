=== Sarga 6 ===

---
1.
ayam ākhyāti me rāma sacivo mantrisattamaḥ |
hanumān yannimittaṁ tvaṁ nirjanaṁ vanam āgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
lakṣmaṇena saha bhrātrā vasataś ca vane tava |
rakṣasāpahr̥tā bhāryā maithilī janakātmajā ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tvayā viyuktā rudatī lakṣmaṇena ca dhīmatā |
antaraṁ prepsunā tena hatvā gr̥dhraṁ jaṭāyuṣam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
bhāryāviyogajaṁ duḥkhaṁ nacirāt tvaṁ vimokṣyase |
ahaṁ tām ānayiṣyāmi naṣṭāṁ vedaśrutiṁ yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
rasātale vā vartantīṁ vartantīṁ vā nabhastale |
aham ānīya dāsyāmi tava bhāryām ariṁdama ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
idaṁ tathyaṁ mama vacas tvam avehi ca rāghava |
tyaja śokaṁ mahābāho tāṁ kāntām ānayāmi te ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
anumānāt tu jānāmi maithilī sā na saṁśayaḥ |
hriyamāṇā mayā dr̥ṣṭā rakṣasā krūrakarmaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
krośantī rāma rāmeti lakṣmaṇeti ca visvaram |
sphurantī rāvaṇasyāṅke pannagendravadhūr yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
ātmanā pañcamaṁ māṁ hi dr̥ṣṭvā śailataṭe sthitam |
uttarīyaṁ tayā tyaktaṁ śubhāny ābharaṇāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tāny asmābhir gr̥hītāni nihitāni ca rāghava |
ānayiṣyāmy ahaṁ tāni pratyabhijñātum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tam abravīt tato rāmaḥ sugrīvaṁ priyavādinam |
ānayasva sakhe śīghraṁ kimarthaṁ pravilambase ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
evam uktas tu sugrīvaḥ śailasya gahanāṁ guhām |
praviveśa tataḥ śīghraṁ rāghavapriyakāmyayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
uttarīyaṁ gr̥hītvā tu śubhāny ābharaṇāni ca |
idaṁ paśyeti rāmāya darśayām āsa vānaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tato gr̥hītvā tadvāsaḥ śubhāny ābharaṇāni ca |
abhavad bāṣpasaṁruddho nīhāreṇeva candramāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sītāsnehapravr̥ttena sa tu bāṣpeṇa dūṣitaḥ |
hā priyeti rudan dhairyam utsr̥jya nyapatat kṣitau ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
hr̥di kr̥tvā sa bahuśas tam alaṁkāram uttamam |
niśaśvāsa bhr̥śaṁ sarpo bilastha iva roṣitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
avicchinnāśruvegas tu saumitriṁ vīkṣya pārśvataḥ |
paridevayituṁ dīnaṁ rāmaḥ samupacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
paśya lakṣmaṇa vaidehyā saṁtyaktaṁ hriyamāṇayā |
uttarīyam idaṁ bhūmau śarīrād bhūṣaṇāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
śādvalinyāṁ dhruvaṁ bhūmyāṁ sītayā hriyamāṇayā |
utsr̥ṣṭaṁ bhūṣaṇam idaṁ tathārūpaṁ hi dr̥śyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
brūhi sugrīva kaṁ deśaṁ hriyantī lakṣitā tvayā |
rakṣasā raudrarūpeṇa mama prāṇasamā priyā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
kva vā vasati tad rakṣo mahad vyasanadaṁ mama |
yannimittam ahaṁ sarvān nāśayiṣyāmi rākṣasān ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
haratā maithilīṁ yena māṁ ca roṣayatā bhr̥śam |
ātmano jīvitāntāya mr̥tyudvāram apāvr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
mama dayitatamā hr̥tā vanād; rajanicareṇa vimathya yena sā |
kathaya mama ripuṁ tam adya vai; pravagapate yamasaṁnidhiṁ nayāmi ||

=== meaning ===

---
