=== Sarga 34 ===

---
1.
tathā bruvāṇaṁ saumitriṁ pradīptam iva tejasā |
abravīl lakṣmaṇaṁ tārā tārādhipanibhānanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
naivaṁ lakṣmaṇa vaktavyo nāyaṁ paruṣam arhati |
harīṇām īśvaraḥ śrotuṁ tava vaktrād viśeṣataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
naivākr̥tajñaḥ sugrīvo na śaṭho nāpi dāruṇaḥ |
naivānr̥takatho vīra na jihmaś ca kapīśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
upakāraṁ kr̥taṁ vīro nāpy ayaṁ vismr̥taḥ kapiḥ |
rāmeṇa vīra sugrīvo yad anyair duṣkaraṁ raṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
rāmaprasādāt kīrtiṁ ca kapirājyaṁ ca śāśvatam |
prāptavān iha sugrīvo rumāṁ māṁ ca paraṁtapa ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
suduḥkhaṁ śāyitaḥ pūrvaṁ prāpyedaṁ sukham uttamam |
prāptakālaṁ na jānīte viśvāmitro yathā muniḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
ghr̥tācyāṁ kila saṁsakto daśavarṣāṇi lakṣmaṇa |
aho ’manyata dharmātmā viśvāmitro mahāmuniḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sa hi prāptaṁ na jānīte kālaṁ kālavidāṁ varaḥ |
viśvāmitro mahātejāḥ kiṁ punar yaḥ pr̥thagjanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
dehadharmaṁ gatasyāsya pariśrāntasya lakṣmaṇa |
avitr̥ptasya kāmeṣu rāmaḥ kṣantum ihārhati ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
na ca roṣavaśaṁ tāta gantum arhasi lakṣmaṇa |
niścayārtham avijñāya sahasā prākr̥to yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sattvayuktā hi puruṣās tvadvidhāḥ puruṣarṣabha |
avimr̥śya na roṣasya sahasā yānti vaśyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
prasādaye tvāṁ dharmajña sugrīvārthe samāhitā |
mahān roṣasamutpannaḥ saṁrambhas tyajyatām ayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
rumāṁ māṁ kapirājyaṁ ca dhanadhānyavasūni ca |
rāmapriyārthaṁ sugrīvas tyajed iti matir mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
samāneṣvyati sugrīvaḥ sītayā saha rāghavam |
śaśāṅkam iva rohiṣyā nihatvā rāvaṇaṁ raṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
śatakoṭisahasrāṇi laṅkāyāṁ kila rakṣasām |
ayutāni ca ṣaṭtriṁśat sahasrāṇi śatāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
ahatvā tāṁś ca durdharṣān rākṣasān kāmarūpiṇaḥ |
na śakyo rāvaṇo hantuṁ yena sā maithilī hr̥tā ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
te na śakyā raṇe hantum asahāyena lakṣmaṇa |
rāvaṇaḥ krūrakarmā ca sugrīveṇa viśeṣataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
evam ākhyātavān vālī sa hy abhijño harīśvaraḥ |
āgamas tu na me vyaktaḥ śravāt tasya bravīmy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tvatsahāyanimittaṁ vai preṣitā haripuṁgavāḥ |
ānetuṁ vānarān yuddhe subahūn hariyūthapān ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tāṁś ca pratīkṣamāṇo ’yaṁ vikrāntān sumahābalān |
rāghavasyārthasiddhyarthaṁ na niryāti harīśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
kr̥tā tu saṁsthā saumitre sugrīveṇa yathāpurā |
adya tair vānarair sarvair āgantavyaṁ mahābalaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
r̥kṣakoṭisahasrāṇi golāṅgūlaśatāni ca |
adya tvām upayāsyanti jahi kopam ariṁdama |
koṭyo ’nekās tu kākutstha kapīnāṁ dīptatejasām ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tava hi mukham idaṁ nirīkṣya kopāt; kṣatajanibhe nayane nirīkṣamāṇāḥ |
harivaravanitā na yānti śāntiṁ; prathamabhayasya hi śaṅkitāḥ sma sarvāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
