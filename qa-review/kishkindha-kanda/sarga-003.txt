=== Sarga 3 ===

---
1.
vaco vijñāya hanumān sugrīvasya mahātmanaḥ |
parvatād r̥śyamūkāt tu pupluve yatra rāghavau ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sa tatra gatvā hanumān balavān vānarottamaḥ |
upacakrāma tau vāgbhir mr̥dvībhiḥ satyavikramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
svakaṁ rūpaṁ parityajya bhikṣurūpeṇa vānaraḥ |
ābabhāṣe ca tau vīrau yathāvat praśaśaṁsa ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
rājarṣidevapratimau tāpasau saṁśitavratau |
deśaṁ katham imaṁ prāptau bhavantau varavarṇinau ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
trāsayantau mr̥gagaṇān anyāṁś ca vanacāriṇaḥ |
pampātīraruhān vr̥kṣān vīkṣamāṇau samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
imāṁ nadīṁ śubhajalāṁ śobhayantau tarasvinau |
dhairyavantau suvarṇābhau kau yuvāṁ cīravāsasau ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
siṁhaviprekṣitau vīrau siṁhātibalavikramau |
śakracāpanibhe cāpe pragr̥hya vipulair bhujaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
śrīmantau rūpasaṁpannau vr̥ṣabhaśreṣṭhavikramau |
hastihastopamabhujau dyutimantau nararṣabhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
prabhayā parvatendro ’yaṁ yuvayor avabhāsitaḥ |
rājyārhāv amaraprakhyau kathaṁ deśam ihāgatau ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
padmapatrekṣaṇau vīrau jaṭāmaṇḍaladhāriṇau |
anyonyasadr̥śau vīrau devalokād ivāgatau ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
yadr̥cchayeva saṁprāptau candrasūryau vasuṁdharām |
viśālavakṣasau vīrau mānuṣau devarūpiṇau ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
siṁhaskandhau mahāsattvau samadāv iva govr̥ṣau |
āyatāś ca suvr̥ttāś ca bāhavaḥ parighottamāḥ |
sarvabhūṣaṇabhūṣārhāḥ kim arthaṁ na vibhūṣitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
ubhau yogyāv ahaṁ manye rakṣituṁ pr̥thivīm imām |
sasāgaravanāṁ kr̥tsnāṁ vindhyameruvibhūṣitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
ime ca dhanuṣī citre ślakṣṇe citrānulepane |
prakāśete yathendrasya vajre hemavibhūṣite ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
saṁpūrṇā niśitair bāṇair tūṇāś ca śubhadarśanāḥ |
jīvitāntakarair ghorair jvaladbhir iva pannagaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
mahāpramāṇau vipulau taptahāṭakabhūṣitau |
khaḍgāv etau virājete nirmuktabhujagāv iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
evaṁ māṁ paribhāṣantaṁ kasmād vai nābhibhāṣathaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
sugrīvo nāma dharmātmā kaś cid vānarayūthapaḥ |
vīro vinikr̥to bhrātrā jagad bhramati duḥkhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
prāpto ’haṁ preṣitas tena sugrīveṇa mahātmanā |
rājñā vānaramukhyānāṁ hanumān nāma vānaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
yuvābhyāṁ saha dharmātmā sugrīvaḥ sakhyam icchati |
tasya māṁ sacivaṁ vittaṁ vānaraṁ pavanātmajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
bhikṣurūpapraticchannaṁ sugrīvapriyakāmyayā |
r̥śyamūkād iha prāptaṁ kāmagaṁ kāmarūpiṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
evam uktvā tu hanumāṁs tau vīrau rāmalakṣmaṇau |
vākyajñau vākyakuśalaḥ punar novāca kiṁ cana ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
etac chrutvā vacas tasya rāmo lakṣmaṇam abravīt |
prahr̥ṣṭavadanaḥ śrīmān bhrātaraṁ pārśvataḥ sthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
sacivo ’yaṁ kapīndrasya sugrīvasya mahātmanaḥ |
tam eva kāṅkṣamāṇasya mamāntikam upāgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tam abhyabhāṣa saumitre sugrīvasacivaṁ kapim |
vākyajñaṁ madhurair vākyaiḥ snehayuktam ariṁdamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
