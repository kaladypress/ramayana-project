=== Sarga 48 ===

---
1.
athāṅgadas tadā sarvān vānarān idam abravīt |
pariśrānto mahāprājñaḥ samāśvāsya śanair vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
vanāni girayo nadyo durgāṇi gahanāni ca |
daryo giriguhāś caiva vicitā naḥ samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tatra tatra sahāsmābhir jānakī na ca dr̥śyate |
tad vā rakṣo hr̥tā yena sītā surasutopamā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
kālaś ca no mahān yātaḥ sugrīvaś cograśāsanaḥ |
tasmād bhavantaḥ sahitā vicinvantu samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
vihāya tandrīṁ śokaṁ ca nidrāṁ caiva samutthitām |
vicinudhvaṁ yathā sītāṁ paśyāmo janakātmajām ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
anirvedaṁ ca dākṣyaṁ ca manasaś cāparājayam |
kāryasiddhikarāṇy āhus tasmād etad bravīmy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
adyāpīdaṁ vanaṁ durgaṁ vicinvantu vanaukasaḥ |
khedaṁ tyaktvā punaḥ sarvaṁ vanam etad vicīyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
avaśyaṁ kriyamāṇasya dr̥śyate karmaṇaḥ phalam |
alaṁ nirvedam āgamya na hi no malinaṁ kṣamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sugrīvaḥ krodhano rājā tīkṣṇadaṇḍaś ca vānarāḥ |
bhetavyaṁ tasya satataṁ rāmasya ca mahātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
hitārtham etad uktaṁ vaḥ kriyatāṁ yadi rocate |
ucyatāṁ vā kṣamaṁ yan naḥ sarveṣām eva vānarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
aṅgadasya vacaḥ śrutvā vacanaṁ gandhamādanaḥ |
uvācāvyaktayā vācā pipāsā śramakhinnayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sadr̥śaṁ khalu vo vākyam aṅgado yad uvāca ha |
hitaṁ caivānukūlaṁ ca kriyatām asya bhāṣitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
punar mārgāmahe śailān kandarāṁś ca darīs tathā |
kānanāni ca śūnyāni giriprasravaṇāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
yathoddiṣṭhāni sarvāṇi sugrīveṇa mahātmanā |
vicinvantu vanaṁ sarve giridurgāṇi sarvaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tataḥ samutthāya punar vānarās te mahābalāḥ |
vindhyakānanasaṁkīrṇāṁ vicerur dakṣiṇāṁ diśam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
te śāradābhrapratimaṁ śrīmadrajataparvatam |
śr̥ṅgavantaṁ darīvantam adhiruhya ca vānarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tatra lodhravanaṁ ramyaṁ saptaparṇavanāni ca |
vicinvanto harivarāḥ sītādarśanakāṅkṣiṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tasyāgram adhirūḍhās te śrāntā vipulavikramāḥ |
na paśyanti sma vaidehīṁ rāmasya mahiṣīṁ priyām ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
te tu dr̥ṣṭigataṁ kr̥tvā taṁ śailaṁ bahukandaram |
avārohanta harayo vīkṣamāṇāḥ samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
avaruhya tato bhūmiṁ śrāntā vigatacetasaḥ |
sthitvā muhūrtaṁ tatrātha vr̥kṣamūlam upāśritāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
te muhūrtaṁ samāśvastāḥ kiṁ cid bhagnapariśramāḥ |
punar evodyatāḥ kr̥tsnāṁ mārgituṁ dakṣiṇāṁ diśam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
hanumatpramukhās te tu prasthitāḥ plavagarṣabhāḥ |
vindhyam evāditas tāvad vicerus te samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
