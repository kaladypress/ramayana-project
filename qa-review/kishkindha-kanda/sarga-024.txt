=== Sarga 24 ===

---
1.
gatāsuṁ vālinaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rāghavas tadanantaram |
abravīt praśritaṁ vākyaṁ sugrīvaṁ śatrutāpanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
na śokaparitāpena śreyasā yujyate mr̥taḥ |
yad atrānantaraṁ kāryaṁ tat samādhātum arhatha ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
lokavr̥ttam anuṣṭheyaṁ kr̥taṁ vo bāṣpamokṣaṇam |
na kālād uttaraṁ kiṁ cit karma śakyam upāsitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
niyataḥ kāraṇaṁ loke niyatiḥ karmasādhanam |
niyatiḥ sarvabhūtānāṁ niyogeṣv iha kāraṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
na kartā kasya cit kaś cin niyoge cāpi neśvaraḥ |
svabhāve vartate lokas tasya kālaḥ parāyaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
na kālaḥ kālam atyeti na kālaḥ parihīyate |
svabhāvaṁ vā samāsādya na kaś cid ativartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
na kālasyāsti bandhutvaṁ na hetur na parākramaḥ |
na mitrajñātisaṁbandhaḥ kāraṇaṁ nātmano vaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
kiṁ tu kāla parīṇāmo draṣṭavyaḥ sādhu paśyatā |
dharmaś cārthaś ca kāmaś ca kālakramasamāhitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
itaḥ svāṁ prakr̥tiṁ vālī gataḥ prāptaḥ kriyāphalam |
dharmārthakāmasaṁyogaiḥ pavitraṁ plavageśvara ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
svadharmasya ca saṁyogāj jitas tena mahātmanā |
svargaḥ parigr̥hītaś ca prāṇān aparirakṣatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
eṣā vai niyatiḥ śreṣṭhā yāṁ gato hariyūthapaḥ |
tad alaṁ paritāpena prāptakālam upāsyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
vacanānte tu rāmasya lakṣmaṇaḥ paravīrahā |
avadat praśritaṁ vākyaṁ sugrīvaṁ gatacetasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
kuru tvam asya sugrīva pretakāryam anantaram |
tārāṅgadābhyāṁ sahito vālino dahanaṁ prati ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
samājñāpaya kāṣṭhāni śuṣkāṇi ca bahūni ca |
candanāni ca divyāni vālisaṁskārakāraṇāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
samāśvāsaya cainaṁ tvam aṅgadaṁ dīnacetasaṁ |
mā bhūr bāliśabuddhis tvaṁ tvadadhīnam idaṁ puram ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
aṅgadas tv ānayen mālyaṁ vastrāṇi vividhāni ca |
ghr̥taṁ tailam atho gandhān yac cātra samanantaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tvaṁ tāra śibikāṁ śīghram ādāyāgaccha saṁbhramāt |
tvarā guṇavatī yuktā hy asmin kāle viśeṣataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
sajjībhavantu plavagāḥ śibikāvāhanocitāḥ |
samarthā balinaś caiva nirhariṣyanti vālinam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
evam uktvā tu sugrīvaṁ sumitrānandavardhanaḥ |
tasthau bhrātr̥samīpastho lakṣmaṇaḥ paravīrahā ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
lakṣmaṇasya vacaḥ śrutvā tāraḥ saṁbhrāntamānasaḥ |
praviveśa guhāṁ śīghraṁ śibikāsaktamānasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
ādāya śibikāṁ tāraḥ sa tu paryāpayat punaḥ |
vānarair uhyamānāṁ tāṁ śūrair udvahanocitaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tato vālinam udyamya sugrīvaḥ śibikāṁ tadā |
āropayata vikrośann aṅgadena sahaiva tu ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
āropya śibikāṁ caiva vālinaṁ gatajīvitam |
alaṁkāraiś ca vividhair mālyair vastraiś ca bhūṣitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
ājñāpayat tadā rājā sugrīvaḥ plavageśvaraḥ |
aurdhvadehikam āryasya kriyatām anurūpataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
viśrāṇayanto ratnāni vividhāni bahūni ca |
agrataḥ plavagā yāntu śibikā tadanantaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
rājñām r̥ddhiviśeṣā hi dr̥śyante bhuvi yādr̥śāḥ |
tādr̥śaṁ vālinaḥ kṣipraṁ prākurvann aurdhvadehikam ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
aṅgadam aprigr̥hyāśu tāraprabhr̥tayas tathā |
krośantaḥ prayayuḥ sarve vānarā hatabāndhavāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tārāprabhr̥tayaḥ sarvā vānaryo hatayūthapāḥ |
anujagmur hi bhartāraṁ krośantyaḥ karuṇasvanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
tāsāṁ ruditaśabdena vānarīṇāṁ vanāntare |
vanāni girayaḥ sarve vikrośantīva sarvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
puline girinadyās tu vivikte jalasaṁvr̥te |
citāṁ cakruḥ subahavo vānarā vanacāriṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
avaropya tataḥ skandhāc chibikāṁ vahanocitāḥ |
tasthur ekāntam āśritya sarve śokasamanvitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
tatas tārā patiṁ dr̥ṣṭvā śibikātalaśāyinam |
āropyāṅke śiras tasya vilalāpa suduḥkhitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
janaṁ ca paśyasīmaṁ tvaṁ kasmāc chokābhipīḍitam |
prahr̥ṣṭam iva te vaktraṁ gatāsor api mānada |
astārkasamavarṇaṁ ca lakṣyate jīvato yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
eṣa tvāṁ rāmarūpeṇa kālaḥ karṣati vānara |
yena sma vidhavāḥ sarvāḥ kr̥tā ekeṣuṇā raṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
imās tās tava rājendravānaryo vallabhāḥ sadā |
pādair vikr̥ṣṭam adhvānam āgatāḥ kiṁ na budhyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
taveṣṭā nanu nāmaitā bhāryāś candranibhānanāḥ |
idānīṁ nekṣase kasmāt sugrīvaṁ plavageśvaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
ete hi sacivā rājaṁs tāraprabhr̥tayas tava |
puravāsijanaś cāyaṁ parivāryāsate ’nagha ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
visarjayainān pravalān yathocitam ariṁdama |
tataḥ krīḍāmahe sarvā vaneṣu madirotkaṭāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
evaṁ vilapatīṁ tārāṁ patiśokapariplutām |
utthāpayanti sma tadā vānaryaḥ śokakarśitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
sugrīveṇa tataḥ sārdham aṅgadaḥ pitaraṁ rudan |
citām āropayām āsa śokenābhihatendriyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
tato ’gniṁ vidhivad dattvā so ’pasavyaṁ cakāra ha |
pitaraṁ dīrgham adhvānaṁ prasthitaṁ vyākulendriyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
saṁskr̥tya vālinaṁ te tu vidhipūrvaṁ plavaṁgamāḥ |
ājagmur udakaṁ kartuṁ nadīṁ śītajalāṁ śubhām ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
tatas te sahitās tatra aṅgadaṁ sthāpya cāgrataḥ |
sugrīvatārāsahitāḥ siṣicur vāline jalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
sugrīveṇaiva dīnena dīno bhūtvā mahābalaḥ |
samānaśokaḥ kākutsthaḥ pretakāryāṇy akārayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
