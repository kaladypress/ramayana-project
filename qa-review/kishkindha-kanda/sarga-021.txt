=== Sarga 21 ===

---
1.
tato nipatitāṁ tārāṁ cyutāṁ tārām ivāmbarāt |
śanair āśvāsayām āsa hanūmān hariyūthapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
guṇadoṣakr̥taṁ jantuḥ svakarmaphalahetukam |
avyagras tad avāpnoti sarvaṁ pretya śubhāśubham ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
śocyā śocasi kaṁ śocyaṁ dīnaṁ dīnānukampase |
kaś ca kasyānuśocyo ’sti dehe ’smin budbudopame ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
aṅgadas tu kumāro ’yaṁ draṣṭavyo jīvaputrayā |
āyatyā ca vidheyāni samarthāny asya cintaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
jānāsy aniyatām evaṁ bhūtānām āgatiṁ gatim |
tasmāc chubhaṁ hi kartavyaṁ paṇḍite naihalaukikam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
yasmin harisahasrāṇi prayutāny arbudāni ca |
vartayanti kr̥tāṁśāni so ’yaṁ diṣṭāntam āgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
yad ayaṁ nyāyadr̥ṣṭārthaḥ sāmadānakṣamāparaḥ |
gato dharmajitāṁ bhūmiṁ nainaṁ śocitum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sarve ca hariśārdūla putraś cāyaṁ tavāṅgadaḥ |
haryr̥ṣkapatirājyaṁ ca tvatsanātham anindite ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tāv imau śokasaṁtaptau śanaiḥ preraya bhāmini |
tvayā parigr̥hīto ’yam aṅgadaḥ śāstu medinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
saṁtatiś ca yathādr̥ṣṭā kr̥tyaṁ yac cāpi sāmpratam |
rājñas tat kriyatāṁ sarvam eṣa kālasya niścayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
saṁskāryo harirājas tu aṅgadaś cābhiṣicyatām |
siṁhāsanagataṁ putraṁ paśyantī śāntim eṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sā tasya vacanaṁ śrutvā bhartr̥vyasanapīḍitā |
abravīd uttaraṁ tārā hanūmantam avasthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
aṅgada pratirūpāṇāṁ putrāṇām ekataḥ śatam |
hatasyāpy asya vīrasya gātrasaṁśleṣaṇaṁ varam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
na cāhaṁ harirājasya prabhavāmy aṅgadasya vā |
pitr̥vyastasya sugrīvaḥ sarvakāryeṣv anantaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
na hy eṣā buddhir āstheyā hanūmann aṅgadaṁ prati |
pitā hi bandhuḥ putrasya na mātā harisattama ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
na hi mama harirājasaṁśrayāt; kṣamataram asti paratra ceha vā |
abhimukhahatavīrasevitaṁ; śayanam idaṁ mama sevituṁ kṣamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
