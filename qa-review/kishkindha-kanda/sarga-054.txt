=== Sarga 54 ===

---
1.
śrutvā hanumato vākyaṁ praśritaṁ dharmasaṁhitam |
svāmisatkārasaṁyuktam aṅgado vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sthairyaṁ sarvātmanā śaucam ānr̥śaṁsyam athārjavam |
vikramaiś caiva dhairyaṁ ca sugrīve nopapadyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
bhrātur jyeṣṭhasya yo bhāryāṁ jīvito mahiṣīṁ priyām |
dharmeṇa mātaraṁ yas tu svīkaroti jugupsitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
kathaṁ sa dharmaṁ jānīte yena bhrātrā durātmanā |
yuddhāyābhiniyuktena bilasya pihitaṁ mukham ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
satyāt pāṇigr̥hītaś ca kr̥takarmā mahāyaśāḥ |
vismr̥to rāghavo yena sa kasya sukr̥taṁ smaret ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
lakṣmaṇasya bhayād yena nādharmabhayabhīruṇā |
ādiṣṭā mārgituṁ sītāṁ dharmam asmin kathaṁ bhavet ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tasmin pāpe kr̥taghne tu smr̥tihīne calātmani |
āryaḥ ko viśvasej jātu tat kulīno jijīviṣuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
rājye putraṁ pratiṣṭhāpya saguṇo nirguṇo ’pi vā |
kathaṁ śatrukulīnaṁ māṁ sugrīvo jīvayiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
bhinnamantro ’parāddhaś ca hīnaśaktiḥ kathaṁ hy aham |
kiṣkindhāṁ prāpya jīveyam anātha iva durbalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
upāṁśudaṇḍena hi māṁ bandhanenopapādayet |
śaṭhaḥ krūro nr̥śaṁsaś ca sugrīvo rājyakāraṇāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
bandhanāc cāvasādān me śreyaḥ prāyopaveśanam |
anujānīta māṁ sarve gr̥hān gacchantu vānarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
ahaṁ vaḥ pratijānāmi na gamiṣyāmy ahaṁ purīm |
ihaiva prāyam āsiṣye śreyo maraṇam eva me ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
abhivādanapūrvaṁ tu rājā kuśalam eva ca |
vācyas tato yavīyān me sugrīvo vānareśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
ārogyapūrvaṁ kuśalaṁ vācyā mātā rumā ca me |
mātaraṁ caiva me tārām āśvāsayitum arhatha ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
prakr̥tyā priyaputrā sā sānukrośā tapasvinī |
vinaṣṭaṁ mām iha śrutvā vyaktaṁ hāsyati jīvitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
etāvad uktvā vacanaṁ vr̥ddhān apy abhivādya ca |
saṁviveśāṅgado bhūmau rudan darbheṣu durmanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tasya saṁviśatas tatra rudanto vānararṣabhāḥ |
nayanebhyaḥ pramumucur uṣṇaṁ vai vāriduḥkhitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
sugrīvaṁ caiva nindantaḥ praśaṁsantaś ca vālinam |
parivāryāṅgado sarve vyavasyan prāyam āsitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
mataṁ tad vāliputrasya vijñāya plavagarṣabhāḥ |
upaspr̥śyodakaṁ sarve prāṅmukhāḥ samupāviśan |
dakṣiṇāgreṣu darbheṣu udaktīraṁ samāśritāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
sa saṁviśadbhir bahubhir mahīdharo; mahādrikūṭapramitaiḥ plavaṁgamaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
babhūva saṁnāditanirjharāntaro; bhr̥śaṁ nadadbhir jaladair ivolbaṇaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
