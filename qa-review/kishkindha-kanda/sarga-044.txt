=== Sarga 44 ===

---
1.
tad ugraśāsanaṁ bhartur vijñāya haripuṁgavāḥ |
śalabhā iva saṁchādya medinīṁ saṁpratasthire ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
rāmaḥ prasravaṇe tasmin nyavasat sahalakṣmaṇaḥ |
pratīkṣamāṇas taṁ māsaṁ yaḥ sītādhigame kr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
uttarāṁ tu diśaṁ ramyāṁ girirājasamāvr̥tām |
pratasthe sahasā vīro hariḥ śatabalis tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
pūrvāṁ diśaṁ prati yayau vinato hariyūthapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tārāṅgadādi sahitaḥ plavagaḥ pavanātmajaḥ |
agastyacaritām āśāṁ dakṣiṇāṁ hariyūthapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
paścimāṁ tu diśaṁ ghorāṁ suṣeṇaḥ plavageśvaraḥ |
pratasthe hariśārdūlo bhr̥śaṁ varuṇapālitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tataḥ sarvā diśo rājā codayitvā yathā tatham |
kapisenā patīn mukhyān mumoda sukhitaḥ sukham ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
evaṁ saṁcoditāḥ sarve rājñā vānarayūthapāḥ |
svāṁ svāṁ diśam abhipretya tvaritāḥ saṁpratasthire ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
nadantaś connadantaś ca garjantaś ca plavaṁgamāḥ |
kṣvelanto dhāvamānāś ca yayuḥ plavagasattamāḥ |
ānayiṣyāmahe sītāṁ haniṣyāmaś ca rāvaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
aham eko haniṣyāmi prāptaṁ rāvaṇam āhave |
tataś conmathya sahasā hariṣye janakātmajām ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
vepamānaṁ śrameṇādya bhavadbhiḥ sthīyatām iti |
eka evāhariṣyāmi pātālād api jānakīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
vidhamiṣyāmy ahaṁ vr̥kṣān dārayiṣyāmy ahaṁ girīn |
dharaṇīṁ dārayiṣyāmi kṣobhayiṣyāmi sāgarān ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
ahaṁ yojanasaṁkhyāyāḥ plavitā nātra saṁśayaḥ |
śataṁ yojanasaṁkhyāyāḥ śataṁ samadhikaṁ hy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
bhūtale sāgare vāpi śaileṣu ca vaneṣu ca |
pātālasyāpi vā madhye na mamācchidyate gatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
ity ekaikaṁ tadā tatra vānarā baladarpitāḥ |
ūcuś ca vacanaṁ tasmin harirājasya saṁnidhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
