=== Sarga 31 ===

---
1.
aṅgadasya vacaḥ śrutvā sugrīvaḥ sacivaiḥ saha |
lakṣmaṇaṁ kupitaṁ śrutvā mumocāsanam ātmavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sacivān abravīd vākyaṁ niścitya gurulāghavam |
mantrajñān mantrakuśalo mantreṣu pariniṣṭhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
na me durvyāhr̥taṁ kiṁ cin nāpi me duranuṣṭhitam |
lakṣmaṇo rāghavabhrātā kruddhaḥ kim iti cintaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
asuhr̥dbhir mamāmitrair nityam antaradarśibhiḥ |
mama doṣān asaṁbhūtāñ śrāvito rāghavānujaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
atra tāvad yathābuddhi sarvair eva yathāvidhi |
bhavadbhir niścayas tasya vijñeyo nipuṇaṁ śanaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
na khalv asti mama trāso lakṣmaṇān nāpi rāghavāt |
mitraṁ tv asthāna kupitaṁ janayaty eva saṁbhramam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sarvathā sukaraṁ mitraṁ duṣkaraṁ paripālanam |
anityatvāt tu cittānāṁ prītir alpe ’pi bhidyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
atonimittaṁ trasto ’haṁ rāmeṇa tu mahātmanā |
yan mamopakr̥taṁ śakyaṁ pratikartuṁ na tan mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sugrīveṇaivam uktas tu hanumān haripuṁgavaḥ |
uvāca svena tarkeṇa madhye vānaramantriṇām ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
sarvathā naitad āścaryaṁ yat tvaṁ harigaṇeśvara |
na vismarasi susnigdham upakārakr̥taṁ śubham ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
rāghaveṇa tu śūreṇa bhayam utsr̥jya dūrataḥ |
tvatpriyārthaṁ hato vālī śakratulyaparākramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sarvathā praṇayāt kruddho rāghavo nātra saṁśayaḥ |
bhrātaraṁ sa prahitavām̐l lakṣmaṇaṁ lakṣmivardhanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tvaṁ pramatto na jānīṣe kālaṁ kalavidāṁ vara |
phullasaptacchadaśyāmā pravr̥ttā tu śarac chivā ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
nirmala grahanakṣatrā dyauḥ pranaṣṭabalāhakā |
prasannāś ca diśaḥ sarvāḥ saritaś ca sarāṁsi ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
prāptam udyogakālaṁ tu nāvaiṣi haripuṁgava |
tvaṁ pramatta iti vyaktaṁ lakṣmaṇo ’yam ihāgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
ārtasya hr̥tadārasya paruṣaṁ puruṣāntarāt |
vacanaṁ marṣaṇīyaṁ te rāghavasya mahātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
kr̥tāparādhasya hi te nānyat paśyāmy ahaṁ kṣamam |
antareṇāñjaliṁ baddhvā lakṣmaṇasya prasādanāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
niyuktair mantribhir vācyo avaśyaṁ pārthivo hitam |
ata eva bhayaṁ tyaktvā bravīmy avadhr̥taṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
abhikruddhaḥ samartho hi cāpam udyamya rāghavaḥ |
sadevāsuragandharvaṁ vaśe sthāpayituṁ jagat ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
na sa kṣamaḥ kopayituṁ yaḥ prasādya punar bhavet |
pūrvopakāraṁ smaratā kr̥tajñena viśeṣataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tasya mūrdhnā praṇamya tvaṁ saputraḥ sasuhr̥jjanaḥ |
rājaṁs tiṣṭha svasamaye bhartur bhāryeva tadvaśe ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
na rāmarāmānujaśāsanaṁ tvayā; kapīndrayuktaṁ manasāpy apohitum |
mano hi te jñāsyati mānuṣaṁ balaṁ; sarāghavasyāsya surendravarcasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
