=== Sarga 1 ===

---
1.
sa tāṁ puṣkariṇīṁ gatvā padmotpalajhaṣākulām |
rāmaḥ saumitrisahito vilalāpākulendriyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tasya dr̥ṣṭvaiva tāṁ harṣād indriyāṇi cakampire |
sa kāmavaśam āpannaḥ saumitrim idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
saumitre paśya pampāyāḥ kānanaṁ śubhadarśanam |
yatra rājanti śailābhā drumāḥ saśikharā iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
māṁ tu śokābhisaṁtaptam ādhayaḥ pīḍayanti vai |
bharatasya ca duḥkhena vaidehyā haraṇena ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
adhikaṁ pravibhāty etan nīlapītaṁ tu śādvalam |
drumāṇāṁ vividhaiḥ puṣpaiḥ paristomair ivārpitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
sukhānilo ’yaṁ saumitre kālaḥ pracuramanmathaḥ |
gandhavān surabhir māso jātapuṣpaphaladrumaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
paśya rūpāṇi saumitre vanānāṁ puṣpaśālinām |
sr̥jatāṁ puṣpavarṣāṇi varṣaṁ toyamucām iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
prastareṣu ca ramyeṣu vividhāḥ kānanadrumāḥ |
vāyuvegapracalitāḥ puṣpair avakiranti gām ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
mārutaḥ sukhaṁ saṁsparśe vāti candanaśītalaḥ |
ṣaṭpadair anukūjadbhir vaneṣu madhugandhiṣu ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
giriprastheṣu ramyeṣu puṣpavadbhir manoramaiḥ |
saṁsaktaśikharā śailā virājanti mahādrumaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
puṣpitāgrāṁś ca paśyemān karṇikārān samantataḥ |
hāṭakapratisaṁchannān narān pītāmbarān iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
ayaṁ vasantaḥ saumitre nānāvihaganāditaḥ |
sītayā viprahīṇasya śokasaṁdīpano mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
māṁ hi śokasamākrāntaṁ saṁtāpayati manmathaḥ |
hr̥ṣṭaḥ pravadamānaś ca samāhvayati kokilaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
eṣa dātyūhako hr̥ṣṭo ramye māṁ vananirjhare |
praṇadan manmathāviṣṭaṁ śocayiṣyati lakṣmaṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
vimiśrā vihagāḥ pumbhir ātmavyūhābhinanditāḥ |
bhr̥ṅgarājapramuditāḥ saumitre madhurasvarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
māṁ hi sā mr̥gaśāvākṣī cintāśokabalātkr̥tam |
saṁtāpayati saumitre krūraś caitravanānilaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
śikhinībhiḥ parivr̥tā mayūrā girisānuṣu |
manmathābhiparītasya mama manmathavardhanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
paśya lakṣṇama nr̥tyantaṁ mayūram upanr̥tyati |
śikhinī manmathārtaiṣā bhartāraṁ girisānuṣu ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
mayūrasya vane nūnaṁ rakṣasā na hr̥tā priyā |
mama tv ayaṁ vinā vāsaḥ puṣpamāse suduḥsahaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
paśya lakṣmaṇa puṣpāṇi niṣphalāni bhavanti me |
puṣpabhārasamr̥ddhānāṁ vanānāṁ śiśirātyaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
vadanti rāvaṁ muditāḥ śakunāḥ saṁghaśaḥ kalam |
āhvayanta ivānyonyaṁ kāmonmādakarā mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
nūnaṁ paravaśā sītā sāpi śocaty ahaṁ yathā |
śyāmā padmapalāśākṣī mr̥dubhāṣā ca me priyā ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
eṣa puṣpavaho vāyuḥ sukhasparśo himāvahaḥ |
tāṁ vicintayataḥ kāntāṁ pāvakapratimo mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tāṁ vinātha vihaṁgo ’sau pakṣī praṇaditas tadā |
vāyasaḥ pādapagataḥ prahr̥ṣṭam abhinardati ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
eṣa vai tatra vaidehyā vihagaḥ pratihārakaḥ |
pakṣī māṁ tu viśālākṣyāḥ samīpam upaneṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
paśya lakṣmaṇa saṁnādaṁ vane madavivardhanam |
puṣpitāgreṣu vr̥kṣeṣu dvijānām upakūjatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
saumitre paśya pampāyāś citrāsu vanarājiṣu |
nalināni prakāśante jale taruṇasūryavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
eṣā prasannasalilā padmanīlotpalāyatā |
haṁsakāraṇḍavākīrṇā pampā saugandhikāyutā ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
cakravākayutā nityaṁ citraprasthavanāntarā |
mātaṅgamr̥gayūthaiś ca śobhate salilārthibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
padmakośapalāśāni draṣṭuṁ dr̥ṣṭir hi manyate |
sītāyā netrakośābhyāṁ sadr̥śānīti lakṣmaṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
padmakesarasaṁsr̥ṣṭo vr̥kṣāntaraviniḥsr̥taḥ |
niḥśvāsa iva sītāyā vāti vāyur manoharaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
saumitre paśya pampāyā dakṣiṇe girisānuni |
puṣpitāṁ karṇikārasya yaṣṭiṁ paramaśobhanām ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
adhikaṁ śailarājo ’yaṁ dhātubhis tu vibhūṣitaḥ |
vicitraṁ sr̥jate reṇuṁ vāyuvegavighaṭṭitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
giriprasthās tu saumitre sarvataḥ saṁprapuṣpitaiḥ |
niṣpatraiḥ sarvato ramyaiḥ pradīpā iva kuṁśukaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
pampātīraruhāś ceme saṁsaktā madhugandhinaḥ |
mālatīmallikāṣaṇḍāḥ karavīrāś ca puṣpitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
ketakyaḥ sinduvārāś ca vāsantyaś ca supuṣpitāḥ |
mādhavyo gandhapūrṇāś ca kundagulmāś ca sarvaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
ciribilvā madhūkāś ca vañjulā bakulās tathā |
campakās tilakāś caiva nāgavr̥kṣāś ca puṣpitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
nīpāś ca varaṇāś caiva kharjūrāś ca supuṣpitāḥ |
aṅkolāś ca kuraṇṭāś ca cūrṇakāḥ pāribhadrakāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
cūtāḥ pāṭalayaś caiva kovidārāś ca puṣpitāḥ |
mucukundārjunāś caiva dr̥śyante girisānuṣu ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
ketakoddālakāś caiva śirīṣāḥ śiṁśapā dhavāḥ |
śālmalyaḥ kiṁśukāś caiva raktāḥ kurabakās tathā |
tiniśā nakta mālāś ca candanāḥ syandanās tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
vividhā vividhaiḥ puṣpais tair eva nagasānuṣu |
vikīrṇaiḥ pītaraktābhāḥ saumitre prastarāḥ kr̥tāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
himānte paśya saumitre vr̥kṣāṇāṁ puṣpasaṁbhavam |
puṣpamāse hi taravaḥ saṁgharṣād iva puṣpitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
paśya śītajalāṁ cemāṁ saumitre puṣkarāyutām |
cakravākānucaritāṁ kāraṇḍavaniṣevitām |
plavaiḥ krauñcaiś ca saṁpūrṇāṁ varāhamr̥gasevitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
adhikaṁ śobhate pampāvikūjadbhir vihaṁgamaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
dīpayantīva me kāmaṁ vividhā muditā dvijāḥ |
śyāmāṁ candramukhīṁ smr̥tvā priyāṁ padmanibhekṣaṇām ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
paya sānuṣu citreṣu mr̥gībhiḥ sahitān mr̥gān |
māṁ punar mr̥gaśāvākṣyā vaidehyā virahīkr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
evaṁ sa vilapaṁs tatra śokopahatacetanaḥ |
avekṣata śivāṁ pampāṁ ramyavārivahāṁ śubhām ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
nirīkṣamāṇaḥ sahasā mahātmā; sarvaṁ vanaṁ nirjharakandaraṁ ca |
udvignacetāḥ saha lakṣmaṇena; vicārya duḥkhopahataḥ pratasthe ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
tāv r̥ṣyamūkaṁ sahitau prayātau; sugrīvaśākhāmr̥gasevitaṁ tam |
trastās tu dr̥ṣṭvā harayo babhūvur; mahaujasau rāghavalakṣmaṇau tau ||

=== meaning ===

---
