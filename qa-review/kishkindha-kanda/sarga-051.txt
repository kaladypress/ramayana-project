=== Sarga 51 ===

---
1.
atha tān abravīt sarvān viśrāntān hariyūthapān |
idaṁ vacanam ekāgrā tāpasī dharmacāriṇī ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
vānarā yadi vaḥ khedaḥ pranaṣṭaḥ phalabhakṣaṇāt |
yadi caitan mayā śrāvyaṁ śrotum icchāmi kathyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tasyās tad vacanaṁ śrutvā hanumān mārutātmajaḥ |
ārjavena yathātattvam ākhyātum upacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
rājā sarvasya lokasya mahendravaruṇopamaḥ |
rāmo dāśarathiḥ śrīmān praviṣṭo daṇḍakāvanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
lakṣmaṇena saha bhrātrā vaidehyā cāpi bhāryayā |
tasya bhāryā janasthānād rāvaṇena hr̥tā balāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
vīras tasya sakhā rājñaḥ sugrīvo nāma vānaraḥ |
rājā vānaramukhyānāṁ yena prasthāpitā vayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
agastyacaritām āśāṁ dakṣiṇāṁ yamarakṣitām |
sahaibhir vānarair mukhyair aṅgadapramukhair vayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
rāvaṇaṁ sahitāḥ sarve rākṣasaṁ kāmarūpiṇam |
sītayā saha vaidehyā mārgadhvam iti coditāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
vicitya tu vayaṁ sarve samagrāṁ dakṣiṇāṁ diśam |
bubhukṣitāḥ pariśrāntā vr̥kṣamūlam upāśritāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
vivarṇavadanāḥ sarve sarve dhyānaparāyaṇāḥ |
nādhigacchāmahe pāraṁ magnāś cintāmahārṇave ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
cārayantas tataś cakṣur dr̥ṣṭavanto mahad bilam |
latāpādapasaṁchannaṁ timireṇa samāvr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
asmād dhaṁsā jalaklinnāḥ pakṣaiḥ salilareṇubhiḥ |
kurarāḥ sārasāś caiva niṣpatanti patatriṇaḥ |
sādhv atra praviśāmeti mayā tūktāḥ plavaṁgamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
teṣām api hi sarveṣām anumānam upāgatam |
gacchāmaḥ praviśāmeti bhartr̥kāryatvarānvitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tato gāḍhaṁ nipatitā gr̥hya hastau parasparam |
idaṁ praviṣṭāḥ sahasā bilaṁ timirasaṁvr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
etan naḥ kāyam etena kr̥tyena vayam āgatāḥ |
tvāṁ caivopagatāḥ sarve paridyūnā bubhukṣitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
ātithyadharmadattāni mūlāni ca phalāni ca |
asmābhir upabhuktāni bubhukṣāparipīḍitaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
yat tvayā rakṣitāḥ sarve mriyamāṇā bubhukṣayā |
brūhi pratyupakārārthaṁ kiṁ te kurvantu vānarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
evam uktā tu sarvajñā vānarais taiḥ svayaṁprabhā |
pratyuvāca tataḥ sarvān idaṁ vānarayūthapam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
sarveṣāṁ parituṣṭāsmi vānarāṇāṁ tarasvinām |
carantyā mama dharmeṇa na kāryam iha kena cit ||

=== meaning ===

---
