=== Sarga 26 ===

---
1.
abhiṣikte tu sugrīve praviṣṭe vānare guhām |
ājagāma saha bhrātrā rāmaḥ prasravaṇaṁ girim ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
śārdūlamr̥gasaṁghuṣṭaṁ siṁhair bhīmaravair vr̥tam |
nānāgulmalatāgūḍhaṁ bahupādapasaṁkulam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
r̥kṣavānaragopucchair mārjāraiś ca niṣevitam |
megharāśinibhaṁ śailaṁ nityaṁ śucijalāśrayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tasya śailasya śikhare mahatīm āyatāṁ guhām |
pratyagr̥hṇata vāsārthaṁ rāmaḥ saumitriṇā saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
avasat tatra dharmātmā rāghavaḥ sahalakṣmaṇaḥ |
bahudr̥śyadarīkuñje tasmin prasravaṇe girau ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
susukhe ’pi bahudravye tasmin hi dharaṇīdhare |
vasatas tasya rāmasya ratir alpāpi nābhavat |
hr̥tāṁ hi bhāryāṁ smarataḥ prāṇebhyo ’pi garīyasīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
udayābhyuditaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā śaśāṅkaṁ ca viśeṣataḥ |
āviveśa na taṁ nidrā niśāsu śayanaṁ gatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tat samutthena śokena bāṣpopahatacetasaṁ |
taṁ śocamānaṁ kākutsthaṁ nityaṁ śokaparāyaṇam |
tulyaduḥkho ’bravīd bhrātā lakṣmaṇo ’nunayan vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
alaṁ vīra vyathāṁ gatvā na tvaṁ śocitum arhasi |
śocato hy avasīdanti sarvārthā viditaṁ hi te ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
bhavān kriyāparo loke bhavān devaparāyaṇaḥ |
āstiko dharmaśīlaś ca vyavasāyī ca rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
na hy avyavasitaḥ śatruṁ rākṣasaṁ taṁ viśeṣataḥ |
samarthas tvaṁ raṇe hantuṁ vikramair jihmakāriṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
samunmūlaya śokaṁ tvaṁ vyavasāyaṁ sthiraṁ kuru |
tataḥ saparivāraṁ taṁ nirmūlaṁ kuru rākṣasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
pr̥thivīm api kākutstha sasāgaravanācalām |
parivartayituṁ śaktaḥ kim aṅga puna rāvaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
ahaṁ tu khalu te vīryaṁ prasuptaṁ pratibodhaye |
dīptair āhutibhiḥ kāle bhasmac channam ivānalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
lakṣmaṇasya tu tad vākyaṁ pratipūjya hitaṁ śubham |
rāghavaḥ suhr̥daṁ snigdham idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
vācyaṁ yad anuraktena snigdhena ca hitena ca |
satyavikrama yuktena tad uktaṁ lakṣmaṇa tvayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
eṣa śokaḥ parityaktaḥ sarvakāryāvasādakaḥ |
vikrameṣv apratihataṁ tejaḥ protsāhayāmy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
śaratkālaṁ pratīkṣe ’ham iyaṁ prāvr̥ḍ upasthitā |
tataḥ sarāṣṭraṁ sagaṇaṁ rākṣasaṁ taṁ nihanmy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā hr̥ṣṭo rāmasya lakṣmaṇaḥ |
punar evābravīd vākyaṁ saumitrir mitranandanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
etat te sadr̥śaṁ vākyam uktaṁ śatrunibarhaṇa |
idānīm asi kākutstha prakr̥tiṁ svām upāgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
vijñāya hy ātmano vīryaṁ tathyaṁ bhavitum arhasi |
etat sadr̥śam uktaṁ te śrutasyābhijanasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tasmāt puruṣaśārdūla cintayañ śatrunigraham |
varṣārātram anuprāptam atikrāmaya rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
niyamya kopaṁ pratipālyatāṁ śarat; kṣamasva māsāṁś caturo mayā saha |
vasācale ’smin mr̥garājasevite; saṁvardhayañ śatruvadhe samudyataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
