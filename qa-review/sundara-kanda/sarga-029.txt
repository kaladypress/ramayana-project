=== Sarga 29 ===

---
1.
tathāgatāṁ tāṁ vyathitām aninditāṁ vyapetaharṣāṁ paridīnamānasām |
śubhāṁ nimittāni śubhāni bhejire naraṁ śriyā juṣṭam ivopajīvinaḥ ||

> **Meaning 1:** As the faultless, auspicious Sītā stood there like this —
> distressed, all her joy gone, her heart utterly dejected — good omens came to
> her, just as dependents gather around a prosperous man.

---
2.
tasyāḥ śubhaṁ vāmam arālapakṣma rājīvr̥taṁ kr̥ṣṇaviśālaśuklam |
prāspandataikaṁ nayanaṁ sukeśyā mīnāhataṁ padmam ivābhitāmram ||

> **Meaning 2:** The lovely left eye of that fair-tressed lady — with its
> curving lashes framing it, its dark centre and wide white — began to throb on
> its own, like a reddish lotus nudged by a fish.

---
3.
bhujaśca cārvañcitapīnavr̥ttaḥ parārdhya kālāgurucandanārhaḥ |
anuttamenādhyuṣitaḥ priyeṇa cireṇa vāmaḥ samavepatāśu ||

> **Meaning 3:** Her left arm also began to tremble suddenly — that beautiful,
> gently curved, full and rounded arm, worthy of the finest dark aloe and
> sandal paste, on which her matchless beloved, Śrī Rāma, had rested his head
> for so long.

---
4.
gajendrahastapratimaśca pīnaḥ tayordvayoḥ saṁhatayoḥ sujātaḥ |
praspandamānaḥ punarūrurasyā rāmaṁ purastāt sthitam ācacakṣe ||

> **Meaning 4:** Then one of her thighs — full and finely shaped like the trunk
> of a great elephant, one of a closely matched pair — began to throb as well,
> as if announcing that Rāma was standing right in front of her.

---
5.
śubhaṁ punarhemasamānavarṇam īṣadrajodhvastam ivāmalākṣyāḥ |
vāsaḥ sthitāyāḥ śikharāgradantyāḥ kiṁ cit parisraṁsata cārugātryāḥ ||

> **Meaning 5:** And as she stood there — clear-eyed, graceful, her teeth
> bright and finely pointed — her beautiful golden garment, now slightly dulled
> with dust, slipped down a little (an omen indicating reunion with her
> husband).

---
6.
etairnimittairaparaiśca subhrūḥ saṁbodhitā prāg api sādhusiddhaiḥ |
vātātapaklāntam iva pranaṣṭaṁ varṣeṇa bījaṁ pratisaṁjaharṣa ||

> **Meaning 6:** Awakened by these omens and by others that had already proved
> true in the past, the fair-browed Sītā came back to life with joy — like a
> seed, withered by wind and sun and almost lost, that springs back to life
> with the rain.

---
7.
tasyāḥ punarbimbaphalopamauṣṭhaṁ svakṣibhrukeśāntam arālapakṣma |
vaktraṁ babhāse sitaśukladaṁṣṭraṁ rāhormukhāc candra iva pramuktaḥ ||

> **Meaning 7:** Then her face began to shine once more — her lips red like
> ripe bimba fruit, her lovely eyes, brows and hairline, her curving lashes and
> gleaming white teeth — like the moon set free from the mouth of Rāhu.

---
8.
sā vītaśokā vyapanītatandrī śāntajvarā harṣavibuddhasattvā |
aśobhatāryā vadanena śukle śītānśunā rātririvoditena ||

> **Meaning 8:** Her grief gone, her weariness lifted, the fever of her sorrow
> cooled and her spirit awakened by joy, the noble Sītā shone with her radiant
> face — like a night in the bright fortnight lit up by the rising moon.

---
