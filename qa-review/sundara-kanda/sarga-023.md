=== Sarga 23 ===

---

1.
ity uktvā maithilīṁ rājā rāvaṇaḥ śatrurāvaṇaḥ |
sandiśya ca tataḥ sarvā rākṣasīr nirjagāma ha ||

2.
niṣkrānte rākṣasendre tu punar antaḥpuraṁ gate |
rākṣasyo bhīmarūpās tāḥ sītāṁ samabhidudruvuḥ ||

3.
tataḥ sītām upāgamya rākṣasyaḥ krodhamūrchitāḥ |
paraṁ paruṣayā vācā vaidehīm idam abruvan ||

4.
paulastyasya variṣṭhasya rāvaṇasya mahātmanaḥ |
daśagrīvasya bhāryātvaṁ sīte na bahu manyase ||

> **Meaning 1-4:** Having spoken thus to Maithilī and commanded all the
> Rākṣasīs, King Rāvaṇa, the tormentor of foes, departed. When the
> king of the Rākṣasas had withdrawn into his inner apartments, those
> Rākṣasīs of terrifying forms rushed upon Sītā. Approaching her,
> intoxicated with anger, they spoke to Vaidehī in harsh tones: "O
> Sītā! Do you not consider it a great honor to become the wife of the
> ten-headed Rāvaṇa, the most excellent descendant of Pulastya?"
---

5.
tatas tv ekajaṭā nāma rākṣasī vākyam abravīt |
āmantrya krodhatāmrākṣī sītāṁ karatalodarīm ||

6.
prajāpatīnāṁ ṣaṇṇān tu caturtho yaḥ prajāpatiḥ |
mānaso brahmaṇaḥ putraḥ pulastya iti viśrutaḥ ||

7.
pulastyasya tu tejasvī maharṣir mānasaḥ sutaḥ |
nāmnā sa viśravā nāma prajāpatisamaprabhaḥ ||

8.
tasya putro viśālākṣi rāvaṇaḥ śatrurāvaṇaḥ |
tasya tvaṁ rākṣasendrasya bhāryā bhavitum arhasi |
mayoktañ cārusarvāṅgi vākyaṁ kiṁ nānumanyase ||

9.
tato harijaṭā nāma rākṣasī vākyam abravīt |
vivr̥tya nayane kopān mārjārasadr̥śekṣaṇā ||

10.
yena devās trayastriṅśad devarājaś ca nirjitaḥ |
tasya tvaṁ rākṣasendrasya bhāryā bhavitum arhasi ||

11.
vīryotsiktasya śūrasya saṅgrāmeṣv anivartinaḥ |
balino vīryayuktasyā bhāryātvaṁ kiṁ na lapsyase ||

> **Meaning 5-11:** Then, a Rākṣasī named Ekajaṭā, her eyes red with
> rage, addressed slender-waisted Sītā and spoke: "Among the six
> Prajāpatis, the fourth is renowned as Pulastya, the son born from the
> thought of Brahmā. The brilliant son of Pulastya's mind is the great
> sage named Viśravas, who possesses the radiance of a Prajāpati. His
> son, wide-eyed one, is Rāvaṇa, the tormentor of foes. You are worthy
> to become the wife of that king of the Rākṣasas! Why do you not heed
> my words?" Then, a Rākṣasī named Harijaṭā, her cat-like eyes rolling
> with fury, spoke: "You are worthy to become the wife of that king of
> the Rākṣasas who has vanquished the thirty-three Devas along with
> Indra himself! Why do you not desire the lordship of that powerful
> hero, proud of his valor and unyielding in battle?"
---

12.
tatas tu praghasā nāma rākṣasī krodhamūrchitā |
bhartsayantī tadā ghoram idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

13.
priyāṁ bahumatāṁ bhāryāṁ tyaktvā rājā mahābalaḥ |
sarvāsāñ ca mahābhāgāṁ tvām upaiṣyati rāvaṇaḥ ||

14.
samr̥ddhaṁ strīsahasreṇa nānāratnopaśobhitam |
antaḥpuraṁ samutsr̥jya tvām upaiṣyati rāvaṇaḥ ||

15.
asakr̥d devatā yuddhe nāgagandharvadānavāḥ |
nirjitāḥ samare yena sa te pārśvam upāgataḥ ||

16.
tasya sarvasamr̥ddhasyā rāvaṇasya mahātmanaḥ |
kimarthaṁ rākṣasendrasya bhāryātvaṁ necchase ’dhame ||

> **Meaning 12-16:** Then, a Rākṣasī named Praghasā, overwhelmed by anger,
> fiercely threatened her and spoke these words: "Abandoning his dear and highly
> esteemed wife, who is the most fortunate of all, the greatly mighty King Rāvaṇa
> is desiring you! Forsaking his inner apartments, which are filled with
> thousands of women and adorned with various gems, Rāvaṇa desires to be with you. He
> who has repeatedly conquered the Devas, Nāgas, Gandharvas, and Dānavas in battle
> has now come to your side. O lowly one, why do you not desire the wifehood of that
> high-souled Rāvaṇa, the king of the Rākṣasas, who is endowed with all
> prosperity?"
---

17.
tatas tu durmukhī nāma rākṣasī vākyam abravīt |
yasya sūryo na tapati bhīto yasya ca mārutaḥ |
na vāti smāyatāpāṅge kin tvaṁ tasya na tiṣṭhasi ||

18.
puṣpavr̥ṣṭiñ ca taravo mumucur yasya vai bhayāt |
śailāś ca subhru pānīyañ jaladāś ca yadecchati ||

19.
tasya nairr̥tarājasya rājarājasya bhāmini |
kin tvaṁ na kuruṣe buddhim bhāryārthe rāvaṇasya hi ||

20.
sādhu te tattvato devi kathitaṁ sādhu bhāmini |
gr̥hāṇa susmite vākyam anyathā na bhaviṣyasi ||

> **Meaning 17-20:** Then, a Rākṣasī named Durmukhī spoke: "O bright-
> eyed one! Out of fear of him, the sun does not blaze too fiercely and
> the wind does not blow too boldly — why do you not submit? Out of
> fear of him, the trees release showers of flowers, and the mountains
> and clouds pour forth water at his mere wish. Why do you not resolve
> to become the wife of Rāvaṇa, the king of the Nairr̥tas, the king of
> kings? What has been spoken to you is truly for your own good, O
> radiant one. Accept our words — or you shall not live!"
---
