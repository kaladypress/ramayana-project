=== Sarga 57 ===

---
1.
etad ākhyānaṁ tat sarvaṁ hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ |
bhūyaḥ samupacakrāma vacanaṁ vaktum uttaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
saphalo rāghavodyogaḥ sugrīvasya ca saṁbhramaḥ |
śīlam āsādya sītāyā mama ca plavanaṁ mahat ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
āryāyāḥ sadr̥śaṁ śīlaṁ sītāyāḥ plavagarṣabhāḥ |
tapasā dhārayel lokān kruddhā vā nirdahed api ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
sarvathātipravr̥ddho ’sau rāvaṇo rākṣasādhipaḥ |
yasya tāṁ spr̥śato gātraṁ tapasā na vināśitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
na tad agniśikhā kuryāt saṁspr̥ṣṭā pāṇinā satī |
janakasyātmajā kuryād utkrodhakaluṣīkr̥tā ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
aśokavanikāmadhye rāvaṇasya durātmanaḥ |
adhastāc chiṁśapāvr̥kṣe sādhvī karuṇam āsthitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
rākṣasībhiḥ parivr̥tā śokasaṁtāpakarśitā |
meghalekhāparivr̥tā candralekheva niṣprabhā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
acintayantī vaidehī rāvaṇaṁ baladarpitam |
pativratā ca suśroṇī avaṣṭabdhā ca jānakī ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
anuraktā hi vaidehī rāmaṁ sarvātmanā śubhā |
ananyacittā rāme ca paulomīva puraṁdare ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tad ekavāsaḥsaṁvītā rajodhvastā tathaiva ca |
śokasaṁtāpadīnāṅgī sītā bhartr̥hite ratā ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sā mayā rākṣasī madhye tarjyamānā muhur muhuḥ |
rākṣasībhir virūpābhir dr̥ṣṭā hi pramadā vane ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
ekaveṇīdharā dīnā bhartr̥cintāparāyaṇā |
adhaḥśayyā vivarṇāṅgī padminīva himāgame ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
rāvaṇād vinivr̥ttārthā martavyakr̥taniścayā |
kathaṁ cin mr̥gaśāvākṣī viśvāsam upapāditā ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tataḥ saṁbhāṣitā caiva sarvam arthaṁ ca darśitā |
rāmasugrīvasakhyaṁ ca śrutvā prītim upāgatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
niyataḥ samudācāro bhaktir bhartari cottamā ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
yan na hanti daśagrīvaṁ sa mahātmā daśānanaḥ |
nimittamātraṁ rāmas tu vadhe tasya bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
evam āste mahābhāgā sītā śokaparāyaṇā |
yad atra pratikartavyaṁ tat sarvam upapādyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
