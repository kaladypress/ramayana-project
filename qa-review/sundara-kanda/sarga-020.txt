=== Sarga 20 ===

---
1.
sa tāṁ parivr̥tāṁ dīnāṁ nirānandāṁ tapasvinīm |
sākārair madhurair vākyair nyadarśayata rāvaṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 1:** Rāvaṇa revealed his intentions with enticing, sweet words and
> alluring gestures to that sorrowful, joyless, and ascetic princess surrounded
> by Rākṣasīs:

---
2.
māṁ dr̥ṣṭvā nāganāsorugūhamānā stanodaram |
adarśanam ivātmānaṁ bhayān netuṁ tvam icchasi ||

> **Meaning 2:** "Seeing me, you hide your breasts and belly behind your thighs
> that taper like elephant trunks, wishing out of fear to make yourself
> invisible.

---
3.
kāmaye tvāṁ viśālākṣi bahumanyasva māṁ priye |
sarvāṅgaguṇasampanne sarvalokamanohare ||

> **Meaning 3:** I desire you, wide-eyed one! Value me, my beloved — you who are
> flawless in every feature, enchanting to all the worlds!

---
4.
neha kecin manuṣyā vā rākṣasāḥ kāmarūpiṇaḥ |
vyapasarpatu te sīte bhayaṁ mattaḥ samutthitam ||

> **Meaning 4:** There are no humans here, nor any shape-shifting Rākṣasas;
> Sītā, let all fear of me completely depart from you.

---
5.
svadharme rakṣasāṁ bhīru sarvathaiṣa na saṁśayaḥ |
gamanaṁ vā parastrīṇāṁ haraṇaṁ sampramathya vā ||

> **Meaning 5:** Gentle one, to approach the wives of others or abduct them by
> force is by all means the recognized way of Rākṣasas; of this there is no
> doubt.

---
6.
evaṁ caitad akāmāṁ ca na tvāṁ sprakṣyāmi maithili |
kāmaṁ kāmaḥ śarīre me yathākāmaṁ pravartatām ||

> **Meaning 6:** Yet even so, Maithilī, I will not touch you against your will,
> however wildly passion may rage within my body.

---
7.
devi neha bhayaṁ kāryaṁ mayi viśvasihi priye |
praṇayasva ca tattvena maivaṁ bhūḥ śokalālasā ||

> **Meaning 7:** Devī, you have nothing to fear here; trust me, beloved. Respond
> to my love sincerely, and do not abandon yourself to sorrow.

---
8.
ekaveṇī dharāśayyā dhyānaṁ malinam ambaram |
asthāne ’py upavāsaś ca naitāny aupayikāni te ||

> **Meaning 8:** A single braid, sleeping on bare ground, mournful brooding,
> soiled clothes, and pointless fasting — none of these befit you.

---
9.
vicitrāṇi ca mālyāni candanāny agarūṇi ca |
vividhāni ca vāsāṁsi divyāny ābharaṇāni ca ||

> **Meaning 9:** Exquisite garlands, fragrant sandalwood, aloe wood, robes of
> every weave, and celestial jewels,

---
10.
mahārhāṇi ca pānāni yānāni śayanāni ca |
gītaṁ nr̥ttaṁ ca vādyaṁ ca labha māṁ prāpya maithili ||

> **Meaning 10:** rare drinks, royal chariots, luxurious couches, song, dance,
> and music — all these are yours once you accept me, Maithilī.

---
11.
strīratnam asi maivaṁ bhūḥ kuru gātreṣu bhūṣaṇam |
māṁ prāpya tu kathaṁ hi syās tvam anarhā suvigrahe ||

> **Meaning 11:** You are the crown jewel of womanhood; do not remain like this.
> Adorn your form with ornaments! Having won me, exquisitely formed one, how
> could you go unadorned?

---
12.
idaṁ te cārusañjātaṁ yauvanaṁ vyativartate |
yad atītaṁ punar naiti srotaḥ śīghram apām iva ||

> **Meaning 12:** This lovely youth of yours is slipping away; once gone, it
> never returns, like the swift rush of river waters.

---
13.
tvāṁ kr̥tvoparato manye rūpakartā sa viśvakr̥t |
na hi rūpopamā tv anyā tavāsti śubhadarśane ||

> **Meaning 13:** Radiant one, I believe the Creator himself ceased all work
> after fashioning you, for nowhere in existence is there any beauty to compare
> with yours.

---
14.
tvāṁ samāsādya vaidehi rūpayauvanaśālinīm |
kaḥ pumān ativarteta sākṣād api pitāmahaḥ ||

> **Meaning 14:** Having beheld you, Vaidehī, glowing with such beauty and
> youth, what man could turn away — even the Grandfather Brahmā himself?

---
15.
yad yat paśyāmi te gātraṁ śītāṁśusadr̥śānane |
tasmiṁs tasmin pr̥thuśroṇi cakṣur mama nibadhyate ||

> **Meaning 15:** Moon-faced lady of graceful bearing, wherever my gaze falls
> upon your form, there my eyes remain spellbound!

---
16.
bhava maithili bhāryā me moham enaṁ visarjaya |
bahvīnām uttamastrīṇāṁ mamāgramahiṣī bhava ||

> **Meaning 16:** Become my wife, Maithilī! Cast aside this stubborn delusion.
> Reign as chief queen over my hundreds of peerless women.

---
17.
lokebhyo yāni ratnāni sampramathyāhr̥tāni me |
tāni te bhīru sarvāṇi rājyaṁ caitad ahaṁ ca te ||

> **Meaning 17:** Gentle lady, all the treasures I have seized by conquest
> across the worlds are yours; this kingdom is yours, and I myself am yours.

---
18.
vijitya pr̥thivīṁ sarvāṁ nānānagaramālinīm |
janakāya pradāsyāmi tava hetor vilāsini ||

> **Meaning 18:** Enchanting one, I will conquer the entire earth with its
> garland of cities and hand it over to King Janaka, all for your sake.

---
19.
neha paśyāmi loke ’nyaṁ yo me pratibalo bhavet |
paśya me sumahad vīryam apratidvandvam āhave ||

> **Meaning 19:** In all the worlds, I see no one who could stand as my equal;
> consider my tremendous might, unmatched on any battlefield!

---
20.
asakr̥t saṁyuge bhagnā mayā vimr̥ditadhvajāḥ |
aśaktāḥ pratyanīkeṣu sthātuṁ mama surāsurāḥ ||

> **Meaning 20:** Repeatedly broken in combat, their banners shattered beneath
> my feet, neither Devas nor Asuras can face me in the frontline of battle.

---
21.
iccha māṁ kriyatām adya pratikarma tavottamam |
saprabhāṇy avasajjantāṁ tavāṅge bhūṣaṇāni ca ||

> **Meaning 21:** Desire me! Let the finest royal adornments be prepared for you
> today, and let radiant jewels grace your form.

---
22.
sādhu paśyāmi te rūpaṁ saṁyuktaṁ pratikarmaṇā |
pratikarmābhisaṁyuktā dākṣiṇyena varānane ||

> **Meaning 22:** Fair-faced one, yield to my plea and let yourself be adorned,
> so that I may feast my eyes on your beauty crowned with royal elegance.

---
23.
bhuṅkṣva bhogān yathākāmaṁ piba bhīru ramasva ca |
yatheṣṭaṁ ca prayaccha tvaṁ pr̥thivīṁ vā dhanāni ca ||

> **Meaning 23:** Shy one, revel in every pleasure as you wish, drink and
> rejoice, and give away lands or vast fortunes to whomever you please!

---
24.
lalasva mayi visrabdhā dhr̥ṣṭam ājñāpayasva ca |
matprabhāvāl lalantyāś ca lalantāṁ bāndhavās tava ||

> **Meaning 24:** Enjoy yourself with me in full confidence and command me
> boldly; and as you rejoice through my power, let your kinsmen rejoice as well.

---
25.
r̥ddhiṁ mamānupaśya tvaṁ śriyaṁ bhadre yaśaś ca me |
kiṁ kariṣyasi rāmeṇa subhage cīravāsasā ||

> **Meaning 25:** Auspicious lady, look upon my boundless wealth, my splendor,
> and my fame! Beautiful one, what will you do with Rāma, a mere wanderer
> wrapped in rags of bark?

---
26.
nikṣiptavijayo rāmo gataśrīr vanagocaraḥ |
vratī sthaṇḍilaśāyī ca śaṅke jīvati vā na vā ||

> **Meaning 26:** Rāma's chances of victory are finished; stripped of fortune,
> wandering in wilderness, bound by austere vows and sleeping on the bare
> ground, I doubt if he is even alive!

---
27.
na hi vaidehi rāmas tvāṁ draṣṭuṁ vāpy upalapsyate |
puro balākair asitair meghair jyotsnām ivāvr̥tām ||

> **Meaning 27:** Vaidehī, Rāma will never set eyes on you again, just as
> moonlight veiled by dark, rain-laden clouds heralded by cranes is lost to
> sight.

---
28.
na cāpi mama hastāt tvāṁ prāptum arhati rāghavaḥ |
hiraṇyakaśipuḥ kīrtim indrahastagatām iva ||

> **Meaning 28:** Nor can Rāghava ever wrest you from my grasp, any more than
> Hiraṇyakaśipu could reclaim his lost renown once it passed into the hands of
> Indra.

---
29.
cārusmite cārudati cārunetre vilāsini |
mano harasi me bhīru suparṇaḥ pannagaṁ yathā ||

> **Meaning 29:** O enchanting one — your smile, your teeth, your luminous eyes
> — shy lady, you seize my heart just as Garuḍa swoops down upon a serpent!

---
30.
kliṣṭakauśeyavasanāṁ tanvīm apy analaṅkr̥tām |
tāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā sveṣu dāreṣu ratiṁ nopalabhāmy aham ||

> **Meaning 30:** Seeing you, though slender, unadorned, and wrapped in soiled
> silk, I no longer find any joy in my own wives.

---
31.
antaḥpuranivāsinyaḥ striyaḥ sarvaguṇānvitāḥ |
yāvantyo mama sarvāsām aiśvaryaṁ kuru jānaki ||

> **Meaning 31:** Jānakī, rule as sovereign over all the women of my inner
> palace, however many there be, every one endowed with beauty and grace.

---
32.
mama hy asitakeśānte trailokyapravarāḥ striyaḥ |
tās tvāṁ paricariṣyanti śriyam apsaraso yathā ||

> **Meaning 32:** Dark-tressed one, my wives are the pick of all three worlds,
> yet they will attend upon you just as the Apsarās serve Goddess Śrī.

---
33.
yāni vaiśravaṇe subhru ratnāni ca dhanāni ca |
tāni lokāṁś ca suśroṇi māṁ ca bhuṅkṣva yathāsukham ||

> **Meaning 33:** Fair-browed lady of graceful bearing, enjoy all the gems and
> riches I seized from Kubera, enjoy the worlds, and delight in me at your
> pleasure!

---
34.
na rāmas tapasā devi na balena na vikramaiḥ |
na dhanena mayā tulyas tejasā yaśasāpi vā ||

> **Meaning 34:** Devī, Rāma is not my equal in ascetic power, nor in strength,
> nor in prowess, nor in wealth, nor in splendor, nor even in renown!

---
35.
piba vihara ramasva bhuṅkṣva bhogān dhananicayaṁ pradiśāmi medinīṁ ca |
mayi lala lalane yathāsukhaṁ tvaṁ tvayi ca sametya lalantu bāndhavās te ||

> **Meaning 35:** Drink, revel, rejoice, and savor every pleasure! I will give
> you mountains of wealth and the earth itself; take your delight in me,
> graceful lady, and let your kinsmen rejoice alongside you.

---
36.
kusumitatarujālasantatāni bhramarayutāni samudratīrajāni |
kanakavimalahārabhūṣitāṅgī vihara mayā saha bhīru kānanāni ||

> **Meaning 36:** Adorned with a necklace of pure gold, gentle one, come wander
> with me through the coastal forests, filled with dense groves of flowering
> trees and humming with swarms of bees!"
