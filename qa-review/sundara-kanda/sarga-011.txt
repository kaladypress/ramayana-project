=== Sarga 11 ===

---
1.
avadhūya ca tāṁ buddhiṁ babhūvāvasthitastadā |
jagāma cāparāṁ cintāṁ sītāṁ prati mahākapiḥ ||

> **Meaning 1:** Shaking off that mistaken thought, the great Kapi regained his
> composure and turned his mind to another line of reasoning concerning Sītā.

---
2.
na rāmeṇa viyuktā sā svaptum arhati bhāminī |
na bhoktuṁ nāpy alaṁkartuṁ na pānam upasevitum ||

> **Meaning 2:** "Separated from Rāma, that noble lady could never sleep like
> this; she would not eat, nor adorn herself, nor partake of wine.

---
3.
nānyaṁ naram upasthātuṁ surāṇām api ceśvaram |
na hi rāmasamaḥ kaścid vidyate tridaśeṣv api |


> **Meaning 3:** Nor could she ever attend upon another man, even if he were the
> ruler of the gods — for among all the devatās, there is no one equal to Rāma."

---
4.
anyeyam iti niścitya pānabhūmau cacāra saḥ |
pānabhūmau hariśreṣṭhaḥ sītāsaṁdarśanotsukaḥ ||

5.
krīḍitenāparāḥ klāntā gītena ca tathā parāḥ |
nr̥ttena cāparāḥ klāntāḥ pānaviprahatāstathā ||

> **Meaning 4-5:** Concluding that this woman was someone else, the best of
> Haris, eager to find Sītā, moved about the banquet hall. There he saw women
> worn out from sporting, singing, dancing, and drinking.

---
6.
murajeṣu mr̥daṅgeṣu pīṭhikāsu ca saṁsthitāḥ |
tathāstaraṇamukhyyeṣu saṁviṣṭāścāparāḥ striyaḥ ||

> **Meaning 6:** Some women were leaning upon their muraja and mr̥daṅga drums,
> some rested against stools, while others lay stretched out upon luxurious
> rugs.

---
7.
aṅganānāṁ sahasreṇa bhūṣitena vibhūṣaṇaiḥ |
rūpasaṁlāpaśīlena yuktagītārthabhāṣiṇā ||

> **Meaning 7:** The Hari troop leader saw a thousand women adorned with fine
> jewelry, gifted in beauty and charming conversation, skilled in singing
> meaningful songs,

---
8.
deśakālābhiyuktena yuktavākyābhidhāyinā |
ratābhiratasaṁsuptaṁ dadarśa hariyūthapaḥ ||

> **Meaning 8:** speaking fitting words suited to time and place, now fast
> asleep after their passionate revels.
9.
anyatrāpi varastrīṇāṁ rūpasaṁlāpaśālinām |
sahasraṁ yuvatīnāṁ tu prasuptaṁ sa dadarśa ha ||

> **Meaning 9:** In another section, he saw thousands of other lovely young
> women, graceful in appearance and speech, sleeping soundly.
10.
tāsāṁ madhye mahābāhuḥ śuśubhe rākṣaseśvaraḥ |
goṣṭhe mahati mukhyānāṁ gavāṁ madhye yathā vr̥ṣaḥ ||

> **Meaning 10:** In their midst, the mighty-armed king of Rākṣasas shone like a
> bull amidst a herd of choice cows in a vast cattle fold.

---
11.
sa rākṣasendraḥ śuśubhe tābhiḥ parivr̥taḥ svayam |
kareṇubhiryathāraṇyaṁ parikīrṇo mahādvipaḥ ||

> **Meaning 11:** Surrounded by them, that king of Rākṣasas looked like a great
> wild elephant encircled by female elephants in the forest.

---
12.
sarvakāmairupetāṁ ca pānabhūmiṁ mahātmanaḥ |
dadarśa kapiśārdūlaḥ tasya rakṣaḥpatergr̥he ||

> **Meaning 12:** In the mansion of that king of Rākṣasas, the tiger among Kapis
> observed the banquet hall, provided with every object of pleasure.

---
13.
mr̥gāṇāṁ mahiṣāṇāṁ ca varāhāṇāṁ ca bhāgaśaḥ |
tatra nyastāni māṁsāni pānabhūmau dadarśa saḥ ||

> **Meaning 13:** He saw portions of venison, buffalo meat, and wild boar laid
> out neatly in courses in that banquet hall.

---
14.
raukmeṣu ca viśaleṣu bhājaneṣv ardhabhakṣitān |
dadarśa kapiśārdūla mayūrān kukkuṭāṁstathā ||

> **Meaning 14:** In wide golden vessels, the tiger among Kapis saw half-eaten
> peafowl and fowl.

---
15.
varāhavārdhrāṇasakān dadhisauvarcalāyutān |
śalyān mr̥gamayūrāṁśca hanūmān anvavaikṣata ||

> **Meaning 15:** Hanumān noticed dishes of wild boar and rhinoceros seasoned
> with curds and rock salt, as well as porcupines, deer, and peafowl.

---
16.
kr̥karān vividhān siddhāṁḥ cakorān ardhabhakṣitān |
mahiṣān ekaśalyāṁśca chāgāṁśca kr̥taniṣṭhitān |


> **Meaning 16:** He saw various roasted partridges, half-eaten cakora birds,
> buffalo meat roasted on skewers, and well-prepared goat meat.

---
17.
lekhyam uccāvacaṁ peyaṁ bhojyāni vividhāni ca ||
tathāmlalavaṇottaṁsaiḥ vividhai rāgaṣāḍavaiḥ |


> **Meaning 17:** There were various delicacies to lick, drinks to sip, and
> diverse foods to chew, served with tangy sour and salty sauces and spiced
> fruit preserves.

---
18.
hāra nūpurakeyūraiḥ apaviddhairmahādhanaiḥ ||
pānabhājanavikṣiptaiḥ phalaiśca vividhairapi |


> **Meaning 18:** With costly necklaces, anklets, and armlets tossed aside, cups
> strewn about, and various fruits scattered across the floor,

---
19.
kr̥tapuṣpopahārā bhūḥ adhikaṁ puṣyati śriyam |
tatra tatra ca vinyastaiḥ suśliṣṭaiḥ śayanāsanaiḥ ||


> **Meaning 19:** strewn with offerings of fresh flowers and furnished here and
> there with well-crafted couches and seats, the floor displayed extraordinary
> elegance.

---
20.
pānabhūmirvinā vahniṁ pradīptevopalakṣyate |
bahuprakārairvividhaiḥ varasaṁskārasaṁskr̥taiḥ ||


> **Meaning 20:** That banquet hall seemed ablaze even without fire, brilliant
> with meats of many kinds expertly seasoned in diverse styles.

---
21.
māṁsaiḥ kuśalasaṁyuktaiḥ pānabhūmigataiḥ pr̥thak |
divyāḥ prasannā vividhāḥ surāḥ kr̥tasurā api ||

22.
śarkarāsavamādhvīkāḥ puṣpāsavaphalāsavāḥ |
vāsacūrṇaiśca vividhaiḥ mr̥ṣṭāstaistaiḥ pr̥thakpr̥thak ||

> **Meaning 21-22:** Beside the expertly prepared meats arranged separately in
> the banquet hall, there were various divine, clear wines and brewed liquors —
> sugar wines, honey wines, flower-infused spirits, and fruit brandies — each
> delicately refined with fragrant aromatic powders.

---
23.
saṁtatā śuśubhe bhūmiḥ mālyaiśca bahusaṁsthitaiḥ |
hiraṇmayaiśca karakaiḥ bhājanaiḥ sphāṭikairapi ||


> **Meaning 23:** The floor shone brightly, strewn with strands of garlands,
> golden pitchers, and crystal flagons.

---
24.
jāmbūnadamayaiścānyaiḥ karakairabhisaṁvr̥tā |
rājateṣu ca kumbheṣu jāmbūnadamayeṣu ca ||


> **Meaning 24:** Surrounded by other golden ewers, and contained in jars of
> silver and gold,

---
25.
pānaśreṣṭhaṁ tadā bhūri kapistatra dadarśa ha |
so ’paśyac chātakumbhāni śīdhormaṇimayāni ca ||


> **Meaning 25:** the Kapi saw abundant quantities of choice wine; he saw golden
> goblets and gem-encrusted flagons filled with liquor.

---
26.
rājatāni ca pūrṇāni bhājanāni mahākapiḥ |
kva cid ardhāvaśeṣāṇi kva cit pītāni sarvaśaḥ ||


> **Meaning 26:** The great Kapi saw silver vessels filled to the brim — some
> half-empty, some drained completely dry,

---
27.
kva cin naiva prapītāni pānāni sa dadarśa ha |
kva cid bhakṣyāṁśca vividhān kva cit pānāni bhāgaśaḥ |
kva cid annāvaśeṣāṇi paśyan vai vicacāra ha ||

> **Meaning 27:** and some drinks untouched. He wandered through, observing
> diverse delicacies in one spot, portions of drinks in another, and leftover
> foods here and there.

---
28.
kva cit prabhinnaiḥ karakaiḥ kva cid āloḍitairghaṭaiḥ |
kva cit saṁpr̥ktamālyāni jalāni ca phalāni ca ||

> **Meaning 28:** Here ewers were tipped over, there flagons were overturned,
> and in places spilled water and fruits were mixed with fallen garlands.

---
29.
śayanāny atra nārīṇāṁ śūnyāni bahudhā punaḥ |
parasparaṁ samāśliṣya kāścit suptā varāṅganāḥ ||

> **Meaning 29:** In many places the couches were vacant, as several lovely
> women slept embracing one another on the floor.

---
30.
kā cic ca vastram anyasyā apahr̥tyopaguhya ca |
upagamyābalā suptā nidrābalaparājitā ||

> **Meaning 30:** One woman, pulling another's garment over herself and wrapping
> it around her body, had fallen asleep beside her, overcome by the power of
> sleep.

---
31.
tāsām ucchvāsavātena vastraṁ mālyaṁ ca gātrajam |
nātyarthaṁ spandate citraṁ prāpya mandam ivānilam ||

> **Meaning 31:** Stiffened by their perfumes, the garments and garlands on
> their bodies barely stirred, gently fanned by the soft breeze of their breath.

---
32.
candanasya ca śītasya śīdhormadhurasasya ca |
vividhasya ca mālyasya puṣpasya vividhasya ca ||

> **Meaning 32:** The scent of cooling sandalwood, the sweet fragrance of wine,
> and the perfume of varied garlands and blooming flowers

---
33.
bahudhā mārutastatra gandhaṁ vividham udvahan |
snānānāṁ candanānāṁ ca dhūpānāṁ caiva mūrchitaḥ ||


> **Meaning 33:** were carried about in diverse ways by the breeze, blending
> with the aromas of bath powders, sandalwood pastes, and rich incense.

---
34.
pravavau surabhirgandhaḥ vimāne puṣpake tadā |
śyāmāvadātāstatrānyāḥ kāścit kr̥ṣṇā varāṅganāḥ ||


> **Meaning 34:** That delightful fragrance wafted throughout the Puṣpaka
> Vimāna; there lay lovely women — some fair-skinned, some dark-complexioned,
> and some of golden hue.

---
35.
kāścit kāñcanavarṇāṅgyaḥ pramadā rākṣasālaye |
tāsāṁ nidrāvaśatvāc ca madanena vimūrchitam ||


> **Meaning 35:** In that Rākṣasa palace, some women possessed golden-hued
> forms, languid with sleep and faint with passion.

---
36.
padminīnāṁ prasuptānāṁ rūpam āsīd yathaiva hi |
evaṁ sarvam aśeṣeṇa rāvaṇāntaḥpuraṁ kapiḥ |
dadarśa sumahātejā na dadarśa ca jānakīm ||

> **Meaning 36:** They looked like sleeping lotus beds closed for the night.
> Thus the glorious Kapi examined every part of Rāvaṇa's inner quarters, yet he
> did not find Jānakī.

---
37.
nirīkṣamāṇaśca tataḥ tāḥ striyaḥ sa mahākapiḥ |
jagāma mahatīṁ cintāṁ dharmasādhvasaśaṅkitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 37:** Beholding those sleeping women, the great Kapi fell into deep
> reflection, troubled by anxiety over dharma:

---
38.
paradārāvarodhasya prasuptasya nirīkṣaṇam |
idaṁ khalu mamātyarthaṁ dharmalopaṁ kariṣyati ||

> **Meaning 38:** "Looking upon the sleeping wives of another man in their
> private quarters — will this not cause a serious violation of dharma?

---
39.
na hi me paradārāṇāṁ dr̥ṣṭirviṣayavartinī |
ayaṁ cātra mayā dr̥ṣṭaḥ paradāraparigrahaḥ ||

> **Meaning 39:** Never has my gaze wandered after another's wife with lustful
> desire; yet here I have scrutinized another man's harem."

---
40.
tasya prādurabhūc cintāpunar anyā manasvinaḥ |
niścitaikāntacittasya kāryaniścayadarśinī ||

> **Meaning 40:** Then another thought arose in the mind of that thoughtful
> hero, whose intellect was resolute and single-minded in discerning duty:

---
41.
kāmaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā mayā sarvā viśvastā rāvaṇastriyaḥ |
na tu me manasaḥ kiṁ cid vaikr̥tyam upapadyate ||

> **Meaning 41:** "Granted that I have seen all these wives of Rāvaṇa sleeping
> without reserve, yet not the slightest impure agitation has arisen in my mind.

---
42.
mano hi hetuḥ sarveṣām indriyāṇāṁ pravartate |
śubhāśubhāsv avasthāsu tac ca me suvyavasthitam ||

> **Meaning 42:** The mind alone is the prompter of all the senses in either
> virtuous or sinful states, and my mind is firmly anchored in purity.

---
43.
nānyatra hi mayā śakyā vaidehī parimārgitum |
striyo hi strīṣu dr̥śyante sadā saṁparimārgaṇe ||

> **Meaning 43:** Moreover, I could not search for Vaidehī anywhere else; when
> searching for a woman, she can only be looked for among other women.

---
44.
yasya sattvasya yā yoniḥ tasyāṁ tat parimārgyate |
na śakyaṁ pramadā naṣṭā mr̥gīṣu parimārgitum ||

> **Meaning 44:** A creature must always be sought among its own kind; a missing
> woman cannot be looked for among female deer.

---
45.
tad idaṁ mārgitaṁ tāvac chuddhena manasā mayā |
rāvaṇāntaḥpuraṁ saraṁ dr̥śyate na ca jānakī ||

> **Meaning 45:** With an immaculate mind I have searched through the whole of
> Rāvaṇa's inner quarters, yet Jānakī is nowhere to be seen."

---
46.
devagandharvakanyāśca nāgakanyāśca vīryavān |
avekṣamāṇo hanumān naivāpaśyata jānakīm ||

> **Meaning 46:** Inspecting the daughters of devas, Gandharvas, and Nāgas, the
> valiant Hanumān still did not see Jānakī.

---
47.
tām apaśyan kapistatra paśyaṁścānyā varastriyaḥ |
apakramya tadā vīraḥ pradhyātum upacakrame ||

> **Meaning 47:** Failing to see her there while viewing those other noble
> women, the heroic Kapi withdrew and began to reflect deeply.

---
48.
sa bhūyastu paraṁ śrīmān mārutiryatnamāsthitaḥ |
āpānabhūmimutsṛjya tāṁ vicetuṁ pracakrame ||

> **Meaning 48:** Summoning greater effort once more, the glorious son of Māruta
> left the banquet hall and set out to search for her in other places.
