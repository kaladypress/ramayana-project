=== Sarga 49 ===

---
1.
taṁ samīkṣya mahāsattvaṁ sattvavān harisattamaḥ |
vākyam arthavad avyagras tam uvāca daśānanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
ahaṁ sugrīvasaṁdeśād iha prāptas tavālayam |
rākṣasendra harīśas tvāṁ bhrātā kuśalam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
bhrātuḥ śr̥ṇu samādeśaṁ sugrīvasya mahātmanaḥ |
dharmārthopahitaṁ vākyam iha cāmutra ca kṣamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
rājā daśaratho nāma rathakuñjaravājimān |
piteva bandhur lokasya sureśvarasamadyutiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
jyeṣṭhas tasya mahābāhuḥ putraḥ priyakaraḥ prabhuḥ |
pitur nideśān niṣkrāntaḥ praviṣṭo daṇḍakāvanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
lakṣmaṇena saha bhrātrā sītayā cāpi bhāryayā |
rāmo nāma mahātejā dharmyaṁ panthānam āśritaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tasya bhāryā vane naṣṭā sītā patim anuvratā |
vaidehasya sutā rājño janakasya mahātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sa mārgamāṇas tāṁ devīṁ rājaputraḥ sahānujaḥ |
r̥śyamūkam anuprāptaḥ sugrīveṇa ca saṁgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tasya tena pratijñātaṁ sītāyāḥ parimārgaṇam |
sugrīvasyāpi rāmeṇa harirājyaṁ niveditam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tatas tena mr̥dhe hatvā rājaputreṇa vālinam |
sugrīvaḥ sthāpito rājye haryr̥kṣāṇāṁ gaṇeśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sa sītāmārgaṇe vyagraḥ sugrīvaḥ satyasaṁgaraḥ |
harīn saṁpreṣayām āsa diśaḥ sarvā harīśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tāṁ harīṇāṁ sahasrāṇi śatāni niyutāni ca |
dikṣu sarvāsu mārgante adhaś copari cāmbare ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
vainateya samāḥ ke cit ke cit tatrānilopamāḥ |
asaṁgagatayaḥ śīghrā harivīrā mahābalāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
ahaṁ tu hanumān nāma mārutasyaurasaḥ sutaḥ |
sītāyās tu kr̥te tūrṇaṁ śatayojanam āyatam |
samudraṁ laṅghayitvaiva tāṁ didr̥kṣur ihāgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tad bhavān dr̥ṣṭadharmārthas tapaḥ kr̥taparigrahaḥ |
paradārān mahāprājña noparoddhuṁ tvam arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
na hi dharmaviruddheṣu bahv apāyeṣu karmasu |
mūlaghātiṣu sajjante buddhimanto bhavadvidhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
kaś ca lakṣmaṇamuktānāṁ rāmakopānuvartinām |
śarāṇām agrataḥ sthātuṁ śakto devāsureṣv api ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
na cāpi triṣu lokeṣu rājan vidyeta kaś cana |
rāghavasya vyalīkaṁ yaḥ kr̥tvā sukham avāpnuyāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tat trikālahitaṁ vākyaṁ dharmyam arthānubandhi ca |
manyasva naradevāya jānakī pratidīyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
dr̥ṣṭā hīyaṁ mayā devī labdhaṁ yad iha durlabham |
uttaraṁ karma yac cheṣaṁ nimittaṁ tatra rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
lakṣiteyaṁ mayā sītā tathā śokaparāyaṇā |
gr̥hya yāṁ nābhijānāsi pañcāsyām iva pannagīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
neyaṁ jarayituṁ śakyā sāsurair amarair api |
viṣasaṁsr̥ṣṭam atyarthaṁ bhuktam annam ivaujasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tapaḥsaṁtāpalabdhas te yo ’yaṁ dharmaparigrahaḥ |
na sa nāśayituṁ nyāyya ātmaprāṇaparigrahaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
avadhyatāṁ tapobhir yāṁ bhavān samanupaśyati |
ātmanaḥ sāsurair devair hetus tatrāpy ayaṁ mahān ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
sugrīvo na hi devo ’yaṁ nāsuro na ca mānuṣaḥ |
na rākṣaso na gandharvo na yakṣo na ca pannagaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
mānuṣo rāghavo rājan sugrīvaś ca harīśvaraḥ |
tasmāt prāṇaparitrāṇaṁ kathaṁ rājan kariṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
na tu dharmopasaṁhāram adharmaphalasaṁhitam |
tad eva phalam anveti dharmaś cādharmanāśanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
prāptaṁ dharmaphalaṁ tāvad bhavatā nātra saṁśayaḥ |
phalam asyāpy adharmasya kṣipram eva prapatsyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
janasthānavadhaṁ buddhvā buddhvā vālivadhaṁ tathā |
rāmasugrīvasakhyaṁ ca budhyasva hitam ātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
kāmaṁ khalv aham apy ekaḥ savājirathakuñjarām |
laṅkāṁ nāśayituṁ śaktas tasyaiṣa tu viniścayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
rāmeṇa hi pratijñātaṁ haryr̥kṣagaṇasaṁnidhau |
utsādanam amitrāṇāṁ sītā yais tu pradharṣitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
apakurvan hi rāmasya sākṣād api puraṁdaraḥ |
na sukhaṁ prāpnuyād anyaḥ kiṁ punas tvadvidho janaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
yāṁ sītety abhijānāsi yeyaṁ tiṣṭhati te vaśe |
kālarātrīti tāṁ viddhi sarvalaṅkāvināśinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
tad alaṁ kālapāśena sītā vigraharūpiṇā |
svayaṁ skandhāvasaktena kṣamam ātmani cintyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
sītāyās tejasā dagdhāṁ rāmakopaprapīḍitām |
dahyamanām imāṁ paśya purīṁ sāṭṭapratolikām ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
sa sauṣṭhavopetam adīnavādinaḥ; kaper niśamyāpratimo ’priyaṁ vacaḥ |
daśānanaḥ kopavivr̥ttalocanaḥ; samādiśat tasya vadhaṁ mahākapeḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
