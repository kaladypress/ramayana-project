=== Sarga 49 ===

---
1.
tataḥ sa karmaṇā tasya vismito bhīmavikramaḥ |
hanumān roṣatāmrākṣaḥ rakṣaḥ adhipam avaikṣata ||

> **Meaning 1:** Hanumān, of fearsome valor, was astonished at what Rāvaṇa's
> attendants had done. His eyes turned copper-red with anger, and he looked at
> the king of the Rākṣasas.

---
2.
bhājamānaṁ mahārheṇa kāñcanena virājatā |
muktājālāvr̥tenātha mukuṭena mahādyutim ||

> **Meaning 2:** He saw Rāvaṇa radiant in a priceless gold crown, gleaming and
> covered with nets of pearls, shining with great splendor.

---
3.
vajrasaṁyogasaṁyuktaiḥ mahārhamaṇivigrahaiḥ |
haimairābharaṇaiścitraiḥ manaseva prakalpitaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 3:** He was resplendent in wondrous gold ornaments set with polished diamonds and invaluable gems, crafted as if by the power of thought itself.

---
4.
mahārhakṣaumasaṁvītaṁ raktacandanarūṣitam |
svanuliptaṁ vicitrābhiḥ vividhabhiśca bhaktibhiḥ ||

> **Meaning 4:** He was draped in priceless silk and smeared with red sandalwood paste. His body was beautifully anointed with tripuṇḍra marks drawn with musk, glowing alongside various intricate patterns.

---
5.
vipulairdarśanīyaiśca rakṣākṣairbhīmadarśanaiḥ |
dīptatīkṣṇamahādaṁṣṭraiḥ pralambadaśanacchadaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 5:** His faces were large and striking, with terrifying eyes,
> bright, sharp, enormous fangs, and long, hanging lips.

---
6.
śirobhirdaśabhirvīraṁ bhrājamānaṁ mahaujasaṁ |
nānāvyālasamākīrṇaiḥ śikharairiva mandaram ||

> **Meaning 6:** He was a mighty hero with ten heads, brilliant and full of
> power, like Mount Mandara with its peaks crowded with many kinds of snakes.

---
7.
nīlāñjanacaya prakhyaṁ hāreṇorasi rājatā |
pūrṇacandrābhavaktreṇa sabalākam ivāmbudam ||

> **Meaning 7:** His body was dark like a heap of black collyrium, and a
> necklace shone on his chest. His face glowed like the full moon, like a rain
> cloud with a line of cranes beneath it.

---
8.
bāhubhirbaddhakeyūraiḥ candanottamarūṣitaiḥ |
bhrājamānāṅgadaiḥ pīnaiḥ pañcaśīrṣairivoragaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 8:** His thick arms were bound with armlets and smeared with the
> finest sandalwood, and they shone with bracelets, like five-hooded serpents.

---
9.
mahati sphāṭike citre ratnasaṁyogasaṁskr̥te |
uttamāstaraṇāstīrṇe upaviṣṭaṁ varāsane ||

> **Meaning 9:** He sat on a splendid seat, upon a large, intricately made
> crystal throne inlaid with jewels and spread with the finest coverings.

---
10.
alaṁkr̥tābhiratyarthaṁ pramadābhiḥ samantataḥ |
vālavyajanahastābhiḥ ārāt samupasevitam ||

> **Meaning 10:** Beautifully adorned women stood all around him, holding
> graceful cāmaras in their hands, attending him from close by.

---
11.
durdhareṇa prahastena mahāpārśvena rakṣasā |
mantribhirmantratattvajñaiḥ nikumbhena ca mantriṇā ||

> **Meaning 11:** Around him sat the Rākṣasas Durdhara, Prahasta and Mahāpārśva,
> ministers who knew the essence of counsel, and the minister Nikumbha.

---
12.
upopaviṣṭaṁ rakṣobhiḥ caturbhirbaladarpitaiḥ |
kr̥tsnaiḥ parivr̥taṁ lokaṁ caturbhiriva sāgaraiḥ ||

> **Meaning 12:** With four proud and powerful Rākṣasas surrounding him, he looked like the whole world encircled by the four oceans.

---
13.
mantribhirmantratattvajñaiḥ anyaiśca śubhabuddhibhiḥ |
anvāsyamānaṁ sacivaiḥ surairiva sureśvaram ||

> **Meaning 13:** Other ministers who knew the essence of counsel, and other
> advisers of good judgment, waited on him as the devatās wait on their king,
> Indra.

---
14.
apaśyad rākṣasapatiṁ hanūmān atitejasaṁ |
viṣṭhitaṁ meruśikhare satoyam iva toyadam ||

> **Meaning 14:** Hanumān saw the Rākṣasa king, full of blazing energy, seated
> like a rain-bearing cloud resting on the peak of Mount Meru.

---
15.
sa taiḥ saṁpīḍyamānaḥ api rakṣobhirbhīmavikramaiḥ |
vismayaṁ paramaṁ gatvā rakṣaḥ adhipam avaikṣata ||

> **Meaning 15:** Though the Rākṣasas of fearsome might were roughly pressing
> him on all sides, Hanumān was filled with great wonder and kept gazing at
> Rāvaṇa.

---
16.
bhrājamānaṁ tato dr̥ṣṭvā hanumān rākṣaseśvaram |
manasā cintayām āsa tejasā tasya mohitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 16:** Seeing the Rākṣasa king shining before him, Hanumān was
> overwhelmed by his brilliance and thought to himself:

---
17.
aho rūpam aho dhairyam aho sattvam aho dyutiḥ |
aho rākṣasarājasya sarvalakṣaṇayuktatā ||

> **Meaning 17:** What beauty, what steadiness, what strength, what radiance!
> The king of the Rākṣasas has every mark of greatness.

---
18.
yady adharmo na balavān syād ayaṁ rākṣaseśvaraḥ |
syād ayaṁ suralokasya saśakrasyāpi rakṣitā ||

> **Meaning 18:** If he were not so unrighteous, this king of the Rākṣasas could
> protect even the world of the devatās, along with Indra.

---
19.
asya krūrair nṛśaṃsaiśca karmabhir lokakutsitaiḥ |
tena bibhyati khalv asmāl lokāḥ sāmaradānavāḥ |


> **Meaning 19:** It is because of his cruel and ruthless deeds, despised by the world, that all the realms, along with the devatās and dānavas, tremble in fear of him.

---
20.
ayaṁ hy utsahate kruddhaḥ kartum ekārṇavaṁ jagat |
iti cintāṁ bahuvidhām akaron matimān kapiḥ |
dr̥ṣṭvā rākṣasarājasya prabhāvam amitaujasaḥ ||

> **Meaning 20:** 'If enraged, he possesses the power to turn the entire world into a single cosmic ocean.' The wise Kapi pondered these many thoughts as he gazed upon the immense, unmeasured power of the Rākṣasa king.

---
