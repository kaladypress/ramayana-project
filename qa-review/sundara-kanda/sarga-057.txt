=== Sarga 57 ===

---
1.
āplutya ca mahāvegaḥ pakṣavān iva parvataḥ |
bhujaṅgayakṣagandharvaprabuddhakamalotpalam ||

> **Meaning 1:** Then the swift Hanumān leapt up like a winged mountain and sped
> across the ocean of the sky. In that ocean, the serpents, Yakṣas, and
> Gandharvas were its blooming lotuses and lilies,

---
2.
sacandrakumudaṁ ramyaṁ sārkakāraṇḍavaṁ śubham |
tiṣyaśravaṇakadambam abhraśaivalaśādvalam ||

> **Meaning 2:** the moon was its white water-lily and the sun its Kāraṇḍava
> duck. It was lovely and auspicious. The stars Tiṣya and Śravaṇa were its
> flocks of swans, and the clouds were its moss and green grass.

---
3.
punarvasu mahāmīnaṁ lohitāṅgamahāgraham |
airāvatamahādvīpaṁ svātīhaṁsaviloḍitam ||

> **Meaning 3:** Punarvasu was its great fish, Lohitāṅga (Mars) its great
> crocodile, Airāvata its great island, and Svātī the swan gliding across its
> surface.

---
4.
vātasaṁghātajātormiṁ candrāṁśuśiśirāmbumat |
hanumān mārutagatir mahānaur iva sāgaram |
apāram apariśrāntaḥ pupluve gaganārṇavam ||

> **Meaning 4:** Its waves were raised by gusts of wind, and its waters were
> cooled by moonbeams. Across that boundless ocean of the sky, Hanumān, moving
> as fast as the wind, sailed on without tiring, like a great ship crossing the
> sea.

---
5.
grasamāna ivākāśaṁ tārādhipam ivālikhan |
harann iva sanakṣatraṁ gaganaṁ sārkamaṇḍalam ||

> **Meaning 5:** He seemed to be swallowing the sky, grazing the moon, and
> carrying off the heavens with all their stars and the sun's disc.

---
6.
mārutasyālayaṁ śrīmān kapir vyomacaro mahān |
hanūmān meghajālāni vikarṣann iva gacchati ||

> **Meaning 6:** The glorious, mighty Kapi Hanumān, traveler of the sky, moved
> through the realm of Māruta as if he were dragging the masses of clouds along
> with him.

---
7.
pāṇḍurāruṇavarṇāni nīlamāñjiṣṭhakāni ca |
haritāruṇavarṇāni mahābhrāṇi cakāśire ||

> **Meaning 7:** Great clouds shone all around him — white and reddish, dark
> blue and madder-red, green and crimson.

---
8.
praviśann abhrajālāni niṣkramaṁś ca punaḥ punaḥ |
pracchannaś ca prakāśaś ca candramā iva lakṣyate ||

> **Meaning 8:** Entering the banks of cloud and coming out again and again, now
> hidden and now visible, he looked like the moon.

---
9.
vividhābhra ghanāpanna gōcarō dhavalāṁbaraḥ |
dr̥śyādr̥śya tanurvīraḥ tadā caṁdrāyatē’ṁbarē || 

> **Meaning 9:** Passing through dense clouds of every kind, the hero, wrapped
> in white, his body now seen and now unseen, looked like the moon in the sky.

---
10.
tārkṣyāyamāṇō gaganē babhāsē vāyunaṁdanaḥ |
dārayanmēghabr̥ṁdāni niṣpataṁśca punaḥ punaḥ || 

> **Meaning 10:** Vāyunandana shone in the sky like Tārkṣya himself, tearing
> through the masses of cloud and bursting out of them again and again.

---
11.
nadannādēna mahatā mēghasvana mahāsvanaḥ |
pravarān rākṣasān hatvā nāma viśrāvya cātmanaḥ || 

> **Meaning 11:** Roaring with a mighty sound like the thunder of clouds, he
> came back. He had killed the foremost Rākṣasas and made his own name known.

---
12.
ākulāṁ nagarīṁ kr̥tvā vyathayitvā ca rāvaṇam |
ardayitvā balaṁ ghōraṁ vaidēhīmabhivādya ca |
ājagāma mahātējāḥ punarmadhyēna sāgaram || 12 |

> **Meaning 12:** He had thrown the city into turmoil, shaken Rāvaṇa, crushed
> his fearsome army, and bowed to Vaidehī. Now that radiant hero was returning
> across the middle of the ocean.

---
13.
parvatēṁdraṁ sunābhaṁ ca samupaspr̥śya vīryavān |
jyāmukta iva nārācō mahāvēgō’bhyupāgataḥ ||

> **Meaning 13:** Touching Sunābha, the king of mountains, on the way, the
> valiant one rushed on at tremendous speed, like an iron arrow released from a
> bowstring.

---
14.
sa kiṁ cid anusaṁprāptaḥ samālokya mahāgirim |
mahendrameghasaṁkāśaṁ nanāda haripuṁgavaḥ ||

> **Meaning 14:** When he had come a little closer and saw the great mountain
> Mahendra, which looked like a towering mass of clouds, the Haripuṅgava let out
> a roar.

---
15.
sa pūrayāmāsa kapiḥ diśō daśa samaṁtataḥ |
nadannādēna mahatā mēghasvanamahāsvanaḥ || 

> **Meaning 15:** With that loud roar, deep as thunder, the Kapi filled all ten
> directions around him.

---
16.
sa t    aṁ dēśamanuprāptaḥ suhr̥ddharśanalālasaḥ |
nanāda hariśārdūlō lāṁgūlaṁ cāpyakaṁpayat || 

> **Meaning 16:** Reaching that region, eager to see his friends, Hariśārdūla
> roared and swished his tail.

---
17.
tasya nānadyamānasya suparṇacaritē pathi |
phalatīvāsya ghōṣēṇa gaganaṁ sārkamaṁḍalam || 

> **Meaning 17:** As he roared again and again along the path that Suparṇa
> travels, the whole sky, with the sun's disc, seemed to split apart at the
> sound.

---
18.
yē tu tatrōttarē tīrē samudrasya mahābalāḥ |
pūrvaṁ saṁviṣṭhitā śśūrāḥ vāyuputradidr̥kṣavaḥ || 18 ||

> **Meaning 18:** Meanwhile, the mighty heroes who had been waiting on the
> northern shore of the ocean, eager to see Vāyuputra,

---
19.
mahatō vātanunnasya tōyadasyēva garjitam |
śuśruvustē tadā ghōṣaṁ ūruvēgaṁ hanūmataḥ || 19 ||

> **Meaning 19:** heard the rushing sound of Hanumān's thighs as he flew, like
> the rumble of a great cloud driven by the wind.

---
20.
tē dīnavadanā ssarvē śuśruvuḥ kānanaukasaḥ |
vānarēṁdrasya nirghōṣaṁ parjanyaninadōpamam ||

> **Meaning 20:** All those forest-dwellers, whose faces had been gloomy, heard
> the roar of the great Vānara, like the rumbling of a rain cloud.

---
21.
niśamya nadato nādaṁ vānarās te samantataḥ |
babhūvur utsukāḥ sarve suhr̥ddarśanakāṅkṣiṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 21:** Hearing him roar, the Vānaras everywhere grew restless with
> excitement, longing to see their friend.

---
22.
jāmbavān sa hariśreṣṭhaḥ prītisaṁhr̥ṣṭamānasaḥ |
upāmantrya harīn sarvān idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

> **Meaning 22:** Jāmbavān, best of the Haris, his heart thrilled with joy,
> called all the Haris together and said:

---
23.
sarvathā kr̥takāryo ’sau hanūmān nātra saṁśayaḥ |
na hy asyākr̥takāryasya nāda evaṁvidho bhavet ||

> **Meaning 23:** "Hanumān has succeeded completely — there's no doubt about it.
> If he had failed, he would never roar like this."

---
24.
tasyā bāhūruvegaṁ ca ninādaṁ ca mahātmanaḥ |
niśamya harayo hr̥ṣṭāḥ samutpetus tatas tataḥ ||

> **Meaning 24:** Hearing the rush of the great one's arms and thighs and his
> roaring, the Haris jumped up joyfully, here and there.

---
25.
te nagāgrān nagāgrāṇi śikharāc chikharāṇi ca |
prahr̥ṣṭāḥ samapadyanta hanūmantaṁ didr̥kṣavaḥ ||

> **Meaning 25:** Delighted and eager to see Hanumān, they leapt from treetop to
> treetop and from peak to peak.

---
26.
te prītāḥ pādapāgreṣu gr̥hya śākhāḥ supuṣpitāḥ |
vāsāṁsīva prakāśāni samāvidhyanta vānarāḥ ||

> **Meaning 26:** Perched happily on the treetops, the Vānaras grabbed flowering
> branches and waved them like bright banners of cloth.

---
27.
girigahvarasaṁlīnō yathā garjati mārutaḥ |
ēvaṁ jagarja balavān hanūmānmārutātmajaḥ ||

> **Meaning 27:** Just as the wind roars when it is trapped in a mountain cave,
> so the mighty Hanumān, Mārutātmaja, roared.

---
28.
tam abhraghanasaṁkāśam āpatantaṁ mahākapim |
dr̥ṣṭvā te vānarāḥ sarve tasthuḥ prāñjalayas tadā ||

> **Meaning 28:** Seeing that great Kapi swooping down, looking like a dense
> mass of clouds, all the Vānaras stood with their palms joined.

---
29.
tatas tu vegavāṁs tasya girer girinibhaḥ kapiḥ |
nipapāta mahendrasya śikhare pādapākule ||

> **Meaning 29:** Then the swift Kapi, huge as a mountain himself, came down on
> the summit of Mahendra, which was thick with trees.

---
30.
harṣēṇāpūryamāṇō’sau ramyē parvata nirjharē |
chinnapakṣa ivākāśātpapāta dharaṇīdharaḥ ||

> **Meaning 30:** Overflowing with joy, he landed beside a lovely mountain
> waterfall, like a mountain falling from the sky after its wings had been cut
> off.

---
31.
tatas te prītamanasaḥ sarve vānarapuṁgavāḥ |
hanūmantaṁ mahātmānaṁ parivāryopatasthire ||

> **Meaning 31:** Then all the foremost Vānaras, glad at heart, crowded around
> the great-souled Hanumān and stood close to him.

---
32.
parivārya ca te sarve parāṁ prītim upāgatāḥ |
prahr̥ṣṭavadanāḥ sarve tam arogam upāgatam ||

> **Meaning 32:** Gathered around him, they were overjoyed, their faces beaming,
> to see him back safe and sound.

---
33.
upāyanāni cādāya mūlāni ca phalāni ca |
pratyarcayan hariśreṣṭhaṁ harayo mārutātmajam ||

> **Meaning 33:** Bringing gifts of roots and fruits, the Haris honored
> Hariśreṣṭha, the son of Māruta.

---
34.
vinedur muditāḥ ke cic cakruḥ kila kilāṁ tathā |
hr̥ṣṭāḥ pādapaśākhāś ca āninyur vānararṣabhāḥ ||

> **Meaning 34:** Some shouted with delight, others made joyful "kila-kila"
> sounds, and the happy Vānararṣabhas brought branches from the trees.

---
35.
hanūmāṁs tu gurūn vr̥ddhāñ jāmbavat pramukhāṁs tadā |
kumāram aṅgadaṁ caiva so ’vandata mahākapiḥ ||

> **Meaning 35:** Then the great Kapi Hanumān bowed respectfully to the elders
> and seniors, led by Jāmbavān, and to Prince Aṅgada.

---
36.
sa tābhyāṁ pūjitaḥ pūjyaḥ kapibhiś ca prasāditaḥ |
dr̥ṣṭā devīti vikrāntaḥ saṁkṣepeṇa nyavedayat ||

> **Meaning 36:** Honored by those two and fussed over by the Kapis, the valiant
> Hanumān, himself worthy of honor, told them briefly, "I have seen the Devī."

---
37.
niṣasāda ca hastena gr̥hītvā vālinaḥ sutam |
ramaṇīye vanoddeśe mahendrasya gires tadā ||

> **Meaning 37:** Taking Vāli's son by the hand, he sat down with him in a
> lovely forest spot on Mount Mahendra.

---
38.
hanūmān abravīd dhr̥ṣṭas tadā tān vānararṣabhān |
aśokavanikāsaṁsthā dr̥ṣṭā sā janakātmajā ||

> **Meaning 38:** Then Hanumān happily told those Vānararṣabhas, "I have seen
> Janakātmajā in the Aśoka grove.

---
39.
rakṣyamāṇā sughorābhī rākṣasībhir aninditā |
ekaveṇīdharā bālā rāmadarśanalālasā |
upavāsapariśrāntā malinā jaṭilā kr̥śā ||

> **Meaning 39:** That blameless young woman is guarded by terrifying Rākṣasīs.
> She wears her hair in a single braid and longs only to see Rāma. She is worn
> out from fasting — unwashed, her hair matted, and very thin."

---
40.
tato dr̥ṣṭeti vacanaṁ mahārtham amr̥topamam |
niśamya māruteḥ sarve muditā vānarā bhavan ||

> **Meaning 40:** When they heard Māruti's words "I have seen her" — words full
> of meaning and sweet as nectar — all the Vānaras were overjoyed.

---
41.
kṣveḍanty anye nadanty anye garjanty anye mahābalāḥ |
cakruḥ kila kilām anye pratigarjanti cāpare ||

> **Meaning 41:** Some of those mighty ones whooped, some roared, some bellowed,
> some made "kila-kila" sounds, and others roared back in answer.

---
42.
ke cid ucchritalāṅgūlāḥ prahr̥ṣṭāḥ kapikuñjarāḥ |
añcitāyatadīrghāṇi lāṅgūlāni pravivyadhuḥ ||

> **Meaning 42:** Some of the Kapikuñjaras, thrilled, raised their tails high
> and lashed about their long, curving tails.

---
43.
apare tu hanūmantaṁ vānarā vāraṇopamam |
āplutya giriśr̥ṅgebhyaḥ saṁspr̥śanti sma harṣitāḥ ||

> **Meaning 43:** Other Vānaras, full of joy, jumped down from the mountain
> peaks just to touch Hanumān, who stood among them like a mighty elephant.

---
44.
uktavākyaṁ hanūmantam aṅgadas tu tadābravīt |
sarveṣāṁ harivīrāṇāṁ madhye vācam anuttamām ||

> **Meaning 44:** When Hanumān had finished speaking, Aṅgada addressed him in
> the midst of all the Hari heroes with these excellent words:

---
45.
sattve vīrye na te kaś cit samo vānaravidyate |
yad avaplutya vistīrṇaṁ sāgaraṁ punar āgataḥ ||

> **Meaning 45:** "Vānara, no one comes close to you in courage and valor. You
> leapt across this vast ocean and came back again.

---
46.
jīvitasya pradātā naḥ tvam eko vānarottama |
tvatprasādāt sameṣyāmaḥ siddhārthā rāghaveṇa saha ||

> **Meaning 46:** Best of Vānaras, you alone have given us back our lives.
> Thanks to you, we will meet Rāghava with our mission accomplished.

---
47.
ahō svāmini tē bhaktiḥ ahō vīryamahō dhr̥tiḥ ||

> **Meaning 47:** What devotion to your master! What valor! What steadfastness!

---
48.
diṣṭyā dr̥ṣṭā tvayā devī rāmapatnī yaśasvinī |
diṣṭyā tyakṣyati kākutsthaḥ śokaṁ sītā viyogajam ||

> **Meaning 48:** How fortunate that you have seen the glorious Devī, Rāma's
> wife! And how fortunate that Kākutstha will now let go of the grief he feels
> at being separated from Sītā!"

---
49.
tato ’ṅgadaṁ hanūmantaṁ jāmbavantaṁ ca vānarāḥ |
parivārya pramuditā bhejire vipulāḥ śilāḥ ||

> **Meaning 49:** Then the Vānaras, overjoyed, gathered around Aṅgada, Hanumān,
> and Jāmbavān and sat down on large boulders.

---
50.
upaviṣṭā girestasya śilāsu vipulāsu te |
śrotukāmāḥ samudrasya laṅghanaṁ vānarottamāḥ ||

> **Meaning 50:** Seated on the broad rocks of that mountain, the best of the
> Vānaras were eager to hear about the crossing of the ocean

---
51.
darśanaṁ cāpi laṅkāyāḥ sītāyā rāvaṇasya ca |
tasthuḥ prāñjalayaḥ sarve hanūmad vadanonmukhāḥ ||

> **Meaning 51:** and how he had seen Laṅkā, Sītā, and Rāvaṇa. They all waited
> with palms joined, their eyes fixed on Hanumān's face.

---
52.
tasthau tatrāṅgadaḥ śrīmān vānarair bahubhir vr̥taḥ |
upāsyamāno vibudhair divi devapatir yathā ||

> **Meaning 52:** The glorious Aṅgada sat there, surrounded by many Vānaras,
> like the king of the devatās in heaven, attended by the devatās.

---
53.
hanūmatā kīrtimatā yaśasvinā; tathāṅgadenāṅgadabaddhabāhunā |
mudā tadādhyāsitam unnataṁ mahan; mahīdharāgraṁ jvalitaṁ śriyābhavat ||

> **Meaning 53:** With the famous and glorious Hanumān and with Aṅgada, his arms
> bound with armlets, sitting on it happily, that lofty mountain summit blazed
> with splendor.

---
