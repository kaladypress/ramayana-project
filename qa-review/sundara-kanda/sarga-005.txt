=== Sarga 5 ===

---
1.
tataḥ sa madhyaṁ gatam aṁśumantaṁ
jyotsnāvitānaṁ mahad udvamantam |
dadarśa dhīmān divi bhānumantaṁ
goṣṭhe vr̥ṣaṁ mattam iva bhramantam ||

> **Meaning 1:** Then the wise Hanumān saw the radiant moon climbed to mid-sky,
> spreading a vast canopy of moonlight and roaming through the heavens like an
> impassioned bull wandering in a pen.

---
2.
lokasya pāpāni vināśayantaṁ
mahodadhiṁ cāpi samedhayantam |
bhūtāni sarvāṇi virājayantaṁ
dadarśa śītāṁśum athābhiyāntam ||

> **Meaning 2:** He watched the cool-rayed moon gliding on, washing away the
> sins and sorrows of the world, swelling the great ocean with its tides, and
> illuminating all living beings.

---
3.
yā bhāti lakṣmīr bhuvi mandarasthā
tathā pradoṣeṣu ca sāgarasthā |
tathaiva toyeṣu ca puṣkarasthā
rarāja sā cāruniśākarasthā ||

> **Meaning 3:** That very splendor which graces Mount Mandara on earth, which
> glows upon the ocean at dusk, and which rests upon lotuses in the water, now
> blazed forth upon that lovely moon.

---
4.
haṁso yathā rājatapañjurasthaḥ
siṁho yathā mandarakandarasthaḥ |
vīro yathā garvitakuñjarasthaḥ
candro ’pi babhrāja tathāmbarasthaḥ ||

> **Meaning 4:** Like a royal swan resting in a cage of silver, like a proud
> lion in a cave of Mount Mandara, or like a valiant warrior mounted upon a
> spirited elephant, the moon shone high in the heavens.

---
5.
sthitaḥ kakudmān iva tīkṣṇaśr̥ṅgaḥ
mahācalaḥ śveta ivoccaśr̥ṅgaḥ |
hastīva jāmbūnadabaddhaśr̥ṅgaḥ
vibhāti candraḥ paripūrṇaśr̥ṅgaḥ ||

> **Meaning 5:** With its crescent horns sharp and full, the moon shone like a
> horned bull, like a towering white mountain with lofty peaks, or like an
> elephant with tusks tipped in gold.

---
6.
vinaṣṭaśītāṁbutuṣārapaṁkō
mahāgrahagrāha vinaṣṭapaṁkaḥ |
prakāśalakṣmyāśraya nirmalāṁkō
rarāja caṁdrō bhagavān śaśāṁkaḥ ||

> **Meaning 6:** Cleansed of the cold dews and chill mists, freed from the grasp
> of eclipsing planets, the divine moon, adorned with its spotless rabbit-mark,
> shone as the very sanctuary of radiant splendor.

---
7.
śilātalaṁ prāpya yathā mr̥gēṁdrō
mahāraṇaṁ prāpya yathā gajēṁdraḥ |
rājyaṁ samāsādya yathā narēṁdraḥ
tathā prakāśō virarāja caṁdraḥ ||

> **Meaning 7:** Like a king of beasts surveying its rocky plateau, like an
> elephant king entering a vast battle, or like a sovereign monarch crowned in
> his realm, the luminous moon shone in all its majesty.

---
8.
prakāśacaṁdrōdaya naṣṭadōṣaḥ
pravr̥ttarakṣaḥ piśitāśadōṣaḥ |
rāmābhirāmērita cittadōṣaḥ
svargaprakāśō bhagavān pradōṣaḥ ||

> **Meaning 8:** With its shadows banished by the bright moonrise, the night was
> alive with fierce flesh-eating Rākṣasas; yet as passion stirred in lovers'
> hearts, that evening hour sparkled with the splendor of heaven.

---
9.
taṁtrīsvanāḥ karṇasukhāḥ pravr̥ttāḥ
svapaṁti nāryaḥ patibhissuvr̥ttāḥ |
naktaṁcarāścāpi tathā pravr̥ttāḥ
vihartumatyadbhutaraudravr̥ttāḥ ||

> **Meaning 9:** Sweet strains of lute strings, pleasing to the ear, echoed
> softly as faithful wives slept beside their husbands; meanwhile,
> night-prowlers of astonishing and terrifying deeds set out on their nocturnal
> revels.

---
10.
mattapramattāni samākulāni
rathāśvabhadrāsana saṁkulāni |
vīraśriyā cāpi samākulāni
dadarśa dhīmān sa kapiḥ kulāni ||

> **Meaning 10:** The wise Kapi observed noble mansions bustling with people
> carefree and intoxicated, packed with chariots, horses, and splendid thrones,
> overflowing with the martial pride of warriors.

---
11.
parasparaṁ cādhikamākṣipaṁti
bhujāṁśca pīnānadhinikṣipaṁti |
mattapralāpānadhivikṣipaṁti
mattāni cānyōnya madhikṣipaṁti ||

> **Meaning 11:** In their drunkenness, the demons jostled and challenged one
> another, flinging their muscular arms about, babbling loudly, and hurling
> insults at one another.

---
12.
rakṣāṁsi vakṣāṁsi ca vikṣipaṁti
gātrāṇi kāṁtāsu ca vikṣipaṁti |
rūpāṇi citrāṇi ca vikṣipaṁti
dr̥ḍhāni cāpāni ca vikṣipaṁti ||

> **Meaning 12:** The Rākṣasas puffed out their chests, threw their arms around
> their beloved women, assumed diverse colorful forms, and brandished their
> sturdy bows.

---
13.
dadarśa kāṁtāśca samālabhaṁtyaḥ
tathāparāstatra puna ssvapaṁtyaḥ |
surūpavaktrāśca tathā hasaṁtyaḥ
kruddhāḥ parāścāpi viniśśvasaṁtyaḥ ||

> **Meaning 13:** He saw women anointing themselves with fragrant sandal paste,
> others drifting back to sleep, lovely-faced ladies laughing merrily, and some
> sighing in petulant anger.

---
14.
mahāgajaiścāpi tathā nadadbhiḥ
supūjitaiścāpi tathā susadbhiḥ |
rarāja vīraiśca viniśśvasadbhiḥ
hradō bhujaṁgairiva niśśvasadbhiḥ ||

> **Meaning 14:** With trumpeting great elephants, well-honored noble warriors,
> and breathing heroes of great strength, the city shone like a deep mountain
> lake filled with hissing serpents.

---
15.
buddhipradhānān rucirābhidhānān
saṁśraddhadhānān jagataḥ pradhānān |
nānāvidhānān rucirābhidhānān
dadarśa tasyāṁ puri yātudhānān ||

> **Meaning 15:** In that city he saw Yātudhānas of sharp intellect, bearing
> pleasing names, steadfast in loyalty, honored as leaders of their people, and
> distinguished by various forms and titles.

---
16.
nanaṁda dr̥ṣṭvā sa ca tān surūpān
nānāguṇānātmaguṇānurūpān |
vidyōtamānān sa tadānurūpān
dadarśa kāṁścicca punarvirūpān ||

> **Meaning 16:** He was pleased to see many of handsome appearance, endowed
> with various virtues matching their nature, glowing with noble bearing, while
> also seeing others of hideous and distorted form.

---
17.
tatō varārhā ssuviśuddhabhāvāḥ
tēṣāṁ striyastatra mahānubhāvāḥ |
priyēṣu pānēṣu ca saktabhāvāḥ
dadarśa tārā iva suprabhāvāḥ ||

> **Meaning 17:** Then he saw their noble women, worthy of the finest luxuries,
> pure in emotion, devoted to their lovers and fond of sweet wine, gleaming with
> radiant beauty like constellations of stars.

---
18.
śriyā jvalaṁtīstrapayōpagūḍhāḥ
niśīthakālē ramaṇōpagūḍhāḥ |
dadarśa kāścit pramadōpagūḍhāḥ
yathā vihaṁgāḥ kusumōpagūḍhāḥ ||

> **Meaning 18:** Radiant with beauty and veiled in modesty, some were embraced
> by their lovers in the dead of night, while others were lost in passionate
> joy, like birds nestled within dense blossoms.

---
19.
anyāḥ punarharmya talōpaviṣṭāḥ
tatra priyāṁkēṣu sukhōpaviṣṭāḥ |
bhartuḥ priyā dharmaparā niviṣṭāḥ
dadarśa dhīmān madanābhiviṣṭāḥ ||

> **Meaning 19:** The wise Kapi saw still other women sitting on rooftop
> terraces, resting happily in the laps of their beloveds—devoted to virtue,
> cherished by their husbands, and overcome by deep affection.

---
20.
aprāvr̥tāḥ kāṁcanarājivarṇāḥ
kāścit parārthyā stapanīyavarṇāḥ |
punaśca kāścicchaśa lakṣmavarṇāḥ
kāṁtaprahīṇā rucirāṁgavarṇāḥ ||

> **Meaning 20:** Some were uncovered, glowing with complexions like stripes of
> gold; some of the highest beauty shone like molten gold; while others,
> deserted by their lovers, were pale as the moon's mark despite their charming
> forms.

---
21.
tataḥ priyān prāpya manō’bhirāmān
suprītiyuktāḥ prasamīkṣya rāmāḥ |
gr̥hēṣu hr̥ṣṭāḥ paramābhirāmāḥ
haripravīraḥ sa dadarśa rāmāḥ ||

> **Meaning 21:** The heroic Hari saw enchanting women in their homes,
> overflowing with delight upon being reunited with the cherished lovers of
> their hearts, gazing with deep affection upon their partners.

---
22.
caṁdraprakāśāśca hi vaktramālāḥ
vakrākṣipakṣmāśca sunētramālāḥ |
vibhūṣaṇānāṁ ca dadarśa mālāḥ
śatahradānāmiva cārumālāḥ ||

> **Meaning 22:** He saw rows of moon-like faces with curved eyelashes and
> luminous eyes, and sparkling garlands of jewelry flashing like chains of
> lightning.

---
23.
na tvēva sītāṁ paramābhijātāṁ
pathi sthitē rājakulē prajātām |
latāṁ praphullāmiva sādhujātāṁ
dadarśa tanvīṁ manasābhijātām ||

> **Meaning 23:** But he did not find Sītā—born of an illustrious royal house
> established in righteousness, delicate like a flowering creeper of noble
> stock, and exquisite in mind and spirit.

---
24.
sanātanē vartmani sanniviṣṭāṁ
rāmēkṣaṇāṁ tāṁ madanābhiviṣṭām |
bharturmana śśrīmadanupraviṣṭāṁ
strībhyō varābhyaśca sadā viśiṣṭām ||

> **Meaning 24:** He did not find her who was anchored on the eternal path of
> virtue, her gaze fixed solely on Rāma, consumed by love for him, dwelling
> perpetually in her husband's noble heart, surpassing the finest women of the
> world.

---
25.
uṣṇārditāṁ sānusr̥tāsrakaṁṭhīṁ
purā varārhōttama niṣkakaṁṭhīm |
sujātapakṣmāmabhiraktakaṁṭhīm
vanē pranr̥ttāmiva nīlakaṁṭhīm ||

> **Meaning 25:** He did not see her who was distressed by grief with tears
> streaming down her neck, who once wore a precious necklace of gold coins; her
> eyelashes were graceful and her voice was melodious, like a peahen dancing in
> the forest.

---
26.
avyaktarēkhāmiva caṁdrarēkhāṁ
pāṁsupradigdhāmiva hēmarēkhām |
kṣataprarūḍhāmiva bāṇarēkhāṁ
vāyuprabhinnāmiva mēgharēkhām ||

> **Meaning 26:** She was like a faint crescent moon obscured by clouds, like a
> streak of pure gold covered in dust, like an arrow-wound healing over, or like
> a wisp of cloud scattered by the wind.

---
27.
sītāmapaśyanmanujēśvarasya
rāmasya patnīṁ vadatāṁ varasya |
babhūva duḥkhābhihataścirasya
plavaṁgamō maṁda ivācirasya ||

> **Meaning 27:** Failing to see Sītā, the wife of Rāma, the ruler of men and
> foremost of eloquent speakers, the Plavaṅgama was overcome by deep sorrow, his
> spirit weighed down in despondency.

---
