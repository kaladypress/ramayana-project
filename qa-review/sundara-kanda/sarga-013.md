=== Sarga 13 ===

---
1.
vimānāt tu susaṁkramya prākāraṁ hariyūthapaḥ |
hanūmān vegavān āsīd yathā vidyudghanāntare ||


---
2.
saṁparikramya hanumān rāvaṇasya niveśanān |
adr̥ṣṭvā jānakīṁ sītām abravīd vacanaṁ kapiḥ ||


---
3.
bhūyiṣṭhaṁ loḍitā laṅkā rāmasya caratā priyam |
na hi paśyāmi vaidehīṁ sītāṁ sarvāṅgaśobhanām ||


---
4.
palvalāni taṭākāni sarāṁsi saritastathā |
nadyo ’nūpavanāntāśca durgāśca dharaṇīdharāḥ |
loḍitā vasudhā sarvā na ca paśyāmi jānakīm ||


---
5.
iha saṁpātinā sītā rāvaṇasya niveśane |
ākhyātā gr̥dhrarājena na ca paśyāmi tām aham ||


> **Meaning 1-5:** Like a streak of lightning that flashes brilliantly among the
> clouds and merges into them in a moment, the foremost of the Vānaras,
> Hanumān, slipped from the mansion to the boundary wall in a split second.
> Having gone around all the mansions of Rāvaṇa and not finding any trace of
> Jānakī, Hanumān spoke these words to himself: "Wandering for the success of
> Rāma's task, I have thoroughly searched the entirety of Laṅkā. Yet, I could
> not find a trace of Sītā Dēvi, the Vaidehī, who is beautiful in all her
> limbs. Small water puddles, tanks, lakes, small streams, rivers, watery
> forest regions, and impenetrable mountains—without leaving anything
> unsearched, I have scoured all of Laṅkā. But Jānakī Dēvi alone is not to be
> seen. I had heard from the king of vultures, Sampāti, that Sītā is right
> here in the mansion of Rāvaṇa, yet I have not been able to catch a trace of
> her."
---



---
6.
kiṁ nu sītātha vaidehī maithilī janakātmajā |
upatiṣṭheta vivaśā rāvaṇaṁ duṣṭacāriṇam ||


---
7.
kṣipram utpatato manye sītām ādāya rakṣasaḥ |
bibhyato rāmabāṇānām antarā patitā bhavet ||


---
8.
atha vā hriyamāṇāyāḥ pathi siddhaniṣevite |
manye patitam āryāyā hr̥dayaṁ prekṣya sāgaram ||


---
9.
rāvaṇasyoruvegena bhujābhyāṁ pīḍitena ca |
tayā manye viśālākṣyā tyaktaṁ jīvitam āryayā ||


---
10.
upary upari vā nūnaṁ sāgaraṁ kramatastadā |
viveṣṭamānā patitā samudre janakātmajā ||


---
11.
āho kṣudreṇa cānena rakṣantī śīlam ātmanaḥ |
abandhurbhakṣitā sītā rāvaṇena tapasvinī ||


---
12.
atha vā rākṣasendrasya patnībhirasitekṣaṇā |
aduṣṭā duṣṭabhāvābhiḥ bhakṣitā sā bhaviṣyati ||


> **Meaning 6-12:** "Could it be that Sītā, the Vaidehī, the Maithilī, the
> daughter of Janaka, has helplessly submitted to the evil-behaving Rāvaṇa?
> (This is impossible). I think that when Rāvaṇa abducted her and flew away
> with great speed out of fear of Rāma's arrows, she might have fallen midway
> into the ocean! Or, while the venerable Sītā was being carried by him
> through the skyway, looking at the boundless ocean below, her heart might
> have shattered with fear and she might have fallen into the sea! Squeezed
> by the immense speed of Rāvaṇa and the tight grip of his arms, I think
> that supremely pure, large-eyed lady might have given up her life. Or
> surely, while he was crossing higher and higher over the ocean, she might
> have fallen into the sea while struggling. Alas, with no one to rescue her
> and being completely helpless, the ascetic Sītā, in her attempt to protect
> her chastity, might have been devoured by this lowly Rāvaṇa! Or perhaps,
> seeing the exceptionally chaste Sītā Dēvi with her beautiful dark eyes, the
> wives of Rāvaṇa, overcome with jealousy, might have devoured her!"
---



---
13.
saṁpūrṇacandrapratimaṁ padmapatranibhekṣaṇam |
rāmasya dhyāyatī vaktraṁ pañcatvaṁ kr̥paṇā gatā ||


---
14.
hā rāma lakṣmaṇety eva hāyodhyeti ca maithilī |
vilapya bahu vaidehī nyastadehā bhaviṣyati ||


---
15.
atha vā nihitā manye rāvaṇasya niveśane |
nūnaṁ lālapyate mandaṁ pañjarastheva śārikā ||


---
16.
janakasya kule jātā rāmapatnī sumadhyamā |
katham utpalapatrākṣī rāvaṇasya vaśaṁ vrajet ||


---
17.
vinaṣṭā vā pranaṣṭā vā mr̥tā vā janakātmajā |
rāmasya priyabhāryasya na nivedayituṁ kṣamam ||


---
18.
nivedyamāne doṣaḥ syād doṣaḥ syād anivedane |
kathaṁ nu khalu kartavyaṁ viṣamaṁ pratibhāti me ||


---
19.
asminn evaṁgate karye prāptakālaṁ kṣamaṁ ca kim |
bhaved iti matiṁ bhūyaḥ hanumān pravicārayan ||


> **Meaning 13-19:** "Constantly thinking of the face of Rāma—who possesses
> eyes like lotus petals and a countenance resembling the full moon—that
> pitiful lady has met her end. Crying out 'O Rāma! O Lakṣmaṇa! O Ayodhyā!',
> the Vaidehī, having lamented profusely, must have given up her body. Or
> perhaps, I think she is kept hidden in the mansion of Rāvaṇa, like a
> captured myna bird in a cage, constantly weeping softly. Born in the
> lineage of Janaka and wife of Rāma, how could that lady with eyes like
> lotus petals submit to the will of Rāvaṇa? Whether the daughter of Janaka
> is ruined, lost, or dead, it is not advisable to report this to Rāma,
> whose beloved wife she is. If it is reported, it would be a fault; if it
> is not reported, it would also be a fault. How should this be done? It
> appears to be a terrible dilemma for me." Thus, reflecting on the current
> situation, Hanumān pondered over what would be the right and timely course
> of action.
---



---
20.
yadi sītām adr̥ṣṭvāhaṁ vānarendrapurīm itaḥ |
gamiṣyāmi tataḥ ko me puruṣārtho bhaviṣyati ||


---
21.
mamedaṁ laṅghanaṁ vyarthaṁ sāgarasya bhaviṣyati |
praveśaściva laṅkāyā rākṣasānāṁ ca darśanam ||


---
22.
kiṁ vā vakṣyati sugrīvaḥ harayo va samāgatāḥ |
kiṣkindhāṁ samanuprāptau tau vā daśarathātmajau ||


---
23.
gatvā tu yadi kākutsthaṁ vakṣyāmi param apriyam |
na dr̥ṣṭeti mayā sītā tatastyakṣyanti jīvitam ||


---
24.
paruṣaṁ dāruṇaṁ krūraṁ tīkṣṇam indriyatāpanam |
sītānimittaṁ durvākyaṁ śrutvā sa na bhaviṣyati ||


---
25.
taṁ tu kr̥cchragataṁ dr̥ṣṭvā pañcatvagatamānasaṁ |
bhr̥śānurakto medhāvī na bhaviṣyati lakṣmaṇaḥ ||


---
26.
vinaṣṭau bhrātarau śrutvā bharato ’pi mariṣyati |
bharataṁ ca mr̥taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā śatrughno na bhaviṣyati ||


---
27.
putrān mr̥tān samīkṣyātha na bhaviṣyanti mātaraḥ |
kausalyā ca sumitrā ca kaikeyī ca na saṁśayaḥ ||


> **Meaning 20-27:** "If I go from here to the city of Kiṣkindhā without
> seeing Sītā, what will be the purpose of my manly efforts? This crossing
> of the ocean by me will become vain, as will the entry into Laṅkā and the
> sighting of the Rākṣasas. What will Sugrīva, or the assembled Vānaras, or
> those two sons of Daśaratha say when I reach Kiṣkindhā? If I go and tell
> Rāma the highly unpleasant news that Sītā was not seen by me, he will then
> give up his life. Hearing the harsh, dreadful, cruel, piercing, and
> sense-tormenting dreadful words that 'Sītā was not found', he will not
> survive. Seeing Śrī Rāma subjected to intense agony and facing death, the
> extremely devoted and highly intelligent Lakṣmaṇa will not survive.
> Hearing that his two brothers have perished, Bharata too will die, and
> seeing Bharata dead, Śatrughna will not survive. Seeing their sons dead,
> the mothers—Kausalyā, Sumitrā, and Kaikeyī—will certainly not survive,
> without a doubt."
---



---
28.
kr̥tajñaḥ satyasaṁdhaśca sugrīvaḥ plavagādhipaḥ |
rāmaṁ tathā gataṁ dr̥ṣṭvā tatastyakṣyanti jīvitam ||


---
29.
durmanā vyathitā dīnā nirānandā tapasvinī |
pīḍitā bhartr̥śokena rumā tyakṣyati jīvitam ||


---
30.
vālijena tu duḥkhena pīḍitā śokakarśitā |
pañcatvagamane rājñaḥ tārāpi na bhaviṣyati ||


---
31.
mātāpitrorvināśena sugrīva vyasanena ca |
kumāro ’py aṅgadaḥ kasmād dhārayiṣyati jīvitam ||


---
32.
bhartr̥jena tu śokena abhibhūtā vanaukasaḥ |
śirāṁsy abhihaniṣyanti talairmuṣṭibhireva ca ||


---
33.
sāntvenānupradānena mānena ca yaśasvinā |
lālitāḥ kapirājena prāṇāṁstyakṣyanti vānarāḥ ||


---
34.
na vaneṣu na śaileṣu na nirodheṣu vā punaḥ |
krīḍām anubhaviṣyanti sametya kapikuñjarāḥ ||


---
35.
saputradārāḥ sāmātyā bhartr̥vyasanapīḍitāḥ |
śailāgrebhyaḥ patiṣyanti sametya viṣameṣu ca ||


> **Meaning 28-35:** "Seeing Rāma in such a state, the grateful and truthful
> king of the Vānaras, Sugrīva, will then give up his life. The ascetic
> Rumā, distressed, tormented, miserable, and devoid of joy, oppressed by
> the grief of her husband, will give up her life. Oppressed by the grief
> born from Vālī, tormented by sorrow, and with the king having met his end,
> Tārā too will not survive. With the death of his parents and the demise of
> Sugrīva, why would the prince Aṅgada continue to hold onto his life?
> Overwhelmed by the grief born from the death of their lord, the
> forest-dwellers will strike their heads with their palms and fists.
> Nurtured with consolations, gifts, and honor by the illustrious king of
> the Vānaras, the Vānaras will give up their lives. The foremost of the
> Vānaras will no longer gather to engage in their joyful pastimes in the
> forests, on the mountains, or in their secret retreats. Oppressed by the
> loss of their lord, they will gather with their sons, wives, and
> ministers, and throw themselves from the mountain peaks into the valleys."
---



---
36.
viṣam udbandhanaṁ vāpi praveśaṁ jvalanasya vā |
upavāsam atho śastraṁ pracariṣyanti vānarāḥ ||


---
37.
ghoram ārodanaṁ manye gate mayi bhaviṣyati |
ikṣvākukulanāśaśca nāśaścaiva vanaukasām ||


---
38.
so ’haṁ naiva gamiṣyāmi kiṣkindhāṁ nagarīm itaḥ |
na hi śakṣyāmy ahaṁ draṣṭuṁ sugrīvaṁ maithilīṁ vinā ||


---
39.
mayy agacchati cehasthe dharmātmānau mahārathau |
āśayā tau dhariṣyete vanarāśca manasvinaḥ ||


---
40.
hastādāno mukhādānaḥ niyato vr̥kṣamūlikaḥ |
vānaprastho bhaviṣyāmi adr̥ṣṭvā janakātmajām ||


---
41.
sāgarānūpaje deśe bahumūlaphalodake |
citāṁ kr̥tvā pravekṣyāmi samiddham araṇīsutam ||


---
42.
upaviṣṭasya vā samyag liṅginaṁ sādhayiṣyataḥ |
śarīraṁ bhakṣayiṣyanti vāyasāḥ śvāpadāni ca ||


---
43.
idam apy r̥ṣibhirdr̥ṣṭaṁ niryāṇam iti me matiḥ |
samyag āpaḥ pravekṣyāmi na cet paśyāmi jānakīm ||


> **Meaning 36-43:** "The Vānaras will resort to poison, hanging, entering a
> fire, fasting, or using weapons. I think there will be a terrible wailing
> if I return, resulting in the destruction of the Ikṣvāku lineage and the
> annihilation of the Vānaras. Therefore, I will not go from here to the
> city of Kiṣkindhā. I will not be able to see Sugrīva without Maithilī. If
> I stay here and do not return, those two righteous and great warriors, Rāma
> and Lakṣmaṇa, will sustain themselves on hope, and so will the wise
> Vānaras. Without seeing the daughter of Janaka, I will become a
> Vānaprastha (forest-hermit), accepting whatever falls into my hands or
> mouth, keeping my senses controlled, and living under a tree. Or, having
> built a pyre on a seashore abundant with roots, fruits, and water, I will
> enter the blazing fire. Or, while I sit perfectly engaged in fasting until
> death, the crows and beasts of prey will devour my body. I consider this to
> be the method of passing away as approved by the sages. Otherwise, I will
> enter the waters to attain Jalasamādhi if I do not see Jānakī."
---



---
44.
sujātamūlā subhagā kīrtimālāyaśasvinī |
prabhagnā cirarātrīyaṁ mama sītām apaśyataḥ ||


---
45.
tāpaso vā bhaviṣyāmi niyato vr̥kṣamūlikaḥ |
netaḥ pratigamiṣyāmi tām adr̥ṣṭvāsitekṣaṇām ||


---
46.
yadītaḥ pratigacchāmi sītām anadhigamya tām |
aṅgadaḥ sahitaiḥ sarvaiḥ vānarairna bhaviṣyati ||


---
47.
vināśe bahavo doṣā jīvan prāpnoti bhadrakam |
tasmāt prāṇān dhariṣyāmi dhruvo jīvati saṁgamaḥ ||


---
48.
evaṁ bahuvidhaṁ duḥkhaṁ manasā dhārayan muhuḥ |
nādhyagacchat tadā pāraṁ śokasya kapikuñjaraḥ ||


---
49.
rāvaṇaṁ vā vadhiṣyāmi daśagrīvaṁ mahābalam |
kāmam astu hr̥tā sītā pratyācīrṇaṁ bhaviṣyati ||


---
50.
atha vainaṁ samutkṣipya upary upari sāgaram |
rāmāyopahariṣyāmi paśuṁ paśupateriva ||


---
51.
iti cintā samāpannaḥ sītām anadhigamya tām |
dhyānaśokā parītātmā cintayām āsa vānaraḥ ||


> **Meaning 44-51:** "At the beginning of this night, I defeated the guardian
> deity of Laṅkā, gaining an auspicious and well-rooted garland of fame.
> Made beautiful by the moonrise, this glorious night became famous as the
> night I entered Laṅkā. Yet, because I have not found Sītā, all the glory
> of this long, sleepless night has been shattered and rendered as useless
> as rose water poured into ashes! Or I will become an ascetic, living with
> controlled senses under a tree, but I will not return from here without
> seeing that lady of dark eyes. If I return from here without finding Sītā,
> Aṅgada along with all the assembled Vānaras will not survive. There are
> many disasters in dying (suicide); if one stays alive, one can obtain
> happiness. For those who stay alive, reuniting someday is certain.
> Therefore, I will sustain my life." Though holding such various sorrows in
> his mind repeatedly, that foremost of the Vānaras could not reach the end
> of his grief at that time. "Or I will kill Rāvaṇa, the ten-necked one of
> immense strength. Even if Sītā has been abducted, my revenge will have
> been taken. Or else, lifting him up and carrying him high over the ocean,
> I will offer him to Rāma like a sacrificial animal to Paśupati (Śiva)."
> Engulfed in such thoughts, having not found Sītā, Hanumān, his mind
> overwhelmed with meditation and sorrow, pondered again.
---



---
52.
yāvat sītāṁ na paśyāmi rāmapatnīṁ yaśasvinīm |
tāvad etāṁ purīṁ laṅkāṁ vicinomi punaḥ punaḥ ||


---
53.
saṁpāti vacanāc cāpi rāmaṁ yady ānayāmy aham |
apaśyan rāghavo bhāryāṁ nirdahet sarvavānarān ||


---
54.
ihaiva niyatāhāraḥ vatsyāmi niyatendriyaḥ |
na matkr̥te vinaśyeyuḥ sarve te naravānarāḥ ||


---
55.
aśokavanikā cāpi mahatīyaṁ mahādrumā |
imām abhigamiṣyāmi na hīyaṁ vicitā mayā ||


---
56.
vasūn rudrāṁstathādityān aśvinau maruto ’pi ca |
namaskr̥tvā gamiṣyāmi rakṣasāṁ śokavardhanaḥ ||


---
57.
jitvā tu rākṣasān devīm ikṣvākukulanandinīm |
saṁpradāsyāmi rāmāyā yathāsiddhiṁ tapasvine ||


---
58.
sa muhūrtam iva dhyātvā cintāvigrathitendriyaḥ |
udatiṣṭhan mahābāhuḥ hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ ||


> **Meaning 52-58:** "As long as I do not see Sītā, the illustrious wife of
> Rāma, I will continue to search this city of Laṅkā again and again. If,
> based on Sampāti's words, I were to just bring Rāma here, the descendant
> of Raghu, not seeing his wife, would burn all the Vānaras to ashes. I will
> dwell right here, regulating my diet and controlling my senses. All those
> men and Vānaras should not perish because of me. Here is a great Aśoka
> grove with massive trees. I will go there, as it remains unexplored by me.
> Bowing to the Vasus, Rudras, Ādityas, Aśvins, and Maruts, I shall proceed
> to become a source of immense grief for the Rākṣasas. Having conquered all
> the Rākṣasas, I will offer the virtuous Sītā, the lady who brings joy to
> the Ikṣvāku lineage, to Śrī Rāma, just as the fruit of penance is yielded
> to a great ascetic." Thus, the great-armed Hanumān, the son of Vāyu, whose
> mind was entangled in thought, meditated for a moment and then stood up.
---



---
59.
namo ’stu rāmāya salakṣmaṇāya devyai ca tasyai janakātmajāyai |
namo ’stu rudrendrayamānilebhyaḥ namo ’stu candrārkamarudgaṇebhyaḥ ||


---
60.
sa tebhyastu namaskr̥tvā sugrīvāya ca mārutiḥ |
diśaḥ sarvāḥ samālokya aśokavanikāṁ prati ||


---
61.
sa gatvā manasā pūrvam aśokavanikāṁ śubhām |
uttaraṁ cintayām āsa vānaro mārutātmajaḥ ||


---
62.
dhruvaṁ tu rakṣobahulā bhaviṣyati vanākulā |
aśokavanikā cintyā sarvasaṁskārasaṁskr̥tā ||


---
63.
rakṣiṇaścātra vihitā nūnaṁ rakṣanti pādapān |
bhagavān api sarvātmā nātikṣobhaṁ pravāyati ||


---
64.
saṁkṣipto ’yaṁ mayātmā ca rāmārthe rāvaṇasya ca |
siddhiṁ me saṁvidhāsyanti devāḥ sarṣigaṇāstv iha ||


> **Meaning 59-64:** "Namaskārams to Rāma along with Lakṣmaṇa, and to that
> divine lady, the daughter of Janaka. Namaskārams to Rudra, Indra, Yama,
> and Vāyu. Namaskārams to the Moon, the Sun, and the hosts of Maruts."
> Having offered his pranāmams to them and to Sugrīva, Māruti, surveying
> all the directions, set his mind towards the Aśoka grove. Having first
> determined in his mind to proceed to the auspicious Aśoka grove, the
> Vānara, the son of Vāyu, thought about his next steps. "The Aśoka grove,
> well-maintained with all kinds of care, will surely be filled with many
> Rākṣasas and crowded with trees. The guards stationed there will
> certainly be protecting the trees, and the omnipresent Lord Vāyu (Wind
> God) will not blow there with great turbulence. I have shrunk my body
> for the sake of Rāma's mission and to remain unseen by Rāvaṇa. May the gods and
> the hosts of sages here grant me success."
---



---
65.
brahmā svayambhūrbhagavān devāścaiva diśantu me |
siddhim agniśca vāyuśca puruhūtaśca vajradhr̥t ||


---
66.
varuṇaḥ pāśahastaśca somādityai tathaiva ca |
aśvinau ca mahātmānau marutaḥ sarva eva ca ||


---
67.
siddhiṁ sarvāṇi bhūtāni bhūtānāṁ caiva yaḥ prabhuḥ |
dāsyanti mama ye cānye adr̥ṣṭāḥ pathi gocarāḥ ||


---
68.
tad unnasaṁ pāṇḍuradantam avraṇaṁ śucismitaṁ padmapalāśalocanam |
drakṣye tad āryāvadanaṁ kadā nv ahaṁ prasannatārādhipatulyadarśanam ||


---
69.
kṣudreṇa pāpena nr̥śaṁsakarmaṇā sudāruṇālāṁkr̥taveṣadhāriṇā |
balābhibhūtā abalā tapasvinī kathaṁ nu me dr̥ṣṭapathe ’dya sā bhavet ||


> **Meaning 65-69:** "May the self-born Brahmā, the Devatās, Agni, Vāyu,
> Indra the wielder of the thunderbolt, Varuṇa with the noose in hand, the
> Moon, the Sun, the great-souled Aśvins, and all the Maruts grant me
> success. May all beings, Śrīmahāviṣṇu who is the master of all living
> creatures, and the other deities wandering on the unseen path, grant me
> success in my endeavor. When will I see that noble lady's flawless face,
> which possesses a prominent nose, white teeth, a pure smile, and eyes like
> lotus petals, and which is as beautiful as the clear moon? Forcibly
> abducted by that lowly one, a sinner who commits cruel deeds, and who
> despite his terrifying nature wears an adorned guise (with marks like a
> tilaka and tripuṇḍra), how can that helpless lady, who is constantly
> meditating on Śrī Rāma every moment like an ascetic, come into my line of
> sight today?"




---
