=== Sarga 62 ===

---
1.
tān uvāca hariśreṣṭho hanūmān vānararṣabhaḥ |
avyagramanaso yūyaṁ madhu sevata vānarāḥ ||

> **Meaning 1:** Hanumān, the best of the Haris and a bull among Vānaras, said
> to them, "Vānaras, enjoy the honey without a care."

---
2.
śrutvā hanumato vākyaṁ harīṇāṁ pravaro ’ṅgadaḥ |
pratyuvāca prasannātmā pibantu harayo madhu ||

> **Meaning 2:** Hearing Hanumān's words, Aṅgada, the foremost of the Haris,
> replied in good spirits, "Let the Haris drink the honey.

---
3.
avaśyaṁ kr̥takāryasya vākyaṁ hanumato mayā |
akāryam api kartavyaṁ kim aṅga punar īdr̥śam ||

> **Meaning 3:** Hanumān has accomplished his mission, so I must follow his word
> even if it means doing something improper — let alone something like this."

---
4.
andagasya mukhāc chrutvā vacanaṁ vānararṣabhāḥ |
sādhu sādhv iti saṁhr̥ṣṭā vānarāḥ pratyapūjayan ||

> **Meaning 4:** Hearing these words from Aṅgada's mouth, the bulls among
> Vānaras were thrilled. "Well said! Well said!" they cried, and they honored
> him.

---
5.
pūjayitvāṅgadaṁ sarve vānarā vānararṣabham |
jagmur madhuvanaṁ yatra nadīvega iva drutam ||

> **Meaning 5:** After honoring Aṅgada, the bull among Vānaras, all the Vānaras
> rushed to Madhuvana like the current of a river.

---
6.
te prahr̥ṣṭā madhuvanaṁ pālān ākramya vīryataḥ ||

7.
atisargāc ca paṭavo dr̥ṣṭvā śrutvā ca maithilīm |
papu ssarvē madhu tadā rasavatphalamādaduḥ ||

> **Meaning 6-7:** Overjoyed, they forced their way past the guards of
> Madhuvana. Bold because they had been given free rein, and because they had
> seen and heard of Maithilī, they all drank the honey then and took the juicy
> fruits.

---
8.
utpatya ca tataḥ sarve vanapālān samāgatāḥ |
tāḍayanti sma śataśaḥ saktān madhuvane tadā ||

> **Meaning 8:** Then they all leapt up and ganged up on the forest guards who
> were blocking them in Madhuvana, and beat them by the hundreds.

---
9.
madhūni droṇamātrāṇi bahubhiḥ parigr̥hya te |
ghnanti sma sahitāḥ sarve bhakṣayanti tathāpare ||

> **Meaning 9:** Many of them grabbed honeycombs as big as a droṇa measure. Some
> used them to strike the guards together, while others ate them.

---
10.
ke cit pītvāpavidhyanti madhūni madhupiṅgalāḥ |
madhūcciṣṭena ke cic ca jaghnur anyonyam utkaṭāḥ ||

> **Meaning 10:** Some, honey-tawny in color, drank the honey and then threw the
> combs away. Some, wild with excitement, pelted one another with the leftover
> wax.

---
11.
apare vr̥kṣamūleṣu śākhāṁ gr̥hya vyavasthitaḥ |
atyarthaṁ ca madaglānāḥ parṇāny āstīrya śerate ||

> **Meaning 11:** Others sat at the foot of the trees holding on to a branch.
> Completely worn out by intoxication, they spread leaves on the ground and lay
> down to sleep.

---
12.
unmattabhūtāḥ plavagā madhumattāś ca hr̥ṣṭavat |
kṣipanty api tathānyonyaṁ skhalanty api tathāpare ||

> **Meaning 12:** The Plavagas, drunk on honey, behaved like madmen in their
> glee. Some shoved one another, while others stumbled about.

---
13.
ke cit kṣveḍān prakurvanti ke cit kūjanti hr̥ṣṭavat |
harayo madhunā mattāḥ ke cit suptā mahītale ||

> **Meaning 13:** Some let out loud roars, some warbled happily. Some of the
> Haris, drunk on honey, fell asleep on the ground.

---
14.
kr̥tvā kēciddhasaṁtyanyē kēcitkurvaṁti cētarat |
kr̥tvā kēcidvadaṁtyanyē kēcidbudhyaṁti cētarat || 

> **Meaning 14:** Some did something and laughed while others did the opposite.
> Some did something and talked about it, while others thought up something
> else.

---
15.
ye ’py atra madhupālāḥ syuḥ preṣyā dadhimukhasya tu |
te ’pi tair vānarair bhīmaiḥ pratiṣiddhā diśo gatāḥ ||

> **Meaning 15:** Even the honey-guards who were there, Dadhimukha's servants,
> were driven off by those fearsome Vānaras and fled in all directions.

---
16.
jānubhiś ca prakr̥ṣṭāś ca devamārgaṁ ca darśitāḥ |
abruvan paramodvignā gatvā dadhimukhaṁ vacaḥ ||

> **Meaning 16:** They were dragged by their knees and tossed up into the sky,
> the path of the devatās. Deeply shaken, they went to Dadhimukha and said:

---
17.
hanūmatā dattavarair hataṁ madhuvanaṁ balāt |
vayaṁ ca jānubhiḥ kr̥ṣṭā devamārgaṁ ca darśitāḥ ||

> **Meaning 17:** "Given free rein by Hanumān, they have wrecked Madhuvana by
> force. They dragged us by our knees and flung us up into the sky."

---
18.
tato dadhimukhaḥ kruddho vanapas tatra vānaraḥ |
hataṁ madhuvanaṁ śrutvā sāntvayām āsa tān harīn ||

> **Meaning 18:** Then Dadhimukha, the Vānara guardian of the forest, was
> furious to hear that Madhuvana had been wrecked. He calmed those Haris down
> and said:

---
19.
etāgacchata gacchāmo vānarān atidarpitān |
balenāvārayiṣyāmo madhu bhakṣayato vayam ||

> **Meaning 19:** "Come, let's go. We will use force to stop these arrogant
> Vānaras who are eating the honey."

---
20.
śrutvā dadhimukhasyedaṁ vacanaṁ vānararṣabhāḥ |
punar vīrā madhuvanaṁ tenaiva sahitā yayuḥ ||

> **Meaning 20:** Hearing Dadhimukha's words, those heroic bulls among Vānaras
> went back to Madhuvana with him.

---
21.
madhye caiṣāṁ dadhimukhaḥ pragr̥hya sumahātarum |
samabhyadhāvad vegenā te ca sarve plavaṁgamāḥ ||

> **Meaning 21:** Dadhimukha, in their midst, picked up a huge tree and charged
> forward at full speed, and all those Plavaṅgamas charged with him.

---
22.
te śilāḥ pādapāṁś cāpi pāṣāṇāṁś cāpi vānarāḥ |
gr̥hītvābhyāgaman kruddhā yatra te kapikuñjarāḥ ||

> **Meaning 22:** The Vānaras seized rocks, trees, and boulders, and in their
> anger they advanced to where those Kapikuñjaras were.

---
23.
te svāmivacanaṁ vīrā hr̥dayeṣv avasajya tat |
tvarayā hy abhyadhāvanta sālatālaśilāyudhāḥ ||

> **Meaning 23:** Taking their master's command to heart, the heroes charged
> swiftly, armed with śāla trees, palmyra palms, and rocks.

---
24.
vr̥kṣasthāṁś ca talasthāṁś ca vānarān baladarpitān |
abhyakrāmanta te vīrāḥ pālās tatra sahasraśaḥ ||

> **Meaning 24:** Those heroic guards, in their thousands, attacked the Vānaras
> who were swollen with pride in their strength, whether perched in the trees or
> standing on the ground.

---
25.
atha dr̥ṣṭvā dadhimukhaṁ kruddhaṁ vānarapuṁgavāḥ |
abhyadhāvanta vegena hanūmatpramukhās tadā ||

> **Meaning 25:** Then the bulls among Vānaras, led by Hanumān, saw that
> Dadhimukha was angry and rushed at him at full speed.

---
26.
taṁ savr̥kṣaṁ mahābāhum āpatantaṁ mahābalam |
āryakaṁ prāharat tatra bāhubhyāṁ kupito ’ṅgadaḥ ||

> **Meaning 26:** As that mighty, strong-armed Vānara came charging with a tree
> in hand, Aṅgada lost his temper and struck his own elder with both arms.

---
27.
madāndhaś a na vedainam āryako ’yaṁ mameti saḥ |
athainaṁ niṣpipeṣāśu vegavad vasudhātale ||

> **Meaning 27:** Blinded by drink, Aṅgada did not recognize him as his own
> elder kinsman. He quickly and violently pinned him down and pounded him
> against the ground.

---
28.
sa bhagnabāhur vimukho vihvalaḥ śoṇitokṣitaḥ |
mumoha sahasā vīro muhūrtaṁ kapikuñjaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 28:** His arms broken, his face turned away, shaken and soaked in
> blood, that heroic Kapikuñjara suddenly fainted for a moment.

---
29.
sa samāśvasya sahasā saṁkr̥ddhō rājamātulaḥ |
vānarānvārayāmāsa daṁḍēna madhumōhitān ||

> **Meaning 29:** The king's uncle quickly came to, and in a rage he drove back
> the honey-dazed Vānaras with a staff.

---
30.
sa kathaṁ cid vimuktas tair vānarair vānararṣabhaḥ |
uvācaikāntam āgamya bhr̥tyāṁs tān samupāgatān ||

> **Meaning 30:** When that bull among Vānaras had somehow got free of the
> Vānaras, he went to a quiet spot and spoke to his servants who had gathered
> there:

---
31.
ete tiṣṭhantu gacchāmo bhartā no yatra vānaraḥ |
sugrīvo vipulagrīvaḥ saha rāmeṇa tiṣṭhati ||

> **Meaning 31:** "Let them stay here. We will go to where our master, the
> broad-necked Vānara Sugrīva, is staying with Rāma.

---
32.
sarvaṁ caivāṅgade doṣaṁ śrāvayiṣyāmi pārthiva |
amarṣī vacanaṁ śrutvā ghātayiṣyati vānarān ||

> **Meaning 32:** I will tell the king all about Aṅgada's offense. When he hears
> it, he will be furious and have these Vānaras killed.

---
33.
iṣṭaṁ madhuvanaṁ hy etat sugrīvasya mahātmanaḥ |
pitr̥paitāmahaṁ divyaṁ devair api durāsadam ||

> **Meaning 33:** This Madhuvana is dear to the great-souled Sugrīva. It is a
> divine forest, inherited from his father and grandfather, and even the devatās
> cannot easily enter it.

---
34.
sa vānarān imān sarvān madhulubdhān gatāyuṣaḥ |
ghātayiṣyati daṇḍena sugrīvaḥ sasuhr̥jjanān ||

> **Meaning 34:** Sugrīva will punish all these honey-greedy Vānaras — their
> lives are as good as over — and have them killed along with their friends.

---
35.
vadhyā hy ete durātmāno nr̥pājñā paribhāvinaḥ |
amarṣaprabhavo roṣaḥ saphalo no bhaviṣyati ||

> **Meaning 35:** These wicked ones deserve to die, since they have defied the
> king's command. Our anger, born of outrage, will not go unanswered."

---
36.
evam uktvā dadhimukho vanapālān mahābalaḥ |
jagāma sahasotpatya vanapālaiḥ samanvitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 36:** After saying this to the forest guards, the mighty Dadhimukha
> suddenly leapt up into the sky and set off with them.

---
37.
nimeṣāntaramātreṇa sa hi prāpto vanālayaḥ |
sahasrāṁśusuto dhīmān sugrīvo yatra vānaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 37:** In just the blink of an eye, that forest-dweller reached the
> place where the wise Vānara Sugrīva, son of the thousand-rayed Sun, was.

---
38.
rāmaṁ ca lakṣmaṇaṁ caiva dr̥ṣṭvā sugrīvam eva ca |
samapratiṣṭhāṁ jagatīm ākāśān nipapāta ha ||

> **Meaning 38:** Seeing Rāma, Lakṣmaṇa, and Sugrīva, he dropped from the sky
> onto the level ground.

---
39.
sa nipatya mahāvīryaḥ sarvais taiḥ parivāritaḥ |
harir dadhimukhaḥ pālaiḥ pālānāṁ parameśvaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 39:** Having landed, the mighty Hari Dadhimukha, supreme chief of
> the guards, stood surrounded by all those guards.

---
40.
sa dīnavadano bhūtvā kr̥tvā śirasi cāñjalim |
sugrīvasya śubhau mūrdhnā caraṇau pratyapīḍayat ||

> **Meaning 40:** With a sorrowful face and his palms joined above his head, he
> bowed his head down to Sugrīva's sacred feet.

---
