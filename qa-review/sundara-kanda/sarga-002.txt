=== Sarga 2 ===

---
1.
sa sāgaramanādhr̥ṣyam atikramya mahābalaḥ |
trikūṭaśikhare laṅkāṁ sthitāṁ svastho dadarśa ha ||

> **Meaning 1:** Having crossed the unassailable ocean, the immensely powerful
> hero stood composed and looked upon Laṅkā perched on the peak of Mount
> Trikūṭa.

---
2.
tataḥ pādapamuktena puṣpavarṣeṇa vīryavān |
abhivr̥ṣṭaḥ sthitastatra babhau puṣpamayo yathā ||

> **Meaning 2:** As the valiant one paused there, showered by a rain of blossoms
> shed by the trees, he shone as if formed entirely of flowers.

---
3.
yojanānāṁ śataṁ śrīmāṁstīrtvāpyuttamavikramaḥ |
aniśvasan kapistatra na glānimadhigacchati ||

> **Meaning 3:** Though the glorious Kapi of supreme prowess had traversed a
> hundred yojanas, he was not even out of breath, nor did he feel any fatigue.

---
4.
śatānyahaṁ yojanānāṁ krameyaṁ subahūnyapi |
kiṁ punaḥ sāgarasyāntaṁ saṁkhyātaṁ śatayojanam ||

> **Meaning 4:** "I could easily cross many hundreds of yojanas, so what is this
> ocean whose span measures a mere hundred yojanas?"

---
5.
sa tu vīryavatāṁ śreṣṭhaḥ plavatāmapi cottamaḥ |
jagāma vegavān laṅkāṁ laṅghayitvā mahodadhim ||

> **Meaning 5:** Foremost among the mighty and greatest of those who leap,
> endowed with tremendous speed, he pressed on toward Laṅkā after crossing the
> vast ocean.

---
6.
śādvalāni ca nīlāni gandhavanti vanāni ca |
gaṇḍavanti ca madhyena jagāma nagavanti ca ||

> **Meaning 6:** He passed through dark green meadows, fragrant woods, rocky
> foothills, and hill ranges.

---
7.
śailāṁśca tarusaṁchannān vanarājīśca puṣpitāḥ |
abhicakrāma tejasvī hanumān plavagarṣabhaḥ ||

> **Meaning 7:** The radiant Hanumān, bull among the Plavagas, strode across
> tree-covered hills and flowering forest groves.

---
8.
sa tasminn acale tiṣṭhan vanānyupavanāni ca |
sa nagāgre ca tāṁ laṅkāṁ dadarśa pavanātmajaḥ ||

> **Meaning 8:** Standing upon that mountain, the son of Pavana looked upon the
> forests, the pleasure gardens, and the city of Laṅkā perched on the mountain
> peak.

---
9.
saralān karṇikārāṁśca kharjūrāṁśca supuṣpitān |
priyālān muculindāṁśca kuṭajān ketakān api ||

> **Meaning 9:** He saw Sarala, Karṇikāra, and Kharjūra trees in full bloom,
> along with Priyāla, Muculinda, Kuṭaja, and Ketaka trees.

---
10.
priyaṅgūn gandhapūrṇāṁśca nīpān saptacchadāṁstathā |
asanān kovidārāṁśca karavīrāṁśca puṣpitān ||

> **Meaning 10:** He saw fragrant Priyaṅgu, Nīpa, and Saptacchada trees, as well
> as Asana, Kovidāra, and blossoming Karavīra trees.

---
11.
puṣpabhāranibaddhāṁśca tathā mukulitān api |
pādapān vihagākīrṇān pavanādhūtamastakān ||

> **Meaning 11:** The trees were heavy with loads of blossoms or covered with
> tender buds, crowded with birds, their crests swaying gently in the wind.

---
12.
haṁsakāraṇḍavākīrṇā vāpīḥ padmotpalāyutāḥ |
ākrīḍān vividhān ramyān vividhāṁśca jalāśayān ||

> **Meaning 12:** He saw stepped pools covered with lotuses and blue
> water-lilies, alive with swans and Kāraṇḍavas, alongside lovely pleasure
> grounds and various reservoirs of water.

---
13.
saṁtatān vividhair vr̥kṣaiḥ sarvartuphalapuṣpitaiḥ |
udyānāni ca ramyāṇi dadarśa kapikuñjaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 13:** The Kapikuñjara surveyed the charming gardens filled with
> diverse trees bearing flowers and fruits in all seasons.

---
14.
samāsādya ca lakṣmīvām̐llaṅkāṁ rāvaṇapālitām |
parikhābhiḥ sapadmābhiḥ sotpalābhiralaṁkr̥tām ||

> **Meaning 14:** Approaching Laṅkā, ruled by Rāvaṇa, the glorious hero saw it
> encircled by defensive moats adorned with white lotuses and blue water-lilies.

---
15.
sītāpaharaṇārthena rāvaṇena surakṣitām |
samantād vicaradbhiśca rākṣasairugradhanvibhiḥ ||

> **Meaning 15:** Because of his abduction of Sītā, Rāvaṇa had fortified it
> securely, with fierce Rākṣasa archers constantly patrolling on every side.

---
16.
kāñcanenāvr̥tāṁ ramyāṁ prākāreṇa mahāpurīm |
gr̥haiśca grahasaṁkāśaiḥ śāradāṁbudasannibhaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 16:** The beautiful great city was enclosed by a golden rampart and
> filled with sky-high mansions resembling heavenly bodies and pale autumn
> clouds.

---
17.
pāṁḍurābhiḥ pratōlībhiḥ uccābhirabhisaṁvr̥tām |
aṭṭālakaśatākīrṇāṁ patākādhvajamālinīm ||

> **Meaning 17:** It was lined with broad, gleaming white avenues, crowded with
> hundreds of watchtowers, and draped with rows of flags and banners.

---
18.
toraṇaiḥ kāñcanair divyair latāpaṅktivicitritaiḥ |
dadarśa hanumām̐llaṅkāṁ divi devapurīmiva ||

> **Meaning 18:** With its splendid golden gateways carved with rows of
> flowering creepers, Hanumān saw Laṅkā looking like the celestial city of the
> devatās in heaven.

---
19.
girimūrdhni sthitāṁ laṅkāṁ pāṇḍurair bhavanaiḥ śubhaiḥ |
dadarśa sa kapiḥ śrīmān puramākāśagaṁ yathā ||

> **Meaning 19:** Perched on the mountain crest with its gleaming white
> mansions, Laṅkā appeared to the glorious Kapi like a city suspended in the
> sky.

---
20.
pālitāṁ rākṣasendreṇa nirmitāṁ viśvakarmaṇā |
plavamānāmivākāśe dadarśa hanumān purīm ||

> **Meaning 20:** Built by Viśvakarmā and ruled by the king of the Rākṣasas, the
> city seemed to Hanumān as if floating in mid-air.

---
21.
vapraprākārajaghanāṁ vipulāṁbunavāṁbarām |
śataghnīśūlakēśāṁtāṁ aṭṭālakavataṁsakām ||

> **Meaning 21:** Its earthworks and ramparts were like hips, the vast
> surrounding waters like a new garment, its spiked catapults and tridents like
> locks of hair, and its watchtowers like ear-ornaments.

---
22.
manasēva kr̥tāṁ laṁkāṁ nirmitāṁ viśvakarmaṇā |
dvāramuttaramāsādya ciṁtayāmāsa vānaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 22:** Laṅkā seemed fashioned purely out of thought by Viśvakarmā;
> having reached its northern gate, the Vānara paused in contemplation.

---
23.
kailāsaśikharaprakhyāṁ ālikhaṁtīmivāṁbaram |
ḍīyamānāmivākāśaṁ ucchritairbhavanōttamaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 23:** Rising like the peak of Mount Kailāsa, scraping the firmament
> and soaring into the sky with its towering, magnificent palaces,

---
24.
saṁpūrṇāṁ rākṣasair ghorair nāgair bhogavatīmiva |
acintyāṁ sukr̥tāṁ spaṣṭāṁ kuberādhyuṣitāṁ purā ||

> **Meaning 24:** teeming with fierce Rākṣasas like Bhogavatī with venomous
> serpents, it was unimaginably grand, flawlessly built, beautifully planned,
> and once inhabited by Kubera.

---
25.
daṁṣṭribhir bahubhiḥ śūraiḥ śūlapaṭṭiśapāṇibhiḥ |
rakṣitāṁ rākṣasair ghoraiḥ guhāmāśīviṣairiva ||

> **Meaning 25:** It was guarded by numerous fierce and valiant fanged Rākṣasas
> bearing spears and battleaxes, like a cave guarded by deadly vipers.

---
26.
tasyāśca mahatīṁ guptiṁ sāgaraṁ ca nirīkṣya saḥ |
rāvaṇaṁ ca ripuṁ ghoraṁ cintayāmāsa vānaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 26:** Surveying its massive fortifications, the vast ocean, and the
> dread enemy Rāvaṇa, the Vānara thought to himself:

---
27.
āgatyāpīha harayo bhaviṣyanti nirarthakāḥ |
na hi yuddhena vai laṅkā śakyā jetuṁ surairapi ||

> **Meaning 27:** "Even if the Haris manage to reach here, their coming will be
> in vain, for Laṅkā cannot be conquered in battle even by the devatās.

---
28.
imāṁ tu viṣamāṁ durgāṁ laṅkāṁ rāvaṇapālitām |
prāpyāpi sa mahābāhuḥ kiṁ kariṣyati rāghavaḥ ||

> **Meaning 28:** Even if the mighty-armed Rāghava reaches this perilous,
> fortress-like city ruled by Rāvaṇa, what can he achieve?

---
29.
avakāśo na sāntvasya rākṣaseṣvabhigamyate |
na dānasya na bhedasya naiva yuddhasya dr̥śyate ||

> **Meaning 29:** Among these Rākṣasas there is no scope for gentle persuasion,
> no room for bribery, no possibility of sowing division, and no chance of
> victory through open war.

---
30.
caturṇāmeva hi gatir vānarāṇāṁ mahātmanām |
vāliputrasya nīlasya mama rājñaśca dhīmataḥ ||

> **Meaning 30:** Only four of the great Vānaras can make the crossing here:
> Vāli's son Aṅgada, Nīla, our wise king Sugrīva, and myself.

---
31.
yāvaj jānāmi vaidehīṁ yadi jīvati vā na vā |
tatraiva cintayiṣyāmi dr̥ṣṭvā tāṁ janakātmajām ||

> **Meaning 31:** First let me find out whether Vaidehī is still alive or not;
> once I have seen Janaka's daughter, I will consider what to do."

---
32.
tataḥ sa cintayāmāsa muhūrtaṁ kapikuñjaraḥ |
giriśr̥ṅge sthitastasmin rāmasyābhyudaye rataḥ ||

> **Meaning 32:** Standing upon that mountain peak, the Kapikuñjara, devoted to
> Rāma's welfare, reflected for a while:

---
33.
anena rūpeṇa mayā na śakyā rakṣasāṁ purī |
praveṣṭuṁ rākṣasair guptā krūrairbalasamanvitaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 33:** "In this present form, it is impossible for me to enter the
> city of the Rākṣasas, guarded by cruel and mighty demons.

---
34.
ugraujaso mahāvīryo balavantaśca rākṣasāḥ |
vañcanīyā mayā sarve jānakīṁ parimārgitā ||

> **Meaning 34:** These Rākṣasas possess fierce energy, immense prowess, and
> great physical strength; I must slip past all of them while searching for
> Jānakī.

---
35.
lakṣyālakṣyeṇa rūpeṇa rātrau laṅkā purī mayā |
praveṣṭuṁ prāptakālaṁ me kr̥tyaṁ sādhayituṁ mahat ||

> **Meaning 35:** To accomplish this great mission, the right moment has come
> for me to enter the city of Laṅkā by night in an unnoticeable form."

---
36.
tāṁ purīṁ tādr̥śīṁ dr̥ṣṭvā durādharṣāṁ surāsuraiḥ |
hanūmāṁścintayāmāsa viniḥśvasya muhurmuhuḥ ||

> **Meaning 36:** Looking upon that city, unassailable even to gods and demons,
> Hanumān sighed repeatedly and thought:

---
37.
kenopāyena paśyeyaṁ maithilīṁ janakātmajām |
adr̥ṣṭo rākṣasendreṇa rāvaṇena durātmanā ||

> **Meaning 37:** "By what means can I see Maithilī, Janaka's daughter, without
> being seen by the wicked Rāvaṇa, king of the Rākṣasas?

---
38.
na vinaśyet kathaṁ kāryaṁ rāmasya viditātmanaḥ |
ekāmekaśca paśyeyaṁ rahite janakātmajām ||

> **Meaning 38:** How can the mission of the wise Rāma remain safe from ruin?
> How can I meet Janaka's daughter alone in seclusion?

---
39.
bhūtāścārtho vipadyante deśakālavirodhitāḥ |
viklavaṁ dūtamāsādya tamaḥ sūryodaye yathā ||

> **Meaning 39:** Even well-conceived plans come to grief when undertaken out of
> season and place, or when handled by an indecisive envoy, just as darkness
> vanishes at sunrise.

---
40.
arthānarthāntare buddhir niścitāpi na śobhate |
ghātayanti hi kāryāṇi dūtāḥ paṇḍitamāninaḥ ||

> **Meaning 40:** Even a firm intellect falters in discerning between gain and
> loss when envoys merely fancy themselves clever; such messengers ruin their
> own missions.

---
41.
na vinaśyet kathaṁ kāryaṁ vaiklavyaṁ na kathaṁ bhavet |
laṅghanaṁ ca samudrasya kathaṁ nu na vr̥thā bhavet ||

> **Meaning 41:** How can this mission remain unharmed? How can I avoid making a
> blunder? And how can my leap across the ocean not go to waste?

---
42.
mayi dr̥ṣṭe tu rakṣobhī rāmasya viditātmanaḥ |
bhaved vyarthamidaṁ kāryaṁ rāvaṇānarthamicchataḥ ||

> **Meaning 42:** If the Rākṣasas catch sight of me, this mission of the wise
> Rāma, who seeks Rāvaṇa's destruction, will end in failure.

---
43.
na hi śakyaṁ kva cit sthātumavijñātena rākṣasaiḥ |
api rākṣasarūpeṇa kimutānyena kena cit ||

> **Meaning 43:** It is impossible to remain anywhere here unnoticed by the
> Rākṣasas, even in the disguise of a Rākṣasa — let alone in any other form!

---
44.
vāyurapyatra nājñātaścared iti matir mama |
na hyasty aviditaṁ kiṁ cid rākṣasānāṁ balīyasām ||

> **Meaning 44:** I think even the wind cannot move here undetected, for nothing
> escapes the notice of these formidable Rākṣasas.

---
45.
ihāhaṁ yadi tiṣṭhāmi svena rūpeṇa saṁvr̥taḥ |
vināśamupayāsyāmi bharturarthaśca hīyate ||

> **Meaning 45:** If I stay here in my natural form, I will meet destruction,
> and my master's objective will be lost.

---
46.
tad ahaṁ svena rūpeṇa rajanyāṁ hrasvatāṁ gataḥ |
laṅkāmabhipatiṣyāmi rāghavasyārthasiddhaye ||

> **Meaning 46:** Therefore, shrinking my form to a tiny size under cover of
> night, I will enter Laṅkā for the success of Rāghava's mission.

---
47.
rāvaṇasya purīṁ rātrau praviśya sudurāsadām |
vicinvan bhavanaṁ sarvaṁ drakṣyāmi janakātmajām ||

> **Meaning 47:** Slipping by night into this impenetrable city of Rāvaṇa, I
> will search through every mansion until I see Janaka's daughter."

---
48.
iti saṁcintya hanumān sūryasyāstamayaṁ kapiḥ |
ācakāṅkṣe tadā vīrā vaidehyā darśanotsukaḥ |

> **Meaning 48:** Having resolved on this, the heroic Kapi Hanumān, eager to see
> Vaidehī, waited for sunset.

---
49.
sūryē cāstaṁgatē rātrau dēhaṁ saṁkṣipya mārutiḥ |
pr̥ṣadaṁśakamātra ssan babhūvādbhutadarśanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 49:** When the sun had set and night fell, Māruti shrank his body to
> the size of a cat, presenting an astonishing sight.

---
50.
pradoṣakāle hanumāṁstūrṇamutpatya vīryavān |
praviveśa purīṁ ramyāṁ suvibhaktamahāpatham ||

> **Meaning 50:** At dusk, the valiant Hanumān leaped up swiftly and entered the
> charming city with its well-proportioned, broad avenues.

---
51.
prāsādamālāvitatāṁ stambhaiḥ kāñcanarājataiḥ |
śātakumbhamayair jālair gandharvanagaropamām ||

> **Meaning 51:** With its rows of mansions supported by pillars of gold and
> silver and adorned with golden lattice windows, it looked like a city of the
> Gandharvas.

---
52.
saptabhaumāṣṭabhaumaiśca sa dadarśa mahāpurīm |
talaiḥ sphāṭikasaṁpūrṇaiḥ kārtasvaravibhūṣitaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 52:** He saw that great city filled with seven- and eight-storied
> mansions whose floors were inlaid with crystal and embellished with gold.

---
53.
vaidūryamaṇicitraiśca muktājālavibhūṣitaiḥ |
talaiḥ śuśubhire tāni bhavanānyatra rakṣasām ||

> **Meaning 53:** The mansions of the Rākṣasas shone resplendent with floors
> inlaid with cat's-eye gems and decorated with networks of pearls.

---
54.
kāñcanāni vicitrāṇi toraṇāni ca rakṣasām |
laṅkāmuddyotayāmāsuḥ sarvataḥ samalaṁkr̥tām ||

> **Meaning 54:** The ornate golden gateway arches of the Rākṣasas illuminated
> the splendidly adorned city of Laṅkā on all sides.

---
55.
acintyāmadbhutākārāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā laṅkāṁ mahākapiḥ |
āsīd viṣaṇṇo hr̥ṣṭaśca vaidehyā darśanotsukaḥ ||

> **Meaning 55:** Seeing Laṅkā, unfathomable and wondrous in form, the Mahākapi
> felt both dejection and wonder, yet remained eager to find Vaidehī.

---
56.
sa pāṇḍurodviddhavimānamālinīṁ
 mahārhajāmbūnadajālatoraṇām |
yaśasvināṁ rāvaṇabāhupālitāṁ
  kṣapācarair bhīmabalaiḥ samāvr̥tām ||

> **Meaning 56:** He saw the glorious city ringed with towering white palaces,
> fitted with costly golden lattice-work arches, guarded by the mighty arms of
> Rāvaṇa, and held by night-roaming demons of terrifying strength.

---
57.
candro ’pi sācivyamivāsya kurvaṁs
 tārāgaṇair madhyagato virājan
jyotsnāvitānena vitatya lokam
 uttiṣṭhate naikasahasraraśmiḥ ||

> **Meaning 57:** As if offering him help, the moon rose with thousands of rays,
> spreading a canopy of moonlight over the earth and shining brightly in the
> midst of the star-strewn sky.

---
58.
śaṅkhaprabhaṁ kṣīramr̥ṇālavarṇam
 udgacchamānaṁ vyavabhāsamānam  |
dadarśa candraṁ sa kapipravīraḥ
 poplūyamānaṁ sarasīva haṁsaṁ ||

> **Meaning 58:** The heroic Kapipravīra saw the radiant moon ascending,
> gleaming like a white conch shell and pale as milk or lotus stalks, swimming
> through the heavens like a swan upon a lake.

---
