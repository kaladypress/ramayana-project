=== Sarga 2 ===

---
1.
sa sāgaram anādhr̥ṣyam atikramya mahābalaḥ |
trikūṭaśikhare laṅkāṁ sthitāṁ svastho dadarśa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tataḥ pādapamuktena puṣpavarṣeṇa vīryavān |
abhivr̥ṣṭaḥ sthitas tatra babhau puṣpamayo yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
yojanānāṁ śataṁ śrīmāṁs tīrtvāpy uttamavikramaḥ |
aniśvasan kapis tatra na glānim adhigacchati ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
śatāny ahaṁ yojanānāṁ krameyaṁ subahūny api |
kiṁ punaḥ sāgarasyāntaṁ saṁkhyātaṁ śatayojanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
sa tu vīryavatāṁ śreṣṭhaḥ plavatām api cottamaḥ |
jagāma vegavām̐l laṅkāṁ laṅghayitvā mahodadhim ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
śādvalāni ca nīlāni gandhavanti vanāni ca |
gaṇḍavanti ca madhyena jagāma nagavanti ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
śailāṁś ca tarusaṁchannān vanarājīś ca puṣpitāḥ |
abhicakrāma tejasvī hanumān plavagarṣabhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sa tasminn acale tiṣṭhan vanāny upavanāni ca |
sa nagāgre ca tāṁ laṅkāṁ dadarśa pavanātmajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
saralān karṇikārāṁś ca kharjūrāṁś ca supuṣpitān |
priyālān muculindāṁś ca kuṭajān ketakān api ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
priyaṅgūn gandhapūrṇāṁś ca nīpān saptacchadāṁs tathā |
asanān kovidārāṁś ca karavīrāṁś ca puṣpitān ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
puṣpabhāranibaddhāṁś ca tathā mukulitān api |
pādapān vihagākīrṇān pavanādhūtamastakān ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
haṁsakāraṇḍavākīrṇā vāpīḥ padmotpalāyutāḥ |
ākrīḍān vividhān ramyān vividhāṁś ca jalāśayān ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
saṁtatān vividhair vr̥kṣaiḥ sarvartuphalapuṣpitaiḥ |
udyānāni ca ramyāṇi dadarśa kapikuñjaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
samāsādya ca lakṣmīvām̐l laṅkāṁ rāvaṇapālitām |
parikhābhiḥ sapadmābhiḥ sotpalābhir alaṁkr̥tām ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sītāpaharaṇārthena rāvaṇena surakṣitām |
samantād vicaradbhiś ca rākṣasair ugradhanvibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
kāñcanenāvr̥tāṁ ramyāṁ prākāreṇa mahāpurīm |
aṭṭālakaśatākīrṇāṁ patākādhvajamālinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
toraṇaiḥ kāñcanair divyair latāpaṅktivicitritaiḥ |
dadarśa hanumām̐l laṅkāṁ divi devapurīm iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
girimūrdhni sthitāṁ laṅkāṁ pāṇḍurair bhavanaiḥ śubhaiḥ |
dadarśa sa kapiḥ śrīmān puram ākāśagaṁ yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
pālitāṁ rākṣasendreṇa nirmitāṁ viśvakarmaṇā |
plavamānām ivākāśe dadarśa hanumān purīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
saṁpūrṇāṁ rākṣasair ghorair nāgair bhogavatīm iva |
acintyāṁ sukr̥tāṁ spaṣṭāṁ kuberādhyuṣitāṁ purā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
daṁṣṭribhir bahubhiḥ śūraiḥ śūlapaṭṭiśapāṇibhiḥ |
rakṣitāṁ rākṣasair ghorair guhām āśīviṣair api ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
vapraprākārajaghanāṁ vipulāmbunavāmbarām |
śataghnīśūlakeśāntām aṭṭālakavataṁsakām ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
dvāram uttaram āsādya cintayām āsa vānaraḥ |
kailāsaśikharaprakhyam ālikhantam ivāmbaram |
dhriyamāṇam ivākāśam ucchritair bhavanottamaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tasyāś ca mahatīṁ guptiṁ sāgaraṁ ca nirīkṣya saḥ |
rāvaṇaṁ ca ripuṁ ghoraṁ cintayām āsa vānaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
āgatyāpīha harayo bhaviṣyanti nirarthakāḥ |
na hi yuddhena vai laṅkā śakyā jetuṁ surair api ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
imāṁ tu viṣamāṁ durgāṁ laṅkāṁ rāvaṇapālitām |
prāpyāpi sa mahābāhuḥ kiṁ kariṣyati rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
avakāśo na sāntvasya rākṣaseṣv abhigamyate |
na dānasya na bhedasya naiva yuddhasya dr̥śyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
caturṇām eva hi gatir vānarāṇāṁ mahātmanām |
vāliputrasya nīlasya mama rājñaś ca dhīmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
yāvaj jānāmi vaidehīṁ yadi jīvati vā na vā |
tatraiva cintayiṣyāmi dr̥ṣṭvā tāṁ janakātmajām ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
tataḥ sa cintayām āsa muhūrtaṁ kapikuñjaraḥ |
giriśr̥ṅge sthitas tasmin rāmasyābhyudaye rataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
anena rūpeṇa mayā na śakyā rakṣasāṁ purī |
praveṣṭuṁ rākṣasair guptā krūrair balasamanvitaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
ugraujaso mahāvīryo balavantaś ca rākṣasāḥ |
vañcanīyā mayā sarve jānakīṁ parimārgitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
lakṣyālakṣyeṇa rūpeṇa rātrau laṅkā purī mayā |
praveṣṭuṁ prāptakālaṁ me kr̥tyaṁ sādhayituṁ mahat ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
tāṁ purīṁ tādr̥śīṁ dr̥ṣṭvā durādharṣāṁ surāsuraiḥ |
hanūmāṁś cintayām āsa viniḥśvasya muhur muhuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
kenopāyena paśyeyaṁ maithilīṁ janakātmajām |
adr̥ṣṭo rākṣasendreṇa rāvaṇena durātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
na vinaśyet kathaṁ kāryaṁ rāmasya viditātmanaḥ |
ekām ekaś ca paśyeyaṁ rahite janakātmajām ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
bhūtāś cārtho vipadyante deśakālavirodhitāḥ |
viklavaṁ dūtam āsādya tamaḥ sūryodaye yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
arthānarthāntare buddhir niścitāpi na śobhate |
ghātayanti hi kāryāṇi dūtāḥ paṇḍitamāninaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
na vinaśyet kathaṁ kāryaṁ vaiklavyaṁ na kathaṁ bhavet |
laṅghanaṁ ca samudrasya kathaṁ nu na vr̥thā bhavet ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
mayi dr̥ṣṭe tu rakṣobhī rāmasya viditātmanaḥ |
bhaved vyartham idaṁ kāryaṁ rāvaṇānartham icchataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
na hi śakyaṁ kva cit sthātum avijñātena rākṣasaiḥ |
api rākṣasarūpeṇa kim utānyena kena cit ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
vāyur apy atra nājñātaś cared iti matir mama |
na hy asty aviditaṁ kiṁ cid rākṣasānāṁ balīyasām ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
ihāhaṁ yadi tiṣṭhāmi svena rūpeṇa saṁvr̥taḥ |
vināśam upayāsyāmi bhartur arthaś ca hīyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
tad ahaṁ svena rūpeṇa rajanyāṁ hrasvatāṁ gataḥ |
laṅkām abhipatiṣyāmi rāghavasyārthasiddhaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
rāvaṇasya purīṁ rātrau praviśya sudurāsadām |
vicinvan bhavanaṁ sarvaṁ drakṣyāmi janakātmajām ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
iti saṁcintya hanumān sūryasyāstamayaṁ kapiḥ |
ācakāṅkṣe tadā vīrā vaidehyā darśanotsukaḥ |
pr̥ṣadaṁśakamātraḥ san babhūvādbhutadarśanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
pradoṣakāle hanumāṁs tūrṇam utpatya vīryavān |
praviveśa purīṁ ramyāṁ suvibhaktamahāpatham ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
prāsādamālāvitatāṁ stambhaiḥ kāñcanarājataiḥ |
śātakumbhamayair jālair gandharvanagaropamām ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
saptabhaumāṣṭabhaumaiś ca sa dadarśa mahāpurīm |
talaiḥ sphāṭikasaṁpūrṇaiḥ kārtasvaravibhūṣitaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
vaidūryamaṇicitraiś ca muktājālavibhūṣitaiḥ |
talaiḥ śuśubhire tāni bhavanāny atra rakṣasām ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
kāñcanāni vicitrāṇi toraṇāni ca rakṣasām |
laṅkām uddyotayām āsuḥ sarvataḥ samalaṁkr̥tām ||

=== meaning ===

---
52.
acintyām adbhutākārāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā laṅkāṁ mahākapiḥ |
āsīd viṣaṇṇo hr̥ṣṭaś ca vaidehyā darśanotsukaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
53.
sa pāṇḍurodviddhavimānamālinīṁ; mahārhajāmbūnadajālatoraṇām |
yaśasvināṁ rāvaṇabāhupālitāṁ; kṣapācarair bhīmabalaiḥ samāvr̥tām ||

=== meaning ===

---
54.
candro ’pi sācivyam ivāsya kurvaṁs; tārāgaṇair madhyagato virājan |
jyotsnāvitānena vitatya lokam; uttiṣṭhate naikasahasraraśmiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
55.
śaṅkhaprabhaṁ kṣīramr̥ṇālavarṇam; udgacchamānaṁ vyavabhāsamānam |
dadarśa candraṁ sa kapipravīraḥ; poplūyamānaṁ sarasīva haṁsaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
