=== Sarga 65 ===

---
1.
tataḥ prasravaṇaṁ śailaṁ te gatvā citrakānanam |
praṇamya śirasā rāmaṁ lakṣmaṇaṁ ca mahābalam ||

> **Meaning 1:** Reaching Mount Prasravaṇa with its picturesque forests, they
> bowed their heads to Rāma and the mighty Lakṣmaṇa.

---
2.
yuvarājaṁ puraskr̥tya sugrīvam abhivādya ca |
pravr̥ttam atha sītāyāḥ pravaktum upacakramuḥ ||

> **Meaning 2:** Placing crown prince Aṅgada in front and saluting Sugrīva, they
> began to report the news of Sītā.

---
3.
rāvaṇāntaḥpure rodhaṁ rākṣasībhiś ca tarjanam |
rāme samanurāgaṁ ca yaś cāpi samayaḥ kr̥taḥ ||

> **Meaning 3:** They told of her confinement in Rāvaṇa's palace, the threats of
> the Rākṣasīs, her deep love for Rāma, and the time limit that had been set.

---
4.
etad ākhyānti te sarve harayo rāma saṁnidhau |
vaidehīm akṣatāṁ śrutvā rāmas tūttaram abravīt ||

> **Meaning 4:** As all those Haris recounted this in Rāma's presence, hearing
> that Vaidehī was unharmed, Rāma spoke in reply:

---
5.
kva sītā vartate devī kathaṁ ca mayi vartate |
etan me sarvam ākhyāta vaidehīṁ prati vānarāḥ ||

> **Meaning 5:** "Where is Devī Sītā living, and what are her feelings toward
> me? Vānaras, tell me everything about Vaidehī!"

---
6.
rāmasya gaditaṁ śrutva harayo rāmasaṁnidhau |
codayanti hanūmantaṁ sītāvr̥ttāntakovidam ||

> **Meaning 6:** Hearing Rāma's words, the Haris present before Rāma urged
> Hanumān forward, for he knew the full account of Sītā.

---
7.
śrutvā tu vacanaṁ teṣāṁ hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ |
praṇamya śirasā dēvyai sītāyai tāṃ diśaṃ prati | 
uvāca vākyaṁ vākyajñaḥ sītāyā darśanaṁ yathā ||

> **Meaning 7:** Hearing their words, Hanumān, the son of Māruta and master of
> speech, bowed his head in the direction of Devī Sītā and spoke, describing how
> he had found her.

---
8.
taṃ maṇiṃ kāñcanaṃ divyaṃ dīpyamānaṃ svatējasā|
dattvā rāmāya hanumāṃstataḥ prāñjalirabravīt||

> **Meaning 8:** Handing Rāma that divine golden jewel blazing with its own
> luster, Hanumān stood with joined palms and said:

---
9.
samudraṁ laṅghayitvāhaṁ śatayojanam āyatam |
agacchaṁ jānakīṁ sītāṁ mārgamāṇo didr̥kṣayā ||

> **Meaning 9:** "Leaping across the ocean a hundred yojanas wide, I went
> searching for Jānakī Sītā, longing to see her.

---
10.
tatra laṅketi nagarī rāvaṇasya durātmanaḥ |


> **Meaning 10:** There lies the city called Laṅkā, belonging to the wicked
> Rāvaṇa,

---
11.
dakṣiṇasya samudrasya tīre vasati dakṣiṇe |
tatra dr̥ṣṭā mayā sītā rāvaṇāntaḥpure satī ||


> **Meaning 11:** situated on the southern shore of the southern sea. There I
> saw the virtuous Sītā in Rāvaṇa's palace grounds.

---
12.
saṁnyasya tvayi jīvantī rāmā rāma manoratham |
dr̥ṣṭā me rākṣasī madhye tarjyamānā muhur muhuḥ ||


> **Meaning 12:** Rāma, that lovely lady lives only by centering all her
> thoughts on you; I saw her in the midst of the Rākṣasīs, constantly threatened
> by them.

---
13.
rākṣasībhir virūpābhī rakṣitā pramadāvane |
duḥkham āpadyate devī tavāduḥkhocitā satī ||


> **Meaning 13:** Guarded by hideous Rākṣasīs in the pleasure grove, your chaste
> devī, who never deserved any suffering, is undergoing great grief.

---
14.
rāvaṇāntaḥpure ruddhvā rākṣasībhiḥ surakṣitā |
ekaveṇīdharā dīnā tvayi cintāparāyaṇā ||


> **Meaning 14:** Confined in Rāvaṇa's palace grounds and closely watched by
> Rākṣasīs, she wears her hair in a single braid, sorrowful and entirely
> absorbed in thoughts of you.

---
15.
adhaḥśayyā vivarṇāṅgī padminīva himāgame |
rāvaṇād vinivr̥ttārthā martavyakr̥taniścayā ||


> **Meaning 15:** Sleeping on the bare ground, pale and withered like a lotus
> pond at the onset of winter frost, she spurns Rāvaṇa completely and is
> resolved to die.

---
16.
devī kathaṁ cit kākutstha tvanmanā mārgitā mayā |
ikṣvākuvaṁśavikhyātiṁ śanaiḥ kīrtayatānagha ||


> **Meaning 16:** Faultless Kākutstha, keeping her mind on you, that devī was
> found by me with great effort as I softly sang the renowned praises of the
> Ikṣvāku dynasty.

---
17.
sa mayā naraśārdūla viśvāsam upapāditā |
tataḥ saṁbhāṣitā devī sarvam arthaṁ ca darśitā ||


> **Meaning 17:** Tiger among men, I won her confidence, and then spoke with the
> devī and explained the entire situation to her.

---
18.
rāmasugrīvasakhyaṁ ca śrutvā prītim upāgatā |
niyataḥ samudācāro bhaktiś cāsyās tathā tvayi ||

> **Meaning 18:** Hearing of the friendship between Rāma and Sugrīva, she was
> overjoyed. Her conduct remains steadfast, and her devotion to you is
> unwavering.

---
19.
evaṁ mayā mahābhāgā dr̥ṣṭā janakanandinī |
ugreṇa tapasā yuktā tvadbhaktyā puruṣarṣabha ||

> **Meaning 19:** Foremost of men, thus did I see the illustrious daughter of
> Janaka, engaged in severe austerity and wholly devoted to you.

---
20.
abhijñānaṁ ca me dattaṁ yathāvr̥ttaṁ tavāntike |
citrakūṭe mahāprājña vāyasaṁ prati rāghava ||

> **Meaning 20:** Wise Rāghava, she gave me a token of recognition — the
> incident with the crow that happened in your presence at Citrakūṭa.

---
21.
vijñāpyaś ca nara vyāghro rāmo vāyusuta tvayā |
akhileneha yad dr̥ṣṭam iti mām āha jānakī ||

> **Meaning 21:** Jānakī said to me: 'Son of Vāyu, you must report everything
> you have seen here to Rāma, that tiger among men.'

---
22.
ayaṁcāsmai pradātavyaṁ yatnāt suparirakṣitam |
bruvatā vacanāny evaṁ sugrīvasyopaśr̥ṇvataḥ ||

> **Meaning 22:** And while speaking these words within Sugrīva's hearing, she
> added: 'This jewel, preserved with great care, must be given to him.'

---
23.
eṣa cūḍāmaṇiḥ śrīmān mayā te yatnarakṣitaḥ |
manaḥśilāyās tikalas taṁ smarasveti cābravīt ||

> **Meaning 23:** She said, 'This splendid crest jewel has been carefully
> guarded by me for you. And remember the tilaka of red arsenic paste you drew
> on my forehead.'

---
24.
eṣa niryātitaḥ śrīmān mayā te vārisaṁbhavaḥ |
etaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā pramodiṣye vyasane tvām ivānagha ||

> **Meaning 24:** 'Faultless one, this splendid gem born of the sea is now sent
> to you by me. In my sorrow, seeing it used to bring me joy, as if I were
> seeing you.'

---
25.
jīvitaṁ dhārayiṣyāmi māsaṁ daśarathātmaja |
ūrdhvaṁ māsān na jīveyaṁ rakṣasāṁ vaśam āgatā ||

> **Meaning 25:** 'Son of Daśaratha, I will hold on to my life for one month
> more; fallen into the clutches of the Rākṣasas, I cannot survive beyond a
> month.'

---
26.
iti mām abravīt sītā kr̥śāṅgī dharma cāriṇī |
rāvaṇāntaḥpure ruddhā mr̥gīvotphullalocanā ||

> **Meaning 26:** So Sītā spoke to me — slender, walking the path of dharma,
> confined in Rāvaṇa's palace grounds, her wide eyes like those of a frightened
> doe.

---
27.
etad eva mayākhyātaṁ sarvaṁ rāghava yad yathā |
sarvathā sāgarajale saṁtāraḥ pravidhīyatām ||

> **Meaning 27:** Rāghava, I have reported all this to you exactly as it took
> place. Now, by all means, let arrangements be made to cross the waters of the
> ocean."

---
28.
tau jātāśvāsau rājaputrau viditvā; tac cābhijñānaṁ rāghavāya pradāya |
devyā cākhyātaṁ sarvam evānupūrvyād; vācā saṁpūrṇaṁ vāyuputraḥ śaśaṁsa ||

> **Meaning 28:** Seeing that the two princes were comforted, and having handed
> that token of recognition to Rāghava, the son of Vāyu related in full speech
> everything the devī had said, in exact sequence.

---
