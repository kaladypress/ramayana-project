=== Sarga 27 ===

---
1.
ity uktāḥ sītayā ghoraṁ rākṣasyaḥ krodhamūrchitāḥ |
kāścij jagmustad ākhyātuṁ rāvaṇasya tarasvinaḥ ||

> **Meaning 1:** Having been spoken to thus in fearsome words
> by Sītā, some of the Rākṣasīs, intoxicated with rage,
> departed to report her defiance to the mighty Rāvaṇa.

---
2.
tataḥ sītām upāgamya rākṣasyo ghoradarśanāḥ |
punaḥ paruṣam ekārtham anarthārtham athābruvan ||

3.
hantedānīṁ tavānārye sīte pāpaviniścaye |
rākṣasyo bhakṣayiṣyanti māṁsam etad yathāsukham ||

> **Meaning 2-3:** Then the terrifying Rākṣasīs approached Sītā again and
> spoke harsh words aimed solely at her ruin: "Well then, you wretched and
> wicked-minded Sītā! The Rākṣasīs will now feast on your flesh however they
> please!"

---
4.
sītāṁ tābhiranāryābhiḥ dr̥ṣṭvā saṁtarjitāṁ tadā |
rākṣasī trijaṭāvr̥ddhā śayānā vākyam abravīt ||

> **Meaning 4:** Seeing Sītā being thus threatened by those
> ignoble Rākṣasīs, the aged Rākṣasī Trijaṭā, who had been
> lying down, spoke these words:

---
5.
ātmānaṁ khādatānāryā na sītāṁ bhakṣayiṣyatha |
janakasya sutām iṣṭāṁ snuṣāṁ daśarathasya ca ||

> **Meaning 5:** "Devour yourselves, O ignoble ones! You
> shall not consume Sītā — the beloved daughter of Janaka
> and the daughter-in-law of Daśaratha!"

---
6.
svapnaḥ hi adya mayā dr̥ṣṭaḥ dāruṇo romaharṣaṇaḥ |
rākṣasānām abhāvāya bharturasyā bhavāya ca ||

> **Meaning 6:** "For I have seen a dream today — dreadful
> and hair-raising — portending the destruction of the
> Rākṣasas and the triumph of her husband!"

---
7.
evam uktāstrijaṭayā rākṣasyaḥ krodhamūrchitāḥ |
sarvā evābruvan bhītāḥ trijaṭāṁ tām idaṁ vacaḥ ||

> **Meaning 7:** Hearing Trijaṭā speak thus, all those
> Rākṣasīs — though intoxicated with anger — became
> frightened and addressed her with these words:

---
8.
kathayasva tvayā dr̥ṣṭaḥ svapne ayaṁ kīdr̥śo niśi |
tāsāṁ śrutvā tu vacanaṁ rākṣasīnāṁ mukhodgatam ||

9.
uvāca vacanaṁ kāle trijaṭāsvapnasaṁśritam |
gajadantamayīṁ divyāṁ śibikām antarikṣagām ||

10.
yuktāṁ vājisahasreṇa svayam āsthāya rāghavaḥ |
śuklamālyāṁbaradharō lakṣmaṇēna sahāgataḥ ||

> **Meaning 8-10:** "Tell us about this dream you had tonight — what was it
> like?" Hearing the Rākṣasīs' words, Trijaṭā told them about the dream she had
> seen at dawn: "I saw Rāghava wearing white garlands and white garments. He had
> mounted a divine ivory palanquin that flies through the sky, drawn by a
> thousand horses, and Lakṣmaṇa was with him."

---
11.
svapne cādya mayā dr̥ṣṭā sītā śuklāmbarāvr̥tā |
sāgareṇa parikṣiptaṁ śvetaparvatam āsthitā |
rāmeṇa saṁgatā sītā bhāskareṇa prabhā yathā ||

> **Meaning 11:** "In my dream today, I saw Sītā dressed in white garments,
> seated on a white mountain surrounded by the ocean. She was united with Rāma,
> as inseparable from him as sunlight is from the sun."

---
12.
rāghavaśca mayā dr̥ṣṭaḥ caturdantaṁ mahāgajam ||

13.
ārūḍhaḥ śailasaṁkāśaṁ cacāra sahalakṣmaṇaḥ |
tatastau naraśārdūlau dīpyamānau svatejasā ||

14.
śuklamālyāmbaradharau jānakīṁ paryupasthitau |
tatastasya nagasyāgre ākāśasthasya dantinaḥ ||

15.
bhartrā parigr̥hītasya jānakī skandham āśritā |
bharturaṅkāt samutpatya tataḥ kamalalocanā ||

16.
candrasūryau mayā dr̥ṣṭā pāṇibhyāṁ parimārjatī |
tatastābhyāṁ kumārābhyām āsthitaḥ sa gajottamaḥ |
sītayā ca viśālākṣyā laṅkāyā upari sthitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 12-16:** "I saw Rāghava, together with Lakṣmaṇa,
> mounted upon a great four-tusked elephant, massive as a
> mountain, roaming about. Then those two tigers among men,
> blazing with their own splendour, wearing white garlands
> and garments, approached Jānakī. Upon the summit of that
> elephant, hovering in the sky, held by her husband, Jānakī
> took her place on its shoulder. Then, leaping from her
> husband's lap, I saw the lotus-eyed one touching the sun
> and the moon with her two hands. That supreme elephant,
> mounted by those two princes and wide-eyed Sītā, stood
> poised above Laṅkā."

---
17.
pāṇḍurarṣabhayuktena rathenāṣṭayujā svayam |
ihōpayātaḥ kākutsthaḥ sītayā saha bhāryayā ||

18.
śuklamālyāmbaradharaḥ lakṣmaṇena samāgataḥ |
lakṣmaṇena saha bhrātrā sītayā saha bhāryayā ||

> **Meaning 17-18:** "Rāma, son of Raghu, himself came here in a
> chariot yoked to eight white bulls, together with his wife
> Sītā. Wearing white garlands and garments, joined by his
> brother Lakṣmaṇa, he arrived with Lakṣmaṇa and with
> his wife Sītā."

---
19.
lakṣmaṇēna saha bhrātrā sītayā saha vīryavān |
āruhya puṣpakaṁ divyaṁ vimānaṁ sūryasannibham ||

20.
uttarāṁ diśamālōkya jagāma puruṣōttamaḥ |
ēvaṁ svapnē mayā dr̥ṣṭō rāmō viṣṇuparākramaḥ |
lakṣmaṇēna saha bhrātrā sītayā saha bhāryayā || 20 ||

21.
na hi rāmō mahātējāḥ śakyō jētuṁ surāsuraiḥ |
rākṣasairvāpi cānyairvā svargaḥ pāpajanairiva || 21 ||

> **Meaning 19-21:** "The valiant one, together with his
> brother Lakṣmaṇa and his wife Sītā, ascended the divine
> Puṣpaka Vimāna, resplendent as the sun, and gazing toward
> the northern direction, Puruṣōttama departed. Thus in my
> dream I beheld Rāma, whose prowess equals that of Viṣṇu,
> together with his brother Lakṣmaṇa and his wife Sītā.
> Indeed, the supremely radiant Rāma cannot be conquered by
> Suras or Asuras, by Rākṣasas or any others — just as
> Svarga cannot be attained by the sinful."

---
22.
rāvaṇaśca mayā dr̥ṣṭaḥ kṣitau tailasamukṣitaḥ ||

23.
raktavāsāḥ pibanmattaḥ karavīrakr̥tasrajaḥ |
vimānāt puṣpakād adya rāvaṇaḥ patito bhuvi ||

> **Meaning 22-23:** "I also saw Rāvaṇa, his body smeared
> with oil, lying upon the ground. Clad in red garments,
> drinking and intoxicated, wearing garlands of Karavīra
> flowers — Rāvaṇa had fallen from the Puṣpaka Vimāna
> to the earth."

---
24.
kr̥ṣyapāṇaḥ striyā dr̥ṣṭaḥ muṇḍaḥ kr̥ṣṇāmbaraḥ punaḥ |
rathena kharayuktena raktamālyānulepanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 24:** "Then again he was seen being dragged by a
> woman, his head shaven, clad in black garments, mounted
> upon a chariot yoked to donkeys, wearing red garlands and
> anointed with red unguents."

---
25.
pibaṁstailaṁ hasan nr̥tyan bhrāṁtacittākulēṁdriyaḥ |
gardabhēna yayau śīghraṁ dakṣiṇāṁ diśamāsthitaḥ ||

26.
punarēva mayā dr̥ṣṭō rāvaṇaḥ rākṣasēśvaraḥ |
patitaḥ avākchirā bhūmau gardabhādbhayamōhitaḥ ||

27.
sahasōtthāya saṁbhrāṁtō bhayārtaḥ madavihvalaḥ |
unmatta iva digvāsāḥ durvākyaṁ pralapan bahu ||

28.
durgaṁdhaṁ dussahaṁ ghōraṁ timiraṁ narakōpamam |
malapaṁkaṁ praviśyāśu magnastatra sa rāvaṇaḥ ||

29.
prasthitaḥ dakṣiṇām āśāṁ praviṣṭaḥ kardamaṁ hradam |
kaṇṭhe baddhvā daśagrīvaṁ pramadā raktavāsinī ||

30.
kālī kardamaliptāṅgī diśaṁ yāmyāṁ prakarṣati |
ēvaṁ tatra mayā dr̥ṣṭaḥ kuṁbhakarṇō niśācaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 25-30:** "Drinking oil, laughing, dancing — his
> mind deranged, his senses in disarray — he swiftly rode
> a donkey toward the southern direction. Again I saw
> Rāvaṇa, the Rākṣaseśvara, fallen headlong to the earth,
> bewildered with terror from the donkey. Rising suddenly,
> agitated, stricken with fear, reeling with intoxication,
> like a madman, clad in nothing, babbling foul words —
> he quickly entered a foul-smelling, unbearable, dreadful,
> dark pit of filth, resembling Naraka, and sank into it.
> Having set out toward the southern direction, he entered a
> muddy pool. A woman clad in red, dark-complexioned, her
> body smeared with mud, binding Daśagrīva by the neck, was
> dragging him toward the direction of Yama. Thus was
> Kumbhakarṇa, the night-stalker, also seen by me there."

---
31.
rāvaṇasya sutāssarvē dr̥ṣṭāstaila samukṣitāḥ |
varāheṇa daśagrīvaḥ śiṁśumāreṇa cendrajit |
uṣṭreṇa kumbhakarṇaśca prayāto dakṣiṇāṁ diśam ||

> **Meaning 31:** "All the sons of Rāvaṇa were seen smeared
> with oil. Daśagrīva upon a boar, Indrajit upon a
> crocodile, and Kumbhakarṇa upon a camel — all heading
> toward the southern direction."

---
32.
ēkastatra mayā dr̥ṣṭaḥ śvētacchatrō vibhīṣaṇaḥ ||

33.
śuklamālyāṁbaradharaḥ śuklagaṁdhānulēpanaḥ |
śaṁkhaduṁdhubhinirghōṣaiḥ nr̥ttagītairalaṁkr̥taḥ ||

34.
āruhya śailasaṁkāśaṁ mēghastanitanissvanam |
caturdaṁtaṁ gajaṁ divyaṁ āstē tatra vibhīṣaṇaḥ ||

35.
caturbhi ssacivaissārthaṁ vaihāyasamupasthitaḥ ||

36.
samājaśca mahān vr̥ttaḥ gītavāditraniḥsvanaḥ |
pibatāṁ raktamālyānāṁ rakṣasāṁ raktavāsasām ||

> **Meaning 32-36:** "But one alone was seen by me there —
> Vibhīṣaṇa, under a white parasol. Wearing white garlands
> and garments, anointed with white sandal paste, adorned
> with the resounding of conches and drums and with dance and
> song — Vibhīṣaṇa sat upon a divine four-tusked elephant,
> massive as a mountain, thundering like a cloud. Accompanied
> by four ministers, he hovered in the sky. A grand assembly
> had gathered, resounding with music and instruments, of
> Rākṣasas wearing red garlands and red garments, drinking."

---
37.
laṅkā ceyaṁ purī ramyā savājirathasaṁkulā |
sāgare patitā dr̥ṣṭā bhagnagopuratoraṇā ||

> **Meaning 37:** "This beautiful city of Laṅkā, teeming
> with horses and chariots, was seen fallen into the ocean,
> its gateways and arches shattered."

---
38.
laṁkā dr̥ṣṭā mayā svapnē rāvaṇēnābhirakṣitā |
dagdhā rāmasya dūtēna vānarēṇa tarasvinā ||

39.
pītva tailaṁ pranr̥ttāśca prahasantyo mahāsvanāḥ |
laṅkāyāṁ bhasmarūkṣāyāṁ sarvā rākṣasayoṣitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 38-39:** "In my dream, I saw the Laṅkā
> protected by Rāvaṇa — set ablaze by a swift Vānara. In that Laṅkā, reduced to rough
> ashes, all the Rākṣasa women had drunk oil and were
> dancing wildly, laughing aloud with terrible cries."

---
40.
kumbhakarṇādayaśceme sarve rākṣasapuṁgavāḥ |
raktaṁ nivasanaṁ gr̥hya praviṣṭā gomayahrade ||

> **Meaning 40:** "All the foremost Rākṣasas — Kumbhakarṇa
> and the rest — wearing red garments, had entered a pool
> of cow dung."

---
41.
apagacchata naśyadhvaṁ sītām āpnoti rāghavaḥ |
ghātayet paramāmarṣī sarvaiḥ sārdhaṁ hi rākṣasaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 41:** "Begone! Perish! Rāghava shall recover
> Sītā. That supremely wrathful one will slay all the
> Rākṣasas together."

---
42.
priyāṁ bahumatāṁ bhāryāṁ vanavāsam anuvratām |
bhartsitāṁ tarjitāṁ vāpi nānumaṁsyati rāghavaḥ ||

> **Meaning 42:** "His beloved and highly esteemed wife, who
> followed him loyally into the forest exile — whether
> she is rebuked or threatened — Rāghava will never
> forgive it."

---
43.
tad alaṁ krūravākyairvaḥ sāntvam evābhidhīyatām |
abhiyācāma vaidehīm etad dhi mama rocate ||

> **Meaning 43:** "Therefore, enough of your cruel words! Let
> only soothing words be spoken. Let us entreat Vaidehī —
> this is what I advise."

---
44.
yasyā hy evaṁ vidhaḥ svapnaḥ duḥkhitāyāḥ pradr̥śyate |
sā duḥkhairbahubhirmuktā priyaṁ prāpnoty anuttamam ||

> **Meaning 44:** "When a dream like this is seen about a woman in sorrow, she will be freed from all her suffering and attain supreme happiness."

---
45.
bhartsitām api yācadhvaṁ rākṣasyaḥ kiṁ vivakṣayā |
rāghavād dhi bhayaṁ ghoraṁ rākṣasānām upasthitam ||

> **Meaning 45:** "Even though she has been threatened,
> entreat her, O Rākṣasīs — what more is there to say?
> For a terrible fear from Rāghava looms over all the
> Rākṣasas."

---
46.
praṇipāta prasannā hi maithilī janakātmajā |
alam eṣā paritrātuṁ rākṣasyo mahato bhayāt ||

> **Meaning 46:** "Maithilī, the daughter of Janaka, is
> gracious when approached with humility. She alone is
> capable of saving us Rākṣasīs from that great terror."

---
47.
api cāsyā viśālākṣyā na kiṁ cid upalakṣaye |
viruddham api cāṅgeṣu susūkṣmam api lakṣmaṇam ||

> **Meaning 47:** "Moreover, I do not see even the slightest inauspicious mark
> anywhere on the body of this wide-eyed princess."

---
48.
chāyā vaiguṇya mātraṁ tu śaṅke duḥkham upasthitam |
aduḥkhārhām imāṁ devīṁ vaihāyasam upasthitām ||

> **Meaning 48:** "I suspect her suffering is merely a passing
> shadow of ill fortune. This divine lady, who does not
> deserve grief, is destined for an exalted state."

---
49.
arthasiddhiṁ tu vaidehyāḥ paśyāmy aham upasthitām |
rākṣasendravināśaṁ ca vijayaṁ rāghavasya ca ||

> **Meaning 49:** "I foresee the fulfilment of Vaidehī's
> purpose drawing near — along with the destruction of the
> Rākṣasendra and the victory of Rāghava."

---
50.
nimittabhūtam etat tu śrotum asyā mahat priyam |
dr̥śyate ca sphurac cakṣuḥ padmapatram ivāyatam ||

> **Meaning 50:** "This is an omen indicating that she will
> soon hear tidings of great joy. See how her eye, broad as
> a lotus petal, is quivering!"

---
51.
īṣac ca hr̥ṣito vāsyā dakṣiṇāyā hy adakṣiṇaḥ |
akasmād eva vaidehyā bāhurekaḥ prakampate ||

52.
kareṇuhastapratimaḥ savyaścoruranuttamaḥ |
vepan sūcayatīvāsyā rāghavaṁ purataḥ sthitam ||

> **Meaning 51-52:** "The inauspicious right side of her
> garment is slightly ruffled. Suddenly, one arm of
> Vaidehī's trembles of its own accord. And her left thigh,
> exquisite as the trunk of a she-elephant — quivering, it
> seems to herald that Rāghava stands before her."

---
53.
pakṣī ca śākhā nilayaṁ praviṣṭaḥ punaḥ punaścottamasāntvavādī |
sukhāgatāṁ vācam udīrayāṇaḥ punaḥ punaścodayatīva hr̥ṣṭaḥ ||

> **Meaning 53:** "A bird has entered its nest on a branch and is singing sweetly
> again and again. It seems to comfort her and joyously urge her on, as if to
> say, 'Sītā, do not fear! Rāma is right before you—speak words of welcome to
> him!'"

---

54.
tatassā hrīmatī bālā bharturvijayaharṣitā |
avōcadyadi tattathyaṁ bhavēyaṁ śaraṇaṁ hi vaḥ ||

> **Meaning 54:** Then Sītā, that modest young woman, elated at the prospect of
> her husband's victory, spoke: "If this dream proves true, I will indeed be a
> refuge for all of you."

---
