=== Sarga 64 ===

---
1.
evam ukto hanumatā rāmo daśarathātmajaḥ |
taṁ maṇiṁ hr̥daye kr̥tvā praruroda salakṣmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
taṁ tu dr̥ṣṭvā maṇiśreṣṭhaṁ rāghavaḥ śokakarśitaḥ |
netrābhyām aśrupūrṇābhyāṁ sugrīvam idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
yathaiva dhenuḥ sravati snehād vatsasya vatsalā |
tathā mamāpi hr̥dayaṁ maṇiratnasya darśanāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
maṇiratnam idaṁ dattaṁ vaidehyāḥ śvaśureṇa me |
vadhūkāle yathā baddham adhikaṁ mūrdhni śobhate ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
ayaṁ hi jalasaṁbhūto maṇiḥ pravarapūjitaḥ |
yajñe paramatuṣṭena dattaḥ śakreṇa dhīmatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
imaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā maṇiśreṣṭhaṁ tathā tātasya darśanam |
adyāsmy avagataḥ saumya vaidehasya tathā vibhoḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
ayaṁ hi śobhate tasyāḥ priyāyā mūrdhni me maṇiḥ |
adyāsya darśanenāhaṁ prāptāṁ tām iva cintaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
kim āha sītā vaidehī brūhi saumya punaḥ punaḥ |
parāsum iva toyena siñcantī vākyavāriṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
itas tu kiṁ duḥkhataraṁ yad imaṁ vārisaṁbhavam |
maṇiṁ paśyāmi saumitre vaidehīm āgataṁ vinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
ciraṁ jīvati vaidehī yadi māsaṁ dhariṣyati |
kṣaṇaṁ saumya na jīveyaṁ vinā tām asitekṣaṇām ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
naya mām api taṁ deśaṁ yatra dr̥ṣṭā mama priyā |
na tiṣṭheyaṁ kṣaṇam api pravr̥ttim upalabhya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
kathaṁ sā mama suśroṇi bhīru bhīruḥ satī tadā |
bhayāvahānāṁ ghorāṇāṁ madhye tiṣṭhati rakṣasām ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
śāradas timironmukho nūnaṁ candra ivāmbudaiḥ |
āvr̥taṁ vadanaṁ tasyā na virājati rākṣasaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
kim āha sītā hanumaṁs tattvataḥ kathayasva me |
etena khalu jīviṣye bheṣajenāturo yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
madhurā madhurālāpā kim āha mama bhāminī |
madvihīnā varārohā hanuman kathayasva me |
duḥkhād duḥkhataraṁ prāpya kathaṁ jīvati jānakī ||

=== meaning ===

---
