=== Sarga 64 ===

---
1.
sugrīveṇaivam uktas tu hr̥ṣṭo dadhimukhaḥ kapiḥ |
rāghavaṁ lakṣmaṇaṁ caiva sugrīvaṁ cābhyavādayat ||

> **Meaning 1:** Addressed thus by Sugrīva, the Kapi Dadhimukha rejoiced and
> bowed in salutation to Rāghava, Lakṣmaṇa, and Sugrīva.

---
2.
sa praṇamya ca sugrīvaṁ rāghavau ca mahābalau |
vānaraiḥ sahitaiḥ śūrair divam evotpapāta ha ||

> **Meaning 2:** Bowing to Sugrīva and the two mighty descendants of Raghu, he
> leapt straight into the sky along with the heroic Vānara guards.

---
3.
sa yathaivāgataḥ pūrvaṁ tathaiva tvarito gataḥ |
nipatya gaganād bhūmau tad vanaṁ praviveśa ha ||

> **Meaning 3:** Returning just as swiftly as he had come, he dropped down from
> the sky to the earth and entered that forest.

---
4.
sa praviṣṭo madhuvanaṁ dadarśa hariyūthapān |
vimadān uddhatān sarvān mehamānān madhūdakam ||

> **Meaning 4:** Entering Madhuvana, he saw the Hari troop leaders — sobered
> from intoxication, brimming with high spirits, discharging the honey-water
> they had drunk.

---
5.
sa tān upāgamad vīro baddhvā karapuṭāñjalim |
uvāca vacanaṁ ślakṣṇam idaṁ hr̥ṣṭavad aṅgadam ||

> **Meaning 5:** Approaching them, that hero joined his hands in reverence and
> addressed Aṅgada with these courteous and cheerful words:

---
6.
saumya roṣo na kartavyo yad ebhir abhivāritaḥ |
ajñānād rakṣibhiḥ krodhād bhavantaḥ pratiṣedhitāḥ ||

> **Meaning 6:** "Gentle prince, do not be angry that these guards tried to stop
> you; out of ignorance and hot temper, they stood in your way.

---
7.
śrāntō dūrādanuprāptō bhakṣayasva svakaṃ madhu|
yuvarājas tvam īśaś ca vanasyāsya mahābala ||

> **Meaning 7:** You arrived exhausted from afar — enjoy your own honey! Mighty
> prince, you are the crown prince and master of this forest.

---
8.
maurkhyāt pūrvaṁ kr̥to doṣas tad bhavān kṣantum arhati |
yathaiva hi pitā te ’bhūt pūrvaṁ harigaṇeśvaraḥ ||


> **Meaning 8:** You should forgive the mistake made earlier out of foolishness.
> For just as your father was formerly the ruler of the Hari host,

---
9.
tathā tvam api sugrīvo nānyas tu harisattama |
ākhyātaṁ hi mayā gatvā pitr̥vyasya tavānagha ||


> **Meaning 9:** so too are you, and so is Sugrīva — there is no difference,
> best among Haris. Faultless one, I went and reported everything to your uncle,

---
10.
ihopayānaṁ sarveṣām eteṣāṁ vanacāriṇām |
sa tvadāgamanaṁ śrutvā sahaibhir hariyūthapaiḥ ||


> **Meaning 10:** telling him of the arrival here of all these forest-dwellers.
> When he heard of your arrival along with these Hari troop leaders,

---
11.
prahr̥ṣṭo na tu ruṣṭo ’sau vanaṁ śrutvā pradharṣitam |
prahr̥ṣṭo māṁ pitr̥vyas te sugrīvo vānareśvaraḥ ||


> **Meaning 11:** your uncle Sugrīva, king of the Vānaras, was not angry at all
> to hear that the forest was plundered; instead, he was overjoyed, and in his
> joy

---
12.
śīghraṁ preṣaya sarvāṁs tān iti hovāca pārthivaḥ |
śrutvā dadhimukhasyaitad vacanaṁ ślakṣṇam aṅgadaḥ ||


> **Meaning 12:** the king told me, 'Send them all here at once!' Hearing these
> courteous words from Dadhimukha, Aṅgada,

---
13.
abravīt tān hariśreṣṭho vākyaṁ vākyaviśāradaḥ |
śaṅke śruto ’yaṁ vr̥ttānto rāmeṇa hariyūthapāḥ ||


> **Meaning 13:** the best among Haris and skilled in speech, said to them:
> 'Hari troop leaders, I believe Rāma has heard of our news.

---
14.
ayaṃ ca harṣaḥ prakhyāti tēna jānāmi hētunā |
tat kṣamaṁ nēha naḥ sthātuṁ kr̥tē kāryē paraṁtapāḥ ||

> **Meaning 14:** And this joy displayed by the king makes that clear to me.
> Therefore, scorchers of foes, now that our mission is accomplished, it is not
> fitting for us to linger here.

---
15.
pītvā madhu yathākāmaṁ viśrāntā vanacāriṇaḥ |
kiṁ śeṣaṁ gamanaṁ tatra sugrīvo yatra me guruḥ ||

> **Meaning 15:** You forest-dwellers have drunk honey to your hearts' content
> and rested; what remains now but to go to where my elder Sugrīva is?

---
16.
sarve yathā māṁ vakṣyanti sametya hariyūthapāḥ |
tathāsmi kartā kartavye bhavadbhiḥ paravān aham ||

> **Meaning 16:** Whatever all you Hari troop leaders tell me together, that I
> will do as our duty; in all matters I am guided by you.

---
17.
nājñāpayitum īśo ’haṁ yuvarājo ’smi yady api |
ayuktaṁ kr̥takarmāṇo yūyaṁ dharṣayituṁ mayā ||

> **Meaning 17:** Even though I am the crown prince, I have no authority to
> order you about. Now that you have accomplished the mission, it would be wrong
> of me to impose upon you.'

---
18.
bruvataś cāṅgadaś caivaṁ śrutvā vacanam avyayam |
prahr̥ṣṭamanaso vākyam idam ūcur vanaukasaḥ ||

> **Meaning 18:** Hearing these modest and noble words spoken by Aṅgada, the
> forest-dwellers were delighted at heart and replied:

---
19.
evaṁ vakṣyati ko rājan prabhuḥ san vānararṣabha |
aiśvaryamadamatto hi sarvo ’ham iti manyate ||

> **Meaning 19:** 'Bull among Vānaras, what master speaks like this? For anyone
> drunk with the pride of power thinks only of himself!

---
20.
tava cedaṁ susadr̥śaṁ vākyaṁ nānyasya kasya cit |
saṁnatir hi tavākhyāti bhaviṣyac chubhabhāgyatām ||

> **Meaning 20:** Such speech is worthy of you alone, and of no one else; your
> humility clearly foretells your future greatness and prosperity.

---
21.
sarve vayam api prāptās tatra gantuṁ kr̥takṣaṇāḥ |
sa yatra harivīrāṇāṁ sugrīvaḥ patir avyayaḥ ||

> **Meaning 21:** All of us are fully prepared to go where Sugrīva, the
> steadfast master of the Hari heroes, resides.

---
22.
tvayā hy anuktair haribhir naiva śakyaṁ padāt padam |
kva cid gantuṁ hariśreṣṭha brūmaḥ satyam idaṁ tu te ||

> **Meaning 22:** Foremost of Haris, without your word the Haris cannot move a
> single step anywhere — we tell you the plain truth.'

---
23.
evaṁ tu vadatāṁ teṣām aṅgadaḥ pratyabhāṣata |
bāḍhaṁ gacchāma ity uktvā utpapāta mahītalāt ||

> **Meaning 23:** As they spoke thus, Aṅgada replied, 'Yes, let us go,' and
> leapt up from the ground.

---
24.
utpatantam anūtpetuḥ sarve te hariyūthapāḥ |
kr̥tvākāśaṁ nirākāśaṁ yajñotkṣiptā ivānalāḥ ||

> **Meaning 24:** As he leapt, all those Hari troop leaders leapt up after him,
> crowding the sky like flames shooting up from a sacrificial fire.

---
25.
te ’mbaraṁ sahasotpatya vegavantaḥ plavaṁgamāḥ |
vinadanto mahānādaṁ ghanā vāteritā yathā ||

> **Meaning 25:** Soaring into the sky with great speed, those Plavaṅgamas
> roared loudly like clouds driven by the wind.

---
26.
aṅgade hy ananuprāpte sugrīvo vānarādhipaḥ ||


> **Meaning 26:** Even before Aṅgada arrived, Sugrīva, the sovereign of the
> Vānaras,

---
27.
uvāca śokopahataṁ rāmaṁ kamalalocanam |
samāśvasihi bhadraṁ te dr̥ṣṭā devī na saṁśayaḥ ||
> **Meaning 27:** spoke to lotus-eyed Rāma, who was stricken with grief: 'Take
> heart, may all be well with you! The devī has been found, without doubt.

---
28.
nāgantum iha śakyaṁ tair atīte samaye hi naḥ |
aṅgadasya praharṣācca jānāmi śubhadarśanam ||
> **Meaning 28:** They could never have returned here after the time limit had
> expired, and from Aṅgada's great joy I know that she has been seen.

---
29.
na matsakāśam āgacchet kr̥tye hi vinipātite |
yuvarājo mahābāhuḥ plavatāṁ pravaro ’ṅgadaḥ ||

> **Meaning 29:** If the mission had failed, the mighty-armed crown prince
> Aṅgada, foremost among the leaping hosts, would never have come into my
> presence.

---
30.
yady apy akr̥takr̥tyānām īdr̥śaḥ syād upakramaḥ |
bhavet tu dīnavadano bhrāntaviplutamānasaḥ ||

> **Meaning 30:** Even if those who failed could behave in this way, he would
> have had a gloomy face and an anxious, unsettled mind.

---
31.
pitr̥paitāmahaṁ caitat pūrvakair abhirakṣitam |
na me madhuvanaṁ hanyād ahr̥ṣṭaḥ plavageśvaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 31:** Madhuvana is ancestral property, protected by my forefathers;
> that leader of the Plavagas would never have ravaged it unless he were
> celebrating.'

---
32.
kausalyā suprajā rāma samāśvasihi suvrata |
dr̥ṣṭā devī na saṁdeho na cānyena hanūmatā ||


> **Meaning 32:** 'Rāma of noble vows, son of Kausalyā, take heart! The devī has
> been found, without doubt, and by none other than Hanumān.

---
33.
na hy anyaḥ karmaṇo hetuḥ sādhane tadvidho bhavet |
hanūmati hi siddhiś ca matiś ca matisattama |
vyavasāyaś ca vīryaṁ ca sūrye teja iva dhruvam ||

> **Meaning 33:** For there is no one else capable of achieving such an
> undertaking. In Hanumān, foremost among the wise, reside capability, sharp
> intellect, steadfast resolve, and valor, as surely as radiance resides in the
> sun.

---
34.
jāmbavān yatra netā syād aṅgadaś ca baleśvaraḥ |
hanūmāṁś cāpy adhiṣṭhātā na tasya gatir anyathā ||

> **Meaning 34:** Where Jāmbavān is the guide, Aṅgada commands the force, and
> Hanumān oversees the task, its success cannot be otherwise.

---
35.
mā bhūś cintā samāyuktaḥ saṁpraty amitavikrama |
yadā hi darpitōdagrāḥ saṅgatāḥ kānanaukasaḥ ||
> **Meaning 35:** Warrior of boundless valor, do not worry now. For when the
> forest-dwellers have banded together with such proud exuberance,

---
36.
naiṣāmakṛtakāryāṇāmīdṛśaḥ syādupakramaḥ|
vanabhaṅgēna jānāmi madhūnāṃ bhakṣaṇēna ca||

> **Meaning 36:** they would never behave this way if their work were
> unfinished. From their destruction of the grove and their consuming of the
> honey, I know it.'
---

37.
tataḥ kila kilā śabdaṁ śuśrāvāsannam ambare ||

> **Meaning 37:** Then in the sky close by, he heard the joyful clamor of shouts
> and cheers.

---
38.
hanūmat karmadr̥ptānāṁ nardatāṁ kānanaukasām |
kiṣkindhām upayātānāṁ siddhiṁ kathayatām iva ||

> **Meaning 38:** The forest-dwellers, proud of Hanumān's achievement, roared
> aloud as they approached Kiṣkindhā, as if announcing their triumph.

---
39.
tataḥ śrutvā ninādaṁ taṁ kapīnāṁ kapisattamaḥ |
āyatāñcitalāṅgūlaḥ so ’bhavad dhr̥ṣṭamānasaḥ ||

> **Meaning 39:** Hearing that roar of the Kapis, Sugrīva, foremost among Kapis,
> lifted his long tail in excitement and rejoiced at heart.

---
40.
ājagmus te ’pi harayo rāmadarśanakāṅkṣiṇaḥ |
aṅgadaṁ purataḥ kr̥tvā hanūmantaṁ ca vānaram ||

> **Meaning 40:** Eager to see Rāma, those Haris arrived, placing Aṅgada and the
> Vānara Hanumān in the lead.

---
41.
te ’ṅgadapramukhā vīrāḥ prahr̥ṣṭāś ca mudānvitāḥ |
nipetur harirājasya samīpe rāghavasya ca ||

> **Meaning 41:** Those heroes led by Aṅgada, overjoyed and filled with delight,
> landed near the king of Haris and Rāghava.

---
42.
hanūmāṁś ca mahābahuḥ praṇamya śirasā tataḥ |
niyatām akṣatāṁ devīṁ rāghavāya nyavedayat ||

> **Meaning 42:** Then the mighty-armed Hanumān bowed his head and reported to
> Rāghava that the devī was steadfast in her devotion and unharmed.

---
43.
dṛṣṭā devīti hanumadvadanādamṛtōpamam|
ākarṇya vacanaṃ rāmō harṣamāpa salakṣmaṇaḥ||

> **Meaning 43:** Hearing those nectar-like words 'The devī has been seen' from
> Hanumān's lips, Rāma, together with Lakṣmaṇa, experienced boundless joy.

---
44.
niścitārthaṁ tatas tasmin sugrīvaṁ pavanātmaje |
lakṣmaṇaḥ prītimān prītaṁ bahumānād avaikṣata ||

> **Meaning 44:** Seeing that the son of Pavana had confirmed the certainty of
> the mission, the delighted Lakṣmaṇa looked at the joyful Sugrīva with deep
> respect.

---
45.
prītyā ca ramamāṇo ’tha rāghavaḥ paravīrahā |
bahu mānena mahatā hanūmantam avaikṣata ||

> **Meaning 45:** And Rāghava, slayer of enemy heroes, filled with joy and
> affection, gazed upon Hanumān with immense esteem and admiration.

---
