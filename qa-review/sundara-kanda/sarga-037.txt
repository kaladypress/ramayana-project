=== Sarga 37 ===

---
1.
sītā tad vacanaṁ śrutvā pūrṇacandranibhānanā |
hanūmantam uvācedaṁ dharmārthasahitaṁ vacaḥ ||

> **Meaning 1:** Hearing those words, Sītā, whose face was like the full moon,
> said this to Hanumān, words full of dharma and good sense:

---
2.
amr̥taṁ viṣasaṁsr̥ṣṭaṁ tvayā vānarabhāṣitam |
yacca nānyamanā rāmaḥ yacca śokaparāyaṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 2:** "Vānara, what you have told me is nectar mixed with poison:
> that Rāma thinks of nothing else but me, and that he is sunk in grief.

---
3.
aiśvarye vā suvistīrṇe vyasane vā sudāruṇe |
rajjveva puruṣaṁ baddhvā kr̥tāntaḥ parikarṣati ||

> **Meaning 3:** "Whether a person is in great prosperity or in terrible
> misfortune, fate ties him up as if with a rope and drags him along.

---
4.
vidhirnūnam asaṁhāryaḥ prāṇināṁ plavagottama |
saumitriṁ māṁ ca rāmaṁ ca vyasanaiḥ paśya mohitān ||

> **Meaning 4:** "Best of the Plavagas, destiny surely cannot be avoided by any
> living being. Look at Saumitri, at me, and at Rāma — all of us bewildered by
> misfortune.

---
5.
śokasyāsya kadā pāraṁ rāghavaḥ adhigamiṣyati |
plavamānaḥ pariśrāntaḥ hatanauḥ sāgare yathā ||

> **Meaning 5:** "When will Rāghava reach the far shore of this grief, like a
> shipwrecked man, worn out, struggling to stay afloat in the ocean?

---
6.
rākṣasānāṁ kṣayaṁ kr̥tvā sūdayitvā ca rāvaṇam |
laṅkām unmūlitāṁ kr̥tvā kadā drakṣyati māṁ patiḥ ||

> **Meaning 6:** "When will my husband destroy the rākṣasas, kill Rāvaṇa, tear
> Laṅkā up by the roots, and see me again?

---
7.
sa vācyaḥ saṁtvarasveti yāvad eva na pūryate |
ayaṁ saṁvatsaraḥ kālaḥ tāvad dhi mama jīvitam ||

> **Meaning 7:** "Tell him to hurry. I will stay alive only until this year runs
> out.

---
8.
vartate daśamo māsaḥ dvau tu śeṣau plavaṁgama |
rāvaṇena nr̥śaṁsena samayo yaḥ kr̥to mama ||

> **Meaning 8:** "This is the tenth month, Plavaṅgama; only two remain of the
> time limit that cruel Rāvaṇa set for me.

---
9.
vibhīṣaṇena ca bhrātrā mama niryātanaṁ prati |
anunītaḥ prayatnena na ca tat kurute matim ||

> **Meaning 9:** "His brother Vibhīṣaṇa tried hard to persuade him to return me to srirama,
> but Rāvaṇa will not even consider it.

---
10.
mama pratipradānaṁ hi rāvaṇasya na rocate |
rāvaṇaṁ mārgate saṁkhye mr̥tyuḥ kālavaśaṁ gatam ||

> **Meaning 10:** "Rāvaṇa does not like the idea of giving me back. Death is
> hunting for him in battle, and he is already in the grip of Time.

---
11.
jyeṣṭhā kanyānalā nama vibhīṣaṇasutā kape |
tayā mamaitad ākhyātaṁ mātrā prahitayā svayam ||

> **Meaning 11:** "Kapi, Vibhīṣaṇa's eldest daughter, a girl named Analā, told
> me this herself. Her mother had sent her to me.

---
12.
avindhyo nāma medhāvī vidvān rākṣasapuṁgavaḥ |
dhr̥timāñ śīlavān vr̥ddhaḥ rāvaṇasya susaṁmataḥ ||

> **Meaning 12:** "There is a wise and learned rākṣasa chief named Avindhya —
> steady, of good character, elderly, and highly respected by Rāvaṇa.

---
13.
rāmāt kṣayam anuprāptaṁ rakṣasāṁ pratyacodayat |
na ca tasyāpi duṣṭātmā śr̥ṇoti vacanaṁ hitam ||

> **Meaning 13:** "He warned Rāvaṇa that the rākṣasas would be destroyed by
> Rāma. But that wicked one will not listen even to his good advice.

---
14.
āśaṁseti hariśreṣṭha kṣipraṁ māṁ prāpsyate patiḥ |
antarātmā hi me śuddhaḥ tasmiṁśca bahavo guṇāḥ ||

> **Meaning 14:** "Best of the Haris, My husband will soon win me back,
> because my heart is pure, and he has so many virtues.

---
15.
utsāhaḥ pauruṣaṁ sattvam ānr̥śaṁsyaṁ kr̥tajñatā |
vikramaśca prabhāvaśca santi vānararāghave ||

> **Meaning 15:** "Vānara, Rāghava has energy, manliness, strength of spirit,
> kindness, gratitude, valor, and power.

---
16.
caturdaśasahasrāṇi rākṣasānāṁ jaghāna yaḥ |
janasthāne vinā bhrātrā śatruḥ kastasya nodvijet ||

> **Meaning 16:** "He killed fourteen thousand rākṣasas at Janasthāna without
> even his brother's help. What enemy would not tremble before him?

---
17.
na sa śakyastulayituṁ vyasanaiḥ puruṣarṣabhaḥ |
ahaṁ tasyānubhāvajñā śakrasyeva pulomajā ||

> **Meaning 17:** "That bull among men cannot be shaken by misfortune. I know
> his greatness, just as Sacidevi knows Indra's.

---
18.
śarajālāṁśumāñ śūraḥ kape rāmadivākaraḥ |
śatrurakṣomayaṁ toyam upaśoṣaṁ nayiṣyati ||

> **Meaning 18:** "O Kapi, just as the sun dries up water with its rays, the heroic Rāma will dry up the enemy rākṣasa host with his arrows."

---
19.
iti saṁjalpamānāṁ tāṁ rāmārthe śokakarśitām |
aśrusaṁpūrṇavadanām uvāca hanumān kapiḥ ||

> **Meaning 19:** As she spoke like this, wasted by grief over Rāma, her face
> flooded with tears, the Kapi Hanumān said to her:

---
20.
śrutvaiva tu vaco mahyaṁ kṣipram eṣyati rāghavaḥ |
camūṁ prakarṣan mahatīṁ haryr̥kṣagaṇasaṁkulām ||

> **Meaning 20:** "As soon as he hears my report, Rāghava will come quickly,
> leading a vast army teeming with hosts of Haris and Ṛkṣas.

---
21.
atha vā mocayiṣyāmi tām adyaiva hi rākṣasāt |
asmād duḥkhād upāroha mama pr̥ṣṭham anindite ||

> **Meaning 21:** "Or else I will free you from this rākṣasa today, and from
> this misery. Faultless lady, climb onto my back.

---
22.
tvaṁ hi pr̥ṣṭhagatāṁ kr̥tvā saṁtariṣyāmi sāgaram |
śaktirasti hi me voḍhuṁ laṅkām api sarāvaṇām ||

> **Meaning 22:** "With you on my back, I will cross the ocean. I have the
> strength to carry even Laṅkā itself, along with Rāvaṇa.

---
23.
ahaṁ prasravaṇasthāya rāghavāyādya maithili |
prāpayiṣyāmi śakrāya havyaṁ hutam ivānalaḥ ||

> **Meaning 23:** "Maithilī, this very day I will bring you to Rāghava on Mount
> Prasravaṇa, the way Agni carries an offering poured into the fire to Śakra.

---
24.
drakṣyasy adyaiva vaidehi rāghavaṁ sahalakṣmaṇam |
vyavasāya samāyuktaṁ viṣṇuṁ daityavadhe yathā ||

> **Meaning 24:** "Vaidehī, today you will see Rāghava together with Lakṣmaṇa,
> full of resolve, like Viṣṇu set on destroying the daityas.

---
25.
tvaddarśanakr̥totsāham āśramasthaṁ mahābalam |
puraṁdaram ivāsīnaṁ nāgarājasya mūrdhani ||

> **Meaning 25:** "You will see that mighty one, eager to see you, sitting in
> his hermitage like Purandara seated on the head of the king of elephants.

---
26.
pr̥ṣṭham āroha me devi mā vikāṅkṣasva śobhane |
yogam anviccha rāmeṇa śaśāṅkeneva rohiṇī ||

> **Meaning 26:** "Climb onto my back, devī. Do not hesitate, O auspicious one. Seek your union with Rāma, as Rohiṇī seeks the moon.

---
27.
kathayantīva candreṇa sūryeṇeva suvarcalā |
matpr̥ṣṭham adhiruhya tvaṁ tarākāśamahārṇavam ||

> **Meaning 27:** "Seated upon my back, cross this great ocean through the sky,
> as though conversing with the moon and with the radiant sun.

---
28.
na hi me saṁprayātasya tvām ito nayataḥ aṅgane |
anugantuṁ gatiṁ śaktāḥ sarve laṅkānivāsinaḥ ||

> **Meaning 28:** "Gracious lady, once I set out carrying you away from here,
> none of the inhabitants of Laṅkā will be able to follow my path.

---
29.
yathaivāham iha prāptaḥ tathaivāham asaṁśayam |
yāsyāmi paśya vaidehi tvām udyamya vihāyasaṁ ||

> **Meaning 29:** "Just as I came here, Vaidehī, I will go back without a doubt,
> lifting you up into the sky. Just watch."

---
30.
maithilī tu hariśreṣṭhāc chrutvā vacanam adbhutam |
harṣavismitasarvāṅgī hanūmantam athābravīt ||

> **Meaning 30:** Hearing these astonishing words from the best of the Haris,
> Maithilī thrilled with joy and wonder from head to toe, and she said to
> Hanumān:

---
31.
hanūman dūram adhvanaṁ kathaṁ māṁ voḍhum icchasi |
tad eva khalu te manye kapitvaṁ hariyūthapa ||

> **Meaning 31:** "Hanumān, how can you want to carry me so far? Leader of the
> Hari troops, I think this is just your vanara nature showing.

---
32.
kathaṁ vālpaśarīrastvaṁ mām ito netum icchasi |
sakāśaṁ mānavendrasya bharturme plavagarṣabha ||

> **Meaning 32:** "Bull among Plavagas, with that small body of yours, how do
> you expect to take me from here to my husband, the king of men?"

---
33.
sītāyā vacanaṁ śrutvā hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ |
cintayām āsa lakṣmīvān navaṁ paribhavaṁ kr̥tam ||

> **Meaning 33:** Hearing Sītā's words, the splendid Hanumān, son of Māruta,
> felt that he had been slighted in a new way.

---
34.
na me jānāti sattvaṁ vā prabhāvaṁ vāsitekṣaṇā |
tasmāt paśyatu vaidehī yad rūpaṁ mama kāmataḥ ||

> **Meaning 34:** He thought, "The dark-eyed Vaidehī doesn't know my strength or
> my power. So let her see the form I can take at will."

---
35.
iti saṁcintya hanumāṁḥ tadā plavagasattamaḥ |
darśayām āsa vaidehyāḥ svarūpam arimardanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 35:** With this thought, Hanumān, the best of the Plavagas and
> crusher of enemies, then showed Vaidehī his true form.

---
36.
sa tasmāt pādapād dhīmān āplutya plavagarṣabhaḥ |
tato vardhitum ārebhe sītāpratyayakāraṇāt ||

> **Meaning 36:** The wise bull among Plavagas leapt down from that tree, and
> then began to grow, to win Sītā's trust.

---
37.
merumandārasaṁkāśaḥ babhau dīptānalaprabhaḥ |
agrato vyavatasthe ca sītāyā vānararṣabhaḥ ||

> **Meaning 37:** Like Meru or Mandara, blazing like a fire, the bull among
> vānaras stood before Sītā.

---
38.
hariḥ parvatasaṁkāśaḥ tāmravaktro mahābalaḥ |
vajradaṁṣṭranakho bhīmaḥ vaidehīm idam abravīt ||

> **Meaning 38:** That mighty Hari, huge as a mountain, with a coppery face and
> fearsome teeth and nails like vajras, said this to Vaidehī:

---
39.
saparvatavanoddeśāṁ sāṭṭaprākāratoraṇām |
laṅkām imāṁ sanathāṁ vā nayituṁ śaktirasti me ||

> **Meaning 39:** "I have the strength to carry off this Laṅkā, with its
> mountains and forests, its towers, ramparts, and gateways — even with its
> lord.

---
40.
tad avasthāpya tāṁ buddhiḥ alaṁ devi vikāṅkṣayā |
viśokaṁ kuru vaidehi rāghavaṁ sahalakṣmaṇam ||

> **Meaning 40:** "So make up your mind, devī. Enough hesitation. Vaidehī, free
> Rāghava and Lakṣmaṇa from their grief."

---
41.
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvācalasaṁkāśam uvāca janakātmajā |
padmapatraviśālākṣī mārutasyaurasaṁ sutam ||

> **Meaning 41:** Seeing him, huge as a mountain, the daughter of Janaka,
> wide-eyed as lotus petals, said to the true son of Māruta:

---
42.
tava sattvaṁ balaṁ caiva vijānāmi mahākape |
vāyoriva gatiṁ cāpi tejaścāgnirivādbhutam ||

> **Meaning 42:** "Great Kapi, I know your strength of spirit and your power. I
> know you move like the wind and blaze with a wondrous energy like fire.

---
43.
prākr̥taḥ anyaḥ kathaṁ cemāṁ bhūmim āgantum arhati |
udadheraprameyasya pāraṁ vānarapuṁgava ||

> **Meaning 43:** "How could anyone ordinary reach this land on the far shore of
> the boundless ocean, bull among vānaras?

---
44.
jānāmi gamane śaktiṁ nayane cāpi te mama |
avaśyaṁ sāmpradhāryāśu kāryasiddhirihātmanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 44:** "I know you have the strength to go, and to carry me as well.
> But I must think carefully about how to make our mission succeed.

---
45.
ayuktaṁ tu kapiśreṣṭha mayā gantuṁ tvayā saha |
vāyuvegasavegasya vego māṁ mohayet tava ||

> **Meaning 45:** "Best of the Kapis, it is not right for me to go with you. You
> move as fast as the wind, and your speed could make me faint.

---
46.
aham ākāśam āsaktā upary upari sāgaram |
prapateyaṁ hi te pr̥ṣṭhād bhayād vegena gacchataḥ ||

> **Meaning 46:** "High in the sky, far above the ocean, I could fall from your
> back out of fear, with you moving so fast.

---
47.
patitā sāgare cāhaṁ timinakrajhaṣākule |
bhayeyam āśu vivaśā yādasām annam uttamam ||

> **Meaning 47:** "And if I fell into the ocean, teeming with timis, crocodiles,
> and fish, I would quickly become, helpless, a choice meal for the sea
> creatures.

---
48.
na ca śakṣye tvayā sārdhaṁ gantuṁ śatruvināśana |
kalatravati saṁdehaḥ tvayy api syād asaṁśayam ||

> **Meaning 48:** "O destroyer of foes, I do not have the courage to travel with
> you. For in the effort to protect me, your own safety would surely be placed in
> grave peril.

---
49.
hriyamāṇāṁ tu māṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rākṣasā bhīmavikramāḥ |
anugaccheyurādiṣṭā rāvaṇena durātmanā ||

> **Meaning 49:** "When they see me being carried off, the rākṣasas, terrible in
> their valor, would chase after us on the orders of wicked Rāvaṇa.

---
50.
taistvaṁ parivr̥taḥ śūraiḥ śūlam udgara pāṇibhiḥ |
bhavestvaṁ saṁśayaṁ prāptaḥ mayā vīra kalatravān ||

> **Meaning 50:** "Hero, surrounded by those warriors with spears and maces in
> their hands, you would be in danger because you had me to protect.

---
51.
sāyudhā bahavo vyomni rākṣasāstvaṁ nirāyudhaḥ |
kathaṁ śakṣyasi saṁyātuṁ māṁ caiva parirakṣitum ||

> **Meaning 51:** "There would be many armed rākṣasas in the sky, and you have
> no weapons. How could you fight and protect me at the same time?

---
52.
yudhyamānasya rakṣobhiḥ tatastaiḥ krūrakarmabhiḥ |
prapateyaṁ hi te pr̥ṣṭhad bhayārtā kapisattama ||

> **Meaning 52:** "Best of the Kapis, while you fought with those cruel
> rākṣasas, I would fall from your back in terror.

---
53.
atha rakṣāṁsi bhīmāni mahānti balavanti ca |
kathaṁ cit sāmparāye tvāṁ jayeyuḥ kapisattama ||

> **Meaning 53:** "And those huge, powerful, fearsome rākṣasas might somehow
> defeat you in battle, best of the Kapis.

---
54.
atha vā yudhyamānasya pateyaṁ vimukhasya te |
patitāṁ ca gr̥hītvā māṁ nayeyuḥ pāparākṣasāḥ ||

> **Meaning 54:** "Or while you were fighting and had turned away, I might fall,
> and those wicked rākṣasas would seize me and carry me off.

---
55.
māṁ vā hareyustvaddhastād viśaseyurathāpi vā |
avyavasthau hi dr̥śyete yuddhe jayaparājayau ||

> **Meaning 55:** "They might snatch me from your hands, or even kill me. In
> battle, victory and defeat are never certain.

---
56.
ahaṁ vāpi vipadyeyaṁ rakṣobhirabhitarjitā |
tvatprayatno hariśreṣṭha bhaven niṣphala eva tu ||

> **Meaning 56:** "Or I might die of fright from the rākṣasas' threats, and then
> all your effort, best of the Haris, would be wasted.

---
57.
kāmaṁ tvam api paryāptaḥ nihantuṁ sarvarākṣasān |
rāghavasya yaśo hīyet tvayā śastaistu rākṣasaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 57:** "Of course, you are quite able to kill all the rākṣasas. But
> if you killed them, Rāghava's glory would be diminished.

---
58.
atha vādāya rakṣāṁsi nyasyeyuḥ saṁvr̥te hi mām |
yatra te nābhijānīyuḥ harayo nāpi rāghavaḥ ||

> **Meaning 58:** "Or the rākṣasas might take me and hide me somewhere secret,
> where neither the Haris nor Rāghava could find me.

---
59.
ārambhastu madarthaḥ ayaṁ tatastava nirarthakaḥ |
tvayā hi saha rāmasya mahān āgamane guṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 59:** "Then all your effort on my behalf would be for nothing. But
> if Rāma comes here with you, much good will come of it.

---
60.
mayi jīvitam āyattaṁ rāghavasya mahātmanaḥ |
bhrātr̥̄ṇāṁ ca mahābāhaḥ tava rājakulasya ca ||

> **Meaning 60:** "Mighty-armed one, the life of the great-souled Rāghava
> depends on me, and so do the lives of his brothers and your king Sugrīva.

---
61.
tau nirāśau madarthe tu śokasaṁtāpakarśitau |
saha sarvarkṣaharibhiḥ tyakṣyataḥ prāṇasaṁgraham ||

> **Meaning 61:** "If those two lose all hope for me, worn down by grief and
> anguish, they will give up their lives, along with all the Ṛkṣas and Haris.

---
62.
bharturbhaktiṁ puraskr̥tya rāmād anyasya vānara |
nāhaṁ spraṣṭuṁ padā gātram iccheyaṁ vānarottama ||

> **Meaning 62:** "Vānara, best of the vānaras, out of devotion to my husband I
> would not willingly touch the body of anyone but Rāma, not even with my foot.

---
63.
yad ahaṁ gātrasaṁsparśaṁ rāvaṇasya gatā balāt |
anīśā kiṁ kariṣyāmi vināthā vivaśā satī ||

> **Meaning 63:** "When I was forced to touch Rāvaṇa's body, I was helpless,
> without protector, with no power over what happened. What could I do?

---
64.
yadi rāmo daśagrīvam iha hatvā sarākṣasaṁ |
mām ito gr̥hya gaccheta tat tasya sadr̥śaṁ bhavet ||

> **Meaning 64:** "If Rāma kills Daśagrīva here along with the rākṣasas, and
> takes me away from here himself, that would be worthy of him.

---
65.
śrutā hi dr̥ṣṭāśca mayā parākramā mahātmanastasya raṇāvamardinaḥ |
na devagandharvabhujaṁgarākṣasā bhavanti rāmeṇa samā hi saṁyuge ||

> **Meaning 65:** "I have heard of, and seen, the mighty deeds of that
> great-souled Sri Rama, who crushes all in battle. Neither devas, gandharvas, nāgas,
> nor rākṣasas are equal to him in combat.

---
66.
samīkṣya taṁ saṁyati citrakārmukaṁ mahābalaṁ vāsavatulyavikramam |
salakṣmaṇaṁ ko viṣaheta rāghavaṁ hutāśanaṁ dīptam ivānileritam ||

> **Meaning 66:** "Who could stand against Rāghava with Lakṣmaṇa, when they see
> him in battle with his splendid bow — mighty, as valiant as Indra, blazing
> like a fire fanned by the wind?

---
67.
salakṣmaṇaṁ rāghavam ājimardanaṁ diśāgajaṁ mattam iva vyavasthitam |
saheta ko vānaramukhya saṁyuge yugāntasūryapratimaṁ śarārciṣam ||

> **Meaning 67:** "Chief of the vānaras, who could withstand Rāghava with
> Lakṣmaṇa in battle — that crusher of armies, standing firm like a maddened
> elephant of the quarters, his arrows blazing like the sun during pralaya?

---
68.
sa me hariśreṣṭha salakṣmaṇaṁ patiṁ sayūthapaṁ kṣipram ihopapādaya |
cirāya rāmaṁ prati śokakarśitāṁ kuruṣva māṁ vānaramukhya harṣitām ||

> **Meaning 68:** "So, best of the Haris, chief of the vānaras, bring my husband
> here quickly, with Lakṣmaṇa and the troop leaders. I have long been worn thin
> with grief for Rāma — make me happy again."

---
