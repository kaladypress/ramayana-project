=== Sarga 22 ===

---
1.
tataḥ sītām upāgamya rākṣasyo vikr̥tānanāḥ |
paruṣaṁ paruṣā nārya ūcus tā vākyam apriyam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
kiṁ tvam antaḥpure sīte sarvabhūtamanohare |
mahārhaśayanopete na vāsam anumanyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
mānuṣī mānuṣasyaiva bhāryātvaṁ bahu manyase |
pratyāhara mano rāmān na tvaṁ jātu bhaviṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
mānuṣī mānuṣaṁ taṁ tu rāmam icchasi śobhane |
rājyād bhraṣṭam asiddhārthaṁ viklavaṁ tam anindite ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
rākṣasīnāṁ vacaḥ śrutvā sītā padmanibhekṣaṇā |
netrābhyām aśrupūrṇābhyām idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
yad idaṁ lokavidviṣṭam udāharatha saṁgatāḥ |
naitan manasi vākyaṁ me kilbiṣaṁ pratitiṣṭhati ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
na mānuṣī rākṣasasya bhāryā bhavitum arhati |
kāmaṁ khādata māṁ sarvā na kariṣyāmi vo vacaḥ |
dīno vā rājyahīno vā yo me bhartā sa me guruḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sītāyā vacanaṁ śrutvā rākṣasyaḥ krodhamūrchitāḥ |
bhartsayanti sma paruṣair vākyai rāvaṇacoditāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
avalīnaḥ sa nirvākyo hanumāñ śiṁśapādrume |
sītāṁ saṁtarjayantīs tā rākṣasīr aśr̥ṇot kapiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tām abhikramya saṁrabdhā vepamānāṁ samantataḥ |
bhr̥śaṁ saṁlilihur dīptān pralambadaśanacchadān ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
ūcuś ca paramakruddhāḥ pragr̥hyāśu paraśvadhān |
neyam arhati bhartāraṁ rāvaṇaṁ rākṣasādhipam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sā bhartsyamānā bhīmābhī rākṣasībhir varānanā |
sā bāṣpam apamārjantī śiṁśapāṁ tām upāgamat ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tatas tāṁ śiṁśapāṁ sītā rākṣasībhiḥ samāvr̥tā |
abhigamya viśālākṣī tasthau śokapariplutā ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tāṁ kr̥śāṁ dīnavadanāṁ malināmbaradhāriṇīm |
bhartsayāṁ cakrire bhīmā rākṣasyas tāḥ samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tatas tāṁ vinatā nāma rākṣasī bhīmadarśanā |
abravīt kupitākārā karālā nirṇatodarī ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sīte paryāptam etāvad bhartr̥sneho nidarśitaḥ |
sarvatrātikr̥taṁ bhadre vyasanāyopakalpate ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
parituṣṭāsmi bhadraṁ te mānuṣas te kr̥to vidhiḥ |
mamāpi tu vacaḥ pathyaṁ bruvantyāḥ kuru maithili ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
rāvaṇaṁ bhaja bhartāraṁ bhartāraṁ sarvarakṣasām |
vikrāntaṁ rūpavantaṁ ca sureśam iva vāsavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
dakṣiṇaṁ tyāgaśīlaṁ ca sarvasya priyavādinam |
mānuṣaṁ kr̥paṇaṁ rāmaṁ tyaktvā rāvaṇam āśraya ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
divyāṅgarāgā vaidehi divyābharaṇabhūṣitā |
adya prabhr̥ti sarveṣāṁ lokānām īśvarī bhava |
agneḥ svāhā yathā devī śacīvendrasya śobhane ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
kiṁ te rāmeṇa vaidehi kr̥paṇena gatāyuṣā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
etad uktaṁ ca me vākyaṁ yadi tvaṁ na kariṣyasi |
asmin muhūrte sarvās tvāṁ bhakṣayiṣyāmahe vayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
anyā tu vikaṭā nāma lambamānapayodharā |
abravīt kupitā sītāṁ muṣṭim udyamya garjatī ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
bahūny apratirūpāṇi vacanāni sudurmate |
anukrośān mr̥dutvāc ca soḍhāni tava maithili |
na ca naḥ kuruṣe vākyaṁ hitaṁ kālapuraskr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
ānītāsi samudrasya pāram anyair durāsadam |
rāvaṇāntaḥpuraṁ ghoraṁ praviṣṭā cāsi maithili ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
rāvaṇasya gr̥he rudhā asmābhis tu surakṣitā |
na tvāṁ śaktaḥ paritrātum api sākṣāt puraṁdaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
kuruṣva hitavādinyā vacanaṁ mama maithili |
alam aśruprapātena tyaja śokam anarthakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
bhaja prītiṁ praharṣaṁ ca tyajaitāṁ nityadainyatām |
sīte rākṣasarājena saha krīḍa yathāsukham ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
jānāsi hi yathā bhīru strīṇāṁ yauvanam adhruvam |
yāvan na te vyatikrāmet tāvat sukham avāpnuhi ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
udyānāni ca ramyāṇi parvatopavanāni ca |
saha rākṣasarājena cara tvaṁ madirekṣaṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
strīsahasrāṇi te sapta vaśe sthāsyanti sundari |
rāvaṇaṁ bhaja bhartāraṁ bhartāraṁ sarvarakṣasām ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
utpāṭya vā te hr̥dayaṁ bhakṣayiṣyāmi maithili |
yadi me vyāhr̥taṁ vākyaṁ na yathāvat kariṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
tataś caṇḍodarī nāma rākṣasī krūradarśanā |
bhrāmayantī mahac chūlam idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
imāṁ hariṇalokākṣīṁ trāsotkampapayodharām |
rāvaṇena hr̥tāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā daurhr̥do me mahān abhūt ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
yakr̥tplīham athotpīḍaṁ hr̥dayaṁ ca sabandhanam |
antrāṇy api tathā śīrṣaṁ khādeyam iti me matiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
tatas tu praghasā nāma rākṣasī vākyam abravīt |
kaṇṭham asyā nr̥śaṁsāyāḥ pīḍayāmaḥ kim āsyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
nivedyatāṁ tato rājñe mānuṣī sā mr̥teti ha |
nātra kaś cana saṁdehaḥ khādateti sa vakṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
tatas tv ajāmukhī nāma rākṣasī vākyam abravīt |
viśasyemāṁ tataḥ sarvān samān kuruta pīlukān ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
vibhajāma tataḥ sarvā vivādo me na rocate |
peyam ānīyatāṁ kṣipraṁ mālyaṁ ca vividhaṁ bahu ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
tataḥ śūrpaṇakhā nāma rākṣasī vākyam abravīt |
ajāmukhā yad uktaṁ hi tad eva mama rocate ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
surā cānīyatāṁ kṣipraṁ sarvaśokavināśinī |
mānuṣaṁ māṁsam āsādya nr̥tyāmo ’tha nikumbhilām ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
evaṁ saṁbhartsyamānā sā sītā surasutopamā |
rākṣasībhiḥ sughorābhir dhairyam utsr̥jya roditi ||

=== meaning ===

---
