=== Sarga 23 ===

---
1.
ity uktvā maithilīṁ rājā rāvaṇaḥ śatrurāvaṇaḥ |
sandiśya ca tataḥ sarvā rākṣasīr nirjagāma ha ||

> **Meaning 1:** Having spoken thus to Maithilī and given his commands to all
> the Rākṣasīs, King Rāvaṇa, the scourge of his foes, strode out.

---
2.
niṣkrānte rākṣasendre tu punar antaḥpuraṁ gate |
rākṣasyo bhīmarūpās tāḥ sītāṁ samabhidudruvuḥ ||

> **Meaning 2:** As soon as the king of Rākṣasas departed and retired to his
> palace, those hideous Rākṣasīs charged straight at Sītā.

---
3.
tataḥ sītām upāgamya rākṣasyaḥ krodhamūrchitāḥ |
paraṁ paruṣayā vācā vaidehīm idam abruvan ||

> **Meaning 3:** Closing in on Sītā and seething with fury, the Rākṣasīs snarled
> at Vaidehī with vicious words:

---
4.
paulastyasya variṣṭhasya rāvaṇasya mahātmanaḥ |
daśagrīvasya bhāryātvaṁ sīte na bahu manyase ||

> **Meaning 4:** "Listen, Sītā! Don't you think it's a great honor to become the
> wife of the mighty ten-headed Rāvaṇa, foremost of Pulastya's line?"

---
5.
tatas tv ekajaṭā nāma rākṣasī vākyam abravīt |
āmantrya krodhatāmrākṣī sītāṁ karatalodarīm ||

> **Meaning 5:** Then a Rākṣasī named Ekajaṭā, her eyes crimson with rage,
> confronted slender Sītā:

---
6.
prajāpatīnāṁ ṣaṇṇān tu caturtho yaḥ prajāpatiḥ |
mānaso brahmaṇaḥ putraḥ pulastya iti viśrutaḥ ||

> **Meaning 6:** "Among the six Prajāpatis, the fourth is the mind-born son of
> Brahmā, famed as Pulastya.

---
7.
pulastyasya tu tejasvī maharṣir mānasaḥ sutaḥ |
nāmnā sa viśravā nāma prajāpatisamaprabhaḥ ||

> **Meaning 7:** Pulastya's mind-born son was the radiant sage named Viśravas,
> whose splendor equaled that of a Prajāpati himself.

---
8.
tasya putro viśālākṣi rāvaṇaḥ śatrurāvaṇaḥ |
tasya tvaṁ rākṣasendrasya bhāryā bhavitum arhasi |
mayoktañ cārusarvāṅgi vākyaṁ kiṁ nānumanyase ||

> **Meaning 8:** Wide-eyed girl, his son is Rāvaṇa, the terror of his enemies!
> You ought to be the wife of that king of Rākṣasas; why don't you agree to what
> I tell you, beautiful one?"

---
9.
tato harijaṭā nāma rākṣasī vākyam abravīt |
vivr̥tya nayane kopān mārjārasadr̥śekṣaṇā ||

> **Meaning 9:** Then a Rākṣasī named Harijaṭā glared with furious, cat-like
> eyes and barked:

---
10.
yena devās trayastriṁśad devarājaś ca nirjitaḥ |
tasya tvaṁ rākṣasendrasya bhāryā bhavitum arhasi ||

> **Meaning 10:** "He conquered the thirty-three gods along with the king of
> gods himself! You ought to be proud to become the wife of that king of
> Rākṣasas!

---
11.
vīryotsiktasya śūrasya saṅgrāmeṣv anivartinaḥ |
balino vīryayuktasyā bhāryātvaṁ kiṁ na lapsyase ||

> **Meaning 11:** Why won't you accept being the wife of this mighty hero,
> brimming with prowess and unyielding in battle?"

---
12.
tatas tu praghasā nāma rākṣasī krodhamūrchitā |
bhartsayantī tadā ghoram idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

13.
priyāṁ bahumatāṁ bhāryāṁ tyaktvā rājā mahābalaḥ |
sarvāsāñ ca mahābhāgāṁ tvām upaiṣyati rāvaṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 12-13:** Then a Rākṣasī named Praghasā, boiling with rage, hurled
> fierce threats at her: "The mighty King Rāvaṇa is willing to turn away from
> his cherished, highly honored queen, foremost among all women, just to come to
> you!

---
14.
samr̥ddhaṁ strīsahasreṇa nānāratnopaśobhitam |
antaḥpuraṁ samutsr̥jya tvām upaiṣyati rāvaṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 14:** Leaving behind his palace filled with thousands of women and
> glittering with every jewel, Rāvaṇa wants to be with you!

---
15.
asakr̥d devatā yuddhe nāgagandharvadānavāḥ |
nirjitāḥ samare yena sa te pārśvam upāgataḥ ||

> **Meaning 15:** He who has repeatedly routed the gods, Nāgas, Gandharvas, and
> Dānavas in battle has now come courting you!

---
16.
tasya sarvasamr̥ddhasyā rāvaṇasya mahātmanaḥ |
kimarthaṁ rākṣasendrasya bhāryātvaṁ necchase ’dhame ||

> **Meaning 16:** Wretch! Why don't you want to be the wife of the mighty
> Rāvaṇa, the king of Rākṣasas, who possesses every conceivable luxury?"

---
17.
yasya sūryo na tapati bhīto yasya ca mārutaḥ |
na vāti smāyatāpāṅge kin tvaṁ tasya na tiṣṭhasi ||

> **Meaning 17:** "Out of fear of him, the sun dares not burn too hot and the
> wind dares not blow too hard; wide-eyed fool, why won't you submit to him?

---
18.
puṣpavr̥ṣṭiñ ca taravo mumucur yasya vai bhayāt |
śailāś ca subhru pānīyañ jaladāś ca yadecchati ||

> **Meaning 18:** Out of fear of him, the trees shower down blossoms, and the
> mountains and clouds yield water whenever he desires, fair-browed one!

---
19.
tasya nairr̥tarājasya rājarājasya bhāmini |
kin tvaṁ na kuruṣe buddhim bhāryārthe rāvaṇasya hi ||

> **Meaning 19:** Why don't you make up your mind to be the wife of that king of
> the Nairr̥tas, the king of kings?

---
20.
sādhu te tattvato devi kathitaṁ sādhu bhāmini |
gr̥hāṇa susmite vākyam anyathā na bhaviṣyasi ||

> **Meaning 20:** We are telling you this for your own good, lady! Listen to our
> advice, bright-smiling one, or you won't live to see another day!"

---
