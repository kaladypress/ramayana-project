=== Sarga 44 ===

---
1.
hatān mantrisutān buddhvā vānareṇa mahātmanā |
rāvaṇaḥ saṁvr̥tākāraś cakāra matim uttamām ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sa virūpākṣayūpākṣau durdharaṁ caiva rākṣasaṁ |
praghasaṁ bhāsakarṇaṁ ca pañcasenāgranāyakān ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
saṁdideśa daśagrīvo vīrān nayaviśāradān |
hanūmadgrahaṇe vyagrān vāyuvegasamān yudhi ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
yāta senāgragāḥ sarve mahābalaparigrahāḥ |
savājirathamātaṅgāḥ sa kapiḥ śāsyatām iti ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
yat taiś ca khalu bhāvyaṁ syāt tam āsādya vanālayam |
karma cāpi samādheyaṁ deśakālavirodhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
na hy ahaṁ taṁ kapiṁ manye karmaṇā pratitarkayan |
sarvathā tan mahad bhūtaṁ mahābalaparigraham |
bhaved indreṇa vā sr̥ṣṭam asmadarthaṁ tapobalāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sanāgayakṣagandharvā devāsuramaharṣayaḥ |
yuṣmābhiḥ sahitaiḥ sarvair mayā saha vinirjitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tair avaśyaṁ vidhātavyaṁ vyalīkaṁ kiṁ cid eva naḥ |
tad eva nātra saṁdehaḥ prasahya parigr̥hyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
nāvamanyo bhavadbhiś ca hariḥ krūraparākramaḥ |
dr̥ṣṭā hi harayaḥ śīghrā mayā vipulavikramāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
vālī ca saha sugrīvo jāmbavāṁś ca mahābalaḥ |
nīlaḥ senāpatiś caiva ye cānye dvividādayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
naiva teṣāṁ gatir bhīmā na tejo na parākramaḥ |
na matir na balotsāho na rūpaparikalpanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
mahat sattvam idaṁ jñeyaṁ kapirūpaṁ vyavasthitam |
prayatnaṁ mahad āsthāya kriyatām asya nigrahaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
kāmaṁ lokās trayaḥ sendrāḥ sasurāsuramānavāḥ |
bhavatām agrataḥ sthātuṁ na paryāptā raṇājire ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tathāpi tu nayajñena jayam ākāṅkṣatā raṇe |
ātmā rakṣyaḥ prayatnena yuddhasiddhir hi cañcalā ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
te svāmivacanaṁ sarve pratigr̥hya mahaujasaḥ |
samutpetur mahāvegā hutāśasamatejasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
rathaiś ca mattair nāgaiś ca vājibhiś ca mahājavaiḥ |
śastraiś ca vividhais tīkṣṇaiḥ sarvaiś copacitā balaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tatas taṁ dadr̥śur vīrā dīpyamānaṁ mahākapim |
raśmimantam ivodyantaṁ svatejoraśmimālinam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
toraṇasthaṁ mahāvegaṁ mahāsattvaṁ mahābalam |
mahāmatiṁ mahotsāhaṁ mahākāyaṁ mahābalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
taṁ samīkṣyaiva te sarve dikṣu sarvāsv avasthitāḥ |
tais taiḥ praharaṇair bhīmair abhipetus tatas tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tasya pañcāyasās tīkṣṇāḥ sitāḥ pītamukhāḥ śarāḥ |
śirasty utpalapatrābhā durdhareṇa nipātitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
sa taiḥ pañcabhir āviddhaḥ śaraiḥ śirasi vānaraḥ |
utpapāta nadan vyomni diśo daśa vinādayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tatas tu durdharo vīraḥ sarathaḥ sajjakārmukaḥ |
kirañ śaraśatair naikair abhipede mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
sa kapir vārayām āsa taṁ vyomni śaravarṣiṇam |
vr̥ṣṭimantaṁ payodānte payodam iva mārutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
ardyamānas tatas tena durdhareṇānilātmajaḥ |
cakāra ninadaṁ bhūyo vyavardhata ca vegavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
sa dūraṁ sahasotpatya durdharasya rathe hariḥ |
nipapāta mahāvego vidyudrāśir girāv iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tatas taṁ mathitāṣṭāśvaṁ rathaṁ bhagnākṣakūvaram |
vihāya nyapatad bhūmau durdharas tyaktajīvitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
taṁ virūpākṣayūpākṣau dr̥ṣṭvā nipatitaṁ bhuvi |
saṁjātaroṣau durdharṣāv utpetatur ariṁdamau ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
sa tābhyāṁ sahasotpatya viṣṭhito vimale ’mbare |
mudgarābhyāṁ mahābāhur vakṣasy abhihataḥ kapiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
tayor vegavator vegaṁ vinihatya mahābalaḥ |
nipapāta punar bhūmau suparṇasamavikramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
sa sālavr̥kṣam āsādya samutpāṭya ca vānaraḥ |
tāv ubhau rākṣasau vīrau jaghāna pavanātmajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
tatas tāṁs trīn hatāñ jñātvā vānareṇa tarasvinā |
abhipede mahāvegaḥ prasahya praghaso harim ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
bhāsakarṇaś ca saṁkruddhaḥ śūlam ādāya vīryavān |
ekataḥ kapiśārdūlaṁ yaśasvinam avasthitau ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
paṭṭiśena śitāgreṇa praghasaḥ pratyapothayat |
bhāsakarṇaś ca śūlena rākṣasaḥ kapisattamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
sa tābhyāṁ vikṣatair gātrair asr̥gdigdhatanūruhaḥ |
abhavad vānaraḥ kruddho bālasūryasamaprabhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
samutpāṭya gireḥ śr̥ṅgaṁ samr̥gavyālapādapam |
jaghāna hanumān vīro rākṣasau kapikuñjaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
tatas teṣv avasanneṣu senāpatiṣu pañcasu |
balaṁ tad avaśeṣaṁ tu nāśayām āsa vānaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
aśvair aśvān gajair nāgān yodhair yodhān rathai rathān |
sa kapir nāśayām āsa sahasrākṣa ivāsurān ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
hatair nāgaiś ca turagair bhagnākṣaiś ca mahārathaiḥ |
hataiś ca rākṣasair bhūmī ruddhamārgā samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
tataḥ kapis tān dhvajinīpatīn raṇe; nihatya vīrān sabalān savāhanān |
tad eva vīraḥ parigr̥hya toraṇaṁ; kr̥takṣaṇaḥ kāla iva prajākṣaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
