=== Sarga 44 ===

---
1.
saṁdiṣṭo rākṣasendreṇa prahastasya suto balī |
jambumālī mahādaṁṣṭraḥ nirjagāma dhanurdharaḥ ||

> **Meaning 1:** Ordered by the king of the Rākṣasas, Jambumālī, the mighty son
> of Prahasta, set out with his bow. He had huge fangs.

---
2.
raktamālyāmbaradharaḥ sragvī rucirakuṇḍalaḥ |
mahān vivr̥ttanayanaḥ caṇḍaḥ samaradurjayaḥ ||

> **Meaning 2:** He wore red garlands and red clothes, with bright earrings. He
> was big, with rolling eyes, fierce, and very hard to defeat in battle.

---
3.
dhanuḥ śakradhanuḥ prakhyaṁ mahad rucirasāyakam |
visphārayāṇo vegena vajrāśanisamasvanam ||

> **Meaning 3:** He twanged his huge bow, which looked like Indra's rainbow and
> was fitted with bright arrows. The sound was as loud as thunder and lightning.

---
4.
tasya visphāraghoṣeṇa dhanuṣo mahatā diśaḥ |
pradiśaśca nabhaścaiva sahasā samapūryata ||

> **Meaning 4:** The mighty twang of his bow suddenly filled all the directions,
> the in-between directions, and the sky.

---
5.
rathena kharayuktena tam āgatam udīkṣya saḥ |
hanūmān vegasaṁpannaḥ jaharṣa ca nanāda ca ||

> **Meaning 5:** Seeing him arrive in a chariot drawn by donkeys, Hanumān, full
> of energy, was delighted and roared.

---
6.
taṁ toraṇaviṭaṅkasthaṁ hanūmantaṁ mahākapim |
jambumālī mahābāhuḥ vivyādha niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||

> **Meaning 6:** As Hanumān, the great Kapi, sat on the archway beam of the
> gate, mighty-armed Jambumālī pierced him with sharp arrows.

---
7.
ardhacandreṇa vadane śirasy ekena karṇinā |
bāhvorvivyādha nārācaiḥ daśabhistaṁ kapīśvaram ||

> **Meaning 7:** He struck the lord of the Kapis in the face with a
> crescent-headed arrow, in the head with one barbed arrow, and in the arms with
> ten iron arrows.

---
8.
tasya tacchuśubhe tāmraṁ śareṇābhihataṁ mukham |
śaradīvāmbujaṁ phullaṁ viddhaṁ bhāskararaśminā ||

> **Meaning 8:** Hit by the arrow, Hanumān's coppery-red face shone, like a
> blooming lotus in autumn pierced by a ray of the sun.

---

9.
tattasya raktaṁ raktēna raṁjitaṁ śuśubhē mukham |
yathākāśē mahāpadmaṁ siktaṁ caṁdanabiṁdubhiḥ |

> **Meaning 9:** His face, red and stained with red blood, shone like a great
> red lotus in the sky sprinkled with drops of sandal paste.

---
10.
cukopa bāṇābhihataḥ rākṣasasya mahākapiḥ |
tataḥ pārśve ativipulāṁ dadarśa mahatīṁ śilām ||

> **Meaning 10:** Struck by the arrow, the great Kapi flew into a rage. Then he
> saw a huge, broad rock lying nearby.

---
11.
tarasā tāṁ samutpāṭya cikṣepa balavad balī |
tāṁ śarairdaśabhiḥ kruddhaḥ tāḍayām āsa rākṣasaḥ ||

> **Meaning 11:** Powerful Hanumān swiftly tore it up and hurled it with great
> force. Furious, the Rākṣasa smashed it with ten arrows.

---
12.
vipannaṁ karma tad dr̥ṣṭvā hanūmāṁścaṇḍavikramaḥ |
sālaṁ vipulam utpāṭya bhrāmayām āsa vīryavān ||

> **Meaning 12:** Seeing his effort fail, Hanumān, fierce in his valor and full
> of strength, uprooted a huge sāla tree and swung it around.

---
13.
bhrāmayantaṁ kapiṁ dr̥ṣṭvā sālavr̥kṣaṁ mahābalam |
cikṣepa subahūn bāṇāñ jambumālī mahābalaḥ ||

> **Meaning 13:** Seeing the Kapi whirling the mighty sāla tree, mighty
> Jambumālī shot a great many arrows.

---
14.
sālaṁ caturbhirciccheda vānaraṁ pañcabhirbhuje |
urasy ekena bāṇena daśabhistu stanāntare ||

> **Meaning 14:** He cut the sāla tree with four arrows, struck the Vānara's arm
> with five, his chest with one, and the space between his breasts with ten.

---
15.
sa śaraiḥ pūritatanuḥ krodhena mahatā vr̥taḥ |
tam eva parighaṁ gr̥hya bhrāmayām āsa vegitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 15:** His body riddled with arrows and filled with great anger,
> Hanumān grabbed an iron club and swung it around fast.

---
16.
ativegaḥ ativegena bhrāmayitvā balotkaṭaḥ |
parighaṁ pātayām āsa jambumālermahorasi ||

> **Meaning 16:** Whirling it with tremendous speed and surging strength, he
> brought the club down on Jambumālī's broad chest.

---
17.
tasya caiva śiro nāsti na bāhū na ca jānunī |
na dhanurna ratho nāśvāḥ tatrādr̥śyanta neṣavaḥ ||

> **Meaning 17:** Nothing was left of Jambumālī: no head, no arms, no knees,
> no bow, no chariot, no donkeys, and no arrows could be seen there.

---
18.
sa hatastarasā tena jambumālī mahārathaḥ |
papāta nihato bhūmau cūrṇitāṅgavibhūṣaṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 18:** Struck swiftly by Hanumān, Jambumālī, the great
> chariot-warrior, fell dead on the ground, his ornaments crushed along with his
> body.

---
19.
jambumāliṁ ca nihataṁ kiṁkarāṁśca mahābalān |
cukrodha rāvaṇaḥ śrutvā kopasaṁraktalocanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 19:** Hearing that Jambumālī and the mighty Kiṅkaras were dead,
> Rāvaṇa flew into a rage, his eyes red with fury.

---
20.
sa roṣasaṁvartitatāmralocanaḥ prahastaputre nihate mahābale |
amātyaputrān ativīryavikramān samādideśāśu niśācareśvaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 20:** With eyes burning copper-red with rage at the death of mighty
> Prahasta's son, the lord of the night-wanderers quickly gave orders to the
> ministers' sons, who were famed for their valor and strength.

---
