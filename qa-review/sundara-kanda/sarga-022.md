=== Sarga 22 ===

---

1.
sītāyā vacanaṁ śrutvā paruṣaṁ rākṣasādhipaḥ |
pratyuvāca tataḥ sītāṁ vipriyaṁ priyadarśanām ||

2.
yathā yathā sāntvayitā vaśyaḥ strīṇāṁ tathā tathā |
yathā yathā priyaṁ vaktā paribhūtas tathā tathā ||

3.
sanniyacchati me krodhaṁ tvayi kāmaḥ samutthitaḥ |
dravato mārgam āsādya hayān iva susārathiḥ ||

4.
vāmaḥ kāmo manuṣyāṇāṁ yasmin kila nibadhyate |
jane tasmiṁs tv anukrośaḥ snehaś ca kila jāyate ||

5.
etasmāt kāraṇān na tāṁ ghatayāmi varānane |
vadhārhām avamānārhāṁ mithyāpravrajite ratām ||

6.
paruṣāṇi hi vākyāni yāni yāni bravīṣi mām |
teṣu teṣu vadho yuktas tava maithili dāruṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 1-6:** Hearing Sītā's harsh words, the lord of the
> Rākṣasas replied with bitter words to her, radiant even in her
> defiance: "The more a man consoles and submits to women, the more he
> is scorned for speaking tender words. The desire that has arisen in me
> for you restrains my anger, as a skilled charioteer reins in galloping
> horses. Indeed, the passion of men is perverse — on whomever it fixes,
> toward that person compassion and tenderness are born. For this very
> reason, fair-faced one, I do not kill you — though you are worthy of
> death and disgrace for clinging to Śrī Rāma. For each harsh word you
> hurl at me, O Maithilī, a terrible death would be fitting!"
---

7.
evam uktvā tu vaidehīṁ rāvaṇo rākṣasādhipaḥ |
krodhasamrambhasamyuktaḥ sītām uttaram abravīt ||

8.
dvau māsau rakṣitavyau me yo ’vadhis te mayā kr̥taḥ |
tataḥ śayanam āroha mama tvaṁ varavarṇini ||

9.
ūrdhvaṁ dvābhyām tu māsābhyāṁ bhartāraṁ māmanicchatīm |
mama tvāṁ prātarāśārtham ārabhante mahānase ||

10.
tāṁ tarjyamānāṁ samprekṣya rākṣasendreṇa jānakīm |
devagandharvakanyās tā viṣedur vipulekṣaṇāḥ ||

11.
oṣṭhaprakārair aparā netravaktrais tathāparāḥ |
sītām āśvāsayām āsus tarjitāṁ tena rakṣasā ||

12.
tābhir āśvāsitā sītā rāvaṇaṁ rākṣasādhipam |
uvācātmahitaṁ vākyaṁ vr̥ttaśauṇḍīryagarvitam ||

> **Meaning 7-12:** Having spoken thus to Vaidehī, Rāvaṇa, filled with
> fury, spoke further: "The two months I granted you — that limit shall
> stand. After that, O resplendent one, you must ascend my bed! But if,
> past those two months, you still refuse me as husband, my cooks will
> prepare you in the kitchen for my morning meal!" Seeing Jānakī
> threatened thus by the king of the Rākṣasas, those wide-eyed
> daughters of the Devas and Gandharvas grew sorrowful. Some consoled
> the frightened Sītā with the movements of their lips, others with
> gestures of their eyes and faces. Heartened by them, Sītā — proud of
> her virtuous conduct and nobility — spoke to Rāvaṇa words that were
> for his own good.
---

13.
nūnaṁ na te janaḥ kaś cid asin niḥśreyase sthitaḥ |
nivārayati yo na tvāṁ karmaṇo ’smād vigarhitāt ||

14.
māṁ hi dharmātmanaḥ patnīṁ śacīm iva śacīpateḥ |
tvadanyas triṣu lokeṣu prārthayen manasāpi kaḥ ||

15.
rākṣasādhama rāmasya bhāryām amitatejasaḥ |
uktavān asi yat pāpaṁ kva gatas tasya mokṣyase ||

16.
yathā dr̥ptaś ca mātaṅgaḥ śaśaś ca sahitau vane |
tathā dviradavad rāmas tvaṁ nīca śaśavat smr̥taḥ ||

17.
sa tvam ikṣvākunāthaṁ vai kṣipann iha na lajjase |
cakṣuṣo viṣayaṁ tasya na tāvad upagacchasi ||

18.
ime te nayane krūre virūpe kr̥ṣṇapiṅgale |
kṣitau na patite kasmān mām anāryanirīkṣitaḥ ||

19.
tasya dharmātmanaḥ patnīṁ snuṣāṁ daśarathasya ca |
kathaṁ vyāharato māṁ te na jihvā pāpa śīryate ||

20.
asandeśāt tu rāmasya tapasaś cānupālanāt |
na tvāṁ kurmi daśagrīva bhasma bhasmārhatejasā ||

21.
nāpahartum ahaṁ śakyā tasya rāmasya dhīmataḥ |
vidhis tava vadhārthāya vihito nātra saṁśayaḥ ||

22.
śūreṇa dhanadabhrātā balaiḥ samuditena ca |
apohya rāmaṁ kasmād dhi dāracauryaṁ tvayā kr̥tam ||

> **Meaning 13-22:** Sītā said: "Surely there is no one here who
> desires your well-being, for no one restrains you from this abominable
> act. As Śacī belongs to Indra, I am the lawful wife of the righteous
> Śrī Rāma. Who else in all the three worlds would dare desire me, even
> in thought? You alone have committed this outrage! O vilest of
> Rākṣasas! Having spoken such sinful words to the wife of Rāma, whose
> splendor is immeasurable — where will you flee to escape the
> consequences? Your readiness to fight Rāma is a hare challenging a
> rutting elephant. Rāma is the elephant; you, O despicable one, are
> the hare! Are you not ashamed of reviling the sovereign of the
> Ikṣvākus while you dare not even approach the range of his sight? O
> wicked one! Why have these cruel, misshapen, dark-brown eyes of yours
> not fallen from their sockets while gazing at me? While you speak
> thus to me — the wife of that righteous one, the daughter-in-law of
> Daśaratha — how does your tongue not wither and fall, O sinful one?
> Only because I lack a mandate from Śrī Rāma, and because I must
> preserve the merit of my penance, do I not reduce you to ashes with
> my own blazing splendor, O ten-headed one! I could not have been
> taken from the presence of that wise Śrī Rāma by force — this was
> ordained by destiny for your destruction alone; there is no doubt.
> A heroic brother of Kubera, fully equipped with armies — yet you
> committed the theft of a wife like a coward, only after luring Śrī
> Rāma away!"
---

23.
sītāyā vacanaṁ śrutvā rāvaṇo rākṣasādhipaḥ |
vivr̥tya nayane krūre jānakīm anvavaikṣata ||

24.
nīlajīmūtasaṅkāśo mahābhujaśirodharaḥ |
siṁhasattvagatiḥ śrīmān dīptajihvogralocanaḥ ||

25.
calāgramakuṭaḥ prāṁśuś citramālyānulepanaḥ |
raktamālyāmbaradharas taptāṅgadavibhūṣaṇaḥ ||

26.
śroṇīsūtreṇa mahatā mekakena susaṁvr̥taḥ |
amr̥totpādanaddhena bhujaṅgeneva mandaraḥ ||

27.
tābhyāṁ sa paripūrṇābhyāṁ bhujābhyāṁ rākṣaseśvaraḥ |
śuśubhe’calasaṅkāśaḥ śr̥ṅgābhyām iva mandaraḥ ||

28.
taruṇādityavarṇābhyāṁ kuṇḍalābhyāṁ vibhūṣitaḥ |
raktapallavapuṣpābhyām aśokābhyām ivācalaḥ ||

29.
sa kalpavr̥kṣapratimo vasanta iva mūrtimān |
śmaśānacaityapratimo bhūṣito’pi bhayaṅkaraḥ ||

30.
avekṣamāṇo vaidehīṁ kopasamraktalocanaḥ |
uvāca rāvaṇaḥ sītāṁ bhujaṅga iva niḥśvasan ||

> **Meaning 23-30:** Hearing the words of Sītā, Rāvaṇa, the lord of the
> Rākṣasas, rolled his cruel eyes and glared at Jānakī. Resembling a dark cloud,
> having mighty arms and a thick neck, moving with the strength and gait of a
> lion, he possessed a blazing tongue and fierce eyes. He wore a tall crown that
> swayed at its tip, was adorned with colorful garlands and unguents, wore red
> garments, and was decorated with armlets of refined gold. Bound by a great
> golden girdle around his waist, he resembled Mount Mandara wrapped by the
> great serpent during the churning of the nectar. With those two full, plump
> arms, the lord of the Rākṣasas, resembling a mountain, shone like Mount
> Mandara with its two peaks. Adorned with earrings shining like the morning
> sun, he looked like a mountain flanked by two Aśoka trees full of red sprouts
> and flowers. Though he resembled a Kalpavriksha (wish-yielding tree) and
> looked like the season of spring incarnate, he was terrifying despite being
> heavily adorned, much like an altar in a cremation ground! Gazing at Vaidehī
> with eyes bloodshot with anger, Rāvaṇa spoke to Sītā, hissing like a serpent.
---

31.
anayenābhisampannam arthahīnam anuvrate |
nāśayāmy aham adya tvāṁ sūryaḥ sandhyām ivaujasā ||

32.
ity uktvā maithilīṁ rājā rāvaṇaḥ śatrurāvaṇaḥ |
sandideśa tataḥ sarvā rākṣasīr ghoradarśanāḥ ||

33.
ekākṣīm ekakarṇāṁ ca karṇaprāvaraṇāṁ tathā |
gokarṇīṁ hastikarṇīṁ ca lambakarṇīm akarṇikām ||

34.
hastipadya śvapadyau ca gopadīṁ pādacūlikām |
ekākṣīm ekapādīṁ ca pr̥thupādīm apādikām ||

35.
atimātraśirogrīvām atimātrakucodarīm |
atimātrāsyanetrāṁ ca dīrghajihvām ajihvikām ||

36.
anāsikāṁ siṁhamukhīṁ gomukhīṁ sūkarīmukhīm |
yathā madvaśagā sītā kṣipraṁ bhavati jānakī ||

37.
tathā kuruta rākṣasyaḥ sarvāḥ kṣipraṁ sametya ca |
pratilomānulomaiś ca sāmadānādibhedanaiḥ |
āvartayata vaidehīṁ daṇḍasyodyamanena ca ||

> **Meaning 31-37:** "Sītā follows one who has strayed from
> righteousness and is bereft of wealth. For following such a man, I
> shall destroy you this very day, as the sun destroys the darkness of
> twilight with its radiant force!" Having said this to Maithilī, King
> Rāvaṇa, the tormentor of foes, commanded all those terrible-looking
> Rākṣasīs: those with a single eye, those with a single ear, those
> whose ears served as covering, those with the ears of cows or
> elephants, those with pendulous ears, and those with none at all;
> those with the feet of elephants, dogs, or cows, those with tufts of
> hair upon their feet, those with a single foot, broad feet, or no
> feet; those with monstrously huge heads, necks, breasts, and bellies,
> those with gaping mouths and bulging eyes, those with long tongues,
> and those with none; those without noses, and those with the faces of
> lions, cows, and pigs. He commanded them: "See to it that Sītā,
> Janaka's daughter, swiftly submits to my will! Gather together
> swiftly, all of you Rākṣasīs! By means agreeable and disagreeable,
> by conciliation, gifts, sowing dissension, and the threat of
> punishment — bring Vaidehī around to me."
---

38.
iti pratisamādiśya rākṣasendraḥ punaḥ punaḥ |
kāmamanyuparītātmā jānakīṁ paryatarjayat ||

39.
upagamya tataḥ kṣipraṁ rākṣasī dhānyamālinī |
pariṣvajya daśagrīvam idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

40.
mayā krīḍa mahārāja sītayā kiṁ tavānayā |
vivarṇayā kr̥paṇayā mānuṣyā rākṣaseśvara ||

41.
nūnam asyā mahārāja na divyān bhogasattamān |
vidadhāty amaraśreṣṭhaḥ tava bāhubalārjitān ||

42.
akāmāṁ kāmayānasya śarīram upatapyate |
icchantīṁ kāmayānasya prītir bhavati śobhanā ||

> **Meaning 38-42:** Commanding them thus again and again, the king of
> the Rākṣasas, his mind overcome by lust and rage, repeatedly
> threatened Jānakī. Then a Rākṣasī named Dhānyamālinī swiftly
> approached, embraced the ten-headed one, and spoke: "Delight in me, O
> great King! What use is this Sītā to you — pallid, wretched, and
> merely human? Surely, O great King, the foremost of the gods has not
> ordained for her to enjoy those supreme divine pleasures won by the
> might of your arms. The body of a man who desires a woman who does
> not desire him is only tormented. But when desire meets desire — that
> union alone brings true joy!"
---

43.
evam uktas tu rākṣasyā samutkṣiptas tato balī |
jvaladbhāskaravarṇābhaṁ praviveśa niveśanam ||

44.
prasthitaḥ sa daśagrīvaḥ kampayann iva medinīm |
jvaladbhāskaravarṇābhaṁ praviveśa niveśanam ||

45.
devagandharvakanyāś ca nāgakanyāś ca tās tataḥ |
parivārya daśagrīvaṁ viviśus tad gr̥hottamam ||

46.
sa maithilīṁ dharmaparām avasthitāṁ pravepamānāṁ paribhartsya rāvaṇaḥ |
vihāya sītāṁ madanena mohitaḥ svam eva veśma praviveśa bhāsvaram ||

> **Meaning 43-46:** Addressed thus by the Rākṣasī, the mighty one was
> led away. As the ten-headed one strode forth, shaking the very earth,
> he entered his abode, which blazed with the brilliance of the sun.
> Surrounding him, all the daughters of the Devas, Gandharvas, and
> Nāgas followed into that splendid dwelling. Having fiercely
> threatened Maithilī — who stood trembling, yet steadfast in dharma —
> Rāvaṇa, deluded by desire, abandoned Sītā and entered his own
> resplendent palace.
---
