=== Sarga 14 ===

---
1.
praśasya tu praśastavyāṁ sītāṁ tāṁ haripuṁgavaḥ |
guṇābhirāmaṁ rāmaṁ ca punaś cintāparo ’bhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sa muhūrtam iva dhyātvā bāṣpaparyākulekṣaṇaḥ |
sītām āśritya tejasvī hanumān vilalāpa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
mānyā guruvinītasya lakṣmaṇasya gurupriyā |
yadi sītāpi duḥkhārtā kālo hi duratikramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
rāmasya vyavasāyajñā lakṣmaṇasya ca dhīmataḥ |
nātyarthaṁ kṣubhyate devī gaṅgeva jaladāgame ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tulyaśīlavayovr̥ttāṁ tulyābhijanalakṣaṇām |
rāghavo ’rhati vaidehīṁ taṁ ceyam asitekṣaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā navahemābhāṁ lokakāntām iva śriyam |
jagāma manasā rāmaṁ vacanaṁ cedam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
asyā hetor viśālākṣyā hato vālī mahābalaḥ |
rāvaṇapratimo vīrye kabandhaś ca nipātitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
virādhaś ca hataḥ saṁkhye rākṣaso bhīmavikramaḥ |
vane rāmeṇa vikramya mahendreṇeva śambaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
caturdaśasahasrāṇi rakṣasāṁ bhīmakarmaṇām |
nihatāni janasthāne śarair agniśikhopamaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
kharaś ca nihataḥ saṁkhye triśirāś ca nipātitaḥ |
dūṣaṇaś ca mahātejā rāmeṇa viditātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
aiśvaryaṁ vānarāṇāṁ ca durlabhaṁ vālipālitam |
asyā nimitte sugrīvaḥ prāptavām̐l lokasatkr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sāgaraś ca mayā krāntaḥ śrīmān nadanadīpatiḥ |
asyā hetor viśālākṣyāḥ purī ceyaṁ nirīkṣitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
yadi rāmaḥ samudrāntāṁ medinīṁ parivartayet |
asyāḥ kr̥te jagac cāpi yuktam ity eva me matiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
rājyaṁ vā triṣu lokeṣu sītā vā janakātmajā |
trailokyarājyaṁ sakalaṁ sītāyā nāpnuyāt kalām ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
iyaṁ sā dharmaśīlasya maithilasya mahātmanaḥ |
sutā janakarājasya sītā bhartr̥dr̥ḍhavratā ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
utthitā medinīṁ bhittvā kṣetre halamukhakṣate |
padmareṇunibhaiḥ kīrṇā śubhaiḥ kedārapāṁsubhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
vikrāntasyāryaśīlasya saṁyugeṣv anivartinaḥ |
snuṣā daśarathasyaiṣā jyeṣṭhā rājño yaśasvinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
dharmajñasya kr̥tajñasya rāmasya viditātmanaḥ |
iyaṁ sā dayitā bhāryā rākṣasī vaśam āgatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
sarvān bhogān parityajya bhartr̥snehabalāt kr̥tā |
acintayitvā duḥkhāni praviṣṭā nirjanaṁ vanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
saṁtuṣṭā phalamūlena bhartr̥śuśrūṣaṇe ratā |
yā parāṁ bhajate prītiṁ vane ’pi bhavane yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
seyaṁ kanakavarṇāṅgī nityaṁ susmitabhāṣiṇī |
sahate yātanām etām anarthānām abhāginī ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
imāṁ tu śīlasaṁpannāṁ draṣṭum icchati rāghavaḥ |
rāvaṇena pramathitāṁ prapām iva pipāsitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
asyā nūnaṁ punar lābhād rāghavaḥ prītim eṣyati |
rājā rājyaparibhraṣṭaḥ punaḥ prāpyeva medinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
kāmabhogaiḥ parityaktā hīnā bandhujanena ca |
dhārayaty ātmano dehaṁ tatsamāgamakāṅkṣiṇī ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
naiṣā paśyati rākṣasyo nemān puṣpaphaladrumān |
ekasthahr̥dayā nūnaṁ rāmam evānupaśyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
bhartā nāma paraṁ nāryā bhūṣaṇaṁ bhūṣaṇād api |
eṣā hi rahitā tena śobhanārhā na śobhate ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
duṣkaraṁ kurute rāmo hīno yad anayā prabhuḥ |
dhārayaty ātmano dehaṁ na duḥkhenāvasīdati ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
imām asitakeśāntāṁ śatapatranibhekṣaṇām |
sukhārhāṁ duḥkhitāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā mamāpi vyathitaṁ manaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
kṣitikṣamā puṣkarasaṁnibhākṣī; yā rakṣitā rāghavalakṣmaṇābhyām |
sā rākṣasībhir vikr̥tekṣaṇābhiḥ; saṁrakṣyate saṁprati vr̥kṣamūle ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
himahatanalinīva naṣṭaśobhā; vyasanaparamparayā nipīḍyamānā |
sahacararahiteva cakravākī; janakasutā kr̥paṇāṁ daśāṁ prapannā ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
asyā hi puṣpāvanatāgraśākhāḥ; śokaṁ dr̥ḍhaṁ vai janayaty aśokāḥ |
himavyapāyena ca mandaraśmir; abhyutthito naikasahasraraśmiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
ity evam arthaṁ kapir anvavekṣya; sīteyam ity eva niviṣṭabuddhiḥ |
saṁśritya tasmin niṣasāda vr̥kṣe; balī harīṇām r̥ṣabhas tarasvī ||

=== meaning ===

---
