=== Sarga 15 ===

---
1.
sa vīkṣamāṇas tatrastho mārgamāṇaś ca maithilīm |
avekṣamāṇaś ca mahīṁ sarvāṁ tām anvavaikṣata ||

> **Meaning 1:** Perched there, searching for Maithilī and surveying the ground
> below, he looked all around that entire grove.

---
2.
santāna kalatābhiś ca pādapair upaśobhitām |
divyagandharasopetāṁ sarvataḥ samalaṅkr̥tām ||

> **Meaning 2:** It was adorned with santānaka trees and climbing creepers,
> blessed with celestial fragrances and juices, and richly decorated on all
> sides.

---
3.
tāṁ sa nandanasaṅkāśāṁ mr̥gapakṣibhir āvr̥tām |
harmyaprāsādasambādhāṁ kokilākulaniḥsvanām ||

> **Meaning 3:** Resembling the Nandana garden of Indra, it was filled with deer
> and birds, crowded with mansions and palaces, and echoing with the sweet calls
> of cuckoos.

---
4.
kāñcanotpalapadmābhir vāpībhir upaśobhitām |
bahvāsanakuthopetāṁ bahubhūmigr̥hāyutām ||

> **Meaning 4:** It was beautified by ponds adorned with golden water lilies and
> lotuses, furnished with numerous seats and embroidered rugs, and featured many
> underground chambers.

---
5.
sarvartukusumai ramyaiḥ phalavadbhiś ca pādapaiḥ |
puṣpitānām aśokānāṁ śriyā sūryodayaprabhām ||

> **Meaning 5:** With delightful trees bearing flowers and fruits of every
> season, and glowing with the beauty of blooming aśoka trees, it shone like the
> rising sun.

---
6.
pradīptām iva tatrastho mārutiḥ samudaikṣata |
niṣpatraśākhāṁ vihagaiḥ kriyamāṇām ivāsakr̥t ||

> **Meaning 6:** Stationed there, Māruti gazed at the grove, which appeared
> ablaze as the branches were repeatedly stripped of leaves by swarms of
> settling birds.

---
7.
viniṣpatadbhiḥ śataśaś citraiḥ puṣpāvataṁsakaiḥ |
āmūlapuṣpanicitair aśokaiḥ śokanāśanaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 7:** As hundreds of birds darted away carrying colorful flower
> ornaments in their beaks, the sorrow-dispelling aśoka trees blossomed with
> clusters of flowers from their very roots.

---
8.
puṣpabhārātibhāraiś ca spr̥śadbhir iva medinīm |
karṇikāraiḥ kusumitaiḥ kiṁśukaiś ca supuṣpitaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 8:** The blooming karṇikāra trees, heavily laden with flowers,
> seemed almost to touch the earth, alongside the brightly blossoming kiṁśuka
> trees.

---
9.
sa deśaḥ prabhayā teṣāṁ pradīpta iva sarvataḥ |
punnāgāḥ saptaparṇāś ca campakoddālakās tathā ||

> **Meaning 9:** That entire region seemed ablaze with their brilliance; there
> stood punnāga, saptaparṇa, campaka, and uddālaka trees,

---
10.
vivr̥ddhamūlā bahavaḥ śobhante sma supuṣpitāḥ |
śātakumbhanibhāḥ ke cit ke cid agniśikhopamāḥ ||

> **Meaning 10:** with sturdy, deep roots, all flowering splendidly; some aśoka
> trees shone like gold, others like flames of fire,

---
11.
nīlāñjananibhāḥ ke cit tatrāśokāḥ sahasraśaḥ |
nandanaṁ vividhodyānaṁ citraṁ caitrarathaṁ yathā ||

> **Meaning 11:** and some of the thousands of aśoka trees there were dark like
> blue collyrium, making the varied grove look wondrous like Indra's Nandana or
> Kubera's Caitraratha garden.

---
12.
ativr̥ttam ivācintyaṁ divyaṁ ramyaṁ śriyā vr̥tam |
dvitīyam iva cākāśaṁ puṣpajyotirgaṇāyutam ||

> **Meaning 12:** Surpassing all imagination, celestial, delightful, and bathed
> in splendor, it looked like a second sky studded with constellations of starry
> blossoms.

---
13.
puṣparatnaśataiś citraṁ pañcamaṁ sāgaraṁ yathā |
sarvartupuṣpair nicitaṁ pādapair madhugandhibhiḥ ||

> **Meaning 13:** Wondrous with hundreds of floral jewels, it appeared like a
> fifth ocean, filled with trees of every season bearing sweet honey scents.

---
14.
nānāninādair udyānaṁ ramyaṁ mr̥gagaṇair dvijaiḥ |
anekagandhapravahaṁ puṇyagandhaṁ manoramam |
śailendram iva gandhāḍhyaṁ dvitīyaṁ gandhamādanam ||

> **Meaning 14:** Echoing with varied sounds of herds of deer and flocks of
> birds, wafting multiple pure and enchanting aromas, that garden was laden with
> scents like a second Mount Gandhamādana, the king of mountains.

---
15.
aśokavanikāyāṁ tu tasyāṁ vānarapuṅgavaḥ |
sa dadarśāvidūrasthaṁ caityaprāsādam ūrjitam ||

> **Meaning 15:** In that Aśoka grove, the foremost among Vānaras saw a towering
> Caitya palace standing not far away.

---
16.
madhye stambhasahasreṇa sthitaṁ kailāsapāṇḍuram |
pravālakr̥tasopānaṁ taptakāñcanavedikam ||

> **Meaning 16:** Supported in the center by a thousand pillars, it gleamed
> white like Mount Kailāśa, with stairs crafted of coral and platforms of molten
> gold.

---
17.
muṣṇantam iva cakṣūṁṣi dyotamānam iva śriyā |
vimalaṁ prāṁśubhāvatvād ullikhantam ivāmbaram ||

> **Meaning 17:** Dazzling the eyes and blazing with beauty, its pure, soaring
> height seemed to scrape the sky.

---
18.
tato malinasaṁvītāṁ rākṣasībhiḥ samāvr̥tām ||

> **Meaning 18:** Then he saw a woman clad in soiled clothes, surrounded by
> Rākṣasīs,

---
19.
upavāsakr̥śāṁ dīnāṁ niḥśvasāntīṁ punaḥ punaḥ |
dadarśa śuklapakṣādau candrarekhām ivāmalām ||

> **Meaning 19:** emaciated from fasting, forlorn, sighing repeatedly, looking
> like the pure crescent moon at the start of the bright fortnight.

---
20.
mandaprakhyāyamānena rūpeṇa ruciraprabhām |
pinaddhāṁ dhūmajālena śikhām iva vibhāvasoḥ ||

> **Meaning 20:** Though her radiant charm was faintly discernable beneath her
> faded appearance, she looked like a tongue of fire veiled in dense smoke.

---
21.
pītenaikena saṁvītāṁ kliṣṭenottamavāsasā |
sapaṅkām analaṅkārāṁ vipadmām iva padminīm ||

> **Meaning 21:** Wrapped in a single, worn, yet noble yellow silk garment,
> stained with dust and devoid of adornments, she was like a lotus pool stripped
> of its blossoms.

---
22.
vrīḍitāṁ duḥkhasantaptāṁ parimlānāṁ tapasvinīm |
graheṇāṅgārakeṇaiva pīḍitām iva rohiṇīm ||

> **Meaning 22:** Bashful, consumed by grief, withered and ascetic in demeanor,
> she appeared like the constellation Rohiṇī eclipsed by the planet Mars.

---
23.
aśrupūrṇamukhīṁ dīnāṁ kr̥śām ananaśena ca |
śokadhyānaparāṁ dīnāṁ nityaṁ duḥkhaparāyaṇām ||

> **Meaning 23:** With her face bathed in tears, she was miserable and wasted by
> abstaining from food, immersed in sorrowful contemplation, forever resigned to
> grief.

---
24.
priyaṁ janam apaśyantīṁ paśyantīṁ rākṣasīgaṇam |
svagaṇena mr̥gīṁ hīnāṁ śvagaṇābhivr̥tām iva ||

> **Meaning 24:** Unable to see her loved ones and looking only upon the band of
> Rākṣasīs, she resembled a doe separated from her herd and surrounded by a pack
> of hounds.

---
25.
nīlanāgābhayā veṇyā jaghanaṁ gatayaikayā |
nīlayā nīradāpāye vanarājya mahimiva||

> **Meaning 25:** With her hair gathered in a single dark braid reaching her
> hips like a black serpent, she looked like the earth flanked by a dark line of
> forests at the end of the monsoon.

---
26.
sukhārhāṁ duḥkhasantaptāṁ vyasanānām akodivām |
tāṁ samīkṣya viśālākṣīm adhikaṁ malināṁ kr̥śām ||

> **Meaning 26:** Worthy of every comfort yet scorched by anguish, completely
> unaccustomed to calamity, that wide-eyed lady sat deeply soiled and emaciated.

---
27.
tarkayām āsa sīteti kāraṇair upapādibhiḥ |
hriyamāṇā tadā tena rakṣasā kāmarūpiṇā ||

> **Meaning 27:** Seeing her, he deduced by sound reasoning that this must be
> Sītā; for when she was carried away by that form-shifting Rākṣasa,

---
28.
yathārūpā hi dr̥ṣṭā vai tathārūpeyam aṅganā ||

pūrṇacandrānanāṁ subhrūṁ cāruvr̥ttapayodharām |

> **Meaning 28:** she had appeared of this very likeness. With a face like the
> full moon, lovely brows, and well-rounded breasts,

---
29.
kurvantīṁ prabhayā devīṁ sarvā vitimirā diśaḥ ||

tāṁ nīlakeśīṁ bimbauṣṭhīṁ sumadhyāṁ supratiṣṭhitām |

> **Meaning 29:** that devī dispelled darkness from every quarter with her
> brilliance. With dark hair, lips red like the bimba fruit, a slender waist,
> and a noble bearing,

---
30.
sītāṁ padmapalāśākṣīṁ manmathasya ratiṁ yathā ||

iṣṭāṁ sarvasya jagataḥ pūrṇacandraprabhām iva |

> **Meaning 30:** with eyes like lotus petals, she looked like Rati to Manmatha,
> beloved by the entire world like the radiance of the full moon.

---
31.
bhūmau sutanum āsīnāṁ niyatām iva tāpasīm ||

niḥśvāsabahulāṁ bhīruṁ bhujagendravadhūm iva |

> **Meaning 31:** That lady of exquisite form sat upon the bare ground like an
> austere ascetic; sighing heavily and trembling with fear, she looked like the
> captive bride of a serpent king.

---
32.
śokajālena mahatā vitatena na rājatīm ||

saṁsaktāṁ dhūmajālena śikhām iva vibhāvasoḥ |

> **Meaning 32:** Her radiance was dimmed by the vast web of sorrow spread
> around her, like a flame of fire wrapped in dense coils of smoke.

---
33.
tāṁ smr̥tīm iva sandigdhām r̥ddhiṁ nipatitām iva ||

vihatām iva ca śraddhām āśāṁ pratihatām iva |

> **Meaning 33:** She was like a traditional memory grown doubtful, like fallen
> prosperity, like shattered faith, or like dashed hope.

---
34.
sopasargāṁ yathā siddhiṁ buddhiṁ sakaluṣām iva |
abhūtenāpavādena kīrtiṁ nipatitām iva ||

> **Meaning 34:** She seemed like success marred by obstacles, like an intellect
> clouded with confusion, or like high renown tarnished by unfounded slander.

---
35.
rāmoparodhavyathitāṁ rakṣoharaṇakarśitām |
abalāṁ mr̥gaśāvākṣīṁ vīkṣamāṇāṁ tatas tataḥ ||

> **Meaning 35:** Grieving at being kept from Rāma, worn down by her abduction
> by the Rākṣasa, that helpless woman with eyes like a young fawn glanced
> fearfully this way and that.

---
36.
bāṣpāmbupratipūrṇena kr̥ṣṇavaktrākṣipakṣmaṇā |
vadanenāprasannena niḥśvasantīṁ punaḥ punaḥ ||

> **Meaning 36:** Her face was clouded with grief and her dark eyelashes were
> drenched in tears as she sighed again and again.

---
37.
malapaṅkadharāṁ dīnāṁ maṇḍanārhām amaṇḍitām |
prabhāṁ nakṣatrarājasya kālameghair ivāvr̥tām ||

> **Meaning 37:** Covered in dust and grime, forlorn, worthy of royal ornaments
> yet unadorned, she looked like the radiance of the moon veiled by dark storm
> clouds.

---
38.
tasya sandidihe buddhir muhuḥ sītāṁ nirīkṣya tu |
āmnāyānām ayogena vidyāṁ praśithilām iva ||

> **Meaning 38:** Looking at her repeatedly, Hanumān's mind was clouded with
> doubt, as sacred learning decays when unpracticed through neglect of
> tradition.

---
39.
duḥkhena bubudhe sītāṁ hanumān analaṅkr̥tām |
saṁskāreṇa yathāhīnāṁ vācam arthāntaraṁ gatām ||

> **Meaning 39:** Hanumān recognized the unadorned Sītā only with great
> difficulty, just as speech lacking grammatical refinement loses its clear
> meaning.

---
40.
tāṁ samīkṣya viśālākṣīṁ rājaputrīm aninditām |
tarkayām āsa sīteti kāraṇair upapādayan ||

> **Meaning 40:** Gazing at that wide-eyed, faultless princess, he reasoned that
> she was indeed Sītā, establishing it through clear evidence.

---
41.
vaidehyā yāni cāṅgeṣu tadā rāmo ’nvakīrtayat |
tāny ābharaṇajālāni gātraśobhīny alakṣayat ||

> **Meaning 41:** He observed on her the very suite of ornaments enhancing her
> form that Rāma had spoken of when describing Vaidehī.

---
42.
sukr̥tau karṇaveṣṭau ca śvadaṁṣṭrau ca susaṁsthitau |
maṇividrumacitrāṇi hasteṣv ābharaṇāni ca ||

> **Meaning 42:** He saw her finely crafted earrings, the well-fitted
> ear-ornaments shaped like wild dog fangs, and the wondrous jewelry studded
> with gems and coral upon her arms and wrists.

---
43.
śyāmāni cirayuktatvāt tathā saṁsthānavanti ca |
tāny evaitāni manye ’haṁ yāni rāmo ’vnakīrtayat ||

> **Meaning 43:** Though darkened and tarnished from being worn constantly for
> so long, they still held their distinct designs; he thought, "These are surely
> the very ornaments that Rāma described.

---
44.
tatra yāny avahīnāni tāny ahaṁ nopalakṣaye |
yāny asyā nāvahīnāni tānīmāni na saṁśayaḥ ||

> **Meaning 44:** Those which she dropped, I do not see here; but those which
> she did not cast off are undoubtedly these before me.

---
45.
pītaṁ kanakapaṭṭābhaṁ srastaṁ tad vasanaṁ śubham |
uttarīyaṁ nagāsaktaṁ tadā dr̥ṣṭaṁ plavaṅgamaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 45:** That auspicious, shining yellow upper garment like molten
> gold, which had slipped down and caught on the mountain tree, was seen that
> day by the Vānaras.

---
46.
bhūṣaṇāni ca mukhyāni dr̥ṣṭāni dharaṇītale |
anayaivāpaviddhāni svanavanti mahānti ca ||

> **Meaning 46:** And the precious, resonant ornaments that were seen fallen
> upon the earth were certainly cast away by her as well.

---
47.
idaṁ ciragr̥hītatvād vasanaṁ kliṣṭavattaram |
tathā hi nūnaṁ tad varṇaṁ tathā śrīmad yathetarat ||

> **Meaning 47:** This garment she wears, though much worn and frayed from long
> use, is indeed of the same hue and splendor as that other piece.

---
48.
iyaṁ kanakavarṇāṅgī rāmasya mahiṣī priyā |
pranaṣṭāpi satī yasya manaso na praṇaśyati ||

> **Meaning 48:** This lady of golden complexion is the beloved queen of Rāma;
> though lost to his sight, this chaste woman has never vanished from his heart.

---
49.
iyaṁ sā yat kr̥te rāmaś caturbhiḥ paritapyate |
kāruṇyenānr̥śaṁsyena śokena madanena ca ||

> **Meaning 49:** This is she for whose sake Rāma is tormented by four feelings:
> compassion, tenderness, grief, and longing love.

---
50.
strī pranaṣṭeti kāruṇyād āśritety ānr̥śaṁsyataḥ |
patnī naṣṭeti śokena priyeti madanena ca ||

> **Meaning 50:** Compassion because a woman is lost, tenderness because she
> depended entirely on him, grief because his wife is gone, and love because she
> is his cherished beloved.

---
51.
asyā devyā yathā rūpam aṅgapratyaṅgasauṣṭhavam |
rāmasya ca yathārūpaṁ tasyeyam asitekṣaṇā ||

> **Meaning 51:** Judging by this devī's beauty and the exquisite perfection of
> her every feature, and considering Rāma's noble form, this dark-eyed lady is
> perfectly matched to him."

---
52.
asyā devyā manas tasmiṁs tasya cāsyāṁ pratiṣṭhitam |
teneyaṁ sa ca dharmātmā muhūrtam api jīvati ||

> **Meaning 52:** This devī's mind is anchored wholly in him, and his in her; it
> is because of this that she and that righteous soul continue to live even for
> a moment.

---
53.
duṣkaraṁ kṛtavān rāmo hīno yadanayā prabhuḥ |
dhārayatyātmano dehaṁ na śokenāvasīdati ||

> **Meaning 53:** Rāma, that capable master, has accomplished what is nearly
> impossible: deprived of her, he still sustains his body and does not sink
> beneath despair.

---
54.
duṣkaraṁ kurute rāmo ya imāṁ mattakāśinīm |
sītāṁ vinā mahābāhur muhūrtam api jīvati ||

> **Meaning 54:** Mighty-armed Rāma performs an impossible feat in surviving
> even for a single moment without Sītā, this enchanting lady.

---
55.
evaṁ sītāṁ tadā dr̥ṣṭvā hr̥ṣṭaḥ pavanasambhavaḥ |
jagāma manasā rāmaṁ praśaśaṁsa ca taṁ prabhum ||

> **Meaning 55:** Having thus seen Sītā, the son of Pavana rejoiced in his
> heart; he turned his thoughts to Rāma and praised that noble master.
