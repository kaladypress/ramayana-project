=== Sarga 11 ===

---
1.
vimānāt tu susaṁkramya prākāraṁ hariyūthapaḥ |
hanūmān vegavān āsīd yathā vidyudghanāntare ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
saṁparikramya hanumān rāvaṇasya niveśanān |
adr̥ṣṭvā jānakīṁ sītām abravīd vacanaṁ kapiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
bhūyiṣṭhaṁ loḍitā laṅkā rāmasya caratā priyam |
na hi paśyāmi vaidehīṁ sītāṁ sarvāṅgaśobhanām ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
palvalāni taṭākāni sarāṁsi saritas tathā |
nadyo ’nūpavanāntāś ca durgāś ca dharaṇīdharāḥ |
loḍitā vasudhā sarvā na ca paśyāmi jānakīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
iha saṁpātinā sītā rāvaṇasya niveśane |
ākhyātā gr̥dhrarājena na ca paśyāmi tām aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
kiṁ nu sītātha vaidehī maithilī janakātmajā |
upatiṣṭheta vivaśā rāvaṇaṁ duṣṭacāriṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
kṣipram utpatato manye sītām ādāya rakṣasaḥ |
bibhyato rāmabāṇānām antarā patitā bhavet ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
atha vā hriyamāṇāyāḥ pathi siddhaniṣevite |
manye patitam āryāyā hr̥dayaṁ prekṣya sāgaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
rāvaṇasyoruvegena bhujābhyāṁ pīḍitena ca |
tayā manye viśālākṣyā tyaktaṁ jīvitam āryayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
upary upari vā nūnaṁ sāgaraṁ kramatas tadā |
viveṣṭamānā patitā samudre janakātmajā ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
āho kṣudreṇa cānena rakṣantī śīlam ātmanaḥ |
abandhur bhakṣitā sītā rāvaṇena tapasvinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
atha vā rākṣasendrasya patnībhir asitekṣaṇā |
aduṣṭā duṣṭabhāvābhir bhakṣitā sā bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
saṁpūrṇacandrapratimaṁ padmapatranibhekṣaṇam |
rāmasya dhyāyatī vaktraṁ pañcatvaṁ kr̥paṇā gatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
hā rāma lakṣmaṇety eva hāyodhyeti ca maithilī |
vilapya bahu vaidehī nyastadehā bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
atha vā nihitā manye rāvaṇasya niveśane |
nūnaṁ lālapyate mandaṁ pañjarastheva śārikā ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
janakasya kule jātā rāmapatnī sumadhyamā |
katham utpalapatrākṣī rāvaṇasya vaśaṁ vrajet ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
vinaṣṭā vā pranaṣṭā vā mr̥tā vā janakātmajā |
rāmasya priyabhāryasya na nivedayituṁ kṣamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
nivedyamāne doṣaḥ syād doṣaḥ syād anivedane |
kathaṁ nu khalu kartavyaṁ viṣamaṁ pratibhāti me ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
asminn evaṁgate karye prāptakālaṁ kṣamaṁ ca kim |
bhaved iti matiṁ bhūyo hanumān pravicārayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
yadi sītām adr̥ṣṭvāhaṁ vānarendrapurīm itaḥ |
gamiṣyāmi tataḥ ko me puruṣārtho bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
mamedaṁ laṅghanaṁ vyarthaṁ sāgarasya bhaviṣyati |
praveśaś civa laṅkāyā rākṣasānāṁ ca darśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
kiṁ vā vakṣyati sugrīvo harayo va samāgatāḥ |
kiṣkindhāṁ samanuprāptau tau vā daśarathātmajau ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
gatvā tu yadi kākutsthaṁ vakṣyāmi param apriyam |
na dr̥ṣṭeti mayā sītā tatas tyakṣyanti jīvitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
paruṣaṁ dāruṇaṁ krūraṁ tīkṣṇam indriyatāpanam |
sītānimittaṁ durvākyaṁ śrutvā sa na bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
taṁ tu kr̥cchragataṁ dr̥ṣṭvā pañcatvagatamānasaṁ |
bhr̥śānurakto medhāvī na bhaviṣyati lakṣmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
vinaṣṭau bhrātarau śrutvā bharato ’pi mariṣyati |
bharataṁ ca mr̥taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā śatrughno na bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
putrān mr̥tān samīkṣyātha na bhaviṣyanti mātaraḥ |
kausalyā ca sumitrā ca kaikeyī ca na saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
kr̥tajñaḥ satyasaṁdhaś ca sugrīvaḥ plavagādhipaḥ |
rāmaṁ tathā gataṁ dr̥ṣṭvā tatas tyakṣyanti jīvitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
durmanā vyathitā dīnā nirānandā tapasvinī |
pīḍitā bhartr̥śokena rumā tyakṣyati jīvitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
vālijena tu duḥkhena pīḍitā śokakarśitā |
pañcatvagamane rājñas tārāpi na bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
mātāpitror vināśena sugrīva vyasanena ca |
kumāro ’py aṅgadaḥ kasmād dhārayiṣyati jīvitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
bhartr̥jena tu śokena abhibhūtā vanaukasaḥ |
śirāṁsy abhihaniṣyanti talair muṣṭibhir eva ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
sāntvenānupradānena mānena ca yaśasvinā |
lālitāḥ kapirājena prāṇāṁs tyakṣyanti vānarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
na vaneṣu na śaileṣu na nirodheṣu vā punaḥ |
krīḍām anubhaviṣyanti sametya kapikuñjarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
saputradārāḥ sāmātyā bhartr̥vyasanapīḍitāḥ |
śailāgrebhyaḥ patiṣyanti sametya viṣameṣu ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
viṣam udbandhanaṁ vāpi praveśaṁ jvalanasya vā |
upavāsam atho śastraṁ pracariṣyanti vānarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
ghoram ārodanaṁ manye gate mayi bhaviṣyati |
ikṣvākukulanāśaś ca nāśaś caiva vanaukasām ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
so ’haṁ naiva gamiṣyāmi kiṣkindhāṁ nagarīm itaḥ |
na hi śakṣyāmy ahaṁ draṣṭuṁ sugrīvaṁ maithilīṁ vinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
mayy agacchati cehasthe dharmātmānau mahārathau |
āśayā tau dhariṣyete vanarāś ca manasvinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
hastādāno mukhādāno niyato vr̥kṣamūlikaḥ |
vānaprastho bhaviṣyāmi adr̥ṣṭvā janakātmajām ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
sāgarānūpaje deśe bahumūlaphalodake |
citāṁ kr̥tvā pravekṣyāmi samiddham araṇīsutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
upaviṣṭasya vā samyag liṅginaṁ sādhayiṣyataḥ |
śarīraṁ bhakṣayiṣyanti vāyasāḥ śvāpadāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
idam apy r̥ṣibhir dr̥ṣṭaṁ niryāṇam iti me matiḥ |
samyag āpaḥ pravekṣyāmi na cet paśyāmi jānakīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
sujātamūlā subhagā kīrtimālāyaśasvinī |
prabhagnā cirarātrīyaṁ mama sītām apaśyataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
tāpaso vā bhaviṣyāmi niyato vr̥kṣamūlikaḥ |
netaḥ pratigamiṣyāmi tām adr̥ṣṭvāsitekṣaṇām ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
yadītaḥ pratigacchāmi sītām anadhigamya tām |
aṅgadaḥ sahitaiḥ sarvair vānarair na bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
vināśe bahavo doṣā jīvan prāpnoti bhadrakam |
tasmāt prāṇān dhariṣyāmi dhruvo jīvati saṁgamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
evaṁ bahuvidhaṁ duḥkhaṁ manasā dhārayan muhuḥ |
nādhyagacchat tadā pāraṁ śokasya kapikuñjaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
rāvaṇaṁ vā vadhiṣyāmi daśagrīvaṁ mahābalam |
kāmam astu hr̥tā sītā pratyācīrṇaṁ bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
atha vainaṁ samutkṣipya upary upari sāgaram |
rāmāyopahariṣyāmi paśuṁ paśupater iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
iti cintā samāpannaḥ sītām anadhigamya tām |
dhyānaśokā parītātmā cintayām āsa vānaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
52.
yāvat sītāṁ na paśyāmi rāmapatnīṁ yaśasvinīm |
tāvad etāṁ purīṁ laṅkāṁ vicinomi punaḥ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
53.
saṁpāti vacanāc cāpi rāmaṁ yady ānayāmy aham |
apaśyan rāghavo bhāryāṁ nirdahet sarvavānarān ||

=== meaning ===

---
54.
ihaiva niyatāhāro vatsyāmi niyatendriyaḥ |
na matkr̥te vinaśyeyuḥ sarve te naravānarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
55.
aśokavanikā cāpi mahatīyaṁ mahādrumā |
imām abhigamiṣyāmi na hīyaṁ vicitā mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
56.
vasūn rudrāṁs tathādityān aśvinau maruto ’pi ca |
namaskr̥tvā gamiṣyāmi rakṣasāṁ śokavardhanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
57.
jitvā tu rākṣasān devīm ikṣvākukulanandinīm |
saṁpradāsyāmi rāmāyā yathāsiddhiṁ tapasvine ||

=== meaning ===

---
58.
sa muhūrtam iva dhyātvā cintāvigrathitendriyaḥ |
udatiṣṭhan mahābāhur hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
59.
namo ’stu rāmāya salakṣmaṇāya; devyai ca tasyai janakātmajāyai |
namo ’stu rudrendrayamānilebhyo; namo ’stu candrārkamarudgaṇebhyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
60.
sa tebhyas tu namaskr̥tvā sugrīvāya ca mārutiḥ |
diśaḥ sarvāḥ samālokya aśokavanikāṁ prati ||

=== meaning ===

---
61.
sa gatvā manasā pūrvam aśokavanikāṁ śubhām |
uttaraṁ cintayām āsa vānaro mārutātmajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
62.
dhruvaṁ tu rakṣobahulā bhaviṣyati vanākulā |
aśokavanikā cintyā sarvasaṁskārasaṁskr̥tā ||

=== meaning ===

---
63.
rakṣiṇaś cātra vihitā nūnaṁ rakṣanti pādapān |
bhagavān api sarvātmā nātikṣobhaṁ pravāyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
64.
saṁkṣipto ’yaṁ mayātmā ca rāmārthe rāvaṇasya ca |
siddhiṁ me saṁvidhāsyanti devāḥ sarṣigaṇās tv iha ||

=== meaning ===

---
65.
brahmā svayambhūr bhagavān devāś caiva diśantu me |
siddhim agniś ca vāyuś ca puruhūtaś ca vajradhr̥t ||

=== meaning ===

---
66.
varuṇaḥ pāśahastaś ca somādityai tathaiva ca |
aśvinau ca mahātmānau marutaḥ sarva eva ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
67.
siddhiṁ sarvāṇi bhūtāni bhūtānāṁ caiva yaḥ prabhuḥ |
dāsyanti mama ye cānye adr̥ṣṭāḥ pathi gocarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
68.
tad unnasaṁ pāṇḍuradantam avraṇaṁ; śucismitaṁ padmapalāśalocanam |
drakṣye tad āryāvadanaṁ kadā nv ahaṁ; prasannatārādhipatulyadarśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
69.
kṣudreṇa pāpena nr̥śaṁsakarmaṇā; sudāruṇālāṁkr̥taveṣadhāriṇā |
balābhibhūtā abalā tapasvinī; kathaṁ nu me dr̥ṣṭapathe ’dya sā bhavet ||

=== meaning ===

---
