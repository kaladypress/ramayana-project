=== Sarga 10 ===

---
1.
sa tasya madhye bhavanasya vānaro; latāgr̥hāṁś citragr̥hān niśāgr̥hān |
jagāma sītāṁ prati darśanotsuko; na caiva tāṁ paśyati cārudarśanām ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sa cintayām āsa tato mahākapiḥ; priyām apaśyan raghunandanasya tām |
dhruvaṁ nu sītā mriyate yathā na me; vicinvato darśanam eti maithilī ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sā rākṣasānāṁ pravareṇa bālā; svaśīlasaṁrakṣaṇa tat parā satī |
anena nūnaṁ pratiduṣṭakarmaṇā; hatā bhaved āryapathe pare sthitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
virūparūpā vikr̥tā vivarcaso; mahānanā dīrghavirūpadarśanāḥ |
samīkṣya sā rākṣasarājayoṣito; bhayād vinaṣṭā janakeśvarātmajā ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
sītām adr̥ṣṭvā hy anavāpya pauruṣaṁ; vihr̥tya kālaṁ saha vānaraiś ciram |
na me ’sti sugrīvasamīpagā gatiḥ; sutīkṣṇadaṇḍo balavāṁś ca vānaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
dr̥ṣṭam antaḥpuraṁ sarvaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rāvaṇayoṣitaḥ |
na sītā dr̥śyate sādhvī vr̥thā jāto mama śramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
kiṁ nu māṁ vānarāḥ sarve gataṁ vakṣyanti saṁgatāḥ |
gatvā tatra tvayā vīra kiṁ kr̥taṁ tad vadasva naḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
adr̥ṣṭvā kiṁ pravakṣyāmi tām ahaṁ janakātmajām |
dhruvaṁ prāyam upeṣyanti kālasya vyativartane ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
kiṁ vā vakṣyati vr̥ddhaś ca jāmbavān aṅgadaś ca saḥ |
gataṁ pāraṁ samudrasya vānarāś ca samāgatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
anirvedaḥ śriyo mūlam anirvedaḥ paraṁ sukham |
bhūyas tāvad viceṣyāmi na yatra vicayaḥ kr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
anirvedo hi satataṁ sarvārtheṣu pravartakaḥ |
karoti saphalaṁ jantoḥ karma yac ca karoti saḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tasmād anirveda kr̥taṁ yatnaṁ ceṣṭe ’ham uttamam |
adr̥ṣṭāṁś ca viceṣyāmi deśān rāvaṇapālitān ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
āpānaśālāvicitās tathā puṣpagr̥hāṇi ca |
citraśālāś ca vicitā bhūyaḥ krīḍāgr̥hāṇi ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
niṣkuṭāntararathyāś ca vimānāni ca sarvaśaḥ |
iti saṁcintya bhūyo ’pi vicetum upacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
bhūmīgr̥hāṁś caityagr̥hān gr̥hātigr̥hakān api |
utpatan nipataṁś cāpi tiṣṭhan gacchan punaḥ kva cit ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
apāvr̥ṇvaṁś ca dvārāṇi kapāṭāny avaghaṭṭayan |
praviśan niṣpataṁś cāpi prapatann utpatann api |
sarvam apy avakāśaṁ sa vicacāra mahākapiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
caturaṅgulamātro ’pi nāvakāśaḥ sa vidyate |
rāvaṇāntaḥpure tasmin yaṁ kapir na jagāma saḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
prākarāntararathyāś ca vedikaś caityasaṁśrayāḥ |
śvabhrāś ca puṣkariṇyaś ca sarvaṁ tenāvalokitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
rākṣasyo vividhākārā virūpā vikr̥tās tathā |
dr̥ṣṭā hanūmatā tatra na tu sā janakātmajā ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
rūpeṇāpratimā loke varā vidyādhara striyaḥ |
dr̥ṣṭā hanūmatā tatra na tu rāghavanandinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
nāgakanyā varārohāḥ pūrṇacandranibhānanāḥ |
dr̥ṣṭā hanūmatā tatra na tu sītā sumadhyamā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
pramathya rākṣasendreṇa nāgakanyā balād dhr̥tāḥ |
dr̥ṣṭā hanūmatā tatra na sā janakanandinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
so ’paśyaṁs tāṁ mahābāhuḥ paśyaṁś cānyā varastriyaḥ |
viṣasāda mahābāhur hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
udyogaṁ vānarendrāṇaṁ plavanaṁ sāgarasya ca |
vyarthaṁ vīkṣyānilasutaś cintāṁ punar upāgamat ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
avatīrya vimānāc ca hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ |
cintām upajagāmātha śokopahatacetanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
