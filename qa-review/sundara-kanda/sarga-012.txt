=== Sarga 12 ===

---
1.
sa tasya madhye bhavanasya vānaraḥ latāgr̥hāṁścitragr̥hān niśāgr̥hān |
jagāma sītāṁ prati darśanotsukaḥ na caiva tāṁ paśyati cārudarśanām ||

> **Meaning 1:** In the center of that palace complex, eager to see Sītā, the
> Vānara explored bower houses, picture galleries, and night chambers, but he
> could not find that lovely lady.

---
2.
sa cintayām āsa tato mahākapiḥ priyām apaśyan raghunandanasya tām |
dhruvaṁ nu sītā mriyate yathā na me vicinvato darśanam eti maithilī ||

> **Meaning 2:** Not finding the beloved of the joy of Raghu, the great Kapi
> thought in distress: "Surely Sītā must be dead, since Maithilī is nowhere to
> be seen despite my searching.

---
3.
sā rākṣasānāṁ pravareṇa bālā svaśīlasaṁrakṣaṇa tat parā satī |
anena nūnaṁ pratiduṣṭakarmaṇā hatā bhaved āryapathe pare sthitā ||

> **Meaning 3:** Devoted to guarding her virtue and steadfast on the noble path,
> that chaste young woman has surely been killed by this villainous king of
> Rākṣasas.

---
4.
virūparūpā vikr̥tā vivarcasaḥ mahānanā dīrghavirūpadarśanāḥ |
samīkṣya sā rākṣasarājayoṣitaḥ bhayād vinaṣṭā janakeśvarātmajā ||

> **Meaning 4:** Or perhaps the daughter of King Janaka died of sheer terror
> upon seeing the wives of the Rākṣasa king — misshapen, deformed, pale,
> huge-faced, and grotesque in appearance.

---
5.
sītām adr̥ṣṭvā hy anavāpya pauruṣaṁ vihr̥tya kālaṁ saha vānaraiściram |
na me ’sti sugrīvasamīpagā gatiḥ sutīkṣṇadaṇḍo balavāṁśca vānaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 5:** Without finding Sītā, failing in this feat of courage, and
> having spent so much time away with the Vānaras, I cannot return to Sugrīva;
> that powerful Vānara king enforces fierce punishment.

---
6.
dr̥ṣṭam antaḥpuraṁ sarvaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rāvaṇayoṣitaḥ |
na sītā dr̥śyate sādhvī vr̥thā jāto mama śramaḥ ||

> **Meaning 6:** I have searched the entire inner palace and seen all of
> Rāvaṇa's wives, but the virtuous Sītā is nowhere to be found. All my toil has
> been in vain!

---
7.
kiṁ nu māṁ vānarāḥ sarve gataṁ vakṣyanti saṁgatāḥ |
gatvā tatra tvayā vīra kiṁ kr̥taṁ tad vadasva naḥ ||

> **Meaning 7:** What will all the gathered Vānaras say to me when I return?
> 'Hero, tell us, what did you accomplish when you went there?'

---
8.
adr̥ṣṭvā kiṁ pravakṣyāmi tām ahaṁ janakātmajām |
dhruvaṁ prāyam upeṣyanti kālasya vyativartane ||

> **Meaning 8:** If I have not seen the daughter of Janaka, what will I tell
> them? With the time limit expired, they will surely resolve to fast unto
> death.

---
9.
kiṁ vā vakṣyati vr̥ddhaśca jāmbavān aṅgadaśca saḥ |
gataṁ pāraṁ samudrasya vānarāśca samāgatāḥ ||

> **Meaning 9:** What will the aged Jāmbavān say, and what will Aṅgada say, and
> all the gathered Vānaras, once I have crossed back over the ocean?"

---
10.
anirvedaḥ śriyo mūlam anirvedaḥ paraṁ sukham |
bhūyastāvad viceṣyāmi na yatra vicayaḥ kr̥taḥ ||

> **Meaning 10:** "Perseverance is the root of all success; non-despondency is
> the highest happiness. I will search once more in places I have not yet
> looked!

---
11.
anirvedo hi satataṁ sarvārtheṣu pravartakaḥ |
karoti saphalaṁ jantoḥ karma yac ca karoti saḥ ||

> **Meaning 11:** Relentless determination drives every purposeful effort; it
> brings to fruition whatever action a living being undertakes.

---
12.
tasmād anirveda kr̥taṁ yatnaṁ ceṣṭe ’ham uttamam |
adr̥ṣṭāṁśca viceṣyāmi deśān rāvaṇapālitān ||

> **Meaning 12:** Therefore, shaking off all despair, I will make the utmost
> effort and scour every unseen corner of the realms ruled by Rāvaṇa."

---
13.
āpānaśālāvicitāḥ tathā puṣpagr̥hāṇi ca |
citraśālāśca vicitā bhūyaḥ krīḍāgr̥hāṇi ca ||

> **Meaning 13:** "The banquet halls were searched, as were the flower
> pavilions; the picture galleries were examined, and so were the pleasure
> houses.

---
14.
niṣkuṭāntararathyāśca vimānāni ca sarvaśaḥ |
iti saṁcintya bhūyo ’pi vicetum upacakrame ||

> **Meaning 14:** The paths between inner gardens and the aerial chambers were
> searched on every side." Reflecting thus, he set out to search yet again.

---
15.
bhūmīgr̥hāṁścaityagr̥hān gr̥hātigr̥hakān api |
utpatan nipataṁścāpi tiṣṭhan gacchan punaḥ kva cit ||

> **Meaning 15:** Leaping up and dropping down, stopping at times and moving on,
> he searched underground cellars, sanctuary halls, and detached inner mansions.

---
16.
apāvr̥ṇvaṁśca dvārāṇi kapāṭāny avaghaṭṭayan |
praviśan niṣpataṁścāpi prapatann utpatann api ||


> **Meaning 16:** Opening doorways, pushing past door leaves, entering and
> exiting, springing down and leaping high,

---
17.
sarvam apy avakāśaṁ sa vicacāra mahākapiḥ |
caturaṅgulamātro ’pi nāvakāśaḥ sa vidyate |
rāvaṇāntaḥpure tasmin yaṁ kapirna jagāma saḥ ||

> **Meaning 17:** the great Kapi roamed through every available space. There was
> not even four finger-breadths of space in Rāvaṇa's inner palace that the Kapi
> did not visit.

---
18.
prākarāntararathyāśca vedikaścaityasaṁśrayāḥ |
śvabhrāśca puṣkariṇyaśca sarvaṁ tenāvalokitam ||

> **Meaning 18:** The avenues between inner walls, the platforms around shrines,
> underground pits, and lotus ponds — everything was scrutinized by him.

---
19.
rākṣasyo vividhākārā virūpā vikr̥tāstathā |
dr̥ṣṭā hanūmatā tatra na tu sā janakātmajā ||

> **Meaning 19:** Rākṣasīs of diverse forms, deformed and grotesque, were seen
> there by Hanumān, but not the daughter of Janaka.

---
20.
rūpeṇāpratimā loke varā vidyādhara striyaḥ |
dr̥ṣṭā hanūmatā tatra na tu rāghavanandinī ||

> **Meaning 20:** Vidyādhara women of peerless beauty on earth were seen there
> by Hanumān, but not the joy of Rāghava.

---
21.
nāgakanyā varārohāḥ pūrṇacandranibhānanāḥ |
dr̥ṣṭā hanūmatā tatra na tu sītā sumadhyamā ||

> **Meaning 21:** Nāga maidens of graceful bearing, with faces like the full
> moon, were seen there by Hanumān, but not the slender-waisted Sītā.

---
22.
pramathya rākṣasendreṇa nāgakanyā balād dhr̥tāḥ |
dr̥ṣṭā hanūmatā tatra na sā janakanandinī ||

> **Meaning 22:** Nāga maidens overpowered and abducted by force by the king of
> Rākṣasas were seen there by Hanumān, but not the joy of Janaka.

---
23.
so ’paśyaṁstāṁ mahābāhuḥ paśyaṁścānyā varastriyaḥ |
viṣasāda mahābāhuḥ hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ ||

> **Meaning 23:** Failing to find Sītā while seeing all those other noble women,
> the mighty-armed Hanumān, son of Māruta, fell into dejection.

---
24.
udyogaṁ vānarendrāṇaṁ plavanaṁ sāgarasya ca |
vyarthaṁ vīkṣyānilasutaḥ cintāṁ punarupāgamat ||

> **Meaning 24:** Seeing the great efforts of the Vānara chiefs and his own leap
> across the ocean coming to nothing, the son of Anila was once again plunged
> into anxiety.

---
25.
avatīrya vimānāc ca hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ |
cintām upajagāmātha śokopahatacetanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 25:** Descending from the aerial palace, Hanumān, the son of Māruta,
> was weighed down with sorrow, his mind reeling with anxious thoughts.

---
