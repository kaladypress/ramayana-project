=== Sarga 32 ===

---
1.
tata śśākhāṁtarē līnaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā calitamānasā |
vēṣṭitārjunavastraṁ taṁ vidyutsaṁghātapiṁgalam ||

2.
sā dadarśa kapiṁ tatra praśritaṁ priyavādinam |
phullāśōkōtkarābhāsaṁ taptacāmīkarēkṣaṇam |
maithilī ciṁtayāmāsa vismayaṁ paramaṁ gatā ||

> **Meaning 1-2:** Then, her heart shaken, Maithilī caught sight of Hanumān
> nestled among the branches — wrapped in white cloth, tawny as a gathering of
> lightning. There Sītā saw the vānara, modest and gentle of speech, glowing
> like a mass of blossoming aśoka flowers, his eyes like molten gold. Filled with
> utter amazement, she fell into thought.

---
3.
ahō bhīmamidaṁ rūpaṁ vānarasya durāsadam |
durnirīkṣamiti jñātvā punarēva mumōha sā ||

> **Meaning 3:** "How fearsome this vānara's form is — unapproachable, hard
> even to look upon!" Thinking this, she fainted once more.

---
4.
vilalāpa bhr̥śaṁ sītā karuṇaṁ bhayamōhitā |
rāmarāmēti duḥkhārtā lakṣmaṇēti ca bhāminī ||

5.
rurōda bahudhā sītā maṁdaṁ maṁdasvarā satī ||

> **Meaning 4-5:** Overcome by fear and stricken with grief, the graceful Sītā
> lamented piteously, crying "Rāma! Rāma!" and "Lakṣmaṇa!" Again and again the
> virtuous Sītā wept, her voice soft and faint.

---
6.
sā taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā hariśrēṣṭhaṁ vinītavadupasthitam |
maithilī ciṁtayāmāsa svapnaḥ ayamiti bhāminī ||

> **Meaning 6:** Seeing that Kapivara standing humbly before her, the
> graceful Maithilī thought, "This must be a dream."

---
7.
sā vīkṣamāṇā pr̥thu bhugnavaktraṁ
śākhāmr̥gēṁdrasya yathōktakāram |
dadarśa piṁgādhipatēramātyaṁ
vātātmajaṁ buddhimatāṁ variṣṭham ||

> **Meaning 7:** As she looked on, she saw him clearly — the son of the Wind, with his broad, curved face. Foremost among the wise, he is the minister of Sugrīva, who carries out his every command.

---
8.
sā taṁ samīkṣyaiva bhr̥śaṁ visaṁjñā
gatāsukalpēva babhūva sītā |
cirēṇa saṁjñāṁ pratilabhya bhūyaḥ
viciṁtayāmāsa viśālanētrā ||

> **Meaning 8:** The moment she saw him, Sītā lost all consciousness and lay as
> if lifeless. Regaining her senses after a long while, the wide-eyed lady began
> to reflect once more:

---
9.
svapnē mayā’yaṁ vikr̥taḥ adya dr̥ṣṭaḥ
śākhāmr̥ga śśāstragaṇairniṣiddhaḥ |
svastyastu rāmāya sa lakṣmaṇāya
tathā piturmē janakasya rājñaḥ ||

> **Meaning 9:** "Today I have seen a misshapen vānara in a dream — a sight
> the scriptures call ill-omened. May all be well with Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa, and
> with my father, King Janaka.

---
10.
svapnaḥ api nāyaṁ nahi mē asti nidrā
śōkēna duḥkhēna ca pīḍitāyāḥ |
sukhaṁ hi mē nāsti yataḥ asmi hīnā
tēnēṁdupūrṇapratimānanēna ||

> **Meaning 10:** "But this cannot be a dream, for sleep never comes to me,
> tormented as I am by sorrow and grief. All happiness has left me, since I am
> parted from Śrī Rāma, whose face is like the full moon.

---
11.
rāmēti rāmēti sadaiva buddhyā
viciṁtya vācā bruvatī tamēva |
tasyānurūpāṁ ca kathāṁ tamartham
ēvaṁ prapaśyāmi tathā śr̥ṇōmi ||

> **Meaning 11:** "As I am continuously meditating on Śrī Rāma in my mind
> and chanting 'Rāma, Rāma', I am accordingly hearing only his stories and seeing
> only his form.

---
12.
ahaṁ hi tasyādya manōbhavēna
saṁpīḍitā tadgatasarvabhāvā |
viciṁtayaṁtī satataṁ tamēva
tathaiva paśyāmi tathā śr̥ṇōmi ||

> **Meaning 12:** "Tormented by my love for him, my entire being is devoted to him. Because I think of him constantly, it is only him I see, and only him I hear.

---
13.
manōrathassyāditi ciṁtayāmi
tathāpi buddhyā ca vitarkayāmi |
kiṁ kāraṇaṁ tasya hi nāsti rūpaṁ
suvyaktarūpaśca vadatyayaṁ mām ||

> **Meaning 13:** "I wonder if this is just a longing of my heart. Yet when I
> reason it through — how can that be? A longing has no form, but this one has
> a clear form, and he speaks to me.

---
14.
namaḥ astu vācaspatayē savajriṇē svayaṁbhuvē caiva hutāśanāya ca |
anēna cōktaṁ yadidaṁ mamāgratō vanaukasā tacca tathāstu nānyathā ||

> **Meaning 14:** "I bow to Bṛhaspati, god of speech, to Indra who wields the
> thunderbolt, to Brahmā, and to Agni, the Fire. May all that this
> vānara has said before me prove true, and nothing else."

---
