=== Sarga 32 ===

---
1.
tasyās tad vacanaṁ śrutvā hanūmān hariyūthapaḥ |
duḥkhād duḥkhābhibhūtāyāḥ sāntam uttaram abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
ahaṁ rāmasya saṁdeśād devi dūtas tavāgataḥ |
vaidehi kuśalī rāmas tvāṁ ca kauśalam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
yo brāhmam astraṁ vedāṁś ca veda vedavidāṁ varaḥ |
sa tvāṁ dāśarathī rāmo devi kauśalam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
lakṣmaṇaś ca mahātejā bhartus te ’nucaraḥ priyaḥ |
kr̥tavāñ śokasaṁtaptaḥ śirasā te ’bhivādanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
sā tayoḥ kuśalaṁ devī niśamya narasiṁhayoḥ |
prītisaṁhr̥ṣṭasarvāṅgī hanūmāntam athābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
kalyāṇī bata gatheyaṁ laukikī pratibhāti me |
ehi jīvantam ānado naraṁ varṣaśatād api ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tayoḥ samāgame tasmin prītir utpāditādbhutā |
paraspareṇa cālāpaṁ viśvastau tau pracakratuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tasyās tad vacanaṁ śrutvā hanūmān hariyūthapaḥ |
sītāyāḥ śokadīnāyāḥ samīpam upacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
yathā yathā samīpaṁ sa hanūmān upasarpati |
tathā tathā rāvaṇaṁ sā taṁ sītā pariśaṅkate ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
aho dhig dhik kr̥tam idaṁ kathitaṁ hi yad asya me |
rūpāntaram upāgamya sa evāyaṁ hi rāvaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tām aśokasya śākhāṁ sā vimuktvā śokakarśitā |
tasyām evānavadyāṅgī dharaṇyāṁ samupāviśat ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
avandata mahābāhus tatas tāṁ janakātmajām |
sā cainaṁ bhayavitrastā bhūyo naivābhyudaikṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā vandamānaṁ tu sītā śaśinibhānanā |
abravīd dīrgham ucchvasya vānaraṁ madhurasvarā ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
māyāṁ praviṣṭo māyāvī yadi tvaṁ rāvaṇaḥ svayam |
utpādayasi me bhūyaḥ saṁtāpaṁ tan na śobhanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
svaṁ parityajya rūpaṁ yaḥ parivrājakarūpadhr̥t |
janasthāne mayā dr̥ṣṭas tvaṁ sa evāsi rāvaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
upavāsakr̥śāṁ dīnāṁ kāmarūpa niśācara |
saṁtāpayasi māṁ bhūyaḥ saṁtāpaṁ tan na śobhanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
yadi rāmasya dūtas tvam āgato bhadram astu te |
pr̥cchāmi tvāṁ hariśreṣṭha priyā rāma kathā hi me ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
guṇān rāmasya kathaya priyasya mama vānara |
cittaṁ harasi me saumya nadīkūlaṁ yathā rayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
aho svapnasya sukhatā yāham evaṁ cirāhr̥tā |
preṣitaṁ nāma paśyāmi rāghaveṇa vanaukasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
svapne ’pi yady ahaṁ vīraṁ rāghavaṁ sahalakṣmaṇam |
paśyeyaṁ nāvasīdeyaṁ svapno ’pi mama matsarī ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
nāhaṁ svapnam imaṁ manye svapne dr̥ṣṭvā hi vānaram |
na śakyo ’bhyudayaḥ prāptuṁ prāptaś cābhyudayo mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
kiṁ nu syāc cittamoho ’yaṁ bhaved vātagatis tv iyam |
unmādajo vikāro vā syād iyaṁ mr̥gatr̥ṣṇikā ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
atha vā nāyam unmādo moho ’py unmādalakṣmaṇaḥ |
saṁbudhye cāham ātmānam imaṁ cāpi vanaukasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
ity evaṁ bahudhā sītā saṁpradhārya balābalam |
rakṣasāṁ kāmarūpatvān mene taṁ rākṣasādhipam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
etāṁ buddhiṁ tadā kr̥tvā sītā sā tanumadhyamā |
na prativyājahārātha vānaraṁ janakātmajā ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
sītāyāś cintitaṁ buddhvā hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ |
śrotrānukūlair vacanais tadā tāṁ saṁpraharṣayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
āditya iva tejasvī lokakāntaḥ śaśī yathā |
rājā sarvasya lokasya devo vaiśravaṇo yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
vikrameṇopapannaś ca yathā viṣṇur mahāyaśāḥ |
satyavādī madhuravāg devo vācaspatir yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
rūpavān subhagaḥ śrīmān kandarpa iva mūrtimān |
sthānakrodhaprahartā ca śreṣṭho loke mahārathaḥ |
bāhucchāyām avaṣṭabdho yasya loko mahātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
apakr̥ṣyāśramapadān mr̥garūpeṇa rāghavam |
śūnye yenāpanītāsi tasya drakṣyasi yat phalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
nacirād rāvaṇaṁ saṁkhye yo vadhiṣyati vīryavān |
roṣapramuktair iṣubhir jvaladbhir iva pāvakaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
tenāhaṁ preṣito dūtas tvatsakāśam ihāgataḥ |
tvadviyogena duḥkhārtaḥ sa tvāṁ kauśalam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
lakṣmaṇaś ca mahātejāḥ sumitrānandavardhanaḥ |
abhivādya mahābāhuḥ so ’pi kauśalam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
rāmasya ca sakhā devi sugrīvo nāma vānaraḥ |
rājā vānaramukhyānāṁ sa tvāṁ kauśalam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
nityaṁ smarati rāmas tvāṁ sasugrīvaḥ salakṣmaṇaḥ |
diṣṭyā jīvasi vaidehi rākṣasī vaśam āgatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
nacirād drakṣyase rāmaṁ lakṣmaṇaṁ ca mahāratham |
madhye vānarakoṭīnāṁ sugrīvaṁ cāmitaujasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
ahaṁ sugrīvasacivo hanūmān nāma vānaraḥ |
praviṣṭo nagarīṁ laṅkāṁ laṅghayitvā mahodadhim ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
kr̥tvā mūrdhni padanyāsaṁ rāvaṇasya durātmanaḥ |
tvāṁ draṣṭum upayāto ’haṁ samāśritya parākramam ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
nāham asmi tathā devi yathā mām avagacchasi |
viśaṅkā tyajyatām eṣā śraddhatsva vadato mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
