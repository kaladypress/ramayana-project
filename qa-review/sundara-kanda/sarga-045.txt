=== Sarga 45 ===

---
1.
tataste rākṣasendreṇa coditā mantriṇaḥ sutāḥ |
niryayurbhavanāt tasmāt sapta saptārcivarcasaḥ ||

> **Meaning 1:** Then, urged on by the king of the Rākṣasas, the seven sons of
> the ministers came out of that palace, each blazing like fire.

---
2.
mahābalaparīvārā dhanuṣmanto mahābalāḥ |
kr̥tāstrāstravidāṁ śreṣṭhāḥ parasparajayaiṣiṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 2:** They had huge armies around them and carried bows. All were
> mighty, among the best of those who know the use of divine weapons, and each
> was eager to outdo the others in victory.

---
3.
hemajālaparikṣiptaiḥ dhvajavadbhiḥ patākibhiḥ |
toyadasvananirghoṣaiḥ vājiyuktairmahārathaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 3:** They rode great chariots yoked with horses, covered in golden
> netting, with flags and banners, and rumbling like thunder clouds.

---
4.
taptakāñcanacitrāṇi cāpāny amitavikramāḥ |
visphārayantaḥ saṁhr̥ṣṭāḥ taḍidvanta ivāmbudāḥ ||

> **Meaning 4:** Fearless in their valor, they were thrilled as they twanged
> their bows, which were decorated with gleaming gold. They looked like clouds
> streaked with lightning.

---
5.
jananyastāstatasteṣāṁ viditvā kiṁkarān hatān |
babhūvuḥ śokasaṁbhrāntāḥ sabāndhavasuhr̥jjanāḥ ||

> **Meaning 5:** Their mothers heard that the Kiṁkaras had been slain. Together
> with their relatives and friends, they were overcome with grief and thrown into
> confusion.

---
6.
te parasparasaṁgharṣāḥ taptakāñcanabhūṣaṇāḥ |
abhipeturhanūmantaṁ toraṇastham avasthitam ||

> **Meaning 6:** Wearing ornaments of gleaming gold, and pressing close against
> one another, the sons rushed at Hanumān, who stood waiting on the archway.

---
7.
sr̥janto bāṇavr̥ṣṭiṁ te rathagarjitaniḥsvanāḥ |
vr̥ṣṭimanta ivāmbhodā vicerurnairr̥tarṣabhāḥ ||

> **Meaning 7:** Those bulls among the Nairṛtas moved about, pouring down
> showers of arrows. The roar of their chariots sounded like rain clouds pouring
> down rain.

---
8.
avakīrṇastatastābhiḥ hanūmāñ śaravr̥ṣṭibhiḥ |
abhavat saṁvr̥tākāraḥ śailarāḍ iva vr̥ṣṭibhiḥ ||

> **Meaning 8:** Covered by those showers of arrows, Hanumān looked like a king
> of mountains hidden under rain.

---
9.
sa śarān vañcayām āsa teṣām āśucaraḥ kapiḥ |
rathavegāṁśca vīrāṇāṁ vicaran vimale ambare ||

> **Meaning 9:** The swift Kapi dodged the arrows of those Rākṣasa warriors as he
> moved through the clear sky, evading the rush of their speeding chariots.

---
10.
sa taiḥ krīḍan dhanuṣmadbhiḥ vyomni vīraḥ prakāśate |
dhanuṣmadbhiryathā meghaiḥ mārutaḥ prabhurambare ||

> **Meaning 10:** Hanumān, the hero, sported in battle with those bowmen in the
> sky, treating the fight as a game. He shone like Māruta, the wind-god and lord
> of the sky, sporting among clouds bearing rainbows.

---
11.
sa kr̥tvā ninadaṁ ghoraṁ trāsayaṁstāṁ mahācamūm |
cakāra hanumān vegaṁ teṣu rakṣaḥsu vīryavān ||

> **Meaning 11:** Letting out a terrifying roar that frightened the great army,
> the mighty Hanumān charged at those Rākṣasas.

---
12.
talenābhihanat kāṁścit pādaiḥ kāṁścit paraṁtapaḥ |
muṣṭinābhyahanat kāṁścin nakhaiḥ kāṁścid vyadārayat ||

> **Meaning 12:** That scorcher of enemies struck some with his palm and some
> with his feet. He hit some with his fist and tore some apart with his nails.

---
13.
pramamāthorasā kāṁścid ūrubhyām aparān kapiḥ |
ke cit tasyaiva nādena tatraiva patitā bhuvi ||

> **Meaning 13:** The Kapi crushed some with his chest and others with his
> thighs. A few fell to the ground right there, just from his roar.

---
14.
tatasteṣv avapanneṣu bhūmau nipatiteṣu ca |
tat sainyam agamat sarvaṁ diśo daśabhayārditam ||

> **Meaning 14:** As some were dying and others lay fallen on the ground, the
> whole army fled in all ten directions, struck with fear.

---
15.
vinedurvisvaraṁ nāgā nipeturbhuvi vājinaḥ |
bhagnanīḍadhvajacchatraiḥ bhūśca kīrṇābhavad rathaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 15:** The elephants screamed wildly and the horses collapsed on the
> ground. The earth was littered with broken chariots, smashed nests, flags, and
> parasols.

---
16.
sravatā rudhirēṇātha sravaṁtyō darśitāḥ pathi |
vividhaiśca svarairlaṁkā nanāda vikr̥taṁ tadā ||

> **Meaning 16:** Streams of blood poured out and flowed along the road. Laṅkā
> cried out in many different voices, horribly, at that time.

---
17.
sa tān pravr̥ddhān vinihatya rākṣasān mahābalaścaṇḍaparākramaḥ kapiḥ |
yuyutsuranyaiḥ punareva rākṣasaiḥ tad eva vīraḥ abhijagāma toraṇam ||

> **Meaning 17:** The mighty Kapi, fierce in his valor, killed those arrogant
> Rākṣasas. The hero was eager to fight more of them, so he went back to the
> archway.

---
