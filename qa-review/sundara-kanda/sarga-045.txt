=== Sarga 43 ===

---
1.
tatas te rākṣasendreṇa coditā mantriṇaḥ sutāḥ |
niryayur bhavanāt tasmāt sapta saptārcivarcasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
mahābalaparīvārā dhanuṣmanto mahābalāḥ |
kr̥tāstrāstravidāṁ śreṣṭhāḥ parasparajayaiṣiṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
hemajālaparikṣiptair dhvajavadbhiḥ patākibhiḥ |
toyadasvananirghoṣair vājiyuktair mahārathaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
taptakāñcanacitrāṇi cāpāny amitavikramāḥ |
visphārayantaḥ saṁhr̥ṣṭās taḍidvanta ivāmbudāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
jananyas tās tatas teṣāṁ viditvā kiṁkarān hatān |
babhūvuḥ śokasaṁbhrāntāḥ sabāndhavasuhr̥jjanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
te parasparasaṁgharṣās taptakāñcanabhūṣaṇāḥ |
abhipetur hanūmantaṁ toraṇastham avasthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sr̥janto bāṇavr̥ṣṭiṁ te rathagarjitaniḥsvanāḥ |
vr̥ṣṭimanta ivāmbhodā vicerur nairr̥tarṣabhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
avakīrṇas tatas tābhir hanūmāñ śaravr̥ṣṭibhiḥ |
abhavat saṁvr̥tākāraḥ śailarāḍ iva vr̥ṣṭibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sa śarān vañcayām āsa teṣām āśucaraḥ kapiḥ |
rathavegāṁś ca vīrāṇāṁ vicaran vimale ’mbare ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
sa taiḥ krīḍan dhanuṣmadbhir vyomni vīraḥ prakāśate |
dhanuṣmadbhir yathā meghair mārutaḥ prabhur ambare ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sa kr̥tvā ninadaṁ ghoraṁ trāsayaṁs tāṁ mahācamūm |
cakāra hanumān vegaṁ teṣu rakṣaḥsu vīryavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
talenābhihanat kāṁś cit pādaiḥ kāṁś cit paraṁtapaḥ |
muṣṭinābhyahanat kāṁś cin nakhaiḥ kāṁś cid vyadārayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
pramamāthorasā kāṁś cid ūrubhyām aparān kapiḥ |
ke cit tasyaiva nādena tatraiva patitā bhuvi ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tatas teṣv avapanneṣu bhūmau nipatiteṣu ca |
tat sainyam agamat sarvaṁ diśo daśabhayārditam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
vinedur visvaraṁ nāgā nipetur bhuvi vājinaḥ |
bhagnanīḍadhvajacchatrair bhūś ca kīrṇābhavad rathaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sa tān pravr̥ddhān vinihatya rākṣasān; mahābalaś caṇḍaparākramaḥ kapiḥ |
yuyutsur anyaiḥ punar eva rākṣasais; tad eva vīro ’bhijagāma toraṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
