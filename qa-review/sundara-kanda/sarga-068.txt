=== Sarga 68 ===

---
1.
athāham uttaraṁ devyā punar uktaḥ sasaṁbhramam |
tava snehān naravyāghra sauhāryād anumānya ca ||

> **Meaning 1:** Tiger among men, out of her deep affection and love for you,
> the devī spoke to me again with great emotion, showing me great respect:

---
2.
evaṁ bahuvidhaṁ vācyo rāmo dāśarathis tvayā |
yathā mām āpnuyāc chīghraṁ hatvā rāvaṇam āhave ||

> **Meaning 2:** "You must speak to Rāma, son of Daśaratha, in every possible
> way, so that he may kill Rāvaṇa in battle and rescue me quickly.

---
3.
yadi vā manyase vīra vasaikāham ariṁdama |
kasmiṁś cit saṁvr̥te deśe viśrāntaḥ śvo gamiṣyasi ||

> **Meaning 3:** "Or if you think it wise, hero, tamer of enemies, stay here for
> just one day in some secluded spot. After you have rested, you can leave
> tomorrow.

---
4.
mama cāpy alpabhāgyāyāḥ sāmnidhyāt tava vānara |
asya śokavipākasya muhūrtaṁ syād vimokṣaṇam ||

> **Meaning 4:** "Vānara, for an unfortunate woman like me, having you near
> would bring even a moment's relief from this overwhelming grief.

---
5.
gate hi tvayi vikrānte punarāgamanāya vai |
prāṇānām api saṁdeho mama syān nātra saṁśayaḥ ||

> **Meaning 5:** "For once you, heroic one, leave to return, there is no doubt
> that my very life will be in peril.

---
6.
tavādarśanajaḥ śoko bhūyo māṁ paritāpayet |
duḥkhād duḥkhaparābhūtāṁ durgatāṁ duḥkhabhāginīm ||

> **Meaning 6:** "The sorrow of not seeing you will torment me even further,
> plunged as I am from sorrow into deeper sorrow, helpless and miserable.

---
7.
ayaṁ tu vīrasaṁdehas tiṣṭhatīva mamāgrataḥ |
sumahāṁs tvatsahāyeṣu haryr̥kṣeṣu asaṁśayaḥ ||

> **Meaning 7:** "Hero, a very grave doubt stands before my mind regarding your
> companions, the Haris and the R̥kṣas:

---
8.
kathaṁ nu khalu duṣpāraṁ tariṣyanti mahodadhim |
tāni haryr̥kṣasainyāni tau vā naravarātmajau ||

> **Meaning 8:** "How will those armies of Haris and R̥kṣas, or those two sons
> of the foremost of men, cross this vast ocean that is so difficult to bridge?

---
9.
trayāṇām eva bhūtānāṁ sāgarasyāsya laṅghane |
śaktiḥ syād vainateyasya vāyor vā tava vānagha ||

> **Meaning 9:** "Faultless one, only three beings have the power to cross this
> ocean: Vainateya, the wind, or you.

---
10.
tad asmin kāryaniyoge vīraivaṁ duratikrame |
kiṁ paśyasi samādhānaṁ brūhi kāryavidāṁ vara ||

> **Meaning 10:** "Therefore, hero, foremost among those skilled in action, when
> this task is so hard to accomplish, what solution do you see? Tell me!

---
11.
kāmam asya tvam evaikaḥ kāryasya parisādhane |
paryāptaḥ paravīraghna yaśasyas te balodayaḥ ||

> **Meaning 11:** "Slayer of enemy heroes, you alone are certainly enough to
> accomplish this mission; displaying your strength will bring you great renown.

---
12.
balaiḥ samagrair yadi māṁ hatvā rāvaṇam āhave |
vijayī svāṁ purīṁ rāmo nayet tat syād yaśaskaram ||

> **Meaning 12:** "Yet if Rāma kills Rāvaṇa in battle with his entire army, and
> takes me back to his city in triumph, that will bring him true glory.

---
13.
yathāhaṁ tasya vīrasya vanād upadhinā hr̥tā |
rakṣasā tad bhayād eva tathā nārhati rāghavaḥ ||

> **Meaning 13:** "Just as that Rākṣasa stole me away from that hero from the
> forest through deceit out of sheer fear of him, it is not fitting for Rāghava
> to act in that way.

---
14.
balais tu saṁkulāṁ kr̥tvā laṅkāṁ parabalārdanaḥ |
māṁ nayed yadi kākutsthas tat tasya sadr̥śaṁ bhavet ||

> **Meaning 14:** "If Kākutstha, crusher of enemy armies, overruns Laṅkā with
> his troops and leads me away, that will be truly worthy of him.

---
15.
tad yathā tasya vikrāntam anurūpaṁ mahātmanaḥ |
bhavaty āhavaśūrasya tathā tvam upapādaya ||

> **Meaning 15:** "Therefore, arrange matters in such a way that it befits the
> prowess of that great soul, a warrior renowned in battle."

---
16.
tad arthopahitaṁ vākyaṁ praśritaṁ hetusaṁhitam |
niśamyāhaṁ tataḥ śeṣaṁ vākyam uttaram abruvam ||

> **Meaning 16:** Hearing those meaningful, humble, and well-reasoned words of
> hers, I gave her the rest of my reply:

---
17.
devi haryr̥kṣasainyānām īśvaraḥ plavatāṁ varaḥ |
sugrīvaḥ sattvasaṁpannas tavārthe kr̥taniścayaḥ ||

> **Meaning 17:** "Devī, Sugrīva, the master of the armies of Haris and R̥kṣas
> and foremost among all who leap, is endowed with immense courage and is firmly
> resolved on your rescue.

---
18.
tasya vikramasaṁpannāḥ sattvavanto mahābalāḥ |
manaḥsaṁkalpasaṁpātā nideśe harayaḥ sthitāḥ ||

> **Meaning 18:** "Under his command stand Haris of mighty prowess, courageous
> and immensely strong, who can fly with the speed of thought.

---
19.
yeṣāṁ nopari nādhastān na tiryak sajjate gatiḥ |
na ca karmasu sīdanti mahatsv amitatejasaḥ ||

> **Meaning 19:** "Their course cannot be blocked above, below, or sideways;
> beings of boundless energy, they never falter in monumental tasks.

---
20.
asakr̥t tair mahābhāgair vānarair balasaṁyutaiḥ |
pradakṣiṇīkr̥tā bhūmir vāyumārgānusāribhiḥ ||

> **Meaning 20:** "Following the pathway of the wind, those illustrious and
> mighty Vānaras have circled the entire earth again and again.

---
21.
madviśiṣṭāś ca tulyāś ca santi tatra vanaukasaḥ |
mattaḥ pratyavaraḥ kaś cin nāsti sugrīvasaṁnidhau ||

> **Meaning 21:** "In Sugrīva's presence there are forest-dwellers superior to
> me and equal to me; there is none there who is inferior to me!

---
22.
ahaṁ tāvad iha prāptaḥ kiṁ punas te mahābalāḥ |
na hi prakr̥ṣṭāḥ preṣyante preṣyante hītare janāḥ ||

> **Meaning 22:** "If someone like me could reach here, what of those mighty
> warriors? For the foremost are never sent on reconnaissance missions — only
> lesser ones are sent!

---
23.
tad alaṁ paritāpena devi manyur vyapaitu te |
ekotpātena te laṅkām eṣyanti hariyūthapāḥ ||

> **Meaning 23:** "Therefore, devī, enough of grieving! Cast away your despair.
> In a single leap, those Hari troop leaders will reach Laṅkā.

---
24.
mama pr̥ṣṭhagatau tau ca candrasūryāv ivoditau |
tvatsakāśaṁ mahābhāge nr̥siṁhāv āgamiṣyataḥ ||

> **Meaning 24:** "And riding upon my back like the rising sun and moon, those
> two lions among men will come to you, blessed lady!

---
25.
arighnaṁ siṁhasaṁkāśaṁ kṣipraṁ drakṣyasi rāghavam |
lakṣmaṇaṁ ca dhanuṣpāṇiṁ laṅkā dvāram upasthitam ||

> **Meaning 25:** "You will soon see Rāghava, slayer of foes, lion-like,
> together with Lakṣmaṇa, bow in hand, standing right at the gates of Laṅkā.

---
26.
nakhadaṁṣṭrāyudhān vīrān siṁhaśārdūlavikramān |
vānarān vānarendrābhān kṣipraṁ drakṣyasi saṁgatān ||

> **Meaning 26:** "You will soon see the gathered Vānara heroes — armed with
> nails and fangs, possessing the prowess of lions and tigers, majestic like
> kings of Vānaras.

---
27.
śailāmbudan nikāśānāṁ laṅkāmalayasānuṣu |
nardatāṁ kapimukhyānām acirāc choṣyase svanam ||

> **Meaning 27:** "Soon you will hear the roar of the Kapi chiefs, resembling
> mountains and clouds, bellowing upon the ridges of Mount Malaya in Laṅkā.

---
28.
nivr̥ttavanavāsaṁ ca tvayā sārdham ariṁdamam |
abhiṣiktam ayodhyāyāṁ kṣipraṁ drakṣyasi rāghavam ||

> **Meaning 28:** "And having completed his exile in the forest, you will soon
> see Rāghava, tamer of enemies, consecrated as king in Ayodhyā together with
> you."

---
29.
tato mayā vāgbhir adīnabhāṣiṇī; śivābhir iṣṭābhir abhiprasāditā |
jagāma śāntiṁ mama maithilātmajā; tavāpi śokena tathābhipīḍitā ||

> **Meaning 29:** Comforted thus by my auspicious and reassuring words, that
> noble-spoken daughter of Mithilā found peace of mind, deeply tormented though
> she was by grief in separation from you.

---
