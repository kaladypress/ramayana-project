=== Sarga 31 ===

---
1.
tām abravīn mahātejā hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ |
śirasy añjalim ādhāya sītāṁ madhurayā girā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
kā nu padmapalāśākṣī kliṣṭakauśeyavāsinī |
drumasya śākhām ālambya tiṣṭhasi tvam aninditā ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
kimarthaṁ tava netrābhyāṁ vāri sravati śokajam |
puṇḍarīkapalāśābhyāṁ viprakīrṇam ivodakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
surāṇām asurāṇāṁ ca nāgagandharvarakṣasām |
yakṣāṇāṁ kiṁnarāṇāṁ ca kā tvaṁ bhavasi śobhane ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
kā tvaṁ bhavasi rudrāṇāṁ marutāṁ vā varānane |
vasūnāṁ vā varārohe devatā pratibhāsi me ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
kiṁ nu candramasā hīnā patitā vibudhālayāt |
rohiṇī jyotiṣāṁ śreṣṭhā śreṣṭhā sarvaguṇānvitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
kopād vā yadi vā mohād bhartāram asitekṣaṇā |
vasiṣṭhaṁ kopayitvā tvaṁ nāsi kalyāṇy arundhatī ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
ko nau putraḥ pitā bhrāta bhartā vā te sumadhyame |
asmāl lokād amuṁ lokaṁ gataṁ tvam anuśocasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
vyañjanāni hi te yāni lakṣaṇāni ca lakṣaye |
mahiṣī bhūmipālasya rājakanyāsi me matā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
rāvaṇena janasthānād balād apahr̥tā yadi |
sītā tvam asi bhadraṁ te tan mamācakṣva pr̥cchataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sā tasya vacanaṁ śrutvā rāmakīrtanaharṣitā |
uvāca vākyaṁ vaidehī hanūmantaṁ drumāśritam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
duhitā janakasyāhaṁ vaidehasya mahātmanaḥ |
sītā ca nāma nāmnāhaṁ bhāryā rāmasya dhīmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
samā dvādaśa tatrāhaṁ rāghavasya niveśane |
bhuñjānā mānuṣān bhogān sarvakāmasamr̥ddhinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tatas trayodaśe varṣe rājyenekṣvākunandanam |
abhiṣecayituṁ rājā sopādhyāyaḥ pracakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tasmin saṁbhriyamāṇe tu rāghavasyābhiṣecane |
kaikeyī nāma bhartāraṁ devī vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
na pibeyaṁ na khādeyaṁ pratyahaṁ mama bhojanam |
eṣa me jīvitasyānto rāmo yady abhiṣicyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
yat tad uktaṁ tvayā vākyaṁ prītyā nr̥patisattama |
tac cen na vitathaṁ kāryaṁ vanaṁ gacchatu rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
sa rājā satyavāg devyā varadānam anusmaran |
mumoha vacanaṁ śrutvā kaikeyyāḥ krūram apriyam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tatas tu sthaviro rājā satyadharme vyavasthitaḥ |
jyeṣṭhaṁ yaśasvinaṁ putraṁ rudan rājyam ayācata ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
sa pitur vacanaṁ śrīmān abhiṣekāt paraṁ priyam |
manasā pūrvam āsādya vācā pratigr̥hītavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
dadyān na pratigr̥hṇīyān na brūyat kiṁ cid apriyam |
api jīvitahetor hi rāmaḥ satyaparākramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
sa vihāyottarīyāṇi mahārhāṇi mahāyaśāḥ |
visr̥jya manasā rājyaṁ jananyai māṁ samādiśat ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
sāhaṁ tasyāgratas tūrṇaṁ prasthitā vanacāriṇī |
na hi me tena hīnāyā vāsaḥ svarge ’pi rocate ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
prāg eva tu mahābhāgaḥ saumitrir mitranandanaḥ |
pūrvajasyānuyātrārthe drumacīrair alaṁkr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
te vayaṁ bhartur ādeśaṁ bahu mānyadr̥ḍhavratāḥ |
praviṣṭāḥ sma purād dr̥ṣṭaṁ vanaṁ gambhīradarśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
vasato daṇḍakāraṇye tasyāham amitaujasaḥ |
rakṣasāpahr̥tā bhāryā rāvaṇena durātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
dvau māsau tena me kālo jīvitānugrahaḥ kr̥taḥ |
ūrdhvaṁ dvābhyāṁ tu māsābhyāṁ tatas tyakṣyāmi jīvitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
