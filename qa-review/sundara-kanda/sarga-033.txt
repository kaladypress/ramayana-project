=== Sarga 33 ===

---
1.
saḥ avatīrya drumāttasmāt vidrumapratimānanaḥ |
vinītavēṣaḥ kr̥paṇaḥ praṇipatyōpasr̥tya ca ||

2.
tāmabravīnmahātējā hanūmānmārutātmajaḥ |
śirasyaṁjalimādhāya sītāṁ madhurayā girā ||

> **Meaning 1-2:** Climbing down from the tree, his face glowing like coral,
> humble in bearing and meek in manner, the radiant Hanumān, son of the Wind,
> came near, bowed low, and with palms joined above his head, spoke to Sītā in a
> sweet voice:

---
3.
kā nu padmapalāśākṣi kliṣṭakauśēyavāsini |
drumasya śākhāmālaṁbya tiṣṭhasi tvamaniṁditē ||

> **Meaning 3:** "Who are you, lady of flawless beauty, with eyes like lotus
> petals and garments of soiled silk, standing here clinging to the branch of
> this tree?

---
4.
kimarthaṁ tava nētrābhyāṁ vāri sravati śōkajam |
puṁḍarīkapalāśābhyāṁ viprakīrṇamivōdakam ||

> **Meaning 4:** "Why do tears of grief stream from your eyes, like drops of
> water scattered from the petals of a white lotus?

---
5.
surāṇāmasurāṇāṁ vā nāgagaṁdharvarakṣasām |
yakṣāṇāṁ kinnarāṇāṁ vā kā tvaṁ bhavasi śōbhanē ||

> **Meaning 5:** "O graceful lady, who are you? Are you one of the devas or asuras,
> the nāgas, gandharvas or rākṣasas, the yakṣas or kinnaras?

---
6.
kā tvaṁ bhavasi rudrāṇāṁ marutāṁ vā varānanē |
vasūnāṁ vā varārōhē dēvatā pratibhāsi mē ||

> **Meaning 6:** "Fair-faced lady, graceful in form, do you belong to the
> Rudras, the Maruts or the Vasus? To me you seem a goddess.

---
7.
kiṁ nu caṁdramasā hīnā patitā vibudhālayāt |
rōhiṇī jyōtiṣāṁ śrēṣṭhā śrēṣṭhā sarvaguṇānvitā |
kā tvaṁ bhavasi kalyāṇi tvamaniṁditalōcanē ||

> **Meaning 7:** "Are you Rohiṇī, finest of the stars and graced with every
> virtue, fallen from the realm of the gods and parted from the Moon? Who are
> you, gracious lady with faultless eyes?

---
8.
kōpādvā yadi vā mōhāt bhartāramasitēkṣaṇē |
vasiṣṭhaṁ kōpayitvā tvaṁ nāsi kalyāṇyaruṁdhatī ||

> **Meaning 8:** "Or, dark-eyed lady, are you the blessed Arundhatī, who in a
> moment of anger or confusion provoked her husband Vasiṣṭha and came here?

---
9.
kō nu putraḥ pitā bhrātā bhartā vā tē sumadhyamē |
asmāllōkādamuṁ lōkaṁ gataṁ tvamanuśōcasi ||

> **Meaning 9:** "O slender-waisted lady, who is your son? Who is your father?
> Who is your brother? Who is your husband? Are you grieving for someone who has
> passed from this world to the next?"

---
10.
rōdanādatiniśśvāsāt bhūmisaṁsparśanādapi |
na tvāṁ dēvīmahaṁ manyē rājña ssaṁjñāvadhāraṇāt ||

> **Meaning 10:** "Yet from your weeping, your deep sighs, your feet touching
> the earth, and the royal marks you bear, I do not think you are a goddess.

---
11.
vyaṁjanāni ca tē yāni lakṣaṇāni ca lakṣayē |
mahiṣī bhūmipālasya rājakanyāsi mē matā ||

> **Meaning 11:** "From the signs and features I see in you, I take you to be
> the chief queen of a ruler of the earth, and a princess by birth.

---
12.
rāvaṇēna janasthānāt balādapahr̥tā yadi |
sītā tvamasi bhadraṁ tē tanmamācakṣva pr̥cchataḥ ||

> **Meaning 12:** "If you are Sītā, carried off by force from Janasthāna by
> Rāvaṇa — may all be well with you — then tell me, for I ask.

---
13.
yathā hi tava vai dainyaṁ rūpaṁ cāpyatimānuṣam |
tapasā cānvitō vēṣaḥ tvaṁ rāmamahiṣī dhruvam ||

> **Meaning 13:** "Your sorrow, your beauty beyond anything human, and your
> ascetic's dress all tell me that you must surely be Rāma's queen."

---
14.
sā tasya vacanaṁ śrutvā rāmakīrtanaharṣitā |
uvāca vākyaṁ vaidēhī hanūmaṁtaṁ drumāśritam ||

> **Meaning 14:** Hearing his words and overjoyed at the praise of Rāma,
> Vaidehī spoke to Hanumān as he stood by the tree:

---
15.
pr̥thivyāṁ rājasiṁhānāṁ mukhyasya viditātmanaḥ |
snuṣā daśarathasyāhaṁ śatrusainyapratāpinaḥ ||

> **Meaning 15:** "I am the daughter-in-law of Daśaratha — foremost among the
> lion-like kings of the earth, deeply wise, and the scourge of enemy
> armies.

---
16.
duhitā janakasyāhaṁ vaidēhasya mahātmanaḥ |
sītā ca nāma nāmnā’haṁ bhāryā rāmasya dhīmataḥ ||

> **Meaning 16:** "I am the daughter of great-souled Janaka, king of Videha. My
> name is Sītā, and I am the wife of wise Rāma.

---
17.
samā dvādaśa tatrāhaṁ rāghavasya nivēśanē |
bhuṁjānā mānuṣān bhōgān sarvakāmasamr̥ddhinī ||

> **Meaning 17:** "For twelve years I lived in Rāghava's palace, enjoying every
> human pleasure, with every wish fulfilled.

---
18.
tatra trayōdaśē varṣē rājyēnēkṣvākunaṁdanam |
abhiṣēcayituṁ rājā sōpādhyāyaḥ pracakramē ||

> **Meaning 18:** "Then, in the thirteenth year, the king, together with his
> preceptors, began preparations to crown Rāma, the joy of the Ikṣvākus, as heir
> to the kingdom.

---
19.
tasmin saṁbhriyamāṇē tu rāghavasyābhiṣēcanē |
kaikēyī nāma bhartāraṁ dēvī vacanamabravīt ||

> **Meaning 19:** "While everything was being gathered for Rāghava's
> coronation, Queen Kaikeyī said to King Daśaratha:

---
20.
na pibēyaṁ na khādēyaṁ pratyahaṁ mama bhōjanam |
ēṣa mē jīvitasyāṁtō rāmō yadyabhiṣicyatē ||

> **Meaning 20:** "'If Rāma is crowned, that will be the end of my life. From
> this day on, I will neither drink nor eat my daily food.

---
21.
yattaduktaṁ tvayā vākyaṁ prītyā nr̥patisattama |
taccēnna vitathaṁ kāryaṁ vanaṁ gacchatu rāghavaḥ ||

> **Meaning 21:** "'Best of kings, if the promise you once made me out of love
> is not to be broken, then let Rāghava go to the forest.'

---
22.
sa rājā satyavāgdēvyāḥ varadānamanusmaran |
mumōha vacanaṁ śrutvā kaikēyyāḥ krūramapriyam ||

> **Meaning 22:** "The king, ever true to his word, remembered the boons he had
> granted the queen — and on hearing Kaikeyī's cruel, bitter words, he fainted.

---
23.
tatastu sthavirō rājā satyē dharmē vyavasthitaḥ |
jyēṣṭhaṁ yaśasvinaṁ putraṁ rudan rājyamayācata ||

> **Meaning 23:** "Then the aged king, standing firm in truth and dharma,
> weeping, asked his illustrious eldest son to give up the kingdom.

---
24.
sa piturvacanaṁ śrīmān abhiṣēkāt paraṁ priyam |
manasā pūrvamāsādya vācā pratigr̥hītavān ||

> **Meaning 24:** "Glorious Rāma, to whom his father's word was dearer even than
> his coronation, accepted it first in his heart, and then gave his word.

---
25.
dadyānna pratigr̥hṇīyāt na brūyāt kiṁcidapriyam |
api jīvitahētōrvā rāma ssatyaparākramaḥ ||

> **Meaning 25:** "For Rāma, whose valour rests on truth, only gives and never
> takes; and he would never speak an unkind word, not even to save his own life.

---
26.
sa vihāyōttarīyāṇi mahārhāṇi mahāyaśāḥ |
visr̥jya manasā rājyaṁ jananyai māṁ samādiśat ||

> **Meaning 26:** "Laying aside his costly upper garments and renouncing the
> kingdom in his heart, that illustrious one entrusted me to the care of his
> mother, Kausalya.

---
27.
sāhaṁ tasyāgratastūrṇaṁ prasthitā vanacāriṇī |
na hi mē tēna hīnāyā vāsa ssvargē api rōcatē ||

> **Meaning 27:** "But I set out at once, ahead of him, to live in the forest —
> for without him, even a home in heaven would hold no joy for me.

---
28.
prāgēva tu mahābhāgaḥ saumitrirmitranaṁdanaḥ |
pūrvajasyānuyātrārthē drumacīrairalaṁkr̥taḥ ||

> **Meaning 28:** "Even before us, the noble son of Sumitrā,Lakṣmaṇa, who brings joy to his
> friends, had dressed himself in garments of bark to follow his elder brother.

---
29.
tē vayaṁ bharturādēśaṁ bahumānya dr̥ḍhavratāḥ |
praviṣṭā ssma purā’dr̥ṣṭaṁ vanaṁ gaṁbhīradarśanam ||

> **Meaning 29:** "Honouring our king's command and firm in our vows, we entered
> the deep, forbidding forest, a place we had never seen before.

---
30.
vasatō daṁḍakāraṇyē tasyāhamamitaujasaḥ |
rakṣasā’pahr̥tā bhāryā rāvaṇēna durātmanā ||

> **Meaning 30:** "While we were living in the Daṇḍaka forest, I, the wife of
> Śrī Rāma, the hero of boundless might, was carried off by the wicked rākṣasa
> Rāvaṇa.

---
31.
dvau māsau tēna mē kālō jīvitānugrahaḥ kr̥taḥ |
ūrdhvaṁ dvābhyāṁ tu māsābhyāṁ tatastyakṣyāmi jīvitam ||

> **Meaning 31:** "Rāvaṇa has granted me two more months to live. When those two
> months are over, I will give up my life."

---
