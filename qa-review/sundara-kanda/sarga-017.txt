=== Sarga 17 ===

---
1.
tataḥ kumudaṣaṇḍābho nirmalaṁ nirmalaḥ svayam |
prajagāma nabhaś candro haṁso nīlam ivodakam ||

> **Meaning 1:** Then the spotless moon, glowing like a bed of white water
> lilies, traversed the clear sky like a swan gliding across deep blue water.

---
2.
sācivyam iva kurvan sa prabhayā nirmalaprabhaḥ |
candramā raśmibhiḥ śītaiḥ siṣeve pavanātmajam ||

> **Meaning 2:** As if lending assistance with his pure radiance, the bright
> moon served the son of Pavana with his cool beams.

---
3.
sa dadarśa tataḥ sītāṁ pūrṇacandranibhānanām |
śokabhārair iva nyastāṁ bhārair nāvam ivāmbhasi ||

> **Meaning 3:** Then he saw Sītā, her face like the full moon, weighed down by
> the burden of sorrow like a heavily laden boat sinking in deep waters.

---
4.
didr̥kṣamāṇo vaidehīṁ hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ |
sa dadarśāvidūrasthā rākṣasīr ghoradarśanāḥ ||

> **Meaning 4:** As he gazed intent on seeing Vaidehī, Hanumān, the son of
> Māruta, saw terrifying Rākṣasīs stationed close by.

---
5.
ekākṣīm ekakarṇāṁ ca karṇaprāvaraṇāṁ tathā |
akarṇāṁ śaṅkukarṇāṁ ca mastakocchvāsanāsikām ||

> **Meaning 5:** Some had only one eye, some a single ear, some used their ears
> as blankets to cover themselves; some had no ears at all, some had spike-like
> ears, and some had nostrils that breathed out through the top of their heads.

---
6.
atikāyottamāṅgīṁ ca tanudīrghaśirodharām |
dhvastakeśīṁ tathākeśīṁ keśakambaladhāriṇīm ||

> **Meaning 6:** Some had colossal heads set on massive bodies, while others had
> long, slender necks; some had disheveled matted hair, some were bald, and some
> wore shaggy cloaks made of hair.

---
7.
lambakarṇalalāṭāṁ ca lambodarapayodharām |
lambauṣṭhīṁ cibukauṣṭhīṁ ca lambāsyāṁ lambajānukām ||

> **Meaning 7:** Some had drooping ears and sagging foreheads, pendulous bellies
> and sagging breasts; some had hanging lips, some had lips resting on their
> chins, some had elongated faces, and some had sagging knees.

---
8.
hrasvāṁ dīrghāṁ ca kubjāṁ ca vikaṭāṁ vāmanāṁ tathā |
karālāṁ bhugnavastrāṁ ca piṅgākṣīṁ vikr̥tānanām ||

> **Meaning 8:** Some were short, some extraordinarily tall, some hunchbacked,
> some misshapen, and some were dwarfs; some were dreadful in twisted attire,
> with yellowish eyes and monstrous faces.

---
9.
vikr̥tāḥ piṅgalāḥ kālīḥ krodhanāḥ kalahapriyāḥ |
kālāyasamahāśūlakūṭamudgaradhāriṇīḥ ||

> **Meaning 9:** Grotesque, tawny, pitch-black, easily enraged, and fond of
> quarrels, they brandished massive spears, iron spikes, and mallets forged of
> black iron.

---
10.
varāhamr̥gaśārdūlamahiṣājaśivā mukhāḥ |
gajoṣṭrahayapādāś ca nikhātaśiraso ’parāḥ ||

> **Meaning 10:** Some had the faces of wild boars, deer, tigers, buffaloes,
> goats, or jackals; others had the feet of elephants, camels, or horses, and
> some had heads sunken deep into their chests.

---
11.
ekahastaikapādāś ca kharakarṇyaśvakarṇikāḥ |
gokarṇīr hastikarṇīś ca harikarṇīs tathāparāḥ ||

> **Meaning 11:** Some had a single hand and a single foot; some had ears like
> donkeys, horses, cows, elephants, or Haris.

---
12.
anāsā atināsāś ca tiryan nāsā vināsikāḥ |
gajasaṁnibhanāsāś ca lalāṭocchvāsanāsikāḥ ||

> **Meaning 12:** Some were noseless, some had enormous noses, some had crooked
> noses or nostrils turned sideways; some had trunk-like noses like elephants,
> and some had nostrils breathing out from their foreheads.

---
13.
hastipādā mahāpādā gopādāḥ pādacūlikāḥ |
atimātraśirogrīvā atimātrakucodarīḥ ||

> **Meaning 13:** Some had the feet of elephants, giant feet, cattle hooves, or
> hairy tufts upon their feet; some had monstrous heads and necks, and enormous
> breasts and bellies.

---
14.
atimātrāsya netrāś ca dīrghajihvānakhās tathā |
ajāmukhīr hastimukhīr gomukhīḥ sūkarīmukhīḥ ||

> **Meaning 14:** Some had cavernous mouths and bulging eyes, long projecting
> tongues and claws; some had faces like goats, elephants, cows, or sows.

---
15.
hayoṣṭrakharavaktrāś ca rākṣasīr ghoradarśanāḥ |
śūlamudgarahastāś ca krodhanāḥ kalahapriyāḥ ||

> **Meaning 15:** Others had the faces of horses, camels, and donkeys—these
> dreadful Rākṣasīs held spears and mallets in hand, fierce-tempered and
> delighting in strife.

---
16.
karālā dhūmrakeśīś ca rakṣasīr vikr̥tānanāḥ |
pibantīḥ satataṁ pānaṁ sadā māṁsasurāpriyāḥ ||

> **Meaning 16:** Fierce, with smoke-colored hair and deformed features, these
> female demons were constantly drinking liquor, ever craving meat and strong
> spirits.

---
17.
māṁsaśoṇitadigdhāṅgīr māṁsaśoṇitabhojanāḥ |
tā dadarśa kapiśreṣṭho romaharṣaṇadarśanāḥ ||

> **Meaning 17:** Their bodies were smeared with raw flesh and blood, their food
> was meat and blood; the Kapīśreṣṭha looked upon these hideous creatures whose
> very sight made the hair stand on end.

---
18.
skandhavantam upāsīnāḥ parivārya vanaspatim |
tasyādhastāc ca tāṁ devīṁ rājaputrīm aninditām ||

> **Meaning 18:** They sat in a circle guarding a massive, thick-trunked tree,
> beneath which sat that divine, faultless princess.

---
19.
lakṣayām āsa lakṣmīvān hanūmāñ janakātmajām |
niṣprabhāṁ śokasaṁtaptāṁ malasaṁkulamūrdhajām ||

> **Meaning 19:** The glorious Hanumān observed Janaka's daughter, faded in
> luster, scorched by grief, her hair unwashed and matted.

---
20.
kṣīṇapuṇyāṁ cyutāṁ bhūmau tārāṁ nipatitām iva |
cāritrya vyapadeśāḍhyāṁ bhartr̥darśanadurgatām ||

> **Meaning 20:** She looked like a fallen star dropped to the earth when its
> store of merit was spent; rich in virtue and renowned character, she was
> destitute only in being denied the sight of her husband.

---
21.
bhūṣaṇair uttamair hīnāṁ bhartr̥vātsalyabhūṣitām |
rākṣasādhipasaṁruddhāṁ bandhubhiś ca vinākr̥tām ||

> **Meaning 21:** Stripped of fine ornaments, her only adornment was her
> unwavering love for her husband; imprisoned by the king of Rākṣasas, she was
> severed from all her kin.

---
22.
viyūthāṁ siṁhasaṁruddhāṁ baddhāṁ gajavadhūm iva |
candralekhāṁ payodānte śāradābhrair ivāvr̥tām ||

> **Meaning 22:** Like a female elephant separated from her herd and held
> captive by a lion, or like the crescent moon veiled by autumn clouds when the
> rainy season ends,

---
23.
kliṣṭarūpām asaṁsparśād ayuktām iva vallakīm |
sītāṁ bhartr̥hite yuktām ayuktāṁ rakṣasāṁ vaśe ||

> **Meaning 23:** neglected and worn from lack of care like an untuned vīṇā,
> Sītā, devoted solely to her husband's welfare, sat unsuitably trapped under
> the power of Rākṣasas.

---
24.
aśokavanikāmadhye śokasāgaram āplutām |
tābhiḥ parivr̥tāṁ tatra sagrahām iva rohiṇīm ||


> **Meaning 24:** Submerged in an ocean of grief in the heart of the Aśoka
> grove, she was surrounded by those monsters like the star Rohiṇī besieged by
> malefic planets.

---
25.
dadarśa hanumān devīṁ latām akusumām iva |
sā malena ca digdhāṅgī vapuṣā cāpy alaṁkr̥tā |
mr̥ṇālī paṅkadigdheva vibhāti ca na bhāti ca ||

> **Meaning 25:** Hanumān beheld that devī resembling a flowering creeper
> stripped of its blossoms; her form was soiled with dirt yet graced by natural
> elegance, like a mud-smeared lotus stalk that shines and yet seems dimmed.

---
26.
malinena tu vastreṇa parikliṣṭena bhāminīm |
saṁvr̥tāṁ mr̥gaśāvākṣīṁ dadarśa hanumān kapiḥ ||

> **Meaning 26:** Wrapped in a single soiled and frayed garment, that noble lady
> with fawn-like eyes was observed by the Kapi Hanumān.

---
27.
tāṁ devīṁ dīnavadanām adīnāṁ bhartr̥tejasā |
rakṣitāṁ svena śīlena sītām asitalocanām ||

> **Meaning 27:** Though her face was woeful, that devī remained undefeated
> through her husband's spiritual luster; protected by her own stainless
> character, dark-eyed Sītā sat firm.

---
28.
tāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā hanumān sītāṁ mr̥gaśāvanibhekṣaṇām |
mr̥gakanyām iva trastāṁ vīkṣamāṇāṁ samantataḥ ||

> **Meaning 28:** Seeing Sītā with eyes like a young fawn, glancing fearfully
> all around like a startled doe,

---
29.
dahantīm iva niḥśvāsair vr̥kṣān pallavadhāriṇaḥ |
saṁghātam iva śokānāṁ duḥkhasyormim ivotthitām ||

> **Meaning 29:** scorching the leafy trees with her hot sighs, seeming like an
> embodiment of sorrows or a rising crest of grief,

---
30.
tāṁ kṣāmāṁ suvibhaktāṅgīṁ vinābharaṇaśobhinīm |
praharṣam atulaṁ lebhe mārutiḥ prekṣya maithilīm ||

> **Meaning 30:** slender, exquisitely formed, radiating natural beauty even
> without ornaments, Māruti experienced boundless joy upon beholding Maithilī.

---
31.
harṣajāni ca so ’śrūṇi tāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā madirekṣaṇām |
mumoca hanumāṁs tatra namaś cakre ca rāghavam ||

> **Meaning 31:** Tears of joy streamed from Hanumān's eyes as he beheld that
> enchanting-eyed princess, and in that moment he offered his reverent
> salutations to Rāghava.

---
32.
namaskr̥tvā ca rāmāya lakṣmaṇāya ca vīryavān |
sītādarśanasaṁhr̥ṣṭo hanūmān saṁvr̥to ’bhavat ||

> **Meaning 32:** Having bowed in mind to Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa, the valiant
> Hanumān, ecstatic at having seen Sītā, remained quietly hidden within the
> tree.

---
