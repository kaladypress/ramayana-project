=== Sarga 30 ===

---
1.
hanumān api vikrāntaḥ sarvaṁ śuśrāva tattvataḥ |
sītāyāstrijaṭāyāśca rākṣasīnāṁ ca tarjanam ||

> **Meaning 1:** The valiant Hanumān, too, heard everything exactly as it
> happened — the words of Sītā and Trijaṭā, and the threats of the rākṣasīs.

---
2.
avekṣamāṇastāṁ devīṁ devatām iva nandane |
tato bahuvidhāṁ cintāṁ cintayām āsa vānaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 2:** Gazing at that noble lady,Sitā, who seemed like a goddess in the
> Nandana garden, Hanumān then turned many thoughts over in his mind.

---
3.
yāṁ kapīnāṁ sahasrāṇi subahūny ayutāni ca |
dikṣu sarvāsu mārgante seyam āsāditā mayā ||

> **Meaning 3:** "This Sītā, whom thousands upon thousands of vānaras are searching
> for in every direction — I have found her.

---
4.
cāreṇa tu suyuktena śatroḥ śaktim avekṣitā |
gūḍhena caratā tāvad avekṣitam idaṁ mayā ||

> **Meaning 4:** "Moving in secret like a skilled spy, I have taken the
> measure of the enemy's strength. I have completely observed and grasped all these matters.

---
5.
rākṣasānāṁ viśeṣaśca purī ceyam avekṣitā |
rākṣasādhipaterasya prabhāvo rāvaṇasya ca ||

> **Meaning 5:** "I have observed the nature of the rākṣasas, this city, and
> the might and attitude of Rāvaṇa, king of the rākṣasas.

---
6.
yuktaṁ tasyāprameyasya sarvasattvadayāvataḥ |
samāśvāsayituṁ bhāryāṁ patidarśanakāṅkṣiṇīm ||

> **Meaning 6:** "Sītā devī, the wife of that Śrī Rāma who shows compassion to
> all beings and possesses boundless strength and valor, is yearning for the sight
> of her husband. Now it is only proper that I console her."

---
7.
aham āśvāsayāmy enāṁ pūrṇacandranibhānanām |
adr̥ṣṭaduḥkhāṁ duḥkhasya na hy antam adhigacchatīm ||

> **Meaning 7:** "Sītā devī, whose radiant face resembles the full moon, had
> never before known any kind of sorrow. But now she is collapsing under a grief
> that seems to have no end. I shall console her."

---
8.
yadi hy aham imāṁ devīṁ śokopahatacetanām |
anāśvāsya gamiṣyāmi doṣavad gamanaṁ bhavet ||

> **Meaning 8:** "If I leave without consoling this noble lady, whose mind is
> crushed by grief, my journey would be meaningless."

---
9.
gate hi mayi tatreyaṁ rājaputrī yaśasvinī |
paritrāṇam avindantī jānakī jīvitaṁ tyajet ||

> **Meaning 9:** "If I leave without speaking to her, this illustrious princess Jānakī, seeing no
> hope of rescue, may well give up her life.

---
10.
mayā ca sa mahābāhuḥ pūrṇacandranibhānanaḥ |
samāśvāsayituṁ nyāyyaḥ sītādarśanalālasaḥ ||

> **Meaning 10:** "And mighty-armed Rāma, whose face is like the full moon and
> who yearns to see Sītā, also deserves to be comforted by me.

---
11.
niśācarīṇāṁ pratyakṣam akṣamaṁ cābhibhāṣaṇam |
kathaṁ nu khalu kartavyam idaṁ kr̥cchra gato hy aham ||

> **Meaning 11:** "Yet it would be unwise to speak to her in full view of these
> night-roaming women. How am I to do this? I am truly in a difficult position.

---
12.
anena rātriśeṣeṇa yadi nāśvāsyate mayā |
sarvathā nāsti saṁdehaḥ parityakṣyati jīvitam ||

> **Meaning 12:** "If I do not console her in what remains of this night,
> there is no doubt at all — she will give up her life.

---
13.
rāmaśca yadi pr̥cchen māṁ kiṁ māṁ sītābravīd vacaḥ |
kim ahaṁ taṁ pratibrūyām asaṁbhāṣya sumadhyamām ||

> **Meaning 13:** "And if Rāma asks me, 'What message did Sītā send for me?' —
> what could I tell him, without having spoken to that slender-waisted lady?

---
14.
sītāsaṁdeśarahitaṁ mām itastvarayā gatam |
nirdahed api kākutsthaḥ kruddhastīvreṇa cakṣuṣā ||

> **Meaning 14:** "If I hurry back from here without a message from Sītā,
> Śrī Rāma in his anger may burn me up with his fierce gaze.

---
15.
yadi ced yojayiṣyāmi bhartāraṁ rāmakāraṇāt |
vyartham āgamanaṁ tasya sasainyasya bhaviṣyati ||

> **Meaning 15:** "And even if I rouse my master Sugrīva to act for Rāma's
> cause, his march here with his army would be in vain (should Sītā give up
> her life in the meantime).

---
16.
antaraṁ tv aham āsādya rākṣasīnām iha sthitaḥ |
śanairāśvāsayiṣyāmi saṁtāpabahulām imām ||

> **Meaning 16:** "So I will stay right here, wait for a moment when the
> rākṣasīs are off guard, and gently console her, weighed down as she is by
> sorrow.

---
17.
ahaṁ hy atitanuścaiva vanaraśca viśeṣataḥ |
vācaṁ codāhariṣyāmi mānuṣīm iha saṁskr̥tām ||

> **Meaning 17:** "I am currently in a very tiny form, and a vānara besides.
> In the manner of ordinary humans, I shall speak with her in a simple and polite language.

---
18.
yadi vācaṁ pradāsyāmi dvijātiriva saṁskr̥tām |
rāvaṇaṁ manyamānā māṁ sītā bhītā bhaviṣyati ||

> **Meaning 18:** "But if I speak polished Sanskrit like a learned brāhmaṇa,
> Sītā will take me for Rāvaṇa and be frightened.

---
19.
avaśyam eva vaktavyaṁ mānuṣaṁ vākyam arthavat |
mayā sāntvayituṁ śakyā nānyatheyam aninditā ||

> **Meaning 19:** "I must speak meaningful words in the everyday language of
> ordinary people. There is no other way to comfort this pure-hearted lady.

---
20.
seyam ālokya me rūpaṁ jānakī bhāṣitaṁ tathā |
rakṣobhistrāsitā pūrvaṁ bhūyastrāsaṁ gamiṣyati ||

> **Meaning 20:** "Seeing my form and hearing my speech, Jānakī, who is
already terrified by the rākṣasīs, will be frightened again.

---
21.
tato jātaparitrāsā śabdaṁ kuryān manasvinī |
jānamānā viśālākṣī rāvaṇaṁ kāmarūpiṇam ||

> **Meaning 21:** "Then, gripped by fear, this proud, wide-eyed lady may cry
> out, believing me to be Rāvaṇa, who can take on any form at will.

---
22.
sītayā ca kr̥te śabde sahasā rākṣasīgaṇaḥ |
nānāpraharaṇo ghoraḥ sameyād antakopamaḥ ||

> **Meaning 22:** "And the moment Sītā cries out, the dreadful band of
> rākṣasīs, armed with every kind of weapon, will rush in like Death itself.

---
23.
tato māṁ saṁparikṣipya sarvato vikr̥tānanāḥ |
vadhe ca grahaṇe caiva kuryuryatnaṁ yathābalam ||

> **Meaning 23:** "Those hideous-faced women will then surround me on every
> side and try with all their strength to kill me or capture me.

---
24.
taṁ māṁ śākhāḥ praśākhāśca skandhāṁścottamaśākhinām |
dr̥ṣṭvā viparidhāvantaṁ bhaveyurbhayaśaṅkitāḥ ||

> **Meaning 24:** "Watching me dart across the branches, boughs and trunks of
> these towering trees, they will be seized with fear and alarm.

---
25.
mama rūpaṁ ca saṁprekṣya vanaṁ vicarato mahat |
rākṣasyo bhayavitrastā bhaveyurvikr̥tānanāḥ ||

> **Meaning 25:** "And seeing my huge form as I range through this great
> grove, the misshapen rākṣasīs will be struck with terror.

---
26.
tataḥ kuryuḥ samāhvānaṁ rākṣasyo rakṣasām api |
rākṣasendraniyuktānāṁ rākṣasendraniveśane ||

> **Meaning 26:** "Then the rākṣasīs will call for the rākṣasas whom the
> rākṣasa king has posted in his palace.

---
27.
te śūlaśaranistriṁśa vividhāyudhapāṇayaḥ |
āpateyurvimarde asmin vegenodvignakāriṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 27:** "Then, gripping spears, arrows, swords and weapons of every
> kind, they will storm into the fray, spreading panic as they come.

---
28.
saṁkruddhastaistu paritaḥ vidhaman rakṣasāṁ balam |
śaknuyaṁ na tu saṁprāptuṁ paraṁ pāraṁ mahodadheḥ ||

> **Meaning 28:** "Even if, in my fury, I scatter the rākṣasa host that hems
> me in on all sides, I may no longer have the strength to reach the far shore
> of the great ocean.

---
29.
māṁ vā gr̥hṇīyurāplutya bahavaḥ śīghrakāriṇaḥ |
syād iyaṁ cāgr̥hītārthā mama ca grahaṇaṁ bhavet ||

> **Meaning 29:** "Or many of them, swift to act, may leap on me and seize me.
> Then Sītā would never learn why I came, and I would be taken captive.

---
30.
hiṁsābhirucayo hiṁsyuḥ imāṁ vā janakātmajām |
vipannaṁ syāt tataḥ kāryaṁ rāmasugrīvayoridam ||

> **Meaning 30:** "Or those creatures who delight in cruelty may kill Janaka's
> daughter. Then this whole mission of Rāma and Sugrīva would be ruined.

---
31.
uddeśe naṣṭamārge asmin rākṣasaiḥ parivārite |
sāgareṇa parikṣipte gupte vasati jānakī ||

> **Meaning 31:** "Jānakī lives hidden in this remote place, where no path is
> known — ringed by rākṣasas and encircled by the sea.

---
32.
viśaste vā gr̥hīte vā rakṣobhirmayi saṁyuge |
nānyaṁ paśyāmi rāmasya sahāyaṁ kāryasādhane ||

> **Meaning 32:** "If I am slain or captured by the rākṣasas in battle, I see
> no one else who could help Rāma accomplish his purpose.

---
33.
vimr̥śaṁśca na paśyāmi yo hate mayi vānaraḥ |
śatayojanavistīrṇaṁ laṅghayeta mahodadhim ||

> **Meaning 33:** "However much I think it over, I cannot see a single vānara
> who could leap across this ocean, a hundred yojanas wide, if I were killed.

---
34.
kāmaṁ hantuṁ samarthaḥ asmi sahasrāṇy api rakṣasām |
na tu śakṣyāmi saṁprāptuṁ paraṁ pāraṁ mahodadheḥ ||

> **Meaning 34:** "I can certainly slay rākṣasas by the thousands — but then I
> would not be able to reach the far shore of the great ocean in time.

---
35.
asatyāni ca yuddhāni saṁśayo me na rocate |
kaśca niḥsaṁśayaṁ kāryaṁ kuryāt prājñaḥ sasaṁśayam ||

> **Meaning 35:** "The outcome of battle is never certain, and I have no taste
> for doubt. What wise person would turn a sure task into an uncertain one?

---
36.
eṣa doṣo mahān hi syān mama sītābhibhāṣaṇe |
prāṇatyāgaśca vaidehyā bhaved anabhibhāṣaṇe ||

> **Meaning 36:** "If I speak to Sītā, there is this great danger. Yet if I do
> not speak to her, Vaidehī may give up her life.

---
37.
bhūtāścārthā vinaśyanti deśakālavirodhitāḥ |
viklavaṁ dūtam āsādya tamaḥ sūryodaye yathā ||

> **Meaning 37:** "Even a mission already within reach falls apart in the hands
> of a bewildered messenger who acts against the demands of time and place —
> just as darkness melts away at sunrise.

---
38.
arthānarthāntare buddhiḥ niścitāpi na śobhate |
ghātayanti hi kāryāṇi dūtāḥ paṇḍitamāninaḥ ||

> **Meaning 38:** "Even a firm decision, made by weighing what helps against
> what harms, comes to nothing in such hands. Messengers who fancy themselves
> wise have wrecked many a mission.

---
39.
na vinaśyet kathaṁ kāryaṁ vaiklavyaṁ na kathaṁ bhavet |
laṅghanaṁ ca samudrasya kathaṁ nu na vr̥thā bhavet ||

> **Meaning 39:** "How can I keep this mission from being ruined? How can I
> avoid losing my nerve? How can I make sure my leap across the ocean was not
> in vain?

---
40.
kathaṁ nu khalu vākyaṁ me śr̥ṇuyān nodvijeta ca |
iti saṁcintya hanumāṁḥ cakāra matimān matim ||

> **Meaning 40:** "How can I get her to listen to my words without being
> alarmed?" Turning this over carefully, wise Hanumān made up his mind.

---
41.
rāmam akliṣṭakarmāṇaṁ svabandhum anukīrtayan |
nainām udvejayiṣyāmi tad bandhugatamānasām ||

> **Meaning 41:** "Śrī Rāma can accomplish even the most difficult tasks with ease, and he is my master. Right now, her mind is completely absorbed in him. Therefore, by singing his praises, I will ensure that she does not succumb to fear and doubt."

---
42.
ikṣvākūṇāṁ variṣṭhasya rāmasya viditātmanaḥ |
śubhāni dharmayuktāni vacanāni samarpayan ||

> **Meaning 42:** "Offering her the auspicious and righteous words of Rāma,
> foremost of the Ikṣvākus and master of himself,

---
43.
śrāvayiṣyāmi sarvāṇi madhurāṁ prabruvan giram |
śraddhāsyati yathā hīyaṁ tathā sarvaṁ samādadhe ||

> **Meaning 43:** "and speaking in a gentle voice, I will let her hear all of
> them. I will present everything in such a way that she comes to trust me."

---
44.
iti sa bahuvidhaṁ mahānubhāvaḥ jagatipateḥ pramadām avekṣamāṇaḥ |
madhuram avitathaṁ jagāda vākyaṁ drumaviṭapāntaram āsthito hanūmān ||

> **Meaning 44:** Having reflected in many ways like this, the great-souled
> Hanumān, gazing at the beloved wife of Jagannātha, spoke sweet and
> truthful words from his perch among the branches of the tree.

---
