=== Sarga 30 ===

---
1.
tataḥ śākhāntare līnaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā calitamānasā |
sā dadarśa kapiṁ tatra praśritaṁ priyavādinam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sā tu dr̥ṣṭvā hariśreṣṭhaṁ vinītavad upasthitam |
maithilī cintayām āsa svapno ’yam iti bhāminī ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sā taṁ samīkṣyaiva bhr̥śaṁ visaṁjñā; gatāsukalpeva babhūva sītā |
cireṇa saṁjñāṁ pratilabhya caiva; vicintayām āsa viśālanetrā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
svapno mayāyaṁ vikr̥to ’dya dr̥ṣṭaḥ; śākhāmr̥gaḥ śāstragaṇair niṣiddhaḥ |
svasty astu rāmāya salakṣmaṇāya; tathā pitur me janakasya rājñaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
svapno ’pi nāyaṁ na hi me ’sti nidrā; śokena duḥkhena ca pīḍitāyāḥ |
sukhaṁ hi me nāsti yato ’smi hīnā; tenendupūrṇapratimānanena ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
ahaṁ hi tasyādya mano bhavena; saṁpīḍitā tad gatasarvabhāvā |
vicintayantī satataṁ tam eva; tathaiva paśyāmi tathā śr̥ṇomi ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
manorathaḥ syād iti cintayāmi; tathāpi buddhyā ca vitarkayāmi |
kiṁ kāraṇaṁ tasya hi nāsti rūpaṁ; suvyaktarūpaś ca vadaty ayaṁ mām ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
namo ’stu vācaspataye savajriṇe; svayambhuve caiva hutāśanāya |
anena coktaṁ yad idaṁ mamāgrato; vanaukasā tac ca tathāstu nānyathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
