=== Sarga 16 ===

---
1.
praśasya tu praśastavyāṁ sītāṁ tāṁ haripuṁgavaḥ |
guṇābhirāmaṁ rāmaṁ ca punaś cintāparo ’bhavat ||

> **Meaning 1:** Having praised Sītā, who is worthy of praise, and Rāma,
> delightful in his virtues, that foremost among Haris again became lost in
> thought.

---
2.
sa muhūrtam iva dhyātvā bāṣpaparyākulekṣaṇaḥ |
sītām āśritya tejasvī hanumān vilalāpa ha ||

> **Meaning 2:** Reflecting for a moment with eyes swimming in tears, the
> radiant Hanumān lamented over Sītā's plight:

---
3.
mānyā guruvinītasya lakṣmaṇasya gurupriyā |
yadi sītāpi duḥkhārtā kālo hi duratikramaḥ ||

> **Meaning 3:** "If even Sītā, who is revered by elders, beloved to her family,
> and respected by the disciplined Lakṣmaṇa, is stricken with sorrow, then Time
> is truly insurmountable!

---
4.
rāmasya vyavasāyajñā lakṣmaṇasya ca dhīmataḥ |
nātyarthaṁ kṣubhyate devī gaṅgeva jaladāgame ||

> **Meaning 4:** Knowing the resolve of Rāma and the wise Lakṣmaṇa, this devī
> does not lose her composure entirely, just as the Gaṅgā holds steady even with
> the arrival of the rains.

---
5.
tulyaśīlavayovr̥ttāṁ tulyābhijanalakṣaṇām |
rāghavo ’rhati vaidehīṁ taṁ ceyam asitekṣaṇā ||

> **Meaning 5:** Rāghava is truly worthy of Vaidehī, who matches him in
> character, age, conduct, lineage, and auspicious marks; and this dark-eyed
> lady is equally worthy of him."

---
6.
tāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā navahemābhāṁ lokakāntām iva śriyam |
jagāma manasā rāmaṁ vacanaṁ cedam abravīt ||

> **Meaning 6:** Gazing upon her, who possessed the luster of freshly smelted
> gold and resembled Goddess Lakṣmī, cherished by all the world, he approached
> Rāma in his thoughts and said:

---
7.
asyā hetor viśālākṣyā hato vālī mahābalaḥ |
rāvaṇapratimo vīrye kabandhaś ca nipātitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 7:** "It was for the sake of this wide-eyed lady that the mighty
> Vālī was slain, and Kabandha, whose prowess rivaled Rāvaṇa's, was brought
> down.

---
8.
virādhaś ca hataḥ saṁkhye rākṣaso bhīmavikramaḥ |
vane rāmeṇa vikramya mahendreṇeva śambaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 8:** In the forest, the terribly valiant Rākṣasa Virādha was struck
> down in combat by Rāma's valor, just as the demon Śambara was slain by mighty
> Indra.

---
9.
caturdaśasahasrāṇi rakṣasāṁ bhīmakarmaṇām |
nihatāni janasthāne śarair agniśikhopamaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 9:** Fourteen thousand Rākṣasas of dread deeds were destroyed in
> Janasthāna by his arrows that blazed like tongues of fire.

---
10.
kharaś ca nihataḥ saṁkhye triśirāś ca nipātitaḥ |
dūṣaṇaś ca mahātejā rāmeṇa viditātmanā ||

> **Meaning 10:** Khara was slain in battle, Triśiras was struck down, and the
> formidable Dūṣaṇa was killed by the wise and self-mastered Rāma.

---
11.
aiśvaryaṁ vānarāṇāṁ ca durlabhaṁ vālipālitam |
asyā nimitte sugrīvaḥ prāptavām̐l lokasatkr̥tam ||

> **Meaning 11:** It was on her account that Sugrīva attained the kingship of
> the Vānaras, so hard to win, formerly guarded by Vālī and revered by the
> world.

---
12.
sāgaraś ca mayā krāntaḥ śrīmān nadanadīpatiḥ |
asyā hetor viśālākṣyāḥ purī ceyaṁ nirīkṣitā ||

> **Meaning 12:** It is for the sake of this wide-eyed lady that I crossed the
> glorious ocean, king of rivers and streams, and searched this entire city.

---
13.
yadi rāmaḥ samudrāntāṁ medinīṁ parivartayet |
asyāḥ kr̥te jagac cāpi yuktam ity eva me matiḥ ||

> **Meaning 13:** If Rāma were to turn the entire ocean-girdled earth or even
> the whole universe upside down for her sake, I believe it would be entirely
> justified!

---
14.
rājyaṁ vā triṣu lokeṣu sītā vā janakātmajā |
trailokyarājyaṁ sakalaṁ sītāyā nāpnuyāt kalām ||

> **Meaning 14:** If sovereignty over the three worlds were weighed against
> Sītā, the daughter of Janaka, the empire of all three worlds combined would
> not equal a single fraction of her worth.

---
15.
iyaṁ sā dharmaśīlasya maithilasya mahātmanaḥ |
sutā janakarājasya sītā bhartr̥dr̥ḍhavratā ||

> **Meaning 15:** This is she: the daughter of the noble and virtuous King
> Janaka of Mithilā, Sītā, unshakeable in her devotion to her husband.

---
16.
utthitā medinīṁ bhittvā kṣetre halamukhakṣate |
padmareṇunibhaiḥ kīrṇā śubhaiḥ kedārapāṁsubhiḥ ||

> **Meaning 16:** She who arose cleaving through the soil when the field was
> furrowed by the plow's blade, dusted with the auspicious, lotus-pollen-like
> soil of the field.

---
17.
vikrāntasyāryaśīlasya saṁyugeṣv anivartinaḥ |
snuṣā daśarathasyaiṣā jyeṣṭhā rājño yaśasvinī ||

> **Meaning 17:** This renowned princess is the eldest daughter-in-law of King
> Daśaratha, a hero of noble character who never turned back in battle.

---
18.
dharmajñasya kr̥tajñasya rāmasya viditātmanaḥ |
iyaṁ sā dayitā bhāryā rākṣasī vaśam āgatā ||

> **Meaning 18:** This is the cherished wife of the righteous, grateful, and
> self-realized Rāma, now fallen under the power of Rākṣasīs!

---
19.
sarvān bhogān parityajya bhartr̥snehabalāt kr̥tā |
acintayitvā duḥkhāni praviṣṭā nirjanaṁ vanam ||

> **Meaning 19:** Driven by the power of love for her husband, she renounced all
> royal comforts, disregarded every hardship, and entered the uninhabited
> forest.

---
20.
saṁtuṣṭā phalamūlena bhartr̥śuśrūṣaṇe ratā |
yā parāṁ bhajate prītiṁ vane ’pi bhavane yathā ||

> **Meaning 20:** Content with fruits and roots and devoted to attending upon
> her husband, she experienced the greatest joy in the forest, just as if she
> were living in a palace.

---
21.
seyaṁ kanakavarṇāṅgī nityaṁ susmitabhāṣiṇī |
sahate yātanām etām anarthānām abhāginī ||

> **Meaning 21:** That lady of golden complexion, who always speaks with a sweet
> smile and never deserved misfortune, is now enduring this terrible agony.

---
22.
imāṁ tu śīlasaṁpannāṁ draṣṭum icchati rāghavaḥ |
rāvaṇena pramathitāṁ prapām iva pipāsitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 22:** Rāghava longs to see this woman of flawless conduct, who is
> now ravaged by Rāvaṇa, just as a thirsty traveler longs for a ruined
> water-station.

---
23.
asyā nūnaṁ punar lābhād rāghavaḥ prītim eṣyati |
rājā rājyaparibhraṣṭaḥ punaḥ prāpyeva medinīm ||

> **Meaning 23:** Upon regaining her, Rāghava will surely feel the same joy that
> a deposed king feels when he reclaims his realm.

---
24.
kāmabhogaiḥ parityaktā hīnā bandhujanena ca |
dhārayaty ātmano dehaṁ tatsamāgamakāṅkṣiṇī ||

> **Meaning 24:** Forsaking pleasures and comforts, cut off from her kin, she
> preserves her life only out of her deep yearning to be reunited with him.

---
25.
naiṣā paśyati rākṣasyo nemān puṣpaphaladrumān |
ekasthahr̥dayā nūnaṁ rāmam evānupaśyati ||

> **Meaning 25:** She does not even notice these Rākṣasīs, nor these trees laden
> with flowers and fruits; with a singularly focused heart, she sees only Rāma.

---
26.
bhartā nāma paraṁ nāryā bhūṣaṇaṁ bhūṣaṇād api |
eṣā hi rahitā tena śobhanārhā na śobhate ||

> **Meaning 26:** A husband is truly a woman's greatest ornament, surpassing all
> other jewels; deprived of him, this lady, though worthy of radiant beauty,
> does not shine.

---
27.
duṣkaraṁ kurute rāmo hīno yad anayā prabhuḥ |
dhārayaty ātmano dehaṁ na duḥkhenāvasīdati ||

> **Meaning 27:** Rāma, that capable master, achieves an impossible feat by
> sustaining his life and refusing to sink under grief despite being separated
> from her.

---
28.
imām asitakeśāntāṁ śatapatranibhekṣaṇām |
sukhārhāṁ duḥkhitāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā mamāpi vyathitaṁ manaḥ ||

> **Meaning 28:** Seeing this lady of dark tresses and lotus-petal eyes, who
> deserves only happiness yet suffers so bitterly, even my mind is shaken with
> grief.

---
29.
kṣitikṣamā puṣkarasaṁnibhākṣī; yā rakṣitā rāghavalakṣmaṇābhyām |
sā rākṣasībhir vikr̥tekṣaṇābhiḥ; saṁrakṣyate saṁprati vr̥kṣamūle ||

> **Meaning 29:** She whose forbearance matches the earth and whose eyes are
> like lotuses, who was once protected by Rāghava and Lakṣmaṇa, is now watched
> over beneath a tree by hideous-eyed Rākṣasīs.

---
30.
himahatanalinīva naṣṭaśobhā; vyasanaparamparayā nipīḍyamānā |
sahacararahiteva cakravākī; janakasutā kr̥paṇāṁ daśāṁ prapannā ||

> **Meaning 30:** Like a lotus blighted by frost, its beauty withered, tormented
> by continuous calamities like a female cakravāka separated from her mate, the
> daughter of Janaka has fallen into a pitiful state.

---
31.
asyā hi puṣpāvanatāgraśākhāḥ; śokaṁ dr̥ḍhaṁ vai janayaty aśokāḥ |
himavyapāyena ca mandaraśmir; abhyutthito naikasahasraraśmiḥ ||

> **Meaning 31:** These aśoka trees, their branch tips heavy with flowers, and
> the moon rising with thousands of cool beams as winter passes, only deepen her
> intense grief."

---
32.
ity evam arthaṁ kapir anvavekṣya; sīteyam ity eva niviṣṭabuddhiḥ |
saṁśritya tasmin niṣasāda vr̥kṣe; balī harīṇām r̥ṣabhas tarasvī ||

> **Meaning 32:** Reflecting upon all this, his intellect firmly resolved that
> this was indeed Sītā, that mighty and swift hero, the bull among Haris,
> remained perched within that tree.

---
