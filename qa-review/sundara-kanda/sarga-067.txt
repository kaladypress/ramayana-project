=== Sarga 67 ===

---
1.
evam uktas tu hanumān rāghaveṇa mahātmanā |
sītāyā bhāṣitaṁ sarvaṁ nyavedayata rāghave ||

> **Meaning 1:** Addressed thus by the great-souled Rāghava, Hanumān reported to
> Rāghava everything Sītā had said:

---
2.
idam uktavatī devī jānakī puruṣarṣabha |
pūrvavr̥ttam abhijñānaṁ citrakūṭe yathā tatham ||

> **Meaning 2:** "Foremost of men, Devī Jānakī spoke these words, recalling in
> full truth the past token of recognition that took place on Citrakūṭa:

---
3.
sukhasuptā tvayā sārdhaṁ jānakī pūrvam utthitā |
vāyasaḥ sahasotpatya virarāda stanāntare ||

> **Meaning 3:** 'Having slept peacefully with you, Jānakī woke up first.
> Suddenly a crow flew down and clawed at her breast.

---
4.
paryāyeṇa ca suptas tvaṁ devyaṅke bharatāgraja |
punaś ca kila pakṣī sa devyā janayati vyathām ||

> **Meaning 4:** In turn you fell asleep, elder brother of Bharata, resting in
> the devī's lap. Then that bird came back and began tormenting the devī again.

---
5.
tataḥ punar upāgamya virarāda bhr̥śaṁ kila |
tatas tvaṁ bodhitas tasyāḥ śoṇitena samukṣitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 5:** Returning once more, he tore into her flesh viciously. You were
> awakened then, sprinkled by drops of her blood.

---
6.
vāyasena ca tenaiva satataṁ bādhyamānayā |
bodhitaḥ kila devyās tvaṁ sukhasuptaḥ paraṁtapa ||

> **Meaning 6:** Scorcher of foes, sleeping so peacefully, you were awakened by
> the devī as she was relentlessly harassed by that crow.

---
7.
tāṁ tu dr̥ṣṭvā mahābāho rāditāṁ ca stanāntare |
āśīviṣa iva kruddho niḥśvasann abhyabhāṣathāḥ ||

> **Meaning 7:** Mighty-armed hero, seeing her torn at the breast, you hissed in
> fury like a venomous serpent and said:

---
8.
nakhāgraiḥ kena te bhīru dāritaṁ tu stanāntaram |
kaḥ krīḍati saroṣeṇa pañcavaktreṇa bhoginā ||

> **Meaning 8:** "Gentle lady, who has ripped your breast with sharp claws? Who
> is trifling with an enraged five-hooded serpent?"

---
9.
nirīkṣamāṇaḥ sahasā vāyasaṁ samavaikṣatāḥ |
nakhaiḥ sarudhirais tīkṣṇair mām evābhimukhaṁ sthitam ||

> **Meaning 9:** Glancing around, you suddenly caught sight of the crow standing
> right in front of me, its sharp claws stained with blood.

---
10.
sutaḥ kila sa śakrasya vāyasaḥ patatāṁ varaḥ |
dharāntaracaraḥ śīghraṁ pavanasya gatau samaḥ ||

> **Meaning 10:** That crow, foremost among winged creatures, was none other
> than the son of Śakra, capable of darting through the earth and swift as the
> wind.

---
11.
tatas tasmin mahābāho kopasaṁvartitekṣaṇaḥ |
vāyase tvaṁ kr̥tvāḥ krūrāṁ matiṁ matimatāṁ vara ||

> **Meaning 11:** Mighty-armed prince, best among the wise, your eyes rolling in
> wrath, you resolved on a dreadful punishment for that crow.

---
12.
sa darbhaṁ saṁstarād gr̥hya brahmāstreṇa nyayojayaḥ |
sa dīpta iva kālāgnir jajvālābhimukhaḥ khagam ||

> **Meaning 12:** You pulled a blade of darbha grass from your mat and charged
> it with the Brahmāstra. Blazing like the fire of cosmic dissolution, it flared
> toward the bird.

---
13.
sa tvaṁ pradīptaṁ cikṣepa darbhaṁ taṁ vāyasaṁ prati |
tatas tu vāyasaṁ dīptaḥ sa darbho ’nujagāma ha ||

> **Meaning 13:** You hurled that blazing grass blade at the crow, and the
> burning darbha pursued the bird relentlessly.

---
14.
sa pitrā ca parityaktaḥ suraiḥ sarvair maharṣibhiḥ |
trīm̐l lokān saṁparikramya trātāraṁ nādhigacchati ||

> **Meaning 14:** Abandoned by his father, by all the gods, and by the great
> r̥ṣis, he traversed all three worlds but found no protector anywhere.

---
15.
taṁ tvaṁ nipatitaṁ bhūmau śaraṇyaḥ śaraṇāgatam |
vadhārham api kākutstha kr̥payā paripālayaḥ ||

> **Meaning 15:** When he fell to the ground seeking refuge, you, the refuge of
> all, spared his life out of mercy, Kākutstha, even though he deserved death.

---
16.
mogham astraṁ na śakyaṁ tu kartum ity eva rāghava |
tatas tasyākṣikākasya hinasti sma sa dakṣiṇam ||

> **Meaning 16:** Knowing that the divine weapon could never go in vain,
> Rāghava, you had it strike the crow's right eye.

---
17.
rāma tvāṁ sa namaskr̥tvā rājño daśarathasya ca |
visr̥ṣṭas tu tadā kākaḥ pratipede kham ālayam ||

> **Meaning 17:** Bowing low to you and to King Daśaratha, the crow was released
> and flew back into the sky to his home.

---
18.
evam astravidāṁ śreṣṭhaḥ sattvavāñ śīlavān api |
kimartham astraṁ rakṣaḥsu na yojayasi rāghava ||

> **Meaning 18:** Rāghava, you are foremost among masters of weapons, powerful
> and righteous — why then do you not unleash your weapons upon the Rākṣasas?

---
19.
na nāgā nāpi gandharvā nāsurā na marudgaṇāḥ |
tava rāma mukhe sthātuṁ śaktāḥ pratisamādhitum ||

> **Meaning 19:** Neither Nāgas, nor Gandharvas, nor Asuras, nor the hosts of
> the Maruts are able to stand before you and counter you in battle, Rāma!

---
20.
tava vīryavataḥ kaccin mayi yady asti saṁbhramaḥ |
kṣipraṁ suniśitair bāṇair hanyatāṁ yudhi rāvaṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 20:** If you, full of valor, still have any tender regard for me,
> let Rāvaṇa be slain swiftly in battle with your sharp arrows!

---
21.
bhrātur ādeśam ādāya lakṣmaṇo vā paraṁtapaḥ |
sa kimarthaṁ naravaro na māṁ rakṣati rāghavaḥ ||

> **Meaning 21:** Or Lakṣmaṇa, the scorcher of foes, acting on his brother's
> command — why does that best of men, born of Raghu, not rescue me?

---
22.
śaktau tau puruṣavyāghrau vāyvagnisamatejasau |
surāṇām api durdharṣau kimarthaṁ mām upekṣataḥ ||

> **Meaning 22:** Those two tigers among men are capable and match wind and fire
> in brilliance, unassailable even by the gods — why do they ignore me?

---
23.
mamaiva duṣkr̥taṁ kiṁ cin mahad asti na saṁśayaḥ |
samarthau sahitau yan māṁ nāpekṣete paraṁtapau ||

> **Meaning 23:** Surely I must have committed some grave past sin, for both
> those scorchers of foes, powerful though they are, do not look after me.'

---
24.
vaidehyā vacanaṁ śrutvā karuṇaṁ sāśrubhāṣitam |
punar apy aham āryāṁ tām idaṁ vacanam abruvam ||

> **Meaning 24:** Hearing these piteous words spoken in tears by Vaidehī, I
> spoke once more to that noble lady:

---
25.
tvacchokavimukho rāmo devi satyena te śape |
rāme duḥkhābhibhūte ca lakṣmaṇaḥ paritapyate ||

> **Meaning 25:** 'Devī, I swear to you in truth: Rāma has turned his back on
> all joys in his grief for you; and with Rāma overwhelmed by sorrow, Lakṣmaṇa
> grieves bitterly too.

---
26.
kathaṁ cid bhavatī dr̥ṣṭā na kālaḥ pariśocitum |
imaṁ muhūrtaṁ duḥkhānām antaṁ drakṣyasi bhāmini ||

> **Meaning 26:** Now that you have been found after great effort, this is no
> time to despair; beautiful lady, you will soon see the end of all your
> sorrows!

---
27.
tāv ubhau naraśārdūlau rājaputrāv ariṁdamau |
tvaddarśanakr̥totsāhau laṅkāṁ bhasmīkariṣyataḥ ||

> **Meaning 27:** Those two princes, tigers among men and tamers of enemies,
> fueled by the eagerness to see you, will burn Laṅkā to ashes.

---
28.
hatvā ca samare raudraṁ rāvaṇaṁ saha bāndhavam |
rāghavas tvāṁ mahābāhuḥ svāṁ purīṁ nayate dhruvam ||

> **Meaning 28:** Slaying the ferocious Rāvaṇa in battle along with all his
> kinsmen, the mighty-armed Rāghava will surely escort you back to his own city.

---
29.
yat tu rāmo vijānīyād abhijñānam anindite |
prītisaṁjananaṁ tasya pradātuṁ tattvam arhasi ||

> **Meaning 29:** Blameless lady, please give me a token of recognition that
> Rāma will recognize, something that will bring joy to his heart.'

---
30.
sābhivīkṣya diśaḥ sarvā veṇyudgrathanam uttamam |
muktvā vastrād dadau mahyaṁ maṇim etaṁ mahābala ||

> **Meaning 30:** Mighty hero, glancing in all directions, she untied this
> splendid jewel from her upper garment — once worn in her braid — and handed it
> to me.

---
31.
pratigr̥hya maṇiṁ divyaṁ tava heto raghūttama |
śirasā saṁpraṇamyainām aham āgamane tvare ||

> **Meaning 31:** Best of Raghus, receiving that divine jewel for your sake, I
> bowed my head to her in reverence and hastened to depart.

---
32.
gamane ca kr̥totsāham avekṣya varavarṇinī |
vivardhamānaṁ ca hi mām uvāca janakātmajā ||
> **Meaning 32:** Seeing me expand my body, eager to set off on the return leap,
> the radiant daughter of Janaka spoke to me,

---
33.
aśrupūrṇamukhī dīnā bāṣpasaṁdigdhabhāṣiṇī ||

> **Meaning 33:** her face bathed in tears, brokenhearted, her voice choked with
> sobs.

---
34.
māmutpatanasambhrāntā śōkavēgasamāhatā|
māmuvāca tataḥ sītā subhagō'si mahākapē||

> **Meaning 34:** Alarmed at my impending leap and overcome by a fresh surge of
> grief, Sītā said to me, 'Great Kapi, you are fortunate indeed,

---
35.
yaddrakṣyasi mahābāhuṃ rāmaṃ kamalalōcanam|
lakṣmaṇaṃ ca mahābāhuṃ dēvaraṃ mē yaśasvinam||

36.
sītayā caivamuktō'hamabruvaṃ maithilīṃ tadā|
pṛṣṭhamārōha mē dēvi kṣipraṃ janakanandini||

> **Meaning 35-36:** for you will see the mighty-armed, lotus-eyed Rāma, and my
> glorious brother-in-law, the mighty-armed Lakṣmaṇa.' When Sītā said this to
> me, I replied to Maithilī, 'Devī, joy of Janaka, climb onto my back at once!

---
37.
yāvattē darśayāmyādya sugrīvaṃ sahalakṣmaṇam|
rāghavaṃ ca mahābhāgē bhartāramasitēkṣaṇē||

> **Meaning 37:** Noble, dark-eyed lady, today itself I will bring you before
> Sugrīva, Lakṣmaṇa, and your husband Rāghava.'

---
38.
sābravīnmāṃ tatō dēvī naiṣa dharmō mahākapē|
yattē pṛṣṭhaṃ niṣēvē'haṃ svavaśā haripuṅgava||
> **Meaning 38:** Then the devī said to me, 'Great Kapi, bull among Haris, it is
> against dharma for me to mount your back of my own free will.

---
39.
purā ca yadahaṃ vīra spṛṣṭā gātrēṣu rakṣasā|
tatrāhaṃ kiṃ kariṣyāmi kālēnōpanipīḍitā||

> **Meaning 39:** Hero, when the Rākṣasa touched my body earlier, what could I
> do, helpless as I was and crushed by circumstance?

---
40.
gaccha tvaṃ kapiśārdūla yatra tau nṛpatēḥ sutau|
ityēvaṃ māṃ samābhāṣya bhūyaḥ sandēṣṭumāsthitā||

> **Meaning 40:** Go, tiger among Kapis, to where those two sons of the king
> are.' Speaking thus to me, she proceeded to give me another message:

---
41.
hanuman siṁhasaṁkāśau tāv ubhau rāmalakṣmaṇau |
sugrīvaṁ ca sahāmātyaṁ sarvān brūyā anāmayam ||

> **Meaning 41:** 'Hanumān, greet both lion-like brothers, Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa,
> and Sugrīva with his ministers, wishing them all good health.

---
42.
yathā ca sa mahābāhur māṁ tārayati rāghavaḥ |
asmād duḥkhāmbusaṁrodhāt tat samādhātum arhasi ||

> **Meaning 42:** And you must present matters in such a way that the
> mighty-armed Rāghava rescues me from this ocean of misery.

---
43.
imaṁ ca tīvraṁ mama śokavegaṁ; rakṣobhir ebhiḥ paribhartsanaṁ ca |
brūyās tu rāmasya gataḥ samīpaṁ; śivaś ca te ’dhvāstu haripravīra ||

> **Meaning 43:** When you reach Rāma's side, tell him of the intense flood of
> my grief and the constant threats from these Rākṣasas. May your journey be
> blessed and safe, heroic Hari!'

---
44.
etat tavāryā nr̥parājasiṁha; sītā vacaḥ prāha viṣādapūrvam |
etac ca buddhvā gaditaṁ mayā tvaṁ; śraddhatsva sītāṁ kuśalāṁ samagrām ||

> **Meaning 44:** Lion among kings, your noble Sītā spoke these sorrowful words.
> Hearing what I have recounted, you may rest assured that Sītā is entirely safe
> and unharmed."

---
