=== Sarga 22 ===

---
1.
sītāyā vacanaṁ śrutvā paruṣaṁ rākṣasādhipaḥ |
pratyuvāca tataḥ sītāṁ vipriyaṁ priyadarśanām ||

> **Meaning 1:** Hearing Sītā's harsh words, the king of Rākṣasas spoke
> unpleasantly to that lady of pleasant appearance:

---
2.
yathā yathā sāntvayitā vaśyaḥ strīṇāṁ tathā tathā |
yathā yathā priyaṁ vaktā paribhūtas tathā tathā ||

> **Meaning 2:** "The more a man tries to soothe and yield to a woman, the more
> she controls him; the more kindly he speaks, the more she insults him!

---
3.
sanniyacchati me krodhaṁ tvayi kāmaḥ samutthitaḥ |
dravato mārgam āsādya hayān iva susārathiḥ ||

> **Meaning 3:** Only the passion that has arisen in me for you restrains my
> wrath, as a skilled charioteer reins in galloping horses upon reaching the
> road.

---
4.
vāmaḥ kāmo manuṣyāṇāṁ yasmin kila nibadhyate |
jane tasmiṁs tv anukrośaḥ snehaś ca kila jāyate ||

> **Meaning 4:** Cruel is the nature of desire in men: toward the very person to
> whom one's heart is bound, tenderness and love are born!

---
5.
etasmāt kāraṇān na tāṁ ghatayāmi varānane |
vadhārhām avamānārhāṁ mithyāpravrajite ratām ||

> **Meaning 5:** It is solely for this reason, fair-faced one, that I do not
> slay you, though you deserve death and humiliation for doting on a false
> hermit.

---
6.
paruṣāṇi hi vākyāni yāni yāni bravīṣi mām |
teṣu teṣu vadho yuktas tava maithili dāruṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 6:** For every harsh word you fling at me, Maithilī, a terrible
> death is your rightful due."

---
7.
evam uktvā tu vaidehīṁ rāvaṇo rākṣasādhipaḥ |
krodhasamrambhasamyuktaḥ sītām uttaram abravīt ||

> **Meaning 7:** Having spoken thus to Vaidehī, Rāvaṇa, the king of Rākṣasas,
> seething with anger and agitation, declared to Sītā:

---
8.
dvau māsau rakṣitavyau me yo ’vadhis te mayā kr̥taḥ |
tataḥ śayanam āroha mama tvaṁ varavarṇini ||

> **Meaning 8:** "Two months still remain of the time limit I set for you; after
> that, lady of golden complexion, you must ascend my bed!

---
9.
dvābhyām ūrdhvaṁ tu māsābhyāṁ bhartāraṁ mām anicchatīm |
mama tvāṁ prātarāśārtham ārabhante mahānase ||

> **Meaning 9:** If after these two months you still refuse to accept me as your
> husband, my cooks will carve you up in the royal kitchen for my morning meal!"

---
10.
tāṁ tarjyamānāṁ samprekṣya rākṣasendreṇa jānakīm |
devagandharvakanyās tā viṣedur vipulekṣaṇāḥ ||

> **Meaning 10:** Seeing Jānakī thus threatened by the king of Rākṣasas, the
> wide-eyed daughters of Devas and Gandharvas standing nearby were filled with
> sorrow.

---
11.
oṣṭhaprakārair aparā netravaktrais tathāparāḥ |
sītām āśvāsayām āsus tarjitāṁ tena rakṣasā ||

> **Meaning 11:** Some with movements of their lips, others with their eyes and
> facial expressions, secretly comforted Sītā as she was terrified by that
> Rākṣasa.

---
12.
tābhir āśvāsitā sītā rāvaṇaṁ rākṣasādhipam |
uvācātmahitaṁ vākyaṁ vr̥ttaśauṇḍīryagarvitam ||

> **Meaning 12:** Reassured by them, Sītā, proud in the purity of her conduct
> and virtue, addressed words for his own welfare to Rāvaṇa, the king of
> Rākṣasas:

---
13.
nūnaṁ na te janaḥ kaś cid asin niḥśreyase sthitaḥ |
nivārayati yo na tvāṁ karmaṇo ’smād vigarhitāt ||

> **Meaning 13:** "Surely there is no one here devoted to your true well-being,
> for no one restrains you from this contemptible crime!

---
14.
māṁ hi dharmātmanaḥ patnīṁ śacīm iva śacīpateḥ |
tvadanyas triṣu lokeṣu prārthayen manasāpi kaḥ ||

> **Meaning 14:** Who else in all the three worlds except you would dare even in
> thought to desire me, the wife of the righteous Rāma, as sacred as Śacī to
> Indra?

---
15.
rākṣasādhama rāmasya bhāryām amitatejasaḥ |
uktavān asi yat pāpaṁ kva gatas tasya mokṣyase ||

> **Meaning 15:** Vile Rākṣasa, having uttered such sinful words to the wife of
> the immeasurably radiant Rāma, where can you flee to escape retribution?

---
16.
yathā dr̥ptaś ca mātaṅgaḥ śaśaś ca sahitau vane |
tathā dviradavad rāmas tvaṁ nīca śaśavat smr̥taḥ ||

> **Meaning 16:** As an enraged royal elephant and a hare together in the
> forest, so is Rāma like the majestic elephant, and you, base wretch, are like
> the helpless hare!

---
17.
sa tvam ikṣvākunāthaṁ vai kṣipann iha na lajjase |
cakṣuṣo viṣayaṁ tasya na tāvad upagacchasi ||

> **Meaning 17:** Are you not ashamed to insult that ruler of the Ikṣvākus,
> simply because you have not yet come within the range of his sight?

---
18.
ime te nayane krūre virūpe kr̥ṣṇapiṅgale |
kṣitau na patite kasmān mām anāryanirīkṣitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 18:** Why have those two cruel, deformed, dark-yellow eyes of yours
> not dropped to the earth for having cast their wicked gaze upon me?

---
19.
tasya dharmātmanaḥ patnīṁ snuṣāṁ daśarathasya ca |
kathaṁ vyāharato māṁ te na jihvā pāpa śīryate ||

> **Meaning 19:** How is it that your tongue does not split into pieces, you
> sinner, as you speak such filth to the wife of that righteous prince and
> daughter-in-law of Daśaratha?

---
20.
asandeśāt tu rāmasya tapasaś cānupālanāt |
na tvāṁ kurmi daśagrīva bhasma bhasmārhatejasā ||

> **Meaning 20:** Daśagrīva, only because I have received no command from Rāma
> and must preserve the spiritual power of my ascetic vows, I do not reduce you
> to ash right now with my fiery radiance!

---
21.
nāpahartum ahaṁ śakyā tasya rāmasya dhīmataḥ |
vidhis tava vadhārthāya vihito nātra saṁśayaḥ ||

> **Meaning 21:** I could never have been abducted from the side of the wise
> Rāma; this has all been ordained by destiny solely to bring about your
> destruction, there is no doubt!

---
22.
śūreṇa dhanadabhrātā balaiḥ samuditena ca |
apohya rāmaṁ kasmād dhi dāracauryaṁ tvayā kr̥tam ||

> **Meaning 22:** You, claiming to be a hero, the brother of Kubera, backed by
> vast armies—why did you steal another man's wife by stealth after luring Rāma
> away?"

---
23.
sītāyā vacanaṁ śrutvā rāvaṇo rākṣasādhipaḥ |
vivr̥tya nayane krūre jānakīm anvavaikṣata ||

> **Meaning 23:** Hearing Sītā's words, Rāvaṇa, the king of Rākṣasas, rolled his
> cruel eyes and glared furiously at Jānakī.

---
24.
nīlajīmūtasaṅkāśo mahābhujaśirodharaḥ |
siṁhasattvagatiḥ śrīmān dīptajihvogralocanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 24:** Resembling a dark thundercloud, with massive arms and a thick
> neck, moving with the strength and stride of a lion, he flashed his tongue
> with fierce, blazing eyes.

---
25.
calāgramakuṭaḥ prāṁśuś citramālyānulepanaḥ |
raktamālyāmbaradharas taptāṅgadavibhūṣaṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 25:** Tall, with his swaying crown aloft, adorned with colorful
> garlands and unguents, wearing red garlands and scarlet robes, he was decked
> with armlets of refined gold.

---
26.
śroṇīsūtreṇa mahatā mekakena susaṁvr̥taḥ |
amr̥totpādanaddhena bhujaṁgeneva mandaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 26:** Girded around his waist with a great golden sash, he resembled
> Mount Mandara wrapped with the serpent during the churning for nectar.

---
27.
taruṇādityavarṇābhyāṁ kuṇḍalābhyāṁ vibhūṣitaḥ |
raktapallavapuṣpābhyām aśokābhyām ivācalaḥ ||

> **Meaning 27:** Adorned with earrings glowing like the morning sun, he looked
> like a mountain flanked by two blossoming aśoka trees covered with red shoots
> and flowers.

---
28.
sa kalpavr̥kṣapratimo vasanta iva mūrtimān |
śmaśānacaityapratimo bhūṣito’pi bhayaṅkaraḥ ||

29.
avekṣamāṇo vaidehīṁ kopasamraktalocanaḥ |
uvāca rāvaṇaḥ sītāṁ bhujaṅga iva niḥśvasan ||

30.
anayenābhisampannam arthahīnam anuvrate |
nāśayāmy aham adya tvāṁ sūryaḥ sandhyām ivaujasā ||

> **Meaning 28-30:** Resembling a wish-granting tree and looking like Spring
> incarnate, he was terrifying despite his lavish adornments, like an altar in a
> burning-ground. Glaring at Vaidehī with eyes bloodshot with rage and hissing
> like a serpent, Rāvaṇa spoke to Sītā: "Because you cling devotedly to one who
> is fallen from righteousness and stripped of all wealth, I will destroy you
> this very day, as the sun dissolves the evening twilight with its blazing
> power!"

---
32.
ity uktvā maithilīṁ rājā rāvaṇaḥ śatrurāvaṇaḥ |
sandideśa tataḥ sarvā rākṣasīr ghoradarśanāḥ ||

> **Meaning 32:** Having thus threatened Maithilī, King Rāvaṇa, the terror of
> his enemies, issued his command to all those hideous-looking Rākṣasīs:

---
33.
ekākṣīm ekakarṇāṁ ca karṇaprāvaraṇāṁ tathā |
gokarṇīṁ hastikarṇīṁ ca lambakarṇīm akarṇikām ||

> **Meaning 33:** those with one eye, one ear, or ears like blankets, those with
> ears of cows or elephants, those with drooping ears, or no ears at all;

---
34.
hastipadya śvapadyau ca gopadīṁ pādacūlikām |
ekākṣīm ekapādīṁ ca pr̥thupādīm apādikām ||

> **Meaning 34:** those with the feet of elephants, dogs, or cattle, or with
> tufted feet; those with a single eye, a single foot, broad feet, or no feet;

---
35.
atimātraśirogrīvām atimātrakucodarīm |
atimātrāsyanetrāṁ ca dīrghajihvām ajihvikām ||


> **Meaning 35:** those with monstrously oversized heads and necks, swollen
> breasts and bellies, gaping mouths, bulging eyes, long tongues, or no tongues;

---
36.
anāsikāṁ siṁhamukhīṁ gomukhīṁ sūkarīmukhīm |
yathā madvaśagā sītā kṣipraṁ bhavati jānakī ||


> **Meaning 36:** and those without noses, or with the faces of lions, cows, and
> sows, saying: "See to it that Sītā, Janaka's daughter, submits quickly to my
> will!

---
37.
tathā kuruta rākṣasyaḥ sarvāḥ kṣipraṁ sametya ca |
pratilomānulomaiś ca sāmadānādibhedanaiḥ |
āvartayata vaidehīṁ daṇḍasyodyamanena ca ||

> **Meaning 37:** Gather together at once, all of you Rākṣasīs, and by harsh or
> gentle means, through conciliation, gifts, sowing dissension, or the threat of
> force, bring Vaidehī around to me."

---
38.
iti pratisamādiśya rākṣasendraḥ punaḥ punaḥ |
kāmamanyuparītātmā jānakīṁ paryatarjayat ||

> **Meaning 38:** Having repeatedly instructed them thus, the king of Rākṣasas,
> his heart seized by lust and fury, gave vent to his threats against Jānakī.

---
39.
upagamya tataḥ kṣipraṁ rākṣasī dhānyamālinī |
pariṣvajya daśagrīvam idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

> **Meaning 39:** Then a Rākṣasī named Dhānyamālinī stepped forward swiftly,
> embraced the ten-headed Daśagrīva, and said:

---
40.
mayā krīḍa mahārāja sītayā kiṁ tavānayā |
vivarṇayā kr̥paṇayā mānuṣyā rākṣaseśvara ||

41.
nūnam asyā mahārāja na divyān bhogasattamān |
vidadhāty amaraśreṣṭhaḥ tava bāhubalārjitān ||

42.
akāmāṁ kāmayānasya śarīram upatapyate |
icchantīṁ kāmayānasya prītir bhavati śobhanā ||

---
43.
evam uktas tu rākṣasyā samutkṣiptas tato balī |
jvaladbhāskaravarṇābhaṁ praviveśa niveśanam ||

> **Meaning 40-43:** "Enjoy yourself with me, great King! What do you want with
> this pale, wretched, mere mortal woman, sovereign of Rākṣasas? Surely, great
> King, the foremost of gods has not destined her to enjoy those supreme divine
> pleasures won by the might of your arms! The body of a man who longs for an
> unwilling woman is only consumed by fever; but when he loves a woman who
> desires him in return, that love is glorious!" Spoken to thus by the Rākṣasī,
> the mighty monarch was turned away from his wrath and led off.

---
44.
prasthitaḥ sa daśagrīvaḥ kampayann iva medinīm |
jvaladbhāskaravarṇābhaṁ praviveśa niveśanam ||

45.
devagandharvakanyāś ca nāgakanyāś ca tās tataḥ |
parivārya daśagrīvaṁ viviśus tad gr̥hottamam ||

> **Meaning 44-45:** Strode forth as if shaking the earth, the ten-headed one
> entered his palace, which blazed with the splendor of the radiant sun.
> Surrounding the ten-headed Daśagrīva, the captive daughters of the Devas,
> Gandharvas, and Nāgas followed him into that magnificent mansion.

---
46.
sa maithilīṁ dharmaparām avasthitāṁ pravepamānāṁ paribhartsya rāvaṇaḥ |
vihāya sītāṁ madanena mohitaḥ svam eva veśma praviveśa bhāsvaram ||

> **Meaning 46:** Having thus threatened Maithilī—who stood trembling, yet
> unshakeably anchored in righteousness—Rāvaṇa, bewildered by passion, abandoned
> Sītā and returned to his own blazing abode.

---
