=== Sarga 40 ===

---
1.
śrutvā tu vacanaṁ tasya vāyusūnormahātmanaḥ |
uvācātmahitaṁ vākyaṁ sītā surasutopamā ||

> **Meaning 1:** After hearing the words of Hanumān, the great-souled son of
> Vāyu, Sītā, radiant as a daughter of the devatās, spoke words meant for her
> own good.

---
2.
tvāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā priyavaktāraṁ saṁprahr̥ṣyāmi vānara |
ardhasaṁjātasasyeva vr̥ṣṭiṁ prāpya vasuṁdharā ||

> **Meaning 2:** "Vānara, seeing you and hearing your kind words fills me with
> joy, like the earth when rain falls on a field where the crops have half
> grown.

---
3.
yathā taṁ puruṣavyāghraṁ gātraiḥ śokābhikarśitaiḥ |
saṁspr̥śeyaṁ sakāmāhaṁ tathā kuru dayāṁ mayi ||

> **Meaning 3:** "Grief has left me greatly emaciated. Be kind to me, so that
> I may embrace Rāma, that tiger among men, and fulfill my longing."

---
4.
abhijñānaṁ ca rāmasya dattaṁ harigaṇottama |
kṣiptām īṣikāṁ kākasya kopād ekākṣiśātanīm ||

> **Meaning 4:** Best of the vānaras, I will also give you a sign for Rāma:
> the blade of grass he hurled at the crow in anger, which destroyed the crow's
> one eye.

---
5.
manaḥśilāyāstikalaḥ gaṇḍapārśve niveśitaḥ |
tvayā pranaṣṭe tilake taṁ kila smartum arhasi ||

> **Meaning 5:** When the mark on my forehead was wiped away, you placed a spot
> of red arsenic on the side of my cheek. You surely remember that.

---
6.
sa vīryavān kathaṁ sītāṁ hr̥tāṁ samanumanyase |
vasantīṁ rakṣasāṁ madhye mahendravaruṇopama ||

> **Meaning 6:** Mighty as Mahendra and Varuṇa, how can you accept that your
> wife was stolen and now lives among the Rākṣasas?

---
7.
eṣa cūḍāmaṇirdivyaḥ mayā suparirakṣitaḥ |
etaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā prahr̥ṣyāmi vyasane tvām ivānagha ||

> **Meaning 7:** This divine crest-jewel I have guarded with great care. Seeing
> it, I rejoice in my suffering as if I were seeing you, blameless one.

---
8.
eṣa niryātitaḥ śrīmān mayā te vārisaṁbhavaḥ |
ataḥ paraṁ na śakṣyāmi jīvituṁ śokalālasā ||

> **Meaning 8:** I have now handed over to you this radiant jewel born of the
> water. After this, I won't be able to live, weighed down by sorrow.

---
9.
asahyāni ca duḥkhāni vācaśca hr̥dayacchidaḥ |
rākṣasīnāṁ sughorāṇāṁ tvatkr̥te marṣayāmy aham ||

> **Meaning 9:** For your sake I put up with unbearable pains and the
> heart-piercing words of the terrifying Rākṣasīs.

---
10.
dhārayiṣyāmi māsaṁ tu jīvitaṁ śatrusūdana |
māsād ūrdhvaṁ na jīviṣye tvayā hīnā nr̥pātmaja ||

> **Meaning 10:** Destroyer of enemies, I will keep myself alive for one month.
> After a month I will not live, separated from you, prince.

---
11.
ghoro rākṣasarājaḥ ayaṁ dr̥ṣṭiśca na sukhā mayi |
tvāṁ ca śrutvā vipadyantaṁ na jīveyam ahaṁ kṣaṇam ||

> **Meaning 11:** This Rākṣasa king is dreadful, and his gaze on me is not
> kind. If I hear that you are delayed in reaching me, I will not be able to
> live even a moment longer."

---
12.
vaidehyā vacanaṁ śrutvā karuṇaṁ sāśrubhāṣitam |
athābravīn mahātejā hanumān mārutātmajaḥ ||

> **Meaning 12:** After hearing Vaidehī's pitiful words, spoken through tears,
> the mighty Hanumān, son of Māruta, replied.

---
13.
tvacchokavimukho rāmaḥ devi satyena te śape |
rāme śokābhibhūte tu lakṣmaṇaḥ paritapyate ||

> **Meaning 13:** "Devī, I swear to you by truth that Rāma has no peace of mind
> because of your grief. With Rāma overcome by sorrow, Lakṣmaṇa suffers too.

---
14.
dr̥ṣṭā kathaṁ cid bhavatī na kālaḥ pariśocitum |
imaṁ muhūrtaṁ duḥkhānām antaṁ drakṣyasi bhāmini ||

> **Meaning 14:** I have found you at last. This is no time for lament. From
> this moment you will see the end of your sorrows, noble lady.

---
15.
tāv ubhau puruṣavyāghrau rājaputrāv aninditau |
tvaddarśanakr̥totsāhau laṅkāṁ bhasmīkariṣyataḥ ||

> **Meaning 15:** Those two tigers among men, the blameless princes, are filled
> with eagerness at news of seeing you, and they will reduce Laṅkā to ash.

---
16.
hatvā tu samare krūraṁ rāvaṇaṁ saha bāndhavam |
rāghavau tvāṁ viśālākṣi svāṁ purīṁ prāpayiṣyataḥ ||

> **Meaning 16:** After killing the cruel Rāvaṇa and his kinsmen in battle, the
> two Rāghavas will bring you back to their own city, large-eyed princess.

---
17.
yat tu rāmo vijānīyād abhijñānam anindite |
prītisaṁjananaṁ tasya bhūyastvaṁ dātum arhasi ||

> **Meaning 17:** Blameless lady, give me one more sign that Rāma will recognize
> and that will bring him joy."

---
18.
sābravīd dattam eveha mayābhijñānam uttamam |
etad eva hi rāmasya dr̥ṣṭvā matkeśabhūṣaṇam ||


> **Meaning 18:** Sītā replied, "I have already given the best sign. Seeing this
> ornament from my hair will be enough for Rāma.

---
19.
śraddheyaṁ hanuman vākyaṁ tava vīra bhaviṣyati |
sa taṁ maṇivaraṁ gr̥hya śrīmān plavagasattamaḥ ||


> **Meaning 19:** Hanumān, he will believe your words, brave one." Then that
> glorious best of the Plavagas took the excellent jewel.

---

20.
praṇamya śirasā devīṁ gamanāyopacakrame |
tam utpātakr̥totsāham avekṣya haripuṁgavam ||

21.
vardhamānaṁ mahāvegam uvāca janakātmajā |
aśrupūrṇamukhī dīnā bāṣpagadgadayā girā ||

> **Meaning 20-21:** He bowed his head to the Devī and began to leave. Seeing
> that bull among the vānaras, eager to leap and growing in his mighty
> speed, Janaka's daughter spoke to him, her face full of tears, sad, and her
> voice choked with weeping.

---
22.
hanūman siṁhasaṁkāśau bhrātarau rāmalakṣmaṇau |
sugrīvaṁ ca sahāmātyaṁ sarvān brūyā anāmayam ||

> **Meaning 22:** "Hanumān, give my good wishes to the two brothers Rāma and
> Lakṣmaṇa, who are like lions, to Sugrīva with his ministers, and to everyone.

---
23.
yathā ca sa mahābāhuḥ māṁ tārayati rāghavaḥ |
asmād duḥkhāmbusaṁrodhāt tat samādhātum arhasi ||

> **Meaning 23:** Please arrange things so that mighty-armed Rāghava rescues me
> from this ocean of sorrow.

---
24.
imaṁ ca tīvraṁ mama śokavegaṁ rakṣobhirebhiḥ paribhartsanaṁ ca |
brūyāstu rāmasya gataḥ samīpaṁ śivaśca te adhvāstu haripravīra ||

> **Meaning 24:** Tell Rāma, once you reach him, about my fierce grief and how
> these Rākṣasīs threaten me. May your journey be safe, best of the Hari
> heroes."

---
25.
sa rājaputryā prativeditārthaḥ kapiḥ kr̥tārthaḥ parihr̥ṣṭacetāḥ |
tad alpaśeṣaṁ prasamīkṣya kāryaṁ diśaṁ hy udīcīṁ manasā jagāma ||

> **Meaning 25:** Having learned everything Sītā wanted conveyed, Hanumān was
> glad and felt his purpose was fulfilled. Seeing that little of his task
> remained, he set his mind on the northern direction and went.

---
