=== Sarga 26 ===

---
1.
prasaktāśrumukhīty evaṁ bruvantī janakātmajā |
adhomukhamukhī bālā vilaptum upacakrame ||

> **Meaning 1:** With tears streaming ceaselessly down her
> face, speaking thus, the young daughter of Janaka, her
> face downcast, began to lament.

---
2.
unmatteva pramatteva bhrāntacitteva śocatī |
upāvr̥ttā kiśorīva viveṣṭantī mahītale ||

> **Meaning 2:** Grieving, she appeared as if maddened, as if
> delirious, as if her mind had been unmoored — rolling upon
> the ground and writhing like a young filly.

---
3.
rāghavasyāpramattasya rakṣasā kāmarūpiṇā |
rāvaṇena pramathyāham ānītā krośatī balāt ||

4.
rākṣasī vaśam āpannā bhartyamānā sudāruṇam |
cintayantī suduḥkhārtā nāhaṁ jīvitum utsahe ||

> **Meaning 3-4:** "I, the wife of the ever-vigilant Rāghava, was forcibly seized
> by the shape-shifting Rākṣasa Rāvaṇa and carried away even as I cried out.
> Now fallen under the power of these Rākṣasīs, threatened most cruelly, wracked
> with unbearable grief — I have no desire to live."

---
5.
na hi me jīvitenārthaḥ naivārthairna ca bhūṣaṇaiḥ |
vasantyā rākṣasī madhye vinā rāmaṁ mahāratham ||

6.
aśmasāramidaṁ nūnaṁ athavāpyajarāmaram |
hr̥dayaṁ mama yēnēdaṁ na duḥkhēnāvaśīryatē ||

> **Meaning 5-6:** "I have no use for life, nor for wealth,
> nor for ornaments — dwelling among these Rākṣasīs,
> separated from Rāma, that mighty warrior. Surely
> this heart of mine is made of iron — or perhaps it is
> immortal and ageless — for it does not shatter under this
> weight of grief."

---
7.
dhiṅ mām anāryām asatīṁ yāhaṁ tena vinā kr̥tā |
muhūrtam api rakṣāmi jīvitaṁ pāpajīvitā ||

8.
kā ca me jīvite śraddhā sukhe vā taṁ priyaṁ vinā |
bhartāraṁ sāgarāntāyā vasudhāyāḥ priyaṁ vadam ||

> **Meaning 7-8:** "Shame on me, ignoble and wretched that I am! Parted from him, I still hold onto this sinful life, keeping myself alive for even a moment longer! What faith can I have in living, what hope of happiness, without that beloved husband — the most cherished man on this earth bounded by the seas?"

---
9.
bhidyatāṁ bhakṣyatāṁ vāpi śarīraṁ visr̥jāmy aham |
na cāpy ahaṁ ciraṁ duḥkhaṁ saheyaṁ priyavarjitā ||

> **Meaning 9:** "Let this body be torn apart or devoured — I
> relinquish it! I cannot endure this suffering for long,
> deprived of my beloved."

---
10.
caraṇenāpi savyena na spr̥śeyaṁ niśācaram |
rāvaṇaṁ kiṁ punarahaṁ kāmayeyaṁ vigarhitam ||

> **Meaning 10:** "I would not touch that night-stalker even
> with my left foot — how then could I ever desire that
> despicable Rāvaṇa?"

---
11.
pratyākhyātaṁ na jānāti nātmānaṁ nātmanaḥ kulam |
yo nr̥śaṁsa svabhāvena māṁ prārthayitum icchati ||

> **Meaning 11:** "He does not comprehend that he has been
> rejected; he knows neither himself nor the standing of his
> own lineage — he who, being cruel by nature, dares to
> pursue me."

---
12.
chinnā bhinnā vibhaktā vā dīpte vāgnau pradīpitā |
rāvaṇaṁ nopatiṣṭheyaṁ kiṁ pralāpena vaściram ||

> **Meaning 12:** "Whether I am cut to pieces, broken apart,
> dismembered, or cast into a blazing fire — I would never
> submit to Rāvaṇa! But what use is this prolonged
> lamentation?"

---
13.
khyātaḥ prājñaḥ kr̥tajñaśca sānukrośaśca rāghavaḥ |
sadvr̥tto niranukrośaḥ śaṅke madbhāgyasaṁkṣayāt ||

> **Meaning 13:** "Rāghava is renowned, wise, grateful, and
> compassionate. Yet he of noble conduct seems merciless
> toward me — I suspect it is due to the exhaustion of my
> good fortune."

---
14.
rākṣasānāṁ janasthāne sahasrāṇi caturdaśa |
yenaikena nirastāni sa māṁ kiṁ nābhipadyate ||

15.
niruddhā rāvaṇenāham alpavīryeṇa rakṣasā |
samarthaḥ khalu me bhartā rāvaṇaṁ hantum āhave ||

> **Meaning 14-15:** "He who single-handedly annihilated
> fourteen thousand Rākṣasas at Janasthāna — why does he
> not come to my aid? I am imprisoned by Rāvaṇa, a Rākṣasa
> of meagre prowess — my husband is surely capable of
> slaying him in battle!"

---
16.
virādho daṇḍakāraṇye yena rākṣasapuṁgavaḥ |
raṇe rāmeṇa nihataḥ sa māṁ kiṁ nābhipadyate ||

> **Meaning 16:** "He by whom the mighty Rākṣasa Virādha was
> slain in battle in the Daṇḍaka forest — why does he not
> come for me?"

---
17.
kāmaṁ madhye samudrasya laṅkeyaṁ duṣpradharṣaṇā |
na tu rāghavabāṇānāṁ gatirodhī ha vidyate ||

> **Meaning 17:** "Let this Laṅkā be ever so impregnable,
> situated in the midst of the ocean — there exists no
> obstacle that can block the flight of Rāghava's arrows."

---
18.
kiṁ nu tat kāraṇaṁ yena rāmo dr̥ḍhaparākramaḥ |
rakṣasāpahr̥tāṁ bhāryām iṣṭāṁ nābhyavapadyate ||

19.
ihasthāṁ māṁ na jānīte śaṅke lakṣmaṇapūrvajaḥ |
jānann api hi tejasvī dharṣaṇāṁ marṣayiṣyati ||

> **Meaning 18-19:** "What could be the reason that Rāma, of
> unyielding valour, does not come to recover his beloved
> wife, abducted by a Rākṣasa? I suspect the elder brother
> of Lakṣmaṇa does not know that I am here — for even
> knowing it, would that radiant hero ever tolerate such an
> outrage?"

---
20.
hr̥teti yaḥ adhigatvā māṁ rāghavāya nivedayet |
gr̥dhrarājaḥ api sa raṇe rāvaṇena nipātitaḥ ||

21.
kr̥taṁ karma mahat tena māṁ tadābhyavapadyatā |
tiṣṭhatā rāvaṇadvandve vr̥ddhenāpi jaṭāyuṣā ||

> **Meaning 20-21:** "Even the king of vultures, Jaṭāyu, who
> could have told Rāghava that I was abducted — even he was
> struck down by Rāvaṇa in battle. A mighty deed was
> performed by that aged Jaṭāyu, who stood his ground in
> single combat against Rāvaṇa, striving to rescue me."

---
22.
yadi mām iha jānīyād vartamānāṁ sa rāghavaḥ |
adya bāṇairabhikruddhaḥ kuryāl lokam arākṣasaṁ ||

23.
vidhamec ca purīṁ laṅkāṁ śoṣayec ca mahodadhim |
rāvaṇasya ca nīcasya kīrtiṁ nāma ca nāśayet ||

> **Meaning 22-23:** "If Rāghava were to learn that I am
> here, enraged, he would render this world devoid of
> Rākṣasas with his arrows this very day! He would demolish
> the city of Laṅkā, dry up the great ocean, and obliterate
> both the fame and the very name of that vile Rāvaṇa!"

---
24.
tato nihatanathānāṁ rākṣasīnāṁ gr̥he gr̥he |
yathāham evaṁ rudatī tathā bhūyo na saṁśayaḥ ||


> **Meaning 24:** "Then, in house after house, the Rākṣasīs will weep for their
> slain husbands just as I am weeping now. There is no doubt about this."

---
25.
anviṣya rakṣasāṁ laṅkāṁ kuryād rāmaḥ salakṣmaṇaḥ |
na hi tābhyāṁ ripurdr̥ṣṭaḥ muhūtam api jīvati ||

26.
citā dhūmākulapathā gr̥dhramaṇḍalasaṁkulā |
acireṇa tu laṅkeyaṁ śmaśānasadr̥śī bhavet ||

> **Meaning 25-26:** "Having sought out the Rākṣasas'
> Laṅkā, Rāma along with Lakṣmaṇa would lay waste to it —
> for no enemy beheld by those two survives even a moment.
> Before long, this Laṅkā would become like a cremation
> ground, its roads choked with the smoke of funeral pyres,
> swarming with circles of vultures."

---
27.
acireṇaiva kālena prāpsyāmy eva manoratham |
duṣprasthānaḥ ayam ākhyāti sarveṣāṁ vo viparyayaḥ ||

28.
yādr̥śāni tu dr̥śyante laṅkāyām aśubhāni tu |
acireṇaiva kālena bhaviṣyati hataprabhā ||

> **Meaning 27-28:** "Before long, I will surely attain my heart's desire. This
> wicked course of action foretells the ruin of you all! Judging by the
> inauspicious signs visible in Laṅkā, this city will soon lose all its
> splendour."

---
29.
nūnaṁ laṅkā hate pāpe rāvaṇe rākṣasādhipe |
śoṣaṁ yāsyati durdharṣā pramadā vidhavā yathā ||

30.
puṇyotsavasamr̥ddhā ca naṣṭabhartrī sarākṣasā |
bhaviṣyati purī laṅkā naṣṭabhartrī yathāṅganā ||

> **Meaning 29-30:** "Surely, once the wicked Rāvaṇa, the
> Rākṣasādhipa, is slain, this impregnable Laṅkā will
> wither away like a widow — like a woman who has lost her
> husband. This city, once prosperous with auspicious
> festivals, with its Rākṣasas bereft of their king, will
> become like a woman who has lost her husband."

---
31.
nūnaṁ rākṣasakanyānāṁ rudantīnāṁ gr̥he gr̥he |
śroṣyāmi nacirād eva duḥkhārtānām iha dhvanim ||

32.
sāndhakārā hatadyotā hatarākṣasapuṁgavā |
bhaviṣyati purī laṅkā nirdagdhā rāmasāyakaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 31-32:** "Before long, I shall hear in every
> house the wailing of Rākṣasa maidens, stricken with grief.
> Plunged into darkness, its splendour extinguished, its
> foremost warriors slain — the city of Laṅkā will be
> reduced to ashes by Rāma's arrows."

---
33.
yadi nāma sa śūro māṁ rāmo raktāntalocanaḥ |
jānīyād vartamānāṁ hi rāvaṇasya niveśane ||

34.
anena tu nr̥śaṁsena rāvaṇenādhamena me |
samayo yastu nirdiṣṭaḥ tasya kālaḥ ayam āgataḥ ||

> **Meaning 33-34:** "If only that hero Rāma, with eyes
> reddened at their corners, were to learn that I am
> confined in Rāvaṇa's abode! This cruel and base Rāvaṇa
> has set a deadline for me — but the time for his death
> has now arrived."

---
35.
akāryaṁ ye na jānanti nairr̥tāḥ pāpakāriṇaḥ |
adharmāt tu mahotpātaḥ bhaviṣyati hi sāmpratam ||

36.
naite dharmaṁ vijānanti rākṣasāḥ piśitāśanāḥ |
dhruvaṁ māṁ prātarāśārthe rākṣasaḥ kalpayiṣyati ||

> **Meaning 35-36:** "These sinful Rākṣasas do not know what
> is forbidden. From their adharma, a great calamity will
> surely arise now. These flesh-eating Rākṣasas do not
> understand dharma. The Rākṣasa will certainly have me
> prepared for his morning meal."

---
37.
sāhaṁ kathaṁ kariṣyāmi taṁ vinā priyadarśanam |
rāmaṁ raktāntanayanam apaśyantī suduḥkhitā ||

> **Meaning 37:** "What am I to do without him? Consumed with anguish, I am unable to see my handsome Rāma with his red-tinged eyes."

---
38.
yadi kaścit pradātā me viṣasyādya bhaved iha |
kṣipraṁ vaivasvataṁ devaṁ paśyeyaṁ patinā vinā ||

> **Meaning 38:** "If only someone here today would give me
> poison — I would swiftly behold Yama, the god of
> death, bereft of my husband."

---
39.
nājānāj jīvatīṁ rāmaḥ sa māṁ lakṣmaṇapūrvajaḥ |
jānantau tau na kuryātāṁ norvyāṁ hi mama mārgaṇam ||

40.
nūnaṁ mamaiva śokena sa vīro lakṣmaṇāgrajaḥ |
devalokam ito yātaḥ tyaktvā dehaṁ mahītale ||

> **Meaning 39-40:** "Rāma, the elder brother of Lakṣmaṇa,
> surely does not know that I am alive. Had they known, those
> two would not fail to search the earth for me. Surely that
> hero, the elder brother of Lakṣmaṇa, overcome by grief for
> me, has departed to the realm of the devatās, having
> abandoned his body upon the earth."

---
41.
dhanyā devāḥ sagandharvāḥ siddhāśca paramarṣayaḥ |
mama paśyanti ye nāthaṁ rāmaṁ rājīvalocanam ||

> **Meaning 41:** "Blessed are the Devatās along with the
> Gandharvas, the Siddhas and the great Ṛṣis, who behold
> my swami — lotus-eyed Rāma!"

---
42.
atha vā na hi tasyārthe dharmakāmasya dhīmataḥ |
mayā rāmasya rājarṣeḥ bhāryayā paramātmanaḥ ||

43.
dr̥śyamāne bhavet prītaḥ sauhr̥daṁ nāsty apaśyataḥ |
nāśayanti kr̥taghrāstu na rāmo nāśayiṣyati ||

> **Meaning 42-43:** "Or perhaps that wise one, devoted to
> dharma, the Rājarṣi Rāma, the supreme soul — has no
> further use for me, his wife. One is affectionate toward
> what is before one's eyes; there is no love for what is
> unseen. But the ungrateful destroy bonds — Rāma would
> never do so."

---
44.
kiṁ nu me na guṇāḥ ke cit kiṁ vā bhāgya kṣayo hi me |
yāhaṁ sītā varārheṇa hīnā rāmeṇa bhāminī ||

> **Meaning 44:** "Do I possess no virtues at all? Or is it
> the complete ruin of my fortune? For I, Sītā — spirited
> and deserving of the noblest — am separated from Rāma!"

---
45.
śreyo me jīvitān martuṁ vihīnā yā mahātmanā |
rāmād akliṣṭacāritrāc chūrāc chatrunibarhaṇāt ||

> **Meaning 45:** "It is better for me to die than to live,
> bereft as I am of that noble-souled one — Rāma, of
> effortless valour, the hero, the destroyer of foes."

---
46.
atha vā nyastaśastrau tau vane mūlaphalāśanau |
bhrātarau hi nara śreṣṭhau carantau vanagocarau ||

47.
atha vā rākṣasendreṇa rāvaṇena durātmanā |
chadmanā ghātitau śūrau bhrātarau rāmalakṣmaṇau ||

> **Meaning 46-47:** "Or perhaps those two brothers, the
> foremost among men, have laid down their weapons and wander
> the forest, subsisting on roots and fruits. Or perhaps the
> heroic brothers Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa have been treacherously
> slain by the wicked Rākṣasendra, Rāvaṇa!"

---
48.
sāham evaṁgate kāle martum icchāmi sarvathā |
na ca me vihito mr̥tyuḥ asmin duḥkhe api vartati ||

> **Meaning 48:** "With matters having come to such a pass, I
> wish to die by any means whatsoever. But death has not been
> ordained for me — even amid this suffering, it does not
> come."

---
49.
dhanyāḥ khalu mahātmānaḥ munayaḥ satyasaṁmatāḥ |
jitātmāno mahābhāgā yeṣāṁ na staḥ priyāpriye ||

50.
priyān na saṁbhaved duḥkham apriyād adhikaṁ bhayam |
tābhyāṁ hi ye viyujyante namasteṣāṁ mahātmanām ||

> **Meaning 49-50:** "Truly blessed are those great-souled
> sages, honoured for their truthfulness, who have conquered
> their senses — illustrious ones for whom neither the
> pleasant nor the unpleasant exist. From the pleasant arises
> grief; from the unpleasant, excessive fear. I bow to those
> noble souls who have freed themselves from both."

---
51.
sāhaṁ tyaktā priyeṇeha rāmeṇa viditātmanā |
prāṇāṁstyakṣyāmi pāpasya rāvaṇasya gatā vaśam ||

> **Meaning 51:** "Abandoned here by my beloved, the
> self-realized Rāma, and fallen under the power of the
> wicked Rāvaṇa — I shall give up my life."

---
