=== Sarga 49 ===

---
1.
taṁ samīkṣya mahāsattvaṁ sattvavān harisattamaḥ |
vākyam arthavad avyagraḥ tam uvāca daśānanam ||

> **Meaning 1:** Seeing the mighty Daśānana, the steadfast Hanumān, best among
> the Haris, calmly spoke to him words full of meaning.

---
2.
ahaṁ sugrīvasaṁdeśād iha prāptastavālayam |
rākṣasendra harīśastvāṁ bhrātā kuśalam abravīt ||

> **Meaning 2:** "King of the Rākṣasas, I have come to your palace on Sugrīva's
> orders. Hariśa Sugrīva, your brother, asks after your well-being.

---
3.
bhrātuḥ śr̥ṇu samādeśaṁ sugrīvasya mahātmanaḥ |
dharmārthopahitaṁ vākyam iha cāmutra ca kṣamam ||

> **Meaning 3:** Listen to the message of your brother, the great-souled
> Sugrīva. It is full of dharma and purpose, and it is good for you both in this
> world and the next.

---
4.
rājā daśaratho nāma rathakuñjaravājimān |
piteva bandhurlokasya sureśvarasamadyutiḥ ||

> **Meaning 4:** There was a king named Daśaratha, rich in chariots, elephants
> and horses. Like a father, he was a protector to the whole world, and he shone
> like the king of the devathās.

---
5.
jyeṣṭhastasya mahābāhuḥ putraḥ priyakaraḥ prabhuḥ |
piturnideśān niṣkrāntaḥ praviṣṭo daṇḍakāvanam ||

> **Meaning 5:** His eldest son, the mighty-armed and powerful Rāma, who always
> did what pleased his father, left the kingdom at his father's command and
> entered the Daṇḍaka forest.

---
6.
lakṣmaṇena saha bhrātrā sītayā cāpi bhāryayā |
rāmo nāma mahātejā dharmyaṁ panthānam āśritaḥ ||

> **Meaning 6:** Rāma, of great radiance, went along the path of dharma,
> together with his brother Lakṣmaṇa and his wife Sītā.

---
7.
tasya bhāryā vane naṣṭā sītā patim anuvratā |
vaidehasya sutā rājñaḥ janakasya mahātmanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 7:** In the forest his wife Sītā, devoted to her husband, was lost
> to him. She is the daughter of the great-souled Janaka, king of Videha.

---
8.
sa mārgamāṇastāṁ devīṁ rājaputraḥ sahānujaḥ |
r̥śyamūkam anuprāptaḥ sugrīveṇa ca saṁgataḥ ||

> **Meaning 8:** Searching for the princess, that son of a king, together with
> his younger brother, reached Ṛśyamūka and met Sugrīva.

---
9.
tasya tena pratijñātaṁ sītāyāḥ parimārgaṇam |
sugrīvasyāpi rāmeṇa harirājyaṁ niveditam ||

> **Meaning 9:** Rāma promised Sugrīva that he would search for Sītā, and Rāma
> also pledged the kingdom of the Haris to Sugrīva.

---
10.
tatastena mr̥dhe hatvā rājaputreṇa vālinam |
sugrīvaḥ sthāpito rājye haryr̥kṣāṇāṁ gaṇeśvaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 10:** Then the prince killed Vālī in battle and crowned Sugrīva as
> king, as lord of the hosts of Haris and Ṛkṣas.

---
11.
tvayā vijñātapūrvaśca vālī vānarapuṁgavaḥ |
rāmēṇa nihatassaṁkhyē śarēṇaikēna vānaraḥ ||

---
12.
sa sītāmārgaṇe vyagraḥ sugrīvaḥ satyasaṁgaraḥ |
harīn saṁpreṣayām āsa diśaḥ sarvā harīśvaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 12:** Sugrīva, true to his word and eager to find Sītā, sent Haris
> out in every direction, as the lord of the Haris.

---
13.
tāṁ harīṇāṁ sahasrāṇi śatāni niyutāni ca |
dikṣu sarvāsu mārgante adhaścopari cāmbare ||

> **Meaning 13:** Thousands, hundreds of thousands and millions of Haris are
> searching in every direction, below, above and across the sky.

---
14.
vainateya samāḥ ke cit ke cit tatrānilopamāḥ |
asaṁgagatayaḥ śīghrā harivīrā mahābalāḥ ||

> **Meaning 14:** Some of these mighty Hari heroes are like Garuḍa and some like
> the wind. Their speed is swift and nothing can stop their movement.

---
15.
ahaṁ tu hanumān nāma mārutasyaurasaḥ sutaḥ |
sītāyāstu kr̥te tūrṇaṁ śatayojanam āyatam ||

16.
samudraṁ laṅghayitvaiva tāṁ didr̥kṣurihāgataḥ |
bhramatā ca mayā dr̥ṣṭā gr̥hē tē janakātmajā ||

> **Meaning 15-16:** I am Hanumān, the son of Māruta, born from the wind-god
> himself. For Sītā's sake I quickly leapt across the ocean, a hundred yojanas
> wide. I came here wanting to see her, and as I wandered about, I saw Janaka's
> daughter in your house.

---
17.
tad bhavān dr̥ṣṭadharmārthaḥ tapaḥ kr̥taparigrahaḥ |
paradārān mahāprājña noparoddhuṁ tvam arhasi ||

> **Meaning 17:** You understand dharma and artha, and you have gathered great
> merit through austerity. O wise one, you should not keep another man's wife
> confined.

---
18.
na hi dharmaviruddheṣu bahv apāyeṣu karmasu |
mūlaghātiṣu sajjante buddhimanto bhavadvidhāḥ ||

> **Meaning 18:** Wise men like you do not get drawn into deeds that go against
> dharma, bring great ruin, and cut down one's own roots.

---
19.
kaśca lakṣmaṇamuktānāṁ rāmakopānuvartinām |
śarāṇām agrataḥ sthātuṁ śakto devāsureṣv api ||

> **Meaning 19:** Who can stand before the arrows released by Lakṣmaṇa, which
> follow Rāma's anger? Not even among devathās and asuras is anyone able to.

---
20.
na cāpi triṣu lokeṣu rājan vidyeta kaścana |
rāghavasya vyalīkaṁ yaḥ kr̥tvā sukham avāpnuyāt ||

> **Meaning 20:** And there is no one in the three worlds, king, who could wrong
> Rāghava and then be happy.

---
21.
tat trikālahitaṁ vākyaṁ dharmyam arthānubandhi ca |
manyasva naradevāya jānakī pratidīyatām ||

> **Meaning 21:** So accept these words, which serve you in all three times,
> which are in line with dharma and bring benefit. Return Jānakī to the king of
> men.

---
22.
dr̥ṣṭā hīyaṁ mayā devī labdhaṁ yad iha durlabham |
uttaraṁ karma yaccheṣaṁ nimittaṁ tatra rāghavaḥ ||

> **Meaning 22:** I have seen this goddess, and I have found what is hard to
> find. What remains to be done is the next step, and in that Rāghava is the one
> who will act.

---
23.
lakṣiteyaṁ mayā sītā tathā śokaparāyaṇā |
gr̥hya yāṁ nābhijānāsi pañcāsyām iva pannagīm ||

> **Meaning 23:** I have seen how Sītā is sunk in grief. You don't realize that
> in seizing her you have taken hold of a five-headed serpent.

---
24.
neyaṁ jarayituṁ śakyā sāsurairamarairapi |
viṣasaṁsr̥ṣṭam atyarthaṁ bhuktam annam ivaujasā ||

> **Meaning 24:** She cannot be safely retained even by the devas and asuras
> with all their might, just as food heavily mixed with poison can never be
> digested.

---
25.
tapaḥsaṁtāpalabdhaste yaḥ ayaṁ dharmaparigrahaḥ |
na sa nāśayituṁ nyāyya ātmaprāṇaparigrahaḥ ||

> **Meaning 25:** This store of dharma you have earned through the heat of your
> austerities is something that should not be destroyed. It is what protects
> your own life.

---
26.
avadhyatāṁ tapobhiryāṁ bhavān samanupaśyati |
ātmanaḥ sāsurairdevaiḥ hetustatrāpy ayaṁ mahān ||

> **Meaning 26:** You consider yourself unkillable by asuras and devathās
> because of your austerities. But in that very matter, this is a great reason
> against you.

---
27.
sugrīvo na hi devaḥ ayaṁ nāsuro na ca mānuṣaḥ |
na rākṣaso na gandharvaḥ na yakṣo na ca pannagaḥ ||

> **Meaning 27:** Sugrīva is not a god, not an asura, not a human, not a
> Rākṣasa, not a Gandharva, not a Yakṣa, and not a Nāga.

---
28.
mānuṣo rāghavo rājan sugrīvaśca harīśvaraḥ |
tasmāt prāṇaparitrāṇaṁ kathaṁ rājan kariṣyasi ||

> **Meaning 28:** Rāghava is a human, king, and Sugrīva is the lord of the
> Haris. So how will you protect your life?

---
29.
na tu dharmopasaṁhāram adharmaphalasaṁhitam |
tad eva phalam anveti dharmaścādharmanāśanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 29:** Dharma does not go together with adharma and its fruit. Each
> one follows its own result, and dharma destroys adharma.

---
30.
prāptaṁ dharmaphalaṁ tāvad bhavatā nātra saṁśayaḥ |
phalam asyāpy adharmasya kṣipram eva prapatsyase ||

> **Meaning 30:** You have already received the fruit of your dharma, there is
> no doubt about it. You will soon receive the fruit of this adharma too.

---
31.
janasthānavadhaṁ buddhvā buddhvā vālivadhaṁ tathā |
rāmasugrīvasakhyaṁ ca budhyasva hitam ātmanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 31:** Remember the destruction of Janasthāna and the killing of
> Vālī, and recognize the friendship of Rāma and Sugrīva. Understand what is
> good for you.

---
32.
kāmaṁ khalv aham apy ekaḥ savājirathakuñjarām |
laṅkāṁ nāśayituṁ śaktaḥ tasyaiṣa tu viniścayaḥ ||

> **Meaning 32:** Surely I alone could destroy Laṅkā, with its horses, chariots
> and elephants. But that is not what Rāma has decided.

---
33.
rāmeṇa hi pratijñātaṁ haryr̥kṣagaṇasaṁnidhau |
utsādanam amitrāṇāṁ sītā yaistu pradharṣitā ||

> **Meaning 33:** Rāma has made a vow in front of the assembled Haris and Ṛkṣas
> to wipe out those enemies who have wronged Sītā.

---
34.
apakurvan hi rāmasya sākṣād api puraṁdaraḥ |
na sukhaṁ prāpnuyād anyaḥ kiṁ punastvadvidho janaḥ ||

> **Meaning 34:** Even Purandara himself, if he wronged Rāma, could not find
> happiness. What then of a man like you?

---
35.
yāṁ sītety abhijānāsi yeyaṁ tiṣṭhati te vaśe |
kālarātrīti tāṁ viddhi sarvalaṅkāvināśinīm ||

> **Meaning 35:** The one you think of as Sītā, who is now under your control,
> know her to be Kālarātri, the night of death, who will destroy all of Laṅkā.

---
36.
tad alaṁ kālapāśena sītā vigraharūpiṇā |
svayaṁ skandhāvasaktena kṣamam ātmani cintyatām ||

> **Meaning 36:** So away with this noose of death in the form of Sītā, which
> you have fastened around your own neck. Think about what is best for your own
> welfare.

---
37.
sītāyāstejasā dagdhāṁ rāmakopaprapīḍitām |
dahyamanām imāṁ paśya purīṁ sāṭṭapratolikām ||

> **Meaning 37:** Regard this city, with its watchtowers and gateways, as
> already ablaze—scorched by Sītā's power and consumed by Rāma's wrath.

---
38.
svāni mitrāṇi maṁtrīṁśca jñātīn bhrātr̥̄n sutān hitān |
bhōgān dārāṁśca laṁkāṁ ca mā vināśa mupānaya ||

> **Meaning 38:** Do not bring your own friends, ministers, kinsmen, brothers,
> sons, well-wishers, enjoyments, wives, and Laṅkā itself to destruction.

---
39.
satyaṁ rākṣasarājēṁdra śr̥ṇuṣva vacanaṁ mama |
rāmadāsasya dūtasya vānarasya viśēṣataḥ ||

> **Meaning 39:** King of the Rākṣasas, listen to my truthful words—the words of
> a servant of Rāma, an envoy, and especially a Vānara.

---
40.
sarvān lōkān susaṁhr̥tya sabhūtān sacarācarān |
punarēva tathā sraṣṭuṁ śaktaḥ rāmō mahāyaśāḥ ||

> **Meaning 40:** The illustrious Rāma is capable of completely destroying all the
> worlds along with all their beings, moving and unmoving, and creating them all
> over again just as they were.

---
41.
dēvāsura narēṁdrēṣu yakṣarakṣōgaṇēṣu ca |
vidyādharēṣu sarvēṣu gaṁdharvēṣūragēṣu ca ||

42.
siddhēṣu kinnarēṁdrēṣu patatriṣu ca sarvataḥ |
sarvabhūtēṣu sarvatra sarvakālēṣu nāsti saḥ |
yō rāmaṁ pratiyudhyēta viṣṇutulyaparākramam ||

> **Meaning 41-42:** Among devas, asuras, and kings of men; among hordes of
> yakṣas and rākṣasas; among all vidyādharas, gandharvas, and serpents; among
> siddhas, kinnara chiefs, and birds everywhere—among all beings anywhere and at
> any time, there is no one who could stand in battle against Rāma, whose prowess
> equals that of Viṣṇu.

---
43.
sarvalōkēśvarasyaivaṁ kr̥tvā vipriyamuttamam |
rāmasya rājasiṁhasya durlabhaṁ tava jīvitam ||

> **Meaning 43:** Having thus done the greatest offense to Rāma, a lion among
> kings and the lord of all the worlds, it will be impossible for you to save
> your life.

---
44.
dēvāśca daityāśca niśācarēṁdra
gaṁdharva vidyādhara nāgayakṣāḥ |
rāmasya lōkatrayanāyakasya
sthātuṁ na śaktāssamarēṣu sarvē ||

> **Meaning 44:** Devas and daityas, gandharvas, vidyādharas, nāgas, and yakṣas—
> none of them, king of the night-wanderers, are able to stand in battle before
> Rāma, the lord of the three worlds.

---
45.
brahmā svayaṁbhū ścaturānanō vā
rudrastrinētrastripurāṁtakaḥ vā |
iṁdrō mahēṁdra ssuranāyakō vā
trātuṁ na śaktā yudhi rāmavadhyam ||

> **Meaning 45:** Neither the self-born, four-faced Brahmā, nor the three-eyed
> Rudra, destroyer of Tripura, nor Mahendra, the king of the devas, can protect
> in battle anyone marked for death by Rāma.

---
46.
sa sauṣṭhavōpētamadīnavādinaḥ
kapērniśamyāpratimaḥ apriyaṁ vacaḥ |
daśānanaḥ kōpavivr̥ttalōcanaḥ
samādiśattasya vadhaṁ mahākapēḥ || 46 ||

> **Meaning 46:** Hearing these unwelcome yet excellent and fearless words from
> the hanumān, the peerless Daśānana, his eyes rolling with rage, ordered that
> the great vanara be put to death.
