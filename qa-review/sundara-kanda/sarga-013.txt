=== Sarga 13 ===

---
1.
vimānāt tu susaṁkramya prākāraṁ hariyūthapaḥ |
hanūmān vegavān āsīd yathā vidyudghanāntare ||

> **Meaning 1:** Leaping swiftly from the aerial palace onto the rampart, the
> Hari troop leader Hanumān flashed like lightning darting through a dark cloud.

---
2.
saṁparikramya hanumān rāvaṇasya niveśanān |
adr̥ṣṭvā jānakīṁ sītām abravīd vacanaṁ kapiḥ ||

> **Meaning 2:** Having thoroughly searched Rāvaṇa's residences without finding
> Jānakī Sītā, the Kapi spoke these words to himself:

---
3.
bhūyiṣṭhaṁ loḍitā laṅkā rāmasya caratā priyam |
na hi paśyāmi vaidehīṁ sītāṁ sarvāṅgaśobhanām ||

> **Meaning 3:** "Serving Rāma's cause, I have searched almost all of Laṅkā, yet
> I do not see Vaidehī Sītā, radiant in every way.

---
4.
palvalāni taṭākāni sarāṁsi saritastathā |
nadyo ’nūpavanāntāśca durgāśca dharaṇīdharāḥ |
loḍitā vasudhā sarvā na ca paśyāmi jānakīm ||

> **Meaning 4:** Ponds, tanks, lakes, streams, rivers, marshy forest tracts, and
> inaccessible mountains — I have scoured this entire land, yet I do not see
> Jānakī.

---
5.
iha saṁpātinā sītā rāvaṇasya niveśane |
ākhyātā gr̥dhrarājena na ca paśyāmi tām aham ||

> **Meaning 5:** The king of vultures, Sampāti, stated that Sītā was here in
> Rāvaṇa's dwelling, yet I cannot find her.

---
6.
kiṁ nu sītātha vaidehī maithilī janakātmajā |
upatiṣṭheta vivaśā rāvaṇaṁ duṣṭacāriṇam ||

> **Meaning 6:** Could Sītā Vaidehī, Maithilī, daughter of Janaka, ever
> helplessly submit to the wicked Rāvaṇa? Never!

---
7.
kṣipram utpatato manye sītām ādāya rakṣasaḥ |
bibhyato rāmabāṇānām antarā patitā bhavet ||

> **Meaning 7:** As the demon flew up swiftly carrying Sītā away, terrified of
> Rāma's arrows, she may have slipped and fallen mid-flight.

---
8.
atha vā hriyamāṇāyāḥ pathi siddhaniṣevite |
manye patitam āryāyā hr̥dayaṁ prekṣya sāgaram ||

> **Meaning 8:** Or perhaps, as she was carried along the skyway frequented by
> the Siddhas, looking down upon the boundless ocean, that noble lady's heart
> gave out in fright.

---
9.
rāvaṇasyoruvegena bhujābhyāṁ pīḍitena ca |
tayā manye viśālākṣyā tyaktaṁ jīvitam āryayā ||

> **Meaning 9:** Or by the tremendous speed of Rāvaṇa's flight and the crushing
> grip of his arms, that noble, wide-eyed lady may have breathed her last.

---
10.
upary upari vā nūnaṁ sāgaraṁ kramatastadā |
viveṣṭamānā patitā samudre janakātmajā ||

> **Meaning 10:** Or while he was soaring high over the sea, struggling to break
> free, the daughter of Janaka may have plunged into the ocean depths.

---
11.
āho kṣudreṇa cānena rakṣantī śīlam ātmanaḥ |
abandhurbhakṣitā sītā rāvaṇena tapasvinī ||

> **Meaning 11:** Or has this vile wretch Rāvaṇa devoured the helpless,
> suffering Sītā because she guarded her virtue without any protector near?

---
12.
atha vā rākṣasendrasya patnībhirasitekṣaṇā |
aduṣṭā duṣṭabhāvābhiḥ bhakṣitā sā bhaviṣyati ||

> **Meaning 12:** Or could that innocent, dark-eyed lady have been eaten by the
> wicked-minded wives of the Rākṣasa king?

---
13.
saṁpūrṇacandrapratimaṁ padmapatranibhekṣaṇam |
rāmasya dhyāyatī vaktraṁ pañcatvaṁ kr̥paṇā gatā ||

> **Meaning 13:** Perhaps that poor lady passed away, keeping her mind fixed on
> Rāma's face, which resembles the full moon and has eyes like lotus petals.

---
14.
hā rāma lakṣmaṇety eva hāyodhyeti ca maithilī |
vilapya bahu vaidehī nyastadehā bhaviṣyati ||

> **Meaning 14:** Crying piteously, 'O Rāma! O Lakṣmaṇa! O Ayodhyā!' Vaidehī
> Maithilī may have given up her life in grief.

---
15.
atha vā nihitā manye rāvaṇasya niveśane |
nūnaṁ lālapyate mandaṁ pañjarastheva śārikā ||

> **Meaning 15:** Or she is hidden away in Rāvaṇa's palace, weeping softly and
> plaintively like a maina trapped in a cage.

---
16.
janakasya kule jātā rāmapatnī sumadhyamā |
katham utpalapatrākṣī rāvaṇasya vaśaṁ vrajet ||

> **Meaning 16:** Born into the noble house of Janaka, wife of Rāma, how could
> that slender-waisted lady with eyes like lotus petals ever yield to Rāvaṇa?

---
17.
vinaṣṭā vā pranaṣṭā vā mr̥tā vā janakātmajā |
rāmasya priyabhāryasya na nivedayituṁ kṣamam ||

> **Meaning 17:** If the daughter of Janaka is lost, vanished, or dead, it is
> impossible for me to report this to Rāma, who loves his wife so dearly.

---
18.
nivedyamāne doṣaḥ syād doṣaḥ syād anivedane |
kathaṁ nu khalu kartavyaṁ viṣamaṁ pratibhāti me ||

> **Meaning 18:** Telling him brings disaster, yet not reporting brings disaster
> too. What on earth should I do? The dilemma seems insurmountable!

---
19.
asminn evaṁgate karye prāptakālaṁ kṣamaṁ ca kim |
bhaved iti matiṁ bhūyaḥ hanumān pravicārayan ||

> **Meaning 19:** 'In such a critical situation, what is timely and right to
> do?' Hanumān pondered deeply once again.

---
20.
yadi sītām adr̥ṣṭvāhaṁ vānarendrapurīm itaḥ |
gamiṣyāmi tataḥ ko me puruṣārtho bhaviṣyati ||

> **Meaning 20:** 'If I go back from here to the city of the Vānara king without
> seeing Sītā, what will my mission have achieved?

---
21.
mamedaṁ laṅghanaṁ vyarthaṁ sāgarasya bhaviṣyati |
praveśaściva laṅkāyā rākṣasānāṁ ca darśanam ||

> **Meaning 21:** My leap across the ocean will have been useless, as will my
> entry into Laṅkā and my reconnaissance of the Rākṣasas.

---
22.
kiṁ vā vakṣyati sugrīvaḥ harayo va samāgatāḥ |
kiṣkindhāṁ samanuprāptau tau vā daśarathātmajau ||

> **Meaning 22:** What will Sugrīva say, or the assembled Haris, or the two sons
> of Daśaratha when they arrive at Kiṣkindhā?

---
23.
gatvā tu yadi kākutsthaṁ vakṣyāmi param apriyam |
na dr̥ṣṭeti mayā sītā tatastyakṣyanti jīvitam ||

> **Meaning 23:** If I return and break this terrible news to Kākutstha — 'I did
> not find Sītā' — he will give up his life on the spot.

---
24.
paruṣaṁ dāruṇaṁ krūraṁ tīkṣṇam indriyatāpanam |
sītānimittaṁ durvākyaṁ śrutvā sa na bhaviṣyati ||

> **Meaning 24:** Hearing those harsh, terrible, cruel, and piercing words
> concerning Sītā, burning his very senses, he will not survive.

---
25.
taṁ tu kr̥cchragataṁ dr̥ṣṭvā pañcatvagatamānasaṁ |
bhr̥śānurakto medhāvī na bhaviṣyati lakṣmaṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 25:** Seeing him in agony and resolved on death, the wise Lakṣmaṇa,
> so deeply attached to his brother, will not survive either.

---
26.
vinaṣṭau bhrātarau śrutvā bharato ’pi mariṣyati |
bharataṁ ca mr̥taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā śatrughno na bhaviṣyati ||

> **Meaning 26:** Hearing that both brothers have perished, Bharata too will
> die; and seeing Bharata dead, Śatrughna will not live.

---
27.
putrān mr̥tān samīkṣyātha na bhaviṣyanti mātaraḥ |
kausalyā ca sumitrā ca kaikeyī ca na saṁśayaḥ ||

> **Meaning 27:** Seeing their sons dead, the mothers — Kausalyā, Sumitrā, and
> Kaikeyī — will surely cease to live.

---
28.
kr̥tajñaḥ satyasaṁdhaśca sugrīvaḥ plavagādhipaḥ |
rāmaṁ tathā gataṁ dr̥ṣṭvā tatastyakṣyanti jīvitam ||

> **Meaning 28:** Sugrīva, the king of the Plavagas, grateful and true to his
> vow, seeing Rāma gone, will then abandon his own life.

---
29.
durmanā vyathitā dīnā nirānandā tapasvinī |
pīḍitā bhartr̥śokena rumā tyakṣyati jīvitam ||

> **Meaning 29:** Brokenhearted, stricken with sorrow, joyless and miserable,
> Rumā will end her life in grief for her husband.

---
30.
vālijena tu duḥkhena pīḍitā śokakarśitā |
pañcatvagamane rājñaḥ tārāpi na bhaviṣyati ||

> **Meaning 30:** Already worn thin by grief over Vāli, Tārā too will not
> survive the death of King Sugrīva.

---
31.
mātāpitrorvināśena sugrīva vyasanena ca |
kumāro ’py aṅgadaḥ kasmād dhārayiṣyati jīvitam ||

> **Meaning 31:** With the death of his mother and father, and the catastrophe
> befalling Sugrīva, how could Prince Aṅgada cling to life?

---
32.
bhartr̥jena tu śokena abhibhūtā vanaukasaḥ |
śirāṁsy abhihaniṣyanti talairmuṣṭibhireva ca ||

> **Meaning 32:** Overcome with grief for their king, the forest-dwellers will
> beat their heads with their palms and fists.

---
33.
sāntvenānupradānena mānena ca yaśasvinā |
lālitāḥ kapirājena prāṇāṁstyakṣyanti vānarāḥ ||

> **Meaning 33:** Cherished by the glorious king of Kapis with comforting words,
> gifts, and respect, the Vānaras will give up their lives.

---
34.
na vaneṣu na śaileṣu na nirodheṣu vā punaḥ |
krīḍām anubhaviṣyanti sametya kapikuñjarāḥ ||

> **Meaning 34:** Never again will those Kapikuñjaras gather to sport in the
> forests, on the mountains, or in caves.

---
35.
saputradārāḥ sāmātyā bhartr̥vyasanapīḍitāḥ |
śailāgrebhyaḥ patiṣyanti sametya viṣameṣu ca ||

> **Meaning 35:** Stricken by their master's tragedy, along with their wives,
> children, and ministers, they will gather and fling themselves from mountain
> peaks into rocky chasms.

---
36.
viṣam udbandhanaṁ vāpi praveśaṁ jvalanasya vā |
upavāsam atho śastraṁ pracariṣyanti vānarāḥ ||

> **Meaning 36:** The Vānaras will take poison, hang themselves, enter blazing
> fire, starve themselves to death, or fall on their own weapons.

---
37.
ghoram ārodanaṁ manye gate mayi bhaviṣyati |
ikṣvākukulanāśaśca nāśaścaiva vanaukasām ||

> **Meaning 37:** If I return, I foresee terrible lamentation, the extinction of
> the Ikṣvāku line, and the annihilation of all the forest-dwellers!

---
38.
so ’haṁ naiva gamiṣyāmi kiṣkindhāṁ nagarīm itaḥ |
na hi śakṣyāmy ahaṁ draṣṭuṁ sugrīvaṁ maithilīṁ vinā ||

> **Meaning 38:** Therefore, I will not return to the city of Kiṣkindhā from
> here; for without Maithilī, I cannot bear to face Sugrīva.

---
39.
mayy agacchati cehasthe dharmātmānau mahārathau |
āśayā tau dhariṣyete vanarāśca manasvinaḥ ||

> **Meaning 39:** If I stay here and do not return, those two righteous
> chariot-warriors and the resolute Vānaras will sustain their lives in hope.

---
40.
hastādāno mukhādānaḥ niyato vr̥kṣamūlikaḥ |
vānaprastho bhaviṣyāmi adr̥ṣṭvā janakātmajām ||

> **Meaning 40:** Living on whatever falls into my hands or mouth, disciplined,
> dwelling at the foot of trees, I will become a hermit without seeing Janaka's
> daughter.

---
41.
sāgarānūpaje deśe bahumūlaphalodake |
citāṁ kr̥tvā pravekṣyāmi samiddham araṇīsutam ||

> **Meaning 41:** Or in this coastal land rich in roots, fruits, and water, I
> will build a funeral pyre and enter the blazing fire.

---
42.
upaviṣṭasya vā samyag liṅginaṁ sādhayiṣyataḥ |
śarīraṁ bhakṣayiṣyanti vāyasāḥ śvāpadāni ca ||

> **Meaning 42:** Or as I sit in steady contemplation seeking to dissolve my
> physical form, crows and wild beasts can devour my body.

---
43.
idam apy r̥ṣibhirdr̥ṣṭaṁ niryāṇam iti me matiḥ |
samyag āpaḥ pravekṣyāmi na cet paśyāmi jānakīm ||

> **Meaning 43:** Sages have sanctioned this way of departure too, I believe: if
> I do not find Jānakī, I will walk straight into the sea.

---
44.
sujātamūlā subhagā kīrtimālāyaśasvinī |
prabhagnā cirarātrīyaṁ mama sītām apaśyataḥ ||

> **Meaning 44:** The long-standing garland of my fame, deeply rooted, glorious,
> and renowned, is shattered because I cannot find Sītā.

---
45.
tāpaso vā bhaviṣyāmi niyato vr̥kṣamūlikaḥ |
netaḥ pratigamiṣyāmi tām adr̥ṣṭvāsitekṣaṇām ||

> **Meaning 45:** Or I will become an ascetic, living strictly at the foot of
> trees; I will never return from here without finding that dark-eyed lady.

---
46.
yadītaḥ pratigacchāmi sītām anadhigamya tām |
aṅgadaḥ sahitaiḥ sarvaiḥ vānarairna bhaviṣyati ||

> **Meaning 46:** If I return from here without discovering Sītā, Aṅgada and all
> the Vānaras together will perish.

---
47.
vināśe bahavo doṣā jīvan prāpnoti bhadrakam |
tasmāt prāṇān dhariṣyāmi dhruvo jīvati saṁgamaḥ ||

> **Meaning 47:** Yet death brings many evils, while as long as a person lives,
> he may attain good fortune. Therefore, I will hold on to my life; if one
> remains alive, reunion is certain!'

---
48.
evaṁ bahuvidhaṁ duḥkhaṁ manasā dhārayan muhuḥ |
nādhyagacchat tadā pāraṁ śokasya kapikuñjaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 48:** Weighing such manifold sorrow in his mind again and again, the
> Kapikuñjara could see no far shore to his grief.

---
49.
rāvaṇaṁ vā vadhiṣyāmi daśagrīvaṁ mahābalam |
kāmam astu hr̥tā sītā pratyācīrṇaṁ bhaviṣyati ||

> **Meaning 49:** 'Or I will kill the mighty, ten-necked Rāvaṇa! Granted that
> Sītā was abducted, this will be fitting retribution!

---
50.
atha vainaṁ samutkṣipya upary upari sāgaram |
rāmāyopahariṣyāmi paśuṁ paśupateriva ||

> **Meaning 50:** Or seizing him and carrying him across the ocean, I will
> deliver him to Rāma like a sacrificial beast to the god Paśupati!'

---
51.
iti cintā samāpannaḥ sītām anadhigamya tām |
dhyānaśokā parītātmā cintayām āsa vānaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 51:** Anxious at not having found Sītā, enveloped in brooding
> sorrow, the Vānara reflected:

---
52.
yāvat sītāṁ na paśyāmi rāmapatnīṁ yaśasvinīm |
tāvad etāṁ purīṁ laṅkāṁ vicinomi punaḥ punaḥ ||

> **Meaning 52:** 'As long as I do not find Sītā, the glorious wife of Rāma, I
> will scour this city of Laṅkā again and again.

---
53.
saṁpāti vacanāc cāpi rāmaṁ yady ānayāmy aham |
apaśyan rāghavo bhāryāṁ nirdahet sarvavānarān ||

> **Meaning 53:** If I bring Rāma here solely on Sampāti's word, and Rāghava
> does not see his wife, his wrath would consume all the Vānaras.

---
54.
ihaiva niyatāhāraḥ vatsyāmi niyatendriyaḥ |
na matkr̥te vinaśyeyuḥ sarve te naravānarāḥ ||

> **Meaning 54:** I will stay right here, controlling my diet and subduing my
> senses, so that all those men and Vānaras do not perish on my account.

---
55.
aśokavanikā cāpi mahatīyaṁ mahādrumā |
imām abhigamiṣyāmi na hīyaṁ vicitā mayā ||

> **Meaning 55:** Look, here is the vast Aśoka grove with its huge trees! I will
> enter it, for I have not searched it yet.

---
56.
vasūn rudrāṁstathādityān aśvinau maruto ’pi ca |
namaskr̥tvā gamiṣyāmi rakṣasāṁ śokavardhanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 56:** Bowing to the Vasus, Rudras, Ādityas, the Aśvins, and the
> Maruts, I will go forward to multiply the grief of the Rākṣasas.

---
57.
jitvā tu rākṣasān devīm ikṣvākukulanandinīm |
saṁpradāsyāmi rāmāyā yathāsiddhiṁ tapasvine ||

> **Meaning 57:** Defeating the Rākṣasas, I will restore the devī, the joy of
> the Ikṣvāku line, to the ascetic Rāma, like the attainment of spiritual
> perfection.'

---
58.
sa muhūrtam iva dhyātvā cintāvigrathitendriyaḥ |
udatiṣṭhan mahābāhuḥ hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ ||

> **Meaning 58:** Having meditated for a moment, his senses freed from the knots
> of anxious doubt, the mighty-armed Hanumān, son of Māruta, rose to his feet.

---
59.
namo ’stu rāmāya salakṣmaṇāya devyai ca tasyai janakātmajāyai |
namo ’stu rudrendrayamānilebhyaḥ namo ’stu candrārkamarudgaṇebhyaḥ ||

> **Meaning 59:** "Salutations to Rāma and to Lakṣmaṇa, and to Devī Sītā, the
> daughter of Janaka! Salutations to Rudra, Indra, Yama, and Anila! Salutations
> to the moon, the sun, and the host of the Maruts!"

---
60.
sa tebhyastu namaskr̥tvā sugrīvāya ca mārutiḥ |
diśaḥ sarvāḥ samālokya aśokavanikāṁ prati ||

> **Meaning 60:** Bowing to them and to Sugrīva, the son of Māruta looked in
> every direction toward the Aśoka grove.

---
61.
sa gatvā manasā pūrvam aśokavanikāṁ śubhām |
uttaraṁ cintayām āsa vānaro mārutātmajaḥ ||

> **Meaning 61:** Entering that lovely Aśoka grove in his mind first, the
> Vānara, son of Māruta, considered his next steps:

---
62.
dhruvaṁ tu rakṣobahulā bhaviṣyati vanākulā |
aśokavanikā cintyā sarvasaṁskārasaṁskr̥tā ||

> **Meaning 62:** "This Aśoka grove, dense with trees and landscaped with every
> elegance, must surely be heavily guarded by many Rākṣasas.

---
63.
rakṣiṇaścātra vihitā nūnaṁ rakṣanti pādapān |
bhagavān api sarvātmā nātikṣobhaṁ pravāyati ||

> **Meaning 63:** Guards stationed here must certainly be watching over the
> trees; even the divine Wind, the soul of all, breathes softly so as not to
> agitate them.

---
64.
saṁkṣipto ’yaṁ mayātmā ca rāmārthe rāvaṇasya ca |
siddhiṁ me saṁvidhāsyanti devāḥ sarṣigaṇāstv iha ||

> **Meaning 64:** For Rāma's purpose and to elude Rāvaṇa, I have shrunk my form;
> the devas along with the hosts of r̥ṣis will grant me success here.

---
65.
brahmā svayambhūrbhagavān devāścaiva diśantu me |
siddhim agniśca vāyuśca puruhūtaśca vajradhr̥t ||

> **Meaning 65:** May the self-born Lord Brahmā, the devas, Agni, Vāyu, and the
> thunderbolt-wielding Indra grant me success!

---
66.
varuṇaḥ pāśahastaśca somādityai tathaiva ca |
aśvinau ca mahātmānau marutaḥ sarva eva ca ||

> **Meaning 66:** May Varuṇa with the noose in hand, the moon and the sun, the
> great-souled Aśvins, and all the Maruts grant me success!

---
67.
siddhiṁ sarvāṇi bhūtāni bhūtānāṁ caiva yaḥ prabhuḥ |
dāsyanti mama ye cānye adr̥ṣṭāḥ pathi gocarāḥ ||

> **Meaning 67:** May all living beings, the sovereign of all beings, and
> whatever other divinities dwell unseen along my path grant me success!

---
68.
tad unnasaṁ pāṇḍuradantam avraṇaṁ śucismitaṁ padmapalāśalocanam |
drakṣye tad āryāvadanaṁ kadā nv ahaṁ prasannatārādhipatulyadarśanam ||

> **Meaning 68:** When will I see that noble lady's face — with its prominent
> nose, gleaming white teeth, unblemished beauty, pure smile, and lotus-petal
> eyes, radiant as the serene full moon?

---
69.
kṣudreṇa pāpena nr̥śaṁsakarmaṇā sudāruṇālāṁkr̥taveṣadhāriṇā |
balābhibhūtā abalā tapasvinī kathaṁ nu me dr̥ṣṭapathe ’dya sā bhavet ||

> **Meaning 69:** How will that frail, suffering lady, overpowered by force by
> this vile, sinful, cruel-deeded wretch disguised in terrible splendor, appear
> before my eyes today?"

---
