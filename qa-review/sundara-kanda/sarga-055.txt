=== Sarga 55 ===

---
1.
laṁkāṁ samastāṁ saṁdīpya lāṁgūlāgniṁ mahābalaḥ |
nirvāpayāmāsa tadā samudrē harisattamaḥ || 1 ||


---
2.
saṁdīpyamānāṁ vidhvastāṁ trastarakṣo gaṇāṁ purīm |
avekṣya hānumām̐l laṅkāṁ cintayām āsa vānaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 1-2:** Having set all of Laṅkā ablaze, the mighty Hanumān, the
> foremost among Haris, quenched the fire on his tail in the ocean. Then,
> looking at the city of Laṅkā still burning and ruined, its hosts of Rākṣasas
> terrified, the Vānara began to worry.

---
3.
tasyābhūt sumahāṁs trāsaḥ kutsā cātmany ajāyata |
laṁkāṁ pradahatā karma kiṁsvit kr̥tam idaṁ mayā ||

> **Meaning 3:** A great fear came over him, and he felt ashamed of himself.
> "What have I done by burning Laṅkā?" he thought.

---
4.
dhanyās te puruṣaśreṣṭha ye buddhyā kopam utthitam |
nirundhanti mahātmāno dīptam agnim ivāmbhasā ||

> **Meaning 4:** "Blessed are those noble souls, the best of men, who put out
> their rising anger with wisdom, the way water puts out a blazing fire.

---
5.
kruddhaḥ pāpaṁ na kuryāt kaḥ kruddhō hanyādgurūnapi |
kruddhaḥ paruṣayā vācā narassādhūnadhikṣipēt || 5 ||

> **Meaning 5:** An angry person will commit any sin. An angry person may even
> kill his elders. An angry person will insult good people with harsh words.

---
6.
vācyāvācyaṁ prakupitō na vijānāti karhicit |
nākāryamasti kruddhasya nāvācyaṁ vidyatē kvacit || 6 ||

> **Meaning 6:** A person in a rage never knows what should or shouldn't be
> said. Nothing is beyond a furious man to do, and nothing is too terrible for
> him to say.

---
7.
ya ssamutpatitaṁ krōdhaṁ kṣamayaiva nirasyati |
yathōragastvacaṁ jīrṇāṁ sa vai puruṣa ucyatē || 7 ||

> **Meaning 7:** The one who throws off his rising anger with patience, the way
> a snake sheds its old skin, is truly called a man.

---
8.
dhigastu māṁ sudurbuddhiṁ nirlajjaṁ pāpakr̥ttamam |
aciṁtayitvā tāṁ sītāṁ agnidaṁ svāmighātukam || 8 ||

> **Meaning 8:** Shame on me — foolish, shameless, the worst of sinners! I
> didn't think of Sītā, and I set the fire that could destroy my own master's
> cause.

---
9.
yadi dagdhā tv iyaṁ laṅkā nūnam āryāpi jānakī |
dagdhā tena mayā bhartur hataṁ kāryam ajānatā ||

> **Meaning 9:** If all of Laṅkā has burned, then noble Jānakī must surely have
> burned too. Through my own ignorance, I have ruined my master's mission.

---
10.
yad artham ayam ārambhas tat kāryam avasāditam |
mayā hi dahatā laṅkāṁ na sītā parirakṣitā ||

> **Meaning 10:** The very purpose of this whole undertaking has been wasted. In
> burning Laṅkā, I failed to protect Sītā.

---
11.
īṣatkāryam idaṁ kāryaṁ kr̥tam āsīn na saṁśayaḥ |
tasya krodhābhibhūtena mayā mūlakṣayaḥ kr̥taḥ ||

> **Meaning 11:** There's no doubt that this task was only a small one to begin
> with. Overcome by anger, I have destroyed its very root.

---
12.
vinaṣṭā jānakī vyaktaṁ na hy adagdhaḥ pradr̥śyate |
laṅkāyāḥ kaś cid uddeśaḥ sarvā bhasmīkr̥tā purī ||

> **Meaning 12:** Jānakī is surely lost. No part of Laṅkā can be seen unburned.
> The whole city has been reduced to ashes.

---
13.
yadi tad vihataṁ kāryaṁ mayā prajñāviparyayāt |
ihaiva prāṇasaṁnyāso mamāpi hy atirocate ||

> **Meaning 13:** If I have ruined the mission through this lapse of judgment,
> then giving up my life right here is the only fitting thing for me.

---
14.
kim agnau nipatāmy adya āhosvid vaḍavāmukhe |
śarīram āho sattvānāṁ dadmi sāgaravāsinām ||

> **Meaning 14:** Should I throw myself into fire today, or into the submarine
> fire under the sea, or offer my body to the creatures that live in the ocean?

---
15.
kathaṁ hi jīvatā śakyo mayā draṣṭuṁ harīśvaraḥ |
tau vā puruṣaśārdūlau kāryasarvasvaghātinā ||

> **Meaning 15:** How can I stay alive and face Sugrīva, the king of the
> Vānaras, or those two tigers among men, when I have destroyed the whole
> purpose of our mission?

---
16.
mayā khalu tad evedaṁ roṣadoṣāt pradarśitam |
prathitaṁ triṣu lokeṣu kapitam anavasthitam ||

> **Meaning 16:** My anger and my fault have shown to all three worlds that the
> fickleness of a Vānara is truly unsteady.

---
17.
dhig astu rājasaṁ bhāvam anīśam anavasthitam |
īśvareṇāpi yad rāgān mayā sītā na rakṣitā ||

> **Meaning 17:** Shame on this passionate, unrestrained, unsteady nature of
> mine! Though I am capable, I let my anger keep me from protecting Sītā.

---
18.
vinaṣṭāyāṁ tu sītāyāṁ tāv ubhau vinaśiṣyataḥ |
tayor vināśe sugrīvaḥ sabandhur vinaśiṣyati ||

> **Meaning 18:** If Sītā has perished, those two brothers will perish too. And
> when they die, Sugrīva will die along with his family.

---
19.
etad eva vacaḥ śrutvā bharato bhrātr̥vatsalaḥ |
dharmātmā sahaśatrughnaḥ kathaṁ śakṣyati jīvitum ||

> **Meaning 19:** When Bharata, so devoted to his brother, hears this news, how
> can that righteous soul go on living, together with Śatrughna?

---
20.
ikṣvākuvaṁśe dharmiṣṭhe gate nāśam asaṁśayam |
bhaviṣyanti prajāḥ sarvāḥ śokasaṁtāpapīḍitāḥ ||

> **Meaning 20:** When the righteous Ikṣvāku dynasty is certainly destroyed, all
> its people will be crushed by grief and sorrow.

---
21.
tad ahaṁ bhāgyarahito luptadharmārthasaṁgrahaḥ |
roṣadoṣaparītātmā vyaktaṁ lokavināśanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 21:** So I am unlucky, having ruined the aim of dharma and artha. My
> mind is stained by the fault of anger, and I have clearly become a destroyer
> of the world.

---
22.
iti cintayatas tasya nimittāny upapedire |
pūram apy upalabdhāni sākṣāt punar acintayat ||

> **Meaning 22:** As he was thinking this, good omens appeared — signs he had
> seen before — and he reflected on them again.

---
23.
atha vā cārusarvāṅgī rakṣitā svena tejasā |
na naśiṣyati kalyāṇī nāgnir agnau pravartate ||

> **Meaning 23:** Or perhaps she is of faultless beauty and protected by her own
> radiance. That gentle one will not perish, for fire does not burn fire.

---
24.
na hi dharmān manas tasya bhāryām amitatejasaḥ |
svacāritrābhiguptāṁ tāṁ spraṣṭum arhati pāvakaḥ ||

> **Meaning 24:** Fire cannot touch the wife of Rāma, whose power is boundless.
> She is guarded by her own virtue, and Agni would never think of harming her.

---
25.
nūnaṁ rāmaprabhāvena vaidehyāḥ sukr̥tena ca |
yan māṁ dahanakarmāyaṁ nādahad dhavyavāhanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 25:** Surely it was through Rāma's power and Vaidehī's merit that
> Agni, the carrier of offerings, did not burn me, though his very work is to
> burn.

---
26.
trayāṇāṁ bharatādīnāṁ bhrātr̥̄ṇāṁ devatā ca yā |
rāmasya ca manaḥkāntā sā kathaṁ vinaśiṣyati ||

> **Meaning 26:** She is the very devatā of the three brothers, Bharata and the
> others, and the beloved of Rāma's heart. How could she perish?

---
27.
yad vā dahanakarmāyaṁ sarvatra prabhur avyayaḥ |
na me dahati lāṅgūlaṁ katham āryāṁ pradhakṣyati ||

> **Meaning 27:** Agni is the master of all things everywhere, and he did not
> burn my tail. How then could he burn noble Sītā?

---
28.
punaścāciṁtayattatra hanumān vismitastadā |
hiraṇyanābhasya girēḥ jalamadhyē pradarśanam || 28 ||

> **Meaning 28:** Then Hanumān thought again, amazed, of Hiraṇyanābha, the
> mountain that rose from the middle of the water and showed itself to him.

---
29.
tapasā satyavākyena ananyatvāc ca bhartari |
api sā nirdahed agniṁ na tām agniḥ pradhakṣyati ||

> **Meaning 29:** Through her austerity, her truthful word, and her
> single-minded devotion to her husband, she could burn fire itself. Fire will
> never burn her.

---
30.
sa tathā cintayaṁs tatra devyā dharmaparigraham |
śuśrāva hanumān vākyaṁ cāraṇānāṁ mahātmanām ||

> **Meaning 30:** As he stood there thinking this about the devī who held fast
> to dharma, Hanumān heard the words of the great Cāraṇas.

---
31.
aho khalu kr̥taṁ karma durviṣahyaṁ hanūmatā |
agniṁ visr̥jatābhīkṣṇaṁ bhīmaṁ rākṣasasadmani ||

> **Meaning 31:** "Oh, what a deed Hanumān has done, one that no one else could
> bear! He kept unleashing terrible fire in the homes of the Rākṣasas.

---
32.
prapalāyitarakṣaḥ strī bālavr̥ddhasamākulā |
janakōlāhalādhmātā kraṁdaṁtīvādrikaṁdarē ||

> **Meaning 32:** The Rākṣasa women, children, and elders ran about in panic.
> The city roared with the uproar of its people, like a mountain cave echoing
> with cries.

---
33.
dagdheyaṁ nagarī laṅkā sāṭṭaprākāratoraṇā |
jānakī na ca dagdheti vismayo ’dbhuta eva naḥ ||

> **Meaning 33:** This whole city of Laṅkā, with its watchtowers, walls, and
> gateways, has burned. Yet Jānakī has not burned. This is truly astonishing to
> us."

---
34.
iti śuśrāva hanumān vācaṁ tāmamr̥tōpamām |
babhūva cāsya manasō harṣastatkālasaṁbhavaḥ ||

> **Meaning 34:** Hanumān heard those nectar-like words, and at that moment joy
> rose in his heart.

---
35.
sa nimittaiś ca dr̥ṣṭārthaiḥ kāraṇaiś ca mahāguṇaiḥ |
r̥ṣivākyaiś ca hanumān abhavat prītamānasaḥ ||

> **Meaning 35:** By the omens he had seen come true, by solid reasons, and by
> the words of the r̥ṣis, Hanumān became glad at heart.

---
36.
tataḥ kapiḥ prāptamanorathārthas; tām akṣatāṁ rājasutāṁ viditvā |
pratyakṣatas tāṁ punar eva dr̥ṣṭvā; pratiprayāṇāya matiṁ cakāra ||

> **Meaning 36:** Then the Kapi, his heart's desire fulfilled, knew that the
> princess was unharmed. Having seen her once more with his own eyes, he decided
> to begin his journey back.

---
