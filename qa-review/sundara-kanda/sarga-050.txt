=== Sarga 48 ===

---
1.
tam udvīkṣya mahābāhuḥ piṅgākṣaṁ purataḥ sthitam |
roṣeṇa mahatāviṣṭaḥ rāvaṇo lokarāvaṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 1:** Rāvaṇa, the mighty-armed one who makes the whole world cry out,
> looked at the tawny-eyed Hanumān standing in front of him. He was filled with
> great anger.

---
2.
śaṁkāhr̥tātmā dadhyau sa kapīṁdraṁ tējasāvr̥tam |
kimēṣa bhagavānnaṁdī bhavēt sākṣādihāgataḥ || 2 ||

---
3.
yēna śaptaḥ asmi kailāsē mayā saṁcālitē purā |
saḥ ayaṁ vānaramūrti ssyāt kiṁsvidbāṇaḥ api vā’suraḥ ||

> **Meaning 2-3:** Yet his heart was shaken by doubt, and he wondered about the
> Vānara chief who shone with such radiance. "Could this be the blessed Nandī
> himself, come here in person? Long ago, when I shook Mount Kailāsa, he cursed
> me. Is he now appearing as a Vānara? Or could he be Bāṇa, or some asura?"

---
4.
sa rājā roṣatāmrākṣaḥ prahastaṁ mantrisattamam |
kālayuktam uvācedaṁ vaco vipulam arthavat ||

> **Meaning 4:** His eyes red with anger, the king spoke to Prahasta, the best
> of his ministers. His words were weighty and meaningful, and suited the
> moment.

---
5.
durātmā pr̥cchyatām eṣa kutaḥ kiṁ vāsya kāraṇam |
vanabhaṅge ca kaḥ asyārtho rākṣasīnāṁ ca tarjane ||

> **Meaning 5:** "Question this wicked one. Where has he come from, and what is
> his reason? Why did he destroy the grove, and what did he gain by threatening
> the Rākṣasīs?"

---
6.
matpurī mapradhr̥ṣyāṁ vā gamanē kiṁ prayōjanam |
āyōdhanē vā kiṁ kāryaṁ pr̥cchyatā mēṣa durmatiḥ ||

> **Meaning 6:** "What is his purpose in entering my city, which no one can
> approach? Why did he fight? Ask this fool all of this."

---
7.
rāvaṇasya vacaḥ śrutvā prahasto vākyam abravīt |
samāśvasihi bhadraṁ te na bhīḥ kāryā tvayā kape ||

> **Meaning 7:** Hearing Rāvaṇa's words, Prahasta spoke to Hanumān. "Calm down,
> and may you be well. You have no need to be afraid, Kapi."

---
8.
yadi tāvat tvam indreṇa preṣito rāvaṇālayam |
tattvam ākhyāhi mā te bhūd bhayaṁ vānara mokṣyase ||

> **Meaning 8:** "If Indra sent you to Rāvaṇa's palace, tell us the truth. Don't
> be afraid, Vānara. You will be released."

---
9.
yadi vaiśravaṇasya tvaṁ yamasya varuṇasya ca |
cārurūpam idaṁ kr̥tvā yamasya varuṇasya ca ||

> **Meaning 9:** "Perhaps you were sent by Kubera, or by Yama or Varuṇa, and
> took this charming form to enter here."

---
10.
viṣṇunā preṣito vāpi dūto vijayakāṅkṣiṇā |
na hi te vānaraṁ tejaḥ rūpamātraṁ tu vānaram ||

> **Meaning 10:** "Or perhaps Viṣṇu, who longs for victory, sent you as his
> messenger. Your power is not that of an ordinary Vānara, and only your
> appearance is that of a Vānara."

---
11.
tattvataḥ kathayasvādya tato vānara mokṣyase |
anr̥taṁ vadataścāpi durlabhaṁ tava jīvitam ||

> **Meaning 11:** "Tell us the truth today, Vānara, and you will be set free. If
> you lie, your life will be hard to save."

---
12.
atha vā yannimittaste praveśo rāvaṇālaye |
evam ukto harivaraḥ tadā rakṣogaṇeśvaram ||
> **Meaning 12:** "Or tell us the reason you entered Rāvaṇa's palace." Spoken to
> like this, Hanumān, the best of the Haris, then answered the lord of the
> Rākṣasa hosts.

---
13.

abravīn nāsmi śakrasya yamasya varuṇasya vā |
dhanadena na me sakhyaṁ viṣṇunā nāsmi coditaḥ ||
> **Meaning 13:** "I am not sent by Indra, Yama, or Varuṇa. I have no friendship
> with Kubera, and Viṣṇu has not sent me."

---
14.

jātireva mama tv eṣā vānaraḥ aham ihāgataḥ |
darśane rākṣasendrasya durlabhe tad idaṁ mayā ||
> **Meaning 14:** "I am a Vānara by birth, and I came here as one. It is very
> hard to get an audience with the king of the Rākṣasas, so I did this."

---
15.

vanaṁ rākṣasarājasya darśanārthe vināśitam ||
tataste rākṣasāḥ prāptā balino yuddhakāṅkṣiṇaḥ |
> **Meaning 15:** "I destroyed the grove of the Rākṣasa king only to get to see
> him. Then strong Rākṣasas came, eager for battle."

---
16.

rakṣaṇārthaṁ ca dehasya pratiyuddhā mayā raṇe ||
astrapāśairna śakyaḥ ahaṁ baddhuṁ devāsurairapi |
> **Meaning 16:** "I fought them back in battle to protect my own body. Neither
> devatās nor asuras can bind me with weapons or nooses."

---
17.

pitāmahād eva varaḥ mamāpy eṣaḥ abhyupāgataḥ ||
rājānaṁ draṣṭukāmena mayāstram anuvartitam |
> **Meaning 17:** "This boon was given to me by Brahmā himself. I allowed the
> astra to bind me because I wanted to see the king."

---
18.

vimukto aham astreṇa rākṣasaistv atipīḍitaḥ |
kēnacidrājakāryēṇa saṁprāptaḥ asmi tavāṁtikam ||
> **Meaning 18:** "I was freed from the astra, but the Rākṣasas treated me very
> harshly. I have come to you on some royal business."

---
19.
dūtaḥ aham iti vijñeyo rāghavasyāmitaujasaḥ |
śrūyatāṁ cāpi vacanaṁ mama pathyam idaṁ prabho ||

> **Meaning 19:** "Know that I am the messenger of Rāghava, whose power is
> boundless. King, please also listen to this message of mine, which is for your
> good."

---
