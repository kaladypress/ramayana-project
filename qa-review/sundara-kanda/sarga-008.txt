=== Sarga 8 ===

---
1.
tatra divyopamaṁ mukhyaṁ sphāṭikaṁ ratnabhūṣitam |
avekṣamāṇo hanumān dadarśa śayanāsanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tasya caikatame deśe so ’gryamālyavibhūṣitam |
dadarśa pāṇḍuraṁ chatraṁ tārādhipatisaṁnibham ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
bālavyajanahastābhir vījyamānaṁ samantataḥ |
gandhaiś ca vividhair juṣṭaṁ varadhūpena dhūpitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
paramāstaraṇāstīrṇam āvikājinasaṁvr̥tam |
dāmabhir varamālyānāṁ samantād upaśobhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tasmiñ jīmūtasaṁkāśaṁ pradīptottamakuṇḍalam |
lohitākṣaṁ mahābāhuṁ mahārajatavāsasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
lohitenānuliptāṅgaṁ candanena sugandhinā |
saṁdhyāraktam ivākāśe toyadaṁ sataḍidguṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
vr̥tam ābharaṇair divyaiḥ surūpaṁ kāmarūpiṇam |
savr̥kṣavanagulmāḍhyaṁ prasuptam iva mandaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
krīḍitvoparataṁ rātrau varābharaṇabhūṣitam |
priyaṁ rākṣasakanyānāṁ rākṣasānāṁ sukhāvaham ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
pītvāpy uparataṁ cāpi dadarśa sa mahākapiḥ |
bhāskare śayane vīraṁ prasuptaṁ rākṣasādhipam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
niḥśvasantaṁ yathā nāgaṁ rāvaṇaṁ vānarottamaḥ |
āsādya paramodvignaḥ so ’pāsarpat subhītavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
athārohaṇam āsādya vedikāntaram āśritaḥ |
suptaṁ rākṣasaśārdūlaṁ prekṣate sma mahākapiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
śuśubhe rākṣasendrasya svapataḥ śayanottamam |
gandhahastini saṁviṣṭe yathāprasravaṇaṁ mahat ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
kāñcanāṅgadanaddhau ca dadarśa sa mahātmanaḥ |
vikṣiptau rākṣasendrasya bhujāv indradhvajopamau ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
airāvataviṣāṇāgrair āpīḍitakr̥tavraṇau |
vajrollikhitapīnāṁsau viṣṇucakraparikṣitau ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
pīnau samasujātāṁsau saṁgatau balasaṁyutau |
sulakṣaṇa nakhāṅguṣṭhau svaṅgulītalalakṣitau ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
saṁhatau parighākārau vr̥ttau karikaropamau |
vikṣiptau śayane śubhre pañcaśīrṣāv ivoragau ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
śaśakṣatajakalpena suśītena sugandhinā |
candanena parārdhyena svanuliptau svalaṁkr̥tau ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
uttamastrīvimr̥ditau gandhottamaniṣevitau |
yakṣapannagagandharvadevadānavarāviṇau ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
dadarśa sa kapis tasya bāhū śayanasaṁsthitau |
mandarasyāntare suptau mahārhī ruṣitāv iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tābhyāṁ sa paripūrṇābhyāṁ bhujābhyāṁ rākṣasādhipaḥ |
śuśubhe ’calasaṁkāśaḥ śr̥ṅgābhyām iva mandaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
cūtapuṁnāgasurabhir bakulottamasaṁyutaḥ |
mr̥ṣṭānnarasasaṁyuktaḥ pānagandhapuraḥsaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tasya rākṣasasiṁhasya niścakrāma mukhān mahān |
śayānasya viniḥśvāsaḥ pūrayann iva tad gr̥ham ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
muktāmaṇivicitreṇa kāñcanena virājatā |
mukuṭenāpavr̥ttena kuṇḍalojjvalitānanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
raktacandanadigdhena tathā hāreṇa śobhitā |
pīnāyataviśālena vakṣasābhivirājitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
pāṇḍureṇāpaviddhena kṣaumeṇa kṣatajekṣaṇam |
mahārheṇa susaṁvītaṁ pītenottamavāsasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
māṣarāśipratīkāśaṁ niḥśvasantaṁ bhujaṅgavat |
gāṅge mahati toyānte prasutamiva kuñjaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
caturbhiḥ kāñcanair dīpair dīpyamānaiś caturdiśam |
prakāśīkr̥tasarvāṅgaṁ meghaṁ vidyudgaṇair iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
pādamūlagatāś cāpi dadarśa sumahātmanaḥ |
patnīḥ sa priyabhāryasya tasya rakṣaḥpater gr̥he ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
śaśiprakāśavadanā varakuṇḍalabhūṣitāḥ |
amlānamālyābharaṇā dadarśa hariyūthapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
nr̥ttavāditrakuśalā rākṣasendrabhujāṅkagāḥ |
varābharaṇadhāriṇyo niṣannā dadr̥śe kapiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
vajravaidūryagarbhāṇi śravaṇānteṣu yoṣitām |
dadarśa tāpanīyāni kuṇḍalāny aṅgadāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
tāsāṁ candropamair vaktraiḥ śubhair lalitakuṇḍalaiḥ |
virarāja vimānaṁ tan nabhas tārāgaṇair iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
madavyāyāmakhinnās tā rākṣasendrasya yoṣitaḥ |
teṣu teṣv avakāśeṣu prasuptās tanumadhyamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
kā cid vīṇāṁ pariṣvajya prasuptā saṁprakāśate |
mahānadīprakīrṇeva nalinī potam āśritā ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
anyā kakṣagatenaiva maḍḍukenāsitekṣaṇā |
prasuptā bhāminī bhāti bālaputreva vatsalā ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
paṭahaṁ cārusarvāṅgī pīḍya śete śubhastanī |
cirasya ramaṇaṁ labdhvā pariṣvajyeva kāminī ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
kā cid aṁśaṁ pariṣvajya suptā kamalalocanā |
nidrāvaśam anuprāptā sahakānteva bhāminī ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
anyā kanakasaṁkāśair mr̥dupīnair manoramaiḥ |
mr̥daṅgaṁ paripīḍyāṅgaiḥ prasuptā mattalocanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
bhujapārśvāntarasthena kakṣageṇa kr̥śodarī |
paṇavena sahānindyā suptā madakr̥taśramā ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
ḍiṇḍimaṁ parigr̥hyānyā tathaivāsaktaḍiṇḍimā |
prasuptā taruṇaṁ vatsam upagūhyeva bhāminī ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
kā cid āḍambaraṁ nārī bhujasaṁbhogapīḍitam |
kr̥tvā kamalapatrākṣī prasuptā madamohitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
kalaśīm apaviddhyānyā prasuptā bhāti bhāminī |
vasante puṣpaśabalā māleva parimārjitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
pāṇibhyāṁ ca kucau kā cit suvarṇakalaśopamau |
upagūhyābalā suptā nidrābalaparājitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
anyā kamalapatrākṣī pūrṇendusadr̥śānanā |
anyām āliṅgya suśroṇī prasuptā madavihvalā ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
ātodyāni vicitrāṇi pariṣvajya varastriyaḥ |
nipīḍya ca kucaiḥ suptāḥ kāminyaḥ kāmukān iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
tāsām ekāntavinyaste śayānāṁ śayane śubhe |
dadarśa rūpasaṁpannām aparāṁ sa kapiḥ striyam ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
muktāmaṇisamāyuktair bhūṣaṇaiḥ suvibhūṣitām |
vibhūṣayantīm iva ca svaśriyā bhavanottamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
gaurīṁ kanakavarṇābhām iṣṭām antaḥpureśvarīm |
kapir mandodarīṁ tatra śayānāṁ cārurūpiṇīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
sa tāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā mahābāhur bhūṣitāṁ mārutātmajaḥ |
tarkayām āsa sīteti rūpayauvanasaṁpadā |
harṣeṇa mahatā yukto nananda hariyūthapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
āsphoṭayām āsa cucumba pucchaṁ; nananda cikrīḍa jagau jagāma |
stambhān arohan nipapāta bhūmau; nidarśayan svāṁ prakr̥tiṁ kapīnām ||

=== meaning ===

---
