=== Sarga 8 ===

---
1.
sa tasya madhyē bhavanasya saṁsthitaṁ
mahadvimānaṁ maṇivajracitritam |
pratapta jāṁbūnadajāla kr̥trimaṁ
dadarśa vīraḥ pavanātmajaḥ kapiḥ ||

> **Meaning 1:** Standing in the center of that palace complex, the heroic Kapi,
> son of Pavana, saw the immense aerial chariot, adorned with gems and diamonds
> and fashioned with latticework of refined gold.

---
2.
tadapramēyāpratikāra kr̥trimaṁ
kr̥taṁ svayaṁ sādhviti viśvakarmaṇā |
divaṁ gataṁ vāyupatha pratiṣṭhitaṁ
vyarājatādityapathasya lakṣmavat ||

> **Meaning 2:** Wrought with immeasurable and matchless skill by Viśvakarmā
> himself, who deemed it his masterpiece, it was capable of soaring through
> heaven along the wind's course, shining like a jewel along the path of the
> sun.

---
3.
na tatra kiṁcinna kr̥taṁ prayatnatō
na tatra kiṁcinna mahārharatnavat |
na tē viśēṣā niyatāssurēṣvapi
na tatra kiṁcinna mahāviśēṣavat ||

> **Meaning 3:** There was nothing in it that was not crafted with exquisite
> care, nothing that was not set with priceless gems, nothing that did not
> possess supreme distinction — features not found fixed even among the devatās.

---
4.
tapaḥ samādhāna parākramārjitaṁ
manassamādhāna vicāracāriṇam |
anēkasaṁsthāna viśēṣanirmitaṁ
tatastatastulya viśēṣa darśanam ||

> **Meaning 4:** Won through intense austerity and prowess, it moved wherever
> the mind directed it. Constructed with countless distinct chambers, it
> presented an equally marvelous appearance in every part.

---
5.
manassamādhāya tu śīghragāminaṁ
durāvaraṁ mārutatulya gāminam |
mahātmanāṁ puṇyakr̥tāṁ mahardhināṁ
yaśasvinā magryamudā mivālayam ||

> **Meaning 5:** Moving at tremendous speed at the mere focus of the mind,
> irresistible and swift as the wind, it seemed like the supreme abode of joy
> reserved for glorious, great-souled beings of immense merit and prosperity.

---
6.
viśēṣamālaṁbya viśēṣasaṁsthitaṁ
vicitrakūṭaṁ bahukūṭamaṁḍitam |
manōbhirāmaṁ śaradiṁdunirmalaṁ
vicitrakūṭaṁ śikharaṁ girēryathā ||

> **Meaning 6:** Supported by extraordinary design and standing in unique
> grandeur, adorned with numerous wonderful spires, it was delightful to the
> heart, spotless as the autumn moon, resembling the multi-peaked summit of
> Mount Citrakūṭa.

---
7.
vahaṁti yaṁ kuṁḍalaśōbhitānanāḥ
mahāśanā vyōmacarā niśācarāḥ |
vivr̥tta vidhvasta viśālalōcanāḥ
mahājavā bhūtagaṇāssahasraśaḥ ||

> **Meaning 7:** It was borne by thousands of sky-roaming night-stalkers and
> swift spirits with huge appetites, their faces adorned with earrings and their
> wide, rolling eyes ablaze.

---
8.
vasaṁtapuṣpōtkara cārudarśanaṁ
vasaṁtamāsādapi kāṁtadarśanam |
sa puṣpakaṁ tatra vimānamuttamaṁ
dadarśa tadvānaravīrasattamaḥ ||

> **Meaning 8:** More delightful to look upon than a profusion of spring
> blossoms, lovelier even than the season of spring itself, that supreme aerial
> chariot, the Puṣpaka, was seen there by that best among Vānara heroes.
