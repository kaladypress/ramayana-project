=== Sarga 3 ===

---
1.
sa lambaśikhare lambe lambatoyadasaṁnibhe |
sattvam āsthāya medhāvī hanumān mārutātmajaḥ ||

> **Meaning 1:** Gathering his inner courage, the wise Hanumān, son of Māruta,
> stood poised upon the towering crest of Mount Lamba, which loomed like a dark,
> hanging rain cloud.

---
2.
niśi laṅkāṁ mahāsattvo viveśa kapikuñjaraḥ |
ramyakānanatoyāḍhyāṁ purīṁ rāvaṇapālitām ||

> **Meaning 2:** Under the shroud of night, that hero of immense strength, the
> Kapikuñjara, entered Laṅkā, the city guarded by Rāvaṇa, rich in delightful
> forests and sparkling water reservoirs.

---
3.
śāradāmbudharaprakhyair bhavanair upaśobhitām |
sāgaropamanirghoṣāṁ sāgarānilasevitām ||

> **Meaning 3:** It was adorned with palaces gleaming like autumn clouds, its
> deep murmur rivaled the roar of the ocean, and it was constantly caressed by
> the ocean breeze.

---
4.
supuṣṭabalasaṁguptāṁ yathaiva viṭapāvatīm |
cārutoraṇaniryūhāṁ pāṇḍuradvāratoraṇām ||

> **Meaning 4:** Well-defended by thriving legions of warriors, it rivaled
> Kubera's city Viṭapāvatī, embellished with graceful turrets, arched entries,
> and pale-white gateways.

---
5.
bhujagācaritāṁ guptāṁ śubhāṁ bhogavatīm iva |
tāṁ savidyudghanākīrṇāṁ jyotirmārganiṣevitām ||

> **Meaning 5:** Frequented and guarded by serpent-like warriors, it looked like
> the subterranean Bhogavatī, surrounded by lightning-filled clouds and touching
> the stellar pathways of the sky.

---
6.
caṇḍamārutanirhrādāṁ yathendrasyāmarāvatīm |
śātakumbhena mahatā prākāreṇābhisaṁvr̥tām ||

> **Meaning 6:** Echoing with the whistle of fierce winds, it resembled Indra's
> Amarāvatī, encircled completely by a massive rampart of molten gold.

---
7.
kiṅkiṇījālaghoṣābhiḥ patākābhir alaṁkr̥tām |
āsādya sahasā hr̥ṣṭaḥ prākāram abhipedivān ||

> **Meaning 7:** Decked with fluttering banners chiming with nets of tiny bells,
> the rampart was approached swiftly by Hanumān, whose heart thrilled with joy.

---
8.
vismayāviṣṭahr̥dayaḥ purīm ālokya sarvataḥ |
jāmbūnadamayair dvārair vaidūryakr̥tavedikaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 8:** Filled with wonder at heart, he surveyed the city on all sides,
> gazing at gates wrought of refined gold with daises carved of lapis lazuli.

---
9.
maṇisphaṭika muktābhir maṇikuṭṭimabhūṣitaiḥ |
taptahāṭakaniryūhai rājatāmalapāṇḍuraiḥ ||

> **Meaning 9:** The floors were paved with mosaics of gems, crystal, and
> pearls, while turrets of heated gold stood alongside walls gleaming with
> spotless white silver.

---
10.
vaidūryatalasopānaiḥ sphāṭikāntarapāṁsubhiḥ |
cārusaṁjavanopetaiḥ kham ivotpatitaiḥ śubhaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 10:** Its staircases of cat's-eye gems were strewn with glittering
> crystal dust, leading to magnificent quadrangles that seemed to leap upward
> into the sky.

---
11.
krauñcabarhiṇasaṁghuṣṭe rājahaṁsaniṣevitaiḥ |
tūryābharaṇanirghoṣaiḥ sarvataḥ pratināditām ||

> **Meaning 11:** Resounding with the calls of krauñca birds and peacocks and
> graced by royal swans, the city echoed in every quarter with musical
> instruments and the chime of jewelry.

---
12.
vasvokasārāpratimāṁ samīkṣya nagarīṁ tataḥ |
kham ivotpatitāṁ laṅkāṁ jaharṣa hanumān kapiḥ ||

> **Meaning 12:** Seeing Laṅkā, which rivaled Kubera's celestial capital
> Vasvokasārā and seemed to soar into the heavens, the Kapi Hanumān rejoiced.

---
13.
tāṁ samīkṣya purīṁ laṅkāṁ rākṣasādhipateḥ śubhām |
anuttamām r̥ddhiyutāṁ cintayām āsa vīryavān ||

> **Meaning 13:** Observing that splendid city of the Rākṣasa king, endowed with
> unsurpassed prosperity, the valiant hero reflected within himself:

---
14.
neyam anyena nagarī śakyā dharṣayituṁ balāt |
rakṣitā rāvaṇabalair udyatāyudhadhāribhiḥ ||

> **Meaning 14:** "This city cannot be taken by assault by any ordinary force,
> defended as it is by Rāvaṇa's troops with their weapons perpetually raised!

---
15.
kumudāṅgadayor vāpi suṣeṇasya mahākapeḥ |
prasiddheyaṁ bhaved bhūmir maindadvividayor api ||

> **Meaning 15:** This fortress might be accessible only to Kumuda, Aṅgada, the
> great Kapi Suṣeṇa, or the two brothers Mainda and Dvivida.

---
16.
vivasvatas tanūjasya hareś ca kuśaparvaṇaḥ |
r̥kṣasya ketumālasya mama caiva gatir bhavet ||

> **Meaning 16:** It could be entered only by the son of Vivasvān, the Hari
> Kuśaparvan, the bear chieftain Ketumāla, and myself."

---
17.
samīkṣya tu mahābāho rāghavasya parākramam |
lakṣmaṇasya ca vikrāntam abhavat prītimān kapiḥ ||

> **Meaning 17:** Yet, reflecting upon the immense prowess of mighty-armed
> Rāghava and the valor of Lakṣmaṇa, the Kapi felt heartened and confident.

---
18.
tāṁ ratnavasanopetāṁ koṣṭhāgārāvataṁsakām |
yantrāgārastanīm r̥ddhāṁ pramadām iva bhūṣitām ||

> **Meaning 18:** He looked upon that prosperous city as upon an adorned woman:
> dressed in robes of jewels, with storehouses for ear-ornaments and armories
> for her breasts.

---
19.
tāṁ naṣṭatimirāṁ dīpair bhāsvaraiś ca mahāgr̥haiḥ |
nagarīṁ rākṣasendrasya dadarśa sa mahākapiḥ ||

> **Meaning 19:** The great Kapi saw that capital of the Rākṣasa king, where
> darkness was banished by bright torches and glowing mansions.

---
20.
praviṣṭaḥ sattvasaṁpanno niśāyāṁ mārutātmajaḥ |
sa mahāpatham āsthāya muktāpuṣpavirājitam ||

> **Meaning 20:** Venturing inside under cover of night, full of resolve, the
> son of Māruta stepped onto the royal highway strewn with pearls and flowers.

---
21.
hasitodghuṣṭaninadais tūryaghoṣa puraḥ saraiḥ |
vajrāṅkuśanikāśaiś ca vajrajālavibhūṣitaiḥ |
gr̥hamedhaiḥ purī ramyā babhāse dyaur ivāmbudaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 21:** Filled with bursts of laughter, preceded by festive music, and
> lined with grand houses adorned with diamond lattices and motifs resembling
> lightning bolts, that lovely city shone like the sky veiled in clouds.

---
22.
prajajvāla tadā laṅkā rakṣogaṇagr̥haiḥ śubhaiḥ |
sitābhrasadr̥śaiś citraiḥ padmasvastikasaṁsthitaiḥ |
vardhamānagr̥haiś cāpi sarvataḥ suvibhāṣitaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 22:** Laṅkā sparkled with the splendid mansions of the Rākṣasas,
> resembling white clouds, built in lotus, svastika, and vardhamāna designs, and
> richly decorated on all sides.

---
23.
tāṁ citramālyābharaṇāṁ kapirājahitaṁkaraḥ |
rāghavārthaṁ carañ śrīmān dadarśa ca nananda ca ||

> **Meaning 23:** Roaming for the sake of Rāghava, that glorious hero devoted to
> the welfare of the king of Kapis observed the city arrayed with colorful
> garlands and ornaments, and rejoiced.

---
24.
śuśrāva madhuraṁ gītaṁ tristhānasvarabhūṣitam |
strīṇāṁ madasamr̥ddhānāṁ divi cāpsarasām iva ||

> **Meaning 24:** He heard sweet songs spanning the three registers sung by
> women in their revelry, sounding like singing Apsarās in heaven.

---
25.
śuśrāva kāñcīninādaṁ nūpurāṇāṁ ca niḥsvanam |
sopānaninadāṁś caiva bhavaneṣu mahātmanam |
āsphoṭitaninādāṁś ca kṣveḍitāṁś ca tatas tataḥ ||

> **Meaning 25:** He heard the tinkling of waistbands, the chiming of anklets,
> and the footsteps on staircases in the palaces of the nobles, along with the
> arm-clapping and martial roars of warriors here and there.

---
26.
svādhyāya niratāṁś caiva yātudhānān dadarśa saḥ |
rāvaṇastavasaṁyuktān garjato rākṣasān api ||

> **Meaning 26:** He saw Yātudhānas engaged in sacred Vedic recitation, as well
> as fierce Rākṣasas roaring out praises in honor of Rāvaṇa.

---
27.
rājamārgaṁ samāvr̥tya sthitaṁ rakṣobalaṁ mahat |
dadarśa madhyame gulme rākṣasasya carān bahūn ||

> **Meaning 27:** He saw a massive army of Rākṣasas occupying the royal highway,
> and stationed at the central outpost, numerous sentries and scouts of the
> king.

---
28.
dīkṣitāñ jaṭilān muṇḍān go’jināmbaravāsasaḥ |
darbhamuṣṭipraharaṇān agnikuṇḍāyudhāṁs tathā ||

> **Meaning 28:** He saw consecrated initiates—some with matted locks, some
> shaven-headed, some clad in deerskins and ox-hides—bearing handfuls of darbha
> grass as weapons and holding fire-vessels.

---
29.
kūṭamudgarapāṇīṁś ca daṇḍāyudhadharān api |
ekākṣānekakarṇāṁś ca calallambapayodharān ||

> **Meaning 29:** He saw guards armed with iron mallets and clubs, some with a
> single eye or multiple ears, and some with sagging, pendulous bellies.

---
30.
karālān bhugnavaktrāṁś ca vikaṭān vāmanāṁs tathā |
dhanvinaḥ khaḍginaś caiva śataghnī musalāyudhān |
parighottamahastāṁś ca vicitrakavacojjvalān ||

> **Meaning 30:** He saw dreadful guards with twisted faces, misshapen bodies,
> and dwarfish statures, standing alongside archers, swordsmen, warriors armed
> with śataghnīs, pestles, and massive iron bars, all gleaming in ornate armor.

---
31.
nātiṣṭhūlān nātikr̥śān nātidīrghātihrasvakān |
virūpān bahurūpāṁś ca surūpāṁś ca suvarcasaḥ ||

> **Meaning 31:** He saw warriors neither too stout nor too thin, neither
> excessively tall nor short—some grotesque, some shapeshifters, and others
> handsome and of radiant vitality.

---
32.
śaktivr̥kṣāyudhāṁś caiva paṭṭiśāśanidhāriṇaḥ |
kṣepaṇīpāśahastāṁś ca dadarśa sa mahākapiḥ ||

> **Meaning 32:** The great Kapi saw warriors carrying javelins, uprooted trees,
> spears, thunderbolt-like weapons, slings, and nooses in their hands.

---
33.
sragviṇas tv anuliptāṁś ca varābharaṇabhūṣitān |
tīkṣṇaśūladharāṁś caiva vajriṇaś ca mahābalān ||

> **Meaning 33:** Wearing garlands, anointed with sandalwood paste, adorned with
> fine ornaments, those mighty warriors carried sharp spears and thunderbolt
> weapons.

---
34.
śatasāhasram avyagram ārakṣaṁ madhyamaṁ kapiḥ |
prākārāvr̥tam atyantaṁ dadarśa sa mahākapiḥ ||

> **Meaning 34:** Past them, the great Kapi observed the central garrison: a
> hundred thousand alert and disciplined troops stationed securely behind inner
> ramparts.

---
35.
triviṣṭapanibhaṁ divyaṁ divyanādavināditam |
vājiheṣitasaṁghuṣṭaṁ nāditaṁ bhūṣaṇais tathā ||

> **Meaning 35:** Resembling heaven itself, the palace resounded with celestial
> music, the neighing of spirited horses, and the ceaseless chiming of
> ornaments.

---
36.
rathair yānair vimānaiś ca tathā gajahayaiḥ śubhaiḥ |
vāraṇaiś ca caturdantaiḥ śvetābhranicayopamaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 36:** It was filled with war chariots, palanquins, flying vimānas,
> noble horses and elephants, including magnificent four-tusked elephants that
> looked like heaps of white clouds.

---
37.
bhūṣitaṁ ruciradvāraṁ mattaiś ca mr̥gapakṣibhiḥ |
rākṣasādhipater guptam āviveśa gr̥haṁ kapiḥ ||

> **Meaning 37:** Passing through lovely gateways adorned with cheerful animals
> and birds, the Kapi slipped quietly into the guarded palace of the Rākṣasa
> king.

---
