=== Sarga 38 ===

---
1.
tataḥ sa kapiśārdūlaḥ tena vākyena harṣitaḥ |
sītām uvāca tacchrutvā vākyaṁ vākyaviśāradaḥ ||

> **Meaning 1:** Pleased by her words, that tiger among the Kapis, skilled in
> speech, replied to Sītā:

---
2.
yuktarūpaṁ tvayā devi bhāṣitaṁ śubhadarśane |
sadr̥śaṁ strīsvabhāvasya sādhvīnāṁ vinayasya ca ||

> **Meaning 2:** "Devī, lovely one, what you have said is right. It suits a
> woman's nature and the modesty of a faithful wife.

---
3.
strītvaṁ na tu samarthaṁ hi sāgaraṁ vyativartitum |
mām adhiṣṭhāya vistīrṇaṁ śatayojanam āyatam ||

> **Meaning 3:** "It is true that, as a woman, you could not cross the ocean, a
> hundred yojanas wide, riding on me.

---
4.
dvitīyaṁ kāraṇaṁ yacca bravīṣi vinayānvite |
rāmād anyasya nārhāmi saṁsparśam iti jānaki ||

> **Meaning 4:** "And the second reason you give, modest Jānakī — that you must
> not touch anyone but Rāma —

---
5.
etat te devi sadr̥śaṁ patnyāstasya mahātmanaḥ |
kā hy anyā tvām r̥te devi brūyād vacanam īdr̥śam ||

> **Meaning 5:** "is worthy of you, devī, the wife of that great-souled one. Who
> but you, devī, would speak like this?

---
6.
śroṣyate caiva kākutsthaḥ sarvaṁ niravaśeṣataḥ |
ceṣṭitaṁ yat tvayā devi bhāṣitaṁ mama cāgrataḥ ||

> **Meaning 6:** "Devī, Kākutstha will hear everything, in full — all that you
> have done and said in front of me.

---
7.
kāraṇairbahubhirdevi rāma priyacikīrṣayā |
snehapraskannamanasā mayaitat samudīritam ||

> **Meaning 7:** "Devī, I suggested this for many reasons — wanting to please
> Rāma, my heart overflowing with affection.

---
8.
laṅkāyā duṣpraveśatvād dustaratvān mahodadheḥ |
sāmarthyād ātmanaścaiva mayaitat samudāhr̥tam ||

> **Meaning 8:** "I proposed it because Laṅkā is so hard to enter, because the
> great ocean is so hard to cross, and because I trust my own strength.

---
9.
icchāmi tvāṁ samānetum adyaiva raghubandhunā |
gurusnehena bhaktyā ca nānyathā tad udāhr̥tam ||

> **Meaning 9:** "I wanted to bring you back to Rāma, the pride of the Raghus,
> this very day. I said it out of devotion and love for my master, and for no
> other reason.

---
10.
yadi notsahase yātuṁ mayā sārdham anindite |
abhijñānaṁ prayaccha tvaṁ jānīyād rāghavo hi yat ||

> **Meaning 10:** "Faultless lady, if you are not willing to come with me, then
> give me a token that Rāghava will recognize."

---
11.
evam uktā hanumatā sītā surasutopamā |
uvāca vacanaṁ mandaṁ bāṣpapragrathitākṣaram ||

> **Meaning 11:** Addressed this way by Hanumān, Sītā, who was like a daughter
> of the devas, spoke slowly, her words choked with tears:

---
12.
idaṁ śreṣṭham abhijñānaṁ brūyāstvaṁ tu mama priyam |
śailasya citrakūṭasya pāde pūrvottare tadā ||

> **Meaning 12:** "Tell my beloved this, the best of all tokens: Back then, at
> the north-eastern foot of Mount Citrakūṭa,

---
13.
tāpasāśramavāsinyāḥ prājyamūlaphalodake |
tasmin siddhāśrame deśe mandākinyā adūrataḥ ||

> **Meaning 13:** "when I was living in a hermitage of ascetics, rich in roots,
> fruits, and water, in that place where siddhas dwelt, not far from the
> Mandākinī —

---
14.
tasyopavanaṣaṇḍeṣu nānāpuṣpasugandhiṣu |
vihr̥tya salilaklinnā tavāṅke samupāviśam ||

> **Meaning 14:** "after wandering through its groves, fragrant with every kind
> of flower, I came back wet from the water and sat down in your lap.

---
15.
tato māṁsasamāyuktaḥ vāyasaḥ paryatuṇḍayat |
tam ahaṁ loṣṭam udyamya vārayāmi sma vāyasaṁ ||

> **Meaning 15:** "Then a crow, greedy for meat, kept pecking at me. I picked up
> a clod of earth and tried to drive it away.

---
16.
dārayan sa ca māṁ kākaḥ tatraiva parilīyate |
na cāpy uparaman māṁsād bhakṣārthī balibhojanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 16:** "But the crow kept tearing at me and hid right there. Hungry
> for food, that eater of offerings would not stop attacking.

---
17.
utkarṣantyāṁ ca raśanāṁ kruddhāyāṁ mayi pakṣiṇe |
sraṁsamāne ca vasane tato dr̥ṣṭā tvayā hy aham ||

> **Meaning 17:** "As I angrily tugged at my girdle to strike at the bird, my
> garment slipped — and that is when you saw me.

---
18.
tvayā vihasitā cāhaṁ kruddhā saṁlajjitā tadā |
bhakṣya gr̥ddhena kālena dāritā tvām upāgatā ||

> **Meaning 18:** "You laughed at me, and I was angry and embarrassed. Torn by
> that greedy crow, I came to you for comfort.

---
19.
āsīnasya ca te śrāntā punarutsaṅgam āviśam |
krudhyantī ca prahr̥ṣṭena tvayāhaṁ parisāntvitā ||

> **Meaning 19:** "Tired, I sat in your lap again as you sat there. I was still
> angry, and you, delighted, soothed me.

---
20.
bāṣpapūrṇamukhī mandaṁ cakṣuṣī parimārjatī |
lakṣitāhaṁ tvayā nātha vāyasena prakopitā ||

> **Meaning 20:** "My face was wet with tears, and I was slowly wiping my eyes.
> You noticed, my husband, how the crow had upset me.

---
21.
pariśramāt prasuptā ca rāghavāṁkē apyahaṁciram |
paryāyēṇa prasuptaśca mamāṁkē bharatāgrajaḥ ||

22.
sa tatra punarēvātha vāyasassamupāgamat |
tata ssuptaprabuddhāṁ māṁ rāmasyāṁkāt samutthitām |
vāyasassahasāgamya vidadāra stanāṁtarē ||

23.
punaḥ puna rathōtpatya vidadāra sa māṁ bhr̥śam |
tata ssamukṣitō rāmō muktai śśōṇitabiṁdubhiḥ ||

24.
vāyasēna tatastēna balavat kliśyamānayā |
sa mayā bōdhita śśrīmān sukhasuptaḥ paraṁtapaḥ ||

25.
sa māṁ dr̥ṣṭvā mahābāhuḥ vitunnāṁ stanayōstadā |
āśīviṣa iva kruddhaḥ śvasan vākyamabhāṣata ||

26.
kena te nāganāsoru vikṣataṁ vai stanāntaram |
kaḥ krīḍati saroṣeṇa pañcavaktreṇa bhoginā ||

> **Meaning 21-26:** "Worn out, I fell asleep in Rāghava's lap for a long time,
> and then, in turn, Raghava slept in mine. Then the crow came
> back. As I woke and rose from Rāma's lap, the crow suddenly swooped down and
> tore at me between my breasts. Again and again it flew up and clawed at me
> fiercely, and Rāma was spattered with the drops of blood that fell. Badly hurt
> by that crow, I woke the glorious scorcher of foes from his peaceful sleep.
> When the mighty-armed one saw the wounds on my breasts, he hissed like an
> angry serpent and said: 'Beautiful one, who has torn your breast? Who is
> playing with an angry five-headed snake?'

---
27.
vīkṣamāṇastatastaṁ vai vāyasaṁ samavaikṣathāḥ |
nakhaiḥ sarudhiraistīkṣṇaiḥ mām evābhimukhaṁ sthitam ||

28.
putraḥ kila sa śakrasya vāyasaḥ patatāṁ varaḥ |
dharāntaracaraḥ śīghraṁ pavanasya gatau samaḥ ||

> **Meaning 27-28:** "Looking around, he saw that crow facing him, its sharp
> claws wet with blood. That crow, best of birds, was said to be the son of
> Śakra. It moved swiftly along the ground and was as fast as the wind.

---
29.
tatastasmin mahābāhuḥ kopasaṁvartitekṣaṇaḥ |
vāyase kr̥tavān krūrāṁ matiṁ matimatāṁ vara ||

> **Meaning 29:** "Then he, wisest of the wise, mighty-armed one, eyes rolling
> with anger, made a fierce resolve against that crow.

---
30.
sa darbhasaṁstarād gr̥hya brahmaṇaḥ astreṇa yojayaḥ |
sa dīpta iva kālāgniḥ jajvālābhimukho dvijam ||

> **Meaning 30:** "He took a blade of darbha grass from the mat and charged it
> with the Brahma astra. It blazed like the fire at the end of time, aimed at
> the bird.

---
31.
sa taṁ pradīptaṁ cikṣēpa darbhaṁ taṁ vāyasaṁ prati |
tatastaṁ vāyasaṁ darbhaḥ saḥ aṁbarē anujagāma ha ||

32.
anusr̥ṣṭastadā kālaḥ jagāma vividhāṁ gatim |
trāṇakāma imaṁ lokaṁ sarvaṁ vai vicacāra ha ||

> **Meaning 31-32:** "He hurled that blazing darbha at the crow, and the darbha
> chased the crow through the sky. Pursued, the crow fled in every direction,
> roaming the whole world in search of protection.

---
33.
sa pitrā ca parityaktaḥ suraiḥ sarvairmaharṣibhiḥ |
trīm̐l lokān saṁparikramya tvām eva śaraṇaṁ gataḥ ||

> **Meaning 33:** "Abandoned by its father (Indra), by all the devas, and by the great
> ṛṣis, it went around the three worlds and came to Sri rama alone for refuge.

---
34.
na śarma labdhvā lokeṣu tvām eva śaraṇaṁ gataḥ |
taṁ tvaṁ nipatitaṁ bhūmau śaraṇyaḥ śaraṇāgatam |
vadhārham api kākutstha kr̥payā paryapālayaḥ ||


> **Meaning 34:** "Finding no peace in any world, it came to Sri rama alone for refuge. It fell to the ground before him, and Sri rama, protector of all who seek refuge, spared it out of compassion, though it deserved to die.

---
35.
paridyūnaṁ viṣaṇṇaṁ ca sa tvam āyāntam uktavān |
moghaṁ kartuṁ na śakyaṁ tu brāhmam astraṁ tad ucyatām |
hinastu dakṣiṇākṣi tvat śara ityatha saḥ abravīt ||

> **Meaning 35:** "When it came to Raghava, exhausted and despairing, he told it:
> 'The Brahma astra cannot be made useless. Tell me what to do.' Then the crow
> said: 'Let your arrow destroy my right eye.'

---
36.
tatastasyākṣi kākasya hinasti sma sa dakṣiṇam |
dattvā sa dakṣiṇaṁ nētraṁ prāṇēbhyaḥ parirakṣitaḥ ||
(tadā prabhṛti kākānāmekamakṣīti viśrutam)||

> **Meaning 36:** "So the astra destroyed the crow's right eye. By giving up its
> right eye, it saved its life. (Ever since then, crows have been known as
> one-eyed.)

---
37.
sa rāmāya namaskr̥tvā rājñē daśarathāya ca |
visr̥ṣṭastēna vīrēṇa pratipēdē svamālayam ||

> **Meaning 37:** "It bowed to Rāma and to King Daśaratha, and, sent away by
> that hero, it returned to its home.

---
38.
matkr̥te kākamātre api brahmāstraṁ samudīritam |
kasmād yo māṁ harat tvattaḥ kṣamase taṁ mahīpate ||

> **Meaning 38:** "For my sake you used the Brahma astra on a mere crow. Then
> why, king, do you put up with the one who stole me from you?

---
39.
sa kuruṣva mahotsāhaṁ kr̥pāṁ mayi nararṣabha |
tvayā nāthavatī nātha hyanāthā iva dr̥śyatē ||


> **Meaning 39:** "So summon your great resolve and have compassion on me, bull
> among men. You are my protector, yet I look like someone who has no one.

---
40.
ānr̥śaṁsyaṁ paro dharmaḥ tvatta eva mayā śrutaḥ |
jānāmi tvāṁ mahāvīryaṁ mahotsāhaṁ mahābalam ||

41.
apārapāram akṣobhyaṁ gāmbhīryāt sāgaropamam |
bhartāraṁ sasamudrāyā dharaṇyā vāsavopamam ||

> **Meaning 40-41:** "'Kindness is the highest dharma' — I learned that from
> you. I know you are mighty, full of energy, and strong; boundless, unshakable,
> deep as the ocean; the master of the sea-girt earth, equal to Vāsava.

---
42.
evam astravidāṁ śreṣṭhaḥ sattvavān balavān api |
kimartham astraṁ rakṣaḥsu na yojayasi rāghava ||

> **Meaning 42:** "Rāghava, you are the best of those who know astras, full of
> courage and strength. Why don't you use your astras on the rākṣasas?

---
43.
na nāgā nāpi gandharvā nāsurā na marudgaṇāḥ |
rāmasya samare vegaṁ śaktāḥ prati samādhitum ||

> **Meaning 43:** "Neither nāgas, nor gandharvas, nor asuras, nor the hosts of
> Maruts can withstand Rāma's force in battle.

---
44.
tasyā vīryavataḥ kaścid yady asti mayi saṁbhramaḥ |
kimarthaṁ na śaraistīkṣṇaiḥ kṣayaṁ nayati rākṣasān ||

> **Meaning 44:** "If that mighty one cares for me at all, why doesn't he
> destroy the rākṣasas with his sharp arrows?

---
45.
bhrāturādeśam ādāya lakṣmaṇo vā paraṁtapaḥ |
kasya hetorna māṁ vīraḥ paritrāti mahābalaḥ ||

> **Meaning 45:** "Or why doesn't the heroic, mighty Lakṣmaṇa, scorcher of foes,
> rescue me at his brother's command?

---
46.
yadi tau puruṣavyāghrau vāyvindrasamatejasau |
surāṇām api durdharṣaḥ kimarthaṁ mām upekṣataḥ ||

> **Meaning 46:** "If those two tigers among men are as powerful as Vāyu and
> Indra, and unconquerable even by the devas, why are they ignoring me?

---
47.
mamaiva duṣkr̥taṁ kiṁ cin mahad asti na saṁśayaḥ |
samarthāv api tau yan māṁ nāvekṣete paraṁtapau ||

> **Meaning 47:** "There must be some great wrong I have done, without a doubt,
> since those two scorchers of foes, though they are able, do not come for me."

---
48.
vaidēhyā vacanaṁ śrutvā karuṇaṁ sāśru bhāṣitam |
athābravīnmahātējāḥ hanumān mārutātmajaḥ ||

49.
tvacchōkavimukhō rāmō dēvi satyēna mē śapē |
rāmē duḥkhābhipannē ca lakṣmaṇaḥ paritapyatē ||

50.
kathaṁcidbhavatī dr̥ṣṭā na kālaḥ pariśōcitum |
imaṁ muhūrtaṁ duḥkhānāṁ drakṣyasyaṁtamaniṁditē ||

51.
tāvubhau puruṣavyāghrau rājaputrau mahābalau |
tvaddarśanakr̥tōtsāhau laṁkāṁ bhasmīkariṣyataḥ ||

52.
hattvā ca samarē krūraṁ rāvaṇaṁ sahabāṁdhavam |
rāghavastvāṁ viśālākṣi nēṣyati svāṁ purīṁ prati ||

53.
brūhi yadrāghavō vācyō lakṣmaṇaśca mahābalaḥ |
sugrīvō vāpi tējasvī harayaḥ api samāgatāḥ ||

54.
ityuktavati tasmiṁśca sītā surasutōpamā |
uvāca śōkasaṁtaptā hanumaṁtaṁ plavaṁgamam ||


55.
kausalyā lokabhartāraṁ suṣuve yaṁ manasvinī |
taṁ mamārthe sukhaṁ pr̥ccha śirasā cābhivādaya ||

> **Meaning 48-55:** Hearing Vaidehī's pitiful words, spoken through tears, the
> glorious Hanumān, son of Māruta, said: "Devī, I swear to you by the truth:
> grief for you has made Rāma turn away from everything else. And since Rāma is
> overwhelmed by sorrow, Lakṣmaṇa suffers too. At last you have been found —
> this is no time to grieve. Faultless lady, very soon you will see the end of
> your sorrows. Those two tigers among men, the mighty princes, eager to see
> you, will burn Laṅkā to ashes. After killing cruel Rāvaṇa in battle along with
> his kin, Rāghava will take you, wide-eyed one, back to his own city. Tell me
> what I should say to Rāghava, to mighty Lakṣmaṇa, to glorious Sugrīva, and to
> all the Haris gathered there." When he had said this, Sītā, like a daughter of
> the devas, burning with grief, said to Hanumān the Plavaṅgama: "On my behalf,
> ask after the welfare of the one whom wise Kausalyā bore, the protector of the
> world, and bow your head to him in greeting.

---
56.
srajaśca sarvaratnāni priyā yāśca varāṅganāḥ |
aiśvaryaṁ ca viśālāyāṁ pr̥thivyām api durlabham |
pitaraṁ mātaraṁ caiva saṁmānyābhiprasādya ca ||

> **Meaning 56:** "Garlands, every kind of gem, beloved and beautiful women, and
> royal power hard to find even on this wide earth — he gave all these up, and
> after honoring and winning over his father and mother,

---
57.
anupravrajito rāmaṁ sumitrā yena suprajāḥ |
ānukūlyena dharmātmā tyaktvā sukham anuttamam ||

> **Meaning 57:** "he followed Rāma into exile. Because of him Sumitrā is
> blessed with a noble son. Righteous and devoted, he gave up the greatest
> happiness

---
58.
anugacchati kākutsthaṁ bhrātaraṁ pālayan vane |
siṁhaskandho mahābāhuḥ manasvī priyadarśanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 58:** "and follows his brother Kākutstha, protecting him in the
> forest — lion-shouldered, mighty-armed, high-minded, and handsome.

---
59.
pitr̥vad vartate rāme mātr̥van māṁ samācaran |
hriyamāṇāṁ tadā vīraḥ na tu māṁ veda lakṣmaṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 59:** "He treats Rāma like a father and me like a mother. Yet heroic
> Lakṣmaṇa did not know when I was being carried off.

---
60.
vr̥ddhopasevī lakṣmīvāñ śakto na bahubhāṣitā |
rājaputraḥ priyaśreṣṭhaḥ sadr̥śaḥ śvaśurasya me ||

> **Meaning 60:** "He serves his elders. He is splendid and capable, and he
> doesn't talk much. That prince, dearest of the dear, is very dearest to my husband.

---
61.
mattaḥ priyataro nityaṁ bhrātā rāmasya lakṣmaṇaḥ |
niyukto dhuri yasyāṁ tu tām udvahati vīryavān ||

> **Meaning 61:** "Lakṣmaṇa, Rāma's brother, has always been dearer to him than
> I am. Mighty as he is, he carries any burden he is given.

---
62.
yaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rāghavo naiva vr̥ddham āryam anusmarat |
sa mamārthāya kuśalaṁ vaktavyo vacanān mama ||

63.
mr̥durnityaṁ śucirdakṣaḥ priyo rāmasya lakṣmaṇaḥ |
yathā hi vānaraśrēṣṭha duḥkhakṣayakarō bhavēt ||


64.
tvamasmin kāryaniryōgē pramāṇaṁ harisattama |
rāghavastvatsamāraṁbhāt mayi yatnaparō bhavēt ||

> **Meaning 62-64:** "Seeing him, Rāghava no longer even misses his noble late
> father. Greet him for me and ask after his welfare — Lakṣmaṇa, always gentle,
> pure, and capable, and dear to Rāma. Best of the vānaras, do whatever will
> bring my sorrows to an end. Best of the Haris, this whole mission rests on
> you. Through your efforts, Rāghava will strive to rescue me.

---
65.
idaṁ brūyāśca me nāthaṁ śūraṁ rāmaṁ punaḥ punaḥ |
jīvitaṁ dhārayiṣyāmi māsaṁ daśarathātmaja |
ūrdhvaṁ māsān na jīveyaṁ satyenāhaṁ bravīmi te ||

> **Meaning 65:** "And tell my husband, heroic Rāma, again and again: 'Son of
> Daśaratha, I will hold on to life for one more month. Beyond that month I will
> not live. I tell you this truly.'

---
66.
rāvaṇenoparuddhāṁ māṁ nikr̥tyā pāpakarmaṇā |
trātum arhasi vīra tvaṁ pātālād iva kauśikīm ||

> **Meaning 66:** "'O great hero, wicked Rāvaṇa has imprisoned me through treachery. You
> must rescue me, as Kauśikī was rescued from Pātāla.'"

---
67.
tato vastragataṁ muktvā divyaṁ cūḍāmaṇiṁ śubham |
pradeyo rāghavāyeti sītā hanumate dadau ||

> **Meaning 67:** Then Sītā untied the beautiful, divine cūḍāmaṇi from her
> garment and gave it to Hanumān, saying, "Give this to Rāghava."

---
68.
pratigr̥hya tato vīraḥ maṇiratnam anuttamam |
aṅgulyā yojayām āsa na hy asyā prābhavad bhujaḥ ||

> **Meaning 68:** The hero took that peerless jewel and slipped it onto his
> finger, since it would not go over his arm.

---
69.
maṇiratnaṁ kapivaraḥ pratigr̥hyābhivādya ca |
sītāṁ pradakṣiṇaṁ kr̥tvā praṇataḥ pārśvataḥ sthitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 69:** Taking the jewel, the best of the Kapis bowed to Sītā, walked
> around her respectfully, and stood humbly at her side.

---
70.
harṣeṇa mahatā yuktaḥ sītādarśanajena saḥ |
hr̥dayena gato rāmaṁ śarīreṇa tu viṣṭhitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 70:** Filled with the great joy of having seen Sītā, in his heart he
> had already gone to Rāma, though his body remained there.

---
71.
maṇivaram upagr̥hya taṁ mahārhaṁ janakanr̥pātmajayā dhr̥taṁ prabhāvāt |
girivarapavanāvadhūtamuktaḥ sukhitamanāḥ pratisaṁkramaṁ prapede ||

> **Meaning 71:** Holding that precious jewel, which the daughter of King Janaka
> had worn, he felt like a great mountain freed from the buffeting of a mighty
> wind. With a happy heart, he prepared to return.

---
