=== Sarga 24 ===

---
1.
tataḥ sītām upāgamya rākṣasyo vikr̥tānanāḥ |
paruṣam paruṣā nārya ūcus tā vākyam apriyam ||

> **Meaning 1:** Approaching Sītā, those harsh-natured Rākṣasīs with deformed
> faces spoke cruel and unpleasant words to her:

---
2.
kin tvam antaḥpure sīte sarvabhūtamanohare |
mahārhaśayanopete na vāsam anumanyase ||

> **Meaning 2:** "Listen, Sītā! Why won't you agree to live in the royal palace,
> which enchants all creatures and is furnished with the most luxurious beds?

---
3.
mānuṣī mānuṣasyaiva bhāryātvaṁ bahu manyase |
pratyāhara mano rāmān na tvaṁ jātu bhaviṣyasi ||

> **Meaning 3:** Being a human woman, you think so highly of being the wife of a
> mere mortal! Turn your mind away from Rāma; you will never be his again!

---
4.
trailokya vasubhōktāraṁ rāvaṇaṁ rākṣasēśvaram |
bhartāram upasaṅgamya viharasva yathāsukham || 

5.
mānuṣī mānuṣan tan tu rāmam icchasi śobhane |
rājyād bhraṣṭam asiddhārthaṁ viklavan tam anindite ||

> **Meaning 4-5:** Go to Rāvaṇa, the ruler of Rākṣasas who enjoys the riches of
> all three worlds, take him as your husband, and enjoy yourself as you please!
> Why do you, a human woman, still pine for that human Rāma, who has lost his
> kingdom, failed in his aims, and wanders in despair, blameless lady?"

---
6.
rākṣasīnāṁ vacaḥ śrutvā sītā padmanibhekṣaṇā |
netrābhyām aśrupūrṇābhyām idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

> **Meaning 6:** Hearing the taunts of the Rākṣasīs, lotus-eyed Sītā, her eyes
> filled with tears, replied:

---
7.
yad idaṁ lokavidviṣṭam udāharatha saṅgatāḥ |
naitan manasi vākyam me kilbiṣam pratitiṣṭhati ||

> **Meaning 7:** "What you have all gathered here to say is despised by the
> entire world. Such vile words will never find a place in my heart!

---
8.
na mānuṣī rākṣasasya bhāryā bhavitum arhati |
kāmaṅ khādata mān sarvā na kariṣyāmi vo vacaḥ ||

> **Meaning 8:** A human woman can never be the wife of a Rākṣasa. You may eat
> me if you wish, but I will never do your bidding!

---
9.
dīno vā rājyahīno vā yo me bhartā sa me guruḥ |
tan nityam anuraktāsmi yathā sūryaṁ suvarcalā || 

> **Meaning 9:** Whether he is wretched or stripped of his kingdom, he who is my
> husband is my highest master; I am eternally devoted to him, just as Suvarcalā
> is to the sun.

---
10.
yathā śacī mahābhāgā śakraṁ samupatiṣṭhati |
arundhatī vasiṣṭhañ ca rohiṇī śaśinaṁ yathā ||

11.
lopāmudrā yathāgastyaṁ sukanyā cyavanaṁ yathā |
sāvitrī satyavantañ ca kapilaṁ śrīmatī yathā ||

12.
saudāsam madayantīva keśinī sagaraṁ yathā |
naiṣadhan damayantīva bhaimī patim anuvratā |
tathāham ikṣvākuvaraṁ rāmam patim anuvratā ||

13.
sītāyā vacanaṁ śrutvā rākṣasyaḥ krodhamūrchitāḥ |
bhartsayanti sma paruṣair vākyai rāvaṇacoditāḥ ||

14.
avalīnaḥ sa nirvākyo hanumāñ śiṁśapādrume |
sītāṁ santarjayantīs tā rākṣasīr aśr̥ṇot kapiḥ ||

> **Meaning 10-14:** Just as noble Śacī attends upon Indra, Arundhatī upon
> Vasiṣṭha, Rohiṇī upon the moon, Lopāmudrā upon Agastya, Sukanyā upon Cyavana,
> Sāvitrī upon Satyavān, Śrīmatī upon Kapila, Madayantī upon Saudāsa, Keśinī
> upon Sagara, and Damayantī, daughter of Bhīma, upon her husband Nala—so am I
> entirely devoted to my husband Rāma, the foremost of the Ikṣvākus." Hearing
> Sītā's words, the Rākṣasīs boiled over with rage and, urged on by Rāvaṇa's
> orders, hurled vicious threats at her, while the Kapi Hanumān, hiding
> motionless and silent in the śiṁśapā tree, listened to them menacing Sītā.

---
15.
tām abhikramya saṁrabdhā vepamānāṁ samantataḥ |
bhr̥śaṁ saṁlilihur dīptān pralambadaśanacchadān ||

> **Meaning 15:** Furious, they surrounded the trembling princess from all sides
> and fiercely licked their red, drooping lips.

---
16.
ūcuś ca paramakruddhāḥ pragr̥hyāśu paraśvadhān |
neyam arhati bhartāraṁ rāvaṇaṁ rākṣasādhipam ||

> **Meaning 16:** Seizing their sharp battle-axes in violent rage, they
> screamed: "She does not deserve to have Rāvaṇa, the king of Rākṣasas, as her
> husband!"

---
17.
sā bhartsyamānā bhīmābhī rākṣasībhir varānanā |
sā bāṣpam apamārjantī śiṁśapān tām upāgamat ||

> **Meaning 17:** Threatened by those dreadful Rākṣasīs, that fair-faced lady
> wiped away her tears and shrank back toward the śiṁśapā tree.

---
18.
tatas tāṁ śiṁśapāṁ sītā rākṣasībhiḥ samāvr̥tā |
abhigamya viśālākṣī tasthau śokapariplutā ||

> **Meaning 18:** Reaching the śiṁśapā tree surrounded by the Rākṣasīs,
> wide-eyed Sītā stood there, completely overwhelmed by grief.

---
19.
tāṅ kr̥śān dīnavadanām malināmbaradhāriṇīm |
bhartsayāñ cakrire bhīmā rākṣasyas tāḥ samantataḥ ||

> **Meaning 19:** Those terrifying Rākṣasīs hemmed her in on every side,
> menacing her as she stood emaciated, sorrow-faced, and wrapped in soiled
> garments.

---
20.
tatas tāṁ vinatā nāma rākṣasī bhīmadarśanā |
abravīt kupitākārā karālā nirṇatodarī ||

> **Meaning 20:** Then a hideous Rākṣasī named Vinatā, furious in look,
> terrifying, and with a sunken belly, snarled at her:

---
21.
sīte paryāptam etāvad bhartr̥sneho nidarśitaḥ |
sarvatrātikr̥tam bhadre vyasanāyopakalpate ||

> **Meaning 21:** "Sītā, you have shown more than enough love for your husband;
> pushing anything too far only brings disaster, good woman!

---
22.
parituṣṭāsmi bhadran te mānuṣas te kr̥to vidhiḥ |
mamāpi tu vacaḥ pathyam bruvantyāḥ kuru maithili ||

> **Meaning 22:** I'm satisfied that you've done your duty as a human woman; may
> good come to you. Now listen to the sensible advice I'm giving you, Maithilī!

---
23.
rāvaṇam bhaja bhartāram bhartāraṁ sarvarakṣasām |
vikrāntan rūpavantañ ca sureśam iva vāsavam ||

> **Meaning 23:** Accept Rāvaṇa, the king of all Rākṣasas, as your husband—he is
> valiant, handsome, and rivals Indra himself!

---
24.
dakṣiṇan tyāgaśīlañ ca sarvasya priyavādinam |
mānuṣaṅ kr̥paṇan rāmaṁ tyaktvā rāvaṇam āśraya ||

> **Meaning 24:** He is generous, open-handed, and speaks kindly to everyone;
> leave that wretched human Rāma behind and take refuge with Rāvaṇa!

---
25.
divyāṅgarāgā vaidehi divyābharaṇabhūṣitā |
adya prabhr̥ti sarveṣāl lokānām īśvarī bhava ||

> **Meaning 25:** Decked in divine cosmetics and celestial jewels, Vaidehī,
> become the sovereign queen of all the worlds from this very day!

---
26.
agneḥ svāhā yathā devī śacīvendrasya śobhane |
kin te rāmeṇa vaidehi kr̥paṇena gatāyuṣā ||

> **Meaning 26:** Be to him as the goddess Svāhā is to Agni, or Śacī to Indra,
> lovely lady! What use to you is Rāma, a miserable creature whose life is
> practically over?

---
27.
etad uktañ ca me vākyan yadi tvaṁ na kariṣyasi |
asmin muhūrte sarvās tvām bhakṣayiṣyāmahe vayam ||

> **Meaning 27:** If you don't listen to what I'm telling you, we will all
> devour you this very moment!"

---
28.
anyā tu vikaṭā nāma lambamānapayodharā |
abravīt kupitā sītām muṣṭim udyamya garjatī ||

> **Meaning 28:** Then another Rākṣasī named Vikaṭā, with sagging breasts, shook
> her fist and roared furiously at Sītā:

---
29.
bahūny apratirūpāṇi vacanāni sudurmate |
anukrośān mr̥dutvāc ca soḍhāni tava maithili ||

> **Meaning 29:** "Stupid woman! Out of pity and soft-heartedness, Maithilī, we
> have put up with far too many of your insulting words!

---
30.
na ca naḥ kuruṣe vākyaṁ hitaṅ kālapuraskr̥tam |
ānītāsi samudrasya pāram anyair durāsadam ||

> **Meaning 30:** You refuse to accept our sound advice given at the right time.
> You've been brought across an ocean that no one else can ever cross!

---
31.
rāvaṇāntaḥpuraṅ ghoram praviṣṭā cāsi maithili |
rāvaṇasya gr̥he rudhā asmābhis tu surakṣitā ||

> **Meaning 31:** You are trapped inside Rāvaṇa's formidable palace, Maithilī,
> locked within his house and closely guarded by us!

---
32.
na tvāṅ śaktaḥ paritrātum api sākṣāt purandaraḥ |
kuruṣva hitavādinyā vacanam mama maithili ||

> **Meaning 32:** Not even Indra himself could rescue you now! So listen to what
> I'm telling you for your own good, Maithilī!

---
33.
alam aśruprapātena tyaja śokam anarthakam |
bhaja prītim praharṣañ ca tyajaitān nityadainyatām ||

> **Meaning 33:** Stop crying! Cast off this pointless grief. Embrace joy and
> pleasure, and give up this constant misery!

---
34.
sīte rākṣasarājena saha krīḍa yathāsukham |
jānāsi hi yathā bhīru strīṇāṁ yauvanam adhruvam ||

> **Meaning 34:** Sītā, enjoy yourself with the king of Rākṣasas as you please!
> You know very well, timid girl, that a woman's youth is fleeting!

---
35.
yāvan na te vyatikrāmet tāvat sukham avāpnuhi |
udyānāni ca ramyāṇi parvatopavanāni ca ||

> **Meaning 35:** Take your pleasure before it slips away! In lovely gardens and
> mountain groves,

---
36.
saha rākṣasarājena cara tvan madirekṣaṇe |
strīsahasrāṇi te sapta vaśe sthāsyanti sundari ||

> **Meaning 36:** roam freely with the king of Rākṣasas, dark-eyed beauty! Seven
> thousand women will bow to your command, gorgeous lady!

---
37.
rāvaṇam bhaja bhartāram bhartāraṁ sarvarakṣasām ||
utpāṭya vā te hr̥dayam bhakṣayiṣyāmi maithili |
yadi me vyāhr̥taṁ vākyan na yathāvat kariṣyasi ||

> **Meaning 37:** Accept Rāvaṇa, the ruler of all Rākṣasas, as your husband! If
> you don't do exactly as I say, Maithilī, I'll rip your heart out and eat it!"

---
38.
tataś caṇḍodarī nāma rākṣasī krūradarśanā |
bhrāmayantī mahac chūlam idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

> **Meaning 38:** Then a Rākṣasī named Caṇḍodarī, of dread appearance,
> brandished a huge spear and snarled:

---
39.
imān hariṇalokākṣīn trāsotkampapayodharām |
rāvaṇena hr̥tān dr̥ṣṭvā daurhr̥do me mahān abhūt ||

> **Meaning 39:** "Seeing this doe-eyed woman with breasts trembling from
> fright, dragged here by Rāvaṇa, an immense craving has overcome me!

---
40.
yakr̥tplīham athotpīḍaṁ hr̥dayañ ca sabandhanam |
antrāṇy api tathā śīrṣaṅ khādeyam iti me matiḥ ||

> **Meaning 40:** I want to feast on her liver, her spleen, her heart and its
> arteries, her intestines, and her head!"

---
41.
tatas tu praghasā nāma rākṣasī vākyam abravīt |

> **Meaning 41:** Then a Rākṣasī named Praghasā declared:

---
42.
kaṇṭham asyā nr̥śaṁsāyāḥ pīḍayāmaḥ kim āsyate |
nivedyatān tato rājñe mānuṣī sā mr̥teti ha ||

> **Meaning 42:** "Why are we just sitting around? Let's wring this cruel
> woman's neck! Then we'll report to the king that the human woman is dead!

---
43.
nātra kaścana sandehaḥ khādateti sa vakṣyati |
tatas tv ajāmukhī nāma rākṣasī vākyam abravīt ||

> **Meaning 43:** There's no doubt about it—he'll tell us, 'Eat her!' Then
> another Rākṣasī named Ajāmukhī chimed in:

---
44.
viśasyemān tataḥ sarvān samān kuruta pīlukān |
vibhajāma tataḥ sarvā vivādo me na rocate |
peyam ānīyatāṅ kṣipram mālyañ ca vividham bahu ||

> **Meaning 44:** "Chop her up into equal bites, and let's divide her among
> ourselves; I don't want any arguing! Bring out plenty of drinks and fresh
> garlands right now!

---
45.
tataḥ śūrpaṇakhā nāma rākṣasī vākyam abravīt |

> **Meaning 45:** Then a Rākṣasī named Śūrpaṇakhā said:

---
46.
ajāmukhā yad uktaṁ hi tad eva mama rocate |
surā cānīyatāṅ kṣipraṁ sarvaśokavināśinī |
mānuṣam māṁsam āsādya nr̥tyāmo ’tha nikumbhilām ||

> **Meaning 46:** "I like what Ajāmukhī says! Bring out the sorrow-banishing
> liquor right away; once we have the human meat, let's go dance at Nikumbhilā!"

---
47.
evan sambhartsyamānā sā sītā surasutopamā |
rākṣasībhiḥ sughorābhir dhairyam utsr̥jya roditi ||

> **Meaning 47:** Threatened in this dreadful manner by those terrifying
> Rākṣasīs, Sītā, who looked like the daughter of a god, lost all her composure
> and wept aloud.

---
