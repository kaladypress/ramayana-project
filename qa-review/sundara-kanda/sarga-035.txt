=== Sarga 35 ===

---
1.
tāṁ tu rāma kathāṁ śrutvā vaidehī vānararṣabhāt |
uvāca vacanaṁ sāntvam idaṁ madhurayā girā ||

> **Meaning 1:** After hearing that account of Rāma from the foremost of the
> vānaras, Vaidehī spoke these gentle words to him in a sweet voice:

---
2.
kva te rāmeṇa saṁsargaḥ kathaṁ jānāsi lakṣmaṇam |
vānarāṇāṁ narāṇāṁ ca katham āsīt samāgamaḥ ||

> **Meaning 2:** "Where did you come into contact with Rāma? How do you know
> Lakṣmaṇa? How did vānaras and humans ever come together?

---
3.
yāni rāmasya liṅgāni lakṣmaṇasya ca vānara |
tāni bhūyaḥ samācakṣva na māṁ śokaḥ samāviśet ||

> **Meaning 3:** "Vānara, describe once more the marks of Rāma and of
> Lakṣmaṇa, so that grief does not overwhelm me.

---
4.
kīdr̥śaṁ tasya saṁsthānaṁ rūpaṁ rāmasya kīdr̥śam |
katham ūrū kathaṁ bāhū lakṣmaṇasya ca śaṁsa me ||

> **Meaning 4:** "What is his build like? What does Rāma look like? What are
> his thighs like, and his arms? And tell me about Lakṣmaṇa too."

---
5.
evam uktastu vaidehyā hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ |
tato rāmaṁ yathātattvam ākhyātum upacakrame ||

> **Meaning 5:** Asked this by Vaidehī, Hanumān, the son of Māruta, began to
> describe Rāma exactly as he truly is.

---
6.
jānantī bata diṣṭyā māṁ vaidehi paripr̥cchasi |
bhartuḥ kamalapatrākṣi saṁkhyānaṁ lakṣmaṇasya ca ||

> **Meaning 6:** "How fortunate, O lotus-eyed Vaidehī! You already know, and
> still you question me about the features of your husband and of Lakṣmaṇa."

---
7.
yāni rāmasya cihnāni lakṣmaṇasya ca yāni vai |
lakṣitāni viśālākṣi vadataḥ śr̥ṇu tāni me ||

> **Meaning 7:** "Wide-eyed one, listen as I tell you the marks I have noticed
> on Rāma and on Lakṣmaṇa.

---
8.
rāmaḥ kamalapatrākṣaḥ sarvabhūtamanoharaḥ |
rūpadākṣiṇyasaṁpannaḥ prasūto janakātmaje ||

> **Meaning 8:** "Daughter of Janaka, Rāma is lotus-eyed and wins the hearts of
> all beings. He was born gifted with beauty and graciousness.

---
9.
tejasādityasaṁkāśaḥ kṣamayā pr̥thivīsamaḥ |
br̥haspatisamo buddhyā yaśasā vāsavopamaḥ ||

> **Meaning 9:** "In radiance he is like the sun, in patience equal to the
> earth, in wisdom like Br̥haspati, and in fame like Indra.

---
10.
rakṣitā jīvalokasya svajanasya ca rakṣitā |
rakṣitā svasya vr̥ttasya dharmasya ca paraṁtapaḥ ||

> **Meaning 10:** "He protects the world of the living, and he protects his own
> people. That scorcher of foes guards his own conduct and guards dharma.

---
11.
rāmo bhāmini lokasya cāturvarṇyasya rakṣitā |
maryādānāṁ ca lokasya kartā kārayitā ca saḥ ||

> **Meaning 11:** "Noble lady, Rāma is the protector of the world and of the
> four varṇas. He observes the bounds of right conduct for the world himself,
> and ensures that others follow them too.

---
12.
arciṣmān arcitaḥ atyarthaṁ brahmacaryavrate sthitaḥ |
sādhūnām upakārajñaḥ pracārajñaśca karmaṇām ||

> **Meaning 12:** "He is radiant and deeply honored, steadfast in his vow of
> brahmacarya. He remembers the good done by the virtuous, and knows how to
> practice righteous deeds for the welfare of the world and inspire others to
> practice them."

---
13.
rājavidyāvinītaśca brāhmaṇānām upāsitā |
śrutavāñ śīlasaṁpannaḥ vinītaśca paraṁtapaḥ ||

> **Meaning 13:** "He is trained in the science of kingship and serves the
> brāhmaṇas with respect. He is learned and of fine character — humble, yet a
> scorcher of foes.

---
14.
yajurvedavinītaśca vedavidbhiḥ supūjitaḥ |
dhanurvede ca vede ca vedāṅgeṣu ca niṣṭhitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 14:** "He is schooled in the Yajurveda and highly honored by those
> who know the Vedas. He is accomplished in Dhanurveda, in the R̥gveda and
> Sāmaveda, and in the Vedāṅgas."

---
15.
vipulāṁso mahābāhuḥ kambugrīvaḥ śubhānanaḥ |
gūḍhajatruḥ sutāmrākṣaḥ rāmo devi janaiḥ śrutaḥ ||

> **Meaning 15:** "O Devī, Rāma is broad-shouldered and mighty-armed, with a
> neck shaped like a conch and an auspicious face. His collarbones are well
> concealed, and his eyes are tinged with a lovely red."

---
16.
dundubhisvananirghoṣaḥ snigdhavarṇaḥ pratāpavān |
samaḥ samavibhaktāṅgaḥ varṇaṁ śyāmaṁ samāśritaḥ ||

> **Meaning 16:** "His voice is deep and resonant like a dundubhi drum. His complexion is
> lustrous, and he is endowed with majesty and valor. He is well built, perfectly proportioned in
> form, and dark (śyāma) in color.

---
17.
tristhirastripralaṁbaśca trisamastriṣu cōnnataḥ |
tritāmrastriṣu ca snigdhō gaṁbhīrastriṣu nityaśaḥ || 17 ||

18.
trivalīvāṁstryavanataḥ caturvyaṁgastriśīrṣavān |
catuṣkalaścaturlēkhaḥ catuṣkiṣkuścatussamaḥ ||

> **Meaning 17-18:** "He bears the auspicious physical signs detailed in the traditional science of marks. He is firm in three places: his chest, wrists, and fists. He is extended in three: his eyebrows, arms, and generative organs. He is perfectly proportioned in three: his hair, generative organs, and knees. He is elevated in three: his chest, abdomen, and navel.
He possesses a coppery-red hue in the corners of his eyes, his nails, palms, and the soles of his feet. He is glossy in three places: his hair, the lines of his feet, and his generative organs. He is deep and majestic in three: his voice, his gait, and his navel. He bears three folds of skin upon his abdomen and three auspicious lines upon his neck. He is gently hollowed at his breast, nipples, and the lines of his feet, while his neck, back, calves, and generative organs are robust and short.
He bears three whorls of hair upon his crown. He possesses four distinct lines—upon his forehead, the base of his thumbs, his palms, and his soles—indicating his mastery of the four Vedas and his longevity. He stands exactly four cubits (ninety-six aṅgulas) tall, and his arms, knees, thighs, and calves are perfectly symmetrical and evenly matched.

---
19.
caturdaśasamadvandvaḥ caturdaṣṭaścaturgatiḥ |
mahauṣṭhahanunāsaśca pañcasnigdhaḥ aṣṭavaṁśavān ||

> **Meaning 19:** "He possesses fourteen paired features that are perfectly symmetrical and evenly matched: his eyebrows, nostrils, eyes, ears, lips, nipples, elbows, wrists, knees, generative organs, buttocks, hands, feet, and the prominent muscles of his hips. He bears four sharp, perfectly formed canine teeth. He walks with four noble gaits, moving with the majestic grace and power of a lion, a tiger, an elephant, and a bull. His lips, his chin, and his nose are beautifully proportioned. He is glossy in five places: his eyes, his teeth, his skin, his feet, and his hair. O Jānakī, he possesses eight features of perfect length and proportion: his torso, his fingers, his toes, his hands, his nose, his eyes, his ears, and his generative organs.

---
20.
daśapadmo daśabr̥hat tribhirvyāpto dviśuklavān |
ṣaḍunnato navatanuḥ tribhirvyāpnoti rāghavaḥ ||

> **Meaning 20:** Rāghava possesses ten features as beautiful as lotuses: his face, his eyes, his mouth, his tongue, his lips, his jaws, his nipples, his nails, his hands, and his feet. He bears ten broad and majestic features: his head, his forehead, his ears, his neck, his chest, his heart, his mouth, his hands, his feet, and his back. He is filled with three world-renowned excellences: his radiance, his fame, and his prosperity. He possesses pure whiteness in his teeth and his eyes, perfectly reflecting the absolute purity of both his paternal and maternal lineages.
He is elevated in six places: his armpits, his abdomen, his chest, his nose, his shoulders, and his forehead. He possesses nine attributes that are extraordinarily fine and subtle: his finger joints, the hair of his head, his body hair, his nails, his skin, his generative organ, his mustache, his vision, and his intellect. He excels across three times, flawlessly practicing Dharma, Artha, and Kāma during the morning, noon, and afternoon.

---
21.
satyadharmaparaḥ śrīmān saṁgrahānugrahe rataḥ |
deśakālavibhāgajñaḥ sarvalokapriyaṁvadaḥ ||

> **Meaning 21:** "He is devoted to truth and dharma, and is graced with immense fortune. He takes joy in welcoming others and acting with boundless kindness. He understands the exact nature of time and place, and speaks with a gentle grace to all.

---
22.
bhrātā ca tasya dvaimātraḥ saumitriraparājitaḥ |
anurāgeṇa rūpeṇa guṇaiścaiva tathāvidhaḥ ||

> **Meaning 22:** "His brother Saumitri is a peerless, unconquered warrior. As the beloved son of two mothers, Kausalyā and Sumitrā, he is the exact reflection of Rāma in his deep devotion, his physical form, and his noble virtues.

---
23.
sa suvarṇacchaviśśrīmān rāmaśśyāmo mahāyaśāḥ |
tāvubhau naraśārdūlau tvaddarśanasamutsukau ||

24.
vicinvantau mahīṁ kr̥tsnām asmābhirabhisaṅgatau |
tvām eva mārgamāṇo tau vicarantau vasuṁdharām ||


> **Meaning 23-24:** "While the illustrious Rāma is dark as a raincloud, Lakṣmaṇa possesses a radiant golden complexion. Those two tigers among men are profoundly yearning to see you. Searching only for you, those two brothers scoured the earth, wandering its four corners until they met us.

---
25.
dadarśaturmr̥gapatiṁ pūrvajenāvaropitam |
r̥śyamūkasya pr̥ṣṭhe tu bahupādapasaṁkule |
bhrāturbhāryārtam āsīnaṁ sugrīvaṁ priyadarśanam ||

> **Meaning 25:** "While wandering, they chanced upon the glorious king of the Vanaras, Sugriva. He was seated on the slopes of Mount Rishyamuka, which was thickly covered with trees, grieving intensely for the wife who had been forcibly taken from him by his elder brother, Vālī.

---
26.
vayaṁ tu harirājaṁ taṁ sugrīvaṁ satyasaṁgaram |
paricaryāmahe rājyāt pūrvajenāvaropitam ||

> **Meaning 26:** "We serve that king of the Haris, Sugrīva, who is true to his word and who was driven from his kingdom by his elder brother.

---
27.
tatastau cīravasanau dhanuḥpravarapāṇinau |

28.
r̥śyamūkasya śailasya ramyaṁ deśam upāgatau |
sa tau dr̥ṣṭvā naravyāghrau dhanvinau vānararṣabhaḥ ||

> **Meaning 27-28:** "Then those two, dressed in bark garments and holding excellent bows, arrived at a lovely spot on Mount Ṛśyamūka. When that bull among vānaras saw those tigers among men.

---
29.
avapluto girestasya śikharaṁ bhayamohitaḥ |
tataḥ sa śikhare tasmin vānarendro vyavasthitaḥ |
tayoḥ samīpaṁ mām eva preṣayām āsa satvaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 29:** "Seeing them, sugrīva panicked and leapt up to the summit of the mountain.
> Settling on that peak, the king of the vānaras quickly sent me alone to
> them.

---
30.
tāv ahaṁ puruṣavyāghrau sugrīvavacanāt prabhū ||


> **Meaning 30:** "At Sugrīva's word, I approached those two tigers among men,
> those noble masters,

---
31.
rūpalakṣaṇasaṁpannau kr̥tāñjalirupasthitaḥ |
tau parijñātatattvārthau mayā prītisamanvitau ||


> **Meaning 31:** "so handsome and so full of auspicious marks, and I stood
> before them with joined palms. Once they had learned the true state of things
> from me, they were pleased with me,

---
32.
pr̥ṣṭham āropya taṁ deśaṁ prāpitau puruṣarṣabhau |
niveditau ca tattvena sugrīvāya mahātmane ||

> **Meaning 32:** "and I carried those two bulls among men on my back to that
> place. There I introduced them truthfully to the great-souled Sugrīva.

---
33.
tayoranyonyasaṁbhāṣād bhr̥śaṁ prītirajāyata |
tatra tau kīrtisaṁpannau harīśvaranareśvarau |
parasparakr̥tāśvāsau kathayā pūrvavr̥ttayā ||

> **Meaning 33:** "As they talked with each other, a deep affection grew
> between them. There, those two renowned ones — the king of the Haris and the
> king of men — reassured each other by sharing what had happened to them
> before.

---
34.
tatassa svāntayāmāsa sugrīvaṁ lakṣmaṇāgrajaḥ |
strīhetorvālinā bhrātrā nirastam uru tejasā ||

> **Meaning 34:** "Then Lakṣmaṇa's elder brother comforted Sugrīva, who had been
> driven out over a woman by his brother Vālī, a being of immense might.

---
35.
tatastvan nāśajaṁ śokaṁ rāmasyākliṣṭakarmaṇaḥ |
lakṣmaṇo vānarendrāya sugrīvāya nyavedayat ||

> **Meaning 35:** "After that, Lakṣmaṇa told Sugrīva, king of the vānaras,
> about the grief that Rāma — tireless in all he does — felt at losing you.

---
36.
sa śrutvā vānarendrastu lakṣmaṇeneritaṁ vacaḥ |
tadāsīn niṣprabhaḥ atyarthaṁ grahagrasta ivāṁśumān ||

> **Meaning 36:** "When the vānara king heard what Lakṣmaṇa said, he lost all
> his luster, like the sun seized by an eclipse.

---
37.
tatastvadgātraśobhīni rakṣasā hriyamāṇayā |
yāny ābharaṇajālāni pātitāni mahītale ||

> **Meaning 37:** "Then all the ornaments that had graced your body — the ones
> you dropped to the ground as the rākṣasa was carrying you off —

---
38.
tāni sarvāṇi rāmāya ānīya hariyūthapāḥ |
saṁhr̥ṣṭā darśayām āsuḥ gatiṁ tu na vidustava ||

> **Meaning 38:** "the Vānara leaders gathered up and joyfully brought to Rāma
> to show him. But they did not know where you had been taken.

---
39.
tāni rāmāya dattāni mayaivopahr̥tāni ca |


> **Meaning 39:** "Earlier, when those ornaments fell to the earth with a ringing sound, it was I who gathered them and handed them over to Sugrīva.

---
40.
svanavanty avakīrṇanti tasmin vihatacetasi |
tāny aṅke darśanīyāni kr̥tvā bahuvidhaṁ tataḥ ||


> **Meaning 40:** "Sugrīva then presented those jewels to Śrī Rāma. The moment he beheld them, his mind was shattered and he lost consciousness. Then, placing your beautiful ornaments in his lap, the divine-formed Rāma grieved in countless ways.

---
41.
tena devaprakāśena devena paridevitam |
paśyatastasyā rudataḥ tāmyataśca punaḥ punaḥ ||


> **Meaning 41:** Gazing upon them again and again, he wept aloud.

---
42.
prādīpayan dāśaratheḥ tāni śokahutāśanam ||

43.
śayitaṁ ca ciraṁ tena duḥkhārtena mahātmanā |
mayāpi vividhairvākyaiḥ kr̥cchrād utthāpitaḥ punaḥ ||

> **Meaning 42-43:** "n this way, the sight of those very ornaments continuously fanned the fire of his grief. Stricken with sorrow, the great-souled one lay on the ground for a long time. With many kinds of words, I managed with great difficulty to get him back on his feet.

---
44.
tāni dr̥ṣṭvā mahārhāṇi darśayitvā muhurmuhuḥ |
rāghavaḥ sahasaumitriḥ sugrīve sa nyavedayat ||

> **Meaning 44:** "After looking at those precious ornaments and showing them
> again and again, Rāghava, together with Saumitri, handed them over to
> Sugrīva.

---
45.
sa tavādarśanād ārye rāghavaḥ paritapyate |
mahatā jvalatā nityam agninevāgniparvataḥ ||

> **Meaning 45:** "Noble lady, unable to see you, Rāghava is continually consumed by grief, blazing like a mighty mountain engulfed in a great flame.

---
46.
tvatkr̥te tam anidrā ca śokaścintā ca rāghavam |
tāpayanti mahātmānam agnyagāram ivāgnayaḥ ||

> **Meaning 46:** "Because of you, sleeplessness, grief, and worry torment the
> great-souled Rāghava, just as the sacred fires heat a fire-sanctuary.

---
47.
tavādarśanaśokena rāghavaḥ pravicālyate |
mahatā bhūmikampena mahān iva śiloccayaḥ ||

> **Meaning 47:** "The grief of not seeing you shakes Rāghava to his core, like the
> way a great earthquake shakes a mighty mountain.

---
48.
kānānāni suramyāṇi nadīprasravaṇāni ca |
caran na ratim āpnoti tvam apaśyan nr̥pātmaje ||

> **Meaning 48:** "Princess, he wanders through lovely forests, past rivers and
> waterfalls, but without you he finds no joy in any of it.

---
49.
sa tvāṁ manujaśārdūlaḥ kṣipraṁ prāpsyati rāghavaḥ |
samitrabāndhavaṁ hatvā rāvaṇaṁ janakātmaje ||

> **Meaning 49:** "Daughter of Janaka, Rāghava, that tiger among men, will soon
> win you back after killing Rāvaṇa along with his allies and kinsmen.

---
50.
sahitau rāmasugrīvāv ubhāv akurutāṁ tadā |
samayaṁ vālinaṁ hantuṁ tava cānveṣaṇaṁ tathā ||

> **Meaning 50:** "Then Rāma and Sugrīva together made a pact: to kill Vālī,
> and to search for you.

---
51.
tatastābhyāṁ kumārābhyāṁ vīrābhyāṁ sa harīśvaraḥ |
kiṣkindhāṁ samupāgamya vālī yuddhe nipātitaḥ ||

52.
tato nihatya tarasā rāmo vālinam āhave |
sarvarkṣaharisaṁghānāṁ sugrīvam akarot patim ||

> **Meaning 51-52:** "After that, the vānara king went to Kiṣkindhā with those
> two heroic princes, and Vālī was struck down in battle. Having swiftly slain
> Vālī in combat, Rāma made Sugrīva king over all the hosts of Ṛkṣas and
> Haris.

---
53.
rāmasugrīvayoraikyaṁ devy evaṁ samajāyata |
hanūmantaṁ ca māṁ viddhi tayordūtam ihāgatam ||

> **Meaning 53:** "That, devī, is how the alliance between Rāma and Sugrīva came
> about. Know that I am Hanumān, their messenger, who has come here.

---
54.
svarājyaṁ prāpya sugrīvaḥ samanīya mahāharīn |
tvadarthaṁ preṣayām āsa diśo daśa mahābalān ||

> **Meaning 54:** "Once he had regained his kingdom, Sugrīva gathered the great
> vānaras and, for your sake, sent those mighty warriors out in all ten
> directions.

---
55.
ādiṣṭā vānarendreṇa sugrīveṇa mahaujasaḥ |
adrirājapratīkāśāḥ sarvataḥ prasthitā mahīm ||

> **Meaning 55:** "Commanded by Sugrīva, king of the vānaras, those powerful
> ones, huge as the king of mountains, set out across the earth in every
> direction.

---

56.
tatastē mārgamāṇā vai sugrīvavacanāturāḥ |
caranti vasudhāṁ kr̥tsnāṁ vayam anye ca vānarāḥ ||

> **Meaning 56:** "Pressed by Sugrīva's orders, they are roaming the whole earth
> searching for you — we and the other vānaras alike.

57.
aṅgado nāma lakṣmīvān vālisūnurmahābalaḥ |
prasthitaḥ kapiśārdūlaḥ tribhāgabalasaṁvr̥taḥ ||

> **Meaning 57:** "The splendid Aṅgada, mighty son of Vālī and a tiger among
> the Kapis, set out at the head of a third of the army.

---
58.
teṣāṁ no vipranaṣṭānāṁ vindhye parvatasattame |
bhr̥śaṁ śokaparītanām ahorātragaṇā gatāḥ ||

> **Meaning 58:** "We lost our way on the Vindhya, finest of mountains, and many
> days and nights went by while we were overwhelmed with grief.

---
59.
te vayaṁ kāryanairāśyāt kālasyātikrameṇa ca |
bhayācca kapirājasya prāṇāṁstyaktuṁ vyavasthitāḥ ||

> **Meaning 59:** "Despairing of our mission, having overrun the time we were
> given, and afraid of the king of the Kapis, we resolved to give up our lives.

---
60.
vicitya vanadurgāṇi giriprasravaṇāni ca |
anāsādya padaṁ devyāḥ prāṇāṁstyaktuṁ vyavasthitāḥ ||

> **Meaning 60:** "We had searched dense forests, mountains, and waterfalls
> without finding any trace of the devī, so we resolved to give up our lives.

---
61.
tatastasya girermūrdhni vayaṁ prāyopaveśmahe |
dṛṣṭvā prāyopaviṣṭāṁśca sarvānvānarapuṁgavān ||

62.
bhr̥śaṁ śokārṇave magnaḥ paryadevayad aṅgadaḥ |
tava nāśaṁ ca vaidehi vālinaśca tathā vadham |
prāyopaveśam asmākaṁ maraṇaṁ ca jaṭāyuṣaḥ ||

> **Meaning 61-62:** "So we sat down on the summit of that mountain to fast
> until death. Seeing all those bull-like vānaras fasting to die, Aṅgada sank
> into an ocean of grief and lamented — Vaidehī — your loss, the killing of
> Vālī, our fast unto death, and the death of Jaṭāyu.

---
63.
teṣāṁ naḥ svāmisaṁdeśān nirāśānāṁ mumūrṣatām |
kāryahetorivāyātaḥ śakunirvīryavān mahān ||

> **Meaning 63:** "Since the appointed time set by our king had passed, we abandoned all hope. As we sat there preparing to fast unto death, a massive and powerful bird arrived, as if drawn by providence.

---
64.
gr̥dhrarājasya sodaryaḥ saṁpātirnāma gr̥dhrarāṭ |
śrutvā bhrātr̥vadhaṁ kopād idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

> **Meaning 64:** "It was Sampāti, king of the vultures, own brother of the
> vulture king Jaṭāyu. Hearing that his brother had been killed, he spoke these
> words in anger:

---
65.
yavīyān kena me bhrātā hataḥ kva ca vināśitaḥ |
etad ākhyātum icchāmi bhavadbhirvānarottamāḥ ||

> **Meaning 65:** "'Who killed my younger brother? Where was he struck down? I
> want you to tell me this, best of the vānaras.'

---
66.
aṅgadaḥ akathayat tasya janasthāne mahad vadham |
rakṣasā bhīmarūpeṇa tvām uddiśya yathātatham ||

> **Meaning 66:** "Aṅgada recounted to him how Jaṭāyu was brutally slain in Janasthāna by a terrifying rākṣasa, fighting for your sake.

---
67.
jaṭāyostu vadhaṁ śrutvā duḥkhitaḥ saḥ aruṇātmajaḥ |
tvām āha sa varārohe vasantīṁ rāvaṇālaye ||

> **Meaning 67:** "Grieved to hear of Jaṭāyu's death, that son of Aruṇa told us,
> graceful lady, that you were living in Rāvaṇa's palace.

---
68.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā saṁpāteḥ prītivardhanam |
aṅgadapramukhāḥ sarve tataḥ saṁprasthitā vayam ||

> **Meaning 68:** "Hearing Sampāti's words, which filled us with joy, all of us,
> led by Aṅgada, set out from there.

69.
vindhyādutthāya saṁprāptāḥ sāgarasyāntamuttaram |
tvaddarśanakr̥totsāhā hr̥ṣṭāstuṣṭāḥ plavaṁgamāḥ ||

> **Meaning 69:** "Rising from the Vindhya, we Plavaṅgamas reached the northern
> shore of the ocean — glad and delighted, and eager to see you.

---
70.
aṅgadapramukhāḥ sarve velopāntamupasthitāḥ ||

71.
cintāṁ jagmuḥ punarbhītāstvaddarśanasamutsukāḥ |
athāhaṁ harisainyasya sāgaraṁ dr̥śya sīdataḥ ||


> **Meaning 70-71:** "All of them, led by Aṅgada, came to the edge of the
> shore. Longing to see you, they grew worried and afraid all over again. Then,
> seeing the vānara army losing heart at the sight of the ocean,

---
72.
vyavadhūya bhayaṁ tīvraṁ yojanānāṁ śataṁ plutaḥ |
laṅkā cāpi mayā rātrau praviṣṭā rākṣasākulā ||


> **Meaning 72:** "I shook off my intense fear and leapt across a hundred
> yojanas. And at night I entered Laṅkā, which swarms with rākṣasas.

---
73.
rāvaṇaśca mayā dr̥ṣṭaḥ tvaṁ ca śokanipīḍitā |
etat te sarvam ākhyātaṁ yathāvr̥ttam anindite ||

> **Meaning 73:** "I saw Rāvaṇa, and I saw you, crushed by grief. Faultless
> lady, I have now told you everything just as it happened.

---
74.
abhibhāṣasva māṁ devi dūto dāśaratheraham |
taṁ māṁ rāmakr̥todyogaṁ tvannimittam ihāgatam ||


> **Meaning 74:** "Speak to me, devī. I am the messenger of Daśaratha's son. I
> have come here for your sake, as part of Rāma's mission.

---
75.
sugrīva sacivaṁ devi budhyasva pavanātmajam |
kuśalī tava kākutsthaḥ sarvaśastrabhr̥tāṁ varaḥ ||


> **Meaning 75:** "Know me, devī, as Sugrīva's minister, the son of the Wind.
> Your swami, the best of all who bear weapons, is well.

---
76.
gurorārādhane yuktaḥ lakṣmaṇaśca sulakṣaṇaḥ |
tasya vīryavato devi bhartustava hite rataḥ ||


> **Meaning 76:** "And Lakṣmaṇa, who bears every auspicious mark, is devoted to
> serving his elder brother. Devī, he is wholly committed to the welfare of
> your mighty husband.

---
77.
aham ekastu saṁprāptaḥ sugrīvavacanād iha |
mayeyam asahāyena caratā kāmarūpiṇā |
dakṣiṇā dig anukrāntā tvanmārgavicayaiṣiṇā ||

> **Meaning 77:** "I have come here alone, at Sugrīva's word. Traveling without
> companions and changing my form at will, I have crossed the southern region,
> searching for your trail.

---
78.
diṣṭyāhaṁ harisainyānāṁ tvannāśam anuśocatām |

79.
apaneṣyāmi saṁtāpaṁ tavābhigamaśaṁsanāt |
diṣṭyā hi na mama vyarthaṁ devi sāgaralaṅghanam ||


> **Meaning 78-79:** "How fortunate! For the vānara armies, who are grieving over
> your loss, I will end their anguish by telling them that I found you.
> How fortunate, devī, that my leap across the ocean was not in vain!

---
80.
prāpsyāmy aham idaṁ diṣṭyā tvaddarśanakr̥taṁ yaśaḥ ||
rāghavaśca mahāvīryaḥ kṣipraṁ tvām abhipatsyate |
samitrabāndhavaṁ hatvā rāvaṇaṁ rākṣasādhipam ||

> **Meaning 80:** "By good fortune I will win the glory of having found you. And
> mighty Rāghava will soon come for you, after killing Rāvaṇa, king of the
> rākṣasas, along with his allies and kinsmen.

---
81.
mālyavān nāma vaidehi girīṇām uttamo giriḥ |
tato gacchati gokarṇaṁ parvataṁ kesarī hariḥ ||

> **Meaning 81:** "Vaidehī, there is a mountain called Malyavān, the finest of
> mountains. From there, the vānara Kesarī went to Mount Gokarṇa.

---
82.
sa ca devarṣibhirdr̥ṣṭaḥ pitā mama mahākapiḥ |
tīrthe nadīpateḥ puṇye śambasādanam uddharat ||

> **Meaning 82:** "A rākṣasa named Śambasādana was causing havoc at a holy tīrtha on the ocean shore. When the devas and ṛṣis prayed to him, my father, Kesari, vanquished that rākṣasa.

---
83.
tasyāhaṁ hariṇaḥ kṣetre jāto vātena maithili |
hanūmān iti vikhyātaḥ loke svenaiva karmaṇā ||


> **Meaning 83:** "Maithilī, By the grace of Vāyudeva, I was born as a son to Añjanādevī, the wife of that king of Kapis. Because of my deeds, I gained renown in the world as Hanuman.

---
84.
viśvāsārthaṁ tu vaidehi bharturuktā mayā guṇāḥ |
acirād rāghavo devīṁ tvāmito nayitā'naghe |

85.
evaṁ viśvāsitā sītā hetubhiḥ śokakarśitā |
upapannairabhijñānaiḥ dūtaṁ tam avagacchati ||

> **Meaning 84-85:** "Vaidehī, I have described your husband's qualities so
> that you can trust me. Very soon, sinless devī, Rāghava will take you away
> from here." Reassured in this way by sound reasons, Sītā, worn thin by grief,
> recognized him as a true messenger through the fitting tokens he gave.

---
86.
atulaṁ ca gatā harṣaṁ praharṣeṇa tu jānakī |
netrābhyāṁ vakrapakṣmābhyāṁ mumocānandajaṁ jalam ||

> **Meaning 86:** Jānakī was filled with boundless joy, and from her eyes with
> their curved lashes she shed tears of happiness.

---
87.
cāru taccānanaṁ tasyāḥ tāmraśuklāyatekṣaṇam |
aśobhata viśālākṣyā rāhumukta ivoḍurāṭ ||


> **Meaning 87:** The lovely face of that wide-eyed lady, with its long eyes of
> white tinged with red, shone like the moon freed from Rāhu.

---
88.
hanūmantaṁ kapiṁ vyaktaṁ manyate nānyatheti sā |
athovāca hanūmāṁstām uttaraṁ priyadarśanām ||

> **Meaning 88:** She was now sure that he was truly Hanumān the Kapi and no one
> else. Then Hanumān spoke again to that lovely lady:

---
89.
etatte sarvamākhyātaṁ samāśvasihi maithili |
kiṁ karomi kathaṁ vā te rocate pratiyāmyahaṁ ||

90.
hate asure saṁyati śambasādane kapipravīreṇa maharṣicodanāt |
tataḥ asmi vāyuprabhavo hi maithili prabhāvatastatpratimaśca vānaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 89-90:** "I have told you everything. Take heart, Maithilī. What
> can I do for you? What would you like? I will go back now. When the asura
> Śambasādana was killed in battle by that great vānara hero at the urging of
> the maharṣis, I was born from the Wind, Maithilī — and I am a vānara equal to
> him in power."
