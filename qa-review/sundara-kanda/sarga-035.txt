=== Sarga 34 ===

---
1.
bhūya eva mahātejā hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ |
abravīt praśritaṁ vākyaṁ sītāpratyayakāraṇāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
vānaro ’haṁ mahābhāge dūto rāmasya dhīmataḥ |
rāmanāmāṅkitaṁ cedaṁ paśya devy aṅgulīyakam |
samāśvasihi bhadraṁ te kṣīṇaduḥkhaphalā hy asi ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
gr̥hītvā prekṣamāṇā sā bhartuḥ karavibhūṣaṇam |
bhartāram iva saṁprāptā jānakī muditābhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
cāru tad vadanaṁ tasyās tāmraśuklāyatekṣaṇam |
babhūva praharṣodagraṁ rāhumukta ivoḍurāṭ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tataḥ sā hrīmatī bālā bhartuḥ saṁdeśaharṣitā |
parituṭṣā priyaṁ śrutvā prāśaṁsata mahākapim ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
vikrāntas tvaṁ samarthas tvaṁ prājñas tvaṁ vānarottama |
yenedaṁ rākṣasapadaṁ tvayaikena pradharṣitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
śatayojanavistīrṇaḥ sāgaro makarālayaḥ |
vikramaślāghanīyena kramatā goṣpadīkr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
na hi tvāṁ prākr̥taṁ manye vanaraṁ vanararṣabha |
yasya te nāsti saṁtrāso rāvaṇān nāpi saṁbhramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
arhase ca kapiśreṣṭha mayā samabhibhāṣitum |
yady asi preṣitas tena rāmeṇa viditātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
preṣayiṣyati durdharṣo rāmo na hy aparīkṣitam |
parākramam avijñāya matsakāśaṁ viśeṣataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
diṣṭyā ca kuśalī rāmo dharmātmā dharmavatsalaḥ |
lakṣmaṇaś ca mahātejāḥ sumitrānandavardhanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
kuśalī yadi kākutsthaḥ kiṁ nu sāgaramekhalām |
mahīṁ dahati kopena yugāntāgnir ivotthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
atha vā śaktimantau tau surāṇām api nigrahe |
mamaiva tu na duḥkhānām asti manye viparyayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
kaccic ca vyathate rāmaḥ kaccin na paripatyate |
uttarāṇi ca kāryāṇi kurute puruṣottamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
kaccin na dīnaḥ saṁbhrāntaḥ kāryeṣu ca na muhyati |
kaccin puruṣakāryāṇi kurute nr̥pateḥ sutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
dvividhaṁ trividhopāyam upāyam api sevate |
vijigīṣuḥ suhr̥t kaccin mitreṣu ca paraṁtapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
kaccin mitrāṇi labhate mitraiś cāpy abhigamyate |
kaccit kalyāṇamitraś ca mitraiś cāpi puraskr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
kaccid āśāsti devānāṁ prasādaṁ pārthivātmajaḥ |
kaccit puruṣakāraṁ ca daivaṁ ca pratipadyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
kaccin na vigatasneho vivāsān mayi rāghavaḥ |
kaccin māṁ vyasanād asmān mokṣayiṣyati vānaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
sukhānām ucito nityam asukhānām anūcitaḥ |
duḥkham uttaram āsādya kaccid rāmo na sīdati ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
kausalyāyās tathā kaccit sumitrāyās tathaiva ca |
abhīkṣṇaṁ śrūyate kaccit kuśalaṁ bharatasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
mannimittena mānārhaḥ kaccic chokena rāghavaḥ |
kaccin nānyamanā rāmaḥ kaccin māṁ tārayiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
kaccid akṣauhiṇīṁ bhīmāṁ bharato bhrātr̥vatsalaḥ |
dhvajinīṁ mantribhir guptāṁ preṣayiṣyati matkr̥te ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
vānarādhipatiḥ śrīmān sugrīvaḥ kaccid eṣyati |
matkr̥te haribhir vīrair vr̥to dantanakhāyudhaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
kaccic ca lakṣmaṇaḥ śūraḥ sumitrānandavardhanaḥ |
astravic charajālena rākṣasān vidhamiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
raudreṇa kaccid astreṇa rāmeṇa nihataṁ raṇe |
drakṣyāmy alpena kālena rāvaṇaṁ sasuhr̥jjanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
kaccin na tad dhemasamānavarṇaṁ; tasyānanaṁ padmasamānagandhi |
mayā vinā śuṣyati śokadīnaṁ; jalakṣaye padmam ivātapena ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
dharmāpadeśāt tyajataś ca rājyāṁ; māṁ cāpy araṇyaṁ nayataḥ padātim |
nāsīd vyathā yasya na bhīr na śokaḥ; kaccit sa dhairyaṁ hr̥daye karoti ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
na cāsya mātā na pitā na cānyaḥ; snehād viśiṣṭo ’sti mayā samo vā |
tāvad dhy ahaṁ dūtajijīviṣeyaṁ; yāvat pravr̥ttiṁ śr̥ṇuyāṁ priyasya ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
itīva devī vacanaṁ mahārthaṁ; taṁ vānarendraṁ madhurārtham uktvā |
śrotuṁ punas tasya vaco ’bhirāmaṁ; rāmārthayuktaṁ virarāma rāmā ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
sītāyā vacanaṁ śrutvā mārutir bhīmavikramaḥ |
śirasy añjalim ādhāya vākyam uttaram abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
na tvām ihasthāṁ jānīte rāmaḥ kamalalocanaḥ |
śrutvaiva tu vaco mahyaṁ kṣipram eṣyati rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
camūṁ prakarṣan mahatīṁ haryr̥ṣkagaṇasaṁkulām |
viṣṭambhayitvā bāṇaughair akṣobhyaṁ varuṇālayam |
kariṣyati purīṁ laṅkāṁ kākutsthaḥ śāntarākṣasām ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
tatra yady antarā mr̥tyur yadi devāḥ sahāsurāḥ |
sthāsyanti pathi rāmasya sa tān api vadhiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
tavādarśanajenārye śokena sa pariplutaḥ |
na śarma labhate rāmaḥ siṁhārdita iva dvipaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
dardareṇa ca te devi śape mūlaphalena ca |
malayena ca vindhyena meruṇā mandareṇa ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
yathā sunayanaṁ valgu bimbauṣṭhaṁ cārukuṇḍalam |
mukhaṁ drakṣyasi rāmasya pūrṇacandram ivoditam ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
kṣipraṁ drakṣyasi vaidehi rāmaṁ prasravaṇe girau |
śatakratum ivāsīnaṁ nākapr̥ṣṭhasya mūrdhani ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
na māṁsaṁ rāghavo bhuṅkte na cāpi madhusevate |
vanyaṁ suvihitaṁ nityaṁ bhaktam aśnāti pañcamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
naiva daṁśān na maśakān na kīṭān na sarīsr̥pān |
rāghavo ’panayed gatrāt tvadgatenāntarātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
nityaṁ dhyānaparo rāmo nityaṁ śokaparāyaṇaḥ |
nānyac cintayate kiṁ cit sa tu kāmavaśaṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
anidraḥ satataṁ rāmaḥ supto ’pi ca narottamaḥ |
sīteti madhurāṁ vāṇīṁ vyāharan pratibudhyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
dr̥ṣṭvā phalaṁ vā puṣpaṁ vā yac cānyat strīmanoharam |
bahuśo hā priyety evaṁ śvasaṁs tvām abhibhāṣate ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
sa devi nityaṁ paritapyamānas; tvām eva sītety abhibhāṣamāṇaḥ |
dhr̥tavrato rājasuto mahātmā; tavaiva lābhāya kr̥taprayatnaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
sā rāmasaṁkīrtanavītaśokā; rāmasya śokena samānaśokā |
śaranmukhenāmbudaśeṣacandrā; niśeva vaidehasutā babhūva ||

=== meaning ===

---
