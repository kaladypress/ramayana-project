=== Sarga 58 ===

---
1.
tatas tasya gireḥ śr̥ṅge mahendrasya mahābalāḥ |
hanumatpramukhāḥ prītiṁ harayo jagmur uttamām ||

> **Meaning 1:** Then, on the peak of Mount Mahendra, the mighty Haris, with
> Hanumān at their head, experienced immense joy.

---
2.
prītimatsūpaviṣṭeṣu vānareṣu mahātmasu |
taṁ tataḥ pratisaṁhr̥ṣṭaḥ prītimantaṁ mahākapim ||


> **Meaning 2:** When the great-souled Vānaras were all seated happily,
> Jāmbavān, filled with delight, turned to the joyful great Kapi.

---
3.
jāmbavān kāryavr̥ttāntam apr̥cchad anilātmajam |
kathaṁ dr̥ṣṭā tvayā devī kathaṁ vā tatra vartate ||


> **Meaning 3:** Jāmbavān asked the son of Anila about how the mission had
> unfolded: "How did you find the devī, and how is she living there?

---
4.
tasyāṁ vā sa kathaṁ vr̥ttaḥ krūrakarmā daśānanaḥ |
tattvataḥ sarvam etan naḥ prabrūhi tvaṁ mahākape ||

> **Meaning 4:** And how does the cruel Daśānana treat her? Great Kapi, tell us
> the truth about all of this.

---
5.
sambhāṣitā kathaṁ devī kiṁ ca sā pratyabhāṣata |
śrutārthāś cintayiṣyāmo bhūyaḥ kāryaviniścayam ||

> **Meaning 5:** How did you speak with the devī, and what did she reply? Once
> we hear the facts, we will consider our next course of action.

---
6.
yaś cārthas tatra vaktavyo gatair asmābhir ātmavān |
rakṣitavyaṁ ca yat tatra tad bhavān vyākarotu naḥ ||

> **Meaning 6:** Tell us clearly what we should say when we go there, and what
> we should keep to ourselves."

---
7.
sa niyuktas tatas tena saṁprahr̥ṣṭatanūruhaḥ |
namasyañ śirasā devyai sītāyai pratyabhāṣata ||

> **Meaning 7:** Prompted by him, his hair standing on end with delight, Hanumān
> bowed his head in reverence to Devī Sītā and replied:

---
8.
pratyakṣam eva bhavatāṁ mahendrāgrāt kham āplutaḥ |
udadher dakṣiṇaṁ pāraṁ kāṅkṣamāṇaḥ samāhitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 8:** "As you all saw with your own eyes, I leapt into the sky from
> the summit of Mahendra, intent on reaching the southern shore of the ocean.

---
9.
gacchataś ca hi me ghoraṁ vighnarūpam ivābhavat |
kāñcanaṁ śikharaṁ divyaṁ paśyāmi sumanoharam ||

> **Meaning 9:** As I was flying, an obstacle seemed to appear in my path — I
> saw a wondrous, enchanting golden mountain peak.

---
10.
sthitaṁ panthānam āvr̥tya mene vighnaṁ ca taṁ nagam |
upasaṁgamya taṁ divyaṁ kāñcanaṁ nagasattamam ||

> **Meaning 10:** It stood blocking my path, and I took the mountain for an
> obstacle. I approached that wondrous, golden jewel of a mountain,

---
11.
kr̥tā me manasā buddhir bhettavyo ’yaṁ mayeti ca |
prahataṁ ca mayā tasya lāṅgūlena mahāgireḥ ||

> **Meaning 11:** and decided in my mind that I had to shatter it. So I struck
> that great mountain with my tail.

---
12.
śikharaṁ sūryasaṁkāśaṁ vyaśīryata sahasradhā |
vyavasāyaṁ ca me buddhvā sa hovāca mahāgiriḥ ||

> **Meaning 12:** Its sun-like peak broke into a thousand pieces. Understanding
> my resolve, the great mountain said:

---
13.
putreti madhurāṁ bāṇīṁ manaḥprahlādayann iva |
pitr̥vyaṁ cāpi māṁ viddhi sakhāyaṁ mātariśvanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 13:** Calling me 'Son' in a sweet voice that cheered my heart, he
> said, 'Know me to be your uncle, a dear friend of Mātariśvan.

---
14.
mainākam iti vikhyātaṁ nivasantaṁ mahodadhau ||
pakṣvavantaḥ purā putra babhūvuḥ parvatottamāḥ |
chandataḥ pr̥thivīṁ cerur bādhamānāḥ samantataḥ ||

> **Meaning 14:** I am known as Maināka, living in the great ocean. In ancient
> times, my son, the greatest mountains had wings and flew across the earth as
> they pleased, causing havoc everywhere.

---
15.
śrutvā nagānāṁ caritaṁ mahendraḥ pākaśāsanaḥ |
ciccheda bhagavān pakṣān vajreṇaiṣāṁ sahasraśaḥ ||

> **Meaning 15:** Hearing of the mountains' conduct, the glorious Mahendra, the
> subduer of Pāka, sheared off their wings by the thousands with his vajra.

---
16.
ahaṁ tu mokṣitas tasmāt tava pitrā mahātmanā |
mārutena tadā vatsa prakṣipto ’smi mahārṇave ||

> **Meaning 16:** But your great-souled father Māruta rescued me then, my dear
> child, and flung me into the great ocean.

---
17.
rāmasya ca mayā sāhye vartitavyam ariṁdama |
rāmo dharmabhr̥tāṁ śreṣṭho mahendrasamavikramaḥ ||

> **Meaning 17:** Subduer of foes, I must help Rāma, for Rāma is foremost among
> upholders of dharma and equals Mahendra in valor.'

---
18.
etac chrutvā mayā tasya mainākasya mahātmanaḥ ||


> **Meaning 18:** Having heard these words from the great-souled Maināka,

---
19.
kāryam āvedya tu girer uddhataṁ ca mano mama |
tena cāham anujñāto mainākena mahātmanā ||


> **Meaning 19:** I told the mountain my mission, and my mind was eager to press
> on. The great-souled Maināka then gave me leave to go.

---
20.
sa cāpyaṁtarhita śśailō mānuṣēṇa vapuṣmatā |
śarīrēṇa mahāśailaḥ śailēna ca mahōdadhau ||

> **Meaning 20:** That great mountain vanished, withdrawing his human form into
> his mountain body within the vast ocean.

---
21.
uttamaṁ javam āsthāya śeṣam adhvānam āsthitaḥ |
tato ’haṁ suciraṁ kālaṁ vegenābhyagamaṁ pathi ||

> **Meaning 21:** Summoning my greatest speed, I set out on the rest of the
> journey and flew swiftly along the path for a long time.

---
22.
tataḥ paśyāmy ahaṁ devīṁ surasāṁ nāgamātaram |
samudramadhye sā devī vacanaṁ mām abhāṣata ||

> **Meaning 22:** Then in the middle of the sea I saw Devī Surasā, mother of the
> Nāgas. That devatā spoke to me:

---
23.
mama bhakṣyaḥ pradiṣṭas tvam amārair harisattamam |
tatas tvāṁ bhakṣayiṣyāmi vihitas tvaṁ cirasya me ||

> **Meaning 23:** 'Foremost of Haris, the devatās have appointed you as my food,
> so I will eat you. You have been granted to me after a long time.'

---
24.
evam uktaḥ surasayā prāñjaliḥ praṇataḥ sthitaḥ |
vivarṇavadano bhūtvā vākyaṁ cedam udīrayam ||

> **Meaning 24:** Addressed thus by Surasā, I stood with joined palms, bowed,
> and with a pale face spoke these words:

---
25.
rāmo dāśarathiḥ śrīmān praviṣṭo daṇḍakāvanam |
lakṣmaṇena saha bhrātrā sītayā ca paraṁtapaḥ ||

> **Meaning 25:** 'The glorious Rāma, son of Daśaratha and scorcher of foes,
> entered the Daṇḍaka forest with his brother Lakṣmaṇa and with Sītā.

---
26.
tasya sītā hr̥tā bhāryā rāvaṇena durātmanā |
tasyāḥ sakāśaṁ dūto ’haṁ gamiṣye rāmaśāsanāt ||

> **Meaning 26:** His wife Sītā was abducted by the wicked Rāvaṇa. By Rāma's
> command, I am going to her as a messenger.

---
27.
kartum arhasi rāmasya sāhyaṁ viṣayavāsini |
atha vā maithilīṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rāmaṁ cākliṣṭakāriṇam ||

> **Meaning 27:** You live in this realm, so you ought to assist Rāma. Or else,
> let me first see Maithilī and Rāma of untiring deeds,

---
28.
āgamiṣyāmi te vaktraṁ satyaṁ pratiśr̥ṇoti me |
evam uktā mayā sā tu surasā kāmarūpiṇī ||

> **Meaning 28:** and then I will enter your mouth — I give you my solemn
> promise.' When I said this to her, Surasā, who could assume any form at will,

---
29.
abravīn nātivarteta kaś cid eṣa varo mama |
evam uktaḥ surasayā daśayojanam āyataḥ ||

> **Meaning 29:** replied, 'No one passes me by; this is the boon granted to
> me.' Addressed thus by Surasā, I expanded myself to ten yojanas across.

---
30.
tato ’rdhaguṇavistāro babhūvāhaṁ kṣaṇena tu |
matpramāṇānurūpaṁ ca vyāditaṁ tanmukhaṁ tayā ||

> **Meaning 30:** In an instant I grew half as wide again, and she opened her
> mouth to match my size.

---
31.
tad dr̥ṣṭvā vyāditaṁ tv āsyaṁ hrasvaṁ hy akaravaṁ vapuḥ |
tasmin muhūrte ca punar babhūvāṅguṣṭhasaṁmitaḥ ||
> **Meaning 31:** Seeing her mouth gaping wide, I shrank my body, and at that
> very moment became as small as a thumb.

---
32.
abhipatyāśu tad vaktraṁ nirgato ’haṁ tataḥ kṣaṇāt |
abravīt surasā devī svena rūpeṇa māṁ punaḥ ||

> **Meaning 32:** Darting swiftly into her mouth, I came right out the next
> instant. Then Devī Surasā resumed her true form and spoke to me again:

---
33.
arthasiddhyai hariśreṣṭha gaccha saumya yathāsukham |
samānaya ca vaidehīṁ rāghaveṇa mahātmanā ||
> **Meaning 33:** 'Go happily on your way, gentle one, best among Haris, to
> fulfill your mission! Reunite Vaidehī with the great-souled Rāghava!

---
34.
sukhī bhava mahābāho prītāsmi tava vānara |
tato ’haṁ sādhu sādhvīti sarvabhūtaiḥ praśaṁsitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 34:** Be blessed, mighty-armed Vānara, I am pleased with you.' Then
> all beings praised me, calling out, 'Well done! Well done!'

---
35.
tato ’ntarikṣaṁ vipulaṁ pluto ’haṁ garuḍo yathā | 
chāyā me nigr̥hītā ca na ca paśyāmi kiṁ cana ||

> **Meaning 35:** Then I soared into the wide sky like Garuḍa. But suddenly my
> shadow was seized, though I could see nothing catching me.

---
36.

so ’haṁ vigatavegas tu diśo daśa vilokayan |
na kiṁ cit tatra paśyāmi yena me ’pahr̥tā gatiḥ ||

> **Meaning 36:** With my speed checked, I looked in all ten directions, but
> could see nothing that was holding back my flight.

---
37.
tato me buddhir utpannā kiṁ nāma gamane mama |
īdr̥śo vighna utpanno rūpaṁ yatra na dr̥śyate ||

> **Meaning 37:** Then the thought struck me: 'What kind of obstacle has arisen
> in my path, where no form can be seen?'

---
38.
adho bhāgena me dr̥ṣṭiḥ śocatā pātitā mayā |
tato ’drākṣam ahaṁ bhīmāṁ rākṣasīṁ salile śayām ||

> **Meaning 38:** As I wondered in distress, I cast my eyes downward, and there
> I saw a terrifying Rākṣasī lying in the water.

---
39.
prahasya ca mahānādam ukto ’haṁ bhīmayā tayā |
avasthitam asaṁbhrāntam idaṁ vākyam aśobhanam ||

> **Meaning 39:** Laughing with a roar like thunder, that terrifying creature
> spoke these crude words to me as I hovered there calmly:

---
40.
kvāsi gantā mahākāya kṣudhitāyā mamepsitaḥ |
bhakṣaḥ prīṇaya me dehaṁ ciram āhāravarjitam ||

> **Meaning 40:** 'Where are you going, huge one? You are the food I crave in my
> hunger! Satisfy my body, which has gone without food for so long!'

---
41.
bāḍham ity eva tāṁ vāṇīṁ pratyagr̥hṇām ahaṁ tataḥ |
āsya pramāṇād adhikaṁ tasyāḥ kāyam apūrayam ||

> **Meaning 41:** I accepted her challenge with 'So be it,' and made my body
> swell larger than the span of her mouth.

---
42.
tasyāś cāsyaṁ mahad bhīmaṁ vardhate mama bhakṣaṇe |
na ca māṁ sā tu bubudhe mama vā vikr̥taṁ kr̥tam ||

> **Meaning 42:** Her dreadful, gaping mouth widened to swallow me, but she did
> not realize what I was doing or notice my transformation.

---
43.
tato ’haṁ vipulaṁ rūpaṁ saṁkṣipya nimiṣāntarāt |
tasyā hr̥dayam ādāya prapatāmi nabhastalam ||

> **Meaning 43:** Then in the blink of an eye I contracted my huge form, tore
> out her heart, and shot back up into the sky.

---
44.
sā visr̥ṣṭabhujā bhīmā papāta lavaṇāmbhasi |
mayā parvatasaṁkāśā nikr̥ttahr̥dayā satī ||

> **Meaning 44:** Her arms flung wide, that terrifying mountain of a Rākṣasī
> fell into the salt sea once I had ripped out her heart.

---
45.
śr̥ṇomi khagatānāṁ ca siddhānāṁ cāraṇaiḥ saha |
rākṣasī siṁhikā bhīmā kṣipraṁ hanumatā hr̥tā ||

> **Meaning 45:** Then I heard the sky-dwelling Siddhas and Cāraṇas proclaim:
> 'Hanumān has swiftly slain the terrifying Rākṣasī Siṁhikā!'

---
46.
tāṁ hatvā punar evāhaṁ kr̥tyam ātyayikaṁ smaran |
gatvā ca mahad adhvānaṁ paśyāmi nagamaṇḍitam |
dakṣiṇaṁ tīram udadher laṅkā yatra ca sā purī ||

> **Meaning 46:** Having killed her, mindful of my urgent task, I traveled a
> great distance and saw the southern shore of the ocean, adorned with hills,
> where that city of Laṅkā lies.

---
47.
astaṁ dinakare yāte rakṣasāṁ nilayaṁ purīm |
praviṣṭo ’ham avijñāto rakṣobhir bhīmavikramaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 47:** When the sun set, I entered the city, the stronghold of the
> Rākṣasas, unseen by the fiercely powerful demons.

---
48.
tatra praviśataścāpi kalpāṁtaghanasannibhā |
aṭṭahāsaṁ vimuṁcaṁtī nārī kāpyutthitā puraḥ ||

> **Meaning 48:** As I was entering, a woman rose up before me like a cloud at
> the dissolution of the world, letting out a burst of mocking laughter.

---
49.
jighāṁsaṁtīṁ tatastāṁ tu jvaladagniśirōruhām ||

> **Meaning 49:** She wanted to kill me, her hair blazing like fire.

---

50.
savyamuṣṭiprahārēṇa parājitya subhairavām |
pradōṣakālē praviśaṁ bhītayāhaṁ tayōditaḥ ||

> **Meaning 50:** Striking that terrifying creature with my left fist, I subdued
> her. As I entered at dusk, she said to me in fear:

---
51.
ahaṁ laṁkāpurī vīra nirjitā vikramēṇa tē |
yasmāttasmādvijētāsi sarvarakṣāṁsyaśēṣataḥ ||

> **Meaning 51:** 'Hero, I am the city of Laṅkā, conquered by your valor.
> Because of this, you will vanquish every single one of the Rākṣasas.'

---
52.
tatrāhaṁ sarvarātraṁ tu vicinvañ janakātmajām |
rāvaṇāntaḥpuragato na cāpaśyaṁ sumadhyamām ||

> **Meaning 52:** I searched for Janaka's daughter throughout the night, even
> entering Rāvaṇa's inner quarters, but I could not find the slender-waisted
> lady.

---
53.
tataḥ sītām apaśyaṁs tu rāvaṇasya niveśane |
śokasāgaram āsādya na pāram upalakṣaye ||

> **Meaning 53:** Unable to find Sītā in Rāvaṇa's palace, I sank into an ocean
> of sorrow and could see no far shore.

---
54.
śocatā ca mayā dr̥ṣṭaṁ prākāreṇa samāvr̥tam |
kāñcanena vikr̥ṣṭena gr̥hopavanam uttamam ||

> **Meaning 54:** In my grief I noticed a splendid palace garden, surrounded by
> an imposing golden wall.

---
55.
sa prākāram avaplutya paśyāmi bahupādapam ||
aśokavanikāmadhye śiṁśapāpādapo mahān ||

> **Meaning 55:** Leaping over the wall, I saw a grove with many trees, and in
> the middle of the Aśoka garden stood a great śiṁśapā tree.

---
56.
tam āruhya ca paśyāmi kāñcanaṁ kadalī vanam |
adūrē śiṁśapāvr̥kṣāt paśyāmi vanavarṇinīm ||
> **Meaning 56:** Climbing into it, I looked down upon a grove of golden
> plantains, and not far from the śiṁśapā tree I saw a fair lady.

---
57.
śyāmāṁ kamalapatrākṣīm upavāsakr̥śānanām |
tadēkavāsa ssaṁvītāṁ rajōdhvastaśirōruhām ||
> **Meaning 57:** She was youthful and dark-complexioned, with lotus eyes, her
> face emaciated from fasting, wearing a single garment, her hair dulled with
> dust.

---
58.
śōkasaṁtāpadīnāṁgīṁ sītāṁ bhartr̥hitē sthitām |
rākṣasībhir virūpābhiḥ krūrābhir abhisaṁvr̥tām ||

59.
māṁsaśoṇitabhakṣyābhir vyāghrībhir hariṇīṁ yathā |
sā mayā rākṣasīmadhyē tarjyamānā muhurmuhuḥ ||

> **Meaning 58-59:** Her form was worn with sorrow and grief, yet she remained
> devoted to her husband's welfare, surrounded by misshapen, cruel Rākṣasīs who
> feed on flesh and blood, like a deer trapped among tigresses. There in the
> midst of the Rākṣasīs, she was constantly threatened.

---
60.
ēkavēṇīdharā dīnā bhartr̥ciṁtāparāyaṇā |
bhūmiśayyā vivarṇāṁgī padminīva himāgamē ||
> **Meaning 60:** Wearing her hair in a single braid, heartbroken, completely
> absorbed in thoughts of her husband, sleeping on the bare ground, pale and
> withered like a lotus pond at the onset of winter frost,

---
61.
rāvaṇā dvinivr̥ttārthā martavyakr̥tāṁniścayā |
kathaṁcinmr̥gaśābākṣī tūrṇamāsāditā mayā ||

> **Meaning 61:** spurning Rāvaṇa and resolved to die, that fawn-eyed lady was
> at last discovered by me.

---
62.
tāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā tādr̥śīṁ nārīṁ rāmapatnīm aninditām |
tatraiva śiṁśapāvr̥kṣe paśyann aham avasthitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 62:** Seeing Rāma's blameless wife in such a state, I stayed right
> there in the śiṁśapā tree, watching her.

---
63.
tato halahalāśabdaṁ kāñcīnūpuramiśritam |
śr̥ṇomy adhikagambhīraṁ rāvaṇasya niveśane ||

> **Meaning 63:** Then from Rāvaṇa's dwelling I heard a loud commotion, deeply
> resounding, mingled with the tinkling of girdles and anklets.

---
64.
tato ’haṁ paramodvignaḥ svarūpaṁ pratyasaṁharam |
ahaṁ ca śiṁśapāvr̥kṣe pakṣīva gahane sthitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 64:** Very anxious, I contracted my form and hid among the dense
> foliage of the śiṁśapā tree like a bird.

---
65.
tato rāvaṇadārāś ca rāvaṇaś ca mahābalaḥ |
taṁ deśaṁ samanuprāptā yatra sītābhavat sthitā ||

> **Meaning 65:** Then the mighty Rāvaṇa and his wives arrived at the spot where
> Sītā was sitting.

---
66.
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvātha varārohā sītā rakṣogaṇeśvaram |
saṁkucyorū stanau pīnau bāhubhyāṁ parirabhya ca ||

> **Meaning 66:** Seeing the king of the Rākṣasas, Sītā, graceful in form,
> shrank her thighs together and clasped her full breasts with her arms.

---
67.
vitrastāṁ paramōdvignāṁ vīkṣamāṇāṁ tatastataḥ |
trāṇaṁ kiṁcidapaśyaṁtīṁ vēpamānāṁ tapasvinīm || 

> **Meaning 67:** Terrified and deeply distressed, she glanced here and there,
> seeing no protection anywhere, trembling in her misery.

---

68.
tām uvāca daśagrīvaḥ sītāṁ paramaduḥkhitām |
avākśirāḥ prapatito bahu manyasva mām iti ||

> **Meaning 68:** Daśagrīva said to that deeply sorrowing Sītā, bowing his head
> low: 'Accept me and think well of me!

---
69.
yadi cet tvaṁ tu māṁ darpān nābhinandasi garvite |
dvimāsānantaraṁ sīte pāsyāmi rudhiraṁ tava ||

> **Meaning 69:** Proud woman, if out of pride you do not accept me, then in two
> months' time, Sītā, I will drink your blood.'

---
70.
etac chrutvā vacas tasya rāvaṇasya durātmanaḥ |
uvāca paramakruddhā sītā vacanam uttamam ||

> **Meaning 70:** Hearing these words from the wicked Rāvaṇa, Sītā was furious
> and spoke these noble words:

---
71.
rākṣasādhama rāmasya bhāryām amitatejasaḥ |
ikṣvākukulanāthasya snuṣāṁ daśarathasya ca ||


> **Meaning 71:** 'Vile Rākṣasa, I am the wife of Rāma of boundless splendor,
> sovereign of the Ikṣvāku line, and daughter-in-law of Daśaratha!

---
72.
avācyaṁ vadato jihvā kathaṁ na patitā tava |
kiṁsvid vīryaṁ tavānārya yo māṁ bhartur asaṁnidhau ||

> **Meaning 72:** How did your tongue not fall out when speaking such
> unspeakable words? What valor do you have, ignoble wretch, who in my husband's
> absence

---
73.
apahr̥tyāgataḥ pāpa tenādr̥ṣṭo mahātmanā |
na tvaṁ rāmasya sadr̥śo dāsye ’py asyā na yujyase |
yajñīyaḥ satyavāk caiva raṇaślāghī ca rāghavaḥ ||

> **Meaning 73:** abducted me and fled here unseen by that great-souled hero?
> You are not Rāma's equal — you are not even fit to be his slave! Rāghava is
> worthy of worship, truthful in speech, and renowned in battle.'

---
74.
jānakyā paruṣaṁ vākyam evam ukto daśānanaḥ |
jajvāla sahasā kopāc citāstha iva pāvakaḥ ||

> **Meaning 74:** Addressed so harshly by Jānakī, Daśānana instantly flared up
> in rage like a fire on a funeral pyre.

---
75.

---
151.
sumahaty aparādhe ’pi dūtasyātulavikramaḥ |
virūpakaraṇaṁ dr̥ṣṭaṁ na vadho ’stīha śāstrataḥ ||

> **Meaning 151:** Even for a grave offense, warrior of peerless valor,
> mutilation is prescribed for an envoy, but never execution according to the
> treatises.'

---
152.
vibhīṣaṇenaivam ukto rāvaṇaḥ saṁdideśa tān |
rākṣasān etad evādya lāṅgūlaṁ dahyatām iti ||

> **Meaning 152:** Advised thus by Vibhīṣaṇa, Rāvaṇa commanded the Rākṣasas:
> 'Let his tail be set on fire today!'

---
153.
tatas tasya vacaḥ śrutvā mama pucchaṁ samantataḥ |
veṣṭitaṁ śaṇavalkaiś ca paṭaiḥ kārpāsakais tathā ||

> **Meaning 153:** Hearing his command, they wrapped my tail all over with hemp
> bark and cotton cloths.

---
154.
rākṣasāḥ siddhasaṁnāhās tatas te caṇḍavikramāḥ |
tad ādīpyanta me pucchaṁ hanantaḥ kāṣṭhamuṣṭibhiḥ ||

> **Meaning 154:** Those fierce Rākṣasas, fully prepared, set fire to my tail
> and beat me with wooden fists.

---
155.
baddhasya bahubhiḥ pāśair yantritasya ca rākṣasaiḥ |
na me pīḍā bhavet kā cid didr̥kṣor nagarīṁ divā ||

> **Meaning 155:** Though bound tight by many ropes and restrained by the
> Rākṣasas, I felt no distress, for I wanted to inspect the city by daylight.

---
156.
tatas te rākṣasāḥ śūrā baddhaṁ mām agnisaṁvr̥tam |
aghoṣayan rājamārge nagaradvāram āgatāḥ ||

> **Meaning 156:** Then those heroic Rākṣasas paraded me along the royal highway
> and brought me to the city gate, bound and wrapped in flames.

---
157.
tato ’haṁ sumahad rūpaṁ saṁkṣipya punar ātmanaḥ |
vimocayitvā taṁ bandhaṁ prakr̥tiṣṭhaḥ sthitaḥ punaḥ ||

> **Meaning 157:** Then I shrank my huge form, slipped free of those bonds, and
> stood restored to my natural size.

---
158.
āyasaṁ parighaṁ gr̥hya tāni rakṣāṁsy asūdayam |
tatas tan nagaradvāraṁ vegenāplutavān aham ||

> **Meaning 158:** Grabbing an iron beam, I struck down those Rākṣasas and
> swiftly leapt up onto the city gate.

---
159.
pucchena ca pradīptena tāṁ purīṁ sāṭṭagopurām |
dahāmy aham asaṁbhrānto yugāntāgnir iva prajāḥ ||

> **Meaning 159:** With my burning tail, calm and unhurried, I torched the city
> with its towers and gateways, like the fire at the end of an era consuming all
> creatures.

---

160.
vinaṣṭā jānakī vyaktaṁ na hyadagdhaḥ pradr̥śyatē |
laṁkāyāṁ kaściduddhēśaḥ sarvā bhasmīkr̥tā purī ||
> **Meaning 160:** Then fear struck me: 'Jānakī must surely have perished, for
> not a spot in Laṅkā can be seen unburnt — the entire city has been reduced to
> ashes!

---
161.
dahatā ca mayā laṁkāṁ dagdhā sītā na saṁśayaḥ |
rāmasya hi mahatkāryaṁ mayēdaṁ vitathīkr̥tam ||

> **Meaning 161:** In burning Laṅkā, I have burned Sītā without doubt; I have
> ruined Rāma's great purpose!'

---
162.
iti śōkasamāviṣṭaḥ ciṁtāmahamupāgataḥ |
athāhaṁ vācamaśrauṣaṁ cāraṇānāṁ śubhākṣarām ||
> **Meaning 162:** As I was overwhelmed by grief and anxiety, I heard the
> auspicious voices of the Cāraṇas

---
163.
jānakī na ca dagdhēti vismayōdaṁtabhāṣitāṁ |
tatō mē buddhirutpannā śrutvā tāmadbhutāṁ giram |
adagdhā jānakītyēvaṁ nimittaiścōpalakṣitā || 

> **Meaning 163:** speaking in wonder that Jānakī had not perished in the
> flames. Hearing those wondrous words, the realization dawned on me that Jānakī
> was unharmed, and auspicious omens confirmed it.

---

164.
dīpyamānē tu lāṁgūlē na māṁ dahati pāvakaḥ ||

> **Meaning 164:** Even while my tail blazed, the fire had not burned me.

---

165.
hr̥dayaṁ ca prahr̥ṣṭaṁ mē vātā ssurabhigaṁdhinaḥ |
tairnimittaiśca dr̥ṣṭāthaiḥ kāraṇaiśca mahāguṇaiḥ ||
> **Meaning 165:** My heart had felt cheerful, and the breezes smelled fragrant.
> From these verified signs and compelling reasons,

---
166.
r̥ṣivākyaiśca siddhārthaiḥ abhavaṁ hr̥ṣṭamānasaḥ |
punardr̥ṣṭvā ca vaidēhīṁ visr̥ṣṭaśca tayā punaḥ ||

> **Meaning 166:** and from the infallible words of the sages, my heart was
> overjoyed. I saw Vaidehī once more, and she gave me leave to depart.

---
167.
tataḥ parvatamāsādya tatrāriṣṭamahaṁ punaḥ |
pratiplavanamārēbhē yuṣmr̥ddharśanakāṁkṣayā ||

> **Meaning 167:** Then I ascended Mount Ariṣṭa there and began my return leap,
> longing to see you all.

---

168.
tataḥ pavanacaṁdrārka siddhagaṁdharvasēvitam |
paṁthānamahamākramya bhavatō dr̥ṣṭavāniha ||

> **Meaning 168:** Traveling along that path frequented by the wind, moon, sun,
> Siddhas, and Gandharvas, I have reached you here.

---

169.
rāghavasya prabhāvēṇa bhavatāṁ caiva tējasā |
sugrīvasya ca kāryārthaṁ mayā sarvamanuṣṭhitam ||

> **Meaning 169:** By the power of Rāghava and the spiritual strength of all of
> you, I have accomplished everything for the service of Sugrīva.

---
170.
ētatsarvaṁ mayā tatra yathāvadupapāditam |
atra yanna kr̥taṁ śēṣaṁ tatsarvaṁ kriyatāmiti ||
> **Meaning 170:** I have related all this to you exactly as it happened. Now
> let whatever remains to be done here be carried out."
