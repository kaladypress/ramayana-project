=== Sarga 12 ===

---
1.
sa tasya madhye bhavanasya vānaraḥ latāgr̥hāṁścitragr̥hān niśāgr̥hān |
jagāma sītāṁ prati darśanotsukaḥ na caiva tāṁ paśyati cārudarśanām ||


---
2.
sa cintayām āsa tato mahākapiḥ priyām apaśyan raghunandanasya tām |
dhruvaṁ nu sītā mriyate yathā na me vicinvato darśanam eti maithilī ||


---
3.
sā rākṣasānāṁ pravareṇa bālā svaśīlasaṁrakṣaṇa tat parā satī |
anena nūnaṁ pratiduṣṭakarmaṇā hatā bhaved āryapathe pare sthitā ||


---
4.
virūparūpā vikr̥tā vivarcasaḥ mahānanā dīrghavirūpadarśanāḥ |
samīkṣya sā rākṣasarājayoṣitaḥ bhayād vinaṣṭā janakeśvarātmajā ||


> **Meaning 1-4:** Searching for Sītā, Hanumān wandered through the creeper
> bowers, picture galleries, and night-houses within that palace, but eager as
> he was, he could not see that lady of beautiful appearance, Sītā Dēvi. Then,
> not seeing the beloved wife of Śrī Rāma, Māruti began to think. "Truly, Sītā
> might not have kept her life. Otherwise, despite searching so much, why would
> her sight elude me? Jānakī Dēvi follows the most exalted path of Dharma and
> is deeply devoted to protecting her character. Such a chaste wife must have
> certainly been killed by Rāvaṇa, the Rākṣasa king of evil tendencies. Or
> perhaps, seeing those Rākṣasa women who have terrifying forms, cruel natures,
> deformed faces, distorted eyes, and are devoid of luster, Jānakī Dēvi might
> have become terrified and perished out of fear."
---



---
5.
sītām adr̥ṣṭvā hy anavāpya pauruṣaṁ vihr̥tya kālaṁ saha vānaraiściram |
na me ’sti sugrīvasamīpagā gatiḥ sutīkṣṇadaṇḍo balavāṁśca vānaraḥ ||


---
6.
dr̥ṣṭam antaḥpuraṁ sarvaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rāvaṇayoṣitaḥ |
na sītā dr̥śyate sādhvī vr̥thā jāto mama śramaḥ ||


---
7.
kiṁ nu māṁ vānarāḥ sarve gataṁ vakṣyanti saṁgatāḥ |
gatvā tatra tvayā vīra kiṁ kr̥taṁ tad vadasva naḥ ||


---
8.
adr̥ṣṭvā kiṁ pravakṣyāmi tām ahaṁ janakātmajām |
dhruvaṁ prāyam upeṣyanti kālasya vyativartane ||


---
9.
kiṁ vā vakṣyati vr̥ddhaśca jāmbavān aṅgadaśca saḥ |
gataṁ pāraṁ samudrasya vānarāśca samāgatāḥ ||


> **Meaning 5-9:** "Having spent so much time with the Vānaras in search of
> Sītā, all my strenuous endeavors have become entirely fruitless. In such a
> state, it is not proper to approach Sugrīva. That powerful king will surely
> inflict a severe punishment. I have seen the entire inner apartment and all
> the wives of Rāvaṇa, but the chaste Sītā is not seen. My effort has been in
> vain. What will all the assembled Vānaras say to me when I return? 'O
> valiant one, what have you done after going there? Tell us.' What can I say
> to them without seeing the daughter of Janaka? As the allotted time has
> already passed, they will surely embrace death by fasting. What will the
> venerable Jāmbavān, Aṅgada, and the assembled Vānaras who have crossed the
> ocean say?"
---



---
10.
anirvedaḥ śriyo mūlam anirvedaḥ paraṁ sukham |
bhūyastāvad viceṣyāmi na yatra vicayaḥ kr̥taḥ ||


---
11.
anirvedo hi satataṁ sarvārtheṣu pravartakaḥ |
karoti saphalaṁ jantoḥ karma yac ca karoti saḥ ||


---
12.
tasmād anirveda kr̥taṁ yatnaṁ ceṣṭe ’ham uttamam |
adr̥ṣṭāṁśca viceṣyāmi deśān rāvaṇapālitān ||


---
13.
āpānaśālāvicitāḥ tathā puṣpagr̥hāṇi ca |
citraśālāśca vicitā bhūyaḥ krīḍāgr̥hāṇi ca ||


---
14.
niṣkuṭāntararathyāśca vimānāni ca sarvaśaḥ |
iti saṁcintya bhūyo ’pi vicetum upacakrame ||


> **Meaning 10-14:** "Perseverance is the root of prosperity. Unyielding
> enthusiasm brings supreme happiness. Therefore, I will search again where a
> search has not yet been made. Such untiring zeal is always the driving
> force for success in all endeavors. It makes whatever action a living being
> undertakes fruitful. Therefore, I will make the utmost effort, completely
> free from despair. I will search the unexplored places ruled by Rāvaṇa. The
> banquet halls, flower houses, picture galleries, and playhouses have been
> searched. The gardens, inner paths, and Vimānas have all been thoroughly
> examined." Thinking thus, he began to search again.
---



---
15.
bhūmīgr̥hāṁścaityagr̥hān gr̥hātigr̥hakān api |
utpatan nipataṁścāpi tiṣṭhan gacchan punaḥ kva cit ||


---
16.
apāvr̥ṇvaṁśca dvārāṇi kapāṭāny avaghaṭṭayan |
praviśan niṣpataṁścāpi prapatann utpatann api ||



---
17.
sarvam apy avakāśaṁ sa vicacāra mahākapiḥ |
caturaṅgulamātro ’pi nāvakāśaḥ sa vidyate |
rāvaṇāntaḥpure tasmin yaṁ kapirna jagāma saḥ ||


---
18.
prākarāntararathyāśca vedikaścaityasaṁśrayāḥ |
śvabhrāśca puṣkariṇyaśca sarvaṁ tenāvalokitam ||


> **Meaning 15-18:** He went through underground chambers, shrines, and smaller
> structures beyond the main houses. Jumping up, jumping down, standing,
> walking, and rushing again. Opening doors, pushing open panels, entering,
> exiting, falling forward, and springing up. The Mahākapi roamed through every
> single space. In that inner apartment of Rāvaṇa, there was not even a space of
> four fingers width where the Kapi did not go. The spaces between the boundary
> walls, the pathways, the platforms, the sanctuaries, the hollows, and the
> lotus ponds—all of these were examined by him.
---



---
19.
rākṣasyo vividhākārā virūpā vikr̥tāstathā |
dr̥ṣṭā hanūmatā tatra na tu sā janakātmajā ||


---
20.
rūpeṇāpratimā loke varā vidyādhara striyaḥ |
dr̥ṣṭā hanūmatā tatra na tu rāghavanandinī ||


---
21.
nāgakanyā varārohāḥ pūrṇacandranibhānanāḥ |
dr̥ṣṭā hanūmatā tatra na tu sītā sumadhyamā ||


---
22.
pramathya rākṣasendreṇa nāgakanyā balād dhr̥tāḥ |
dr̥ṣṭā hanūmatā tatra na sā janakanandinī ||


---
23.
so ’paśyaṁstāṁ mahābāhuḥ paśyaṁścānyā varastriyaḥ |
viṣasāda mahābāhuḥ hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ ||


---
24.
udyogaṁ vānarendrāṇaṁ plavanaṁ sāgarasya ca |
vyarthaṁ vīkṣyānilasutaḥ cintāṁ punarupāgamat ||


---
25.
avatīrya vimānāc ca hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ |
cintām upajagāmātha śokopahatacetanaḥ ||


> **Meaning 19-25:** Hanumān saw Rākṣasa women of various forms, some deformed
> and some distorted, but he did not see the daughter of Janaka. He saw
> Vidyādhara women unparalleled in beauty in the world, but he did not see the
> wife of Raghava. He saw Nāga maidens with beautiful hips and faces
> like the full moon, but he did not see the slender-waisted Sītā. He saw Nāga
> maidens forcibly abducted by the lord of the Rākṣasas after defeating them,
> but not the daughter of Janaka. The great-armed son of Vāyu, the great-armed
> Hanumān, fell into despair, not seeing her while seeing other excellent women.
> Seeing the efforts of the Vānara leaders and the crossing of the ocean in
> vain, the son of the Wind God once again fell into deep thought. Descending
> from the Vimāna, Hanumān, the son of Vāyu, his mind overcome with grief, fell
> into deep contemplation.
