=== Sarga 20 ===

---

1.
sa tāṁ parivr̥tāṁ dīnāṁ nirānandāṁ tapasvinīm |
sākārair madhurair vākyair nyadarśayata rāvaṇaḥ ||

2.
māṁ dr̥ṣṭvā nāganāsorugūhamānā stanodaram |
adarśanam ivātmānaṁ bhayān netuṁ tvam icchasi ||

3.
kāmaye tvāṁ viśālākṣi bahumanyasva māṁ priye |
sarvāṅgaguṇasampanne sarvalokamanohare ||

4.
neha kecin manuṣyā vā rākṣasāḥ kāmarūpiṇaḥ |
vyapasarpatu te sīte bhayaṁ mattaḥ samutthitam ||

> **Meaning 1-4:** Rāvaṇa revealed his feelings through sweet words and
> gestures to that chaste and ascetic lady, who was miserable, joyless, and
> surrounded by Rākṣasīs: "Seeing me, you hide yourself, pressing your
> thighs against your body, wishing to vanish out of fear. But I desire
> you — you whose beauty is flawless in every way, who captivates all the
> worlds! O beloved, hold me in esteem! O Sītā! There are no human beings
> here, nor shape-shifting Rākṣasas. Let your fear of me entirely
> depart."
---

5.
svadharme rakṣasāṁ bhīru sarvathaiṣa na saṁśayaḥ |
gamanaṁ vā parastrīṇāṁ haraṇaṁ sampramathya vā ||

6.
evaṁ caitad akāmāṁ ca na tvāṁ sprakṣyāmi maithili |
kāmaṁ kāmaḥ śarīre me yathākāmaṁ pravartatām ||

7.
devi neha bhayaṁ kāryaṁ mayi viśvasihi priye |
praṇayasva ca tattvena maivaṁ bhūḥ śokalālasā ||

> **Meaning 5-7:** "Approaching the wives of others or abducting them by
> force is by all means the natural dharma of the Rākṣasas — there is no
> doubt about this. Even so, O Maithilī, I will not touch you against
> your will. Let desire act as it wishes within my body. O divine one!
> There is no cause for fear here. Trust in me, beloved! Show your
> affection truly, and do not remain so absorbed in sorrow."
---

8.
ekaveṇī dharāśayyā dhyānaṁ malinam ambaram |
asthāne ’py upavāsaś ca naitāny aupayikāni te ||

9.
vicitrāṇi ca mālyāni candanāny agarūṇi ca |
vividhāni ca vāsāṁsi divyāny ābharaṇāni ca ||

10.
mahārhāṇi ca pānāni yānāni śayanāni ca |
gītaṁ nr̥ttaṁ ca vādyaṁ ca labha māṁ prāpya maithili ||

11.
strīratnam asi maivaṁ bhūḥ kuru gātreṣu bhūṣaṇam |
māṁ prāpya tu kathaṁ hi syās tvam anarhā suvigrahe ||

> **Meaning 8-11:** "A single braid, sleeping on the bare ground,
> brooding, soiled garments, and untimely fasting — these do not befit
> you. O Maithilī! With me, you shall have exquisite garlands,
> sandalwood paste, aloe wood, garments of every kind, divine ornaments,
> the rarest drinks, conveyances, beds, song, dance, and instrumental
> music! You are the very jewel of womanhood — do not remain like this.
> Grace yourself with adornments! How can one so exquisitely formed
> remain unadorned?"
---

12.
idaṁ te cārusañjātaṁ yauvanaṁ vyativartate |
yad atītaṁ punar naiti srotaḥ śīghram apām iva ||

13.
tvāṁ kr̥tvoparato manye rūpakartā sa viśvakr̥t |
na hi rūpopamā tv anyā tavāsti śubhadarśane ||

14.
tvāṁ samāsādya vaidehi rūpayauvanaśālinīm |
kaḥ pumān ativarteta sākṣād api pitāmahaḥ ||

15.
yad yat paśyāmi te gātraṁ śītāṁśusadr̥śānane |
tasmiṁs tasmin pr̥thuśroṇi cakṣur mama nibadhyate ||

> **Meaning 12-15:** "This beautiful youth of yours is passing away.
> Once gone, it does not return — like the swift current of water. I
> believe that Lord Brahmā, sculptor of all forms, ceased his work after
> creating you! There is no beauty in all the worlds that can be
> compared to yours. O Vaidehī! Having encountered you, radiant with
> beauty and youth, what man could turn away — even Lord Brahmā himself?
> O moon-faced one! Wherever my gaze falls upon you, there my eyes
> remain fixed."
---

16.
bhava maithili bhāryā me moham enaṁ visarjaya |
bahvīnām uttamastrīṇāṁ mamāgramahiṣī bhava ||

17.
lokebhyo yāni ratnāni sampramathyāhr̥tāni me |
tāni te bhīru sarvāṇi rājyaṁ caitad ahaṁ ca te ||

18.
vijitya pr̥thivīṁ sarvāṁ nānānagaramālinīm |
janakāya pradāsyāmi tava hetor vilāsini ||

19.
neha paśyāmi loke ’nyaṁ yo me pratibalo bhavet |
paśya me sumahad vīryam apratidvandvam āhave ||

20.
asakr̥t saṁyuge bhagnā mayā vimr̥ditadhvajāḥ |
aśaktāḥ pratyanīkeṣu sthātuṁ mama surāsurāḥ ||

> **Meaning 16-20:** "O Maithilī, become mine! Cast off this delusion.
> I have brought many excellent women from across the worlds — among all
> of them, become my chief queen! Whatever gems have been wrested by me
> from the worlds, all of them are yours. This kingdom and I myself are
> yours. O enchanting one! Having conquered the entire earth, garlanded
> with countless cities, I shall offer it to King Janaka for your sake.
> I do not see anyone in all the worlds who could be my equal. Behold my
> immense valor, unmatched in battle! Repeatedly defeated by me, their
> banners crushed, neither the Devas nor the Asuras can stand against
> me."
---

21.
iccha māṁ kriyatām adya pratikarma tavottamam |
saprabhāṇy avasajjantāṁ tavāṅge bhūṣaṇāni ca ||

22.
sādhu paśyāmi te rūpaṁ saṁyuktaṁ pratikarmaṇā |
pratikarmābhisaṁyuktā dākṣiṇyena varānane ||

23.
bhuṅkṣva bhogān yathākāmaṁ piba bhīru ramasva ca |
yatheṣṭaṁ ca prayaccha tvaṁ pr̥thivīṁ vā dhanāni ca ||

24.
lalasva mayi visrabdhā dhr̥ṣṭam ājñāpayasva ca |
matprabhāvāl lalantyāś ca lalantāṁ bāndhavās tava ||

> **Meaning 21-24:** "Desire me! Let the finest adornment be done for
> you today — let radiant ornaments grace your form. How I wish to see
> your beauty arrayed in splendor! O fair-faced one, accept this with
> graciousness! Enjoy pleasures as you wish — drink, rejoice! Bestow
> wealth or lands upon whomever you desire! Delight in me with
> confidence, and give commands boldly! By my power, as you rejoice, let
> your kinsmen rejoice as well."
---

25.
r̥ddhiṁ mamānupaśya tvaṁ śriyaṁ bhadre yaśaś ca me |
kiṁ kariṣyasi rāmeṇa subhage cīravāsasā ||

26.
nikṣiptavijayo rāmo gataśrīr vanagocaraḥ |
vratī sthaṇḍilaśāyī ca śaṅke jīvati vā na vā ||

27.
na hi vaidehi rāmas tvāṁ draṣṭuṁ vāpy upalapsyate |
puro balākair asitair meghair jyotsnām ivāvr̥tām ||

28.
na cāpi mama hastāt tvāṁ prāptum arhati rāghavaḥ |
hiraṇyakaśipuḥ kīrtim indrahastagatām iva ||

> **Meaning 25-28:** "O auspicious one! Behold my prosperity, my
> wealth, my fame! What will you do with Śrī Rāma, who is clad in bark
> garments? He has lost his chance for victory, is bereft of fortune,
> and wanders the forest. He observes ascetic vows and sleeps on the
> bare ground — I doubt whether he is even alive! O Vaidehī! Śrī Rāma
> will not even glimpse you again, just as moonlight concealed by dark
> clouds preceded by cranes cannot be seen. Hiraṇyakaśipu was able to
> regain his wife from the hands of Indra, but Rāghava will never
> reclaim you from mine."
---

29.
cārusmite cārudati cārunetre vilāsini |
mano harasi me bhīru suparṇaḥ pannagaṁ yathā ||

30.
kliṣṭakauśeyavasanāṁ tanvīm apy analaṅkr̥tām |
tāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā sveṣu dāreṣu ratiṁ nopalabhāmy aham ||

31.
antaḥpuranivāsinyaḥ striyaḥ sarvaguṇānvitāḥ |
yāvantyo mama sarvāsām aiśvaryaṁ kuru jānaki ||

32.
mama hy asitakeśānte trailokyapravarāḥ striyaḥ |
tās tvāṁ paricariṣyanti śriyam apsaraso yathā ||

33.
yāni vaiśravaṇe subhru ratnāni ca dhanāni ca |
tāni lokāṁś ca suśroṇi māṁ ca bhuṅkṣva yathāsukham ||

34.
na rāmas tapasā devi na balena na vikramaiḥ |
na dhanena mayā tulyas tejasā yaśasāpi vā ||

> **Meaning 29-34:** "O enchanting one — your smile, your radiance,
> your eyes — you steal my heart, as Garuḍa snatches the serpent.
> Seeing you slender and unadorned, clad in soiled silk, I no longer
> find pleasure in my own wives. O Jānakī! Exercise your lordship over
> all the women of my inner apartments, however many they may be, every
> one endowed with virtue. O dark-tressed one! My wives are the finest
> women in the three worlds — yet they will attend upon you, as the
> Apsarās attend upon Goddess Śrī. O graceful one! Enjoy the gems and
> vast wealth I have won from Kubera, the riches of the three worlds,
> and remain happily with me! O divine one! Śrī Rāma is not my equal —
> not in penance, nor in strength, nor in valor, nor in wealth, nor in
> brilliance, nor even in fame!"
---

35.
piba vihara ramasva bhuṅkṣva bhogān dhananicayaṁ pradiśāmi medinīṁ ca |
mayi lala lalane yathāsukhaṁ tvaṁ tvayi ca sametya lalantu bāndhavās te ||

36.
kusumitatarujālasantatāni bhramarayutāni samudratīrajāni |
kanakavimalahārabhūṣitāṅgī vihara mayā saha bhīru kānanāni ||

> **Meaning 35-36:** "Drink, sport, rejoice, and savour every pleasure!
> I will bestow upon you hoards of wealth and the earth itself. Delight
> in me — and united with you, let your kinsmen also rejoice. O shy
> one! Your form adorned with a necklace of pure gold, come wander with
> me through the forests along the seashore, filled with clusters of
> blossoming trees and alive with the hum of bees."
---
