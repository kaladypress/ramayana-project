=== Sarga 25 ===

---
1.
tathā tāsāṁ vadantīnāṁ paruṣaṁ dāruṇaṁ bahu |
rākṣasīnām asaumyānāṁ ruroda janakātmajā ||

> **Meaning 1:** As those fearsome Rākṣasīs continued to speak
> many harsh and cruel words, the daughter of Janaka wept.

---
2.
evam uktā tu vaidehī rākṣasībhirmanasvinī |
uvāca paramatrastā bāṣpagadgadayā girā ||

3.
na mānuṣī rākṣasasya bhāryā bhavitum arhati |
kāmaṁ khādata māṁ sarvā na kariṣyāmi vo vacaḥ ||

> **Meaning 2-3:** Addressed thus by the Rākṣasīs, the
> strong-willed Vaidehī, utterly terrified, spoke in a voice
> choked with tears: "A human woman can never become the wife
> of a Rākṣasa. You may all devour me if you wish — I will
> not obey your words."

---
4.
sā rākṣasī madhyagatā sītā surasutopamā |
na śarma lebhe duḥkhārtā rāvaṇena ca tarjitā ||

5.
vepate smādhikaṁ sītā viśantīvāṅgam ātmanaḥ |
vane yūthaparibhraṣṭā mr̥gī kokairivārditā ||

> **Meaning 4-5:** Surrounded by the Rākṣasīs, Sītā —
> radiant as a daughter of the devatās — found no peace,
> stricken with grief and terrorized by Rāvaṇa. Sītā
> trembled violently, as if shrinking into herself, like a
> doe separated from her herd in the forest, beset by wolves.

---
6.
sā tv aśokasya vipulāṁ śākhām ālambya puṣpitām |
cintayām āsa śokena bhartāraṁ bhagnamānasā ||

7.
sā snāpayantī vipulau stanau netrajalasravaiḥ |
cintayantī na śokasya tadāntam adhigacchati ||

> **Meaning 6-7:** Clinging to a large, blossoming branch of
> the Aśoka tree, her spirit broken with grief, Sītā thought
> of her husband. As tears streamed from her eyes and bathed
> her bosom, she kept brooding but could find no end to her
> sorrow.

---
8.
sā vepamānā patitā pravāte kadalī yathā |
rākṣasīnāṁ bhayatrastā vivarṇavadanābhavat ||

9.
tasyā sā dīrghavipulā vepantyāḥ sītayā tadā |
dadr̥śe kampinī veṇī vyālīva parisarpatī ||

> **Meaning 8-9:** Trembling and fallen like a plantain tree
> in a gale, terrified of the Rākṣasīs, her face lost all
> colour. As Sītā trembled, her long and thick braid, shaking
> along with her, appeared like a serpent slithering about.

---
10.
sā niḥśvasantī duḥkhārtā śokopahatacetanā |
ārtā vyasr̥jad aśrūṇi maithilī vilalāpa ha ||

11.
hā rāmeti ca duḥkhārtā punarhā lakṣmaṇeti ca |
hā śvaśru mama kausalye hā sumitreti bhāvini ||

> **Meaning 10-11:** Sighing deeply, tormented by grief, her
> mind overwhelmed by sorrow, the anguished Maithilī shed
> tears and lamented aloud: "Alas, Rāma! Alas, Lakṣmaṇa!
> Alas, my mother-in-law Kausalyā! Alas, noble Sumitrā!"

---
12.
lokapravādaḥ satyaḥ ayaṁ paṇḍitaiḥ samudāhr̥taḥ |
akāle durlabhaḥ mr̥tyuḥ striyā vā puruṣasya vā ||

13.
yatrāham ābhiḥ krūrābhī rākṣasībhirihārditā |
jīvāmi hīnā rāmeṇa muhūrtam api duḥkhitā ||

> **Meaning 12-13:** "True indeed is that saying uttered by
> the wise: death is impossible to obtain before its
> appointed time, whether for a woman or a man. For here I
> am, tormented by these cruel Rākṣasīs, separated from
> Rāma, afflicted every moment — and yet I live!"

---
14.
eṣālpapuṇyā kr̥paṇā vinaśiṣyāmy anāthavat |
samudramadhye nau pūrṇā vāyuvegairivāhatā ||

15.
bhartāraṁ tam apaśyantī rākṣasīvaśam āgatā |
sīdāmi khalu śokena kūlaṁ toyahataṁ yathā ||

> **Meaning 14-15:** "Wretched and of meagre fortune, I shall
> perish like one without a protector — like a fully laden
> ship battered by furious winds in the midst of the ocean.
> Unable to see my husband, fallen under the power of these
> Rākṣasīs, I am crumbling away with grief, like a
> riverbank eroded by the relentless current."

---
16.
taṁ padmadalapatrākṣaṁ siṁhavikrāntagāminam |
dhanyāḥ paśyanti me nāthaṁ kr̥tajñaṁ priyavādinam ||

> **Meaning 16:** "My husband has eyes like lotus petals, and he walks with the majestic courage of a lion. He is grateful, and he speaks affectionately. Blessed are those who get to see my swami!"

---
17.
sarvathā tena hīnāyā rāmeṇa viditātmanā |
tīṣkṇaṁ viṣam ivāsvādya durlabhaṁ mama jīvitam ||

> **Meaning 17:** "Separated from Śrī Rāma, who is world-renowned for his virtues, it is unbearable for me to go on living, as if I had swallowed a deadly poison"

---
18.
kīdr̥śaṁ tu mayā pāpaṁ purā dehāntare kr̥tam |
yenedaṁ prāpyate duḥkhaṁ mayā ghoraṁ sudāruṇam ||

> **Meaning 18:** "What kind of sin must I have committed in
> a previous birth, that I now endure this dreadful and
> exceedingly terrible suffering?"

---
19.
jīvitaṁ tyaktum icchāmi śokena mahatā vr̥tā |
rākṣasībhiśca rakṣantyā rāmo nāsādyate mayā ||

> **Meaning 19:** "Engulfed by this immense grief, I wish to
> abandon my life. Guarded as I am by these Rākṣasīs,
> Rāma remains beyond my reach."

---
20.
dhig astu khalu mānuṣyaṁ dhig astu paravaśyatām |
na śakyaṁ yat parityaktum ātmacchandena jīvitam ||

> **Meaning 20:** "Curse this human existence! Curse this dependence on another! My life is in the hands of my husband, so I do not have the right to end it. And because human beings have the wisdom to know right from wrong, I cannot simply abandon my life just because I want to."

---
