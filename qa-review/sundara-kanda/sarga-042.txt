=== Sarga 42 ===

---
1.
tataḥ pakṣininādena vr̥kṣabhaṅgasvanena ca |
babhūvustrāsasaṁbhrāntāḥ sarve laṅkānivāsinaḥ ||

> **Meaning 1:** Then, at the cries of the birds and the sound of trees
> breaking, everyone living in Laṅkā was thrown into fear and confusion.

---
2.
vidrutāśca bhayatrastā vinedurmr̥gapakṣuṇaḥ |
rakṣasāṁ ca nimittāni krūrāṇi pratipedire ||

> **Meaning 2:** Terrified animals and birds ran off in every direction, crying
> out. Cruel omens began to appear before the Rākṣasas.

---
3.
tato gatāyāṁ nidrāyāṁ rākṣasyo vikr̥tānanāḥ |
tad vanaṁ dadr̥śurbhagnaṁ taṁ ca vīraṁ mahākapim ||

> **Meaning 3:** Then the hideous-faced Rākṣasīs woke from their sleep. They saw
> the ruined grove and that heroic, mighty Vānara.

---
4.
sa tā dr̥ṣṭva mahābāhuḥ mahāsattvo mahābalaḥ |
cakāra sumahad rūpaṁ rākṣasīnāṁ bhayāvaham ||

> **Meaning 4:** When he saw them, the strong-armed Hanumān, great in spirit and
> great in strength, took on an enormous form that filled the Rākṣasīs with
> dread.

---
5.
tatastaṁ girisaṁkāśam atikāyaṁ mahābalam |
rākṣasyo vānaraṁ dr̥ṣṭvā papracchurjanakātmajām ||

> **Meaning 5:** Seeing that Vānara, huge as a mountain, gigantic and immensely
> strong, the Rākṣasīs questioned Janaka's daughter.

---
6.
kaḥ ayaṁ kasya kuto vāyaṁ kiṁnimittam ihāgataḥ |
kathaṁ tvayā sahānena saṁvādaḥ kr̥ta ity uta ||

> **Meaning 6:** "Who is this? Whose is he, and where has he come from? Why has
> he come here? And how is it that he has been talking with you?

---
7.
ācakṣva no viśālākṣi mā bhūt te subhage bhayam |
saṁvādam asitāpāṅge tvayā kiṁ kr̥tavān ayam ||

> **Meaning 7:** Tell us, wide-eyed one. Don't be afraid, fortunate lady.
> Dark-eyed one, what did he talk about with you?"

---
8.
athābravīt tadā sādhvī sītā sarvāṅgaśobhanā |
rakṣasāṁ kāmarūpāṇāṁ vijñāne mama kā gatiḥ ||

> **Meaning 8:** Then the virtuous Sītā, radiant in every way, said: "How could
> I possibly know about Rākṣasas who can take any form they like?

---
9.
yūyam evāsya jānīta yaḥ ayaṁ yad vā kariṣyati |
ahireva aheḥ pādān vijānāti na saṁśayaḥ ||

> **Meaning 9:** You yourselves know who he is and what he will do. Only a snake
> can see a snake's feet — there's no doubt about that.

---
10.
aham apy asya bhītāsmi nainaṁ jānāmi kaḥ anvayam |
vedmi rākṣasam evainaṁ kāmarūpiṇam āgatam ||

> **Meaning 10:** I'm afraid of him too. I don't know who he is. I only know he
> is some Rākṣasa who has come here in a form of his own choosing."

---
11.
vaidehyā vacanaṁ śrutvā rākṣasyo vidrutā drutam |
sthitāḥ kāścid gatāḥ kāścid rāvaṇāya niveditum ||

> **Meaning 11:** Hearing Vaidehī's words, the Rākṣasīs scattered quickly. Some
> stayed where they were; others went to report to Rāvaṇa.

---
12.
rāvaṇasya samīpe tu rākṣasyo vikr̥tānanāḥ |
virūpaṁ vānaraṁ bhīmam ākhyātum upacakramuḥ ||

> **Meaning 12:** Standing before Rāvaṇa, the hideous-faced Rākṣasīs began to
> describe the misshapen, fearsome Vānara.

---
13.
aśokavanikā madhye rājan bhīmavapuḥ kapiḥ |
sītayā kr̥tasaṁvādaḥ tiṣṭhaty amitavikramaḥ ||

> **Meaning 13:** "King, in the middle of the Aśoka grove there is a Kapi with a
> terrifying body and boundless strength. He has spoken with Sītā and is still
> there.

---
14.
na ca taṁ jānakī sītā hariṁ hariṇalocaṇā |
asmābhirbahudhā pr̥ṣṭā nivedayitum icchati ||

> **Meaning 14:** We asked Janaka's daughter Sītā, the doe-eyed one, about that
> Hari again and again, but she won't tell us anything about him.

---
15.
vāsavasya bhaved dūtaḥ dūto vaiśravaṇasya vā |
preṣito vāpi rāmeṇa sītānveṣaṇakāṅkṣayā ||

> **Meaning 15:** He may be a messenger of Vāsava (Indra), or of Vaiśravaṇa
> (Kubera). Or he may have been sent by Rāma, wanting to find Sītā.

---
16.
tena tvadbhūtarūpeṇa yat tat tava manoharam |
nānāmr̥gagaṇākīrṇaṁ pramr̥ṣṭaṁ pramadāvanam ||

> **Meaning 16:** That creature of extraordinary form has wrecked your lovely
> pleasure garden, which was full of all kinds of animals.

---
17.
na tatra kaścid uddeśaḥ yastena na vināśitaḥ |
yatra sā jānakī sītā sa tena na vināśitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 17:** There is no part of it that he hasn't destroyed — except the
> place where Janaka's daughter Sītā sits. That he has left untouched.

---
18.
jānakīrakṣaṇārthaṁ vā śramād vā nopalabhyate |
atha vā kaḥ śramastasya saiva tenābhirakṣitā ||

> **Meaning 18:** Whether it was to protect Jānakī, or because he got tired, we
> can't tell. But what tiredness could he feel? It must be that he deliberately
> protected her.

---
19.
cārupallavapatrāḍhyaṁ yaṁ sītā svayam āsthitā |
pravr̥ddhaḥ śiṁśapāvr̥kṣaḥ sa ca tenābhirakṣitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 19:** The tall Śiṁśapā tree, thick with lovely shoots and leaves,
> under which Sītā herself sits — he has spared that too.

---
20.
tasyograrūpasyograṁ tvaṁ daṇḍam ājñātum arhasi |
sītā saṁbhāṣitā yena tad vanaṁ ca vināśitam ||

> **Meaning 20:** You should give a harsh punishment to that fierce-looking
> creature who spoke with Sītā and destroyed that grove.

---
21.
manaḥparigr̥hītāṁ tāṁ tava rakṣogaṇeśvara |
kaḥ sītām abhibhāṣeta yo na syāt tyaktajīvitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 21:** Lord of the Rākṣasa hosts, Sītā is the one your heart has
> claimed. Who would dare to speak to her unless he had already given up on his
> life?"

---
22.
rākṣasīnāṁ vacaḥ śrutvā rāvaṇo rākṣaseśvaraḥ |
hutāgiriva jajvāla kopasaṁvartitekṣaṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 22:** Hearing the words of the Rākṣasīs, Rāvaṇa, king of the
> Rākṣasas, blazed up like a fire fed with offerings, his eyes rolling in anger.

---
23.
tasya kruddhasya nētrābhyāṁ prāpatannāsrabiṁdavaḥ |
dīptābhyāmiva dīpābhyāṁ sārciṣaḥ snēhabiṁdavaḥ ||

24.
ātmanaḥ sadr̥śāñ śūrān kiṁkarān nāma rākṣasān |
vyādideśa mahātejā nigrahārthaṁ hanūmataḥ ||

> **Meaning 23-24:** Tears of rage fell from the angry king's eyes, like drops
> of hot oil dripping from two blazing lamps. Then the mighty Rāvaṇa ordered
> Rākṣasas called the Kiṅkaras, heroes equal to himself, to capture Hanumān.

---
25.
teṣām aśītisāhasraṁ kiṁkarāṇāṁ tarasvinām |
niryayurbhavanāt tasmāt kūṭamudgarapāṇayaḥ ||

> **Meaning 25:** Eighty thousand of those swift Kiṅkaras marched out of that
> palace, carrying hammers and iron clubs (kūṭa and mudgara) in their hands.

---
26.
mahodarā mahādaṁṣṭrā ghorarūpā mahābalāḥ |
yuddhābhimanasaḥ sarve hanūmadgrahaṇonmukhāḥ ||

> **Meaning 26:** They were big-bellied, with huge fangs, terrifying to look at
> and enormously strong. All of them were eager for battle and set on seizing
> Hanumān.

---
27.
te kapiṁ taṁ samāsādya toraṇastham avasthitam |
abhipeturmahāvegāḥ pataṅgā iva pāvakam ||

> **Meaning 27:** Reaching the Kapi as he stood on the gateway arch, they rushed
> at him with great speed, like moths flying into a fire.

---
28.
te gadābhirvicitrābhiḥ parighaiḥ kāñcanāṅgadaiḥ |
ājaghnurvānaraśreṣṭhaṁ śarairādityasaṁnibhaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 28:** They struck the best of the Vānaras with ornate maces, with
> iron bars (parigha) bound in gold, and with arrows that shone like the sun.

---
29.
mudgaraiḥ paṭṭiśaiśśūlaiḥ prāsatōmaraśaktibhiḥ |
parivārya hanūmaṁtaṁ sahasā tasthuragrataḥ ||

30.
hanūmān api tejasvī śrīmān parvatasaṁnibhaḥ |
kṣitāv āvidhya lāṅgūlaṁ nanāda ca mahāsvanam ||

> **Meaning 29-30:** With hammers, sharp-edged paṭṭiśas, tridents, darts,
> javelins (tomara), and spears (śakti), they quickly surrounded Hanumān and
> stood in front of him. But the radiant, glorious Hanumān, huge as a mountain,
> lashed his tail on the ground and let out a mighty roar.

---
31.
sa bhūtvā sumahākāyō hanumānmārutātmajaḥ |
dhr̥ṣṭamāsphōṭayāmāsa laṁkāṁ śabdēna pūrayan ||


> **Meaning 31:** Hanumān, the son of Māruta, grew to an enormous size. Boldly
> he slapped his arms, filling Laṅkā with the sound.

---
32.
tasyāsphōṭitaśabdēna mahatā sānunādinā |
pēturvihaṁgā gaganāt uccaiścēdamaghōṣayat ||

> **Meaning 32:** At that loud, echoing slap, birds fell from the sky. Then he
> proclaimed at the top of his voice:

---
33.
jayatyatibalō rāmō lakṣmaṇaśca mahābalaḥ |
rājā jayati sugrīvō rāghavēṇābhipālitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 33:** "Victory to Rāma, the immensely strong! Victory to the mighty
> Lakṣmaṇa! Victory to King Sugrīva, protected by Rāghava!

---
34.
dāsaḥ ahaṁ kōsalēṁdrasya rāmasyākliṣṭa karmaṇaḥ |
hanumān śatrusainyānāṁ nihaṁtā mārutātmajaḥ ||

> **Meaning 34:** I am the servant of Rāma, lord of Kosala, whose deeds are
> never wearisome to him. I am Hanumān, son of Māruta, destroyer of enemy
> armies.

---
35.
na rāvaṇasahasraṁ mē yuddhē pratibalaṁ bhavēt |
śilābhistu praharataḥ pādapaiśca sahasraśaḥ || 35 ||

36.
ardayitvā purīṁ laṁkāṁ abhivādya ca maithilīm |
samr̥ddhārthō gamiṣyāmi miṣatāṁ sarvarakṣasām ||

> **Meaning 35-36:** Not even a thousand Rāvaṇas could match me in battle when I
> strike with rocks and trees by the thousands. I will crush the city of Laṅkā,
> pay my respects to Maithilī, and leave with my mission accomplished, while all
> the Rākṣasas look on."

---
37.
tasya saṁnādaśabdena te abhavan bhayaśaṅkitāḥ |
dadr̥śuśca hanūmantaṁ saṁdhyāmegham ivonnatam ||

> **Meaning 37:** At the sound of his roar, the Kiṅkaras were seized with fear.
> They saw Hanumān towering high, like an evening cloud.

---
38.
svāmisaṁdeśaniḥśaṅkāḥ tataste rākṣasāḥ kapim |
citraiḥ praharaṇairbhīmaiḥ abhipetustatastataḥ ||

> **Meaning 38:** Then, made bold by their master's orders, the Rākṣasas
> attacked the Kapi from every side with all kinds of fearsome weapons.

---
39.
sa taiḥ parivr̥taḥ śūraiḥ sarvataḥ sa mahābalaḥ |
āsasādāyasaṁ bhīmaṁ parighaṁ toraṇāśritam ||

> **Meaning 39:** Surrounded on all sides by those warriors, the mighty hero
> took hold of a terrible iron bar (parigha) fixed to the gateway.

---
40.
sa taṁ parigham ādāya jaghāna rajanīcarān |
sa pannagam ivādāya sphurantaṁ vinatāsutaḥ ||

> **Meaning 40:** Seizing that bar, he struck down the night-roamers, just as
> Vinatā's son (Garuḍa) seizes a writhing serpent.

---
41.
vicacārāmbare vīraḥ parigr̥hya ca mārutiḥ |
sūdayāmāsa vajreṇa daityāniva sahasradṛk ||

> **Meaning 41:** Holding the bar, the heroic son of Māruta moved through the
> sky and cut down the Rākṣasas, as thousand-eyed Indra destroyed the Daityas
> with his thunderbolt (vajra).

---
42.
sa hatvā rākṣasān vīraḥ kiṁkarān mārutātmajaḥ |
yuddhākāṅkṣī punarvīraḥ toraṇaṁ samupasthitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 42:** After killing the Kiṅkara Rākṣasas, the heroic son of Māruta,
> still eager for battle, took his place on the gateway again.

---
43.
tatastasmād bhayān muktāḥ kati cit tatra rākṣasāḥ |
nihatān kiṁkarān sarvān rāvaṇāya nyavedayan ||

> **Meaning 43:** Then a few Rākṣasas who had escaped from that terror reported
> to Rāvaṇa that all the Kiṅkaras had been killed.

---
44.
sa rākṣasānāṁ nihataṁ mahābalaṁ niśamya rājā parivr̥ttalocanaḥ |
samādideśāpratimaṁ parākrame prahastaputraṁ samare sudurjayam ||

> **Meaning 44:** Hearing that the mighty army of Rākṣasas had been killed, the
> king rolled his eyes in fury. He ordered Prahasta's son — matchless in valor
> and almost impossible to defeat in battle — to go out and fight.

---
