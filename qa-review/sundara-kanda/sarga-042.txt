=== Sarga 40 ===

---
1.
tataḥ pakṣininādena vr̥kṣabhaṅgasvanena ca |
babhūvus trāsasaṁbhrāntāḥ sarve laṅkānivāsinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
vidrutāś ca bhayatrastā vinedur mr̥gapakṣuṇaḥ |
rakṣasāṁ ca nimittāni krūrāṇi pratipedire ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tato gatāyāṁ nidrāyāṁ rākṣasyo vikr̥tānanāḥ |
tad vanaṁ dadr̥śur bhagnaṁ taṁ ca vīraṁ mahākapim ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
sa tā dr̥ṣṭva mahābāhur mahāsattvo mahābalaḥ |
cakāra sumahad rūpaṁ rākṣasīnāṁ bhayāvaham ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tatas taṁ girisaṁkāśam atikāyaṁ mahābalam |
rākṣasyo vānaraṁ dr̥ṣṭvā papracchur janakātmajām ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
ko ’yaṁ kasya kuto vāyaṁ kiṁnimittam ihāgataḥ |
kathaṁ tvayā sahānena saṁvādaḥ kr̥ta ity uta ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
ācakṣva no viśālākṣi mā bhūt te subhage bhayam |
saṁvādam asitāpāṅge tvayā kiṁ kr̥tavān ayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
athābravīt tadā sādhvī sītā sarvāṅgaśobhanā |
rakṣasāṁ kāmarūpāṇāṁ vijñāne mama kā gatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
yūyam evāsya jānīta yo ’yaṁ yad vā kariṣyati |
ahir eva aheḥ pādān vijānāti na saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
aham apy asya bhītāsmi nainaṁ jānāmi ko ’nvayam |
vedmi rākṣasam evainaṁ kāmarūpiṇam āgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
vaidehyā vacanaṁ śrutvā rākṣasyo vidrutā drutam |
sthitāḥ kāś cid gatāḥ kāś cid rāvaṇāya niveditum ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
rāvaṇasya samīpe tu rākṣasyo vikr̥tānanāḥ |
virūpaṁ vānaraṁ bhīmam ākhyātum upacakramuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
aśokavanikā madhye rājan bhīmavapuḥ kapiḥ |
sītayā kr̥tasaṁvādas tiṣṭhaty amitavikramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
na ca taṁ jānakī sītā hariṁ hariṇalocaṇā |
asmābhir bahudhā pr̥ṣṭā nivedayitum icchati ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
vāsavasya bhaved dūto dūto vaiśravaṇasya vā |
preṣito vāpi rāmeṇa sītānveṣaṇakāṅkṣayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tena tvadbhūtarūpeṇa yat tat tava manoharam |
nānāmr̥gagaṇākīrṇaṁ pramr̥ṣṭaṁ pramadāvanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
na tatra kaś cid uddeśo yas tena na vināśitaḥ |
yatra sā jānakī sītā sa tena na vināśitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
jānakīrakṣaṇārthaṁ vā śramād vā nopalabhyate |
atha vā kaḥ śramas tasya saiva tenābhirakṣitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
cārupallavapatrāḍhyaṁ yaṁ sītā svayam āsthitā |
pravr̥ddhaḥ śiṁśapāvr̥kṣaḥ sa ca tenābhirakṣitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tasyograrūpasyograṁ tvaṁ daṇḍam ājñātum arhasi |
sītā saṁbhāṣitā yena tad vanaṁ ca vināśitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
manaḥparigr̥hītāṁ tāṁ tava rakṣogaṇeśvara |
kaḥ sītām abhibhāṣeta yo na syāt tyaktajīvitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
rākṣasīnāṁ vacaḥ śrutvā rāvaṇo rākṣaseśvaraḥ |
hutāgir iva jajvāla kopasaṁvartitekṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
ātmanaḥ sadr̥śāñ śūrān kiṁkarān nāma rākṣasān |
vyādideśa mahātejā nigrahārthaṁ hanūmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
teṣām aśītisāhasraṁ kiṁkarāṇāṁ tarasvinām |
niryayur bhavanāt tasmāt kūṭamudgarapāṇayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
mahodarā mahādaṁṣṭrā ghorarūpā mahābalāḥ |
yuddhābhimanasaḥ sarve hanūmadgrahaṇonmukhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
te kapiṁ taṁ samāsādya toraṇastham avasthitam |
abhipetur mahāvegāḥ pataṅgā iva pāvakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
te gadābhir vicitrābhiḥ parighaiḥ kāñcanāṅgadaiḥ |
ājaghnur vānaraśreṣṭhaṁ śarair ādityasaṁnibhaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
hanūmān api tejasvī śrīmān parvatasaṁnibhaḥ |
kṣitāv āvidhya lāṅgūlaṁ nanāda ca mahāsvanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
tasya saṁnādaśabdena te ’bhavan bhayaśaṅkitāḥ |
dadr̥śuś ca hanūmantaṁ saṁdhyāmegham ivonnatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
svāmisaṁdeśaniḥśaṅkās tatas te rākṣasāḥ kapim |
citraiḥ praharaṇair bhīmair abhipetus tatas tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
sa taiḥ parivr̥taḥ śūraiḥ sarvataḥ sa mahābalaḥ |
āsasādāyasaṁ bhīmaṁ parighaṁ toraṇāśritam ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
sa taṁ parigham ādāya jaghāna rajanīcarān ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
sa pannagam ivādāya sphurantaṁ vinatāsutaḥ |
vicacārāmbare vīraḥ parigr̥hya ca mārutiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
sa hatvā rākṣasān vīraḥ kiṁkarān mārutātmajaḥ |
yuddhākāṅkṣī punar vīras toraṇaṁ samupasthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
tatas tasmād bhayān muktāḥ kati cit tatra rākṣasāḥ |
nihatān kiṁkarān sarvān rāvaṇāya nyavedayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
sa rākṣasānāṁ nihataṁ mahābalaṁ; niśamya rājā parivr̥ttalocanaḥ |
samādideśāpratimaṁ parākrame; prahastaputraṁ samare sudurjayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
