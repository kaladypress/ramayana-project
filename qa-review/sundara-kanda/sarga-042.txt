=== Sarga 41 ===

---
1.
tataḥ sa kiṁkarān hatvā hanūmān dhyānam āsthitaḥ |
vanaṁ bhagnaṁ mayā caityaprāsādo na vināśitaḥ |
tasmāt prāsādam apy evam imaṁ vidhvaṁsayāmy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
iti saṁcintya hanumān manasā darśayan balam |
caityaprāsādam āplutya meruśr̥ṅgam ivonnatam |
āruroha hariśreṣṭho hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
saṁpradhr̥ṣya ca durdharṣaś caityaprāsādam unnatam |
hanūmān prajvalam̐l lakṣmyā pāriyātropamo ’bhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
sa bhūtvā tu mahākāyo hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ |
dhr̥ṣṭam āsphoṭayām āsa laṅkāṁ śabdena pūrayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tasyāsphoṭitaśabdena mahatā śrotraghātinā |
petur vihaṁgā gaganād uccaiś cedam aghoṣayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
jayaty atibalo rāmo lakṣmaṇaś ca mahābalaḥ |
rājā jayati sugrīvo rāghaveṇābhipālitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
dāso ’haṁ kosalendrasya rāmasyākliṣṭakarmaṇaḥ |
hanumāñ śatrusainyānāṁ nihantā mārutātmajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
na rāvaṇasahasraṁ me yuddhe pratibalaṁ bhavet |
śilābhis tu praharataḥ pādapaiś ca sahasraśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
ardayitvā purīṁ laṅkām abhivādya ca maithilīm |
samr̥ddhārtho gamiṣyāmi miṣatāṁ sarvarakṣasām ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
evam uktvā vimānasthaś caityasthān haripuṁgavaḥ |
nanāda bhīmanirhrādo rakṣasāṁ janayan bhayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tena śabdena mahatā caityapālāḥ śataṁ yayuḥ |
gr̥hītvā vividhān astrān prāsān khaḍgān paraśvadhān |
visr̥janto mahākṣayā mārutiṁ paryavārayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
āvarta iva gaṅgāyās toyasya vipulo mahān |
parikṣipya hariśreṣṭhaṁ sa babhau rakṣasāṁ gaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tato vātātmajaḥ kruddho bhīmarūpaṁ samāsthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
prāsādasya mahāṁs tasya stambhaṁ hemapariṣkr̥tam |
utpāṭayitvā vegena hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ |
tatas taṁ bhrāmayām āsa śatadhāraṁ mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa rākṣasaśataṁ hatvā vajreṇendra ivāsurān |
antarikṣasthitaḥ śrīmān idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
mādr̥śānāṁ sahasrāṇi visr̥ṣṭāni mahātmanām |
balināṁ vānarendrāṇāṁ sugrīvavaśavartinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
śataiḥ śatasahasraiś ca koṭībhir ayutair api |
āgamiṣyati sugrīvaḥ sarveṣāṁ vo niṣūdanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
neyam asti purī laṅkā na yūyaṁ na ca rāvaṇaḥ |
yasmād ikṣvākunāthena baddhaṁ vairaṁ mahātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
