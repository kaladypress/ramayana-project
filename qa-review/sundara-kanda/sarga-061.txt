=== Sarga 61 ===

---
1.
tato jāmbavato vākyam agr̥hṇanta vanaukasaḥ |
aṅgadapramukhā vīrā hanūmāṁś ca mahākapiḥ ||

> **Meaning 1:** Then the heroic forest-dwellers, led by Aṅgada, together with
> the great Kapi Hanumān, accepted Jāmbavān's advice.

---
2.
prītimantas tataḥ sarve vāyuputrapuraḥsarāḥ |
mahendrāgraṁ parityajya pupluvuḥ plavagarṣabhāḥ ||

> **Meaning 2:** Then all those bulls among the Plavagas, delighted, with
> Vāyuputra in the lead, left the summit of Mahendra and leapt into the sky.

---
3.
merumandarasaṁkāśā mattā iva mahāgajāḥ |
chādayanta ivākāśaṁ mahākāyā mahābalāḥ ||

> **Meaning 3:** Huge in body and immense in strength, like Meru and Mandara,
> like great elephants in rut, they seemed to cover the whole sky.

---
4.
sabhājyamānaṁ bhūtais tam ātmavantaṁ mahābalam |
hanūmantaṁ mahāvegaṁ vahanta iva dr̥ṣṭibhiḥ ||

> **Meaning 4:** All beings honored Hanumān — self-possessed, mighty, and
> tremendously swift — and the Vānaras kept their eyes fixed on him, as if
> carrying him along with their gaze.

---
5.
rāghave cārthanirvr̥ttiṁ bhartuś ca paramaṁ yaśaḥ |
samādhāya samr̥ddhārthāḥ karmasiddhibhir unnatāḥ ||

> **Meaning 5:** They were set on bringing Rāghava's purpose to fulfillment and
> winning great glory for their master. Their aim was achieved, and they were
> lifted high by the success of their mission.

---
6.
priyākhyānonmukhāḥ sarve sarve yuddhābhinandinaḥ |
sarve rāmapratīkāre niścitārthā manasvinaḥ ||

> **Meaning 6:** All of them were eager to share the good news, all of them were
> eager for battle, and all of them, high-minded as they were, were determined
> to help Rāma strike back.

---
7.
plavamānāḥ kham āplutya tatas te kānanaukṣakaḥ |
nandanopamam āsedur vanaṁ drumalatāyutam ||

> **Meaning 7:** Leaping and soaring through the sky, those forest-dwellers
> reached a forest like Nandana, full of trees and creepers.

---
8.
yat tan madhuvanaṁ nāma sugrīvasyābhirakṣitam |
adhr̥ṣyaṁ sarvabhūtānāṁ sarvabhūtamanoharam ||

> **Meaning 8:** It was the forest called Madhuvana, protected by Sugrīva. No
> being could trespass on it, and it delighted the heart of every being.

---
9.
yad rakṣati mahāvīryaḥ sadā dadhimukhaḥ kapiḥ |
mātulaḥ kapimukhyasya sugrīvasya mahātmanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 9:** The mighty Kapi Dadhimukha, maternal uncle of the great-souled
> Sugrīva, chief of the Kapis, always guarded it.

---
10.
te tad vanam upāgamya babhūvuḥ paramotkaṭāḥ |
vānarā vānarendrasya manaḥkāntatamaṁ mahat ||

> **Meaning 10:** When the Vānaras reached that great forest, the place dearest
> to the heart of the king of the Vānaras, they became wildly excited.

---
11.
tatas te vānarā hr̥ṣṭā dr̥ṣṭvā madhuvanaṁ mahat |
kumāram abhyayācanta madhūni madhupiṅgalāḥ ||

> **Meaning 11:** Then the joyful Vānaras, tawny as honey, saw the great
> Madhuvana and begged the prince for honey.

---
12.
tataḥ kumāras tān vr̥ddhāñ jāmbavatpramukhān kapīn |
anumānya dadau teṣāṁ nisargaṁ madhubhakṣaṇe ||

> **Meaning 12:** The prince first took the consent of the elders led by
> Jāmbavān, and then gave them permission to eat the honey.

---
13.
te nisṛṣṭāḥ kumāreṇa dhīmatā vālisūnunā |
harayas tān apadyanta drumān madhukarākulān ||

> **Meaning 13:** Given leave by the wise prince, Vāli's son, the Haris went to
> the trees that were swarming with bees.
---

14.
bhakṣayantas tathaivānye mūlāni ca phalāni ca |
jagmuḥ praharṣaṁ te sarve babhūvuś ca madotkaṭāḥ ||

> **Meaning 14:** Others ate roots and fruits as well. All of them were thrilled
> and became wildly drunk.
---

15.
tataś cānumatāḥ sarve saṁprahr̥ṣṭā vanaukasaḥ |
muditāś ca tatas te ca pranr̥tyanti tatas tataḥ ||

> **Meaning 15:** Now that they had permission, all the forest-dwellers were
> overjoyed, and in their delight they danced about here and there.

---
16.
gāyanti ke cit praṇamanti ke cin; nr̥tyanti ke cit prahasanti ke cit |
patanti ke cid vicaranti ke cit; plavanti ke cit pralapanti ke cit ||

> **Meaning 16:** Some sang, some bowed, some danced, some laughed. Some fell
> down, some wandered about, some leapt, some babbled.

---
17.
parasparaṁ kēcidupāśrayaṁtē
parasparaṁ kēcidupākramaṁtē |
parasparaṁ kēcidupabruvaṁtē
parasparaṁ kēcidupāramaṁtē ||

> **Meaning 17:** Some leaned on one another, some went at one another, some
> chattered with one another, some played with one another.
---

18.
drumāddrumaṁ kēcidabhidravaṁtē
kṣitau nagāgrānnipataṁti kēcit |
mahītalāt kēcidudīrṇavēgāḥ
mahādrumāgrāṇyabhisaṁpataṁti ||

> **Meaning 18:** Some rushed from tree to tree, some dropped from the treetops
> to the ground. Some, bursting with speed, shot up from the ground to the tops
> of great trees.

---
19.
gāyaṁtamanyaḥ prahasannupaiti
hasaṁtamanyaḥ prarudannupaiti |
rudaṁtamanyaḥ praṇudannupaiti
nudaṁtamanyaḥ praṇadannupaiti ||

> **Meaning 19:** One came laughing up to another who was singing; another came
> weeping up to one who was laughing. Another came shoving one who was weeping,
> and another came roaring at one who was shoving.
---

20.
samākulaṁ tatkapisainyamāsīt
madhuprasānōtkaṭa sattvacēṣṭam |
na cātra kaścinna babhūva mattō
na cātra kaścinna babhūva tr̥ptaḥ ||

> **Meaning 20:** That Kapi army was in total uproar, their behavior wild and
> reckless from drinking honey. There was no one there who was not drunk, and no
> one who was not fully satisfied.

---
21.
tatō vanaṁ tatparibhakṣyamāṇam
drumāṁśca vidhvaṁsitapatrapuṣpān |
samīkṣya kōpāddhadhivakranāmā
nivārayāmāsa kapiḥ kapīṁstān ||

> **Meaning 21:** Then the Kapi named Dadhimukha saw the forest being devoured
> and the trees stripped of their leaves and flowers. In anger he tried to stop
> those Kapis.
---

22.
sa taiḥ pravr̥ddhaiḥ paribhartsyamānō
vanasya gōptā harivīravr̥ddhaḥ |
cakāra bhūyō matimugratējāḥ
vanasya rakṣāṁ prati vānarēbhyaḥ ||

> **Meaning 22:** Though those swaggering Vānaras threatened him, the guardian
> of the forest — an aged Hari-hero of fierce energy — once again made up his
> mind to protect the forest from them.
---

23.
uvāca kāṁścit paruṣāṇi dhr̥ṣṭaṁ
asakta manyāṁśca talairjaghāna |
samētya kaiścit kalahaṁ cakāra
tathaiva sāmnōpajagāma kāṁścit ||

> **Meaning 23:** To some he spoke harshly and boldly; others he struck with his
> palms without holding back. He got into fights with some, and with others he
> tried gentle persuasion.
---

24.
sa tairmadātsaṁparivārya vākyaiḥ
balāccha tēna prativāryamāṇaiḥ |
pradharṣita styaktabhayai ssamētya
prakr̥ṣyatē cāpyanavēkṣya dōṣam ||

> **Meaning 24:** He tried to hold them back with words and with force, but in
> their drunkenness they surrounded him. Losing all fear, they came at him
> together, knocked him down, and dragged him around, with no thought for how
> wrong it was.
---

25.
nakhaistudaṁtō daśanairdaśaṁtaḥ
talaiśca pādaiśca samāpayaṁtaḥ |
madātkapiṁ taṁ kapaya ssamagrāḥ
mahāvanaṁ nirviṣayaṁ ca cakruḥ ||

> **Meaning 25:** Scratching him with their nails, biting him with their teeth,
> and pounding him with their palms and feet, all the Kapis in their drunkenness
> nearly finished that Kapi off — and they stripped the great forest bare.
---
