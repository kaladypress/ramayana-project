=== Sarga 42 ===

---
1.
saṁdiṣṭo rākṣasendreṇa prahastasya suto balī |
jambumālī mahādaṁṣṭro nirjagāma dhanurdharaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
raktamālyāmbaradharaḥ sragvī rucirakuṇḍalaḥ |
mahān vivr̥ttanayanaś caṇḍaḥ samaradurjayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
dhanuḥ śakradhanuḥ prakhyaṁ mahad rucirasāyakam |
visphārayāṇo vegena vajrāśanisamasvanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tasya visphāraghoṣeṇa dhanuṣo mahatā diśaḥ |
pradiśaś ca nabhaś caiva sahasā samapūryata ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
rathena kharayuktena tam āgatam udīkṣya saḥ |
hanūmān vegasaṁpanno jaharṣa ca nanāda ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
taṁ toraṇaviṭaṅkasthaṁ hanūmantaṁ mahākapim |
jambumālī mahābāhur vivyādha niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
ardhacandreṇa vadane śirasy ekena karṇinā |
bāhvor vivyādha nārācair daśabhis taṁ kapīśvaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tasya tac chuśubhe tāmraṁ śareṇābhihataṁ mukham |
śaradīvāmbujaṁ phullaṁ viddhaṁ bhāskararaśminā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
cukopa bāṇābhihato rākṣasasya mahākapiḥ |
tataḥ pārśve ’tivipulāṁ dadarśa mahatīṁ śilām ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tarasā tāṁ samutpāṭya cikṣepa balavad balī |
tāṁ śarair daśabhiḥ kruddhas tāḍayām āsa rākṣasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
vipannaṁ karma tad dr̥ṣṭvā hanūmāṁś caṇḍavikramaḥ |
sālaṁ vipulam utpāṭya bhrāmayām āsa vīryavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
bhrāmayantaṁ kapiṁ dr̥ṣṭvā sālavr̥kṣaṁ mahābalam |
cikṣepa subahūn bāṇāñ jambumālī mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sālaṁ caturbhir ciccheda vānaraṁ pañcabhir bhuje |
urasy ekena bāṇena daśabhis tu stanāntare ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sa śaraiḥ pūritatanuḥ krodhena mahatā vr̥taḥ |
tam eva parighaṁ gr̥hya bhrāmayām āsa vegitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
ativego ’tivegena bhrāmayitvā balotkaṭaḥ |
parighaṁ pātayām āsa jambumāler mahorasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tasya caiva śiro nāsti na bāhū na ca jānunī |
na dhanur na ratho nāśvās tatrādr̥śyanta neṣavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
sa hatas tarasā tena jambumālī mahārathaḥ |
papāta nihato bhūmau cūrṇitāṅgavibhūṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
jambumāliṁ ca nihataṁ kiṁkarāṁś ca mahābalān |
cukrodha rāvaṇaḥ śrutvā kopasaṁraktalocanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
sa roṣasaṁvartitatāmralocanaḥ; prahastaputre nihate mahābale |
amātyaputrān ativīryavikramān; samādideśāśu niśācareśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
