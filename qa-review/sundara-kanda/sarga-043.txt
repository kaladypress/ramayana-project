=== Sarga 43 ===

---
1.
tataḥ sa kiṁkarān hatvā hanūmān dhyānam āsthitaḥ |
vanaṁ bhagnaṁ mayā caityaprāsādo na vināśitaḥ ||



> **Meaning 1:** After killing the Kiṅkaras, Hanumān stopped to think: "I've
> wrecked the grove, but I haven't destroyed the Caitya palace.

---
2.
tasmāt prāsādam apy evam imaṁ vidhvaṁsayāmy aham |
iti saṁcintya hanumān manasā darśayan balam ||

3.
caityaprāsādam āplutya meruśr̥ṅgam ivonnatam |
āruroha hariśreṣṭhaḥ hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ ||

> **Meaning 2-3:** So I'll tear down this palace too." With that thought,
> Hanumān, best of the Haris, decided to show his strength. He leapt onto the
> Caitya palace, which rose high like the peak of Meru, and climbed up it —
> Hanumān, son of Māruta.

---
4.
āruhya girisaṁkāśaṁ prāsādaṁ hariyūthapaḥ |
babhau sa sumahātējāḥ pratisūrya ivōditaḥ ||

> **Meaning 4:** Having climbed the palace, which looked like a mountain,
> Hanumān, leader of the Hari troops, shone with immense splendor, like a second
> sun risen in the sky.

---
5.  
saṁpradhr̥ṣya ca durdharṣaḥ caityaprāsādam unnatam |
hanūmān prajvalam̐l lakṣmyā pāriyātropamaḥ abhavat ||

> **Meaning 5:** Hanumān, unstoppable, overpowered the towering Caitya palace.
> Blazing with radiance, he stood there like the Pāriyātra mountain.

---
6.
sa bhūtvā tu mahākāyaḥ hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ |
dhr̥ṣṭam āsphoṭayām āsa laṅkāṁ śabdena pūrayan ||

> **Meaning 6:** Then Hanumān, son of Māruta, grew to a huge size. He slapped
> his arms boldly and filled all of Laṅkā with the sound.

---
7.
tasyāsphoṭitaśabdena mahatā śrotraghātinā |
peturvihaṁgā gaganād uccaiścedam aghoṣayat ||

> **Meaning 7:** That loud, ear-splitting slap made birds fall from the sky.
> Then he shouted these words in a booming voice:

---
8.
astravijjayatāṁ rāmaḥ lakṣmaṇaśca mahābalaḥ |
rājā jayati sugrīvaḥ rāghaveṇābhipālitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 8:** "Victory to Rāma, the master of weapons, and to mighty
> Lakṣmaṇa! Victory to King Sugrīva, who is protected by Rāghava!

---
9.
dāsaḥ ahaṁ kosalendrasya rāmasyākliṣṭakarmaṇaḥ |
hanumāñ śatrusainyānāṁ nihantā mārutātmajaḥ ||

> **Meaning 9:** I am the servant of Rāma, king of Kosala, whose deeds are never
> tired or tainted. I am Hanumān, son of Māruta, destroyer of enemy armies.

---
10.
na rāvaṇasahasraṁ me yuddhe pratibalaṁ bhavet |
śilābhistu praharataḥ pādapaiśca sahasraśaḥ ||

> **Meaning 10:** Even a thousand Rāvaṇas could not match me in battle, as I
> strike with rocks and with thousands of trees.

---
11.
ardayitvā purīṁ laṅkām abhivādya ca maithilīm |
samr̥ddhārtho gamiṣyāmi miṣatāṁ sarvarakṣasām ||

> **Meaning 11:** I will crush the city of Laṅkā, bow to Maithilī, and leave
> with my purpose fulfilled, while all the Rākṣasas look on."

---
12.
evam uktvā vimānasthaḥ caityasthān haripuṁgavaḥ |
nanāda bhīmanirhrādaḥ rakṣasāṁ janayan bhayam ||

> **Meaning 12:** Having said this from atop the palace, Hanumān, bull among the
> Plavaṅgamas, roared in a terrifying voice that struck fear into the Rākṣasas.

---
13.
tena śabdena mahatā caityapālāḥ śataṁ yayuḥ |
gr̥hītvā vividhān astrān prāsān khaḍgān paraśvadhān ||

14.
visr̥janto mahākṣayā mārutiṁ paryavārayan |
tē gadābhirvicitrābhiḥ parighaiḥ kāṁcanāṁgadaiḥ ||
> **Meaning 13-14:** At that great roar, a hundred guards of the Caitya palace
> rushed out. They carried many kinds of weapons: spears, swords and axes. With
> fierce eyes they hurled them and surrounded Māruti, striking with colorful
> maces and iron clubs studded with gold.

---
15.
ājaghnurvānaraśrēṣṭhaṁ bāṇaiścādityasannibhaiḥ |
āvarta iva gaṅgāyāḥ toyasya vipulo mahān ||


> **Meaning 15:** They struck the best of the Vānaras with arrows that blazed
> like the sun. They circled him like a huge, swirling whirlpool in the waters
> of the Gaṅgā.

---
16.
parikṣipya hariśreṣṭhaṁ sa babhau rakṣasāṁ gaṇaḥ |
tato vātātmajaḥ kruddhaḥ bhīmarūpaṁ samāsthitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 16:** The mass of Rākṣasas, closing in around Hariśreṣṭha, looked
> magnificent. Then Vātātmaja flew into a rage and took on a terrifying form.

---
17.
prāsādasya mahāṁstasya stambhaṁ hemapariṣkr̥tam |
utpāṭayitvā vegena hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ ||


18.
tatastaṁ bhrāmayām āsa śatadhāraṁ mahābalaḥ |
tatra cāgnissamabhavat prāsādaścāpyadahyata || 18 ||

> **Meaning 17-18:** Hanumān, son of Māruta, tore a huge pillar decorated with
> gold out of that palace in one forceful pull. Mighty Hanumān whirled it
> around, a hundred edges flashing. Fire broke out, and the palace began to
> burn.

---
19.
dahyamānaṁ tatō dr̥ṣṭvā prāsādaṁ hariyūthapaḥ
sa rākṣasaśataṁ hatvā vajreṇendra ivāsurān |
antarikṣasthitaḥ śrīmān idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

> **Meaning 19:** Seeing the palace burning, Hanumān, leader of the Hari troops,
> killed those hundred Rākṣasas, as Indra kills Asuras with his thunderbolt.
> Then, radiant in the sky, he spoke these words:

---
20.
mādr̥śānāṁ sahasrāṇi visr̥ṣṭāni mahātmanām ||

21.
balināṁ vānarendrāṇāṁ sugrīvavaśavartinām |
aṭaṁti vasudhāṁ kr̥tsnāṁ vayamanyē ca vānarāḥ || 21 ||

> **Meaning 20-21:** "Thousands of great and powerful Vānara chiefs like me have
> been sent out, living under Sugrīva's command. We and many other Vānaras are
> roaming the whole earth.

---
22.
daśanāgabalāḥ kēcit kēciddaśaguṇōttarāḥ |
kēcinnāgasahasrasya babhūvustulyavikramāḥ ||

saṁti caughabalāḥ kēcit kēcidvāyubalōpamāḥ |
apramēyabalāścānyē tatrāsan hariyūthapāḥ || 23 ||

> **Meaning 22-23:** Some have the strength of ten elephants, some ten times more.
> Some match a thousand elephants in valor. Some are as strong as a flood, and
> some as strong as the wind. Others among those Hari leaders have strength
> beyond measure.

---
24.
īdr̥gvidhaistu haribhiḥ vr̥tō daṁtanakhāyudhaiḥ |
śatai śśatasahasraiśca kōṭībhirayutairapi ||

> **Meaning 24:** Surrounded by Haris like these, armed with teeth and claws, in
> hundreds, thousands, lakhs, crores and tens of thousands,

---
25.
āgamiṣyati sugrīvaḥ sarvēṣāṁ vō niṣūdanaḥ |
nēyamasti purī laṁkā na yūyaṁ na ca rāvaṇaḥ |
yasmādikṣvākunāthēna baddhaṁ vairaṁ mahātmanā || 25 ||

> **Meaning 25:** Sugrīva will come, and he will be the end of every one of you.
> This city of Laṅkā will not remain, nor will you, nor Rāvaṇa — because the
> great ruler of the Ikṣvākus has declared this feud."
