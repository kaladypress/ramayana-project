=== Sarga 56 ===

---
1.
tatas tu śiṁśapāmūle jānakīṁ paryavasthitām |
abhivādyābravīd diṣṭyā paśyāmi tvām ihākṣatām ||

> **Meaning 1:** Then Hanumān went to Jānakī, who was sitting at the foot of the
> Śiṁśapā tree. He bowed to her and said, "Thank heaven I see you here
> unharmed."

---
2.
tatas taṁ prasthitaṁ sītā vīkṣamāṇā punaḥ punaḥ |
bhartr̥snehānvitaṁ vākyaṁ hanūmantam abhāṣata ||

> **Meaning 2:** As he was about to leave, Sītā looked at him again and again
> and spoke to Hanumān, her words full of love for her husband.

---
3.
tvaṁ manyase tāta vasaikāhamihānagha |
kvacit susaṁvṛte deśe viśrāntaḥ śvo gamiṣyasi ||

> **Meaning 3:** "Dear one, sinless one, if you think it right, stay here for
> one day. Rest in some well-hidden place and leave tomorrow.

---
4.
mama caivālpabhāgyāyāḥ sānnidhyāttava vānara |
śokasyāsyāprameyasya muhūrtaṁ syād api kṣayaḥ ||

> **Meaning 4:** Vānara, unlucky as I am, having you near me might ease this
> boundless grief of mine, even if only for a moment.

---
5.
gate hi hariśārdūla punaḥ samprāptaye tvayi |
prāṇeṣvapi na viśvāso mama vānarapuṅgava ||

> **Meaning 5:** Once you leave, Hariśārdūla, best of Vānaras, I can't be sure
> I'll even stay alive until you come back.

---
6.
adarśanaṁ ca te vīra bhūyo māṁ tāpayiṣyati |
duḥkhād duḥkhataraṁ prāptāṁ durmanāṁ śokakarśitām ||

> **Meaning 6:** Hero, not seeing you again will torment me. I've gone from
> sorrow to deeper sorrow, and I'm already low in spirit and worn down by grief.

---
7.
ayaṁ tu vīra sandehastiṣṭhatīva mamāgrataḥ |
sumahāṁstvatsahāyeṣu haryṛkṣeṣu mahābala ||

> **Meaning 7:** But, mighty hero, one great doubt stands before me about your
> allies, the Haris and Ṛkṣas.

---
8.
kathaṁ nu khalu duṣpāraṁ santariṣyanti sāgaram |
tāni haryṛkṣasainyāni tau vā naravarātmajau ||

> **Meaning 8:** How will those armies of Haris and Ṛkṣas, or those two sons of
> the best of men, ever cross this ocean that is so hard to cross?

---
9.
trayāṇāmeva bhūtānāṁ sāgarasyāsya laṅghane |
śaktiḥ syādvainateyasya tava vā mārutasya vā ||

> **Meaning 9:** Only three beings have the power to leap across this ocean:
> Vainateya, you, or Māruta, the wind.

---
10.
tadatra kāryanirbandhe samutpanne durāsade |
kiṁ paśyasi samādhānaṁ tvaṁ hi kāryaviśāradaḥ ||

> **Meaning 10:** So, with this task facing such a hard obstacle, what solution
> do you see? You're the one who knows how to get things done.

---
11.
kāmam asya tvam evaikaḥ kāryasya parisādhane |
paryāptaḥ paravīraghna yaśasyas te balodayaḥ ||

> **Meaning 11:** Of course, slayer of enemy heroes, you alone are enough to
> accomplish this task, and the strength that rises in you brings you glory.

---
12.
śaraissu saṁkulāṁ kr̥tvā laṁkāṁ parabalārdanaḥ |
māṁ nayēdyadi kākutsthaḥ tattasya sadr̥śaṁ bhavēt ||

> **Meaning 12:** But if Kākutstha, crusher of enemy armies, were to cover Laṅkā
> with his arrows and take me away himself, that would be worthy of him.


13.
tad yathā tasya vikrāntam anurūpaṁ mahātmanaḥ |
bhavaty āhavaśūrasya tattvam evopapādaya ||

> **Meaning 13:** So make sure it happens in a way that matches the valor of
> that great-souled hero of battle."

---
14.
tad arthopahitaṁ vākyaṁ praśritaṁ hetusaṁhitam |
niśamya hanumāṁs tasyā vākyam uttaram abravīt ||

> **Meaning 14:** Hearing her words, full of meaning, gentle, and well reasoned,
> Hanumān gave her his reply.

---
15.
devi haryṛkṣasainyānām īśvaraḥ plavatāṁ varaḥ |
sugrīvaḥ sattvasampannas tavārthe kṛtaniścayaḥ ||

> **Meaning 15:** "Devī, Sugrīva, lord of the armies of Haris and Ṛkṣas and best
> among the leapers, is full of courage and has firmly resolved to act for your
> sake.

---
16.
sa vānarasahasrāṇāṁ koṭībhirabhisaṁvṛtaḥ |
kṣiprameṣyati vaidehi sugrīvaḥ plavagādhipaḥ ||

> **Meaning 16:** Vaidehī, Sugrīva, king of the Plavagas, will come here
> quickly, surrounded by thousands of crores of Vānaras.

---
17.
tau ca vīrau naravarau sahitau rāmalakṣmaṇau |
āgamya nagarīṁ laṅkāṁ sāyakairvidhamiṣyataḥ ||

> **Meaning 17:** And those two heroes, the best of men, Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa, will
> come here together to the city of Laṅkā and shatter it with their arrows.

---
18.
sagaṇaṁ rāvaṇaṁ hatvā nacirādraghunandanaḥ |
tvāmādāya varārohe svāṁ purīṁ prati yāsyati ||

> **Meaning 18:** Before long, Raghunandana will kill Rāvaṇa along with all his
> followers, and then, graceful one, he will take you back with him to his own
> city.

---
19.
samāśvasihi bhadraṁ te bhava tvaṁ kālakāṅkṣiṇī |
kṣipraṁ drakṣyasi rāmeṇa nihataṁ rāvaṇaṁ raṇe ||

> **Meaning 19:** Take heart, and may all be well with you. Wait for the right
> time. Soon you will see Rāvaṇa killed by Rāma in battle.

---
20.
nihate rākṣasendre ca saputrāmātyabāndhave |
tvaṁ sameṣyasi rāmeṇa śaśāṅkeneva rohiṇī ||

> **Meaning 20:** Once the king of the Rākṣasas has been killed, along with his
> sons, ministers, and relatives, you will be reunited with Rāma, just as Rohiṇī
> is with the moon.

---
21.
kṣipram eṣyati kākutstho haryr̥kṣapravarair vr̥taḥ |
yas te yudhi vijityārīñ śokaṁ vyapanayiṣyati ||

> **Meaning 21:** Kākutstha will come soon, surrounded by the foremost of the
> Haris and Ṛkṣas. He will defeat his enemies in battle and take away your
> grief."

---
22.
evam āśvāsya vaidehīṁ hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ |
gamanāya matiṁ kr̥tvā vaidehīm abhyavādayat ||

> **Meaning 22:** Having comforted Vaidehī in this way, Hanumān, son of Māruta,
> made up his mind to leave and bowed to Vaidehī.

---
23.
rākṣasān pravarān hatvā nāma viśrāvya cātmanaḥ |
samāśvāsya ca vaidehīṁ darśayitvā paraṁ balam ||

> **Meaning 23:** He had killed the leading Rākṣasas, made his own name known,
> comforted Vaidehī, and shown his supreme strength.

---
24.
nagarīm ākulāṁ kṛtvā vañcayitvā ca rāvaṇam |
darśayitvā balaṁ ghoraṁ vaidehīm abhivādya ca ||

> **Meaning 24:** He had thrown the city into chaos, outwitted Rāvaṇa, displayed
> his fearsome power, and bowed to Vaidehī.

---
25.
pratigantuṁ manaścakre punar madhyena sāgaram |
tataḥ sa kapiśārdūlaḥ svāmisaṁdarśanotsukaḥ ||


> **Meaning 25:** Now he decided to go back across the middle of the ocean. Then
> that Kapiśārdūla, eager to see his master,

---
26.
āruroha giriśreṣṭham ariṣṭam arimardanaḥ |
tuṅgapadmakajuṣṭābhir nīlābhir vanarājibhiḥ ||


> **Meaning 26:** climbed Ariṣṭa, the finest of mountains — he, the crusher of
> enemies. The mountain was covered with dark-blue stretches of forest, thick
> with tall Padmaka trees.

---
27.
sottarīyam ivāmbhodaiḥ śṛṅgāntaravilambibhiḥ |
bodhyamānam iva prītyā divākarakaraiḥ śubhaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 27:** The clouds hanging between its peaks looked like an upper
> cloth draped over it. The bright rays of the sun seemed to be waking it
> gently, with affection.

---
28.
unmiṣantam ivādhūtaiḥ locanair iva dhātubhiḥ |
toyaughanisvanair mandraiḥ prādhītam iva parvatam ||

> **Meaning 28:** Its streaks of minerals, like eyes, seemed to be opening. With
> the deep roar of its rushing waters, the mountain seemed to be reciting the
> Vedas.

---

29.
pragītam iva vispaṣṭaiḥ nānāprasravaṇasvanaiḥ |
devadārubhir atyuccaiḥ ūrdhvabāhum iva sthitam ||

> **Meaning 29:** With the clear sounds of its many springs, it seemed to be
> singing. With its towering Devadāru trees, it seemed to stand with its arms
> raised high.

---
30.
prapātajalanirghoṣaiḥ prākruṣṭam iva sarvataḥ |
vepamānam iva śyāmaiḥ kampamānaiḥ śaradvanaiḥ ||

31.
veṇubhir mārutoddhūtaiḥ kūjantam iva kīcakaiḥ |
niśvasantam ivāmarṣāt ghorair āśīviṣottamaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 30-31:** With the thunder of its waterfalls, it seemed to be crying
> out on every side. With its dark autumn forests swaying, it seemed to tremble.
> With its Kīcaka bamboos whistling in the wind, it seemed to be humming. With
> its fearsome, deadly serpents, it seemed to be hissing in anger.

---
32.
nīhārakṛtagambhīraiḥ dhyāyantam iva gahvaraiḥ |
meghapādanibhaiḥ pādaiḥ prakrāntam iva sarvataḥ ||

> **Meaning 32:** With its caves made deep and dim by mist, it seemed lost in
> meditation. With its foothills, like the feet of clouds, it seemed to be
> striding out in every direction.

---
33.
jṛmbhamāṇam ivākāśe śikharair abhramālibhiḥ |
kūṭaiśca bahudhā kīrṇaiḥ śobhitaṁ bahukandharaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 33:** With its cloud-wreathed peaks, it seemed to be yawning into
> the sky. It was beautiful with summits scattered everywhere and with many
> caves.

---
34.
sālatālāśvakarṇaiś ca vaṁśaiś ca bahubhir vr̥tam |
latāvitānair vitataiḥ puṣpavadbhir alaṁkr̥tam ||


> **Meaning 34:** It was surrounded by Sāla, Tāla, and Aśvakarṇa trees and many
> bamboos, and adorned with wide canopies of flowering creepers.

--- 

35.
nānāmr̥gagaṇākīrṇaṁ dhātuniṣyandabhūṣitam |
bahuprasravaṇopetaṁ śilāsaṁcayasaṁkaṭam ||

> **Meaning 35:** It was crowded with herds of many kinds of animals, adorned
> with flowing streams of minerals, full of springs, and packed with heaps of
> rock.

---
36.
maharṣiyakṣagandharvakiṁnaroragasevitam |
latāpādapasaṁbādhaṁ siṁhākulitakandaram ||


> **Meaning 36:** Maharṣis, Yakṣas, Gandharvas, Kinnaras, and Uragas visited it.
> It was dense with creepers and trees, and lions filled its caves.

---
37.
vyāghrasaṁghasamākīrṇaṁ svādumūlaphaladrumam |
tam ārurohātibalaḥ parvataṁ plavagottamaḥ |
rāmadarśanaśīghreṇa praharṣeṇābhicoditaḥ ||

> **Meaning 37:** Tigers roamed it in packs, and its trees bore sweet roots and
> fruits. That was the mountain the immensely strong Plavagottama climbed,
> driven by joy and eager to see Rāma quickly.

---
38.
tena pādatalākrāntā ramyeṣu girisānuṣu |
saghoṣāḥ samaśīryanta śilāś cūrṇīkr̥tās tataḥ ||

> **Meaning 38:** As his feet pressed down on its lovely slopes, the rocks
> crumbled with a roar and were ground to powder.

---
39.
sa tam āruhya śailendraṁ vyavardhata mahākapiḥ |
dakṣiṇād uttaraṁ pāraṁ prārthayam̐l lavaṇāmbhasaḥ ||

> **Meaning 39:** Having climbed that king of mountains, the great Kapi grew in
> size, longing to reach the northern shore of the salt ocean from the southern
> one.

---
40.
adhiruhya tato vīraḥ parvataṁ pavanātmajaḥ |
dadarśa sāgaraṁ bhīmaṁ mīnoraganiṣevitam ||

> **Meaning 40:** From the top of the mountain, the hero, son of Pavana, looked
> out at the fearsome ocean, home to fish and serpents.

---
41.
sa māruta ivākāśaṁ mārutasyātmasaṁbhavaḥ |
prapede hariśārdūlo dakṣiṇād uttarāṁ diśam ||

> **Meaning 41:** Like the wind itself moving through the sky, Hariśārdūla, born
> of Māruta, set out from the south toward the north.

---
42.
sa tadā pīḍitas tena kapinā parvatottamaḥ ||

> **Meaning 42:** At that moment, the great mountain, pressed down by the Kapi,

---
43.
rarāsa saha tair bhūtaiḥ prāviśad vasudhātalam ||
kampamānaiś ca śikharaiḥ patadbhir api ca drumaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 43:** roared along with the creatures living on it and sank into the
> earth, its peaks shaking and its trees crashing down.

---
44.
tasyoruvegān mathitāḥ pādapāḥ puṣpaśālinaḥ |
nipetur bhūtale rugṇāḥ śakrāyudhahatā iva ||

> **Meaning 44:** Shaken by the force of his thighs, the flowering trees broke
> and fell to the ground, as if struck by Śakra's thunderbolt.

---
45.
kandarodarasaṁsthānāṁ pīḍitānāṁ mahaujasām |
siṁhānāṁ ninado bhīmo nabho bhindan sa śuśruve ||

> **Meaning 45:** The terrifying roar of the mighty lions, crushed inside the
> hollows of the caves, was heard tearing through the sky.

---
46.
srastavyāviddhavasanā vyākulīkr̥tabhūṣaṇā |
vidyādharyaḥ samutpetuḥ sahasā dharaṇīdharāt ||

> **Meaning 46:** Vidyādharīs suddenly flew up from the mountain, their clothes
> slipping and in disarray, their ornaments jumbled.

---
47.
atipramāṇā balino dīptajihvā mahāviṣāḥ |
nipīḍitaśirogrīvā vyaveṣṭanta mahāhayaḥ ||

> **Meaning 47:** Huge, powerful serpents with fiery, flickering tongues and
> deadly venom writhed, their heads and necks crushed.

---
48.
kiṁnaroragagandharvayakṣavidyādharās tathā |
pīḍitaṁ taṁ nagavaraṁ tyaktvā gaganam āsthitāḥ ||

> **Meaning 48:** Kinnaras, Uragas, Gandharvas, Yakṣas, and Vidyādharas also
> abandoned that great, crushed mountain and took to the sky.

---
49.
sa ca bhūmidharaḥ śrīmān balinā tena pīḍitaḥ |
savr̥kṣaśikharodagrāḥ praviveśa rasātalam ||

> **Meaning 49:** That splendid mountain, crushed by the mighty Hanumān, sank
> down into Rasātala along with its trees and tall peaks.

---
50.
daśayojanavistāras triṁśadyojanam ucchritaḥ |
dharaṇyāṁ samatāṁ yātaḥ sa babhūva dharādharaḥ ||

> **Meaning 50:** Ten yojanas wide and thirty yojanas high, the mountain was
> flattened level with the ground.

---
51.
lilaṅghiṣur bhīmaṁ salilaṁ lavaṇārṇavam |
kallolāsphālavelāntam utpapāta nabho hariḥ ||

> **Meaning 51:** Wanting to leap across the fearsome salt ocean, whose shores
> were lashed by crashing waves, the Hari sprang up into the sky.

---
