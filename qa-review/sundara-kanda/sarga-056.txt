=== Sarga 54 ===

---
1.
tatas tu śiṁśapāmūle jānakīṁ paryavasthitām |
abhivādyābravīd diṣṭyā paśyāmi tvām ihākṣatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tatas taṁ prasthitaṁ sītā vīkṣamāṇā punaḥ punaḥ |
bhartr̥snehānvitaṁ vākyaṁ hanūmantam abhāṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
kāmam asya tvam evaikaḥ kāryasya parisādhane |
paryāptaḥ paravīraghna yaśasyas te balodayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
balais tu saṁkulāṁ kr̥tvā laṅkāṁ parabalārdanaḥ |
māṁ nayed yadi kākutsthas tasya tat sādr̥śaṁ bhavet ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tad yathā tasya vikrāntam anurūpaṁ mahātmanaḥ |
bhavaty āhavaśūrasya tattvam evopapādaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tad arthopahitaṁ vākyaṁ praśritaṁ hetusaṁhitam |
niśamya hanumāṁs tasyā vākyam uttaram abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
kṣipram eṣyati kākutstho haryr̥kṣapravarair vr̥taḥ |
yas te yudhi vijityārīñ śokaṁ vyapanayiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
evam āśvāsya vaidehīṁ hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ |
gamanāya matiṁ kr̥tvā vaidehīm abhyavādayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tataḥ sa kapiśārdūlaḥ svāmisaṁdarśanotsukaḥ |
āruroha giriśreṣṭham ariṣṭam arimardanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tuṅgapadmakajuṣṭābhir nīlābhir vanarājibhiḥ |
sālatālāśvakarṇaiś ca vaṁśaiś ca bahubhir vr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
latāvitānair vitataiḥ puṣpavadbhir alaṁkr̥tam |
nānāmr̥gagaṇākīrṇaṁ dhātuniṣyandabhūṣitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
bahuprasravaṇopetaṁ śilāsaṁcayasaṁkaṭam |
maharṣiyakṣagandharvakiṁnaroragasevitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
latāpādapasaṁbādhaṁ siṁhākulitakandaram |
vyāghrasaṁghasamākīrṇaṁ svādumūlaphaladrumam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tam ārurohātibalaḥ parvataṁ plavagottamaḥ |
rāmadarśanaśīghreṇa praharṣeṇābhicoditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tena pādatalākrāntā ramyeṣu girisānuṣu |
saghoṣāḥ samaśīryanta śilāś cūrṇīkr̥tās tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sa tam āruhya śailendraṁ vyavardhata mahākapiḥ |
dakṣiṇād uttaraṁ pāraṁ prārthayam̐l lavaṇāmbhasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
adhiruhya tato vīraḥ parvataṁ pavanātmajaḥ |
dadarśa sāgaraṁ bhīmaṁ mīnoraganiṣevitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
sa māruta ivākāśaṁ mārutasyātmasaṁbhavaḥ |
prapede hariśārdūlo dakṣiṇād uttarāṁ diśam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
sa tadā pīḍitas tena kapinā parvatottamaḥ |
rarāsa saha tair bhūtaiḥ prāviśad vasudhātalam |
kampamānaiś ca śikharaiḥ patadbhir api ca drumaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tasyoruvegān mathitāḥ pādapāḥ puṣpaśālinaḥ |
nipetur bhūtale rugṇāḥ śakrāyudhahatā iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
kandarodarasaṁsthānāṁ pīḍitānāṁ mahaujasām |
siṁhānāṁ ninado bhīmo nabho bhindan sa śuśruve ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
srastavyāviddhavasanā vyākulīkr̥tabhūṣaṇā |
vidyādharyaḥ samutpetuḥ sahasā dharaṇīdharāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
atipramāṇā balino dīptajihvā mahāviṣāḥ |
nipīḍitaśirogrīvā vyaveṣṭanta mahāhayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
kiṁnaroragagandharvayakṣavidyādharās tathā |
pīḍitaṁ taṁ nagavaraṁ tyaktvā gaganam āsthitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
sa ca bhūmidharaḥ śrīmān balinā tena pīḍitaḥ |
savr̥kṣaśikharodagrāḥ praviveśa rasātalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
daśayojanavistāras triṁśadyojanam ucchritaḥ |
dharaṇyāṁ samatāṁ yātaḥ sa babhūva dharādharaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
