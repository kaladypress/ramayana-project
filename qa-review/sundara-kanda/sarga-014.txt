=== Sarga 14 ===

---
1.
sa muhūrtam iva dhyatvā manasā cādhigamya tām |
avapluto mahātejāḥ prākāraṁ tasya veśmanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 1:** Having reflected for a moment and determined her presence in
> his mind, the radiant hero leapt down onto the boundary wall of that
> residence.

---
2.
sa tu saṁhr̥ṣṭasarvāṅgaḥ prākārastho mahākapiḥ |
puṣpitāgrān vasantādau dadarśa vividhān drumān ||

> **Meaning 2:** Standing on the rampart, his body thrilling with joy, the great
> Kapi saw diverse trees in full blossom at the arrival of spring.

---
3.
sālān aśokān bhavyāṁś ca campakāṁś ca supuṣpitān |
uddālakān nāgavr̥kṣāṁś cūtān kapimukhān api ||

> **Meaning 3:** He saw blooming śāla, aśoka, bhavya, campaka, uddālaka,
> nāgavr̥kṣa, mango, and kapimukha trees.

---
4.
athāmravaṇasañchannāṁ latāśatasamāvr̥tām |
jyāmukta iva nārācaḥ pupluve vr̥kṣavāṭikām ||

> **Meaning 4:** Then, like an iron arrow loosed from a bowstring, he leapt into
> the tree garden, which was shaded by mango groves and wrapped in hundreds of
> flowering creepers.

---
5.
sa praviṣya vicitrāṁ tāṁ vihagair abhināditām |
rājataiḥ kāñcanaiś caiva pādapaiḥ sarvatovr̥tām ||

> **Meaning 5:** Entering that wondrous orchard, resonant with the songs of
> birds and filled on all sides with trees crafted of silver and gold,

---
6.
vihagair mr̥gasaṅghaiś ca vicitrāṁ citrakānanām |
uditādityasaṅkāśāṁ dadarśa hanumān kapiḥ ||

> **Meaning 6:** the Kapi Hanumān saw that marvelous forest teeming with birds
> and deer, glowing like the rising sun.

---
7.
vr̥tāṁ nānāvidhair vr̥kṣaiḥ puṣpopagaphalopagaiḥ |
kokilair bhr̥ṅgarājaiś ca mattair nityaniṣevitām ||

> **Meaning 7:** It was crowded with diverse trees laden with flowers and
> fruits, constantly frequented by cuckoos and joyful bhṛṅgarāja birds.

---
8.
prahr̥ṣṭamanuje kale mr̥gapakṣisamākule |
mattabarhiṇasaṅghuṣṭāṁ nānādvijagaṇāyutām ||

> **Meaning 8:** In that season that gladdens human hearts, the garden teemed
> with birds and beasts, echoing with the calls of peacocks in rut and filled
> with flocks of various birds.

---
9.
mārgamāṇo varārohāṁ rājaputrīm aninditām |
sukhaprasuptān vihagān bodhayām āsa vānaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 9:** As he searched for the blameless princess of graceful form, the
> Vānara woke the birds that were sleeping peacefully.

---
10.
utpatadbhir dvijagaṇaiḥ pakṣaiḥ sālāḥ samāhatāḥ |
anekavarṇā vividhā mumucuḥ puṣpavr̥ṣṭayaḥ ||

> **Meaning 10:** As flocks of birds took flight, their wings brushed against
> the śāla trees, which released a shower of multicolored blossoms.

---
11.
puṣpāvakīrṇaḥ śuśubhe hanumān mārutātmajaḥ |
aśokavanikāmadhye yathā puṣpamayo giriḥ ||

> **Meaning 11:** Showered with blossoms in the middle of the Aśoka grove,
> Hanumān, the son of Māruta, shone like a mountain of flowers.

---
12.
diśaḥ sarvābhidāvantaṁ vr̥kṣaṣaṇḍagataṁ kapim |
dr̥ṣṭvā sarvāṇi bhūtāni vasanta iti menire ||

> **Meaning 12:** Seeing the Kapi darting in all directions through the groves
> of trees, all creatures took him to be the spirit of Spring himself.

---
13.
vr̥kṣebhyaḥ patitaiḥ puṣpair avakīrṇā pr̥thagvidhaiḥ |
rarāja vasudhā tatra pramadeva vibhūṣitā ||

> **Meaning 13:** Carpeted with varied blossoms fallen from the trees, the earth
> there shone like an adorned woman.

---
14.
tarasvinā te taravas tarasābhiprakampitāḥ |
kusumāni vicitrāṇi sasr̥juḥ kapinā tadā ||

> **Meaning 14:** Shaken violently by the swift Kapi, those trees shed their
> multicolored flowers in abundance.

---
15.
nirdhūtapatraśikharāḥ śīrṇapuṣpaphaladrumāḥ |
nikṣiptavastrābharaṇā dhūrtā iva parājitāḥ ||

> **Meaning 15:** With their treetops stripped of leaves, their flowers and
> fruits shaken down, the trees looked like defeated gamblers stripped of their
> clothes and ornaments.

---
16.
hanūmatā vegavatā kampitās te nagottamāḥ |
puṣpaparṇaphalāny āśu mumucuḥ puṣpaśālinaḥ ||

> **Meaning 16:** Shaken by the swift Hanumān, those noble flowering trees
> quickly shed their blossoms, leaves, and fruits.

---
17.
vihaṅgasaṅghair hīnās te skandhamātrāśrayā drumāḥ |
babhūvur agamāḥ sarve māruteneva nirdhutāḥ ||

> **Meaning 17:** Abandoned by flocks of birds and reduced to bare trunks, all
> those trees stood still as if shattered by a hurricane.

---
18.
vidhūtakeśī yuvatir yathā mr̥ditavarṇikā |
niṣpītaśubhadantauṣṭhī nakhair dantaiś ca vikṣatā ||

> **Meaning 18:** Like a young woman with disheveled hair, her cosmetic paste
> rubbed off, her lips bitten and kissed, marked by scratches and teeth,

---
19.
tathā lāṅgūlahastaiś ca caraṇābhyāṁ ca marditā |
babhūvāśokavanikā prabhagnavarapādapā ||

> **Meaning 19:** so the Aśoka grove, with its choice trees broken and battered
> by Hanumān's tail, hands, and feet, lay disheveled and ravaged.

---
20.
mahālatānāṁ dāmāni vyadhamat tarasā kapiḥ |
yathā prāvr̥ṣi vindhyasya meghajālāni mārutaḥ ||

> **Meaning 20:** The Kapi tore through garlands of massive creepers with speed,
> as the monsoon wind scatters banks of clouds over the Vindhya mountains.

---
21.
sa tatra maṇibhūmīś ca rājatīś ca manoramāḥ |
tathā kāñcanabhūmīś ca vicaran dadr̥śe kapiḥ ||

> **Meaning 21:** Roaming there, the Kapi saw enchanting grounds paved with
> gems, silver, and gold.

---
22.
vāpīś ca vividhākārāḥ pūrṇāḥ paramavāriṇā |
mahārhair maṇisopānair upapannās tatas tataḥ ||

> **Meaning 22:** He saw ponds of various shapes filled with pristine water,
> fitted here and there with valuable jeweled stairways.

---
23.
muktāpravālasikatā sphaṭikāntarakuṭṭimāḥ |
kāñcanais tarubhiś citrais tīrajair upaśobhitāḥ ||

> **Meaning 23:** Their sands were made of pearls and coral, their bed-floors
> inlaid with crystal, and they were adorned on their banks with marvelous
> golden trees.

---
24.
phullapadmotpalavanāś cakravākopakūjitāḥ |
natyūharutasaṅghuṣṭā haṁsasārasanāditāḥ ||

> **Meaning 24:** They were filled with beds of blooming lotuses and water
> lilies, echoing with the calls of cakravākas, resounding with the cries of
> water-fowls, and musical with the sounds of swans and sārasa cranes.

---
25.
dīrghābhir drumayuktābhiḥ saridbhiś ca samantataḥ |
amr̥topamatoyābhiḥ śivābhir upasaṁskr̥tāḥ ||

> **Meaning 25:** They were fed on all sides by long tree-lined rivulets
> carrying nectar-like, wholesome water.

---
26.
latāśatair avatatāḥ santānakasamāvr̥tāḥ |
nānāgulmāvr̥tavanāḥ karavīrakr̥tāntarāḥ ||

> **Meaning 26:** They were draped with hundreds of creepers, shaded by
> santānaka trees, covered with various thickets, and bordered by karavīra
> bushes.

---
27.
tato ’mbudharasaṅkāśaṁ pravr̥ddhaśikharaṁ girim |
vicitrakūṭaṁ kūṭaiś ca sarvataḥ parivāritam ||

> **Meaning 27:** Then the tiger among Kapis saw a charming pleasure mountain
> resembling a rain cloud, with towering summits and flanked on all sides by
> smaller peaks,

---
28.
śilāgr̥hair avatataṁ nānāvr̥kṣaiḥ samāvr̥tam |
dadarśa kapiśārdūlo ramyaṁ jagati parvatam ||

> **Meaning 28:** fitted with stone pavilions and surrounded by diverse trees —
> an enchanting hill standing in that garden.

---
29.
dadarśa ca nagāt tasmān nadīṁ nipatitāṁ kapiḥ |
aṅkād iva samutpatya priyasya patitāṁ priyām ||

> **Meaning 29:** From that hill, the Kapi saw a river tumbling down like an
> affectionate woman slipping from her lover's lap.

---
30.
jale nipatitāgraiś ca pādapair upaśobhitām |
vāryamāṇām iva kruddhāṁ pramadāṁ priyabandhubhiḥ ||

> **Meaning 30:** With the branches of trees on its banks dipping into the
> water, it looked like an angry woman held back by loving kinsfolk.

---
31.
punar āvr̥ttatoyāṁ ca dadarśa sa mahākapiḥ |
prasannām iva kāntasya kāntāṁ punar upasthitām ||

> **Meaning 31:** Then the great Kapi saw the river's water winding back in a
> curve, resembling a mollified lady returning to her beloved.

---
32.
tasyādūrāt sa padminyo nānādvijagaṇāyutāḥ |
dadarśa kapiśārdūlo hanumān mārutātmajaḥ ||

> **Meaning 32:** Not far from there, the tiger among Kapis, Hanumān, son of
> Māruta, saw lotus ponds teeming with diverse flocks of water birds.

---
33.
kr̥trimāṁ dīrghikāṁ cāpi pūrṇāṁ śītena vāriṇā |
maṇipravarasopānāṁ muktāsikataśobhitām ||

> **Meaning 33:** He also saw an artificial pleasure lake filled with cool
> water, boasting staircases of precious gems and sands of pearls.

---
34.
vividhair mr̥gasaṅghaiś ca vicitrāṁ citrakānanām |
prāsādaiḥ sumahadbhiś ca nirmitair viśvakarmaṇā ||

> **Meaning 34:** It was embellished with various herds of deer, diverse
> colorful woods, and great pavilions built by Viśvakarmā.

---
35.
kānanaiḥ kr̥trimaiś cāpi sarvataḥ samalaṅkr̥tām |
ye ke cit pādapās tatra puṣpopagaphalopagāḥ ||

> **Meaning 35:** Adorned on all sides with artificial groves, whatever trees
> stood there bearing flowers and fruits

---
36.
sacchatrāḥ savitardīkāḥ sarve sauvarṇavedikāḥ |
latāpratānair bahubhiḥ parṇaiś ca bahubhir vr̥tām ||

> **Meaning 36:** were fitted with canopies, viewing platforms, and golden
> daises.

---
37.
kāñcanīṁ śiṁśupām ekāṁ dadarśa sa mahākapiḥ |
vṛtāṁ hemamayībhistu vedikābhiḥ samantataḥ ||

> **Meaning 37:** The great Kapi saw a single golden śiṁśapā tree, enveloped in
> dense spreading creepers and leaves, surrounded on all sides by platforms of
> gold.

---
38.
so ’paśyad bhūmibhāgāṁś ca gartaprasravaṇāni ca |
suvarṇavr̥kṣān aparān dadarśa śikhisannibhān ||

> **Meaning 38:** He observed the grounds, hollows, and watercourses, and saw
> other golden trees blazing like fire.

---
39.
teṣāṁ drumāṇāṁ prabhayā meror iva mahākapiḥ |
amanyata tadā vīraḥ kāñcano ’smīti vānaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 39:** Bathed in the radiant glow of those trees, the heroic Vānara,
> the great Kapi, felt as if he too had turned to gold, like Mount Meru.

---
40.
tāṁ kāñcanais tarugaṇair mārutena ca vījitām |
kiṅkiṇīśatanirghoṣāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā vismayam āgamat ||

> **Meaning 40:** Seeing that golden tree fanned by the breeze, tinkling with
> the chiming of hundreds of tiny bells, he was struck with wonder.

---
41.
supuṣpitāgrāṁ rucirāṁ taruṇāṅkurapallavām |
tām āruhya mahāvegaḥ śiṁśapāṁ parṇasaṁvr̥tām ||

> **Meaning 41:** Climbing into that beautiful śiṁśapā tree, laden with
> blossoms, tender sprouts, and dense foliage, the swift Hanumān thought:

---
42.
ito drakṣyāmi vaidehīṁ rāma darśanalālasām |
itaś cetaś ca duḥkhārtāṁ sampatantīṁ yadr̥cchayā ||

> **Meaning 42:** "From here I will see Vaidehī, who longs to see Rāma, as she
> wanders here and there in her grief.

---
43.
aśokavanikā ceyaṁ dr̥ḍhaṁ ramyā durātmanaḥ |
campakaiś candanaiś cāpi bakulaiś ca vibhūṣitā ||

> **Meaning 43:** This Aśoka grove of the wicked Rāvaṇa is exceedingly
> enchanting, adorned with campaka, sandalwood, and bakula trees.

---
44.
iyaṁ ca nalinī ramyā dvijasaṅghaniṣevitā |
imāṁ sā rāmamahiṣī nūnam eṣyati jānakī ||

> **Meaning 44:** And this delightful lotus pond is frequented by flocks of
> birds; Jānakī, the queen of Rāma, will surely come here.

---
45.
sā rāma rāmamahiṣī rāghavasya priyā sadā |
vanasañcārakuśalā nūnam eṣyati jānakī ||

> **Meaning 45:** Ever dear to Rāghava, adept at walking in forest paths, that
> queen of Rāma will certainly visit this place.

---
46.
atha vā mr̥gaśāvākṣī vanasyāsya vicakṣaṇā |
vanam eṣyati sā ceha rāmacintānukarśitā ||

> **Meaning 46:** Or that fawn-eyed lady, knowing the ways of this wood, will
> come here to wander, worn thin by thoughts of Rāma.

---
47.
rāmaśokābhisantaptā sā devī vāmalocanā |
vanavāsaratā nityam eṣyate vanacāriṇī ||

> **Meaning 47:** Tormented by grief for Rāma, that beautiful-eyed devī, who has
> always loved living in the wild, will come walking in this grove.

---
48.
vanecarāṇāṁ satataṁ nūnaṁ spr̥hayate purā |
rāmasya dayitā bhāryā janakasya sutā satī ||

> **Meaning 48:** For in the past, Rāma's beloved wife, the virtuous daughter of
> Janaka, always cherished the gentle forest creatures.

---
49.
sandhyākālamanāḥ śyāmā dhruvam eṣyati jānakī |
nadīṁ cemāṁ śivajalāṁ sandhyārthe varavarṇinī ||

> **Meaning 49:** Mindful of the evening twilight hour, that fair lady Jānakī
> will surely come to this auspicious stream to perform her sandhyā rites.

---
50.
tasyāś cāpy anurūpeyam aśokavanikā śubhā |
śubhā yā pārthivendrasya patnī rāmasya sammitā ||

> **Meaning 50:** This lovely Aśoka grove is truly worthy of her — the radiant
> wife of the king of kings and the worthy counterpart of Rāma.

---
51.
yadi jivati sā devī tārādhipanibhānanā |
āgamiṣyati sāvaśyam imāṁ śivajalāṁ nadīm ||

> **Meaning 51:** If that moon-faced devī is alive, she will inevitably come to
> this river of pure, auspicious waters."

---
52.
evaṁ tu matvā hanumān mahātmā pratīkṣamāṇo manujendrapatnīm |
avekṣamāṇaś ca dadarśa sarvaṁ supuṣpite parṇaghane nilīnaḥ ||

> **Meaning 52:** Thinking thus and waiting for the wife of the king of men, the
> great-souled Hanumān concealed himself within the dense foliage of that
> blossoming tree and kept watch over everything.
