=== Sarga 34 ===

---
1.
tasyāstad vacanaṁ śrutvā hanūmān hariyūthapaḥ |
duḥkhād duḥkhābhibhūtāyāḥ sāntam uttaram abravīt ||

> **Meaning 1:** Hearing those words from her, who was utterly overwhelmed by
> grief, Hanumān, the commander of the vānaras, gave this comforting reply:

---
2.
ahaṁ rāmasya saṁdeśād devi dūtastavāgataḥ |
vaidehi kuśalī rāmaḥ tvāṁ ca kauśalam abravīt ||

> **Meaning 2:** "O noble lady, I have come as a messenger bearing a message
> from Rāma. Vaidehī, Rāma is well, and he asks after your welfare.

---
3.
yo brāhmam astraṁ vedāṁśca veda vedavidāṁ varaḥ |
sa tvāṁ dāśarathī rāmaḥ devi kauśalam abravīt ||

> **Meaning 3:** "He who knows the Brahmā weapon and the Vedas, who is the
> foremost among those who know the Vedas — that son of Daśaratha, Rāma, asks
> after your welfare.

---
4.
lakṣmaṇaśca mahātejā bhartuste anucaraḥ priyaḥ |
kr̥tavāñ śokasaṁtaptaḥ śirasā te abhivādanam ||

> **Meaning 4:** "And the glorious Lakṣmaṇa, the beloved companion of your
> husband — tormented by grief, he bows his head in greeting to you."

---
5.
sā tayoḥ kuśalaṁ devī niśamya narasiṁhayoḥ |
prītisaṁhr̥ṣṭasarvāṅgī hanūmāntam athābravīt ||

> **Meaning 5:** Hearing that those two lion-like men, Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa, were
> well, the noble lady's entire body thrilled with joy, and she said to Hanumān:

---
6.
kalyāṇī bata gatheyaṁ laukikī pratibhāti me |
ehi jīvantam ānadaḥ naraṁ varṣaśatād api ||

> **Meaning 6:** "How true that worldly saying seems to me: 'If one stays alive,
> joy comes even after a hundred years!'"

---
7.
tayoḥ samāgame tasmin prītirutpāditādbhutā |
paraspareṇa cālāpaṁ viśvastau tau pracakratuḥ ||

> **Meaning 7:** Wondrous joy sprang up in their meeting, and the two spoke to
> each other in mutual trust.

---
8.
tasyāstad vacanaṁ śrutvā hanūmān hariyūthapaḥ |
sītāyāḥ śokadīnāyāḥ samīpam upacakrame ||

> **Meaning 8:** Hearing those words from Sītā, who was wretched with grief, the
> vānara commander Hanumān drew a little closer.

---
9.
yathā yathā samīpaṁ sa hanūmān upasarpati |
tathā tathā rāvaṇaṁ sā taṁ sītā pariśaṅkate ||

> **Meaning 9:** But the closer Hanumān came, the more Sītā began to suspect
> that he was Rāvaṇa.

---
10.
aho dhig dhik kr̥tam idaṁ kathitaṁ hi yad asya me |
rūpāntaram upāgamya sa evāyaṁ hi rāvaṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 10:** "Oh, shame! Shame on what I have done in speaking to him! This
> is surely Rāvaṇa himself, come in another form."

---
11.
tām aśokasya śākhāṁ sā vimuktvā śokakarśitā |
tasyām evānavadyāṅgī dharaṇyāṁ samupāviśat ||

> **Meaning 11:** Letting go of the branch of the aśoka tree, that lady of
> flawless beauty, wasted by grief, sank down upon the earth.

---
12.
avandata mahābāhuḥ tatastāṁ janakātmajām |
sā cainaṁ bhayavitrastā bhūyo naivābhyudaikṣata ||

> **Meaning 12:** Mighty-armed Hanumān then bowed to Janaka's daughter, but
> struck with terror, she would not look up at him again.

---
13.
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā vandamānaṁ tu sītā śaśinibhānanā |
abravīd dīrgham ucchvasya vānaraṁ madhurasvarā ||

> **Meaning 13:** Seeing him bowing before her, Sītā, whose face was like the
> moon, drew a long sigh and spoke to the vānara in a sweet voice:

---
14.
māyāṁ praviṣṭo māyāvī yadi tvaṁ rāvaṇaḥ svayam |
utpādayasi me bhūyaḥ saṁtāpaṁ tan na śobhanam ||

> **Meaning 14:** "If you are the trickster Rāvaṇa himself, wearing a disguise,
> you are causing me fresh agony — and that is not right.

---
15.
svaṁ parityajya rūpaṁ yaḥ parivrājakarūpadhr̥t |
janasthāne mayā dr̥ṣṭaḥ tvaṁ sa evāsi rāvaṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 15:** "You are that same Rāvaṇa who abandoned his own form and
> appeared before me in Janasthāna, wearing the guise of a wandering ascetic.

---
16.
upavāsakr̥śāṁ dīnāṁ kāmarūpa niśācara |
saṁtāpayasi māṁ bhūyaḥ saṁtāpaṁ tan na śobhanam ||

> **Meaning 16:** "O night-roamer who can take any form, you are causing fresh
> agony to me, a wretched woman wasted by fasting — and that is not right.

---
17.
athavā naitadēvaṁ hi yanmayā pariśaṁkitam |
manasō hi mama prītiḥ utpannā tava darśanāt ||

> **Meaning 17:** "Or perhaps my suspicions are wrong. For merely seeing you has
> brought joy to my heart.

---
18.
yadi rāmasya dūtastvam āgato bhadram astu te |
pr̥cchāmi tvāṁ hariśreṣṭha priyā rāma kathā hi me ||

> **Meaning 18:** "If you have come as Rāma's messenger, may all be well with
> you. I ask you, finest of vānaras — for the tale of Rāma is dear to me —

---
19.
guṇān rāmasya kathaya priyasya mama vānara |
cittaṁ harasi me saumya nadīkūlaṁ yathā rayaḥ ||

> **Meaning 19:** "Speak of the virtues of my beloved Rāma, O vānara. Gentle
> one, you are sweeping away my grief, just as a rushing current sweeps away a
> riverbank.

---
20.
aho svapnasya sukhatā yāham evaṁ cirāhr̥tā |
preṣitaṁ nāma paśyāmi rāghaveṇa vanaukasaṁ ||

> **Meaning 20:** "Ah, how sweet a dream this is! It has been a long time since
> I was carried off. After all this time, I see before my eyes a forest-dweller
> sent by Rāghava."

---
21.
svapne api yady ahaṁ vīraṁ rāghavaṁ sahalakṣmaṇam |
paśyeyaṁ nāvasīdeyaṁ svapnaḥ api mama matsarī ||

> **Meaning 21:** "If I could see the hero Rāghava with Lakṣmaṇa even in a
> dream, I would not despair — but even my dreams are stingy toward me.

---
22.
nāhaṁ svapnam imaṁ manye svapne dr̥ṣṭvā hi vānaram |
na śakyaḥ abhyudayaḥ prāptuṁ prāptaścābhyudayo mama ||

> **Meaning 22:** "Yet I do not think this is a dream, for seeing a vānara in a
> dream brings no good fortune, and good fortune has come to me.

---
23.
kiṁ nu syāccittamohaḥ ayaṁ bhaved vātagatistv iyam |
unmādajo vikāro vā syād iyaṁ mr̥gatr̥ṣṇikā ||

> **Meaning 23:** "Could this be a delusion of my mind? Could it be an
> imbalance of vāta, a distortion born of madness, or a mere mirage?"

---
24.
atha vā nāyam unmādaḥ mohaḥ apy unmādalakṣmaṇaḥ |
saṁbudhye cāham ātmānam imaṁ cāpi vanaukasaṁ ||

> **Meaning 24:** "No, this is not madness, nor a delusion that looks like
> madness. I clearly know who I am, and I clearly see this forest-dweller."

---
25.
ity evaṁ bahudhā sītā saṁpradhārya balābalam |
rakṣasāṁ kāmarūpatvān mene taṁ rākṣasādhipam ||

> **Meaning 25:** Weighing the strengths and weaknesses of the matter in many
> ways like this, Sītā, knowing that rākṣasas can take any form at will,
> concluded that he was the rākṣasa king.

---
26.
etāṁ buddhiṁ tadā kr̥tvā sītā sā tanumadhyamā |
na prativyājahārātha vānaraṁ janakātmajā ||

> **Meaning 26:** Having made up her mind, that slender-waisted daughter of
> Janaka spoke no more in reply to the vānara.

---
27.
sītāyāścintitaṁ buddhvā hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ |
śrotrānukūlairvacanaiḥ tadā tāṁ saṁpraharṣayat ||

> **Meaning 27:** Understanding Sītā's thoughts, Hanumān, son of the Wind, then
> brought her joy with words that were pleasing to the ear:

---
28.
āditya iva tejasvī lokakāntaḥ śaśī yathā |
rājā sarvasya lokasya devo vaiśravaṇo yathā ||

> **Meaning 28:** "Sri Rāma is brilliant like the sun, beloved of the world like the
> moon. He is a king to the whole world, like the deity Kubera.

---
29.
vikrameṇopapannaśca yathā viṣṇurmahāyaśāḥ |
satyavādī madhuravāg devo vācaspatiryathā ||

> **Meaning 29:** "He is endowed with valour like the glorious Viṣṇu. He is
> truthful and sweet of speech like Bṛhaspati, lord of speech.

---
30.
rūpavān subhagaḥ śrīmān kandarpa iva mūrtimān |
sthānakrodhaprahartā ca śreṣṭho loke mahārathaḥ |
bāhucchāyām avaṣṭabdhaḥ yasya loko mahātmanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 30:** "He is handsome, charming, and radiant — love incarnate. When
> angered at the right time, he strikes with deadly force. He is the foremost of
> great warriors in the world, and the whole world rests beneath the shelter of
> that great-souled Rāma's arms.

---
31.
apakr̥ṣyāśramapadān mr̥garūpeṇa rāghavam ||


32.
śūnye yenāpanītāsi tasya drakṣyasi yat phalam |
nacirād rāvaṇaṁ saṁkhye yo vadhiṣyati vīryavān ||

33.
roṣapramuktairiṣubhiḥ jvaladbhiriva pāvakaiḥ |
tenāhaṁ preṣito dūtaḥ tvatsakāśam ihāgataḥ ||

> **Meaning 31-33:** "You will soon see the terrible price he must pay for
> taking the form of a deer to draw Rāghava away, and carrying you off from the
> empty hermitage. That valiant hero will slay Rāvaṇa in battle before long with arrows loosed in anger that blaze like fire. I am a messenger sent by him, and I have come into your presence.

---




---
34.
tvadviyogena duḥkhārtaḥ sa tvāṁ kauśalam abravīt |
lakṣmaṇaśca mahātejāḥ sumitrānandavardhanaḥ ||

35.
abhivādya mahābāhuḥ saḥ api kauśalam abravīt |
rāmasya ca sakhā devi sugrīvo nāma vānaraḥ ||


rājā vānaramukhyānāṁ sa tvāṁ kauśalam abravīt |
nityaṁ smarati rāmastvāṁ sasugrīvaḥ salakṣmaṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 34-36:** "Tormented by grief at being parted from you, he asks after
> your welfare. And glorious Lakṣmaṇa, the joy of Sumitrā, that mighty-armed one bows in greeting and also asks after
> your welfare. O noble lady, Sugrīva the vānara, Rāma's friend and king of the foremost vānaras, asks after your welfare.
> Rāma, along with Sugrīva and Lakṣmaṇa, remembers you constantly.

---


37.
diṣṭyā jīvasi vaidehi rākṣasī vaśam āgatā |
nacirād drakṣyase rāmaṁ lakṣmaṇaṁ ca mahāratham ||

38.
madhye vānarakoṭīnāṁ sugrīvaṁ cāmitaujasaṁ |
ahaṁ sugrīvasacivaḥ hanūmān nāma vānaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 37-38:** "By the grace of destiny you are still alive, Vaidehī, even
> though you have fallen into the power of the rākṣasīs! Before long you will see
> Rāma and the great warrior Lakṣmaṇa and Sugrīva of boundless might, amidst ten million vānaras.
> I am Sugrīva's minister, a vānara named Hanumān.

---


39.
praviṣṭo nagarīṁ laṅkāṁ laṅghayitvā mahodadhim |
kr̥tvā mūrdhni padanyāsaṁ rāvaṇasya durātmanaḥ ||

40.
tvāṁ draṣṭum upayātaḥ ahaṁ samāśritya parākramam |
nāham asmi tathā devi yathā mām avagacchasi |
viśaṅkā tyajyatām eṣā śraddhatsva vadato mama ||

> **Meaning 39-40:** "Having leapt across the great ocean, I entered the city of
> Laṅkā. Planting my foot on the head of the wicked Rāvaṇa I have come to see you, relying on my own strength. O noble
> lady, I am not what you take me for. Cast aside this suspicion and trust my
> words!

---
