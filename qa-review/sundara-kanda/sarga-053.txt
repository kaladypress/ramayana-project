=== Sarga 53 ===

---
1.
saṁdīpyamānāṁ vidhvastāṁ trastarakṣo gaṇāṁ purīm |
avekṣya hānumām̐l laṅkāṁ cintayām āsa vānaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tasyābhūt sumahāṁs trāsaḥ kutsā cātmany ajāyata |
laṅkāṁ pradahatā karma kiṁsvit kr̥tam idaṁ mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
dhanyās te puruṣaśreṣṭha ye buddhyā kopam utthitam |
nirundhanti mahātmāno dīptam agnim ivāmbhasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
yadi dagdhā tv iyaṁ laṅkā nūnam āryāpi jānakī |
dagdhā tena mayā bhartur hataṁ kāryam ajānatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
yad artham ayam ārambhas tat kāryam avasāditam |
mayā hi dahatā laṅkāṁ na sītā parirakṣitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
īṣatkāryam idaṁ kāryaṁ kr̥tam āsīn na saṁśayaḥ |
tasya krodhābhibhūtena mayā mūlakṣayaḥ kr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
vinaṣṭā jānakī vyaktaṁ na hy adagdhaḥ pradr̥śyate |
laṅkāyāḥ kaś cid uddeśaḥ sarvā bhasmīkr̥tā purī ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
yadi tad vihataṁ kāryaṁ mayā prajñāviparyayāt |
ihaiva prāṇasaṁnyāso mamāpi hy atirocate ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
kim agnau nipatāmy adya āhosvid vaḍavāmukhe |
śarīram āho sattvānāṁ dadmi sāgaravāsinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
kathaṁ hi jīvatā śakyo mayā draṣṭuṁ harīśvaraḥ |
tau vā puruṣaśārdūlau kāryasarvasvaghātinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
mayā khalu tad evedaṁ roṣadoṣāt pradarśitam |
prathitaṁ triṣu lokeṣu kapitam anavasthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
dhig astu rājasaṁ bhāvam anīśam anavasthitam |
īśvareṇāpi yad rāgān mayā sītā na rakṣitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
vinaṣṭāyāṁ tu sītāyāṁ tāv ubhau vinaśiṣyataḥ |
tayor vināśe sugrīvaḥ sabandhur vinaśiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
etad eva vacaḥ śrutvā bharato bhrātr̥vatsalaḥ |
dharmātmā sahaśatrughnaḥ kathaṁ śakṣyati jīvitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
ikṣvākuvaṁśe dharmiṣṭhe gate nāśam asaṁśayam |
bhaviṣyanti prajāḥ sarvāḥ śokasaṁtāpapīḍitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tad ahaṁ bhāgyarahito luptadharmārthasaṁgrahaḥ |
roṣadoṣaparītātmā vyaktaṁ lokavināśanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
iti cintayatas tasya nimittāny upapedire |
pūram apy upalabdhāni sākṣāt punar acintayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
atha vā cārusarvāṅgī rakṣitā svena tejasā |
na naśiṣyati kalyāṇī nāgnir agnau pravartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
na hi dharmān manas tasya bhāryām amitatejasaḥ |
svacāritrābhiguptāṁ tāṁ spraṣṭum arhati pāvakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
nūnaṁ rāmaprabhāvena vaidehyāḥ sukr̥tena ca |
yan māṁ dahanakarmāyaṁ nādahad dhavyavāhanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
trayāṇāṁ bharatādīnāṁ bhrātr̥̄ṇāṁ devatā ca yā |
rāmasya ca manaḥkāntā sā kathaṁ vinaśiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
yad vā dahanakarmāyaṁ sarvatra prabhur avyayaḥ |
na me dahati lāṅgūlaṁ katham āryāṁ pradhakṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tapasā satyavākyena ananyatvāc ca bhartari |
api sā nirdahed agniṁ na tām agniḥ pradhakṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
sa tathā cintayaṁs tatra devyā dharmaparigraham |
śuśrāva hanumān vākyaṁ cāraṇānāṁ mahātmanām ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
aho khalu kr̥taṁ karma durviṣahyaṁ hanūmatā |
agniṁ visr̥jatābhīkṣṇaṁ bhīmaṁ rākṣasasadmani ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
dagdheyaṁ nagarī laṅkā sāṭṭaprākāratoraṇā |
jānakī na ca dagdheti vismayo ’dbhuta eva naḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
sa nimittaiś ca dr̥ṣṭārthaiḥ kāraṇaiś ca mahāguṇaiḥ |
r̥ṣivākyaiś ca hanumān abhavat prītamānasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tataḥ kapiḥ prāptamanorathārthas; tām akṣatāṁ rājasutāṁ viditvā |
pratyakṣatas tāṁ punar eva dr̥ṣṭvā; pratiprayāṇāya matiṁ cakāra ||

=== meaning ===

---
