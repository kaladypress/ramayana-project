=== Sarga 51 ===

---
1.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā daśagrīvo mahābalaḥ |
deśakālahitaṁ vākyaṁ bhrātur uttamam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
samyag uktaṁ hi bhavatā dūtavadhyā vigarhitā |
avaśyaṁ tu vadhād anyaḥ kriyatām asya nigrahaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
kapīnāṁ kila lāṅgūlam iṣṭaṁ bhavati bhūṣaṇam |
tad asya dīpyatāṁ śīghraṁ tena dagdhena gacchatu ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tataḥ paśyantv imaṁ dīnam aṅgavairūpyakarśitam |
samitrā jñātayaḥ sarve bāndhavāḥ sasuhr̥jjanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
ājñāpayad rākṣasendraḥ puraṁ sarvaṁ sacatvaram |
lāṅgūlena pradīptena rakṣobhiḥ pariṇīyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā rākṣasāḥ kopakarkaśāḥ |
veṣṭante tasya lāṅgūlaṁ jīrṇaiḥ kārpāsikaiḥ paṭaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
saṁveṣṭyamāne lāṅgūle vyavardhata mahākapiḥ |
śuṣkam indhanam āsādya vaneṣv iva hutāśanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tailena pariṣicyātha te ’gniṁ tatrāvapātayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
lāṅgūlena pradīptena rākṣasāṁs tān apātayat |
roṣāmarṣaparītātmā bālasūryasamānanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
sa bhūyaḥ saṁgataiḥ krūrai rākasair harisattamaḥ |
nibaddhaḥ kr̥tavān vīras tatkālasadr̥śīṁ matim ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
kāmaṁ khalu na me śaktā nibadhasyāpi rākṣasāḥ |
chittvā pāśān samutpatya hanyām aham imān punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sarveṣām eva paryāpto rākṣasānām ahaṁ yudhi |
kiṁ tu rāmasya prītyarthaṁ viṣahiṣye ’ham īdr̥śam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
laṅkā carayitavyā me punar eva bhaved iti |
rātrau na hi sudr̥ṣṭā me durgakarmavidhānataḥ |
avaśyam eva draṣṭavyā mayā laṅkā niśākṣaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
kāmaṁ bandhaiś ca me bhūyaḥ pucchasyoddīpanena ca |
pīḍāṁ kurvantu rakṣāṁsi na me ’sti manasaḥ śramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tatas te saṁvr̥tākāraṁ sattvavantaṁ mahākapim |
parigr̥hya yayur hr̥ṣṭā rākṣasāḥ kapikuñjaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
śaṅkhabherīninādais tair ghoṣayantaḥ svakarmabhiḥ |
rākṣasāḥ krūrakarmāṇaś cārayanti sma tāṁ purīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
hanumāṁś cārayām āsa rākṣasānāṁ mahāpurīm |
athāpaśyad vimānāni vicitrāṇi mahākapiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
saṁvr̥tān bhūmibhāgāṁś ca suvibhaktāṁś ca catvarān |
rathyāś ca gr̥hasaṁbādhāḥ kapiḥ śr̥ṅgāṭakāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
catvareṣu catuṣkeṣu rājamārge tathaiva ca |
ghoṣayanti kapiṁ sarve cārīka iti rākṣasāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
dīpyamāne tatas tasya lāṅgūlāgre hanūmataḥ |
rākṣasyas tā virūpākṣyaḥ śaṁsur devyās tad apriyam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
yas tvayā kr̥tasaṁvādaḥ sīte tāmramukhaḥ kapiḥ |
lāṅgūlena pradīptena sa eṣa pariṇīyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
śrutvā tad vacanaṁ krūram ātmāpaharaṇopamam |
vaidehī śokasaṁtaptā hutāśanam upāgamat ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
maṅgalābhimukhī tasya sā tadāsīn mahākapeḥ |
upatasthe viśālākṣī prayatā havyavāhanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
yady asti patiśuśrūṣā yady asti caritaṁ tapaḥ |
yadi cāsty ekapatnītvaṁ śīto bhava hanūmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
yadi kaś cid anukrośas tasya mayy asti dhīmataḥ |
yadi vā bhāgyaśeṣaṁ me śīto bhava hanūmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
yadi māṁ vr̥ttasaṁpannāṁ tatsamāgamalālasām |
sa vijānāti dharmātmā śīto bhava hanūmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
yadi māṁ tārayaty āryaḥ sugrīvaḥ satyasaṁgaraḥ |
asmād duḥkhān mahābāhuḥ śīto bhava hanūmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tatas tīkṣṇārcir avyagraḥ pradakṣiṇaśikho ’nalaḥ |
jajvāla mr̥gaśāvākṣyāḥ śaṁsann iva śivaṁ kapeḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
dahyamāne ca lāṅgūle cintayām āsa vānaraḥ |
pradīpto ’gnir ayaṁ kasmān na māṁ dahati sarvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
dr̥śyate ca mahājvālaḥ karoti ca na me rujam |
śiśirasyeva saṁpāto lāṅgūlāgre pratiṣṭhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
atha vā tad idaṁ vyaktaṁ yad dr̥ṣṭaṁ plavatā mayā |
rāmaprabhāvād āścaryaṁ parvataḥ saritāṁ patau ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
yadi tāvat samudrasya mainākasya ca dhīmatha |
rāmārthaṁ saṁbhramas tādr̥k kim agnir na kariṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
sītāyāś cānr̥śaṁsyena tejasā rāghavasya ca |
pituś ca mama sakhyena na māṁ dahati pāvakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
bhūyaḥ sa cintayām āsa muhūrtaṁ kapikuñjaraḥ |
utpapātātha vegena nanāda ca mahākapiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
puradvāraṁ tataḥ śrīmāñ śailaśr̥ṅgam ivonnatam |
vibhaktarakṣaḥsaṁbādham āsasādānilātmajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
sa bhūtvā śailasaṁkāśaḥ kṣaṇena punar ātmavān |
hrasvatāṁ paramāṁ prāpto bandhanāny avaśātayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
vimuktaś cābhavac chrīmān punaḥ parvatasaṁnibhaḥ |
vīkṣamāṇaś ca dadr̥śe parighaṁ toraṇāśritam ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
sa taṁ gr̥hya mahābāhuḥ kālāyasapariṣkr̥tam |
rakṣiṇas tān punaḥ sarvān sūdayām āsa mārutiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
sa tān nihatvā raṇacaṇḍavikramaḥ; samīkṣamāṇaḥ punar eva laṅkām |
pradīptalāṅgūlakr̥tārcimālī; prakāśatāditya ivāṁśumālī ||

=== meaning ===

---
