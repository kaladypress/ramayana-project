=== Sarga 51 ===

---
1.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā daśagrīvo mahābalaḥ |
deśakālahitaṁ vākyaṁ bhrāturuttamam abravīt ||

> **Meaning 1:** Hearing Vibhīṣaṇa's words, the mighty Daśagrīva answered his
> brother with a reply that suited the place and the moment.

---
2.
samyag uktaṁ hi bhavatā dūtavadhyā vigarhitā |
avaśyaṁ tu vadhād anyaḥ kriyatām asya nigrahaḥ ||

> **Meaning 2:** Rāvaṇa said: "You have spoken rightly. Killing a messenger is
> condemned. But some other punishment must surely be given to him, short of
> death."

---
3.
kapīnāṁ kila lāṅgūlam iṣṭaṁ bhavati bhūṣaṇam |
tad asya dīpyatāṁ śīghraṁ tena dagdhena gacchatu ||

> **Meaning 3:** "Vānaras treasure their tails as their favorite ornament. So
> set his tail on fire at once, and let him go away with it burnt."

---
4.
tataḥ paśyantv imaṁ dīnam aṅgavairūpyakarśitam |
samitrā jñātayaḥ sarve bāndhavāḥ sasuhr̥jjanāḥ ||

> **Meaning 4:** "Then all his friends, relatives, kinsmen and well-wishers can
> see him pitiful, wasted by the disfigurement of his body."

---
5.
ājñāpayad rākṣasendraḥ puraṁ sarvaṁ sacatvaram |
lāṅgūlena pradīptena rakṣobhiḥ pariṇīyatām ||

> **Meaning 5:** The lord of the Rākṣasas gave the order: "Let the Rākṣasas take
> him through the whole city, with its squares and crossroads, with his tail
> blazing."

---
6.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā rākṣasāḥ kopakarkaśāḥ |
veṣṭante tasya lāṅgūlaṁ jīrṇaiḥ kārpāsikaiḥ paṭaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 6:** Hearing his command, the Rākṣasas, fierce with anger, wrapped
> Hanumān's tail in old cotton rags.

---
7.
saṁveṣṭyamāne lāṅgūle vyavardhata mahākapiḥ |
śuṣkam indhanam āsādya vaneṣv iva hutāśanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 7:** As his tail was being wrapped, the great Kapi grew larger, like
> a forest fire that has reached dry fuel.

---
8.
tailena pariṣicyātha te agniṁ tatrāvapātayan |
lāṅgūlena pradīptena rākṣasāṁstān apātayat |
roṣāmarṣaparītātmā bālasūryasamānanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 8:** They soaked the cloth in oil and set it alight. Burning with
> fury and indignation, with a face like the rising sun, Hanumān struck down the
> Rākṣasas with his blazing tail.

---
11.
sa bhūyaḥ saṁgataiḥ krūrai rākasairharisattamaḥ |
nibaddhaḥ kr̥tavān vīraḥ tatkālasadr̥śīṁ matim ||

> **Meaning 11:** Then a crowd of cruel Rākṣasas gathered and bound him again.
> The heroic Hariśreṣṭha, tied up, made a decision that fit the moment.

---
12.
kāmaṁ khalu na me śaktā nibadhasyāpi rākṣasāḥ |
chittvā pāśān samutpatya hanyām aham imān punaḥ ||

> **Meaning 12:** "Though bound, the Rākṣasas are certainly not strong enough to
> hold me. I could snap these ropes, leap up, and strike them down again."

---
13.
sarveṣām eva paryāptaḥ rākṣasānām ahaṁ yudhi ||

14.
kiṁ tu rāmasya prītyarthaṁ viṣahiṣye aham īdr̥śam |
laṅkā carayitavyā me punareva bhaved iti ||

15.
rātrau na hi sudr̥ṣṭā me durgakarmavidhānataḥ |
avaśyam eva draṣṭavyā mayā laṅkā niśākṣaye ||

> **Meaning 13-15:** "I am more than a match for all the Rākṣasas in battle.
> But to please Rāma I will put up with this treatment. I need to go through
> Laṅkā again. At night I could not examine its fortifications properly, but now
> in broad daylight, Laṅkā must surely be surveyed by me."

---
16.
kāmaṁ bandhaiśca me bhūyaḥ pucchasyoddīpanena ca |
pīḍāṁ kurvantu rakṣāṁsi na me asti manasaḥ śramaḥ ||

> **Meaning 16:** "Let the Rākṣasas torment me as they like, with more bindings
> and with the burning of my tail. I feel no weariness in my mind."

---
17.
tataste saṁvr̥tākāraṁ sattvavantaṁ mahākapim |
parigr̥hya yayurhr̥ṣṭā rākṣasāḥ kapikuñjaram ||

> **Meaning 17:** Then the delighted Rākṣasas took hold of the mighty
> Kapikuñjara, who stayed calm and composed, and led him away.

---
18.
śaṅkhabherīninādaistaiḥ ghoṣayantaḥ svakarmabhiḥ |
rākṣasāḥ krūrakarmāṇaḥ cārayanti sma tāṁ purīm ||

> **Meaning 18:** Blowing conchs and beating drums, proclaiming their deeds, the
> cruel Rākṣasas paraded him through that city.

---
19.
anvīyamānō rakṣōbhiḥ yayau sukhamariṁdamaḥ |
hanumāṁścārayām āsa rākṣasānāṁ mahāpurīm ||


> **Meaning 19:** Followed by the Rākṣasas, Hanumān, destroyer of enemies,
> walked easily through the great city of the Rākṣasas.

---
20.
athāpaśyad vimānāni vicitrāṇi mahākapiḥ |
saṁvr̥tān bhūmibhāgāṁśca suvibhaktāṁśca catvarān ||


> **Meaning 20:** Then the great Kapi saw wonderful mansions, enclosed stretches
> of ground, and well-laid-out squares.

---
21.
vīthīśca gr̥hasaṁbādhāḥ kapiśśr̥ṁgāṭakāni ca |
tathā rathyōparathyāśca tathaiva gr̥hakāṁtarān |
gr̥hāṁśca mēghasaṁkāśān dadarśa pavanātmajaḥ ||

22.
catvareṣu catuṣkeṣu rājamārge tathaiva ca |
ghoṣayanti kapiṁ sarve cārīka iti rākṣasāḥ ||

> **Meaning 21-22:** Hanumān, son of the Wind, saw the lanes crowded with
> houses, the crossroads, the main streets and side streets, the spaces between
> houses, and houses like clouds. At the squares, crossroads and royal highway,
> all the Rākṣasas proclaimed that the Kapi was a spy.

---
23.
strībālavr̥ddhā nirjagmuḥ tatra tatra kutūhalāt |
taṁ pradīpita lāṁgūlaṁ hanumaṁtaṁ didr̥kṣavaḥ ||

24.
dīpyamāne tatastasya lāṅgūlāgre hanūmataḥ |
rākṣasyastā virūpākṣyaḥ śaṁsurdevyāstad apriyam ||

> **Meaning 23-24:** Women, children and the old came out here and there,
> curious to see Hanumān with his burning tail. As the tip of Hanumān's tail
> blazed, the ugly-eyed Rākṣasīs carried the unwelcome news to the goddess Sītā.

---
25.
yastvayā kr̥tasaṁvādaḥ sīte tāmramukhaḥ kapiḥ |
lāṅgūlena pradīptena sa eṣa pariṇīyate ||

> **Meaning 25:** "Sītā, the copper-faced Kapi who spoke with you is being
> paraded around with his tail on fire."

---
26.
śrutvā tad vacanaṁ krūram ātmāpaharaṇopamam |
vaidehī śokasaṁtaptā hutāśanam upāgamat ||

> **Meaning 26:** Hearing those cruel words, which felt as agonizing as her
> own abduction, Vaidehī, tormented with grief, turned in prayer to the Fire-god.

---
27.
maṅgalābhimukhī tasya sā tadāsīn mahākapeḥ |
upatasthe viśālākṣī prayatā havyavāhanam ||

> **Meaning 27:** The wide-eyed Sītā, pure and controlled, stood facing Agni and
> prayed for the good of Hanumān.

---
28.
yady asti patiśuśrūṣā yady asti caritaṁ tapaḥ |
yadi cāsty ekapatnītvaṁ śīto bhava hanūmataḥ ||

> **Meaning 28:** "If I have served my husband, if I have performed austerities,
> and if I have been devoted to one husband alone, be cool for Hanumān."

---
29.
yadi kaścid anukrośaḥ tasya mayy asti dhīmataḥ |
yadi vā bhāgyaśeṣaṁ me śīto bhava hanūmataḥ ||

> **Meaning 29:** "If that wise one has any compassion for me, or if any good
> fortune remains for me, be cool for Hanumān."

---
30.
yadi māṁ vr̥ttasaṁpannāṁ tatsamāgamalālasām |
sa vijānāti dharmātmā śīto bhava hanūmataḥ ||

> **Meaning 30:** "If that righteous one knows that I am virtuous and longing to
> be reunited with him, be cool for Hanumān."

---
31.
yadi māṁ tārayaty āryaḥ sugrīvaḥ satyasaṁgaraḥ |
asmād duḥkhān mahābāhuḥ śīto bhava hanūmataḥ ||

> **Meaning 31:** "If noble Sugrīva, true to his word, will bring me across from
> this sorrow, be cool for Hanumān."

---
32.
tatastīkṣṇārciravyagraḥ pradakṣiṇaśikhaḥ analaḥ |
jajvāla mr̥gaśāvākṣyāḥ śaṁsann iva śivaṁ kapeḥ ||

> **Meaning 32:** Then the fire, with sharp flames and calm, its tongue turning
> to the right, blazed up as if announcing the Kapi's safety to the doe-eyed
> Sītā.

---

33.
hanumajjanakaścāpi pucchānalayutaḥ anilaḥ |
vavau svāsthyakarō dēvyāḥ prālēyānilaśītalaḥ ||

> **Meaning 33:** Hanumān's father, Anila, the Wind, also blew, carrying a
> snow-cool chill that comforted the goddess.

---

34.
dahyamāne ca lāṅgūle cintayām āsa vānaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 34:** As his tail burned, the Vānara began to think.

---
35.
pradīptaḥ agnirayaṁ kasmān na māṁ dahati sarvataḥ |
dr̥śyate ca mahājvālaḥ karoti ca na me rujam ||

> **Meaning 35:** "This fire is blazing all around, so why does it not burn me?
> Its flames are huge and visible, yet it causes me no pain."

---
36.
śiśirasyeva saṁpātaḥ lāṅgūlāgre pratiṣṭhitaḥ |
atha vā tad idaṁ vyaktaṁ yad dr̥ṣṭaṁ plavatā mayā ||

> **Meaning 36:** "It feels like a fall of frost on the tip of my tail. Or else
> what I saw while leaping over the ocean must be true."

---
37.
rāmaprabhāvād āścaryaṁ parvataḥ saritāṁ patau |
yadi tāvat samudrasya mainākasya ca dhīmatha |
rāmārthaṁ saṁbhramastādr̥k kim agnirna kariṣyati ||

> **Meaning 37:** "It is Rāma's power. It is amazing that the mountain Maināka
> and the ocean, lord of rivers, acted with such eagerness for Rāma's sake. Then
> why would fire not do the same?"

---
38.
sītāyāścānr̥śaṁsyena tejasā rāghavasya ca |
pituśca mama sakhyena na māṁ dahati pāvakaḥ ||

> **Meaning 38:** "Through Sītā's compassion, Rāghava's power, and my father's
> friendship with Agni, the fire does not burn me."

---
39.
bhūyaḥ sa cintayām āsa muhūrtaṁ kapikuñjaraḥ ||

40.
kathamasmadvidhasyeha bandhanaṁ rākṣasādhamaiḥ |
pratikriyā syādyuktā syāt satī mahyaṁ parākrame ||

41.
tataśchittvā ca tān pāśān vegavān vai mahākapiḥ |
utpapātātha vegena nanāda ca mahākapiḥ ||

> **Meaning 39-41:** The Kapikuñjara thought again for a moment. "How can
> binding by these lowest Rākṣasas be fitting for someone like me? It would be
> right for me to respond with my strength." Then the swift Hanumān broke those
> ropes, leapt up with great speed, and roared aloud.

---
42.
puradvāraṁ tataḥ śrīmāñ śailaśr̥ṅgam ivonnatam |
vibhaktarakṣaḥsaṁbādham āsasādānilātmajaḥ ||

> **Meaning 42:** Then the glorious son of the Wind reached the city gate, high
> as a mountain peak, where the Rākṣasas were thinly spread out.

---
43.
sa bhūtvā śailasaṁkāśaḥ kṣaṇena punarātmavān |
hrasvatāṁ paramāṁ prāptaḥ bandhanāny avaśātayat ||

> **Meaning 43:** Becoming huge as a mountain, then in an instant shrinking to
> the smallest size, that self-controlled one slipped out of his bindings.

---
44.
vimuktaścābhavacchrīmān punaḥ parvatasaṁnibhaḥ |
vīkṣamāṇaśca dadr̥śe parighaṁ toraṇāśritam ||

> **Meaning 44:** Freed, the glorious Hanumān again became like a mountain, and
> looking around he noticed an iron bar resting at the archway.

---
45.
sa taṁ gr̥hya mahābāhuḥ kālāyasapariṣkr̥tam |
rakṣiṇastān punaḥ sarvān sūdayām āsa mārutiḥ ||

> **Meaning 45:** The mighty-armed Māruti seized that bar, which was fitted with
> black iron, and killed all the guards again.

---
46.
sa tān nihatvā raṇacaṇḍavikramaḥ samīkṣamāṇaḥ punareva laṅkām |
pradīptalāṅgūlakr̥tārcimālī prakāśatāditya ivāṁśumālī ||

> **Meaning 46:** Having killed them, Hanumān, fierce and fearless in battle,
> looked toward Laṅkā once more. With his blazing tail making a garland of
> flames, he shone like the sun with its rays.

---
