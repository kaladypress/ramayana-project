=== Sarga 21 ===

---

1.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā sītā raudrasya rakṣasaḥ |
ārtā dīnasvarā dīnaṁ pratyuvāca śanair vacaḥ ||

2.
duḥkhārtā rudatī sītā vepamānā tapasvinī |
cintayantī varārohā patim eva pativratā ||

3.
tr̥ṇam antarataḥ kr̥tvā pratyuvāca śucismitā |
nivartaya mano mattaḥ svajane kriyatāṁ manaḥ ||

4.
na māṁ prārthayituṁ yuktas tvaṁ siddhim iva pāpakr̥t |
akāryaṁ na mayā kāryam ekapatnyā vigarhitam ||

> **Meaning 1-4:** Hearing the words of that fierce Rākṣasa, Sītā,
> afflicted and sorrowful, slowly gave this reply. Stricken with grief,
> weeping, trembling, devoted to her penance, that faithful wife thought
> only of her husband. Placing a blade of grass between herself and
> Rāvaṇa, the pure-hearted Sītā replied: "Turn your mind away from me
> and fix it upon your own wives. You are not fit to desire me, just as
> a sinner is not fit to attain spiritual perfection. I am the wife of
> Śrī Rāmacandra alone, devoted to a single husband. Such a despicable
> deed can never be done by me."
---

5.
kulaṁ samprāptayā puṇyaṁ kule mahati jātayā |
evam uktvā tu vaidehī rāvaṇaṁ taṁ yaśasvinī ||

6.
rākṣasaṁ pr̥ṣṭhataḥ kr̥tvā bhūyo vacanam abravīt |
nāham aupayikī bhāryā parabhāryā satī tava ||

7.
sādhu dharmam avekṣasva sādhu sādhuvrataṁ cara |
yathā tava tathānyeṣāṁ rakṣyā dārā niśācara |
ātmānam upamāṁ kr̥tvā sveṣu dāreṣu ramyatām ||

8.
atuṣṭaṁ sveṣu dāreṣu capalaṁ calitendriyam |
nayanti nikr̥tiprajñāṁ paradārāḥ parābhavam ||

> **Meaning 5-8:** Having spoken thus to Rāvaṇa, the illustrious
> Vaidehī — born in the great lineage of Janaka, wedded into the sacred
> house of the Ikṣvākus — turned her back to him and spoke again: "A
> chaste woman and the wife of another, I can never become yours.
> Observe dharma! Follow the vow of the virtuous! O night-ranger! Just
> as your wives are to be protected by you, so too are the wives of
> others. Taking yourself as the measure, delight only in your own
> wives. A man dissatisfied with his own wives, unable to master his
> restless senses — such a wretch is brought to ruin by the wives of
> others."
---

9.
iha santo na vā santi sato vā nānuvartase |
tathā hi viparītā te buddhir ācāravarjitā ||

10.
vaco mithyā praṇītātmā pathyam uktaṁ vicakṣaṇaiḥ |
rākṣasānām abhāvāya tvaṁ vā na pratipadyase ||

11.
akr̥tātmānam āsādya rājānam anaye ratam |
samr̥ddhāni vinaśyanti rāṣṭrāṇi nagarāṇi ca ||

12.
tatheyaṁ tvāṁ samāsādya laṅkā ratnaughasaṅkulā |
aparādhāt tavaikasya nacirād vinaśiṣyati ||

13.
svakr̥tair hanyamānasya rāvaṇādīrghadarśinaḥ |
abhinandanti bhūtāni vināśe pāpakarmaṇaḥ ||

14.
evaṁ tvāṁ pāpakarmāṇaṁ vakṣyanti nikr̥tā janāḥ |
diṣṭyaitad vyasanaṁ prāpto raudra ity eva harṣitāḥ ||

> **Meaning 9-14:** "Are there no virtuous people here? Or do you not follow the
> virtuous? That is why your mind is so perverted and devoid of good conduct. You,
> whose mind is guided by falsehood, surely do not heed the beneficial words
> spoken by the wise, and it will lead to the destruction of the Rākṣasas. Having
> attained an unrefined king who delights in unjust ways, prosperous kingdoms and
> cities meet their destruction. Similarly, this Laṅkā, filled with multitudes of
> gems, having attained you, will soon be destroyed due to the offense of you
> alone. When a short-sighted and sinful person is struck down by his own deeds,
> all beings rejoice at his destruction, O Rāvaṇa! Thus, those people who were
> oppressed by you will speak of you, a perpetrator of sinful deeds, with joy: 'By
> good fortune, this fierce one has met his doom!'"
---

15.
śakyā lobhayituṁ nāham aiśvaryeṇa dhanena vā |
ananyā rāghaveṇāhaṁ bhāskareṇa prabhā yathā ||

16.
upadhāya bhujaṁ tasya lokanāthasya satkr̥tam |
kathaṁ nāmopadhāsyāmi bhujam anyasya kasya cit ||

17.
aham aupayikī bhāryā tasyaiva vasudhāpateḥ |
vratasnātasya viprasya vidyeva viditātmanaḥ ||

18.
sādhu rāvaṇa rāmeṇa māṁ samānaya duḥkhitām |
vane vāśitayā sārdhaṁ kareṇveva gajādhipam ||

> **Meaning 15-18:** "I cannot be tempted by power or by wealth. I am
> inseparable from Rāghava, as sunlight is from the sun. Having rested
> upon the honored arm of the Protector of the World, how could I ever
> rest upon the arm of another? I am the rightful wife of that King of
> the Earth alone — as sacred knowledge belongs to a self-realized
> Brahmin who has fulfilled his vows. O Rāvaṇa, if you would act
> righteously — as a wild she-elephant is reunited with the lord of the
> herd, reunite me, who am stricken with sorrow, with Śrī Rāma."
---

19.
mitram aupayikaṁ kartuṁ rāmaḥ sthānaṁ parīpsatā |
vadhaṁ cānicchatā ghoraṁ tvayāsau puruṣarṣabhaḥ ||

20.
viditaḥ sa hi dharmajñaḥ śaraṇāgatavatsalaḥ |
tena maitrī bhavatu te yadi jīvitum icchasi ||

21.
prasādayasva tvaṁ cainaṁ śaraṇāgatavatsalam |
māṁ cāsmai prayato bhūtvā niryātayitum arhasi ||

22.
evaṁ hi te bhavet svasti sampradāya raghūttame |
anyathā tvaṁ hi kurvāṇo vadhaṁ prāpsyasi rāvaṇa ||

> **Meaning 19-22:** "That foremost among men, Śrī Rāma, is the most suitable one
> to make an ally by you, if you wish to preserve your position and do not desire
> a terrible death. He is known to be the knower of dharma and is affectionate to
> those who seek refuge. Let there be friendship between you and him, if you wish
> to live. Propitiate him, who is affectionate to those who seek refuge! With a
> pure heart, you ought to return me to him. By doing thus, restoring me to the
> foremost of the Raghus, well-being will come to you. By doing otherwise, O
> Rāvaṇa, you will surely meet your death."
---

23.
varjayed vajram utsr̥ṣṭaṁ varjayed antakaś ciram |
tvadvidhaṁ na tu saṅkruddho lokanāthaḥ sa rāghavaḥ ||

24.
rāmasya dhanuṣaḥ śabdaṁ śroṣyasi tvaṁ mahāsvanam |
śatakratuvisr̥ṣṭasya nirghoṣam aśaner iva ||

25.
iha śīghraṁ suparvāṇo jvalitāsyā ivoragāḥ |
iṣavo nipatiṣyanti rāmalakṣmaṇalakṣaṇāḥ ||

26.
rakṣāṁsi parinighnantaḥ puryām asyāṁ samantataḥ |
asampātaṁ kariṣyanti patantaḥ kaṅkavāsasaḥ ||

27.
rākṣasendramahāsarpān sa rāmagaruḍo mahān |
uddhariṣyati vegena vainateya ivoragān ||

28.
apaneṣyati māṁ bhartā tvattaḥ śīghram arindamaḥ |
asurebhyaḥ śriyaṁ dīptāṁ viṣṇus tribhir iva kramaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 23-28:** "A hurled thunderbolt might spare you; even the god of death
> might spare you for a long time; but that Lord of the World, Rāghava, when
> enraged, will not spare someone like you! You shall hear the great twang of Śrī
> Rāma's bow, resembling the roar of a thunderbolt hurled by Indra. Divine arrows
> bearing the names of Śrī Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa, possessing smooth joints and spewing
> fire like hissing serpents, will soon fall upon this city. Slaughtering the
> Rākṣasas all around, those continuous showers of arrows, feathered with heron
> plumes, will completely cover Laṅkā, leaving not even a speck of space
> untouched. That great Garuḍa in the form of the highly valorous Śrī Rāma will
> swiftly destroy the serpents in the form of the Rākṣasas, just as Vainateya
> destroys real serpents. My husband, the destroyer of enemies, will swiftly take
> me away from you, just as Lord Viṣṇu, taking the form of Trivikrama, took back
> the radiant Goddess of Fortune from the Asuras with His three strides."
---

29.
janasthāne hatasthāne nihate rakṣasāṁ bale |
aśaktena tvayā rakṣaḥ kr̥tam etad asādhu vai ||

30.
āśramaṁ tu tayoḥ śūnyaṁ praviśya narasiṁhayoḥ |
gocaraṁ gatayor bhrātror apanītā tvayādhama ||

31.
na hi gandham upāghrāya rāmalakṣmaṇayos tvayā |
śakyaṁ sandarśane sthātuṁ śunā śārdūlayor iva ||

32.
tasya te vigrahe tābhyāṁ yugagrahaṇam asthiram |
vr̥trasyevendrabāhubhyāṁ bāhor ekasya nigrahaḥ ||

33.
kṣipraṁ tava sa nātho me rāmaḥ saumitriṇā saha |
toyam alpam ivādityaḥ prāṇān ādāsyate śaraiḥ ||

34.
giriṁ kuberasya gato ’tha vālayaṁ sabhāṁ gato vā varuṇasya rājñaḥ |
asaṁśayaṁ dāśarather na mokṣyase mahādrumaḥ kālahato ’śaner iva ||

> **Meaning 29-34:** "O Rākṣasa! When the army of the Rākṣasas was slain and
> Janasthāna was devastated, this wicked deed was committed by you, who were
> powerless to face them directly. O vile one! Entering their empty hermitage when
> those two brothers, the lions among men, had gone out of sight, I was abducted
> by you. Just as a dog cannot stand the sight after catching the scent of two
> tigers, you are not capable of standing before Śrī Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa after
> merely catching their scent! If you were to engage in battle with them, your
> grasp on the yoke of combat would be unsteady, just as Vr̥trāsura, fighting
> with a single arm against Indra's two arms, faced certain defeat. My husband,
> Śrī Rāma, along with Lakṣmaṇa, will swiftly dry up your life-breath with his
> arrows, just as the sun dries up a shallow puddle of water. Even if you flee
> to the mountain of Kubera, or enter his abode, or go to the court of King
> Varuṇa, you will undoubtedly not escape from the son of Daśaratha, just as a
> massive tree cannot escape being struck by lightning."
---
