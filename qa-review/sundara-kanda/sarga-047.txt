=== Sarga 47 ===

---
1.
tataḥ sa karmaṇā tasya vismito bhīmavikramaḥ |
hanumān roṣatāmrākṣo rakṣo’dhipam avaikṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
bhājamānaṁ mahārheṇa kāñcanena virājatā |
muktājālāvr̥tenātha mukuṭena mahādyutim ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
vajrasaṁyogasaṁyuktair mahārhamaṇivigrahaiḥ |
haimair ābharaṇaiś citrair manaseva prakalpitaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
mahārhakṣaumasaṁvītaṁ raktacandanarūṣitam |
svanuliptaṁ vicitrābhir vividhabhiś ca bhaktibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
vipulair darśanīyaiś ca rakṣākṣair bhīmadarśanaiḥ |
dīptatīkṣṇamahādaṁṣṭraiḥ pralambadaśanacchadaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
śirobhir daśabhir vīraṁ bhrājamānaṁ mahaujasaṁ |
nānāvyālasamākīrṇaiḥ śikharair iva mandaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
nīlāñjanacaya prakhyaṁ hāreṇorasi rājatā |
pūrṇacandrābhavaktreṇa sabalākam ivāmbudam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
bāhubhir baddhakeyūraiś candanottamarūṣitaiḥ |
bhrājamānāṅgadaiḥ pīnaiḥ pañcaśīrṣair ivoragaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
mahati sphāṭike citre ratnasaṁyogasaṁskr̥te |
uttamāstaraṇāstīrṇe upaviṣṭaṁ varāsane ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
alaṁkr̥tābhir atyarthaṁ pramadābhiḥ samantataḥ |
vālavyajanahastābhir ārāt samupasevitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
durdhareṇa prahastena mahāpārśvena rakṣasā |
mantribhir mantratattvajñair nikumbhena ca mantriṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
upopaviṣṭaṁ rakṣobhiś caturbhir baladarpitaiḥ |
kr̥tsnaiḥ parivr̥taṁ lokaṁ caturbhir iva sāgaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
mantribhir mantratattvajñair anyaiś ca śubhabuddhibhiḥ |
anvāsyamānaṁ sacivaiḥ surair iva sureśvaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
apaśyad rākṣasapatiṁ hanūmān atitejasaṁ |
viṣṭhitaṁ meruśikhare satoyam iva toyadam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa taiḥ saṁpīḍyamāno ’pi rakṣobhir bhīmavikramaiḥ |
vismayaṁ paramaṁ gatvā rakṣo’dhipam avaikṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
bhrājamānaṁ tato dr̥ṣṭvā hanumān rākṣaseśvaram |
manasā cintayām āsa tejasā tasya mohitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
aho rūpam aho dhairyam aho sattvam aho dyutiḥ |
aho rākṣasarājasya sarvalakṣaṇayuktatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
yady adharmo na balavān syād ayaṁ rākṣaseśvaraḥ |
syād ayaṁ suralokasya saśakrasyāpi rakṣitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tena bibhyati khalv asmāl lokāḥ sāmaradānavāḥ |
ayaṁ hy utsahate kruddhaḥ kartum ekārṇavaṁ jagat ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
iti cintāṁ bahuvidhām akaron matimān kapiḥ |
dr̥ṣṭvā rākṣasarājasya prabhāvam amitaujasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
