=== Sarga 46 ===

---
1.
tatas tu rakṣo’dhipatir mahātmā; hanūmatākṣe nihate kumāre |
manaḥ samādhāya tadendrakalpaṁ; samādideśendrajitaṁ sa roṣāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tvam astravic chastrabhr̥tāṁ variṣṭhaḥ; surāsurāṇām api śokadātā |
sureṣu sendreṣu ca dr̥ṣṭakarmā; pitāmahārādhanasaṁcitāstraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tavāstrabalam āsādya nāsurā na marudgaṇāḥ |
na kaś cit triṣu lokeṣu saṁyuge na gataśramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
bhujavīryābhiguptaś ca tapasā cābhirakṣitaḥ |
deśakālavibhāgajñas tvam eva matisattamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
na te ’sty aśakyaṁ samareṣu karmaṇā; na te ’sty akāryaṁ matipūrvamantraṇe |
na so ’sti kaś cit triṣu saṁgraheṣu vai; na veda yas te ’strabalaṁ balaṁ ca te ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
mamānurūpaṁ tapaso balaṁ ca te; parākramaś cāstrabalaṁ ca saṁyuge |
na tvāṁ samāsādya raṇāvamarde; manaḥ śramaṁ gacchati niścitārtham ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
nihatā iṁkarāḥ sarve jambumālī ca rākṣasaḥ |
amātyaputrā vīrāś ca pañca senāgrayāyinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sahodaras te dayitaḥ kumāro ’kṣaś ca sūditaḥ |
na tu teṣv eva me sāro yas tvayy ariniṣūdana ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
idaṁ hi dr̥ṣṭvā matiman mahad balaṁ; kapeḥ prabhāvaṁ ca parākramaṁ ca |
tvam ātmanaś cāpi samīkṣya sāraṁ; kuruṣva vegaṁ svabalānurūpam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
balāvamardas tvayi saṁnikr̥ṣṭe; yathā gate śāmyati śāntaśatrau |
tathā samīkṣyātmabalaṁ paraṁ ca; samārabhasvāstravidāṁ variṣṭha ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
na khalv iyaṁ matiḥ śreṣṭhā yat tvāṁ saṁpreṣayāmy aham |
iyaṁ ca rājadharmāṇāṁ kṣatrasya ca matir matā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
nānāśastraiś ca saṁgrāme vaiśāradyam ariṁdama |
avaśyam eva boddhavyaṁ kāmyaś ca vijayo raṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tataḥ pitus tad vacanaṁ niśamya; pradakṣiṇaṁ dakṣasutaprabhāvaḥ |
cakāra bhartāram adīnasattvo; raṇāya vīraḥ pratipannabuddhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tatas taiḥ svagaṇair iṣṭair indrajit pratipūjitaḥ |
yuddhoddhatakr̥totsāhaḥ saṁgrāmaṁ pratipadyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
śrīmān padmapalāśākṣo rākṣasādhipateḥ sutaḥ |
nirjagāma mahātejāḥ samudra iva parvasu ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sa pakṣi rājopamatulyavegair; vyālaiś caturbhiḥ sitatīkṣṇadaṁṣṭraiḥ |
rathaṁ samāyuktam asaṁgavegaṁ; samārurohendrajid indrakalpaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
sa rathī dhanvināṁ śreṣṭhaḥ śastrajño ’stravidāṁ varaḥ |
rathenābhiyayau kṣipraṁ hanūmān yatra so ’bhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
sa tasya rathanirghoṣaṁ jyāsvanaṁ kārmukasya ca |
niśamya harivīro ’sau saṁprahr̥ṣṭataro ’bhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
sumahac cāpam ādāya śitaśalyāṁś ca sāyakān |
hanūmantam abhipretya jagāma raṇapaṇḍitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tasmiṁs tataḥ saṁyati jātaharṣe; raṇāya nirgacchati bāṇapāṇau |
diśaś ca sarvāḥ kaluṣā babhūvur; mr̥gāś ca raudrā bahudhā vineduḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
samāgatās tatra tu nāgayakṣā; maharṣayaś cakracarāś ca siddhāḥ |
nabhaḥ samāvr̥tya ca pakṣisaṁghā; vinedur uccaiḥ paramaprahr̥ṣṭāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
āyantaṁ sarathaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā tūrṇam indrajitaṁ kapiḥ |
vinanāda mahānādaṁ vyavardhata ca vegavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
indrajit tu rathaṁ divyam āsthitaś citrakārmukaḥ |
dhanur visphārayām āsa taḍidūrjitaniḥsvanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tataḥ sametāv atitīkṣṇavegau; mahābalau tau raṇanirviśaṅkau |
kapiś ca rakṣo’dhipateś ca putraḥ; surāsurendrāv iva baddhavairau ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
sa tasya vīrasya mahārathasyā; dhanuṣmataḥ saṁyati saṁmatasya |
śarapravegaṁ vyahanat pravr̥ddhaś; cacāra mārge pitur aprameyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tataḥ śarān āyatatīkṣṇaśalyān; supatriṇaḥ kāñcanacitrapuṅkhān |
mumoca vīraḥ paravīrahantā; susaṁtatān vajranipātavegān ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
sa tasya tat syandananiḥsvanaṁ ca; mr̥daṅgabherīpaṭahasvanaṁ ca |
vikr̥ṣyamāṇasya ca kārmukasya; niśamya ghoṣaṁ punar utpapāta ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
śarāṇām antareṣv āśu vyavartata mahākapiḥ |
haris tasyābhilakṣasya mokṣayam̐l lakṣyasaṁgraham ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
śarāṇām agratas tasya punaḥ samabhivartata |
prasārya hastau hanumān utpapātānilātmajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
tāv ubhau vegasaṁpannau raṇakarmaviśāradau |
sarvabhūtamanogrāhi cakratur yuddham uttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
hanūmato veda na rākṣaso ’ntaraṁ; na mārutis tasya mahātmano ’ntaram |
parasparaṁ nirviṣahau babhūvatuḥ; sametya tau devasamānavikramau ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
tatas tu lakṣye sa vihanyamāne; śareṣu mogheṣu ca saṁpatatsu |
jagāma cintāṁ mahatīṁ mahātmā; samādhisaṁyogasamāhitātmā ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
tato matiṁ rākṣasarājasūnuś; cakāra tasmin harivīramukhye |
avadhyatāṁ tasya kapeḥ samīkṣya; kathaṁ nigacched iti nigrahārtham ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
tataḥ paitāmahāṁ vīraḥ so ’stram astravidāṁ varaḥ |
saṁdadhe sumahātejās taṁ haripravaraṁ prati ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
avadhyo ’yam iti jñātvā tam astreṇāstratattvavit |
nijagrāha mahābāhur mārutātmajam indrajit ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
tena baddhas tato ’streṇa rākṣasena sa vānaraḥ |
abhavan nirviceṣṭaś ca papāta ca mahītale ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
tato ’tha buddhvā sa tadāstrabandhaṁ; prabhoḥ prabhāvād vigatālpavegaḥ |
pitāmahānugraham ātmanaś ca; vicintayām āsa haripravīraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
tataḥ svāyambhuvair mantrair brahmāstram abhimantritam |
hanūmāṁś cintayām āsa varadānaṁ pitāmahāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
na me ’strabandhasya ca śaktir asti; vimokṣaṇe lokaguroḥ prabhāvāt |
ity evam evaṁvihito ’strabandho; mayātmayoner anuvartitavyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
sa vīryam astrasya kapir vicārya; pitāmahānugraham ātmanaś ca |
vimokṣaśaktiṁ paricintayitvā; pitāmahājñām anuvartate sma ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
astreṇāpi hi baddhasya bhayaṁ mama na jāyate |
pitāmahamahendrābhyāṁ rakṣitasyānilena ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
grahaṇe cāpi rakṣobhir mahan me guṇadarśanam |
rākṣasendreṇa saṁvādas tasmād gr̥hṇantu māṁ pare ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
sa niścitārthaḥ paravīrahantā; samīkṣya karī vinivr̥ttaceṣṭaḥ |
paraiḥ prasahyābhigatair nigr̥hya; nanāda tais taiḥ paribhartsyamānaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
tatas taṁ rākṣasā dr̥ṣṭvā nirviceṣṭam ariṁdamam |
babandhuḥ śaṇavalkaiś ca drumacīraiś ca saṁhataiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
sa rocayām āsa paraiś ca bandhanaṁ; prasahya vīrair abhinigrahaṁ ca |
kautūhalān māṁ yadi rākṣasendro; draṣṭuṁ vyavasyed iti niścitārthaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
sa baddhas tena valkena vimukto ’streṇa vīryavān |
astrabandhaḥ sa cānyaṁ hi na bandham anuvartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
athendrajit taṁ drumacīrabandhaṁ; vicārya vīraḥ kapisattamaṁ tam |
vimuktam astreṇa jagāma cintām; anyena baddho hy anuvartate ’stram ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
aho mahat karma kr̥taṁ nirarthakaṁ; na rākṣasair mantragatir vimr̥ṣṭā |
punaś ca nāstre vihate ’stram anyat; pravartate saṁśayitāḥ sma sarve ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
astreṇa hanumān mukto nātmānam avabudhyate |
kr̥ṣyamāṇas tu rakṣobhis taiś ca bandhair nipīḍitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
hanyamānas tataḥ krūrai rākṣasaiḥ kāṣṭhamuṣṭibhiḥ |
samīpaṁ rākṣasendrasya prākr̥ṣyata sa vānaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
athendrajit taṁ prasamīkṣya muktam; astreṇa baddhaṁ drumacīrasūtraiḥ |
vyadarśayat tatra mahābalaṁ taṁ; haripravīraṁ sagaṇāya rājñe ||

=== meaning ===

---
52.
taṁ mattam iva mātaṅgaṁ baddhaṁ kapivarottamam |
rākṣasā rākṣasendrāya rāvaṇāya nyavedayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
53.
ko ’yaṁ kasya kuto vāpi kiṁ kāryaṁ ko vyapāśrayaḥ |
iti rākṣasavīrāṇāṁ tatra saṁjajñire kathāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
54.
hanyatāṁ dahyatāṁ vāpi bhakṣyatām iti cāpare |
rākṣasās tatra saṁkruddhāḥ parasparam athābruvan ||

=== meaning ===

---
55.
atītya mārgaṁ sahasā mahātmā; sa tatra rakṣo’dhipapādamūle |
dadarśa rājñaḥ paricāravr̥ddhān; gr̥haṁ mahāratnavibhūṣitaṁ ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
56.
sa dadarśa mahātejā rāvaṇaḥ kapisattamam |
rakṣobhir vikr̥tākāraiḥ kr̥ṣyamāṇam itas tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
57.
rākṣasādhipatiṁ cāpi dadarśa kapisattamaḥ |
tejobalasamāyuktaṁ tapantam iva bhāskaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
58.
sa roṣasaṁvartitatāmradr̥ṣṭir; daśānanas taṁ kapim anvavekṣya |
athopaviṣṭān kulaśīlavr̥ddhān; samādiśat taṁ prati mantramukhyān ||

=== meaning ===

---
59.
yathākramaṁ taiḥ sa kapiś ca pr̥ṣṭaḥ; kāryārtham arthasya ca mūlam ādau |
nivedayām āsa harīśvarasya; dūtaḥ sakāśād aham āgato ’smi ||

=== meaning ===

---
