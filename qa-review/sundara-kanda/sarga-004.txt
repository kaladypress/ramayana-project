=== Sarga 4 ===

---
1.
tataḥ sa madhyaṁ gatam aṁśumantaṁ; jyotsnāvitānaṁ mahad udvamantam |
dadarśa dhīmān divi bhānumantaṁ; goṣṭhe vr̥ṣaṁ mattam iva bhramantam ||

> **Meaning 1:** Then the wise Kapi saw the radiant moon climbed to mid-sky,
> spreading a vast canopy of moonlight, roaming across the heavens like an
> impassioned bull wandering in its pen.

---
2.
lokasya pāpāni vināśayantaṁ; mahodadhiṁ cāpi samedhayantam |
bhūtāni sarvāṇi virājayantaṁ; dadarśa śītāṁśum athābhiyāntam ||

> **Meaning 2:** He watched the cool-rayed moon gliding on, dispelling the
> darkness and sorrows of the world, swelling the great ocean with its tides,
> and illuminating all living beings.

---
3.
yā bhāti lakṣmīr bhuvi mandarasthā; tathā pradoṣeṣu ca sāgarasthā |
tathaiva toyeṣu ca puṣkarasthā; rarāja sā cāruniśākarasthā ||

> **Meaning 3:** The same splendor that shines on Mount Mandara on earth, that
> glows upon the sea at dusk, and that rests upon the lotus in the waters, now
> blazed forth upon that lovely moon.

---
4.
haṁso yathā rājatapañjurasthaḥ; siṁho yathā mandarakandarasthaḥ |
vīro yathā garvitakuñjarasthaś; candro ’pi babhrāja tathāmbarasthaḥ ||

> **Meaning 4:** Like a swan resting in a cage of silver, like a lion in a cave
> on Mount Mandara, or like a proud hero mounted on a spirited elephant, the
> moon shone high in the sky.

---
5.
sthitaḥ kakudmān iva tīkṣṇaśr̥ṅgo; mahācalaḥ śveta ivoccaśr̥ṅgaḥ |
hastīva jāmbūnadabaddhaśr̥ṅgo; vibhāti candraḥ paripūrṇaśr̥ṅgaḥ ||

> **Meaning 5:** With its horns full and sharp, the moon shone like a horned
> bull, like a towering white mountain with lofty peaks, or like an elephant
> with tusks capped in gold.

---
6.
prakāśacandrodayanaṣṭadoṣaḥ; pravr̥ddharakṣaḥ piśitāśadoṣaḥ |
rāmābhirāmeritacittadoṣaḥ; svargaprakāśo bhagavān pradoṣaḥ ||

> **Meaning 6:** With the darkness dispelled by the bright moonrise, the night
> was alive with fierce flesh-eating Rākṣasas. Stirs of passion touched hearts
> at the sight of lovely women, and that divine night sparkled with the splendor
> of heaven.

---
7.
tantrī svanāḥ karṇasukhāḥ pravr̥ttāḥ; svapanti nāryaḥ patibhiḥ suvr̥ttāḥ |
naktaṁcarāś cāpi tathā pravr̥ttā; vihartum atyadbhutaraudravr̥ttāḥ ||

> **Meaning 7:** Sweet strains of lute strings, pleasing to the ear, echoed
> softly as faithful wives slept beside their husbands. Meanwhile,
> night-stalkers of astonishing and terrifying deeds set out to revel in the
> night.

---
8.
mattapramattāni samākulāni; rathāśvabhadrāsanasaṁkulāni |
vīraśriyā cāpi samākulāni; dadarśa dhīmān sa kapiḥ kulāni ||

> **Meaning 8:** The wise Kapi observed noble households bustling with people
> intoxicated and carefree, packed with chariots, horses, and splendid thrones,
> overflowing with the glory of warriors.

---
9.
parasparaṁ cādhikam ākṣipanti; bhujāṁś ca pīnān adhivikṣipanti |
mattapralāpān adhivikṣipanti; mattāni cānyonyam adhikṣipanti ||

> **Meaning 9:** The demons jostled and challenged one another, flinging their
> muscular arms about, babbling drunken talk, and hurling insults at each other
> in their intoxication.

---
10.
rakṣāṁsi vakṣāṁsi ca vikṣipanti; gātrāṇi kāntāsu ca vikṣipanti |
dadarśa kāntāś ca samālapanti; tathāparās tatra punaḥ svapanti ||

> **Meaning 10:** He saw Rākṣasas puffing out their chests, draping their bodies
> over their lovers, while some women talked intimately with their partners and
> others lay asleep.

---
11.
mahāgajaiś cāpi tathā nadadbhiḥ; sūpūjitaiś cāpi tathā susadbhiḥ |
rarāja vīraiś ca viniḥśvasadbhir; hrado bhujaṅgair iva niḥśvasadbhiḥ ||

> **Meaning 11:** With trumpeting great elephants, well-honored noble warriors,
> and heroes breathing deeply in their might, the city shone like a deep lake
> filled with hissing serpents.

---
12.
buddhipradhānān rucirābhidhānān; saṁśraddadhānāñ jagataḥ pradhānān |
nānāvidhānān rucirābhidhānān; dadarśa tasyāṁ puri yātudhānān ||

> **Meaning 12:** In that city he saw Yātudhānas of sharp intellect, bearing
> pleasing names, steadfast in loyalty, leaders among their people, possessing
> diverse forms and honorable titles.

---
13.
nananda dr̥ṣṭvā sa ca tān surūpān; nānāguṇān ātmaguṇānurūpān |
vidyotamānān sa ca tān surūpān; dadarśa kāṁś cic ca punar virūpān ||

> **Meaning 13:** He was amazed to see many of handsome appearance, endowed with
> various virtues matching their character, resplendent and noble, while seeing
> others of hideous and grotesque form.

---
14.
tato varārhāḥ suviśuddhabhāvās; teṣāṁ striyas tatra mahānubhāvāḥ |
priyeṣu pāneṣu ca saktabhāvā; dadarśa tārā iva suprabhāvāḥ ||

> **Meaning 14:** Then he saw their noble women, worthy of the best luxuries,
> pure in emotion, devoted to their lovers and fond of sweet wine, gleaming with
> radiant beauty like stars.

---
15.
śriyā jvalantīs trapayopagūḍhā; niśīthakāle ramaṇopagūḍhāḥ |
dadarśa kāś cit pramadopagūḍhā; yathā vihaṁgāḥ kusumopagūḍāḥ ||

> **Meaning 15:** Blazing with splendor and veiled in modest charm, some were
> embraced by their lovers in the middle of the night, while others were lost in
> passionate delight, like birds nestled in blooming blossoms.

---
16.
anyāḥ punar harmyatalopaviṣṭās; tatra priyāṅkeṣu sukhopaviṣṭāḥ |
bhartuḥ priyā dharmaparā niviṣṭā; dadarśa dhīmān manadābhiviṣṭāḥ ||

> **Meaning 16:** The wise Kapi saw still other women sitting on rooftop
> terraces, resting happily in the laps of their beloveds — devoted to dharma,
> dear to their husbands, and overcome by deep affection.

---
17.
aprāvr̥tāḥ kāñcanarājivarṇāḥ; kāś cit parārdhyās tapanīyavarṇāḥ |
punaś ca kāś cic chaśalakṣmavarṇāḥ; kāntaprahīṇā rucirāṅgavarṇāḥ ||

> **Meaning 17:** Some were uncovered, glowing with the color of gold stripes;
> some of the finest beauty had complexions of molten gold; while others,
> forsaken by their lovers, were pale as the moon's mark despite their lovely
> forms.

---
18.
tataḥ priyān prāpya mano’bhirāmān; suprītiyuktāḥ prasamīkṣya rāmāḥ |
gr̥heṣu hr̥ṣṭāḥ paramābhirāmā; haripravīraḥ sa dadarśa rāmāḥ ||

> **Meaning 18:** The heroic Hari saw beautiful women in their homes, delighted
> to have found the lovers of their hearts, gazing with deep affection upon
> their charming partners.

---
19.
candraprakāśāś ca hi vaktramālā; vakrākṣipakṣmāś ca sunetramālāḥ |
vibhūṣaṇānāṁ ca dadarśa mālāḥ; śatahradānām iva cārumālāḥ ||

> **Meaning 19:** He saw rows of moon-bright faces with curved eyelashes and
> beautiful eyes, and radiant garlands of jewelry shining like streaks of
> lightning.

---
20.
na tv eva sītāṁ paramābhijātāṁ; pathi sthite rājakule prajātām |
latāṁ praphullām iva sādhujātāṁ; dadarśa tanvīṁ manasābhijātām ||

> **Meaning 20:** But he did not see Sītā — born of a royal lineage rooted in
> righteousness, delicate like a flowering creeper of noble stock, exquisite in
> mind and spirit.

---
21.
sanātane vartmani saṁniviṣṭāṁ; rāmekṣaṇīṁ tāṁ madanābhiviṣṭām |
bhartur manaḥ śrīmad anupraviṣṭāṁ; strībhyo varābhyaś ca sadā viśiṣṭām ||

> **Meaning 21:** He did not find her who was established on the eternal path of
> virtue, her gaze ever fixed on Rāma, consumed by love for him, dwelling
> constantly in her husband's noble heart, superior to all the finest women.

---
22.
uṣṇārditāṁ sānusr̥tāsrakaṇṭhīṁ; purā varārhottamaniṣkakaṇṭhīm |
sujātapakṣmām abhiraktakaṇṭhīṁ; vane pravr̥ttām iva nīlakaṇṭhīm ||

> **Meaning 22:** He did not see her, tormented by sorrow with tears streaming
> down her throat, who once wore a precious golden necklace; her eyelashes were
> graceful and her voice was melodious, like a peahen dancing in the forest.

---
23.
avyaktalekhām iva candralekhāṁ; pāṁsupradigdhām iva hemalekhām |
kṣataprarūḍhām iva bāṇalekhāṁ; vāyuprabhinnām iva meghalekhām ||

> **Meaning 23:** She was like the crescent moon obscured by clouds, like a
> streak of gold covered with dust, like an arrow-wound starting to scar, or
> like a line of clouds scattered by the wind.

---
24.
sītām apaśyan manujeśvarasya; rāmasya patnīṁ vadatāṁ varasya |
babhūva duḥkhābhihataś cirasya; plavaṁgamo manda ivācirasya ||

> **Meaning 24:** Failing to see Sītā, the wife of Rāma, the ruler of men and
> foremost of eloquent speakers, the Plavaṅgama was stricken with sorrow, his
> spirit weighed down in despondency.

---
