=== Sarga 52 ===

---
1.
vīkṣamāṇas tato laṅkāṁ kapiḥ kr̥tamanorathaḥ |
vardhamānasamutsāhaḥ kāryaśeṣam acintayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
kiṁ nu khalv aviśiṣṭaṁ me kartavyam iha sāmpratam |
yad eṣāṁ rakṣasāṁ bhūyaḥ saṁtāpajananaṁ bhavet ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
vanaṁ tāvat pramathitaṁ prakr̥ṣṭā rākṣasā hatāḥ |
balaikadeśaḥ kṣapitaḥ śeṣaṁ durgavināśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
durge vināśite karma bhavet sukhapariśramam |
alpayatnena kārye ’smin mama syāt saphalaḥ śramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
yo hy ayaṁ mama lāṅgūle dīpyate havyavāhanaḥ |
asya saṁtarpaṇaṁ nyāyyaṁ kartum ebhir gr̥hottamaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tataḥ pradīptalāṅgūlaḥ savidyud iva toyadaḥ |
bhavanāgreṣu laṅkāyā vicacāra mahākapiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
mumoca hanumān agniṁ kālānalaśikhopamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
śvasanena ca saṁyogād ativego mahābalaḥ |
kālāgnir iva jajvāla prāvardhata hutāśanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
pradīptam agniṁ pavanas teṣu veśmasu cārayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tāni kāñcanajālāni muktāmaṇimayāni ca |
bhavanāny avaśīryanta ratnavanti mahānti ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tāni bhagnavimānāni nipetur vasudhātale |
bhavanānīva siddhānām ambarāt puṇyasaṁkṣaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
vajravidrumavaidūryamuktārajatasaṁhitān |
vicitrān bhavanād dhātūn syandamānān dadarśa saḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
nāgnis tr̥pyati kāṣṭhānāṁ tr̥ṇānāṁ ca yathā tathā |
hanūmān rākṣasendrāṇāṁ vadhe kiṁ cin na tr̥pyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
hutāśanajvālasamāvr̥tā sā; hatapravīrā parivr̥ttayodhā |
hanūmātaḥ krodhabalābhibhūtā; babhūva śāpopahateva laṅkā ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sasaṁbhramaṁ trastaviṣaṇṇarākṣasāṁ; samujjvalaj jvālahutāśanāṅkitām |
dadarśa laṅkāṁ hanumān mahāmanāḥ; svayambhukopopahatām ivāvanim ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sa rākṣasāṁs tān subahūṁś ca hatvā; vanaṁ ca bhaṅktvā bahupādapaṁ tat |
visr̥jya rakṣo bhavaneṣu cāgniṁ; jagāma rāmaṁ manasā mahātmā ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
laṅkāṁ samastāṁ saṁdīpya lāṅgūlāgniṁ mahākapiḥ |
nirvāpayām āsa tadā samudre harisattamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
