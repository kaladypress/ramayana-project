=== Sarga 3 ===

---
1.
sa lambaśikhare lambe lambatoyadasaṁnibhe |
sattvam āsthāya medhāvī hanumān mārutātmajaḥ ||

2.
niśi laṅkāṁ mahāsattvo viveśa kapikuñjaraḥ |
ramyakānanatoyāḍhyāṁ purīṁ rāvaṇapālitām ||

3.
śāradāmbudharaprakhyair bhavanair upaśobhitām |
sāgaropamanirghoṣāṁ sāgarānilasevitām ||

> **Meaning 1-3:** The greatly energetic and courageous Hanumān, son of Vāyu, entered Rāvaṇa's city of Laṅkā. It was perched on the Laṁba (Trikūṭa) mountain, appearing like a towering cloud, and was adorned with beautiful gardens and water bodies.Laṅkā was adorned with palaces as serene as the autumn sky, yet filled with wild roars from the rakshasas that echoed over the sound of the ocean, and it seemed ocean was serving Lanka by  was constantly fanning the sea breeze
---

4.
supuṣṭabalasaṁguptāṁ yathaiva viṭapāvatīm |
cārutoraṇaniryūhāṁ pāṇḍuradvāratoraṇām ||

5.
bhujagācaritāṁ guptāṁ śubhāṁ bhogavatīm iva |
tāṁ savidyudghanākīrṇāṁ jyotirmārganiṣevitām |
maṁdamāruta saṁcārāṁ yathēṁdrasyāmarāvatīm ||

6.
śātakumbhena mahatā prākāreṇābhisaṁvr̥tām |
kiṅkiṇījālaghoṣābhiḥ patākābhir alaṁkr̥tām ||

7.
āsādya sahasā hr̥ṣṭaḥ prākāram abhipedivān |
vismayāviṣṭahr̥dayaḥ purīm ālokya sarvataḥ |

> **Meaning 4-7:** Guarded by well-nourished troops, it resembled Viṭapāvatī (the city of Kubera). It had beautiful gateways and turrets, and pale white arches. Frequented by serpents (Nāgas) and well-protected, it was like the auspicious Bhogavatī (the capital of the Nāgas). Filled with clouds carrying lightning and traversing the path of the stars, roaring with fierce winds, it was like Indra's Amarāvatī. Surrounded by a massive golden boundary wall, and decorated with flags echoing with the sound of tinkling bells, Hanumān, upon reaching it, was filled with joy and swiftly approached the rampart.His heart filled with wonder, he gazed at the city in all directions. 
---

8.
jāmbūnadamayairdvāraiḥ vaidūryakr̥tavedikaiḥ ||

9.
maṇisphaṭikamuktābhiḥ maṇikuṭṭimabhūṣitaiḥ |
taptahāṭakaniryūhai rājatāmalapāṇḍuraiḥ ||

10.
vaidūryatalasopānaiḥ sphāṭikāntarapāṁsubhiḥ |
cārusaṁjavanopetaiḥ khamivotpatitaiśśubhaiḥ ||

11.
krauñcabarhiṇasaṁghuṣṭe rājahaṁsaniṣevitaiḥ |
tūryābharaṇanirghoṣaiḥ sarvataḥ pratināditām ||

12.
vasvokasārāpratimāṁ samīkṣya nagarīṁ tataḥ |
kham ivotpatitāṁ laṅkāṁ jaharṣa hanumān kapiḥ ||

> **Meaning 8-12:**  It had gateways made of precious gold (Jāmbūnada) with raised platforms of cat's-eye gems (Vaidūrya). Adorned with gems, crystals, and pearls, the jeweled floors shone brightly. The turrets were made of refined gold, flanked by pale, pure silver. The staircases had steps of cat's-eye gems, with crystal dust sprinkled in between. The beautiful mansions seemed to soar into the sky. Resounding with the sounds of Krauñca birds and peacocks, frequented by royal swans (Rājahaṁsa), and echoing everywhere with the sounds of musical instruments (Tūrya) and the tinkling of ornaments, Hanumān rejoiced upon seeing Lanka, which resembled Vasvokasārā (a name for the capital of Kubera, Alakapuri), rising as if into the sky.
---

13.
tāṁ samīkṣya purīṁ laṅkāṁ rākṣasādhipateḥ śubhām |
anuttamām r̥ddhiyutāṁ cintayāmāsa vīryavān ||

14.
neyam anyena nagarī śakyā dharṣayituṁ balāt |
rakṣitā rāvaṇabalaiḥ udyatāyudhadhāribhiḥ ||

15.
kumudāṅgadayōrvāpi suṣeṇasya mahākapeḥ |
prasiddheyaṁ bhaved bhūmir maindadvividayorapi ||

16.
vivasvatas tanūjasya hareśca kuśaparvaṇaḥ |
r̥kṣasya ketumālasya mama caiva gatirbhavet ||

17.
samīkṣya tu mahābāho rāghavasya parākramam |
lakṣmaṇasya ca vikrāntam abhavat prītimān kapiḥ ||

> **Meaning 13-17:** Observing the beautiful, unsurpassed, and prosperous city of Lanka belonging to the king of Rākṣasas, the valiant one pondered: "This city cannot be conquered by force by anyone else, guarded as it is by Rāvaṇa's forces wielding upraised weapons. This land may be accessible only to Kumuda, Aṅgada, the great Vānara Suṣeṇa, Mainda, and Dvivida. It may also be accessible to Sugrīva (the son of Sūrya), the Vānara Kuśaparvan, Jāmbavān, Ketumāla, and myself." When the valiant Hanumān contemplated the great prowess of Rāghava (Rāma) and the courage of Lakshmaṇa, he became filled with contentment. (However mighty be the city of Lanka, it cannot withstand the strength of Shri Rama and Lakshmana Swami).
---

18.
tāṁ ratnavasanopetāṁ koṣṭhāgārāvataṁsakām |
yantrāgārastanīm r̥ddhāṁ pramadāmiva bhūṣitām ||

19.
tāṁ naṣṭatimirāṁ dīpair bhāsvaraiś ca mahāgr̥haiḥ |
nagarīṁ rākṣasendrasya dadarśa mahākapiḥ ||

> **Meaning 18-19:** Hanumān saw the city adorned like a prosperous woman, with gems for her garments, armories for her earrings, and arsenals for her breasts. The great Vānara saw the city of the Rākṣasa king, its darkness dispelled by radiant lamps and glowing mansions.
---

20.
atha sā hariśārdūlaṁ praviśaṁtaṁ mahābalam |
nagarī svēna rūpēṇa dadarśa pavanātmajam ||

21.
sā taṁ harivaraṁ dr̥ṣṭvā laṁkā rāvaṇapālitā |
svayamēvōtthitā tatra vikr̥tānanadarśanā ||

22.
purastāt kapivaryasya vāyusūnōratiṣṭhata |
muṁcamānā mahānādaṁ abravīt pavanātmajam ||

23.
kastvaṁ kēna ca kāryēṇa iha prāptō vanālaya |
kathayasvēha yattattvaṁ yāvatprāṇā dharaṁti tē ||

24.
na śakyaṁ khalviyaṁ laṁkā pravēṣṭuṁ vānara tvayā |
rakṣitā rāvaṇabalaiḥ abhiguptā samaṁtataḥ ||

> **Meaning 20-24:** Then, the presiding deity of the city (Laṅkā), in her own form, saw the powerful Hanumān, as he was entering.Laṅkā (the presiding deity of the city), protected by Rāvaṇa, having seen the Hanuman, rose up there with cruel countenance. That deity of Lanka stood before him and spoke to Hanuman with a terrible roar :"O Vānara, identify yourself and state your purpose for coming here! Speak the truth before I take your life.“O Vānara, it is impossible for you to enter this Lanka which is well - guarded from all directions”. 
---

25.
atha tāmabravīdvīrō hanumānagrataḥ sthitām | 
kathayiṣyāmi tē tattvaṁ yanmāṁ tvaṁ paripr̥cchasi ||

26.
kā tvaṁ virūpanayanā puradvārē’vatiṣṭha sē |
kimarthaṁ cāpi māṁ ruddhvā nirbhartsayasi dāruṇā ||

27.
hanumadvacanaṁ śrutvā laṁkā sā kāmarūpiṇī |
uvāca vacanaṁ kruddhā paruṣaṁ pavanātmajam ||

28.
ahaṁ rākṣasarājasya rāvaṇasya mahātmanaḥ |
ājñāpratīkṣā durdharṣā rakṣāmi nagarīmimām ||

29.
na śakyā māmavajñāya pravēṣṭuṁ nagarī tvayā |
adya prāṇaiḥ parityaktaḥ svapsyasē nihatō mayā ||

30.
ahaṁ hi nagarī laṁkā svayamēva plavaṁgama |
sarvataḥ parirakṣāmi hyētattē kathitaṁ mayā ||

> **Meaning 25-30:** The heroic Hanumān then replied standing before her: "I will tell you the truth to what you ask of me. But who are you, with deformed eyes, standing at the city gate? And why do you fiercely block and threaten me?" Hearing Hanumān's words, Laṅkā, who could assume any form at will, spoke harshly and angrily to the son of Vāyu: "I am the one who awaits the commands of the great-souled Rāvaṇa, the king of Rākṣasas. Unassailable, I guard this city! It is not possible for you to enter the city by disregarding me. Today, you will die here and enter into long sleep. O Plavaṅgama, I myself am the city of Laṅkā, and I protect it from all sides. Thus I have declared this to you."
---

31.
laṁkāyā vacanaṁ śrutvā hanumān mārutātmajaḥ |
yatnavān sa hariśrēṣṭhaḥ sthitaśśaila ivāparaḥ ||

32.
sa tāṁ strīrūpavikr̥tāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā vānarapuṁgavaḥ |
ābabhāṣē’tha mēdhāvī sattvavān plavagarṣabhaḥ ||

33.
drakṣyāmi nagarīṁ laṁkāṁ sāṭṭaprākāratōraṇām |
ityarthamiha saṁprāptaḥ paraṁ kautūhalaṁ hi mē ||

34.
vanānyupavanānīha laṁkāyāḥ kānanāni ca |
sarvatō gr̥hamukhyāni draṣṭu māgamanaṁ hi mē ||

35.
tasya tadvacanaṁ śrutvā laṁkā sā kāmarūpiṇī |
bhūya ēva punarvākyaṁ babhāṣē paruṣākṣaram ||

36.
māmanirjitya durbuddhē rākṣasēśvarapālitām |
na śakyamadya tē draṣṭuṁ purīyaṁ vānarādhama ||

37.
tatassa kapiśārdūlaḥ tāmuvāca niśācarīm |
dr̥ṣṭvā purīmimāṁ bhadrē punaryāsyē yathāgatam ||

> **Meaning 31-37:** Hearing the words of Laṅkā, Hanumān, the son of Vāyu and the foremost among Vānaras, stood resolute like another mountain. Seeing her in the deformed shape of a woman, the intelligent and courageous Vānarapuṅgava spoke: "I have come here simply to see the city of Laṅkā with its towers, ramparts, and gateways, for my curiosity is great. My arrival is only to see the forests, gardens, groves, and the preeminent mansions of Laṅkā." Hearing his words, Laṅkā, capable of assuming any form, spoke harsh words again: "O foolish one, without defeating me, who is protected by the king of Rākṣasas, you cannot see this city today, O lowest of Vānaras!" Then the Hanumān replied humbly to the night-wandering woman: "O auspicious one, after seeing this city, I will return the way I came."
---

38.
tataḥ kr̥tvā mahānādaṁ sā vai laṁkā bhayāvaham |
talēna vānaraśrēṣṭhaṁ tāḍayāmāsa vēgitā ||

39.
tatassa kapiśārdūlaḥ laṁkayā tāḍitō bhr̥śam |
nanāda sumahānādaṁ vīryavān pavanātmajaḥ ||

40.
tata ssaṁvartayāmāsa vāmahastasya sō’ṁgulīḥ |
muṣṭinābhijaghānaināṁ hanumān krōdhamūrchitaḥ ||

41.
strīcēti manyamānēna nātikrōdhaḥ svayaṁ kr̥taḥ ||
sā tu tēna prahārēṇa vihvalāṁgī niśācarī |

42.
papāta sahasā bhūmau vikr̥tānanadarśanā |
tatastu hanumān prājñaḥ tāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā vinipātitām ||

> **Meaning 38-42:** Letting out a terrifying roar, Laṅkā forcefully struck Hanumān with her palm. Deeply struck by Laṅkā, the valiant son of Vāyu, roared a mighty roar. Then, filled with anger, Hanumān folded the fingers of his left hand into a fist and struck her. However, considering her to be a woman, he intentionally restrained his full anger. Struck by that blow, the night-wandering demoness with a deformed face became overwhelmed, her limbs trembling, and she suddenly collapsed to the ground.
---

43.
kr̥pāṁ cakāra tējasvī manyamānaḥ striyaṁ tu tām |
tatō vai bhr̥śasaṁvignā laṁkā sā gadgadākṣaram ||

44.
uvācā garvitaṁ vākyaṁ hanūmaṁtaṁ plavaṁgamam |
prasīda sumahābāhō trāyasva harisattama ||

45.
samayē saumya tiṣṭhaṁti sattvavaṁtō mahābalāḥ |
ahaṁ tu nagarī laṁkā svayamēva plavaṁgama ||

46.
nirjitāhaṁ tvayā vīra vikramēṇa mahābala |
idaṁ tu tathyaṁ śr̥ṇu vai bruvaṁtyā mē harīśvara ||

47.
svayaṁbhuvā purā dattaṁ varadānaṁ yathā mama |
yadā tvāṁ vānaraḥ kaścit vikramādvaśamānayēt ||

48.
tadā tvayā hi vijñēyaṁ rakṣasāṁ bhayamāgatam |
sa hi mē samayassaumya prāptō’dya tava darśanāt ||

49.
svayaṁbhūvihita ssatyō na tasyāsti vyatikramaḥ |
sītānimittaṁ rājñastu rāvaṇasya durātmanaḥ ||

50.
rakṣasāṁ caiva sarvēṣāṁ vināśa ssamupāgataḥ |
tatpraviśya hariśrēṣṭha purīṁ rāvaṇapālitām |
vidhatsva sarvakāryāṇi yāni yānīha vāṁchasi ||

51.
praviśya śāpōpahatāṁ harīśvaraḥ
śubhāṁ purīṁ rākṣasamukhyapālitām |
yadr̥cchayā tvaṁ janakātmajāṁ satīṁ
vimārga sarvatra gatō yathāsukham ||

> **Meaning 42-50:** Seeing her fallen, the wise and radiant Hanumān felt compassion, mindful that she was a woman.Extremely terrified, Laṅkā, whose pride had been shattered, spoke to Hanumān with choked voice: "Be pleased, O mighty-armed one! Save me, O foremost among Vānaras! O gentle one, those with great strength and courage abide by righteous limits. O Plavaṅgama, I myself am the city of Laṅkā. O valiant one, I have been conquered by your sheer prowess. O Harīśvara, listen to the truth as I tell you the boon given to me in the past by Svayaṁbhū (Brahmā): 'When a Vānara subdues you by force, then you must understand that fear has come upon the Rākṣasas.' O gentle one, that time has come for me today by your sight! The decree of Brahmā is true, and there is no altering it. Because of Sītā, the destruction of the wicked king Rāvaṇa and of all the Rākṣasas is now imminent. Therefore, O Hariśrēṣṭha, enter the city ruled by Rāvaṇa and accomplish all the tasks you desire! O Harīśvara, having entered this auspicious city protected by the chief of Rākṣasas—which is now struck by a curse—search everywhere for the virtuous daughter of Janaka, going wherever you please."
