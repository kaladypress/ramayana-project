=== Sarga 63 ===

---
1.
tato mūrdhnā nipatitaṁ vānaraṁ vānararṣabhaḥ |
dr̥ṣṭvaivodvignahr̥dayo vākyam etad uvāca ha ||

> **Meaning 1:** Seeing the Vānara fallen prostrate with his head at his feet,
> the bull among Vānaras became anxious at heart and spoke these words:

---
2.
uttiṣṭhottiṣṭha kasmāt tvaṁ pādayoḥ patito mama |
abhayaṁ te bhaved vīra satyam evābhidhīyatām ||

> **Meaning 2:** "Rise, rise! Why have you fallen at my feet? Hero, you need not
> fear anything — tell me the plain truth!

---
3.
kiṁ sambhramādidaṁ dīna brūhi yadvaktumarhasi |
kaccinmadhuvane svasti śrotumicchāmi vānara ||

> **Meaning 3:** Why are you so distressed and agitated? Tell me what you have
> to say. Vānara, is all well with Madhuvana? I want to know."

---
4.
sa samaśvāsitas tena sugrīveṇa mahātmanā |
utthāya ca mahāprājño vākyaṁ dadhimukho ’bravīt ||

> **Meaning 4:** Reassured by the great-souled Sugrīva, the highly intelligent
> Dadhimukha stood up and said:

---
5.
naivarkṣarajasā rājan na tvayā nāpi vālinā |
vanaṁ nisr̥ṣṭapūrvaṁ hi bhakṣitaṁ tat tu vānaraiḥ ||

> **Meaning 5:** "King, that forest was never thrown open by R̥kṣarajas, nor by
> Vāli, nor by you. Yet the Vānaras have pillaged it!

---
6.
nivāryamāṇāste sarve mayā saha vanecaraiḥ |
acintayitvā māṁ hṛṣṭā bhakṣayanti pibanti ca ||

> **Meaning 6:** When the forest guards and I tried to stop them, they paid no
> attention to me; in their drunken glee, they kept eating the fruits and
> drinking the honey.

---
7.
ebhiḥ pradharṣitāś caiva vāritā vanarakṣibhiḥ |
madhūny acintayitvemān bhakṣayanti pibanti ca ||

> **Meaning 7:** Though held back by these forest guards, they attacked them,
> disregarded them completely, and kept devouring the honey and eating the
> fruits.

---
8.
śiṣṭam atrāpavidhyanti bhakṣayanti tathāpare |
nivāryamāṇās te sarve bhruvau vai darśayanti hi ||

> **Meaning 8:** Some eat the honey and fling away what is left, while others
> gorge themselves. When we try to stop them, they knit their eyebrows in anger
> at us.

---
9.
ime hi saṁrabdhatarās tathā taiḥ saṁpradharṣitāḥ |
vārayanto vanāt tasmāt kruddhair vānarapuṁgavaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 9:** When these guards tried to keep them out of that forest, those
> enraged bulls among Vānaras grew even more violent and attacked them fiercely.

---
10.
tatas tair bahubhir vīrair vānarair vānararṣabhāḥ |
saṁraktanayanaiḥ krodhād dharayaḥ saṁpracālitāḥ ||

> **Meaning 10:** Then, bull among Vānaras, those many heroic Vānaras, their
> eyes blood-red with rage, drove our Haris away.

---
11.
pāṇibhir nihatāḥ ke cit ke cij jānubhir āhatāḥ |
prakr̥ṣṭāś ca yathākāmaṁ devamārgaṁ ca darśitāḥ ||

> **Meaning 11:** Some were struck with fists, some were kicked with knees,
> dragged about at will, and tossed up into the sky, the path of the devatās.

---
12.
evam ete hatāḥ śūrās tvayi tiṣṭhati bhartari |
kr̥tsnaṁ madhuvanaṁ caiva prakāmaṁ taiḥ prabhakṣyate ||

> **Meaning 12:** Even while you rule as our master, these brave guards have
> been beaten this way, and they are devouring all of Madhuvana as they please."

---
13.
evaṁ vijñāpyamānaṁ tu sugrīvaṁ vānararṣabham |
apr̥cchat taṁ mahāprājño lakṣmaṇaḥ paravīrahā ||

> **Meaning 13:** As Sugrīva, the bull among Vānaras, was being informed in this
> manner, the wise Lakṣmaṇa, slayer of enemy heroes, asked him:

---
14.
kim ayaṁ vānaro rājan vanapaḥ pratyupasthitaḥ |
kaṁ cārtham abhinirdiśya duḥkhito vākyam abravīt ||

> **Meaning 14:** "King, why has this Vānara forest guard come here? What matter
> is he referring to, and why is he speaking in such distress?"

---
15.
evam uktas tu sugrīvo lakṣmaṇena mahātmanā |
lakṣmaṇaṁ pratyuvācedaṁ vākyaṁ vākyaviśāradaḥ ||

> **Meaning 15:** Addressed thus by the great-souled Lakṣmaṇa, Sugrīva, skilled
> in speech, replied to Lakṣmaṇa:

---
16.
ārya lakṣmaṇa saṁprāha vīro dadhimukhaḥ kapiḥ |
aṅgadapramukhair vīrair bhakṣitaṁ madhuvānaraiḥ ||

> **Meaning 16:** "Noble Lakṣmaṇa, the heroic Kapi Dadhimukha reports that the
> heroic Vānaras, led by Aṅgada, have consumed the honey.

---
17.
vicitya dakṣiṇāmāśāṁ āgatairharipuṁgavaiḥ |
naiṣām akr̥takr̥tyānām īdr̥śaḥ syād upakramaḥ ||

> **Meaning 17:** Those bulls among Haris have returned after searching the
> southern direction. If their mission had failed, they would never behave like
> this!

---
18.
vārayanto bhṛśaṁ prāptāḥ pālā jānubhirāhatāḥ |
tathā na gaṇitaścāyaṁ kapirdadhimukho balī ||

> **Meaning 18:** The guards who came to stop them were struck down and beaten
> with knees, and even this powerful Kapi Dadhimukha was completely disregarded.

---
19.
dr̥ṣṭā devī na saṁdeho na cānyena hanūmatā |
patirmama vanāsyāṃ asmābhiḥ sthāpitaḥ svayaṃ||

> **Meaning 19:** The devī has been found, without a doubt, and by none other
> than Hanumān — for no one else could have accomplished it, and Dadhimukha was
> placed as guardian of that forest by ourselves.

---
20.
na hy anyaḥ sādhane hetuḥ karmaṇo ’sya hanūmataḥ |
kāryasiddhir hanumati matiś ca haripuṁgava ||


> **Meaning 20:** Indeed, no one other than Hanumān could be the instrument for
> achieving this feat. In Hanumān reside success in action, sharp intellect,
> bull among Haris,

---
21.
vyavasāyaś ca vīryaṁ ca śrutaṁ cāpi pratiṣṭhitam |
jāmbavān yatra netā syād aṅgadasya baleśvaraḥ ||


> **Meaning 21:** as well as steadfast resolve, prowess, and sacred learning.
> Where Jāmbavān is the guide and Aṅgada is commander of the force,

---
22.
hanūmāṁś cāpy adhiṣṭhātā na tasya gatir anyathā |
aṅgadapramukhair vīrair hataṁ madhuvanaṁ kila ||


> **Meaning 22:** and Hanumān oversees the task, its success cannot be
> otherwise. It is reported that the heroes led by Aṅgada have ransacked
> Madhuvana.

---
23.
vicintya dakṣiṇām āśām āgatair haripuṁgavaiḥ |
āgataiś ca praviṣṭaṁ tad yathā madhuvanaṁ hi taiḥ |


> **Meaning 23:** Having searched the southern quarter, those bulls among Haris
> returned, and as soon as they arrived they burst into Madhuvana.

---
24.
dharṣitaṁ ca vanaṁ kr̥tsnam upayuktaṁ ca vānaraiḥ |
vāritāḥ sahitāḥ pālās tathā jānubhir āhatāḥ ||

> **Meaning 24:** The entire forest was plundered and enjoyed by the Vānaras;
> the guards who tried to hold them back were attacked together and struck with
> knees.

---
25.
etadartham ayaṁ prāpto vaktuṁ madhuravāg iha |
nāmnā dadhimukho nāma hariḥ prakhyātavikramaḥ ||

> **Meaning 25:** For this reason, this sweet-spoken Hari of renowned valor,
> named Dadhimukha, has come here to report what happened.

---
26.
dr̥ṣṭā sītā mahābāho saumitre paśya tattvataḥ |
abhigamya yathā sarve pibanti madhu vānarāḥ ||

> **Meaning 26:** Mighty-armed Saumitri, understand the truth: Sītā has been
> found! See how all the Vānaras gathered and drank the honey without any fear.

---
27.
na cāpy adr̥ṣṭvā vaidehīṁ viśrutāḥ puruṣarṣabha |
vanaṁ dātta varaṁ divyaṁ dharṣayeyur vanaukasaḥ ||

> **Meaning 27:** Bull among men, if those renowned forest-dwellers had not seen
> Vaidehī, they would never have dared to ravage this divine forest, protected
> by a boon."

---
28.
tataḥ prahr̥ṣṭo dharmātmā lakṣmaṇaḥ saharāghavaḥ |
śrutvā karṇasukhāṁ vāṇīṁ sugrīvavadanāc cyutām ||

> **Meaning 28:** Hearing these words, so sweet to the ear, flowing from
> Sugrīva's lips, the righteous Lakṣmaṇa and Rāghava were overjoyed.

---
29.
prāhr̥ṣyata bhr̥śaṁ rāmo lakṣmaṇaś ca mahāyaśāḥ |
śrutvā dadhimukhasyedaṁ sugrīvas tu prahr̥ṣya ca ||


> **Meaning 29:** Rāma and the illustrious Lakṣmaṇa rejoiced exceedingly. Having
> heard Dadhimukha's report, Sugrīva too was delighted, and

---
30.
vanapālaṁ punar vākyaṁ sugrīvaḥ pratyabhāṣata |
prīto ’smi saumya yad bhuktaṁ vanaṁ taiḥ kr̥takarmabhiḥ ||

> **Meaning 30:** Sugrīva spoke once more to the forest guard: "Gentle one, I am
> glad that those heroes who accomplished their mission have enjoyed the forest.

---
31. 
marṣitaṁ marṣaṇīyaṁ ca ceṣṭitaṁ kr̥takarmaṇām ||
gaccha śīghraṃ madhuvanaṃ saṃrakṣasva tvameva hi,
śīghraṃ preṣaya sarvāṃstān hanumantatpramukhān kapīn|

> **Meaning 31:** The actions of those who have fulfilled their purpose are
> forgiven and indeed ought to be forgiven. Go quickly, protect Madhuvana
> yourself, and send all those Kapis led by Hanumān here at once!

---
32.
icchāmi śīghraṁ hanumatpradhānān; śākhāmr̥gāṁs tān mr̥garājadarpān |
draṣṭuṁ kr̥tārthān saha rāghavābhyāṁ; śrotuṁ ca sītādhigame prayatnam ||

> **Meaning 32:** Along with both descendants of Raghu, I wish to see those
> branch-roaming Kapis at once — proud as lions with Hanumān at their head,
> triumphant in their goal — and to hear of their efforts in finding Sītā."

---
33.
prītisphītākṣau saṁprahr̥ṣṭau kumārau
dr̥ṣṭvā siddhārthau vānarāṇāṁ ca rājā |
aṁgai ssaṁhr̥ṣṭaiḥ karmasiddhiṁ viditvā
bāhvōrāsannāṁ sō’ timātraṁ nanaṁda ||

> **Meaning 33:** Seeing the two princes, their eyes wide with joy and delight
> at the success of their quest, the king of the Vānaras felt his body thrill
> with exhilaration; knowing that the mission was fulfilled and within their
> grasp, he rejoiced exceedingly.

---
