=== Sarga 18 ===

---
1.
tathā viprekṣamāṇasya vanaṁ puṣpitapādapam |
vicinvataś ca vaidehīṁ kiṁ cic cheṣā niśābhavat ||

> **Meaning 1:** As Hanumān was thus observing the grove of flowering trees and
> searching for Vaidehī, the night drew near its close.

---
2.
ṣaḍaṅgavedaviduṣāṁ kratupravarayājinām |
śuśrāva brahmaghoṣāṁś ca virātre brahmarakṣasām ||

> **Meaning 2:** Toward the break of dawn, he heard the chanting of the Vedas by
> the Brahma-rākṣasas, who were master scholars of the six auxiliary sciences of
> the Veda and seasoned performers of foremost sacrifices.

---
3.
atha maṅgalavāditraiḥ śabdaiḥ śrotramanoharaiḥ |
prābodhyata mahābāhur daśagrīvo mahābalaḥ ||

> **Meaning 3:** Then, accompanied by the melodious, auspicious sounds of
> musical instruments pleasing to the ear, the mighty-armed and powerful
> Daśagrīva was awakened.

---
4.
vibudhya tu yathākālaṁ rākṣasendraḥ pratāvapān |
srastamālyāmbaradharo vaidehīm anvacintayat ||

> **Meaning 4:** Awakening at the proper hour, the radiant king of Rākṣasas, his
> garlands and robes slipping, turned his thoughts at once to Vaidehī.

---
5.
bhr̥śaṁ niyuktas tasyāṁ ca madanena madotkaṭaḥ |
na sa taṁ rākṣasaḥ kāmaṁ śaśākātmani gūhitum ||

> **Meaning 5:** Being passionately drawn to her and overwhelmed with the
> intoxication of desire, that Rākṣasa could not conceal the lust burning within
> his heart.

---
6.
sa sarvābharaṇair yukto bibhrac chriyam anuttamām |
tāṁ nagair vividhair juṣṭāṁ sarvapuṣpaphalopagaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 6:** Adorned with all his jewels and radiating unsurpassed splendor,
> he set out for that grove graced by diverse trees bearing blossoms and fruits
> of every season.

---
7.
vr̥tāṁ puṣkariṇībhiś ca nānāpuṣpopaśobhitām |
sadāmadaiś ca vihagair vicitrāṁ paramādbhutām ||

> **Meaning 7:** It was surrounded by lotus ponds, beautified with colorful
> blossoms, and filled with birds perpetually exhilarated with joy—a place truly
> wondrous.

---
8.
īhāmr̥gaiś ca vividhaiś vr̥tāṁ dr̥ṣṭimanoharaiḥ |
vīthīḥ saṁprekṣamāṇaś ca maṇikāñcanatoraṇāḥ ||

> **Meaning 8:** He walked along observing the pathways spanned by archways of
> gems and gold, stocked with delightful ornamental animal sculptures and
> teeming with captivating deer.

---
9.
nānāmr̥gagaṇākīrṇāṁ phalaiḥ prapatitair vr̥tām |
aśokavanikām eva prāviśat saṁtatadrumām ||

> **Meaning 9:** Crowded with varied herds of deer and carpeted with fallen
> fruits, that dense grove of continuous trees—the Aśoka grove—was entered by
> him.

---
10.
aṅganāśatamātraṁ tu taṁ vrajantam anuvrajat |
mahendram iva paulastyaṁ devagandharvayoṣitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 10:** As that scion of Pulastya strode forth, a retinue of about a
> hundred women followed him, just as celestial maidens and Gandharva women
> attend upon great Indra.

---
11.
dīpikāḥ kāñcanīḥ kāś cij jagr̥hus tatra yoṣitaḥ |
bālavyajanahastāś ca tālavr̥ntāni cāparāḥ ||

> **Meaning 11:** Among them, some women carried golden torches, while others
> held fly-whisks and palm-leaf fans in their hands.

---
12.
kāñcanair api bhr̥ṅgārair jahruḥ salilam agrataḥ |
maṇḍalāgrān asīṁś caiva gr̥hyānyāḥ pr̥ṣṭhato yayuḥ ||

> **Meaning 12:** Some walked in front carrying water in golden pitchers, while
> others walked behind bearing curved scimitars.

---
13.
kā cid ratnamayīṁ pātrīṁ pūrṇāṁ pānasya bhāminī |
dakṣiṇā dakṣiṇenaiva tadā jagrāha pāṇinā ||

> **Meaning 13:** One capable woman carried with her right hand a jeweled goblet
> filled with wine.

---
14.
rājahaṁsapratīkāśaṁ chatraṁ pūrṇaśaśiprabham |
sauvarṇadaṇḍam aparā gr̥hītvā pr̥ṣṭhato yayau ||

> **Meaning 14:** Another walked behind holding a white parasol with a golden
> staff, resembling a royal swan and glowing like the full moon.

---
15.
nidrāmadaparītākṣyo rāvaṇasyottamastriyaḥ |
anujagmuḥ patiṁ vīraṁ ghanaṁ vidyullatā iva ||

> **Meaning 15:** With eyes misty with sleep and intoxication, those choice
> wives of Rāvaṇa followed their heroic husband like flashes of lightning
> following a dark cloud.

---
16.
vyāviddhahārakēyūrāḥ samāmr̥ditavarṇakāḥ |
samāgaḷitakēśāṁtāḥ sasvēdavadanāstathā || 16 ||

17.
ghūrṇaṁtyō madaśēṣēṇa nidrayā ca śubhānanāḥ |
svēdakliṣṭāṁgakusumāḥ sumālyākulamūrdhajāḥ || 17 ||

18.
prayāṁtaṁ nairr̥tapatiṁ nāryō madiralōcanāḥ |
bahumānācca kāmācca priyā bhāryāstamanvayuḥ || 18 ||

19.
sa ca kāmaparādhīnaḥ patistāsāṁ mahābalaḥ |
sītāsaktamanā maṁdō maṁdāṁcitagatirbabhau || 19 ||

20.
tataḥ kāñcīninādaṁ ca nūpurāṇāṁ ca niḥsvanam |
śuśrāva paramastrīṇāṁ sa kapir mārutātmajaḥ ||

> **Meaning 16-20:** With their pearl necklaces and armlets tossed about, the
> cosmetic sandal paste on their bodies rubbed away, their hair falling loose,
> and their faces beaded with sweat, those lovely women swayed under lingering
> intoxication and sleep. Their floral garlands were crushed with sweat and
> tangled in their tresses as those intoxicating-eyed wives, driven by deep
> affection and passion, followed their husband, the king of the Nairr̥tas.
> Wholly enslaved by lust, that mighty husband walked with a slow, swaying gait,
> his mind obsessively fixed on Sītā. Just then, that Kapi, the son of Māruta,
> heard the soft tinkling of jeweled waistbands and the chiming of anklets of
> those noble women.

---
21.
taṁ cāpratimakarmāṇam acintyabalapauruṣam |
dvāradeśam anuprāptaṁ dadarśa hanumān kapiḥ ||

> **Meaning 21:** The Kapi Hanumān saw that figure of matchless deeds and
> unimaginable strength and prowess arriving at the entrance.

---
22.
dīpikābhir anekābhiḥ samantād avabhāsitam |
gandhatailāvasiktābhir dhriyamāṇābhir agrataḥ ||

> **Meaning 22:** He was illuminated on all sides by numerous torches fueled by
> fragrant oils and carried ahead of him.

---
23.
kāmadarpamadair yuktaṁ jihmatāmrāyatekṣaṇam |
samakṣam iva kandarpam apaviddha śarāsanam ||

> **Meaning 23:** Infused with desire, pride, and passion, his long reddish eyes
> glancing slantwise, he looked like the god of love himself, having laid aside
> his bow.

---
24.
mathitāmr̥taphenābham arajo vastram uttamam |
salīlam anukarṣantaṁ vimuktaṁ saktam aṅgade ||

> **Meaning 24:** As he walked, he playfully pulled at his flawless upper
> garment, white as the churned froth of nectar, which had slipped down and
> caught on his armlet.

---
25.
taṁ patraviṭape līnaḥ patrapuṣpaghanāvr̥taḥ |
samīpam upasaṁkrāntaṁ nidhyātum upacakrame ||

> **Meaning 25:** Hidden in the branching boughs and thickly screened by dense
> leaves and blossoms, Hanumān began to watch him closely as he drew near.

---
26.
avekṣamāṇaś ca tato dadarśa kapikuñjaraḥ |
rūpayauvanasaṁpannā rāvaṇasya varastriyaḥ ||

> **Meaning 26:** Looking carefully, the Kapikuñjara saw the choice women of
> Rāvaṇa, endowed with dazzling youth and beauty.

---
27.
tābhiḥ parivr̥to rājā surūpābhir mahāyaśāḥ |
tanmr̥gadvijasaṁghuṣṭaṁ praviṣṭaḥ pramadāvanam ||

> **Meaning 27:** Surrounded by those lovely women, the renowned king entered
> that pleasure garden, which was resounding with the calls of beasts and birds.

---
28.
kṣībo vicitrābharaṇaḥ śaṅkukarṇo mahābalaḥ |
tena viśravasaḥ putraḥ sa dr̥ṣṭo rākṣasādhipaḥ ||

> **Meaning 28:** Hanumān saw the son of Viśravas, the mighty king of Rākṣasas,
> intoxicated, adorned with diverse ornaments, and bearing pointed ears.

---
29.
vr̥taḥ paramanārībhis tārābhir iva candramāḥ |
taṁ dadarśa mahātejās tejovantaṁ mahākapiḥ ||

> **Meaning 29:** Surrounded by supreme women like the moon encircled by stars,
> that radiant monarch was watched by the glorious great Kapi.

---
30.
rāvaṇo ’yaṁ mahābāhur iti saṁcintya vānaraḥ |
avapluto mahātejā hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ ||

> **Meaning 30:** Realizing, "This is indeed the mighty-armed Rāvaṇa," the
> radiant Vānara Hanumān, son of Māruta, shifted down slightly.

---
31.
sa tathāpy ugratejāḥ san nirdhūtas tasya tejasā |
patraguhyāntare sakto hanūmān saṁvr̥to ’bhavat ||

> **Meaning 31:** Though endowed with fierce luster himself, Hanumān drew back
> before Rāvaṇa's overwhelming presence and remained concealed deep within the
> thick foliage.

---
32.
sa tām asitakeśāntāṁ suśroṇīṁ saṁhatastanīm |
didr̥kṣur asitāpāṅgīm upāvartata rāvaṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 32:** Yearning to behold that dark-tressed lady of graceful bearing,
> firm breasts, and dark-rimmed eyes, Rāvaṇa advanced toward her.

---
