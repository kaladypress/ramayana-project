=== Sarga 28 ===

---
1.
hanumān api vikrāntaḥ sarvaṁ śuśrāva tattvataḥ |
sītāyās trijaṭāyāś ca rākṣasīnāṁ ca tarjanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
avekṣamāṇas tāṁ devīṁ devatām iva nandane |
tato bahuvidhāṁ cintāṁ cintayām āsa vānaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
yāṁ kapīnāṁ sahasrāṇi subahūny ayutāni ca |
dikṣu sarvāsu mārgante seyam āsāditā mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
cāreṇa tu suyuktena śatroḥ śaktim avekṣitā |
gūḍhena caratā tāvad avekṣitam idaṁ mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
rākṣasānāṁ viśeṣaś ca purī ceyam avekṣitā |
rākṣasādhipater asya prabhāvo rāvaṇasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
yuktaṁ tasyāprameyasya sarvasattvadayāvataḥ |
samāśvāsayituṁ bhāryāṁ patidarśanakāṅkṣiṇīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
aham āśvāsayāmy enāṁ pūrṇacandranibhānanām |
adr̥ṣṭaduḥkhāṁ duḥkhasya na hy antam adhigacchatīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
yadi hy aham imāṁ devīṁ śokopahatacetanām |
anāśvāsya gamiṣyāmi doṣavad gamanaṁ bhavet ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
gate hi mayi tatreyaṁ rājaputrī yaśasvinī |
paritrāṇam avindantī jānakī jīvitaṁ tyajet ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
mayā ca sa mahābāhuḥ pūrṇacandranibhānanaḥ |
samāśvāsayituṁ nyāyyaḥ sītādarśanalālasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
niśācarīṇāṁ pratyakṣam akṣamaṁ cābhibhāṣaṇam |
kathaṁ nu khalu kartavyam idaṁ kr̥cchra gato hy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
anena rātriśeṣeṇa yadi nāśvāsyate mayā |
sarvathā nāsti saṁdehaḥ parityakṣyati jīvitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
rāmaś ca yadi pr̥cchen māṁ kiṁ māṁ sītābravīd vacaḥ |
kim ahaṁ taṁ pratibrūyām asaṁbhāṣya sumadhyamām ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sītāsaṁdeśarahitaṁ mām itas tvarayā gatam |
nirdahed api kākutsthaḥ kruddhas tīvreṇa cakṣuṣā ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
yadi ced yojayiṣyāmi bhartāraṁ rāmakāraṇāt |
vyartham āgamanaṁ tasya sasainyasya bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
antaraṁ tv aham āsādya rākṣasīnām iha sthitaḥ |
śanair āśvāsayiṣyāmi saṁtāpabahulām imām ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
ahaṁ hy atitanuś caiva vanaraś ca viśeṣataḥ |
vācaṁ codāhariṣyāmi mānuṣīm iha saṁskr̥tām ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
yadi vācaṁ pradāsyāmi dvijātir iva saṁskr̥tām |
rāvaṇaṁ manyamānā māṁ sītā bhītā bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
avaśyam eva vaktavyaṁ mānuṣaṁ vākyam arthavat |
mayā sāntvayituṁ śakyā nānyatheyam aninditā ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
seyam ālokya me rūpaṁ jānakī bhāṣitaṁ tathā |
rakṣobhis trāsitā pūrvaṁ bhūyas trāsaṁ gamiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tato jātaparitrāsā śabdaṁ kuryān manasvinī |
jānamānā viśālākṣī rāvaṇaṁ kāmarūpiṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
sītayā ca kr̥te śabde sahasā rākṣasīgaṇaḥ |
nānāpraharaṇo ghoraḥ sameyād antakopamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tato māṁ saṁparikṣipya sarvato vikr̥tānanāḥ |
vadhe ca grahaṇe caiva kuryur yatnaṁ yathābalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
taṁ māṁ śākhāḥ praśākhāś ca skandhāṁś cottamaśākhinām |
dr̥ṣṭvā viparidhāvantaṁ bhaveyur bhayaśaṅkitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
mama rūpaṁ ca saṁprekṣya vanaṁ vicarato mahat |
rākṣasyo bhayavitrastā bhaveyur vikr̥tānanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tataḥ kuryuḥ samāhvānaṁ rākṣasyo rakṣasām api |
rākṣasendraniyuktānāṁ rākṣasendraniveśane ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
te śūlaśaranistriṁśa vividhāyudhapāṇayaḥ |
āpateyur vimarde ’smin vegenodvignakāriṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
saṁkruddhas tais tu parito vidhaman rakṣasāṁ balam |
śaknuyaṁ na tu saṁprāptuṁ paraṁ pāraṁ mahodadheḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
māṁ vā gr̥hṇīyur āplutya bahavaḥ śīghrakāriṇaḥ |
syād iyaṁ cāgr̥hītārthā mama ca grahaṇaṁ bhavet ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
hiṁsābhirucayo hiṁsyur imāṁ vā janakātmajām |
vipannaṁ syāt tataḥ kāryaṁ rāmasugrīvayor idam ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
uddeśe naṣṭamārge ’smin rākṣasaiḥ parivārite |
sāgareṇa parikṣipte gupte vasati jānakī ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
viśaste vā gr̥hīte vā rakṣobhir mayi saṁyuge |
nānyaṁ paśyāmi rāmasya sahāyaṁ kāryasādhane ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
vimr̥śaṁś ca na paśyāmi yo hate mayi vānaraḥ |
śatayojanavistīrṇaṁ laṅghayeta mahodadhim ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
kāmaṁ hantuṁ samartho ’smi sahasrāṇy api rakṣasām |
na tu śakṣyāmi saṁprāptuṁ paraṁ pāraṁ mahodadheḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
asatyāni ca yuddhāni saṁśayo me na rocate |
kaś ca niḥsaṁśayaṁ kāryaṁ kuryāt prājñaḥ sasaṁśayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
eṣa doṣo mahān hi syān mama sītābhibhāṣaṇe |
prāṇatyāgaś ca vaidehyā bhaved anabhibhāṣaṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
bhūtāś cārthā vinaśyanti deśakālavirodhitāḥ |
viklavaṁ dūtam āsādya tamaḥ sūryodaye yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
arthānarthāntare buddhir niścitāpi na śobhate |
ghātayanti hi kāryāṇi dūtāḥ paṇḍitamāninaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
na vinaśyet kathaṁ kāryaṁ vaiklavyaṁ na kathaṁ bhavet |
laṅghanaṁ ca samudrasya kathaṁ nu na vr̥thā bhavet ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
kathaṁ nu khalu vākyaṁ me śr̥ṇuyān nodvijeta ca |
iti saṁcintya hanumāṁś cakāra matimān matim ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
rāmam akliṣṭakarmāṇaṁ svabandhum anukīrtayan |
nainām udvejayiṣyāmi tad bandhugatamānasām ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
ikṣvākūṇāṁ variṣṭhasya rāmasya viditātmanaḥ |
śubhāni dharmayuktāni vacanāni samarpayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
śrāvayiṣyāmi sarvāṇi madhurāṁ prabruvan giram |
śraddhāsyati yathā hīyaṁ tathā sarvaṁ samādadhe ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
iti sa bahuvidhaṁ mahānubhāvo; jagatipateḥ pramadām avekṣamāṇaḥ |
madhuram avitathaṁ jagāda vākyaṁ; drumaviṭapāntaram āsthito hanūmān ||

=== meaning ===

---
