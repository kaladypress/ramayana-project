=== Sarga 28 ===

---
1.
sā rākṣasendrasya vaco niśamya tad rāvaṇasyāpriyam apriyārtā |
sītā vitatrāsa yathā vanānte siṁhābhipannā gajarājakanyā ||

> **Meaning 1:** Hearing those cruel words of Rāvaṇa, the Rākṣasendra, Sītā —
> already tormented by misfortune — trembled with fear, like a young
> she-elephant, seized by a lion at the edge of
> the forest.

---
2.
sā rākṣasī madhyagatā ca bhīruḥ vāgbhirbhr̥śaṁ rāvaṇatarjitā ca |
kāntāramadhye vijane visr̥ṣṭā bāleva kanyā vilalāpa sītā ||

> **Meaning 2:** Surrounded by the Rākṣasīs and harshly threatened by Rāvaṇa's
> words, the gentle Sītā wept like a young girl abandoned in the middle of a
> lonely, deserted forest.

---
3.
satyaṁ batedaṁ pravadanti loke nākālamr̥tyurbhavatīti santaḥ |
yatrāham evaṁ paribhartsyamānā jīvāmi kiṁ cit kṣaṇam apy apuṇyā ||

> **Meaning 3:** "Alas, the wise are right when they say that no one in this world dies before their appointed time. Otherwise, how could I — bereft of all merit — remain alive for even a moment while being threatened like this?"

---
4.
sukhād vihīnaṁ bahuduḥkhapūrṇam idaṁ tu nūnaṁ hr̥dayaṁ sthiraṁ me |
vidīryate yan na sahasradhādya vajrāhataṁ śr̥ṅgam ivācalasya ||

> **Meaning 4:** "This heart of mine — robbed of all happiness and filled with
> endless sorrow — must surely be made of stone. Otherwise, why doesn't it
> shatter into a thousand pieces today, like a mountain peak struck by a
> thunderbolt?"

---
5.
naivāsti nūnaṁ mama doṣam atra vadhyāham asyāpriyadarśanasya |
bhāvaṁ na cāsyāham anupradātum alaṁ dvijo mantram ivādvijāya ||

> **Meaning 5:** "Surely it is no sin for me to end my life now. I am going to be killed by this hideous one anyway, for I simply cannot give my heart to him — just as a dvija cannot pass on a sacred mantra to someone who is not a dvija."

---
6.
nūnaṁ mamāṅgāny acirād anāryaḥ śastraiḥ śitaiśchetsyati rākṣasendraḥ |
tasminn anāgacchati lokanāthe garbhasthajantoriva śalyakr̥ntaḥ ||

> **Meaning 6:** "Since Rāma, the protector of the world, has not come, this
> ignoble Rākṣasendra will soon cut my body to pieces with sharp weapons — the
> way a surgeon cuts apart an unborn child trapped in the womb."

---
7.
duḥkhaṁ batedaṁ mama duḥkhitāyā māsau cirāyābhigamiṣyato dvau |
baddhasya vadhyasya yathā niśānte rājāparādhād iva taskarasya ||

> **Meaning 7:** "Alas! For me, who has been immersed in sorrow for so long,
> the two-month deadline will soon come to an end. My situation is like that
> of a bound thief who, condemned for a crime against the king, is about to be
> executed at dawn."

---
8.
hā rāma hā lakṣmaṇa hā sumitre hā rāma mātaḥ saha me jananyā |
eṣā vipadyāmy aham alpabhāgyā mahārṇave nauriva mūḍha vātā ||

> **Meaning 8:** "Oh Rāma, Lakṣmaṇa, and Sumitrā! Oh mother of Rāma, and my own
> mother! Unfortunate as I am, I am perishing like a ship caught in a storm on
> the great ocean."

---
9.
tarasvinau dhārayatā mr̥gasya sattvena rūpaṁ manujendraputrau |
nūnaṁ viśastau mama kāraṇāt tau siṁharṣabhau dvāv iva vaidyutena ||

> **Meaning 9:** "That creature in the guise of a deer must surely have slain
> those two mighty princes because of me — just as lightning strikes down two
> great lions."

---
10.
nūnaṁ sa kālo mr̥garūpadhārī mām alpabhāgyāṁ lulubhe tadānīm |
yatrāryaputraṁ visasarja mūḍhā rāmānujaṁ lakṣmaṇapūrvakaṁ ca ||

> **Meaning 10:** "It must have been death itself taking the form of that deer
> to lure an unlucky woman like me. Fool that I was, I sent away my noble
> husband, and then his younger brother Lakṣmaṇa as well."

---
11.
hā rāma satyavrata dīrghavāhaḥ hā pūrṇacandrapratimānavaktra |
hā jīvalokasya hitaḥ priyaśca vadhyāṁ na māṁ vetsi hi rākṣasānām ||

> **Meaning 11:** "Oh Rāma — faithful to your vows, mighty-armed, and
> radiant as the full moon! You are a beloved friend to the whole world, yet
> you remain completely unaware that I am about to die at the hands of these
> Rākṣasas."

---
12.
ananyadevatvam iyaṁ kṣamā ca bhūmau ca śayyā niyamaśca dharme |
pativratātvaṁ viphalaṁ mamedaṁ kr̥taṁ kr̥taghneṣv iva mānuṣāṇām ||

> **Meaning 12:** "My unwavering devotion to you as my only god, my endurance,
> my sleeping on the bare earth, and my strict adherence to dharma as a
> faithful wife have all been in vain, just like kindness shown to the
> ungrateful."

---
13.
mogho hi dharmaścarito mamāyaṁ tathaikapatnītvam idaṁ nirartham |
yā tvāṁ na paśyāmi kr̥śā vivarṇā hīnā tvayā saṁgamane nirāśā ||

> **Meaning 13:** "Because I can no longer see you, my practice of dharma has
> been useless, and my devotion to you alone has been meaningless. Separated
> from you, I have grown thin and pale, and I have lost all hope of ever being
> with you again."

---
14.
piturnirdeśaṁ niyamena kr̥tvā vanān nivr̥ttaścaritavrataśca |
strībhistu manye vipulekṣaṇābhiḥ saṁraṁsyase vītabhayaḥ kr̥tārthaḥ ||

> **Meaning 14:** "After faithfully carrying out your father's command and
> completing your vow, you will return to Ayodhyā from the forest. I imagine
> that with your purpose fulfilled and all fear gone, you will enjoy the
> company of other wide-eyed women."

---
15.
ahaṁ tu rāma tvayi jātakāmā ciraṁ vināśāya nibaddhabhāvā |
moghaṁ caritvātha tapovrataṁ ca tyakṣyāmi dhig jīvitam alpabhāgyā ||

> **Meaning 15:** "But I have only brought about my own ruin, Rāma, by loving
> you and binding my heart to you for so long. My penance and vows have been
> entirely in vain, and now I must give up my life. Shame on this life of
> mine, unfortunate as I am!"

---
16.
sā jīvitaṁ kṣipram ahaṁ tyajeyaṁ viṣeṇa śastreṇa śitena vāpi |
viṣasya dātā na tu me asti kaścic chastrasya vā veśmani rākṣasasya ||

> **Meaning 16:** "I would gladly end my life right now with poison or a sharp
> weapon, but there is no one in the house of this Rākṣasa who will give me
> either."

---

17.
itīva sītā bahudhā vilapya sarvātmanā rāmam anusmarantī |
pravepamānā pariśuṣkavaktrā nagottamaṁ puṣpitam āsasāda ||

> **Meaning 17:** Lamenting in this way, with her whole being focused on Rāma,
> Sītā walked up to that magnificent flowering tree, trembling and with her
> mouth parched.

---

18.
śokābhitaptā bahudhā vicintya sītātha veṇyudgrathanaṁ gr̥hītvā |
udbadhya veṇyudgrathanena śīghram ahaṁ gamiṣyāmi yamasya mūlam ||

> **Meaning 18:** Burning with grief and lost in thought, Sītā took hold of her
> long braid and decided, "I will quickly hang myself with this braid and go
> to the realm of Yama."

---
19.
upasthitā sā mr̥dursarvagātrī śākhāṁ gr̥hītvātha nagasya tasya |
tasyāstu rāmaṁ pravicintayantyā rāmānujaṁ svaṁ ca kulaṁ śubhāṅgyāḥ ||

20.
śokānimittāni tadā bahūni dhairyārjitāni pravarāṇi loke |
prādurnimittāni tadā babhūvuḥ purāpi siddhāny upalakṣitāni ||

> **Meaning 19-20:** The tender and delicate Sītā stood holding a branch of
> that tree. Then she began to think of Rāma, Lakṣmaṇa, and her own noble
> family. Just then, many auspicious omens reappeared to the graceful one.
> These omens — world-renowned for yielding good results — dispel sorrow and
> restore courage, and they had already appeared to her in the past to
> indicate the arrival of Rāma.

---
