=== Sarga 27 ===

---
1.
tathāgatāṁ tāṁ vyathitām aninditāṁ; vyapetaharṣāṁ paridīnamānasām |
śubhāṁ nimittāni śubhāni bhejire; naraṁ śriyā juṣṭam ivopajīvinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tasyāḥ śubhaṁ vāmam arālapakṣma; rājīvr̥taṁ kr̥ṣṇaviśālaśuklam |
prāspandataikaṁ nayanaṁ sukeśyā; mīnāhataṁ padmam ivābhitāmram ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
bhujaś ca cārvañcitapīnavr̥ttaḥ; parārdhya kālāgurucandanārhaḥ |
anuttamenādhyuṣitaḥ priyeṇa; cireṇa vāmaḥ samavepatāśu ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
gajendrahastapratimaś ca pīnas; tayor dvayoḥ saṁhatayoḥ sujātaḥ |
praspandamānaḥ punar ūrur asyā; rāmaṁ purastāt sthitam ācacakṣe ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
śubhaṁ punar hemasamānavarṇam; īṣadrajodhvastam ivāmalākṣyāḥ |
vāsaḥ sthitāyāḥ śikharāgradantyāḥ; kiṁ cit parisraṁsata cārugātryāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
etair nimittair aparaiś ca subhrūḥ; saṁbodhitā prāg api sādhusiddhaiḥ |
vātātapaklāntam iva pranaṣṭaṁ; varṣeṇa bījaṁ pratisaṁjaharṣa ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tasyāḥ punar bimbaphalopamauṣṭhaṁ; svakṣibhrukeśāntam arālapakṣma |
vaktraṁ babhāse sitaśukladaṁṣṭraṁ; rāhor mukhāc candra iva pramuktaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sā vītaśokā vyapanītatandrī; śāntajvarā harṣavibuddhasattvā |
aśobhatāryā vadanena śukle; śītānśunā rātrir ivoditena ||

=== meaning ===

---
