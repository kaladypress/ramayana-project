=== Sarga 21 ===

---
1.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā sītā raudrasya rakṣasaḥ |
ārtā dīnasvarā dīnaṁ pratyuvāca śanair vacaḥ ||

> **Meaning 1:** Hearing those words of the fierce Rākṣasa, Sītā, distressed and
> speaking in a faint, trembling voice, slowly replied:

---
2.
duḥkhārtā rudatī sītā vepamānā tapasvinī |
cintayantī varārohā patim eva pativratā ||

> **Meaning 2:** Tormented by grief, weeping and trembling, that devoted,
> ascetic lady of graceful bearing thought only of her husband.

---
3.
tr̥ṇam antarataḥ kr̥tvā pratyuvāca śucismitā |
nivartaya mano mattaḥ svajane kriyatāṁ manaḥ ||

> **Meaning 3:** Placing a blade of grass between them, the pure-smiling lady
> spoke: "Turn your mind away from me, and turn it instead to your own wives!

---
4.
na māṁ prārthayituṁ yuktas tvaṁ siddhim iva pāpakr̥t |
akāryaṁ na mayā kāryam ekapatnyā vigarhitam ||

> **Meaning 4:** You have no right to covet me, any more than a sinner has a
> right to attain spiritual perfection; as a woman devoted to a single husband,
> I will never commit such a vile and contemptible act!

---
5.
kulaṁ samprāptayā puṇyaṁ kule mahati jātayā |
evam uktvā tu vaidehī rāvaṇaṁ taṁ yaśasvinī ||

> **Meaning 5:** Born into an illustrious lineage and wedded into a sacred
> family, I could never do this." Having spoken thus to Rāvaṇa, renowned
> Vaidehī,

---
6.
rākṣasaṁ pr̥ṣṭhataḥ kr̥tvā bhūyo vacanam abravīt |
nāham aupayikī bhāryā parabhāryā satī tava ||

> **Meaning 6:** turned her back upon the Rākṣasa and spoke again: "Being a
> chaste woman and the wife of another, I can never be a fit wife for you.

---
7.
sādhu dharmam avekṣasva sādhu sādhuvrataṁ cara |
yathā tava tathānyeṣāṁ rakṣyā dārā niśācara |
ātmānam upamāṁ kr̥tvā sveṣu dāreṣu ramyatām ||

> **Meaning 7:** Reflect carefully on dharma, and strictly observe the vows of
> the virtuous! Night-ranger, just as your wives must be protected, so too must
> the wives of others; make yourself the measure of others, and take pleasure
> only in your own wives.

---
8.
atuṣṭaṁ sveṣu dāreṣu capalaṁ calitendriyam |
nayanti nikr̥tiprajñāṁ paradārāḥ parābhavam ||

> **Meaning 8:** A man discontent with his own wives, fickle, and enslaved by
> his senses—such a depraved fool is dragged to ruin by coveting the wives of
> others.

---
9.
iha santo na vā santi sato vā nānuvartase |
tathā hi viparītā te buddhir ācāravarjitā ||

> **Meaning 9:** Are there no righteous men here in Laṅkā, or do you simply
> refuse to follow them? For your intellect is completely perverse and devoid of
> all moral conduct.

---
10.
vaco mithyā praṇītātmā pathyam uktaṁ vicakṣaṇaiḥ |
rākṣasānām abhāvāya tvaṁ vā na pratipadyase ||

> **Meaning 10:** Guided by delusion, you pay no heed to the wise counsel spoken
> for your own good, hastening the destruction of the Rākṣasas.

---
11.
akr̥tātmānam āsādya rājānam anaye ratam |
samr̥ddhāni vinaśyanti rāṣṭrāṇi nagarāṇi ca ||

> **Meaning 11:** When ruled by an undisciplined king who delights in
> lawlessness, prosperous nations and cities crumble to ruin.

---
12.
tatheyaṁ tvāṁ samāsādya laṅkā ratnaughasaṅkulā |
aparādhāt tavaikasya nacirād vinaśiṣyati ||

> **Meaning 12:** So too this Laṅkā, packed with hoards of jewels, having you
> for its master, will soon be annihilated because of your crime alone.

---
13.
svakr̥tair hanyamānasya rāvaṇādīrghadarśinaḥ |
abhinandanti bhūtāni vināśe pāpakarmaṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 13:** When a short-sighted and wicked person is struck down by the
> consequence of his own misdeeds, Rāvaṇa, all living creatures rejoice at his
> destruction!

---
14.
evaṁ tvāṁ pāpakarmāṇaṁ vakṣyanti nikr̥tā janāḥ |
diṣṭyaitad vyasanaṁ prāpto raudra ity eva harṣitāḥ ||

> **Meaning 14:** The very people you have oppressed will look upon your corpse
> and exclaim with joy: 'By good fortune, this tyrant has met his ruin!'

---
15.
śakyā lobhayituṁ nāham aiśvaryeṇa dhanena vā |
ananyā rāghaveṇāhaṁ bhāskareṇa prabhā yathā ||

> **Meaning 15:** I cannot be seduced by power or tempted by wealth; I am
> inseparable from Rāghava, as light is from the sun.

---
16.
upadhāya bhujaṁ tasya lokanāthasya satkr̥tam |
kathaṁ nāmopadhāsyāmi bhujam anyasya kasya cit ||

> **Meaning 16:** Having rested my head upon the honored arm of the Sovereign of
> the World, how could I ever rest upon the arm of any other?

---
17.
aham aupayikī bhāryā tasyaiva vasudhāpateḥ |
vratasnātasya viprasya vidyeva viditātmanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 17:** I am the rightful wife of that master of the earth alone, just
> as sacred knowledge belongs exclusively to an enlightened, vow-observing
> brāhmaṇa.

---
18.
sādhu rāvaṇa rāmeṇa māṁ samānaya duḥkhitām |
vane vāśitayā sārdhaṁ kareṇveva gajādhipam ||

> **Meaning 18:** Do the right thing, Rāvaṇa: reunite sorrow-stricken me with
> Rāma, just as a wild female elephant in the forest is reunited with the leader
> of the herd.

---
19.
mitram aupayikaṁ kartuṁ rāmaḥ sthānaṁ parīpsatā |
vadhaṁ cānicchatā ghoraṁ tvayāsau puruṣarṣabhaḥ ||

> **Meaning 19:** If you wish to preserve your position and avoid a terrifying
> death, that foremost among men, Rāma, is the only one you should seek to
> befriend.

---
20.
viditaḥ sa hi dharmajñaḥ śaraṇāgatavatsalaḥ |
tena maitrī bhavatu te yadi jīvitum icchasi ||

> **Meaning 20:** For he is renowned as a knower of righteousness and
> compassionate to those who seek refuge; forge friendship with him, if you have
> any desire to live!

---
21.
prasādayasva tvaṁ cainaṁ śaraṇāgatavatsalam |
māṁ cāsmai prayato bhūtvā niryātayitum arhasi ||

> **Meaning 21:** Seek his pardon, for he protects those who surrender; with a
> humble and pure heart, you must hand me back to him.

---
22.
evaṁ hi te bhavet svasti sampradāya raghūttame |
anyathā tvaṁ hi kurvāṇo vadhaṁ prāpsyasi rāvaṇa ||

> **Meaning 22:** Only by returning me to the foremost of the Raghus will you
> find safety; if you act otherwise, Rāvaṇa, you will meet certain death.

---
23.
varjayed vajram utsr̥ṣṭaṁ varjayed antakaś ciram |
tvadvidhaṁ na tu saṅkruddho lokanāthaḥ sa rāghavaḥ ||

> **Meaning 23:** A hurled thunderbolt might miss you, and Antaka, the god of
> death, might spare you for a time; but once enraged, that protector of the
> world, Rāghava, will never spare a creature like you!

---
24.
rāmasya dhanuṣaḥ śabdaṁ śroṣyasi tvaṁ mahāsvanam |
śatakratuvisr̥ṣṭasya nirghoṣam aśaner iva ||

> **Meaning 24:** Soon you will hear the deafening roar of Rāma's bowstring,
> booming like the crash of a thunderbolt loosed by Indra.

---
25.
iha śīghraṁ suparvāṇo jvalitāsyā ivoragāḥ |
iṣavo nipatiṣyanti rāmalakṣmaṇalakṣaṇāḥ ||

> **Meaning 25:** Smooth-jointed arrows inscribed with the names of Rāma and
> Lakṣmaṇa, hissing with fiery heads like venomous serpents, will rain down upon
> this place without delay.

---
26.
rakṣāṁsi parinighnantaḥ puryām asyāṁ samantataḥ |
asampātaṁ kariṣyanti patantaḥ kaṅkavāsasaḥ ||

> **Meaning 26:** Striking down the Rākṣasas on every side throughout this city,
> those falling shafts feathered with heron plumes will leave not a single foot
> of ground untouched.

---
27.
rākṣasendramahāsarpān sa rāmagaruḍo mahān |
uddhariṣyati vegena vainateya ivoragān ||

> **Meaning 27:** That great Garuḍa who is Rāma will swoop down in his fury and
> tear apart the giant serpents who are the Rākṣasa lords, just as Vainateya
> rips up real venomous snakes!

---
28.
apaneṣyati māṁ bhartā tvattaḥ śīghram arindamaḥ |
asurebhyaḥ śriyaṁ dīptāṁ viṣṇus tribhir iva kramaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 28:** My husband, the conqueror of foes, will quickly rescue me from
> you, just as Viṣṇu reclaimed the radiant Goddess Śrī from the Asuras with
> three strides.

---
29.
janasthāne hatasthāne nihate rakṣasāṁ bale |
aśaktena tvayā rakṣaḥ kr̥tam etad asādhu vai ||

> **Meaning 29:** When Janasthāna was laid waste and the army of Rākṣasas wiped
> out, you, cowardly Rākṣasa, committed this despicable deed because you were
> powerless to face them in the open!

---
30.
āśramaṁ tu tayoḥ śūnyaṁ praviśya narasiṁhayoḥ |
gocaraṁ gatayor bhrātror apanītā tvayādhama ||

> **Meaning 30:** Vile wretch! Sneaking into the empty hermitage when those two
> lion-like brothers were out of sight, you stole me away!

---
31.
na hi gandham upāghrāya rāmalakṣmaṇayos tvayā |
śakyaṁ sandarśane sthātuṁ śunā śārdūlayor iva ||

> **Meaning 31:** Catching merely the scent of Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa, you would
> never have dared stand before them—no more than a mongrel dog can stand before
> two tigers!

---
32.
tasya te vigrahe tābhyāṁ yugagrahaṇam asthiram |
vr̥trasyevendrabāhubhyāṁ bāhor ekasya nigrahaḥ ||

> **Meaning 32:** In combat against them, your hold on the yoke of battle would
> be broken instantly, just as the single-armed demon Vr̥tra was crushed by the
> two arms of Indra.

---
33.
kṣipraṁ tava sa nātho me rāmaḥ saumitriṇā saha |
toyam alpam ivādityaḥ prāṇān ādāsyate śaraiḥ ||

> **Meaning 33:** My master Rāma, alongside Saumitri, will swiftly drain your
> life-breaths with his arrows, as the blazing sun evaporates a shallow pool of
> water.

---
34.
giriṁ kuberasya gato ’tha vālayaṁ sabhāṁ gato vā varuṇasya rājñaḥ |
asaṁśayaṁ dāśarather na mokṣyase mahādrumaḥ kālahato ’śaner iva ||

> **Meaning 34:** Whether you flee to the mountain of Kubera, hide in his
> palace, or take refuge in the assembly hall of King Varuṇa, you will never
> escape the son of Daśaratha, any more than a doomed tree can escape a
> thunderbolt!"
