=== Sarga 36 ===

---
1.
bhūya eva mahātejā hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ |
abravīt praśritaṁ vākyaṁ sītāpratyayakāraṇāt ||

> **Meaning 1:** Then the glorious Hanumān, son of Māruta, spoke again with
> humility, to win Sītā's trust:

---
2.
vānaraḥ ahaṁ mahābhāge dūto rāmasya dhīmataḥ |
rāmanāmāṅkitaṁ cedaṁ paśya devy aṅgulīyakam ||

3.
pratyayārthaṁ tavānītaṁ tena dattaṁ mahātmanā |
samāśvasihi bhadraṁ te kṣīṇaduḥkhaphalā hy asi ||

> **Meaning 2-3:** "Noble lady, I am a vānara, the messenger of the wise Rāma.
> Look, devī — here is a ring engraved with Rāma's name. The great-souled one
> gave it to me, and I have brought it so that you can be sure. Take heart, and
> bless you — your suffering is nearly over."

---
4.
(ityuktvā pradadau tasyai sītāyai vānarottamaḥ |)
gr̥hītvā prekṣamāṇā sā bhartuḥ karavibhūṣaṇam |
bhartāram iva saṁprāptā jānakī muditābhavat ||

> **Meaning 4:** Having spoken thus, that best of vānaras presented ring to Sītā.Jānakī took her husband's ornament and gazed at it. She was
> overjoyed, as if her husband himself had come to her.

---
5.
cāru tad vadanaṁ tasyāḥ tāmraśuklāyatekṣaṇam |
babhūva praharṣodagraṁ rāhumukta ivoḍurāṭ ||

> **Meaning 5:** Her lovely face, with its long eyes of white tinged with red,
> lit up with delight, like the moon freed from Rāhu.

---
6.
tataḥ sā hrīmatī bālā bhartuḥ saṁdeśaharṣitā |
parituṭṣā priyaṁ śrutvā prāśaṁsata mahākapim ||

> **Meaning 6:** Then the modest young princess, thrilled by her husband's
> message and comforted by the good news, praised the great Kapi:

---
7.
vikrāntastvaṁ samarthastvaṁ prājñastvaṁ vānarottama |
yenedaṁ rākṣasapadaṁ tvayaikena pradharṣitam ||

> **Meaning 7:** "You are brave, capable, and wise, best of the vānaras, since
> you alone have overpowered this stronghold of the rākṣasas.

---
8.
śatayojanavistīrṇaḥ sāgaro makarālayaḥ |
vikramaślāghanīyena kramatā goṣpadīkr̥taḥ ||

> **Meaning 8:** "The ocean, home of the makaras, stretches a hundred yojanas,
> yet with your praiseworthy strength you crossed it as if it were a puddle in a
> cow's hoofprint.

---
9.
na hi tvāṁ prākr̥taṁ manye vanaraṁ vanararṣabha |
yasya te nāsti saṁtrāsaḥ rāvaṇān nāpi saṁbhramaḥ ||

> **Meaning 9:** "Bull among vānaras, I don't think you are an ordinary vānara,
> since you feel neither fear nor alarm at Rāvaṇa.

---
10.
arhase ca kapiśreṣṭha mayā samabhibhāṣitum |
yady asi preṣitastena rāmeṇa viditātmanā ||

> **Meaning 10:** "Best of the Kapis, you deserve to speak with me, as you have
> been sent by that mahatma srirama.

---
11.
preṣayiṣyati durdharṣaḥ rāmo na hy aparīkṣitam |
parākramam avijñāya matsakāśaṁ viśeṣataḥ ||

> **Meaning 11:** "The invincible Rāma would never send anyone he had not tested
> — especially not to me — without knowing his strength.

---
12.
diṣṭyā ca kuśalī rāmaḥ dharmātmā dharmavatsalaḥ |
lakṣmaṇaśca mahātejāḥ sumitrānandavardhanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 12:** "I am fortunate that — righteous Rāma, who loves dharma
> — and the glorious Lakṣmaṇa too, who brings such joy to Sumitrā.

---
13.
kuśalī yadi kākutsthaḥ kiṁ nu sāgaramekhalām |
mahīṁ dahati kopena yugāntāgnirivotthitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 13:** "But if Kākutstha is well, why doesn't he rise in anger like
> the fire at the end of an age and burn up the earth, girdled by the sea?

---
14.
atha vā śaktimantau tau surāṇām api nigrahe |
mamaiva tu na duḥkhānām asti manye viparyayaḥ ||

> **Meaning 14:** "Or perhaps those two are able to subdue even the devas, and
> it is only my sufferings that will never end.

---
15.
kaccicca vyathate rāmaḥ kaccin na paripatyate |
uttarāṇi ca kāryāṇi kurute puruṣottamaḥ ||

> **Meaning 15:** "Is Rāma in pain? Is he tormented? Is that best of men doing
> what must be done next?

---
16.
kaccin na dīnaḥ saṁbhrāntaḥ kāryeṣu ca na muhyati |
kaccin puruṣakāryāṇi kurute nr̥pateḥ sutaḥ ||

> **Meaning 16:** "I hope he is not downcast or flustered, and not confused
> about what he must do. Is the king's son putting forth every human effort?

---
17.
dvividhaṁ trividhopāyam upāyam api sevate |
vijigīṣuḥ suhr̥t kaccin mitreṣu ca paraṁtapaḥ ||

> **Meaning 17:** "Is Śrī Rāma, that terror to his enemies, affectionately employing the strategies of sāma and dāna toward his allies, and applying the strategies of dāna, bhēda, and daṇḍa against his foes with a desire for victory?

---
18.
kaccin mitrāṇi labhate mitraiścāpy abhigamyate |
kaccit kalyāṇamitraśca mitraiścāpi puraskr̥taḥ ||

> **Meaning 18:** "Is he winning allies, and are allies coming to him? Does he
> have good friends, and do his friends honor him?

---
19.
kaccid āśāsti devānāṁ prasādaṁ pārthivātmajaḥ |
kaccit puruṣakāraṁ ca daivaṁ ca pratipadyate ||

> **Meaning 19:** "Does the prince pray for the grace of the devas? Does he rely
> on both human effort and destiny?

---
20.
kaccin na vigatasnehaḥ vivāsān mayi rāghavaḥ |
kaccin māṁ vyasanād asmān mokṣayiṣyati vānaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 20:** "Has Rāghava lost his love for me because I have been away?
> Vānara, will he free me from this misery?

---
21.
sukhānām ucito nityam asukhānām anūcitaḥ |
duḥkham uttaram āsādya kaccid rāmo na sīdati ||

> **Meaning 21:** "He has always been used to comfort and never to hardship. Now
> that one sorrow follows another, is Rāma breaking down?

---
22.
kausalyāyāstathā kaccit sumitrāyāstathaiva ca |
abhīkṣṇaṁ śrūyate kaccit kuśalaṁ bharatasya ca ||

> **Meaning 22:** "Does he hear news often of Kausalyā's and Sumitrā's welfare,
> and of Bharata's?

---
23.
mannimittena mānārhaḥ kaccicchokena rāghavaḥ |
kaccin nānyamanā rāmaḥ kaccin māṁ tārayiṣyati ||

> **Meaning 23:** "Is Rāghava, who deserves such honor, consumed by grief
> because of me? Is his mind turned to anything else? Will Rāma rescue me?

---
24.
kaccid akṣauhiṇīṁ bhīmāṁ bharato bhrātr̥vatsalaḥ |
dhvajinīṁ mantribhirguptāṁ preṣayiṣyati matkr̥te ||

> **Meaning 24:** "Will Bharata, who loves his brother, send a fearsome
> akṣauhiṇī, an army with banners guarded by his ministers, for my sake?

---
25.
vānarādhipatiḥ śrīmān sugrīvaḥ kaccid eṣyati |
matkr̥te haribhirvīraiḥ vr̥to dantanakhāyudhaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 25:** "Will the glorious Sugrīva, king of the vānaras, come for my
> sake, surrounded by heroic Haris whose weapons are their teeth and claws?

---
26.
kaccicca lakṣmaṇaḥ śūraḥ sumitrānandavardhanaḥ |
astraviccharajālena rākṣasān vidhamiṣyati ||

> **Meaning 26:** "And will brave Lakṣmaṇa, the joy of Sumitrā, master of
> astras, blast the rākṣasas apart with a storm of arrows?

---
27.
raudreṇa kaccid astreṇa rāmeṇa nihataṁ raṇe |
drakṣyāmy alpena kālena rāvaṇaṁ sasuhr̥jjanam ||

> **Meaning 27:** "Will I soon see Rāvaṇa, along with his friends and kin,
> struck down in battle by Rāma with the fierce Raudra astra?

---
28.
kaccin na tad dhemasamānavarṇaṁ tasyānanaṁ padmasamānagandhi |
mayā vinā śuṣyati śokadīnaṁ jalakṣaye padmam ivātapena ||

> **Meaning 28:** "That face of srirama, golden in color and fragrant as a lotus —
> without me, is it withering with grief, like a lotus scorched by the sun when
> the water dries up?

---
29.
dharmāpadeśāt tyajataśca rājyāṁ māṁ cāpy araṇyaṁ nayataḥ padātim |
nāsīd vyathā yasya na bhīrna śokaḥ kaccit sa dhairyaṁ hr̥daye karoti ||

> **Meaning 29:** "When he gave up his kingdom for the sake of dharma and led me
> on foot into the forest, he felt no pain, no fear, no grief. Is he keeping
> that courage in his heart now?

---
30.
na cāsya mātā na pitā na cānyaḥ snehād viśiṣṭaḥ asti mayā samo vā |
tāvad dhy ahaṁ dūtajijīviṣeyaṁ yāvat pravr̥ttiṁ śr̥ṇuyāṁ priyasya ||

> **Meaning 30:** "Neither his mother, nor his father, nor anyone else is dearer
> to him than I am, or even as dear. I want to live only as long as I
> can hear news of my beloved."

---
31.
itīva devī vacanaṁ mahārthaṁ taṁ vānarendraṁ madhurārtham uktvā |
śrotuṁ punastasya vacaḥ abhirāmaṁ rāmārthayuktaṁ virarāma rāmā ||

> **Meaning 31:** Having spoken these deeply meaningful, sweetly intended words
> to that chief of the vānaras, the beautiful devī fell silent, waiting to hear
> again his delightful words about Rāma.

---
32.
sītāyā vacanaṁ śrutvā mārutirbhīmavikramaḥ |
śirasy añjalim ādhāya vākyam uttaram abravīt ||

> **Meaning 32:** Hearing Sītā's words, Māruti, of fearsome valor, raised his
> joined palms to his head and replied:

---
33.
na tvām ihasthāṁ jānīte rāmaḥ kamalalocanaḥ |
tena tvāṁ nānayatyāśu śacīmiva puraṁdaraḥ ||


> **Meaning 33:** "Lotus-eyed Rāma does not know that you are here. That is why
> he has not yet come to take you back, the way Purandara rescued Śacī.

---
34.
śrutvaiva tu vaco mahyaṁ kṣipram eṣyati rāghavaḥ |
camūṁ prakarṣan mahatīṁ haryr̥ṣkagaṇasaṁkulām ||

35.
viṣṭambhayitvā bāṇaughaiḥ akṣobhyaṁ varuṇālayam |
kariṣyati purīṁ laṅkāṁ kākutsthaḥ śāntarākṣasām ||

> **Meaning 34-35:** "As soon as he hears my report, Rāghava will come quickly,
> leading a vast army teeming with hosts of Haris and Ṛkṣas. With floods of
> arrows Kākutstha will hold back the unshakable ocean, home of Varuṇa, and
> leave the city of Laṅkā emptied of rākṣasas.

---
36.
tatra yady antarā mr̥tyuḥ yadi devāḥ sahāsurāḥ |
sthāsyanti pathi rāmasya sa tān api vadhiṣyati ||

> **Meaning 36:** "If Death himself, or the devas together with the asuras,
> stand in Rāma's way, he will kill even them.

---
37.
tavādarśanajenārye śokena sa pariplutaḥ |
na śarma labhate rāmaḥ siṁhārdita iva dvipaḥ ||

> **Meaning 37:** "Noble lady, overwhelmed by the grief of not seeing you, Rāma
> finds no peace, like an elephant mauled by a lion.

---
38.
malayena ca vindhyena meruṇā mandareṇa ca |
dardareṇa ca te devi śape mūlaphalena ca ||


> **Meaning 38:** "Devī, I swear to you by Malaya and Vindhya, by Meru, Mandara,
> and Dardara, and by the roots and fruits we live on,

---
39.
yathā sunayanaṁ valgu bimbauṣṭhaṁ cārukuṇḍalam |
mukhaṁ drakṣyasi rāmasya pūrṇacandram ivoditam ||

> **Meaning 39:** "that you will see Rāma's face — with its beautiful eyes, lips
> red as bimba fruit, and lovely earrings — rising before you like the full
> moon.

---
40.
kṣipraṁ drakṣyasi vaidehi rāmaṁ prasravaṇe girau |
śatakratum ivāsīnaṁ nākapr̥ṣṭhasya mūrdhani ||

> **Meaning 40:** "Vaidehī, you will soon see Rāma on Mount Prasravaṇa, seated
> like Śatakratu on the summit of heaven.

---
41.
na māṁsaṁ rāghavo bhuṅkte na cāpi madhusevate |
vanyaṁ suvihitaṁ nityaṁ bhaktam aśnāti pañcamam ||

> **Meaning 41:** "Rāghava does not eat meat or drink honey wine. Every day, at
> evening, he eats only simple forest food, ordained by his vows.

---
42.
naiva daṁśān na maśakān na kīṭān na sarīsr̥pān |
rāghavaḥ apanayed gatrāt tvadgatenāntarātmanā ||

> **Meaning 42:** "His whole inner self is fixed on you, so Rāghava doesn't even
> brush away the gadflies, mosquitoes, insects, or creeping things from his
> body.

---
43.
nityaṁ dhyānaparo rāmaḥ nityaṁ śokaparāyaṇaḥ |
nānyaccintayate kiṁ cit sa tu kāmavaśaṁ gataḥ ||

> **Meaning 43:** "Rāma is always lost in thought, always sunk in grief. He
> thinks of nothing else; he is completely in the grip of longing.

---
44.
anidraḥ satataṁ rāmaḥ suptaḥ api ca narottamaḥ |
sīteti madhurāṁ vāṇīṁ vyāharan pratibudhyate ||

> **Meaning 44:** "Rāma, best of men, hardly ever sleeps. Even when he does, he
> wakes up calling out the sweet word, 'Sītā!'

---
45.
dr̥ṣṭvā phalaṁ vā puṣpaṁ vā yaccānyat strīmanoharam |
bahuśo hā priyety evaṁ śvasaṁstvām abhibhāṣate ||

> **Meaning 45:** "Whenever he sees a fruit, a flower, or anything else a woman
> would love, he sighs again and again and speaks to you: 'Ah, my beloved!'

---
46.
sa devi nityaṁ paritapyamānaḥ tvām eva sītety abhibhāṣamāṇaḥ |
dhr̥tavrato rājasuto mahātmā tavaiva lābhāya kr̥taprayatnaḥ ||

> **Meaning 46:** "Devī, the great-souled prince is always in anguish, calling
> out only 'Sītā.' Firm in his vows, he is putting all his effort into winning
> you back."

---
47.
sā rāmasaṁkīrtanavītaśokā rāmasya śokena samānaśokā |
śaranmukhenāmbudaśeṣacandrā niśeva vaidehasutā babhūva ||

> **Meaning 47:** Hearing Rāma praised, the daughter of Videha was freed from
> grief, yet she grieved with Rāma's grief. She was like an autumn night at its
> beginning, with the moon and the last of the clouds.

---
