=== Sarga 36 ===

---
1.
tataḥ sa kapiśārdūlas tena vākyena harṣitaḥ |
sītām uvāca tac chrutvā vākyaṁ vākyaviśāradaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
yuktarūpaṁ tvayā devi bhāṣitaṁ śubhadarśane |
sadr̥śaṁ strīsvabhāvasya sādhvīnāṁ vinayasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
strītvaṁ na tu samarthaṁ hi sāgaraṁ vyativartitum |
mām adhiṣṭhāya vistīrṇaṁ śatayojanam āyatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
dvitīyaṁ kāraṇaṁ yac ca bravīṣi vinayānvite |
rāmād anyasya nārhāmi saṁsparśam iti jānaki ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
etat te devi sadr̥śaṁ patnyās tasya mahātmanaḥ |
kā hy anyā tvām r̥te devi brūyād vacanam īdr̥śam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
śroṣyate caiva kākutsthaḥ sarvaṁ niravaśeṣataḥ |
ceṣṭitaṁ yat tvayā devi bhāṣitaṁ mama cāgrataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
kāraṇair bahubhir devi rāma priyacikīrṣayā |
snehapraskannamanasā mayaitat samudīritam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
laṅkāyā duṣpraveśatvād dustaratvān mahodadheḥ |
sāmarthyād ātmanaś caiva mayaitat samudāhr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
icchāmi tvāṁ samānetum adyaiva raghubandhunā |
gurusnehena bhaktyā ca nānyathā tad udāhr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
yadi notsahase yātuṁ mayā sārdham anindite |
abhijñānaṁ prayaccha tvaṁ jānīyād rāghavo hi yat ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
evam uktā hanumatā sītā surasutopamā |
uvāca vacanaṁ mandaṁ bāṣpapragrathitākṣaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
idaṁ śreṣṭham abhijñānaṁ brūyās tvaṁ tu mama priyam |
śailasya citrakūṭasya pāde pūrvottare tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tāpasāśramavāsinyāḥ prājyamūlaphalodake |
tasmin siddhāśrame deśe mandākinyā adūrataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tasyopavanaṣaṇḍeṣu nānāpuṣpasugandhiṣu |
vihr̥tya salilaklinnā tavāṅke samupāviśam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
paryāyeṇa prasuptaś ca mamāṅke bharatāgrajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tato māṁsasamāyukto vāyasaḥ paryatuṇḍayat |
tam ahaṁ loṣṭam udyamya vārayāmi sma vāyasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
dārayan sa ca māṁ kākas tatraiva parilīyate |
na cāpy uparaman māṁsād bhakṣārthī balibhojanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
utkarṣantyāṁ ca raśanāṁ kruddhāyāṁ mayi pakṣiṇe |
sraṁsamāne ca vasane tato dr̥ṣṭā tvayā hy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tvayā vihasitā cāhaṁ kruddhā saṁlajjitā tadā |
bhakṣya gr̥ddhena kālena dāritā tvām upāgatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
āsīnasya ca te śrāntā punar utsaṅgam āviśam |
krudhyantī ca prahr̥ṣṭena tvayāhaṁ parisāntvitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
bāṣpapūrṇamukhī mandaṁ cakṣuṣī parimārjatī |
lakṣitāhaṁ tvayā nātha vāyasena prakopitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
āśīviṣa iva kruddhaḥ śvasān vākyam abhāṣathāḥ |
kena te nāganāsoru vikṣataṁ vai stanāntaram |
kaḥ krīḍati saroṣeṇa pañcavaktreṇa bhoginā ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
vīkṣamāṇas tatas taṁ vai vāyasaṁ samavaikṣathāḥ |
nakhaiḥ sarudhirais tīkṣṇair mām evābhimukhaṁ sthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
putraḥ kila sa śakrasya vāyasaḥ patatāṁ varaḥ |
dharāntaracaraḥ śīghraṁ pavanasya gatau samaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tatas tasmin mahābāhuḥ kopasaṁvartitekṣaṇaḥ |
vāyase kr̥tavān krūrāṁ matiṁ matimatāṁ vara ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
sa darbhasaṁstarād gr̥hya brahmaṇo ’streṇa yojayaḥ |
sa dīpta iva kālāgnir jajvālābhimukho dvijam ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
cikṣepitha pradīptāṁ tām iṣīkāṁ vāyasaṁ prati |
anusr̥ṣṭas tadā kālo jagāma vividhāṁ gatim |
trāṇakāma imaṁ lokaṁ sarvaṁ vai vicacāra ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
sa pitrā ca parityaktaḥ suraiḥ sarvair maharṣibhiḥ |
trīm̐l lokān saṁparikramya tvām eva śaraṇaṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
taṁ tvaṁ nipatitaṁ bhūmau śaraṇyaḥ śaraṇāgatam |
vadhārham api kākutstha kr̥payā paryapālayaḥ |
na śarma labdhvā lokeṣu tvām eva śaraṇaṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
paridyūnaṁ viṣaṇṇaṁ ca sa tvam āyāntam uktavān |
moghaṁ kartuṁ na śakyaṁ tu brāhmam astraṁ tad ucyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
tatas tasyākṣi kākasya hinasti sma sa dakṣiṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
sa te tadā namaskr̥tvā rājñe daśarathāya ca |
tvayā vīra visr̥ṣṭas tu pratipede svam ālayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
matkr̥te kākamātre ’pi brahmāstraṁ samudīritam |
kasmād yo māṁ harat tvattaḥ kṣamase taṁ mahīpate ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
sa kuruṣva mahotsāhaṁ kr̥pāṁ mayi nararṣabha |
ānr̥śaṁsyaṁ paro dharmas tvatta eva mayā śrutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
jānāmi tvāṁ mahāvīryaṁ mahotsāhaṁ mahābalam |
apārapāram akṣobhyaṁ gāmbhīryāt sāgaropamam |
bhartāraṁ sasamudrāyā dharaṇyā vāsavopamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
evam astravidāṁ śreṣṭhaḥ sattvavān balavān api |
kimartham astraṁ rakṣaḥsu na yojayasi rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
na nāgā nāpi gandharvā nāsurā na marudgaṇāḥ |
rāmasya samare vegaṁ śaktāḥ prati samādhitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
tasyā vīryavataḥ kaś cid yady asti mayi saṁbhramaḥ |
kimarthaṁ na śarais tīkṣṇaiḥ kṣayaṁ nayati rākṣasān ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
bhrātur ādeśam ādāya lakṣmaṇo vā paraṁtapaḥ |
kasya hetor na māṁ vīraḥ paritrāti mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
yadi tau puruṣavyāghrau vāyvindrasamatejasau |
surāṇām api durdharṣo kimarthaṁ mām upekṣataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
mamaiva duṣkr̥taṁ kiṁ cin mahad asti na saṁśayaḥ |
samarthāv api tau yan māṁ nāvekṣete paraṁtapau ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
kausalyā lokabhartāraṁ suṣuve yaṁ manasvinī |
taṁ mamārthe sukhaṁ pr̥ccha śirasā cābhivādaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
srajaś ca sarvaratnāni priyā yāś ca varāṅganāḥ |
aiśvaryaṁ ca viśālāyāṁ pr̥thivyām api durlabham ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
pitaraṁ mātaraṁ caiva saṁmānyābhiprasādya ca |
anupravrajito rāmaṁ sumitrā yena suprajāḥ |
ānukūlyena dharmātmā tyaktvā sukham anuttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
anugacchati kākutsthaṁ bhrātaraṁ pālayan vane |
siṁhaskandho mahābāhur manasvī priyadarśanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
pitr̥vad vartate rāme mātr̥van māṁ samācaran |
hriyamāṇāṁ tadā vīro na tu māṁ veda lakṣmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
vr̥ddhopasevī lakṣmīvāñ śakto na bahubhāṣitā |
rājaputraḥ priyaśreṣṭhaḥ sadr̥śaḥ śvaśurasya me ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
mattaḥ priyataro nityaṁ bhrātā rāmasya lakṣmaṇaḥ |
niyukto dhuri yasyāṁ tu tām udvahati vīryavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
yaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rāghavo naiva vr̥ddham āryam anusmarat |
sa mamārthāya kuśalaṁ vaktavyo vacanān mama |
mr̥dur nityaṁ śucir dakṣaḥ priyo rāmasya lakṣmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
idaṁ brūyāś ca me nāthaṁ śūraṁ rāmaṁ punaḥ punaḥ |
jīvitaṁ dhārayiṣyāmi māsaṁ daśarathātmaja |
ūrdhvaṁ māsān na jīveyaṁ satyenāhaṁ bravīmi te ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
rāvaṇenoparuddhāṁ māṁ nikr̥tyā pāpakarmaṇā |
trātum arhasi vīra tvaṁ pātālād iva kauśikīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
52.
tato vastragataṁ muktvā divyaṁ cūḍāmaṇiṁ śubham |
pradeyo rāghavāyeti sītā hanumate dadau ||

=== meaning ===

---
53.
pratigr̥hya tato vīro maṇiratnam anuttamam |
aṅgulyā yojayām āsa na hy asyā prābhavad bhujaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
54.
maṇiratnaṁ kapivaraḥ pratigr̥hyābhivādya ca |
sītāṁ pradakṣiṇaṁ kr̥tvā praṇataḥ pārśvataḥ sthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
55.
harṣeṇa mahatā yuktaḥ sītādarśanajena saḥ |
hr̥dayena gato rāmaṁ śarīreṇa tu viṣṭhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
56.
maṇivaram upagr̥hya taṁ mahārhaṁ; janakanr̥pātmajayā dhr̥taṁ prabhāvāt |
girivarapavanāvadhūtamuktaḥ; sukhitamanāḥ pratisaṁkramaṁ prapede ||

=== meaning ===

---
