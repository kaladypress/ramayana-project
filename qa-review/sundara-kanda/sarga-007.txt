=== Sarga 7 ===

---
1.
sa veśmajālaṁ balavān dadarśa vyāsaktavaidūryasuvarṇajālam |
yathā mahat prāvr̥ṣi meghajālaṁ vidyutpinaddhaṁ savihaṁgajālam ||

> **Meaning 1:** The mighty Kapi saw a network of palaces adorned with
> latticework of cat's-eye gems and gold, looking like a vast mass of rain
> clouds in the monsoon, laced with lightning and accompanied by flocks of
> birds.

---
2.
niveśanānāṁ vividhāśca śālāḥ pradhānaśaṅkhāyudhacāpaśālāḥ |
manoharāścāpi punarviśālā dadarśa veśmādriṣu candraśālāḥ ||

> **Meaning 2:** He saw diverse halls within the mansions — great armories
> storing conches, weapons, and bows — and spacious, enchanting moonlit terrace
> pavilions set atop palace towers.

---
3.
gr̥hāṇi nānāvasurājitāni devāsuraiścāpi supūjitāni |
sarvaiśca doṣaiḥ parivarjitāni kapirdadarśa svabalārjitāni ||

> **Meaning 3:** The Kapi beheld mansions glittering with various treasures,
> honored even by devatās and Asuras, free of all flaws, won by the Rākṣasas
> through their own prowess.

---
4.
tāni prayatnābhisamāhitāni mayena sākṣād iva nirmitāni |
mahītale sarvaguṇottarāṇi dadarśa laṅkādhipatergr̥hāṇi ||

> **Meaning 4:** As if fashioned by the architect Maya himself with utmost care
> and artistry, those palaces of the ruler of Laṅkā stood unmatched on earth,
> endowed with every excellence.

---
5.
tato dadarśocchritamegharūpaṁ manoharaṁ kāñcanacārurūpam |
rakṣo’dhipasyātmabalānurūpaṁ gr̥hottamaṁ hy apratirūparūpam ||

> **Meaning 5:** Then he saw the supreme palace of the king of Rākṣasas,
> towering like a cloud, enchanting with its radiant golden form, worthy of his
> mighty power, peerless in beauty.

---
6.
mahītale svargam iva prakīrṇaṁ śriyā jvalantaṁ bahuratnakīrṇam |
nānātarūṇāṁ kusumāvakīrṇaṁ girerivāgraṁ rajasāvakīrṇam ||

> **Meaning 6:** It seemed like heaven itself transplanted to earth, blazing
> with splendor, studded with countless gems, strewn with blossoms from various
> trees like a mountain peak dusted with floral pollen.

---
7.
nārīpravekairiva dīpyamānaṁ taḍidbhirambhodavad arcyamānam |
haṁsapravekairiva vāhyamānaṁ śriyā yutaṁ khe sukr̥tāṁ vimānam ||

> **Meaning 7:** Radiant with exquisite women like a cloud illumined by flashes
> of lightning, it looked like a splendid celestial aerial chariot soaring
> through the sky for the virtuous, as if borne aloft by noble swans.

---
8.
yathā nagāgraṁ bahudhātucitraṁ yathā nabhaśca grahacandracitram |
dadarśa yuktīkr̥tameghacitraṁ vimānaratnaṁ bahuratnacitram ||

> **Meaning 8:** Like a mountain summit variegated with diverse ores, like the
> sky adorned with planets and the moon, or like artistic cloud formations, he
> saw that jewel among aerial chariots, decorated with myriad gems.

---
9.
mahī kr̥tā parvatarājipūrṇā śailāḥ kr̥tā vr̥kṣavitānapūrṇāḥ |
vr̥kṣāḥ kr̥tāḥ puṣpavitānapūrṇāḥ puṣpaṁ kr̥taṁ kesarapatrapūrṇam ||

> **Meaning 9:** On it, simulated earth was wrought complete with mountain
> ranges; the hills were fashioned with canopies of trees; the trees were
> crafted full of blossom canopies; and each blossom was detailed with filaments
> and petals.

---
10.
kr̥tāni veśmāni ca pāṇḍurāṇi tathā supuṣpā api puṣkariṇyaḥ |
punaśca padmāni sakesarāṇi dhanyāni citrāṇi tathā vanāni ||

> **Meaning 10:** Brilliant white mansions were carved upon it, along with lotus
> ponds filled with blossoming water lilies, lotuses complete with inner
> filaments, and enchanting, richly varied pleasure groves.

---
11.
puṣpāhvayaṁ nāma virājamānaṁ ratnaprabhābhiśca vivardhamānam |
veśmottamānām api coccamānaṁ mahākapistatra mahāvimānam ||

> **Meaning 11:** There the great Kapi saw that glorious aerial chariot named
> Puṣpaka, radiant and magnified by the luster of its gems, towering above the
> finest palaces.

---
12.
kr̥tāśca vaidūryamayā vihaṁgā rūpyapravālaiśca tathā vihaṁgāḥ |
citrāśca nānāvasubhirbhujaṁgā jātyānurūpāsturagāḥ śubhāṅgāḥ ||

> **Meaning 12:** Crafted upon it were birds of cat's-eye gems, birds of silver
> and coral, serpents fashioned from various precious stones, and noble horses
> of exquisite form and breeding.

---
13.
pravālajāmbūnadapuṣpapakṣāḥ salīlam āvarjitajihmapakṣāḥ |
kāmasya sākṣād iva bhānti pakṣāḥ kr̥tā vihaṁgāḥ sumukhāḥ supakṣāḥ ||

> **Meaning 13:** Beautiful birds were sculpted with fine beaks and lovely wings
> made of coral and gold blossoms, their wings playfully curved and angled,
> gleaming like companions of the god of love.

---
14.
niyujyamānāśca gajāḥ suhastāḥ sakesarāścotpalapatrahastāḥ |
babhūva devī ca kr̥tā suhastā lakṣmīstathā padmini padmahastā ||

> **Meaning 14:** Elephants with graceful trunks, holding lotus petals with
> their filaments, were depicted bathing her, and Devī Lakṣmī herself was
> sculpted with lovely hands, holding a lotus, seated in a lotus pool.

---
15.
itīva tad gr̥ham abhigamya śobhanaṁ savismayo nagam iva cāruśobhanam |
punaśca tat paramasugandhi sundaraṁ himātyaye nagam iva cārukandaram ||

> **Meaning 15:** Filled with wonder, Hanumān approached that magnificent
> mansion gleaming like a majestic mountain, fragrant and beautiful like a
> mountain with lovely caves at the close of winter.

---
16.
tataḥ sa tāṁ kapirabhipatya pūjitāṁ caran purīṁ daśamukhabāhupālitām |
adr̥śya tāṁ janakasutāṁ supūjitāṁ suduḥkhitāṁ patiguṇaveganirjitām ||

> **Meaning 16:** Leaping across and wandering through that splendid city
> protected by the ten-headed king's arms, the Kapi did not see the revered
> daughter of Janaka, who was sorrowing deeply and wholly absorbed in her
> husband's virtues.

---
17.
tatastadā bahuvidhabhāvitātmanaḥ kr̥tātmano janakasutāṁ suvartmanaḥ |
apaśyato ’bhavad atiduḥkhitaṁ manaḥ sucakṣuṣaḥ pravicarato mahātmanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 17:** Then, as he searched all around with keen eyes, the mind of
> that great-souled, disciplined, and wise hero was overcome with deep sorrow at
> not finding Janaka's daughter, who walked the path of righteousness.

---
