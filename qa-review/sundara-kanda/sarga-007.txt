=== Sarga 6 ===

---
1.
sa veśmajālaṁ balavān dadarśa; vyāsaktavaidūryasuvarṇajālam |
yathā mahat prāvr̥ṣi meghajālaṁ; vidyutpinaddhaṁ savihaṁgajālam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
niveśanānāṁ vividhāś ca śālāḥ; pradhānaśaṅkhāyudhacāpaśālāḥ |
manoharāś cāpi punar viśālā; dadarśa veśmādriṣu candraśālāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
gr̥hāṇi nānāvasurājitāni; devāsuraiś cāpi supūjitāni |
sarvaiś ca doṣaiḥ parivarjitāni; kapir dadarśa svabalārjitāni ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tāni prayatnābhisamāhitāni; mayena sākṣād iva nirmitāni |
mahītale sarvaguṇottarāṇi; dadarśa laṅkādhipater gr̥hāṇi ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tato dadarśocchritamegharūpaṁ; manoharaṁ kāñcanacārurūpam |
rakṣo’dhipasyātmabalānurūpaṁ; gr̥hottamaṁ hy apratirūparūpam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
mahītale svargam iva prakīrṇaṁ; śriyā jvalantaṁ bahuratnakīrṇam |
nānātarūṇāṁ kusumāvakīrṇaṁ; girer ivāgraṁ rajasāvakīrṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
nārīpravekair iva dīpyamānaṁ; taḍidbhir ambhodavad arcyamānam |
haṁsapravekair iva vāhyamānaṁ; śriyā yutaṁ khe sukr̥tāṁ vimānam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
yathā nagāgraṁ bahudhātucitraṁ; yathā nabhaś ca grahacandracitram |
dadarśa yuktīkr̥tameghacitraṁ; vimānaratnaṁ bahuratnacitram ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
mahī kr̥tā parvatarājipūrṇā; śailāḥ kr̥tā vr̥kṣavitānapūrṇāḥ |
vr̥kṣāḥ kr̥tāḥ puṣpavitānapūrṇāḥ; puṣpaṁ kr̥taṁ kesarapatrapūrṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
kr̥tāni veśmāni ca pāṇḍurāṇi; tathā supuṣpā api puṣkariṇyaḥ |
punaś ca padmāni sakesarāṇi; dhanyāni citrāṇi tathā vanāni ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
puṣpāhvayaṁ nāma virājamānaṁ; ratnaprabhābhiś ca vivardhamānam |
veśmottamānām api coccamānaṁ; mahākapis tatra mahāvimānam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
kr̥tāś ca vaidūryamayā vihaṁgā; rūpyapravālaiś ca tathā vihaṁgāḥ |
citrāś ca nānāvasubhir bhujaṁgā; jātyānurūpās turagāḥ śubhāṅgāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
pravālajāmbūnadapuṣpapakṣāḥ; salīlam āvarjitajihmapakṣāḥ |
kāmasya sākṣād iva bhānti pakṣāḥ; kr̥tā vihaṁgāḥ sumukhāḥ supakṣāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
niyujyamānāś ca gajāḥ suhastāḥ; sakesarāś cotpalapatrahastāḥ |
babhūva devī ca kr̥tā suhastā; lakṣmīs tathā padmini padmahastā ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
itīva tad gr̥ham abhigamya śobhanaṁ; savismayo nagam iva cāruśobhanam |
punaś ca tat paramasugandhi sundaraṁ; himātyaye nagam iva cārukandaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tataḥ sa tāṁ kapir abhipatya pūjitāṁ; caran purīṁ daśamukhabāhupālitām |
adr̥śya tāṁ janakasutāṁ supūjitāṁ; suduḥkhitāṁ patiguṇaveganirjitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tatas tadā bahuvidhabhāvitātmanaḥ; kr̥tātmano janakasutāṁ suvartmanaḥ |
apaśyato ’bhavad atiduḥkhitaṁ manaḥ; sucakṣuṣaḥ pravicarato mahātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
