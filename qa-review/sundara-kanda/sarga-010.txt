=== Sarga 10 ===

---
1.
tatra divyōpamaṁ mukhyaṁ sphāṭikaṁ ratnabhūṣitam |
avēkṣamāṇō hanumān dadarśa śayanāsanam ||

> **Meaning 1:** Looking all around, Hanumān saw a supreme, bed-chamber platform
> made of crystal and adorned with gems, resembling something divine.

---
2.
dāṁtakāṁcanacitrāṁgaiḥ vaiḍūryaiśca varāsanaiḥ |
mahārhāstaraṇōpētaiḥ upapannaṁ mahādhanaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 2:** It was furnished with costly and splendid seats adorned with
> carved ivory, gold, and cat's-eye gems, and covered with priceless carpets.

---
3.
tasyacaikatamē dēśē sōgrya’mālā vibhūṣitam |
dadarśa pāṁḍuracchatraṁ tārādhipatisannibham ||

> **Meaning 3:** In one part of it, he saw a bright white umbrella decorated
> with fine garlands, shining like the full moon.

---
4.
jātarūpaparikṣiptaṁ citrabhānusamaprabham |
aśōkamālāvitataṁ dadarśa paramāsanam ||

> **Meaning 4:** He saw a magnificent couch inlaid with pure gold, blazing with
> the brilliance of fire, draped with garlands of aśoka blossoms.

---
5.
vālavyajanahastābhiḥ vījyamānaṁ samaṁtataḥ |
gaṁdhaiśca vividhairjuṣṭaṁ varadhūpēna dhūpitam ||

> **Meaning 5:** It was fanned on all sides by women holding yak-tail whisks,
> scented with diverse perfumes, and fragrant with the smoke of rich incense.

---
6.
paramāstaraṇāstīrṇaṁ āvikājinasaṁvr̥tam |
dāmabhirvaramālyānāṁ samaṁtādupaśōbhitam ||

> **Meaning 6:** It was spread with luxurious coverings, draped with soft
> sheepskin and deer-pelt rugs, and adorned on all sides with strands of choice
> garlands.

---
7.
tasmin jīmūtasaṁkāśaṁ pradīptōttamakuṁḍalam |
lōhitākṣaṁ mahābāhuṁ mahārajatavāsasam ||

> **Meaning 7:** Reclining upon it, dark as a rain cloud, wearing glowing
> earrings, red-eyed, mighty-armed, dressed in gold-embroidered robes,

---
8.
lōhitēnānuliptāṁgaṁ caṁdanēna sugaṁdhinā |
saṁdhyāraktamivākāśē tōyadaṁ sataṭidgaṇam ||

> **Meaning 8:** his body anointed with fragrant red sandalwood, looking like a
> cloud reddened by twilight in the sky accompanied by flashes of lightning,

---
9.
vr̥tamābharaṇairdivyaiḥ surūpaṁ kāmarūpiṇam |
savr̥kṣavanagulmāḍhyaṁ prasuptamiva maṁdaram ||

> **Meaning 9:** adorned with divine ornaments, handsome and capable of changing
> forms at will, appearing like Mount Mandara asleep with all its trees,
> forests, and thickets,

---
10.
krīḍitvōparataṁ rātrau varābharaṇabhūṣitam |
priyaṁ rākṣasakanyānāṁ rākṣasānāṁ sukhāvaham ||

> **Meaning 10:** having finished his nocturnal revels, decked in fine
> ornaments, dear to the Rākṣasa maidens and the giver of joy to all Rākṣasas,

---
11.
pītvāpyuparataṁ cāpi dadarśa sa mahākapiḥ |
bhāsvarē śayanē vīraṁ prasuptaṁ rākṣasādhipam ||

> **Meaning 11:** having drunk his fill and fallen asleep on that radiant couch,
> the heroic king of Rākṣasas was seen by the great Kapi.

---
12.
niśśvasaṁtaṁ yathā nāgaṁ rāvaṇaṁ vānararṣabhaḥ |
āsādya paramōdvignaḥ sōpāsarpat subhītavat ||

> **Meaning 12:** Coming near Rāvaṇa as he hissed in his sleep like a venomous
> serpent, the bull among Vānaras felt deep apprehension and stepped back like
> one afraid.

---
13.
athārōhaṇamāsādya vēdikāṁtaramāśritaḥ |
suptaṁ rākṣasaśārdūlaṁ prēkṣatē sma mahākapiḥ ||

> **Meaning 13:** Then, climbing the steps and taking shelter on another
> elevated dais, the great Kapi observed that sleeping tiger among Rākṣasas.

---
14.
śuśubhē rākṣasēṁdrasya svapata śśayanōttamam |
gaṁdhahastini saṁviṣṭē yathā prasravaṇaṁ mahat ||

> **Meaning 14:** That splendid couch looked magnificent with the sleeping king
> of Rākṣasas upon it, like a vast plateau of Mount Prasravaṇa with a lordly
> scent-elephant resting upon it.

---
15.
kāṁcanāṁgadasannaddhau ca dadarśa sa mahātmanaḥ |
vikṣiptau rākṣasēṁdrasya bhujāviṁdradhvajōpamau ||

> **Meaning 15:** He saw the outstretched arms of that mighty king of Rākṣasas,
> bound with golden armlets and resembling the banner-poles of Indra.

---
16.
airāvataviṣāṇāgraiḥ āpīḍanakr̥tavraṇau |
vajrōllikhitapīnāṁsau viṣṇucakraparikṣatau ||

> **Meaning 16:** They bore scars made by the thrusts of Airāvata's tusk-tips,
> their thick shoulders were furrowed by the thunderbolt of Indra, and marked by
> the blows of Viṣṇu's discus.

---
17.
pīnau samasujātāṁsau saṁgatau balasaṁyutau |
sulakṣaṇanakhāṁguṣṭau svaṁgulītalalakṣitau ||

> **Meaning 17:** Well-proportioned and thick-set, symmetrical and powerful,
> with auspicious nails, thumbs, and fingers marked with clear lines,

---
18.
saṁhatau parighākārau vr̥ttau karikarōpamau |
vikṣiptau śayanē śubhrē paṁcaśīrṣāvivōragau ||

> **Meaning 18:** massive as iron bars, rounded like the trunks of elephants,
> those arms lay flung across the white couch like two five-headed serpents.

---
19.
śaśakṣatajakalpēna suśītēna sugaṁdhinā |
caṁdanēna parārdhyēna svanuliptau svalaṁkr̥tau ||

> **Meaning 19:** They were richly anointed with the finest, cooling red
> sandalwood paste, crimson as hare's blood, and adorned with elegant ornaments.

---
20.
uttamastrī vimr̥ditau gaṁdhōttamaniṣēvitau |
yakṣapannaga gaṁdharva dēvadānavarāviṇau ||

> **Meaning 20:** Caressed by noble women, fragrant with choice perfumes, those
> arms had made Yakṣas, Pannagas, Gandharvas, devas, and Dānavas weep in terror.

---
21.
dadarśa sa kapistasya bāhū śayanasaṁsthitau |
maṁdarasyāṁtarē suptau mahāhī ruṣitāviva ||

> **Meaning 21:** The Kapi saw his arms resting on the bed like two great
> venomous serpents asleep in a gorge of Mount Mandara.

---
22.
tābhyāṁ sa paripūrṇābhyāṁ bhujābhyāṁ rākṣasēśvaraḥ |
śuśubhē’calasaṁkāśaḥ śr̥ṁgābhyāmiva maṁdaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 22:** With those two powerful arms, the king of Rākṣasas looked
> magnificent like a mountain, resembling Mount Mandara with its two lofty
> peaks.

---
23.
cūtapunnāga surabhiḥ vakulōttamasaṁyutaḥ |
mr̥ṣṭānnarasa saṁyuktaḥ pānagaṁdhapurassaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 23:** Fragrant with mango and punnāga blossoms, combined with the
> scent of choice bakula flowers, carrying the aroma of delicious food and the
> fragrance of fine wines,

---
24.
tasya rākṣasasiṁhasya niścakrāma mahāmukhāt |
śayānasya viniśśvāsaḥ pūrayanniva tadgr̥ham ||

> **Meaning 24:** the breath came forth from that sleeping lion of a Rākṣasa's
> wide mouth, seeming to fill the entire chamber.

---
25.
muktāmaṇivicitrēṇa kāṁcanēna virājitam |
makuṭēnāpavr̥ttēna kuṁḍalōjvalitānanam ||

> **Meaning 25:** His face was illuminated by gleaming earrings, while his
> golden crown, patterned with pearls and gems, was tilted slightly to one side.

---
26.
raktacaṁdanadigdhēna tathā hārēṇa śōbhinā |
pīnāyataviśālēna vakṣasābhivirājitam ||

> **Meaning 26:** He was splendid with his broad, thick, and expansive chest,
> anointed with red sandalwood and adorned with sparkling necklaces.

---
27.
pāṁḍarēṇāpaviddhēna kṣaumēṇa kṣatajēkṣaṇam |
mahārhēṇa susaṁvītaṁ pītēnōttamavāsasā ||

> **Meaning 27:** His red eyes were closed, his white silk upper cloth was
> tossed aside, and he was wrapped in an exquisite, costly yellow garment.

---
28.
māṣarāśipratīkāśaṁ niśśvasaṁtaṁ bhujaṁgavat |
gāṁgē mahati tōyāṁtē prasuptamiva kuṁjaram ||

> **Meaning 28:** Dark as a heap of black beans, hissing like a serpent, he
> looked like a great elephant asleep by the waters of the sacred Gaṅgā.

---
29.
caturbhiḥ kāṁcanairdīpaiḥ dīpyamānacaturdiśam |
prakāśīkr̥ta sarvāṁgaṁ mēghaṁ vidyudgaṇairiva ||

> **Meaning 29:** With four golden lamps blazing in the four corners, his entire
> form was illuminated, looking like a dark cloud lit up by clusters of
> lightning flashes.

---
30.
pādamūlagatāścāpi dadarśa sumahātmanaḥ
patnīssa priyabhāryasya tasya rakṣaḥpatērgr̥hē ||

> **Meaning 30:** In that mansion of the king of Rākṣasas, who was devoted to
> his wives, the Kapi also saw his spouses resting at his feet.

---
31.
śaśiprakāśavadanāḥ cārukuṁḍalabhūṣitāḥ |
amlānamālyābharaṇā dadarśa hariyūthapaḥ ||

> **Meaning 31:** The Hari troop leader saw women with faces bright as the moon,
> adorned with charming earrings and unfaded garlands and ornaments.

---
32.
nr̥ttavāditrakuśalāḥ rākṣasēṁdrabhujāṁkagāḥ |
varābharaṇadhāriṇyaḥ niṣaṇṇā dadr̥śē hariḥ ||

> **Meaning 32:** The Hari saw women skilled in dance and instrumental music,
> nestled near the arms and lap of the king of Rākṣasas, wearing splendid
> jewelry.

---
33.
vajravaiḍūrya garbhāṇi śravaṇāṁtēṣu yōṣitām |
dadarśa tāpanīyāni kuṁḍalānyaṁgadāni ca ||

> **Meaning 33:** He saw golden earrings set with diamonds and cat's-eye gems
> dangling from their ears, and fine armlets on their arms.

---
34.
tāsāṁ caṁdrōpamairvaktraiḥ śubhairlalitakuṁḍalaiḥ |
virarāja vimānaṁ tat nabhastārāgaṇairiva ||

> **Meaning 34:** With their moon-like faces and graceful, gleaming earrings,
> that chamber shone like the sky adorned with clusters of stars.

---
35.
madavyāyāmakhinnāstāḥ rākṣasēṁdrasya yōṣitaḥ |
tēṣu tēṣvavakāśēṣu prasuptā stanumadhyamāḥ ||

> **Meaning 35:** Exhausted from drinking and dancing, those slender-waisted
> wives of the Rākṣasa king lay fast asleep in various places.

---
36.
aṁgahāraistathaivānyā kōmalairnr̥ttaśālinī |
vinyastaśubhasarvāṁgī prasuptā varavarṇinī ||

> **Meaning 36:** Skilled in dance, one fair lady lay asleep with her graceful
> form relaxed, frozen in a gentle dance posture.

---
37.
kācidvīṇāṁ pariṣvajya prasuptā saṁprakāśatē |
mahānadīprakīrṇēva nalinī pōtamāśritā ||

> **Meaning 37:** One woman lay clasping her vīṇā, glowing like a lotus clinging
> to a boat in a wide river.

---
38.
anyā kakṣagatēnaiva maḍḍukēnāsitēkṣaṇā |
prasuptā bhāminī bhāti bālaputrēva vatsalā ||

> **Meaning 38:** Another dark-eyed beauty slept holding a maḍḍuka drum under
> her arm, looking like an affectionate mother holding her young child.

---
39.
paṭahaṁ cārusarvāṁgī pīḍya śētē śubhastanī |
cirasya ramaṇaṁ labdhvā pariṣvajyēva bhāminī ||

> **Meaning 39:** A radiant woman of faultless beauty slept pressing a paṭaha
> drum to her bosom, as though embracing a lover she had found after a long
> separation.

---
40.
kācidvaṁśaṁ pariṣvajya suptā kamalalōcanā |
rahaḥ priyatamaṁ gr̥hya sakāmēva ca kāminī ||

> **Meaning 40:** A lotus-eyed woman slept embracing her flute, looking like a
> passionate woman holding her beloved in secret.

---
41.
vipaṁcīṁ parigr̥hyānyā niyatā nr̥ttaśālinī |
nidrāvaśamanuprāptā sahakāṁtēva bhāminī ||

> **Meaning 41:** Another dancer, clasping her vipañcī lute, had succumbed to
> sleep as though resting beside her lover.

---
42.
anyā kanakasaṁkāśaiḥ mr̥dupīnairmanōramaiḥ
mr̥daṁgaṁ paripīḍyāṁgaiḥ prasuptā mattalōcanā ||

> **Meaning 42:** Another woman with languid eyes slept pressing her mr̥daṅga
> drum against her soft, plump, golden form.

---
43.
bhujapārśvāṁtarasthēna kakṣagēṇa kr̥śōdarī |
paṇavēva sahāniṁdyā suptā madakr̥tāṁśramā ||

> **Meaning 43:** A slender-waisted, blameless woman, worn out by wine, slept
> holding a paṇava drum under her arm and beside her flank.

---
44.
ḍiṁḍimaṁ parigr̥hyānyā tathaivāsakta ḍiṁḍimā |
prasuptā taruṇaṁ vatsaṁ upagūhyēva bhāminī ||

> **Meaning 44:** Another woman lay holding a ḍiṇḍima drum, clinging to it as
> though embracing a tender young calf.

---
45.
kācidāḍaṁbaraṁ nārī bhujasaṁyōgapīḍitam |
kr̥tvā kamalapatrākṣī prasuptā madamōhitā ||

> **Meaning 45:** Another lotus-eyed woman, overcome by wine, lay asleep
> clutching an āḍambara drum tightly in her arms.

---
46.
kalaśīmapavidhyānyā prasuptā bhāti bhāminī |
vasaṁtē puṣpaśabalā mālēva parimārjitā ||

> **Meaning 46:** Another lovely woman lay asleep with her pitcher knocked
> aside, looking like a colorful floral garland dropped in springtime.

---
47.
pāṇibhyāṁ ca kucau kācit suvarṇakalaśōpamau |
upagūhyābalā suptā nidrābalaparājitā ||

> **Meaning 47:** Overcome by the power of sleep, another young woman slept with
> her hands clasping her bosom, which resembled golden jars.

---
48.
anyā kamalapatrākṣī pūrṇēṁdusadr̥śānanā |
anyāmāliṁgya suśrōṇīṁ prasuptā madavihvalā ||

> **Meaning 48:** Another lotus-eyed woman with a face like the full moon, tipsy
> with wine, slept embracing another graceful companion.

---
49.
ātōdyāni vicitrāṇi pariṣvajya varastriyaḥ |
nipīḍya ca kucaissuptāḥ kāminyaḥ kāmukāniva ||

> **Meaning 49:** Clasping their varied musical instruments and pressing them to
> their chests, those beautiful women slept like passionate women holding their
> lovers.

---
50.
tāsāmēkāṁtavinyastē śayānāṁ śayanē śubhē |
dadarśa rūpasaṁpannāṁ aparāṁ sa kapiḥ striyam ||

> **Meaning 50:** On a splendid couch set apart from the rest, the Kapi saw
> another woman of exquisite beauty lying asleep.

---
51.
muktāmaṇisamāyuktaiḥ bhūṣaṇai ssuvibhūṣitām |
vibhūṣayaṁtīmiva tat svaśriyā bhavanōttamam ||

> **Meaning 51:** Adorned with jewelry of pearls and gems, she seemed to
> illuminate that magnificent palace with her own natural radiance.

---
52.
gaurīṁ kanakavarṇāṁgīṁ iṣṭāmaṁtaḥ purēśvarīm |
kapirmaṁḍōdarīṁ tatra śayānāṁ cārurūpiṇīm ||

> **Meaning 52:** The Kapi saw the lovely Mandodarī lying there — fair,
> golden-complexioned, the beloved queen of the inner palace.

---
53.
sa tāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā mahābāhuḥ bhūṣitāṁ mārutātmajaḥ |
tarkayāmāsa sītēti rūpayauvanasaṁpadā |
harṣēṇa mahatā yuktō nanaṁda hariyūthapaḥ ||

> **Meaning 53:** Seeing her adorned and endowed with such wealth of beauty and
> youth, the mighty-armed son of Māruta assumed she must be Sītā; and the Hari
> troop leader rejoiced, filled with immense joy.

---
54.
āsphōṭayāmāsa cucuṁba pucchaṁ
nanaṁda cikrīḍa jagau jagāma |
staṁbhānarōhannipapāta bhūmau
nidarśayan svāṁ prakr̥tiṁ kapīnām ||

> **Meaning 54:** He slapped his arms, kissed his tail, rejoiced, pranced about,
> sang, moved here and there, climbed pillars and dropped to the floor,
> displaying the true nature of Kapis.
