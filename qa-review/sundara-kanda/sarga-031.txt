=== Sarga 31 ===

---
1.
evaṁ bahuvidhāṁ cintāṁ cintayitvā mahākapiḥ |
saṁśrave madhuraṁ vākyaṁ vaidehyā vyājahāra ha ||

> **Meaning 1:** Having pondered in many ways like this, Hanumān spoke
> gentle words, softly enough for Vaidehī to hear:

---
2.
rājā daśaratho nāma rathakuñjaravājinām |
puṇyaśīlo mahākīrtiḥ r̥jurāsīn mahāyaśāḥ ||

3.
rājarṣīṇāṁ guṇaśrēṣṭhaḥ tapasā carṣibhissamaḥ |
cakravartikule jātaḥ puraṁdarasamo bale ||

> **Meaning 2:** "There was a great king named Daśaratha, a man of pure
> nature and great renown, who possessed the might of divine chariots, elephants,
> and noble horses. Celebrated for his righteous deeds, he was upright in
> nature and famous for his valor and heroism.
>
> **Meaning 3:** "Foremost in virtue among royal sages and equal to the great
> sages in austerity, he was born into a line of emperors and rivalled Indra in
> strength.

---
4.
ahiṁsāratirakṣudraḥ ghr̥ṇī satyaparākramaḥ |
mukhyaścekṣvākuvaṁśasya lakṣmīvām̐l lakṣmivardhanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 4:** "He delighted in non-violence, was never petty, and was full
> of compassion; his valour was grounded in truth. Foremost of the Ikṣvāku line,
> he was blessed with fortune and brought fortune to others.

---
5.
pārthivavyañjanairyuktaḥ pr̥thuśrīḥ pārthivarṣabhaḥ |
pr̥thivyāṁ caturantāyāṁ viśrutaḥ sukhadaḥ sukhī ||

> **Meaning 5:** "Bearing every mark of a true king, rich in splendour and the
> finest of rulers, he was celebrated across the whole earth, bounded by its four
> seas. Happy himself, he brought happiness to all.

---
6.
tasya putraḥ priyo jyeṣṭhaḥ tārādhipanibhānanaḥ |
rāmo nāma viśeṣajñaḥ śreṣṭhaḥ sarvadhanuṣmatām ||

> **Meaning 6:** "His eldest and dearest son, whose face is like the moon,
> is named Rāma — the embodiment of wisdom, and the finest of all archers.

---
7.
rakṣitā svasya vr̥ttasya svajanasyāpi rakṣitā |
rakṣitā jīvalokasya dharmasya ca paraṁtapaḥ ||

> **Meaning 7:** "That scourge of his enemies upholds his own noble character and
> protects his people; he is the guardian of all living beings, and of dharma itself.

---
8.
tasya satyābhisaṁdhasya vr̥ddhasya vacanāt pituḥ |
sabhāryaḥ saha ca bhrātrā vīraḥ pravrajito vanam ||

> **Meaning 8:** "At the word of his aged father, who was ever true to his
> promise, that hero went into exile in the forest with his wife and his
> brother.

---
9.
tena tatra mahāraṇye mr̥gayāṁ paridhāvatā |
rākṣasā nihatāḥ śūrā bahavaḥ kāmarūpiṇaḥ ||

10.
janasthānavadhaṁ śrutvā hatau ca kharadūṣaṇau |
tatastv amarṣāpahr̥tā jānakī rāvaṇena tu ||
vaṁcayitvā vanē rāmaṁ mr̥garūpēṇa māyayā ||

11.
sa mārgamāṇastāṁ dēvīṁ rāma ssītāmaniṁditām |
āsasāda vanē mitraṁ sugrīvaṁ nāma vānaram ||

12.
tatassa vālinaṁ hatvā rāmaḥ parapuraṁjayaḥ |
prāyacchat kapirājyaṁ tat sugrīvāya mahābalaḥ ||

13.
sugrīvēṇāpi saṁdiṣṭāḥ harayaḥ kāmarūpiṇaḥ |
dikṣu sarvāsu tāṁ dēvīṁ vicinvaṁti sahasraśaḥ ||

14.
ahaṁ saṁpātivacanāt śatayōjanamāyatam |
asyā hētōrviśālākṣyāḥ sāgaraṁ vēgavān plutaḥ ||

> **Meaning 9-14:** "As he ranged through the great forest hunting, he slew
> many valiant rākṣasas who could take any form at will. Hearing of the
> slaughter at Janasthāna and the deaths of Khara and Dūṣaṇa, Rāvaṇa in his rage
> carried off Jānakī, after luring Rāma away through the illusion of a deer.
> While searching for the pure-hearted lady Sītā, Rāma found a friend in the
> forest — the vānara named Sugrīva. Then mighty Rāma, conqueror of enemies,
> slew Vālī and gave the kingdom of the vānaras to Sugrīva. At
> Sugrīva's command, vānaras by the thousands, able to take any form at will,
> are searching for that noble lady in every direction. And I, on the advice of
> Saṁpāti, have leapt swiftly across the ocean, a hundred yojanas wide, for the
> sake of this wide-eyed lady.

---
15.
yathārūpāṁ yathāvarṇāṁ yathālakṣmīṁ viniścitām |
aśrauṣaṁ rāghavasyāhaṁ seyam āsāditā mayā ||

> **Meaning 15:** "Her form, her complexion, and her grace are exactly as I heard
> them described by Rāghava. This is Sītā, and I have found her."

---
16.
virarāmaivam uktvāsau vācaṁ vānarapuṁgavaḥ |
jānakī cāpi tacchrutvā vismayaṁ paramaṁ gatā ||

> **Meaning 16:** Having spoken thus, that finest of vānaras fell silent. And
> Jānakī, hearing his words, was filled with the deepest wonder.

---
17.
tataḥ sā vakrakeśāntā sukeśī keśasaṁvr̥tam |
unnamya vadanaṁ bhīruḥ śiṁśapāvr̥kṣam aikṣata ||

> **Meaning 17:** The face of that timid, fair-tressed lady was hidden behind
> a veil of her curly hair. Lifting her head, she gazed up into the śiṁśapā tree.

---
18.
niśamya sītā vacanaṁ kapēśca
diśaśca sarvāḥ pradiśaśca vīkṣya |
svayaṁ praharṣaṁ paramaṁ jagāma
sarvātmanā rāmamanusmaraṁtī ||

19.
sā tiryag ūrdhvaṁ ca tathāpy adhastān nirīkṣamāṇā tam acintya buddhim |
dadarśa piṅgādhipateramātyaṁ vātātmajaṁ sūryam ivodayastham ||

> **Meaning 18-19:** Hearing Hanumān's words, Sītā looked all around her —
> to every quarter and every point between — and, meditating on Rāma with her
> whole being, was filled with supreme joy. Searching to the side, above and
> below, she saw him: the minister of Sugrīva, the son of the Wind, of
> unfathomable wisdom — shining like the rising sun.

---
