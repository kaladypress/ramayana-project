=== Sarga 54 ===

---
1.
vīkṣamāṇastatō laṁkāṁ kapiḥ kr̥tamanōrathaḥ |
vardhamāna samutsāhaḥ kāryaśēṣamaciṁtayat || 1 ||

> **Meaning 1:** Looking at Laṅkā, the Kapi felt his purpose had been fulfilled.
> His enthusiasm kept growing, and he began to think about what was still left
> to do.

---
2.
kinnu khalvavaśiṣṭaṁ mē kartavyamiha sāṁpratam |
yadēṣāṁ rakṣasāṁ bhūyaḥ saṁtāpajananaṁ bhavēt || 2 ||

> **Meaning 2:** "What is there still left for me to do here now that would
> cause these Rākṣasas further distress?

---
3.
vanaṁ tāvatpramathitaṁ prakr̥ṣṭā rākṣasā hatāḥ |
balaikadēśaḥ kṣapitaḥ śēṣaṁ durgavināśanam || 3 ||

> **Meaning 3:** The grove has been torn apart, the best of the Rākṣasas have
> been killed, and part of their army has been destroyed. Only the destruction
> of the fortress remains.

---
4.
durgē vināśitē karma bhavētsukhapariśramam |
alpayatnēna kāryē’smin mama syātsaphala śśramaḥ || 4 ||

> **Meaning 4:** If the fortress is destroyed, the rest of the work will be
> easy, and my effort in this task will bear fruit with very little additional
> trouble.

---
5.
yō hyayaṁ mama lāṁgūlē dīpyatē havyavāhanaḥ |
asya saṁtarpaṇaṁ nyāyyaṁ kartumēbhirgr̥hōttamaiḥ || 5 ||

> **Meaning 5:** This fire blazing on my tail should be satisfied. It is only
> right to feed it with these finest of houses."

---
6.
tataḥ pradīptalāṁgūlaḥ savidyudiva tōyadaḥ |
bhavanāgrēṣu laṁkāyāḥ vicacāra mahākapiḥ || 6 ||

> **Meaning 6:** Then Mahākapi Hanumān, his tail ablaze, moved across the
> rooftops of Laṅkā like a rain cloud lit by lightning.

---
7.
gr̥hādgr̥haṁ rākṣasānāṁ udyānāni ca vānaraḥ |
vīkṣamāṇō hyasaṁtrastaḥ prāsādāṁśca cacāra saḥ || 7 ||

> **Meaning 7:** Fearless, the Vānara went from house to house, looking over the
> Rākṣasas' gardens and mansions as he roamed.

---
8.
avaplutya mahāvēgaḥ prahastasya nivēśanam |
agniṁ tatra sa nikṣipya śvasanēna samō balī || 8 ||

> **Meaning 8:** Leaping with tremendous speed, the mighty Hanumān, who was
> equal to the wind, reached the residence of Prahasta and set it on fire.

---
9.
tatō’nyatpupluvē vēśma mahāpārśvasya vīryavān |
mumōca hanumānagniṁ kālānalaśikhōpamam || 9 ||

> **Meaning 9:** Then the powerful Hanumān sprang to another mansion, that of
> Mahāpārśva, and let loose a fire that looked like the flame of the doomsday
> blaze.

---
10.
vajradaṁṣṭrasya ca tathā pupluvē sa mahākapiḥ |
śukasya ca mahātējāḥ sāraṇasya ca dhīmataḥ || 10 ||

> **Meaning 10:** In the same way the great Kapi leapt to the homes of
> Vajradaṃṣṭra, the mighty Śuka, and the wise Sāraṇa.

---
11.
tathā cēṁdrajitō vēśma dadāha hariyūthapaḥ |
jaṁbumālēḥ sumālēśca dadāha bhavanaṁ tataḥ || 11 ||

> **Meaning 11:** The leader of the Hari host then burned the house of Indrajit,
> and after that the mansions of Jambumāli and Sumāli.

---
12.
raśmikētōśca bhavanaṁ sūryaśatrōstathaiva ca |
hrasvakarṇasya daṁṣṭrasya rōmaśasya ca rakṣasaḥ || 12 ||

> **Meaning 12:** He burned the mansions of Raśmiketu, Sūryaśatru, Hrasvakarṇa,
> Daṃṣṭra, and the Rākṣasa Romaśa.

---
13.
yuddhōnmattasya mattasya dhvajagrīvasya rakṣasaḥ |
vidyujjihvasya ghōrasya tathā hastimukhasya ca || 13 ||

> **Meaning 13:** He burned the houses of Yuddhonmatta, Matta, the Rākṣasa
> Dhvajagrīva, the fearsome Vidyujjihva, and Hastimukha.

---
14.
karālasya piśācasya śōṇitākṣasya caiva hi |
kuṁbhakarṇasya bhavanaṁ makarākṣasya caiva hi || 14 ||

> **Meaning 14:** He burned the houses of Karāla, Piśāca, Śoṇitākṣa,
> Kumbhakarṇa, and Makarākṣa.

---
15.
yajñaśatrōśca bhavanaṁ brahmaśatrōstathaiva ca |
narāṁtakasya kuṁbhasya nikuṁbhasya durātmanaḥ || 15 ||

> **Meaning 15:** He burned the houses of Yajñaśatru, Brahmaśatru, Narāntaka,
> Kumbha, and the wicked Nikumbha.

---
16.
varjayitvā mahātējāḥ vibhīṣaṇa gr̥haṁ prati |
kramamāṇaḥ kramēṇaiva dadāha haripuṁgavaḥ || 16 ||

> **Meaning 16:** The mighty Hariśreṣṭha moved on step by step, burning house
> after house, but he skipped only the house of Vibhīṣaṇa.

---
17.
tēṣu tēṣu mahārhēṣu bhavanēṣu mahāyaśāḥ |
gr̥hēṣvr̥ddhimatāmr̥ddhiṁ dadāha sa mahākapiḥ || 17 ||

> **Meaning 17:** In those costly mansions, the famous Mahākapi burned away the
> wealth of the rich who lived there.

---
18.
sarvēṣāṁ samatikramya rākṣasēṁdrasya vīryavān |
āsasādātha lakṣmīvān rāvaṇasya nivēśanam || 18 ||

> **Meaning 18:** Passing by all the others, the powerful and radiant Hanumān
> came at last to the palace of Rāvaṇa, the king of the Rākṣasas.

---
19.
tatastasmin gr̥hē mukhyē nānāratnavibhūṣitē |
mērumaṁdarasaṁkāśē sarvamaṁgalaśōbhitē || 19 ||

> **Meaning 19:** That chief mansion was decorated with many kinds of jewels, as
> grand as Mount Meru or Mandara, and graced with every auspicious sign.

---
20.
pradīptamagnimutsr̥jya lāṁgūlāgrē pratiṣṭhitam |
nanāda hanumānvīrō yugāṁta jaladō yathā || 20 ||

> **Meaning 20:** Hanumān let the blazing fire on the tip of his tail loose
> there and roared like a cloud at the end of an age.

---
21.
śvasanēna ca saṁyōgāt ativēgō mahābalaḥ |
kālāgniriva jajvāla prāvardhata hutāśanaḥ || 21 ||

> **Meaning 21:** Joined by the wind, the mighty fire grew very fast. It blazed
> like the fire of doomsday and kept on spreading.

---
22.
pradīpta magniṁ pavanaḥ tēṣu vēśmasvacārayat |
abhūcchvasana saṁyōgāt ativēgō hutāśanaḥ || 22 ||

> **Meaning 22:** The wind drove the blazing fire through those mansions. With
> the wind's help, the fire grew extremely swift.

---
23.
tāni kāṁcanajālāni muktāmaṇimayāni ca |
bhavanānyavaśīryaṁta ratnavaṁti mahāṁti ca || 23 ||

> **Meaning 23:** Those great houses, with their golden lattices and their
> inlays of pearls and gems, collapsed one after another.

---
24.
tāni bhagnavimānāni nipēturvasudhātalē |
bhavanānīva siddānāṁ aṁbarāt puṇyasaṁkṣayē || 24 ||

> **Meaning 24:** Their smashed palaces crashed to the ground like the mansions
> of the Siddhas falling from the sky when their merit is used up.

---
25.
saṁjajñē tumulaśśabdō rākṣasānāṁ pradhāvatām |
svagr̥hasya paritrāṇē bhagnōtsāha gataśriyām |
nūnamēṣō’gnirāyātaḥ kapirūpēṇa hā iti || 25 ||

> **Meaning 25:** A tumult rose from the Rākṣasas as they ran about, their
> spirit broken and their fortunes gone, trying to save their homes. "Surely
> this is Agni himself, come in the form of a Kapi. Alas!" they cried.

---
26.
kraṁdaṁtya ssahasā pētuḥ stanaṁdhayadharāḥ striyaḥ |
kāścidagniparītēbhyō harmēbhyō muktamūrdhajāḥ || 26 ||

> **Meaning 26:** Women with babies at the breast cried out and suddenly fell
> down. Some, with hair loose, fled from the mansions that were caught in the
> fire.

---
27.
pataṁtyō rējirē’bhrēbhyaḥ saudāminya ivāṁbarāt || 27 ||

> **Meaning 27:** As they fell from the high buildings, they shone like flashes
> of lightning falling from the clouds out of the sky.

---
28.
vajravidruma vaiḍūrya muktārajata saṁhitān |
vicitrān bhavanān dhātūn syaṁdamānān dadarśa saḥ || 28 ||

> **Meaning 28:** Hanumān saw the diamonds, coral, cat's-eye gems, pearls, and
> silver in those splendid mansions melting and flowing out.

---
29.
nāgnistr̥pyati kāṣṭhānāṁ tr̥ṇānāṁ hariyūthapaḥ |
nāgnērnāpi viśastānāṁ rākṣasānāṁ vasuṁdharā || 29 ||

> **Meaning 29:** Just as fire is never satisfied by wood or grass, leader of
> the Hari host, Hanumān was never satisfied with burning, nor is the earth ever
> satisfied with the slain Rākṣasas.

---
30.
kvacit kiṁśukasaṁkāśāḥ kvacicchālmalisannibhāḥ |
kvacit kuṁkumasaṁkāśāḥ śikhā vahnēścakāśirē || 30 ||

> **Meaning 30:** Here the flames of the fire looked like Kiṃśuka blossoms,
> there like the silk-cotton tree, and elsewhere like saffron.

---
31.
hanūmatā vēgavatā vānarēṇa mahātmanā |
laṁkāpuraṁ pradagdhaṁ tat rudrēṇa tripuraṁ yathā || 31 ||

> **Meaning 31:** The swift and great-souled Vānara Hanumān burned the city of
> Laṅkā, just as Rudra once burned Tripura.

---
32.
tatastu laṁkāpuraparvatāgrē
samutthitō bhīmaparākramō’gniḥ |
prasārya cūḍāvalayaṁ pradīptō
hanūmatā vēgavatā visr̥ṣṭaḥ || 32 ||

> **Meaning 32:** Then, on the mountain peaks of Laṅkā, the dreadful and
> powerful fire rose up. Released by the swift Hanumān, it spread out its mass
> of flame and blazed fiercely.

---
33.
yugāṁta kālānalatulyavēgaḥ
samārutōgnirvavr̥dhē divispr̥k |
vidhūmaraśmirbhavanēṣu saktō
rakṣaśśarīrājyasamarpatārciḥ || 33 ||

> **Meaning 33:** Its speed matched the fire of doomsday. Fanned by the wind, it
> grew until it touched the sky, its rays free of smoke. It clung to the houses,
> and its flames were fed by the bodies of Rākṣasas like ghee.

---
34.
ādityakōṭī sadr̥śa ssutējāḥ
laṁkāṁ samastāṁ parivārya tiṣṭhan |
śabdai ranēkairaśaniprarūḍhaiḥ
bhiṁdannivāṁḍaṁ prababhau mahāgniḥ || 34 ||

> **Meaning 34:** Shining like ten million suns, the great fire stood encircling
> the whole of Laṅkā. With many roaring sounds like thunderbolts, it seemed to
> be splitting the cosmic egg.

---
35.
tatrāṁbarādagniratipravr̥ddhō
rūkṣaprabhaḥ kiṁśukapuṣpacūḍaḥ |
nirvāṇadhūmākularājayaśca
nīlōtpalābhāḥ pracakāśirē’bhrāḥ || 35 ||

> **Meaning 35:** Then the fire grew tremendously, and its light looked harsh,
> its crest like Kiṃśuka flowers. Dark smoke streaked across the sky, and the
> clouds took on the look of blue lotuses.

---
36.
vajrī mahēṁdrastridaśēśvarō vā
sākṣādyamō vā varuṇō’nilō vā |
rudrō’gnirarkō dhanadaśca sōmō
na vānarō’yaṁ svayamēva kālaḥ || 36 ||

> **Meaning 36:** The Rākṣasas wondered, "Is he Indra the wielder of the
> thunderbolt, the king of the devatās? Is he Yama himself, or Varuṇa, Anila,
> Rudra, Agni, Sūrya, Kubera, or Soma? No, this is no Vānara. This is Kāla
> himself."

---
37.
kiṁ brahmaṇa ssarvapitāmahasya
sarvasya dhātuścaturānanasya |
ihāgatō vānararūpadhārī
rakṣōpasaṁhārakaraḥ prakōpaḥ || 37 ||

> **Meaning 37:** "Or has the angry power of four-faced Brahmā, the grandfather
> of all, creator of everything, come here in the form of a Vānara to destroy
> the Rākṣasas?"

---
38.
kiṁ vaiṣṇavaṁ vā kapirūpamētya
rakṣōvināśāya paraṁ sutējaḥ |
anaṁtamavyaktamaciṁtyamēkaṁ
svamāyayā sāṁpratamāgataṁ vā || 38 ||

> **Meaning 38:** "Or has the supreme radiance of Viṣṇu, who is endless,
> unmanifest, beyond thought, and one without a second, come in the form of a
> Kapi through his own māyā to destroy the Rākṣasas?"

---
39.
ityēva mūcurbahavō viśiṣṭāḥ
rakṣōgaṇāstatra samētya sarvē |
saprāṇisaṁghāṁ sagr̥hāṁ savr̥kṣāṁ
dagdhāṁ purīṁ tāṁ sahasā samīkṣya || 39 ||

> **Meaning 39:** Many distinguished Rākṣasas gathered there and spoke in this
> way, seeing the whole city suddenly burned, with its creatures, houses, and
> trees.

---
40.
tatastu laṁkā sahasā pradagdhā
sarākṣasā sāśvarathā sanāgā |
sapakṣisaṁghā samr̥gā savr̥kṣā
rurōda dīnā tumulaṁ saśabdam || 40 ||

> **Meaning 40:** Then Laṅkā, suddenly set ablaze with its Rākṣasas, horses,
> chariots, elephants, birds, deer, and trees, wept in misery with a great din
> of cries.

---
41.
hā tāta hā putraka kāṁta mitra
hā jīvitaṁ bhōgayutaṁ supuṇyam |
rakṣōbhirēvaṁ bahudhā bruvadbhiḥ
śabdaḥ kr̥tō ghōratarassubhīmaḥ || 41 ||

> **Meaning 41:** "Alas, father! Alas, my son! Alas, beloved! Alas, friend!
> Alas, my life of pleasures and great merit!" The Rākṣasas cried out in many
> ways, and their wailing grew dreadful and terrifying.

---
42.
hutāśanajvālasamāvr̥tā sā
hatapravīrā parivr̥ttayōdhā |
hanūmataḥ krōdhabalābhibhūtā
babhūva śāpōpahatēva laṁkā || 42 ||

> **Meaning 42:** Surrounded by flames of fire, with her great heroes slain and
> her warriors scattered, Laṅkā, crushed by the power of Hanumān's anger, looked
> as though she had been struck by a curse.

---
43.
sa saṁbhrama trasta viṣaṇṇarākṣasāṁ
samujjvalajjvāla hutāśanāṁkitām |
dadarśa laṁkāṁ hanumānmahāmanāḥ
svayaṁbhū kōpōpahatāmivāvanim || 43 ||

> **Meaning 43:** The wise Hanumān looked at Laṅkā, where the terrified,
> despairing Rākṣasas ran in confusion and blazing flames marked the city, like
> the earth scorched by the anger of Svayambhū.

---
44.
bhaṁktvā vanaṁ pādaparatnasaṁkulaṁ
hatvā tu rakṣāṁsi mahāṁti saṁyugē |
dagdhvā purīṁ tāṁ gr̥haratnamālinīṁ
tasthau hanūmān pavanātmajaḥ kapiḥ || 44 ||

> **Meaning 44:** After breaking the grove crowded with fine trees, killing many
> great Rākṣasas in battle, and burning the city adorned with garlands of
> jeweled houses, Hanumān, the Kapi son of the Wind, stood there.

---
45.
trikūṭaśr̥ṁgāgratalē vicitrē
pratiṣṭhitō vānararājasiṁhaḥ |
pradīptalāṁgūla kr̥tārcimālī
vyarājatāditya ivāṁśumālī || 45 ||

> **Meaning 45:** Standing on the wondrous summit of Mount Trikūṭa, the lion
> among the Vānara kings shone like the sun with its garland of rays, wearing a
> garland of flames from his blazing tail.

---
46.
sa rākṣasāṁstān subahūṁśca hatvā
vanaṁ ca bhaṁktvā bahupādapaṁ tat |
visr̥jya rakṣōbhavanēṣu cāgniṁ
jagāma rāmaṁ manasā mahātmā || 46 ||

> **Meaning 46:** After killing those many Rākṣasas, breaking the grove with its
> many trees, and setting fire to the Rākṣasas' mansions, the great-souled
> Hanumān turned his thoughts to Rāma.

---
47.
tatastu taṁ vānaravīramukhyaṁ
mahābalaṁ mārutatulyavēgam |
mahāmatiṁ vāyusutaṁ variṣṭhaṁ
pratuṣṭuvurdēvagaṇāśca sarvē || 47 ||

> **Meaning 47:** Then all the groups of devatās praised that foremost Vānara
> hero, the mighty son of Vāyu, swift as the wind, wise, and most excellent.

---
48.
bhaṁktvā vanaṁ mahātējāḥ hatvā rakṣāṁsi saṁyugē |
dagdhvā laṁkāpurīṁ ramyāṁ rarāja sa mahākapiḥ || 48 ||

> **Meaning 48:** After breaking the grove, killing the Rākṣasas in battle, and
> burning the beautiful city of Laṅkā, the mighty Mahākapi shone with glory.

---
49.
tatra dēvā ssagaṁdharvāḥ siddhāśca paramarṣayaḥ |
dr̥ṣṭvā laṁkāṁ pradagdhāṁ tāṁ vismayaṁ paramaṁ gatāḥ || 49 ||

> **Meaning 49:** The devatās, together with the Gandharvas, Siddhas, and great
> ṛṣis, saw Laṅkā burned and were filled with great wonder.

---
50.
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā vānaraśrēṣṭhaṁ hanumaṁtaṁ mahākapim |
kālāgniriti saṁciṁtya sarvabhūtāni tatrasuḥ || 50 ||

> **Meaning 50:** Seeing that best of Vānaras, the great Kapi Hanumān, all
> beings there were terrified, thinking he was Kālāgni, the fire of doomsday.

---
51.
dēvāśca sarvē munipuṁgavāśca
gaṁdharva vidyādhara nāga yakṣāḥ |
bhūtāni sarvāṇi mahāṁti tatra
jagmuḥ parāṁ prītimatulyarūpām || 51 ||

> **Meaning 51:** All the devatās, the greatest munis, Gandharvas, Vidyādharas,
> Nāgas, Yakṣas, and all the great beings there felt a supreme joy beyond
> compare.

---
