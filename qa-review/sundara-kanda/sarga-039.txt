=== Sarga 39 ===

---
1.
maṇiṁ dattvā tataḥ sītā hanūmantam athābravīt |
abhijñānam abhijñātam etad rāmasya tattvataḥ ||

> **Meaning 1:** After giving him the jewel, Sītā said to Hanumān, "Rāma knows
> this token well. He will recognize it for exactly what it is.
---
2.
maṇiṁ tu dr̥ṣṭvā rāmo vai trayāṇāṁ saṁsmariṣyati |
vīro jananyā mama ca rājño daśarathasya ca ||

> **Meaning 2:** "When Rāma sees this jewel, he will remember three people
> at once — my mother, my father, and King Daśaratha.
---
3.
sa bhūyas tvaṁ samutsāhe codito harisattama |
asmin kāryasamārambhe pracintaya yaduttaram ||

> **Meaning 3:** "Best of Haris, you are now being urged on to even greater
> effort. Think about what needs to happen next in this undertaking.
---
4.
tvam asmin kāryaniryoge pramāṇaṁ harisattama |
tasya cintaya yo yatno duḥkhakṣayakaro bhavet ||

> **Meaning 4:** "Best of Haris, in carrying out this task, you are the one who
> is capable and competent. Think about what effort would bring my suffering to an end."
---
5.
tasya ciṁtayatō yatnō duḥkhakṣayakarō bhavēt |
sa tatheti pratijñāya mārutirbhīmavikramaḥ |
śirasāvandya vaidehīṁ gamanāyopacakrame ||

> **Meaning 5:** "Think about the effort that would end my suffering."
> Promising, "I will," Māruti, of fearsome valor, bowed his head to Vaidehī and
> got ready to leave.
---
6.
jñātvā saṁprasthitaṁ devī vānaraṁ mārutātmajam |
bāṣpagadgadayā vācā maithilī vākyam abravīt ||

> **Meaning 6:** Seeing that the Vānara, son of Māruta, was about to set out,
> the devī Maithilī spoke to him, her voice choked with tears:
---
7.
hanuman kuśalaṁ brūyāḥ sahitau rāmalakṣmaṇau ||
8.
sugrīvaṁ ca sahāmātyaṁ vr̥ddhān sarvāṁśca vānarān |
brūyāstvaṁ vānaraśrēṣṭha kuśalaṁ dharmasaṁhitam ||

> **Meaning 7-8:** "Hanumān, give my good wishes to Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa together.
> And, best of Vānaras, give my good wishes, as dharma requires, to Sugrīva and
> his ministers, and to all the elder Vānaras.
---
9.
yathā ca sa mahābāhur māṁ tārayati rāghavaḥ |
asmād duḥkhāmbusaṁrodhāt tvaṁ samādhātum arhasi ||

> **Meaning 9:** "Mighty-armed Rāghava can carry me across this flood of grief
> that hems me in. You must see to it that he does.
---
10.
jīvantīṁ māṁ yathā rāmaḥ saṁbhāvayati kīrtimān |
tat tvayā hanuman vācyaṁ vācā dharmam avāpnuhi ||

> **Meaning 10:** "O Hanumān, speak to renowned Rāma in such a way that he
> comes to protect me and preserve my life while I am still alive. Through your
> words, earn the merit of dharma."
---
11.
nityam utsāhayuktāś ca vācaḥ śrutvā mayeritāḥ |
vardhiṣyate dāśaratheḥ pauruṣaṁ madavāptaye ||

> **Meaning 11:** "When Dāśarathi hears the words I have spoken, always full of
> encouragement, his valor will grow, and he will be set on winning me back.
---
12.
matsaṁdeśayutā vācas tvattaḥ śrutvaiva rāghavaḥ |
parākramavidhiṁ vīro vidhivat saṁvidhāsyati ||

> **Meaning 12:** "Once Rāghava hears from you the words that carry my message,
> the hero will plan his course of action and carry it out properly."
---
13.
sītāyās tad vacaḥ śrutvā hanumān mārutātmajaḥ |
śirasy añjalim ādhāya vākyam uttaram abravīt ||

> **Meaning 13:** Hearing Sītā's words, Hanumān, son of Māruta, joined his palms
> above his head and replied:
---
14.
kṣipram eṣyati kākutstho haryr̥kṣapravarair vr̥taḥ |
yas te yudhi vijityārīñ śokaṁ vyapanayiṣyati ||

> **Meaning 14:** "Kākutstha will come soon, surrounded by the foremost of the
> Haris and R̥kṣas. He will defeat his enemies in battle and drive away your
> grief.
---
15.
na hi paśyāmi martyeṣu nāmareṣv asureṣu vā |
yas tasya vamato bāṇān sthātum utsahate'grataḥ ||

> **Meaning 15:** "Indeed, I see no one among humans, devatās, or Asuras who could stand
> in front of him when he lets loose his arrows.
---
16.
apy arkam api parjanyam api vaivasvataṁ yamam |
sa hi soḍhuṁ raṇe śaktas tavahetor viśeṣataḥ ||

> **Meaning 16:** "He can face even the Sun, Parjanya, or Yama, son of Vivasvān,
> in battle — and all the more so for your sake.
---
17.
sa hi sāgaraparyantāṁ mahīṁ śāsitum īhate |
tvan nimitto hi rāmasya jayo janakanandini ||

> **Meaning 17:** "He is worthy to rule the whole earth, bounded by the ocean.
> Janakanandinī, it is because of you that Rāma will win his victory."
---
18.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā samyak satyaṁ subhāṣitam |
jānakī bahu mene'tha vacanaṁ cedam abravīt ||

> **Meaning 18:** Hearing his words — truthful, well spoken, and fitting —
> Jānakī valued them highly and then said this:
---
19.
tatas taṁ prasthitaṁ sītā vīkṣamāṇā punaḥ punaḥ |
bhartuḥ snehānvitaṁ vākyaṁ sauhārdād anumānayat ||

> **Meaning 19:** Then, as he was about to leave, Sītā looked at him again and
> again, and out of affection she spoke to him words full of affection for her
> husband.
---
20.
yadi vā manyase vīra vasaikāham ariṁdama |
kasmiṁś cit saṁvr̥te deśe viśrāntaḥ śvo gamiṣyasi ||

> **Meaning 20:** "O hero, tamer of enemies, if you think it right, stay one more
> day. Rest in some hidden spot and leave tomorrow.
---
21.
mama ced alpabhāgyāyāḥ sāmnidhyāt tava vīryavān |
asya śokasya mahato muhūrtaṁ mokṣaṇaṁ bhavet ||

> **Meaning 21:** "I have so little good fortune — but with you near me, mighty
> one, I might find relief from this great sorrow, even if just for a moment.
---
22.
gate hi hariśārdūla punarāgamanāya tu |
prāṇānām api saṁdeho mama syān nātra saṁśayaḥ ||

> **Meaning 22:** "Once you leave, Hariśārdūla, even if only to come back again,
> my life itself will be in doubt. There is no question about that.
---
23.
tavādarśanajaḥ śoko bhūyo māṁ paritāpayet |
duḥkhād duḥkhaparāmr̥ṣṭāṁ dīpayann iva vānara ||

> **Meaning 23:** "Not seeing you, Vānara, the grief will torment me even more.
> It will burn me like fire as I suffer one sorrow after another.
---
24.
ayaṁ ca vīra saṁdehas tiṣṭhatīva mamāgrataḥ |
sumahāṁs tvatsahāyeṣu haryr̥kṣeṣu harīśvara ||

> **Meaning 24:** "And there is another great doubt, kapivara, that seems to stand
> right in front of me, Hanumān — about the Haris and R̥kṣas who are
> your allies.
---
25.
kathaṁ nu khalu duṣpāraṁ tariṣyanti mahodadhim |
tāni haryr̥kṣasainyāni tau vā naravarātmajau ||

> **Meaning 25:** "How will those armies of Haris and R̥kṣas, or those two
> princes, ever cross this vast ocean that is so hard to cross?
---
26.
trayāṇām eva bhūtānāṁ sāgarasyeha laṅghane |
śaktiḥ syād vainateyasya tava vā mārutasya vā ||

> **Meaning 26:** "Only three beings have the power to leap across this ocean:
> Vainateya, you, and Māruta, the Wind.
---
27.
tad asmin kāryaniryoge vīraivaṁ duratikrame |
kiṁ paśyasi samādhānaṁ tvaṁ hi kāryavidāṁ varaḥ ||

> **Meaning 27:** "So, Hanumān, in this task that is so hard to accomplish, what
> solution do you see? You are the best of those who know how to get things
> done.
---
28.
kāmam asya tvam evaikaḥ kāryasya parisādhane |
paryāptaḥ paravīraghna yaśasyas te balodayaḥ ||

> **Meaning 28:** "Of course, destroyer of enemy heroes, you alone are enough to
> accomplish this task. The rise of your strength brings you glory.
---
29.
balaiḥ samagrair yadi māṁ rāvaṇaṁ jitya saṁyuge |
vijayī svapuraṁ yāyāt tat tu me syād yaśaskaram ||

> **Meaning 29:** "But if Rāma defeats Rāvaṇa in battle with all his forces,
> wins me back, and returns victorious to his own city, that is what will bring
> me honor.
---
30.
balais tu saṁkulāṁ kr̥tvā laṅkāṁ parabalārdanaḥ |
māṁ nayed yadi kākutsthas tat tasya sadr̥śaṁ bhavet ||

> **Meaning 30:** "If Kākutstha, crusher of enemy armies, covers Laṅkā with his
> arrows and then takes me away, that would be worthy of him.
---
31.
tad yathā tasya vikrāntam anurūpaṁ mahātmanaḥ |
bhaved āhava śūrasya tathā tvam upapādaya ||

> **Meaning 31:** "So make sure things happen in a way that fits the valor of
> that great-souled hero of battle."
---
32.
tad arthopahitaṁ vākyaṁ sahitaṁ hetusaṁhitam |
niśamya hanumāñ śeṣaṁ vākyam uttaram abravīt ||

> **Meaning 32:** Hearing her words — full of meaning, affectionate, and well
> reasoned — Hanumān gave his final reply:
---
33.
devi haryr̥kṣasainyānām īśvaraḥ plavatāṁ varaḥ |
sugrīvaḥ sattvasaṁpannas tavārthe kr̥taniścayaḥ ||

> **Meaning 33:** "Devī, Sugrīva, master of the armies of Haris and R̥kṣas and
> the best of leapers, is full of courage and has made up his mind to act for
> your sake.
---
34.
sa vānarasahasrāṇāṁ koṭībhir abhisaṁvr̥taḥ |
kṣipram eṣyati vaidehi rākṣasānāṁ nibarhaṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 34:** "Vaidehī, surrounded by countless crores of Vānaras, he will
> come soon to destroy the Rākṣasas.
---
35.
tasya vikramasaṁpannāḥ sattvavanto mahābalāḥ |
manaḥsaṁkalpasaṁpātā nideśe harayaḥ sthitāḥ ||

> **Meaning 35:** "Under his command are Haris full of valor, courage, and great
> strength, who can go wherever their minds wish, as fast as thought.
---
36.
yeṣāṁ nopari nādhastān na tiryak sajjate gatiḥ |
na ca karmasu sīdanti mahatsv amitatejasaḥ ||

> **Meaning 36:** "Their movement is never blocked — not upward, not downward,
> not sideways. Their power is boundless, and they never falter, even in the
> greatest tasks.
---
37.
asakr̥t tair mahotsahaiḥ sasāgaradharādharā |
pradakṣiṇīkr̥tā bhūmir vāyumārgānusāribhiḥ ||

> **Meaning 37:** "Following the path of the wind, those tireless ones have
> circled the earth, with its oceans and mountains, many times.
---
38.
madviśiṣṭāś ca tulyāś ca santi tatra vanaukasaḥ |
mattaḥ pratyavaraḥ kaś cin nāsti sugrīvasaṁnidhau ||

> **Meaning 38:** "Among those forest-dwellers there are some greater than me
> and some equal to me. There is no one in Sugrīva's company who is less than
> me.
---
39.
ahaṁ tāvad iha prāptaḥ kiṁ punas te mahābalāḥ |
na hi prakr̥ṣṭāḥ preṣyante preṣyante hītare janāḥ ||

> **Meaning 39:** "If even I have made it here, what about those mighty ones?
> The most eminent are never sent on errands — only others are sent.
---
40.
tad alaṁ paritāpena devi śoko vyapaitu te |
ekotpātena te laṅkām eṣyanti hariyūthapāḥ ||

> **Meaning 40:** "So stop grieving, devī. Let your sorrow go. The Hariyūthapas
> will reach Laṅkā in a single leap.
---
41.
mama pr̥ṣṭhagatau tau ca candrasūryāv ivoditau |
tvatsakāśaṁ mahāsattvau nr̥siṁhāv āgamiṣyataḥ ||

> **Meaning 41:** "Those two great-souled lions among men will ride on my back
> and come to you, like the sun and moon risen together.
---
42.
tau hi vīrau naravarau sahitau rāmalakṣmaṇau |
āgamya nagarīṁ laṅkāṁ sāyakair vidhamiṣyataḥ ||

> **Meaning 42:** "Those two heroes, the best of men, Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa
> together, will reach the city of Laṅkā and shatter it with their arrows.
---
43.
sagaṇaṁ rāvaṇaṁ hatvā rāghavo raghunandanaḥ |
tvām ādāya varārohe svapuraṁ pratiyāsyati ||

> **Meaning 43:** "Rāghava, the joy of the Raghus, will kill Rāvaṇa along with
> his followers, take you with him, graceful one, and return to his own city.
---
44.
tad āśvasihi bhadraṁ te bhava tvaṁ kālakāṅkṣiṇī |
nacirād drakṣyase rāmaṁ prajvajantam ivānilam ||

> **Meaning 44:** "So take heart. May all go well with you. Wait for the right
> time. Very soon you will see Rāma, blazing like fire.
---
45.
nihate rākṣasendre ca saputrāmātyabāndhave |
tvaṁ sameṣyasi rāmeṇa śaśāṅkeneva rohiṇī ||

> **Meaning 45:** "When the king of the Rākṣasas has been killed, along with his
> sons, ministers, and kin, you will be reunited with Rāma, like Rohiṇī with the
> moon.
---
46.
kṣipraṁ tvaṁ devi śokasya pāraṁ yāsyasi maithili |
rāvaṇaṁ caiva rāmeṇa nihataṁ drakṣyase'cirāt ||

> **Meaning 46:** "Devī Maithilī, you will soon reach the far shore of your
> grief, and before long you will see Rāvaṇa killed by Rāma."
---
47.
evam āśvasya vaidehīṁ hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ |
gamanāya matiṁ kr̥tvā vaidehīṁ punar abravīt ||

> **Meaning 47:** Having comforted Vaidehī this way, Hanumān, son of Māruta,
> made up his mind to leave and spoke to her once more:
---
48.
tam arighnaṁ kr̥tātmānaṁ kṣipraṁ drakṣyasi rāghavam |
lakṣmaṇaṁ ca dhanuṣpāṇiṁ laṅkādvāram upasthitam ||

> **Meaning 48:** "You will soon see Rāghava, slayer of enemies and master of
> himself, and Lakṣmaṇa, bow in hand, standing at the gate of Laṅkā.
---
49.
nakhadaṁṣṭrāyudhān vīrān siṁhaśārdūlavikramān |
vānarān vāraṇendrābhān kṣipraṁ drakṣyasi saṁgatān ||

> **Meaning 49:** "You will soon see the Vānara heroes gathered together — their
> nails and fangs are their weapons, their valor is that of lions and tigers,
> and they look like mighty elephants.
---
50.
śailāmbudanikāśānāṁ laṅkāmalayasānuṣu |
nardatāṁ kapimukhyānām ārye yūthāny anekaśaḥ ||

> **Meaning 50:** "Noble lady, you will see many troops of the leading Kapis,
> huge as mountains and clouds, roaring on the slopes of Laṅkā's Malaya
> mountain.
---
51.
sa tu marmaṇi ghoreṇa tāḍito manmatheṣuṇā |
na śarma labhate rāmaḥ siṁhārdita iva dvipaḥ ||

> **Meaning 51:** "But Rāma, struck in a vital spot by Manmatha's cruel arrow,
> finds no peace, like an elephant attacked by a lion.
---
52.
mā rudo devi śokena mā bhūt te manaso'priyam |
śacīva pathyā śakreṇa bhartrā nāthavatī hy asi ||

> **Meaning 52:** "Devī, don't weep in sorrow. Don't let anything trouble your
> mind. You have a husband to protect you, just as Śacī has Śakra.
---
53.
rāmād viśiṣṭaḥ ko'nyo'sti kaś cit saumitriṇā samaḥ |
agnimārutakalpau tau bhrātarau tava saṁśrayau ||

> **Meaning 53:** "Who is greater than Rāma? Who is equal to Saumitri? Those two
> brothers, like fire and wind, are your refuge.
---
54.
nāsmiṁś ciraṁ vatsyasi devi deśe; rakṣogaṇair adhyuṣito'tiraudre |
na te cirād āgamanaṁ priyasya; kṣamasva matsaṁgamakālamātram ||

> **Meaning 54:** "Devī, you will not stay long in this fearsome land where
> hordes of Rākṣasas live. Your beloved will come before long. Just bear it
> until I reach him."
---
