=== Sarga 60 ===

---
1.
tadvacanaṁ śrutvā vālisūnurabhāṣata |
ayuktaṁ tu vinā devīṁ dṛṣṭavadbhiśca vānarāḥ |
samīpaṁ gantumasmābhī rāghavasya mahātmanaḥ||

2.
vāyusūnorbalenaiva dagdhā laṅketi naḥ śrutam |
dṛṣṭā devī na cānītā iti tatra niveditum |
na yuktamiva paśyāmi bhavadbhiḥ khyātapauruṣaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 1-2:** When he heard those words, Vāli's son Aṅgada said: "Vānaras,
> we have seen the queen, but it isn't right for us to go to the noble Rāghava
> without her. We have heard that Laṅkā was burned by the strength of Vāyu's son
> alone. All of you are famous for your courage. I don't think it's right to go
> there and report, 'We saw the queen, but we didn't bring her back.'

---
3.
na hi vaḥ plavane kaścinnāpi kaścit parākrame |
tulyaḥ sāmaradaityeṣu lokeṣu harisattamāḥ ||

> **Meaning 3:** "You finest of the Haris, in all the worlds, including the
> devatās and the Daityas, no one equals you in leaping, and no one equals you
> in valor.

---
4.
jitvā laṅkāṁ sarakṣaughāṁ hatvā taṁ rāvaṇaṁ raṇe |
sītāmādāya gacchāma siddhārthā hṛṣṭamānasāḥ ||

> **Meaning 4:** "Let's conquer Laṅkā with its hordes of Rākṣasas, kill that
> Rāvaṇa in battle, and take Sītā with us. Then we can go back with our mission
> accomplished and our hearts full of joy.

---
5.
teṣvevaṁ hatavīreṣu rākṣaseṣu hanūmatā |
kimanyadatra kartavyaṁ gṛhītvā yāma jānakīm ||

> **Meaning 5:** "Hanumān has already killed the Rākṣasa heroes. What else is
> there to do here? Let's take Jānakī and go.
---
6.
rāmalakṣmaṇayormadhye nyasyāma janakātmajām |
kiṁ vyalīkaistu tān sarvān vānarān vānararṣabhān ||

> **Meaning 6:** "We'll place Janaka's daughter between Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa. Why
> trouble all those other Vānaras, those bulls among the Vānaras, back home?

---
7.
vayameva hi gatvā tān hatvā rākṣasapuṅgavān |
rāghavaṁ draṣṭumarhāmaḥ sugrīvaṁ sahalakṣmaṇam ||

> **Meaning 7:** "We ourselves should go, kill those leading Rākṣasas, and then
> go to see Rāghava, Sugrīva, and Lakṣmaṇa."

---
8.
tamevaṁ kṛtasaṅkalpaṁ jāmbavān harisattamaḥ |
uvāca paramaprīto vākyamarthavadarthavit ||

> **Meaning 8:** Aṅgada had made up his mind. Jāmbavān, the finest of the Haris,
> who understood what really mattered, was very pleased with him, but he replied
> with words full of sense.

---
9.
naiṣā buddhirmahābuddhe yad bravīṣi mahākape |
vicetuṁ vayamājñaptā dakṣiṇāṁ diśamuttamām ||

> **Meaning 9:** "You are very wise, great Kapi, but what you're suggesting
> isn't the right course. We were ordered to search the great southern
> direction.
---
10.
nānetuṁ kapirājena naiva rāmeṇa dhīmatā |
kathañcinnirjitāṁ sītāmasmābhirnābhirocayet ||

> **Meaning 10:** "We were not ordered to bring her back, either by the king of
> the Kapis or by the wise Rāma. Rāma would not be pleased with a Sītā somehow
> won back by us.
---
11.
rāghavo rājaśārdūlaḥ kulaṁ vyapadiśan svakam |
pratijñāya svayaṁ rājā sītāvijayamagrataḥ ||

> **Meaning 11:** "Rāghava, a tiger among kings, has himself publicly vowed, in
> the name of his own lineage, that he will win Sītā back,
---
12.
sarveṣāṁ kapimukhyānāṁ kathaṁ mithyā kariṣyati |
viphalaṁ karma ca kṛtaṁ bhavettuṣṭirna tasya ca |
vṛthā ca darśitaṁ vīryaṁ bhaved vānarapuṅgavāḥ ||

> **Meaning 12:** "and he made that vow in front of all the chief Kapis. How
> could he let it turn out false? Our work would be wasted, and he would not be
> satisfied. The valor we displayed would also be for nothing, you bulls among
> the Vānaras.
---
13.
tasmād gacchāma vai sarve yatra rāmaḥ salakṣmaṇaḥ |
sugrīvaśca mahātejāḥ kāryasyāsya nivedane ||

> **Meaning 13:** "So let all of us go to where Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa are, and the
> brilliant Sugrīva, and report what we have done.
---
14.
na tāvadeṣā matirātmano me
yathā bhavān paśyati rājaputra |
yathā tu rāmasya matirniviṣṭā
tathā bhavān paśyatu kāryasiddhim ||

> **Meaning 14:** "Prince, this way of seeing things is not my own opinion, as
> you might think. Look at the success of this task the way Rāma has set his
> mind on it."
---
