=== Sarga 66 ===

---
1.
evam ukto hanumatā rāmo daśarathātmajaḥ |
taṁ maṇiṁ hr̥daye kr̥tvā praruroda salakṣmaṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 1:** Addressed thus by Hanumān, Rāma, the son of Daśaratha, pressed
> the jewel to his heart and wept aloud together with Lakṣmaṇa.

---
2.
taṁ tu dr̥ṣṭvā maṇiśreṣṭhaṁ rāghavaḥ śokakarśitaḥ |
netrābhyām aśrupūrṇābhyāṁ sugrīvam idam abravīt ||

> **Meaning 2:** Looking at that finest of jewels, Rāghava, wasted with sorrow,
> spoke to Sugrīva with eyes brimful of tears:

---
3.
yathaiva dhenuḥ sravati snehād vatsasya vatsalā |
tathā mamāpi hr̥dayaṁ maṇiratnasya darśanāt ||

> **Meaning 3:** "Just as an affectionate cow lets down milk out of tender love
> for her calf, so my heart melts at the sight of this jewel.

---
4.
maṇiratnam idaṁ dattaṁ vaidehyāḥ śvaśureṇa me |
vadhūkāle yathā baddham adhikaṁ mūrdhni śobhate ||

> **Meaning 4:** This jewel was given to Vaidehī by my father-in-law at the time
> of our wedding, and it looked even more exquisite fastened upon her head.

---
5.
ayaṁ hi jalasaṁbhūto maṇiḥ pravarapūjitaḥ |
yajñe paramatuṣṭena dattaḥ śakreṇa dhīmatā ||

> **Meaning 5:** Born of the sea and revered by the foremost of beings, this gem
> was given by the wise Śakra, immensely pleased during a sacrifice.

---
6.
imaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā maṇiśreṣṭhaṁ tathā tātasya darśanam |
adyāsmy avagataḥ saumya vaidehasya tathā vibhoḥ ||

> **Meaning 6:** Gentle friend, seeing this finest of jewels today, I feel as if
> I am seeing my father, and also the illustrious king of Videha.

---
7.
ayaṁ hi śobhate tasyāḥ priyāyā mūrdhni me maṇiḥ |
adyāsya darśanenāhaṁ prāptāṁ tām iva cintaye ||

> **Meaning 7:** Truly this jewel shone on my beloved's head; looking upon it
> today, I feel as if I have her back with me.

---
8.
kim āha sītā vaidehī brūhi saumya punaḥ punaḥ |
parāsum iva toyena siñcantī vākyavāriṇā ||

> **Meaning 8:** Gentle friend, tell me again and again what Sītā Vaidehī said —
> your words are like cool water reviving a dying man!

---
9.
itas tu kiṁ duḥkhataraṁ yad imaṁ vārisaṁbhavam |
maṇiṁ paśyāmi saumitre vaidehīm āgataṁ vinā ||

> **Meaning 9:** Saumitri, what greater sorrow could there be than this: seeing
> this sea-born gem arrived here, but without Vaidehī?

---
10.
ciraṁ jīvati vaidehī yadi māsaṁ dhariṣyati |
kṣaṇaṁ saumya na jīveyaṁ vinā tām asitekṣaṇām ||

> **Meaning 10:** If Vaidehī can survive for a whole month, she is living long
> indeed; for without that dark-eyed lady, gentle friend, I cannot live even for
> a moment!

---
11.
naya mām api taṁ deśaṁ yatra dr̥ṣṭā mama priyā |
na tiṣṭheyaṁ kṣaṇam api pravr̥ttim upalabhya ca ||

> **Meaning 11:** Take me too to that place where my beloved was seen! Having
> received news of her, I cannot wait even for an instant.

---
12.
kathaṁ sā mama suśroṇi bhīru bhīruḥ satī tadā |
bhayāvahānāṁ ghorāṇāṁ madhye tiṣṭhati rakṣasām ||

> **Meaning 12:** How can my graceful, gentle beloved, so timid and chaste, stay
> there in the midst of those terrifying, dreadful Rākṣasas?

---
13.
śāradas timironmukho nūnaṁ candra ivāmbudaiḥ |
āvr̥taṁ vadanaṁ tasyā na virājati rākṣasaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 13:** Surely her face, surrounded by Rākṣasas, is robbed of its
> brilliance, like the autumn moon obscured by dark clouds when freed from the
> dark fortnight.

---
14.
kim āha sītā hanumaṁs tattvataḥ kathayasva me |
etena khalu jīviṣye bheṣajenāturo yathā ||

> **Meaning 14:** Hanumān, tell me truthfully what Sītā said. By your words I
> will keep living, like a sick man revived by medicine.

---
15.
madhurā madhurālāpā kim āha mama bhāminī |
madvihīnā varārohā hanuman kathayasva me |
duḥkhād duḥkhataraṁ prāpya kathaṁ jīvati jānakī ||

> **Meaning 15:** Hanumān, tell me what my sweet, soft-spoken beloved said!
> Bereft of me, how does that graceful lady go on living? Plunged from one
> sorrow into a deeper sorrow, how does Jānakī survive?"

---
