=== Sarga 6 ===

---
1.
sa nikāmaṁ vināmeṣu vicaran kāmarūpadhr̥k |
vicacāra kapirlaṅkāṁ lāghavena samanvitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 1:** Moving with swift agility, able to assume any form at will, the
> Kapi roamed at his pleasure through the lanes and mansions of Laṅkā.

---
2.
āsasādātha lakṣmīvān rākṣasendraniveśanam |
prākāreṇārkavarṇena bhāsvareṇābhisaṁvr̥tam ||

> **Meaning 2:** Then that glorious hero reached the palace of the Rākṣasa king,
> encircled by a radiant rampart glowing with the brilliance of the sun.

---
3.
rakṣitaṁ rākṣasairbhīmaiḥ siṁhairiva mahad vanam |
samīkṣamāṇo bhavanaṁ cakāśe kapikuñjaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 3:** It was guarded by terrifying Rākṣasas just as a vast forest is
> protected by ferocious lions; looking upon that palace, the Kapikuñjara shone
> with wonder.

---
4.
rūpyakopahitaiścitraiḥ toraṇairhemabhūṣitaiḥ |
vicitrābhiśca kakṣyābhiḥ dvāraiśca rucirairvr̥tam ||

> **Meaning 4:** It was enclosed by marvelous archways overlaid with silver and
> decorated with gold, fitted with exquisite courtyards and splendid gateways.

---
5.
gajāsthitairmahāmātraiḥ śūraiśca vigataśramaiḥ |
upasthitam asaṁhāryaiḥ hayaiḥ syandanayāyibhiḥ ||

> **Meaning 5:** It was attended by brave, untiring commanders mounted on noble
> elephants, and by invincible chariot horses harnessed to swift war-cars.

---
6.
siṁhavyāghratanutrāṇaiḥ dāntakāñcanarājataiḥ |
ghoṣavadbhirvicitraiśca sadā vicaritaṁ rathaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 6:** The palace grounds were constantly traversed by rumbling,
> ornate chariots armored with lion and tiger skins, embellished with ivory,
> gold, and silver.

---
7.
bahuratnasamākīrṇaṁ parārdhyāsanabhājanam |
mahārathasamāvāsaṁ mahārathamahāsanam ||

> **Meaning 7:** It was strewn with numerous jewels, furnished with priceless
> seats and vessels, and served as the vast abode and assembly ground of great
> chariot warriors.

---
8.
dr̥śyaiśca paramodāraiḥ taistaiśca mr̥gapakṣibhiḥ |
vividhairbahusāhasraiḥ paripūrṇaṁ samantataḥ ||

> **Meaning 8:** It was filled on all sides with thousands of captivating,
> magnificent animals and birds of every variety.

---
9.
vinītairantapālaiśca rakṣobhiśca surakṣitam |
mukhyābhiśca varastrībhiḥ paripūrṇaṁ samantataḥ ||

> **Meaning 9:** Securely guarded by disciplined border-wardens and stalwart
> Rākṣasas, it was filled in every quarter with choice, peerless women.

---
10.
muditapramadā ratnaṁ rākṣasendraniveśanam |
varābharaṇanirhrādaiḥ samudrasvananiḥsvanam ||

> **Meaning 10:** Resounding with the laughter of delighted, jewel-like women
> and the chiming of their finest ornaments, that palace of the Rākṣasa king
> roared like the ocean swell.

---
11.
tad rājaguṇasaṁpannaṁ mukhyaiśca varacandanaiḥ |
mahājanaiḥ samākīrṇaṁ siṁhairiva mahadvanam ||

> **Meaning 11:** Endowed with royal majesty and scented with the finest
> sandalwood, it was crowded with eminent nobles just as a mighty forest is
> filled with lions.

---
12.
bherīmr̥daṅgābhirutaṁ śaṅkhaghoṣavināditam |
nityārcitaṁ parvahutaṁ pūjitaṁ rākṣasaiḥ sadā ||

> **Meaning 12:** It echoed with the boom of kettle-drums and mr̥daṅgas and the
> sounding of conches; it was a sanctuary where daily oblations were offered,
> sacred sacrifices were performed on festive days, and worship was constantly
> maintained by the Rākṣasas.

---
13.
samudram iva gambhīraṁ samudram iva niḥsvanam |
mahātmāno mahad veśma mahāratnaparicchadam ||

> **Meaning 13:** Vast and deep as the ocean, resounding like the ocean's roar,
> that grand mansion of the mighty king was hung with tapestries of precious
> gems.

---
14.
mahāratnasamākīrṇaṁ dadarśa sa mahākapiḥ |
virājamānaṁ vapuṣā gajāśvarathasaṁkulam ||

> **Meaning 14:** Strewn with great jewels, gleaming with architectural
> grandeur, and crowded with elephants, horses, and chariots, that residence was
> observed by the great Kapi.

---
15.
laṅkābharaṇamityeva so’manyata mahākapiḥ |
cacāra hanumāṁstatra rāvaṇasya samīpataḥ ||

> **Meaning 15:** Deeming it the very crown jewel of Laṅkā, the great Kapi
> Hanumān moved cautiously through the precincts close to Rāvaṇa's inner palace.

---
16.
gr̥hād gr̥haṁ rākṣasānām udyānāni ca vānaraḥ |
vīkṣamāṇo hyasaṁtrastaḥ prāsādāṁśca cacāra saḥ ||

> **Meaning 16:** The fearless Vānara wandered from house to house, inspecting
> the gardens and mansions of the Rākṣasas.

---
17.
avaplutya mahāvegaḥ prahastasya niveśanam |
tato’nyat pupluve veśma mahāpārśvasya vīryavān ||

> **Meaning 17:** Leaping down with tremendous speed, the valiant hero entered
> the mansion of Prahasta, and from there bounded to the house of Mahāpārśva.

---
18.
atha meghapratīkāśaṁ kumbhakarṇaniveśanam |
vibhīṣaṇasya ca tathā pupluve sa mahākapiḥ ||

> **Meaning 18:** Then the great Kapi leapt to the cloud-like mansion of
> Kumbhakarṇa, and then to the abode of Vibhīṣaṇa.

---
19.
mahodarasya ca tathā virūpākṣasya caiva hi |
vidyujjihvasya bhavanaṁ vidyunmālestathaiva ca |

> **Meaning 19:** He visited the houses of Mahodara, Virūpākṣa, Vidyujjihva, and
> Vidyunmālin.

---
20.
vajradaṁṣṭrasya ca tathā pupluve sa mahākapiḥ |
śukasya ca mahāvegaḥ sāraṇasya ca dhīmataḥ ||

> **Meaning 20:** The swift great Kapi bounded to the house of Vajradaṁṣṭra, and
> to those of Śuka and the wise Sāraṇa.

---
21.
tathā cendrajito veśma jagāma hariyūthapaḥ |
jambumāleḥ sumāleśca jagāma hariyūthapaḥ ||

> **Meaning 21:** Then the Hariyūthapa went to the palace of Indrajit, and to
> the residences of Jambumālī and Sumālī.

---
22.
raśmiketośca bhavanaṁ sūryaśatrostathaiva ca |
vajrakāyasya ca tathā pupluvē sa mahākapiḥ ||

> **Meaning 22:** The great Kapi bounded next to the mansions of Raśmiketu,
> Sūryaśatru, and Vajrakāya.

---
23.
dhūmrākṣasya ca saṁpātēḥ bhavanaṁ mārutātmajaḥ |
vidyudrūpasya bhīmasya ghanasya vighanasya ca ||

> **Meaning 23:** The son of Māruta searched the homes of Dhūmrākṣa, Saṁpāti,
> Vidyudrūpa, Bhīma, Ghana, and Vighana.

---
24.
śukanābhasya vakrasya śaṭhasya vikaṭasya ca |
hrasvakarṇasya daṁṣṭrasya romaśasya ca rakṣasaḥ ||

> **Meaning 24:** He inspected the dwellings of Śukanābha, Vakra, Śaṭha, Vikaṭa,
> Hrasvakarṇa, Daṁṣṭra, and the Rākṣasa Romaśa.

---
25.
yuddhonmattasya mattasya dhvajagrīvasya nādinaḥ |
vidyujjihvendrajihvānāṁ tathā hastimukhasya ca ||

> **Meaning 25:** He searched the homes of Yuddhonmatta, Matta, Dhvajagrīva,
> Nādī, Vidyujjihva, Indrajihva, and Hastimukha.

---
26.
karālasya piśācasya śoṇitākṣasya caiva hi |
kramamāṇaḥ krameṇaiva hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ ||

> **Meaning 26:** Proceeding methodically one by one, Hanumān, the son of
> Māruta, examined the residences of Karāla, Piśāca, and Śoṇitākṣa.

---
27.
teṣu teṣu mahārheṣu bhavaneṣu mahāyaśāḥ |
teṣām r̥ddhimatām r̥ddhiṁ dadarśa sa mahākapiḥ ||

> **Meaning 27:** In those luxurious palaces, the renowned great Kapi beheld the
> immense wealth and splendor of those opulent Rākṣasa chiefs.

---
28.
sarveṣāṁ samatikramya bhavanāni samantataḥ |
āsasādātha lakṣmīvān rākṣasendraniveśanam ||

> **Meaning 28:** Having passed by the mansions of all those chiefs on every
> side, the glorious hero finally arrived at the supreme residence of the
> Rākṣasa king.

---
29.
rāvaṇasyopaśāyinyaḥ dadarśa harisattamaḥ |
vicaran hariśārdūlaḥ rākṣasīrvikr̥tekṣaṇāḥ ||

> **Meaning 29:** Moving about, that foremost of Haris, the tiger among Haris,
> saw deformed-eyed Rākṣasīs stationed around Rāvaṇa's sleeping chambers.

---
30.
śūlamudgarahastāśca śakto tomaradhāriṇīḥ |
dadarśa vividhān gulmāṁḥ tasya rakṣaḥpatergr̥he ||

> **Meaning 30:** In the palace of that sovereign of Rākṣasas, he saw diverse
> platoons of sentries armed with spears, iron clubs, javelins, and lances.

---
31.
rākṣasāṁśca mahākāyān nānāpraharaṇōdyatān |
raktāñ śvetān sitāṁścaiva harīṁścaiva mahājavān ||

> **Meaning 31:** He saw colossal Rākṣasas ready with various weapons, and
> exceptionally swift steeds of red, piebald, and pure white hues.

---
32.
kulīnān rūpasaṁpannān gajān paragajārujān |
niṣṭhitān gajaśikṣāyām airāvatasamān yudhi ||

> **Meaning 32:** He saw noble, handsome war-elephants, trained to crush enemy
> tusker formations, masterfully schooled in battle tactics and rivaling
> Airāvata in combat.

---
33.
nihantr̥̄n parasainyānāṁ gr̥he tasmin dadarśa saḥ |
kṣarataśca yathā meghān sravataśca yathā girīn ||

> **Meaning 33:** In that palace he saw those destroyers of hostile armies
> dripping temporal rut like raining clouds and flowing mountain springs.

---
34.
meghastanitanirghoṣān durdharṣān samare paraiḥ |
sahasraṁ vāhinīstatra jāmbūnadapariṣkr̥tāḥ ||

> **Meaning 34:** Roaring like rumbling thunderclouds and unassailable in
> battle, thousands of troops and war contingents were stationed there, adorned
> with refined gold.

---
35.
hemajālairavicchinnāḥ taruṇādityasaṁnibhāḥ |
dadarśa rākṣasendrasya rāvaṇasya niveśane ||

> **Meaning 35:** Draped in unbroken meshes of gold and glowing like the morning
> sun, those battalions and transports stood within the residence of King
> Rāvaṇa.

---
36.
śibikā vividhākārāḥ sa kapirmārutātmajaḥ |
latāgr̥hāṇi citrāṇi citraśālāgr̥hāṇi ca ||

> **Meaning 36:** The Kapi, son of Māruta, saw palanquins of varied designs,
> enchanting creeper-bowers, and exquisite picture galleries.

---
37.
krīḍāgr̥hāṇi cānyāni dāruparvatakān api |
kāmasya gr̥hakaṁ ramyaṁ divāgr̥hakam eva ca |
dadarśa rākṣasendrasya rāvaṇasya niveśane ||

> **Meaning 37:** Within the residence of King Rāvaṇa, he saw pleasure
> pavilions, artificial wooden hills, charming sporting chambers, and daytime
> retreats.

---
38.
sa mandaratalaprakhyaṁ mayūrasthānasaṁkulam |

> **Meaning 38:** That foremost mansion, vast as a plateau of Mount Mandara, was
> crowded with decorative peacock roosts.

---
39.
dhvajayaṣṭibhirākīrṇaṁ dadarśa bhavanottamam |
anantaratnanicayaṁ nidhijālaṁ samantataḥ ||

> **Meaning 39:** Bristling with flagstaffs and filled with endless heaps of
> jewels, it held networks of hidden treasures on every side.

---
40.
dhīraniṣṭhitakarmāntaṁ gr̥haṁ bhūtapateriva ||
arcirbhiścāpi ratnānāṁ tejasā rāvaṇasya ca |

> **Meaning 40:** Executed with flawless craftsmanship, it rivaled the palace of
> Kubera himself; through the radiance of its gems and the majesty of Rāvaṇa,

---
41.
virarājātha tad veśma raśmimān iva raśmibhiḥ |
jāmbūnadamayāny eva śayanāny āsanāni ca |
bhājanāni ca śubhrāṇi dadarśa hariyūthapaḥ ||

> **Meaning 41:** that mansion blazed like the sun with its rays; and the
> Hariyūthapa saw beds, seats, and gleaming vessels fashioned entirely of pure
> gold.

---
42.
madhvāsavakr̥takledaṁ maṇibhājanasaṁkulam |
manoramam asaṁbādhaṁ kuberabhavanaṁ yathā ||

> **Meaning 42:** Moist with sweet wines and intoxicating liquors, crowded with
> jeweled goblets, it was spacious and delightful like the celestial palace of
> Kubera.

---
43.
nūpurāṇāṁ ca ghoṣeṇa kāñcīnāṁ ninadena ca |
mr̥daṅgatalaghoṣaiśca ghoṣavadbhirvināditam ||

> **Meaning 43:** It resonated with the chiming of anklets, the tinkling of
> jeweled waistbands, and the booming rhythms of mr̥daṅga drums.

---
44.
prāsādasaṁghātayutaṁ strīratnaśatasaṁkulam |
suvyūḍhakakṣyaṁ hanumān praviveśa mahāgr̥ham ||

> **Meaning 44:** Flanked by clusters of palaces, crowded with hundreds of
> jewel-like women, and laid out with well-ordered chambers, Hanumān stepped
> into that magnificent mansion.
