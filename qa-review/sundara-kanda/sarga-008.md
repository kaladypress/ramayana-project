=== Sarga 8 ===

---
1.
sa tasya madhyē bhavanasya saṁsthitaṁ
mahadvimānaṁ maṇivajracitritam |
pratapta jāṁbūnadajāla kr̥trimaṁ
dadarśa vīraḥ pavanātmajaḥ kapiḥ ||

2.
tadapramēyāpratikāra kr̥trimaṁ
kr̥taṁ svayaṁ sādhviti viśvakarmaṇā |
divaṁ gataṁ vāyupatha pratiṣṭhitaṁ
vyarājatādityapathasya lakṣmavat ||

3.
na tatra kiṁcinna kr̥taṁ prayatnatō
na tatra kiṁcinna mahārharatnavat |
na tē viśēṣā niyatāssurēṣvapi
na tatra kiṁcinna mahāviśēṣavat ||

4.
tapaḥ samādhāna parākramārjitaṁ
manassamādhāna vicāracāriṇam |
anēkasaṁsthāna viśēṣanirmitaṁ
tatastatastulya viśēṣa darśanam ||

> **Meaning 1-4:** That heroic Kapi and son of Vāyu, Hanumān, saw the great
> Vimāna situated in the middle of that mansion. Decorated with diamonds and
> other gems, it was exquisitely crafted with latticed windows of refined
> Jāmbūnada gold. With its craftsmanship being immeasurable and unreproducible,
> it was created by Viśvakarmā himself with absolute perfection. Stationed in
> the aerial path, it ascended towards the heavens and shone brightly like the
> sun traversing its celestial route. There was nothing on it that was not
> crafted with immense effort, and nothing that was not adorned with highly
> precious gems. Such extraordinary features are not consistently found even
> among the Devas, and every single part of it was endowed with great
> distinction. It was acquired through the prowess of intense austerities. It
> moved entirely according to the thoughts and intentions of its occupant.
> Constructed with many uniquely specialized structures (such as exterior
> pavilions, balconies, turrets, and towers), its brilliant features looked
> equally well-proportioned and perfect from every angle.
---

5.
manassamādhāya tu śīghragāminaṁ
durāvaraṁ mārutatulya gāminam |
mahātmanāṁ puṇyakr̥tāṁ mahardhināṁ
yaśasvinā magryamudā mivālayam ||

6.
viśēṣamālaṁbya viśēṣasaṁsthitaṁ
vicitrakūṭaṁ bahukūṭamaṁḍitam |
manōbhirāmaṁ śaradiṁdunirmalaṁ
vicitrakūṭaṁ śikharaṁ girēryathā ||

7.
vahaṁti yaṁ kuṁḍalaśōbhitānanāḥ
mahāśanā vyōmacarā niśācarāḥ |
vivr̥tta vidhvasta viśālalōcanāḥ
mahājavā bhūtagaṇāssahasraśaḥ ||

8.
vasaṁtapuṣpōtkara cārudarśanaṁ
vasaṁtamāsādapi kāṁtadarśanam |
sa puṣpakaṁ tatra vimānamuttamaṁ
dadarśa tadvānaravīrasattamaḥ ||

> **Meaning 5-8:** Unobstructed and traveling with the speed of the wind, it
> moved swiftly by merely focusing the mind upon it. It resembled the heavenly
> abode of the illustrious and great-souled ones who have performed meritorious
> deeds, who possess great opulence, and who experience the highest joy.
> Established with extraordinary distinction, it was adorned with numerous
> wonderful towers and peaks. Pleasing to the mind and as pure and bright as the
> autumn moon, it looked like the magnificent summit of a mountain with
> picturesque peaks. It was supported by sculpted figures of thousands of
> sky-ranging night-wanderers and swift-moving Bhūtas, depicted as huge eaters
> with wide, round, and fearsome eyes, and whose faces were adorned with
> gleaming earrings. That best among the heroic Vānaras, Hanumān, beheld that
> most excellent Puṣpaka Vimāna there, which was as beautiful to look at as a
> vibrant cluster of spring flowers, and even more captivating to behold than
> the enchanting month of spring itself.
---
