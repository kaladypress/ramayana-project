=== Sarga 48 ===
Arrival at Mithilā; Gautama's Curse on Indra and Ahalyā

---
1.
pr̥ṣṭvā tu kuśalaṁ tatra parasparasamāgamē |
kathāntē sumatirvākyaṁ vyājahāra mahāmunim ||

> **Meaning 1:** After they had met and inquired after one another's well-being,
> at the close of their conversation Sumati spoke to the great muni:

---
2.
imau kumārau bhadraṁ tē dēvatulyaparākramau |
gajasiṁhagatī vīrau śārdūlavr̥ṣabhōpamau ||

> **Meaning 2:** "May all be well with you! These two heroic young princes have
> the valor of devatās, the stride of elephants and lions, and the bearing of
> tigers and bulls.

---
3.
padmapatraviśālākṣau khaḍgatūṇīdhanurdharau |
aśvināviva rūpēṇa samupasthitayauvanau ||

> **Meaning 3:** "With large eyes like lotus petals, bearing swords, quivers,
> and bows, they resemble the Aśvins in beauty and have just reached the
> threshold of youth.

---
4.
yadr̥cchayaiva gāṁ prāptau dēvalōkādivāmarau |
kathaṁ padbhyāmiha prāptau kimarthaṁ kasya vā munē ||

> **Meaning 4:** "Muni, they look like two devatās who have come down to earth
> from Devaloka of their own accord. How is it that they have come here walking
> on the bare ground? What is the reason for their coming, and whose sons are
> they?

---
5.
bhūṣayantāvimaṁ dēśaṁ candrasūryāvivāmbaram |
parasparasya sadr̥śau pramāṇēṅgitacēṣṭitaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 5:** "They adorn this land just as the moon and the sun adorn the
> sky, and they closely resemble one another in stature, gesture, and movement.

---
6.
kimarthaṁ ca naraśrēṣṭhau samprāptau durgamē pathi |
varāyudhadharau vīrau śrōtumicchāmi tattvataḥ ||

> **Meaning 6:** "Why have these two heroes, foremost of men and bearing fine
> weapons, traveled along this difficult road? I wish to hear the truth of it."

---
7.
tasya tadvacanaṁ śrutvā yathāvr̥ttaṁ nyavēdayat |
siddhāśramanivāsaṁ ca rākṣasānāṁ vadhaṁ tathā ||

> **Meaning 7:** Hearing his words, Viśvāmitra recounted everything as it had
> happened, including their stay at Siddhāśrama and the slaying of the rākṣasas.

---
8.
viśvāmitravacaḥ śrutvā rājā paramaharṣitaḥ |
atithī paramau prāptau putrau daśarathasya tau |
pūjayāmāsa vidhivatsatkārārhau mahābalau ||

> **Meaning 8:** On hearing Viśvāmitra's account, the king was overjoyed to have
> Daśaratha's two mighty sons arrive as supreme guests, and he honored them in
> the traditional manner, for they were worthy of every hospitality.

---
9.
tataḥ paramasatkāraṁ sumatēḥ prāpya rāghavau |
uṣya tatra niśāmēkāṁ jagmaturmithilāṁ tataḥ ||

> **Meaning 9:** After receiving the highest hospitality from Sumati and staying
> there for one night, the two Rāghavas set out for Mithilā.

---
10.
tāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā munayaḥ sarvē janakasya purīṁ śubhām |
sādhu sādhviti śaṁsantō mithilāṁ samapūjayan ||

> **Meaning 10:** Catching sight of Janaka's auspicious city of Mithilā, all the
> munis praised and honored it, exclaiming, "Wonderful! Wonderful!"

---
11.
mithilōpavanē tatra āśramaṁ dr̥śya rāghavaḥ |
purāṇaṁ nirjanaṁ ramyaṁ papraccha munipuṅgavam ||

> **Meaning 11:** In a grove on the outskirts of Mithilā, Rāghava saw an
> ancient, deserted, yet beautiful āśrama and asked the foremost of munis:

---
12.
śrīmadāśramasaṅkāśaṁ kiṁ nvidaṁ munivarjitam |
śrōtumicchāmi bhagavankasyāyaṁ pūrva āśramaḥ ||

> **Meaning 12:** "Revered sage, why is this place, which looks like a splendid
> āśrama, devoid of munis? I wish to hear whose āśrama this once was."

---
13.
tacchrutā rāghavēṇōktaṁ vākyaṁ vākyaviśāradaḥ |
pratyuvāca mahātējā viśvamitrō mahāmuniḥ ||

> **Meaning 13:** Hearing the words spoken by Rāghava, the deeply radiant great
> muni Viśvāmitra, eloquent in speech, replied:

---
14.
hanta tē kathayiṣyāmi śr̥ṇu tattvēna rāghava |
yasyaitadāśramapadaṁ śaptaṁ kōpānmahātmanā ||

> **Meaning 14:** "Rāghava, listen as I tell you the true story of whose
> āśrama-site this was, and how its great-souled master cursed it in anger.

---
15.
gautamasya naraśrēṣṭha pūrvamāsīnmahātmanaḥ |
āśramō divyasaṅkāśaḥ surairapi supūjitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 15:** "Foremost of men, long ago this was the āśrama of the
> great-souled Gautama, divine in appearance and honored even by the suras.

---
16.
sa cēha tapa ātiṣṭhadahalyāsahitaḥ purā |
varṣapūgānyanēkāni rājaputra mahāyaśaḥ ||

> **Meaning 16:** "Illustrious prince, here in days of old he practiced tapas
> together with his wife Ahalyā for many thousands of years.

---
17.
tasyāntaraṁ viditvā tu sahasrākṣaḥ śacīpatiḥ |
munivēṣadharō'halyāmidaṁ vacanamabravīt ||

> **Meaning 17:** "Watching for an opportunity when the sage was away, Indra,
> the husband of Śacī, assumed the guise of the muni and spoke these words to
> Ahalyā:

---
18.
r̥tukālaṁ pratīkṣantē nārthinaḥ susamāhitē |
saṅgamaṁ tvahamicchāmi tvayā saha sumadhyamē ||

> **Meaning 18:** "'Graceful one, those overcome by desire do not wait for the
> prescribed season, and I long to unite with you.'

---
19.
munivēṣaṁ sahasrākṣaṁ vijñāya raghunandana |
matiṁ cakāra durmēdhā dēvarājakutūhalāt ||

> **Meaning 19:** "Raghunandana, even though she recognized that it was Indra
> in the guise of the muni, her judgment was clouded, and out of fascination
> with the king of the devatās, she consented.

---
20.
athābravīt suraśrēṣṭhaṁ kr̥tārthēnāntarātmanā |
kr̥tārthō'si suraśrēṣṭha gaccha śīghra mitaḥ prabhō ||

21.
ātmānaṁ māṁ ca dēvēśa sarvadā rakṣa mānadaḥ ||

> **Meaning 20-21:** "Then, feeling satisfied at heart, she said to Indra:
> 'Foremost of the suras, your purpose is accomplished, so leave this place
> quickly! Ruler of the devatās, giver of honor, protect yourself and me at all
> times!'

---
22.
indrastu prahasanvākyamahalyāmidamabravīt |
suśrōṇi parituṣṭō'smi gamiṣyāmi yathāgatam ||

> **Meaning 22:** "Laughing, Indra replied to Ahalyā: 'Graceful one, I am well
> pleased; I will depart just as I came.'

---
23.
ēvaṁ saṅgamya tu tayā niścakrāmōṭajāttataḥ |
sa sambhramāttvaranrāma śaṅkitō gautamaṁ prati ||

> **Meaning 23:** "Rāma, after uniting with her in this way, he stepped out of
> the leaf-hut, hurrying in anxious haste out of fear of Gautama.

---
24.
gautamaṁ sa dadarśātha praviśantaṁ mahāmunim |
dēvadānavadurdharṣaṁ tapōbalasamanvitam ||

> **Meaning 24:** "Just then he saw the great muni Gautama entering, endowed
> with the power of tapas and unassailable even by devatās and dānavas.

---
25.
tīrthōdakapariklinnaṁ dīpyamānamivānalam |
gr̥hītasamidhaṁ tatra sakuśaṁ munipuṅgavam ||
dr̥ṣṭvā surapatistrastō viṣaṇṇavadanō'bhavat ||

> **Meaning 25:** "Soaked with water from the sacred bathing place and blazing
> like fire, the foremost of munis was carrying samidhs and kuśa grass; seeing
> him, the ruler of the suras was terrified and his face turned pale.

---
26.
atha dr̥ṣṭvā sahasrākṣaṁ munivēṣadharaṁ muniḥ |
durvr̥ttaṁ vr̥ttasampannō rōṣādvacanamabravīt ||

> **Meaning 26:** "Seeing the thousand-eyed Indra standing there in the guise of
> a muni after his wicked deed, the righteous sage spoke to him in anger:

---
27.
mama rūpaṁ samāsthāya kr̥tavānasi durmatē |
akartavyamidaṁ yasmādviphalastvaṁ bhaviṣyati ||

> **Meaning 27:** "'Fool! Because you took on my form and committed this act
> that should never be done, you will become emasculated!'

---
28.
gautamēnaivamuktasya sarōṣēṇa mahātmanā |
pētaturvr̥ṣaṇau bhūmau sahasrākṣasya tatkṣaṇāt ||

> **Meaning 28:** "The moment the great-souled Gautama spoke thus in fury, the
> testicles of the thousand-eyed Indra fell to the ground.

---
29.
tathā śaptvā sa vai śakraṁ bhāryāmapi ca śaptavān |
iha varṣasahasrāṇi bahūni tvaṁ nivatsyasi ||

> **Meaning 29:** "Having cursed Śakra in that manner, Gautama then cursed his
> wife as well: 'You will live here for many thousands of years,

---
30.
vāyubhakṣā nirāhārā tapyantī bhasmaśāyinī |
adr̥śyā sarvabhūtānāmāśramē'sminnivatsyasi ||

> **Meaning 30:** "'subsisting on wind alone, without food, doing penance and
> lying on ashes; unseen by all beings, you will remain in this āśrama.

---
31.
yadā caitadvanaṁ ghōraṁ rāmō daśarathātmajaḥ |
āgamiṣyati durdharṣastadā pūtā bhaviṣyasi ||

> **Meaning 31:** "'When Rāma, the invincible son of Daśaratha, comes to this
> desolate forest, your purity will be restored.

---
32.
tasyātithyēna durvr̥ttē lōbhamōhavivarjitā |
matsakāśē mudā yuktā svaṁ vapurdhārayiṣyasi ||

> **Meaning 32:** "'Wicked woman, by offering hospitality to Rāma you will
> become free of greed and delusion, and filled with joy, you will regain your
> own form in my presence.'

---
33.
ēvamuktvā mahātējā gautamō duṣṭacāriṇīm |
imamāśramamutsr̥jya siddhacāraṇasēvitē |
himavacchikharē ramyē tapastēpē mahātapāḥ ||

> **Meaning 33:** "Having spoken thus to his erring wife, the deeply radiant,
> great ascetic Gautama abandoned this āśrama and practiced tapas on a beautiful
> peak of Himavān frequented by Siddhas and Cāraṇas."

---
