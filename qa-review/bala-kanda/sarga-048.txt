=== Sarga 48 ===

---
1.
aphalas tu tataḥ śakro devān agnipurogamān |
abravīt trastavadanaḥ sarṣisaṁghān sacāraṇān ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
kurvatā tapaso vighnaṁ gautamasya mahātmanaḥ |
krodham utpādya hi mayā surakāryam idaṁ kr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
aphalo ’smi kr̥tas tena krodhāt sā ca nirākr̥tā |
śāpamokṣeṇa mahatā tapo ’syāpahr̥taṁ mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tan māṁ suravarāḥ sarve sarṣisaṁghāḥ sacāraṇāḥ |
surasāhyakaraṁ sarve saphalaṁ kartum arhatha ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
śatakrator vacaḥ śrutvā devāḥ sāgnipurogamāḥ |
pitr̥devān upetyāhuḥ saha sarvair marudgaṇaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
ayaṁ meṣaḥ savr̥ṣaṇaḥ śakro hy avr̥ṣaṇaḥ kr̥taḥ |
meṣasya vr̥ṣaṇau gr̥hya śakrāyāśu prayacchata ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
aphalas tu kr̥to meṣaḥ parāṁ tuṣṭiṁ pradāsyati |
bhavatāṁ harṣaṇārthāya ye ca dāsyanti mānavāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
agnes tu vacanaṁ śrutvā pitr̥devāḥ samāgatāḥ |
utpāṭya meṣavr̥ṣaṇau sahasrākṣe nyavedayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tadā prabhr̥ti kākutstha pitr̥devāḥ samāgatāḥ |
aphalān bhuñjate meṣān phalais teṣām ayojayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
indras tu meṣavr̥ṣaṇas tadā prabhr̥ti rāghava |
gautamasya prabhāvena tapasaś ca mahātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tadāgaccha mahāteja āśramaṁ puṇyakarmaṇaḥ |
tārayaināṁ mahābhāgām ahalyāṁ devarūpiṇīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
viśvāmitravacaḥ śrutvā rāghavaḥ sahalakṣmaṇaḥ |
viśvāmitraṁ puraskr̥tya āśramaṁ praviveśa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
dadarśa ca mahābhāgāṁ tapasā dyotitaprabhām |
lokair api samāgamya durnirīkṣyāṁ surāsuraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
prayatnān nirmitāṁ dhātrā divyāṁ māyāmayīm iva |
dhūmenābhiparītāṅgīṁ pūrṇacandraprabhām iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
satuṣārāvr̥tāṁ sābhrāṁ pūrṇacandraprabhām iva |
madhye ’mbhaso durādharṣāṁ dīptāṁ sūryaprabhām iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sa hi gautamavākyena durnirīkṣyā babhūva ha |
trayāṇām api lokānāṁ yāvad rāmasya darśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
rāghavau tu tatas tasyāḥ pādau jagr̥hatus tadā |
smarantī gautamavacaḥ pratijagrāha sā ca tau ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
pādyam arghyaṁ tathātithyaṁ cakāra susamāhitā |
pratijagrāha kākutstho vidhidr̥ṣṭena karmaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
puṣpavr̥ṣṭir mahaty āsīd devadundubhinisvanaiḥ |
gandharvāpsarasāṁ cāpi mahān āsīt samāgamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
sādhu sādhv iti devās tām ahalyāṁ samapūjayan |
tapobalaviśuddhāṅgīṁ gautamasya vaśānugām ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
gautamo ’pi mahātejā ahalyāsahitaḥ sukhī |
rāmaṁ saṁpūjya vidhivat tapas tepe mahātapāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
rāmo ’pi paramāṁ pūjāṁ gautamasya mahāmuneḥ |
sakāśād vidhivat prāpya jagāma mithilāṁ tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
