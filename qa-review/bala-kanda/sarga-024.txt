=== Sarga 24 ===

---
1.
atha tasyāprameyasya muner vacanam uttamam |
śrutvā puruṣaśārdūlaḥ pratyuvāca śubhāṁ giram ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
alpavīryā yadā yakṣāḥ śrūyante munipuṁgava |
kathaṁ nāgasahasrasya dhārayaty abalā balam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
viśvāmitro ’bravīd vākyaṁ śr̥ṇu yena balottarā |
varadānakr̥taṁ vīryaṁ dhārayaty abalā balam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
pūrvam āsīn mahāyakṣaḥ suketur nāma vīryavān |
anapatyaḥ śubhācāraḥ sa ca tepe mahat tapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
pitāmahas tu suprītas tasya yakṣapates tadā |
kanyāratnaṁ dadau rāma tāṭakāṁ nāma nāmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
dadau nāgasahasrasya balaṁ cāsyāḥ pitāmahaḥ |
na tv eva putraṁ yakṣāya dadau brahmā mahāyaśāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tāṁ tu jātāṁ vivardhantīṁ rūpayauvanaśālinīm |
jambhaputrāya sundāya dadau bhāryāṁ yaśasvinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
kasya cit tv atha kālalsya yakṣī putraṁ vyajāyata |
mārīcaṁ nāma durdharṣaṁ yaḥ śāpād rākṣaso ’bhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sunde tu nihate rāma agastyam r̥ṣisattamam |
tāṭakā saha putreṇa pradharṣayitum icchati ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
rākṣasatvaṁ bhajasveti mārīcaṁ vyājahāra saḥ |
agastyaḥ paramakruddhas tāṭakām api śaptavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
puruṣādī mahāyakṣī virūpā vikr̥tānanā |
idaṁ rūpam apahāya dāruṇaṁ rūpam astu te ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
saiṣā śāpakr̥tāmarṣā tāṭakā krodhamūrchitā |
deśam utsādayaty enam agastyacaritaṁ śubham ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
enāṁ rāghava durvr̥ttāṁ yakṣīṁ paramadāruṇām |
gobrāhmaṇahitārthāya jahi duṣṭaparākramām ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
na hy enāṁ śāpasaṁsr̥ṣṭāṁ kaś cid utsahate pumān |
nihantuṁ triṣu lokeṣu tvām r̥te raghunandana ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
na hi te strīvadhakr̥te ghr̥ṇā kāryā narottama |
cāturvarṇyahitārthāya kartavyaṁ rājasūnunā ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
rājyabhāraniyuktānām eṣa dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ |
adharmyāṁ jahi kākutstha dharmo hy asyā na vidyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
śrūyate hi purā śakro virocanasutāṁ nr̥pa |
pr̥thivīṁ hantum icchantīṁ mantharām abhyasūdayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
viṣṇunā ca purā rāma bhr̥gupatnī dr̥ḍhavratā |
anindraṁ lokam icchantī kāvyamātā niṣūditā ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
etaiś cānyaiś ca bahubhī rājaputramahātmabhiḥ |
adharmaniratā nāryo hatāḥ puruṣasattamaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
