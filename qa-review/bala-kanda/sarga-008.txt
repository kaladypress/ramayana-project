=== Sarga 8 ===
King Daśaratha's Resolve to Perform the Aśvamedha Sacrifice

---
1.
tasya tvēvaṁ prabhāvasya dharmajñasya mahātmanaḥ |
sutārthaṁ tapyamānasya nāsīdvaṁśakaraḥ sutaḥ ||

> **Meaning 1:** Daśaratha was a king of such majesty, a knower of dharma and a
> great soul. Yet he grieved for want of a son, because he had no son to carry
> on his line.

---
2.
cintayānasya tasyaivaṁ buddhirāsīnmahātmanaḥ |
sutārthaṁ vājimēdhēna kimarthaṁ na yajāmyaham ||

> **Meaning 2:** As he brooded this way, the great king had a thought: "Why
> don't I perform the aśvamedha sacrifice for the sake of a son?"

---
3.
sa niścitāṁ matiṁ kr̥tvā yaṣṭavyamiti buddhimān |
mantribhiḥ saha dharmātmā sarvairēva kr̥tātmabhiḥ ||

> **Meaning 3:** The wise and righteous king made up his mind that he must offer
> the sacrifice. He then consulted all his ministers, who were self-disciplined
> and accomplished.

---
4.
tatō'bravīdidaṁ rājā sumantraṁ mantrisattamam |
śīghramānaya mē sarvāngurūṁstānsapurōhitān ||

> **Meaning 4:** Then the king said to Sumantra, the best of his ministers,
> "Quickly bring all my gurus here, along with the priests."

---
5.
tataḥ sumantrastvaritaṁ gatvā tvaritavikramaḥ |
samānayatsa tānviprānsamastānvēdapāragān ||

> **Meaning 5:** Sumantra, swift in his movements, went at once. He brought back
> all those brāhmaṇas, who were masters of the Vedas.

---
6.
suyajñaṁ vāmadēvaṁ ca jābālimatha kāśyapam |
purōhitaṁ vasiṣṭhaṁ ca yē cānyē dvijasattamāḥ ||

> **Meaning 6:** They were Suyajña, Vāmadeva, Jābāli and Kāśyapa, the priest
> Vasiṣṭha, and other eminent brāhmaṇas.

---
7.
tānpūjayitvā dharmātmā rājā daśarathastadā |
dharmārthasahitaṁ yuktaṁ ślakṣṇaṁ vacanamabravīt ||

> **Meaning 7:** The righteous King Daśaratha honored them and then spoke
> gentle, fitting words that served dharma and purpose.

---
8.
mama lālapyamānasya sutārthaṁ nāsti vai sukham |
tadarthaṁ hayamēdhēna yakṣyāmīti matirmama ||

> **Meaning 8:** "I grieve deeply for want of a son, and I have no peace of mind.
> For that reason, I have resolved to perform the Aśvamedha sacrifice."

---
9.
tadahaṁ yaṣṭumicchāmi śāstradr̥ṣṭēna karmaṇā |
kathaṁ cāhaṁ vindēyaṁ tattvaṁ cintayata dvijāḥ ||

> **Meaning 9:** "So I wish to perform it by the rite laid down in the śāstras.
> Think it over, brāhmaṇas, and tell me how I can achieve it."

---
10.
tataḥ sādhviti tadvākyaṁ brāhmaṇāḥ pratyapūjayan |
vasiṣṭhapramukhāḥ sarvē pārthivasya mukhāccyutam ||

> **Meaning 10:** The brāhmaṇas, with Vasiṣṭha at their head, all praised the
> king's words as they fell from his lips, saying, "Well said!"

---
11.
ūcuśca paramaprītāḥ sarvē daśarathaṁ vacaḥ |
sambhārāḥ sambhriyantāṁ tē turagaśca vimucyatām ||

> **Meaning 11:** Greatly delighted, they all said to Daśaratha, "Let the
> materials be gathered, and let the horse be set free."

---
12.
sarvathā prāpsyasē putrānabhiprētāṁśca pārthiva |
yasya tē dhārmikī buddhiriyaṁ putrārthamāgatā ||

> **Meaning 12:** "King, you will surely obtain the sons you desire, since your
> mind has turned to dharma in this way, for the sake of sons."

---
13.
tataḥ prītō'bhavadrājā śrutvaitad dvijabhāṣitam |
amātyānbhāṣatē rājā prahr̥ṣṭēnāntarātmanā ||

> **Meaning 13:** The king was pleased to hear what the brāhmaṇas said. With a
> joyful heart he spoke to his ministers.

---
14.
sambhārāḥ sambhriyantāṁ mē gurūṇāṁ vacanādiha |
samarthādhiṣṭhitaścaiva turagō'pi vimucyatām ||

> **Meaning 14:** "On the gurus' word, let the materials be gathered here. Let
> the horse also be set free, guarded by a capable protector."

---
15.
sarayvāścōttarē tīrē yajñabhūmirvidhīyatām |
śāntayaścābhivardhantāṁ yathākalpaṁ yathāvidhi ||

> **Meaning 15:** "Prepare the sacrificial ground on the north bank of the
> Sarayū. Let the rites of peace be carried out fully, in the proper manner and
> by the prescribed rules."

---
16.
śakyaḥ prāptumayaṁ yajñaḥ sarvēṇāpi mahīkṣitā |
nāparādhō bhavētkaṣṭō yadyasminkratusattamē ||

> **Meaning 16:** "Any king would perform this sacrifice, if only there were no
> risk of making a disastrous mistake in this supreme rite."

---
17.
chidraṁ hi mr̥gayantyasya vidvāṁsō brahmarākṣasāḥ |
nihatasya ca yajñasya sadyaḥ kartā vinaśyati ||

> **Meaning 17:** "Learned brahmarākṣasas look for any flaw in it. And when a
> sacrifice is spoiled, the one who performs it is destroyed at once."

---
18.
tadyathā vidhipūrvaṁ mē kraturēṣa samāpyatē |
tathā vidhānaṁ kriyatāṁ samarthāḥ sādhanēṣviti ||

> **Meaning 18:** "So make arrangements so that this rite is completed according
> to the rules. You are capable of arranging the means."

---
19.
tathēti cābruvansarvē mantriṇaḥ pratipūjitāḥ |
pārthivēndrasya tadvākyaṁ yathāpūrvaṁ niśamya tē ||

> **Meaning 19:** All the ministers, who had been honored, said, "So it will
> be." They listened to the king's words, just as before.

---
20.
tathā dvijāstē dharmajñā vardhayantō nr̥pōttamam |
anujñātāstataḥ sarvē punarjagmuryathāgatam ||

> **Meaning 20:** The righteous brāhmaṇas offered their blessings to the king
> and, taking his leave, departed for their homes.

---
21.
visarjayitvā tānviprān sacivānidamabravīt |
r̥tvigbhirupasandiṣṭō yathāvat kraturāpyatām ||

> **Meaning 21:** After sending the brāhmaṇas away, the king said to his
> ministers, "Let the sacrifice be carried out properly, as the priests direct."

---
22.
ityuktvā nr̥paśārdūlaḥ sacivānsamupasthitān |
visarjayitvā svaṁ vēśma pravivēśa mahāmatiḥ ||

> **Meaning 22:** After saying this to the ministers standing by, the wise tiger
> among kings dismissed them and went into his own quarters.

---
23.
tataḥ sa gatvā tāḥ patnīrnarēndrō hr̥dayaṅgamāḥ |
uvāca dīkṣāṁ viśata yakṣyē'haṁ sutakāraṇāt ||

> **Meaning 23:** Then the king went to his wives, who were dear to his heart.
> He said, "Prepare for the initiation. I am going to perform a sacrifice for
> the sake of sons."

---
24.
tāsāṁ tēnātikāntēna vacanēna suvarcasām |
mukhapadmānyaśōbhanta padmānīva himātyayē ||

> **Meaning 24:** At those words, spoken so tenderly, the radiant faces of the
> queens shone, like lotuses when the cold season ends.

---
