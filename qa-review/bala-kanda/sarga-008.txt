=== Sarga 8 ===

---
1.
tasya tv evaṁ prabhāvasya dharmajñasya mahātmanaḥ |
sutārthaṁ tapyamānasya nāsīd vaṁśakaraḥ sutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
cintayānasya tasyaivaṁ buddhir āsīn mahātmanaḥ |
sutārthaṁ vājimedhena kimarthaṁ na yajāmy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sa niścitāṁ matiṁ kr̥tvā yaṣṭavyam iti buddhimān |
mantribhiḥ saha dharmātmā sarvair eva kr̥tātmabhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tato ’bravīd idaṁ rājā sumantraṁ mantrisattamam |
śīghram ānaya me sarvān gurūṁs tān sapurohitān ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
etac chrutvā rahaḥ sūto rājānam idam abravīt |
r̥tvigbhir upadiṣṭo ’yaṁ purāvr̥tto mayā śrutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
sanatkumāro bhagavān pūrvaṁ kathitavān kathām |
r̥ṣīṇāṁ saṁnidhau rājaṁs tava putrāgamaṁ prati ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
kāśyapasya tu putro ’sti vibhāṇḍaka iti śrutaḥ |
r̥śyaśr̥ṅga iti khyātas tasya putro bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sa vane nityasaṁvr̥ddho munir vanacaraḥ sadā |
nānyaṁ jānāti viprendro nityaṁ pitranuvartanāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
dvaividhyaṁ brahmacaryasya bhaviṣyati mahātmanaḥ |
lokeṣu prathitaṁ rājan vipraiś ca kathitaṁ sadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tasyaivaṁ vartamānasya kālaḥ samabhivartata |
agniṁ śuśrūṣamāṇasya pitaraṁ ca yaśasvinam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
etasminn eva kāle tu lomapādaḥ pratāpavān |
aṅgeṣu prathito rājā bhaviṣyati mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tasya vyatikramād rājño bhaviṣyati sudāruṇā |
anāvr̥ṣṭiḥ sughorā vai sarvabhūtabhayāvahā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
anāvr̥ṣṭyāṁ tu vr̥ttāyāṁ rājā duḥkhasamanvitaḥ |
brāhmaṇāñ śrutavr̥ddhāṁś ca samānīya pravakṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
bhavantaḥ śrutadharmāṇo lokacāritravedinaḥ |
samādiśantu niyamaṁ prāyaścittaṁ yathā bhavet ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
vakṣyanti te mahīpālaṁ brāhmaṇā vedapāragāḥ |
vibhāṇḍakasutaṁ rājan sarvopāyair ihānaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
ānāyya ca mahīpāla r̥śyaśr̥ṅgaṁ susatkr̥tam |
prayaccha kanyāṁ śāntāṁ vai vidhinā susamāhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
teṣāṁ tu vacanaṁ śrutvā rājā cintāṁ prapatsyate |
kenopāyena vai śakyam ihānetuṁ sa vīryavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tato rājā viniścitya saha mantribhir ātmavān |
purohitam amātyāṁś ca preṣayiṣyati satkr̥tān ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
te tu rājño vacaḥ śrutvā vyathitā vanatānanāḥ |
na gacchema r̥ṣer bhītā anuneṣyanti taṁ nr̥pam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
vakṣyanti cintayitvā te tasyopāyāṁś ca tān kṣamān |
āneṣyāmo vayaṁ vipraṁ na ca doṣo bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
evam aṅgādhipenaiva gaṇikābhir r̥ṣeḥ sutaḥ |
ānīto ’varṣayad devaḥ śāntā cāsmai pradīyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
r̥śyaśr̥ṅgas tu jāmātā putrāṁs tava vidhāsyati |
sanatkumārakathitam etāvad vyāhr̥taṁ mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
atha hr̥ṣṭo daśarathaḥ sumantraṁ pratyabhāṣata |
yatharṣyaśr̥ṅgas tv ānīto vistareṇa tvayocyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
