=== Sarga 53 ===

---
1.
kāmadhenuṁ vasiṣṭho ’pi yadā na tyajate muniḥ |
tadāsya śabalāṁ rāma viśvāmitro ’nvakarṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
nīyamānā tu śabalā rāma rājñā mahātmanā |
duḥkhitā cintayām āsa rudantī śokakarśitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
parityaktā vasiṣṭhena kim ahaṁ sumahātmanā |
yāhaṁ rājabhr̥tair dīnā hriyeyaṁ bhr̥śaduḥkhitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
kiṁ mayāpakr̥taṁ tasya maharṣer bhāvitātmanaḥ |
yan mām anāgasaṁ bhaktām iṣṭāṁ tyajati dhārmikaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
iti sā cintayitvā tu niḥśvasya ca punaḥ punaḥ |
jagāma vegena tadā vasiṣṭhaṁ paramaujasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
nirdhūya tāṁs tadā bhr̥tyāñ śataśaḥ śatrusūdana |
jagāmānilavegena pādamūlaṁ mahātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
śabalā sā rudantī ca krośantī cedam abravīt |
vasiṣṭhasyāgrataḥ sthitvā meghadundubhirāviṇī ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
bhagavan kiṁ parityaktā tvayāhaṁ brahmaṇaḥ suta |
yasmād rājabhr̥tā māṁ hi nayante tvatsakāśataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
evam uktas tu brahmarṣir idaṁ vacanam abravīt |
śokasaṁtaptahr̥dayāṁ svasāram iva duḥkhitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
na tvāṁ tyajāmi śabale nāpi me ’pakr̥taṁ tvayā |
eṣa tvāṁ nayate rājā balān matto mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
na hi tulyaṁ balaṁ mahyaṁ rājā tv adya viśeṣataḥ |
balī rājā kṣatriyaś ca pr̥thivyāḥ patir eva ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
iyam akṣauhiṇīpūrṇā savājirathasaṁkulā |
hastidhvajasamākīrṇā tenāsau balavattaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
evam uktā vasiṣṭhena pratyuvāca vinītavat |
vacanaṁ vacanajñā sā brahmarṣim amitaprabham ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
na balaṁ kṣatriyasyāhur brāhmaṇo balavattaraḥ |
brahman brahmabalaṁ divyaṁ kṣatrāt tu balavattaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
aprameyabalaṁ tubhyaṁ na tvayā balavattaraḥ |
viśvāmitro mahāvīryas tejas tava durāsadam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
niyuṅkṣva māṁ mahātejas tvadbrahmabalasaṁbhr̥tām |
tasya darpaṁ balaṁ yat tan nāśayāmi durātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
ity uktas tu tayā rāma vasiṣṭhaḥ sumahāyaśāḥ |
sr̥jasveti tadovāca balaṁ parabalārujam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tasyā humbhāravotsr̥ṣṭāḥ pahlavāḥ śataśo nr̥pa |
nāśayanti balaṁ sarvaṁ viśvāmitrasya paśyataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
sa rājā paramakruddhaḥ krodhavisphāritekṣaṇaḥ |
pahlavān nāśayām āsa śastrair uccāvacair api ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
viśvāmitrārditān dr̥ṣṭvā pahlavāñ śataśas tadā |
bhūya evāsr̥jad ghorāñ śakān yavanamiśritān ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tair āsīt saṁvr̥tā bhūmiḥ śakair yavanamiśritaiḥ |
prabhāvadbhir mahāvīryair hemakiñjalkasaṁnibhaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
dīrghāsipaṭṭiśadharair hemavarṇāmbarāvr̥taiḥ |
nirdagdhaṁ tad balaṁ sarvaṁ pradīptair iva pāvakaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tato ’strāṇi mahātejā viśvāmitro mumoca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
