=== Sarga 51 ===
Śatānanda's Joy; Viśvāmitra's Early Life

---
1.
tasya tadvacanaṁ śrutvā viśvāmitrasya dhīmataḥ |
hr̥ṣṭarōmā mahātējāḥ śatānandō mahātapāḥ ||

> **Meaning 1:** Hearing those words of the wise Viśvāmitra, the deeply radiant
> great ascetic Śatānanda felt his hair stand on end with joy.

---
2.
gautamasya sutō jyēṣṭhastapasā dyōtitaprabhaḥ |
rāmasandarśanādēva paraṁ vismayamāgataḥ ||

> **Meaning 2:** Gautama's eldest son, his splendor kindled by tapas, was filled
> with the highest wonder simply at the sight of Rāma.

---
3.
sa tau niṣaṇṇau samprēkṣya sukhāsīnau nr̥pātmajau |
śatānandō muniśrēṣṭhaṁ viśvāmitramathābravīt ||

> **Meaning 3:** Looking upon the two princes seated comfortably at ease,
> Śatānanda spoke to Viśvāmitra, the foremost of munis:

---
4.
api tē muniśārdūla mama mātā yaśasvinī |
darśitā rājaputrāya tapō dīrghamupāgatā ||

> **Meaning 4:** "Foremost of munis, did you show my noble mother—who had
> practiced tapas for so long—to Prince Rāma?

---
5.
api rāmē mahātējō mama mātā yaśasvinī |
vanyairupāharatpūjāṁ pūjārhē sarvadēhinām ||

> **Meaning 5:** "Deeply radiant sage, did my revered mother honor Rāma, who is
> worthy of reverence from all beings, with offerings from the forest?

---
6.
api rāmāya kathitaṁ yathāvr̥ttaṁ purātanam |
mama māturmahātējō dēvēna duranuṣṭhitam ||

> **Meaning 6:** "Radiant sage, did you tell Rāma what happened long ago—the
> transgression involving my mother and the devatā?

---
7.
api kauśika bhadraṁ tē guruṇā mama saṅgatā |
mātā mama muniśrēṣṭha rāmasandarśanāditaḥ ||

> **Meaning 7:** "Kauśika, foremost of munis—may all be well with you—was my
> mother reunited with my father after beholding Rāma?

---
8.
api mē guruṇā rāmaḥ pūjitaḥ kuśikātmaja |
ihāgatō mahātējāḥ pūjāṁ prāpya mahātmanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 8:** "Son of Kuśika, did my father honor Rāma, and has this deeply
> radiant prince come here after receiving the homage of that great-souled sage?

---
9.
api śāntēna manasā gururmē kuśikātmaja |
ihāgatēna rāmēṇa prayatēnābhivāditaḥ ||

> **Meaning 9:** "Kauśika, did Rāma, who has come here, reverently pay his
> respects to my father with a serene mind?"

---
10.
tacchrutvā vacanaṁ tasya viśvāmitrō mahāmuniḥ |
pratyuvāca śatānandaṁ vākyajñō vākyakōvidam ||

> **Meaning 10:** Hearing his words, the great muni Viśvāmitra, well-versed in
> speech, replied to the eloquent Śatānanda:

---
11.
nātikrāntaṁ muniśrēṣṭha yatkartavyaṁ kr̥taṁ mayā |
saṅgatā muninā patnī bhārgavēṇēva rēṇukā ||

> **Meaning 11:** "Foremost of munis, nothing was neglected—I carried out
> everything that needed to be done, and Ahalyā was reunited with Gautama
> just as Reṇukā was with Jamadagni."

---
12.
tacchrutvā vacanaṁ tasya viśvāmitrasya dhīmataḥ |
śatānandō mahātējā rāmaṁ vacanamabravīt ||

> **Meaning 12:** Hearing the words of the wise Viśvāmitra, the deeply radiant
> Śatānanda spoke to Rāma:

---
13.
svāgataṁ tē naraśrēṣṭha diṣṭyā prāptō'si rāghava |
viśvāmitraṁ puraskr̥tya maharṣimaparājitam ||

> **Meaning 13:** "Rāghava, foremost of men, welcome to you! By good fortune you
> have arrived here, led by the unconquered maharṣi Viśvāmitra.

---
14.
acintyakarmā tapasā brahmarṣiramitaprabhaḥ |
viśvāmitrō mahātējā vētsyēnaṁ paramāṁ gatim ||

> **Meaning 14:** "The illustrious Viśvāmitra is a brahmarṣi of boundless
> splendor whose deeds through tapas are beyond comprehension; know him to be
> your supreme refuge.

---
15.
nāsti dhanyatarō rāma tvattō'nyō bhuvi kaścana |
gōptā kuśikaputrastē yēna taptaṁ mahattapaḥ ||

> **Meaning 15:** "Rāma, there is no one on earth more fortunate than you, for
> you have as your protector this son of Kuśika, who has performed tremendous
> tapas.

---
16.
śrūyatāṁ cābhidāsyāmi kauśikasya mahātmanaḥ |
yathābalaṁ yathāvr̥ttaṁ tanmē nigadataḥ śr̥ṇu ||

> **Meaning 16:** "Listen as I recount the power and the life story of the
> great-souled Kauśika; hear it from me as I tell you.

---
17.
rājābhūdēṣa dharmātmā dīrgha kālamarindamaḥ |
dharmajñaḥ kr̥tavidyaśca prajānāṁ ca hitē rataḥ ||

> **Meaning 17:** "For a long time he was a righteous king, a tamer of his foes,
> learned in dharma, accomplished in every branch of knowledge, and devoted to
> the welfare of his people.

---
18.
prajāpatisutastvāsītkuśō nāma mahīpatiḥ |
kuśasya putrō balavānkuśanābhaḥ sudhārmikaḥ ||

> **Meaning 18:** "From Prajāpati came a king named Kuśa, and Kuśa's son was the
> mighty and deeply righteous Kuśanābha.

---
19.
kuśanābhasutastvāsīdgādhirityēva viśrutaḥ |
gādhēḥ putrō mahātējā viśvāmitrō mahāmuniḥ ||

> **Meaning 19:** "Kuśanābha's son was renowned as Gādhi, and Gādhi's son is
> this deeply radiant great muni Viśvāmitra.

---
20.
viśvamitrō mahātējāḥ pālayāmāsa mēdinīm |
bahuvarṣasahasrāṇi rājā rājyamakārayat ||

> **Meaning 20:** "The glorious King Viśvāmitra ruled the earth and governed
> the kingdom for many thousands of years.

---
21.
kadācittu mahātējā yōjayitvā varūthinīm |
akṣauhiṇīparivr̥taḥ paricakrāma mēdinīm ||

> **Meaning 21:** "Once, having marshaled his army, the mighty king set out
> across the earth surrounded by a full akṣauhiṇī.

---
22.
nagarāṇi ca rāṣṭrāṇi saritaśca tathā girīn |
āśramānkramaśō rājā vicarannājagāmaha ||

> **Meaning 22:** "Moving gradually through cities, realms, rivers, mountains,
> and āśramas, the king eventually arrived at Vasiṣṭha's hermitage.

---
23.
vasiṣṭhasyāśramapadaṁ nānāpuṣpaphaladrumam |
nānāmr̥gagaṇākīrṇaṁ siddhacāraṇasēvitam ||

> **Meaning 23:** "Vasiṣṭha's āśrama grounds were filled with trees bearing all
> kinds of flowers and fruits, teeming with herds of various beasts, and
> frequented by Siddhas and Cāraṇas.

---
24.
dēvadānavagandharvaiḥ kinnarairupaśōbhitam |
praśāntahariṇākīrṇaṁ dvijasaṅghaniṣēvitam ||

> **Meaning 24:** "Adorned by devatās, dānavas, Gandharvas, and Kinnaras, it
> abounded with gentle deer and was thronged with flocks of birds.

---
25.
brahmarṣigaṇasaṅkīrṇaṁ dēvarṣigaṇasēvitam |
tapaścaraṇasaṁsiddhairagnikalpairmahātmabhiḥ ||

> **Meaning 25:** "It was filled with hosts of brahmarṣis and frequented by
> hosts of devarṣis—great-souled sages perfected in the practice of tapas and
> blazing like fire.

---
26.
satataṁ saṅkulaṁ śrīmadbrahmakalpairmahātmabhiḥ |
abbhakṣairvāyubhakṣaiśca śīrṇaparṇāśanaistathā ||

> **Meaning 26:** "The āśrama was resplendent and always filled with noble sages
> equal to Brahmā—some sustaining themselves on water, some on air, and others
> on fallen leaves,

---
27.
phalamūlāśanairdāntairjitarōṣairjitēndriyaiḥ |
r̥ṣibhirvālakhilyaiśca japahōmaparāyaṇaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 27:** "and still others living on fruits and roots, self-controlled,
> free of anger, and masters of their senses, along with r̥ṣis and Vālakhilyas
> devoted to japa and homa.

---
28.
vasiṣṭhasyāśramapadaṁ brahmalōkamivāparam |
dadarśa jayatāṁ śrēṣṭha viśvāmitrō mahābalaḥ ||

> **Meaning 28:** "Victorious Rāma, the mighty Viśvāmitra saw Vasiṣṭha's
> āśrama grounds, which looked like a second Brahmaloka."

---
