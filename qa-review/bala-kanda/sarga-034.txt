=== Sarga 34 ===
The Birth of Gādhi; Viśvāmitra's Lineage and the River Kauśikī

---
1.
kr̥tōdvāhē gatē tasminbrahmadattē ca rāghava |
aputraḥ putralābhāya pautrīmiṣṭimakalpayat ||

> **Meaning 1:** "Once Brahmadatta was married and had departed, Rāghava,
> Kuśanābha—who had no son—arranged a sacred yajña to obtain a son.

---
2.
iṣṭyāṁ tu vartamānāyāṁ kuśanābhaṁ mahīpatim |
uvāca paramaprītaḥ kuśō brahmasutastadā ||

> **Meaning 2:** "While the yajña was underway, Kuśanābha's father Kuśa, the
> son of Brahmā, was deeply pleased and spoke to the king:

---
3.
putrastē sadr̥śaḥ putra bhaviṣyati sudhārmikaḥ |
gādhiṁ prāpsyasi tēna tvaṁ kīrtiṁ lōkē ca śāśvatīm ||

> **Meaning 3:** "'My son, you will have a deeply righteous son just like
> yourself. You will be blessed with Gādhi, and through him you will gain
> lasting renown in the world.'

---
4.
ēvamuktvā kuśō rāma kuśanābhaṁ mahīpatim |
jagāmākāśamāviśya brahmalōkaṁ sanātanam ||

> **Meaning 4:** "After saying this to King Kuśanābha, Rāma, Kuśa rose into the
> sky and returned to the eternal Brahmaloka.

---
5.
kasyacittvatha kālasya kuśanābhasya dhīmataḥ |
jajñē paramadharmiṣṭhō gādhirityēva nāmataḥ ||

> **Meaning 5:** "In the course of time, a supremely righteous son named Gādhi
> was born to the wise Kuśanābha.

---
6.
sa pitā mama kākutstha gādhiḥ paramadhārmikaḥ |
kuśavaṁśaprasūtō'smi kauśikō raghunandana ||

> **Meaning 6:** "That supremely righteous Gādhi is my father, Kākutstha. Born
> in Kuśa's lineage, I am Kauśika.

---
7.
pūrvajā bhaginī cāpi mama rāghava suvratā |
nāmnā satyavatī nāma r̥cīkē pratipāditā ||

> **Meaning 7:** "I also had an elder sister of steadfast vows, Rāghava, named
> Satyavatī, who was given in marriage to Ṛcīka.

---
8.
saśarīrā gatā svargaṁ bhartāramanuvartinī |
kauśikī paramōdārā sā pravr̥ttā mahānadī ||

> **Meaning 8:** "Ever devoted to her husband, she ascended to svarga in her
> physical body, and that most noble woman flowed forth on earth as the great
> river Kauśikī.

---
9.
divyā puṇyōdakā ramyā himavantamupāśritā |
lōkasya hitakāmārthaṁ pravr̥ttā bhaginī mama ||

> **Meaning 9:** "Divine, serene, and filled with sacred waters, my sister
> flows along the slopes of Himavat for the good of the entire world.

---
10.
tatō'haṁ himavatpārśvē vasāmi niyataḥ sukham |
bhaginyāḥ snēhasaṁyuktaḥ kauśikyā raghunandana ||

> **Meaning 10:** "That is why I live happily along the slopes of Himavat,
> disciplined in my vows and bound by deep affection for my sister Kauśikī,
> Raghunandana.

---
11.
sā tu satyavatī puṇyā satyē dharmē pratiṣṭhitā |
pativratā mahābhāgā kauśikī saritāṁ varā ||

> **Meaning 11:** "Pure, steadfast in truth and dharma, a devoted wife and
> supremely blessed—that Satyavatī is Kauśikī, foremost among rivers.

---
12.
ahaṁ hi niyamādrāma hitvā tāṁ samupāgataḥ |
siddhāśramamanuprāpya siddhō'smi tava tējasā ||

> **Meaning 12:** "To observe a sacred vow, Rāma, I left her and came to
> Siddhāśrama—and through your power, I have fulfilled it.

---
13.
ēṣā rāma mamōtpattiḥ svasya vaṁśasya kīrtitā |
dēśasya ca mahābāhō yanmāṁ tvaṁ paripr̥cchasi ||

> **Meaning 13:** "This, Rāma, is the account of my birth, my lineage, and the
> region of Girivraja that you asked me about, mighty-armed prince.

---
14.
gatō'rdharātraḥ kākutstha kathāḥ kathayatō mama |
nidrāmabhyēhi bhadraṁ tē mā bhūdvighnō'dhvanīha naḥ ||

> **Meaning 14:** "Midnight has already passed as I told these stories,
> Kākutstha. Go to sleep—blessings upon you—so that nothing hinders our journey
> ahead.

---
15.
niṣpandāstaravaḥ sarvē nilīnā mr̥gapakṣiṇaḥ |
naiśēna tamasā vyāptā diśaśca raghunandana ||

> **Meaning 15:** "All the trees stand motionless, the animals and birds have
> tucked themselves away to rest, and the darkness of night fills every
> direction, Raghunandana.

---
16.
śanairviyujyatē sandhyā nabhō nētrairivāvr̥tam |
nakṣatratārāgahanaṁ jyōtirbhiravabhāsatē ||

> **Meaning 16:** "Twilight is gently fading, and the sky—thick with
> constellations and stars shining with their lights—looks as though dotted with
> countless eyes.

---
17.
uttiṣṭhati ca śītāṁśuḥ śaśī lōkatamōnudaḥ |
hlādayanprāṇināṁ lōkē manāṁsi prabhayā vibhō ||

> **Meaning 17:** "Now the cool-rayed moon is rising, dispelling the world's
> darkness and soothing the hearts of all living beings with its soft radiance,
> mighty prince.

---
18.
naiśāni sarvabhūtāni pracaranti tatastataḥ |
yakṣarākṣasasaṅghāśca raudrāśca piśitāśanāḥ ||

> **Meaning 18:** "All creatures of the night are now roaming everywhere—bands of
> Yakṣas and Rākṣasas, along with fierce flesh-eating beings."

---
19.
ēvamuktvā mahātējā virarāma mahāmuniḥ |
sādhu sādhviti taṁ sarvē munayō hyabhyapūjayan ||

> **Meaning 19:** Having spoken thus, the radiant great sage fell silent, and
> all the assembled munis honored him, exclaiming, "Well spoken! Well spoken!"

---
20.
kuśikānāmayaṁ vaṁśo mahān dharmaparaḥ sadā |
brahmopamā mahātmānaḥ kuśavaṁśyā narottama ||

> **Meaning 20:** "Noblest of men, this great lineage of the Kuśikas is always
> devoted to dharma; the descendants of Kuśa are great souls equal to Brahmā.

---
21.
viśeṣeṇa bhavāneva viśvāmitro mahāyaśāḥ |
kauśikī saritāṁ śreṣṭhā kulodyotakarī tava ||

> **Meaning 21:** "Most of all, you yourself, illustrious Viśvāmitra, and
> Kauśikī, foremost among rivers, bring radiant glory to your family."

---
22.
muditairmuniśārdūlaiḥ praśastaḥ kuśikātmajaḥ |
nidrāmupāgamacchrīmānastaṁ gata ivāṁśumān ||

> **Meaning 22:** Praised by those delighted foremost sages, the glorious son of
> Kuśa went to sleep, looking like the sun sinking to rest.

---
23.
rāmō'pi saha saumitriḥ kiñcidāgatavismayaḥ |
praśasya muniśārdūlaṁ nidrāṁ samupasēvatē ||

> **Meaning 23:** Rāma too, along with Saumitri, filled with quiet wonder,
> praised that foremost among munis and drifted off to sleep.

---
