=== Sarga 34 ===

---
1.
upāsya rātriśeṣaṁ tu śoṇākūle maharṣibhiḥ |
niśāyāṁ suprabhātāyāṁ viśvāmitro ’bhyabhāṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
suprabhātā niśā rāma pūrvā saṁdhyā pravartate |
uttiṣṭhottiṣṭha bhadraṁ te gamanāyābhirocaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tac chrutvā vacanaṁ tasya kr̥tvā paurvāhṇikīṁ kriyām |
gamanaṁ rocayām āsa vākyaṁ cedam uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
ayaṁ śoṇaḥ śubhajalo gādhaḥ pulinamaṇḍitaḥ |
katareṇa pathā brahman saṁtariṣyāmahe vayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
evam uktas tu rāmeṇa viśvāmitro ’bravīd idam |
eṣa panthā mayoddiṣṭo yena yānti maharṣayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
te gatvā dūram adhvānaṁ gate ’rdhadivase tadā |
jāhnavīṁ saritāṁ śreṣṭhāṁ dadr̥śur munisevitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā puṇyasalilāṁ haṁsasārasasevitām |
babhūvur muditāḥ sarve munayaḥ saharāghavāḥ |
tasyās tīre tataś cakrus te āvāsaparigraham ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tataḥ snātvā yathānyāyaṁ saṁtarpya pitr̥devatāḥ |
hutvā caivāgnihotrāṇi prāśya cāmr̥tavad dhaviḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
viviśur jāhnavītīre śucau muditamānasāḥ |
viśvāmitraṁ mahātmānaṁ parivārya samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
saṁprahr̥ṣṭamanā rāmo viśvāmitram athābravīt |
bhagavañ śrotum icchāmi gaṅgāṁ tripathagāṁ nadīm |
trailokyaṁ katham ākramya gatā nadanadīpatim ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
codito rāma vākyena viśvāmitro mahāmuniḥ |
vr̥ddhiṁ janma ca gaṅgāyā vaktum evopacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
śailendro himavān nāma dhātūnām ākaro mahān |
tasya kanyā dvayaṁ rāma rūpeṇāpratimaṁ bhuvi ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
yā meruduhitā rāma tayor mātā sumadhyamā |
nāmnā menā manojñā vai patnī himavataḥ priyā ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tasyāṁ gaṅgeyam abhavaj jyeṣṭhā himavataḥ sutā |
umā nāma dvitīyābhūt kanyā tasyaiva rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
atha jyeṣṭhāṁ surāḥ sarve devatārthacikīrṣayā |
śailendraṁ varayām āsur gaṅgāṁ tripathagāṁ nadīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
dadau dharmeṇa himavāṁs tanayāṁ lokapāvanīm |
svacchandapathagāṁ gaṅgāṁ trailokyahitakāmyayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
pratigr̥hya trilokārthaṁ trilokahitakāriṇaḥ |
gaṅgām ādāya te ’gacchan kr̥tārthenāntarātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
yā cānyā śailaduhitā kanyāsīd raghunandana |
ugraṁ sā vratam āsthāya tapas tepe tapodhanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
ugreṇa tapasā yuktāṁ dadau śailavaraḥ sutām |
rudrāyāpratirūpāya umāṁ lokanamaskr̥tām ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
ete te śaila rājasya sute lokanamaskr̥te |
gaṅgā ca saritāṁ śreṣṭhā umā devī ca rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
etat te dharmam ākhyātaṁ yathā tripathagā nadī |
khaṁ gatā prathamaṁ tāta gatiṁ gatimatāṁ vara ||

=== meaning ===

---
