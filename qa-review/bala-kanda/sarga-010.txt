=== Sarga 10 ===
The Bringing of Sage Ṛśyaśṛṅga to Aṅga and His Marriage to Śāntā

---
1.
sumantraścōditō rājñā prōvācēdaṁ vacastadā |
yatharṣyaśr̥ṅgastvānītaḥ śr̥ṇu mē mantribhiḥ saha ||

> **Meaning 1:** Urged by the king, Sumantra spoke these words: "Listen to me
> recount how the ministers brought Ṛśyaśṛṅga."

---
2.
rōmapādamuvācēdaṁ sahāmātyaḥ purōhitaḥ |
upāyō nirapāyō'yamasmābhirabhicintitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 2:** The priest, together with the ministers, said to Rōmapāda, "We
> have thought of a plan that carries no risk."

---
3.
r̥śyaśr̥ṅgō vanacarastapaḥsvādhyāyanē rataḥ |
anabhijñaḥ sa nārīṇāṁ viṣayāṇāṁ sukhasya ca ||

> **Meaning 3:** "Ṛśyaśṛṅga lives in the forest and is devoted to austerity and
> Vedic study. He knows nothing of women, of sense pleasures, or of the
> happiness they bring."

---
4.
indriyārthairabhimatairnaracittapramāthibhiḥ |
puramānāyayiṣyāmaḥ kṣipraṁ cādhyavasīyatām ||

> **Meaning 4:** "We will bring him to the city with pleasures of the senses
> that he will find appealing, the kind that unsettle the minds of men. Let it
> be decided quickly."

---
5.
gaṇikāstatra gacchantu rūpavatyaḥ svalaṅkr̥tāḥ |
pralōbhya vividhōpāyairānēṣyantīha satkr̥tāḥ ||

> **Meaning 5:** "Let courtesans, beautiful and richly adorned, go there. They
> will tempt him in many ways and bring him here with honor."

---
6.
śrutvā tathēti rājā ca pratyuvāca purōhitam |
purōhitō mantriṇaśca tathā cakruśca tē tadā ||

> **Meaning 6:** Hearing this, the king said, "So be it," to the priest. The
> priest and the ministers then did just that.

---
7.
vāramukhyāstu tacchrutvā vanaṁ praviviśurmahat |
āśramasyāvidūrē'sminyatnaṁ kurvanti darśanē |
r̥ṣiputrasya ghōrasya nityamāśramavāsinaḥ ||

> **Meaning 7:** Hearing this, the foremost among the courtesans entered the
> great forest. Not far from the hermitage, they kept trying to catch sight of
> Ṛśyaśṛṅga, a fierce ascetic who always lived within it.

---
8.
pituḥ sa nityasantuṣṭō nāticakrāma cāśramāt ||

> **Meaning 8:** He was always content with his father and never stepped outside
> the hermitage.

---
9.
na tēna janmaprabhr̥ti dr̥ṣṭapūrvaṁ tapasvinā |
strī vā pumānvā yaccānyatsattvaṁ nagararāṣṭrajam ||

> **Meaning 9:** From birth, the ascetic had never seen a woman, a man, or any
> other being from a city or kingdom.

---
10.
tataḥ kadācittaṁ dēśamājagāma yadr̥cchayā |
vibhāṇḍakasutastatra tāścāpaśyadvarāṅganāḥ ||

> **Meaning 10:** Then, one day, Vibhāṇḍaka's son happened to come to that spot,
> and there he saw those beautiful women.

---
11.
tāścitravēṣāḥ pramadā gāyantyō madhurasvaraiḥ |
r̥ṣiputramupāgamya sarvā vacanamabruvan ||

> **Meaning 11:** Those women, dressed in dazzling clothes and singing in sweet
> voices, came up to the ṛṣi's son and all said to him:

---
12.
kastvaṁ kiṁ vartasē brahmañjñātumicchāmahē vayam |
ēkastvaṁ vijanē ghōrē vanē carasi śaṁsa naḥ ||

> **Meaning 12:** "Who are you, and what do you do, brahmin? We want to know.
> You wander alone in this lonely, dreadful forest. Tell us."

---
13.
adr̥ṣṭarūpāstāstēna kāmyarūpā vanē striyaḥ |
hārdāttasya matirjātā ākhyātuṁ pitaraṁ svakam ||

> **Meaning 13:** He had never seen such forms before, and those enchanting women in the
> forest. Out of affection, he felt moved to tell them about his
> father.

---
14.
pitā vibhāṇḍakō'smākaṁ tasyāhaṁ suta aurasaḥ |
r̥śyaśr̥ṅga iti khyātaṁ nāma karma ca mē bhuvi ||

> **Meaning 14:** "My father is Vibhāṇḍaka, and I am his son. My name is
> Ṛśyaśṛṅga, and my life of penance is well known in this forest."

---
15.
ihāśramapadō'smākaṁ samīpē śubhadarśanāḥ |
kariṣyē vō'tra pūjāṁ vai sarvēṣāṁ vidhipūrvakam ||

> **Meaning 15:** "Our hermitage is close by, fair ones. I will welcome all of
> you there and offer hospitality according to sacred custom."

---
16.
r̥ṣiputravacaḥ śrutvā sarvāsāṁ matirāsa vai |
tadāśramapadaṁ draṣṭuṁ jagmuḥ sarvāśca tēna ha ||

> **Meaning 16:** Hearing the ṛṣi's son, all of them were moved to agree. And so
> they all went with him to see his hermitage.

---
17.
gatānāṁ tu tataḥ pūjāmr̥ṣiputraścakāra ha |
idamarghyamidaṁ pādyamidaṁ mūlaṁ phalaṁ ca naḥ ||

> **Meaning 17:** When they arrived, the ṛṣi's son honored them: "Here is
> arghya, here is pādya, and here are roots and fruits for you."

---
18.
pratigr̥hya tu tāṁ pūjāṁ sarvā ēva samutsukāḥ |
r̥ṣērbhītāśca śīghraṁ tu gamanāya matiṁ dadhuḥ ||

> **Meaning 18:** They accepted his hospitality, but fearful of Sage
> Vibhāṇḍaka's return, they grew anxious and resolved to leave quickly.

---
19.
asmākamapi mukhyāni phalānīmāni vai dvija |
gr̥hāṇa prati bhadraṁ tē bhakṣayasva ca mā ciram ||

> **Meaning 19:** They said, "We have excellent fruits too, brahmin. Please take
> them. May you be well. Eat them without delay."

---
20.
tatastāstaṁ samāliṅgya sarvā harṣasamanvitāḥ |
mōdakānpradadustasmai bhakṣyāṁśca vividhāñśubhān ||

> **Meaning 20:** Then, all of them full of joy, embraced him and gave him
> modakas and many kinds of fine delicacies.

---
21.
tāni cāsvādya tējasvī phalānīti sma manyatē |
anāsvāditapūrvāṇi vanē nityanivāsinā ||

> **Meaning 21:** The radiant young ṛṣi took them for fruits, since as a
> permanent forest-dweller he had never tasted such things before.

---
22.
āpr̥cchya ca tadā vipraṁ vratacaryāṁ nivēdya ca |
gacchanti smāpadēśāttā bhītāstasya pituḥ striyaḥ ||

> **Meaning 22:** Then they took leave of the brahmin, telling him they had a
> vow to keep. Afraid of his father, the women left under that pretext.

---
23.
gatāsu tāsu sarvāsu kāśyapasyātmajō dvijaḥ |
asvasthahr̥dayaścāsīdduḥkhaṁ sma parivartatē ||

> **Meaning 23:** Once all those women had departed, Ṛśyaśṛṅga, the young
> brahmin of Kaśyapa's line, lost his peace of mind and tossed and turned in
> restless longing.

---
24.
tatō'parēdyustaṁ dēśamājagāma sa vīryavān |
manōjñā yatra tā dr̥ṣṭā vāramukhyāḥ svalaṅkr̥tāḥ ||

> **Meaning 24:** The next day, the valiant young man came back to the same
> spot, where he had seen those lovely, richly adorned courtesans.

---
25.
dr̥ṣṭvaiva ca tadā vipramāyāntaṁ hr̥ṣṭamānasāḥ |
upasr̥tya tataḥ sarvāstāstamūcuridaṁ vacaḥ ||

> **Meaning 25:** Seeing the brahmin approaching, they were delighted. They came
> up to him, and all of them said these words:

---
26.
ēhyāśramapadaṁ saumya asmākamiti cābruvan |
tatrāpyēṣa vidhiḥ śrīmānviśēṣēṇa bhaviṣyati ||

> **Meaning 26:** They said, "Come, gentle one, to our hermitage. There, this
> hospitality will be even more special for you."

---
27.
śrutvā tu vacanaṁ tāsāṁ sarvāsāṁ hr̥dayaṅgamam |
gamanāya matiṁ cakrē taṁ ca ninyustadā striyaḥ ||

> **Meaning 27:** Hearing their words, which captivated his heart, he resolved
> to go with them, and the women then led him away to the kingdom of Aṅga.

---
28.
tatra cānīyamānē tu viprē tasminmahātmani |
vavarṣa sahasā dēvō jagatprahlādayaṁstadā ||

> **Meaning 28:** The moment that great-souled brahmin stepped into the realm,
> the Varuṇa devatā suddenly poured down rain, delighting the entire world.

---
29.
varṣēṇaivāgataṁ vipraṁ viṣayaṁ svaṁ narādhipaḥ |
pratyudgamya muniṁ prahvaḥ śirasā ca mahīṁ gataḥ ||

> **Meaning 29:** King Romapāda humbly went out to meet the sage, who had
> arrived in his land with the rain, and bowed to the ground with his head.

---
30.
arghyaṁ ca pradadau tasmai nyāyataḥ susamāhitaḥ |
vavrē prasādaṁ viprēndrānmā vipraṁ manyurāviśēt ||

> **Meaning 30:** With deep reverence, the king offered him arghya according to
> sacred rites, seeking the grace of that foremost brahmin so that no anger
> over being lured there would arise in him.

---
31.
antaḥpuraṁ praviśyāsmai kanyāṁ dattvā yathāvidhi |
śāntāṁ śāntēna manasā rājā harṣamavāpa saḥ ||

> **Meaning 31:** Leading him into the inner palace, the king gave him his
> daughter Śāntā in marriage according to sacred rites. His mind now at peace,
> he was filled with immense joy.

---
32.
ēvaṁ sa nyavasattatra sarvakāmaiḥ supūjitaḥ |
r̥śyaśr̥ṅgō mahātējāḥ śāntayā saha bhāryayā ||

> **Meaning 32:** So Ṛśyaśṛṅga, of great radiance, lived there, honored with
> every comfort, together with his wife Śāntā.

---
