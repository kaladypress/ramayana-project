=== Sarga 10 ===

---
1.
bhūya eva ca rājendra śr̥ṇu me vacanaṁ hitam |
yathā sa devapravaraḥ kathāyām evam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
ikṣvākūṇāṁ kule jāto bhaviṣyati sudhārmikaḥ |
rājā daśaratho nāmnā śrīmān satyapratiśravaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
aṅgarājena sakhyaṁ ca tasya rājño bhaviṣyati |
kanyā cāsya mahābhāgā śāntā nāma bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
putras tv aṅgasya rājñas tu lomapāda iti śrutaḥ |
taṁ sa rājā daśaratho gamiṣyati mahāyaśāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
anapatyo ’smi dharmātmañ śāntābhartā mama kratum |
āhareta tvayājñaptaḥ saṁtānārthaṁ kulasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
śrutvā rājño ’tha tad vākyaṁ manasā sa vicintya ca |
pradāsyate putravantaṁ śāntā bhartāram ātmavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
pratigr̥hya ca taṁ vipraṁ sa rājā vigatajvaraḥ |
āhariṣyati taṁ yajñaṁ prahr̥ṣṭenāntarātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
taṁ ca rājā daśaratho yaṣṭukāmaḥ kr̥tāñjaliḥ |
r̥śyaśr̥ṅgaṁ dvijaśreṣṭhaṁ varayiṣyati dharmavit ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
yajñārthaṁ prasavārthaṁ ca svargārthaṁ ca nareśvaraḥ |
labhate ca sa taṁ kāmaṁ dvijamukhyād viśāṁ patiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
putrāś cāsya bhaviṣyanti catvāro ’mitavikramāḥ |
vaṁśapratiṣṭhānakarāḥ sarvalokeṣu viśrutāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
evaṁ sa devapravaraḥ pūrvaṁ kathitavān kathām |
sanatkumāro bhagavān purā devayuge prabhuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sa tvaṁ puruṣaśārdūla tam ānaya susatkr̥tam |
svayam eva mahārāja gatvā sabalavāhanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
anumānya vasiṣṭhaṁ ca sūtavākyaṁ niśamya ca |
sāntaḥpuraḥ sahāmātyaḥ prayayau yatra sa dvijaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
vanāni saritaś caiva vyatikramya śanaiḥ śanaiḥ |
abhicakrāma taṁ deśaṁ yatra vai munipuṁgavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
āsādya taṁ dvijaśreṣṭhaṁ lomapādasamīpagam |
r̥ṣiputraṁ dadarśādau dīpyamānam ivānalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tato rājā yathānyāyaṁ pūjāṁ cakre viśeṣataḥ |
sakhitvāt tasya vai rājñaḥ prahr̥ṣṭenāntarātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
lomapādena cākhyātam r̥ṣiputrāya dhīmate |
sakhyaṁ saṁbandhakaṁ caiva tadā taṁ pratyapūjayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
evaṁ susatkr̥tas tena sahoṣitvā nararṣabhaḥ |
saptāṣṭadivasān rājā rājānam idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
śāntā tava sutā rājan saha bhartrā viśāmpate |
madīyaṁ nagaraṁ yātu kāryaṁ hi mahad udyatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tatheti rājā saṁśrutya gamanaṁ tasya dhīmataḥ |
uvāca vacanaṁ vipraṁ gaccha tvaṁ saha bhāryayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
r̥ṣiputraḥ pratiśrutya tathety āha nr̥paṁ tadā |
sa nr̥peṇābhyanujñātaḥ prayayau saha bhāryayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tāv anyonyāñjaliṁ kr̥tvā snehāt saṁśliṣya corasā |
nanandatur daśaratho lomapādaś ca vīryavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tataḥ suhr̥dam āpr̥cchya prasthito raghunandanaḥ |
paurebhyaḥ preṣayām āsa dūtān vai śīghragāminaḥ |
kriyatāṁ nagaraṁ sarvaṁ kṣipram eva svalaṁkr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tataḥ prahr̥ṣṭāḥ paurās te śrutvā rājānam āgatam |
tathā pracakrus tat sarvaṁ rājñā yat preṣitaṁ tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tataḥ svalaṁkr̥taṁ rājā nagaraṁ praviveśa ha |
śaṅkhadundubhinirghoṣaiḥ puraskr̥tya dvijarṣabham ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tataḥ pramuditāḥ sarve dr̥ṣṭvā taṁ nāgarā dvijam |
praveśyamānaṁ satkr̥tya narendreṇendrakarmaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
antaḥpuraṁ praveśyainaṁ pūjāṁ kr̥tvā tu śāstrataḥ |
kr̥takr̥tyaṁ tadātmānaṁ mene tasyopavāhanāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
antaḥpurāṇi sarvāṇi śāntāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā tathāgatām |
saha bhartrā viśālākṣīṁ prītyānandam upāgaman ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
pūjyamānā ca tābhiḥ sā rājñā caiva viśeṣataḥ |
uvāsa tatra sukhitā kaṁ cit kālaṁ saha dvijā ||

=== meaning ===

---
