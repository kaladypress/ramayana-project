=== Sarga 29 ===
The Story of Siddhāśrama and Vāmana; Viśvāmitra Begins His Yajña

---
1.
atha tasyāpramēyasya vacanaṁ paripr̥cchataḥ |
viśvāmitrō mahātējā vyākhyātumupacakramē ||

> **Meaning 1:** Then, in response to the question of that immeasurable Rāma,
> the radiant Viśvāmitra began to explain:

---
2.
iha rāma mahābāhō viṣṇurdēvanamaskr̥taḥ |
varṣāṇi subahūnīha tathā yugaśatāni ca ||

3.
tapaścaraṇayōgārthamuvāsa sumahātapāḥ |
ēṣa pūrvāśramō rāma vāmanasya mahātmanaḥ |
siddhāśrama iti khyātaḥ siddhō hyatra mahātapāḥ ||

> **Meaning 2-3:** "Here, mighty-armed Rāma, Viṣṇu—revered by the devatās—lived
> for many years and hundreds of yugas, practicing intense austerity and yoga.
> This was the ancient āśrama of the great-souled Vāmana, Rāma; it is known as
> Siddhāśrama because that great ascetic achieved perfection right here.

---
4.
ētasminnēva kālē tu rājā vairōcanirbaliḥ |
nirjitya daivatagaṇān sēndrān samarudgaṇān |
kārayāmāsa tadrājyaṁ triṣu lōkēṣu viśrutaḥ ||

> **Meaning 4:** "During that very time, King Bali, the son of Virocana,
> conquered the ranks of devatās—including Indra and the Maruts—and ruled that
> kingdom, renowned across the three worlds.

---
5.
balēstu yajamānasya dēvāḥ sāgnipurōgamāḥ |
samāgamya svayaṁ caiva viṣṇumūcurihāśramē ||

> **Meaning 5:** "While Bali was performing a great yajña, the devatās, led by
> Agni, gathered in person and spoke to Viṣṇu right here at this āśrama:

---
6.
balirvairōcanirviṣṇō yajatē yajñamuttamam |
asamāptavratē tasmin svakāryamabhipadyatām ||

> **Meaning 6:** "'Viṣṇu, Bali, the son of Virocana, is performing a supreme
> yajña. Before his yajña vow is completed, accomplish what needs to be done
> for us!

---
7.
yē cainamabhivartantē yācitāra itastataḥ |
yacca yatra yathāvacca sarvaṁ tēbhyaḥ prayacchati ||

> **Meaning 7:** "'Whoever approaches him from anywhere asking for gifts—he
> gives them everything they ask for, wherever and however they wish.

---
8.
sa tvaṁ surahitārthāya māyāyōgamupāśritaḥ |
vāmanatvaṁ gatō viṣṇō kuru kalyāṇamuttamam ||

> **Meaning 8:** "'Therefore, Viṣṇu, for the welfare of the devatās, use your
> māyā, take the form of a dwarf (Vāmana), and bring about this supreme
> blessing for us!'

---
9.
ētasminnantarē rāma kāśyapō'gnisamaprabhaḥ |
adityā sahitō rāma dīpyamāna ivaujasā ||

10.
dēvīsahāyō bhagavān divyaṁ varṣasahasrakam |
vrataṁ samāpya varadaṁ tuṣṭāva madhusūdanam ||

> **Meaning 9-10:** "Rāma, meanwhile Kaśyapa—blazing like fire and shining with
> spiritual radiance—completed a divine vow lasting a thousand years with
> Aditi by his side, and praised Madhusūdana, giver of boons:

---
11.
tapōmayaṁ tapōrāśiṁ tapōmūrtiṁ tapātmakam |
tapasā tvāṁ sutaptēna paśyāmi purōṣōttamam ||

> **Meaning 11:** "'You are the essence of austerity, a treasury of austerity,
> austerity embodied, and austerity itself! Through intense austerity, I see
> you, Puruṣottama.

---
12.
śarīrē tava paśyāmi jagatsarvamidaṁ prabhō |
tvamanādiranirdēśyastvāmahaṁ śaraṇaṁ gataḥ ||

> **Meaning 12:** "'In your body I see this entire universe, Supreme One. You
> are without beginning and beyond description; I have come to you for refuge.'

---
13.
tamuvāca hariḥ prītaḥ kaśyapaṁ dhūtakalmaṣam |
varaṁ varaya bhadraṁ tē varārhō'si matō mama ||

> **Meaning 13:** "Pleased with the pure-hearted Kaśyapa, Hari said to him,
> 'Ask for a boon—blessings be upon you! In my eyes, you truly deserve one.'

---
14.
tacchrutvā vacanaṁ tasya mārīcaḥ kaśyapō'bravīt |
adityā dēvatānāṁ ca mama caivānuyācitam ||

> **Meaning 14:** "Hearing his words, Kaśyapa, son of Marīci, said, 'This is
> what Aditi, the devatās, and I myself earnestly ask of you:

---
15.
varaṁ varada suprītō dātumarhasi suvrata |
putratvaṁ gaccha bhagavannadityā mama cānagha ||

> **Meaning 15:** "'Giver of boons, faithful to your vows, gladly grant this
> boon: sinless Supreme One, become the son of Aditi and me!

---
16.
bhrātā bhava yavīyāṁstvaṁ śakrasyāsurasūdana |
śōkārtānāṁ tu dēvānāṁ sāhāyyaṁ kartumarhasi ||

> **Meaning 16:** "'Become Śakra's younger brother, slayer of asuras, and come
> to the aid of the grief-stricken devatās.

---
17.
ayaṁ siddhāśramō nāma prasādāttē bhaviṣyati |
siddhē karmaṇi dēvēśa uttiṣṭha bhagavannitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 17:** "'By your grace, this place will be known as Siddhāśrama
> ("place of fulfillment"). Once this task is accomplished, ruler of the
> devatās, arise from here, Venerable One!'

---
18.
atha viṣṇurmahātējā adityāṁ samajāyata |
vāmanaṁ rūpamāsthāya vairōcanimupāgamat ||

> **Meaning 18:** "Then the radiant Viṣṇu was born to Aditi, and assuming the
> form of Vāmana, he approached Bali, the son of Virocana.

---
19.
trīnpadānatha bhikṣitvā pratigr̥hya ca mēdinīm |
ākramya lōkān lōkārthō sarvalōkahitē rataḥ ||

20.
mahēndrāya punaḥ prādāt niyamya balimōjasā |
trailōkyaṁ sa mahātējāścakrē śakravaśaṁ punaḥ ||

> **Meaning 19-20:** "Asking for three steps of land and receiving the earth,
> he—ever devoted to the welfare of all worlds—strode across the worlds for
> their sake. Restraining Bali by his power, that radiant one gave the earth
> back to Mahendra and restored the three worlds to Śakra's control.

---
21.
tēnaiṣa pūrvamākrānta āśramaḥ śramanāśanaḥ |
mayāpi bhaktyā tasyaiṣa vāmanasyōpabhujyatē ||

> **Meaning 21:** "This āśrama, which takes away all weariness, was graced by
> him in ancient times; out of devotion to Vāmana, I too live here and enjoy
> its peace.

---
22.
ētamāśramamāyānti rākṣasā vighnakāriṇaḥ |
atraiva puruṣavyāghra hantavyā duṣṭacāriṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 22:** "Rāma, foremost of men, those rākṣasas come to this very
> āśrama to cause disruption; right here those evildoers must be slain.

---
23.
adya gacchāmahē rāma siddhāśramamanuttamam |
tadāśramapadaṁ tāta tavāpyētadyathā mama ||

> **Meaning 23:** "Rāma, today we enter the peerless Siddhāśrama. Dear child,
> this āśrama is as much yours as it is mine."

---
24.
praviśannāśramapadaṁ vyarōcata mahāmuniḥ |
śaśīva gatanīhāraḥ punarvasusamanvitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 24:** As he entered the āśrama grounds, the great sage shone like
> the moon emerging from mist, joined by the Punarvasu constellation.

---
25.
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā munayaḥ sarvē siddhāśramanivāsinaḥ |
utpatyōtpatya sahasā viśvāmitramapūjayan ||

> **Meaning 25:** Seeing him, all the sages living in Siddhāśrama sprang up
> eagerly at once and offered reverence to Viśvāmitra.

---
26.
yathārhaṁ cakrirē pūjāṁ viśvāmitrāya dhīmatē |
tathaiva rājaputrābhyāmakurvannatithikriyām ||

> **Meaning 26:** They honored the wise Viśvāmitra as befitted his stature, and
> likewise offered traditional hospitality to the two princes.

---
27.
muhūrtamatha viśrāntau rājaputrāvarindamau |
prāñjalī muniśārdūlamūcatū raghunandanau ||

> **Meaning 27:** After resting for a short while, the two princes, subduers of
> enemies and scions of Raghu, joined their palms in reverence and said to the
> tiger among sages:

---
28.
adyaiva dīkṣāṁ praviśa bhadraṁ tē munipuṅgava |
siddhāśramō'yaṁ siddhaḥ syāt satyamastu vacastava ||

> **Meaning 28:** "Foremost of sages, enter upon your yajña vow (dīkṣā) today
> itself—blessings be upon you! May this Siddhāśrama truly fulfill its name,
> and may your word prove true."

---
29.
ēvamuktō mahātējā viśvāmitrō mahānr̥ṣiḥ |
pravivēśa tadā dīkṣāṁ niyatō niyatēndriyaḥ ||

> **Meaning 29:** Thus addressed, the radiant great sage Viśvāmitra,
> self-restrained and with senses controlled, entered upon the dīkṣā.

---
30.
kumārāvēva tāṁ rātrimuṣitvā susamāhitau |
prabhātakālē cōtthāya pūrvāṁ sandhyāmupāsya ca ||

31.
sprustodakau śucī paraṁ japyaṁ samāpya niyamēna ca |
hutāgnihōtramāsīnaṁ viśvāmitramavandatām ||

> **Meaning 30-31:** The two princes spent that night there with calm, focused
> minds. Rising at daybreak, they performed their morning sandhyā worship,
> purified themselves with water, duly completed their japa, and bowed to
> Viśvāmitra, who was seated after offering the agnihotra oblation.

---
