=== Sarga 26 ===

---
1.
atha tāṁ rajanīm uṣya viśvāmiro mahāyaśāḥ |
prahasya rāghavaṁ vākyam uvāca madhurākṣaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
patituṣṭo ’smi bhadraṁ te rājaputra mahāyaśaḥ |
prītyā paramayā yukto dadāmy astrāṇi sarvaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
devāsuragaṇān vāpi sagandharvoragān api |
yair amitrān prasahyājau vaśīkr̥tya jayiṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tāni divyāni bhadraṁ te dadāmy astrāṇi sarvaśaḥ |
daṇḍacakraṁ mahad divyaṁ tava dāsyāmi rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
dharmacakraṁ tato vīra kālacakraṁ tathaiva ca |
viṣṇucakraṁ tathātyugram aindraṁ cakraṁ tathaiva ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
vajram astraṁ naraśreṣṭha śaivaṁ śūlavaraṁ tathā |
astraṁ brahmaśiraś caiva aiṣīkam api rāghava |
dadāmi te mahābāho brāhmam astram anuttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
gade dve caiva kākutstha modakī śikharī ubhe |
pradīpte naraśārdūla prayacchāmi nr̥pātmaja ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
dharmapāśam ahaṁ rāma kālapāśaṁ tathaiva ca |
vāruṇaṁ pāśam astraṁ ca dadāny aham anuttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
aśanī dve prayacchāmi śuṣkārdre raghunandana ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
dadāmi cāstraṁ painākam astraṁ nārāyaṇaṁ tathā |
āgneyam astra dayitaṁ śikharaṁ nāma nāmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
vāyavyaṁ prathamaṁ nāma dadāmi tava rāghava |
astraṁ hayaśiro nāma krauñcam astraṁ tathaiva ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
śakti dvayaṁ ca kākutstha dadāmi tava cānagha |
kaṅkālaṁ musalaṁ ghoraṁ kāpālam atha kaṅkaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
dhārayanty asurā yāni dadāmy etāni sarvaśaḥ |
vaidyādharaṁ mahāstraṁ ca nandanaṁ nāma nāmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
asiratnaṁ mahābāho dadāmi nr̥varātmaja |
gāndharvam astraṁ dayitaṁ mānavaṁ nāma nāmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
prasvāpanapraśamane dadmi sauraṁ ca rāghava |
darpaṇaṁ śoṣaṇaṁ caiva saṁtāpanavilāpane ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
madanaṁ caiva durdharṣaṁ kandarpadayitaṁ tathā |
paiśācam astraṁ dayitaṁ mohanaṁ nāma nāmataḥ |
pratīccha naraśārdūla rājaputra mahāyaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tāmasaṁ naraśārdūla saumanaṁ ca mahābalam |
saṁvartaṁ caiva durdharṣaṁ mausalaṁ ca nr̥pātmaja ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
satyam astraṁ mahābāho tathā māyādharaṁ param |
ghoraṁ tejaḥprabhaṁ nāma paratejo’pakarṣaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
somāstraṁ śiśiraṁ nāma tvāṣṭram astraṁ sudāmanam |
dāruṇaṁ ca bhagasyāpi śīteṣum atha mānavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
etān nāma mahābāho kāmarūpān mahābalān |
gr̥hāṇa paramodārān kṣipram eva nr̥pātmaja ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
sthitas tu prāṅmukho bhūtvā śucir nivaratas tadā |
dadau rāmāya suprīto mantragrāmam anuttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
japatas tu munes tasya viśvāmitrasya dhīmataḥ |
upatasthur mahārhāṇi sarvāṇy astrāṇi rāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
ūcuś ca muditā rāmaṁ sarve prāñjalayas tadā |
ime sma paramodāra kiṁkarās tava rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
pratigr̥hya ca kākutsthaḥ samālabhya ca pāṇinā |
manasā me bhaviṣyadhvam iti tāny abhyacodayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tataḥ prītamanā rāmo viśvāmitraṁ mahāmunim |
abhivādya mahātejā gamanāyopacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
