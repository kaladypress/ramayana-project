=== Sarga 26 ===
Rāma Slays Tāṭakā

---
1.
munērvacanamaklībaṁ śrutvā naravarātmajaḥ |
rāghavaḥ prāñjalirbhūtvā pratyuvāca dr̥ḍhavrataḥ ||

> **Meaning 1:** Hearing the sage's resolute words, Rāghava, son of the best of
> kings and firm in his vow, joined his palms in reverence and replied:

---
2.
piturvacananirdēśāt piturvacanagauravāt |
vacanaṁ kauśikasyēti kartavyamaviśaṅkayā ||

3.
anuśiṣṭō'smyayōdhyāyāṁ gurumadhyē mahātmanā |
pitrā daśarathēnāhaṁ nāvajñēyaṁ ca tadvacaḥ ||

> **Meaning 2-3:** "My father's order, which I deeply revere, was 'Do as Kauśika
> says,' and I must carry it out without hesitation. In Ayodhyā, in the presence
> of my teachers, my great-souled father, Daśaratha, gave me that instruction,
> and his word must never be disregarded.

---
4.
sō'haṁ piturvacaḥ śrutvā śāsanādbrahmavādinaḥ |
kariṣyāmi na sandēhastāṭakāvadhamuttamam ||

> **Meaning 4:** "So, having heard my father's words, and following your command
> as a Vedic sage, I will without a doubt carry out this noble deed of slaying
> Tāṭakā.

---
5.
gōbrāhmaṇahitārthāya dēśasyāsya hitāya ca |
tava caivāpramēyasya vacanaṁ kartumudyataḥ ||

> **Meaning 5:** "For the protection of cows and the welfare of brāhmaṇas,
> and for the good of this land, I am ready to carry out your command, for your
> greatness is beyond measure."

---
6.
ēvamuktvā dhanurmadhyē badhvā muṣṭimarindamaḥ |
jyāghōṣamakarōttīvraṁ diśaḥ śabdēna nādayan ||

> **Meaning 6:** Having said this, Rāma, the subduer of enemies, gripped the
> middle of his bow tightly and twanged the bowstring sharply, making all the
> directions resound with the sound.

---
7.
tēna śabdēna vitrastāstāṭakāvanavāsinaḥ |
tāṭakā ca susaṅkruddhā tēna śabdēna mōhitā ||

> **Meaning 7:** Terrified by that sound, the inhabitants of Tāṭakā's forest
> trembled, while Tāṭakā herself grew furious and bewildered by it.

---
8.
taṁ śabdamabhinidhyāya rākṣasī krōdhamūrcchitā |
śrutvā cābhyadravat kruddhā yataḥ śabdō vinissr̥taḥ ||

> **Meaning 8:** Focusing on that sound, the rākṣasī, blinded with rage, heard
> where it came from and charged furiously toward the spot from which it had
> arisen.

---
9.
tāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rāghavaḥ kruddhāṁ vikr̥tāṁ vikr̥tānanām |
pramāṇēnātivr̥ddhāṁ ca lakṣmaṇaṁ sō'bhyabhāṣata ||

> **Meaning 9:** Seeing her enraged, deformed, hideous-faced, and gigantic in
> stature, Rāghava spoke to Lakṣmaṇa:

---
10.
paśya lakṣmaṇa yakṣiṇyā bhairavaṁ dāruṇaṁ vapuḥ |
bhidyēran darśanādasyā bhīrūṇāṁ hr̥dayāni ca ||

> **Meaning 10:** "Look, Lakṣmaṇa, at the yakṣiṇī's terrifying, dreadful form!
> Just seeing her would shatter the hearts of the timid.

---
11.
ētāṁ paśya durādharṣāṁ māyābalasamanvitām |
vinivr̥ttāṁ karōmyadya hr̥takarṇāgranāsikām ||

> **Meaning 11:** "Look at this formidable creature, endowed with the power of
> māyā. Today I will turn her back, with the tips of her ears and her nose
> severed.

---
12.
na hyēnāmutsahē hantuṁ strīsvabhāvēna rakṣitām |
vīryaṁ cāsyā gatiṁ cāpi hanyatāmiti mē matiḥ ||

> **Meaning 12:** "I do not wish to kill her, protected as she is by her
> womanhood. My intention is only to destroy her strength and her ability to
> move about."

---
13.
ēvaṁ bruvāṇē rāmē tu tāṭakā krōdhamūrcchitā |
udyamya bāhū garjantī rāmamēvābhyadhāvata ||

> **Meaning 13:** Even as Rāma was speaking, Tāṭakā, senseless with rage, raised
> both her arms and, roaring, rushed straight at Rāma.

---
14.
viśvāmitrastu brahmarṣirhuṅkārēṇābhibhartsya tām |
svasti rāghavayōrastu jayaṁ caivābhyabhāṣata ||

> **Meaning 14:** The brahmarṣi Viśvāmitra rebuked her with a loud roar of
> "huṁ!" and pronounced a blessing on the two Rāghavas: "May all be well with
> you, and may victory be yours!"

---
15.
uddhunvānā rajō ghōraṁ tāṭakā rāghavāvubhau |
rajō mēghēna mahatā muhūrtaṁ sā vyamōhayat ||

> **Meaning 15:** Stirring up a dreadful storm of dust, Tāṭakā bewildered both
> the Rāghavas for a moment with a vast cloud of dust.

---
16.
tatō māyāṁ samāsthāya śilāvarṣēṇa rāghavau |
avākirat sumahatā tataścukrōdha rāghavaḥ ||

> **Meaning 16:** Then, using her māyā to become invisible, she pelted the two
> Rāghavas with a massive shower of rocks. At that, Rāghava grew angry.

---
17.
śilāvarṣaṁ mahattasyāḥ śaravarṣēṇa rāghavaḥ |
prativāryōpadhāvantyāḥ karau cicchēda patribhiḥ ||

> **Meaning 17:** Blocking her heavy shower of rocks with a volley of arrows,
> Rāghava severed both hands of the charging Tāṭakā with his feathered arrows.

---
18.
tataścchinnabhujāṁ śrāntāmabhyāśē parigarjatīm |
saumitrirakarōt krōdhād hr̥takarṇāgranāsikām ||

> **Meaning 18:** Then, seeing her with severed arms, exhausted, yet still
> roaring nearby, Saumitri angrily sliced off the tips of her ears and her nose.

---
19.
kāmarūpadharā sā tu kr̥tvā rūpāṇyanēkaśaḥ |
antardhānaṁ gatā yakṣī mōhayantī svamāyayā |
aśmavarṣaṁ vimuñcantī bhairavaṁ vicacāra sā ||

> **Meaning 19:** Capable of taking any form at will, that yakṣī assumed many
> different shapes and vanished from sight. Bewildering them with her illusory
> powers, she moved about hurling a terrifying rain of stones.

---
20.
tatastāvaśmavarṣēṇa kīryamāṇau samantataḥ |
dr̥ṣṭvā gādhisutaḥ śrīmānidaṁ vacanamabravīt ||

> **Meaning 20:** Seeing the two brothers pelted from every side by that rain of
> stones, the illustrious son of Gādhi spoke these words:

---
21.
alaṁ tē ghr̥ṇayā rāma pāpaiṣā duṣṭacāriṇī |
yajñavighnakarī yakṣī purā vardhēta māyayā ||

22.
vadhyatāṁ tāvadēvaiṣā purā sandhyā pravartatē |
rakṣāṁsi sandhyākālē tu durdharṣāṇi bhavanti hi ||

> **Meaning 21-22:** "Enough of your pity, Rāma! This wicked, evil-natured
> yakṣī, a destroyer of yajñas, may soon grow even stronger through her māyā.
> Kill her right now, before twilight sets in, for rākṣasas become invincible at
> twilight!"

---
23.
ityuktassa tu tāṁ yakṣīmaśmavr̥ṣṭyābhivarṣiṇīm |
darśayan śabdavēdhitvaṁ tāṁ rurōdha sa sāyakaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 23:** At these words, Rāma showed his skill at shooting by sound and
> with his arrows checked that yakṣī as she poured down a rain of stones.

---
24.
sā ruddhā bāṇajālēna māyābalasamanvitā |
abhidudrāva kākutsthaṁ lakṣamaṇaṁ ca vinēṣudī ||

> **Meaning 24:** Hemmed in by that net of arrows, the yakṣī, still wielding her
> power of māyā, rushed at Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa.

---
25.
tāmāpatantīṁ vēgēna vikrāntāmaśanīmiva |
śarēṇōrasi vivyādha sā papāta mamāra ca ||

> **Meaning 25:** As she came hurtling with tremendous speed, fierce as a bolt
> of lightning, Rāma pierced her in the chest with an arrow; she fell and died.

---
26.
tāṁ hatāṁ bhīmasaṅkāśāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā surapatistadā |
sādhu sādhviti kākutsthaṁ surāścāpyabhipūjayan ||

> **Meaning 26:** Seeing that terrifying creature slain, Indra, king of the
> devatās, and all the devatās praised Rāma, crying, "Well done! Well done!"

---
27.
uvāca paramaprītaḥ sahasrākṣaḥ purandaraḥ |
surāśca sarvē saṁhr̥ṣṭā viśvāmitramathābruvan ||

> **Meaning 27:** Overjoyed, the thousand-eyed Indra and all the delighted
> devatās then spoke to Viśvāmitra:

---
28.
munē kauśika bhadraṁ tē sēndrāḥ sarvē marudgaṇāḥ |
tōṣitāḥ karmaṇānēna snēhaṁ darśaya rāghavē |

> **Meaning 28:** "Sage Kauśika, may blessings be upon you! All the ranks of the
> Maruts, together with Indra, are deeply pleased with this deed. Show your
> affection to Rāghava!

---
29.
prajāpatēḥ kr̥śāśvasya putrānsatyaparākramān |
tapōbalabhr̥tō brahman rāghavāya nivēdaya ||

> **Meaning 29:** "Viśvāmitra, impart to Rāghava the divine astras known as the
> sons of Prajāpati Kr̥śāśva, weapons of unfailing prowess charged with the
> power of austerity.

---
30.
pātrabhūtaśca tē brahmaṁstavānugamanē rataḥ |
kartavyaṁ sumahatkarma surāṇāṁ rājasūnunā ||

> **Meaning 30:** "He is truly worthy of them, and devoted to
> following you. Moreover, this prince has a mighty task to accomplish for the
> devatās."

---
31.
ēvamuktvā surāḥ sarvē jagmurhr̥ṣṭā vihāyasam |
viśvāmitraṁ pūjayantastataḥ sandhyā pravartatē ||

> **Meaning 31:** Having spoken thus, all the devatās departed joyfully into the
> sky, honoring Viśvāmitra. Then twilight set in.

---
32.
tatō munivaraḥ prītastāṭakāvadhatōṣitaḥ |
mūrdhni rāmamupāghrāya idaṁ vacanamabravīt ||

> **Meaning 32:** Then the foremost of sages, delighted and satisfied by the
> slaying of Tāṭakā, kissed Rāma affectionately on the crown of his head and
> spoke these words:

---
33.
ihādya rajanīṁ rāma vasēma śubhadarśana |
śvaḥ prabhātē gamiṣyāmastadāśramapadaṁ mama ||

> **Meaning 33:** "Radiant Rāma, let us spend tonight right here. Tomorrow at
> dawn we will set out for my āśrama."

---
34.
viśvāmitravacaḥ śrutvā hr̥ṣṭō daśarathātmajaḥ |
uvāsa rajanīṁ tatra tāṭakāyā vanē sukham ||

> **Meaning 34:** Hearing Viśvāmitra's words, the son of Daśaratha was pleased
> and spent the night happily there in Tāṭakā's forest.

---
35.
muktaśāpaṁ vanaṁ tacca tasminnēva tadāhani |
ramaṇīyaṁ vibabhrāja yathā caitrarathaṁ vanam ||

> **Meaning 35:** Freed from its curse that very day, the forest shone with
> beauty, like Kubera's Caitraratha grove.

---
36.
nihatya tāṁ yakṣasutāṁ sa rāmaḥ |
praśasyamānaḥ surasiddhasaṅghaiḥ |
uvāsa tasminmuninā sahaiva |
prabhātavēlāṁ pratibōdhyamānaḥ ||

> **Meaning 36:** Having slain that daughter of the yakṣa, Rāma, praised by
> assemblies of devatās and siddhas, stayed there with the sage until
> Viśvāmitra awakened him at the break of dawn.

---
