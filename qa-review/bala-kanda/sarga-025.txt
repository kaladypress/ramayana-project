=== Sarga 25 ===

---
1.
muner vacanam aklībaṁ śrutvā naravarātmajaḥ |
rāghavaḥ prāñjalir bhūtvā pratyuvāca dr̥ḍhavrataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
pitur vacananirdeśāt pitur vacanagauravāt |
vacanaṁ kauśikasyeti kartavyam aviśaṅkayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
anuśiṣṭo ’smy ayodhyāyāṁ gurumadhye mahātmanā |
pitrā daśarathenāhaṁ nāvajñeyaṁ ca tad vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
so ’haṁ pitur vacaḥ śrutvā śāsanād brahma vādinaḥ |
kariṣyāmi na saṁdehas tāṭakāvadham uttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
gobrāhmaṇahitārthāya deśasyāsya sukhāya ca |
tava caivāprameyasya vacanaṁ kartum udyataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
evam uktvā dhanurmadhye baddhvā muṣṭim ariṁdamaḥ |
jyāśabdam akarot tīvraṁ diśaḥ śabdena pūrayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tena śabdena vitrastās tāṭakā vanavāsinaḥ |
tāṭakā ca susaṁkruddhā tena śabdena mohitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
taṁ śabdam abhinidhyāya rākṣasī krodhamūrchitā |
śrutvā cābhyadravad vegād yataḥ śabdo viniḥsr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rāghavaḥ kruddhāṁ vikr̥tāṁ vikr̥tānanām |
pramāṇenātivr̥ddhāṁ ca lakṣmaṇaṁ so ’bhyabhāṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
paśya lakṣmaṇa yakṣiṇyā bhairavaṁ dāruṇaṁ vapuḥ |
bhidyeran darśanād asyā bhīrūṇāṁ hr̥dayāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
enāṁ paśya durādharṣāṁ māyā balasamanvitām |
vinivr̥ttāṁ karomy adya hr̥takarṇāgranāsikām ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
na hy enām utsahe hantuṁ strīsvabhāvena rakṣitām |
vīryaṁ cāsyā gatiṁ cāpi haniṣyāmīti me matiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
evaṁ bruvāṇe rāme tu tāṭakā krodhamūrchitā |
udyamya bāhū garjantī rāmam evābhyadhāvata ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tām āpatantīṁ vegena vikrāntām aśanīm iva |
śareṇorasi vivyādha sā papāta mamāra ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tāṁ hatāṁ bhīmasaṁkāśāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā surapatis tadā |
sādhu sādhv iti kākutsthaṁ surāś ca samapūjayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
uvāca paramaprītaḥ sahasrākṣaḥ puraṁdaraḥ |
surāś ca sarve saṁhr̥ṣṭā viśvāmitram athābruvan ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
mune kauśike bhadraṁ te sendrāḥ sarve marudgaṇāḥ |
toṣitāḥ karmaṇānena snehaṁ darśaya rāghave ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
prajāpater kr̥śāśvasya putrān satyaparākramān |
tapobalabhr̥tān brahman rāghavāya nivedaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
pātrabhūtaś ca te brahmaṁs tavānugamane dhr̥taḥ |
kartavyaṁ ca mahat karma surāṇāṁ rājasūnunā ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
evam uktvā surāḥ sarve hr̥ṣṭā jagmur yathāgatam |
viśvāmitraṁ pūjayitvā tataḥ saṁdhyā pravartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tato munivaraḥ prītis tāṭakā vadhatoṣitaḥ |
mūrdhni rāmam upāghrāya idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
ihādya rajanīṁ rāma vasema śubhadarśana |
śvaḥ prabhāte gamiṣyāmas tad āśramapadaṁ mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
