=== Sarga 25 ===
The Origin and Curse of Tāṭakā; Viśvāmitra Urges Rāma to Slay Her

---
1.
atha tasyāpramēyasya munērvacanamuttamam |
śrutvā puruṣaśārdūlaḥ pratyuvāca śubhāṁ giram ||

> **Meaning 1:** Then, hearing the noble words of that immeasurable sage, the
> tiger among men (Rāma) spoke these gracious words in reply:

---
2.
alpavīryā yadā yakṣāḥ śrūyantē munipuṅgava |
kathaṁ nāgasahasrasya dhārayatyabalā balam ||

> **Meaning 2:** "Foremost of sages, since yakṣas are said to have little
> strength, how does a woman possess the strength of a thousand elephants?"

---
3.
ityuktaṃ vacanaṃ śrutvā rāghavasyāmitojasā |
harṣayan ślakṣṇayā vācā salakṣmaṇamarindamam ||

> **Meaning 3:** Hearing those words spoken by Rāghava, the sage of boundless
> energy cheered the subduer of enemies and Lakṣmaṇa with gentle speech.

---
4.
viśvāmitrō'bravīdvākyaṁ śr̥ṇu yēna balōttarā |
varadānakr̥taṁ vīryaṁ dhārayatyabalā balam ||

> **Meaning 4:** Viśvāmitra said, "Listen to how this woman became superior in
> power and holds the strength given by a boon:

---
5.
pūrvamāsīnmahāyakṣaḥ sukēturnāma vīryavān |
anapatyaḥ śubhācāraḥ sa ca tēpē mahattapaḥ ||

> **Meaning 5:** "Long ago there was a mighty, noble-natured great yakṣa named
> Suketu. Being childless, he performed severe austerities.

---
6.
pitāmahastu suprītastasya yakṣapatēstadā |
kanyāratnaṁ dadau rāma tāṭakāṁ nāma nāmataḥ ||

> **Meaning 6:** "Deeply pleased with that yakṣa chief, Pitāmaha (Brahmā) gave
> him a jewel of a daughter named Tāṭakā, Rāma.

---
7.
dadau nāgasahasrasya balaṁ cāsyāḥ pitāmahaḥ |
na tvēva putraṁ yakṣāya dadau brahmā mahāyaśāḥ ||

> **Meaning 7:** "Pitāmaha gave her the strength of a thousand elephants, yet
> the illustrious Brahmā did not grant the yakṣa a son.

---
8.
tāṁ tu jātāṁ vivardhantīṁ rūpayauvanaśālinīm |
jambhaputrāya sundāya dadau bhāryāṁ yaśasvinīm ||

> **Meaning 8:** "When she was born and grew up, endowed with beauty and youth,
> he gave that renowned daughter in marriage to Sunda, the son of Jambha.

---
9.
kasyacittvatha kālalsya yakṣī putraṁ vyajāyata |
mārīcaṁ nāma durdharṣaṁ yaḥ śāpādrākṣasō'bhavat ||

> **Meaning 9:** "After some time, the yakṣī gave birth to a fierce, invincible
> son named Mārīca, who later became a rākṣasa because of a curse.

---
10.
sundē tu nihatē rāma agastyamr̥ṣisattamam |
tāṭakā saha putrēṇa pradharṣayitumicchati ||

> **Meaning 10:** "When Sunda was slain by Agastya's curse, Rāma, Tāṭakā along
> with her son sought to attack that supreme sage.

---
11.
bhakṣārthaṃ jātasaṃrambhā garjantī sābhyadhāvata |
āpatantīṃ tu tāṃ dṛṣṭvā agastyo bhagavānṛṣiḥ ||

> **Meaning 11:** "Enraged and eager to devour him, she rushed at him roaring.
> Seeing her charging forward, the venerable sage Agastya—

---
12.
rākṣasatvaṁ bhajasvēti mārīcaṁ vyājahāra saḥ |
agastyaḥ paramakruddhastāṭakāmapi śaptavān ||

> **Meaning 12:** "declared to Mārīca, 'Become a rākṣasa!' And in his fierce
> anger, Agastya cursed Tāṭakā as well:

---
13.
puruṣādī mahāyakṣī virūpā vikr̥tānanā |
idaṁ rūpamapahāya dāruṇaṁ rūpamastu tē ||

> **Meaning 13:** "'You will become a man-eating giant yakṣī, ugly and deformed
> of face. Losing this form, may you take on a dreadful appearance!'

---
14.
saiṣā śāpakr̥tāmarṣā tāṭakā krōdhamūrchitā |
dēśamutsādayatyēnamagastyacaritaṁ śubham ||

> **Meaning 14:** "Driven mad with fury and resentment over that curse, this
> Tāṭakā has been laying waste this fair land where Agastya once lived.

---
15.
ēnāṁ rāghava durvr̥ttāṁ yakṣīṁ paramadāruṇām |
gōbrāhmaṇahitārthāya jahi duṣṭaparākramām ||

> **Meaning 15:** "Rāghava, for the welfare of cows and brāhmaṇas, slay this
> wicked, utterly dreadful yakṣī of evil prowess.

---
16.
na hyēnāṁ śāpasaṁsr̥ṣṭāṁ kaścidutsahatē pumān |
nihantuṁ triṣu lōkēṣu tvāmr̥tē raghunandana ||

> **Meaning 16:** "No man in all the three worlds dares to kill her, touched as
> she is by that curse, except you, Raghunandana.

---
17.
na hi tē strīvadhakr̥tē ghr̥ṇā kāryā narōttama |
cāturvarṇyahitārthāya kartavyaṁ rājasūnunā ||

> **Meaning 17:** "Best of men, you must feel no hesitation or pity about
> slaying a woman; a prince must act for the welfare of all four varṇas.

---
18.
nṛśaṃsamanṛśaṃsaṃ vā prajārakṣaṇakāraṇāt |
pātakaṃ vā sadoṣaṃ vā kartavyaṃ rakṣatā sadā ||

> **Meaning 18:** "Whether harsh or gentle, blameless or seeming-wrong, a
> protector must always do whatever is needed to safeguard the people.

---
19.
rājyabhāraniyuktānāmēṣa dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ |
adharmyāṁ jahi kākutstha dharmō hyasyā na vidyatē ||

> **Meaning 19:** "This is the eternal dharma for those entrusted with the
> burden of a kingdom. Slay this unrighteous creature, Kākutstha, for there is
> no dharma in her.

---
20.
śrūyatē hi purā śakrō virōcanasutāṁ nr̥pa |
pr̥thivīṁ hantumicchantīṁ mantharāmabhyasūdayat ||

> **Meaning 20:** "It is heard that long ago, prince, Śakra (Indra) slew
> Mantharā, the daughter of Virocana, when she sought to destroy the earth.

---
21.
viṣṇunā ca purā rāma bhr̥gupatnī dr̥ḍhavratā |
anindraṁ lōkamicchantī kāvyamātā niṣūditā ||

> **Meaning 21:** "And long ago, Rāma, Viṣṇu slew Bhṛgu's wife, Kāvya's
> (Śukrācārya's) mother, who was steadfast in her vow and sought to rid the
> world of Indra.

---
22.
ētaiścānyaiśca bahubhī rājaputramahātmabhiḥ |
adharmaniratā nāryō hatāḥ puruṣasattamaiḥ |
tasmādenāṃ ghṛṇāṃ tyaktvā jahi macchāsanānnṛpa ||

> **Meaning 22:** "Prince, these and many other great-souled, noble beings have
> slain women devoted to adharma. Therefore, cast aside your hesitation and
> slay her at my command."

---
