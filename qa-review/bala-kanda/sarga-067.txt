=== Sarga 67 ===

---
1.
janakena samādiṣṭā dūtās te klāntavāhanāḥ |
trirātram uṣitvā mārge te ’yodhyāṁ prāviśan purīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
te rājavacanād dūtā rājaveśmapraveśitāḥ |
dadr̥śur devasaṁkāśaṁ vr̥ddhaṁ daśarathaṁ nr̥pam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
baddhāñjalipuṭāḥ sarve dūtā vigatasādhvasāḥ |
rājānaṁ prayatā vākyam abruvan madhurākṣaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
maithilo janako rājā sāgnihotrapuraskr̥taḥ |
kuśalaṁ cāvyayaṁ caiva sopādhyāyapurohitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
muhur muhur madhurayā snehasaṁyuktayā girā |
janakas tvāṁ mahārāja pr̥cchate sapuraḥsaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
pr̥ṣṭvā kuśalam avyagraṁ vaideho mithilādhipaḥ |
kauśikānumate vākyaṁ bhavantam idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
pūrvaṁ pratijñā viditā vīryaśulkā mamātmajā |
rājānaś ca kr̥tāmarṣā nirvīryā vimukhīkr̥tāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
seyaṁ mama sutā rājan viśvāmitrapuraḥsaraiḥ |
yadr̥cchayāgatair vīrair nirjitā tava putrakaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tac ca rājan dhanur divyaṁ madhye bhagnaṁ mahātmanā |
rāmeṇa hi mahārāja mahatyāṁ janasaṁsadi ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
asmai deyā mayā sītā vīryaśulkā mahātmane |
pratijñāṁ tartum icchāmi tad anujñātum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sopādhyāyo mahārāja purohitapuraskr̥taḥ |
śīghram āgaccha bhadraṁ te draṣṭum arhasi rāghavau ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
prītiṁ ca mama rājendra nirvartayitum arhasi |
putrayor ubhayor eva prītiṁ tvam api lapsyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
evaṁ videhādhipatir madhuraṁ vākyam abravīt |
viśvāmitrābhyanujñātaḥ śatānandamate sthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
dūtavākyaṁ tu tac chrutvā rājā paramaharṣitaḥ |
vasiṣṭhaṁ vāmadevaṁ ca mantriṇo ’nyāṁś ca so ’bravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
guptaḥ kuśikaputreṇa kausalyānandavardhanaḥ |
lakṣmaṇena saha bhrātrā videheṣu vasaty asau ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
dr̥ṣṭavīryas tu kākutstho janakena mahātmanā |
saṁpradānaṁ sutāyās tu rāghave kartum icchati ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
yadi vo rocate vr̥ttaṁ janakasya mahātmanaḥ |
purīṁ gacchāmahe śīghraṁ mā bhūt kālasya paryayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
mantriṇo bāḍham ity āhuḥ saha sarvair maharṣibhiḥ |
suprītaś cābravīd rājā śvo yātreti sa mantriṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
mantriṇas tu narendrasya rātriṁ paramasatkr̥tāḥ |
ūṣuḥ pramuditāḥ sarve guṇaiḥ sarvaiḥ samanvitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
