=== Sarga 74 ===

---
1.
rāma dāśarathe vīra vīryaṁ te śrūyate ’dhutam |
dhanuṣo bhedanaṁ caiva nikhilena mayā śrutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tad adbhutam acintyaṁ ca bhedanaṁ dhanuṣas tvayā |
tac chrutvāham anuprāpto dhanur gr̥hyāparaṁ śubham ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tad idaṁ ghorasaṁkāśaṁ jāmadagnyaṁ mahad dhanuḥ |
pūrayasva śareṇaiva svabalaṁ darśayasva ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tad ahaṁ te balaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā dhanuṣo ’sya prapūraṇe |
dvandvayuddhaṁ pradāsyāmi vīryaślāghyam idaṁ tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā rājā daśarathas tadā |
viṣaṇṇavadano dīnaḥ prāñjalir vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
kṣatraroṣāt praśāntas tvaṁ brāhmaṇasya mahāyaśāḥ |
bālānāṁ mama putrāṇām abhayaṁ dātum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
bhārgavāṇāṁ kule jātaḥ svādhyāyavrataśālinām |
sahasrākṣe pratijñāya śastraṁ nikṣiptavān asi ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sa tvaṁ dharmaparo bhūtvā kāśyapāya vasuṁdharām |
dattvā vanam upāgamya mahendrakr̥taketanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
mama sarvavināśāya saṁprāptas tvaṁ mahāmune |
na caikasmin hate rāme sarve jīvāmahe vayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
bruvaty evaṁ daśarathe jāmadagnyaḥ pratāpavān |
anādr̥tyaiva tad vākyaṁ rāmam evābhyabhāṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
ime dve dhanuṣī śreṣṭhe divye lokābhiviśrute |
dr̥ḍhe balavatī mukhye sukr̥te viśvakarmaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
atisr̥ṣṭaṁ surair ekaṁ tryambakāya yuyutsave |
tripuraghnaṁ naraśreṣṭha bhagnaṁ kākutstha yat tvayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
idaṁ dvitīyaṁ durdharṣaṁ viṣṇor dattaṁ surottamaiḥ |
samānasāraṁ kākutstha raudreṇa dhanuṣā tv idam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tadā tu devatāḥ sarvāḥ pr̥cchanti sma pitāmaham |
śitikaṇṭhasya viṣṇoś ca balābalanirīkṣayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
abhiprāyaṁ tu vijñāya devatānāṁ pitāmahaḥ |
virodhaṁ janayām āsa tayoḥ satyavatāṁ varaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
virodhe ca mahad yuddham abhavad romaharṣaṇam |
śitikaṇṭhasya viṣṇoś ca parasparajayaiṣiṇoḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tadā taj jr̥mbhitaṁ śaivaṁ dhanur bhīmaparākramam |
huṁkāreṇa mahādevaḥ stambhito ’tha trilocanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
devais tadā samāgamya sarṣisaṁghaiḥ sacāraṇaiḥ |
yācitau praśamaṁ tatra jagmatus tau surottamau ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
jr̥mbhitaṁ tad dhanur dr̥ṣṭvā śaivaṁ viṣṇuparākramaiḥ |
adhikaṁ menire viṣṇuṁ devāḥ sarṣigaṇās tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
dhanū rudras tu saṁkruddho videheṣu mahāyaśāḥ |
devarātasya rājarṣer dadau haste sasāyakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
idaṁ ca viṣṇavaṁ rāma dhanuḥ parapuraṁjayam |
r̥cīke bhārgave prādād viṣṇuḥ sa nyāsam uttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
r̥cīkas tu mahātejāḥ putrasyāpratikarmaṇaḥ |
pitur mama dadau divyaṁ jamadagner mahātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
nyastaśastre pitari me tapobalasamanvite |
arjuno vidadhe mr̥tyuṁ prākr̥tāṁ buddhim āsthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
vadham apratirūpaṁ tu pituḥ śrutvā sudāruṇam |
kṣatram utsādayaṁ roṣāj jātaṁ jātam anekaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
pr̥thivīṁ cākhilāṁ prāpya kāśyapāya mahātmane |
yajñasyānte tadā rāma dakṣiṇāṁ puṇyakarmaṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
dattvā mahendranilayas tapobalasamanvitaḥ |
śrutavān dhanuṣo bhedaṁ tato ’haṁ drutam āgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tad idaṁ vaiṣṇavaṁ rāma pitr̥paitāmahaṁ mahat |
kṣatradharmaṁ puraskr̥tya gr̥hṇīṣva dhanuruttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
yojayasva dhanuḥ śreṣṭhe śaraṁ parapuraṁjayam |
yadi śaknoṣi kākutstha dvandvaṁ dāsyāmi te tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
