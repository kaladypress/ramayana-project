=== Sarga 40 ===

---
1.
putrāṁś ciragatāñ jñātvā sagaro raghunandana |
naptāram abravīd rājā dīpyamānaṁ svatejasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
śūraś ca kr̥tavidyaś ca pūrvais tulyo ’si tejasā |
pitr̥̄ṇāṁ gatim anviccha yena cāśvo ’pahāritaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
antarbhaumāni sattvāni vīryavanti mahānti ca |
teṣāṁ tvaṁ pratighātārthaṁ sāsiṁ gr̥hṇīṣva kārmukam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
abhivādyābhivādyāṁs tvaṁ hatvā vighnakarān api |
siddhārthaḥ saṁnivartasva mama yajñasya pāragaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
evam ukto ’ṁśumān samyak sagareṇa mahātmanā |
dhanur ādāya khaḍgaṁ ca jagāma laghuvikramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
sa khātaṁ pitr̥bhir mārgam antarbhaumaṁ mahātmabhiḥ |
prāpadyata naraśreṣṭha tena rājñābhicoditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
daityadānavarakṣobhiḥ piśācapatagoragaiḥ |
pūjyamānaṁ mahātejā diśāgajam apaśyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sa taṁ pradakṣiṇaṁ kr̥tvā pr̥ṣṭvā caiva nirāmayam |
pitr̥̄n sa paripapraccha vājihartāram eva ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
diśāgajas tu tac chrutvā prītyāhāṁśumato vacaḥ |
āsamañjakr̥tārthas tvaṁ sahāśvaḥ śīghram eṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā sarvān eva diśāgajān |
yathākramaṁ yathānyāyaṁ praṣṭuṁ samupacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
taiś ca sarvair diśāpālair vākyajñair vākyakovidaiḥ |
pūjitaḥ sahayaś caiva gantāsīty abhicoditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
teṣāṁ tad vacanaṁ śrutvā jagāma laghuvikramaḥ |
bhasmarāśīkr̥tā yatra pitaras tasya sāgarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sa duḥkhavaśam āpannas tv asamañjasutas tadā |
cukrośa paramārtas tu vadhāt teṣāṁ suduḥkhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
yajñiyaṁ ca hayaṁ tatra carantam avidūrataḥ |
dadarśa puruṣavyāghro duḥkhaśokasamanvitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
dadarśa puruṣavyāghro kartukāmo jalakriyām |
salilārthī mahātejā na cāpaśyaj jalāśayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
visārya nipuṇāṁ dr̥ṣṭiṁ tato ’paśyat khagādhipam |
pitr̥̄ṇāṁ mātulaṁ rāma suparṇam anilopamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
sa cainam abravīd vākyaṁ vainateyo mahābalaḥ |
mā śucaḥ puruṣavyāghra vadho ’yaṁ lokasaṁmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
kapilenāprameyena dagdhā hīme mahābalāḥ |
salilaṁ nārhasi prājña dātum eṣāṁ hi laukikam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
gaṅgā himavato jyeṣṭhā duhitā puruṣarṣabha |
bhasmarāśīkr̥tān etān pāvayel lokapāvanī ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tayā klinnam idaṁ bhasma gaṅgayā lokakāntayā |
ṣaṣṭiṁ putrasahasrāṇi svargalokaṁ nayiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
gaccha cāśvaṁ mahābhāga saṁgr̥hya puruṣarṣabha |
yajñaṁ paitāmahaṁ vīra nirvartayitum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
suparṇavacanaṁ śrutvā so ’ṁśumān ativīryavān |
tvaritaṁ hayam ādāya punar āyān mahāyaśāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tato rājānam āsādya dīkṣitaṁ raghunandana |
nyavedayad yathāvr̥ttaṁ suparṇavacanaṁ tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tac chrutvā ghorasaṁkāśaṁ vākyam aṁśumato nr̥paḥ |
yajñaṁ nirvartayām āsa yathākalpaṁ yathāvidhi ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
svapuraṁ cāgamac chrīmān iṣṭayajño mahīpatiḥ |
gaṅgāyāś cāgame rājā niścayaṁ nādhyagacchata ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
agatvā niścayaṁ rājā kālena mahatā mahān |
triṁśadvarṣasahasrāṇi rājyaṁ kr̥tvā divaṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
