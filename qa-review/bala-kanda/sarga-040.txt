=== Sarga 40 ===
Sagara's Sons Reduced to Ashes by Kapila

---
1.
dēvatānāṁ vacaḥ śrutvā bhagavānvai pitāmahaḥ |
pratyuvāca susantrastānkr̥tāntabalamōhitān ||

> **Meaning 1:** "Hearing the devatās' words, the revered Brahmā replied to
> them—terrified as they were and bewildered by the power of destiny:

---
2.
yasyēyaṁ vasudhā kr̥tsnā vāsudēvasya dhīmataḥ |
kāpilaṁ rūpamāsthāya dhārayatyaniśaṁ dharām ||

> **Meaning 2:** "'The wise Śrī Mahāviṣṇu, to whom this entire earth belongs,
> has assumed the form of Kapila and unceasingly upholds the world.

---
3.
pr̥thivyāścāpi nirbhēdō dr̥ṣṭa ēva sanātanaḥ |
sagarasya ca putrāṇāṁ vināśō'dīrghajīvinām ||

> **Meaning 3:** "'This cleaving of the earth was ordained from eternity, and so
> too was the destruction of Sagara's short-lived sons.'

---
4.
pitāmahavacaḥ śrutvā trayastriṁśadarindamaḥ |
dēvāḥ paramasaṁhr̥ṣṭāḥ punarjagmuryathāgatam ||

> **Meaning 4:** "Rāma, hearing Brahmā's words, the thirty-three devatās were
> overjoyed and returned to their own abodes.

---
5.
sagarasya ca putrāṇāṁ prādurāsīnmahātmanām |
pr̥thivyāṁ bhidyamānāyāṁ nirghātasamanisvanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 5:** "Meanwhile, as Sagara's noble-souled sons tore open the
> earth, an ominous roar like a thunderclap arose.

---
6.
tatō bhittvā mahīṁ sarvāṁ kr̥tvā cāpi pradakṣiṇam |
sahitāḥ sagarāḥ sarvē pitaraṁ vākyamabruvan ||

> **Meaning 6:** "After digging through the entire earth and circling it
> clockwise, all the sons of Sagara gathered together and told their father:

---
7.
parikrāntā mahī sarvā sattvavantaśca sūditāḥ |
dēvadānavarakṣāṁsi piśācōragakinnarāḥ ||

> **Meaning 7:** "'We have scoured the entire earth and slain its mighty
> beings—devatās, Dānavas, Rākṣasas, Piśācas, Uragas, and Kinnaras.

---
8.
na ca paśyāmahē'śvaṁ tamaśvahartāramēva ca |
kiṁ kariṣyāma bhadraṁ tē buddhiratra vicāryatām ||

> **Meaning 8:** "'Yet we still cannot find the horse, nor the thief who stole
> it. What should we do now? Blessings upon you—please tell us what you advise.'

---
9.
tēṣāṁ tadvacanaṁ śrutvā putrāṇāṁ rājasattamaḥ |
samanyurabravīdvākyaṁ sagarō raghunandana ||

> **Meaning 9:** "Raghunandana, hearing his sons' report, Sagara—noblest of
> kings—replied in anger:

---
10.
bhūyaḥ khanata bhadraṁ vō nirbhidya vasudhātalam |
aśvahartāramāsādya kr̥tārthāśca nivartatha ||

> **Meaning 10:** "'Dig again—blessings upon you—and break deep into the earth!
> Return only when you have found the horse-thief and accomplished your task.'

---
11.
piturvacanamāsthāya sagarasya mahātmanaḥ |
ṣaṣṭiḥ putrasahasrāṇi rasātalamabhidravan ||

> **Meaning 11:** "Obeying the command of their noble-souled father Sagara, the
> sixty thousand sons plunged back down into Rasātala.

---
12.
khanyamānē tatastasmindadr̥śuḥ parvatōpamam |
diśāgajaṁ virūpākṣaṁ dhārayantaṁ mahītalam ||

> **Meaning 12:** "As they dug there, they saw Virūpākṣa, the guardian
> elephant of that direction, huge as a mountain, upholding the earth.

---
13.
saparvatavanāṁ kr̥tsnāṁ pr̥thivīṁ raghunandana |
śirasā dhārayāmāsa virūpākṣō mahāgajaḥ ||

> **Meaning 13:** "Raghunandana, that great elephant Virūpākṣa bore the entire
> earth—with all its mountains and forests—upon his head.

---
14.
yadā parvaṇi kākutstha viśramārthaṁ mahāgajaḥ |
khēdāccālayatē śīrṣaṁ bhūmikampastadhā bhavēt ||

> **Meaning 14:** "Rāma, whenever that great elephant shifts his head out of
> weariness to rest on parvan days, an earthquake occurs.

---
15.
taṁ tē pradakṣiṇaṁ kr̥tvā diśāpālaṁ mahāgajam |
mānayantō hi tē rāma jagmurbhittvā rasātalam ||

> **Meaning 15:** "Rāma, walking clockwise around that great guardian elephant
> and paying him due homage, they dug onward through Rasātala.

---
16.
tataḥ pūrvāṁ diśaṁ bhittvā dakṣiṇāṁ bibhiduḥ punaḥ |
dakṣiṇasyāmapi diśi dadr̥śustē mahāgajam ||

> **Meaning 16:** "Having excavated the east, they dug into the south next, and
> there too they saw a great elephant—

---
17.
mahāpadmaṁ mahātmānaṁ sumahāparvatōpamam |
śirasā dhārayantaṁ tē vismayaṁ jagmuruttamam ||

> **Meaning 17:** "the noble Mahāpadma, vast as a towering mountain, bearing the
> earth upon his head; and they were filled with deep wonder.

---
18.
tataḥ pradakṣiṇaṁ kr̥tvā sagarasya mahātmanaḥ |
ṣaṣṭiḥ putrasahasrāṇi paścimāṁ bibhidurdiśam ||

> **Meaning 18:** "Circling him clockwise, the sixty thousand sons of
> noble-souled Sagara then dug into the west.

---
19.
paścimāyāmapi diśi mahāntamacalōpamam |
diśāgajaṁ saumanasaṁ dadr̥śustē mahābalāḥ ||

> **Meaning 19:** "In the west as well, those mighty princes saw Saumanasa, the
> guardian elephant of that direction, huge as a mountain.

---
20.
taṁ tē pradakṣiṇaṁ kr̥tvā pr̥ṣṭvā cāpi nirāmayam |
khanantaḥ samupakrāntā diśaṁ sōmavatīṁ tadā ||

> **Meaning 20:** "Circling him clockwise and asking after his well-being, they
> dug onward and entered the north.

---
21.
uttarasyāṁ raghuśrēṣṭha dadr̥śurhimapāṇḍuram |
bhadraṁ bhadrēṇa vapuṣā dhārayantaṁ mahīmimām ||

> **Meaning 21:** "Rāma, in the north they saw Bhadra—white as snow—upholding
> this earth with his auspicious form.

---
22.
samālabhya tataḥ sarvē kr̥tvā cainaṁ pradakṣiṇam |
ṣaṣṭiḥ putrasahasrāṇi bibhidurvasudhātalam ||

> **Meaning 22:** "Touching him with reverence and circling him clockwise, all
> sixty thousand sons dug deeper into the earth.

---
23.
tataḥ prāguttarāṁ gatvā sāgarāḥ prathitāṁ diśam |
rōṣādabhyakhanansarvē pr̥thivīṁ sagarātmajāḥ ||

> **Meaning 23:** "Then, moving toward the famed northeast, all the sons of
> Sagara dug furiously into the earth.

---
24.
tē tu sarvē mahātmānō bhīmavēgā mahābalāḥ |
dadr̥śuḥ kapilaṁ tatra vāsudēvaṁ sanātanam ||

> **Meaning 24:** "There, all those noble-souled princes, fierce in speed and
> immense in strength, saw Kapila—the eternal Vāsudeva.

---
25.
hayaṁ ca tasya dēvasya carantamavidūrataḥ |
praharṣamatulaṁ prāptāḥ sarvē tē raghunandana ||

> **Meaning 25:** "And seeing the horse grazing not far from that divine being,
> Rāma, they were all filled with matchless joy.

---
26.
tē taṁ yajñahanaṁ jñātvā krōdhaparyākulēkṣaṇāḥ ||

> **Meaning 26:** "Taking him to be the destroyer of their yajña, their eyes
> wild with rage,

---
27.
khanitralāṅgaladharā nānāvṛkṣaśilādharāḥ |
abhyadhāvanta saṅkruddhāstiṣṭha tiṣṭhēti cābruvan ||

> **Meaning 27:** "armed with spades and plows, and seizing trees and boulders
> of all kinds, they charged at him in fury, shouting, 'Stand your ground!
> Stop!'

---
28.
asmākaṁ tvaṁ hi turagaṁ yajñiyaṁ hr̥tavānasi |
durmēdhastvaṁ hi samprāptānviddhi naḥ sagarātmajān ||

> **Meaning 28:** "'You are the one who stole our yajña horse, you fool! Know
> that we, the sons of Sagara, have come for you!'

---
29.
śrutvā tadvacanaṁ tēṣāṁ kapilō raghunandana |
rōṣēṇa mahatāviṣṭō huṅkāramakarōttadā ||

> **Meaning 29:** "Raghunandana, hearing their words, Kapila was gripped by
> tremendous wrath and let out a thunderous huṅkāra.

---
30.
tatastēnāpramēyēna kapilēna mahātmanā |
bhasmarāśīkr̥tāḥ sarvē kākutstha sagarātmajāḥ ||

> **Meaning 30:** "Rāma, at once all the sons of Sagara were reduced to heaps of
> ash by that immeasurable, great Kapila.

---
