=== Sarga 58 ===

---
1.
uktavākyaṁ tu rājānaṁ kr̥payā kuśikātmajaḥ |
abravīn madhuraṁ vākyaṁ sākṣāc caṇḍālarūpiṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
ikṣvāko svāgataṁ vatsa jānāmi tvāṁ sudhārmikam |
śaraṇaṁ te bhaviṣyāmi mā bhaiṣīr nr̥papuṁgava ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
aham āmantraye sarvān maharṣīn puṇyakarmaṇaḥ |
yajñasāhyakarān rājaṁs tato yakṣyasi nirvr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
guruśāpakr̥taṁ rūpaṁ yad idaṁ tvayi vartate |
anena saha rūpeṇa saśarīro gamiṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
hastaprāptam ahaṁ manye svargaṁ tava nareśvara |
yas tvaṁ kauśikam āgamya śaraṇyaṁ śaraṇaṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
evam uktvā mahātejāḥ putrān paramadhārmikān |
vyādideśa mahāprājñān yajñasaṁbhārakāraṇāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sarvāñ śiṣyān samāhūya vākyam etad uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sarvān r̥ṣivarān vatsā ānayadhvaṁ mamājñayā |
saśiṣyān suhr̥daś caiva sartvijaḥ subahuśrutān ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
yad anyo vacanaṁ brūyān madvākyabalacoditaḥ |
tat sarvam akhilenoktaṁ mamākhyeyam anādr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā diśo jagmus tadājñayā |
ājagmur atha deśebhyaḥ sarvebhyo brahmavādinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
te ca śiṣyāḥ samāgamya muniṁ jvalitatejasam |
ūcuś ca vacanaṁ sarve sarveṣāṁ brahmavādinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
śrutvā te vacanaṁ sarve samāyānti dvijātayaḥ |
sarvadeśeṣu cāgacchan varjayitvā mahodayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
vāsiṣṭhaṁ tac chataṁ sarvaṁ krodhaparyākulākṣaram |
yad āha vacanaṁ sarvaṁ śr̥ṇu tvaṁ munipuṁgava ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
kṣatriyo yājako yasya caṇḍālasya viśeṣataḥ |
kathaṁ sadasi bhoktāro havis tasya surarṣayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
brāhmaṇā vā mahātmāno bhuktvā caṇḍālabhojanam |
kathaṁ svargaṁ gamiṣyanti viśvāmitreṇa pālitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
etad vacanaṁ naiṣṭhuryam ūcuḥ saṁraktalocanāḥ |
vāsiṣṭhā muniśārdūla sarve te samahodayāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
teṣāṁ tad vacanaṁ śrutvā sarveṣāṁ munipuṁgavaḥ |
krodhasaṁraktanayanaḥ saroṣam idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
yad dūṣayanty aduṣṭaṁ māṁ tapa ugraṁ samāsthitam |
bhasmībhūtā durātmāno bhaviṣyanti na saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
adya te kālapāśena nītā vaivasvatakṣayam |
saptajātiśatāny eva mr̥tapāḥ santu sarvaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
śvamāṁsaniyatāhārā muṣṭikā nāma nirghr̥ṇāḥ |
vikr̥tāś ca virūpāś ca lokān anucarantv imān ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
mahodayaś ca durbuddhir mām adūṣyaṁ hy adūṣayat |
dūṣiṭaḥ sarvalokeṣu niṣādatvaṁ gamiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
prāṇātipātanirato niranukrośatāṁ gataḥ |
dīrghakālaṁ mama krodhād durgatiṁ vartayiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
etāvad uktvā vacanaṁ viśvāmitro mahātapāḥ |
virarāma mahātejā r̥ṣimadhye mahāmuniḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
