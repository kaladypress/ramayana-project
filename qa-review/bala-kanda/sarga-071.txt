=== Sarga 71 ===

---
1.
tam uktavantaṁ vaidehaṁ viśvāmitro mahāmuniḥ |
uvāca vacanaṁ vīraṁ vasiṣṭhasahito nr̥pam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
acintyāny aprameyāni kulāni narapuṁgava |
ikṣvākūṇāṁ videhānāṁ naiṣāṁ tulyo ’sti kaś cana ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sadr̥śo dharmasaṁbandhaḥ sadr̥śo rūpasaṁpadā |
rāmalakṣmaṇayo rājan sītā cormilayā saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
vaktavyaṁ ca naraśreṣṭha śrūyatāṁ vacanaṁ mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
bhrātā yavīyān dharmajña eṣa rājā kuśadhvajaḥ |
asya dharmātmano rājan rūpeṇāpratimaṁ bhuvi |
sutādvayaṁ naraśreṣṭha patnyarthaṁ varayāmahe ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
bharatasya kumārasya śatrughnasya ca dhīmataḥ |
varayema sute rājaṁs tayor arthe mahātmanoḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
putrā daśarathasyeme rūpayauvanaśālinaḥ |
lokapālopamāḥ sarve devatulyaparākramāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
ubhayor api rājendra saṁbandhenānubadhyatām |
ikṣvākukulam avyagraṁ bhavataḥ puṇyakarmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
viśvāmitravacaḥ śrutvā vasiṣṭhasya mate tadā |
janakaḥ prāñjalir vākyam uvāca munipuṁgavau ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
sadr̥śaṁ kulasaṁbandhaṁ yad ājñāpayathaḥ svayam |
evaṁ bhavatu bhadraṁ vaḥ kuśadhvajasute ime |
patnyau bhajetāṁ sahitau śatrughnabharatāv ubhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
ekāhnā rājaputrīṇāṁ catasr̥ṇāṁ mahāmune |
pāṇīn gr̥hṇantu catvāro rājaputrā mahābalāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
uttare divase brahman phalgunībhyāṁ manīṣiṇaḥ |
vaivāhikaṁ praśaṁsanti bhago yatra prajāpatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
evam uktvā vacaḥ saumyaṁ pratyutthāya kr̥tāñjaliḥ |
ubhau munivarau rājā janako vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
paro dharmaḥ kr̥to mahyaṁ śiṣyo ’smi bhavatoḥ sadā |
imāny āsanamukhyāni āsetāṁ munipuṁgavau ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
yathā daśarathasyeyaṁ tathāyodhyā purī mama |
prabhutve nāsit saṁdeho yathārhaṁ kartum arhathaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tathā bruvati vaidehe janake raghunandanaḥ |
rājā daśaratho hr̥ṣṭaḥ pratyuvāca mahīpatim ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
yuvām asaṁkhyeya guṇau bhrātarau mithileśvarau |
r̥ṣayo rājasaṁghāś ca bhavadbhyām abhipūjitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
svasti prāpnuhi bhadraṁ te gamiṣyāmi svam ālayam |
śrāddhakarmāṇi sarvāṇi vidhāsya iti cābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tam āpr̥ṣṭvā narapatiṁ rājā daśarathas tadā |
munīndrau tau puraskr̥tya jagāmāśu mahāyaśāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
sa gatvā nilayaṁ rājā śrāddhaṁ kr̥tvā vidhānataḥ |
prabhāte kālyam utthāya cakre godānam uttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
gavāṁ śatasahasrāṇi brāhmaṇebhyo narādhipaḥ |
ekaikaśo dadau rājā putrān uddhiśya dharmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
suvarṇaśr̥ṅgāḥ saṁpannāḥ savatsāḥ kāṁsyadohanāḥ |
gavāṁ śatasahasrāṇi catvāri puruṣarṣabhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
vittam anyac ca subahu dvijebhyo raghunandanaḥ |
dadau godānam uddiśya putrāṇāṁ putravatsalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
sa sutaiḥ kr̥tagodānair vr̥taś ca nr̥patis tadā |
lokapālair ivābhāti vr̥taḥ saumyaḥ prajāpatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
