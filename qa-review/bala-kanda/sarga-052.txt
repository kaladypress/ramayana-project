=== Sarga 52 ===
Vasiṣṭha Welcomes Viśvāmitra; Śabalā Summoned

---
1.
sa dr̥ṣṭvā paramaprītō viśvāmitrō mahābalaḥ |
praṇatō vinayādvīrō vasiṣṭhaṁ japatāṁ varam ||

> **Meaning 1:** Seeing the āśrama, the mighty and valiant Viśvāmitra was
> overjoyed and bowed in humility before Vasiṣṭha, the foremost of sages
> devoted to japa.

---
2.
svāgataṁ tava cētyuktō vasiṣṭhēna mahātmanā |
āsanaṁ cāsya bhagavānvasiṣṭhō vyādidēśa ha ||

> **Meaning 2:** "Welcome to you," said the great-souled Vasiṣṭha, and the
> revered sage offered him a seat.

---
3.
upaviṣṭāya ca tadā viśvāmitrāya dhīmatē |
yathānyāyaṁ munivaraḥ phalamūlamupāharat ||

> **Meaning 3:** When the wise Viśvāmitra had taken his seat, the foremost of
> munis offered him fruits and roots in the customary manner.

---
4.
pratigr̥hya ca tāṁ pūjāṁ vasiṣṭhādrājasattamaḥ |
tapō'gnihōtraśiṣyēṣu kuśalaṁ paryapr̥cchata ||

> **Meaning 4:** Accepting that honor from Vasiṣṭha, the finest of kings
> inquired whether all was well with his tapas, his agnihotra fire, and his
> disciples.

---
5.
viśvāmitrō mahātējā vanaspatigaṇē tathā |
sarvatra kuśalaṁ cāha vasiṣṭhō rājasattamam ||

> **Meaning 5:** The illustrious Viśvāmitra also asked after the trees of the
> grove, and Vasiṣṭha told the noble king that all was well everywhere.

---
6.
sukhōpaviṣṭaṁ rājānaṁ viśvāmitraṁ mahātapāḥ |
papraccha japatāṁ śrēṣṭhō vasiṣṭhō brahmaṇaḥ sutaḥ ||

> **Meaning 6:** While King Viśvāmitra was comfortably seated, the great
> ascetic Vasiṣṭha, son of Brahmā and foremost of sages devoted to japa, asked
> him:

---
7.
kaccittē kuśalaṁ rājankacciddharmēṇa rañjayan |
prajāḥ pālayasē rājanrājavr̥ttēna dhārmika ||

> **Meaning 7:** "'Righteous king, is all well with you? Are you gladdening your
> people through dharma and protecting them in accordance with the duties of a
> king?

---
8.
kaccittē subhr̥tā bhr̥tyāḥ kaccittiṣṭhanti śāsanē |
kaccittē vijitāḥ sarvē ripavō ripusūdana ||

> **Meaning 8:** "'Destroyer of foes, are your servants well supported, do they
> obey your commands, and have all your enemies been conquered?

---
9.
kaccidbalē ca kōśē ca mitrēṣu ca parantapa |
kuśalaṁ tē naravyāghra putrapautrē tathānagha ||

> **Meaning 9:** "'Foremost of men, blameless scorcher of foes, is all well with
> your army, your treasury, your allies, and your sons and grandsons?'"

---
10.
sarvatra kuśalaṁ rājā vasiṣṭhaṁ pratyudāharat |
viśvāmitrō mahātējā vasiṣṭhaṁ vinayānvitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 10:** The glorious King Viśvāmitra respectfully replied to Vasiṣṭha
> that all was well everywhere.

---
11.
kr̥tvōbhau suciraṁ kālaṁ dharmiṣṭhau tāḥ kathāḥ śubhāḥ |
mudā paramayā yuktau prīyētāṁ tau parasparam ||

> **Meaning 11:** After those two supremely righteous men had conversed for a
> long time on auspicious topics, they were filled with the highest joy and
> delighted in each other's company.

---
12.
tatō vasiṣṭhō bhagavānkathāntē raghunandana |
viśvāmitramidaṁ vākyamuvāca prahasanniva ||

> **Meaning 12:** "Raghunandana, at the close of their conversation the revered
> Vasiṣṭha smiled and spoke these words to Viśvāmitra:

---
13.
ātithyaṁ kartumicchāmi balasyāsya mahābala |
tava caivāpramēyasya yathārhaṁ sampratīccha mē ||

> **Meaning 13:** "'Mighty king, I wish to offer hospitality to this army of
> yours as well as to you, whose greatness is immeasurable; please accept it
> from me as befits you.

---
14.
satkriyāṁ tu bhavānētāṁ pratīcchatu mayōdyatām |
rājaṁstvamatithiśrēṣṭhaḥ pūjanīyaḥ prayatnataḥ ||

> **Meaning 14:** "'King, you are the foremost of guests and must be honored
> with every effort; please accept this hospitality that I am ready to offer.'

---
15.
ēvamuktō vasiṣṭhēna viśvāmitrō mahāmatiḥ |
kr̥tamityabravīdrājā pūjāvākyēna mē tvayā ||

> **Meaning 15:** "Addressed thus by Vasiṣṭha, the wise King Viśvāmitra replied:
> 'By your kind words of welcome alone, you have already honored me.

---
16.
phalamūlēna bhagavanvidyatē yattavāśramē |
pādyēnācamanīyēna bhagavaddarśanēna ca ||

> **Meaning 16:** "'Revered sage, with the fruits and roots of your āśrama,
> with pādya and ācamanīya, and by having your darśana,

---
17.
sarvathā ca mahāprājña pūjārhēṇa supūjitaḥ |
gamiṣyāmi namastē'stu maitrēṇēkṣasva cakṣuṣā ||

> **Meaning 17:** "'Sage of great wisdom, I have been thoroughly honored in
> every way by you who are yourself worthy of all honor; I will now take my
> leave—salutations to you, and please look upon me with a friendly eye.'

---
18.
ēvaṁ bruvantaṁ rājānaṁ vasiṣṭhaḥ punarēva hi |
nyamantrayata dharmātmā punaḥ punarudāradhīḥ ||

> **Meaning 18:** "Even as the king spoke thus, the righteous, generous-minded
> Vasiṣṭha pressed his invitation again and again.

---
19.
bāḍhamityēva gādhēyō vasiṣṭhaṁ pratyuvāca ha |
yathā priyaṁ bhagavatastathāstu munisattama ||

> **Meaning 19:** "The son of Gādhi replied to Vasiṣṭha: 'So be it! Foremost of
> munis, let it be as you wish, revered one.'

---
20.
ēvamuktō mahātējā vasiṣṭhō japatāṁ varaḥ |
ājuhāva tataḥ prītaḥ kalmāṣīṁ dhūtakalmaṣaḥ ||

> **Meaning 20:** "At these words, the deeply radiant Vasiṣṭha, foremost of
> sages devoted to japa and free of all blemish, gladly called for his dappled
> cow:

---
21.
ēhyēhi śabalē kṣipraṁ śr̥ṇu cāpi vacō mama |
sabalasyāsya rājarṣēḥ kartuṁ vyavasitō'smyaham ||

> **Meaning 21:** "'Come, Śabalā, come quickly and hear my words! I have
> resolved to host this rājarṣi together with his entire army.

---
22.
bhōjanēna mahārhēṇa satkāraṁ saṁvidhatsva mē |
yasya yasya yathākāmaṁ ṣaḍrasēṣvabhipūjitam ||

> **Meaning 22:** "'On my behalf, prepare a grand welcome with a lavish feast,
> serving each person whatever finest dishes of every flavor they desire.

---
23.
tatsarvaṁ kāmadhugdivyē abhivarṣakr̥tē mama |
rasēnānnēna pānēna lēhyacōṣyēṇa saṁyutam |
annānāṁ nicayaṁ sarvaṁ sr̥jasva śabalē tvara ||

> **Meaning 23:** "'Divine wish-fulfilling Śabalā, pour all of that forth for
> my sake! Make haste and produce an abundance of every kind of food—cooked
> dishes, savory juices, drinks, confections, and succulent delicacies!'"

---
