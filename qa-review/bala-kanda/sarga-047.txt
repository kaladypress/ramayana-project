=== Sarga 47 ===
Origin of the Maruts; Sumati, King of Viśālā, Welcomes Viśvāmitra

---
1.
saptadhā tu kr̥tē garbhē ditiḥ paramaduḥkhitā |
sahasrākṣaṁ durādharṣaṁ vākyaṁ sānunayābravīt ||

> **Meaning 1:** "When her embryo had been split into seven parts, Diti, though
> deeply grieved, spoke gently to the unconquerable thousand-eyed Indra:

---
2.
mamāparādhādgarbhō'yaṁ saptadhā viphalīkr̥taḥ |
nāparādhō'sti dēvēśa tavātra balasūdana ||

> **Meaning 2:** "'Ruler of the devatās, slayer of Bala, it is through my own
> fault that this embryo was split into seven parts and rendered fruitless;
> there is no fault of yours in this.

---
3.
priyaṁ tu kr̥tamicchāmi mama garbhaviparyayē |
marutāṁ saptaṁ saptānāṁ sthānapālā bhavantvimē ||

> **Meaning 3:** "'Yet now that this has happened to my unborn child, I wish
> you would grant me one favor: let these seven become the guardians of the
> seven realms of the Maruts.

---
4.
vātaskandhā imē sapta carantu divi putrakāḥ |
mārutā iti vikhyātā divyarūpā mamātmajāḥ ||

> **Meaning 4:** "'Let these seven sons of mine, of divine form, course
> through the heavens as the seven divisions of the wind, renowned as the
> Mārutas.

---
5.
brahmalōkaṁ caratvēka indralōkaṁ tathāparaḥ |
divi vāyuriti khyātastr̥tīyō'pi mahāyaśāḥ ||

> **Meaning 5:** "'Let one move through Brahmaloka and another through
> Indraloka, while the third, of great renown, moves through the sky, famed as
> Vāyu.

---
6.
catvārastu suraśrēṣṭha diśō vai tava śāsanāt |
sañcariṣyanti bhadraṁ tē dēvabhūtā mamātmajāḥ |
tvatkr̥tēnaiva nāmnā ca mārutā iti viśrutāḥ ||

> **Meaning 6:** "'Foremost of the suras, may all be well with you; let the
> remaining four of my sons roam the four directions as devatās under your
> command, renowned as the Mārutas by the very name born of your words (mā
> ruda).'

---
7.
tasyāstadvacanaṁ śrutvā sahasrākṣaḥ purandaraḥ |
uvāca prāñjalirvākyaṁ ditiṁ balaniṣūdanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 7:** "Hearing those words of hers, the thousand-eyed Purandara,
> slayer of Bala, joined his palms in añjali and spoke to Diti:

---
8.
sarvamētadyathōktaṁ tē bhaviṣyati na saṁśayaḥ |
vicariṣyanti bhadraṁ tē dēvabhūtāstavātmajāḥ ||

> **Meaning 8:** "'All of this will come to pass just as you have said, without
> a doubt. May all be well with you; your sons will roam freely as devatās.'

---
9.
ēvaṁ tau niścayaṁ kr̥tvā mātāputrau tapōvanē |
jagmatustridivaṁ rāma kr̥tārthāviti naḥ śrutam ||

> **Meaning 9:** "Rāma, having reached this agreement in that tapas-grove,
> mother and son both departed for svarga with their aims fulfilled, as we have
> heard.

---
10.
ēṣa dēśaḥ sa kākutstha mahēndrādhyuṣitaḥ purā |
ditiṁ yatra tapaḥ siddhāmēvaṁ paricacāra saḥ ||

> **Meaning 10:** "Kākutstha, this is that very region where Mahendra stayed
> long ago, and where he attended in this way upon Diti as she perfected her
> tapas.

---
11.
ikṣvākōstu naravyāghra putraḥ paramadhārmikaḥ |
alambuṣāyāmutpannō viśāla iti viśrutaḥ ||

> **Meaning 11:** "Foremost of men, King Ikṣvāku had a supremely righteous son
> by his wife Alambuṣā, famed as Viśāla.

---
12.
tēna cāsīdiha sthānē viśālēti purī kr̥tā ||

> **Meaning 12:** "And it was he who founded this city of Viśālā right here.

---
13.
viśālasya sutō rāma hēmacandrō mahābalaḥ |
sucandra iti vikhyātō hēmacandrādanantaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 13:** "Rāma, Viśāla's son was the mighty Hemacandra, and after
> Hemacandra came his son, famed as Sucandra.

---
14.
sucandratanayō rāma dhūmrāśva iti viśrutaḥ |
dhūmrāśvatanayaścāpi sr̥ñjayaḥ samapadyata ||

> **Meaning 14:** "Rāma, Sucandra's son was renowned as Dhūmrāśva, and
> Dhūmrāśva's son was Sr̥ñjaya.

---
15.
sr̥ñjayasya sutaḥ śrīmānsahadēvaḥ pratāpavān |
kuśāśvaḥ sahadēvasya putraḥ paramadhārmikaḥ ||

> **Meaning 15:** "Sr̥ñjaya's son was the illustrious, valorous Sahadeva, and
> Sahadeva's son was the supremely righteous Kuśāśva.

---
16.
kuśāśvasya mahātējāḥ sōmadattaḥ pratāpavān |
sōmadattasya putrastu kākutstha iti viśrutaḥ ||

> **Meaning 16:** "Kuśāśva's son was the deeply radiant, valorous Somadatta, and
> Somadatta's son was famed as Kākutstha.

---
17.
tasya putrō mahātējāḥ sampratyēṣa purīmimām |
āvasatyamaraprakhyaḥ sumatirnāma durjayaḥ ||

> **Meaning 17:** "His brilliant, invincible son, equal to the devatās, is
> named Sumati, and he now lives in and rules this city.

---
18.
ikṣvākōstu prasādēna sarvē vaiśālikā nr̥pāḥ |
dīrghāyuṣō mahātmānō vīryavantaḥ sudhārmikāḥ ||

> **Meaning 18:** "By the grace of Ikṣvāku, all the kings of Viśālā are
> long-lived, noble, valorous, and deeply righteous.

---
19.
ihādya rajanīṁ rāma sukhaṁ vatsyāmahē vayam |
śvaḥ prabhātē naraśrēṣṭha janakaṁ draṣṭumarhasi ||

> **Meaning 19:** "Rāma, best of men, we will spend tonight comfortably here;
> tomorrow morning, you should meet King Janaka."

---
20.
sumatistu mahātējā viśvāmitramupāgatam |
śrutvā naravaraśrēṣṭhaḥ pratyudgacchanmahāyaśāḥ ||

> **Meaning 20:** Meanwhile, hearing that Viśvāmitra had arrived, the
> resplendent and illustrious Sumati, foremost of men, came out to welcome him.

---
21.
pūjāṁ ca paramāṁ kr̥tvā sōpādhyāyaḥ sabāndhavaḥ |
prāñjaliḥ kuśalaṁ pr̥ṣṭvā viśvāmitramathābravīt ||

> **Meaning 21:** Offering him the highest honor together with his teachers and
> kinsmen, he joined his palms in añjali, inquired after his well-being, and
> spoke to Viśvāmitra:

---
22.
dhanyō'smyanugr̥hītō'smi yasya mē viṣayaṁ munē |
samprāptō darśanaṁ caiva nāsti dhanyatarō mama ||

> **Meaning 22:** "Revered muni, I am blessed and truly favored that you have
> come to my kingdom and granted me your darśana; there is no one more fortunate
> than I."

---
