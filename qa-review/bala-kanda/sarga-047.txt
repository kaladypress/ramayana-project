=== Sarga 47 ===

---
1.
pr̥ṣṭvā tu kuśalaṁ tatra parasparasamāgame |
kathānte sumatir vākyaṁ vyājahāra mahāmunim ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
imau kumārau bhadraṁ te devatulyaparākramau |
gajasiṁhagatī vīrau śārdūlavr̥ṣabhopamau ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
padmapatraviśālākṣau khaḍgatūṇīdhanurdharau |
aśvināv iva rūpeṇa samupasthitayauvanau ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
yadr̥cchayaiva gāṁ prāptau devalokād ivāmarau |
kathaṁ padbhyām iha prāptau kimarthaṁ kasya vā mune ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
bhūṣayantāv imaṁ deśaṁ candrasūryāv ivāmbaram |
parasparasya sadr̥śau pramāṇeṅgitaceṣṭitaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
kimarthaṁ ca naraśreṣṭhau saṁprāptau durgame pathi |
varāyudhadharau vīrau śrotum icchāmi tattvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā yathāvr̥ttaṁ nyavedayat |
siddhāśramanivāsaṁ ca rākṣasānāṁ vadhaṁ tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
viśvāmitravacaḥ śrutvā rājā paramaharṣitaḥ |
atithī paramau prāptau putrau daśarathasya tau |
pūjayām āsa vidhivat satkārārhau mahābalau ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tataḥ paramasatkāraṁ sumateḥ prāpya rāghavau |
uṣya tatra niśām ekāṁ jagmatur mithilāṁ tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā munayaḥ sarve janakasya purīṁ śubhām |
sādhu sādhv iti śaṁsanto mithilāṁ samapūjayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
mithilopavane tatra āśramaṁ dr̥śya rāghavaḥ |
purāṇaṁ nirjanaṁ ramyaṁ papraccha munipuṁgavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
śrīmadāśramasaṁkāśaṁ kiṁ nv idaṁ munivarjitam |
śrotum icchāmi bhagavan kasyāyaṁ pūrva āśramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tac chrutā rāghaveṇoktaṁ vākyaṁ vākyaviśāradaḥ |
pratyuvāca mahātejā viśvamitro mahāmuniḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
hanta te kathayiṣyāmi śr̥ṇu tattvena rāghava |
yasyaitad āśramapadaṁ śaptaṁ kopān mahātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
gautamasya naraśreṣṭha pūrvam āsīn mahātmanaḥ |
āśramo divyasaṁkāśaḥ surair api supūjitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sa ceha tapa ātiṣṭhad ahalyāsahitaḥ purā |
varṣapūgāny anekāni rājaputra mahāyaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tasyāntaraṁ viditvā tu sahasrākṣaḥ śacīpatiḥ |
muniveṣadharo ’halyām idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
r̥tukālaṁ pratīkṣante nārthinaḥ susamāhite |
saṁgamaṁ tv aham icchāmi tvayā saha sumadhyame ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
muniveṣaṁ sahasrākṣaṁ vijñāya raghunandana |
matiṁ cakāra durmedhā devarājakutūhalāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
athābravīt suraśreṣṭhaṁ kr̥tārthenāntarātmanā |
kr̥tārtho ’si suraśreṣṭha gaccha śīghram itaḥ prabho |
ātmānaṁ māṁ ca deveśa sarvadā rakṣa mānadaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
indras tu prahasan vākyam ahalyām idam abravīt |
suśroṇi parituṣṭo ’smi gamiṣyāmi yathāgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
evaṁ saṁgamya tu tayā niścakrāmoṭajāt tataḥ |
sa saṁbhramāt tvaran rāma śaṅkito gautamaṁ prati ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
gautamaṁ sa dadarśātha praviśantaṁ mahāmunim |
devadānavadurdharṣaṁ tapobalasamanvitam |
tīrthodakapariklinnaṁ dīpyamānam ivānalam |
gr̥hītasamidhaṁ tatra sakuśaṁ munipuṅgavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
dr̥ṣṭvā surapatis trasto viṣaṇṇavadano ’bhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
atha dr̥ṣṭvā sahasrākṣaṁ muniveṣadharaṁ muniḥ |
durvr̥ttaṁ vr̥ttasaṁpanno roṣād vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
mama rūpaṁ samāsthāya kr̥tavān asi durmate |
akartavyam idaṁ yasmād viphalas tvaṁ bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
gautamenaivam uktasya saroṣeṇa mahātmanā |
petatur vr̥ṣaṇau bhūmau sahasrākṣasya tatkṣaṇāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tathā śaptvā sa vai śakraṁ bhāryām api ca śaptavān |
iha varṣasahasrāṇi bahūni tvaṁ nivatsyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
vāyubhakṣā nirāhārā tapyantī bhasmaśāyinī |
adr̥śyā sarvabhūtānām āśrame ’smin nivatsyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
yadā caitad vanaṁ ghoraṁ rāmo daśarathātmajaḥ |
āgamiṣyati durdharṣas tadā pūtā bhaviṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
tasyātithyena durvr̥tte lobhamohavivarjitā |
matsakāśe mudā yuktā svaṁ vapur dhārayiṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
evam uktvā mahātejā gautamo duṣṭacāriṇīm |
imam āśramam utsr̥jya siddhacāraṇasevite |
himavacchikhare ramye tapas tepe mahātapāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
