=== Sarga 75 ===

---
1.
śrutvā taj jāmadagnyasya vākyaṁ dāśarathis tadā |
gauravād yantritakathaḥ pitū rāmam athābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
śrutavān asmi yat karma kr̥tavān asi bhārgava |
anurundhyāmahe brahman pitur ānr̥ṇyam āsthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
vīryahīnam ivāśaktaṁ kṣatradharmeṇa bhārgava |
avajānāmi me tejaḥ paśya me ’dya parākramam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
ity uktvā rāghavaḥ kruddho bhārgavasya varāyudham |
śaraṁ ca pratisaṁgr̥hya hastāl laghuparākramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
āropya sa dhanū rāmaḥ śaraṁ sajyaṁ cakāra ha |
jāmadagnyaṁ tato rāmaṁ rāmaḥ kruddho ’bravīd vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
brāhmaṇo ’sīti pūjyo me viśvāmitrakr̥tena ca |
tasmāc chakto na te rāma moktuṁ prāṇaharaṁ śaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
imāṁ vā tvadgatiṁ rāma tapobalasamārjitān |
lokān apratimān vāpi haniṣyāmi yad icchasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
na hy ayaṁ vaiṣṇavo divyaḥ śaraḥ parapuraṁjayaḥ |
moghaḥ patati vīryeṇa baladarpavināśanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
varāyudhadharaṁ rāma draṣṭuṁ sarṣigaṇāḥ surāḥ |
pitāmahaṁ puraskr̥tya sametās tatra saṁghaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
gandharvāpsarasaś caiva siddhacāraṇakiṁnarāḥ |
yakṣarākṣasanāgāś ca tad draṣṭuṁ mahad adbhutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
jaḍīkr̥te tadā loke rāme varadhanurdhare |
nirvīryo jāmadagnyo ’sau ramo rāmam udaikṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tejobhir hatavīryatvāj jāmadagnyo jaḍīkr̥taḥ |
rāmaṁ kamala patrākṣaṁ mandaṁ mandam uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
kāśyapāya mayā dattā yadā pūrvaṁ vasuṁdharā |
viṣaye me na vastavyam iti māṁ kāśyapo ’bravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
so ’haṁ guruvacaḥ kurvan pr̥thivyāṁ na vase niśām |
iti pratijñā kākutstha kr̥tā vai kāśyapasya ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tad imāṁ tvaṁ gatiṁ vīra hantuṁ nārhasi rāghava |
manojavaṁ gamiṣyāmi mahendraṁ parvatottamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
lokās tv apratimā rāma nirjitās tapasā mayā |
jahi tāñ śaramukhyena mā bhūt kālasya paryayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
akṣayyaṁ madhuhantāraṁ jānāmi tvāṁ sureśvaram |
dhanuṣo ’sya parāmarśāt svasti te ’stu paraṁtapa ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
ete suragaṇāḥ sarve nirīkṣante samāgatāḥ |
tvām apratimakarmāṇam apratidvandvam āhave ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
na ceyaṁ mama kākutstha vrīḍā bhavitum arhati |
tvayā trailokyanāthena yad ahaṁ vimukhīkr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
śaram apratimaṁ rāma moktum arhasi suvrata |
śaramokṣe gamiṣyāmi mahendraṁ parvatottamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tathā bruvati rāme tu jāmadagnye pratāpavān |
rāmo dāśarathiḥ śrīmāṁś cikṣepa śaram uttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tato vitimirāḥ sarvā diśā copadiśas tathā |
surāḥ sarṣigaṇā rāmaṁ praśaśaṁsur udāyudham ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
rāmaṁ dāśarathiṁ rāmo jāmadagnyaḥ praśasya ca |
tataḥ pradakṣiṇīkr̥tya jagāmātmagatiṁ prabhuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
