=== Sarga 44 ===

---
1.
viśvāmitravacaḥ śrutvā rāghavaḥ sahalakṣmaṇaḥ |
vismayaṁ paramaṁ gatvā viśvāmitram athābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
atyadbhutam idaṁ brahman kathitaṁ paramaṁ tvayā |
gaṅgāvataraṇaṁ puṇyaṁ sāgarasya ca pūraṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tasya sā śarvarī sarvā saha saumitriṇā tadā |
jagāma cintayānasya viśvāmitrakathāṁ śubhām ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tataḥ prabhāte vimale viśvāmitraṁ mahāmunim |
uvāca rāghavo vākyaṁ kr̥tāhnikam ariṁdamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
gatā bhagavatī rātriḥ śrotavyaṁ paramaṁ śrutam |
kṣaṇabhūteva sā rātriḥ saṁvr̥tteyaṁ mahātapaḥ |
imāṁ cintayataḥ sarvāṁ nikhilena kathāṁ tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tarāma saritāṁ śreṣṭhāṁ puṇyāṁ tripathagāṁ nadīm |
naur eṣā hi sukhāstīrṇā r̥ṣīṇāṁ puṇyakarmaṇām |
bhagavantam iha prāptaṁ jñātvā tvaritam āgatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā rāghavasya mahātmanaḥ |
saṁtāraṁ kārayām āsa sarṣisaṁghaḥ sarāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
uttaraṁ tīram āsādya saṁpūjyarṣigaṇaṁ tatha |
gaṅgākūle niviṣṭās te viśālāṁ dadr̥śuḥ purīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tato munivaras tūrṇaṁ jagāma saharāghavaḥ |
viśālāṁ nagarīṁ ramyāṁ divyāṁ svargopamāṁ tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
atha rāmo mahāprājño viśvāmitraṁ mahāmunim |
papraccha prāñjalir bhūtvā viśālām uttamāṁ purīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
kataro rājavaṁśo ’yaṁ viśālāyāṁ mahāmune |
śrotum icchāmi bhadraṁ te paraṁ kautūhalaṁ hi me ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā rāmasya munipuṁgavaḥ |
ākhyātuṁ tat samārebhe viśālasya purātanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
śrūyatāṁ rāma śakrasya kathāṁ kathayataḥ śubhām |
asmin deśe hi yad vr̥ttaṁ śr̥ṇu tattvena rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
pūrvaṁ kr̥tayuge rāma diteḥ putrā mahābalāḥ |
aditeś ca mahābhāgā vīryavantaḥ sudhārmikāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tatas teṣāṁ naraśreṣṭha buddhir āsīn mahātmanām |
amarā nirjarāś caiva kathaṁ syāma nirāmayāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
teṣāṁ cintayatāṁ rāma buddhir āsīd vipaścitām |
kṣīrodamathanaṁ kr̥tvā rasaṁ prāpsyāma tatra vai ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tato niścitya mathanaṁ yoktraṁ kr̥tvā ca vāsukim |
manthānaṁ mandaraṁ kr̥tvā mamanthur amitaujasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
atha dhanvantarir nāma apsarāś ca suvarcasaḥ |
apsu nirmathanād eva rasāt tasmād varastriyaḥ |
utpetur manujaśreṣṭha tasmād apsaraso ’bhavan ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
ṣaṣṭiḥ koṭyo ’bhavaṁs tāsām apsarāṇāṁ suvarcasām |
asaṁkhyeyās tu kākutstha yās tāsāṁ paricārikāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
na tāḥ sma pratigr̥hṇanti sarve te devadānavāḥ |
apratigrahaṇāc caiva tena sādhāraṇāḥ smr̥tāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
varuṇasya tataḥ kanyā vāruṇī raghunandana |
utpapāta mahābhāgā mārgamāṇā parigraham ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
diteḥ putrā na tāṁ rāma jagr̥hur varuṇātmajām |
adites tu sutā vīra jagr̥hus tām aninditām ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
asurās tena daiteyāḥ surās tenāditeḥ sutāḥ |
hr̥ṣṭāḥ pramuditāś cāsan vāruṇī grahaṇāt surāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
uccaiḥśravā hayaśreṣṭho maṇiratnaṁ ca kaustubham |
udatiṣṭhan naraśreṣṭha tathaivāmr̥tam uttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
atha tasya kr̥te rāma mahān āsīt kulakṣayaḥ |
adites tu tataḥ putrā diteḥ putrāṇa sūdayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
aditer ātmajā vīrā diteḥ putrān nijaghnire |
tasmin ghore mahāyuddhe daiteyādityayor bhr̥śam ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
nihatya ditiputrāṁs tu rājyaṁ prāpya puraṁdaraḥ |
śaśāsa mudito lokān sarṣisaṁghān sacāraṇān ||

=== meaning ===

---
