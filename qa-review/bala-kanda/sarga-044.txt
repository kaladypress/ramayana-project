=== Sarga 44 ===
Brahmā Blesses Bhagīratha; Ancestral Rites and Bhagīratha's Return

---
1.
sa gatvā sāgaraṁ rājā gaṅgayānugatastadā |
pravivēśa talaṁ bhūmēryatra tē bhasmasātkr̥tāḥ ||

> **Meaning 1:** "Having reached the ocean with Gaṅgā following close behind
> him, that king entered the depths of the earth where his ancestors had been
> reduced to ashes.

---
2.
bhasmanyathāplutē rāma gaṅgāyāḥ salilēna vai |
sarva lōkaprabhurbrahmā rājānamidamabravīt ||

> **Meaning 2:** "Rāma, once those ashes were drenched by the waters of Gaṅgā,
> Brahmā, ruler of all the worlds, spoke these words to the king:

---
3.
tāritā naraśārdūla divaṁ yātāśca dēvavat |
ṣaṣṭiḥ putrasahasrāṇi sagarasya mahātmanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 3:** "'Greatest of men, the sixty thousand sons of the great Sagara
> have been liberated and have gone to heaven like the devatās.

---
4.
sāgarasya jalaṁ lōkē yāvatsthāsyati pārthiva |
sagarasyātmajāstāvatsvargē sthāsyanti dēvavat ||

> **Meaning 4:** "'As long as the waters of the ocean remain in this world,
> king, Sagara's sons will remain in svarga like the devatās.

---
5.
iyaṁ ca duhitā jyēṣṭhā tava gaṅgā bhaviṣyati |
tvatkr̥tēna ca nāmnā vai lōkē sthāsyati viśrutā ||

> **Meaning 5:** "'And this Gaṅgā will be regarded as your eldest daughter, and
> she will be celebrated throughout the world by a name derived from yours.

---
6.
gaṅgā tripathagā nāma divyā bhāgīrathīti ca |
tripathō bhāvayantīti tatastripathagā smr̥tā ||

> **Meaning 6:** "'She will be known as the divine Gaṅgā, as Tripathagā, and as
> Bhāgīrathī—since she sanctifies the three paths (heaven, earth, and
> Rasātala) as she flows, she is remembered as Tripathagā.

---
7.
pitāmahānāṁ sarvēṣāṁ tvamatra manujādhipa |
kuruṣva salilaṁ rājanpratijñāmapavarjaya ||

> **Meaning 7:** "'Ruler of men, perform tarpaṇa here for all your ancestors
> and bring your vow to completion.

---
8.
pūrvakēṇa hi tē rājaṁstēnātiyaśasā tadā |
dharmiṇāṁ pravarēṇātha naiṣa prāptō manōrathaḥ ||

> **Meaning 8:** "'King, even your illustrious forebear Sagara, foremost among
> the righteous, could not fulfill this cherished wish in his time.

---
9.
tathaivāṁśumatā tāta lōkē'pratimatējasā |
gaṅgāṁ prārthayatā nētuṁ pratijñā nāpavarjitā ||

> **Meaning 9:** "'Dear child, even Aṁśumān—whose radiance had no equal in the
> world—could not complete that vow, though he earnestly sought to bring Gaṅgā
> down.

---
10.
rājarṣiṇā guṇavatā maharṣisamatējasā |
mattulyatapasā caiva kṣatradharmasthitēna ca ||

11.
dilīpēna mahābhāga tava pitrātitējasā |
punarna śaṅkitā nētuṁ gaṅgāṁ prārthayatānagha ||

> **Meaning 10-11:** "'Sinless and fortunate one, even your father Dilīpa—that
> virtuous rājarṣi of surpassing brilliance, equal in radiance to the maharṣis,
> whose tapas matched mine and who stood steadfast in kṣatriya-dharma—could
> not bring Gaṅgā down, though he too strove to lead her here.

---
12.
sā tvayā samatikrāntā pratijñā puruṣarṣabha |
prāptō'si paramaṁ lōkē yaśaḥ paramasammatam ||

> **Meaning 12:** "'Foremost among men, you have carried that vow to completion,
> and you have won supreme, universally honored renown in the world.

---
13.
yacca gaṅgāvataraṇaṁ tvayā kr̥tamarindama |
anēna ca bhavānprāptō dharmasyāyatanaṁ mahat ||

> **Meaning 13:** "'Subduer of foes, through this descent of Gaṅgā that you
> have accomplished, you have become a great repository of dharma.

---
14.
plāvayasva tvamātmānaṁ narōttama sadōcitē |
salilē puruṣavyāghra śuciḥ puṇyaphalō bhava ||

> **Meaning 14:** "'Best of men, bathe in these ever-auspicious waters; be
> cleansed of all inner impurity and enjoy the fruits of your merit.

---
15.
pitāmahānāṁ sarvēṣāṁ kuruṣva salilakriyām |
svasti tē'stu gamiṣyāmi svaṁ lōkaṁ gamyatāṁ nr̥pa ||

> **Meaning 15:** "'King, perform tarpaṇa for all your ancestors. May all be
> well with you—I will return to my own realm now, and you too may return
> home.'

---
16.
ityēvamuktvā dēvēśaḥ sarvalōkapitāmahaḥ |
yathāgataṁ tathāgacchaddēvalōkaṁ mahāyaśāḥ ||

> **Meaning 16:** "Having spoken thus, the illustrious sovereign of the devatās,
> grandfather of all the worlds, returned to devaloka just as he had come.

---
17.
bhagīrathō'pi rājarṣiḥ kr̥tvā salilamuttamam |
yathākramaṁ yathānyāyaṁ sāgarāṇāṁ mahāyaśāḥ ||

18.
kr̥tōdakaḥ śucī rājā svapuraṁ pravivēśa ha |
samr̥ddhārthō naraśrēṣṭha svarājyaṁ praśaśāsa ha ||

> **Meaning 17-18:** "Rāma, foremost among men, the renowned rājarṣi Bhagīratha
> then performed the sacred tarpaṇa for Sagara's sons in proper order and
> according to tradition. Having completed those rites in purity, the king
> returned to his capital and, with his mission fulfilled, ruled his kingdom.

---
19.
pramumōda ca lōkastaṁ nr̥pamāsādya rāghava |
naṣṭaśōkaḥ samr̥ddhārthō babhūva vigatajvaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 19:** "Rāghava, the people rejoiced to have their king back—freed
> from sorrow, thriving in prosperity, and rid of every distress.

---
20.
ēṣa tē rāma gaṅgāyā vistarō'bhihitō mayā |
svasti prāpnuhi bhadraṁ tē sandhyākālō'tivartatē ||

> **Meaning 20:** "Rāma, this is the full account of Gaṅgā that I have narrated
> to you. May blessings and well-being be yours—the hour for evening sandhyā is
> slipping away.

---
21.
dhanyaṁ yaśasyamāyuṣyaṁ svargyaṁ putryamathāpi ca |
idamākhyānamākhyātaṁ gaṅgāvataraṇaṁ mayā ||

> **Meaning 21:** "This story of Gaṅgā's descent that I have told you is a
> source of great blessing—it bestows good fortune, renown, long life, svarga,
> and worthy progeny.

---
22.
prīyantē pitarastasya prīyantē daivatāni ca |
idamākhyānamavyagrō gaṅgāvataraṇaṁ śubham ||

23.
yaḥ śṛṇōti ca kākutstha sarvān kāmānavāpnuyāt |
sarvē pāpāḥ praṇaśyanti āyuḥ kīrtiśca vardhatē ||

> **Meaning 22-23:** "Kākutstha, both the ancestors and the devatās are pleased
> with anyone who listens attentively to this auspicious account of Gaṅgā's
> descent. All their wishes are fulfilled, every sin is wiped away, and their
> lifespan and renown flourish."

---
