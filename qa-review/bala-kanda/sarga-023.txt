=== Sarga 23 ===

---
1.
tataḥ prabhāte vimale kr̥tāhnikam ariṁdamau |
viśvāmitraṁ puraskr̥tya nadyās tīram upāgatau ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
te ca sarve mahātmāno munayaḥ saṁśitavratāḥ |
upasthāpya śubhāṁ nāvaṁ viśvāmitram athābruvan ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
ārohatu bhavān nāvaṁ rājaputrapuraskr̥taḥ |
ariṣṭaṁ gaccha panthānaṁ mā bhūt kālasya paryayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
viśvāmitras tathety uktvā tān r̥ṣīn abhipūjya ca |
tatāra sahitas tābhyāṁ saritaṁ sāgaraṁ gamām ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
atha rāmaḥ sarinmadhye papraccha munipuṅgavam |
vāriṇo bhidyamānasya kim ayaṁ tumulo dhvaniḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
rāghavasya vacaḥ śrutvā kautūhala samanvitam |
kathayām āsa dharmātmā tasya śabdasya niścayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
kailāsaparvate rāma manasā nirmitaṁ saraḥ |
brahmaṇā naraśārdūla tenedaṁ mānasaṁ saraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tasmāt susrāva sarasaḥ sāyodhyām upagūhate |
saraḥpravr̥ttā sarayūḥ puṇyā brahmasaraścyutā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tasyāyam atulaḥ śabdo jāhnavīm abhivartate |
vārisaṁkṣobhajo rāma praṇāmaṁ niyataḥ kuru ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tābhyāṁ tu tāv ubhau kr̥tvā praṇāmam atidhārmikau |
tīraṁ dakṣiṇam āsādya jagmatur laghuvikramau ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sa vanaṁ ghorasaṁkāśaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā nr̥pavarātmajaḥ |
aviprahatam aikṣvākaḥ papraccha munipuṁgavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
aho vanam idaṁ durgaṁ jhillikāgaṇanāditam |
bhairavaiḥ śvāpadaiḥ kīrṇaṁ śakuntair dāruṇārutaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
nānāprakāraiḥ śakunair vāśyadbhir bhairavasvanaiḥ |
siṁhavyāghravarāhaiś ca vāraṇaiś cāpi śobhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
dhavāśvakarṇakakubhair bilvatindukapāṭalaiḥ |
saṁkīrṇaṁ badarībhiś ca kiṁ nv idaṁ dāruṇaṁ vanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tam uvāca mahātejā viśvāmitro mahāmuniḥ |
śrūyatāṁ vatsa kākutstha yasyaitad dāruṇaṁ vanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
etau janapadau sphītau pūrvam āstāṁ narottama |
maladāś ca karūṣāś ca devanirmāṇanirmitau ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
purā vr̥travadhe rāma malena samabhiplutam |
kṣudhā caiva sahasrākṣaṁ brahmahatyā yadāviśat ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tam indraṁ snāpayan devā r̥ṣayaś ca tapodhanāḥ |
kalaśaiḥ snāpayām āsur malaṁ cāsya pramocayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
iha bhūmyāṁ malaṁ dattvā dattvā kārūṣam eva ca |
śarīrajaṁ mahendrasya tato harṣaṁ prapedire ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
nirmalo niṣkarūṣaś ca śucir indro yadābhavat |
dadau deśasya suprīto varaṁ prabhur anuttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
imau janapadau sthītau khyātiṁ loke gamiṣyataḥ |
maladāś ca karūṣāś ca mamāṅgamaladhāriṇau ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
sādhu sādhv iti taṁ devāḥ pākaśāsanam abruvan |
deśasya pūjāṁ tāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā kr̥tāṁ śakreṇa dhīmatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
etau janapadau sthītau dīrghakālam ariṁdama |
maladāś ca karūṣāś ca muditau dhanadhānyataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
kasya cit tv atha kālasya yakṣī vai kāmarūpiṇī |
balaṁ nāgasahasrasya dhārayantī tadā hy abhūt ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tāṭakā nāma bhadraṁ te bhāryā sundasya dhīmataḥ |
mārīco rākṣasaḥ putro yasyāḥ śakraparākramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
imau janapadau nityaṁ vināśayati rāghava |
maladāṁś ca karūṣāṁś ca tāṭakā duṣṭacāriṇī ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
seyaṁ panthānam āvārya vasaty atyardhayojane |
ata eva ca gantavyaṁ tāṭakāyā vanaṁ yataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
svabāhubalam āśritya jahīmāṁ duṣṭacāriṇīm |
manniyogād imaṁ deśaṁ kuru niṣkaṇṭakaṁ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
na hi kaś cid imaṁ deśaṁ śakroty āgantum īdr̥śam |
yakṣiṇyā ghorayā rāma utsāditam asahyayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
etat te sarvam ākhyātaṁ yathaitad daruṇaṁ vanam |
yakṣyā cotsāditaṁ sarvam adyāpi na nivartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
