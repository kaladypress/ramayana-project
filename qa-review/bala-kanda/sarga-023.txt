=== Sarga 23 ===
Kāmāśrama at the Confluence of Gaṅgā and Sarayū; Story of Anaṅga

---
1.
prabhātāyāṁ tu śarvaryāṁ viśvāmitrō mahāmuniḥ |
abhyabhāṣata kākutsthaṁ śayānaṁ parṇasaṁstarē ||

> **Meaning 1:** When the night ended and dawn broke, the great sage Viśvāmitra
> spoke to Kākutstha (Rāma), who was lying on his bed of leaves.

---
2.
kausalyā suprajā rāma pūrvā sandhyā pravartatē |
uttiṣṭha naraśārdūla kartavyaṁ daivamāhnikam ||

> **Meaning 2:** "Rāma, noble son of Kausalyā, the morning twilight has begun.
> Foremost of men, rise, for it is time to perform the daily morning rites."

---
3.
tasyarṣēḥ paramōdāraṁ vacaḥ śrutvā nr̥pātmajau |
snātvā kr̥tōdakau vīrau jēpatuḥ paramaṁ japam ||

> **Meaning 3:** Hearing those noble words of the sage, the two heroic princes
> bathed, offered arghya to Sūrya, and chanted the supreme Gāyatrī japa.

---
4.
kr̥tāhnikau mahāvīryau viśvāmitraṁ tapōdhanam |
abhivādyābhisaṁhr̥ṣṭau gamanāyōpatasthatuḥ ||

> **Meaning 4:** Having completed their morning rites, the two mighty princes
> joyfully bowed to Viśvāmitra, whose wealth was his austerity, and stood ready
> to set out.

---
5.
tau prayātē mahāvīryau divyaṁ tripathagāṁ nadīm |
dadr̥śātē tatastatra sarayvāḥ saṅgamē śubhē ||

> **Meaning 5:** As they traveled on, those two valiant princes saw the divine
> river Gaṅgā, which flows through the three worlds, at its auspicious
> confluence with the Sarayū.

---
6.
tatrāśramapadaṁ puṇyamr̥ṣīṇāmugratējasām |
bahuvarṣasahasrāṇi tapyatāṁ paramaṁ tapaḥ ||

> **Meaning 6:** There stood a sacred hermitage of sages of fierce spiritual
> power, who had practiced the highest austerities for many thousands of years.

---
7.
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā paramaprītau rāghavau puṇyamāśramam |
ūcatustaṁ mahātmānaṁ viśvāmitramidaṁ vacaḥ ||

> **Meaning 7:** Seeing that sacred hermitage, Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa were deeply
> pleased and said to the great-souled Viśvāmitra:

---
8.
kasyāyamāśramaḥ puṇyaḥ kō nvasminvasatē pumān |
bhagavañśrōtumicchāvaḥ paraṁ kautūhalaṁ hi nau ||

> **Meaning 8:** "Whose sacred hermitage is this, and who lives here? Venerable
> sage, we want to hear about it, for our curiosity is great."

---
9.
tayōstadvacanaṁ śrutvā prahasya munipuṅgavaḥ |
abravīcchrūyatāṁ rāma yasyāyaṁ pūrva āśramaḥ ||

> **Meaning 9:** Hearing their question, that foremost of sages smiled and said,
> "Listen, Rāma, to whom this hermitage once belonged.

---
10.
kandarpō mūrtimānāsītkāma ityucyatē budhaiḥ |
tapasyantamiha sthāṇuṁ niyamēna samāhitam ||

> **Meaning 10:** "Kandarpa, whom the wise call Kāma, once had a physical form.
> Here, Sthāṇu (Śiva) was absorbed in disciplined austerity,

---
11.
kr̥tōdvāhaṁ tu dēvēśaṁ gacchantaṁ samarudgaṇam |
dharṣayāmāsa durmēdhā huṅkr̥taśca mahātmanā ||

> **Meaning 11:** "newly wed, that chief of the devatās, walking with the hosts
> of Maruts. The foolish Kāma dared to disrupt his austerity, and was met with
> a fierce roar by the great-souled Śiva.

---
12.
dagdhasya tasya raudrēṇa cakṣuṣā raghunandana |
vyaśīryanta śarīrātsvātsarvagātrāṇi durmatēḥ ||

> **Meaning 12:** "Rāma, when foolish Kāma was burned by Śiva's terrible eye,
> every limb of his body was completely consumed.

---
13.
tasya gātraṁ hataṁ tatra nirdagdhasya mahātmanā |
aśarīraḥ kr̥taḥ kāmaḥ krōdhāddēvēśvarēṇa ha ||

> **Meaning 13:** "His body was destroyed there as the great-souled Śiva burned
> him to ashes; in his anger, the supreme devatā made Kāma bodiless.

---
14.
anaṅga iti vikhyātastadā prabhr̥ti rāghava |
sa cāṅgaviṣayaḥ śrīmānyatrāṅgaṁ sa mumōca ha ||

> **Meaning 14:** "Rāghava, from that time on he has been known as Anaṅga (the
> bodiless one), and that prosperous region where he shed his body became known
> as the land of Aṅga.

---
15.
tasyāyamāśramaḥ puṇyastasyēmē munayaḥ purā |
śiṣyā dharmaparā vīra tēṣāṁ pāpaṁ na vidyatē ||

> **Meaning 15:** "This is his sacred hermitage, and these sages here were once
> his disciples. They are devoted to dharma, brave prince, and there is no sin
> in them.

---
16.
ihādya rajanīṁ rāma vasēma śubhadarśana |
puṇyayōḥ saritōrmadhyē śvastariṣyāmahē vayam ||

> **Meaning 16:** "Handsome Rāma, let us spend tonight here between the two
> sacred rivers; tomorrow we will cross over.

---
17.
abhigacchāmahe sarve śucayaḥ puṇyamāśramam |
snātāśca kr̥tajapyāśca hutahavyā narōttamāḥ ||

> **Meaning 17:** "Best of men, let us all bathe, perform our japa, and offer
> oblations into the sacred fire, and then enter this sacred hermitage."

---
18.
tēṣāṁ saṁvadatāṁ tatra tapō dīrghēṇa cakṣuṣā |
vijñāya paramaprītā munayō harṣamāgaman ||

> **Meaning 18:** Through the divine sight born of their austerity, the sages
> knew they were conversing there and came forward in great joy to meet them.

---
19.
arghyaṁ pādyaṁ tathātithyaṁ nivēdyakuśikātmajē |
rāmalakṣmaṇayōḥ paścādakurvannatithikriyām ||

> **Meaning 19:** Offering arghya, pādya, and hospitality first to Viśvāmitra,
> the son of Kuśika, they then extended the same welcome to Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa.

---
20.
satkāraṁ samanuprāpya kathābhirabhirañjayan |
yathārham ajapan sandhyāṁ r̥ṣayastē samāhitāḥ ||

> **Meaning 20:** After Rāma, Lakṣmaṇa, and Viśvāmitra had received their
> hospitality, those composed sages entertained them with stories and duly
> performed their sandhyā prayers.

---
21.
tatra vāsibhirānītā munibhiḥ suvrataiḥ saha |
nyavasan susukhaṃ tatra kāmāśramapade tathā ||

> **Meaning 21:** Brought inside by the devout sages living there, Rāma,
> Lakṣmaṇa, and Viśvāmitra stayed very comfortably with them at Kāma's
> hermitage.

---
22.
kathābhirabhirāmabhirabhirāmau nṛpātmajau |
ramayāmāsa dharmātmā kauśiko munipuṅgavaḥ ||

> **Meaning 22:** With delightful stories, the righteous Kauśika (Viśvāmitra),
> foremost among sages, entertained the two charming princes, Rāma and
> Lakṣmaṇa.

---
