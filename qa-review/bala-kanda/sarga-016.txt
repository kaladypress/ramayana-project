=== Sarga 16 ===

---
1.
putratvaṁ tu gate viṣṇau rājñas tasya mahātmanaḥ |
uvāca devatāḥ sarvāḥ svayambhūr bhagavān idam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
satyasaṁdhasya vīrasya sarveṣāṁ no hitaiṣiṇaḥ |
viṣṇoḥ sahāyān balinaḥ sr̥jadhvaṁ kāmarūpiṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
māyāvidaś ca śūrāṁś ca vāyuvegasamāñjave |
nayajñān buddhisaṁpannān viṣṇutulyaparākramān ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
asaṁhāryān upāyajñān divyasaṁhananānvitān |
sarvāstraguṇasaṁpannān amr̥taprāśanān iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
apsaraḥsu ca mukhyāsu gandharvīṇāṁ tanūṣu ca |
yakṣapannagakanyāsu r̥ṣkavidyādharīṣu ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
kiṁnarīṇāṁ ca gātreṣu vānarīṇāṁ tanūṣu ca |
sr̥jadhvaṁ harirūpeṇa putrāṁs tulyaparākramān ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
te tathoktā bhagavatā tat pratiśrutya śāsanam |
janayām āsur evaṁ te putrān vānararūpiṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
r̥ṣayaś ca mahātmānaḥ siddhavidyādharoragāḥ |
cāraṇāś ca sutān vīrān sasr̥jur vanacāriṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
te sr̥ṣṭā bahusāhasrā daśagrīvavadhodyatāḥ |
aprameyabalā vīrā vikrāntāḥ kāmarūpiṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
te gajācalasaṁkāśā vapuṣmanto mahābalāḥ |
r̥kṣavānaragopucchāḥ kṣipram evābhijajñire ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
yasya devasya yad rūpaṁ veṣo yaś ca parākramaḥ |
ajāyata samastena tasya tasya sutaḥ pr̥thak ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
golāṅgūlīṣu cotpannāḥ ke cit saṁmatavikramāḥ |
r̥kṣīṣu ca tathā jātā vānarāḥ kiṁnarīṣu ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
śilāpraharaṇāḥ sarve sarve pādapayodhinaḥ |
nakhadaṁṣṭrāyudhāḥ sarve sarve sarvāstrakovidāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
vicālayeyuḥ śailendrān bhedayeyuḥ sthirān drumān |
kṣobhayeyuś ca vegena samudraṁ saritāṁ patim ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
dārayeyuḥ kṣitiṁ padbhyām āplaveyur mahārṇavam |
nabhastalaṁ viśeyuś ca gr̥hṇīyur api toyadān ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
gr̥hṇīyur api mātaṅgān mattān pravrajato vane |
nardamānāṁś ca nādena pātayeyur vihaṁgamān ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
īdr̥śānāṁ prasūtāni harīṇāṁ kāmarūpiṇām |
śataṁ śatasahasrāṇi yūthapānāṁ mahātmanām |
babhūvur yūthapaśreṣṭhā vīrāṁś cājanayan harīn ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
anye r̥kṣavataḥ prasthān upatasthuḥ sahasraśaḥ |
anye nānāvidhāñ śailān kānanāni ca bhejire ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
sūryaputraṁ ca sugrīvaṁ śakraputraṁ ca vālinam |
bhrātarāv upatasthus te sarva eva harīśvarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tair meghavr̥ndācalakūṭakalpair; mahābalair vānarayūthapālaiḥ |
babhūva bhūr bhīmaśarīrarūpaiḥ; samāvr̥tā rāmasahāyahetoḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
