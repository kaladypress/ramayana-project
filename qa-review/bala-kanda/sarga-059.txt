=== Sarga 59 ===

---
1.
tapobalahatān kr̥tvā vāsiṣṭhān samahodayān |
r̥ṣimadhye mahātejā viśvāmitro ’bhyabhāṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
ayam ikṣvākudāyādas triśaṅkur iti viśrutaḥ |
dharmiṣṭhaś ca vadānyaś ca māṁ caiva śaraṇaṁ gataḥ |
svenānena śarīreṇa devalokajigīṣayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
yathāyaṁ svaśarīreṇa devalokaṁ gamiṣyati |
tathā pravartyatāṁ yajño bhavadbhiś ca mayā saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
viśvāmitravacaḥ śrutvā sarva eva maharṣayaḥ |
ūcuḥ sametya sahitā dharmajñā dharmasaṁhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
ayaṁ kuśikadāyādo muniḥ paramakopanaḥ |
yad āha vacanaṁ samyag etat kāryaṁ na saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
agnikalpo hi bhagavāñ śāpaṁ dāsyati roṣitaḥ |
tasmāt pravartyatāṁ yajñaḥ saśarīro yathā divam |
gacched ikṣvākudāyādo viśvāmitrasya tejasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tataḥ pravartyatāṁ yajñaḥ sarve samadhitiṣṭhate ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
evam uktvā maharṣayaḥ saṁjahrus tāḥ kriyās tadā |
yājakāś ca mahātejā viśvāmitro ’bhavat kratau ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
r̥tvijaś cānupūrvyeṇa mantravan mantrakovidāḥ |
cakruḥ sarvāṇi karmāṇi yathākalpaṁ yathāvidhi ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tataḥ kālena mahatā viśvāmitro mahātapāḥ |
cakārāvāhanaṁ tatra bhāgārthaṁ sarvadevatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
nāhyāgamaṁs tadāhūtā bhāgārthaṁ sarvadevatāḥ |
tataḥ krodhasamāviṣṭo viśvamitro mahāmuniḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sruvam udyamya sakrodhas triśaṅkum idam abravīt |
paśya me tapaso vīryaṁ svārjitasya nareśvara ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
eṣa tvāṁ svaśarīreṇa nayāmi svargam ojasā |
duṣprāpaṁ svaśarīreṇa divaṁ gaccha narādhipa ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
svārjitaṁ kiṁ cid apy asti mayā hi tapasaḥ phalam |
rājaṁs tvaṁ tejasā tasya saśarīro divaṁ vraja ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
uktavākye munau tasmin saśarīro nareśvaraḥ |
divaṁ jagāma kākutstha munīnāṁ paśyatāṁ tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
devalokagataṁ dr̥ṣṭvā triśaṅkuṁ pākaśāsanaḥ |
saha sarvaiḥ suragaṇair idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
triśaṅko gaccha bhūyas tvaṁ nāsi svargakr̥tālayaḥ |
guruśāpahato mūḍha pata bhūmim avākśirāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
evam ukto mahendreṇa triśaṅkur apatat punaḥ |
vikrośamānas trāhīti viśvāmitraṁ tapodhanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tac chrutvā vacanaṁ tasya krośamānasya kauśikaḥ |
roṣam āhārayat tīvraṁ tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti cābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
r̥ṣimadhye sa tejasvī prajāpatir ivāparaḥ |
sr̥jan dakṣiṇamārgasthān saptarṣīn aparān punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
nakṣatramālām aparām asr̥jat krodhamūrchitaḥ |
dakṣiṇāṁ diśam āsthāya munimadhye mahāyaśāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
sr̥ṣṭvā nakṣatravaṁśaṁ ca krodhena kaluṣīkr̥taḥ |
anyam indraṁ kariṣyāmi loko vā syād anindrakaḥ |
daivatāny api sa krodhāt sraṣṭuṁ samupacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tataḥ paramasaṁbhrāntāḥ sarṣisaṁghāḥ surarṣabhāḥ |
viśvāmitraṁ mahātmānam ūcuḥ sānunayaṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
ayaṁ rājā mahābhāga guruśāpaparikṣataḥ |
saśarīro divaṁ yātuṁ nārhaty eva tapodhana ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
teṣāṁ tad vacanaṁ śrutvā devānāṁ munipuṁgavaḥ |
abravīt sumahad vākyaṁ kauśikaḥ sarvadevatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
saśarīrasya bhadraṁ vas triśaṅkor asya bhūpateḥ |
ārohaṇaṁ pratijñāya nānr̥taṁ kartum utsahe ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
sargo ’stu saśarīrasya triśaṅkor asya śāśvataḥ |
nakṣatrāṇi ca sarvāṇi māmakāni dhruvāṇy atha ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
yāval lokā dhariṣyanti tiṣṭhantv etāni sarvaśaḥ |
matkr̥tāni surāḥ sarve tad anujñātum arhatha ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
evam uktāḥ surāḥ sarve pratyūcur munipuṁgavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
evaṁ bhavatu bhadraṁ te tiṣṭhantv etāni sarvaśaḥ |
gagane tāny anekāni vaiśvānarapathād bahiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
nakṣatrāṇi muniśreṣṭha teṣu jyotiḥṣu jājvalan |
avākśirās triśaṅkuś ca tiṣṭhatv amarasaṁnibhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
viśvāmitras tu dharmātmā sarvadevair abhiṣṭutaḥ |
r̥ṣibhiś ca mahātejā bāḍham ity āha devatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
tato devā mahātmāno munayaś ca tapodhanāḥ |
jagmur yathāgataṁ sarve yajñasyānte narottama ||

=== meaning ===

---
