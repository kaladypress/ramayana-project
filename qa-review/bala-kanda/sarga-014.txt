=== Sarga 14 ===
Conduct of the Aśvamedha Yajña

---
1.
atha saṁvatsarē pūrṇē tasminprāptē turaṅgamē |
sarayvāścōttarē tīrē rājñō yajñō'bhyavartata ||

> **Meaning 1:** When a full year had passed and the sacred horse had returned,
> the king's yajña commenced on the northern bank of the Sarayū.

---
2.
r̥śyaśr̥ṅgaṁ puraskr̥tya karma cakrurdvijarṣabhāḥ |
aśvamēdhē mahāyajñē rājñō'sya sumahātmanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 2:** With Ṛśyaśṛṅga as the presiding priest, the foremost Brāhmaṇas
> carried out the rites of the Aśvamedha, the great yajña of this noble king.

---
3.
karma kurvanti vidhivadyājakā vēdapāragāḥ |
yathāvidhi yathānyāyaṁ parikrāmanti śāstrataḥ ||

> **Meaning 3:** The priests, masters of the Vedas, performed the rites as
> ordained in the scriptures, proceeding through each stage in proper order and
> with due propriety, exactly as the śāstras direct.

---
4.
pravargyaṁ śāstrataḥ kr̥tvā tathaivōpasadaṁ dvijāḥ |
cakruśca vidhivatsarvamadhikaṁ karma śāstrataḥ ||

> **Meaning 4:** Following the scriptures, the Brāhmaṇas performed the Pravargya
> and the Upasad rites. They also carried out, exactly as prescribed, all the
> additional rites the śāstras ordain.

---
5.
abhipūjya tatō hr̥ṣṭāḥ sarvē cakruryathāvidhi |
prātaḥsavanapūrvāṇi karmāṇi munipuṅgavāḥ ||

> **Meaning 5:** Then, after worshipping the presiding devatās of the rites,
> all those eminent sages, with great delight, performed the rites as
> prescribed, beginning with the morning offerings (Prātaḥsavana).

---
6.
aindraśca vidhivaddattō rājā cābhiṣutō'naghaḥ |
mādhyandinaṁ ca savanaṁ prāvartata yathākramam ||

> **Meaning 6:** The offering to Indra was made as prescribed, and the pure
> Soma was extracted. Then the midday rites (Mādhyandina Savana) proceeded in
> their proper sequence.

---
7.
tr̥tīyasavanaṁ caiva rājñō'sya sumahātmanaḥ |
cakrustē śāstratō dr̥ṣṭvā tathā brāhmaṇapuṅgavāḥ ||

> **Meaning 7:** In the same way, those foremost Brāhmaṇas, consulting the
> śāstras, performed the third rites (Tṛtīya Savana) for this noble king.

---
8.
na cāhutamabhūttatra skhalitaṁ vāpi kiñcana |
dr̥śyatē brahmavatsarvaṁ kṣēmayuktaṁ hi cakrirē ||

> **Meaning 8:** In the proceedings of the yajña, no oblation was left
> unoffered, nor was any offering incorrectly substituted. There was not a
> single flaw in the procedure. Every ritual was executed strictly according
> to the mantras and completed without hindrance.

---
9.
na tēṣvahaḥsu śrāntō vā kṣudhitō vāpi dr̥śyatē |
nāvidvānbrāhmaṇastatra nāśatānucarastathā ||

> **Meaning 9:** Throughout the days of the yajña, no one was seen tired or
> hungry. Not a single Brāhmaṇa was unlearned, nor did any Brāhmaṇa have
> fewer than a hundred students and followers.

---
10.
brāhmaṇā bhuñjatē nityaṁ nāthavantaśca bhuñjatē |
tāpasā bhuñjatē cāpi śramaṇā bhuñjatē tathā ||

> **Meaning 10:** Brāhmaṇas feasted every day, as did people of all varṇas;
> ascetics dined there, and so too did sannyāsīs (renunciates).

---
11.
vr̥ddhāśca vyādhitāścaiva striyō bālāstathaiva ca |
aniśaṁ bhuñjamānānāṁ na tr̥ptirupalabhyatē ||

> **Meaning 11:** The elderly, the sick, women, and children ate day and night,
> yet king Daśaratha never felt satisfied that he had fed them enough.

---
12.
dīyatāṁ dīyatāmannaṁ vāsāṁsi vividhāni ca |
iti sañcōditāstatra tathā cakruranēkaśaḥ ||

> **Meaning 12:** "Serve food! Serve food! And distribute clothes of every kind!"
> Urged on by the ministers, the attendants did so again and again.

---
13.
annakūṭāśca bahavō dr̥śyantē parvatōpamāḥ |
divasē divasē tatra siddhasya vidhivattadā ||

> **Meaning 13:** Day after day, many heaps of cooked rice, prepared according
> to the prescribed ritual rules, could be seen there, rising like mountains.

---
14.
nānādēśādanuprāptāḥ puruṣāḥ strīgaṇāstathā |
annapānaiḥ suvihitāstasminyajñē mahātmanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 14:** Men and crowds of women who had come from many lands to
> witness that noble king's yajña were well provided with food and drink.

---
15.
annaṁ hi vidhivatsvādu praśaṁsanti dvijarṣabhāḥ |
ahō tr̥ptāḥ sma bhadraṁ tē iti śuśrāva rāghavaḥ ||

> **Meaning 15:** The food, prepared according to ritual rules, was truly
> delicious. Daśaratha heard the foremost Brāhmaṇas say, "Ah, we are fully
> satisfied! May all be well with you!"

---
16.
svalaṅkr̥tāśca puruṣā brāhmaṇānparyavēṣayan |
upāsatē ca tānanyē sumr̥ṣṭamaṇikuṇḍalāḥ ||

> **Meaning 16:** Finely adorned men served food to the Brāhmaṇas, while others
> wearing gleaming jeweled earrings attended to their needs.

---
17.
karmāntarē tadā viprā hētuvādānbahūnapi |
prāhuḥ suvāgminō dhīrāḥ parasparajigīṣayā ||

> **Meaning 17:** In the intervals between rites, wise and eloquent Brāhmaṇas
> engaged in many reasoned debates, each trying to outdo the other.

---
18.
divasē divasē tatra saṁstarē kuśalā dvijāḥ |
sarvakarmāṇi cakrustē yathāśāstraṁ pracōditāḥ ||

> **Meaning 18:** Every day, the skilled Brāhmaṇas performed all the rites there,
> exactly as directed by the scriptures.

---
19.
nāṣaḍaṅgavidatrāsīnnāvratō nābahuśrutaḥ |
sadasyastasya vai rājñō nāvādakuśalō dvijaḥ ||

> **Meaning 19:** Among the king's assembly priests (Sadasyas), there was no
> Brāhmaṇa who did not know the six Vedāṅgas, who had not kept his vows, who was
> not widely learned, or who was not skilled in debate.

---
20.
prāptē yūpōcchrayē tasminṣaḍbailvāḥ khādirāstathā |
tāvantō bilvasahitāḥ parṇinaśca tathāparē ||

> **Meaning 20:** When the time came to raise the yūpas (sacred posts) for that
> yajña, six posts of bilva wood, six of khadira, and another six of palāśa
> beside the bilva posts, were all set in place.

---
21.
ślēṣmātakamayō diṣṭō dēvadārumayastathā |
dvāvēva tatra vihitau bāhuvyastaparigrahau ||

> **Meaning 21:** Two more posts were set up there, one of śleṣmātaka wood and
> one of devadāru; and the interval between each post was the span of two
> outstretched arms.

---
22.
kāritāḥ sarva ēvaitē śāstrajñairyajñakōvidaiḥ |
śōbhārthaṁ tasya yajñasya kāñcanālaṅkr̥tā bhavan ||

> **Meaning 22:** All these posts were made by men who knew the śāstras and were
> skilled in the rites of yajña. To add to the splendor of the yajña, they were
> adorned with gold.

---
23.
ēkaviṁśatiyūpāstē ēkaviṁśatyaratnayaḥ |
vāsōbhirēkaviṁśadbhirēkaikaṁ samalaṅkr̥tāḥ ||

> **Meaning 23:** There were twenty-one posts in all, each twenty-one cubits
> (aratnis) high, and every one of them was decorated with cloth.

---
24.
vinyastā vidhivatsarvē śilpibhiḥ sukr̥tā dr̥ḍhāḥ |
aṣṭāśrayaḥ sarva ēva ślakṣṇarūpasamanvitāḥ ||

> **Meaning 24:** Skilled craftsmen crafted them strong and durable, setting
> them in place as prescribed. Every post was eight-sided and smooth, perfectly
> straight without any curve, knot, or hollow.

---
25.
ācchāditāstē vāsōbhiḥ puṣpairgandhaiśca bhūṣitāḥ |
saptarṣayō dīptimantō virājantē yathā divi ||

> **Meaning 25:** Draped in cloth and adorned with flowers and fragrances, they
> shone like the luminous stars of the Saptarṣis in the sky.

---
26.
iṣṭakāśca yathānyāyaṁ kāritāśca pramāṇataḥ |
citō'gnirbrāhmaṇaistatra kuśalaiḥ śulbakarmaṇi ||

> **Meaning 26:** Bricks were made properly and to exact measure. Brāhmaṇas
> skilled in the geometry of altar-building (śulba) built up the fire altar
> there.

---
27.
sa cityō rājasiṁhasya sañcitaḥ kuśalairdvijaiḥ |
garuḍō rukmapakṣō vai triguṇō'ṣṭādaśātmakaḥ ||

> **Meaning 27:** For Daśaratha, that lion among kings, skilled Brāhmaṇas built
> the fire altar in the shape of Garuḍa with golden wings—three times the
> standard size, constructed in eighteen courses of bricks.

---
28.
niyuktāstatra paśavastattaduddiśya daivatam |
uragāḥ pakṣiṇaścaiva yathāśāstraṁ pracōditāḥ ||

> **Meaning 28:** Animals were tied to the posts there, each dedicated to its
> own devatā, along with serpents and birds, as the scriptures direct.

---
29.
śāmitrē tu hayastatra tathā jala carāśca yē |
r̥tvigbhiḥ sarvamēvaitanniyuktaṁ śāstratastadā ||

> **Meaning 29:** At the place of immolation (Śāmitra), the priests tied the
> horse and aquatic creatures such as tortoises to the posts, strictly
> according to the scriptures.

---
30.
paśūnāṁ triśataṁ tatra yūpēṣu niyataṁ tadā |
aśvaratnōttamaṁ tasya rājñō daśarathasya ha ||

> **Meaning 30:** Three hundred animals were tied to the posts there, together
> with King Daśaratha's returned horse—the foremost jewel among horses.

---
31.
kausalyā taṁ hayaṁ tatra paricarya samantataḥ |
kr̥pāṇairviśaśāsainaṁ tribhiḥ paramayā mudā ||

> **Meaning 31:** Kausalyā worshipped that horse, circumambulating it on all
> sides, and with supreme joy, made the ceremonial marks with the three ritual
> blades.

---
32.
patatriṇā tadā sārdhaṁ susthitēna ca cētasā |
avasadrajanīmēkāṁ kausalyā dharmakāmyayā ||

> **Meaning 32:** Desiring to fulfill sacred dharma, Kausalyā, the chief queen,
> stayed one night with that sacrificed horse, with a calm and composed mind.

---
33.
hōtādhvaryustathōdgātā hayēna samayōjayan |
mahiṣyā parivr̥ktyātha vāvātāmaparāṁ tathā ||

> **Meaning 33:** The Hotā, Adhvaryu, and Udgātā then brought Kausalyā, Sumitrā,
> and Kaikeyī into ritual contact with the horse.

---
34.
patatriṇastasya vapāmuddhr̥tya niyatēndriyaḥ |
r̥tvikparama sampannaḥ śrapayāmāsa śāstrataḥ ||

> **Meaning 34:** Then the priest, perfect in his learning and with his senses
> under control, drew out the omentum (vapā) of that horse and cooked it as the
> scriptures prescribe.

---
35.
dhūmagandhaṁ vapāyāstu jighrati sma narādhipaḥ |
yathākālaṁ yathānyāyaṁ nirṇudanpāpamātmanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 35:** At the appointed time and strictly according to ritual
> ordinances, King Daśaratha inhaled the fragrant smoke of the omentum,
> dispelling his sins.

---
36.
hayasya yāni cāṅgāni tāni sarvāṇi brāhmaṇāḥ |
agnau prāsyanti vidhivatsamastāḥ ṣōḍaśartvijaḥ ||

> **Meaning 36:** All sixteen officiating priests together offered all the
> remaining limbs of the horse into the sacred fire, strictly adhering to
> scriptural procedure.

---
37.
plakṣaśākhāsu yajñānāmanyēṣāṁ kriyatē haviḥ |
aśvamēdhasya caikasya vaitasō bhāga iṣyatē ||

> **Meaning 37:** In other yajñas the oblation is placed on branches of the
> plakṣa tree. In the Aśvamedha alone, the portion is offered on cane (vetasa).

---
38.
tryahō'śvamēdhaḥ saṅkhyātaḥ kalpasūtrēṇa brāhmaṇaiḥ |
catuṣṭōmamahastasya prathamaṁ parikalpitam ||

> **Meaning 38:** The Brāhmaṇas, following the Kalpasūtra, reckon the Aśvamedha
> as a three-day rite. Its first day was arranged as the Catuṣṭoma.

---
39.
ukthyaṁ dvitīyaṁ saṅkhyātamatirātraṁ tathōttaram |
kāritāstatra bahavō vihitāḥ śāstradarśanāt ||

> **Meaning 39:** The second day is counted as the Ukthya, and the one after it
> as the Atirātra. Many other rites were also performed there, as the śāstras
> direct:

---
40.
jyōtiṣṭōmāyuṣī caiva atirātrau ca nirmitau |
abhijidviśvajiccaiva aptōryāmō mahākratuḥ ||

> **Meaning 40:** the Jyotiṣṭoma and the Āyus, the Atirātra, the Abhijit, the
> Viśvajit, and the Aptoryāma—six other yajñas.

---
41.
prācīṁ hōtrē dadau rājā diśaṁ svakulavardhanaḥ |
adhvaryavē pratīcīṁ tu brahmaṇē dakṣiṇāṁ diśam ||

> **Meaning 41:** King Daśaratha, who brought prosperity to his lineage, gave
> the eastern lands to the Hotā, the western to the Adhvaryu, and the southern
> lands to the Brahmā.

---
42.
udgātrē tu tathōdīcīṁ dakṣiṇaiṣā vinirmitā |
aśvamēdhē mahāyajñē svayambhuvihitē purā ||

> **Meaning 42:** And likewise the northern lands to the Udgātā—this was the
> dakṣiṇā ordained long ago by Brahmā for the great Aśvamedha yajña.

---
43.
kratuṁ samāpya tu tadā nyāyataḥ puruṣarṣabhaḥ |
r̥tvigbhyō hi dadau rājā dharāṁ tāṁ kratuvardhanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 43:** Having completed the yajña strictly according to sacred
> ordinances, King Daśaratha, that foremost of men who made the sacrifice
> flourish, gave the entire earth to the officiating priests.

---
44.
r̥tvijastvabruvansarvē rājānaṁ gatakalmaṣam |
bhavānēva mahīṁ kr̥tsnāmēkō rakṣitumarhati ||

> **Meaning 44:** But the priests replied to the king, who was now cleansed of
> all sin, "You alone are worthy of ruling and protecting this entire earth!

---
45.
na bhūmyā kāryamasmākaṁ na hi śaktāḥ sma pālanē |
ratāḥ svādhyāyakaraṇē vayaṁ nityaṁ hi bhūmipa ||

> **Meaning 45:** We have no need for land, O King, nor can we govern it, for we
> are ever devoted to Vedic study.

---
46.
niṣkrayaṁ kiñcidēvēha prayacchatu bhavāniti |
maṇiratnaṁ suvarṇaṁ vā gāvō yadvā samudyatam |
tatprayaccha naraśrēṣṭha dharaṇyā na prayōjanam ||

> **Meaning 46:** Please grant us some compensation in exchange instead—whether
> gems, gold, cows, or whatever wealth you wish to offer. Give us that,
> foremost of men, for we have no need for the earth."

---
47.

ēvamuktō narapatiḥ brāhmaṇairvēdapāragaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 47:** When the Brāhmaṇas who had mastered the Vedas spoke thus to
> the king,

---
48.
gavāṁ śatasahasrāṇi daśa tēbhyō dadau nr̥paḥ |
daśakōṭiṁ suvarṇasya rajatasya caturguṇam ||

> **Meaning 48:** the king gave them one million cows, a hundred million gold
> coins, and four times that amount in silver.

---
49.
r̥tvijastu tataḥ sarvē pradaduḥ sahitā vasu |
r̥śyaśr̥ṅgāya munayē vasiṣṭhāya ca dhīmatē ||

> **Meaning 49:** Then all the priests together handed over that wealth to the
> sage Ṛśyaśṛṅga and to the wise Vasiṣṭha.

---
50.
tatastē nyāyataḥ kr̥tvā pravibhāgaṁ dvijōttamāḥ |
suprītamanasaḥ sarvē pratyūcurmuditā bhr̥śam ||

> **Meaning 50:** Those foremost sages then distributed that wealth equitably
> among all the priests. Deeply pleased and overjoyed, they all expressed their
> gratitude and blessings to the king.

---
51.
tataḥ prasarpakēbhyastu hiraṇyaṁ susamāhitaḥ |
jāmbūnadaṁ kōṭisaṅkhyaṁ brāhmaṇēbhyō dadau tadā ||

> **Meaning 51:** Then, with a focused mind, the king gave ten million pieces of
> Jāmbūnada gold to the Brāhmaṇas who had come to witness the yajña
> (prasarpakas).

---
52.
daridrāya dvijāyātha hastābharaṇamuttamam |
kasmaicidyācamānāya dadau rāghavanandanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 52:** Then, after all the treasury wealth had been given away, a poor
> Brāhmaṇa came seeking alms; Daśaratha, the joy of the Raghu dynasty, gave him
> the precious ornament from his own hand.

---
53.
tataḥ prītēṣu vidhivat dvijēṣu dvijavatsalaḥ |
praṇāmamakarōttēṣāṁ harṣavyākulitēndriyaḥ ||

> **Meaning 53:** When the Brāhmaṇas had been honored according to sacred rites
> and were well pleased, King Daśaratha, ever devoted to them, bowed in deep
> reverence before them, his whole being thrilled with joy.

---
54.
tasyāśiṣō'tha vividhā brāhmaṇaiḥ samudāhr̥tāḥ |
udārasya nr̥vīrasya dharaṇyāṁ patitasya ca ||

> **Meaning 54:** The Brāhmaṇas then pronounced many kinds of blessings on that
> generous hero among men as he lay prostrate on the ground.

---
55.
tataḥ prītamanā rājā prāpya yajñamanuttamam |
pāpāpahaṁ svarnayanaṁ dustaraṁ pārthivarṣabhaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 55:** Then the king was glad at heart, having completed that
> unsurpassed yajña, which destroys sin and leads to heaven, and which even the
> greatest kings find hard to achieve.

---
56.
tatō'bravīdr̥śyaśr̥ṅgaṁ rājā daśarathastadā |
kulasya vardhanaṁ tattu kartumarhasi suvrata ||

> **Meaning 56:** King Daśaratha said to Ṛśyaśṛṅga, "O sage of noble vows, please
> now perform the rite that will expand my lineage."

---
57.
tathēti ca sa rājānamuvāca dvijasattamaḥ |
bhaviṣyanti sutā rājaṁścatvārastē kulōdvahāḥ ||

> **Meaning 57:** "So be it," replied that foremost of Brāhmaṇas to the king.
> "Mahārāja, four sons will be born to you, who will carry your lineage
> forward."

---
58.
sa tasya vākyaṁ madhuraṁ niśamya
praṇamya tasmai prayatō nr̥pēndraḥ |
jagāma harṣaṁ paramaṁ mahātmā
taṁ r̥ṣyaśr̥ṅgaṁ punarapyuvāca ||

> **Meaning 58:** Hearing those sweet words, King Daśaratha bowed to him in
> deep reverence. Filled with supreme joy, the noble monarch addressed
> Ṛśyaśṛṅga once more.

---
