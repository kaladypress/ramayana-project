=== Sarga 14 ===

---
1.
medhāvī tu tato dhyātvā sa kiṁ cid idam uttamam |
labdhasaṁjñas tatas taṁ tu vedajño nr̥pam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
iṣṭiṁ te ’haṁ kariṣyāmi putrīyāṁ putrakāraṇāt |
atharvaśirasi proktair mantraiḥ siddhāṁ vidhānataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tataḥ prākramad iṣṭiṁ tāṁ putrīyāṁ putra kāraṇāt |
juhāva cāgnau tejasvī mantradr̥ṣṭena karmaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tato devāḥ sagandharvāḥ siddhāś ca paramarṣayaḥ |
bhāgapratigrahārthaṁ vai samavetā yathāvidhi ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tāḥ sametya yathānyāyaṁ tasmin sadasi devatāḥ |
abruvam̐l lokakartāraṁ brahmāṇaṁ vacanaṁ mahat ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
bhagavaṁs tvatprasādena rāvaṇo nāma rākṣasaḥ |
sarvānno bādhate vīryāc chāsituṁ taṁ na śaknumaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tvayā tasmai varo dattaḥ prītena bhagavan purā |
mānayantaś ca taṁ nityaṁ sarvaṁ tasya kṣamāmahe ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
udvejayati lokāṁs trīn ucchritān dveṣṭi durmatiḥ |
śakraṁ tridaśarājānaṁ pradharṣayitum icchati ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
r̥ṣīn yakṣān sagandharvān asurān brāhmaṇāṁs tathā |
atikrāmati durdharṣo varadānena mohitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
nainaṁ sūryaḥ pratapati pārśve vāti na mārutaḥ |
calormimālī taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā samudro ’pi na kampate ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tan mahan no bhayaṁ tasmād rākṣasād ghoradarśanāt |
vadhārthaṁ tasya bhagavann upāyaṁ kartum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
evam uktaḥ suraiḥ sarvaiś cintayitvā tato ’bravīt |
hantāyaṁ vihitas tasya vadhopāyo durātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tena gandharvayakṣāṇāṁ devadānavarakṣasām |
avadhyo ’smīti vāg uktā tathety uktaṁ ca tan mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
nākīrtayad avajñānāt tad rakṣo mānuṣāṁs tadā |
tasmāt sa mānuṣād vadhyo mr̥tyur nānyo ’sya vidyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
etac chrutvā priyaṁ vākyaṁ brahmaṇā samudāhr̥tam |
devā maharṣayaḥ sarve prahr̥ṣṭās te ’bhavaṁs tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
etasminn antare viṣṇur upayāto mahādyutiḥ |
brahmaṇā ca samāgamya tatra tasthau samāhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tam abruvan surāḥ sarve samabhiṣṭūya saṁnatāḥ |
tvāṁ niyokṣyāmahe viṣṇo lokānāṁ hitakāmyayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
rājño daśarathasya tvam ayodhyādhipater vibho |
dharmajñasya vadānyasya maharṣisamatejasaḥ |
tasya bhāryāsu tisr̥ṣu hrīśrīkīrtyupamāsu ca |
viṣṇo putratvam āgaccha kr̥tvātmānaṁ caturvidham ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tatra tvaṁ mānuṣo bhūtvā pravr̥ddhaṁ lokakaṇṭakam |
avadhyaṁ daivatair viṣṇo samare jahi rāvaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
sa hi devān sagandharvān siddhāṁś ca r̥ṣisattamān |
rākṣaso rāvaṇo mūrkho vīryotsekena bādhate ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tad uddhataṁ rāvaṇam r̥ddhatejasaṁ; pravr̥ddhadarpaṁ tridaśeśvaradviṣam |
virāvaṇaṁ sādhu tapasvikaṇṭakaṁ; tapasvinām uddhara taṁ bhayāvaham ||

=== meaning ===

---
