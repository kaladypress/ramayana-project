=== Sarga 39 ===
Sagara's Yajña; His Sons Dig Up the Earth

---
1.
viśvāmitravacaḥ śrutvā kathāntē raghunandana |
uvāca paramaprītō muniṁ dīptamivānalam ||

> **Meaning 1:** Having heard Viśvāmitra's words at the end of the story,
> Raghunandana, deeply pleased, spoke to the muni, who shone like a blazing
> fire:

---
2.
śrōtumichāmi bhadraṁ tē vistarēṇa kathāmimām |
pūrvakō mē kathaṁ brahmanyajñaṁ vai samupāharat ||

> **Meaning 2:** "Blessings upon you, revered sage—I want to hear this story in
> full detail: how did my ancestor perform that yajña?"

---
3.
viśvāmitrastu kākutsthamuvāca prahasanniva |
śrūyatāṁ vistarō rāma sagarasya mahātmanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 3:** With a gentle smile, Viśvāmitra replied to him, "Listen, Rāma,
> to the full story of the noble-souled Sagara.

---
4.
śaṅkaraśvaśurō nāma himavānacalōttamaḥ |
vindhyaparvatamāsādya nirīkṣētē parasparam ||

> **Meaning 4:** "The foremost of mountains, Himavān—known as Śaṅkara's
> father-in-law—and Mount Vindhya stand facing each other, gazing at one another
> from afar.

---
5.
tayōrmadhyē pravr̥ttō'bhūdyajñaḥ sa puruṣōttama |
sa hi dēśō naravyāghra praśastō yajñakarmaṇi ||

> **Meaning 5:** "Between those two mountains, Rāma, that yajña was begun—for
> that region is celebrated as ideal for performing yajñas.

---
6.
tasyāśvacaryāṁ kākutstha dr̥ḍhadhanvā mahārathaḥ |
aṁśumānakarōttāta sagarasya matē sthitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 6:** "Aṁśumān—a steadfast bowman and mighty mahāratha—guarded the
> wandering yajña horse, my dear Rāma, following Sagara's wishes.

---
7.
tasya parvaṇi taṁ yajñaṁ yajamānasya vāsavaḥ |
rākṣasīṁ tanumāsthāya yajñiyāśvamapāharat ||

> **Meaning 7:** "On a sacred parvan day during the yajña, Indra took on the
> form of a Rākṣasa and stole away the yajña horse.

---
8.
hriyamāṇē tu kākutstha tasminnaśvē mahātmanaḥ |
upādhyāya gaṇāḥ sarvē yajamānamathābruvan ||

> **Meaning 8:** "As the noble-souled king's horse was being carried off, Rāma,
> all the officiating priests spoke to the king:

---
9.
ayaṁ parvaṇi vēgēna yajñiyāśvō'panīyatē |
hartāraṁ jahi kākutstha hayaścaivōpanīyatām ||

> **Meaning 9:** "'Right on this sacred parvan day, the yajña horse is being
> swiftly carried away! Slay the thief, Sagara, and bring the horse back at
> once.

---
10.
yajñacchidraṁ bhavatyētatsarvēṣāmaśivāya naḥ |
tattathā kriyatāṁ rājanyathāchidraḥ kraturbhavēt ||

> **Meaning 10:** "'This is a breach in the yajña, bringing misfortune upon us
> all. Therefore, king, see to it that the yajña remains unbroken.'

---
11.
upādhyāya vacaḥ śrutvā tasminsadasi pārthivaḥ |
ṣaṣṭiṁ putrasahasrāṇi vākyamētaduvāca ha ||

> **Meaning 11:** "Hearing the priests' words in that yajña assembly, the king
> spoke to his sixty thousand sons:

---
12.
gatiṁ putrā na paśyāmi rakṣasāṁ puruṣarṣabhāḥ |
mantrapūtairmahābhāgairāsthitō hi mahākratuḥ ||

> **Meaning 12:** "'My sons, foremost among men, I see no trace of where the
> Rākṣasas have gone—even though this great yajña is attended by illustrious
> sages sanctified by mantras.

---
13.
tadgacchata vicinvadhvaṁ putrakā bhadramastu vaḥ |
samudramālinīṁ sarvāṁ pr̥thivīmanugacchata ||

> **Meaning 13:** "'Go then and search, my sons—blessings upon you! Traverse
> the entire earth, garlanded by the oceans.

---
14.
ēkaikaṁ yōjanaṁ putrā vistāramabhigacchata |
yāvatturagasandarśastāvatkhanata mēdinīm ||

> **Meaning 14:** "'Spread out so that each of you covers a full yojana in
> width, my sons, and dig down into the earth until you find the horse!

---
15.
tamcaiva hayahartāraṁ mārgamāṇā mamājñayā |
dīkṣitaḥ pautrasahitaḥ sōpādhyāyagaṇō hyaham |
iha sthāsyāmi bhadraṁ vō yāvatturagadarśanam ||

> **Meaning 15:** "'Search for the thief who stole the horse, by my command.
> Meanwhile, consecrated for the yajña, I will remain right here with my
> grandson Aṁśumān and the assembly of priests—blessings upon you—until the
> horse is recovered.'

---
16.
ityuktvā hr̥ṣṭamanasō rājaputrā mahābalāḥ |
jagmurmahītalaṁ rāma piturvacanayantritāḥ ||

> **Meaning 16:** "At these words, Rāma, the immensely powerful princes—glad at
> heart and bound by their father's command—went forth across the earth.

---
17.
yōjanāyāmavistāramēkaikō dharaṇītalam |
bibhiduḥ puruṣavyāghra vajrasparśasamairbhujaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 17:** "Each of them dug into a stretch of earth a yojana long and a
> yojana wide, Rāma, cleaving the ground with arms as hard as the vajra.

---
18.
śūlairaśanikalpaiśca halaiścāpi sudāruṇaiḥ |
bhidyamānā vasumatī nanāda raghunandana ||

> **Meaning 18:** "Torn apart by thunderbolt-like pikes and fearsome plows, the
> earth resounded with a mighty roar, Raghunandana.

---
19.
nāgānāṁ vadhyamānānāmasurāṇāṁ ca rāghava |
rākṣasānāṁ ca durdharṣaḥ sattvānāṁ ninadō'bhavat ||

> **Meaning 19:** "Rāghava, a terrible outcry arose from the Nāgas, Asuras,
> Rākṣasas, and all the subterranean beings being slaughtered there.

---
20.
yōjanānāṁ sahasrāṇi ṣaṣṭiṁ tu raghunandana |
bibhidurdharaṇīṁ vīrā rasātalamanuttamam ||

> **Meaning 20:** "For sixty thousand yojanas, Raghunandana, those mighty
> warriors tore through the earth all the way down to the depths of Rasātala.

---
21.
ēvaṁ parvatasambādhaṁ jambūdvīpaṁ nr̥pātmajāḥ |
khanantō nr̥paśārdūla sarvataḥ paricakramuḥ ||

> **Meaning 21:** "In this way, Rāma, the princes ranged across mountain-studded
> Jambūdvīpa, digging it up on every side.

---
22.
tatō dēvāḥ sagandharvāḥ sāsurāḥ sahapannagāḥ |
sambhrāntamanasaḥ sarvē pitāmahamupāgaman ||

> **Meaning 22:** "Then the devatās, together with the Gandharvas, Asuras, and
> great serpents—all deeply alarmed—went to Brahmā.

---
23.
tē prasādya mahātmānaṁ viṣaṇṇavadanāstadā |
ūcuḥ paramasantrastāḥ pitāmahamidaṁ vacaḥ ||

> **Meaning 23:** "With downcast faces and gripped by terror, they bowed before
> the noble-souled Brahmā and spoke:

---
24.
bhagavanpr̥thivī sarvā khanyatē sagarātmajaiḥ |
bahavaśca mahātmānō vadhyantē jalacāriṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 24:** "'Revered one, the entire earth is being dug up by Sagara's
> sons, and countless noble creatures of the waters are being slaughtered!

---
25.
ayaṁ yajñahanō'smākamanēnāśvō'panīyatē |
iti tē sarvabhūtāni nighnanti sagarātmajaḥ ||

> **Meaning 25:** "'"This is the destroyer of our yajña—this is the one who
> stole our horse!" Shouting this, those sons of Sagara are killing every
> creature!'"

---
