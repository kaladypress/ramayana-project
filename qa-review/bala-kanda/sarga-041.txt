=== Sarga 41 ===

---
1.
kāladharmaṁ gate rāma sagare prakr̥tījanāḥ |
rājānaṁ rocayām āsur aṁśumantaṁ sudhārmikam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sa rājā sumahān āsīd aṁśumān raghunandana |
tasya putro mahān āsīd dilīpa iti viśrutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tasmin rājyaṁ samāveśya dilīpe raghunandana |
himavacchikhare ramye tapas tepe sudāruṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
dvātriṁśac ca sahasrāṇi varṣāṇi sumahāyaśāḥ |
tapovanagato rājā svargaṁ lebhe tapodhanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
dilīpas tu mahātejāḥ śrutvā paitāmahaṁ vadham |
duḥkhopahatayā buddhyā niścayaṁ nādhyagacchata ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
kathaṁ gaṅgāvataraṇaṁ kathaṁ teṣāṁ jalakriyā |
tārayeyaṁ kathaṁ caitān iti cintā paro ’bhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tasya cintayato nityaṁ dharmeṇa viditātmanaḥ |
putro bhagīratho nāma jajñe paramadhārmikaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
dilīpas tu mahātejā yajñair bahubhir iṣṭavān |
triṁśadvarṣasahasrāṇi rājā rājyam akārayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
agatvā niścayaṁ rājā teṣām uddharaṇaṁ prati |
vyādhinā naraśārdūla kāladharmam upeyivān ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
indralokaṁ gato rājā svārjitenaiva karmaṇā |
rājye bhagīrathaṁ putram abhiṣicya nararṣabhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
bhagīrathas tu rājarṣir dhārmiko raghunandana |
anapatyo mahātejāḥ prajākāmaḥ sa cāprajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sa tapo dīrgham ātiṣṭhad gokarṇe raghunandana |
ūrdhvabāhuḥ pañcatapā māsāhāro jitendriyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tasya varṣasahasrāṇi ghore tapasi tiṣṭhataḥ |
suprīto bhagavān brahmā prajānāṁ patir īśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tataḥ suragaṇaiḥ sārdham upāgamya pitāmahaḥ |
bhagīrathaṁ mahātmānaṁ tapyamānam athābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
bhagīratha mahābhāga prītas te ’haṁ janeśvara |
tapasā ca sutaptena varaṁ varaya suvrata ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tam uvāca mahātejāḥ sarvalokapitāmaham |
bhagīratho mahābhāgaḥ kr̥tāñjalir avasthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
yadi me bhagavān prīto yady asti tapasaḥ phalam |
sagarasyātmajāḥ sarve mattaḥ salilam āpnuyuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
gaṅgāyāḥ salilaklinne bhasmany eṣāṁ mahātmanām |
svargaṁ gaccheyur atyantaṁ sarve me prapitāmahāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
deyā ca saṁtatir deva nāvasīdet kulaṁ ca naḥ |
ikṣvākūṇāṁ kule deva eṣa me ’stu varaḥ paraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
uktavākyaṁ tu rājānaṁ sarvalokapitāmahaḥ |
pratyuvāca śubhāṁ vāṇīṁ madhurāṁ madhurākṣarām ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
manoratho mahān eṣa bhagīratha mahāratha |
evaṁ bhavatu bhadraṁ te ikṣvākukulavardhana ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
iyaṁ haimavatī gaṅgā jyeṣṭhā himavataḥ sutā |
tāṁ vai dhārayituṁ rājan haras tatra niyujyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
gaṅgāyāḥ patanaṁ rājan pr̥thivī na sahiṣyate |
tāṁ vai dhārayituṁ vīra nānyaṁ paśyāmi śūlinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tam evam uktvā rājānaṁ gaṅgāṁ cābhāṣya lokakr̥t |
jagāma tridivaṁ devaḥ saha sarvair marudgaṇaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
