=== Sarga 41 ===
Aṁśumān Recovers the Yajña Horse; Garuḍa Counsels Him; Sagara Completes the Yajña

---
1.
putrāṁściragatāñjñātvā sagarō raghunandana |
naptāramabravīdrājā dīpyamānaṁ svatējasā ||

> **Meaning 1:** "Realizing that his sons had been gone for a long time,
> Raghunandana, King Sagara spoke to his grandson Aṁśumān, who shone with his
> own radiance:

---
2.
śūraśca kr̥tavidyaśca pūrvaistulyō'si tējasā |
pitr̥̄ṇāṁ gatimanviccha yēna cāśvō'pahāritaḥ ||

> **Meaning 2:** "'You are brave, accomplished in learning, and equal to your
> ancestors in splendor. Go search for what became of your uncles, and for the
> one who stole the horse.

---
3.
antarbhaumāni sattvāni vīryavanti mahānti ca |
tēṣāṁ tvaṁ pratighātārthaṁ sāsiṁ gr̥hṇīṣva kārmukam ||

> **Meaning 3:** "'Mighty and powerful creatures live beneath the earth; take
> your bow and sword to fend them off.

---
4.
abhivādyābhivādyāṁstvaṁ hatvā vighnakarānapi |
siddhārthaḥ sannivartasva mama yajñasya pāragaḥ ||

> **Meaning 4:** "'Pay respect to those who deserve reverence, strike down any
> who obstruct you, and return successful—bringing my yajña to completion.'"

---
5.
ēvamuktōṁ'śumānsamyaksagarēṇa mahātmanā |
dhanurādāya khaḍgaṁ ca jagāma laghuvikramaḥ ||

> **Meaning 5:** "Thus instructed by the noble-souled Sagara, Aṁśumān took up
> his bow and sword and set out with swift strides.

---
6.
sa khātaṁ pitr̥bhirmārgamantarbhaumaṁ mahātmabhiḥ |
prāpadyata naraśrēṣṭha tēna rājñābhicōditaḥ ||

> **Meaning 6:** "Rāma, spurred on by the king, he entered the underground
> passage dug by his great uncles.

---
7.
daityadānavarakṣōbhiḥ piśācapatagōragaiḥ |
pūjyamānaṁ mahātējā diśāgajamapaśyata ||

> **Meaning 7:** "There, the radiant prince saw the guardian elephant of that
> direction being worshipped by Daityas, Dānavas, Rākṣasas, Piśācas, birds, and
> serpents.

---
8.
sa taṁ pradakṣiṇaṁ kr̥tvā pr̥ṣṭvā caiva nirāmayam |
pitr̥̄nsa paripapraccha vājihartāramēva ca ||

> **Meaning 8:** "Walking clockwise around him and asking after his well-being,
> Aṁśumān inquired about his uncles and who had stolen the horse.

---
9.
diśāgajastu tacchrutvā prītyāhāṁśumatō vacaḥ |
āsamañjakr̥tārthastvaṁ sahāśvaḥ śīghramēṣyasi ||

> **Meaning 9:** "Hearing his question, the guardian elephant spoke warmly to
> Aṁśumān: 'Son of Asamañja, you will achieve your goal and soon return with the
> horse.'

---
10.
tasya tadvacanaṁ śrutvā sarvānēva diśāgajān |
yathākramaṁ yathānyāyaṁ praṣṭuṁ samupacakramē ||

> **Meaning 10:** "Hearing those words, he proceeded in due order and with
> proper courtesy to question all the other guardian elephants as well.

---
11.
taiśca sarvairdiśāpālairvākyajñairvākyakōvidaiḥ |
pūjitaḥ sahayaścaiva gantāsītyabhicōditaḥ ||

> **Meaning 11:** "Honored by all those eloquent, wise guardians of the
> directions, he was cheered on with the words, 'You will certainly return with
> the horse.'

---
12.
tēṣāṁ tadvacanaṁ śrutvā jagāma laghuvikramaḥ |
bhasmarāśīkr̥tā yatra pitarastasya sāgarāḥ ||

> **Meaning 12:** "Hearing their assurance, he hastened with swift strides to
> the spot where his uncles, the sons of Sagara, lay reduced to heaps of ash.

---
13.
sa duḥkhavaśamāpannastvasamañjasutastadā |
cukrōśa paramārtastu vadhāttēṣāṁ suduḥkhitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 13:** "Overcome by grief at their destruction, the son of Asamañja
> broke down and wept aloud in deep anguish.

---
14.
yajñiyaṁ ca hayaṁ tatra carantamavidūrataḥ |
dadarśa puruṣavyāghrō duḥkhaśōkasamanvitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 14:** "Filled with sorrow and grief, the prince spotted the yajña
> horse grazing not far away.

---
15.
dadarśa puruṣavyāghrō kartukāmō jalakriyām |
salilārthī mahātējā na cāpaśyajjalāśayam ||

> **Meaning 15:** "Wishing to offer tarpaṇa for his uncles, the radiant prince
> searched for water, but saw no stream or pool anywhere.

---
16.
visārya nipuṇāṁ dr̥ṣṭiṁ tatō'paśyatkhagādhipam |
pitr̥̄ṇāṁ mātulaṁ rāma suparṇamanilōpamam ||

> **Meaning 16:** "Rāma, casting a keen gaze all around, he then saw Garuḍa,
> king of birds—swift as the wind and maternal uncle to his uncles.

---
17.
sa cainamabravīdvākyaṁ vainatēyō mahābalaḥ |
mā śucaḥ puruṣavyāghra vadhō'yaṁ lōkasammataḥ ||

> **Meaning 17:** "The mighty Garuḍa spoke to him: 'Do not grieve, noble
> prince—their death was for the good of all the worlds.

---
18.
kapilēnāpramēyēna dagdhā hīmē mahābalāḥ |
salilaṁ nārhasi prājña dātumēṣāṁ hi laukikam ||

> **Meaning 18:** "'These mighty princes were consumed by the immeasurable
> Kapila; wise prince, you must not offer them ordinary earthly water.

---
19.
gaṅgā himavatō jyēṣṭhā duhitā puruṣarṣabha |
bhasmarāśīkr̥tānētānpāvayēllōkapāvanī ||

> **Meaning 19:** "'Gaṅgā, the eldest daughter of Himavān who sanctifies the
> worlds—she alone can cleanse these ashes, Aṁśumān.

---
20.
tayā klinnamidaṁ bhasma gaṅgayā lōkakāntayā |
ṣaṣṭiṁ putrasahasrāṇi svargalōkaṁ nayiṣyati ||

> **Meaning 20:** "'Once these ashes are moistened by Gaṅgā, beloved by all the
> worlds, she will lead the sixty thousand sons to svarga.

---
21.
gaccha cāśvaṁ mahābhāga saṅgr̥hya puruṣarṣabha |
yajñaṁ paitāmahaṁ vīra nirvartayitumarhasi ||

> **Meaning 21:** "'Now take the horse and go, illustrious prince—complete your
> grandfather's yajña.'"

---
22.
suparṇavacanaṁ śrutvā sōṁ'śumānativīryavān |
tvaritaṁ hayamādāya punarāyānmahāyaśāḥ ||

> **Meaning 22:** "Hearing Garuḍa's words, the immensely powerful and famous
> Aṁśumān swiftly took the horse and returned.

---
23.
tatō rājānamāsādya dīkṣitaṁ raghunandana |
nyavēdayadyathāvr̥ttaṁ suparṇavacanaṁ tathā ||

> **Meaning 23:** "Rāma, approaching the consecrated king, he reported
> everything just as it had happened, along with what Garuḍa had told him.

---
24.
tacchrutvā ghōrasaṅkāśaṁ vākyamaṁśumatō nr̥paḥ |
yajñaṁ nirvartayāmāsa yathākalpaṁ yathāvidhi ||

> **Meaning 24:** "Hearing Aṁśumān's dreadful report, the king nonetheless
> completed the yajña in strict accordance with every rule and sacred
> ordinance.

---
25.
svapuraṁ cāgamacchrīmāniṣṭayajñō mahīpatiḥ |
gaṅgāyāścāgamē rājā niścayaṁ nādhyagacchata ||

> **Meaning 25:** "With his yajña fulfilled, the illustrious monarch returned to
> his capital, yet he could find no way to bring Gaṅgā down to earth.

---
26.
agatvā niścayaṁ rājā kālēna mahatā mahān |
triṁśadvarṣasahasrāṇi rājyaṁ kr̥tvā divaṁ gataḥ ||

> **Meaning 26:** "Without ever finding a way over that long span of time—having
> ruled for thirty thousand years—the great king ascended to svarga.

---
