=== Sarga 22 ===
Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa Depart with Viśvāmitra; Initiation into Balā and Atibalā

---
1.
tathā vasiṣṭhē bruvati rājā daśarathaḥ sutam |
prahr̥ṣṭavadanō rāmamājuhāva salakṣmaṇam ||

> **Meaning 1:** While Vasiṣṭha was still speaking, King Daśaratha, his face
> glowing with joy, summoned his son Rāma along with Lakṣmaṇa.

---
2.
kr̥tasvastyayanaṁ mātrā pitrā daśarathēna ca |
purōdhasā vasiṣṭhēna maṅgalairabhimantritam ||

> **Meaning 2:** His mother Kausalyā and his father Daśaratha performed the
> protective blessing rites for Rāma's journey, and the royal priest Vasiṣṭha
> consecrated him with auspicious mantras.

---
3.
sa putraṁ mūrdhnyupāghrāya rājā daśarathaḥ priyam |
dadau kuśikaputrāya suprītēnāntarātmanā ||

> **Meaning 3:** King Daśaratha kissed his dear son on the crown of the head
> and, with a deeply gladdened heart, entrusted him to Viśvāmitra, the son of
> Kuśika.

---
4.
tatō vāyuḥ sukhasparśō virajaskō vavau tadā |
viśvāmitragataṁ rāmaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rājīvalōcanam ||

> **Meaning 4:** Then, seeing the lotus-eyed Rāma set out with Viśvāmitra, a
> pleasant breeze began to blow, cool to the touch and free of dust.

---
5.
puṣpavr̥ṣṭirmahatyāsīddēvadundubhinisvanaḥ |
śaṅkhadundubhinirghōṣaḥ prayātē tu mahātmani ||

> **Meaning 5:** A great shower of flowers fell from the sky, accompanied by the
> sound of divine dundubhis, and conches and drums resounded as the great-souled
> Rāma set out.

---
6.
viśvāmitrō yayāvagrē tatō rāmō mahāyaśāḥ |
kākapakṣadharō dhanvī taṁ ca saumitriranvagāt ||

> **Meaning 6:** Viśvāmitra walked in front. Behind him came the renowned Rāma,
> bow in hand, wearing his hair in side-locks (kākapakṣa), and Saumitri followed
> close behind.

---
7.
kalāpinau dhanuṣpāṇī śōbhayānau diśō daśa |
viśvāmitraṁ mahātmānaṁ triśīrṣāviva pannagau ||

> **Meaning 7:** Carrying quivers and bows in hand, lighting up all ten
> directions, the two princes walked behind the great-souled Viśvāmitra like
> two three-headed serpents.

---
8.
anujagmaturakṣudrau pitāmahamivāśvinau |
tadā kuśikaputraṁ tu dhanuṣpāṇī svalaṁkr̥tau ||

> **Meaning 8:** Noble in spirit, richly adorned, and carrying their bows, they
> followed the son of Kuśika like the two Aśvins following Pitāmaha.

---
9.
kumārau cāruvapuṣau bhrātarau rāmalakṣmaṇau |
baddhagōdhāṅgulitrāṇau khaḍgavantau mahādyutī ||

> **Meaning 9:** Those two handsome youths, the brothers Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa, wore
> finger-guards of monitor-lizard skin, carried swords, and shone with great
> radiance.

---
10.
anuyātau śriyā dīptau śōbhayētāmaninditau |
sthāṇuṁ dēvamivācintyaṁ kumārāviva pāvakī ||

> **Meaning 10:** Blameless and blazing with splendor as they followed him, they
> adorned Viśvāmitra just as the two Kumāras, sons of Pāvaka (Agni), adorn the
> inconceivable god Sthāṇu (Śiva).

---
11.
adhyardhayōjanaṁ gatvā sarayvā dakṣiṇē taṭē |
rāmēti madhurā vāṇīṁ viśvāmitrō'bhyabhāṣata ||

> **Meaning 11:** After they had walked a yojana and a half and reached the
> southern bank of the Sarayū, Viśvāmitra called out, "Rāma," in a gentle,
> sweet voice and spoke to him.

---
12.
gr̥hāṇa vatsa salilaṁ mā bhūtkālasya paryayaḥ |
mantragrāmaṁ gr̥hāṇa tvaṁ balāmatibalāṁ tathā ||

> **Meaning 12:** "Take water for ācamana, child, and let no time be lost.
> Receive from me this collection of mantras, along with Balā and Atibalā."

---
13.
na śramō na jvarō vā tē na rūpasya viparyayaḥ |
na ca suptaṁ pramattaṁ vā dharṣayiṣyanti nairr̥tāḥ ||

> **Meaning 13:** "With them, you will feel neither fatigue nor fever, nor will
> your form ever fade. And the Rākṣasas (Nairṛtas) will never overpower you,
> whether you are asleep or off your guard."

---
14.
na bāhvōḥ sadr̥śō vīryē pr̥thivyāmasti kaścana |
triṣu lōkēṣu vā rāma na bhavētsadr̥śastava ||

> **Meaning 14:** "Rāma, because of them, there is no one on earth equal to you
> in the strength of your arms, nor will anyone in the three worlds ever be
> your equal."

---
15.
na saubhāgyē na dākṣiṇyē na jñānē buddhiniścayē |
nōttarē pratipattavyō samō lōkē tavānagha ||

> **Meaning 15:** "Neither in good fortune, nor in graciousness, nor in
> knowledge, nor in firmness of judgment, nor in giving a ready reply is there
> anyone in the world equal to you, blameless one."

---
16.
ētadvidyādvayē labdhē bhavitā nāsti tē samaḥ |
balātvatibalā caiva sarvajñānasya mātarau ||

> **Meaning 16:** "Once you have gained these two vidyās, there will be no one
> like you. Balā and Atibalā are the very mothers of all knowledge."

---
17.
kṣutpipāsē na tē rāma bhaviṣyētē narōttama |
balāmatibalāṁ caiva paṭhataḥ pathi rāghava |
gr̥hāṇa sarvalōkasya guptayē raghunandana ||

> **Meaning 17:** "Rāma, best of men, hunger and thirst will never come upon
> you as you recite Balā and Atibalā along the way. Delight of the Raghus,
> accept them for the protection of the entire world."

---
18.
vidyādvayamadhīyānē yaśaścāpyatulaṁ bhuvi |
pitāmahasutē hyētē vidyē tējaḥsamanvitē |
pradātuṁ tava kākutstha sadr̥śastvaṁ hi dhārmika ||

> **Meaning 18:** "By studying these two vidyās, you will also win unmatched
> fame on earth. These radiant vidyās are the daughters of Pitāmaha, and you,
> righteous Kākutstha, are truly worthy of receiving them."

---
19.
kāmaṁ bahuguṇāḥ sarvē tvayyētē nātra saṁśayaḥ |
tapasā sambhr̥tē caitē bahurūpē bhaviṣyataḥ ||

> **Meaning 19:** "Indeed, all these many virtues are already yours, of that
> there is no doubt. Nurtured by tapas, these two vidyās will flourish and
> expand in many ways for you."

---
20.
tatō rāmō jalaṁ spr̥ṣṭvā prahr̥ṣṭavadanaḥ śuciḥ |
pratijagrāha tē vidyē maharṣērbhāvitātmanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 20:** Then Rāma touched the water in purification, his face beaming
> with joy, and received those two vidyās from the great sage Viśvāmitra of
> disciplined mind.

---
21.
vidyāsamuditō rāmaḥ śuśubhē bhūrivikramaḥ |
sahasraraśmirbhagavān śaradīva divākaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 21:** Endowed with those vidyās, the mighty Rāma shone brilliantly,
> like the thousand-rayed sun in the autumn sky.

---
22.
gurukāryāṇi sarvāṇi niyujya kuśikātmajē |
ūṣustāṁ rajanīṁ tatra sarayvāṁ susukhaṁ trayaḥ ||

> **Meaning 22:** After attending to all the duties owed to their guru
> Viśvāmitra, the son of Kuśika, the three of them spent that night very
> comfortably there on the bank of the Sarayū.

---
23.
daśarathanr̥pasūnusattamābhyāṁ
tr̥ṇaśayanē'nucitē sahōṣitābhyām |
kuśikasutavacō'nulālitābhyāṁ |
sukhamiva sā vibabhau vibhāvarī ca ||

> **Meaning 23:** Though those two noblest sons of King Daśaratha were
> unaccustomed to sleeping on beds of grass, soothed by the gentle words of
> Viśvāmitra, Kuśika's son, that night passed peacefully and happily for them.

---
