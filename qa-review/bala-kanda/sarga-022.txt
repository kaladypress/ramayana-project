=== Sarga 22 ===

---
1.
prabhātāyāṁ tu śarvaryāṁ viśvāmitro mahāmuniḥ |
abhyabhāṣata kākutsthaṁ śayānaṁ parṇasaṁstare ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
kausalyā suprajā rāma pūrvā saṁdhyā pravartate |
uttiṣṭha naraśārdūla kartavyaṁ daivam āhnikam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tasyarṣeḥ paramodāraṁ vacaḥ śrutvā nr̥pātmajau |
snātvā kr̥todakau vīrau jepatuḥ paramaṁ japam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
kr̥tāhnikau mahāvīryau viśvāmitraṁ tapodhanam |
abhivādyābhisaṁhr̥ṣṭau gamanāyopatasthatuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tau prayāte mahāvīryau divyaṁ tripathagāṁ nadīm |
dadr̥śāte tatas tatra sarayvāḥ saṁgame śubhe ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tatrāśramapadaṁ puṇyam r̥ṣīṇām ugratejasām |
bahuvarṣasahasrāṇi tapyatāṁ paramaṁ tapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā paramaprītau rāghavau puṇyam āśramam |
ūcatus taṁ mahātmānaṁ viśvāmitram idaṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
kasyāyam āśramaḥ puṇyaḥ ko nv asmin vasate pumān |
bhagavañ śrotum icchāvaḥ paraṁ kautūhalaṁ hi nau ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tayos tad vacanaṁ śrutvā prahasya munipuṁgavaḥ |
abravīc chrūyatāṁ rāma yasyāyaṁ pūrva āśramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
kandarpo mūrtimān āsīt kāma ity ucyate budhaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tapasyantam iha sthāṇuṁ niyamena samāhitam |
kr̥todvāhaṁ tu deveśaṁ gacchantaṁ samarudgaṇam |
dharṣayām āsa durmedhā huṁkr̥taś ca mahātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
dagdhasya tasya raudreṇa cakṣuṣā raghunandana |
vyaśīryanta śarīrāt svāt sarvagātrāṇi durmateḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tasya gātraṁ hataṁ tatra nirdagdhasya mahātmanā |
aśarīraḥ kr̥taḥ kāmaḥ krodhād deveśvareṇa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
anaṅga iti vikhyātas tadā prabhr̥ti rāghava |
sa cāṅgaviṣayaḥ śrīmān yatrāṅgaṁ sa mumoca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tasyāyam āśramaḥ puṇyas tasyeme munayaḥ purā |
śiṣyā dharmaparā vīra teṣāṁ pāpaṁ na vidyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
ihādya rajanīṁ rāma vasema śubhadarśana |
puṇyayoḥ saritor madhye śvas tariṣyāmahe vayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
teṣāṁ saṁvadatāṁ tatra tapo dīrgheṇa cakṣuṣā |
vijñāya paramaprītā munayo harṣam āgaman ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
arghyaṁ pādyaṁ tathātithyaṁ nivedyakuśikātmaje |
rāmalakṣmaṇayoḥ paścād akurvann atithikriyām ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
satkāraṁ samanuprāpya kathābhir abhirañjayan |
nyavasan susukhaṁ tatra kāmāśramapade tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
