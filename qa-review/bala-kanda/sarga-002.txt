=== Sarga 2 ===

---
1.
nāradasya tu tad vākyaṁ śrutvā vākyaviśāradaḥ |
pūjayām āsa dharmātmā sahaśiṣyo mahāmuniḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
yathāvat pūjitas tena devarṣir nāradas tadā |
āpr̥ṣṭvaivābhyanujñātaḥ sa jagāma vihāyasam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sa muhūrtaṁ gate tasmin devalokaṁ munis tadā |
jagāma tamasātīraṁ jāhnavyās tv avidūrataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
sa tu tīraṁ samāsādya tamasāyā mahāmuniḥ |
śiṣyam āha sthitaṁ pārśve dr̥ṣṭvā tīrtham akardamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
akardamam idaṁ tīrthaṁ bharadvāja niśāmaya |
ramaṇīyaṁ prasannāmbu sanmanuṣyamano yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
nyasyatāṁ kalaśas tāta dīyatāṁ valkalaṁ mama |
idam evāvagāhiṣye tamasātīrtham uttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
evam ukto bharadvājo vālmīkena mahātmanā |
prāyacchata munes tasya valkalaṁ niyato guroḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sa śiṣyahastād ādāya valkalaṁ niyatendriyaḥ |
vicacāra ha paśyaṁs tat sarvato vipulaṁ vanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tasyābhyāśe tu mithunaṁ carantam anapāyinam |
dadarśa bhagavāṁs tatra krauñcayoś cārunisvanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tasmāt tu mithunād ekaṁ pumāṁsaṁ pāpaniścayaḥ |
jaghāna vairanilayo niṣādas tasya paśyataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
taṁ śoṇitaparītāṅgaṁ veṣṭamānaṁ mahītale |
bhāryā tu nihataṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rurāva karuṇāṁ giram ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tathā tu taṁ dvijaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā niṣādena nipātitam |
r̥ṣer dharmātmanas tasya kāruṇyaṁ samapadyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tataḥ karuṇaveditvād adharmo ’yam iti dvijaḥ |
niśāmya rudatīṁ krauñcīm idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
mā niṣāda pratiṣṭhāṁ tvam agamaḥ śāśvatīḥ samāḥ |
yat krauñcamithunād ekam avadhīḥ kāmamohitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tasyaivaṁ bruvataś cintā babhūva hr̥di vīkṣataḥ |
śokārtenāsya śakuneḥ kim idaṁ vyāhr̥taṁ mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
cintayan sa mahāprājñaś cakāra matimān matim |
śiṣyaṁ caivābravīd vākyam idaṁ sa munipuṁgavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
pādabaddho ’kṣarasamas tantrīlayasamanvitaḥ |
śokārtasya pravr̥tto me śloko bhavatu nānyathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
śiṣyas tu tasya bruvato muner vākyam anuttamam |
pratijagrāha saṁhr̥ṣṭas tasya tuṣṭo ’bhavad guruḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
so ’bhiṣekaṁ tataḥ kr̥tvā tīrthe tasmin yathāvidhi |
tam eva cintayann artham upāvartata vai muniḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
bharadvājas tataḥ śiṣyo vinītaḥ śrutavān guroḥ |
kalaśaṁ pūrṇam ādāya pr̥ṣṭhato ’nujagāma ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
sa praviśyāśramapadaṁ śiṣyeṇa saha dharmavit |
upaviṣṭaḥ kathāś cānyāś cakāra dhyānam āsthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
ājagāma tato brahmā lokakartā svayaṁ prabhuḥ |
caturmukho mahātejā draṣṭuṁ taṁ munipuṁgavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
vālmīkir atha taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā sahasotthāya vāg yataḥ |
prāñjaliḥ prayato bhūtvā tasthau paramavismitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
pūjayām āsa taṁ devaṁ pādyārghyāsanavandanaiḥ |
praṇamya vidhivac cainaṁ pr̥ṣṭvānāmayam avyayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
athopaviśya bhagavān āsane paramārcite |
vālmīkaye maharṣaye saṁdideśāsanaṁ tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
upaviṣṭe tadā tasmin sākṣāl lokapitāmahe |
tad gatenaiva manasā vālmīkir dhyānam āsthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
pāpātmanā kr̥taṁ kaṣṭaṁ vairagrahaṇabuddhinā |
yas tādr̥śaṁ cāruravaṁ krauñcaṁ hanyād akāraṇāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
śocann eva muhuḥ krauñcīm upaślokam imaṁ punaḥ |
jagāv antargatamanā bhūtvā śokaparāyaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
tam uvāca tato brahmā prahasan munipuṁgavam |
śloka eva tvayā baddho nātra kāryā vicāraṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
macchandād eva te brahman pravr̥tteyaṁ sarasvatī |
rāmasya caritaṁ sarvaṁ kuru tvam r̥ṣisattama ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
dharmātmano guṇavato loke rāmasya dhīmataḥ |
vr̥ttaṁ kathaya dhīrasya yathā te nāradāc chrutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
rahasyaṁ ca prakāśaṁ ca yad vr̥ttaṁ tasya dhīmataḥ |
rāmasya saha saumitre rākṣasānāṁ ca sarvaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
vaidehyāś caiva yad vr̥ttaṁ prakāśaṁ yadi vā rahaḥ |
tac cāpy aviditaṁ sarvaṁ viditaṁ te bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
na te vāg anr̥tā kāvye kā cid atra bhaviṣyati |
kuru rāmakathāṁ puṇyāṁ ślokabaddhāṁ manoramām ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
yāvat sthāsyanti girayaḥ saritaś ca mahītale |
tāvad rāmāyaṇakathā lokeṣu pracariṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
yāvad rāmasya ca kathā tvatkr̥tā pracariṣyati |
tāvad ūrdhvam adhaś ca tvaṁ mallokeṣu nivatsyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
ity uktvā bhagavān brahmā tatraivāntaradhīyata |
tataḥ saśiṣyo vālmīkir munir vismayam āyayau ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
tasya śiṣyās tataḥ sarve jaguḥ ślokam imaṁ punaḥ |
muhur muhuḥ prīyamāṇāḥ prāhuś ca bhr̥śavismitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
samākṣaraiś caturbhir yaḥ pādair gīto maharṣiṇā |
so ’nuvyāharaṇād bhūyaḥ śokaḥ ślokatvam āgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
tasya buddhir iyaṁ jātā vālmīker bhāvitātmanaḥ |
kr̥tsnaṁ rāmāyaṇaṁ kāvyam īdr̥śaiḥ karavāṇy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
udāravr̥ttārthapadair manoramais; tadāsya rāmasya cakāra kīrtimān |
samākṣaraiḥ ślokaśatair yaśasvino; yaśaskaraṁ kāvyam udāradhīr muniḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
