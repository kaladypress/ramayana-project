=== Sarga 63 ===

---
1.
surakāryam idaṁ rambhe kartavyaṁ sumahat tvayā |
lobhanaṁ kauśikasyeha kāmamohasamanvitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tathoktā sāpsarā rāma sahasrākṣeṇa dhīmatā |
vrīḍitā prāñjalir bhūtvā pratyuvāca sureśvaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
ayaṁ surapate ghoro viśvāmitro mahāmuniḥ |
krodham utsrakṣyate ghoraṁ mayi deva na saṁśayaḥ |
tato hi me bhayaṁ deva prasādaṁ kartum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tām uvāca sahasrākṣo vepamānāṁ kr̥tāñjalim |
mā bhaiṣi rambhe bhadraṁ te kuruṣva mama śāsanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
kokilo hr̥dayagrāhī mādhave ruciradrume |
ahaṁ kandarpasahitaḥ sthāsyāmi tava pārśvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tvaṁ hi rūpaṁ bahuguṇaṁ kr̥tvā paramabhāsvaram |
tam r̥ṣiṁ kauśikaṁ rambhe bhedayasva tapasvinam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sā śrutvā vacanaṁ tasya kr̥tvā rūpam anuttamam |
lobhayām āsa lalitā viśvāmitraṁ śucismitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
kokilasya tu śuśrāva valgu vyāharataḥ svanam |
saṁprahr̥ṣṭena manasā tata enām udaikṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
atha tasya ca śabdena gītenāpratimena ca |
darśanena ca rambhāyā muniḥ saṁdeham āgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
sahasrākṣasya tat karma vijñāya munipuṁgavaḥ |
rambhāṁ krodhasamāviṣṭaḥ śaśāpa kuśikātmajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
yan māṁ lobhayase rambhe kāmakrodhajayaiṣiṇam |
daśavarṣasahasrāṇi śailī sthāsyasi durbhage ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
brāhmaṇaḥ sumahātejās tapobalasamanvitaḥ |
uddhariṣyati rambhe tvāṁ matkrodhakaluṣīkr̥tām ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
evam uktvā mahātejā viśvāmitro mahāmuniḥ |
aśaknuvan dhārayituṁ kopaṁ saṁtāpam āgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tasya śāpena mahatā rambhā śailī tadābhavat |
vacaḥ śrutvā ca kandarpo maharṣeḥ sa ca nirgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
kopena sa mahātejās tapo ’paharaṇe kr̥te |
indriyair ajitai rāma na lebhe śāntim ātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
