=== Sarga 31 ===

---
1.
brahmayonir mahān āsīt kuśo nāma mahātapāḥ |
vaidarbhyāṁ janayām āsa caturaḥ sadr̥śān sutān |
kuśāmbaṁ kuśanābhaṁ ca ādhūrtarajasaṁ vasum ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
dīptiyuktān mahotsāhān kṣatradharmacikīrṣayā |
tān uvāca kuśaḥ putrān dharmiṣṭhān satyavādinaḥ |
kriyatāṁ pālanaṁ putrā dharmaṁ prāpsyatha puṣkalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
kuśasya vacanaṁ śrutvā catvāro lokasaṁmatāḥ |
niveśaṁ cakrire sarve purāṇāṁ nr̥varās tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
kuśāmbas tu mahātejāḥ kauśāmbīm akarot purīm |
kuśanābhas tu dharmātmā paraṁ cakre mahodayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
ādhūrtarajaso rāma dharmāraṇyaṁ mahīpatiḥ |
cakre puravaraṁ rājā vasuś cakre girivrajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
eṣā vasumatī rāma vasos tasya mahātmanaḥ |
ete śailavarāḥ pañca prakāśante samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sumāgadhī nadī ramyā māgadhān viśrutāyayau |
pañcānāṁ śailamukhyānāṁ madhye māleva śobhate ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
saiṣā hi māgadhī rāma vasos tasya mahātmanaḥ |
pūrvābhicaritā rāma sukṣetrā sasyamālinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
kuśanābhas tu rājarṣiḥ kanyāśatam anuttamam |
janayām āsa dharmātmā ghr̥tācyāṁ raghunandana ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tās tu yauvanaśālinyo rūpavatyaḥ svalaṁkr̥tāḥ |
udyānabhūmim āgamya prāvr̥ṣīva śatahradāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
gāyantyo nr̥tyamānāś ca vādayantyaś ca rāghava |
āmodaṁ paramaṁ jagmur varābharaṇabhūṣitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
atha tāś cārusarvāṅgyo rūpeṇāpratimā bhuvi |
udyānabhūmim āgamya tārā iva ghanāntare ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tāḥ sarvaguṇasaṁpannā rūpayauvanasaṁyutāḥ |
dr̥ṣṭvā sarvātmako vāyur idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
ahaṁ vaḥ kāmaye sarvā bhāryā mama bhaviṣyatha |
mānuṣas tyajyatāṁ bhāvo dīrgham āyur avāpsyatha ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā vāyor akliṣṭakarmaṇaḥ |
apahāsya tato vākyaṁ kanyāśatam athābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
antaś carasi bhūtānāṁ sarveṣāṁ tvaṁ surottama |
prabhāvajñāś ca te sarvāḥ kim asmān avamanyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
kuśanābhasutāḥ sarvāḥ samarthās tvāṁ surottama |
sthānāc cyāvayituṁ devaṁ rakṣāmas tu tapo vayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
mā bhūt sa kālo durmedhaḥ pitaraṁ satyavādinam |
nāvamanyasva dharmeṇa svayaṁ varam upāsmahe ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
pitā hi prabhur asmākaṁ daivataṁ paramaṁ hi saḥ |
yasya no dāsyati pitā sa no bhartā bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tāsāṁ tad vacanaṁ śrutvā vāyuḥ paramakopanaḥ |
praviśya sarvagātrāṇi babhañja bhagavān prabhuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tāḥ kanyā vāyunā bhagnā viviśur nr̥pater gr̥ham |
dr̥ṣṭvā bhagnās tadā rājā saṁbhrānta idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
kim idaṁ kathyatāṁ putryaḥ ko dharmam avamanyate |
kubjāḥ kena kr̥tāḥ sarvā veṣṭantyo nābhibhāṣatha ||

=== meaning ===

---
