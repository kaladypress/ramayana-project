=== Sarga 31 ===
Departure from Siddhāśrama; Viśvāmitra Leads the Princes and Sages Toward Mithilā

---
1.
atha tāṁ rajanīṁ tatra kr̥tārthau rāmalakṣaṇau |
ūṣaturmuditau vīrau prahr̥ṣṭēnāntarātmanā ||

> **Meaning 1:** Having fulfilled their purpose, the valiant Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa
> spent that night there joyfully, with deeply delighted hearts.

---
2.
prabhātāyāṁ tu śarvaryāṁ kr̥tapaurvāhṇikakriyau |
viśvāmitramr̥ṣīṁścānyānsahitāvabhijagmatuḥ ||

> **Meaning 2:** At daybreak, after performing their morning rites, the two
> brothers went together to Viśvāmitra and the other ṛṣis.

---
3.
abhivādya muniśrēṣṭhaṁ jvalantamiva pāvakam |
ūcaturmadhurōdāraṁ vākyaṁ madhurabhāṣiṇau ||

> **Meaning 3:** Bowing respectfully to that foremost sage, who shone like a
> blazing fire, the two sweet-spoken princes addressed him in gentle and noble
> words:

---
4.
imau svō muniśārdūla kiṅkarau samupasthitau |
ājñāpaya yathēṣṭaṁ vai śāsanaṁ karavāva kim ||

> **Meaning 4:** "Here we stand before you as your servants, great sage. Command
> us as you wish—what order would you like us to carry out?"

---
5.
ēvamuktē tatastābhyāṁ sarva ēva maharṣayaḥ |
viśvāmitraṁ puraskr̥tya rāmaṁ vacanamabruvan ||

> **Meaning 5:** When the two had spoken, all the maharṣis, with Viśvāmitra at
> their head, said to Rāma:

---
6.
maithilasya naraśrēṣṭha janakasya bhaviṣyati |
yajñaḥ paramadharmiṣṭhastatra yāsyāmahē vayam ||

> **Meaning 6:** "Foremost among men, King Janaka of Mithilā is going to perform
> a most righteous yajña, and we are heading there.

---
7.
tvaṁ caiva naraśārdūla sahāsmābhirgamiṣyasi |
adbhutaṁ ca dhanūratnaṁ tatra tvaṁ draṣṭumarhasi ||

> **Meaning 7:** "You too should come with us, brave prince, and there you will
> see a wondrous jewel of a bow.

---
8.
taddhi pūrvaṁ naraśrēṣṭha dattaṁ sadasi daivataiḥ |
apramēyabalaṁ ghōraṁ makhē paramabhāsvaram ||

> **Meaning 8:** "Best of men, long ago the devatās presented that formidable,
> supremely radiant bow of immeasurable power in the assembly at a yajña.

---
9.
nāsya dēvā na gandharvā nāsurā na ca rākṣasāḥ |
kartumārōpaṇaṁ śaktā na kathañcana mānuṣāḥ ||

> **Meaning 9:** "Neither devatās, nor gandharvas, nor asuras, nor rākṣasas, nor
> humans have ever been able to string it by any means.

---
10.
dhanuṣastasya vīryaṁ hi jijñāsantō mahīkṣitaḥ |
na śēkurārōpayituṁ rājaputrā mahābalāḥ ||

> **Meaning 10:** "Kings and mighty princes, eager to test the bow's strength,
> have all failed to string it.

---
11.
taddhanurnaraśārdūla maithilasya mahātmanaḥ |
tatra drakṣyasi kākutstha yajñaṁ cādbhutadarśanam ||

> **Meaning 11:** "There, brave Rāma, you will see that bow belonging to the
> great-souled ruler of Mithilā, along with a truly marvelous yajña.

---
12.
taddhi yajñaphalaṁ tēna maithilēnōttamaṁ dhanuḥ |
yācitaṁ naraśārdūla sunābhaṁ sarvadaivataiḥ ||

> **Meaning 12:** "In fact, mighty prince, that king of Mithilā had asked all
> the devatās for that supreme, finely shaped bow as the fruit of his yajña.

---
13.
āyāgabhūtam nr̥patēḥ tasya vēśmani rāghava |
arcitam vividhaiḥ gandhaiḥ dhūpaiḥ ca aguru gandhbhiḥ ||

> **Meaning 13:** "Kept as a sacred object of worship in that king's palace,
> Rāghava, it is honored with all kinds of fragrant pastes and aguru-scented
> incense."

---
14.
ēvamuktvā munivaraḥ prasthānamakarōttadā |
sarṣisaṅghaḥ sakākutstha āmantrya vanadēvatāḥ ||

> **Meaning 14:** Having said this, the foremost sage Viśvāmitra set out with
> Rāma, Lakṣmaṇa, and the company of ṛṣis, first taking leave of the presiding
> devatās of the forest:

---
15.
svasti vō'stu gamiṣyāmi siddhaḥ siddhāśramādaham |
uttarē jāhnavītīrē himavantaṁ śilōccayam ||

> **Meaning 15:** "May all be well with you! Having fulfilled my vow, I am
> leaving Siddhāśrama for the northern bank of the Jāhnavī, toward the Himavān
> mountain."

---
16.
pradakṣiṇaṁ tataḥ kr̥tvā siddhāśramamanuttamam |
uttarāṁ diśamuddiśya prasthātumupacakramē ||

> **Meaning 16:** Then, after circumambulating the matchless Siddhāśrama, he set
> out toward the north.

---
17.
taṁ vrajantaṁ munivaramanvagādanusāriṇām |
śakaṭī śatamātraṁ tu prayāṇē brahmavādinām ||

> **Meaning 17:** As that foremost sage walked on, a train of about a hundred
> carts belonging to his followers, the Vedic scholars, traveled behind him.

---
18.
mr̥gapakṣigaṇāścaiva siddhāśramanivāsinaḥ |
anujagmurmahātmānaṁ viśvāmitraṁ mahāmunim |
nivartayāmāsa tataḥ sa r̥si sanghaḥ sa pakṣiṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 18:** The deer and flocks of birds living in Siddhāśrama also
> followed the great-souled sage Viśvāmitra, until he and the company of ṛṣis
> gently turned them back.

---
19.
tē gatvā dūramadhvānaṁ lambamānē divākarē |
vāsaṁ cakrurmunigaṇāḥ śōṇākūlē samāhitāḥ ||

> **Meaning 19:** After traveling a long distance, as the sun sank low, the
> composed company of sages made camp on the bank of the Śoṇā river.

---
20.
tē'staṁ gatē dinakarē snātvā hutahutāśanāḥ |
viśvāmitraṁ puraskr̥tya niṣēduramitaujasaḥ ||

> **Meaning 20:** Once the sun had set, those sages of boundless vigor bathed,
> offered oblations to the sacred fire, and sat down with Viśvāmitra at their
> head.

---
21.
rāmō'pi sahasaumitrirmunīṁstānabhipūjya ca |
agratō niṣasādātha viśvāmitrasya dhīmataḥ ||

> **Meaning 21:** Rāma too, along with Saumitri, paid respects to the sages and
> sat down before the wise Viśvāmitra.

---
22.
atha rāmō mahātējā viśvāmitraṁ mahāmunim |
papraccha muniśārdūlaṁ kautūhalasamanvitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 22:** Filled with curiosity, the radiant Rāma then asked the great
> sage Viśvāmitra, foremost among ascetics:

---
23.
bhagavankō nvayaṁ dēśaḥ samr̥ddhavanaśōbhitaḥ |
śrōtumicchāmi bhadraṁ tē vaktumarhasi tattvataḥ ||

> **Meaning 23:** "Venerable sage, what land is this, graced with such lush
> forests? I would like to hear about it—blessings upon you, please tell me its
> true story."

---
24.
cōditō rāmavākyēna kathayāmāsa suvrataḥ |
tasya dēśasya nikhilamr̥ṣimadhyē mahātapāḥ ||

> **Meaning 24:** Prompted by Rāma's request, the steadfast great ascetic
> recounted the full history of that region in the midst of the ṛṣis.

---
