=== Sarga 38 ===
Bhr̥gu's Boon to Sagara; The Birth of Asamañja and the Sixty Thousand Sons

---
1.
tāṁ kathāṁ kauśikō rāmē nivēdya madhurākṣaram |
punarēvāparaṁ vākyaṁ kākutsthamidamabravīt ||

> **Meaning 1:** Having narrated that story to Rāma in sweet, measured words,
> Viśvāmitra spoke to him again, saying:

---
2.
ayōdhyādhipatiḥ śūraḥ pūrvamāsīnnarādhipaḥ |
sagarō nāma dharmātmā prajākāmaḥ sa cāprajaḥ ||

> **Meaning 2:** "Long ago, there was a brave king of Ayodhyā named Sagara.
> Devoted to dharma, he longed for children, yet remained childless.

---
3.
vaidarbhaduhitā rāma kēśinī nāma nāmataḥ |
jyēṣṭhā sagarapatnī sā dharmiṣṭhā satyavādinī ||

> **Meaning 3:** "Rāma, the daughter of the king of Vidarbha, named Keśinī, was
> Sagara's first queen—deeply virtuous and truthful.

---
4.
ariṣṭanēmiduhitā rūpēṇāpratimā bhuvi |
dvitīyā sagarasyāsītpatnī sumatisañjñitā ||

> **Meaning 4:** "The daughter of Ariṣṭanemi, unmatched on earth in beauty, was
> Sagara's second queen, known as Sumati.

---
5.
tābhyāṁ saha tadā rājā patnībhyāṁ taptavāṁstapaḥ |
himavantaṁ samāsādya bhr̥guprasravaṇē girau ||

> **Meaning 5:** "Together with both his queens, the king went to the Himavān
> and practiced severe tapas on the mountain peak Bhr̥guprasravaṇa.

---
6.
atha varṣa śatē pūrṇē tapasārādhitō muniḥ |
sagarāya varaṁ prādādbhr̥guḥ satyavatāṁ varaḥ ||

> **Meaning 6:** "Then, when a full hundred years had passed, the muni
> Bhr̥gu—foremost among the truthful—pleased by their tapas, granted Sagara a
> boon:

---
7.
apatyalābhaḥ sumahānbhaviṣyati tavānagha |
kīrtiṁ cāpratimāṁ lōkē prāpsyasē puruṣarṣabha ||

> **Meaning 7:** "'Sinless one, you will be blessed with many children, and you
> will win unmatched renown in the world, best of men.

---
8.
ēkā janayitā tāta putraṁ vaṁśakaraṁ tava |
ṣaṣṭiṁ putrasahasrāṇi aparā janayiṣyati ||

> **Meaning 8:** "'One of your queens, dear king, will give birth to a son who
> will carry on your line; the other will give birth to sixty thousand sons.'

---
9.
bhāṣamāṇaṁ naravyāghraṁ rājapatnyau prasādya tam |
ūcatuḥ paramaprītē kr̥tāñjalipuṭē tadā ||

> **Meaning 9:** "As he spoke, the two queens bowed humbly before that noblest
> of men and, deeply delighted, joined their palms in añjali and asked:

---
10.
ēkaḥ kasyāḥ sutō brahmankā bahūñjanayiṣyati |
śrōtumicchāvahē brahmansatyamastu vacastava ||

> **Meaning 10:** "'Which of us will have the single son, revered sage, and
> which will bear the many? We wish to hear this, Bhr̥gu—may your words come
> true.'

---
11.
tayōstadvacanaṁ śrutvā bhr̥guḥ parama dhārmikaḥ |
uvāca paramāṁ vāṇīṁ svacchandō'tra vidhīyatām ||

> **Meaning 11:** "Hearing their words, the supremely righteous Bhr̥gu spoke
> this noble reply: 'Let your own choice decide it here.

---
12.
ēkō vaṁśakarō vāstu bahavō vā mahābalāḥ |
kīrtimantō mahōtsāhāḥ kā vā kaṁ varamicchati ||

> **Meaning 12:** "'One choice is a single son to carry on the line; the other is
> many sons of great strength, renown, and boundless energy—which of you wants
> which boon?'

---
13.
munēstu vacanaṁ śrutvā kēśinī raghunandana |
putraṁ vaṁśakaraṁ rāma jagrāha nr̥pasannidhau ||

> **Meaning 13:** "Hearing the muni's words, Raghunandana, Keśinī chose the
> single son who would carry on the line, right there in the king's presence.

---
14.
ṣaṣṭiṁ putrasahasrāṇi suparṇabhaginī tadā |
mahōtsāhānkīrtimatō jagrāha sumatiḥ sutān ||

> **Meaning 14:** "Then Sumati, sister of Garuḍa, chose the sixty thousand sons
> of boundless energy and renown.

---
15.
pradakṣiṇamr̥ṣiṁ kr̥tvā śirasābhipraṇamya ca |
jagāma svapuraṁ rājā sabhāryā raghunandana ||

> **Meaning 15:** "Having circumambulated the r̥ṣi and bowed low with his head
> to the ground, the king returned to his own city with his wives, Raghunandana.

---
16.
atha kālē gatē tasmiñjyēṣṭhā putraṁ vyajāyata |
asamañja iti khyātaṁ kēśinī sagarātmajam ||

> **Meaning 16:** "Then, in due course, the senior queen Keśinī gave birth to
> Sagara's son, known by the name Asamañja.

---
17.
sumatistu naravyāghra garbhatumbaṁ vyajāyata |
ṣaṣṭiḥ putrasahasrāṇi tumbabhēdādviniḥsr̥tāḥ ||

> **Meaning 17:** "As for Sumati, Rāma, she gave birth to a gourd-like embryo;
> when the gourd split open, sixty thousand sons emerged from it.

---
18.
ghr̥tapūrṇēṣu kumbhēṣu dhātryastānsamavardhayan |
kālēna mahatā sarvē yauvanaṁ pratipēdirē ||

> **Meaning 18:** "Nurses tended and raised them in jars filled with ghee, and
> in the fullness of time, all of them grew into youth.

---
19.
atha dīrghēṇa kālēna rūpayauvanaśālinaḥ |
ṣaṣṭiḥ putrasahasrāṇi sagarasyābhavaṁstadā ||

> **Meaning 19:** "Thus, after a long time, Sagara's sixty thousand sons stood
> in the prime of beauty and youth.

---
20.
sa ca jyēṣṭhō naraśrēṣṭha sagarasyātmasambhavaḥ |
bālāngr̥hītvā tu jalē sarayvā raghunandana |
prakṣipya prahasannityaṁ majjatastānnirīkṣya vai ||

> **Meaning 20:** "Meanwhile, Rāma, Asamañja—Sagara's eldest son—would seize
> little children and hurl them into the waters of the Sarayū, laughing aloud as
> he watched them drown.

---
21.
ēvaṁ pāpasamācāraḥ sajjanapratibādhakaḥ |
paurāṇāmahitē yuktaḥ pitrā nirvāsitaḥ purāt ||

> **Meaning 21:** "Acting so wickedly—tormenting good people and bent on
> harming the citizens—he was banished from the city by his father, Sagara.

---
22.
tasya putrōṁ'śumānnāma asamañjasya vīryavān |
sammataḥ sarvalōkasya sarvasyāpi priyaṁvadaḥ ||

> **Meaning 22:** "That Asamañja had a valiant son named Aṁśumān, esteemed by
> all the world and kind-spoken to everyone.

---
23.
tataḥ kālēna mahatā matiḥ samabhijāyata |
sagarasya naraśrēṣṭha yajēyamiti niścitā ||

> **Meaning 23:** "Then, after a long time, Rāma, a firm resolve arose in
> Sagara's mind to perform a yajña.

---
24.
sa kr̥tvā niścayaṁ rājā sōpādhyāyagaṇastadā |
yajñakarmaṇi vēdajñō yaṣṭuṁ samupacakramē ||

> **Meaning 24:** "Having made this decision, the king—well versed in the
> Vedas—together with his assembly of officiating priests, began the rites of
> the yajña."

---
