=== Sarga 38 ===

---
1.
viśvāmitravacaḥ śrutvā kathānte raghunandana |
uvāca paramaprīto muniṁ dīptam ivānalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
śrotum ichāmi bhadraṁ te vistareṇa kathām imām |
pūrvako me kathaṁ brahman yajñaṁ vai samupāharat ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
viśvāmitras tu kākutstham uvāca prahasann iva |
śrūyatāṁ vistaro rāma sagarasya mahātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
śaṁkaraśvaśuro nāma himavān acalottamaḥ |
vindhyaparvatam āsādya nirīkṣete parasparam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tayor madhye pravr̥tto ’bhūd yajñaḥ sa puruṣottama |
sa hi deśo naravyāghra praśasto yajñakarmaṇi ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tasyāśvacaryāṁ kākutstha dr̥ḍhadhanvā mahārathaḥ |
aṁśumān akarot tāta sagarasya mate sthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tasya parvaṇi taṁ yajñaṁ yajamānasya vāsavaḥ |
rākṣasīṁ tanum āsthāya yajñiyāśvam apāharat ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
hriyamāṇe tu kākutstha tasminn aśve mahātmanaḥ |
upādhyāya gaṇāḥ sarve yajamānam athābruvan ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
ayaṁ parvaṇi vegena yajñiyāśvo ’panīyate |
hartāraṁ jahi kākutstha hayaś caivopanīyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
yajñac chidraṁ bhavaty etat sarveṣām aśivāya naḥ |
tat tathā kriyatāṁ rājan yathāchidraḥ kratur bhavet ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
upādhyāya vacaḥ śrutvā tasmin sadasi pārthivaḥ |
ṣaṣṭiṁ putrasahasrāṇi vākyam etad uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
gatiṁ putrā na paśyāmi rakṣasāṁ puruṣarṣabhāḥ |
mantrapūtair mahābhāgair āsthito hi mahākratuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tad gacchata vicinvadhvaṁ putrakā bhadram astu vaḥ |
samudramālinīṁ sarvāṁ pr̥thivīm anugacchata ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
ekaikaṁ yojanaṁ putrā vistāram abhigacchata ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
yāvat turagasaṁdarśas tāvat khanata medinīm |
tam eva hayahartāraṁ mārgamāṇā mamājñayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
dīkṣitaḥ pautrasahitaḥ sopādhyāyagaṇo hy aham |
iha sthāsyāmi bhadraṁ vo yāvat turagadarśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
ity uktvā hr̥ṣṭamanaso rājaputrā mahābalāḥ |
jagmur mahītalaṁ rāma pitur vacanayantritāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
yojanāyām avistāram ekaiko dharaṇītalam |
bibhiduḥ puruṣavyāghra vajrasparśasamair bhujaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
śūlair aśanikalpaiś ca halaiś cāpi sudāruṇaiḥ |
bhidyamānā vasumatī nanāda raghunandana ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
nāgānāṁ vadhyamānānām asurāṇāṁ ca rāghava |
rākṣasānāṁ ca durdharṣaḥ sattvānāṁ ninado ’bhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
yojanānāṁ sahasrāṇi ṣaṣṭiṁ tu raghunandana |
bibhidur dharaṇīṁ vīrā rasātalam anuttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
evaṁ parvatasaṁbādhaṁ jambūdvīpaṁ nr̥pātmajāḥ |
khananto nr̥paśārdūla sarvataḥ paricakramuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tato devāḥ sagandharvāḥ sāsurāḥ sahapannagāḥ |
saṁbhrāntamanasaḥ sarve pitāmaham upāgaman ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
te prasādya mahātmānaṁ viṣaṇṇavadanās tadā |
ūcuḥ paramasaṁtrastāḥ pitāmaham idaṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
bhagavan pr̥thivī sarvā khanyate sagarātmajaiḥ |
bahavaś ca mahātmāno vadhyante jalacāriṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
ayaṁ yajñahano ’smākam anenāśvo ’panīyate |
iti te sarvabhūtāni nighnanti sagarātmajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
