=== Sarga 28 ===
Viśvāmitra Imparts the Withdrawal Mantras; Rāma Asks About Siddhāśrama

---
1.
pratigr̥hya tatō'strāṇi prahr̥ṣṭavadanaḥ śuciḥ |
gacchannēva ca kākutsthō viśvāmitramathābravīt ||

> **Meaning 1:** Having received those astras, the pure-hearted Kākutstha, his
> face beaming with joy, spoke to Viśvāmitra as they walked along:

---
2.
gr̥hītāstrō'smi bhagavan durādharṣaḥ surairapi |
astrāṇāṁ tvahamicchāmi saṁhāraṁ munipuṅgava ||

> **Meaning 2:** "Venerable sage, now that I have received these astras, I am
> invincible even to the devatās. Yet, foremost of sages, I wish to learn how
> to withdraw them."

---
3.
ēvaṁ bruvati kākutsthē viśvāmitrō mahāmuniḥ |
saṁhārān vyājahārātha dhr̥timān suvrataḥ śuciḥ ||

> **Meaning 3:** When Kākutstha said this, the great sage Viśvāmitra—steadfast,
> pure, and faithful to his vows—imparted the mantras for withdrawing them:

---
4.
satyavantaṁ satyakīrtiṁ dhr̥ṣṭaṁ rabhasamēva ca |
pratihārataraṁ nāma parāṅmukhamavāṅmukham ||

5.
lakṣyālakṣyāvimau caiva dr̥ḍhanābhasunābhakau |
daśākṣaśatavaktrau ca daśaśīrṣaśatōdarau ||

6.
padmanābhamahānābhau dundunābhasvanābhakau |
jyōtiṣaṁ śakunaṁ caiva nairāśyavimalāvubhau ||

7.
yaugandharavinidrau ca daityapramadhanau tathā |
śucibāhurmahābāhurniṣkalirvirucistathā |
sārcirmālī dhr̥tirmālī vr̥ttimān rucirastathā ||

8.
pitryaḥ saumanasaścaiva vidhūtamakarāvubhau |
karavīrakaraṁ caiva dhanadhānyau ca rāghava ||

9.
kāmarūpaṁ kāmaruciṁ mōhamāvaraṇaṁ tathā |
jr̥mbhakaṁ sarpanābhaṁ ca panthānavaraṇau tathā ||

10.
bhr̥śāśvatanayān rāma bhāsvarān kāmarūpiṇaḥ |
pratīccha mama bhadraṁ tē pātrabhūtō'si rāghava ||

> **Meaning 4-10:** "Satyavant, Satyakīrti, Dhr̥ṣṭa, and Rabhasa; Pratihāratara,
> Parāṅmukha, and Avāṅmukha; Lakṣya and Alakṣya; Dr̥ḍhanābha and Sunābhaka;
> Daśākṣa and Śatavaktra; Daśaśīrṣa and Śatodara; Padmanābha and Mahānābha;
> Dundunābha and Svanābhaka; Jyotiṣa and Śakuna; Nairāśya and Vimala;
> Yaugandhara and Vinidra; Daitya and Pramadhana; Śucibāhu and Mahābāhu;
> Niṣkali and Viruci; Sārcirmālī and Dhr̥tirmālī; Vr̥ttimān and Rucira; Pitrya
> and Saumanasa; Vidhūta and Makara; Karavīrakara, Dhana, and Dhānya; Kāmarūpa
> and Kāmaruci; Moha and Āvaraṇa; Jr̥mbhaka, Sarpanābha, Panthāna, and Varaṇa.
> Receive these radiant astras, Rāma—the sons of Bhr̥śāśva—who can take any
> form at will. May blessings be upon you, Rāghava, for you are truly worthy of
> them."

---
11.
bāḍhamityēva kākutstha prahr̥ṣṭēnāntarātmanā |
jagrāha mantragrāmaṃ te'pyupatasthuśca rāghavam ||

> **Meaning 11:** "Yes, indeed!" replied Rāma with an overjoyed heart. He
> received that set of mantras, and those astras too appeared before him.

---
12.
divyabhāsvaradēhāśca mūrtimantaḥ sukhapradāḥ |
kēcidaṅgārasadr̥śāḥ kēcid dhūmōpamāstathā |
candrārkasadr̥śāḥ kēcit prahvāñjalipuṭāstathā ||

> **Meaning 12:** Taking visible, divine, radiant forms that were a joy to
> behold—some like glowing embers, some like smoke, and others like the sun and
> moon—they bowed humbly with palms joined in reverence.

---
13.
rāmaṁ prāñjalayō bhūtvā'bruvan madhurabhāṣiṇaḥ |
imē sma naraśārdūla śādhi kiṁ karavāma tē ||

> **Meaning 13:** Speaking sweetly with joined palms, they said to him, "Rāma,
> tiger among men, here we are—command us, what can we do for you?"

---
14.
gamyatāmiti tānāha yathēṣṭaṁ raghunandanaḥ |
mānasāḥ kāryakālēṣu sāhāyyaṁ mē kariṣyatha ||

> **Meaning 14:** Rāma told them, "You may go now, wherever you please. Appear
> before me whenever I think of you, and help me whenever the need arises."

---
15.
atha tē rāmamāmantrya kr̥tvā cāpi pradakṣiṇam |
ēvamastviti kākutsthamuktvā jagmuryathāgatam ||

> **Meaning 15:** Then, taking leave of Rāma and walking around him clockwise in
> reverence, they said to him, "So be it," and departed the way they had come.

---
16.
tatastu rāmaḥ kākutsthaḥ śāsanādbrahmavādinaḥ.
lakṣmaṇāya ca tān sarvān varāstrān raghunandanaḥ.
saṃhārān sa ca saṃhṛṣṭaḥ śrīmāṃstasmai nyavedayat.

> **Meaning 16:** Then, at Viśvāmitra's command, the radiant and joyful Rāma
> imparted to Lakṣmaṇa as well all those supreme astras along with the mantras
> for withdrawing them.

---
17.
sa ca tān rāghavō jñātvā viśvāmitraṁ mahāmunim |
gacchannēvātha madhuraṁ ślakṣṇaṁ vacanamabravīt ||

> **Meaning 17:** Having mastered them, Rāghava walked on with the great sage
> Viśvāmitra and spoke these sweet, gentle words:

---
18.
kiṁ nvētanmēghasaṅkāśaṁ parvatasyāvidūrataḥ |
vr̥kṣakhaṇḍamivābhāti paraṁ kautūhalaṁ hi mē ||

> **Meaning 18:** "What is that cloud-like grove of trees shining not far from
> the mountain? I'm so curious to know!

---
19.
darśanīyaṁ mr̥gākīrṇaṁ manōharamatīva ca |
nānāprakāraiḥ śakunairvalgubhāṣairalaṅkr̥tam ||

> **Meaning 19:** "It's a beautiful sight, teeming with deer, deeply
> enchanting, and graced with many kinds of sweet-singing birds.

---
20.
niḥsr̥tāḥ sma muniśrēṣṭha kāntārādrōmaharṣaṇāt |
anayā tvavagacchāmi dēśasya sukhavattayā |
sarvaṁ mē śaṁsa bhagavan kasyāśramapadaṁ tvidam ||

> **Meaning 20:** "Foremost of sages, we have emerged from that hair-raising
> wilderness, and I can tell by the peacefulness of this place! Venerable sage,
> tell me everything—whose āśrama is this?

---
21.
samprāptā yatra tē pāpā brahmaghnā duṣṭacāriṇaḥ |
tava yajñasya vighnāya durātmānō mahāmunēḥ |
bhagavaṁstasya kō dēśaḥ sā yatra tava yājñikī ||

22.
rakṣitavyā kriyā brahman mayā vadhyāśca rākṣasāḥ |
ētatsarvaṁ muniśrēṣṭa śrōtumicchāmyahaṁ prabhō ||

> **Meaning 21-22:** "Venerable sage, where do those wicked, evil-natured
> destroyers of brāhmaṇas come to disrupt your yajña? And where is your yajña
> ground, Viśvāmitra, where I must protect the ritual and slay those rākṣasas?
> Foremost of sages, I wish to hear all of this."

---
