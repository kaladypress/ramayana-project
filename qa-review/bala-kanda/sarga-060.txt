=== Sarga 60 ===

---
1.
viśvāmitro mahātmātha prasthitān prekṣya tān r̥ṣīn |
abravīn naraśārdūla sarvāṁs tān vanavāsinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
mahāvighnaḥ pravr̥tto ’yaṁ dakṣiṇām āsthito diśam |
diśam anyāṁ prapatsyāmas tatra tapsyāmahe tapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
paścimāyāṁ viśālāyāṁ puṣkareṣu mahātmanaḥ |
sukhaṁ tapaś cariṣyāmaḥ paraṁ tad dhi tapovanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
evam uktvā mahātejāḥ puṣkareṣu mahāmuniḥ |
tapa ugraṁ durādharṣaṁ tepe mūlaphalāśanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
etasminn eva kāle tu ayodhyādhipatir nr̥paḥ |
ambarīṣa iti khyāto yaṣṭuṁ samupacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tasya vai yajamānasya paśum indro jahāra ha |
pranaṣṭe tu paśau vipro rājānam idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
paśur adya hr̥to rājan pranaṣṭas tava durnayāt |
arakṣitāraṁ rājānaṁ ghnanti doṣā nareśvara ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
prāyaścittaṁ mahad dhy etan naraṁ vā puruṣarṣabha |
ānayasva paśuṁ śīghraṁ yāvat karma pravartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
upādhyāya vacaḥ śrutvā sa rājā puruṣarṣabha |
anviyeṣa mahābuddhiḥ paśuṁ gobhiḥ sahasraśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
deśāñ janapadāṁs tāṁs tān nagarāṇi vanāni ca |
āśramāṇi ca puṇyāni mārgamāṇo mahīpatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sa putrasahitaṁ tāta sabhāryaṁ raghunandana |
bhr̥gutuṅge samāsīnam r̥cīkaṁ saṁdadarśa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tam uvāca mahātejāḥ praṇamyābhiprasādya ca |
brahmarṣiṁ tapasā dīptaṁ rājarṣir amitaprabhaḥ |
pr̥ṣṭvā sarvatra kuśalam r̥cīkaṁ tam idaṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
gavāṁ śatasahasreṇa vikriṇīṣe sutaṁ yadi |
paśor arthe mahābhāga kr̥takr̥tyo ’smi bhārgava ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sarve parisr̥tā deśā yajñiyaṁ na labhe paśum |
dātum arhasi mūlyena sutam ekam ito mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
evam ukto mahātejā r̥cīkas tv abravīd vacaḥ |
nāhaṁ jyeṣṭhaṁ naraśreṣṭhaṁ vikrīṇīyāṁ kathaṁ cana ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
r̥cīkasya vacaḥ śrutvā teṣāṁ mātā mahātmanām |
uvāca naraśārdūlam ambarīṣaṁ tapasvinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
mamāpi dayitaṁ viddhi kaniṣṭhaṁ śunakaṁ nr̥pa ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
prāyeṇa hi naraśreṣṭha jyeṣṭhāḥ pitr̥ṣu vallabhāḥ |
mātr̥̄ṇāṁ ca kanīyāṁsas tasmād rakṣe kanīyasam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
uktavākye munau tasmin munipatnyāṁ tathaiva ca |
śunaḥśepaḥ svayaṁ rāma madhyamo vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
pitā jyeṣṭham avikreyaṁ mātā cāha kanīyasam |
vikrītaṁ madhyamaṁ manye rājan putraṁ nayasva mām ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
gavāṁ śatasahasreṇa śunaḥśepaṁ nareśvaraḥ |
gr̥hītvā paramaprīto jagāma raghunandana ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
ambarīṣas tu rājarṣī ratham āropya satvaraḥ |
śunaḥśepaṁ mahātejā jagāmāśu mahāyaśāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
