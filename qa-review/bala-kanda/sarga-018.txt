=== Sarga 18 ===
Birth of Rāma and His Brothers; Arrival of Viśvāmitra

---
1.
nirvr̥ttē tu kratau tasmin hayamēdhē mahātmanaḥ |
pratigr̥hya surā bhāgān pratijagmuryathāgatam ||

> **Meaning 1:** When the great-souled King Daśaratha's Aśvamedha yagna was
> complete, the devatās accepted their shares of the offering and returned to
> their respective abodes.

---
2.
samāptadīkṣāniyamaḥ patnīgaṇasamanvitaḥ |
pravivēśa purīṁ rājā sabhr̥tyabalavāhanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 2:** His vows of initiation fulfilled, the king entered the city
> with his wives, his attendants, his army, and his mounts.

---
3.
yathārhaṁ pūjitāstēna rājñā ca pr̥thivīśvarāḥ |
muditāḥ prayayurdēśān praṇamya munipuṅgavam ||

> **Meaning 3:** The king honored the visiting rulers of the earth as each
> deserved. Glad at heart, those kings bowed to the best of sages and left for
> their own lands.

---
4.
śrīmatāṁ gacchatāṁ tēṣāṁ svagr̥hāṇi purāttataḥ |
balāni rājñāṁ śubhrāṇi prahr̥ṣṭāni cakāśirē ||

> **Meaning 4:** As those illustrious kings departed from the city for their own
> homes, their gleaming armies shone in high spirits.

---
5.
gatēṣu pr̥thivīśēṣu rājā daśarathastadā |
pravivēśa purīṁ śrīmān puraskr̥tya dvijōttamān ||

> **Meaning 5:** When the rulers of the earth had gone, the glorious King
> Daśaratha entered the city, with the best of brahmins walking before him.

---
6.
śāntayā prayayau sārdhamr̥ṣyaśr̥ṅgaḥ supūjitaḥ |
anugamyamānō rājñā ca sānuyātrēṇa dhīmatā ||

> **Meaning 6:** Honored with great respect, Ṛṣyaśṛṅga departed with Śāntā,
> escorted on his way by the wise king and his retinue.

---
7.
ēvaṁ visr̥jya tān sarvān rājā sampūrṇamānasaḥ |
uvāsa sukhitastatra putrōtpattiṁ vicintayan ||

> **Meaning 7:** Having seen them all off in this way, the king, completely
> fulfilled at heart, lived happily there, eagerly awaiting the birth of his
> sons.

---
8.
tatō yajñē samāptē tu r̥tūnāṁ ṣaṭ samatyayuḥ |
tataśca dvādaśē māsē caitrē nāvamikē tithau ||

> **Meaning 8:** After the yagna ended, all six seasons of the year went by.
> Then, in the twelfth month, in Caitra, on the ninth tithi,

---
9.
nakṣatrē'ditidaivatyē svōccasaṁsthēṣu pañcasu |
grahēṣu karkaṭē lagnē vākpatāvindunā saha ||

> **Meaning 9:** under the nakṣatra ruled by Aditi, with five planets in their
> exalted positions, and Cancer rising together with Jupiter and the Moon,

---
10.
prōdyamānē jagannāthaṁ sarvalōkanamaskr̥tam |
kausalyājanayadrāmaṁ sarvalakṣaṇasaṁyutam ||

> **Meaning 10:** while that auspicious ascendant was rising, Kausalyā gave birth
> to Rāma, protector of the world, honored by all the worlds, and marked with
> every auspicious sign.

---
11.
viṣṇōrardhaṁ mahābhāgaṁ putramaikṣvākunandanam |
kausalyā śuśubhē tēna putrēṇāmitatējasā |
yathā varēṇa dēvānāmaditirvajrapāṇinā ||

> **Meaning 11:** Bearing half of Viṣṇu's divine essence, he was a great and
> fortunate son, the joy of the Ikṣvāku line. Kausalyā shone with that son of
> boundless radiance, as Aditi shone with her son Indra, the best of the
> devatās and wielder of the thunderbolt.

---
12.
bharatō nāma kaikēyyāṁ jajñē satyaparākramaḥ |
sākṣādviṣṇōścaturbhāgaḥ sarvaiḥ samuditō guṇaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 12:** Bharata, true in valor, was born to Kaikeyī. He embodied a
> quarter of Viṣṇu's divine essence, and he was endowed with every virtue.

---
13.
atha lakṣmaṇaśatrughnau sumitrājanayat sutau |
vīrau sarvāstrakuśalau viṣṇōrardhasamanvitau ||

> **Meaning 13:** Then Sumitrā gave birth to the two sons Lakṣmaṇa and
> Śatrughna. They were heroes, skilled in every weapon, and both were endowed
> with Viṣṇu's divine essence.

---
14.
puṣyē jātastu bharatō mīnalagnē prasannadhīḥ ||

> **Meaning 14:** Bharata, of serene mind, was born under Puṣya, with Pisces
> rising.

---
15.
sārpē jātau tu saumitrī kuḷīrē'bhyuditē ravau |
rājñaḥ putrā mahātmānaścatvārō jajñirē pr̥thak ||

> **Meaning 15:** The two sons of Sumitrā were born under the Sārpa nakṣatra,
> with Cancer rising and the sun at its height. Thus, the king's four
> great-souled sons were born, one after another.

---
16.
guṇavantō'nurūpāśca rucyā prōṣṭhapadōpamāḥ ||


> **Meaning 16:** All four were virtuous, worthy of their father Daśaratha, and
> shone with a radiance like that of the four bright Bhādrapadā stars.

---
17.
jaguḥ kalaṁ ca gandharvā nanr̥tuścāpsarōgaṇāḥ |
dēvadundubhayō nēduḥ puṣpavr̥ṣṭiśca khātpatat ||

> **Meaning 17:** The Gandharvas sang sweetly and groups of Apsarasas danced.
> The drums of the devatās sounded, and a shower of flowers fell from the sky.

---
18.
utsavaśca mahānāsīdayōdhyāyāṁ janākulaḥ |
rathyāśca janasambādhā naṭanartakasaṅkulāḥ |
gāyanaiśca virāviṇyō vādanaiśca tathāparaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 18:** A grand celebration swept through Ayodhyā, thronging with
> people. The streets were packed with crowds, bustling with actors and
> dancers, and resounded with singers and all kinds of musical instruments.

---
19.
pradēyāṁśca dadau rājā sūtamāgadhavandinām |
brāhmaṇēbhyō dadau vittaṁ gōdhanāni sahasraśaḥ ||

> **Meaning 19:** The king bestowed fitting gifts upon the sūtas, māgadhas, and
> vandins, and to the brahmins he gave wealth and cows by the thousands.

---
20.
atītyaikādaśāhaṁ tu nāmakarma tathākarōt |
jyēṣṭhaṁ rāmaṁ mahātmānaṁ bharataṁ kaikayīsutam ||

21.
saumitrī lakṣmaṇamiti śatrughnamaparaṁ tathā |
vasiṣṭhaḥ paramaprītō nāmāni kurutē tadā ||

> **Meaning 20-21:** After eleven days had passed, the nāmakarma ceremony was
> performed. Delighted, Sage Vasiṣṭha bestowed the names upon the boys: the
> eldest, the great-souled one, was named Rāma; Kaikeyī's son was named
> Bharata; and Sumitrā's two sons were named Lakṣmaṇa and Śatrughna.

---
22.
brāhmaṇān bhōjayāmāsa paurān jānapadānapi |
adadadbrāhmaṇānāṁ ca ratnaughamamitaṁ bahu |
tēṣāṁ janmakriyādīni sarvakarmāṇyakārayat ||

> **Meaning 22:** The king fed the brahmins and the people of the city and
> countryside. He gave the brahmins endless heaps of jewels, and he had all the
> sacred saṁskāras, from the birth ceremonies onward, performed for the boys.

---
23.
tēṣāṁ kēturiva jyēṣṭhō rāmō ratikaraḥ pituḥ |
babhūva bhūyō bhūtānāṁ svayambhūriva sammataḥ ||

> **Meaning 23:** Of them all, the eldest, Rāma, raised the prestige of the
> dynasty like a royal standard flying high. He delighted his father, and all
> beings revered him as they do Viṣṇu himself.

---
24.
sarvē vēdavidaḥ śūrāḥ sarvē lōkahitē ratāḥ |
sarvē jñānōpasampannāḥ sarvē samuditā guṇaiḥ |

> **Meaning 24:** All four princes were learned in the Vedas and brave; they
> delighted in the welfare of the world, were rich in wisdom, and were endowed
> with every noble virtue.

---
25.
tēṣāmapi mahātējā rāmaḥ satyaparākramaḥ ||
iṣṭaḥ sarvasya lōkasya śaśāṅka iva nirmalaḥ |

> **Meaning 25:** Even among those four, Rāma—of supreme radiance and unfailing
> valor—was beloved by the entire world, pure as the spotless moon.

---
26.
gajaskandhē'śvapr̥ṣṭhē ca rathacaryāsu sammataḥ ||
dhanurvēdē ca nirataḥ pitr̥śuśrūṣaṇē rataḥ |

> **Meaning 26:** He was renowned for his mastery in riding elephants and
> horses and in handling chariots, and he was deeply devoted both to the
> science of archery and to serving his parents.

---
27.
bālyāt prabhr̥ti susnigdhō lakṣmaṇō lakṣmivardhanaḥ ||
rāmasya lōkarāmasya bhrāturjyēṣṭhasya nityaśaḥ |

> **Meaning 27:** From childhood, the ever-auspicious Lakṣmaṇa was always
> deeply devoted to his eldest brother Rāma, the delight of the world.

---
28.
sarvapriyakarastasya rāmasyāpi śarīrataḥ |
lakṣmaṇō lakṣmisampannō bahiḥprāṇa ivāparaḥ ||

> **Meaning 28:** Lakṣmaṇa did everything that pleased Rāma, and to Rāma too,
> the graceful Lakṣmaṇa was like a second life-breath moving outside his own
> body.

---
29.
na ca tēna vinā nidrāṁ labhatē puruṣōttamaḥ |
mr̥ṣṭamannamupānītamaśnāti na hi taṁ vinā ||

> **Meaning 29:** Rāma, the foremost among men, could not sleep without Lakṣmaṇa,
> nor would he eat even the finest food brought to him unless Lakṣmaṇa was
> there.

---
30.
yadā hi hayamārūḍhō mr̥gayāṁ yāti rāghavaḥ |
tadainaṁ pr̥ṣṭhatō'bhyēti sa dhanuḥ paripālayan ||

> **Meaning 30:** Whenever Rāghava rode out on horseback to hunt, Lakṣmaṇa
> followed close behind him, guarding him with his bow.

---
31.
bharatasyāpi śatrughnō lakṣmaṇāvarajō hi saḥ |
prāṇaiḥ priyatarō nityaṁ tasya cāsīttathā priyaḥ ||

> **Meaning 31:** In the same way Śatrughna, the younger brother of Lakṣmaṇa,
> was always dearer than life to Bharata, and Bharata was just as dear to him.

---
32.
sa caturbhirmahābhāgaiḥ putrairdaśarathaḥ priyaiḥ |
babhūva paramaprītō dēvairiva pitāmahaḥ ||

> **Meaning 32:** With those four fortunate and beloved sons, Daśaratha was
> supremely happy, like Pitāmaha Brahmā surrounded by the four Dikpālas.

---
33.
tē yadā jñānasampannāḥ sarvē samuditā guṇaiḥ |
hrīmantaḥ kīrtimantaśca sarvajñā dīrghadarśinaḥ ||

34.
tēṣāmēvaṁ prabhāvāṇāṁ sarvēṣāṁ dīptatējasām |
pitā daśarathō hr̥ṣṭō brahmā lōkādhipō yathā ||

> **Meaning 33-34:** When the princes had all grown rich in wisdom and endowed
> with every virtue—modest, renowned, deeply knowledgeable, and far-sighted—and
> shone with such power and blazing splendor, their father Daśaratha rejoiced
> over them, like Brahmā, ruler of the worlds.

---
35.
tē cāpi manujavyāghrā vaidikādhyayanē ratāḥ |
pitr̥śuśrūṣaṇaratā dhanurvēdē ca niṣṭhitāḥ ||

> **Meaning 35:** Those foremost among men were devoted to Vedic study and to
> serving their parents, and were thoroughly accomplished in the science of
> archery.

---
36.
atha rājā daśarathastēṣāṁ dārakriyāṁ prati |
cintayāmāsa dharmātmā sōpādhyāyaḥ sabāndhavaḥ ||

> **Meaning 36:** Then the righteous King Daśaratha, together with his
> preceptors and kinsmen, began to deliberate over the marriages of his sons.

---
37.
tasya cintayamānasya mantri madhyē mahātmanaḥ |
abhyāgacchanmahātējā viśvāmitrō mahāmuniḥ ||

> **Meaning 37:** As the great-souled king thought this over among his
> ministers, the greatly radiant, great sage Viśvāmitra arrived.

---
38.
sa rājñō darśanākāṅkṣī dvārādhyakṣānuvāca ha |
śīghramākhyāta māṁ prāptaṁ kauśikaṁ gādhinaḥ sutam ||

> **Meaning 38:** Wishing to see the king, Viśvāmitra said to the palace
> guards, "Quickly announce that I, Kauśika, the son of Gādhi, have arrived."

---
39.
tacchrutvā vacanaṁ tasya rājñō vēśma pradudruvuḥ |
sambhrāntamanasaḥ sarvē tēna vākyēna cōditāḥ ||

> **Meaning 39:** Hearing his words and urged on by that command, the palace
> guards rushed in awe and agitation to the king's quarters.

---
40.
tē gatvā rājabhavanaṁ viśvāmitramr̥ṣiṁ tadā |
prāptamāvēdayāmāsurnr̥pāyēkṣvākavē tadā ||

> **Meaning 40:** Entering the royal palace, they announced to the Ikṣvāku king
> that Sage Viśvāmitra had arrived.

---
41.
tēṣāṁ tadvacanaṁ śrutvā sapurōdhāḥ samāhitaḥ |
pratyujjagāma saṁhr̥ṣṭō brahmāṇamiva vāsavaḥ ||

> **Meaning 41:** Hearing their words, the king, composed and delighted, went
> out to meet him with his priest, as Indra would go to meet Brahmā.

---
42.
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā jvalitaṁ dīptyā tāpasaṁ saṁśitavratam |
prahr̥ṣṭavadanō rājā tatō'rghyamupahārayat ||

> **Meaning 42:** Seeing the ascetic of strict vows, blazing with radiance, the
> king's face lit up with joy, and he honored him with arghya, pādya, and all
> the customary marks of hospitality.

---
43.
sa rājñaḥ pratigr̥hyārghyaṁ śāstradr̥ṣṭēna karmaṇā |
kuśalaṁ cāvyayaṁ caiva paryapr̥cchannarādhipam ||

> **Meaning 43:** Viśvāmitra accepted the arghya offered by the king according
> to the rites prescribed in the śāstras. He asked the king about his well-being
> and his prosperity.

---
44.
purē kōśē janapadē bāndhavēṣu suhr̥tsu ca |
kuśalaṁ kauśikō rājñaḥ paryapr̥cchat sudhārmikaḥ ||

> **Meaning 44:** The deeply righteous Viśvāmitra asked the king, "Is all
> well with your city, your treasury, and your realm? Are your relatives and
> friends faring well?"

---
45.
api tē sannatāḥ sarvē sāmantā ripavō jitāḥ |
daivaṁ ca mānuṣaṁ caiva karma tē sādhvanuṣṭhitam ||

> **Meaning 45:** "Have all your neighboring rulers bowed to you, and have your
> enemies been defeated? Are your rites, both divine and human, carried out
> well?"

---
46.
vasiṣṭhaṁ ca samāgamya kuśalaṁ munipuṅgavaḥ |
r̥ṣīṁśca tān yathānyāyaṁ mahābhāgānuvāca ha ||

> **Meaning 46:** Viśvāmitra, the foremost of sages, then met Vasiṣṭha and the
> other venerable ṛṣis, and inquired after the well-being of each in due
> order.

---
47.
tē sarvē hr̥ṣṭamanasastasya rājñō nivēśanam |
viviśuḥ pūjitāstēna niṣēduśca yathārhataḥ ||

> **Meaning 47:** Glad at heart, all those sages entered the king's palace and,
> duly honored by him, took their seats according to their rank.

---
48.
atha hr̥ṣṭamanā rājā viśvāmitraṁ mahāmunim |
uvāca paramōdārō hr̥ṣṭastamabhipūjayan ||

> **Meaning 48:** Then the king, glad and most generous of heart, honored the
> great sage Viśvāmitra and spoke to him with joy.

---
49.
yathāmr̥tasya samprāptiryathā varṣamanūdakē |
yathā sadr̥śadārēṣu putrajanmāprajasya vai ||

> **Meaning 49:** "Your coming is like receiving nectar, like rain in a parched
> land, like the birth of a son to a childless man from his worthy
> wife.

---
50.
praṇaṣṭasya yathā lābhō yathā harṣō mahōdayē |
tathaivāgamanaṁ manyē svāgataṁ tē mahāmunē ||

> **Meaning 50:** It is like recovering what was lost, and like the joy of a
> great celebration—so do I cherish your arrival. Welcome, great sage!

---
51.
kaṁ ca tē paramaṁ kāmaṁ karōmi kimu harṣitaḥ |
pātrabhūtō'si mē brahman diṣṭyā prāptō'si mānada ||

> **Meaning 51:** Overjoyed as I am, what dearest wish of yours can I fulfill?
> There is no one worthier of my service than you—it is my great good
> fortune that you have come, gracious sage.

---
52.
adya mē saphalaṁ janma jīvitaṁ ca sujīvitam |
yasmād viprēndramadrākṣaṁ suprabhātā niśā mama ||

> **Meaning 52:** Today my birth has borne fruit and my life has been truly
> well lived, for I have seen the foremost of brahmins—my night has broken
> into a blessed dawn!

---
53.
pūrvaṁ rājarṣiśabdēna tapasā dyōtitaprabhaḥ |
brahmarṣitvamanuprāptaḥ pūjyō'si bahudhā mayā ||

> **Meaning 53:** Earlier, shining with the radiance of your austerities, you
> were known as a rājarṣi. Now you have attained the stature of a brahmarṣi,
> and you are worthy of my reverence in every way.

---
54.
tadadbhutamabhūt vipra pavitraṁ paramaṁ mama |
śubhakṣētragataścāhaṁ tava sandarśanāt prabhō ||

> **Meaning 54:** Your coming is a wonder to me, great sage, and supremely
> purifying. Just by seeing you, venerable one, I have become completely
> blessed and fulfilled.

---
55.
brūhi yat prārthitaṁ tubhyaṁ kāryamāgamanaṁ prati |
icchāmyanugr̥hītō'haṁ tvadarthaṁ parivr̥ddhayē ||

> **Meaning 55:** Tell me what brings you here and what you desire of me. I
> long to be blessed by serving your purpose, for my own flourishing.

---
56.
kāryasya na vimarśaṁ ca gantumarhasi suvrata |
kartā cāhamaśēṣēṇa daivataṁ hi bhavān mama ||

> **Meaning 56:** Do not hesitate over whether the task can be done, sage of
> noble vows—I will carry it out completely, for you are like a devatā to me.

---
57.
mama cāyamanuprāptō mahānabhyudayō dvija |
tavāgamanajaḥ kr̥tsnō dharmaścānuttamō dvija ||

> **Meaning 57:** Through your coming, great sage, this great good fortune has
> reached me, bringing with it the full measure of the highest dharma."

---
58.
iti hr̥dayasukhaṁ niśamya vākyam |
śrutisukhamātmavatā vinītamuktam |
prathitaguṇayaśā guṇairviśiṣṭaḥ |
paramar̥ṣiḥ paramaṁ jagāma harṣam ||

> **Meaning 58:** Hearing these words—so pleasing to the heart and to the
> ear—spoken humbly by the self-controlled king, the great sage, renowned for
> his virtues and preeminent in them, was filled with supreme joy.

---
