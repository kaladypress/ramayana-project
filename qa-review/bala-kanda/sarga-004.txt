=== Sarga 4 ===

---
1.
prāptarājyasya rāmasya vālmīkir bhagavān r̥ṣiḥ |
cakāra caritaṁ kr̥tsnaṁ vicitrapadam ātmavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
kr̥tvā tu tan mahāprājñaḥ sabhaviṣyaṁ sahottaram |
cintayām āsa ko nv etat prayuñjīyād iti prabhuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tasya cintayamānasya maharṣer bhāvitātmanaḥ |
agr̥hṇītāṁ tataḥ pādau muniveṣau kuśīlavau ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
kuśīlavau tu dharmajñau rājaputrau yaśasvinau |
bhrātarau svarasaṁpannau dadarśāśramavāsinau ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
sa tu medhāvinau dr̥ṣṭvā vedeṣu pariniṣṭhitau |
vedopabr̥hmaṇārthāya tāv agrāhayata prabhuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
kāvyaṁ rāmāyaṇaṁ kr̥tsnaṁ sītāyāś caritaṁ mahat |
paulastya vadham ity eva cakāra caritavrataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
pāṭhye geye ca madhuraṁ pramāṇais tribhir anvitam |
jātibhiḥ saptabhir baddhaṁ tantrīlayasamanvitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
hāsyaśr̥ṅgārakāruṇyaraudravīrabhayānakaiḥ |
bībhatsādirasair yuktaṁ kāvyam etad agāyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tau tu gāndharvatattvajñau mūrcchanāsthānakovidau |
bhrātarau svarasaṁpannau gandharvāv iva rūpiṇau ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
rūpalakṣaṇasaṁpannau madhurasvarabhāṣiṇau |
bimbād ivoddhr̥tau bimbau rāmadehāt tathāparau ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tau rājaputrau kārtsnyena dharmyam ākhyānam uttamam |
vāco vidheyaṁ tat sarvaṁ kr̥tvā kāvyam aninditau ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
r̥ṣīṇāṁ ca dvijātīnāṁ sādhūnāṁ ca samāgame |
yathopadeśaṁ tattvajñau jagatus tau samāhitau |
mahātmānau mahābhāgau sarvalakṣaṇalakṣitau ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tau kadā cit sametānām r̥ṣīṇāṁ bhāvitātmanām |
āsīnānāṁ samīpasthāv idaṁ kāvyam agāyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tac chrutvā munayaḥ sarve bāṣpaparyākulekṣaṇāḥ |
sādhu sādhv ity tāv ūcatuḥ paraṁ vismayam āgatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
te prītamanasaḥ sarve munayo dharmavatsalāḥ |
praśaśaṁsuḥ praśastavyau gāyamānau kuśīlavau ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
aho gītasya mādhuryaṁ ślokānāṁ ca viśeṣataḥ |
ciranirvr̥ttam apy etat pratyakṣam iva darśitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
praviśya tāv ubhau suṣṭhu tadā bhāvam agāyatām |
sahitau madhuraṁ raktaṁ saṁpannaṁ svarasaṁpadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
evaṁ praśasyamānau tau tapaḥślāghyair maharṣibhiḥ |
saṁraktataram atyarthaṁ madhuraṁ tāv agāyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
prītaḥ kaś cin munis tābhyāṁ saṁsthitaḥ kalaśaṁ dadau |
prasanno valkalaṁ kaś cid dadau tābhyāṁ mahāyaśāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
āścaryam idam ākhyānaṁ muninā saṁprakīrtitam |
paraṁ kavīnām ādhāraṁ samāptaṁ ca yathākramam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
praśasyamānau sarvatra kadā cit tatra gāyakau |
rathyāsu rājamārgeṣu dadarśa bharatāgrajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
svaveśma cānīya tato bhrātarau sakuśīlavau |
pūjayām āsa pūjārhau rāmaḥ śatrunibarhaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
āsīnaḥ kāñcane divye sa ca siṁhāsane prabhuḥ |
upopaviṣṭaiḥ sacivair bhrātr̥bhiś ca paraṁtapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
dr̥ṣṭvā tu rūpasaṁpannau tāv ubhau vīṇinau tataḥ |
uvāca lakṣmaṇaṁ rāmaḥ śatrughnaṁ bharataṁ tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
śrūyatām idam ākhyānam anayor devavarcasoḥ |
vicitrārthapadaṁ samyag gāyator madhurasvaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
imau munī pārthivalakṣmaṇānvitau; kuśīlavau caiva mahātapasvinau |
mamāpi tad bhūtikaraṁ pracakṣate; mahānubhāvaṁ caritaṁ nibodhata ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tatas tu tau rāmavacaḥ pracoditāv; agāyatāṁ mārgavidhānasaṁpadā |
sa cāpi rāmaḥ pariṣadgataḥ śanair; bubhūṣayāsaktamanā babhūva ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
