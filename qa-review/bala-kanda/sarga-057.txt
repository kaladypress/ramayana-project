=== Sarga 57 ===

---
1.
tatas triśaṅkor vacanaṁ śrutvā krodhasamanvitam |
r̥ṣiputraśataṁ rāma rājānam idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
pratyākhyāto ’si durbuddhe guruṇā satyavādinā |
taṁ kathaṁ samatikramya śākhāntaram upeyivān ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
ikṣvākūṇāṁ hi sarveṣāṁ purodhāḥ paramā gatiḥ |
na cātikramituṁ śakyaṁ vacanaṁ satyavādinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
aśakyam iti covāca vasiṣṭho bhagavān r̥ṣiḥ |
taṁ vayaṁ vai samāhartuṁ kratuṁ śaktāḥ kathaṁ tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
bāliśas tvaṁ naraśreṣṭha gamyatāṁ svapuraṁ punaḥ |
yājane bhagavāñ śaktas trailokyasyāpi pārthiva ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
teṣāṁ tad vacanaṁ śrutvā krodhaparyākulākṣaram |
sa rājā punar evaitān idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
pratyākhyāto ’smi guruṇā guruputrais tathaiva ca |
anyāṁ gatiṁ gamiṣyāmi svasti vo ’stu tapodhanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
r̥ṣiputrās tu tac chrutvā vākyaṁ ghorābhisaṁhitam |
śepuḥ paramasaṁkruddhāś caṇḍālatvaṁ gamiṣyasi |
evam uktvā mahātmāno viviśus te svam āśramam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
atha rātryāṁ vyatītāyāṁ rājā caṇḍālatāṁ gataḥ |
nīlavastradharo nīlaḥ paruṣo dhvastamūrdhajaḥ |
cityamālyānulepaś ca āyasābharaṇo ’bhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā mantriṇaḥ sarve tyaktvā caṇḍālarūpiṇam |
prādravan sahitā rāma paurā ye ’syānugāminaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
eko hi rājā kākutstha jagāma paramātmavān |
dahyamāno divārātraṁ viśvāmitraṁ tapodhanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
viśvāmitras tu taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rājānaṁ viphalīkr̥tam |
caṇḍālarūpiṇaṁ rāma muniḥ kāruṇyam āgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
kāruṇyāt sa mahātejā vākyaṁ parama dhārmikaḥ |
idaṁ jagāda bhadraṁ te rājānaṁ ghoradarśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
kim āgamanakāryaṁ te rājaputra mahābala |
ayodhyādhipate vīra śāpāc caṇḍālatāṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
atha tad vākyam ākarṇya rājā caṇḍālatāṁ gataḥ |
abravīt prāñjalir vākyaṁ vākyajño vākyakovidam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
pratyākhyāto ’smi guruṇā guruputrais tathaiva ca |
anavāpyaiva taṁ kāmaṁ mayā prāpto viparyayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
saśarīro divaṁ yāyām iti me saumyadarśanam |
mayā ceṣṭaṁ kratuśataṁ tac ca nāvāpyate phalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
anr̥taṁ nokta pūrvaṁ me na ca vakṣye kadā cana |
kr̥cchreṣv api gataḥ saumya kṣatradharmeṇa te śape ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
yajñair bahuvidhair iṣṭaṁ prajā dharmeṇa pālitāḥ |
guravaś ca mahātmānaḥ śīlavr̥ttena toṣitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
dharme prayatamānasya yajñaṁ cāhartum icchataḥ |
paritoṣaṁ na gacchanti guravo munipuṁgava ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
daivam eva paraṁ manye pauruṣaṁ tu nirarthakam |
daivenākramyate sarvaṁ daivaṁ hi paramā gatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tasya me paramārtasya prasādam abhikāṅkṣataḥ |
kartum arhasi bhadraṁ te daivopahatakarmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
nānyāṁ gatiṁ gamiṣyāmi nānyaḥ śaraṇam asti me |
daivaṁ puruṣakāreṇa nivartayitum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
