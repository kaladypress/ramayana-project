=== Sarga 70 ===

---
1.
evaṁ bruvāṇaṁ janakaḥ pratyuvāca kr̥tāñjaliḥ |
śrotum arhasi bhadraṁ te kulaṁ naḥ kīrtitaṁ param ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
pradāne hi muniśreṣṭha kulaṁ niravaśeṣataḥ |
vaktavyaṁ kulajātena tan nibodha mahāmune ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
rājābhūt triṣu lokeṣu viśrutaḥ svena karmaṇā |
nimiḥ paramadharmātmā sarvasattvavatāṁ varaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tasya putro mithir nāma janako mithiputrakaḥ |
prathamo janako nāma janakād apy udāvasuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
udāvasos tu dharmātmā jāto vai nandivardhanaḥ |
nandivardhanaputras tu suketur nāma nāmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
suketor api dharmātmā devarāto mahābalaḥ |
devarātasya rājarṣer br̥hadratha iti śrutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
br̥hadrathasya śūro ’bhūn mahāvīraḥ pratāpavān |
mahāvīrasya dhr̥timān sudhr̥tiḥ satyavikramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sudhr̥ter api dharmātmā dhr̥ṣṭaketuḥ sudhārmikaḥ |
dhr̥ṣṭaketos tu rājarṣer haryaśva iti viśrutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
haryaśvasya maruḥ putro maroḥ putraḥ pratīndhakaḥ |
pratīndhakasya dharmātmā rājā kīrtirathaḥ sutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
putraḥ kīrtirathasyāpi devamīḍha iti smr̥taḥ |
devamīḍhasya vibudho vibudhasya mahīdhrakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
mahīdhrakasuto rājā kīrtirāto mahābalaḥ |
kīrtirātasya rājarṣer mahāromā vyajāyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
mahāroṁṇas tu dharmātmā svarṇaromā vyajāyata |
svarṇaroṁṇas tu rājarṣer hrasvaromā vyajāyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tasya putradvayaṁ jajñe dharmajñasya mahātmanaḥ |
jyeṣṭho ’ham anujo bhrātā mama vīraḥ kuśadhvajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
māṁ tu jyeṣṭhaṁ pitā rājye so ’bhiṣicya narādhipaḥ |
kuśadhvajaṁ samāveśya bhāraṁ mayi vanaṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
vr̥ddhe pitari svaryāte dharmeṇa dhuram āvaham |
bhrātaraṁ devasaṁkāśaṁ snehāt paśyan kuśadhvajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
kasya cit tv atha kālasya sāṁkāśyād agamat purāt |
sudhanvā vīryavān rājā mithilām avarodhakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
sa ca me preṣayām āsa śaivaṁ dhanur anuttamam |
sītā kanyā ca padmākṣī mahyaṁ vai dīyatām iti ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tasyāpradānād brahmarṣe yuddham āsīn mayā saha |
sa hato ’bhimukho rājā sudhanvā tu mayā raṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
nihatya taṁ muniśreṣṭha sudhanvānaṁ narādhipam |
sāṁkāśye bhrātaraṁ śūram abhyaṣiñcaṁ kuśadhvajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
kanīyān eṣa me bhrātā ahaṁ jyeṣṭho mahāmune |
dadāmi paramaprīto vadhvau te munipuṁgava ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
sītāṁ rāmāya bhadraṁ te ūrmilāṁ lakṣmaṇāya ca |
vīryaśulkāṁ mama sutāṁ sītāṁ surasutopamām ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
dvitīyām ūrmilāṁ caiva trir vadāmi na saṁśayaḥ |
dadāmi paramaprīto vadhvau te raghunandana ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
rāmalakṣmaṇayo rājan godānaṁ kārayasva ha |
pitr̥kāryaṁ ca bhadraṁ te tato vaivāhikaṁ kuru ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
maghā hy adya mahābāho tr̥tīye divase prabho |
phalgunyām uttare rājaṁs tasmin vaivāhikaṁ kuru |
rāmalakṣmaṇayor arthe dānaṁ kāryaṁ sukhodayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
