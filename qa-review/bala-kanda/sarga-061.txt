=== Sarga 61 ===

---
1.
śunaḥśepaṁ naraśreṣṭha gr̥hītvā tu mahāyaśāḥ |
vyaśrāmyat puṣkare rājā madhyāhne raghunandana ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tasya viśramamāṇasya śunaḥśepo mahāyaśāḥ |
puṣkaraṁ śreṣṭham āgamya viśvāmitraṁ dadarśa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
viṣaṇṇavadano dīnas tr̥ṣṇayā ca śrameṇa ca |
papātāṅke mune rāma vākyaṁ cedam uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
na me ’sti mātā na pitā jñātayo bāndhavāḥ kutaḥ |
trātum arhasi māṁ saumya dharmeṇa munipuṁgava ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
trātā tvaṁ hi muniśreṣṭha sarveṣāṁ tvaṁ hi bhāvanaḥ |
rājā ca kr̥takāryaḥ syād ahaṁ dīrghāyur avyayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
svargalokam upāśnīyāṁ tapas taptvā hy anuttamam |
sa me nātho hy anāthasya bhava bhavyena cetasā |
piteva putraṁ dharmātmaṁs trātum arhasi kilbiṣāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā viśvāmitro mahātapāḥ |
sāntvayitvā bahuvidhaṁ putrān idam uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
yatkr̥te pitaraḥ putrāñ janayanti śubhārthinaḥ |
paralokahitārthāya tasya kālo ’yam āgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
ayaṁ munisuto bālo mattaḥ śaraṇam icchati |
asya jīvitamātreṇa priyaṁ kuruta putrakāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
sarve sukr̥takarmāṇaḥ sarve dharmaparāyaṇāḥ |
paśubhūtā narendrasya tr̥ptim agneḥ prayacchata ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
nāthavāṁś ca śunaḥśepo yajñaś cāvighnato bhavet |
devatās tarpitāś ca syur mama cāpi kr̥taṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
munes tu vacanaṁ śrutvā madhuṣyandādayaḥ sutāḥ |
sābhimānaṁ naraśreṣṭha salīlam idam abruvan ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
katham ātmasutān hitvā trāyase ’nyasutaṁ vibho |
akāryam iva paśyāmaḥ śvamāṁsam iva bhojane ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
teṣāṁ tad vacanaṁ śrutvā putrāṇāṁ munipuṁgavaḥ |
krodhasaṁraktanayano vyāhartum upacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
niḥsādhvasam idaṁ proktaṁ dharmād api vigarhitam |
atikramya tu madvākyaṁ dāruṇaṁ romaharṣaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
śvamāṁsabhojinaḥ sarve vāsiṣṭhā iva jātiṣu |
pūrṇaṁ varṣasahasraṁ tu pr̥thivyām anuvatsyatha ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
kr̥tvā śāpasamāyuktān putrān munivaras tadā |
śunaḥśepam uvācārtaṁ kr̥tvā rakṣāṁ nirāmayām ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
pavitrapāśair āsakto raktamālyānulepanaḥ |
vaiṣṇavaṁ yūpam āsādya vāgbhir agnim udāhara ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
ime tu gāthe dve divye gāyethā muniputraka |
ambarīṣasya yajñe ’smiṁs tataḥ siddhim avāpsyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
śunaḥśepo gr̥hītvā te dve gāthe susamāhitaḥ |
tvarayā rājasiṁhaṁ tam ambarīṣam uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
rājasiṁha mahāsattva śīghraṁ gacchāvahe sadaḥ |
nivartayasva rājendra dīkṣāṁ ca samupāhara ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tad vākyam r̥ṣiputrasya śrutvā harṣaṁ samutsukaḥ |
jagāma nr̥patiḥ śīghraṁ yajñavāṭam atandritaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
sadasyānumate rājā pavitrakr̥talakṣaṇam |
paśuṁ raktāmbaraṁ kr̥tvā yūpe taṁ samabandhayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
sa baddho vāgbhir agryābhir abhituṣṭāva vai surau |
indram indrānujaṁ caiva yathāvan muniputrakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tataḥ prītaḥ sahasrākṣo rahasyastutitarpitaḥ |
dīrgham āyus tadā prādāc chunaḥśepāya rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
sa ca rājā naraśreṣṭha yajñasya ca samāptavān |
phalaṁ bahuguṇaṁ rāma sahasrākṣaprasādajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
viśvāmitro ’pi dharmātmā bhūyas tepe mahātapāḥ |
puṣkareṣu naraśreṣṭha daśavarṣaśatāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
