=== Sarga 50 ===

---
1.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā viśvāmitrasya dhīmataḥ |
hr̥ṣṭaromā mahātejāḥ śatānando mahātapāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
gautamasya suto jyeṣṭhas tapasā dyotitaprabhaḥ |
rāmasaṁdarśanād eva paraṁ vismayam āgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sa tau niṣaṇṇau saṁprekṣya sukhāsīnau nr̥pātmajau |
śatānando muniśreṣṭhaṁ viśvāmitram athābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
api te muniśārdūla mama mātā yaśasvinī |
darśitā rājaputrāya tapo dīrgham upāgatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
api rāme mahātejo mama mātā yaśasvinī |
vanyair upāharat pūjāṁ pūjārhe sarvadehinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
api rāmāya kathitaṁ yathāvr̥ttaṁ purātanam |
mama mātur mahātejo devena duranuṣṭhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
api kauśika bhadraṁ te guruṇā mama saṁgatā |
mātā mama muniśreṣṭha rāmasaṁdarśanād itaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
api me guruṇā rāmaḥ pūjitaḥ kuśikātmaja |
ihāgato mahātejāḥ pūjāṁ prāpya mahātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
api śāntena manasā gurur me kuśikātmaja |
ihāgatena rāmeṇa prayatenābhivāditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tac chrutvā vacanaṁ tasya viśvāmitro mahāmuniḥ |
pratyuvāca śatānandaṁ vākyajño vākyakovidam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
nātikrāntaṁ muniśreṣṭha yat kartavyaṁ kr̥taṁ mayā |
saṁgatā muninā patnī bhārgaveṇeva reṇukā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tac chrutvā vacanaṁ tasya viśvāmitrasya dhīmataḥ |
śatānando mahātejā rāmaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
svāgataṁ te naraśreṣṭha diṣṭyā prāpto ’si rāghava |
viśvāmitraṁ puraskr̥tya maharṣim aparājitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
acintyakarmā tapasā brahmarṣir amitaprabhaḥ |
viśvāmitro mahātejā vetsy enaṁ paramāṁ gatim ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
nāsti dhanyataro rāma tvatto ’nyo bhuvi kaś cana |
goptā kuśikaputras te yena taptaṁ mahat tapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
śrūyatāṁ cābhidāsyāmi kauśikasya mahātmanaḥ |
yathābalaṁ yathāvr̥ttaṁ tan me nigadataḥ śr̥ṇu ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
rājābhūd eṣa dharmātmā dīrgha kālam ariṁdamaḥ |
dharmajñaḥ kr̥tavidyaś ca prajānāṁ ca hite rataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
prajāpatisutas tv āsīt kuśo nāma mahīpatiḥ |
kuśasya putro balavān kuśanābhaḥ sudhārmikaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
kuśanābhasutas tv āsīd gādhir ity eva viśrutaḥ |
gādheḥ putro mahātejā viśvāmitro mahāmuniḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
viśvamitro mahātejāḥ pālayām āsa medinīm |
bahuvarṣasahasrāṇi rājā rājyam akārayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
kadā cit tu mahātejā yojayitvā varūthinīm |
akṣauhiṇīparivr̥taḥ paricakrāma medinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
nagarāṇi ca rāṣṭrāṇi saritaś ca tathā girīn |
āśramān kramaśo rājā vicarann ājagāmaha ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
vasiṣṭhasyāśramapadaṁ nānāpuṣpaphaladrumam |
nānāmr̥gagaṇākīrṇaṁ siddhacāraṇasevitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
devadānavagandharvaiḥ kiṁnarair upaśobhitam |
praśāntahariṇākīrṇaṁ dvijasaṁghaniṣevitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
brahmarṣigaṇasaṁkīrṇaṁ devarṣigaṇasevitam |
tapaścaraṇasaṁsiddhair agnikalpair mahātmabhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
satataṁ saṁkulaṁ śrīmad brahmakalpair mahātmabhiḥ |
abbhakṣair vāyubhakṣaiś ca śīrṇaparṇāśanais tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
phalamūlāśanair dāntair jitaroṣair jitendriyaiḥ |
r̥ṣibhir vālakhilyaiś ca japahomaparāyaṇaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
vasiṣṭhasyāśramapadaṁ brahmalokam ivāparam |
dadarśa jayatāṁ śreṣṭha viśvāmitro mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
