=== Sarga 50 ===
Arrival at Janaka's Yajña; Janaka Welcomes Viśvāmitra

---
1.
tataḥ prāguttarāṁ gatvā rāmaḥ saumitriṇā saha |
viśvāmitraṁ puraskr̥tya yajñavāṭamupāgamat ||

> **Meaning 1:** Proceeding northeast with Saumitri, Rāma followed
> Viśvāmitra's lead and arrived at the yajña enclosure.

---
2.
rāmastu muniśārdūlamuvāca sahalakṣmaṇaḥ |
sādhvī yajñasamr̥ddhirhi janakasya mahātmanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 2:** Rāma, together with Lakṣmaṇa, said to the foremost of munis:
> "Truly grand is the abundance of the great-souled Janaka's yajña!

---
3.
bahūnīha sahasrāṇi nānādēśanivāsinām |
brāhmaṇānāṁ mahābhāga vēdādhyayanaśālinām ||

> **Meaning 3:** "Blessed sage, there are many thousands of brāhmaṇas here from
> various lands, all devoted to Vedic study.

---
4.
r̥ṣivāṭāśca dr̥śyantē śakaṭīśatasaṅkulāḥ |
dēśō vidhīyatāṁ brahmanyatra vatsyāmahē vayam ||

> **Meaning 4:** "Revered sage, the encampments of the r̥ṣis are crowded with
> hundreds of carts; please designate a fitting place for us to stay."

---
5.
rāmasya vacanaṁ śrutvā viśvāmitrō mahāmuniḥ |
nivēśamakarōddēśē viviktē salilāyutē ||

> **Meaning 5:** Hearing Rāma's words, the great muni Viśvāmitra made their camp
> in a secluded spot well supplied with water.

---
6.
viśvāmitraṁ muniśrēṣṭhaṁ śrutvā sa nr̥patistadā |
śatānandaṁ puraskr̥tya purōhitamaninditam ||

> **Meaning 6:** Upon hearing that Viśvāmitra, the foremost of munis, had
> arrived, King Janaka went to meet him with his exemplary purohita Śatānanda
> leading the way.

---
7.
r̥tvijō'pi mahātmānastvarghyamādāya satvaram |
viśvāmitrāya dharmēṇa dadurmantrapuraskr̥tam ||

> **Meaning 7:** The great-souled r̥tvik priests also promptly brought arghya
> and, in keeping with dharma, offered it to Viśvāmitra with sacred mantras.

---
8.
pratigr̥hya tu tāṁ pūjāṁ janakasya mahātmanaḥ |
papraccha kuśalaṁ rājñō yajñasya ca nirāmayam ||

> **Meaning 8:** Accepting that honor from the noble Janaka, Viśvāmitra
> inquired after the king's well-being and asked whether the yajña was
> proceeding without hindrance.

---
9.
sa tāṁścāpi munīnpr̥ṣṭvā sōpādhyāya purōdhasaḥ |
yathānyāyaṁ tataḥ sarvaiḥ samāgacchatprahr̥ṣṭavān ||

> **Meaning 9:** Viśvāmitra then inquired after the well-being of the munis,
> the upādhyāyas, and the purohita who had come with Janaka, and joyfully
> greeted them all with proper courtesy.

---
10.
atha rājā muniśrēṣṭhaṁ kr̥tāñjalirabhāṣata |
āsanē bhagavānāstāṁ sahaibhirmunisattamaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 10:** Then the king joined his palms in añjali and said to the
> foremost of munis: "Revered one, please take your seat together with these
> eminent munis."

---
11.
janakasya vacaḥ śrutvā niṣasāda mahāmuniḥ |
purōdhā r̥tvijaścaiva rājā ca saha mantribhiḥ ||

> **Meaning 11:** Hearing Janaka's words, the great muni sat down, and the
> purohita, the r̥tvik priests, and the king along with his counselors also took
> their seats.

---
12.
āsanēṣu yathānyāyamupaviṣṭānsamantataḥ |
dr̥ṣṭvā sa nr̥patistatra viśvāmitramathābravīt ||

> **Meaning 12:** Seeing them all seated in proper order on every side, the king
> spoke to Viśvāmitra:

---
13.
adya yajñasamr̥ddhirmē saphalā daivataiḥ kr̥tā |
adya yajñaphalaṁ prāptaṁ bhagavaddarśanānmayā ||

> **Meaning 13:** "Today the devatās have made the grandeur of my yajña truly
> fruitful, and today, by having your darśana, I have received the very fruit of
> this yajña!

---
14.
dhanyō'smyanugr̥hītō'smi yasya mē munipuṅgava |
yajñōpasadanaṁ brahmanprāptō'si munibhiḥ saha ||

> **Meaning 14:** "Foremost of munis, I am blessed and deeply favored that you
> have come to my yajña hall accompanied by these munis.

---
15.
dvādaśāhaṁ tu brahmarṣē śēṣamāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ |
tatō bhāgārthinō dēvāndraṣṭumarhasi kauśika ||

> **Meaning 15:** "Brahmarṣi Kauśika, the learned priests say that twelve days
> of the yajña still remain; once those are over, you too will be able to witness
> the devatās arriving to receive their portions."

---
16.
ityuktvā muniśārdūlaṁ prahr̥ṣṭavadanastadā |
punastaṁ paripapraccha prāñjaliḥ prayatō nr̥paḥ ||

> **Meaning 16:** Having spoken thus to the foremost of munis with a joyful
> face, the devout king joined his palms in añjali and asked him earnestly once
> more:

---
17.
imau kumārau bhadraṁ tē dēvatulyaparākramau |
gajasiṁhagatī vīrau śārdūlavr̥ṣabhōpamau ||

> **Meaning 17:** "May all be well with you! These two heroic young princes have
> the valor of devatās, the stride of elephants and lions, and the bearing of
> tigers and bulls.

---
18.
padmapatraviśālākṣau khaḍgatūṇīdhanurdharau |
aśvināviva rūpēṇa samupasthitayauvanau ||

> **Meaning 18:** "With large eyes like lotus petals, bearing swords, quivers,
> and bows, they resemble the Aśvins in beauty and have just reached the
> threshold of youth.

---
19.
yadr̥cchayaiva gāṁ prāptau dēvalōkādivāmarau |
kathaṁ padbhyāmiha prāptau kimarthaṁ kasya vā munē ||

> **Meaning 19:** "Muni, they look like two devatās who have come down to earth
> from Devaloka of their own accord. How is it that they have come here walking
> on the bare ground? What is the reason for their coming, and whose sons are
> they?

---
20.
varāyudhadharau vīrau kasya putrau mahāmunē |
bhūṣayantāvimaṁ dēśaṁ candrasūryāvivāmbaram ||

> **Meaning 20:** "Great muni, whose sons are these two heroes carrying fine
> weapons, who adorn this land just as the moon and the sun adorn the sky?

---
21.
parasparasya sadr̥śau pramāṇēṅgitacēṣṭitaiḥ |
kākapakṣadharau vīrau śrōtumicchāmi tattvataḥ ||

> **Meaning 21:** "They closely resemble one another in stature, gesture, and
> movement, and these two heroes wear the side-locks of youth; I wish to hear
> the truth about them."

---
22.
tasya tadvacanaṁ śrutvā janakasya mahātmanaḥ |
nyavēdayanmahātmānau putrau daśarathasya tau ||

> **Meaning 22:** Hearing the words of the noble Janaka, Viśvāmitra
> introduced them as the two illustrious sons of Daśaratha.

---
23.
siddhāśramanivāsaṁ ca rākṣasānāṁ vadhaṁ tathā |
taccāgamanamavyagraṁ viśālāyāśca darśanam ||

> **Meaning 23:** He recounted their stay at Siddhāśrama and the slaying of the
> rākṣasas, their unhurried journey, and their visit to Viśālā,

---
24.
ahalyādarśanaṁ caiva gautamēna samāgamam |
mahādhanuṣi jijñāsāṁ kartumāgamanaṁ tathā ||

> **Meaning 24:** as well as their meeting with Ahalyā, the reunion with
> Gautama, and their coming here to learn about and see the great bow.

---
25.
ētatsarvaṁ mahātējā janakāya mahātmanē |
nivēdya virarāmātha viśvāmitrō mahāmuniḥ ||

> **Meaning 25:** After relating all of this to the noble King Janaka, the
> deeply radiant great muni Viśvāmitra fell silent.

---
