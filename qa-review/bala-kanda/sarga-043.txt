=== Sarga 43 ===
Gaṅgā's Descent and Liberation of Sagara's Sons

---
1.
dēvadēvē gatē tasmin sōṁ'guṣṭhāgranipīḍitām |
kr̥tvā vasumatīṁ rāma vatsaraṁ samupāsata ||

> **Meaning 1:** "Rāma, when that god of gods had departed, Bhagīratha stood
> pressing the earth with just the tip of his big toe and worshiped Śiva for a
> full year.

---
2.
ūrdhvabāhurnirālambō vāyubhakṣō nirāśrayaḥ |
acalaḥ sthāṇuvatsthitvā rātriṁ divamarindama ||

> **Meaning 2:** "Rāma, subduer of foes, with his arms raised, unsupported and
> without shelter, and subsisting on air alone, he stood motionless like a
> tree-stump day and night.

---
3.
atha saṁvatsarē pūrṇē sarvalōkanamaskr̥taḥ |
umāpatiḥ paśupatī rājānamidamabravīt ||

> **Meaning 3:** "Then, when a full year was complete, Śiva, the consort of Umā,
> revered by all the worlds, spoke these words to the king:

---
4.
prītastē'haṁ naraśrēṣṭha kariṣyāmi tava priyam |
śirasā dhārayiṣyāmi śailarājasutāmaham ||

> **Meaning 4:** "'Foremost among men, I am pleased with you, and I will fulfill
> your wish by holding the daughter of the mountain-king upon my head.'

---
5.
tatō haimavatī jyēṣṭhā sarvalōkanamaskr̥tā |
tadā sātimahadrūpaṁ kr̥tvā vēgaṁ ca duḥsaham |
ākāśādapatadrāma śivē śivaśirasyuta ||

> **Meaning 5:** "Rāma, Gaṅgā, eldest daughter of Himavān and revered by all the
> worlds, then assumed a colossal form and an irresistible force, and plunged
> from the sky onto the auspicious head of Śiva.

---
6.
acintayacca sā dēvī gaṅgā paramadurdharā |
viśāmyahaṁ hi pātālaṁ strōtasā gr̥hya śaṅkaram ||

> **Meaning 6:** "And that goddess Gaṅgā, supremely hard to contain, thought to
> herself, 'I will sweep Śaṅkara along in my current and plunge straight into
> Pātāla.'

---
7.
tasyā valēpanaṁ jñātvā kruddhastu bhagavān haraḥ |
tirōbhāvayituṁ buddhiṁ cakrē trinayanastadā ||

> **Meaning 7:** "Perceiving her arrogance, the divine Śiva grew angry, and the
> three-eyed god resolved to make her vanish completely.

---
8.
sā tasmin patitā puṇyā puṇyē rudrasya mūrdhani |
himavatpratimē rāma jaṭāmaṇḍalagahvarē ||

> **Meaning 8:** "Rāma, sacred Gaṅgā fell upon Śiva's hallowed head, into the
> deep, cavernous tangle of his coiled jaṭās, vast as Mount Himavān.

---
9.
sā kathañcinmahīṁ gantuṁ nāśaknōdyatnamāsthitā |
naiva sā nirgamaṁ lēbhē jaṭāmaṇḍalamantataḥ ||

> **Meaning 9:** "Despite all her efforts, she could not reach the earth at all,
> finding no way out from within that tangle of jaṭās.

---
10.
tatraivābambhramaddēvī saṁvatsaragaṇānbahūn |
tāmapaśyan punastatra tapaḥ paramamāsthitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 10:** "The goddess wandered round and round inside his jaṭās for
> many years. Seeing her held there, Bhagīratha once again undertook supreme
> tapas.

---
11.
sa tēna tōṣitaścāsīdatyantaṁ raghunandana ||
visasarja tatō gaṅgāṁ harō bindusaraḥ prati |

> **Meaning 11:** "Raghunandana, deeply pleased by that tapas, Śiva then
> released Gaṅgā toward Lake Bindusaras.

---
12.
tasyāṁ visr̥jyamānāyāṁ sapta srōtaṁsi jajñirē |
hlādinī pāvanī caiva nalinī ca tathaiva ca ||

> **Meaning 12:** "As she was released, seven streams emerged from her: Hlādinī,
> Pāvanī, and Nalinī,

---
13.
tisraḥ prācīṁ diśaṁ jagmurgaṅgāḥ śivajalāḥ śubhāḥ |
sucakṣuścaiva sītā ca sindhuścaiva mahānadī ||

> **Meaning 13:** "those three auspicious, pure-watered streams of Gaṅgā flowed
> eastward; while Sucakṣu, Sītā, and the mighty river Sindhu,

---
14.
tisrastvētā diśaṁ jagmuḥ pratīcīṁ tu śubhōdakāḥ |
saptamī cānvagāttāsāṁ bhagīratharathaṁ tadā ||

> **Meaning 14:** "these three pure-watered streams flowed westward, while the
> seventh of them followed behind Bhagīratha's chariot.

---
15.
bhagīrathō'pi rajarṣirdivyaṁ syandanamāsthitaḥ |
prāyādagrē mahātējā gaṅgā taṁ cāpyanuvrajat ||

> **Meaning 15:** "Mounted upon his divine chariot, the radiant rājarṣi
> Bhagīratha rode ahead, and Gaṅgā followed closely in his path.

---
16.
gaganācchaṅkaraśirastatō dharaṇimāgatā |
vyasarpata jalaṁ tatra tīvraśabdapuraskr̥tam ||

> **Meaning 16:** "Descending from the sky onto Śaṅkara's head and from there
> onto the earth, her waters surged forward with a thunderous roar.

---
17.
matsyakacchapasaṅghaiśca śiṁśumāragaṇaistathā |
patadbhiḥ patitaiścaiva vyarōcata vasundharā ||

> **Meaning 17:** "With teeming shoals of fish, tortoises, and hosts of
> river-dolphins, some still cascading down from above and others already
> landed, the earth shone in splendor.

---
18.
tatō dēvarṣigandharvā yakṣā siddhagaṇāstathā |
vyalōkayanta tē tatra gaganādgāṁ gatāṁ tadā ||

> **Meaning 18:** "Then devarṣis, Gandharvas, Yakṣas, and hosts of Siddhas
> gathered there and watched as she descended from the sky to the earth.

---
19.
vimānairnagarākārairhayairgajavaraistathā |
pāriplavagatāścāpi dēvatāstatra viṣṭhitāḥ ||

> **Meaning 19:** "Riding city-sized vimānas, swift horses, and noble elephants,
> and hovering eagerly in midair, the devatās assembled there.

---
20.
tadadbhutatamaṁ lōkē gaṅgāpatanamuttamam |
didr̥kṣavō dēvagaṇāḥ samīyuramitaujasaḥ ||

> **Meaning 20:** "Eager to witness Gaṅgā's supreme descent, the greatest wonder
> in all the world, the boundlessly radiant hosts of devatās gathered together.

---
21.
sampatadbhiḥ suragaṇaistēṣāṁ cābharaṇaujasā |
śatādityamivābhāti gaganaṁ gatatōyadam ||

> **Meaning 21:** "With the thronging hosts of suras and the brilliance of their
> ornaments, the cloudless sky blazed as though lit by a hundred suns.

---
22.
śiṁśumārōragagaṇairmīnairapi ca cañcalaiḥ |
vidyudbhiriva vikṣiptairākāśamabhavattadā ||

> **Meaning 22:** "With throngs of river-dolphins, serpents, and darting fish
> scattered across it, the sky looked as though streaked with flashes of
> lightning.

---
23.
pāṇḍuraiḥ salilōtpīḍaiḥ kīryamāṇaiḥ sahasradhā |
śāradābhrairivākīrṇaṁ gaganaṁ haṁsasamplavaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 23:** "As pale-white plumes of spray burst and scattered in a
> thousand directions, the sky seemed filled with autumn clouds and flocks of
> gliding swans.

---
24.
kvacid drutataraṁ yāti kuṭilaṁ kvacidāyatam |
vinataṁ kvaciduddhūtaṁ kvacidyāti śanaiḥ śanaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 24:** "In one place she rushed swiftly forward, in another she
> wound in curves or spread wide; here she plunged down, there she surged
> upward, and elsewhere she flowed gently on.

---
25.
salilēnaiva salilaṁ kvacidabhyāhataṁ punaḥ |
muhurūrdhvapathaṁ gatvā papāta vasudhāṁ punaḥ ||

> **Meaning 25:** "In places, her waters clashed against one another, shooting
> high into the air again and again before plunging back down to the earth.

---
26.
tacchaṅkaraśirōbhraṣṭaṁ bhraṣṭaṁ bhūmitalē punaḥ |
vyarōcata tadā tōyaṁ nirmalaṁ gatakalmaṣam ||

> **Meaning 26:** "Cascading from Śiva's head and falling in turn onto the
> earth below, those crystal-clear, unblemished waters shone in splendor.

---
27.
tatrarṣigaṇagandharvā vasudhātalavāsinaḥ |
bhavāṅgapatitaṁ tōyaṁ pavitramiti paspr̥śuḥ ||

> **Meaning 27:** "There upon the earth, the hosts of r̥ṣis, Gandharvas, and
> all its dwellers touched that water, revering it as sacred for having fallen
> from Śiva's body.

---
28.
śāpāt prapatitā yē ca gaganādvasudhātalam |
kr̥tvā tatrābhiṣēkaṁ tē babhūvurgatakalmaṣāḥ ||

29.
dhūtapāpāḥ punastēna tōyēnātha śubhānvitā |
punarākāśamāviśya svān lōkān pratipēdirē ||

> **Meaning 28-29:** "And those who had fallen from the heavens to the earth
> under a curse bathed in those waters; freed of every blemish, their sins
> washed away and their merit restored, they rose back into the sky and
> returned to their own worlds.

---
30.
mumudē muditō lōkastēna tōyēna bhāsvatā |
kr̥tābhiṣēkō gaṅgāyāṁ babhūva vigataklamaḥ ||

> **Meaning 30:** "Overjoyed by those resplendent waters, the whole world
> rejoiced; bathing in Gaṅgā, all beings were freed from weariness and distress.

---
31.
bhagīrathō'pi rājarṣirdivyaṁ syandanamāsthitaḥ |
prāyādagrē mahātējāstaṁ gaṅgā pr̥ṣṭhatō'nvagāt ||

> **Meaning 31:** "Mounted upon his divine chariot, the radiant rājarṣi
> Bhagīratha drove on ahead, while Gaṅgā followed close behind him.

---
32.
dēvāḥ sarṣigaṇāḥ sarvē daityadānavarākṣasāḥ |
gandharvayakṣapravarāḥ sakinnaramahōragāḥ ||

> **Meaning 32:** "Rāma, all the devatās with the assemblies of r̥ṣis, as well
> as Daityas, Dānavas, Rākṣasas, foremost Gandharvas and Yakṣas, Kinnaras, and
> Mahoragas,

---
33.
sarpāścāpsarasō rāma bhagīratharathānugāḥ |
gaṅgāmanvagaman prītāḥ sarvē jalacarāśca yē ||

> **Meaning 33:** "along with serpents, Apsarās, and all creatures of the water,
> joyfully followed Gaṅgā as she trailed Bhagīratha's chariot.

---
34.
yatō bhagīrathō rājā tatō gaṅgā yaśasvinī |
jagāma saritāṁ śrēṣṭhā sarvapāpapraṇāśinī ||

> **Meaning 34:** "Wherever King Bhagīratha led, there went the illustrious
> Gaṅgā, foremost of all rivers and destroyer of every sin.

---
35.
tatō hi yajamānasya jahnōradbhutakarmaṇaḥ |
gaṅgā samplāvayāmāsa yajñavāṭaṁ mahatmanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 35:** "Along the way, Gaṅgā flooded the yajña-enclosure of the
> noble-souled Jahnu, a sage of wondrous deeds who was then performing a yajña.

---
36.
tasyāvalēpanaṁ jñātvā kruddhō jahnuśca rāghava |
apibacca jalaṁ sarvaṁ gaṅgāyāḥ paramādbhutam ||

> **Meaning 36:** "Rāghava, seeing her arrogance, Jahnu grew angry, and in an
> astonishing feat he drank up all the waters of Gaṅgā.

---
37.
tatō dēvāḥ sagandharvā r̥ṣayaśca suvismitāḥ |
pūjayanti mahātmānaṁ jahnuṁ puruṣasattamam |
gaṅgāṁ cāpi nayanti sma duhitr̥tvē mahātmanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 37:** "Foremost among men, utterly amazed, the devatās, Gandharvas,
> and r̥ṣis paid homage to the great Jahnu and offered Gaṅgā to that revered
> sage as his daughter.

---
38.
tatastuṣṭō mahātējāḥ śrōtrābhyāmasr̥jat prabhuḥ |
tasmājjahnusutā gaṅgā prōcyatē jāhnavīti ca ||

> **Meaning 38:** "Pleased by this, the radiant, mighty sage released her
> through his ears. That is why Gaṅgā is known as Jahnu's daughter and called
> Jāhnavī.

---
39.
jagāma ca punargaṅgā bhagīratharathānugā |
sāgaraṁ cāpi samprāptā sā saritpravarā tadā |
rasātalamupāgacchat siddhyarthaṁ tasya karmaṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 39:** "Once more, Gaṅgā flowed onward behind Bhagīratha's chariot;
> reaching the ocean at last, that foremost of rivers entered Rasātala to
> bring Bhagīratha's mission to completion.

---
40.
bhagīrathō'pi rajārṣirgaṅgāmādāya yatnataḥ |
pitāmahān bhasmakr̥tānapaśyadgatacētanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 40:** "Having brought Gaṅgā there with great effort, the rājarṣi
> Bhagīratha saw his ancestors, who had been reduced to ashes, and was
> overcome with grief.

---
41.
atha tadbhasmanāṁ rāśiṁ gaṅgāsalilamuttamam |
plāvayat pūtapāpmānaḥ svargaṁ praptā raghūttama ||

> **Meaning 41:** "Then, Raghūttama, the supreme waters of Gaṅgā washed over
> that heap of ashes, and, purified of all sin, his ancestors attained svarga.

---
