=== Sarga 43 ===

---
1.
sa gatvā sāgaraṁ rājā gaṅgayānugatas tadā |
praviveśa talaṁ bhūmer yatra te bhasmasātkr̥tāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
bhasmany athāplute rāma gaṅgāyāḥ salilena vai |
sarva lokaprabhur brahmā rājānam idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tāritā naraśārdūla divaṁ yātāś ca devavat |
ṣaṣṭiḥ putrasahasrāṇi sagarasya mahātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
sāgarasya jalaṁ loke yāvat sthāsyati pārthiva |
sagarasyātmajās tāvat svarge sthāsyanti devavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
iyaṁ ca duhitā jyeṣṭhā tava gaṅgā bhaviṣyati |
tvatkr̥tena ca nāmnā vai loke sthāsyati viśrutā ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
gaṅgā tripathagā nāma divyā bhāgīrathīti ca |
tripatho bhāvayantīti tatas tripathagā smr̥tā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
pitāmahānāṁ sarveṣāṁ tvam atra manujādhipa |
kuruṣva salilaṁ rājan pratijñām apavarjaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
pūrvakeṇa hi te rājaṁs tenātiyaśasā tadā |
dharmiṇāṁ pravareṇātha naiṣa prāpto manorathaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tathaivāṁśumatā tāta loke ’pratimatejasā |
gaṅgāṁ prārthayatā netuṁ pratijñā nāpavarjitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
rājarṣiṇā guṇavatā maharṣisamatejasā |
mattulyatapasā caiva kṣatradharmasthitena ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
dilīpena mahābhāga tava pitrātitejasā |
punar na śaṅkitā netuṁ gaṅgāṁ prārthayatānagha ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sā tvayā samatikrāntā pratijñā puruṣarṣabha |
prāpto ’si paramaṁ loke yaśaḥ paramasaṁmatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
yac ca gaṅgāvataraṇaṁ tvayā kr̥tam ariṁdama |
anena ca bhavān prāpto dharmasyāyatanaṁ mahat ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
plāvayasva tvam ātmānaṁ narottama sadocite |
salile puruṣavyāghra śuciḥ puṇyaphalo bhava ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
pitāmahānāṁ sarveṣāṁ kuruṣva salilakriyām |
svasti te ’stu gamiṣyāmi svaṁ lokaṁ gamyatāṁ nr̥pa ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
ity evam uktvā deveśaḥ sarvalokapitāmahaḥ |
yathāgataṁ tathāgacchad devalokaṁ mahāyaśāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
bhagīratho ’pi rājarṣiḥ kr̥tvā salilam uttamam |
yathākramaṁ yathānyāyaṁ sāgarāṇāṁ mahāyaśāḥ |
kr̥todakaḥ śucī rājā svapuraṁ praviveśa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
samr̥ddhārtho naraśreṣṭha svarājyaṁ praśaśāsa ha |
pramumoda ca lokas taṁ nr̥pam āsādya rāghava |
naṣṭaśokaḥ samr̥ddhārtho babhūva vigatajvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
eṣa te rāma gaṅgāyā vistaro ’bhihito mayā |
svasti prāpnuhi bhadraṁ te saṁdhyākālo ’tivartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
dhanyaṁ yaśasyam āyuṣyaṁ svargyaṁ putryam athāpi ca |
idam ākhyānam ākhyātaṁ gaṅgāvataraṇaṁ mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
