=== Sarga 30 ===

---
1.
atha tāṁ rajanīṁ tatra kr̥tārthau rāmalakṣaṇau |
ūṣatur muditau vīrau prahr̥ṣṭenāntarātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
prabhātāyāṁ tu śarvaryāṁ kr̥tapaurvāhṇikakriyau |
viśvāmitram r̥ṣīṁś cānyān sahitāv abhijagmatuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
abhivādya muniśreṣṭhaṁ jvalantam iva pāvakam |
ūcatur madhurodāraṁ vākyaṁ madhurabhāṣiṇau ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
imau svo muniśārdūla kiṁkarau samupasthitau |
ājñāpaya yatheṣṭaṁ vai śāsanaṁ karavāva kim ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
evam ukte tatas tābhyāṁ sarva eva maharṣayaḥ |
viśvāmitraṁ puraskr̥tya rāmaṁ vacanam abruvan ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
maithilasya naraśreṣṭha janakasya bhaviṣyati |
yajñaḥ paramadharmiṣṭhas tatra yāsyāmahe vayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tvaṁ caiva naraśārdūla sahāsmābhir gamiṣyasi |
adbhutaṁ ca dhanūratnaṁ tatra tvaṁ draṣṭum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tad dhi pūrvaṁ naraśreṣṭha dattaṁ sadasi daivataiḥ |
aprameyabalaṁ ghoraṁ makhe paramabhāsvaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
nāsya devā na gandharvā nāsurā na ca rākṣasāḥ |
kartum āropaṇaṁ śaktā na kathaṁ cana mānuṣāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
dhanuṣas tasya vīryaṁ hi jijñāsanto mahīkṣitaḥ |
na śekur āropayituṁ rājaputrā mahābalāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tad dhanur naraśārdūla maithilasya mahātmanaḥ |
tatra drakṣyasi kākutstha yajñaṁ cādbhutadarśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tad dhi yajñaphalaṁ tena maithilenottamaṁ dhanuḥ |
yācitaṁ naraśārdūla sunābhaṁ sarvadaivataiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
evam uktvā munivaraḥ prasthānam akarot tadā |
sarṣisaṁghaḥ sakākutstha āmantrya vanadevatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
svasti vo ’stu gamiṣyāmi siddhaḥ siddhāśramād aham |
uttare jāhnavītīre himavantaṁ śiloccayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
pradakṣiṇaṁ tataḥ kr̥tvā siddhāśramam anuttamam |
uttarāṁ diśam uddiśya prasthātum upacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
taṁ vrajantaṁ munivaram anvagād anusāriṇām |
śakaṭī śatamātraṁ tu prayāṇe brahmavādinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
mr̥gapakṣigaṇāś caiva siddhāśramanivāsinaḥ |
anujagmur mahātmānaṁ viśvāmitraṁ mahāmunim ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
te gatvā dūram adhvānaṁ lambamāne divākare |
vāsaṁ cakrur munigaṇāḥ śoṇākūle samāhitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
te ’staṁ gate dinakare snātvā hutahutāśanāḥ |
viśvāmitraṁ puraskr̥tya niṣedur amitaujasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
rāmo ’pi sahasaumitrir munīṁs tān abhipūjya ca |
agrato niṣasādātha viśvāmitrasya dhīmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
atha rāmo mahātejā viśvāmitraṁ mahāmunim |
papraccha muniśārdūlaṁ kautūhalasamanvitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
bhagavan ko nv ayaṁ deśaḥ samr̥ddhavanaśobhitaḥ |
śrotum icchāmi bhadraṁ te vaktum arhasi tattvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
codito rāmavākyena kathayām āsa suvrataḥ |
tasya deśasya nikhilam r̥ṣimadhye mahātapāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
