=== Sarga 30 ===
Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa Guard Viśvāmitra's Yajña; Defeat of Mārīca and Slaying of Subāhu

---
1.
atha tau dēśakālajñau rājaputrāvarindamau |
dēśē kālē ca vākyajñāvabrūtāṁ kauśikaṁ vacaḥ ||

> **Meaning 1:** Then those two princes, tamers of their foes, who understood
> place and time and knew how to speak fittingly at the right moment, said to
> Kauśika:

---
2.
bhagavañśrōtumicchāvō yasminkālē niśācarau |
saṁrakṣaṇīyau tau brahmannātivartēta tatkṣaṇam ||

> **Meaning 2:** "Venerable sage, we wish to hear at what exact time we must
> guard against those two night-stalkers, so that the moment does not slip past
> us."

---
3.
ēvaṁ bruvāṇau kākutsthau tvaramāṇau yuyutsayā |
sarvē tē munayaḥ prītāḥ praśaśaṁsurnr̥pātmajau ||

> **Meaning 3:** Hearing the two descendants of Kakutstha speak like this in
> their eagerness for battle, all the sages were pleased and praised the two
> princes:

---
4.
adya prabhr̥ti ṣaḍrātraṁ rakṣataṁ rāghavau yuvām |
dīkṣāṁ gatō hyēṣa munirmaunitvaṁ ca gamiṣyati ||

> **Meaning 4:** "From today onward, Rāghavas, stand guard for six nights; this
> sage Viśvāmitra has entered his yajña vow and will now observe silence."

---
5.
tau tu tadvacanaṁ śrutvā rājaputrau yaśasvinau |
anidrau ṣaḍahōrātraṁ tapōvanamarakṣatām ||

> **Meaning 5:** Hearing those words, the two renowned princes went without
> sleep for six days and nights, guarding the penance grove.

---
6.
upāsāṁ cakraturvīrau yattau paramadhanvinau |
rarakṣaturmunivaraṁ viśvāmitramarindamau ||

> **Meaning 6:** Those two heroic, supreme archers, tamers of foes, kept alert
> vigil and protected the foremost sage Viśvāmitra.

---
7.
atha kālē gatē tasminṣaṣṭhē'hani samāgatē |
saumitramabravīdrāmō yattō bhava samāhitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 7:** As those days passed and the sixth day arrived, Rāma said to
> Saumitri, "Be vigilant and stay focused!"

---
8.
rāmasyaivaṁ bruvāṇasya tvaritasya yuyutsayā |
prajajvāla tatō vēdiḥ sōpādhyāyapurōhitā ||

> **Meaning 8:** As Rāma spoke these words in his eagerness for battle, the yajña
> altar blazed up, surrounded by the teachers and priests.

---
9.
sadarbhacamasasrukkā sasamitkusumōccayā |
viśvāmitrēṇa sahitā vēdirjajvāla sartvijā ||

> **Meaning 9:** With darbha grass, camasa cups, sruk ladles, and heaps of
> firewood and flowers, the altar blazed brightly alongside Viśvāmitra and his
> officiating priests.

---
10.
mantravacca yathānyāyaṁ yajñō'sau sampravartatē |
ākāśē ca mahāñśabdaḥ prādurāsīdbhayānakaḥ ||

> **Meaning 10:** As the yajña proceeded duly with Vedic mantras, a tremendous,
> terrifying roar suddenly broke out in the sky.

---
11.
āvārya gaganaṁ mēghō yathā prāvr̥ṣi nirgataḥ |
tathā māyāṁ vikurvāṇau rākṣasāvabhyadhāvatām ||

> **Meaning 11:** Just as monsoon clouds surge forward and cover the sky, the
> two rākṣasas came rushing in, unleashing their māyā.

---
12.
mārīcaśca subāhuśca tayōranucarāstathā |
āgamya bhīmasaṅkāśā rudhiraughānavāsr̥jan ||

> **Meaning 12:** Mārīca and Subāhu, along with their followers—terrifying to
> look at—swooped down and poured down torrents of blood.

---
13.
sā tēna rudhiraughēṇa vēdīṁ vīkṣya samukṣitām |
sahasābhidrutō rāmastānapaśyattatō divi ||

> **Meaning 13:** Seeing the altar spattered by that torrent of blood, Rāma
> sprang forward at once and spotted them in the sky.

---
14.
tāvāpatantau sahasā dr̥ṣṭvā rājīvalōcanaḥ |
lakṣmaṇaṁ tvabhisamprēkṣya rāmō vacanamabravīt ||

> **Meaning 14:** Catching sight of the two of them diving down suddenly,
> lotus-eyed Rāma looked toward Lakṣmaṇa and said:

---
15.
paśya lakṣmaṇa durvr̥ttānrākṣasānpiśitāśanān |
mānavāstrasamādhūtānanilēna yathāghanān ||

> **Meaning 15:** "Watch, Lakṣmaṇa! Like clouds scattered by the wind, these
> wicked, flesh-eating rākṣasas will be blown away by the Mānavāstra—

---
16.
kariṣyāmi na sandēhō nōtsahē hantumīdr̥śān |
ityuktvā vacanaṁ rāmaścāpē sandhāya vēgavān ||

> **Meaning 16:** "I will make sure of it, without a doubt, though I have no
> wish to kill creatures like these." Saying this, swift Rāma fitted an arrow to
> his bow.

---
17.
mānavaṁ paramōdāramastraṁ paramabhāsvaram |
cikṣēpa paramakruddhō mārīcōrasi rāghavaḥ ||

> **Meaning 17:** Filled with fierce anger, Rāghava released the noble, dazzling
> Mānavāstra straight at Mārīca's chest.

---
18.
sa tēna paramāstrēṇa mānavēna samāhitaḥ |
sampūrṇaṁ yōjanaśataṁ kṣiptaḥ sāgarasamplavē ||

> **Meaning 18:** Struck squarely by that supreme Mānavāstra, Mārīca was hurled
> a full hundred yojanas away into the surging ocean.

---
19.
vicētanaṁ vighūrṇantaṁ śītēṣubalapīḍitam |
nirastaṁ dr̥śya mārīcaṁ rāmō lakṣmaṇamabravīt ||

> **Meaning 19:** Seeing Mārīca cast far away—unconscious, reeling, and
> overpowered by the force of Śīteṣu, the Mānavāstra—Rāma said to Lakṣmaṇa:

---
20.
paśya lakṣmaṇa śītēṣuṁ mānavaṁ dharmasaṁhitam |
mōhayitvā nayatyēnaṁ na ca prāṇairviyujyatē ||

> **Meaning 20:** "Look, Lakṣmaṇa, at Śīteṣu, the Mānavāstra bound by dharma—it
> knocks him senseless and carries him off without taking his life.

---
21.
imānapi vadhiṣyāmi nirghr̥ṇānduṣṭacāriṇaḥ |
rākṣasānpāpakarmasthānyajñaghnānrudhirāśanān ||

> **Meaning 21:** "As for these others—ruthless, evil-doing rākṣasas steeped in
> sin, destroyers of yajñas and drinkers of blood—I will slay them!"

---
22.
vigr̥hya sumahaccāstramāgnēyaṁ raghunandanaḥ |
subāhurasi cikṣēpa sa viddhaḥ prāpatadbhuvi ||

> **Meaning 22:** Taking up the mighty Āgneyāstra, Raghunandana shot it into
> Subāhu's chest; pierced through, Subāhu fell dead to the ground.

---
23.
śēṣānvāyavyamādāya nijaghāna mahāyaśāḥ |
rāghavaḥ paramōdārō munīnāṁ mudamāvahan ||

> **Meaning 23:** Then, taking up the Vāyavyāstra, the illustrious and noble
> Rāghava struck down the remaining rākṣasas, bringing immense joy to the sages.

---
24.
sa hatvā rākṣasānsarvānyajñaghnānraghunandanaḥ |
r̥ṣibhiḥ pūjitastatra yathēndrō vijayē purā ||

> **Meaning 24:** After Raghunandana slew all those rākṣasas who were destroying
> the yajña, the ṛṣis honored him there, just as Indra was honored long ago
> after his victory over the rākṣasas.

---
25.
atha yajñē samāptē tu viśvāmitrō mahāmuniḥ |
nirītikā diśō dr̥ṣṭvā kākutsthamidamabravīt ||

> **Meaning 25:** Once the yajña was complete, the great sage Viśvāmitra saw
> that every direction was free of danger and said to Rāma:

---
26.
kr̥tārthō'smi mahābāhō kr̥taṁ guruvacastvayā |
siddhāśramamidaṁ satyaṁ kr̥taṁ rāma mahāyaśaḥ ||

> **Meaning 26:** "My purpose is fulfilled, mighty-armed prince! Illustrious Rāma,
> you have carried out your guru's word and made Siddhāśrama true to its name."

---
