=== Sarga 46 ===
Diti's Tapas at Kuśaplavana; Indra Divides the Embryo

---
1.
hatēṣu tēṣu putrēṣu ditiḥ paramaduḥkhitā |
mārīcaṁ kāśyapaṁ rāma bhartāramidamabravīt ||

> **Meaning 1:** "Rāma, when those sons of hers were slain, Diti was overcome
> with deep grief and spoke these words to her husband, Kaśyapa, son of Marīci:

---
2.
hataputrāsmi bhagavaṁstava putrairmahābalaiḥ |
śakrahantāramicchāmi putraṁ dīrghatapō'rjitam ||

> **Meaning 2:** "'Revered one, your mighty sons have slain my children.
> Through long tapas, I wish to obtain a son who will slay Śakra.

---
3.
sāhaṁ tapaścariṣyāmi garbhaṁ mē dātumarhasi |
īdr̥śaṁ śakrahantāraṁ tvamanujñātumarhasi ||

> **Meaning 3:** "'I will practice tapas for this; please grant me a
> child—please permit me to have such a son, a slayer of Śakra.'

---
4.
tasyāstadvacanaṁ śrutvā mārīcaḥ kāśyapastadā |
pratyuvāca mahātējā ditiṁ paramaduḥkhitām ||

> **Meaning 4:** "Hearing those words of hers, the deeply radiant Kaśyapa, son
> of Marīci, replied to the heartbroken Diti:

---
5.
ēvaṁ bhavatu bhadraṁ tē śucirbhava tapōdhanē |
janayiṣyasi putraṁ tvaṁ śakra hantāramāhavē ||

> **Meaning 5:** "'So be it—blessings upon you! You whose wealth is tapas,
> observe strict ritual purity, and you will give birth to a son who will slay
> Śakra in battle.

---
6.
pūrṇē varṣasahasrē tu śuciryadi bhaviṣyasi |
putraṁ trailōkyabhartāraṁ mattastvaṁ janayiṣyasi ||

> **Meaning 6:** "'If you maintain that ritual purity until a full thousand
> years are complete, you will bear from me a son who will rule over the three
> worlds.'

---
7.
ēvamuktvā mahātējāḥ pāṇinā sa mamārja tām |
samālabhya tataḥ svastītyuktvā sa tapasē yayau ||

> **Meaning 7:** "Having spoken thus, that sage of great radiance gently touched
> her womb with his hand to bless her with the child; then, saying, 'May all be
> well,' he departed to practice tapas.

---
8.
gatē tasminnaraśrēṣṭha ditiḥ paramaharṣitā |
kuśaplavanamāsādya tapastēpē sudāruṇam ||

> **Meaning 8:** "Foremost of men, once he had departed, Diti was filled with
> supreme joy; reaching the region of Kuśaplavana, she practiced exceedingly
> fierce tapas.

---
9.
tapastasyāṁ hi kurvatyāṁ paricaryāṁ cakāra ha |
sahasrākṣō naraśrēṣṭha parayā guṇasampadā ||

> **Meaning 9:** "Best of men, while she was performing that tapas, the
> thousand-eyed Indra attended upon her with the utmost care and excellence of
> conduct.

---
10.
agniṁ kuśānkāṣṭhamapaḥ phalaṁ mūlaṁ tathaiva ca |
nyavēdayatsahasrākṣō yaccānyadapi kāṅkṣitam ||

> **Meaning 10:** "The thousand-eyed Indra brought her fire, kuśa grass,
> firewood, water, as well as fruits and roots, and whatever else she desired.

---
11.
gātrasaṁvāhanaiścaiva śramāpanayanaistathā |
śakraḥ sarvēṣu kālēṣu ditiṁ paricacāra ha ||

> **Meaning 11:** "Gently pressing her weary feet and soothing away her
> exhaustion, Śakra served his stepmother Diti with a son's devotion at all
> times.

---
12.
atha varṣasahasrētu daśōnē raghu nandana |
ditiḥ paramasamprītā sahasrākṣamathābravīt ||

> **Meaning 12:** "Raghunandana, when only ten years remained of the thousand
> years, Diti was deeply pleased and spoke to the thousand-eyed Indra:

---
13.
yācitēna suraśrēṣṭha tava pitrā mahātmanā |
varō varṣasahasrāntē dattō mama sutaṁ prati ||

> **Meaning 13:** "'Foremost of the suras, when I entreated your great father,
> he granted me a boon for a son at the end of a thousand years.

---
14.
tapaścarantyā varṣāṇi daśa vīryavatāṁ vara |
avaśiṣṭāni bhadraṁ tē bhrātaraṁ drakṣyasē tataḥ ||

> **Meaning 14:** "'Best of the valorous, only ten years of my tapas remain.
> Blessings upon you—after that, you will see your brother.

---
15.
tamahaṁ tvatkr̥tē putra samādhāsyē jayōtsukam |
trailōkyavijayaṁ putra saha bhōkṣyasi vijvaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 15:** "'My son, though he will be born intent on conquest, for your
> sake I will reconcile him with you—so that, free from all fear, you may share
> the sovereignty of the three worlds with him.'

---
16.
ēvamuktvā ditiḥ śakraṁ prāptē madhyaṁ divākarē |
nidrayāpahr̥tā dēvī pādau kr̥tvātha śīrṣataḥ ||

> **Meaning 16:** "Having spoken thus to Śakra, when the sun reached midday,
> Diti was overcome by sleep, with her feet touching her head.

---
17.
dr̥ṣṭvā tāmaśuciṁ śakraḥ pādataḥ kr̥tamūrdhajām |
śiraḥsthānē kr̥tau pādau jahāsa ca mumōda ca ||

> **Meaning 17:** "Seeing her in that state of ritual impurity—with her hair
> touching her feet and her feet resting where her head belonged—Śakra laughed
> and rejoiced.

---
18.
tasyāḥ śarīravivaraṁ vivēśa ca purandaraḥ |
garbhaṁ ca saptadhā rāma bibhēda paramātmavān ||

> **Meaning 18:** "Rāma, Purandara then entered her body, and with great
> self-mastery split the embryo into seven parts.

---
19.
bidhyamānastatō garbhō vajrēṇa śataparvaṇā |
rurōda susvaraṁ rāma tatō ditirabudhyata ||

> **Meaning 19:** "Rāma, as the embryo was split by the hundred-jointed vajra,
> it cried out loudly, and at that sound Diti woke up.

---
20.
mā rudō mā rudaścēti garbhaṁ śakrō'bhyabhāṣata |
bibhēda ca mahātējā rudantamapi vāsavaḥ ||

> **Meaning 20:** "'Do not weep! Do not weep! (mā ruda!)' Śakra said to the
> embryo, yet even as it wept, the mighty Vāsava kept splitting it.

---
21.
na hantavyō na hantavya ityēvaṁ ditirabravīt |
niṣpapāta tataḥ śakrō māturvacanagauravāt ||

> **Meaning 21:** "'Do not kill him! Do not kill him!' Diti cried out, and out
> of respect for his mother's words, Śakra emerged from her body.

---
22.
prāñjalirvajrasahitō ditiṁ śakrō'bhyabhāṣata |
aśucirdēvi suptāsi pādayōḥ kr̥tamūrdhajā ||

> **Meaning 22:** "Standing with palms joined in añjali and holding the vajra,
> Śakra said to Diti: 'Devī, you fell asleep in a state of ritual impurity,
> with your hair touching your feet.

---
23.
tadantaramahaṁ labdhvā śakrahantāramāhavē |
abhindaṁ saptadhā dēvi tanmē tvaṁ kṣantumarhasi ||

> **Meaning 23:** "'Seizing that moment, I split the child who was meant to
> slay me in battle into seven parts. Please forgive me for this.'"

---
