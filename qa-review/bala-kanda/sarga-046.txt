=== Sarga 46 ===

---
1.
saptadhā tu kr̥te garbhe ditiḥ paramaduḥkhitā |
sahasrākṣaṁ durādharṣaṁ vākyaṁ sānunayābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
mamāparādhād garbho ’yaṁ saptadhā viphalīkr̥taḥ |
nāparādho ’sti deveśa tavātra balasūdana ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
priyaṁ tu kr̥tam icchāmi mama garbhaviparyaye |
marutāṁ saptaṁ saptānāṁ sthānapālā bhavantv ime ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
vātaskandhā ime sapta carantu divi putrakāḥ |
mārutā iti vikhyātā divyarūpā mamātmajāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
brahmalokaṁ caratv eka indralokaṁ tathāparaḥ |
divi vāyur iti khyātas tr̥tīyo ’pi mahāyaśāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
catvāras tu suraśreṣṭha diśo vai tava śāsanāt |
saṁcariṣyanti bhadraṁ te devabhūtā mamātmajāḥ |
tvatkr̥tenaiva nāmnā ca mārutā iti viśrutāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tasyās tad vacanaṁ śrutvā sahasrākṣaḥ puraṁdaraḥ |
uvāca prāñjalir vākyaṁ ditiṁ balaniṣūdanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sarvam etad yathoktaṁ te bhaviṣyati na saṁśayaḥ |
vicariṣyanti bhadraṁ te devabhūtās tavātmajāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
evaṁ tau niścayaṁ kr̥tvā mātāputrau tapovane |
jagmatus tridivaṁ rāma kr̥tārthāv iti naḥ śrutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
eṣa deśaḥ sa kākutstha mahendrādhyuṣitaḥ purā |
ditiṁ yatra tapaḥ siddhām evaṁ paricacāra saḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
ikṣvākos tu naravyāghra putraḥ paramadhārmikaḥ |
alambuṣāyām utpanno viśāla iti viśrutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tena cāsīd iha sthāne viśāleti purī kr̥tā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
viśālasya suto rāma hemacandro mahābalaḥ |
sucandra iti vikhyāto hemacandrād anantaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sucandratanayo rāma dhūmrāśva iti viśrutaḥ |
dhūmrāśvatanayaś cāpi sr̥ñjayaḥ samapadyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sr̥ñjayasya sutaḥ śrīmān sahadevaḥ pratāpavān |
kuśāśvaḥ sahadevasya putraḥ paramadhārmikaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
kuśāśvasya mahātejāḥ somadattaḥ pratāpavān |
somadattasya putras tu kākutstha iti viśrutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tasya putro mahātejāḥ saṁpraty eṣa purīm imām |
āvasaty amaraprakhyaḥ sumatir nāma durjayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
ikṣvākos tu prasādena sarve vaiśālikā nr̥pāḥ |
dīrghāyuṣo mahātmāno vīryavantaḥ sudhārmikāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
ihādya rajanīṁ rāma sukhaṁ vatsyāmahe vayam |
śvaḥ prabhāte naraśreṣṭha janakaṁ draṣṭum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
sumatis tu mahātejā viśvāmitram upāgatam |
śrutvā naravaraśreṣṭhaḥ pratyudgacchan mahāyaśāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
pūjāṁ ca paramāṁ kr̥tvā sopādhyāyaḥ sabāndhavaḥ |
prāñjaliḥ kuśalaṁ pr̥ṣṭvā viśvāmitram athābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
dhanyo ’smy anugr̥hīto ’smi yasya me viṣayaṁ mune |
saṁprāpto darśanaṁ caiva nāsti dhanyataro mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
