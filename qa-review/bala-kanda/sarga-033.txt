=== Sarga 33 ===

---
1.
kr̥todvāhe gate tasmin brahmadatte ca rāghava |
aputraḥ putralābhāya pautrīm iṣṭim akalpayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
iṣṭyāṁ tu vartamānāyāṁ kuśanābhaṁ mahīpatim |
uvāca paramaprītaḥ kuśo brahmasutas tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
putras te sadr̥śaḥ putra bhaviṣyati sudhārmikaḥ |
gādhiṁ prāpsyasi tena tvaṁ kīrtiṁ loke ca śāśvatīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
evam uktvā kuśo rāma kuśanābhaṁ mahīpatim |
jagāmākāśam āviśya brahmalokaṁ sanātanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
kasya cit tv atha kālasya kuśanābhasya dhīmataḥ |
jajñe paramadharmiṣṭho gādhir ity eva nāmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
sa pitā mama kākutstha gādhiḥ paramadhārmikaḥ |
kuśavaṁśaprasūto ’smi kauśiko raghunandana ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
pūrvajā bhaginī cāpi mama rāghava suvratā |
nāmnā satyavatī nāma r̥cīke pratipāditā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
saśarīrā gatā svargaṁ bhartāram anuvartinī |
kauśikī paramodārā sā pravr̥ttā mahānadī ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
divyā puṇyodakā ramyā himavantam upāśritā |
lokasya hitakāmārthaṁ pravr̥ttā bhaginī mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tato ’haṁ himavatpārśve vasāmi niyataḥ sukham |
bhaginyāḥ snehasaṁyuktaḥ kauśikyā raghunandana ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sā tu satyavatī puṇyā satye dharme pratiṣṭhitā |
pativratā mahābhāgā kauśikī saritāṁ varā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
ahaṁ hi niyamād rāma hitvā tāṁ samupāgataḥ |
siddhāśramam anuprāpya siddho ’smi tava tejasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
eṣā rāma mamotpattiḥ svasya vaṁśasya kīrtitā |
deśasya ca mahābāho yan māṁ tvaṁ paripr̥cchasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
gato ’rdharātraḥ kākutstha kathāḥ kathayato mama |
nidrām abhyehi bhadraṁ te mā bhūd vighno ’dhvanīha naḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
niṣpandās taravaḥ sarve nilīnā mr̥gapakṣiṇaḥ |
naiśena tamasā vyāptā diśaś ca raghunandana ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
śanair viyujyate saṁdhyā nabho netrair ivāvr̥tam |
nakṣatratārāgahanaṁ jyotirbhir avabhāsate ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
uttiṣṭhati ca śītāṁśuḥ śaśī lokatamonudaḥ |
hlādayan prāṇināṁ loke manāṁsi prabhayā vibho ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
naiśāni sarvabhūtāni pracaranti tatas tataḥ |
yakṣarākṣasasaṁghāś ca raudrāś ca piśitāśanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
evam uktvā mahātejā virarāma mahāmuniḥ |
sādhu sādhv iti taṁ sarve munayo hy abhyapūjayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
rāmo ’pi saha saumitriḥ kiṁ cid āgatavismayaḥ |
praśasya muniśārdūlaṁ nidrāṁ samupasevate ||

=== meaning ===

---
