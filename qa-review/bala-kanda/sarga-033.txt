=== Sarga 33 ===
The Birth of Brahmadatta; His Marriage to Kuśanābha's Hundred Daughters

---
1.
tasya tadvacanaṁ śrutvā kuśanābhasya dhīmataḥ |
śirōbhiścaraṇau spr̥ṣṭvā kanyāśatamabhāṣata ||

> **Meaning 1:** "Hearing those words of the wise Kuśanābha, the hundred
> daughters touched his feet with their heads and spoke:

---
2.
vāyuḥ sarvātmakō rājanpradharṣayitumicchati |
aśubhaṁ mārgamāsthāya na dharmaṁ pratyavēkṣatē ||

> **Meaning 2:** "'King, the all-pervading Vāyu wanted to force himself on us;
> taking an evil path, he has no regard for dharma.

---
3.
pitr̥matyaḥ sma bhadraṁ tē svacchandē na vayaṁ sthitāḥ |
pitaraṁ nō vr̥ṇīṣva tvaṁ yadi nō dāsyatē tava ||

> **Meaning 3:** "'We told him, "Blessings upon you, we are under our father's
> authority and are not free to act on our own. Ask him for our hands, and see
> if he will give us to you."

---
4.
tēna pāpānubandhēna vacanaṁ na pratīcchatā |
ēvaṁ bruvantyaḥ sarvāḥ sma vāyunā nihatā bhr̥ṣam ||

> **Meaning 4:** "'Refusing to listen, that wicked-minded Vāyu struck us all
> down violently even as we were speaking.'

---
5.
tāsāṁ tadvacanaṁ śrutvā rājā paramadhārmikaḥ |
pratyuvāca mahātējāḥ kanyāśatamanuttamam ||

> **Meaning 5:** "Hearing their words, the supremely righteous and radiant king
> replied to his hundred matchless daughters:

---
6.
kṣāntaṁ kṣamāvatāṁ putryaḥ kartavyaṁ sumahatkr̥tam |
aikamatyamupāgamya kulaṁ cāvēkṣitaṁ mama ||

> **Meaning 6:** "'You have shown true forbearance, my daughters, and done a
> great and noble thing. By standing united, you have protected the honor of our
> family.

---
7.
alaṅkārō hi nārīṇāṁ kṣamā tu puruṣasya vā |
duṣkaraṁ tacca vaḥ kṣāntaṁ tridaśēṣu viśēṣataḥ |
yādr̥śīrvaḥ kṣamā putryaḥ sarvāsāmaviśēṣataḥ ||

> **Meaning 7:** "'Forbearance is the true ornament of both women and men. What
> you endured was hard to bear, especially at the hands of one of the devatās,
> yet every single one of you, my daughters, showed the same extraordinary
> restraint.

---
8.
kṣamā dānaṁ kṣamā yajñaḥ kṣamā satyaṁ ca putrikāḥ |
kṣamā yaśaḥ kṣamā dharmaḥ kṣamāyāṁ viṣṭhitaṁ jagat ||

> **Meaning 8:** "'Forbearance is charity, forbearance is yajña, forbearance is
> truth, my daughters. Forbearance is renown, forbearance is dharma, and the
> entire world rests upon forbearance.'

---
9.
visr̥jya kanyāḥ kākutstha rājā tridaśavikramaḥ |
mantrajñō mantrayāmāsa pradānaṁ saha mantribhiḥ |
dēśē kālē pradānasya sadr̥śē pratipādanam ||

> **Meaning 9:** "Kākutstha, after sending his daughters away, that king of
> divine valor, wise in statecraft, consulted with his ministers about arranging
> their marriage to a worthy match at the right place and time.

---
10.
ētasminnēva kālē tu cūlī nāma mahāmuniḥ |
ūrdhvarētāḥ śubhācārō brāhmaṁ tapa upāgamat ||

> **Meaning 10:** "During that very time, a great sage named Cūlī, strictly
> celibate and pure in conduct, was practicing intense austerity devoted to
> Brahmā.

---
11.
tapyantaṁ tamr̥ṣiṁ tatra gandharvī paryupāsatē |
sōmadā nāma bhadraṁ tē ūrmilā tanayā tadā ||

> **Meaning 11:** "Rāma, while the ṛṣi was practicing his austerities there, a
> gandharvī named Somadā, the daughter of Ūrmilā, served him faithfully.

---
12.
sā ca taṁ praṇatā bhūtvā śuśrūṣaṇaparāyaṇā |
uvāsa kālē dharmiṣṭhā tasyāstuṣṭō'bhavadguruḥ ||

> **Meaning 12:** "Humble and completely devoted to serving him, that virtuous
> woman stayed there for a long time, and her preceptor grew deeply pleased with
> her.

---
13.
sa ca tāṁ kālayōgēna prōvāca raghunandana |
parituṣṭō'smi bhadraṁ tē kiṁ karōmi tava priyam ||

> **Meaning 13:** "Raghunandana, in due course he said to her, 'I am thoroughly
> pleased with you; blessings upon you! What favor can I do for you?'

---
14.
parituṣṭaṁ muniṁ jñātvā gandharvī madhurasvaram |
uvāca paramaprītā vākyajñā vākyakōvidam ||

> **Meaning 14:** "Seeing that the sage was pleased, the sweet-voiced gandharvī,
> overjoyed and knowing just what to say, spoke to that wise and eloquent sage:

---
15.
lakṣmyā samuditō brāhmyā brahmabhūtō mahātapāḥ |
brāhmēṇa tapasā yuktaṁ putramicchāmi dhārmikam ||

> **Meaning 15:** "'You are filled with the radiance of Brahmā, united with the
> supreme spirit, and rich in austerity. I wish for a righteous son endowed with
> that same spiritual power.

---
16.
apatiścāsmi bhadraṁ tē bhāryā cāsmi na kasyacit |
brāhmēṇōpagatāyāśca dātumarhasi mē sutam ||

> **Meaning 16:** "'May all be well with you; I am unmarried and belong to no
> man. Since I have approached you in pure devotion, please grant me a son
> through your spiritual power.'

---
17.
tasyāḥ prasannō brahmarṣirdadau putramanuttamam |
brahmadatta iti khyātaṁ mānasaṁ cūlinaḥ sutam ||

> **Meaning 17:** "Pleased with her, the brahmarṣi Cūlī granted her a matchless
> son, born of his mere thought, who became renowned as Brahmadatta.

---
18.
sa rājā brahmadattastu purīmadhyavasattadā |
kāmpilyāṁ parayā lakṣmyā dēvarājō yathā divam ||

> **Meaning 18:** "That King Brahmadatta ruled in the city of Kāmpilyā in
> supreme splendor, just as Indra, king of the devatās, rules heaven.

---
19.
sa buddhiṁ kr̥tavānrājā kuśanābhaḥ sudhārmikaḥ |
brahmadattāya kākutstha dātuṁ kanyāśataṁ tadā ||

> **Meaning 19:** "Kākutstha, the deeply righteous King Kuśanābha then decided
> to give his hundred daughters in marriage to Brahmadatta.

---
20.
tamāhūya mahātējā brahmadattaṁ mahīpatiḥ |
dadau kanyāśataṁ rājā suprītēnāntarātmanā ||

> **Meaning 20:** "Inviting Brahmadatta to his court, the radiant monarch gave
> him all hundred daughters with a deeply gladdened heart.

---
21.
yathākramaṁ tataḥ pāṇiṁ jagrāha raghunandana |
brahmadattō mahī pālastāsāṁ dēvapatiryathā ||

> **Meaning 21:** "Raghunandana, King Brahmadatta then took each of their hands
> in marriage in due order, shining like Indra, king of the devatās.

---
22.
spr̥ṣṭamātrē tataḥ pāṇau vikubjā vigatajvarāḥ |
yuktāḥ paramayā lakṣmyā babhuḥ kanyāśataṁ tadā ||

> **Meaning 22:** "The instant he touched their hands, their hunchbacked
> deformities vanished and their suffering ended; all hundred daughters shone
> once more with supreme grace and beauty.

---
23.
sa dr̥ṣṭvā vāyunā muktāḥ kuśanābhō mahīpatiḥ |
babhūva paramaprītō harṣaṁ lēbhē punaḥ punaḥ ||

> **Meaning 23:** "Seeing them freed from Vāyu's affliction, King Kuśanābha was
> overjoyed and felt wave after wave of delight.

---
24.
kr̥tōdvāhaṁ tu rājānaṁ brahmadattaṁ mahīpatiḥ |
sadāraṁ prēṣayāmāsa sōpādhyāya gaṇaṁ tadā ||

> **Meaning 24:** "Once the wedding was complete, King Kuśanābha sent King
> Brahmadatta on his way, accompanied by his brides and his company of
> preceptors.

---
25.
sōmadāpi susaṁhr̥ṣṭā putrasya sadr̥śīṁ kriyām |
yathānyāyaṁ ca gandharvī snuṣāstāḥ pratyanandata |
spr̥ṣṭvā spr̥ṣṭvā ca tāḥ kanyāḥ kuśanābhaṁ praśasya ca ||

> **Meaning 25:** "Somadā too was delighted by a match so worthy of her son. The
> gandharvī warmly welcomed her daughters-in-law as was fitting, affectionately
> embracing each of the brides and praising Kuśanābha."

---
