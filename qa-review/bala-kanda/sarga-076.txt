=== Sarga 76 ===

---
1.
gate rāme praśāntātmā rāmo dāśarathir dhanuḥ |
varuṇāyāprameyāya dadau haste sasāyakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
abhivādya tato rāmo vasiṣṭha pramukhān r̥ṣīn |
pitaraṁ vihvalaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā provāca raghunandanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
jāmadagnyo gato rāmaḥ prayātu caturaṅgiṇī |
ayodhyābhimukhī senā tvayā nāthena pālitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
rāmasya vacanaṁ śrutvā rājā daśarathaḥ sutam |
bāhubhyāṁ saṁpariṣvajya mūrdhni cāghrāya rāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
gato rāma iti śrutvā hr̥ṣṭaḥ pramudito nr̥paḥ |
codayām āsa tāṁ senāṁ jagāmāśu tataḥ purīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
patākādhvajinīṁ ramyāṁ tūryodghuṣṭanināditām |
siktarājapathāṁ ramyāṁ prakīrṇakusumotkarām ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
rājapraveśasumukhaiḥ paurair maṅgalavādibhiḥ |
saṁpūrṇāṁ prāviśad rājā janaughaiḥ samalaṁkr̥tām ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
kausalyā ca sumitrā ca kaikeyī ca sumadhyamā |
vadhūpratigrahe yuktā yāś cānyā rājayoṣitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tataḥ sītāṁ mahābhāgām ūrmilāṁ ca yaśasvinīm |
kuśadhvajasute cobhe jagr̥hur nr̥papatnayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
maṅgalālāpanaiś caiva śobhitāḥ kṣaumavāsasaḥ |
devatāyatanāny āśu sarvās tāḥ pratyapūjayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
abhivādyābhivādyāṁś ca sarvā rājasutās tadā |
remire muditāḥ sarvā bhartr̥bhiḥ sahitā rahaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
kr̥tadārāḥ kr̥tāstrāś ca sadhanāḥ sasuhr̥jjanāḥ |
śuśrūṣamāṇāḥ pitaraṁ vartayanti nararṣabhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
teṣām atiyaśā loke rāmaḥ satyaparākramaḥ |
svayambhūr iva bhūtānāṁ babhūva guṇavattaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
rāmas tu sītayā sārdhaṁ vijahāra bahūn r̥tūn |
manasvī tadgatas tasyā nityaṁ hr̥di samarpitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
priyā tu sītā rāmasya dārāḥ pitr̥kr̥tā iti |
guṇād rūpaguṇāc cāpi prītir bhūyo vyavardhata ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tasyāś ca bhartā dviguṇaṁ hr̥daye parivartate |
antarjātam api vyaktam ākhyāti hr̥dayaṁ hr̥dā ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tasya bhūyo viśeṣeṇa maithilī janakātmajā |
devatābhiḥ samā rūpe sītā śrīr iva rūpiṇī ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tayā sa rājarṣisuto ’bhirāmayā; sameyivān uttamarājakanyayā |
atīva rāmaḥ śuśubhe ’tikāmayā; vibhuḥ śriyā viṣṇur ivāmareśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
