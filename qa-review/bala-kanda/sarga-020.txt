=== Sarga 20 ===
Daśaratha's Reluctance to Part with Rāma; Viśvāmitra's Anger

---
1.
tacchrutvā rājaśārdūla viśvāmitrasya bhāṣitam |
muhūrtamiva niḥsañjñaḥ sañjñāvānidamabravīt ||

> **Meaning 1:** Hearing Viśvāmitra's words, the great king lost consciousness
> for a moment; when he recovered, he spoke these words.

---
2.
ūnaṣōḍaśavarṣō mē rāmō rājīvalōcanaḥ |
na yuddhayōgyatāmasya paśyāmi saha rākṣasaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 2:** My lotus-eyed Rāma is not yet sixteen years old. I do not see
> him as fit to fight against the Rākṣasas.

---
3.
iyamakṣauhiṇī pūrṇā yasyāhaṁ patirīśvaraḥ |
anayā saṁvr̥tō gatvā yōdhāhaṁ tairniśācaraiḥ ||

> **Meaning 3:** I am the sovereign commander of this full akṣauhiṇī army. I
> will march out with it and fight those night-wanderers myself.

---
4.
imē śūrāśca vikrāntā bhr̥tyā mē'straviśāradāḥ |
yōgyā rakṣōgaṇairyōddhuṁ na rāmaṁ nētumarhasi ||

> **Meaning 4:** My soldiers here are brave, mighty, and skilled in weapons —
> well able to take on the Rākṣasa hordes. Please do not take Rāma.

---
5.
ahamēva dhanuṣpāṇirgōptā samaramūrdhani |
yāvatprāṇāndhariṣyāmi tāvadyōtsyē niśācaraiḥ ||

> **Meaning 5:** I myself will go with bow in hand and guard the front line of
> battle. As long as I have breath in me, I will keep fighting those
> night-wanderers.

---
6.
nirvighnā vratacaryā sā bhaviṣyati surakṣitā |
ahaṁ tatra gamiṣyāmi na rāma nētumarhasi ||

> **Meaning 6:** Your sacred observance will be well protected and free of
> obstacles. I will go there myself. Please do not take Rāma.

---
7.
bālō hyakr̥tavidyaśca na ca vētti balābalam |
na cāstrabalasaṁyuktō na ca yuddhaviśāradaḥ |

> **Meaning 7:** He is still a boy who hasn't finished his martial training, and
> he doesn't know his own strength or his enemy's. He does not yet command the
> power of astras, nor is he skilled in warfare.

---
8.
na cāsau rakṣasāṁ yōgyaḥ kūṭayuddhā hi tē dhruvam |
viprayuktō hi rāmēṇa muhūrtamapi nōtsahē |
jīvituṁ muniśārdūla na rāmaṁ nētumarhasi ||

> **Meaning 8:** He is no match for the Rākṣasas, for they always fight with
> deceit. Separated from Rāma, I wouldn't have the will to live even for a
> moment, great sage. Please do not take Rāma.

---
9.
yadi vā rāghavaṁ brahmannētumicchasi suvrata |
caturaṅgasamāyuktaṁ mayā saha ca taṁ naya ||

> **Meaning 9:** Or, if you are set on taking Rāghava, sage of strict vows, then
> take me along with him, accompanied by my fourfold army.

---
10.
ṣaṣṭirvarṣasahasrāṇi jātasya mama kauśika |
duḥkhēnōtpāditaścāyaṁ na rāmaṁ nētumarhasi ||

> **Meaning 10:** Sixty thousand years have passed since I was born, Kauśika,
> and I had this son only after great hardship. Please do not take Rāma.

---
11.
caturṇāmātmajānāṁ hi prītiḥ paramikā mama |
jyēṣṭhaṁ dharmapradhānaṁ ca na rāmaṁ nētumarhasi ||

> **Meaning 11:** Among my four sons, my greatest affection is for him — the
> eldest, who is foremost in dharma. Please do not take Rāma.

---
12.
kiṁ vīryā rākṣasāstē ca kasya putrāśca kē ca tē |
kathaṁ pramāṇāḥ kē caitānrakṣanti munipuṅgava ||

> **Meaning 12:** How strong are these Rākṣasas? Whose sons are they, and who
> are they? How huge are they, great sage, and who stands behind them?

---
13.
kathaṁ ca pratikartavyaṁ tēṣāṁ rāmēṇa rakṣasām |
māmakairvā balairbrahmanmayā vā kūṭayōdhinām ||

> **Meaning 13:** And how can Rāma, or my forces, or I myself counter these
> Rākṣasas who fight by trickery, wise sage?

---
14.
sarvaṁ mē śaṁsa bhagavankathaṁ tēṣāṁ mayā raṇē |
sthātavyaṁ duṣṭabhāvānāṁ vīryōtsiktā hi rākṣasāḥ ||

> **Meaning 14:** Tell me everything, revered sage. How am I to stand against
> them in battle? These Rākṣasas are wicked and swollen with pride in their
> strength.

---
15.
tasya tadvacanaṁ śrutvā viśvāmitrō'bhyabhāṣata |

> **Meaning 15:** Hearing his words, Viśvāmitra replied.

---
16.
paulastyavaṁśaprabhavō rāvaṇō nāma rākṣasaḥ |
sa brahmaṇā dattavarastrailōkyaṁ bādhatē bhr̥śam ||

> **Meaning 16:** There is a Rākṣasa named Rāvaṇa, born in the line of Pulastya.
> He received a boon from Brahmā and now torments the three worlds terribly.

---
17.
mahābalō mahāvīryō rākṣasairbahubhirvr̥taḥ |
śrūyatē hi mahāvīryō rāvaṇō rākṣasādhipaḥ ||

> **Meaning 17:** Immensely strong and valiant, and surrounded by countless
> Rākṣasas, Rāvaṇa is famed as the mighty king of the Rākṣasas.

---
18.
sākṣādvaiśravaṇabhrātā putrō viśravasō munēḥ |
yadā svayaṁ na yajñasya vighnakartā mahābalaḥ ||

> **Meaning 18:** He is none other than the brother of Vaiśravaṇa (Kubera) and
> the son of the sage Viśravas. Even though that mighty Rākṣasa does not
> personally come to obstruct my yajña,

---
19.
tēna sañcōditau tau tu rākṣasau sumahābalau |
mārīcaśca subāhuśca yajñavighnaṁ kariṣyataḥ ||

> **Meaning 19:** instigated by him, those two very powerful Rākṣasas — Mārīca
> and Subāhu — will disrupt the yajña.

---
20.
ityuktō muninā tēna rājōvāca muniṁ tadā |
na hi śaktō'smi saṅgrāmē sthātuṁ tasya durātmanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 20:** Hearing this from the sage, the king replied: I cannot stand
> against that wicked one in battle.

---
21.
sa tvaṁ prasādaṁ dharmajña kuruṣva mama putrakē |
mama caivālpabhāgyasya daivataṁ hi bhavānguruḥ ||

> **Meaning 21:** Knower of dharma, please show grace to my little son and to
> me, unfortunate as I am in failing to fulfill your wish — for you are both my
> guru and my devatā.

---
22.
dēvadānavagandharvā yakṣāḥ patagapannagāḥ |
na śaktā rāvaṇaṁ sōḍhuṁ kiṁ punarmānavā yudhi ||

> **Meaning 22:** Even the devatās, Dānavas, Gandharvas, Yakṣas, Garuḍas, and
> Nāgas cannot withstand Rāvaṇa. What can humans do against him in battle?

---
23.
sa hi vīryavatāṁ vīryamādattē yudhi rākṣasaḥ ||
tēna cāhaṁ na śaktō'smi saṁyōddhuṁ tasya vā balaiḥ |
sabalō vā muniśrēṣṭha sahitō vā mamātmajaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 23:** In battle, that Rākṣasa drains the strength of even the
> mighty. So I cannot fight him or his forces, best of sages, whether with my
> army or with my sons beside me.

---
24.
kathamapyamaraprakhyaṁ saṅgrāmāṇāmakōvidam |
bālaṁ mē tanayaṁ brahmannaiva dāsyāmi putrakam ||

> **Meaning 24:** Great sage, under no circumstances can I part with my young
> son — he is just a boy, radiant like a devatā, and knows nothing of warfare.

---
25.
atha kālōpamau yuddhē sutau sundōpasundayōḥ |
yajñavighnakarau tau tē naiva dāsyāmi putrakam ||

> **Meaning 25:** And those two — Mārīca and Subāhu, the sons of Sunda and
> Upasunda — are like Death itself in battle as they obstruct your yajña. I
> cannot possibly send my dear son to face them.

---
26.
mārīcaśca subāhuśca vīryavantau suśikṣitau |
tayōranyatarēṇāhaṁ yōddhā syāṁ sasuhr̥dgaṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 26:** Mārīca and Subāhu are mighty and well trained. Even with all
> my friends and allies, I cannot fight even one of them.

---
27.
iti narapatijalpanāddvijēndraṁ
kuśikasutaṁ sumahānviveśa manyuḥ |
suhuta iva makhē'gnirājyasiktaḥ
samabhavadujjvalitō maharṣivahniḥ ||

> **Meaning 27:** At this anxious pleading of the king, a fierce anger seized
> Viśvāmitra, the son of Kuśika and foremost of sages. The great sage blazed up
> like a sacrificial fire when ghee is poured into it.

---
