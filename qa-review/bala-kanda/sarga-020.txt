=== Sarga 20 ===

---
1.
tac chrutvā vacanaṁ tasya snehaparyākulākṣaram |
samanyuḥ kauśiko vākyaṁ pratyuvaca mahīpatim ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
pūrhvam arthaṁ pratiśrutya pratijñāṁ hātum icchasi |
rāgavāṇām ayukto ’yaṁ kulasyāsya viparyayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
yad idaṁ te kṣamaṁ rājan gamiṣyāmi yathāgatam |
mithyāpratijñaḥ kākutstha sukhī bhava sabāndhavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tasya roṣaparītasya viśvāmitrasya dhīmataḥ |
cacāla vasudhā kr̥tsnā viveśa ca bhayaṁ surān ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
trastarūpaṁ tu vijñāya jagat sarvaṁ mahān r̥ṣiḥ |
nr̥patiṁ suvrato dhīro vasiṣṭho vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
ikṣvākūṇāṁ kule jātaḥ sākṣād dharma ivāparaḥ |
dhr̥timānsuvrataḥ śrīmān na dharmaṁ hātum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
triṣu lokeṣu vikhyāto dharmātmā iti rāghavaḥ |
svadharmaṁ pratipadyasva nādharmaṁ voḍhum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
saṁśrutyaivaṁ kariṣyāmīty akurvāṇasya rāghava |
iṣṭāpūrtavadho bhūyāt tasmād rāmaṁ visarjaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
kr̥tāstram akr̥tāstraṁ vā nainaṁ śakṣyanti rākṣasāḥ |
guptaṁ kuśikaputreṇa jvalanenāmr̥taṁ yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
eṣa vigrahavān dharma eṣa vīryavatāṁ varaḥ |
eṣa buddhyādhiko loke tapasaś ca parāyaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
eṣo ’strān vividhān vetti trailokye sacarācare |
nainam anyaḥ pumān vetti na ca vetsyanti ke cana ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
na devā narṣayaḥ ke cin nāsurā na ca rākṣasāḥ |
gandharvayakṣapravarāḥ sakiṁnaramahoragāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sarvāstrāṇi kr̥śāśvasya putrāḥ paramadhārmikāḥ |
kauśikāya purā dattā yadā rājyaṁ praśāsati ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
te ’pi putrāḥ kr̥śāśvasya prajāpatisutāsutāḥ |
naikarūpā mahāvīryā dīptimanto jayāvahāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
jayā ca suprabhā caiva dakṣakanye sumadhyame |
te suvāte ’straśastrāṇi śataṁ paramabhāsvaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
pañcāśataṁ sutām̐l lebhe jayā nāma varān purā |
vadhāyāsurasainyānām ameyān kāmarūpiṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
suprabhājanayac cāpi putrān pañcāśataṁ punaḥ |
saṁhārān nāma durdharṣān durākrāmān balīyasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tāni cāstrāṇi vetty eṣa yathāvat kuśikātmajaḥ |
apūrvāṇāṁ ca janane śakto bhūyaś ca dharmavit ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
evaṁ vīryo mahātejā viśvāmitro mahātapāḥ |
na rāmagamane rājan saṁśayaṁ gantum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
