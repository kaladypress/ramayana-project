=== Sarga 9 ===
Sumantra Narrates Sanatkumāra's Prophecy Concerning Sage Ṛśyaśṛṅga

---
1.
ētacchrutvā rahaḥ sūtō rājānamidamabravīt |
r̥tvigbhirupadiṣṭō'yaṁ purāvr̥ttō mayā śrutaḥ ||

> **Meaning 1:** After hearing this, Sumantra, the king's charioteer and
> minister, said to the king in private, "Listen to what the ṛtvijs advised,

---
2.
sanatkumārō bhagavānpūrvaṁ kathitavānkathām |
r̥ṣīṇāṁ sannidhau rājaṁstava putrāgamaṁ prati ||

> **Meaning 2:** "Long ago, in the presence of the sages, the revered
> Sanatkumāra related an account, foretelling how you would be blessed with
> sons, great king."

---
3.
kāśyapasya tu putrō'sti vibhāṇḍaka iti śrutaḥ |
r̥śyaśr̥ṅga iti khyātastasya putrō bhaviṣyati ||

> **Meaning 3:** "Kāśyapa has a son known as Vibhāṇḍaka, and to him will be born
> a son renowned as Ṛśyaśṛṅga."

---
4.
sa vanē nityasaṁvr̥ddhō munirvanacaraḥ sadā |
nānyaṁ jānāti viprēndrō nityaṁ pitranuvartanāt ||

> **Meaning 4:** "Raised entirely in the forest, that sage will always dwell in
> the woods. Constantly attending upon his father, this foremost of brahmins
> will know no other person."

---
5.
dvaividhyaṁ brahmacaryasya bhaviṣyati mahātmanaḥ |
lōkēṣu prathitaṁ rājanvipraiśca kathitaṁ sadā ||

> **Meaning 5:** "Great king, the brahmacarya of that noble soul will be
> twofold, renowned throughout the worlds and ever spoken of by learned
> brahmins."

---
6.
tasyaivaṁ vartamānasya kālaḥ samabhivartata |
agniṁ śuśrūṣamāṇasya pitaraṁ ca yaśasvinam ||

> **Meaning 6:** "While he lived in this way, making daily offerings to Agni and
> serving his renowned father, time went by."

---
7.
ētasminnēva kālē tu rōmapādaḥ pratāpavān |
aṅgēṣu prathitō rājā bhaviṣyati mahābalaḥ ||

> **Meaning 7:** "At that very time, the mighty and glorious Romapāda will
> become a famous king among the people of Aṅga."

---
8.
tasya vyatikramādrājñō bhaviṣyati sudāruṇā |
anāvr̥ṣṭiḥ sughōrā vai sarvabhūtabhayāvahā ||

> **Meaning 8:** "Because of that king's transgression, a terrible and dreadful
> drought will come, bringing fear to all beings."

---
9.
anāvr̥ṣṭyāṁ tu vr̥ttāyāṁ rājā duḥkhasamanvitaḥ |
brāhmaṇāñśrutavr̥ddhāṁśca samānīya pravakṣyati ||

> **Meaning 9:** "When the drought sets in, the king, overcome with grief, will
> gather venerable brahmins learned in the Vedas and say:"

---
10.
bhavantaḥ śrutadharmāṇō lōkacāritravēdinaḥ |
samādiśantu niyamaṁ prāyaścittaṁ yathā bhavēt ||

> **Meaning 10:** "'You know the dharma from the śāstras and you understand how
> the world works. Please prescribe a vow and a penance for setting this
> right.'"

---
11.
vakṣyanti tē mahīpālaṁ brāhmaṇā vēdapāragāḥ |
vibhāṇḍakasutaṁ rājansarvōpāyairihānaya ||

> **Meaning 11:** "Those brahmins, masters of the Vedas, will tell the king: 'O
> king, bring Vibhāṇḍaka's son here by every means.'"

---
12.
ānāyya ca mahīpāla r̥śyaśr̥ṅgaṁ susatkr̥tam |
prayaccha kanyāṁ śāntāṁ vai vidhinā susamāhitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 12:** "'Bring Ṛśyaśṛṅga here, receive him with the highest honor,
> and with full devotion give him your daughter Śāntā in marriage according to
> the sacred rites.'"

---
13.
tēṣāṁ tu vacanaṁ śrutvā rājā cintāṁ prapatsyatē |
kēnōpāyēna vai śakyamihānētuṁ sa vīryavān ||

> **Meaning 13:** "Hearing their words, the king will ponder anxiously: 'By
> what means can that powerful sage be brought here?'"

---
14.
tatō rājā viniścitya saha mantribhirātmavān |
purōhitamamātyāṁśca prēṣayiṣyati satkr̥tān ||

> **Meaning 14:** "Then the prudent king, having resolved upon it with his
> counselors, will honor his family priest and ministers and bid them go forth."

---
15.
tē tu rājñō vacaḥ śrutvā vyathitā vanatānanāḥ |
na gacchēma r̥ṣērbhītā anunēṣyanti taṁ nr̥pam ||

> **Meaning 15:** "Hearing the king's words, they will be perturbed and anxious.
> Bowing down humbly in fear of Sage Vibhāṇḍaka, they will plead with the king:
> 'We are afraid of the ṛṣi; we cannot go!'"

---
16.
vakṣyanti cintayitvā tē tasyōpāyāṁśca tānkṣamān |
ānēṣyāmō vayaṁ vipraṁ na ca dōṣō bhaviṣyati ||

> **Meaning 16:** After careful thought, they will devise a safe strategy and
> reassure the king: "We will bring the brahmin here in such a way that no harm
> or blame will come upon us."

---
17.
ēvamaṅgādhipēnaiva gaṇikābhirr̥ṣēḥ sutaḥ |
ānītō'varṣayaddēvaḥ śāntā cāsmai pradīyatē ||

> **Meaning 17:** "In this way, the ruler of Aṅga had the sage's son brought
> through courtesans. The devatā poured down rain, and Śāntā was given to him in
> marriage."

---
18.
r̥śyaśr̥ṅgastu jāmātā putrāṁstava vidhāsyati |
sanatkumārakathitamētāvadvyāhr̥taṁ mayā ||

> **Meaning 18:** "That very Ṛśyaśṛṅga, as son-in-law, will bless you with sons.
> This is the entire prophecy spoken by Sanatkumāra, just as I have related it
> to you."

---
19.
atha hr̥ṣṭō daśarathaḥ sumantraṁ pratyabhāṣata |
yatharṣyaśr̥ṅgastvānītō vistarēṇa tvayōcyatām ||

> **Meaning 19:** Overjoyed, King Daśaratha said to Sumantra: "Now tell me in
>  detail how Ṛśyaśṛṅga was brought to Aṅga."

---
