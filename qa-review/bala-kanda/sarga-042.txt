=== Sarga 42 ===
Aṁśumān, Dilīpa, and Bhagīratha's Tapas; Brahmā's Boon to Bring Down Gaṅgā

---
1.
kāladharmaṁ gatē rāma sagarē prakr̥tījanāḥ |
rājānaṁ rōcayāmāsuraṁśumantaṁ sudhārmikam ||

> **Meaning 1:** "When Sagara passed away in due time, Rāma, the people chose
> the deeply righteous Aṁśumān as their king.

---
2.
sa rājā sumahānāsīdaṁśumānraghunandana |
tasya putrō mahānāsīddilīpa iti viśrutaḥ ||

> **Meaning 2:** "King Aṁśumān ruled as a truly great monarch, Raghunandana, and
> he had a noble son famed by the name Dilīpa.

---
3.
tasminrājyaṁ samāvēśya dilīpē raghunandana |
himavacchikharē ramyē tapastēpē sudāruṇam ||

> **Meaning 3:** "Entrusting the kingdom to Dilīpa, Raghunandana, Aṁśumān
> retired to a serene peak of Himavān and practiced fierce tapas.

---
4.
dvātriṁśacca sahasrāṇi varṣāṇi sumahāyaśāḥ |
tapōvanagatō rājā svargaṁ lēbhē tapōdhanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 4:** "After spending thirty-two thousand years in that tapas-grove,
> the illustrious king—rich in austerity—attained svarga.

---
5.
dilīpastu mahātējāḥ śrutvā paitāmahaṁ vadham |
duḥkhōpahatayā buddhyā niścayaṁ nādhyagacchata ||

> **Meaning 5:** "Meanwhile, the radiant Dilīpa learned of his grand-uncles'
> destruction, and with his mind clouded by grief, he could find no way forward.

---
6.
kathaṁ gaṅgāvataraṇaṁ kathaṁ tēṣāṁ jalakriyā |
tārayēyaṁ kathaṁ caitāniti cintā parō'bhavat ||

> **Meaning 6:** "'How can Gaṅgā be brought down? How can their tarpaṇa be
> performed? How can I deliver them?'—he became absorbed in this worry.

---
7.
tasya cintayatō nityaṁ dharmēṇa viditātmanaḥ |
putrō bhagīrathō nāma jajñē paramadhārmikaḥ ||

> **Meaning 7:** "While he lived in dharma with a disciplined mind, constantly
> brooding over this, a supremely righteous son named Bhagīratha was born to
> him.

---
8.
dilīpastu mahātējā yajñairbahubhiriṣṭavān |
triṁśadvarṣasahasrāṇi rājā rājyamakārayat ||

> **Meaning 8:** "The radiant King Dilīpa performed many yajñas and ruled his
> kingdom for thirty thousand years.

---
9.
agatvā niścayaṁ rājā tēṣāmuddharaṇaṁ prati |
vyādhinā naraśārdūla kāladharmamupēyivān ||

> **Meaning 9:** "Yet without discovering a way to liberate his ancestors,
> Rāma, the king succumbed to illness and met his appointed end.

---
10.
indralōkaṁ gatō rājā svārjitēnaiva karmaṇā |
rājyē bhagīrathaṁ putramabhiṣicya nararṣabhaḥ ||

> **Meaning 10:** "After consecrating his son Bhagīratha on the throne, that
> foremost among men ascended to Indraloka by the merit of his own deeds.

---
11.
bhagīrathastu rājarṣirdhārmikō raghunandana |
anapatyō mahātējāḥ prajākāmaḥ sa cāprajaḥ ||

> **Meaning 11:** "Bhagīratha was a righteous and radiant rājarṣi,
> Raghunandana; he longed for children, yet remained childless.

---
12.
mantriṣvādhāya tadrājyaṁ gaṅgāvataraṇē rataḥ |
sa tapō dīrghamātiṣṭhadgōkarṇē raghunandana ||

> **Meaning 12:** "Entrusting the kingdom to his ministers and dedicating
> himself to bringing Gaṅgā down, Rāma, he began a long tapas at Gokarṇa.

---
13.
ūrdhvabāhuḥ pañcatapā māsāhārō jitēndriyaḥ |
tasya varṣasahasrāṇi ghōrē tapasi tiṣṭhataḥ ||

> **Meaning 13:** "Standing with his arms raised, surrounded by five fires,
> eating only once a month with all his senses mastered, he endured fierce tapas
> for thousands of years—

---
14.
atītāni mahābāhō tasya rājñō mahātmanaḥ |
suprītō bhagavānbrahmā prajānāṁ patirīśvaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 14:** "until a thousand years and more had passed for that
> noble-souled king—and then, Rāma, the revered Brahmā, sovereign creator of all
> beings, was deeply pleased.

---
15.
tataḥ suragaṇaiḥ sārdhamupāgamya pitāmahaḥ |
bhagīrathaṁ mahātmānaṁ tapyamānamathābravīt ||

> **Meaning 15:** "Accompanied by the assemblies of devatās, Brahmā came to the
> noble-souled Bhagīratha as he stood in tapas and spoke:

---
16.
bhagīratha mahābhāga prītastē'haṁ janēśvara |
tapasā ca sutaptēna varaṁ varaya suvrata ||

> **Meaning 16:** "'Illustrious, steadfast Bhagīratha, great king—I am pleased
> with you and your intense tapas; choose your boon.'

---
17.
tamuvāca mahātējāḥ sarvalōkapitāmaham |
bhagīrathō mahābhāgaḥ kr̥tāñjaliravasthitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 17:** "Standing with hands joined in añjali, the radiant,
> illustrious Bhagīratha replied to the grandfather of all worlds:

---
18.
yadi mē bhagavānprītō yadyasti tapasaḥ phalam |
sagarasyātmajāḥ sarvē mattaḥ salilamāpnuyuḥ ||

> **Meaning 18:** "'Revered one, if you are pleased with me and my tapas has
> borne fruit, let all of Sagara's sons receive the sacred waters from my hands.

---
19.
gaṅgāyāḥ salilaklinnē bhasmanyēṣāṁ mahātmanām |
svargaṁ gacchēyuratyantaṁ sarvē mē prapitāmahāḥ ||

> **Meaning 19:** "'Once the ashes of those noble-souled ancestors are moistened
> by Gaṅgā's waters, may all my forebears ascend forever to svarga.

---
20.
dēyā ca santatirdēva nāvasīdētkulaṁ ca naḥ |
ikṣvākūṇāṁ kulē dēva ēṣa mē'stu varaḥ paraḥ ||

> **Meaning 20:** "'And grant me an heir, divine Brahmā, so that our Ikṣvāku
> line does not die out—let this be my supreme boon.'

---
21.
uktavākyaṁ tu rājānaṁ sarvalōkapitāmahaḥ |
pratyuvāca śubhāṁ vāṇīṁ madhurāṁ madhurākṣarām ||

> **Meaning 21:** "To the king who had spoken thus, Brahmā replied in
> auspicious, gentle, melodious words:

---
22.
manōrathō mahānēṣa bhagīratha mahāratha |
ēvaṁ bhavatu bhadraṁ tē ikṣvākukulavardhana ||

> **Meaning 22:** "'This is a noble wish, Bhagīratha—a true mahāratha and
> upholder of the Ikṣvāku line. Blessings upon you; so be it!

---
23.
iyaṁ haimavatī gaṅgā jyēṣṭhā himavataḥ sutā |
tāṁ vai dhārayituṁ rājanharastatra niyujyatām ||

> **Meaning 23:** "'Here is Gaṅgā, eldest daughter of Himavān. To receive and
> hold her descent, king, turn to Śiva and entrust that task to him.

---
24.
gaṅgāyāḥ patanaṁ rājanpr̥thivī na sahiṣyatē |
tāṁ vai dhārayituṁ vīra nānyaṁ paśyāmi śūlinaḥ ||

> **Meaning 24:** "'The earth cannot withstand the impact of Gaṅgā's fall, king;
> brave prince, I see no one besides the trident-bearer who can hold her.'"

---
25.
tamēvamuktvā rājānaṁ gaṅgāṁ cābhāṣya lōkakr̥t |
jagāma tridivaṁ dēvaḥ saha sarvairmarudgaṇaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 25:** "Having spoken thus to the king and addressed Gaṅgā as well,
> the creator of the worlds returned to svarga together with all the ranks of
> Maruts.

---
