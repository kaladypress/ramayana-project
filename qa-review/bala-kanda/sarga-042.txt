=== Sarga 42 ===

---
1.
devadeve gate tasmin so ’ṅguṣṭhāgranipīḍitām |
kr̥tvā vasumatīṁ rāma saṁvatsaram upāsata ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
atha saṁvatsare pūrṇe sarvalokanamaskr̥taḥ |
umāpatiḥ paśupatī rājānam idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
prītas te ’haṁ naraśreṣṭha kariṣyāmi tava priyam |
śirasā dhārayiṣyāmi śailarājasutām aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tato haimavatī jyeṣṭhā sarvalokanamaskr̥tā |
tadā sātimahad rūpaṁ kr̥tvā vegaṁ ca duḥsaham |
ākāśād apatad rāma śive śivaśirasy uta ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
naiva sā nirgamaṁ lebhe jaṭāmaṇḍalamohitā |
tatraivābabhramad devī saṁvatsaragaṇān bahūn ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
anena toṣitaś cāsīd atyarthaṁ raghunandana |
visasarja tato gaṅgāṁ haro bindusaraḥ prati ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
gaganāc chaṁkaraśiras tato dharaṇim āgatā |
vyasarpata jalaṁ tatra tīvraśabdapuraskr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tato devarṣigandharvā yakṣāḥ siddhagaṇās tathā |
vyalokayanta te tatra gaganād gāṁ gatāṁ tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
vimānair nagarākārair hayair gajavarais tathā |
pāriplavagatāś cāpi devatās tatra viṣṭhitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tad adbhutatamaṁ loke gaṅgāpatanam uttamam |
didr̥kṣavo devagaṇāḥ sameyur amitaujasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
saṁpatadbhiḥ suragaṇais teṣāṁ cābharaṇaujasā |
śatādityam ivābhāti gaganaṁ gatatoyadam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
śiṁśumāroragagaṇair mīnair api ca cañcalaiḥ |
vidyudbhir iva vikṣiptair ākāśam abhavat tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
pāṇḍuraiḥ salilotpīḍaiḥ kīryamāṇaiḥ sahasradhā |
śāradābhrair ivākīrṇaṁ gaganaṁ haṁsasaṁplavaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
kva cid drutataraṁ yāti kuṭilaṁ kva cid āyatam |
vinataṁ kva cid uddhūtaṁ kva cid yāti śanaiḥ śanaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
salilenaiva salilaṁ kva cid abhyāhataṁ punaḥ |
muhur ūrdhvapathaṁ gatvā papāta vasudhāṁ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tac chaṁkaraśirobhraṣṭaṁ bhraṣṭaṁ bhūmitale punaḥ |
vyarocata tadā toyaṁ nirmalaṁ gatakalmaṣam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tatrarṣigaṇagandharvā vasudhātalavāsinaḥ |
bhavāṅgapatitaṁ toyaṁ pavitram iti paspr̥śuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
śāpāt prapatitā ye ca gaganād vasudhātalam |
kr̥tvā tatrābhiṣekaṁ te babhūvur gatakalmaṣāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
dhūpapāpāḥ punas tena toyenātha subhāsvatā |
punar ākāśam āviśya svām̐l lokān pratipedire ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
mumude mudito lokas tena toyena bhāsvatā |
kr̥tābhiṣeko gaṅgāyāṁ babhūva vigataklamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
bhagīratho ’pi rājarṣir divyaṁ syandanam āsthitaḥ |
prāyād agre mahātejās taṁ gaṅgā pr̥ṣṭhato ’nvagāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
devāḥ sarṣigaṇāḥ sarve daityadānavarākṣasāḥ |
gandharvayakṣapravarāḥ sakiṁnaramahoragāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
sarvāś cāpsaraso rāma bhagīratharathānugāḥ |
gaṅgām anvagaman prītāḥ sarve jalacarāś ca ye ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
yato bhagīratho rājā tato gaṅgā yaśasvinī |
jagāma saritāṁ śreṣṭhā sarvapāpavināśinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
