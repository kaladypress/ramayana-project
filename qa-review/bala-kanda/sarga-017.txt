=== Sarga 17 ===
Incarnation of the Devas as Vānara Warriors 

---
1.
putratvaṁ tu gatē viṣṇau rājñastasya mahātmanaḥ |
uvāca dēvatāḥ sarvāḥ svayambhūrbhagavānidam ||

> **Meaning 1:** When Viṣṇu had resolved to become the son of that great-souled
> king, Daśaratha, the revered Brahmā spoke to all the devatās:

---
2.
satyasandhasya vīrasya sarvēṣāṁ nō hitaiṣiṇaḥ |
viṣṇōḥ sahāyānbalinaḥ sr̥jadhvaṁ kāmarūpiṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 2:** "Viṣṇu is heroic, true to his pledge, and ever seeks our
> welfare. Let mighty beings be born to support him, who can assume any form
> at will.

---
3.
māyāvidaśca śūrāṁśca vāyuvēgasamāñjavē |
nayajñānbuddhisampannānviṣṇutulyaparākramān ||

> **Meaning 3:** Let them be born fearless, skilled in magic, as swift as the
> wind, wise in statecraft, full of intelligence, and equal to Viṣṇu in valor.

---
4.
asaṁhāryānupāyajñāndivyasaṁhananānvitān |
sarvāstraguṇasampannānamr̥taprāśanāniva ||

> **Meaning 4:** Let them be born invincible, knowing the right means to every
> end, with divine bodies, and skilled in all astras and śastras, as if they had
> drunk the nectar of immortality.

---
5.
apsaraḥsu ca mukhyāsu gandharvīṇāṁ tanūṣu ca |
yakṣapannagakanyāsu r̥kṣavidyādharīṣu ca |
kinnarīṇāṁ ca gātrēṣu vānarīṇāṁ tanūṣu ca |
sr̥jadhvaṁ harirūpēṇa putrāṁstulyaparākramān ||

> **Meaning 5:** Through the foremost Apsarasas and Gandharvīs, through the
> daughters of Yakṣas and Pannagas, through female bears, Vidyādharīs, Kinnarīs,
> and female Vānaras, bring forth sons in Vānara form, equal to you in valor.

---
6.
pūrvamēva mayā sr̥ṣṭō jāmbavānr̥kṣapuṅgavaḥ |
jr̥mbhamāṇasya sahasā mama vaktrādajāyata ||

> **Meaning 6:** Long ago, I brought forth Jāmbavān, the foremost among bears.
> He was born suddenly from my mouth as I yawned."

---
7.
tē tathōktā bhagavatā tatpratiśrutya śāsanam |
janayāmāsurēvaṁ tē putrānvānararūpiṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 7:** Having heard this command from the blessed Brahmā, they
> accepted it and fathered sons in the form of Vānaras.

---
8.
r̥ṣayaśca mahātmānaḥ siddhavidyādharōragāḥ |
cāraṇāśca sutānvīrānsasr̥jurvanacāriṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 8:** The great-souled ṛṣis, Siddhas, Vidyādharas, Uragas, and
> Cāraṇas likewise brought forth valiant, forest-dwelling Vānara sons.

---
9.
vānarēndraṁ mahēndrābhamindraḥ vālinamātmajam |
sugrīvaṁ janayāmāsa tapanaḥ tapatāṁ varaḥ ||

> **Meaning 9:** Indra fathered Vāli, the Vānara king, who shone like Mahendra
> himself. Tapana, the Sun, foremost among all luminaries, fathered Sugrīva.

---
10.
br̥haspatistvajanayattāraṁ nāma mahākapim |
sarvavānaramukhyānāṁ buddhimantamanuttamam ||

> **Meaning 10:** Bṛhaspati fathered the great Kapi named Tāra, who was the
> wisest and foremost among all the Vānara chiefs.

---
11.
dhanadasya sutaḥ śrīmānvānarō gandhamādanaḥ |
viśvakarmā tvajanayannalaṁ nāma mahākapim ||

> **Meaning 11:** The glorious Vānara Gandhamādana was the son of Dhanada,
> Kubera. Viśvakarmā fathered the great Kapi named Nala.

---
12.
pāvakasya sutaḥ śrīmānnīlō'gnisadr̥śaprabhaḥ |
tējasā yaśasā vīryādatiricyata vānarān ||

> **Meaning 12:** The glorious Nīla was the son of Pāvaka, Agni, and shone with
> a brilliance like fire. In splendor, fame, and valor he surpassed the other
> Vānaras.

---
13.
rūpadraviṇasampannāvaśvinau rūpasammatau |
maindaṁ ca dvividaṁ caiva janayāmāsatuḥ svayam ||

> **Meaning 13:** The two Aśvins, rich in beauty and admired for it, themselves
> fathered Mainda and Dvivida.

---
14.
varuṇō janayāmāsa suṣēṇaṁ nāma vānaram |
śarabhaṁ janayāmāsa parjanyastu mahābalaḥ ||

> **Meaning 14:** Varuṇa fathered the Vānara named Suṣeṇa. The mighty Parjanya
> fathered Śarabha.

---
15.
mārutasyaurasaḥ śrīmānhanumānnāma vānaraḥ |
vajrasaṁhananōpētō vainatēyasamo javē |
sarvavānaramukhyēṣu buddhimānbalavānapi ||

> **Meaning 15:** The glorious Vānara named Hanumān was the son of Māruta. His
> body was as hard as a thunderbolt, and he was as swift as Garuḍa, son of
> Vinatā. Among all the chief Vānaras he was wise and strong.

---
16.
tē sr̥ṣṭā bahusāhasrā daśagrīvavadhōdyatāḥ ||

> **Meaning 16:** Many thousands of them were born, all intent on slaying
> Daśagrīva.

---
17.
apramēyabalā vīrā vikrāntāḥ kāmarūpiṇaḥ |
tē gajācalasaṅkāśā vapuṣmantō mahābalāḥ |
r̥kṣavānaragōpucchāḥ kṣipramēvābhijajñirē ||

> **Meaning 17:** They were valiant, of immeasurable strength and courage, able
> to take any form. Huge and mighty like elephants and mountains, those bears,
> Vānaras, and langurs were soon born.

---
18.
yasya dēvasya yadrūpaṁ vēṣō yaśca parākramaḥ |
ajāyata samaṁ tēna tasya tasya pr̥thakpr̥thak ||

> **Meaning 18:** Whatever form, bearing, and valor each devatā possessed,
> their sons were born with those very same traits.

---
19.
gōlāṅgūlēṣu cōtpannāḥ kiñcidunnatavikramāḥ |
r̥kṣīṣu ca tathā jātā vānarāḥ kinnarīṣu ca ||

> **Meaning 19:** Some were born to langur females, and some, slightly greater
> in valor, were born to bear females and to Kinnarīs. In this manner, the
> Vānaras came into being.

---
20.
dēvā maharṣigandharvāstārkṣyayakṣā yaśasvinaḥ ||
nāgāḥ kimpuruṣāścaiva siddhavidyādharōragāḥ |

> **Meaning 20:** The glorious devatās, maharṣis, Gandharvas, Tārkṣyas, and
> Yakṣas, along with Nāgas, Kimpuruṣas, Siddhas, Vidyādharas, and Uragas,

---
21.
bahavō janayāmāsuḥ hr̥ṣṭāstatra sahasraśaḥ |
vānarān sumahākāyān sarvān vai vanacāriṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 21:** many of them, filled with joy, brought forth thousands of
> Vānaras there, all of them huge in body and all of them forest-dwellers.

---
22.
apsarassu ca mukhyāsu tadā vidyādharīṣu ca |
nāgakanyāsu ca tathā gandharvīṇāṁ tanūṣu ca ||

> **Meaning 22:** They were born then to the foremost Apsarasas, to Vidyādharīs,
> to Nāga women, and to Gandharvīs.

---
23.
kāmarūpabalōpētā yathākāmavicāriṇaḥ |
siṁhaśārdūlasadr̥śā darpēṇa ca balēna ca ||

> **Meaning 23:** They had strength and the power to take any form, and they
> roamed wherever they pleased. In pride and in strength they were like lions
> and tigers.

---
24.
śilāpraharaṇāḥ sarvē sarvē pādapayōdhinaḥ |
nakhadaṁṣṭrāyudhāḥ sarvē sarvē sarvāstrakōvidāḥ ||

> **Meaning 24:** All of them could strike with rocks, and all could fight with
> trees. Claws and fangs were their weapons, and all of them were skilled in
> every astra.

---
25.
vicālayēyuḥ śailēndrānbhēdayēyuḥ sthirāndrumān |
kṣōbhayēyuśca vēgēna samudraṁ saritāṁ patim ||

> **Meaning 25:** They could shake the greatest mountains and uproot the firmest
> trees. With their speed they could churn the ocean, into which all rivers flow.

---
26.
dārayēyuḥ kṣitiṁ padbhyāmāplavēyurmahārṇavam |
nabhastalaṁ viśēyuśca gr̥hṇīyurapi tōyadān ||

> **Meaning 26:** They could tear the earth open with their feet and leap across
> the great sea. They could enter the sky and even seize the clouds.

---
27.
gr̥hṇīyurapi mātaṅgānmattānpravrajatō vanē |
nardamānāśca nādēna pātayēyurvihaṅgamān ||

> **Meaning 27:** They could even seize rutting elephants roaming through the
> forest. Roaring, they could bring birds down from the sky with their cry.

---
28.
īdr̥śānāṁ prasūtāni harīṇāṁ kāmarūpiṇām |
śataṁ śatasahasrāṇi yūthapānāṁ mahātmanām ||

> **Meaning 28:** Ten million such great-souled Vānara commanders, who could
> take any form, were born.

---
29.
tē pradhānēṣu yūthēṣu harīṇāṁ hariyūthapāḥ |
babhūvuryūthapaśrēṣṭhā vīrāṁścājanayanharīn ||

> **Meaning 29:** Those Vānara commanders became the foremost leaders of the
> principal Vānara hosts, and they in turn brought forth valiant Vānaras.

---
30.
anyē r̥kṣavataḥ prasthānupatasthuḥ sahasraśaḥ |
anyē nānāvidhāñśailān bhējirē kānanāni ca ||

> **Meaning 30:** Some, by the thousands, settled on the slopes of Mount
> Ṛkṣavān, while others made their home in various mountains and forests.

---
31.
sūryaputraṁ ca sugrīvaṁ śakraputraṁ ca vālinam |
bhrātarāvupatasthustē sarva ēva hariyūthapāḥ ||

> **Meaning 31:** All those Vānara commanders served the two brothers,
> Sugrīva, the son of the Sun, and Vāli, the son of Indra,

---
32.
nalaṁ nīlaṁ hanūmantamanyāṁśca hariyūthapān |
tē tārkṣyabalasampannāḥ sarvē yuddhaviśāradāḥ |
vicarantō'rdayansarvānsiṁhavyāghramahōragān ||

> **Meaning 32:** along with Nala, Nīla, Hanumān, and the other Vānara
> commanders. Endowed with the strength of Tārkṣya and skilled in battle,
> they struck down lions, tigers, and great serpents as they roamed.

---
33.
tāṃśca sarvān mahābāhuḥ bālī vipulavikramaḥ |
jugōpa bhujavīryēṇa r̥kṣagōpucchavānarān ||

> **Meaning 33:** Mighty-armed Vāli, of vast valor, protected all of them,
> bears, langurs, and Vānaras, with the strength of his arms.

---
34.
taiḥ iyaṁ pr̥thivī śūraiḥ saparvatavanārṇavā |
kīrṇā vividhasaṁsthānairnānāvyañjanalakṣaṇaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 34:** Those mighty warriors, of varied forms and marked with
> countless distinctive features, filled this entire earth, along with its
> mountains, forests, and oceans.

---
35.
tairmēghavr̥ndācalakūṭakalpairmahābalairvānarayūthapālaiḥ |
babhūva bhūrbhīmaśarīrarūpaiḥ samāvr̥tā rāmasahāyahētōḥ ||

> **Meaning 35:** Towering like storm clouds and mountain peaks, and formidable
> in form, those mighty Vānara commanders overspread the entire earth, all to
> support Rāma.

---
