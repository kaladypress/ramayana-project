=== Sarga 6 ===

---
1.
puryāṁ tasyām ayodhyāyāṁ vedavit sarvasaṁgrahaḥ |
dīrghadarśī mahātejāḥ paurajānapadapriyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
ikṣvākūṇām atiratho yajvā dharmarato vaśī |
maharṣikalpo rājarṣis triṣu lokeṣu viśrutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
balavān nihatāmitro mitravān vijitendriyaḥ |
dhanaiś ca saṁcayaiś cānyaiḥ śakravaiśravaṇopamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
yathā manur mahātejā lokasya parirakṣitā |
tathā daśaratho rājā vasañ jagad apālayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tena satyābhisaṁdhena trivargam anutiṣṭhatā |
pālitā sā purī śreṣṭhendreṇa ivāmarāvatī ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tasmin puravare hr̥ṣṭā dharmātmanā bahuśrutāḥ |
narās tuṣṭādhanaiḥ svaiḥ svair alubdhāḥ satyavādinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
nālpasaṁnicayaḥ kaś cid āsīt tasmin purottame |
kuṭumbī yo hy asiddhārtho ’gavāśvadhanadhānyavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
kāmī vā na kadaryo vā nr̥śaṁsaḥ puruṣaḥ kva cit |
draṣṭuṁ śakyam ayodhyāyāṁ nāvidvān na ca nāstikaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sarve narāś ca nāryaś ca dharmaśīlāḥ susaṁyatāḥ |
muditāḥ śīlavr̥ttābhyāṁ maharṣaya ivāmalāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
nākuṇḍalī nāmukuṭī nāsragvī nālpabhogavān |
nāmr̥ṣṭo nānuliptāṅgo nāsugandhaś ca vidyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
nāmr̥ṣṭabhojī nādātā nāpy anaṅgadaniṣkadhr̥k |
nāhastābharaṇo vāpi dr̥śyate nāpy anātmavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
nānāhitāgnir nāyajvā vipro nāpy asahasradaḥ |
kaś cid āsīd ayodhyāyāṁ na ca nirvr̥ttasaṁkaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
svakarmaniratā nityaṁ brāhmaṇā vijitendriyāḥ |
dānādhyayanaśīlāś ca saṁyatāś ca pratigrahe ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
na nāstiko nānr̥tako na kaś cid abahuśrutaḥ |
nāsūyako na cāśakto nāvidvān vidyate tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
na dīnaḥ kṣiptacitto vā vyathito vāpi kaś cana |
kaś cin naro vā nārī vā nāśrīmān nāpy arūpavān |
draṣṭuṁ śakyam ayodhyāyāṁ nāpi rājanyabhaktimān ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
varṇeṣv agryacaturtheṣu devatātithipūjakāḥ |
dīrghāyuṣo narāḥ sarve dharmaṁ satyaṁ ca saṁśritāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
kṣatraṁ brahmamukhaṁ cāsīd vaiśyāḥ kṣatram anuvratāḥ |
śūdrāḥ svadharmaniratās trīn varṇān upacāriṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
sā tenekṣvākunāthena purī suparirakṣitā |
yathā purastān manunā mānavendreṇa dhīmatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
yodhānām agnikalpānāṁ peśalānām amarṣiṇām |
saṁpūrṇākr̥tavidyānāṁ guhākesariṇām iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
kāmbojaviṣaye jātair bāhlīkaiś ca hayottamaiḥ |
vanāyujair nadījaiś ca pūrṇāharihayopamaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
vindhyaparvatajair mattaiḥ pūrṇā haimavatair api |
madānvitair atibalair mātaṅgaiḥ parvatopamaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
añjanād api niṣkrāntair vāmanād api ca dvipaiḥ |
bhadramandrair bhadramr̥gair mr̥gamandraiś ca sā purī ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
nityamattaiḥ sadā pūrṇā nāgair acalasaṁnibhaiḥ |
sā yojane ca dve bhūyaḥ satyanāmā prakāśate ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tāṁ satyanāmāṁ dr̥ḍhatoraṇārgalām; gr̥hair vicitrair upaśobhitāṁ śivām |
purīm ayodhyāṁ nr̥sahasrasaṁkulāṁ; śaśāsa vai śakrasamo mahīpatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
