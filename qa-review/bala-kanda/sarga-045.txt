=== Sarga 45 ===

---
1.
hateṣu teṣu putreṣu ditiḥ paramaduḥkhitā |
mārīcaṁ kāśyapaṁ rāma bhartāram idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
hataputrāsmi bhagavaṁs tava putrair mahābalaiḥ |
śakrahantāram icchāmi putraṁ dīrghatapo’rjitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sāhaṁ tapaś cariṣyāmi garbhaṁ me dātum arhasi |
īdr̥śaṁ śakrahantāraṁ tvam anujñātum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tasyās tad vacanaṁ śrutvā mārīcaḥ kāśyapas tadā |
pratyuvāca mahātejā ditiṁ paramaduḥkhitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
evaṁ bhavatu bhadraṁ te śucir bhava tapodhane |
janayiṣyasi putraṁ tvaṁ śakra hantāram āhave ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
pūrṇe varṣasahasre tu śucir yadi bhaviṣyasi |
putraṁ trailokya hantāraṁ mattas tvaṁ janayiṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
evam uktvā mahātejāḥ pāṇinā sa mamārja tām |
samālabhya tataḥ svastīty uktvā sa tapase yayau ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
gate tasmin naraśreṣṭha ditiḥ paramaharṣitā |
kuśaplavanam āsādya tapas tepe sudāruṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tapas tasyāṁ hi kurvatyāṁ paricaryāṁ cakāra ha |
sahasrākṣo naraśreṣṭha parayā guṇasaṁpadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
agniṁ kuśān kāṣṭham apaḥ phalaṁ mūlaṁ tathaiva ca |
nyavedayat sahasrākṣo yac cānyad api kāṅkṣitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
gātrasaṁvāhanaiś caiva śramāpanayanais tathā |
śakraḥ sarveṣu kāleṣu ditiṁ paricacāra ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
atha varṣasahasretu daśone raghu nandana |
ditiḥ paramasaṁprītā sahasrākṣam athābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tapaś carantyā varṣāṇi daśa vīryavatāṁ vara |
avaśiṣṭāni bhadraṁ te bhrātaraṁ drakṣyase tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tam ahaṁ tvatkr̥te putra samādhāsye jayotsukam |
trailokyavijayaṁ putra saha bhokṣyasi vijvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
evam uktvā ditiḥ śakraṁ prāpte madhyaṁ divākare |
nidrayāpahr̥tā devī pādau kr̥tvātha śīrṣataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
dr̥ṣṭvā tām aśuciṁ śakraḥ pādataḥ kr̥tamūrdhajām |
śiraḥsthāne kr̥tau pādau jahāsa ca mumoda ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tasyāḥ śarīravivaraṁ viveśa ca puraṁdaraḥ |
garbhaṁ ca saptadhā rāma bibheda paramātmavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
bidhyamānas tato garbho vajreṇa śataparvaṇā |
ruroda susvaraṁ rāma tato ditir abudhyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
mā rudo mā rudaś ceti garbhaṁ śakro ’bhyabhāṣata |
bibheda ca mahātejā rudantam api vāsavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
na hantavyo na hantavya ity evaṁ ditir abravīt |
niṣpapāta tataḥ śakro mātur vacanagauravāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
prāñjalir vajrasahito ditiṁ śakro ’bhyabhāṣata |
aśucir devi suptāsi pādayoḥ kr̥tamūrdhajā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tadantaram ahaṁ labdhvā śakrahantāram āhave |
abhindaṁ saptadhā devi tan me tvaṁ kṣantum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
