=== Sarga 45 ===
Crossing the Gaṅgā to Viśālā; The Churning of Kṣīroda

---
1.
viśvāmitravacaḥ śrutvā rāghavaḥ sahalakṣmaṇaḥ |
vismayaṁ paramaṁ gatvā viśvāmitramathābravīt ||

> **Meaning 1:** Having heard Viśvāmitra's words, Rāghava, along with Lakṣmaṇa,
> was filled with supreme wonder and then spoke to Viśvāmitra:

---
2.
atyadbhutamidaṁ brahman kathitaṁ paramaṁ tvayā |
gaṅgāvataraṇaṁ puṇyaṁ sāgarasyāpi pūraṇam ||

> **Meaning 2:** "Most wondrous is this supreme account you have narrated,
> revered sage—the sacred descent of Gaṅgā and the filling of the ocean.

---
3.
tasya sā śarvarī sarvā mama saumitriṇā saha |
jagāma cintayānasya viśvāmitrakathāṁ śubhām ||

> **Meaning 3:** "All that night passed for me, together with Saumitri, as I
> reflected on this auspicious story of yours."

---
4.
tataḥ prabhātē vimalē viśvāmitraṁ tapōdhanam |
uvāca rāghavō vākyaṁ kr̥tāhnikamarindamaḥ ||

> **Meaning 4:** Then, in the clear light of morning, Rāghava, subduer of
> foes, addressed Viśvāmitra, whose wealth was tapas, after the sage had
> completed his morning rites:

---
5.
gatā bhagavatī rātriḥ śrōtavyaṁ paramaṁ śrutam |
kṣaṇabhūtēva nau rātriḥ saṁvr̥ttēyaṁ parantapa |
imāṁ cintayatōḥ sarvāṁ nikhilēna kathāṁ tava ||

> **Meaning 5:** "The blessed night has passed, and we have heard what is
> supreme to hear. Scorcher of foes, this night seemed to pass like a mere
> moment for the two of us as we pondered your entire story in all its detail.

---
6.
tarāma saritaṁ śrēṣṭhaṁ puṇyāṁ tripathagāṁ nadīm ||

> **Meaning 6:** "Let us now cross this foremost of rivers, the sacred
> Tripathagā.

---
7.
naurēṣā hi sukhāstīrṇā r̥ṣīṇāṁ puṇyakarmaṇām |
bhagavantamiha prāptaṁ jñātvā tvaritamāgatā ||

> **Meaning 7:** "Here is a boat of the virtuous r̥ṣis, fitted with comfortable
> seats—it has come swiftly at the news of your honored arrival here."

---
8.
tasya tadvacanaṁ śrutvā rāghavasya mahātmanaḥ |
santāraṁ kārayāmāsa sarṣisaṅghasya kauśikaḥ ||

> **Meaning 8:** Hearing those words of the great Rāghava, Kauśika had them
> ferried across along with the assembled r̥ṣis.

---
9.
uttaraṁ tīramāsādya sampūjyarṣigaṇaṁ tataḥ |
gaṅgākūlē niviṣṭāstē viśālāṁ dadr̥śuḥ purim ||

> **Meaning 9:** Reaching the northern shore and honoring the gathered r̥ṣis,
> they paused on the bank of Gaṅgā and saw the city of Viśālā.

---
10.
tatō munivarastūrṇaṁ jagāma saharāghavaḥ |
viśālāṁ nagarīṁ ramyāṁ divyāṁ svargōpamāṁ tadā ||

> **Meaning 10:** Then that foremost of munis, accompanied by Rāghava, went
> swiftly to the splendid, divine city of Viśālā, which rivaled svarga itself.

---
11.
atha rāmō mahāprājñō viśvāmitraṁ mahāmunim |
papraccha prāñjalirbhūtvā viśālāmuttamāṁ purīm ||

> **Meaning 11:** Then the deeply wise Rāma, joining his palms in reverence,
> asked the great muni Viśvāmitra about the noble city of Viśālā:

---
12.
katamō rājavaṁśō'yaṁ viśālāyāṁ mahāmunē |
śrōtumicchāmi bhadraṁ tē paraṁ kautūhalaṁ hi mē ||

> **Meaning 12:** "Which royal dynasty rules here in Viśālā, great muni? May all
> be well with you—I am truly eager to hear about it."

---
13.
tasya tadvacanaṁ śrutvā rāmasya munipuṅgavaḥ |
ākhyātuṁ tatsamārēbhē viśālasya purātanam ||

> **Meaning 13:** Hearing those words of Rāma, that foremost among munis began
> to narrate the ancient history of Viśāla:

---
14.
śrūyatāṁ rāma śakrasya kathāṁ kathayataḥ śrutām |
asmin dēśē hi yadvr̥ttaṁ śr̥ṇu tattvēna rāghava ||

> **Meaning 14:** "Listen, Rāma, to the story I heard as Śakra himself told
> it—hear the true account of what took place in this very region.

---
15.
pūrvaṁ kr̥tayugē rāma ditēḥ putrā mahābalāḥ |
aditēśca mahābhāgā vīryavantaḥ sudhārmikāḥ ||

> **Meaning 15:** "Rāma, long ago in the Kr̥ta-yuga, the sons of Diti were
> mighty in strength, and the sons of Aditi were greatly fortunate, valorous,
> and deeply righteous.

---
16.
tatastēṣāṁ naravyāghra buddhirāsīnmahātmanām |
amarā nirjarāścaiva kathaṁ syāmō nirāmayāḥ ||

> **Meaning 16:** "Greatest of men, a thought then arose in all those great
> beings: 'How can we become immortal, ageless, and free from disease?'

---
17.
tēṣāṁ cintayatāṁ tatra buddhirāsīdvipaścitām |
kṣīrōdamathanaṁ kr̥tvā rasaṁ prāpsyāma tatra vai ||

> **Meaning 17:** "As those wise ones pondered this, the idea came to them: 'By
> churning Kṣīroda, the Ocean of Milk, we will obtain amr̥ta there.'

---
18.
tatō niścitya mathanaṁ yōktraṁ kr̥tvā ca vāsukim |
manthānaṁ mandaraṁ kr̥tvā mamanthuramitaujasaḥ ||

> **Meaning 18:** "Having decided upon the churning, those beings of boundless
> energy made Vāsuki the churning rope and Mount Mandara the churning rod, and
> began to churn.

---
19.
atha varṣasahasrēṇa yōktraṁ sarpaśirāṁsi ca |
vamantyativiṣaṁ tatra dadaṁśurdaśanaiḥ śilāḥ ||

> **Meaning 19:** "Then, after a thousand years, the serpent heads serving as
> the rope began to spew fierce venom and bit into the rocks with their fangs.

---
20.
utpapātāgnisaṅkāśaṁ hālāhalamahāviṣam |
tēna dagdhaṁ jagatsarvaṁ sadēvāsuramānuṣam ||

> **Meaning 20:** "From this, the great poison Hālāhala, blazing like fire,
> surged forth and scorched the entire world—devatās, asuras, and humans alike.

---
21.
atha dēvā mahādēvaṁ śaṅkaraṁ śaraṇārthinaḥ |
jagmuḥ paśupatiṁ rudraṁ trāhi trāhīti tuṣṭuvuḥ ||

> **Meaning 21:** "Seeking refuge, the devatās then approached Mahādeva—Śaṅkara,
> Paśupati, Rudra—and praised him, crying, 'Save us! Save us!'

---
22.
ēvamuktastatō dēvairdēvadēvēśvaraḥ prabhuḥ |
prādurāsīt tatō'traiva śaṅkhacakradharō hariḥ ||

> **Meaning 22:** "When the devatās spoke thus, the supreme ruler of the
> devatās, the mighty Hari, bearing the conch and discus, also appeared right
> there.

---
23.
uvācainaṁ smitaṁ kr̥tvā rudraṁ śūladharaṁ hariḥ |
daivatairmathyamānē tu tatpūrvaṁ samupasthitam ||

> **Meaning 23:** "With a gentle smile, Hari said to Rudra, bearer of the
> trident: 'As the devatās churned the ocean, this was the very first thing to
> emerge.

---
24.
tattvadīyaṁ suraśrēṣṭha surāṇāmagratō hi yat |
agrapūjāmiha sthitvā gr̥hāṇēdaṁ viṣaṁ prabhō ||

> **Meaning 24:** "'Foremost of the devatās, since you stand first among them
> all, what has emerged first rightfully belongs to you. Remain here, mighty
> one, and accept this poison as your agrapūjā—the first offering of honor.'

---
25.
ityuktvā ca suraśrēṣṭhastatraivāntaradhīyata |
dēvatānāṁ bhayaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā śrutvā vākyaṁ tu śārṅgiṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 25:** "Having spoken thus, that supreme devatā vanished right there.
> Seeing the fear of the devatās and hearing the words of Śārṅgī,

---
26.
hālāhalaṁ viṣaṁ ghōraṁ sañjagrāhāmr̥tōpamam |
dēvān visr̥jya dēvēśō jagāma bhagavān haraḥ ||

> **Meaning 26:** "the revered Hara, sovereign of the devatās, accepted that
> dreadful Hālāhala poison as though it were amr̥ta itself; then, taking leave
> of the devatās, he returned to his abode.

---
27.
tatō dēvāsurāḥ sarvē mamanthū raghunandana |
pravivēśātha pātālaṁ manthānaḥ parvatōttamaḥ ||

> **Meaning 27:** "Raghunandana, all the devatās and asuras then resumed
> churning. As they did, Mount Mandara—that foremost of mountains, their
> churning rod—sank into Pātāla.

---
28.
tatō dēvāḥ sagandharvāḥ tuṣṭuvurmadhusūdanam |
tvaṁ gatiḥ sarvabhūtānāṁ viśēṣēṇa divaukasām ||

> **Meaning 28:** "At that, the devatās, along with the Gandharvas, praised
> Madhusūdana: 'You are the refuge of all beings, and especially of the
> devatās.

---
29.
pālayāsmān mahābāhō girimuddhartumarhasi |
iti śrutvā hr̥ṣīkēśaḥ kāmaṭhaṁ rūpamāsthitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 29:** "'Protect us, mighty-armed one—please lift up the mountain!'
> Hearing this, Viṣṇu assumed the form of a great tortoise.

---
30.
parvataṁ pr̥ṣṭhataḥ kr̥tvā śiśyē tatrōdadhau hariḥ |
parvatāgraṁ tu lōkātmā hastēnākramya kēśavaḥ |
dēvānāṁ madhyataḥ sthitvā mamantha puruṣōttamaḥ ||

> **Meaning 30:** "In his Kūrma-avatāra, Hari lay in the depths of the ocean
> bearing the mountain on his back; yet as the all-pervading soul of the
> universe, that same Puruṣottama Keśava simultaneously held down the
> mountain's peak with his hand and stood among the devatās, churning alongside
> them.

---
31.
atha varṣasahasrēṇa sadaṇḍaḥ sakamaṇḍaluḥ |
pūrvaṁ dhanvantarirnāma apsarāśca suvarcasaḥ ||

> **Meaning 31:** "Then, after a thousand years, Dhanvantari—the personification
> of Āyurveda—emerged first, bearing a staff and a kamaṇḍalu, along with the
> resplendent Apsarās.

---
32.
apsu nirmathanādēva rasāttasmādvarastriyaḥ |
utpēturmanujaśrēṣṭha tasmādapsarasō'bhavan ||

> **Meaning 32:** "Foremost of men, because those exquisite women sprang forth
> from the churning of the milky waters (apsu) and from their essence (rasa),
> they came to be known as Apsarās.

---
33.
ṣaṣṭiḥ kōṭyō'bhavaṁstāsāmapsarāṇāṁ suvarcasām |
asankhyēyāstu kākutstha yāstāsāṁ paricārikāḥ ||

> **Meaning 33:** "Kākutstha, sixty crore of those resplendent Apsarās came
> forth, while their attendants were beyond counting.

---
34.
na tāḥ sma pratigr̥hṇanti sarvē tē dēvadānavāḥ |
apratigrahaṇādēva tā vai sādhāraṇāḥ smr̥tāḥ ||

> **Meaning 34:** "None of those devatās or dānavas accepted them as wives, and
> because they were not taken in marriage, they remained unwedded—not bound to
> any single consort.

---
35.
varuṇasya tataḥ kanyā vāruṇī raghunandana |
utpapāta mahābhāgā mārgamāṇā parigraham ||

> **Meaning 35:** "Raghunandana, next to rise from the ocean was Varuṇa's
> illustrious daughter Vāruṇī—the presiding deity of Surā—seeking to be
> received.

---
36.
ditēḥ putrā na tāṁ rāma jagr̥hurvaruṇātmajām |
aditēstu sutā vīra jagr̥hustāmaninditām ||

> **Meaning 36:** "Rāma, the sons of Diti did not receive Varuṇa's daughter,
> whereas the sons of Aditi welcomed that blameless goddess.

---
37.
asurāstēna daitēyāḥ surāstēnāditēḥ sutāḥ |
hr̥ṣṭāḥ pramuditāścāsan vāruṇīgrahaṇāt surāḥ ||

> **Meaning 37:** "Because they rejected Surā (Vāruṇī), Diti's sons came to be
> known as Asuras, while Aditi's sons became known as Suras; and by welcoming
> Vāruṇī, the Suras were filled with joy and delight.

---
38.
uccaiḥśravā hayaśrēṣṭhō maṇiratnaṁ ca kaustubham |
udatiṣṭhannaraśrēṣṭha tathaivāmr̥tamuttamam ||

> **Meaning 38:** "Foremost of men, then Uccaiḥśravas, the noblest of horses, and
> the peerless jewel Kaustubha arose, along with the supreme amr̥ta.

---
39.
atha tasya kr̥tē rāma mahānāsīt kulakṣayaḥ |
aditēstu tataḥ putrā ditēḥ putrānasūdayan ||

> **Meaning 39:** "Rāma, for the sake of that amr̥ta, a great destruction of
> both lineages then ensued, as the sons of Aditi slew the sons of Diti.

---
40.
ēkatāmagaman sarvē asurā rākṣasaiḥ saha |
yuddhamāsīn mahāghōraṁ vīratrailōkyamōhanam ||

> **Meaning 40:** "Brave prince, all the asuras joined forces with the rākṣasas
> against the Suras, and a terrifying war broke out that bewildered all three
> worlds.

---
41.
yadā kṣayaṁ gataṁ sarvaṁ tadā viṣṇurmahābalaḥ |
amr̥taṁ sō'harattūrṇaṁ māyāmāsthāya mōhinīm ||

> **Meaning 41:** "When everything was nearing ruin, the mighty Viṣṇu, through
> his māyā, assumed the enchanting form of Mohinī and swiftly carried off the
> amr̥ta.

---
42.
yē gatābhimukhaṁ viṣṇumakṣaraṁ puruṣōttamam |
sampiṣṭāstē tadā yuddhē viṣṇunā prabhaviṣṇunā ||

> **Meaning 42:** "Those who marched against the imperishable Viṣṇu,
> Puruṣottama, were crushed in that battle by the all-powerful Viṣṇu.

---
43.
aditērātmajā vīrā ditēḥ putrānnijagh‌nirē |
asmin ghōrē mahāyuddhē daitēyādityayōrbhr̥śam ||

> **Meaning 43:** "In that fierce, great war between the Daityas and the
> Ādityas, the heroic sons of Aditi slaughtered the sons of Diti relentlessly.

---
44.
nihatya ditiputrāṁśca rājyaṁ prāpya purandaraḥ |
śaśāsa muditō lōkān sarṣisanghān sacāraṇān ||

> **Meaning 44:** "Having slain Diti's sons and regained sovereignty, Purandara
> ruled joyfully over all the worlds, together with the assemblies of r̥ṣis and
> Cāraṇas."

---
