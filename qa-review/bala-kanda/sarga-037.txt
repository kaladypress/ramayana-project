=== Sarga 37 ===

---
1.
tāṁ kathāṁ kauśiko rāme nivedya madhurākṣaram |
punar evāparaṁ vākyaṁ kākutstham idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
ayodhyādhipatiḥ śūraḥ pūrvam āsīn narādhipaḥ |
sagaro nāma dharmātmā prajākāmaḥ sa cāprajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
vaidarbhaduhitā rāma keśinī nāma nāmataḥ |
jyeṣṭhā sagarapatnī sā dharmiṣṭhā satyavādinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
ariṣṭanemiduhitā rūpeṇāpratimā bhuvi |
dvitīyā sagarasyāsīt patnī sumatisaṁjñitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tābhyāṁ saha tadā rājā patnībhyāṁ taptavāṁs tapaḥ |
himavantaṁ samāsādya bhr̥guprasravaṇe girau ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
atha varṣa śate pūrṇe tapasārādhito muniḥ |
sagarāya varaṁ prādād bhr̥guḥ satyavatāṁ varaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
apatyalābhaḥ sumahān bhaviṣyati tavānagha |
kīrtiṁ cāpratimāṁ loke prāpsyase puruṣarṣabha ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
ekā janayitā tāta putraṁ vaṁśakaraṁ tava |
ṣaṣṭiṁ putrasahasrāṇi aparā janayiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
bhāṣamāṇaṁ naravyāghraṁ rājapatnyau prasādya tam |
ūcatuḥ paramaprīte kr̥tāñjalipuṭe tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
ekaḥ kasyāḥ suto brahman kā bahūñ janayiṣyati |
śrotum icchāvahe brahman satyam astu vacas tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tayos tad vacanaṁ śrutvā bhr̥guḥ parama dhārmikaḥ |
uvāca paramāṁ vāṇīṁ svacchando ’tra vidhīyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
eko vaṁśakaro vāstu bahavo vā mahābalāḥ |
kīrtimanto mahotsāhāḥ kā vā kaṁ varam icchati ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
munes tu vacanaṁ śrutvā keśinī raghunandana |
putraṁ vaṁśakaraṁ rāma jagrāha nr̥pasaṁnidhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
ṣaṣṭiṁ putrasahasrāṇi suparṇabhaginī tadā |
mahotsāhān kīrtimato jagrāha sumatiḥ sutān ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
pradakṣiṇam r̥ṣiṁ kr̥tvā śirasābhipraṇamya ca |
jagāma svapuraṁ rājā sabhāryā raghunandana ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
atha kāle gate tasmiñ jyeṣṭhā putraṁ vyajāyata |
asamañja iti khyātaṁ keśinī sagarātmajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
sumatis tu naravyāghra garbhatumbaṁ vyajāyata |
ṣaṣṭiḥ putrasahasrāṇi tumbabhedād viniḥsr̥tāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
ghr̥tapūrṇeṣu kumbheṣu dhātryas tān samavardhayan |
kālena mahatā sarve yauvanaṁ pratipedire ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
atha dīrgheṇa kālena rūpayauvanaśālinaḥ |
ṣaṣṭiḥ putrasahasrāṇi sagarasyābhavaṁs tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
sa ca jyeṣṭho naraśreṣṭha sagarasyātmasaṁbhavaḥ |
bālān gr̥hītvā tu jale sarayvā raghunandana |
prakṣipya prahasan nityaṁ majjatas tān nirīkṣya vai ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
paurāṇām ahite yuktaḥ pitrā nirvāsitaḥ purāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tasya putro ’ṁśumān nāma asamañjasya vīryavān |
saṁmataḥ sarvalokasya sarvasyāpi priyaṁvadaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tataḥ kālena mahatā matiḥ samabhijāyata |
sagarasya naraśreṣṭha yajeyam iti niścitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
sa kr̥tvā niścayaṁ rājā sopādhyāyagaṇas tadā |
yajñakarmaṇi vedajño yaṣṭuṁ samupacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
