=== Sarga 37 ===
The Birth of Kārtikeya (Skanda) and His Consecration as Commander

---
1.
tapyamānē tapō dēvē dēvāḥ sarṣigaṇāḥ purā |
sēnāpatimabhīpsantaḥ pitāmahamupāgaman ||

> **Meaning 1:** "Long ago, while Śiva was absorbed in tapas, the devatās and
> the assemblies of ṛṣis, seeking a commander for their armies, approached
> Brahmā.

---
2.
tatō'bruvansurāḥ sarvē bhagavantaṁ pitāmaham |
praṇipatya śubhaṁ vākyaṁ sēndrāḥ sāgnipurōgamāḥ ||

> **Meaning 2:** "Bowing low before the revered Brahmā, all the devatās—with
> Indra and Agni at their head—spoke these earnest words:

---
3.
yō naḥ sēnāpatirdēva dattō bhagavatā purā |
sa tapaḥ paramāsthāya tapyatē sma sahōmayā ||

> **Meaning 3:** "'Revered god, Śiva, the commander you once granted us, has
> embraced supreme tapas and is practicing it together with Umā.

---
4.
yadatrānantaraṁ kāryaṁ lōkānāṁ hitakāmyayā |
saṁvidhatsva vidhānajña tvaṁ hi naḥ paramā gatiḥ ||

> **Meaning 4:** "'Please ordain whatever must be done next for the good of the
> worlds, Brahmā, for you know the right course—you alone are our supreme
> refuge.'

---
5.
dēvatānāṁ vacaḥ śrutvā sarvalōkapitāmahaḥ |
sāntvayanmadhurairvākyaistridaśānidamabravīt ||

> **Meaning 5:** "Hearing the devatās' plea, Brahmā, grandfather of all worlds,
> comforted them with gentle words and replied:

---
6.
śailaputryā yaduktaṁ tanna prajāsyatha patniṣu |
tasyā vacanamakliṣṭaṁ satyamēva na saṁśayaḥ ||

> **Meaning 6:** "'Pārvatī, the mountain's daughter, declared that you will not
> have children with your wives; her unfailing word will surely come true,
> without a doubt.

---
7.
iyamākāśagā gaṅgā yasyāṁ putraṁ hutāśanaḥ |
janayiṣyati dēvānāṁ sēnāpatimarindamam ||

> **Meaning 7:** "'Yet here is Gaṅgā, who courses through the heavens. In her,
> Agni will bring forth a son who will be commander of the devatās and
> conqueror of their foes.

---
8.
jyēṣṭhā śailēndraduhitā mānayiṣyati taṁ sutam |
umāyāstadbahumataṁ bhaviṣyati na saṁśayaḥ ||

> **Meaning 8:** "'Gaṅgā, the eldest daughter of the mountain king, will cherish
> that son, and Umā too will warmly approve of this—of that there is no doubt.'

---
9.
tacchrutvā vacanaṁ tasya kr̥tārthā raghunandana |
praṇipatya surāḥ sarvē pitāmahamapūjayan ||

> **Meaning 9:** "Hearing his words, Raghunandana, all the devatās felt their
> hopes fulfilled, bowed low, and paid homage to Brahmā.

---
10.
tē gatvā parvataṁ rāma kailāsaṁ dhātumaṇḍitam |
agniṁ niyōjayāmāsuḥ putrārthaṁ sarvadēvatāḥ ||

> **Meaning 10:** "Then, Rāma, all the devatās went to Mount Kailāsa, gleaming
> with precious minerals, and entrusted Agni with bringing forth a son:

---
11.
dēvakāryamidaṁ dēva samādhatsva hutāśana |
śailaputryāṁ mahātējō gaṅgāyāṁ tēja utsr̥ja ||

> **Meaning 11:** "'Accomplish this mission for the devatās, divine Agni—pour
> your mighty tejas into Gaṅgā, the mountain's daughter.'

---
12.
dēvatānāṁ pratijñāya gaṅgāmabhyētya pāvakaḥ |
garbhaṁ dhāraya vai dēvi dēvatānāmidaṁ priyam ||

> **Meaning 12:** "Pledging to do so for the devatās, Agni went to Gaṅgā and
> said, 'Receive this sacred seed, goddess—this is the wish of the devatās.'

---
13.
ityētadvacanaṁ śrutvā divyaṁ rūpamadhārayat |
sa tasyā mahimāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā samantādavakīryata ||

> **Meaning 13:** "Hearing Agni's words, Gaṅgā assumed a divine form. Seeing
> her vast majesty, he poured his tejas forth on every side.

---
14.
samantatastadā dēvīmabhyaṣiñcata pāvakaḥ |
sarvasrōtāṁsi pūrṇāni gaṅgāyā raghunandana ||

> **Meaning 14:** "Agni showered the goddess from every side, Raghunandana,
> until all of Gaṅgā's currents were completely filled with his tejas.

---
15.
tamuvāca tatō gaṅgā sarvadēvapurōhitam |
aśaktā dhāraṇē dēva tava tējaḥ samuddhatam |
dahyamānāgninā tēna sampravyathitacētanā ||

> **Meaning 15:** "Scorched by that fire and overwhelmed in spirit, Gaṅgā cried
> out to Agni, priest of all the devatās, 'I cannot bear your surging tejas,
> divine one!'

---
16.
athābravīdidaṁ gaṅgāṁ sarvadēvahutāśanaḥ |
iha haimavatē pādē garbhō'yaṁ sannivēśyatām ||

> **Meaning 16:** "Then Agni, the sacred fire of all the devatās, told Gaṅgā,
> 'Place this sacred seed here at the foothills of Himavat.'

---
17.
śrutvā tvagnivacō gaṅgā taṁ garbhamatibhāsvaram |
utsasarja mahātējāḥ srōtōbhyō hi tadānagha ||

> **Meaning 17:** "Hearing Agni's words, the radiant Gaṅgā released that
> dazzlingly bright seed from her currents, noble Rāma.

---
18.
yadasyā nirgataṁ tasmāttaptajāmbūnadaprabham ||

> **Meaning 18:** "What poured forth from her shone with the brilliance of
> molten jāmbūnada gold—

---
19.
kāñcanaṁ dharaṇīṁ prāptaṁ hiraṇyamamalaṁ śubham |
tāmraṁ kārṣṇāyasaṁ caiva taikṣṇyādēvābhijāyata ||

> **Meaning 19:** "reaching the earth, it turned into pure, auspicious gold and
> silver, while from its fierce intensity came copper and iron.

---
20.
malaṁ tasyābhavattatra trapusīsakamēva ca |
tadētaddharaṇīṁ prāpya nānādhāturavardhata ||

> **Meaning 20:** "Its dross became tin and lead, and upon touching the earth,
> all of it multiplied into every kind of metal and mineral.

---
21.
nikṣiptamātrē garbhē tu tējōbhirabhirañjitam |
sarvaṁ parvatasannaddhaṁ sauvarṇamabhavadvanam ||

> **Meaning 21:** "The instant the seed was laid down, the entire forest [of
> śara reeds] along the mountainside was bathed in its glow and turned
> completely to gold.

---
22.
jātarūpamiti khyātaṁ tadā prabhr̥ti rāghava |
suvarṇaṁ puruṣavyāghra hutāśanasamaprabham |
tr̥ṇavr̥kṣalatāgulmaṁ sarvaṁ bhavati kāṁcanam ||

> **Meaning 22:** "From that day on, Rāghava, gold—radiant as fire—has been
> known as jātarūpa, and every blade of grass, tree, creeper, and bush there
> turned to gold.

---
23.
taṁ kumāraṁ tatō jātaṁ sēndrāḥ sahamarudgaṇāḥ |
kṣīrasambhāvanārthāya kr̥ttikāḥ samayōjayan ||

> **Meaning 23:** "Once the boy was born, Indra and the ranks of Maruts
> appointed the Kṛttikās to nurse him with their milk.

---
24.
tāḥ kṣīraṁ jātamātrasya kr̥tvā samayamuttamam |
daduḥ putrō'yamasmākaṁ sarvāsāmiti niścitāḥ ||

> **Meaning 24:** "Making a solemn pact among themselves, they nursed the
> newborn, declaring with one mind, 'He is the son of us all.'

---
25.
tatastu dēvatāḥ sarvāḥ kārtikēya iti bruvan |
putrastrailōkya vikhyātō bhaviṣyati na saṁśayaḥ ||

> **Meaning 25:** "Then all the devatās named him Kārtikeya [son of the
> Kṛttikās] and declared, 'This son will be famous across all three worlds,
> without a doubt.'

---
26.
tēṣāṁ tadvacanaṁ śrutvā skannaṁ garbhaparisravē |
snāpayanparayā lakṣmyā dīpyamānamivānalam ||

> **Meaning 26:** "Hearing those words of the devatās, the Kṛttikās bathed the
> boy—who had poured forth in that radiant stream, shining with supreme beauty
> like a blazing fire.

---
27.
skanda ityabruvandēvāḥ skannaṁ garbhaparisravāt |
kārtikēyaṁ mahābhāgaṁ kākutsthajvalanōpamam ||

> **Meaning 27:** "Because he had slipped forth [from Gaṅgā] in that flowing
> stream, the devatās also called that illustrious Kārtikeya—bright as fire—by
> the name Skanda, Kākutstha.

---
28.
prādurbhūtaṁ tataḥ kṣīraṁ kr̥ttikānāmanuttamam |
ṣaṇṇāṁ ṣaḍānanō bhūtvā jagrāha stanajaṁ payaḥ ||

> **Meaning 28:** "Milk then welled up abundantly in the six Kṛttikās, and
> taking on six faces, Kārtikeya drank from all six of them at once.

---
29.
gr̥hītvā kṣīramēkāhnā sukumāra vapustadā |
ajayatsvēna vīryēṇa daityasainyagaṇānvibhuḥ ||

> **Meaning 29:** "He drank their milk for only a single day. Even while still
> tender in form, the mighty youth defeated the armies of the Daityas by his own
> prowess.

---
30.
surasēnāgaṇapatiṁ tatastamamaladyutim |
abhyaṣiñcansuragaṇāḥ samētyāgnipurōgamāḥ ||

> **Meaning 30:** "Then the ranks of devatās, led by Agni, gathered together and
> consecrated the spotlessly radiant Kārtikeya as Devasenāpati, commander of
> the celestial armies.

---
31.
ēṣa tē rāma gaṅgāyā vistarō'bhihitō mayā |
kumārasambhavaścaiva dhanyaḥ puṇyastathaiva ca ||

> **Meaning 31:** "I have now told you, Rāma, the full story of Gaṅgā and the
> birth of Kumāra (Kārtikeya)—a sacred account that bestows blessings and
> purifies the heart.

---
32.
bhaktaśca yaḥ kārtikeyey kākutstha bhuvi mānavaḥ |
āyuṣmān putrapautraiśca skandasālokyatāṃ vrajet ||

> **Meaning 32:** "Rāma, any person on earth who is devoted to Kārtikeya will
> enjoy a long life with sons and grandsons, and in the end attain the realm of
> Skanda."

---
