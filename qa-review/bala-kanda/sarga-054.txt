=== Sarga 54 ===

---
1.
tatas tān ākulān dr̥ṣṭvā viśvāmitrāstramohitān |
vasiṣṭhaś codayām āsa kāmadhuk sr̥ja yogataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tasyā humbhāravāj jātāḥ kāmbojā ravisaṁnibhāḥ |
ūdhasas tv atha saṁjātāḥ pahlavāḥ śastrapāṇayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
yonideśāc ca yavanaḥ śakr̥ddeśāc chakās tathā |
romakūpeṣu mecchāś ca harītāḥ sakirātakāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tais tan niṣūditaṁ sainyaṁ viśvamitrasya tatkṣaṇāt |
sapadātigajaṁ sāśvaṁ sarathaṁ raghunandana ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
dr̥ṣṭvā niṣūditaṁ sainyaṁ vasiṣṭhena mahātmanā |
viśvāmitrasutānāṁ tu śataṁ nānāvidhāyudham ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
abhyadhāvat susaṁkruddhaṁ vasiṣṭhaṁ japatāṁ varam |
huṁkāreṇaiva tān sarvān nirdadāha mahān r̥ṣiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
te sāśvarathapādātā vasiṣṭhena mahātmanā |
bhasmīkr̥tā muhūrtena viśvāmitrasutās tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
dr̥ṣṭvā vināśitān putrān balaṁ ca sumahāyaśāḥ |
savrīḍaś cintayāviṣṭo viśvāmitro ’bhavat tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
saṁdura iva nirvego bhagnadaṁṣṭra ivoragaḥ |
uparakta ivādityaḥ sadyo niṣprabhatāṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
hataputrabalo dīno lūnapakṣa iva dvijaḥ |
hatadarpo hatotsāho nirvedaṁ samapadyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sa putram ekaṁ rājyāya pālayeti niyujya ca |
pr̥thivīṁ kṣatradharmeṇa vanam evānvapadyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sa gatvā himavatpārśvaṁ kiṁnaroragasevitam |
mahādevaprasādārthaṁ tapas tepe mahātapāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
kena cit tv atha kālena deveśo vr̥ṣabhadhvajaḥ |
darśayām āsa varado viśvāmitraṁ mahāmunim ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
kimarthaṁ tapyase rājan brūhi yat te vivakṣitam |
varado ’smi varo yas te kāṅkṣitaḥ so ’bhidhīyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
evam uktas tu devena viśvāmitro mahātapāḥ |
praṇipatya mahādevam idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
yadi tuṣṭo mahādeva dhanurvedo mamānagha |
sāṅgopāṅgopaniṣadaḥ sarahasyaḥ pradīyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
yāni deveṣu cāstrāṇi dānaveṣu maharṣiṣu |
gandharvayakṣarakṣaḥsu pratibhāntu mamānagha ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tava prasādād bhavatu devadeva mamepsitam |
evam astv iti deveśo vākyam uktvā divaṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
prāpya cāstrāṇi rājarṣir viśvāmitro mahābalaḥ |
darpeṇa mahatā yukto darpapūrṇo ’bhavat tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
vivardhamāno vīryeṇa samudra iva parvaṇi |
hatam eva tadā mene vasiṣṭham r̥ṣisattamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tato gatvāśramapadaṁ mumocāstrāṇi pārthivaḥ |
yais tat tapovanaṁ sarvaṁ nirdagdhaṁ cāstratejasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
udīryamāṇam astraṁ tad viśvāmitrasya dhīmataḥ |
dr̥ṣṭvā vipradrutā bhītā munayaḥ śataśo diśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
vasiṣṭhasya ca ye śiṣyās tathaiva mr̥gapakṣiṇaḥ |
vidravanti bhayād bhītā nānādigbhyaḥ sahasraśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
vasiṣṭhasyāśramapadaṁ śūnyam āsīn mahātmanaḥ |
muhūrtam iva niḥśabdam āsīd īriṇasaṁnibham ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
vadato vai vasiṣṭhasya mā bhaiṣṭeti muhur muhuḥ |
nāśayāmy adya gādheyaṁ nīhāram iva bhāskaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
evam uktvā mahātejā vasiṣṭho japatāṁ varaḥ |
viśvāmitraṁ tadā vākyaṁ saroṣam idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
āśramaṁ cirasaṁvr̥ddhaṁ yad vināśitavān asi |
durācāro ’si yan mūḍha tasmāt tvaṁ na bhaviṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
ity uktvā paramakruddho daṇḍam udyamya satvaraḥ |
vidhūma iva kālāgnir yamadaṇḍam ivāparam ||

=== meaning ===

---
