=== Sarga 32 ===
The Lineage of Kuśa; Vāyu Curses Kuśanābha's Hundred Daughters

---
1.
brahmayōnirmahānāsītkuśō nāma mahātapāḥ |
akliṣṭavratadharmajñaḥ sajjanapratipūjakaḥ ||

> **Meaning 1:** "There was once a mighty, deeply austere soul named Kuśa, born
> of Brahmā, who knew dharma, kept his vows unbroken, and always honored the
> virtuous.

---
2.
sa mahātmā kulīnāyāṁ yuktāyāṁ sumahābalān |
vaidarbhyāṁ janayāmāsa caturaḥ sadr̥śānsutān ||

> **Meaning 2:** "With his noble-born and worthy wife, the princess of Vidarbha,
> that great-souled king fathered four mighty sons equal to himself:

---
3.
kuśāmbaṁ kuśanābhaṁ ca ādhūrtarajasaṁ vasum |
dīptiyuktānmahōtsāhānkṣatradharmacikīrṣayā ||

> **Meaning 3:** "Kuśāmba, Kuśanābha, Ādhūrtarajasa, and Vasu, all of them
> radiant, full of energy, and eager to uphold kṣatriya-dharma.

---
4.
tānuvāca kuśaḥ putrāndharmiṣṭhānsatyavādinaḥ |
kriyatāṁ pālanaṁ putrā dharmaṁ prāpsyatha puṣkalam ||

> **Meaning 4:** "To those righteous and truthful sons, Kuśa said, 'My sons,
> devote yourselves to protecting the people; through that you will attain
> abundant dharma.'

---
5.
kuśasya vacanaṁ śrutvā catvārō lōkasammatāḥ |
nivēśaṁ cakrirē sarvē purāṇāṁ nr̥varāstadā ||

> **Meaning 5:** "Hearing Kuśa's words, all four of those finest kings,
> respected throughout the world, each founded a city of his own.

---
6.
kuśāmbastu mahātējāḥ kauśāmbīmakarōtpurīm |
kuśanābhastu dharmātmā paraṁ cakrē mahōdayam ||

> **Meaning 6:** "The radiant Kuśāmba built the city of Kauśāmbī, while the
> righteous Kuśanābha founded the splendid city of Mahōdaya.

---
7.
ādhūrtarajasō rāma dharmāraṇyaṁ mahīpatiḥ |
cakrē puravaraṁ rājā vasuścakrē girivrajam ||

> **Meaning 7:** "Rāma, King Ādhūrtarajasa built the great city of Dharmāraṇya,
> and Vasu founded Girivraja.

---
8.
ēṣā vasumatī rāma vasōstasya mahātmanaḥ |
ētē śailavarāḥ pañca prakāśantē samantataḥ ||

> **Meaning 8:** "Rāma, this rich land belongs to that great-souled Vasu. See
> how these five great mountains shine all around!

---
9.
sumāgadhī nadī ramyā māgadhānviśrutāyayau |
pañcānāṁ śailamukhyānāṁ madhyē mālēva śōbhatē ||

> **Meaning 9:** "The beautiful, renowned river Sumāgadhī flows through
> Magadha, gleaming like a garland amidst those five great peaks.

---
10.
saiṣā hi māgadhī rāma vasōstasya mahātmanaḥ |
pūrvābhicaritā rāma sukṣētrā sasyamālinī ||

> **Meaning 10:** "Rāma, this very river Māgadhī, the Śoṇā, belongs to that
> great-souled Vasu, flowing eastward through fertile fields wreathed in crops.

---
11.
kuśanābhastu rājarṣiḥ kanyāśatamanuttamam |
janayāmāsa dharmātmā ghr̥tācyāṁ raghunandana ||

> **Meaning 11:** "Raghunandana, meanwhile the righteous King Kuśanābha, wise as
> a sage, fathered a hundred peerless daughters with Ghr̥tācī.

---
12.
tāstu yauvanaśālinyō rūpavatyaḥ svalaṅkr̥tāḥ |
udyānabhūmimāgamya prāvr̥ṣīva śatahradāḥ ||

> **Meaning 12:** "In the full bloom of youth, beautiful and finely adorned,
> those daughters entered the royal garden like flashes of lightning against
> monsoon clouds.

---
13.
gāyantyō nr̥tyamānāśca vādayantyaśca rāghava |
āmōdaṁ paramaṁ jagmurvarābharaṇabhūṣitāḥ ||

> **Meaning 13:** "Rāghava, decked in exquisite ornaments, they sang, danced,
> and played music, lost in pure delight.

---
14.
atha tāścārusarvāṅgyō rūpēṇāpratimā bhuvi |
udyānabhūmimāgamya tārā iva ghanāntarē ||

> **Meaning 14:** "Radiant in every way and unmatched in beauty on earth, they
> shone in that garden like stars emerging between the clouds.

---
15.
tāḥ sarvaguṇasampannā rūpayauvanasaṁyutāḥ |
dr̥ṣṭvā sarvātmakō vāyuridaṁ vacanamabravīt ||

> **Meaning 15:** "Seeing them blessed with every virtue, beauty, and youth, the
> all-pervading Vāyu spoke to them:

---
16.
ahaṁ vaḥ kāmayē sarvā bhāryā mama bhaviṣyatha |
mānuṣastyajyatāṁ bhāvō dīrghamāyuravāpsyatha ||

> **Meaning 16:** "'I desire all of you, so become my wives! Leave behind your
> human state, and you will gain long life.

---
17.
calaṁ hi yauvanaṁ nityaṁ mānuṣēṣu viśēṣataḥ |
akṣayyaṁ yauvanaṁ prāptā amaryaśca bhaviṣyatha ||

> **Meaning 17:** "'Youth is always fleeting, especially among humans; with me,
> you will enjoy unending youth and become immortal.'

---
18.
tasya tadvacanaṁ śrutvā vāyōrakliṣṭakarmaṇaḥ |
apahāsya tatō vākyaṁ kanyāśatamathābravīt ||

> **Meaning 18:** "Hearing those words of Vāyu, whose power of movement is
> unhindered, the hundred daughters dismissed his words with a laugh and
> answered:

---
19.
antaścarasi bhūtānāṁ sarvēṣāṁ tvaṁ surōttama |
prabhāvajñāśca tē sarvāḥ kimasmānavamanyasē ||

> **Meaning 19:** "'Greatest of devatās, you move within all beings, and we all
> know your power. Why then do you treat us with such disrespect?

---
20.
kuśanābhasutāḥ sarvāḥ samarthāstvāṁ surōttama |
sthānāccyāvayituṁ dēvaṁ rakṣāmastu tapō vayam ||

> **Meaning 20:** "'As Kuśanābha's daughters, we all have the power to bring
> even a devatā like you down from your station, yet we hold back to guard our
> austerity.

---
21.
mā bhūtsa kālō durmēdhaḥ pitaraṁ satyavādinam |
nāvamanyasva dharmēṇa svayaṁ varamupāsmahē ||

> **Meaning 21:** "'Foolish one, may such a time never come! Do not disrespect
> our truthful father, for we abide by dharma in awaiting our husbands.

---
22.
pitā hi prabhurasmākaṁ daivataṁ paramaṁ hi saḥ |
yasya nō dāsyati pitā sa nō bhartā bhaviṣyati ||

> **Meaning 22:** "'Our father is our rightful authority and our highest devatā;
> whoever he gives us to will be our husband.'

---
23.
tāsāṁ tadvacanaṁ śrutvā vāyuḥ paramakōpanaḥ |
praviśya sarvagātrāṇi babhañja bhagavānprabhuḥ ||

> **Meaning 23:** "Hearing their words, the mighty Vāyu flew into a terrible
> rage; entering their bodies, he twisted and bent their forms.

---
24.
tāḥ kanyā vāyunā bhagnā viviśurnr̥patērgr̥ham |
prāpatanbhuvi saṁbhrāntāḥ salajjāḥ sāśrulōcanāḥ ||

> **Meaning 24:** "Bent and crippled by Vāyu, those daughters went into the
> king's palace and fell to the ground, deeply shaken, ashamed, and with eyes
> brimming with tears.

---
25.
sa ca tā dayitā bhagnāḥ kanyāḥ paramaśōbhanāḥ |
dr̥ṣṭvā bhagnāstadā rājā sambhrānta idamabravīt ||

> **Meaning 25:** "Seeing his beloved, once-radiant daughters thus bent and
> crippled, the king was deeply alarmed and cried out:

---
26.
kimidaṁ kathyatāṁ putryaḥ kō dharmamavamanyatē |
kubjāḥ kēna kr̥tāḥ sarvā vēṣṭantyō nābhibhāṣatha ||
ēvaṁ rājā viniḥśvasya samādhiṁ sandadhē tataḥ ||

> **Meaning 26:** "'My daughters, what is this? Tell me, who has disregarded
> dharma? Who has left you all bent and hunchbacked? Why do you writhe and stay
> silent?' Sighing heavily, the king then steadied his mind in deep
> concentration."

---
