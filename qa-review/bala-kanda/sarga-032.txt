=== Sarga 32 ===

---
1.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā kuśanābhasya dhīmataḥ |
śirobhiś caraṇau spr̥ṣṭvā kanyāśatam abhāṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
vāyuḥ sarvātmako rājan pradharṣayitum icchati |
aśubhaṁ mārgam āsthāya na dharmaṁ pratyavekṣate ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
pitr̥matyaḥ sma bhadraṁ te svacchande na vayaṁ sthitāḥ |
pitaraṁ no vr̥ṇīṣva tvaṁ yadi no dāsyate tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tena pāpānubandhena vacanaṁ na pratīcchatā |
evaṁ bruvantyaḥ sarvāḥ sma vāyunā nihatā bhr̥ṣam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tāsāṁ tad vacanaṁ śrutvā rājā paramadhārmikaḥ |
pratyuvāca mahātejāḥ kanyāśatam anuttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
kṣāntaṁ kṣamāvatāṁ putryaḥ kartavyaṁ sumahat kr̥tam |
aikamatyam upāgamya kulaṁ cāvekṣitaṁ mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
alaṁkāro hi nārīṇāṁ kṣamā tu puruṣasya vā |
duṣkaraṁ tac ca vaḥ kṣāntaṁ tridaśeṣu viśeṣataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
yādr̥śīr vaḥ kṣamā putryaḥ sarvāsām aviśeṣataḥ |
kṣamā dānaṁ kṣamā yajñaḥ kṣamā satyaṁ ca putrikāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
kṣamā yaśaḥ kṣamā dharmaḥ kṣamāyāṁ viṣṭhitaṁ jagat |
visr̥jya kanyāḥ kākutstha rājā tridaśavikramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
mantrajño mantrayām āsa pradānaṁ saha mantribhiḥ |
deśe kāle pradānasya sadr̥śe pratipādanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
etasminn eva kāle tu cūlī nāma mahāmuniḥ |
ūrdhvaretāḥ śubhācāro brāhmaṁ tapa upāgamat ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tapyantaṁ tam r̥ṣiṁ tatra gandharvī paryupāsate |
somadā nāma bhadraṁ te ūrmilā tanayā tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sā ca taṁ praṇatā bhūtvā śuśrūṣaṇaparāyaṇā |
uvāsa kāle dharmiṣṭhā tasyās tuṣṭo ’bhavad guruḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sa ca tāṁ kālayogena provāca raghunandana |
parituṣṭo ’smi bhadraṁ te kiṁ karomi tava priyam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
parituṣṭaṁ muniṁ jñātvā gandharvī madhurasvaram |
uvāca paramaprītā vākyajñā vākyakovidam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
lakṣmyā samudito brāhmyā brahmabhūto mahātapāḥ |
brāhmeṇa tapasā yuktaṁ putram icchāmi dhārmikam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
apatiś cāsmi bhadraṁ te bhāryā cāsmi na kasya cit |
brāhmeṇopagatāyāś ca dātum arhasi me sutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tasyāḥ prasanno brahmarṣir dadau putram anuttamam |
brahmadatta iti khyātaṁ mānasaṁ cūlinaḥ sutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
sa rājā brahmadattas tu purīm adhyavasat tadā |
kāmpilyāṁ parayā lakṣmyā devarājo yathā divam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
sa buddhiṁ kr̥tavān rājā kuśanābhaḥ sudhārmikaḥ |
brahmadattāya kākutstha dātuṁ kanyāśataṁ tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tam āhūya mahātejā brahmadattaṁ mahīpatiḥ |
dadau kanyāśataṁ rājā suprītenāntarātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
yathākramaṁ tataḥ pāṇiṁ jagrāha raghunandana |
brahmadatto mahī pālas tāsāṁ devapatir yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
spr̥ṣṭamātre tataḥ pāṇau vikubjā vigatajvarāḥ |
yuktāḥ paramayā lakṣmyā babhuḥ kanyāśataṁ tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
sa dr̥ṣṭvā vāyunā muktāḥ kuśanābho mahīpatiḥ |
babhūva paramaprīto harṣaṁ lebhe punaḥ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
kr̥todvāhaṁ tu rājānaṁ brahmadattaṁ mahīpatiḥ |
sadāraṁ preṣayām āsa sopādhyāya gaṇaṁ tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
somadāpi susaṁhr̥ṣṭā putrasya sadr̥śīṁ kriyām |
yathānyāyaṁ ca gandharvī snuṣās tāḥ pratyanandata ||

=== meaning ===

---
