=== Sarga 21 ===
Vasiṣṭha Counsels Daśaratha; Daśaratha Agrees to Send Rāma

---
1.
tacchrutvā vacanaṁ tasya snēhaparyākulākṣaram |
samanyuḥ kauśikō vākyaṁ pratyuvaca mahīpatim ||

> **Meaning 1:** Hearing those words, choked with fatherly affection, Viśvāmitra
> grew angry and answered the king.

---
2.
pūrhvamarthaṁ pratiśrutya pratijñāṁ hātumicchasi |
rāgavāṇāmayuktō'yaṁ kulasyāsya viparyayaḥ ||

> **Meaning 2:** Having first promised to grant my request, you now wish to
> abandon your vow. Such a reversal is unworthy of the Raghu lineage.

---
3.
yadidaṁ tē kṣamaṁ rājangamiṣyāmi yathāgatam |
mithyāpratijñaḥ kākutstha sukhī bhava sabāndhavaḥ ||

> **Meaning 3:** King, if this seems right to you, I will go back the way I
> came. Kākutstha, live happily with your kinsmen as a man who broke his word!

---
4.
tasya rōṣaparītasya viśvāmitrasya dhīmataḥ |
cacāla vasudhā kr̥tsnā vivēśa ca bhayaṁ surān ||

> **Meaning 4:** As the wise Viśvāmitra was gripped by wrath, the entire earth
> trembled, and fear seized the devatās.

---
5.
trastarūpaṁ tu vijñāya jagatsarvaṁ mahānr̥ṣiḥ |
nr̥patiṁ suvratō dhīrō vasiṣṭhō vākyamabravīt ||

> **Meaning 5:** Seeing the whole world struck with terror, the great sage
> Vasiṣṭha, steadfast in his vows and calm in mind, spoke to the king.

---
6.
ikṣvākūṇāṁ kulē jātaḥ sākṣāddharma ivāparaḥ |
dhr̥timānsuvrataḥ śrīmānna dharmaṁ hātumarhasi ||

> **Meaning 6:** Born in the Ikṣvāku line, you are like dharma itself in person
> — resolute, faithful to your vows, and illustrious. You must not abandon
> dharma.

---
7.
triṣu lōkēṣu vikhyātō dharmātmā iti rāghavaḥ |
svadharmaṁ pratipadyasva nādharmaṁ vōḍhumarhasi ||

> **Meaning 7:** Across all three worlds, a descendant of Raghu is renowned as a
> soul of dharma. Uphold your own dharma — you must not take on adharma.

---
8.
saṁśrutyaivaṁ kariṣyāmītyakurvāṇasya rāghava |
iṣṭāpūrtavadhō bhūyāttasmādrāmaṁ visarjaya ||

> **Meaning 8:** Rāghava, if you promise, "I will do this," and then fail to
> keep your word, all the merit of your yajñas and charitable deeds will be
> lost. Therefore, send Rāma with him.

---
9.
kr̥tāstramakr̥tāstraṁ vā nainaṁ śakṣyanti rākṣasāḥ |
guptaṁ kuśikaputrēṇa jvalanēnāmr̥taṁ yathā ||

> **Meaning 9:** Whether Rāma has mastered astras or not, the Rākṣasas cannot
> harm him while he is protected by Viśvāmitra, the son of Kuśika — just as
> amr̥ta surrounded by blazing fire cannot be touched.

---
10.
ēṣa vigrahavāndharma ēṣa vīryavatāṁ varaḥ |
ēṣa buddhyādhikō lōkē tapasaśca parāyaṇam ||

> **Meaning 10:** He is dharma embodied; he is the foremost among the mighty. He
> surpasses the world in wisdom, and he is the supreme refuge of tapas.

---
11.
ēṣō'strānvividhānvētti trailōkyē sacarācarē |
nainamanyaḥ pumānvētti na ca vētsyanti kēcana ||

> **Meaning 11:** He knows every kind of astra across the three worlds and all
> creation, animate and inanimate. No one else knows them as he does, nor will
> anyone ever know them.

---
12.
na dēvā narṣayaḥ kēcinnāsurā na ca rākṣasāḥ |
gandharvayakṣapravarāḥ sakinnaramahōragāḥ ||

> **Meaning 12:** Neither the devatās, nor any sages, nor Asuras, nor Rākṣasas,
> nor the foremost Gandharvas and Yakṣas, nor Kinnaras and great Nāgas know
> those astras.

---
13.
sarvāstrāṇi kr̥śāśvasya putrāḥ paramadhārmikāḥ |
kauśikāya purā dattā yadā rājyaṁ praśāsati ||

> **Meaning 13:** All those astras are the supremely righteous sons of
> Kr̥śāśva, existing in the form of astras; they were given to Viśvāmitra
> (Kauśika) long ago, when Viśvāmitra was ruling his kingdom.

---
14.
tē'pi putrāḥ kr̥śāśvasya prajāpatisutāsutāḥ |
naikarūpā mahāvīryā dīptimantō jayāvahāḥ ||

> **Meaning 14:** Those sons of Kr̥śāśva were born to the daughters of
> Prajāpati; they take many forms, possess immense power, shine with radiance,
> and always bring victory.

---
15.
jayā ca suprabhā caiva dakṣakanyē sumadhyamē |
tē suvātē'straśastrāṇi śataṁ paramabhāsvaram ||

> **Meaning 15:** Graceful Jayā and Suprabhā, the daughters of Dakṣa, gave
> birth to those hundred supremely radiant astras and weapons.

---
16.
pañcāśataṁ sutām̐llēbhē jayā nāma varānpurā |
vadhāyāsurasainyānāmamēyānkāmarūpiṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 16:** Long ago, Jayā bore fifty extraordinary sons — immeasurable
> in power and able to take any form — for the destruction of the Asura
> armies.

---
17.
suprabhājanayaccāpi putrānpañcāśataṁ punaḥ |
saṁhārānnāma durdharṣāndurākrāmānbalīyasaḥ ||

> **Meaning 17:** And Suprabhā gave birth to another fifty sons, known as the
> Saṁhāras — invincible, unassailable, and immensely strong.

---
18.
tāni cāstrāṇi vēttyēṣa yathāvatkuśikātmajaḥ |
apūrvāṇāṁ ca jananē śaktō bhūyaśca dharmavit ||

> **Meaning 18:** Viśvāmitra, the son of Kuśika, knows all those astras
> thoroughly; and, steadfast in dharma, he even has the power to create new
> ones never seen before.

---
19.
ēvaṁ vīryō mahātējā viśvāmitrō mahātapāḥ |
na rāmagamanē rājansaṁśayaṁ gantumarhasi ||

> **Meaning 19:** Such is the power, brilliance, and great tapas of Viśvāmitra.
> King, you should harbor no doubt about letting Rāma go.

---
20.
tēṣāṁ nigrahaṇē śaktaḥ svayaṁ ca kuśikātmajaḥ |
tava putrahitārthāya tvāmupētyābhiyācatē ||

> **Meaning 20:** The son of Kuśika is fully capable of subduing those Rākṣasas
> himself; he has come to you and asked for Rāma only for your son's own good.

---
21.
iti munivacanātprasannacittō
raghuvr̥ṣabhaśca mumōda pārthivāgryaḥ |
gamanamabhirurōca rāghavasya
prathitayaśāḥ kuśikātmajāya buddhyā ||

> **Meaning 21:** Reassured by Vasiṣṭha's words, the famed King Daśaratha,
> foremost among the Raghus, grew glad at heart and willingly agreed to send
> Rāma with Viśvāmitra, the son of Kuśika.

---
