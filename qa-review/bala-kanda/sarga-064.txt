=== Sarga 64 ===

---
1.
atha haimavatīṁ rāma diśaṁ tyaktvā mahāmuniḥ |
pūrvāṁ diśam anuprāpya tapas tepe sudāruṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
maunaṁ varṣasahasrasya kr̥tvā vratam anuttamam |
cakārāpratimaṁ rāma tapaḥ paramaduṣkaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
pūrṇe varṣasahasre tu kāṣṭhabhūtaṁ mahāmunim |
vighnair bahubhir ādhūtaṁ krodho nāntaram āviśat ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tato devāḥ sagandharvāḥ pannagāsurarākṣasāḥ |
mohitās tejasā tasya tapasā mandaraśmayaḥ |
kaśmalopahatāḥ sarve pitāmaham athābruvan ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
bahubhiḥ kāraṇair deva viśvāmitro mahāmuniḥ |
lobhitaḥ krodhitaś caiva tapasā cābhivardhate ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
na hy asya vr̥jinaṁ kiṁ cid dr̥śyate sūkṣmam apy atha |
na dīyate yadi tv asya manasā yad abhīpsitam |
vināśayati trailokyaṁ tapasā sacarācaram |
vyākulāś ca diśaḥ sarvā na ca kiṁ cit prakāśate ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sāgarāḥ kṣubhitāḥ sarve viśīryante ca parvatāḥ |
prakampate ca pr̥thivī vāyur vāti bhr̥śākulaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
buddhiṁ na kurute yāvan nāśe deva mahāmuniḥ |
tāvat prasādyo bhagavān agnirūpo mahādyutiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
kālāgninā yathā pūrvaṁ trailokyaṁ dahyate ’khilam |
devarājye cikīrṣeta dīyatām asya yan matam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tataḥ suragaṇāḥ sarve pitāmahapurogamāḥ |
viśvāmitraṁ mahātmānaṁ vākyaṁ madhuram abruvan ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
brahmarṣe svāgataṁ te ’stu tapasā sma sutoṣitāḥ |
brāhmaṇyaṁ tapasogreṇa prāptavān asi kauśika ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
dīrgham āyuś ca te brahman dadāmi samarudgaṇaḥ |
svasti prāpnuhi bhadraṁ te gaccha saumya yathāsukham ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
pitāmahavacaḥ śrutvā sarveṣāṁ ca divaukasām |
kr̥tvā praṇāmaṁ mudito vyājahāra mahāmuniḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
brāhmaṇyaṁ yadi me prāptaṁ dīrgham āyus tathaiva ca |
oṁkāro ’tha vaṣaṭkāro vedāś ca varayantu mām ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
kṣatravedavidāṁ śreṣṭho brahmavedavidām api |
brahmaputro vasiṣṭho mām evaṁ vadatu devatāḥ |
yady ayaṁ paramaḥ kāmaḥ kr̥to yāntu surarṣabhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tataḥ prasādito devair vasiṣṭho japatāṁ varaḥ |
sakhyaṁ cakāra brahmarṣir evam astv iti cābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
brahmarṣitvaṁ na saṁdehaḥ sarvaṁ saṁpatsyate tava |
ity uktvā devatāś cāpi sarvā jagmur yathāgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
viśvāmitro ’pi dharmātmā labdhvā brāhmaṇyam uttamam |
pūjayām āsa brahmarṣiṁ vasiṣṭhaṁ japatāṁ varam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
kr̥takāmo mahīṁ sarvāṁ cacāra tapasi sthitaḥ |
evaṁ tv anena brāhmaṇyaṁ prāptaṁ rāma mahātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
eṣa rāma muniśreṣṭha eṣa vigrahavāṁs tapaḥ |
eṣa dharmaḥ paro nityaṁ vīryasyaiṣa parāyaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
śatānandavacaḥ śrutvā rāmalakṣmaṇasaṁnidhau |
janakaḥ prāñjalir vākyam uvāca kuśikātmajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
dhanyo ’smy anugr̥hīto ’smi yasya me munipuṁgava |
yajñaṁ kākutstha sahitaḥ prāptavān asi dhārmika ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
pāvito ’haṁ tvayā brahman darśanena mahāmune |
guṇā bahuvidhāḥ prāptās tava saṁdarśanān mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
vistareṇa ca te brahman kīrtyamānaṁ mahat tapaḥ |
śrutaṁ mayā mahātejo rāmeṇa ca mahātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
sadasyaiḥ prāpya ca sadaḥ śrutās te bahavo guṇāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
aprameyaṁ tapas tubhyam aprameyaṁ ca te balam |
aprameyā guṇāś caiva nityaṁ te kuśikātmaja ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tr̥ptir āścaryabhūtānāṁ kathānāṁ nāsti me vibho |
karmakālo muniśreṣṭha lambate ravimaṇḍalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
śvaḥ prabhāte mahātejo draṣṭum arhasi māṁ punaḥ |
svāgataṁ tapasāṁ śreṣṭha mām anujñātum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
evam uktvā muniśreṣṭhaṁ vaideho mithilādhipaḥ |
pradakṣiṇaṁ cakārāśu sopādhyāyaḥ sabāndhavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
viśvāmitro ’pi dharmātmā saharāmaḥ salakṣmaṇaḥ |
svaṁ vāṭam abhicakrāma pūjyamāno maharṣibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
