=== Sarga 27 ===
Viśvāmitra Imparts the Divine Astras to Rāma

---
1.
atha tāṁ rajanīmuṣya viśvāmirō mahāyaśāḥ |
prahasya rāghavaṁ vākyamuvāca madhurākṣaram ||

> **Meaning 1:** Having spent that night there, the illustrious Viśvāmitra
> smiled and spoke these sweet-toned words to Rāghava:

---
2.
patituṣṭō'smi bhadraṁ tē rājaputra mahāyaśaḥ |
prītyā paramayā yuktō dadāmyastrāṇi sarvaśaḥ ||

> **Meaning 2:** "Renowned prince, I am deeply pleased with you; may all
> blessings be yours! Filled with the highest affection, I now give you all
> these astras,

---
3.
dēvāsuragaṇānvāpi sagandharvōragānapi |
yairamitrānprasahyājau vaśīkr̥tya jayiṣyasi ||

> **Meaning 3:** "with which you will overpower and conquer any enemies in
> battle, even the ranks of devatās, asuras, gandharvas, and nāgas.

---
4.
tāni divyāni bhadraṁ tē dadāmyastrāṇi sarvaśaḥ |
daṇḍacakraṁ mahaddivyaṁ tava dāsyāmi rāghava ||

5.
dharmacakraṁ tatō vīra kālacakraṁ tathaiva ca |
viṣṇucakraṁ tathātyugramaindraṁ cakraṁ tathaiva ca ||

> **Meaning 4-5:** "Blessings on you, I give you all those divine astras.
> Rāghava, I will give you the great divine Daṇḍacakra, and then, brave one, the
> Dharmacakra, the Kālacakra, the fierce Viṣṇucakra, and the Aindra Cakra.

---
6.
vajramastraṁ naraśrēṣṭha śaivaṁ śūlavaraṁ tathā |
astraṁ brahmaśiraścaiva aiṣīkamapi rāghava ||

> **Meaning 6:** "Rāghava, foremost of men, I will also give you the Vajra
> astra, Śiva's supreme trident, the Brahmaśiras astra, and the Aiṣīka astra.

---
7.
dadāmi tē mahābāhō brāhmamastramanuttamam |
gadē dvē caiva kākutstha mōdakī śikharī ubhē ||

> **Meaning 7:** "Mighty-armed Kākutstha, I give you the supreme Brāhma astra,
> and also the two maces, Modakī and Śikharī.

---
8.
pradīptē naraśārdūla prayacchāmi nr̥pātmaja |
dharmapāśamahaṁ rāma kālapāśaṁ tathaiva ca ||

9.
pāśam vāruṇamastraṁ ca dadānyahamanuttamam |
aśanī dvē prayacchāmi śuṣkārdrē raghunandana ||

> **Meaning 8-9:** "Rāma, foremost of men, I give you those two blazing maces,
> along with the Dharmapāśa, the Kālapāśa, and the supreme Vāruṇa-pāśa astra.
> Raghunandana, I also give you the two thunderbolts, Śuṣkā and Ārdrā.

---
10.
dadāmi cāstraṁ painākamastraṁ nārāyaṇaṁ tathā |
āgnēyamastra dayitaṁ śikharaṁ nāma nāmataḥ ||

11.
vāyavyaṁ prathamaṁ nāma dadāmi tava rāghava |
astraṁ hayaśirō nāma krauñcamastraṁ tathaiva ca |
śakti dvayaṁ ca kākutstha dadāmi tava cānagha ||

> **Meaning 10-11:** "I give you the Paināka astra, the Nārāyaṇa astra, and the
> beloved Āgneya astra named Śikhara. Rāghava, I also give you the Vāyavya
> astra called Prathama, the Hayaśiras astra, the Krauñca astra, and the two
> Śaktis, noble Kākutstha.

---
12.
kaṅkālaṁ musalaṁ ghōraṁ kāpālamatha kaṅkaṇam |
dhārayantyasurā yāni dadāmyētāni sarvaśaḥ ||

13.
vaidyādharaṁ mahāstraṁ ca nandanaṁ nāma nāmataḥ |
asiratnaṁ mahābāhō dadāmi nr̥varātmaja ||

> **Meaning 12-13:** "I give you all those weapons wielded by the asuras: the
> Kaṅkāla, the dreadful Musala, the Kāpāla, and the Kaṅkaṇa. Mighty-armed
> prince, I also give you the great Vaidyādhara astra named Nandana, and the
> jewel among swords, Asiratna.

---
14.
gāndharvamastraṁ dayitaṁ mānavaṁ nāma nāmataḥ |
prasvāpanapraśamanē dadmi sauraṁ ca rāghava ||

15.
varṣaṇaṁ śōṣaṇaṁ caiva santāpanavilāpanē |
madanaṁ caiva durdharṣaṁ kandarpadayitaṁ tathā ||

16.
paiśācamastraṁ dayitaṁ mōhanaṁ nāma nāmataḥ |
pratīccha naraśārdūla rājaputra mahāyaśaḥ ||

> **Meaning 14-16:** "Rāghava, renowned prince and foremost of men, receive the
> beloved Gāndharva astra named Mānava, the Prasvāpana and Praśamana astras,
> the Saura astra, the Varṣaṇa, Śoṣaṇa, Santāpana, and Vilāpana astras, the
> irresistible Madana astra dear to Kandarpa, and the favorite Paiśāca astra
> named Mohana!

---
17.
tāmasaṁ naraśārdūla saumanaṁ ca mahābalam |
saṁvartaṁ caiva durdharṣaṁ mausalaṁ ca nr̥pātmaja ||

18.
satyamastraṁ mahābāhō tathā māyādharaṁ param |
ghōraṁ tējaḥprabhaṁ nāma paratējō'pakarṣaṇam ||

19.
sōmāstraṁ śiśiraṁ nāma tvāṣṭramastraṁ sudāmanam |
dāruṇaṁ ca bhagasyāpi śītēṣumatha mānavam ||

> **Meaning 17-19:** "Mighty-armed prince, receive also the Tāmasa, the mighty
> Saumana, the irresistible Saṁvarta, and the Mausala astras; the Satya astra,
> the supreme Māyādhara, and the dreadful Tejaḥprabha, which drains away the
> energy of enemies; and finally the Soma astra called Śiśira, Tvaṣṭā's astra
> called Sudāmana, the terrible astra of Bhaga, and the Śīteṣu and Mānava
> astras.

---
20.
ētānnāma mahābāhō kāmarūpānmahābalān |
gr̥hāṇa paramōdārānkṣipramēva nr̥pātmaja ||

> **Meaning 20:** "Mighty-armed prince, quickly receive these exalted,
> immensely powerful astras that can take any form at will!"

---
21.
sthitastu prāṅmukhō bhūtvā śucirnivaratastadā |
dadau rāmāya suprītō mantragrāmamanuttamam ||

> **Meaning 21:** Facing the east, ritually pure and self-controlled, the
> deeply pleased sage then imparted to Rāma that supreme set of mantras.

---
22.
sarvasaṅgrahaṇaṁ yēṣāṁ daivatairapi durlabham |
tānyastrāṇi tadā viprō rāghavāya nyavēdayat ||

> **Meaning 22:** Viśvāmitra then gave Rāghava all those astras, which are hard
> even for the devatās to master in full.

---
23.
japatastu munēstasya viśvāmitrasya dhīmataḥ |
upatasthurmahārhāṇi sarvāṇyastrāṇi rāghavam ||

> **Meaning 23:** As the wise sage Viśvāmitra chanted the mantras, all those
> precious astras appeared in person before Rāghava.

---
24.
ūcuśca muditā rāmaṁ sarvē prāñjalayastadā |
imē sma paramōdāra kiṅkarāstava rāghava ||

> **Meaning 24:** Joyful and with palms joined in reverence, they all said to
> Rāma, "Most noble Rāghava, here we are, your servants!"

---
25.
pratigr̥hya ca kākutsthaḥ samālabhya ca pāṇinā |
manasā mē bhaviṣyadhvamiti tānyabhyacōdayat ||

> **Meaning 25:** Accepting them and touching them with his hand, Rāma
> instructed them, "Come to me whenever I think of you."

---
26.
tataḥ prītamanā rāmō viśvāmitraṁ mahāmunim |
abhivādya mahātējā gamanāyōpacakramē ||

> **Meaning 26:** Then Rāma, filled with joy and glowing with splendor, bowed
> respectfully to the great sage Viśvāmitra and prepared to depart.

---
