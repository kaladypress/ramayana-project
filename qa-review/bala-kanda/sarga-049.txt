=== Sarga 49 ===
Ahalyā's Redemption

---
1.
aphalastu tataḥ śakrō dēvānagnipurōgamān |
abravīttrastavadanaḥ sarṣisaṅghānsacāraṇān ||

> **Meaning 1:** "Then the emasculated Śakra, his face full of dread, spoke to
> the devatās led by Agni, together with the hosts of r̥ṣis and Cāraṇas:

---
2.
kurvatā tapasō vighnaṁ gautamasya mahātmanaḥ |
krōdhamutpādya hi mayā surakāryamidaṁ kr̥tam ||

> **Meaning 2:** "'By disrupting the tapas of the great-souled Gautama and
> provoking his anger, I carried out this service for the suras.

---
3.
aphalō'smi kr̥tastēna krōdhātsā ca nirākr̥tā |
śāpamōkṣēṇa mahatā tapō'syāpahr̥taṁ mayā ||

> **Meaning 3:** "'In his anger he has emasculated me and cast her off as well;
> by unleashing this great curse, I have drained him of his tapas.

---
4.
tanmāṁ suravarāḥ sarvē sarṣisaṅghāḥ sacāraṇāḥ |
surasāhyakaraṁ sarvē saphalaṁ kartumarhatha ||

> **Meaning 4:** "'Therefore, foremost of the suras, assemblies of r̥ṣis, and
> Cāraṇas, all of you ought to restore my manhood, for I acted in service of the
> suras.'

---
5.
śatakratōrvacaḥ śrutvā dēvāḥ sāgnipurōgamāḥ |
pitr̥dēvānupētyāhuḥ saha sarvairmarudgaṇaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 5:** "Hearing the words of Śatakratu, the devatās led by Agni
> approached the Pitr̥devas together with all the ranks of Maruts and said:

---
6.
ayaṁ mēṣaḥ savr̥ṣaṇaḥ śakrō hyavr̥ṣaṇaḥ kr̥taḥ |
mēṣasya vr̥ṣaṇau gr̥hya śakrāyāśu prayacchata ||

> **Meaning 6:** "'Here is a male sheep with testicles, whereas Śakra has been
> left without his; take the sheep's testicles and give them quickly to Śakra.

---
7.
aphalastu kr̥tō mēṣaḥ parāṁ tuṣṭiṁ pradāsyati |
bhavatāṁ harṣaṇārthāya yē ca dāsyanti mānavāḥ ||

> **Meaning 7:** "'A castrated male sheep will bring you the highest
> satisfaction, and you will bestow rewards upon the humans who offer them to
> delight you.'

---
8.
agnēstu vacanaṁ śrutvā pitr̥dēvāḥ samāgatāḥ |
utpāṭya mēṣavr̥ṣaṇau sahasrākṣē nyavēdayan ||

> **Meaning 8:** "Hearing Agni's words, the assembled Pitr̥devas pulled out the
> male sheep's testicles and attached them to the thousand-eyed Indra.

---
9.
tadā prabhr̥ti kākutstha pitr̥dēvāḥ samāgatāḥ |
aphalānbhuñjatē mēṣānphalaistēṣāmayōjayan ||

> **Meaning 9:** "Kākutstha, from that time onward the assembled Pitr̥devas
> partake of castrated male sheep and reward those who offer them with the
> fruits of their offerings.

---
10.
indrastu mēṣavr̥ṣaṇastadā prabhr̥ti rāghava |
gautamasya prabhāvēna tapasaśca mahātmanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 10:** "Rāghava, from that time onward Indra has had the testicles of
> a male sheep, all because of the power of the great-souled Gautama and his
> tapas.

---
11.
tadāgaccha mahātēja āśramaṁ puṇyakarmaṇaḥ |
tārayaināṁ mahābhāgāmahalyāṁ dēvarūpiṇīm ||

> **Meaning 11:** "Radiant prince, come—enter the āśrama of that sage of holy
> deeds and redeem the great-souled Ahalyā, who is divinely beautiful."

---
12.
viśvāmitravacaḥ śrutvā rāghavaḥ sahalakṣmaṇaḥ |
viśvāmitraṁ puraskr̥tya āśramaṁ pravivēśa ha ||

> **Meaning 12:** Hearing Viśvāmitra's words, Rāghava together with Lakṣmaṇa
> entered the āśrama, placing Viśvāmitra in front.

---
13.
dadarśa ca mahābhāgāṁ tapasā dyōtitaprabhām |
lōkairapi samāgamya durnirīkṣyāṁ surāsuraiḥ ||

> **Meaning 13:** There he saw the great-souled Ahalyā, her radiance kindled by
> tapas—impossible even for all the worlds, suras, and asuras together to gaze
> upon directly.

---
14.
prayatnānnirmitāṁ dhātrā divyāṁ māyāmayīmiva |
dhūmēnābhiparītāṅgīṁ pūrṇacandraprabhāmiva ||

> **Meaning 14:** She looked like a divine wonder created with great care by
> Brahmā, yet shrouded in smoke like the light of a full moon,

---
15.
satuṣārāvr̥tāṁ sābhrāṁ pūrṇacandraprabhāmiva |
madhyē'mbhasō durādharṣāṁ dīptāṁ sūryaprabhāmiva ||

> **Meaning 15:** like the radiance of the full moon veiled by mist and clouds,
> or like the dazzling brilliance of the sun shining upon the midst of the
> waters.

---
16.
sa hi gautamavākyēna durnirīkṣyā babhūva ha |
trayāṇāmapi lōkānāṁ yāvadrāmasya darśanam ||

> **Meaning 16:** For by Gautama's word she had remained invisible to all three
> worlds until Rāma came and saw her.

---
17.
rāghavau tu tatastasyāḥ pādau jagr̥hatustadā |
smarantī gautamavacaḥ pratijagrāha sā ca tau ||

> **Meaning 17:** Then the two Rāghavas clasped her feet, and remembering
> Gautama's words, she welcomed them both.

---
18.
pādyamarghyaṁ tathātithyaṁ cakāra susamāhitā |
pratijagrāha kākutsthō vidhidr̥ṣṭēna karmaṇā ||

> **Meaning 18:** With deep composure she offered them pādya, arghya, and full
> hospitality, and Kākutstha accepted it in accordance with traditional rites.

---
19.
puṣpavr̥ṣṭirmahatyāsīddēvadundubhinisvanaiḥ |
gandharvāpsarasāṁ cāpi mahānāsītsamāgamaḥ ||

> **Meaning 19:** A great shower of flowers fell from the sky to the sound of
> celestial dundubhi drums, and a grand gathering of Gandharvas and Apsarases
> took place.

---
20.
sādhu sādhviti dēvāstāmahalyāṁ samapūjayan |
tapōbalaviśuddhāṅgīṁ gautamasya vaśānugām ||

> **Meaning 20:** Exclaiming, "Wonderful! Wonderful!", the devatās honored
> Ahalyā, whose entire being was sanctified by the power of her tapas and who
> remained devoted to Gautama's will.

---
21.
gautamō'pi mahātējā ahalyāsahitaḥ sukhī |
rāmaṁ sampūjya vidhivattapastēpē mahātapāḥ ||

> **Meaning 21:** Meanwhile, the deeply radiant great ascetic Gautama, happily
> reunited with Ahalyā, honored Rāma in the traditional manner and resumed his
> tapas.

---
22.
rāmō'pi paramāṁ pūjāṁ gautamasya mahāmunēḥ |
sakāśādvidhivatprāpya jagāma mithilāṁ tataḥ ||

> **Meaning 22:** After receiving the highest honor from the great muni Gautama
> as custom required, Rāma set out from there toward Mithilā.

---
