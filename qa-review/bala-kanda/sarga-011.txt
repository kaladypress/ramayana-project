=== Sarga 11 ===
King Daśaratha Brings Sage Ṛśyaśṛṅga and Śāntā to Ayodhyā

---
1.
bhūya ēva ca rājēndra śr̥ṇu mē vacanaṁ hitam |
yathā sa dēvapravaraḥ kathāyāmēvamabravīt ||

> **Meaning 1:** Sumantra continues: "Great king, listen further to my beneficial
> words, as I relate what else that foremost of devatās (Sanatkumāra) spoke in
> his narrative."

---
2.
ikṣvākūṇāṁ kulē jātō bhaviṣyati sudhārmikaḥ |
rājā daśarathō nāmnā śrīmānsatyapratiśravaḥ ||

> **Meaning 2:** "In the Ikṣvāku dynasty will be born a king named
> Daśaratha—deeply righteous, radiant with royal splendor, and firm in keeping
> his word."

---
3.
aṅgarājēna sakhyaṁ ca tasya rājñō bhaviṣyati |
kanyā cāsya mahābhāgā śāntā nāma bhaviṣyati ||

> **Meaning 3:** "That king will form a friendship with the king of Aṅga, and a
> greatly fortunate daughter named Śāntā will be born to Daśaratha."

---
4.
putrastvaṅgasya rājñastu lōmapāda iti śrutaḥ |
taṁ sa rājā daśarathō gamiṣyati mahāyaśāḥ ||

> **Meaning 4:** "The king of Aṅga will have a son known as Romapāda. The
> renowned Daśaratha will go to him."

---
5.
anapatyō'smi dharmātmañśāntābhartā mama kratum |
āharēta tvayājñaptaḥ santānārthaṁ kulasya ca ||

> **Meaning 5:** "Daśaratha will say: 'I have no children, righteous one. If you
> permit it, let Śāntā's husband perform a yajña for me, to bring me sons and
> continue my line.'"

---
6.
śrutvā rājñō'tha tadvākyaṁ manasā sa vicintya ca |
pradāsyatē putravantaṁ śāntā bhartāramātmavān ||

> **Meaning 6:** "Hearing the king's words and reflecting on them, the noble
> Romapāda will send Śāntā's husband with him to bless him with sons."

---
7.
pratigr̥hya ca taṁ vipraṁ sa rājā vigatajvaraḥ |
āhariṣyati taṁ yajñaṁ prahr̥ṣṭēnāntarātmanā ||

> **Meaning 7:** "Welcoming that brahmin, King Daśaratha, relieved of his
> sorrow, will perform that yajña with a joyful heart."

---
8.
taṁ ca rājā daśarathō yaṣṭukāmaḥ kr̥tāñjaliḥ |
r̥śyaśr̥ṅgaṁ dvijaśrēṣṭhaṁ varayiṣyati dharmavit ||

> **Meaning 8:** "Desiring to perform the yajña, the righteous King Daśaratha
> will fold his hands and request Ṛśyaśṛṅga, the foremost of brahmins, to
> officiate."

---
9.
yajñārthaṁ prasavārthaṁ ca svargārthaṁ ca narēśvaraḥ |
labhatē ca sa taṁ kāmaṁ dvijamukhyādviśāṁ patiḥ ||

> **Meaning 9:** "King Daśaratha, ruler of men and protector of his subjects,
> will seek the sage's aid to perform the sacred yajña, to be blessed with
> sons, and to attain heaven. Through that foremost of brahmins, he will obtain
> the complete fulfillment of his heart's desire."

---
10.
putrāścāsya bhaviṣyanti catvārō'mitavikramāḥ |
vaṁśapratiṣṭhānakarāḥ sarvalōkēṣu viśrutāḥ ||

> **Meaning 10:** "Four sons of immeasurable valor will be born to him, famed in
> all the worlds, who will establish and carry on his lineage."

---
11.
ēvaṁ sa dēvapravaraḥ pūrvaṁ kathitavānkathām |
sanatkumārō bhagavānpurā dēvayugē prabhuḥ ||

> **Meaning 11:** "In this way, long ago in the Kṛta Yuga, the revered and
> powerful Sanatkumāra, foremost of devatās, related this account."

---
12.
sa tvaṁ puruṣaśārdūla tamānaya susatkr̥tam |
svayamēva mahārāja gatvā sabalavāhanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 12:** "Therefore, great king, foremost of men, go yourself with your
> royal retinue and chariots, and bring that sage back with the highest
> honors."

---
13.
anumānya vasiṣṭhaṁ ca sūtavākyaṁ niśamya ca |
sāntaḥpuraḥ sahāmātyaḥ prayayau yatra sa dvijaḥ ||

> **Meaning 13:** "Having taken Vasiṣṭha's permission and having heard the
> Sumantra's words, the king set out with his queens and ministers to where
> that brahmin lived."

---
14.
vanāni saritaścaiva vyatikramya śanaiḥ śanaiḥ |
abhicakrāma taṁ dēśaṁ yatra vai munipuṅgavaḥ ||

> **Meaning 14:** "Crossing forests and rivers, traveling slowly and steadily,
> he reached the land where that best of munis lived."

---
15.
āsādya taṁ dvijaśrēṣṭhaṁ lōmapādasamīpagam |
r̥ṣiputraṁ dadarśādau dīpyamānamivānalam ||

> **Meaning 15:** "Arriving there, he first saw the sage's son, Ṛśyaśṛṅga, the
> foremost of brahmins, seated near Romapāda and blazing like fire."

---
16.
tatō rājā yathānyāyaṁ pūjāṁ cakrē viśēṣataḥ |
sakhitvāttasya vai rājñaḥ prahr̥ṣṭēnāntarātmanā ||

> **Meaning 16:** "Then, because of his friendship with Romapāda, the king, with
> a joyful heart, paid him special honor, according to custom."

---
17.
lōmapādēna cākhyātamr̥ṣiputrāya dhīmatē |
sakhyaṁ sambandhakaṁ caiva tadā taṁ pratyapūjayat ||

> **Meaning 17:** "Romapāda told the wise sage's son of the friendship and the
> family bond between them, and Ṛśyaśṛṅga honored Daśaratha in return."

---
18.
ēvaṁ susatkr̥tastēna sahōṣitvā nararṣabhaḥ |
saptāṣṭadivasānrājā rājānamidamabravīt ||

> **Meaning 18:** "Warmly honored in this way, King Daśaratha stayed with him
> for eight days, and then said to King Romapāda:"

---
19.
śāntā tava sutā rājansaha bhartrā viśāmpatē |
madīyaṁ nagaraṁ yātu kāryaṁ hi mahadudyatam ||

> **Meaning 19:** "King, protector of your people, let your daughter Śāntā go
> with her husband to my capital, for a great endeavor is about to be
> undertaken."

---
20.
tathēti rājā saṁśrutya gamanaṁ tasya dhīmataḥ |
uvāca vacanaṁ vipraṁ gaccha tvaṁ saha bhāryayā ||

> **Meaning 20:** "King Romapāda consented to the wise sage's departure, saying,
> 'So be it.' He then said to the brahmin: 'Please journey there together with
> your wife Śāntā.'"

---
21.
r̥ṣiputraḥ pratiśrutya tathētyāha nr̥paṁ tadā |
sa nr̥pēṇābhyanujñātaḥ prayayau saha bhāryayā ||

> **Meaning 21:** "Ṛśyaśṛṅga agreed, saying 'So be it' to the king; and with
> Romapāda's leave, he set out together with his wife."

---
22.
tāvanyōnyāñjaliṁ kr̥tvā snēhātsaṁśliṣya cōrasā |
nanandaturdaśarathō lōmapādaśca vīryavān ||

> **Meaning 22:** "Then Daśaratha and the mighty Romapāda folded their hands to
> each other, embraced warmly out of affection, and were both delighted."

---
23.
tataḥ suhr̥damāpr̥cchya prasthitō raghunandanaḥ |
paurēbhyaḥ prēṣayāmāsa dūtānvai śīghragāminaḥ ||

> **Meaning 23:** "After taking leave of his dear friend Romapāda, King
> Daśaratha, the joy of the Raghus, set out and sent swift messengers ahead to
> the citizens:"

---
24.
kriyatāṁ nagaraṁ sarvaṁ kṣipramēva svalaṅkr̥tam |
dhūpitaṁ siktasammr̥ṣṭaṁ patākābhiralaṅkr̥tam ||

> **Meaning 24:** "'Let the whole city be decorated at once! Clean and sprinkle
> every street with water, perfume the avenues with fragrant incense, and adorn
> the city with flying banners!'"

---
25.
tataḥ prahr̥ṣṭāḥ paurāstē śrutvā rājānamāgatam |
tathā pracakrustatsarvaṁ rājñā yatprēṣitaṁ tadā ||

> **Meaning 25:** "Hearing that the king was returning, the citizens were
> delighted and carried out everything exactly as the king had commanded."

---
26.
tataḥ svalaṅkr̥taṁ rājā nagaraṁ pravivēśa ha |
śaṅkhadundubhinirghōṣaiḥ puraskr̥tya dvijarṣabham ||

> **Meaning 26:** "Then the king entered the beautifully decorated city to the
> sounding of conches and drums, placing that foremost of brahmins, Ṛśyaśṛṅga, in
> front with highest honor."

---
27.
tataḥ pramuditāḥ sarvē dr̥ṣṭvā taṁ nāgarā dvijam |
pravēśyamānaṁ satkr̥tya narēndrēṇēndrakarmaṇā ||

> **Meaning 27:** "All the citizens rejoiced to see that foremost brahmin,
> Ṛśyaśṛṅga, being ushered in and honored by King Daśaratha, whose heroic deeds
> rivaled those of Indra."

---
28.
antaḥpuraṁ pravēśyainaṁ pūjāṁ kr̥tvā tu śāstrataḥ |
kr̥takr̥tyaṁ tadātmānaṁ mēnē tasyōpavāhanāt ||

> **Meaning 28:** "Welcoming him into the inner palace, the king worshipped him
> according to the scriptures, and felt his life's purpose fulfilled by the
> sage's arrival."

---
29.
antaḥpurastriyaḥ sarvāḥ śāntāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā tathāgatām |
saha bhartrā viśālākṣīṁ prītyānandamupāgaman ||

> **Meaning 29:** "Seeing the wide-eyed Śāntā arrive with her husband, all the
> women of the inner palace were filled with joy and affection."

---
30.
pūjyamānā ca tābhiḥ sā rājñā caiva viśēṣataḥ |
uvāsa tatra sukhitā kañcitkālaṁ saha dvijā ||

> **Meaning 30:** "Honored by the palace women and especially by King Daśaratha,
> Śāntā lived there happily with Sage Ṛśyaśṛṅga for some time."

---
