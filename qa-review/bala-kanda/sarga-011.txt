=== Sarga 11 ===

---
1.
tataḥ kāle bahutithe kasmiṁś cit sumanohare |
vasante samanuprāpte rājño yaṣṭuṁ mano ’bhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tataḥ prasādya śirasā taṁ vipraṁ devavarṇinam |
yajñāya varayām āsa saṁtānārthaṁ kulasya vai ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tatheti ca sa rājānam uvāca ca susatkr̥taḥ |
saṁbhārāḥ saṁbhriyantāṁ te turagaś ca vimucyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tato rājābravīd vākyaṁ sumantraṁ mantrisattamam |
sumantrāvāhaya kṣipram r̥tvijo brahmavādinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tataḥ sumantras tvaritaṁ gatvā tvaritavikramaḥ |
samānayat sa tān viprān samastān vedapāragān ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
suyajñaṁ vāmadevaṁ ca jābālim atha kāśyapam |
purohitaṁ vasiṣṭhaṁ ca ye cānye dvijasattamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tān pūjayitvā dharmātmā rājā daśarathas tadā |
idaṁ dharmārthasahitaṁ ślakṣṇaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
mama lālapyamānasya putrārthaṁ nāsti vai sukham |
tadarthaṁ hayamedhena yakṣyāmīti matir mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tad ahaṁ yaṣṭum icchāmi śāstradr̥ṣṭena karmaṇā |
r̥ṣiputraprabhāvena kāmān prāpsyāmi cāpy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tataḥ sādhv iti tad vākyaṁ brāhmaṇāḥ pratyapūjayan |
vasiṣṭhapramukhāḥ sarve pārthivasya mukhāc cyutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
r̥śyaśr̥ṅgapurogāś ca pratyūcur nr̥patiṁ tadā |
saṁbhārāḥ saṁbhriyantāṁ te turagaś ca vimucyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sarvathā prāpyase putrāṁś caturo ’mitavikramān |
yasya te dhārmikī buddhir iyaṁ putrārtham āgatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tataḥ prīto ’bhavad rājā śrutvā tad dvijabhāṣitam |
amātyāṁś cābravīd rājā harṣeṇedaṁ śubhākṣaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
gurūṇāṁ vacanāc chīghraṁ saṁbhārāḥ saṁbhriyantu me |
samarthādhiṣṭhitaś cāśvaḥ sopādhyāyo vimucyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sarayvāś cottare tīre yajñabhūmir vidhīyatām |
śāntayaś cābhivardhantāṁ yathākalpaṁ yathāvidhi ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
śakyaḥ kartum ayaṁ yajñaḥ sarveṇāpi mahīkṣitā |
nāparādho bhavet kaṣṭo yady asmin kratusattame ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
chidraṁ hi mr̥gayante ’tra vidvāṁso brahmarākṣasāḥ |
vidhihīnasya yajñasya sadyaḥ kartā vinaśyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tad yathā vidhipūrvaṁ me kratur eṣa samāpyate |
tathāvidhānaṁ kriyatāṁ samarthāḥ karaṇeṣv iha ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tatheti ca tataḥ sarve mantriṇaḥ pratyapūjayan |
pārthivendrasya tad vākyaṁ yathājñaptam akurvata ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tato dvijās te dharmajñam astuvan pārthivarṣabham |
anujñātās tataḥ sarve punar jagmur yathāgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
gatānāṁ tu dvijātīnāṁ mantriṇas tān narādhipaḥ |
visarjayitvā svaṁ veśma praviveśa mahādyutiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
