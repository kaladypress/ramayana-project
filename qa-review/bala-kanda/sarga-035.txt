=== Sarga 35 ===

---
1.
ukta vākye munau tasminn ubhau rāghavalakṣmaṇau |
pratinandya kathāṁ vīrāv ūcatur munipuṁgavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
dharmayuktam idaṁ brahman kathitaṁ paramaṁ tvayā |
duhituḥ śailarājasya jyeṣṭhāya vaktum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
vistaraṁ vistarajño ’si divyamānuṣasaṁbhavam |
trīn patho hetunā kena pāvayel lokapāvanī ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
kathaṁ gaṅgāṁ tripathagā viśrutā sariduttamā |
triṣu lokeṣu dharmajña karmabhiḥ kaiḥ samanvitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tathā bruvati kākutsthe viśvāmitras tapodhanaḥ |
nikhilena kathāṁ sarvām r̥ṣimadhye nyavedayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
purā rāma kr̥todvāhaḥ śitikaṇṭho mahātapāḥ |
dr̥ṣṭvā ca spr̥hayā devīṁ maithunāyopacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
śitikaṇṭhasya devasya divyaṁ varṣaśataṁ gatam |
na cāpi tanayo rāma tasyām āsīt paraṁtapa ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tato devāḥ samudvignāḥ pitāmahapurogamāḥ |
yad ihotpadyate bhūtaṁ kas tat pratisahiṣyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
abhigamya surāḥ sarve praṇipatyedam abruvan |
devadeva mahādeva lokasyāsya hite rata |
surāṇāṁ praṇipātena prasādaṁ kartum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
na lokā dhārayiṣyanti tava tejaḥ surottama |
brāhmeṇa tapasā yukto devyā saha tapaś cara ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
trailokyahitakāmārthaṁ tejas tejasi dhāraya |
rakṣa sarvān imām̐l lokān nālokaṁ kartum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
devatānāṁ vacaḥ śrutvā sarvalokamaheśvaraḥ |
bāḍham ity abravīt sarvān punaś cedam uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
dhārayiṣyāmy ahaṁ tejas tejasy eva sahomayā |
tridaśāḥ pr̥thivī caiva nirvāṇam adhigacchatu ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
yad idaṁ kṣubhitaṁ sthānān mama tejo hy anuttamam |
dhārayiṣyati kas tan me bruvantu surasattamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
evam uktās tato devāḥ pratyūcur vr̥ṣabhadhvajam |
yat tejaḥ kṣubhitaṁ hy etat tad dharā dhārayiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
evam uktaḥ surapatiḥ pramumoca mahītale |
tejasā pr̥thivī yena vyāptā sagirikānanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tato devāḥ punar idam ūcuś cātha hutāśanam |
praviśa tvaṁ mahātejo raudraṁ vāyusamanvitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tad agninā punar vyāptaṁ saṁjātaḥ śvetaparvataḥ |
divyaṁ śaravaṇaṁ caiva pāvakādityasaṁnibham |
yatra jāto mahātejāḥ kārtikeyo ’gnisaṁbhavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
athomāṁ ca śivaṁ caiva devāḥ sarṣi gaṇās tadā |
pūjayām āsur atyarthaṁ suprītamanasas tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
atha śaila sutā rāma tridaśān idam abravīt |
samanyur aśapat sarvān krodhasaṁraktalocanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
yasmān nivāritā caiva saṁgatā putrakāmyayā |
apatyaṁ sveṣu dāreṣu notpādayitum arhatha |
adya prabhr̥ti yuṣmākam aprajāḥ santu patnayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
evam uktvā surān sarvāñ śaśāpa pr̥thivīm api |
avane naikarūpā tvaṁ bahubhāryā bhaviṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
na ca putrakr̥tāṁ prītiṁ matkrodhakaluṣī kr̥tā |
prāpsyasi tvaṁ sudurmedhe mama putram anicchatī ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tān sarvān vrīḍitān dr̥ṣṭvā surān surapatis tadā |
gamanāyopacakrāma diśaṁ varuṇapālitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
sa gatvā tapa ātiṣṭhat pārśve tasyottare gireḥ |
himavatprabhave śr̥ṅge saha devyā maheśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
eṣa te vistaro rāma śailaputryā niveditaḥ |
gaṅgāyāḥ prabhavaṁ caiva śr̥ṇu me sahalakṣmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
