=== Sarga 35 ===
Arrival at the Gaṅgā; The Birth of Gaṅgā and Umā

---
1.
upāsya rātriśēṣaṁ tu śōṇākūlē maharṣibhiḥ |
niśāyāṁ suprabhātāyāṁ viśvāmitrō'bhyabhāṣata ||

> **Meaning 1:** Having spent the rest of the night on the bank of the Śoṇa with
> the maharṣis, Viśvāmitra spoke as the night gave way to a bright dawn:

---
2.
suprabhātā niśā rāma pūrvā sandhyā pravartatē |
uttiṣṭhōttiṣṭha bhadraṁ tē gamanāyābhirōcaya ||

> **Meaning 2:** "Rāma, the night has dawned brightly, and the morning twilight
> is beginning. Get up, get up—blessings upon you—and make ready for our
> journey."

---
3.
tacchrutvā vacanaṁ tasya kr̥tvā paurvāhṇikīṁ kriyām |
gamanaṁ rōcayāmāsa vākyaṁ cēdamuvāca ha ||

> **Meaning 3:** Hearing his words, Rāma performed his morning rites, got ready
> to depart, and said:

---
4.
ayaṁ śōṇaḥ śubhajalō gādhaḥ pulinamaṇḍitaḥ |
katarēṇa pathā brahmansantariṣyāmahē vayam ||

> **Meaning 4:** "This Śoṇa has clear, pure water, is shallow, and is graced
> with sandy banks. Which path should we take to cross it, wise sage?"

---
5.
ēvamuktastu rāmēṇa viśvāmitrō'bravīdidam |
ēṣa panthā mayōddiṣṭō yēna yānti maharṣayaḥ ||

> **Meaning 5:** Addressed in this way by Rāma, Viśvāmitra replied, "I will show
> you the path the maharṣis travel."

---
6.
evamuktvā r̥ṣayo viśvamitreṇa dhīmatā |
paśyantaste prayātā vai vanāni vividhāni ca ||

> **Meaning 6:** After the wise Viśvāmitra spoke these words, the ṛṣis set out,
> taking in the sight of many different forests along the way.

---
7.
tē gatvā dūramadhvānaṁ gatē'rdhadivasē tadā |
jāhnavīṁ saritāṁ śrēṣṭhāṁ dadr̥śurmunisēvitām ||

> **Meaning 7:** After traveling a long distance, by midday they came upon the
> Jāhnavī, foremost among rivers, frequented by munis.

---
8.
tāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā puṇyasalilāṁ haṁsasārasasēvitām |
babhūvurmuditāḥ sarvē munayaḥ saharāghavāḥ |

> **Meaning 8:** At the sight of her sacred waters, alive with swans and sārasa
> cranes, all the munis and the Rāghavas were filled with joy.

---
9.
tasyāstīrē tataścakrustē āvāsaparigraham |
tataḥ snātvā yathānyāyaṁ santarpya pitr̥dēvatāḥ ||

> **Meaning 9:** They set up their camp along her bank, bathed according to
> proper custom, and offered tarpaṇa to the pitṛs and devatās.

---
10.
hutvā caivāgnihōtrāṇi prāśya cāmr̥tavaddhaviḥ |
viviśurjāhnavītīrē śucau muditamānasāḥ ||

> **Meaning 10:** After offering oblations in the agnihotra fires and partaking
> of the sacred haviḥ—sweet as amṛta—they sat down on the pure bank of the
> Jāhnavī with contented hearts.

---
11.
atha tatra tadā rāmō viśvāmitramathābravīt |
viśvāmitraṁ mahātmānaṁ parivārya samantataḥ ||

> **Meaning 11:** Then, as everyone sat gathered around the noble-souled
> Viśvāmitra on all sides, Rāma spoke to him:

---
12.
bhagavañśrōtumicchāmi gaṅgāṁ tripathagāṁ nadīm |
trailōkyaṁ kathamākramya gatā nadanadīpatim ||

> **Meaning 12:** "Revered sage, I want to hear about the river Gaṅgā, who flows
> through three paths. How did she traverse the three worlds to reach the ocean,
> where all rivers and streams merge?"

---
13.
cōditō rāma vākyēna viśvāmitrō mahāmuniḥ |
vr̥ddhiṁ janma ca gaṅgāyā vaktumēvōpacakramē ||

> **Meaning 13:** Prompted by Rāma's request, the great muni Viśvāmitra began to
> tell the story of Gaṅgā's birth and her descent.

---
14.
śailēndrō himavānnāma dhātūnāmākarō mahān |
tasya kanyā dvayaṁ rāma rūpēṇāpratimaṁ bhuvi ||

> **Meaning 14:** "Rāma, there is a mighty king of mountains named Himavān. He
> is a vast treasury of precious minerals, and he had two daughters unmatched
> in beauty anywhere on earth.

---
15.
yā mēruduhitā rāma tayōrmātā sumadhyamā |
nāmnā mēnā manōjñā vai patnī himavataḥ priyā ||

> **Meaning 15:** "Rāma, their graceful mother was the daughter of Mount Meru.
> Named Menā, she was the charming and beloved wife of Himavān.

---
16.
tasyāṁ gaṅgēyamabhavajjyēṣṭhā himavataḥ sutā |
umā nāma dvitīyābhūtkanyā tasyaiva rāghava ||

> **Meaning 16:** "Rāghava, to her was born this Gaṅgā, Himavān's eldest
> daughter, and his second daughter was named Umā.

---
17.
atha jyēṣṭhāṁ surāḥ sarvē dēvatārthacikīrṣayā |
śailēndraṁ varayāmāsurgaṅgāṁ tripathagāṁ nadīm ||

> **Meaning 17:** "Later, all the devatās, wishing to accomplish a great divine
> purpose, asked the mountain king for his eldest daughter, the river Gaṅgā, who
> flows through three paths.

---
18.
dadau dharmēṇa himavāṁstanayāṁ lōkapāvanīm |
svacchandapathagāṁ gaṅgāṁ trailōkyahitakāmyayā ||

> **Meaning 18:** "In keeping with dharma, Himavān gave them his daughter
> Gaṅgā—who purifies the worlds and moves freely wherever she chooses—for the
> good of all three worlds.

---
19.
pratigr̥hya trilōkārthaṁ trilōkahitakāriṇaḥ |
gaṅgāmādāya tē'gacchankr̥tārthēnāntarātmanā ||

> **Meaning 19:** "Accepting her for the sake of the three worlds, the devatās—
> who work for the welfare of all three realms—took Gaṅgā and departed, deeply
> fulfilled at heart.

---
20.
yā cānyā śailaduhitā kanyāsīdraghunandana |
ugraṁ sā vratamāsthāya tapastēpē tapōdhanā ||

> **Meaning 20:** "Raghunandana, as for the mountain's other daughter, rich in
> spiritual power, she took up a fierce vow and practiced intense tapas.

---
21.
ugrēṇa tapasā yuktāṁ dadau śailavaraḥ sutām |
rudrāyāpratirūpāya umāṁ lōkanamaskr̥tām ||

> **Meaning 21:** "The great mountain king gave his daughter Umā (Pārvatī),
> endowed with fierce tapas and revered by all the worlds, to the matchless
> Rudra.

---
22.
ētē tē śaila rājasya sutē lōkanamaskr̥tē |
gaṅgā ca saritāṁ śrēṣṭhā umā dēvī ca rāghava ||

> **Meaning 22:** "Rāghava, these are the two daughters of the mountain king,
> revered by all the worlds: Gaṅgā, foremost among rivers, and the goddess Umā.

---
23.
ētattē dharmamākhyātaṁ yathā tripathagā nadī |
khaṁ gatā prathamaṁ tāta gatiṁ gatimatāṁ vara ||

> **Meaning 23:** "I have now told you this sacred account of how the river
> Gaṅgā, who flows through three paths, first ascended to the heavens, dear
> Rāma.

---
24.
saiṣā suranadī ramyā śailēndratanayā tadā |
suralōkaṁ samārūḍhā vipāpā jalavāhinī ||

> **Meaning 24:** "Thus, this serene river of the devatās, the daughter of the
> mountain king, ascended to the realm of the devatās, sinless and bearing her
> flowing waters."

---
