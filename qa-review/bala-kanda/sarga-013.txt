=== Sarga 13 ===
Preparations for the Aśvamedha Yajña and Consecration of King Daśaratha

---
1.
punaḥ prāptē vasantē tu pūrṇaḥ saṁvatsarō'bhavat |
prasavārthaṁ gatō yaṣṭuṁ hayamēdhēna vīryavān ||

> **Meaning 1:** When spring arrived again, a full year had passed, and the
> valiant king proceeded to perform the Aśvamēdha yajña for the sake of
> obtaining sons.

---
2.
abhivādya vasiṣṭhaṁ ca nyāyataḥ pratipūjya ca |
abravītpraśritaṁ vākyaṁ prasavārthaṁ dvijōttamam ||

> **Meaning 2:** Bowing to Vasiṣṭha and honoring him with due respect, he spoke
> humbly to that foremost Brāhmaṇa, seeking the birth of sons:

---
3.
yajñō mē kriyatāṁ vipra yathōktaṁ munipuṅgava |
yathā na vighnaḥ kriyatē yajñāṅgēṣu vidhīyatām ||

> **Meaning 3:** "Foremost among sages, learned one, please conduct the yajña
> for us as prescribed in the scriptures, and arrange things so that no
> obstacle arises in any of its rites.

---
4.
bhavānsnigdhaḥ suhr̥nmahyaṁ guruśca paramō mahān |
vōḍhavyō bhavatā caiva bhārō yajñasya cōdyataḥ ||

> **Meaning 4:** You are my affectionate well-wisher, my supreme and venerable
> guru; the burden of this yajña now undertaken must indeed be borne by you."

---
5.
tathēti ca sa rājānamabravīddvijasattamaḥ |
kariṣyē sarvamēvaitadbhavatā yatsamarthitam ||

> **Meaning 5:** That best of Brāhmaṇas replied to the king, "So be it; I will
> carry out everything that you have proposed."

---
6.
tatō'bravīddvijānvr̥ddhānyajñakarmasu niṣṭhitān |
sthāpatyē niṣṭhitāṁścaiva vr̥ddhānparamadhārmikān ||

> **Meaning 6:** Then Vasiṣṭha addressed the elderly Brāhmaṇas experienced in
> rites of the yajña, as well as the senior, deeply righteous masters of Vāstu
> and architecture,

---
7.
karmāntikāñśilpakārānvardhakīnkhanakānapi |
gaṇakāñśilpinaścaiva tathaiva naṭanartakān ||

> **Meaning 7:** the work supervisors, artisans, carpenters, well-diggers,
> accountants, craftsmen, and likewise actors and dancers,

---
8.
tathā śucīñśāstravidaḥ puruṣānsubahuśrutān |
yajñakarma samīhantāṁ bhavantō rājaśāsanāt ||

> **Meaning 8:** as well as trustworthy men skilled in the technical sciences,
> and scholars deeply learned in sacred lore: "By the king's command, all of
> you begin the preparations for the yajña.

---
9.
iṣṭakā bahusāhasrī śīghramānīyatāmiti |
aupakāryāḥ kriyantāṁ ca rājñāṁ bahuguṇānvitāḥ ||

> **Meaning 9:** Have many thousands of bricks brought quickly, and construct
> pavilions for the visiting kings, richly endowed with every comfort.

---
10.
brāhmaṇāvasathāścaiva kartavyāḥ śataśaḥ śubhāḥ |
bhakṣyānnapānairbahubhiḥ samupētāḥ suniṣṭhitāḥ ||

> **Meaning 10:** Build hundreds of fine dwellings for the Brāhmaṇas, sturdily
> constructed to withstand wind and weather, and well stocked with abundant
> foods and drinks.

---
11.
tathā paurajanasyāpi kartavyā bahuvistarāḥ |
āvāsā bahubhakṣyā vai sarvakāmairupasthitāḥ ||

> **Meaning 11:** Likewise, spacious quarters must be prepared for the citizens
> of the capital, stocked with plenty of food and furnished with every comfort.

---
12.
tathā jānapadasyāpi janasya bahuśōbhanam |
dātavyamannaṁ vidhivatsatkr̥tya na tu līlayā ||

> **Meaning 12:** Likewise, fine and wholesome food must be served to the
> people of the countryside with due respect and hospitality, and never
> casually or with indifference.

---
13.
sarvē varṇā yathā pūjāṁ prāpnuvanti susatkr̥tāḥ |
na cāvajñā prayōktavyā kāmakrōdhavaśādapi ||

> **Meaning 13:** so that people of all four varṇas receive due honor and proper
> reception; no one must be treated with disdain, even under the sway of desire
> or anger.

---
14.
yajñakarmasu yē'vyagrāḥ puruṣāḥ śilpinastathā |
tēṣāmapi viśēṣēṇa pūjā kāryā yathākramam ||

> **Meaning 14:** Those workers and craftsmen who are attentively dedicated to
> the tasks of the yajña must especially be honored, according to their
> respective skill and merit.

---
15.
tē ca syuḥ sambhr̥tāḥ sarvē vasubhirbhōjanēna ca |
yathā sarvaṁ suvihitaṁ na kiñcitparihīyatē ||

> **Meaning 15:** They should all be generously provided with wealth and food,
> so that everything is arranged properly and no detail is overlooked.

---
16.
tathā bhavantaḥ kurvantu prītisnigdhēna cētasā |
tataḥ sarvē samāgamya vasiṣṭhamidamabruvan ||

> **Meaning 16:** 'Carry out your work with hearts full of affection and
> goodwill.' Then all of them came together and said to Vasiṣṭha:

---
17.
yathōktaṁ tatkariṣyāmō na kiñcitparihāsyatē |
yathoktaṁ tatkariṣyāmo na kiñcitparihāsyate ||

> **Meaning 17:** 'We will do exactly as you have instructed; nothing whatsoever
> will be neglected.'

---
18.
tataḥ sumantramāhūya vasiṣṭhō vākyamabravīt |
nimantrayasva nr̥patīnpr̥thivyāṁ yē ca dhārmikāḥ ||

> **Meaning 18:** Then Vasiṣṭha summoned Sumantra and said: 'Invite the
> righteous rulers across the earth,

---
19.
brāhmaṇānkṣatriyānvaiśyāñśūdrāṁścaiva sahasraśaḥ |
samānayasva satkr̥tya sarvadēśēṣu mānavān ||

> **Meaning 19:** and respectfully gather people of all lands by the
> thousands—Brāhmaṇas, Kṣatriyas, Vaiśyas, and Śūdras.

---
20.
mithilādhipatiṁ śūraṁ janakaṁ satyavikramam |
niṣṭhitaṁ sarvaśāstrēṣu tathā vēdēṣu niṣṭhitam ||

> **Meaning 20:** Janaka, the ruler of Mithilā, is heroic and truly valiant,
> deeply versed in all the scriptures and established in the Vedas.

---
21.
tamānaya mahābhāgaṁ svayamēva susatkr̥tam |
pūrvasambandhinaṁ jñātvā tataḥ pūrvaṁ bravīmi tē ||

> **Meaning 21:** Go in person to invite and receive that illustrious king with
> great respect. Knowing the longstanding bond between our families, I mention
> him to you before all others.

---
22.
tathā kāśipatiṁ snigdhaṁ satataṁ priyavādinam |
sadvr̥ttaṁ dēvasaṅkāśaṁ svayamēvānayasva ha ||

> **Meaning 22:** Likewise, go in person to invite the king of Kāśī—he is
> affectionate, ever pleasant in speech, virtuous in conduct, and radiant like a
> deva.

---
23.
tathā kēkayarājānaṁ vr̥ddhaṁ paramadhārmikam |
śvaśuraṁ rājasiṁhasya saputraṁ tamihānaya ||

> **Meaning 23:** Invite here also the aged, deeply righteous king of Kekaya,
> the father-in-law of this lion among kings, King Daśaratha, together with his
> son.

---
24.
aṅgēśvaraṁ mahābhāgaṁ rōmapādaṁ susatkr̥tam |
vayasyaṁ rājasiṁhasya tamānaya yaśasvinam ||

> **Meaning 24:** With great honor, invite Romapāda, the renowned and noble
> ruler of Aṅga, the dear companion of this lion among kings.

---
25.
prācīnānsindhusauvīrānsaurāṣṭhrēyāṁśca pārthivān |
dākṣiṇātyānnarēndrāṁśca samastānānayasva ha ||

> **Meaning 25:** Invite the eastern rulers, the kings of Sindhu, Sauvīra, and
> Saurāṣṭra, and all the monarchs of the south.

---
26.
santi snigdhāśca yē cānyē rājānaḥ pr̥thivītalē |
tānānaya yathākṣipraṁ sānugānsahabāndhavān ||

> **Meaning 26:** And all other kings across the earth who are friendly to us,
> invite them swiftly, along with their entourages and kinsmen.'

---
27.
vasiṣṭhavākyaṁ tacchrutvā sumantrastvaritastadā |
vyādiśatpuruṣāṁstatra rājñāmānayanē śubhān ||

> **Meaning 27:** Hearing Vasiṣṭha's command, Sumantra promptly dispatched
> trustworthy messengers to invite those kings.

---
28.
svayamēva hi dharmātmā prayayau muniśāsanāt |
sumantrastvaritō bhūtvā samānētuṁ mahīkṣitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 28:** Then, by the sage's order, the righteous Sumantra himself set
> out in haste to personally escort the foremost rulers.

---
29.
tē ca karmāntikāḥ sarvē vasiṣṭhāya ca dhīmatē |
sarvaṁ nivēdayanti sma yajñē yadupakalpitam ||

> **Meaning 29:** All the work supervisors then reported to the wise Vasiṣṭha
> everything that had been prepared for the yajña.

---
30.
tataḥ prītō dvijaśrēṣṭhastānsarvānpunarabravīt |
avajñayā na dātavyaṁ kasyacillīlayāpi vā |
avajñayā kr̥taṁ hanyāddātāraṁ nātra saṁśayaḥ ||

> **Meaning 30:** Pleased with their report, that foremost of Brāhmaṇas
> instructed them all again: "Nothing must ever be given to anyone with
> disrespect, nor even offhandedly in jest. Whatever is given with contempt
> brings harm to the giver—of this there is no doubt."

---
31.
tataḥ kaiścidahōrātrairupayātā mahīkṣitaḥ |
bahūni ratnānyādāya rājñō daśarathasya ha ||

> **Meaning 31:** Then, after several days and nights, the monarchs arrived,
> bringing many valuable offerings for King Daśaratha.

---
32.
tatō vasiṣṭhaḥ suprītō rājānamidamabravīt ||

> **Meaning 32:** Then Vasiṣṭha, thoroughly delighted, addressed the king:

---
33.
upayātā naravyāghra rājānastava śāsanāt |
mayāpi satkr̥tāḥ sarvē yathārhaṁ rājasattamāḥ ||

> **Meaning 33:** "Mahārāja, the kings have arrived at your invitation,
> and I have honored all these eminent rulers according to their rank.

---
34.
yajñiyaṁ ca kr̥taṁ rājanpuruṣaiḥ susamāhitaiḥ |
niryātu ca bhavānyaṣṭuṁ yajñāyatanamantikāt ||

> **Meaning 34:** Everything required for the yajña has been prepared by
> attentive workers; now, O king, come forth to the yajña grounds nearby to
> begin the rite.

---
35.
sarvakāmairupahr̥tairupētaṁ vai samantataḥ |
draṣṭumarhasi rājēndra manasēva vinirmitam ||

> **Meaning 35:** You should see the yajña grounds, O king, fully equipped
> with every provision and built swiftly, exactly as envisioned."

---
36.
tathā vasiṣṭhavacanādr̥śyaśr̥ṅgasya cōbhayōḥ |
śubhē divasa nakṣatrē niryātō jagatīpatiḥ ||

> **Meaning 36:** Then, following the counsel of both Vasiṣṭha and
> Ṛśyaśṛṅga, the ruler of the earth set out from his palace for the yajña
> grounds on an auspicious day under a propitious constellation.

---
37.
tatō vasiṣṭhapramukhāḥ sarva ēva dvijōttamāḥ |
r̥śyaśr̥ṅgaṁ puraskr̥tya yajñakarmārabhaṁstadā ||

> **Meaning 37:** Then all the foremost Brāhmaṇas, headed by Vasiṣṭha, placed
> Ṛśyaśṛṅga in front and commenced the rituals of the yajña.

---
38.
yajñavāṭaṁ gatāḥ sarvē yathāśāstraṁ yathāvidhi |
śrīmāṁśca saha patnībhī rājā dīkṣāmupāviśat ||

> **Meaning 38:** All entered the yajña enclosure according to scriptural
> ordinances, and the glorious king, together with his queens, undertook the
> sacred vow of consecration.

---
