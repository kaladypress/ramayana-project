=== Sarga 62 ===

---
1.
pūrṇe varṣasahasre tu vratasnātaṁ mahāmunim |
abhyāgacchan surāḥ sarve tapaḥphalacikīrṣavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
abravīt sumahātejā brahmā suruciraṁ vacaḥ |
r̥ṣis tvam asi bhadraṁ te svārjitaiḥ karmabhiḥ śubhaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tam evam uktvā deveśas tridivaṁ punar abhyagāt |
viśvāmitro mahātejā bhūyas tepe mahat tapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tataḥ kālena mahatā menakā paramāpsarāḥ |
puṣkareṣu naraśreṣṭha snātuṁ samupacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tāṁ dadarśa mahātejā menakāṁ kuśikātmajaḥ |
rūpeṇāpratimāṁ tatra vidyutaṁ jalade yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
dr̥ṣṭvā kandarpavaśago munis tām idam abravīt |
apsaraḥ svāgataṁ te ’stu vasa ceha mamāśrame |
anugr̥hṇīṣva bhadraṁ te madanena sumohitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
ity uktā sā varārohā tatrāvāsam athākarot |
tapaso hi mahāvighno viśvāmitram upāgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tasyāṁ vasantyāṁ varṣāṇi pañca pañca ca rāghava |
viśvāmitrāśrame saumya sukhena vyaticakramuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
atha kāle gate tasmin viśvāmitro mahāmuniḥ |
savrīḍa iva saṁvr̥ttaś cintāśokaparāyaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
buddhir muneḥ samutpannā sāmarṣā raghunandana |
sarvaṁ surāṇāṁ karmaitat tapo’paharaṇaṁ mahat ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
ahorātrāpadeśena gatāḥ saṁvatsarā daśa |
kāmamohābhibhūtasya vighno ’yaṁ pratyupasthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
viniḥśvasan munivaraḥ paścāttāpena duḥkhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
bhītām apsarasaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā vepantīṁ prāñjaliṁ sthitām |
menakāṁ madhurair vākyair visr̥jya kuśikātmajaḥ |
uttaraṁ parvataṁ rāma viśvāmitro jagāma ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sa kr̥tvā naiṣṭhikīṁ buddhiṁ jetukāmo mahāyaśāḥ |
kauśikītīram āsādya tapas tepe sudāruṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tasya varṣasahasraṁ tu ghoraṁ tapa upāsataḥ |
uttare parvate rāma devatānām abhūd bhayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
amantrayan samāgamya sarve sarṣigaṇāḥ surāḥ |
maharṣiśabdaṁ labhatāṁ sādhv ayaṁ kuśikātmajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
devatānāṁ vacaḥ śrutvā sarvalokapitāmahaḥ |
abravīn madhuraṁ vākyaṁ viśvāmitraṁ tapodhanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
maharṣe svāgataṁ vatsa tapasogreṇa toṣitaḥ |
mahattvam r̥ṣimukhyatvaṁ dadāmi tava kauśika ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
brahmaṇaḥ sa vacaḥ śrutvā viśvāmitras tapodhanaḥ |
prāñjaliḥ praṇato bhūtvā pratyuvāca pitāmaham ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
brahmarṣi śabdam atulaṁ svārjitaiḥ karmabhiḥ śubhaiḥ |
yadi me bhagavān āha tato ’haṁ vijitendriyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tam uvāca tato brahmā na tāvat tvaṁ jitendriyaḥ |
yatasva muniśārdūla ity uktvā tridivaṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
viprasthiteṣu deveṣu viśvāmitro mahāmuniḥ |
ūrdhvabāhur nirālambo vāyubhakṣas tapaś caran ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
dharme pañcatapā bhūtvā varṣāsv ākāśasaṁśrayaḥ |
śiśire salilasthāyī rātryahāni tapodhanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
evaṁ varṣasahasraṁ hi tapo ghoram upāgamat ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tasmin saṁtapyamāne tu viśvāmitre mahāmunau |
saṁbhramaḥ sumahān āsīt surāṇāṁ vāsavasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
rambhām apsarasaṁ śakraḥ saha sarvair marudgaṇaiḥ |
uvācātmahitaṁ vākyam ahitaṁ kauśikasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
