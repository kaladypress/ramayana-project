=== Sarga 12 ===
King Daśaratha Initiates the Aśvamedha Yajña with Sage Ṛśyaśṛṅga

---
1.
tataḥ kālē bahutithē kasmiṁścitsumanōharē |
vasantē samanuprāptē rājñō yaṣṭuṁ manō'bhavat ||

> **Meaning 1:** Then, after much time had passed and the delightful spring
> season arrived, the desire to perform the yajña arose in King Daśaratha.

---
2.
tataḥ prasādya śirasā taṁ vipraṁ dēvavarṇinam |
yajñāya varayāmāsa santānārthaṁ kulasya vai ||

> **Meaning 2:** Bowing his head in deep reverence, the king sought the grace of
> that sage, who shone with the radiance of a devatā, and requested him to
> officiate at the yajña to perpetuate his lineage.

---
3.
tathēti ca sa rājānamuvāca ca susatkr̥taḥ |
sambhārāḥ sambhriyantāṁ tē turagaśca vimucyatām ||

> **Meaning 3:** Duly honored, Ṛśyaśṛṅga replied, "So be it," and said to the
> king: "Let the preparations for the yajña be gathered, and let the sacrificial
> horse be released."

---
4.
tatō rājābravīdvākyaṁ sumantraṁ mantrisattamam |
sumantrāvāhaya kṣipramr̥tvijō brahmavādinaḥ ||

> **Meaning 4:** The king instructed Sumantra, foremost of his ministers:
> "Sumantra, summon without delay the priests and scholars well-versed in the
> Vedas."

---
5.
tataḥ sumantrastvaritaṁ gatvā tvaritavikramaḥ |
samānayatsa tānviprānsamastānvēdapāragān ||

> **Meaning 5:** Moving swiftly, Sumantra lost no time in gathering all those
> venerable brahmins who had mastered the Vedas.

---
6.
suyajñaṁ vāmadēvaṁ ca jābālimatha kāśyapam |
purōhitaṁ vasiṣṭhaṁ ca yē cānyē dvijasattamāḥ ||

> **Meaning 6:** He brought Suyajña, Vāmadeva, Jābāli, and Kāśyapa, along with
> the royal priest Vasiṣṭha and other eminent sages.

---
7.
tānpūjayitvā dharmātmā rājā daśarathastadā |
idaṁ dharmārthasahitaṁ ślakṣṇaṁ vacanamabravīt ||

> **Meaning 7:** After honoring them duly, the righteous King Daśaratha spoke
> these gentle words, rich in dharma and purpose:

---
8.
mama lālapyamānasya putrārthaṁ nāsti vai sukham |
tadarthaṁ hayamēdhēna yakṣyāmīti matirmama ||

> **Meaning 8:** "I long for sons, and without them I find no happiness. For
> that reason, I have resolved to perform the Aśvamedha yajña."

---
9.
tadahaṁ yaṣṭumicchāmi śāstradr̥ṣṭēna karmaṇā |
r̥ṣiputraprabhāvēna kāmānprāpsyāmi cāpyaham ||

> **Meaning 9:** "I wish to perform this yajña strictly according to the rites
> ordained in the scriptures. Through the spiritual power of Sage Ṛśyaśṛṅga,
> all my desires will surely be fulfilled."

---
10.
tataḥ sādhviti tadvākyaṁ brāhmaṇāḥ pratyapūjayan |
vasiṣṭhapramukhāḥ sarvē pārthivasya mukhāccyutam ||

> **Meaning 10:** Hearing these words spoken by the king, all the brahmins, led
> by Vasiṣṭha, warmly acclaimed them, saying, "Well said!"

---
11.
r̥śyaśr̥ṅgapurōgāśca pratyūcurnr̥patiṁ tadā |
sambhārāḥ sambhriyantāṁ tē turagaśca vimucyatām ||

> **Meaning 11:** Led by Ṛśyaśṛṅga, they replied to the king: "Let the materials
> be gathered, and let the horse be set free."

---
12.
sarvathā prāpyasē putrāṁścaturō'mitavikramān |
yasya tē dhārmikī buddhiriyaṁ putrārthamāgatā ||

> **Meaning 12:** "You will surely have four sons of immeasurable valor, since
> this righteous resolve for sons has come to you."

---
13.
tataḥ prītō'bhavadrājā śrutvā taddvijabhāṣitam |
amātyāṁścābravīdrājā harṣēṇēdaṁ śubhākṣaram ||

> **Meaning 13:** Hearing the words of the brahmins, the king was delighted, and
> with joy he said these auspicious words to his ministers.

---
14.
gurūṇāṁ vacanācchīghraṁ sambhārāḥ sambhriyantu mē |
samarthādhiṣṭhitaścāśvaḥ sōpādhyāyō vimucyatām ||

> **Meaning 14:** "On the command of our preceptors, let the materials for the
> yajña be gathered without delay. Let the sacrificial horse be released,
> guarded by capable warriors and accompanied by an officiating priest."

---
15.
sarayvāścōttarē tīrē yajñabhūmirvidhīyatām |
śāntayaścābhivardhantāṁ yathākalpaṁ yathāvidhi ||

> **Meaning 15:** "Let the sacrificial ground be prepared on the northern bank
> of the Sarayū. Let the peace-invoking rites be performed in full, in
> accordance with tradition and sacred injunctions."

---
16.
śakyaḥ kartumayaṁ yajñaḥ sarvēṇāpi mahīkṣitā |
nāparādhō bhavētkaṣṭō yadyasminkratusattamē ||

> **Meaning 16:** "Any monarch could undertake this yajña, provided no grave
> flaw occurs in this supreme rite."

---
17.
chidraṁ hi mr̥gayantē'tra vidvāṁsō brahmarākṣasāḥ |
vidhihīnasya yajñasya sadyaḥ kartā vinaśyati ||

> **Meaning 17:** "For learned Brahmarākṣasas constantly watch for the slightest
> flaw; and if a yajña is compromised by a lapse in procedure, the performer is
> destroyed at once."

---
18.
tadyathā vidhipūrvaṁ mē kraturēṣa samāpyatē |
tathāvidhānaṁ kriyatāṁ samarthāḥ karaṇēṣviha ||

> **Meaning 18:** "Therefore, make all arrangements so that this yajña is
> completed strictly according to sacred ordinances, for you are all capable
> of carrying out these duties."

---
19.
tathēti ca tataḥ sarvē mantriṇaḥ pratyapūjayan |
pārthivēndrasya tadvākyaṁ yathājñaptamakurvata ||

> **Meaning 19:** Then all the ministers agreed, saying, "So be it," and carried
> out the king's words exactly as instructed.

---
20.
tatō dvijāstē dharmajñamastuvanpārthivarṣabham |
anujñātāstataḥ sarvē punarjagmuryathāgatam ||

> **Meaning 20:** The brahmins praised the king, who knew dharma, and taking
> their leave, departed for their respective hermitages.

---
21.
gatānāṁ tu dvijātīnāṁ mantriṇastānnarādhipaḥ |
visarjayitvā svaṁ vēśma pravivēśa mahādyutiḥ ||

> **Meaning 21:** After the brahmins had gone and he had dismissed the
> ministers, the radiant king entered his own palace.

---
