=== Sarga 36 ===
The Union of Śiva and Umā; Umā Curses the Devatās and the Earth

---
1.
ukta vākyē munau tasminnubhau rāghavalakṣmaṇau |
pratinandya kathāṁ vīrāvūcaturmunipuṅgavam ||

> **Meaning 1:** Once the muni had finished speaking, the two valiant princes,
> Rāghava and Lakṣmaṇa, praised his story and said to the foremost of munis:

---
2.
dharmayuktamidaṁ brahmankathitaṁ paramaṁ tvayā |
duhituḥ śailarājasya jyēṣṭhāya vaktumarhasi ||

> **Meaning 2:** "What you have told us, wise sage, is supreme and rooted in
> dharma. Please tell us more about Gaṅgā, the eldest daughter of the mountain
> king Himavān.

---
3.
vistaraṁ vistarajñō'si divyamānuṣasambhavam |
trīnpathō hētunā kēna pāvayēllōkapāvanī ||

> **Meaning 3:** "You know the full story of her divine and earthly origins. For
> what reason did she, who purifies the worlds, come to sanctify the three
> paths?

---
4.
kathaṁ gaṅgāṁ tripathagā viśrutā sariduttamā |
triṣu lōkēṣu dharmajña karmabhiḥ kaiḥ samanvitā ||

> **Meaning 4:** "How did Gaṅgā, foremost among rivers, become famed as
> Tripathagā, and through what deeds is she celebrated across the three worlds,
> Viśvāmitra, knower of dharma?"

---
5.
tathā bruvati kākutsthē viśvāmitrastapōdhanaḥ |
nikhilēna kathāṁ sarvāmr̥ṣimadhyē nyavēdayat ||

> **Meaning 5:** When Rāma asked this, Viśvāmitra, rich in tapas, recounted
> the whole story in detail before the assembled ṛṣis:

---
6.
purā rāma kr̥tōdvāhaḥ śitikaṇṭhō mahātapāḥ |
dr̥ṣṭvā ca spr̥hayā dēvīṁ maithunāyōpacakramē ||

> **Meaning 6:** "Long ago, Rāma, after his marriage, the dark-throated Śiva—
> great in tapas—gazed upon the goddess Umā with longing and began their wedded
> union.

---
7.
śitikaṇṭhasya dēvasya divyaṁ varṣaśataṁ gatam |
na cāpi tanayō rāma tasyāmāsītparantapa ||

> **Meaning 7:** "A hundred divine years passed for Śiva, yet no son was born
> to them, Rāma.

---
8.
tatō dēvāḥ samudvignāḥ pitāmahapurōgamāḥ |
yadihōtpadyatē bhūtaṁ kastatpratisahiṣyatē ||

> **Meaning 8:** "Seeing so much time pass, the devatās, led by Brahmā, grew
> deeply alarmed and asked themselves, 'If a child is born from such a union,
> who could possibly withstand its power?'

---
9.
abhigamya surāḥ sarvē praṇipatyēdamabruvan |
dēvadēva mahādēva lōkasyāsya hitē rata |
surāṇāṁ praṇipātēna prasādaṁ kartumarhasi ||

> **Meaning 9:** "All the devatās went to Śiva, bowed low, and pleaded: 'God of
> gods, Mahādeva, ever devoted to the world's welfare, accept our homage and be
> gracious to us.

---
10.
na lōkā dhārayiṣyanti tava tējaḥ surōttama |
brāhmēṇa tapasā yuktō dēvyā saha tapaścara ||

> **Meaning 10:** "'The worlds will not be able to bear your tejas, foremost of
> the devatās. Embrace supreme tapas and practice it together with the goddess
> Umā.

---
11.
trailōkyahitakāmārthaṁ tējastējasi dhāraya |
rakṣa sarvānimām̐llōkānnālōkaṁ kartumarhasi ||

> **Meaning 11:** "'For the good of all three worlds, hold your tejas within
> yourself. Protect all these realms—do not bring them to ruin!'

---
12.
dēvatānāṁ vacaḥ śrutvā sarvalōkamahēśvaraḥ |
bāḍhamityabravītsarvānpunaścēdamuvāca ha ||

> **Meaning 12:** "Hearing the devatās' plea, Maheśvara, ruler of all worlds,
> replied, 'So be it,' and then asked them:

---
13.
dhārayiṣyāmyahaṁ tējastējasyēva sahōmayā |
tridaśāḥ pr̥thivī caiva nirvāṇamadhigacchatu ||

> **Meaning 13:** "'Together with Umā, I will hold back and contain my tejas
> within myself. Let the devatās and the earth be at peace.

---
14.
yadidaṁ kṣubhitaṁ sthānānmama tējō hyanuttamam |
dhārayiṣyati kastanmē bruvantu surasattamāḥ ||

> **Meaning 14:** "'Still, this unsurpassed tejas of mine has already been
> stirred from its place—tell me, noblest of the devatās, who will receive and
> hold it?'

---
15.
ēvamuktāstatō dēvāḥ pratyūcurvr̥ṣabhadhvajam |
yattējaḥ kṣubhitaṁ hyētattaddharā dhārayiṣyati ||

> **Meaning 15:** "Addressed in this way, the devatās answered Śiva, whose banner
> bears the bull: 'The earth will receive and hold this tejas that has been
> stirred.'

---
16.
ēvamuktaḥ surapatiḥ pramumōca mahītalē |
tējasā pr̥thivī yēna vyāptā sagirikānanā ||

> **Meaning 16:** "Hearing this, Śiva, ruler of the devatās, released his tejas
> upon the earth, and that fiery power spread across the whole world with all
> its mountains and forests.

---
17.
tatō dēvāḥ punaridamūcuścātha hutāśanam |
praviśa tvaṁ mahātējō raudraṁ vāyusamanvitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 17:** "Then the devatās spoke again to Agni, receiver of oblations:
> 'Accompanied by Vāyu, enter into this mighty tejas of Rudra.'"

---
18.
tadagninā punarvyāptaṁ sañjātaḥ śvētaparvataḥ |
divyaṁ śaravaṇaṁ caiva pāvakādityasannibham |
yatra jātō mahātējāḥ kārtikēyō'gnisambhavaḥ ||

> **Meaning 18:** "When Agni pervaded that tejas, it formed a white mountain
> (Śveta) and a divine grove of śara reeds shining like fire and the sun—and
> there the supremely radiant Kārtikeya was born of Agni.

---
19.
athōmāṁ ca śivaṁ caiva dēvāḥ sarṣi gaṇāstadā |
pūjayāmāsuratyarthaṁ suprītamanasastataḥ ||

> **Meaning 19:** "Then the devatās and the assemblies of ṛṣis worshipped Umā and
> Śiva with deep reverence and joyful hearts.

---
20.
atha śaila sutā rāma tridaśānidamabravīt |
samanyuraśapatsarvānkrōdhasaṁraktalōcanā ||

> **Meaning 20:** "At that moment, Rāma, Pārvatī, the mountain's daughter,
> turned to the devatās, and—filled with indignation, her eyes red with anger—
> she pronounced a curse on them all:

---
21.
yasmānnivāritā caiva saṅgatā putrakāmyayā |
apatyaṁ svēṣu dārēṣu nōtpādayitumarhatha ||

> **Meaning 21:** "'Because you interfered when I was with my husband, longing
> for a son, none of you will be able to father children with your own wives.

---
22.
adya prabhr̥ti yuṣmākamaprajāḥ santu patnayaḥ |
ēvamuktvā surānsarvāñśaśāpa pr̥thivīmapi ||

> **Meaning 22:** "'From this day on, may your wives remain childless.' Having
> cursed all the devatās in this way, she cursed the earth as well:

---
23.
avanē naikarūpā tvaṁ bahubhāryā bhaviṣyasi ||

> **Meaning 23:** "'Earth, you will take on many forms and become the wife of
> many kings!

---
24.
na ca putrakr̥tāṁ prītiṁ matkrōdhakaluṣī kr̥tā |
prāpsyasi tvaṁ sudurmēdhē mama putramanicchatī ||

> **Meaning 24:** "'Blighted by my anger, foolish earth, you will never know the
> joy of a son of your own, since you refused to let me have mine.'

---
25.
tānsarvānvrīḍitāndr̥ṣṭvā surānsurapatistadā |
gamanāyōpacakrāma diśaṁ varuṇapālitām ||

> **Meaning 25:** "Seeing all the devatās standing there abashed, Śiva, ruler of
> the devatās, prepared to depart toward the western quarter guarded by Varuṇa.

---
26.
sa gatvā tapa ātiṣṭhatpārśvē tasyōttarē girēḥ |
himavatprabhavē śr̥ṅgē saha dēvyā mahēśvaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 26:** "Arriving there, Maheśvara settled into tapas together with Umā
> on the northern slope of a peak in the Himavat range.

---
27.
ēṣa tē vistarō rāma śailaputryā nivēditaḥ |
gaṅgāyāḥ prabhavaṁ caiva śr̥ṇu mē sahalakṣmaṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 27:** "I have now told you, Rāma, the full story of Pārvatī, the
> mountain's daughter. Now listen, along with Lakṣmaṇa, as I tell you how Gaṅgā
> came forth."

---
