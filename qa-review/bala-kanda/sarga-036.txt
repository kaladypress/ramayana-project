=== Sarga 36 ===

---
1.
tapyamāne tapo deve devāḥ sarṣigaṇāḥ purā |
senāpatim abhīpsantaḥ pitāmaham upāgaman ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tato ’bruvan surāḥ sarve bhagavantaṁ pitāmaham |
praṇipatya śubhaṁ vākyaṁ sendrāḥ sāgnipurogamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
yo naḥ senāpatir deva datto bhagavatā purā |
sa tapaḥ param āsthāya tapyate sma sahomayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
yad atrānantaraṁ kāryaṁ lokānāṁ hitakāmyayā |
saṁvidhatsva vidhānajña tvaṁ hi naḥ paramā gatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
devatānāṁ vacaḥ śrutvā sarvalokapitāmahaḥ |
sāntvayan madhurair vākyais tridaśān idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
śailaputryā yad uktaṁ tan na prajāsyatha patniṣu |
tasyā vacanam akliṣṭaṁ satyam eva na saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
iyam ākāśagā gaṅgā yasyāṁ putraṁ hutāśanaḥ |
janayiṣyati devānāṁ senāpatim ariṁdamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
jyeṣṭhā śailendraduhitā mānayiṣyati taṁ sutam |
umāyās tad bahumataṁ bhaviṣyati na saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tac chrutvā vacanaṁ tasya kr̥tārthā raghunandana |
praṇipatya surāḥ sarve pitāmaham apūjayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
te gatvā parvataṁ rāma kailāsaṁ dhātumaṇḍitam |
agniṁ niyojayām āsuḥ putrārthaṁ sarvadevatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
devakāryam idaṁ deva samādhatsva hutāśana |
śailaputryāṁ mahātejo gaṅgāyāṁ teja utsr̥ja ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
devatānāṁ pratijñāya gaṅgām abhyetya pāvakaḥ |
garbhaṁ dhāraya vai devi devatānām idaṁ priyam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
ity etad vacanaṁ śrutvā divyaṁ rūpam adhārayat |
sa tasyā mahimāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā samantād avakīryata ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
samantatas tadā devīm abhyaṣiñcata pāvakaḥ |
sarvasrotāṁsi pūrṇāni gaṅgāyā raghunandana ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tam uvāca tato gaṅgā sarvadevapurohitam |
aśaktā dhāraṇe deva tava tejaḥ samuddhatam |
dahyamānāgninā tena saṁpravyathitacetanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
athābravīd idaṁ gaṅgāṁ sarvadevahutāśanaḥ |
iha haimavate pāde garbho ’yaṁ saṁniveśyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
śrutvā tv agnivaco gaṅgā taṁ garbham atibhāsvaram |
utsasarja mahātejāḥ srotobhyo hi tadānagha ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
yad asyā nirgataṁ tasmāt taptajāmbūnadaprabham |
kāñcanaṁ dharaṇīṁ prāptaṁ hiraṇyam amalaṁ śubham ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tāmraṁ kārṣṇāyasaṁ caiva taikṣṇyād evābhijāyata |
malaṁ tasyābhavat tatra trapusīsakam eva ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tad etad dharaṇīṁ prāpya nānādhātur avardhata ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
nikṣiptamātre garbhe tu tejobhir abhirañjitam |
sarvaṁ parvatasaṁnaddhaṁ sauvarṇam abhavad vanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
jātarūpam iti khyātaṁ tadā prabhr̥ti rāghava |
suvarṇaṁ puruṣavyāghra hutāśanasamaprabham ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
taṁ kumāraṁ tato jātaṁ sendrāḥ sahamarudgaṇāḥ |
kṣīrasaṁbhāvanārthāya kr̥ttikāḥ samayojayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tāḥ kṣīraṁ jātamātrasya kr̥tvā samayam uttamam |
daduḥ putro ’yam asmākaṁ sarvāsām iti niścitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tatas tu devatāḥ sarvāḥ kārtikeya iti bruvan |
putras trailokya vikhyāto bhaviṣyati na saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
teṣāṁ tad vacanaṁ śrutvā skannaṁ garbhaparisrave |
snāpayan parayā lakṣmyā dīpyamānam ivānalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
skanda ity abruvan devāḥ skannaṁ garbhaparisravāt |
kārtikeyaṁ mahābhāgaṁ kākutsthajvalanopamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
prādurbhūtaṁ tataḥ kṣīraṁ kr̥ttikānām anuttamam |
ṣaṇṇāṁ ṣaḍānano bhūtvā jagrāha stanajaṁ payaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
gr̥hītvā kṣīram ekāhnā sukumāra vapus tadā |
ajayat svena vīryeṇa daityasainyagaṇān vibhuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
surasenāgaṇapatiṁ tatas tam amaladyutim |
abhyaṣiñcan suragaṇāḥ sametyāgnipurogamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
eṣa te rāma gaṅgāyā vistaro ’bhihito mayā |
kumārasaṁbhavaś caiva dhanyaḥ puṇyas tathaiva ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
