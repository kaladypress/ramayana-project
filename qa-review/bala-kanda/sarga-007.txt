=== Sarga 7 ===

---
1.
aṣṭau babhūvur vīrasya tasyāmātyā yaśasvinaḥ |
śucayaś cānuraktāś ca rājakr̥tyeṣu nityaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
dhr̥ṣṭir jayanto vijayaḥ siddhārtho arthasādhakaḥ |
aśoko mantrapālaś ca sumantraś cāṣṭamo ’bhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
r̥tvijau dvāv abhimatau tasyāstām r̥ṣisattamau |
vasiṣṭho vāmadevaś ca mantriṇaś ca tathāpare ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
śrīmantaś ca mahātmānaḥ śāstrajñā dr̥ḍhavikramāḥ |
kīrtimantaḥ praṇihitā yathāvacanakāriṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tejaḥkṣamāyaśaḥprāptāḥ smitapūrvābhibhāṣiṇaḥ |
krodhāt kāmārthahetor vā na brūyur anr̥taṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
teṣām aviditaṁ kiṁ cit sveṣu nāsti pareṣu vā |
kriyamāṇaṁ kr̥taṁ vāpi cāreṇāpi cikīrṣitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
kuśalā vyavahāreṣu sauhr̥deṣu parīkṣitāḥ |
prāptakālaṁ yathā daṇḍaṁ dhārayeyuḥ suteṣv api ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
kośasaṁgrahaṇe yuktā balasya ca parigrahe |
ahitaṁ cāpi puruṣaṁ na vihiṁsyur adūṣakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
vīrāś ca niyatotsāhā rājaśāstram anuṣṭhitāḥ |
śucīnāṁ rakṣitāraś ca nityaṁ viṣayavāsinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
brahmakṣatram ahiṁsantas te kośaṁ samapūrayan |
sutīkṣṇadaṇḍāḥ saṁprekṣya puruṣasya balābalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
śucīnām ekabuddhīnāṁ sarveṣāṁ saṁprajānatām |
nāsīt pure vā rāṣṭre vā mr̥ṣāvādī naraḥ kva cit ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
kaś cin na duṣṭas tatrāsīt paradāraratir naraḥ |
praśāntaṁ sarvam evāsīd rāṣṭraṁ puravaraṁ ca tat ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
suvāsasaḥ suveśāś ca te ca sarve suśīlinaḥ |
hitārthaṁ ca narendrasya jāgrato nayacakṣuṣā ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
gurau guṇagr̥hītāś ca prakhyātāś ca parākramaiḥ |
videśeṣv api vijñātāḥ sarvato buddhiniścayāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
īdr̥śais tair amātyais tu rājā daśaratho ’naghaḥ |
upapanno guṇopetair anvaśāsad vasuṁdharām ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
avekṣamāṇaś cāreṇa prajā dharmeṇa rañjayan |
nādhyagacchad viśiṣṭaṁ vā tulyaṁ vā śatrum ātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tair mantribhir mantrahitair niviṣṭair; vr̥to ’nuraktaiḥ kuśalaiḥ samarthaiḥ |
sa pārthivo dīptim avāpa yuktas; tejomayair gobhir ivodito ’rkaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
