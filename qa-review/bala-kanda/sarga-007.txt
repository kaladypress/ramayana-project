=== Sarga 7 ===
The Virtues and Excellence of King Daśaratha's Ministers

---
1.
tasyāmātyā guṇairāsannikṣvākōstu mahātmanaḥ |
mantrajñāścēṅgitajñāśca nityaṁ priyahitē ratāḥ ||

> **Meaning 1:** The great Ikṣvāku king Daśaratha had ministers who were
> distinguished by their virtues. They understood counsel, could read a person's
> gestures and expressions, and were always devoted to the king's welfare and
> wishes.

---
2.
aṣṭau babhūvurvīrasya tasyāmātyā yaśasvinaḥ |
śucayaścānuraktāśca rājakr̥tyēṣu nityaśaḥ ||

> **Meaning 2:** That heroic king had eight renowned ministers. They were pure
> in conduct and loyal, and they always attended to the duties of the crown.

---
3.
dhr̥ṣṭirjayantō vijayaḥ siddhārthō arthasādhakaḥ |
aśōkō mantrapālaśca sumantraścāṣṭamō'bhavat ||

> **Meaning 3:** They were Dhr̥ṣṭi, Jayanta, Vijaya, Siddhārtha, Arthasādhaka,
> Aśoka and Mantrapāla, and Sumantra was the eighth.

---
4.
r̥tvijau dvāvabhimatau tasyāstāmr̥ṣisattamau |
vasiṣṭhō vāmadēvaśca mantriṇaśca tathāparē ||

> **Meaning 4:** Two excellent sages, Vasiṣṭha and Vāmadeva, were his honored
> priests, and there were other counselors as well.

---
5.
vidyāvinītā hrīmantaḥ kuśalā niyatēndriyāḥ |
śrīmantaśca mahātmānaḥ śāstrajñā dr̥ḍhavikramāḥ ||

> **Meaning 5:** They were learned and well trained, modest, skilled and
> self-controlled. They were prosperous and noble-minded, masters of the
> śāstras, and firm in valor.

---
6.
kīrtimantaḥ praṇihitā yathāvacanakāriṇaḥ |
tējaḥkṣamāyaśaḥprāptāḥ smitapūrvābhibhāṣiṇaḥ ||

> **Meaning 6:** They were famous and attentive, and they carried out
> instructions exactly as given. They had gained splendor, patience and fame,
> and they always smiled warmly before speaking.

---
7.
krōdhātkāmārthahētōrvā na brūyuranr̥taṁ vacaḥ |
tēṣāmaviditaṁ kiñcitsvēṣu nāsti parēṣu vā |
kriyamāṇaṁ kr̥taṁ vāpi cārēṇāpi cikīrṣitam ||

> **Meaning 7:** Not even out of anger, desire or self-interest would they say
> anything false. Nothing was unknown to them, whether it concerned their own
> people or others, whether it was being done, already done, or even only
> planned, because they kept watch through spies.

---
8.
kuśalā vyavahārēṣu sauhr̥dēṣu parīkṣitāḥ |
prāptakālaṁ yathā daṇḍaṁ dhārayēyuḥ sutēṣvapi ||

> **Meaning 8:** They were skilled in practical affairs and tested in
> friendship. When the time came, they would impose punishment even on their own
> sons.

---
9.
kōśasaṅgrahaṇē yuktā balasya ca parigrahē |
ahitaṁ cāpi puruṣaṁ na vihiṁsyuradūṣakam ||

> **Meaning 9:** They were diligent in filling the treasury and in maintaining
> the army. They would not harm even an enemy if he was innocent.

---
10.
vīrāśca niyatōtsāhā rājaśāstramanuṣṭhitāḥ |
śucīnāṁ rakṣitāraśca nityaṁ viṣayavāsinām ||

> **Meaning 10:** They were brave and always energetic, and they followed the
> science of kingship. They constantly protected the honest people who lived in
> the realm.

---
11.
brahmakṣatramahiṁsantastē kōśaṁ samapūrayan |
sutīkṣṇadaṇḍāḥ samprēkṣya puruṣasya balābalam ||

> **Meaning 11:** Without harming brāhmaṇas or kṣatriyas, they filled the
> treasury. They weighed a person's strength and weakness first, and then
> punished severely when needed.

---
12.
śucīnāmēkabuddhīnāṁ sarvēṣāṁ samprajānatām ||

> **Meaning 12:** They were honest, single-minded, and all-knowing in what they
> did.

---
13.
nāsītpurē vā rāṣṭrē vā mr̥ṣāvādī naraḥ kvacit |
kaścinna duṣṭastatrāsītparadāraratirnaraḥ ||

> **Meaning 13:** There was no liar anywhere, in the city or in the kingdom. No
> one there was wicked, and no man took pleasure in another man's wife.

---
14.
praśāntaṁ sarvamēvāsīdrāṣṭraṁ puravaraṁ ca tat |
suvāsasaḥ suvēśāśca tē ca sarvē suśīlinaḥ ||

> **Meaning 14:** The whole kingdom and its excellent capital were peaceful. All
> the people were well dressed and well groomed, and all of good character.

---
15.
hitārthaṁ ca narēndrasya jāgratō nayacakṣuṣā |
gurau guṇagr̥hītāśca prakhyātāśca parākramaiḥ ||

> **Meaning 15:** For the good of the king they stayed alert, with policy as
> their guiding eye. They had won their guru's approval for their virtues and
> were famous for their courage.

---
16.
vidēśēṣvapi vijñātāḥ sarvatō buddhiniścayāt |
sandhivigrahatattvajñāḥ prakr̥tyā sampadānvitāḥ ||

> **Meaning 16:** They were known even in foreign lands, because their judgments
> were sound everywhere. They understood the truth about making peace and waging
> war, and they were naturally rich in good qualities.

---
17.
mantrasaṁvaraṇē śaktāḥ śaktāḥ sūkṣmāsu buddhiṣu |
nītiśāstraviśēṣajñāḥ satataṁ priyavādinaḥ ||

> **Meaning 17:** They were able to keep counsel secret and sharp in subtle
> matters of reasoning. They knew the finer points of the science of statecraft
> and always spoke pleasantly.

---
18.
īdr̥śaistairamātyaistu rājā daśarathō'naghaḥ |
upapannō guṇōpētairanvaśāsadvasundharām ||

> **Meaning 18:** With such ministers, rich in virtue and ability, the faultless
> King Daśaratha ruled the earth.

---
19.
avēkṣamāṇaścārēṇa prajā dharmēṇa rañjayan |
prajānāṁ pālanaṁ kurvannadharmaṁ parivarjayan ||

> **Meaning 19:** He watched over his people through spies and delighted them by
> following dharma. He protected his subjects and avoided adharma.

---
20.
viśrutastriṣu lōkēṣu vadānyaḥ satyasaṅgaraḥ |
sa tatra puruṣavyāghraḥ śaśāsa pr̥thivīmimām ||

> **Meaning 20:** Famous in the three worlds, generous and true to his word,
> that tiger among men ruled this earth.

---
21.
nādhyagacchadviśiṣṭaṁ vā tulyaṁ vā śatrumātmanaḥ |
mitravānnatasāmantaḥ pratāpahatakaṇṭakaḥ |
sa śaśāsa jagadrājā divaṁ dēvapatiryathā ||

> **Meaning 21:** He found no enemy who was superior or even equal to himself.
> He had friends, his neighboring chiefs bowed to him, and his might had
> eliminated every threat. That king ruled the world as Indra, the ruler of the
> devatās, rules the heavens.

---
22.
tairmantribhirmantrahitairniviṣṭairvr̥tō'nuraktaiḥ kuśalaiḥ samarthaiḥ |
sa pārthivō dīptimavāpa yuktastējōmayairgōbhirivōditō'rkaḥ ||

> **Meaning 22:** Surrounded by those ministers, who were devoted to good
> counsel, loyal, skilled and capable, King Daśaratha shone with splendor, like
> the sun rising with its radiant rays.

---
