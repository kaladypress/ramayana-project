=== Sarga 19 ===

---
1.
tac chrutvā rājaśārdūla viśvāmitrasya bhāṣitam |
muhūrtam iva niḥsaṁjñaḥ saṁjñāvān idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
ūnaṣoḍaśavarṣo me rāmo rājīvalocanaḥ |
na yuddhayogyatām asya paśyāmi saha rākṣasaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
iyam akṣauhiṇī pūrṇā yasyāhaṁ patir īśvaraḥ |
anayā saṁvr̥to gatvā yodhāhaṁ tair niśācaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
ime śūrāś ca vikrāntā bhr̥tyā me ’straviśāradāḥ |
yogyā rakṣogaṇair yoddhuṁ na rāmaṁ netum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
aham eva dhanuṣpāṇir goptā samaramūrdhani |
yāvat prāṇān dhariṣyāmi tāvad yotsye niśācaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
nirvighnā vratacaryā sā bhaviṣyati surakṣitā |
ahaṁ tatra gamiṣyāmi na rāma netum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
bālo hy akr̥tavidyaś ca na ca vetti balābalam |
na cāstrabalasaṁyukto na ca yuddhaviśāradaḥ |
na cāsau rakṣasāṁ yogyaḥ kūṭayuddhā hi te dhruvam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
viprayukto hi rāmeṇa muhūrtam api notsahe |
jīvituṁ muniśārdūla na rāmaṁ netum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
yadi vā rāghavaṁ brahman netum icchasi suvrata |
caturaṅgasamāyuktaṁ mayā saha ca taṁ naya ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
ṣaṣṭir varṣasahasrāṇi jātasya mama kauśika |
duḥkhenotpāditaś cāyaṁ na rāmaṁ netum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
caturṇām ātmajānāṁ hi prītiḥ paramikā mama |
jyeṣṭhaṁ dharmapradhānaṁ ca na rāmaṁ netum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
kiṁ vīryā rākṣasās te ca kasya putrāś ca ke ca te |
kathaṁ pramāṇāḥ ke caitān rakṣanti munipuṁgava ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
kathaṁ ca pratikartavyaṁ teṣāṁ rāmeṇa rakṣasām |
māmakair vā balair brahman mayā vā kūṭayodhinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sarvaṁ me śaṁsa bhagavan kathaṁ teṣāṁ mayā raṇe |
sthātavyaṁ duṣṭabhāvānāṁ vīryotsiktā hi rākṣasāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā viśvāmitro ’bhyabhāṣata |
paulastyavaṁśaprabhavo rāvaṇo nāma rākṣasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sa brahmaṇā dattavaras trailokyaṁ bādhate bhr̥śam |
mahābalo mahāvīryo rākṣasair bahubhir vr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
śrūyate hi mahāvīryo rāvaṇo rākṣasādhipaḥ |
sākṣād vaiśravaṇabhrātā putro viśravaso muneḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
yadā svayaṁ na yajñasya vighnakartā mahābalaḥ |
tena saṁcoditau tau tu rākṣasau sumahābalau |
mārīcaś ca subāhuś ca yajñavighnaṁ kariṣyataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
ity ukto muninā tena rājovāca muniṁ tadā |
na hi śakto ’smi saṁgrāme sthātuṁ tasya durātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
sa tvaṁ prasādaṁ dharmajña kuruṣva mama putrake |
devadānavagandharvā yakṣāḥ patagapannagāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
na śaktā rāvaṇaṁ soḍhuṁ kiṁ punar mānavā yudhi |
sa hi vīryavatāṁ vīryam ādatte yudhi rākṣasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tena cāhaṁ na śakto ’smi saṁyoddhuṁ tasya vā balaiḥ |
sabalo vā muniśreṣṭha sahito vā mamātmajaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
katham apy amaraprakhyaṁ saṁgrāmāṇām akovidam |
bālaṁ me tanayaṁ brahman naiva dāsyāmi putrakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
atha kālopamau yuddhe sutau sundopasundayoḥ |
yajñavighnakarau tau te naiva dāsyāmi putrakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
mārīcaś ca subāhuś ca vīryavantau suśikṣitau |
tayor anyatareṇāhaṁ yoddhā syāṁ sasuhr̥dgaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
