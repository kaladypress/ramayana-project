=== Sarga 19 ===
Viśvāmitra Asks for Rāma to Protect His Yajña

---
1.
tacchrutvā rājasiṁhasya vākyamadbhutavistaram |
hr̥ṣṭarōmā mahātējā viśvāmitrō'bhyabhāṣata ||

> **Meaning 1:** Hearing those vast and wondrous words of the king, the radiant
> Viśvāmitra was thrilled, his hair standing on end, and he replied.

---
2.
sadr̥śaṁ rājaśārdūla tavaitadbhuvi nānyataḥ |
mahāvaṁśaprasūtasya vasiṣṭhavyapadēśinaḥ ||

> **Meaning 2:** Mahārāja, what you have said is worthy of you, and you will
> find nothing like it anywhere else on earth. You were born in a great lineage,
> and Vasiṣṭha is your guide.

---
3.
yattu mē hr̥dgataṁ vākyaṁ tasya kāryasya niścayam |
kuruṣva rājaśārdūla bhava satyapratiśravaḥ ||

> **Meaning 3:** Now hear the matter that is in my heart, and decide to do what
> I ask. Mahārāja, stay true to your word.

---
4.
ahaṁ niyamamātiṣṭhē siddhyarthaṁ puruṣarṣabha |
tasya vighnakarau dvau tu rākṣasau kāmarūpiṇau ||

> **Meaning 4:** Best of men, I am bound by the consecrated vow of a yajña,
> and I seek its completion. But two Rākṣasas who can take any form are
> obstructing it.

---
5.
vratē mē bahuśaścīrṇē samāptyāṁ rākṣasāvimau |
mārīcaśca subāhuśca vīryavantau suśikṣitau ||

> **Meaning 5:** Many times my vow has been carried out, and each time it was
> about to be completed, these two Rākṣasas, Mārīca and Subāhu, powerful and
> well trained, came to ruin it.

---
6.
māṁsarudhiraughēṇa vēdiṁ tāmabhyavarṣatām |
avadhūtē tathā bhūtē tasminniyamaniścayē ||

> **Meaning 6:** When my vow was thrown into disorder like this, they poured a
> flood of flesh and blood over that altar.

---
7.
kr̥taśramō nirutsāhastasmāddēśādapākramē |
na ca mē krōdhamutsraṣṭuṁ buddhirbhavati pārthiva |
tathābhūtā hi sā caryā na śāpastatra mucyatē ||

> **Meaning 7:** Exhausted by my efforts and disheartened, I left that place.
> King, I am not willing to unleash my anger either, because the discipline of
> this yajña vow forbids pronouncing any curse during it.

---
8.
svaputraṁ rājaśārdūla rāmaṁ satyaparākramam ||

> **Meaning 8:** Mahārāja, send your son Rāma with me, whose valor never fails.

---
9.
kākapakṣadharaṁ śūraṁ jyēṣṭhaṁ mē dātumarhasi |
śaktō hyēṣa mayā guptō divyēna svēna tējasā ||

> **Meaning 9:** He is your eldest, brave even as a youth with flowing locks —
> you should send him with me. Protected by me, he is fully capable, through
> his own divine radiance, of destroying them.

---
10.
rākṣasā yē vikartārastēṣāmapi vināśanē |
śrēyaścāsmai pradāsyāmi bahurūpaṁ na saṁśayaḥ ||

> **Meaning 10:** He is capable of destroying those Rākṣasas who cause
> this ruin. And I will bestow upon him great good in many forms — there is no
> doubt of it.

---
11.
trayāṇāmapi lōkānāṁ yēna khyātiṁ gamiṣyati |
na ca tau rāmamāsādya śaktau sthātuṁ kathañcana ||

> **Meaning 11:** Through this he will gain fame throughout all three worlds.
> And once those two confront Rāma, they will not be able to stand their ground
> in any way.

---
12.
na ca tau rāghavādanyō hantumutsahatē pumān |
vīryōtsiktau hi tau pāpau kālapāśavaśaṁ gatau ||

> **Meaning 12:** No man other than Rāghava has the strength to kill them. Those
> two sinners, swollen with pride in their strength, have fallen under the noose
> of Death.

---
13.
rāmasya rājaśārdūla na paryāptau mahātmanaḥ |
na ca putrakr̥taṁ snēhaṁ kartumarhasi pārthiva ||

> **Meaning 13:** Mahārāja, they are no match for the great-souled Rāma. And you
> should not let affection for your son hold you back.

---
14.
ahaṁ tē pratijānāmi hatau tau viddhi rākṣasau |
ahaṁ vēdmi mahātmānaṁ rāmaṁ satyaparākramam |
vasiṣṭhō'pi mahātējā yē cēmē tapasi sthitāḥ ||

> **Meaning 14:** I give you my word — consider those two Rākṣasas as good as
> dead. I truly know the great-souled Rāma and his unfailing valor, and so do
> the radiant Vasiṣṭha and all these sages steadfast in tapas.

---
15.
yadi tē dharmalābhaṁ ca yaśaśca paramaṁ bhuvi |
sthiramicchasi rājēndra rāmaṁ mē dātumarhasi ||

> **Meaning 15:** Mahārāja, if you wish for dharma to flourish and for supreme,
> lasting fame on earth, you should send Rāma with me.

---
16.
yadyabhyanujñāṁ kākutstha dadatē tava mantriṇaḥ |
vasiṣṭhapramukhāḥ sarvē tatō rāmaṁ visarjaya ||

> **Meaning 16:** Kākutstha, if all your ministers, led by Vasiṣṭha, give their
> consent, then send Rāma with me.

---
17.
abhiprētamasaṁsaktamātmajaṁ dātumarhasi |
daśarātraṁ hi yajñasya rāmaṁ rājīvalōcanam ||

> **Meaning 17:** Let go of attachment and willingly send your lotus-eyed son
> Rāma with me for the ten nights of the yajña.

---
18.
nātyēti kālō yajñasya yathāyaṁ mama rāghava |
tathā kuruṣva bhadraṁ tē mā ca śōkē manaḥ kr̥thāḥ ||

> **Meaning 18:** Rāghava, act in time so that the appointed hour of my yajña
> does not slip past. May all go well with you, and do not give way to grief.

---
19.
ityēvamuktvā dharmātmā dharmārthasahitaṁ vacaḥ |
virarāma mahātējā viśvāmitrō mahāmuniḥ ||

> **Meaning 19:** After speaking these words, full of dharma and good purpose,
> the radiant and righteous great sage Viśvāmitra fell silent.

---
20.
sa tanniśamya rājēndrō viśvāmitravacaḥ śubham |
śōkam abhyagamattīvraṁ vyasīdata bhayānvitaḥ |

> **Meaning 20:** Hearing Viśvāmitra's noble words, the king was overcome with
> deep sorrow and sank down, gripped by fear.

---
21.
iti hr̥dayamanōvidāraṇaṁ; munivacanaṁ tadatīva śuśruvān |
narapatiragamadbhayaṁ maha;dvyathitamanāḥ pracacāla cāsanāt ||

> **Meaning 21:** Hearing the sage's words, which tore at his heart and mind,
> the king was seized by great fear. Deeply shaken, he stirred from his seat.

---
