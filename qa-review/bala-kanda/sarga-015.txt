=== Sarga 15 ===
The Devas' Prayer to Viṣṇu for Deliverance from Rāvaṇa

---
1.
mēdhāvī tu tatō dhyātvā sa kiñcididamuttamam |
labdhasañjñastatastaṁ tu vēdajñō nr̥pamabravīt ||

> **Meaning 1:** The wise sage Ṛśyaśṛṅga, who knew the Vedas, meditated for a
> little while. When he came out of his meditation, he spoke these excellent
> words to the king:

---
2.
iṣṭiṁ tē'haṁ kariṣyāmi putrīyāṁ putrakāraṇāt |
atharvaśirasi prōktairmantraiḥ siddhāṁ vidhānataḥ ||

> **Meaning 2:** "I will perform the Putrakāmeṣṭi rite for you for the sake of
> sons. It will be carried out according to the prescribed rules, with the
> mantras taught in the Atharvaśiras, and it will surely bear fruit."

---
3.
tataḥ prākramadiṣṭiṁ tāṁ putrīyāṁ putra kāraṇāt |
juhāva cāgnau tējasvī mantradr̥ṣṭēna karmaṇā ||

> **Meaning 3:** Then the radiant sage began that Putrakāmeṣṭi rite for the sake
> of sons. He offered oblations into the sacred fire, following the rites
> revealed in the mantras.

---
4.
tatō dēvāḥ sagandharvāḥ siddhāśca paramarṣayaḥ |
bhāgapratigrahārthaṁ vai samavētā yathāvidhi ||

> **Meaning 4:** Then the devatās, along with the Gandharvas, the Siddhas, and
> the great ṛṣis, gathered there in the prescribed manner to accept their
> offerings.

---
5.
tāḥ samētya yathānyāyaṁ tasminsadasi dēvatāḥ |
abruvam̐llōkakartāraṁ brahmāṇaṁ vacanaṁ mahat ||

> **Meaning 5:** Taking their assigned places in that sacrificial assembly, the
> devatās addressed these momentous words to Brahmā, the creator of the worlds:

---
6.
bhagavaṁstvatprasādēna rāvaṇō nāma rākṣasaḥ |
sarvānnō bādhatē vīryācchāsituṁ taṁ na śaknumaḥ ||

> **Meaning 6:** "Bhagavān, empowered by the grace you bestowed on him, a
> Rākṣasa named Rāvaṇa is oppressing all of us with his might, and we are
> unable to restrain him.

---
7.
tvayā tasmai varō dattaḥ prītēna bhagavanpurā |
mānayantaśca taṁ nityaṁ sarvaṁ tasya kṣamāmahē ||

> **Meaning 7:** Long ago, Bhagavān, you were pleased with him and gave him a
> boon. Out of respect for that boon, we always put up with everything he does.

---
8.
udvējayati lōkāṁstrīnucchritāndvēṣṭi durmatiḥ |
śakraṁ tridaśarājānaṁ pradharṣayitumicchati ||

> **Meaning 8:** That wicked-minded one terrorizes the three worlds and hates
> anyone who rises high. He even wants to overpower Śakra, the king of the
> devatās.

---
9.
r̥ṣīnyakṣānsagandharvānasurānbrāhmaṇāṁstathā |
atikrāmati durdharṣō varadānēna mōhitaḥ ||

> **Meaning 9:** Unassailable and blinded by the boon, he tramples over ṛṣis,
> Yakṣas, Gandharvas, Asuras, and Brāhmaṇas alike.

---
10.
nainaṁ sūryaḥ pratapati pārśvē vāti na mārutaḥ |
calōrmimālī taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā samudrō'pi na kampatē ||

> **Meaning 10:** The sun reduces his burning heat when shining on him, and the
> wind does not blow harshly near him. Even the ocean, garlanded with rolling
> waves, stills its waters when it sees him.

---
11.
tanmahannō bhayaṁ tasmādrākṣasādghōradarśanāt |
vadhārthaṁ tasya bhagavannupāyaṁ kartumarhasi ||

> **Meaning 11:** So we live in great fear of that terrible-looking Rākṣasa.
> Bhagavān, please find a way to kill him."

---
12.
ēvamuktaḥ suraiḥ sarvaiścintayitvā tatō'bravīt |
hantāyaṁ vihitastasya vadhōpāyō durātmanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 12:** When all the devatās spoke to him like this, Brahmā reflected
> for a moment and said: "Ah! The means to destroy that evil-minded one has
> occurred to me.

---
13.
tēna gandharvayakṣāṇāṁ dēvadānavarakṣasām |
avadhyō'smīti vāguktā tathētyuktaṁ ca tanmayā ||

> **Meaning 13:** He asked me, 'Let me be unkillable by Gandharvas, Yakṣas,
> Devas, Dānavas, and Rākṣasas,' and I said, 'So be it.'

---
14.
nākīrtayadavajñānāttadrakṣō mānuṣāṁstadā |
tasmātsa mānuṣādvadhyō mr̥tyurnānyō'sya vidyatē ||

> **Meaning 14:** Out of contempt, that Rākṣasa did not mention humans at the
> time. So he can be killed by a human. There is no other death for him."

---
15.
ētacchrutvā priyaṁ vākyaṁ brahmaṇā samudāhr̥tam |
dēvā maharṣayaḥ sarvē prahr̥ṣṭāstē'bhavaṁstadā ||

> **Meaning 15:** When they heard these welcome words spoken by Brahmā, all the
> devatās and great ṛṣis were overjoyed.

---
16.
ētasminnantarē viṣṇurupayātō mahādyutiḥ |
śaṅkhacakragadāpāṇiḥ pītavāsā jagatpatiḥ ||

> **Meaning 16:** Just then, Śrī Mahāviṣṇu, the protector of the worlds,
> arrived there glowing with immense radiance, holding the conch, discus, and
> mace in his hands, and dressed in yellow silks.

---
17.
brahmaṇā ca samāgamya tatra tasthau samāhitaḥ |
tamabruvansurāḥ sarvē samabhiṣṭūya sannatāḥ ||

> **Meaning 17:** Meeting with Brahmā, he stood there composed. Then all the
> devatās bowed low in reverence, praised him with hymns, and said:

---
18.
tvāṁ niyōkṣyāmahē viṣṇō lōkānāṁ hitakāmyayā |
rājñō daśarathasya tvamayōdhyādhipatērvibhō ||

> **Meaning 18:** "Śrī Mahāviṣṇu, for the welfare of the worlds, we pray
> that you undertake a mission. O all-pervading one, to King Daśaratha,
> ruler of Ayodhyā—

---
19.
dharmajñasya vadānyasya maharṣisamatējasaḥ |
tasya bhāryāsu tisr̥ṣu hrīśrīkīrtyupamāsu ca ||

> **Meaning 19:** who knows dharma, is generous, and equals the great ṛṣis in
> radiance—through his three wives, who resemble Hrī, Śrī, and Kīrti—modesty,
> prosperity, and fame—

---
20.
viṣṇō putratvamāgaccha kr̥tvātmānaṁ caturvidham |
tatra tvaṁ mānuṣō bhūtvā pravr̥ddhaṁ lōkakaṇṭakam |
avadhyaṁ daivatairviṣṇō samarē jahi rāvaṇam ||

> **Meaning 20:** Viṣṇu, manifest yourself in four forms and be born as his
> sons! Taking human form there, destroy in battle Rāvaṇa—that overgrown
> thorn to the worlds, whom the devatās cannot kill."

---
21.
sa hi dēvānsagandharvānsiddhāṁśca r̥ṣisattamān |
rākṣasō rāvaṇō mūrkhō vīryōtsēkēna bādhatē ||

> **Meaning 21:** That foolish Rākṣasa Rāvaṇa, drunk on his own strength,
> torments the devatās, the Gandharvas, the Siddhas, and the best of the ṛṣis.

---
22.
r̥ṣayaśca tatastēna gandharvāpsarasastathā |
krīḍantō nandanavanē raudrēṇa vinipātitāḥ ||

> **Meaning 22:** That savage one has struck down ṛṣis, and attacked Gandharvas
> and Apsarases wandering at ease in the Nandana gardens.

---
23.
vadhārthaṁ vayamāyātāstasya vai munibhiḥ saha |
siddhagandharvayakṣāśca tatastvāṁ śaraṇaṁ gatāḥ ||

> **Meaning 23:** We have come here with the munis to bring about his death. The
> Siddhas, Gandharvas, and Yakṣas have also come to you for refuge.

---
24.
tvaṁ gatiḥ paramā dēva sarvēṣāṁ naḥ parantapa |
vadhāya dēvaśatrūṇāṁ nr̥ṇāṁ lōkē manaḥ kuru ||

> **Meaning 24:** Deva, scorcher of enemies, you are the supreme refuge for all
> of us. Set your mind on being born in the world of humans to destroy the
> enemies of the devatās."

---
25.
ēvaṁ muktastu dēvēśō viṣṇustridaśapuṅgavaḥ ||

> **Meaning 25:** When they had spoken to him like this, Viṣṇu, the ruler of all
> devatās and foremost among the thirty-three crore devatās,

---
26.
pitāmahapurōgāṁstān sarvalōkanamaskr̥taḥ |
abravīt tridaśān sarvān samētān dharmasaṁhitān ||

> **Meaning 26:** honored by all the worlds, spoke to all the devatās gathered
> there, led by Pitāmaha Brahmā and devoted to dharma:

---
27.
bhayaṁ tyajata bhadraṁ vō hitārthaṁ yudhi rāvaṇam |
saputrapautraṁ sāmātyaṁ samitrajñātibāndhavam |
hatvā krūraṁ durādharṣaṁ dēvarṣīṇāṁ bhayāvaham ||

> **Meaning 27:** "Cast off your fear. May all be well with you. For your
> welfare, I will destroy in battle that cruel and unassailable Rāvaṇa—
> the terror of devatās and ṛṣis—along with his sons, grandsons, ministers,
> friends, kinsmen, and relatives.

---
28.
daśa varṣasahasrāṇi daśa varṣaśatāni ca |
vatsyāmi mānuṣē lōkē pālayanpr̥thivīmimām ||

> **Meaning 28:** Then for ten thousand years, and ten hundred years more, I
> will live in the human world and protect this earth."

---
29.
ēvaṁ dattvā varaṁ dēvō dēvānāṁ viṣṇurātmavān |
mānuṣē cintayāmāsa janmabhūmimathātmanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 29:** Having given this boon to the devatās, the self-possessed
> Mahāviṣṇu considered where, among humans, he would take birth.

---
30.
tataḥ padmapalāśākṣaḥ kr̥tvātmānaṁ caturvidham |
pitaraṁ rōcayāmāsa tadā daśarathaṁ nr̥pam ||

> **Meaning 30:** Then the lotus-eyed one, manifesting himself in four
> forms, chose King Daśaratha as his father.

---
31.
tadā dēvarṣigandharvāḥ sarudrāḥ sāpsarōgaṇāḥ |
stutibhirdivyarūpābhistuṣṭuvurmadhusūdanam ||

> **Meaning 31:** Then the devarṣis, the Gandharvas, the Rudras, and the hosts
> of Apsarases praised Madhusūdana with divine hymns:

---
32.
tamuddhataṁ rāvaṇamugratējasaṁ
pravr̥ddhadarpaṁ tridaśēśvaradviṣam |
virāvaṇaṁ sādhutapasvikaṇṭakaṁ
tapasvināmuddhara taṁ bhayāvaham ||

> **Meaning 32:** "Rāvaṇa is arrogant and fierce in his power, swollen with
> pride, and an enemy of Indra, king of the devatās. He makes the worlds cry
> out. He is a thorn to the good and to ascetics, and a terror to tapasvins.
> Root him out.

---
33.
tamēva hatvā sabalaṁ sabāndhavaṁ
virāvaṇaṁ rāvaṇamugrapauruṣam |
svarlōkamāgaccha gatajvaraściram
surēndraguptaṁ gatadōṣakalmaṣam ||

> **Meaning 33:** "Destroy that Rāvaṇa of cruel might, who makes the worlds
> wail in agony, along with his army and his kin. Then, free of all anguish,
> return to your heavenly realm, guarded by Indra, where passion and hatred
> have no place."

---
