=== Sarga 5 ===

---
1.
sarvāpūrvam iyaṁ yeṣām āsīt kr̥tsnā vasuṁdharā |
prajāpatim upādāya nr̥pāṇāṁ jayaśālinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
yeṣāṁ sa sagaro nāma sāgaro yena khānitaḥ |
ṣaṣṭiḥ putrasahasrāṇi yaṁ yāntaṁ paryavārayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
ikṣvākūṇām idaṁ teṣāṁ rājñāṁ vaṁśe mahātmanām |
mahad utpannam ākhyānaṁ rāmāyaṇam iti śrutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tad idaṁ vartayiṣyāmi sarvaṁ nikhilam āditaḥ |
dharmakāmārthasahitaṁ śrotavyam anasūyayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
kosalo nāma muditaḥ sphīto janapado mahān |
niviṣṭaḥ sarayūtīre prabhūtadhanadhānyavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
ayodhyā nāma nagarī tatrāsīl lokaviśrutā |
manunā mānavendreṇa yā purī nirmitā svayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
āyatā daśa ca dve ca yojanāni mahāpurī |
śrīmatī trīṇi vistīrṇā suvibhaktamahāpathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
rājamārgeṇa mahatā suvibhaktena śobhitā |
muktapuṣpāvakīrṇena jalasiktena nityaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tāṁ tu rājā daśaratho mahārāṣṭravivardhanaḥ |
purīm āvāsayām āsa divi devapatir yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
kapāṭatoraṇavatīṁ suvibhaktāntarāpaṇām |
sarvayantrāyudhavatīm upetāṁ sarvaśilpibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sūtamāgadhasaṁbādhāṁ śrīmatīm atulaprabhām |
uccāṭṭāladhvajavatīṁ śataghnīśatasaṁkulām ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
vadhūnāṭakasaṅghaiś ca saṁyuktāṁ sarvataḥ purīm |
udyānāmravaṇopetāṁ mahatīṁ sālamekhalām ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
durgagambhīraparikhāṁ durgām anyair durāsadām |
vājivāraṇasaṁpūrṇāṁ gobhir uṣṭraiḥ kharais tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sāmantarājasaṅghaiś ca balikarmabhir āvr̥tām |
nānādeśanivāsaiś ca vaṇigbhir upaśobhitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
prasādai ratnavikr̥taiḥ parvatair upaśobhitām |
kūṭāgāraiś ca saṁpūrṇām indrasyevāmarāvatīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
citrām aṣṭāpadākārāṁ varanārīgaṇair yutām |
sarvaratnasamākīrṇāṁ vimānagr̥haśobhitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
gr̥hagāḍhām avicchidrāṁ samabhūmau niveśitām |
śālitaṇḍulasaṁpūrṇām ikṣukāṇḍarasodakām ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
dundubhībhir mr̥daṅgaiś ca vīṇābhiḥ paṇavais tathā |
nāditāṁ bhr̥śam atyarthaṁ pr̥thivyāṁ tām anuttamām ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
vimānam iva siddhānāṁ tapasādhigataṁ divi |
suniveśitaveśmāntāṁ narottamasamāvr̥tām ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
ye ca bāṇair na vidhyanti viviktam aparāparam |
śabdavedhyaṁ ca vitataṁ laghuhastā viśāradāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
siṁhavyāghravarāhāṇāṁ mattānāṁ nadatāṁ vane |
hantāro niśitaiḥ śastrair balād bāhubalair api ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tādr̥śānāṁ sahasrais tām abhipūrṇāṁ mahārathaiḥ |
purīm āvāsayām āsa rājā daśarathas tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tām agnimadbhir guṇavadbhir āvr̥tāṁ; dvijottamair vedaṣaḍaṅgapāragaiḥ |
sahasradaiḥ satyaratair mahātmabhir; maharṣikalpair r̥ṣibhiś ca kevalaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
