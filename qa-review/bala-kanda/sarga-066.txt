=== Sarga 66 ===

---
1.
janakasya vacaḥ śrutvā viśvāmitro mahāmuniḥ |
dhanur darśaya rāmāya iti hovāca pārthivam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tataḥ sa rājā janakaḥ sacivān vyādideśa ha |
dhanur ānīyatāṁ divyaṁ gandhamālyavibhūṣitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
janakena samādiṣṭhāḥ sacivāḥ prāviśan purīm |
tad dhanuḥ purataḥ kr̥tvā nirjagmuḥ pārthivājñayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
nr̥pāṁ śatāni pañcāśad vyāyatānāṁ mahātmanām |
mañjūṣām aṣṭacakrāṁ tāṁ samūhus te kathaṁ cana ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tām ādāya tu mañjūṣām āyatīṁ yatra tad dhanuḥ |
suropamaṁ te janakam ūcur nr̥patimantriṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
idaṁ dhanurvaraṁ rājan pūjitaṁ sarvarājabhiḥ |
mithilādhipa rājendra darśanīyaṁ yadīcchasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
teṣāṁ nr̥po vacaḥ śrutvā kr̥tāñjalir abhāṣata |
viśvāmitraṁ mahātmānaṁ tau cobhau rāmalakṣmaṇau ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
idaṁ dhanurvaraṁ brahmañ janakair abhipūjitam |
rājabhiś ca mahāvīryair aśakyaṁ pūrituṁ tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
naitat suragaṇāḥ sarve nāsurā na ca rākṣasāḥ |
gandharvayakṣapravarāḥ sakiṁnaramahoragāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
kva gatir mānuṣāṇāṁ ca dhanuṣo ’sya prapūraṇe |
āropaṇe samāyoge vepane tolane ’pi vā ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tad etad dhanuṣāṁ śreṣṭham ānītaṁ munipuṁgava |
darśayaitan mahābhāga anayo rājaputrayoḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
viśvāmitras tu dharmātmā śrutvā janakabhāṣitam |
vatsa rāma dhanuḥ paśya iti rāghavam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
maharṣer vacanād rāmo yatra tiṣṭhati tad dhanuḥ |
mañjūṣāṁ tām apāvr̥tya dr̥ṣṭvā dhanur athābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
idaṁ dhanurvaraṁ brahman saṁspr̥śāmīha pāṇinā |
yatnavāṁś ca bhaviṣyāmi tolane pūraṇe ’pi vā ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
bāḍham ity eva taṁ rājā muniś ca samabhāṣata |
līlayā sa dhanur madhye jagrāha vacanān muneḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
paśyatāṁ nr̥ṣahasrāṇāṁ bahūnāṁ raghunandanaḥ |
āropayat sa dharmātmā salīlam iva tad dhanuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
āropayitvā maurvīṁ ca pūrayām āsa vīryavān |
tad babhañja dhanur madhye naraśreṣṭho mahāyaśāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tasya śabdo mahān āsīn nirghātasamanisvanaḥ |
bhūmikampaś ca sumahān parvatasyeva dīryataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
nipetuś ca narāḥ sarve tena śabdena mohitāḥ |
varjayitvā munivaraṁ rājānaṁ tau ca rāghavau ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
pratyāśvaste jane tasmin rājā vigatasādhvasaḥ |
uvāca prāñjalir vākyaṁ vākyajño munipuṁgavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
bhagavan dr̥ṣṭavīryo me rāmo daśarathātmajaḥ |
atyadbhutam acintyaṁ ca atarkitam idaṁ mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
janakānāṁ kule kīrtim āhariṣyati me sutā |
sītā bhartāram āsādya rāmaṁ daśarathātmajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
mama satyā pratijñā ca vīryaśulketi kauśika |
sītā prāṇair bahumatā deyā rāmāya me sutā ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
bhavato ’numate brahmañ śīghraṁ gacchantu mantriṇaḥ |
mama kauśika bhadraṁ te ayodhyāṁ tvaritā rathaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
rājānaṁ praśritair vākyair ānayantu puraṁ mama |
pradānaṁ vīryaśulkāyāḥ kathayantu ca sarvaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
muniguptau ca kākutsthau kathayantu nr̥pāya vai |
prīyamāṇaṁ tu rājānam ānayantu suśīghragāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
kauśikaś ca tathety āha rājā cābhāṣya mantriṇaḥ |
ayodhyāṁ preṣayām āsa dharmātmā kr̥taśāsanāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
