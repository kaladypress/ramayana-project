=== Sarga 3 ===
Synopsis of the Rāmāyaṇa Epic

---
1.
śrutvā vastu samagraṁ taddharmātmā dharmasaṁhitam |
vyaktamanvēṣatē bhūyō yadvr̥ttaṁ tasya dhīmataḥ ||

> **Meaning 1:** Having heard the whole story — rooted in dharma and full of
> dharma — from Nārada, the sage whose soul was devoted to dharma set out to
> search more clearly for everything that had happened in the life of the wise
> Rāma.

---
2.
upaspr̥śyōdakaṁ saṁyanmuniḥ sthitvā kr̥tāñjaliḥ |
prācīnāgrēṣu darbhēṣu dharmēṇānvēṣatē gatim ||

> **Meaning 2:** Sitting on darbha grass laid with its tips pointing east, the
> sage performed ācamanam, and with folded hands, sought out
> the course of those events through the power of his penance.

---
3.
rāmalakṣmaṇasītābhī rājñā daśarathēna ca |
sabhāryēṇa sarāṣṭrēṇa yatprāptaṁ tatra tattvataḥ ||

> **Meaning 3:** He saw in true reality everything that happened to Rāma, Lakṣmaṇa,
> and Sītā, to King Daśaratha with his queens, and to the entire kingdom.

---
4.
hasitaṁ bhāṣitaṁ caiva gatiryā yacca cēṣṭitam |
tatsarvaṁ dharmavīryēṇa yathāvatsamprapaśyati ||

> **Meaning 4:** Their laughter, their speech, their every movement, and all
> that they did — through the power of his penance, he perceived it all exactly
> as it had occurred.

---
5.
strītr̥tīyēna ca tathā yatprāptaṁ caratā vanē |
satyasandhēna rāmēṇa tatsarvaṁ cānvavēkṣitam ||

> **Meaning 5:** And all that the truthful Rāma experienced while wandering in
> the forest with Sītā and Lakṣmaṇa — the sage clearly perceived it all.

---
6.
tataḥ paśyati dharmātmā tatsarvaṁ yōgamāsthitaḥ |
purā yattatra nirvr̥ttaṁ pāṇāvāmalakaṁ yathā ||

> **Meaning 6:** Then, seated in deep yoga, the righteous sage saw everything
> that had taken place in the past as clearly as an āmalaka fruit
> held in the palm of his hand.

---
7.
tatsarvaṁ tattvatō dr̥ṣṭvā dharmēṇa sa mahāmatiḥ |
abhirāmasya rāmasya caritaṁ kartumudyataḥ ||

> **Meaning 7:** Having seen everything in its true reality through the power
> of penance, that great-minded sage set about composing the life story of the
> delightful Rāma.

---
8.
kāmārthaguṇasaṁyuktaṁ dharmārthaguṇavistaram |
samudramiva ratnāḍhyaṁ sarvaśrutimanōharam ||

> **Meaning 8:** Rich in the virtues of kāma and artha, expansive with dharma
> and noble ideals, deep like an ocean filled with gems, and enchanting to the
> ears of all who hear it.

---
9.
sa yathā kathitaṁ pūrvaṁ nāradēna mahātmanā |
raghuvaṁśasya caritaṁ cakāra bhagavānmuniḥ ||

> **Meaning 9:** Exactly as it had been narrated before by the great-souled
> Nārada, the revered sage composed the history of the Raghu dynasty.

---
10.
janma rāmasya sumahadvīryaṁ sarvānukūlatām |
lōkasya priyatāṁ kṣāntiṁ saumyatāṁ satyaśīlatām ||

> **Meaning 10:** He saw it all: Rāma's birth, his great valor, his kindness
> toward everyone, how dear he was to the world, his patience, his gentleness,
> and his truthful nature.

---
11.
nānācitrāḥ kathāścānyā viśvāmitrasahāyanē |
jānakyāśca vivāhaṁ ca dhanuṣaśca vibhēdanam ||

> **Meaning 11:** He saw the many other wonderful stories from the time Rāma
> spent with Viśvāmitra, Jānakī's marriage, and the breaking of the great bow.

---
12.
rāmarāmavivādaṁ ca guṇāndāśarathēstathā |
tathābhiṣēkaṁ rāmasya kaikēyyā duṣṭabhāvatām ||

> **Meaning 12:** He saw the dispute between Rāma and Rāma (Paraśurāma), the
> virtues of Rāma (Daśarathi), the preparations for Rāma's coronation, and
> Kaikeyī's wicked intent.

---
13.
vyāghātaṁ cābhiṣēkasya rāmasya ca vivāsanam |
rājñaḥ śōkavilāpaṁ ca paralōkasya cāśrayam ||

> **Meaning 13:** He saw the coronation blocked, Rāma sent into exile, the
> king's grief and lament, and his passing to the other world.

---
14.
prakr̥tīnāṁ viṣādaṁ ca prakr̥tīnāṁ visarjanam |
niṣādādhipasaṁvādaṁ sūtōpāvartanaṁ tathā ||

> **Meaning 14:** He saw the sorrow of the people, Rāma sending the citizens
> back, his conversation with the Niṣāda chief (Guha), and the return of the
> charioteer (Sumantra).

---
15.
gaṅgāyāścāpi santāraṁ bharadvājasya darśanam |
bharadvājābhyanujñānāccitrakūṭasya darśanam ||

> **Meaning 15:** He saw the crossing of the Gaṅgā, the meeting with
> Bharadvāja, and, with Bharadvāja's permission, the arrival at Citrakūṭa.

---
16.
vāstukarmanivēśaṁ ca bharatāgamanaṁ tathā |
prasādanaṁ ca rāmasya pituśca salilakriyām |
pādukāgryābhiṣēkaṁ ca nandigrāma nivāsanam ||

> **Meaning 16:** He saw the building of the dwelling there, Bharata's
> arrival, his pleading with Rāma to return, the tarpaṇa with water offered
> for their father, the coronation of the sacred sandals, and Bharata's life
> at Nandigrāma.

---
17.
daṇḍakāraṇyagamanaṁ sutīkṣṇēna samāgamam |
darśanaṁ śarabhaṅgasya sutīkṣṇēna samāgamam ||

> **Meaning 17:** He saw Rāma's journey into the Daṇḍaka forest, the meeting
> with Śarabhaṅga, and his encounter with the sage Sutīkṣṇa.

---
18.
anasūyāsamasyāṁ ca aṅgarāgasya cārpaṇam |
agastyadarśanaṁ caiva jaṭāyōrabhisaṅgamam |
pañcavaṭyāśca gamanaṁ śūrpaṇakhyāśca darśanam ||

> **Meaning 18:** He saw Sītā's meeting with Anasūyā and the gift of the
> fragrant body-paste (aṅgarāga), the meeting with Agastya, the encounter with
> Jaṭāyu, the journey to Pañcavaṭī, and the arrival of Śūrpaṇakhā.

---
19.
śūrpaṇakhyāśca saṁvādaṁ virūpakaraṇaṁ tathā |
vadhaṁ kharatriśirasōrutthānaṁ rāvaṇasya ca ||

> **Meaning 19:** He saw the conversation with Śūrpaṇakhā, her disfigurement,
> the slaying of Khara and Triśiras, and Rāvaṇa rising into action.

---
20.
mārīcasya vadhaṁ caiva vaidēhyā haraṇaṁ tathā |
rāghavasya vilāpaṁ ca gr̥dhrarājanibarhaṇam ||

> **Meaning 20:** He saw the killing of Mārīca, the abduction of Vaidehī,
> Rāghava's grief and lament, and the death of the vulture king (Jaṭāyu).

---
21.
kabandhadarśanaṁ caiva pampāyāścāpi darśanam ||

> **Meaning 21:** He saw the encounter with Kabandha and the sight of Lake
> Pampā.

---
22.
śarbaryā darśanaṁ caiva hanūmaddarśanaṁ tathā |
vilāpaṁ caiva pampāyāṁ rāghavasya mahātmanaḥ ||

> **Meaning 22:** He saw the meeting with Śabarī, the meeting with Hanumān, and
> the lament of the great-souled Rāghava at Lake Pampā.

---
23.
r̥ṣyamūkasya gamanaṁ sugrīvēṇa samāgamam |
pratyayōtpādanaṁ sakhyaṁ vālisugrīvavigraham ||

> **Meaning 23:** He saw the journey to Ṛṣyamūka, the meeting with Sugrīva, the
> building of trust, their friendship, and the fight between Vāli and Sugrīva.

---
24.
vālipramathanaṁ caiva sugrīvapratipādanam |
tārāvilāpasamayaṁ varṣarātrinivāsanam ||

> **Meaning 24:** He saw Vāli struck down, Sugrīva placed on the throne, Tārā's
> lament, and the stay through the nights of the rainy season.

---
25.
kōpaṁ rāghavasiṁhasya balānāmupasaṅgraham |
diśaḥ prasthāpanaṁ caiva pr̥thivyāśca nivēdanam ||

> **Meaning 25:** He saw the anger of Rāghava, that lion among men, the
> gathering of the forces, the sending out of search parties in every direction,
> and the description of the earth.

---
26.
aṅgulīyakadānaṁ ca r̥kṣasya biladarśanam |
prāyōpavēśanaṁ caiva sampātēścāpi darśanam ||

> **Meaning 26:** He saw the giving of the ring, the discovery of the Ṛkṣa's
> cave, the Vānaras' vow to fast to death, and the meeting with Sampāti.

---
27.
parvatārōhaṇaṁ caiva sāgarasya ca laṅghanam |
samudravacanāccaiva mainākasya ca darśanam ||

> **Meaning 27:** He saw the climbing of Mount Mahendra, the leap across the
> ocean, the counsel of the Ocean, and the encounter with Mount Maināka.

---
28.
rākṣasītarjanaṁ caiva chāyāgrāhasya darśanam |
siṁhikāyāśca nidhanaṁ laṅkāmalayadarśanam |
rātrau laṅkāpravēśaṁ ca ēkasyāpi vicintanam ||

> **Meaning 28:** He saw the confrontation with the Rākṣasī (Surasā), the
> encounter with the shadow-catching creature (Siṁhikā), her destruction, the
> sight of Mount Malaya at Laṅkā, the entry into Laṅkā by night, and Hanumān
> thinking alone.

---
29.
āpānabhūmigamanamavarōdhasya darśanam |
darśanaṁ rāvaṇasyāpi puṣpakasya ca darśanam ||

> **Meaning 29:** He saw Hanumān go into the banquet halls, inspect the inner
> quarters, behold Rāvaṇa asleep, and gaze upon the aerial chariot Puṣpaka.

---
30.
aśōkavanikāyānaṁ sītāyāścāpi darśanam |
abhijñānapradānaṁ ca sītāyāścāpi bhāṣaṇam ||

> **Meaning 30:** He saw Hanumān go to the Aśoka grove, discover Sītā, deliver
> the token of recognition, and converse with her.

---
31.
rākṣasītarjanaṁ caiva trijaṭāsvapnadarśanam |
maṇipradānaṁ sītāyā vr̥kṣabhaṅgaṁ tathaiva ca ||

> **Meaning 31:** He saw the threats of the Rākṣasīs, Trijaṭā's dream, Sītā
> handing over her jewel, and the breaking down of trees in the grove.

---
32.
rākṣasīvidravaṁ caiva kiṅkarāṇāṁ nibarhaṇam |
grahaṇaṁ vāyusūnōśca laṅkādāhābhigarjanam ||

> **Meaning 32:** He saw the Rākṣasīs fleeing in terror, the slaughter of the
> Kiṅkaras, the capture of Vāyusūnu (Hanumān), and his mighty roar as he set
> Laṅkā ablaze.

---
33.
saṅgamaṁ ca samudrasya nalasētōśca bandhanam |
rāghavāśvāsanaṁ caiva maṇiniryātanaṁ tathā ||

> **Meaning 33:** He saw Hanumān comforting Rāghava, handing over Sītā's jewel,
> the army reaching the ocean shore, and the construction of Nala's bridge.

---
34.
saṅgamaṁ ca samudrasya nalasētōśca bandhanam |
pratāraṁ ca samudrasya rātrau laṅkāvarōdhanam ||

> **Meaning 34:** He saw the building of Nala's bridge across the ocean, the
> army's crossing over the waters, and the siege of Laṅkā by night.

---
35.
vibhīṣaṇēna saṁsargaṁ vadhōpāyanivēdanam |
kumbhakarṇasya nidhanaṁ mēghanādanibarhaṇam ||

> **Meaning 35:** He saw the alliance with Vibhīṣaṇa, his disclosure of the way
> to slay Rāvaṇa, the fall of Kumbhakarṇa, and the death of Meghanāda.

---
36.
rāvaṇasya vināśaṁ ca sītāvāptimarēḥ purē |
vibhīṣaṇābhiṣēkaṁ ca puṣpakasya ca darśanam ||

> **Meaning 36:** He saw the destruction of Rāvaṇa, the recovery of Sītā in the
> enemy's citadel, the coronation of Vibhīṣaṇa, and the sight of the Puṣpaka
> vimāna.

---
37.
ayōdhyāyāśca gamanaṁ bharatēna samāgamam |
rāmābhiṣēkābhyudayaṁ sarvasainyavisarjanam ||

> **Meaning 37:** He saw the return journey to Ayodhyā, the reunion with
> Bharata, the glorious festivities of Rāma's coronation, and the sending home
> of all the allied armies.

---
38.
svarāṣṭrarañjanaṁ caiva vaidēhyāśca visarjanam |
anāgataṁ ca yatkiñcidrāmasya vasudhātalē |
taccakārōttarē kāvyē vālmīkirbhagavānr̥ṣiḥ ||

> **Meaning 38:** He saw Rāma ruling to the delight of his kingdom. And the
> renunciation of Vaidehī, along with all future events of Rāma's life on earth,
> the revered sage Vālmīki composed in the final book, the Uttara Kāṇḍa
