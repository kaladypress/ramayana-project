=== Sarga 65 ===

---
1.
nikumbhaṁ ca hataṁ śrutvā kumbhaṁ ca vinipātitam |
rāvaṇaḥ paramāmarṣī prajajvālānalo yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
nairr̥taḥ krodhaśokābhyāṁ dvābhyāṁ tu parimūrchitaḥ |
kharaputraṁ viśālākṣaṁ makarākṣam acodayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
gaccha putra mayājñapto balenābhisamanvitaḥ |
rāghavaṁ lakṣmaṇaṁ caiva jahi tau savanaukasau ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
rāvaṇasya vacaḥ śrutvā śūro mānī kharātmajaḥ |
bāḍham ity abravīd dhr̥ṣṭo makarākṣo niśācaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
so ’bhivādya daśagrīvaṁ kr̥tvā cāpi pradakṣiṇam |
nirjagāma gr̥hāc chubhrād rāvaṇasyājñayā balī ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
samīpasthaṁ balādhyakṣaṁ kharaputro ’bravīd idam |
ratham ānīyatāṁ śīghraṁ sainyaṁ cānīyatāṁ tvarāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā balādhyakṣo niśācaraḥ |
syandanaṁ ca balaṁ caiva samīpaṁ pratyapādayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
pradakṣiṇaṁ rathaṁ kr̥tvā āruroha niśācaraḥ |
sūtaṁ saṁcodayām āsa śīghraṁ me ratham āvaha ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
atha tān rākṣasān sarvān makarākṣo ’bravīd idam |
yūyaṁ sarve prayudhyadhvaṁ purastān mama rākṣasāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
ahaṁ rākṣasarājena rāvaṇena mahātmanā |
ājñaptaḥ samare hantuṁ tāv ubhau rāmalakṣmaṇau ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
adya rāmaṁ vadhiṣyāmi lakṣmaṇaṁ ca niśācarāḥ |
śākhāmr̥gaṁ ca sugrīvaṁ vānarāṁś ca śarottamaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
adya śūlanipātaiś ca vānarāṇāṁ mahācamūm |
pradahiṣyāmi saṁprāptāṁ śuṣkendhanam ivānalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
makarākṣasya tac chrutvā vacanaṁ te niśācarāḥ |
sarve nānāyudhopetā balavantaḥ samāhitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
te kāmarūpiṇaḥ śūrā daṁṣṭriṇaḥ piṅgalekṣaṇāḥ |
mātaṁgā iva nardanto dhvastakeśā bhayānakāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
parivārya mahākāyā mahākāyaṁ kharātmajam |
abhijagmus tadā hr̥ṣṭāś cālayanto vasuṁdharām ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
śaṅkhabherīsahasrāṇām āhatānāṁ samantataḥ |
kṣveḍitāsphoṭitānāṁ ca tataḥ śabdo mahān abhūt ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
prabhraṣṭo ’tha karāt tasya pratodaḥ sārathes tadā |
papāta sahasā caiva dhvajas tasya ca rakṣasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tasya te rathasaṁyuktā hayā vikramavarjitāḥ |
caraṇair ākulair gatvā dīnāḥ sāsramukhā yayuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
pravāti pavanas tasya sapāṁsuḥ kharadāruṇaḥ |
niryāṇe tasya raudrasya makarākṣasya durmateḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tāni dr̥ṣṭvā nimittāni rākṣasā vīryavattamāḥ |
acintyanirgatāḥ sarve yatra tau rāmalakṣmaṇau ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
ghanagajamahiṣāṅgatulyavarṇāḥ; samaramukheṣv asakr̥d gadāsibhinnāḥ |
aham aham iti yuddhakauśalās te; rajanicarāḥ paribabhramur nadantaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
