=== Sarga 7 ===

---
1.
ity uktā rākṣasendreṇa rākṣasās te mahābalāḥ |
ūcuḥ prāñjalayaḥ sarve rāvaṇaṁ rākṣaseśvaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
rājan parighaśaktyr̥ṣṭiśūlapaṭṭasasaṁkulam |
sumahan no balaṁ kasmād viṣādaṁ bhajate bhavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
kailāsaśikharāvāsī yakṣair bahubhir āvr̥taḥ |
sumahat kadanaṁ kr̥tvā vaśyas te dhanadaḥ kr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
sa maheśvarasakhyena ślāghamānas tvayā vibho |
nirjitaḥ samare roṣāl lokapālo mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
vinihatya ca yakṣaughān vikṣobhya ca vigr̥hya ca |
tvayā kailāsaśikharād vimānam idam āhr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
mayena dānavendreṇa tvadbhayāt sakhyam icchatā |
duhitā tava bhāryārthe dattā rākṣasapuṁgava ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
dānavendro madhur nāma vīryotsikto durāsadaḥ |
vigr̥hya vaśam ānītaḥ kumbhīnasyāḥ sukhāvahaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
nirjitās te mahābāho nāgā gatvā rasātalam |
vāsukis takṣakaḥ śaṅkho jaṭī ca vaśam āhr̥tāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
akṣayā balavantaś ca śūrā labdhavarāḥ punaḥ |
tvayā saṁvatsaraṁ yuddhvā samare dānavā vibho ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
svabalaṁ samupāśritya nītā vaśam ariṁdama |
māyāś cādhigatās tatra bahavo rākṣasādhipa ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
śūrāś ca balavantaś ca varuṇasya sutā raṇe |
nirjitās te mahābāho caturvidhabalānugāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
mr̥tyudaṇḍamahāgrāhaṁ śālmalidvīpamaṇḍitam |
avagāhya tvayā rājan yamasya balasāgaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
jayaś ca viplulaḥ prāpto mr̥tyuś ca pratiṣedhitaḥ |
suyuddhena ca te sarve lokās tatra sutoṣitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
kṣatriyair bahubhir vīraiḥ śakratulyaparākramaiḥ |
āsīd vasumatī pūrṇā mahadbhir iva pādapaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
teṣāṁ vīryaguṇotsāhair na samo rāghavo raṇe |
prasahya te tvayā rājan hatāḥ paramadurjayāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
rājan nāpad ayukteyam āgatā prākr̥tāj janāt |
hr̥di naiva tvayā kāryā tvaṁ vadhiṣyasi rāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
