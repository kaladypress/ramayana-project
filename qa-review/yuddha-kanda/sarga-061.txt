=== Sarga 61 ===

---
1.
tayos tadā sāditayo raṇāgre; mumoha sainyaṁ hariyūthapānām |
sugrīvanīlāṅgadajāmbavanto; na cāpi kiṁ cit pratipedire te ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tato viṣaṇṇaṁ samavekṣya sainyaṁ; vibhīṣaṇo buddhimatāṁ variṣṭhaḥ |
uvāca śākhāmr̥garājavīrān; āśvāsayann apratimair vacobhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
mā bhaiṣṭa nāsty atra viṣādakālo; yad āryaputrāv avaśau viṣaṇṇau |
svayambhuvo vākyam athodvahantau; yat sāditāv indrajidastrajālaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tasmai tu dattaṁ paramāstram etat; svayambhuvā brāhmam amoghavegam |
tan mānayantau yadi rājaputrau; nipātitau ko ’tra viṣādakālaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
brāhmam astraṁ tadā dhīmān mānayitvā tu mārutiḥ |
vibhīṣaṇavacaḥ śrutvā hanūmāṁs tam athābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
etasmin nihate sainye vānarāṇāṁ tarasvinām |
yo yo dhārayate prāṇāṁs taṁ tam āśvāsayāvahe ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tāv ubhau yugapad vīrau hanūmad rākṣasottamau |
ulkāhastau tadā rātrau raṇaśīrṣe viceratuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
chinnalāṅgūlahastorupādāṅguli śiro dharaiḥ |
sravadbhiḥ kṣatajaṁ gātraiḥ prasravadbhiḥ samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
patitaiḥ parvatākārair vānarair abhisaṁkulām |
śastraiś ca patitair dīptair dadr̥śāte vasuṁdharām ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
sugrīvam aṅgadaṁ nīlaṁ śarabhaṁ gandhamādanam |
jāmbavantaṁ suṣeṇaṁ ca vegadarśanam āhukam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
maindaṁ nalaṁ jyotimukhaṁ dvividaṁ panasaṁ tathā |
vibhīṣaṇo hanūmāṁś ca dadr̥śāte hatān raṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
saptaṣaṣṭir hatāḥ koṭyo vānarāṇāṁ tarasvinām |
ahnaḥ pañcamaśeṣeṇa vallabhena svayambhuvaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sāgaraughanibhaṁ bhīmaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā bāṇārditaṁ balam |
mārgate jāmbavantaṁ sma hanūmān savibhīṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
svabhāvajarayā yuktaṁ vr̥ddhaṁ śaraśataiś citam |
prajāpatisutaṁ vīraṁ śāmyantam iva pāvakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
dr̥ṣṭvā tam upasaṁgamya paulastyo vākyam abravīt |
kaccid āryaśarais tīrṣṇair na prāṇā dhvaṁsitās tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
vibhīṣaṇavacaḥ śrutvā jāmbavān r̥kṣapuṁgavaḥ |
kr̥cchrād abhyudgiran vākyam idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
nairr̥tendramahāvīryasvareṇa tvābhilakṣaye |
pīḍyamānaḥ śitair bāṇair na tvāṁ paśyāmi cakṣuṣā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
añjanā suprajā yena mātariśvā ca nairr̥ta |
hanūmān vānaraśreṣṭhaḥ prāṇān dhārayate kva cit ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
śrutvā jāmbavato vākyam uvācedaṁ vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
āryaputrāv atikramya kasmāt pr̥cchasi mārutim ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
naiva rājani sugrīve nāṅgade nāpi rāghave |
ārya saṁdarśitaḥ sneho yathā vāyusute paraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
vibhīṣaṇavacaḥ śrutvā jāmbavān vākyam abravīt |
śr̥ṇu nairr̥taśārdūla yasmāt pr̥cchāmi mārutim ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tasmiñ jīvati vīre tu hatam apy ahataṁ balam |
hanūmaty ujjhitaprāṇe jīvanto ’pi vayaṁ hatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
dhriyate mārutis tāta mārutapratimo yadi |
vaiśvānarasamo vīrye jīvitāśā tato bhavet ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tato vr̥ddham upāgamya niyamenābhyavādayat |
gr̥hya jāmbavataḥ pādau hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
śrutvā hanumato vākyaṁ tathāpi vyathitendriyaḥ |
punarjātam ivātmānaṁ sa mene r̥kṣapuṁgavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tato ’bravīn mahātejā hanūmantaṁ sa jāmbavān |
āgaccha hariśārdūlavānarāṁs trātum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
nānyo vikramaparyāptas tvam eṣāṁ paramaḥ sakhā |
tvatparākramakālo ’yaṁ nānyaṁ paśyāmi kañ cana ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
r̥kṣavānaravīrāṇām anīkāni praharṣaya |
viśalyau kuru cāpy etau sāditau rāmalakṣmaṇau ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
gatvā paramam adhvānam upary upari sāgaram |
himavantaṁ nagaśreṣṭhaṁ hanūman gantum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
tataḥ kāñcanam atyugram r̥ṣabhaṁ parvatottamam |
kailāsaśikharaṁ cāpi drakṣyasy ariniṣūdana ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
tayoḥ śikharayor madhye pradīptam atulaprabham |
sarvauṣadhiyutaṁ vīra drakṣyasy auṣadhiparvatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
tasya vānaraśārdūlacatasro mūrdhni saṁbhavāḥ |
drakṣyasy oṣadhayo dīptā dīpayantyo diśo daśa ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
mr̥tasaṁjīvanīṁ caiva viśalyakaraṇīm api |
sauvarṇakaraṇīṁ caiva saṁdhānīṁ ca mahauṣadhīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
tāḥ sarvā hanuman gr̥hya kṣipram āgantum arhasi |
āśvāsaya harīn prāṇair yojya gandhavahātmajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
śrutvā jāmbavato vākyaṁ hanūmān haripuṁgavaḥ |
āpūryata baloddharṣais toyavegair ivārṇavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
sa parvatataṭāgrasthaḥ pīḍayan parvatottaram |
hanūmān dr̥śyate vīro dvitīya iva parvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
haripādavinirbhinno niṣasāda sa parvataḥ |
na śaśāka tadātmānaṁ soḍhuṁ bhr̥śanipīḍitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
tasya petur nagā bhūmau harivegāc ca jajvaluḥ |
śr̥ṅgāṇi ca vyakīryanta pīḍitasya hanūmatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
tasmin saṁpīḍyamāne tu bhagnadrumaśilātale |
na śekur vānarāḥ sthātuṁ ghūrṇamāne nagottame ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
sa ghūrṇitamahādvārā prabhagnagr̥hagopurā |
laṅkā trāsākulā rātrau pranr̥ttevābhavat tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
pr̥thivīdharasaṁkāśo nipīḍya dharaṇīdharam |
pr̥thivīṁ kṣobhayām āsa sārṇavāṁ mārutātmajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
padbhyāṁ tu śailam āpīḍya vaḍavāmukhavan mukham |
vivr̥tyograṁ nanādoccais trāsayann iva rākṣasān ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
tasya nānadyamānasya śrutvā ninadam adbhutam |
laṅkāsthā rākṣasāḥ sarve na śekuḥ spandituṁ bhayāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
namaskr̥tvātha rāmāya mārutir bhīmavikramaḥ |
rāghavārthe paraṁ karma samaihata paraṁtapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
sa puccham udyamya bhujaṁgakalpaṁ; vinamya pr̥ṣṭhaṁ śravaṇe nikuñcya |
vivr̥tya vaktraṁ vaḍavāmukhābham; āpupluve vyomni sa caṇḍavegaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
sa vr̥kṣaṣaṇḍāṁs tarasā jahāra; śailāñ śilāḥ prākr̥tavānarāṁś ca |
bāhūruvegoddhatasaṁpraṇunnās; te kṣīṇavegāḥ salile nipetuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
sa tau prasāryoragabhogakalpau; bhujau bhujaṁgārinikāśavīryaḥ |
jagāma meruṁ nagarājam agryaṁ; diśaḥ prakarṣann iva vāyusūnuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
sa sāgaraṁ ghūrṇitavīcimālaṁ; tadā bhr̥śaṁ bhrāmitasarvasattvam |
samīkṣamāṇaḥ sahasā jagāma; cakraṁ yathā viṣṇukarāgramuktam ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
sa parvatān vr̥kṣagaṇān sarāṁsi; nadīs taṭākāni purottamāni |
sphītāñjanāṁs tān api saṁprapaśyañ; jagāma vegāt pitr̥tulyavegaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
ādityapatham āśritya jagāma sa gataśramaḥ |
sa dadarśa hariśreṣṭho himavantaṁ nagottamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
nānāprasravaṇopetaṁ bahukaṁdaranirjharam |
śvetābhracayasaṁkāśaiḥ śikharaiś cārudarśanaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
52.
sa taṁ samāsādya mahānagendram; atipravr̥ddhottamaghoraśr̥ṅgam |
dadarśa puṇyāni mahāśramāṇi; surarṣisaṁghottamasevitāni ||

=== meaning ===

---
53.
sa brahmakośaṁ rajatālayaṁ ca; śakrālayaṁ rudraśarapramokṣam |
hayānanaṁ brahmaśiraś ca dīptaṁ; dadarśa vaivasvata kiṁkarāṁś ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
54.
vajrālayaṁ vaiśvaraṇālayaṁ ca; sūryaprabhaṁ sūryanibandhanaṁ ca |
brahmāsanaṁ śaṁkarakārmukaṁ ca; dadarśa nābhiṁ ca vasuṁdharāyāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
55.
kailāsam agryaṁ himavacchilāṁ ca; tatharṣabhaṁ kāñcanaśailam agryam |
sa dīptasarvauṣadhisaṁpradīptaṁ; dadarśa sarvauṣadhiparvatendram ||

=== meaning ===

---
56.
sa taṁ samīkṣyānalaraśmidīptaṁ; visiṣmiye vāsavadūtasūnuḥ |
āplutya taṁ cauṣadhiparvatendraṁ; tatrauṣadhīnāṁ vicayaṁ cakāra ||

=== meaning ===

---
57.
sa yojanasahasrāṇi samatītya mahākapiḥ |
divyauṣadhidharaṁ śailaṁ vyacaran mārutātmajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
58.
mahauṣadhyas tu tāḥ sarvās tasmin parvatasattame |
vijñāyārthinam āyāntaṁ tato jagmur adarśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
59.
sa tā mahātmā hanumān apaśyaṁś; cukopa kopāc ca bhr̥śaṁ nanāda |
amr̥ṣyamāṇo ’gninikāśacakṣur; mahīdharendraṁ tam uvāca vākyam ||

=== meaning ===

---
60.
kim etad evaṁ suviniścitaṁ te; yad rāghave nāsi kr̥tānukampaḥ |
paśyādya madbāhubalābhibhūto; vikīrṇam ātmānam atho nagendra ||

=== meaning ===

---
61.
sa tasya śr̥ṅgaṁ sanagaṁ sanāgaṁ; sakāñcanaṁ dhātusahasrajuṣṭam |
vikīrṇakūṭaṁ calitāgrasānuṁ; pragr̥hya vegāt sahasonmamātha ||

=== meaning ===

---
62.
sa taṁ samutpāṭya kham utpapāta; vitrāsya lokān sasurān surendrān |
saṁstūyamānaḥ khacarair anekair; jagāma vegād garuḍogravīryaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
63.
sa bhāskarādhvānam anuprapannas; tad bhāskarābhaṁ śikharaṁ pragr̥hya |
babhau tadā bhāskarasaṁnikāśo; raveḥ samīpe pratibhāskarābhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
64.
sa tena śailena bhr̥śaṁ rarāja; śailopamo gandhavahātmajas tu |
sahasradhāreṇa sapāvakena; cakreṇa khe viṣṇur ivoddhr̥tena ||

=== meaning ===

---
65.
taṁ vānarāḥ prekṣya tadā vineduḥ; sa tān api prekṣya mudā nanāda |
teṣāṁ samudghuṣṭaravaṁ niśamya; laṅkālayā bhīmataraṁ vineduḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
66.
tato mahātmā nipapāta tasmiñ; śailottame vānarasainyamadhye |
haryuttamebhyaḥ śirasābhivādya; vibhīṣaṇaṁ tatra ca sasvaje saḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
67.
tāv apy ubhau mānuṣarājaputrau; taṁ gandham āghrāya mahauṣadhīnām |
babhūvatus tatra tadā viśalyāv; uttasthur anye ca haripravīrāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
68.
tato harir gandhavahātmajas tu; tam oṣadhīśailam udagravīryaḥ |
nināya vegād dhimavantam eva; punaś ca rāmeṇa samājagāma ||

=== meaning ===

---
