=== Sarga 28 ===

---
1.
naravānararājau tau sa ca vāyusutaḥ kapiḥ |
jāmbavān r̥kṣarājaś ca rākṣasaś ca vibhīṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
aṅgado vāliputraś ca saumitriḥ śarabhaḥ kapiḥ |
suṣeṇaḥ sahadāyādo maindo dvivida eva ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
gajo gavākṣo kumudo nalo ’tha panasas tathā |
amitraviṣayaṁ prāptāḥ samavetāḥ samarthayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
iyaṁ sā lakṣyate laṅkā purī rāvaṇapālitā |
sāsuroragagandharvair amarair api durjayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
kāryasiddhiṁ puraskr̥tya mantrayadhvaṁ vinirṇaye |
nityaṁ saṁnihito hy atra rāvaṇo rākṣasādhipaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tathā teṣu bruvāṇeṣu rāvaṇāvarajo ’bravīt |
vākyam agrāmyapadavat puṣkalārthaṁ vibhīṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
analaḥ śarabhaś caiva saṁpātiḥ praghasas tathā |
gatvā laṅkāṁ mamāmātyāḥ purīṁ punar ihāgatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
bhūtvā śakunayaḥ sarve praviṣṭāś ca ripor balam |
vidhānaṁ vihitaṁ yac ca tad dr̥ṣṭvā samupasthitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
saṁvidhānaṁ yathāhus te rāvaṇasya durātmanaḥ |
rāma tad bruvataḥ sarvaṁ yathātathyena me śr̥ṇu ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
pūrvaṁ prahastaḥ sabalo dvāram āsādya tiṣṭhati |
dakṣiṇaṁ ca mahāvīryau mahāpārśvamahodarau ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
indrajit paścimadvāraṁ rākṣasair bahubhir vr̥taḥ |
paṭṭasāsidhanuṣmadbhiḥ śūlamudgarapāṇibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
nānāpraharaṇaiḥ śūrair āvr̥to rāvaṇātmajaḥ |
rākṣasānāṁ sahasrais tu bahubhiḥ śastrapāṇibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
yuktaḥ paramasaṁvigno rākṣasair bahubhir vr̥taḥ |
uttaraṁ nagaradvāraṁ rāvaṇaḥ svayam āsthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
virūpākṣas tu mahatā śūlakhaḍgadhanuṣmatā |
balena rākṣasaiḥ sārdhaṁ madhyamaṁ gulmam āsthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
etān evaṁvidhān gulmām̐l laṅkāyāṁ samudīkṣya te |
māmakāḥ sacivāḥ sarve śīghraṁ punar ihāgatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
gajānāṁ ca sahasraṁ ca rathānām ayutaṁ pure |
hayānām ayute dve ca sāgrakoṭī ca rakṣasām ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
vikrāntā balavantaś ca saṁyugeṣv ātatāyinaḥ |
iṣṭā rākṣasarājasya nityam ete niśācarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
ekaikasyātra yuddhārthe rākṣasasya viśāṁ pate |
parivāraḥ sahasrāṇāṁ sahasram upatiṣṭhate ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
etāṁ pravr̥ttiṁ laṅkāyāṁ mantriproktaṁ vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
rāmaṁ kamalapatrākṣam idam uttaram abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
kuberaṁ tu yadā rāma rāvaṇaḥ pratyayudhyata |
ṣaṣṭiḥ śatasahasrāṇi tadā niryānti rākṣasāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
parākrameṇa vīryeṇa tejasā sattvagauravāt |
sadr̥śā yo ’tra darpeṇa rāvaṇasya durātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
atra manyur na kartavyo roṣaye tvāṁ na bhīṣaye |
samartho hy asi vīryeṇa surāṇām api nigrahe ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tad bhavāṁś caturaṅgeṇa balena mahatā vr̥taḥ |
vyūhyedaṁ vānarānīkaṁ nirmathiṣyasi rāvaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
rāvaṇāvaraje vākyam evaṁ bruvati rāghavaḥ |
śatrūṇāṁ pratighātārtham idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
pūrvadvāre tu laṅkāyā nīlo vānarapuṁgavaḥ |
prahastaṁ pratiyoddhā syād vānarair bahubhir vr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
aṅgado vāliputras tu balena mahatā vr̥taḥ |
dakṣiṇe bādhatāṁ dvāre mahāpārśvamahodarau ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
hanūmān paścimadvāraṁ nipīḍya pavanātmajaḥ |
praviśatv aprameyātmā bahubhiḥ kapibhir vr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
daityadānavasaṁghānām r̥ṣīṇāṁ ca mahātmanām |
viprakārapriyaḥ kṣudro varadānabalānvitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
parikrāmati yaḥ sarvām̐l lokān saṁtāpayan prajāḥ |
tasyāhaṁ rākṣasendrasya svayam eva vadhe dhr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
uttaraṁ nagaradvāram ahaṁ saumitriṇā saha |
nipīḍyābhipravekṣyāmi sabalo yatra rāvaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
vānarendraś ca balavān r̥kṣarājaś ca jāmbavān |
rākṣasendrānujaś caiva gulme bhavatu madhyame ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
na caiva mānuṣaṁ rūpaṁ kāryaṁ haribhir āhave |
eṣā bhavatu naḥ saṁjñā yuddhe ’smin vānare bale ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
vānarā eva niścihnaṁ svajane ’smin bhaviṣyati |
vayaṁ tu mānuṣeṇaiva sapta yotsyāmahe parān ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
aham eva saha bhrātrā lakṣmaṇena mahaujasā |
ātmanā pañcamaś cāyaṁ sakhā mama vibhīṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
sa rāmaḥ kāryasiddhyartham evam uktvā vibhīṣaṇam |
suvelārohaṇe buddhiṁ cakāra matimān matim ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
tatas tu rāmo mahatā balena; pracchādya sarvāṁ pr̥thivīṁ mahātmā |
prahr̥ṣṭarūpo ’bhijagāma laṅkāṁ; kr̥tvā matiṁ so ’rivadhe mahātmā ||

=== meaning ===

---
