=== Sarga 42 ===

---
1.
dhūmrākṣaṁ prekṣya niryāntaṁ rākṣasaṁ bhīmanisvanam |
vinedur vānarāḥ sarve prahr̥ṣṭā yuddhakāṅkṣiṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
teṣāṁ tu tumulaṁ yuddhaṁ saṁjajñe harirakṣasām |
anyonyaṁ pādapair ghorair nighnataṁ śūlamudgaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
rākṣasair vānarā ghorā vinikr̥ttāḥ samantataḥ |
vānarai rākṣasāś cāpi drumair bhūmau samīkr̥tāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
rākṣasāś cāpi saṁkruddhā vānarān niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ |
vivyadhur ghorasaṁkāśaiḥ kaṅkapatrair ajihmagaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
te gadābhiś ca bhīmābhiḥ paṭṭasaiḥ kūṭamudgaraiḥ |
ghoraiś ca parighaiś citrais triśūlaiś cāpi saṁśitaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
vidāryamāṇā rakṣobhir vānarās te mahābalāḥ |
amarṣāj janitoddharṣāś cakruḥ karmāṇy abhītavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
śaranirbhinnagātrās te śūlanirbhinnadehinaḥ |
jagr̥hus te drumāṁs tatra śilāś ca hariyūthapāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
te bhīmavegā harayo nardamānās tatas tataḥ |
mamanthū rākṣasān bhīmān nāmāni ca babhāṣire ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tad babhūvādbhutaṁ ghoraṁ yuddhaṁ vānararakṣasām |
śilābhir vividhābhiś ca bahuśākhaiś ca pādapaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
rākṣasā mathitāḥ ke cid vānarair jitakāśibhiḥ |
vavarṣū rudhiraṁ ke cin mukhai rudhirabhojanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
pārśveṣu dāritāḥ ke cit ke cid rāśīkr̥tā drumaiḥ |
śilābhiś cūrṇitāḥ ke cit ke cid dantair vidāritāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
dhvajair vimathitair bhagnaiḥ kharaiś ca vinipātitaiḥ |
rathair vidhvaṁsitaiś cāpi patitai rajanīcaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
vānarair bhīmavikrāntair āplutyāplutya vegitaiḥ |
rākṣasāḥ karajais tīkṣṇair mukheṣu vinikartitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
vivarṇavadanā bhūyo viprakīrṇaśiroruhāḥ |
mūḍhāḥ śoṇitagandhena nipetur dharaṇītale ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
naye tu paramakruddhā rākṣasā bhīmavikramāḥ |
talair evābhidhāvanti vajrasparśasamair harīn ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
vanarair āpatantas te vegitā vegavattaraiḥ |
muṣṭibhiś caraṇair dantaiḥ pādapaiś cāpapothitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
sanyaṁ tu vidrutaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā dhūmrākṣo rākṣasarṣabhaḥ |
krodhena kadanaṁ cakre vānarāṇāṁ yuyutsatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
prāsaiḥ pramathitāḥ ke cid vānarāḥ śoṇitasravāḥ |
mudgarair āhatāḥ ke cit patitā dharaṇītale ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
parighair mathitaḥ ke cid bhiṇḍipālair vidāritāḥ |
paṭṭasair āhatāḥ ke cid vihvalanto gatāsavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
ke cid vinihatā bhūmau rudhirārdrā vanaukasaḥ |
ke cid vidrāvitā naṣṭāḥ saṁkruddhai rākṣasair yudhi ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
vibhinnahr̥dayāḥ ke cid ekapārśvena śāyitāḥ |
vidāritāstraśūlai ca ke cid āntrair vinisrutāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tat subhīmaṁ mahad yuddhaṁ harirākasa saṁkulam |
prababhau śastrabahulaṁ śilāpādapasaṁkulam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
dhanurjyātantrimadhuraṁ hikkātālasamanvitam |
mandrastanitasaṁgītaṁ yuddhagāndharvam ābabhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
dhūmrākṣas tu dhanuṣpāṇir vānarān raṇamūrdhani |
hasan vidrāvayām āsa diśas tāñ śaravr̥ṣṭibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
dhūmrākṣeṇārditaṁ sainyaṁ vyathitaṁ dr̥śya mārutiḥ |
abhyavartata saṁkruddhaḥ pragr̥hya vipulāṁ śilām ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
krodhād dviguṇatāmrākṣaḥ pitr̥tulyaparākramaḥ |
śilāṁ tāṁ pātayām āsa dhūmrākṣasya rathaṁ prati ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
āpatantīṁ śilāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā gadām udyamya saṁbhramāt |
rathād āplutya vegena vasudhāyāṁ vyatiṣṭhata ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
sā pramathya rathaṁ tasya nipapāta śilābhuvi |
sacakrakūbaraṁ sāśvaṁ sadhvajaṁ saśarāsanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
sa bhaṅktvā tu rathaṁ tasya hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ |
rakṣasāṁ kadanaṁ cakre saskandhaviṭapair drumaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
vibhinnaśiraso bhūtvā rākṣasāḥ śoṇitokṣitāḥ |
drumaiḥ pramathitāś cānye nipetur dharaṇītale ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
vidrāvya rākṣasaṁ sainyaṁ hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ |
gireḥ śikharam ādāya dhūmrākṣam abhidudruve ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
tam āpatantaṁ dhūmrākṣo gadām udyamya vīryavān |
vinardamānaḥ sahasā hanūmantam abhidravat ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
tataḥ kruddhas tu vegena gadāṁ tāṁ bahukaṇṭakām |
pātayām āsa dhūmrākṣo mastake tu hanūmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
tāḍitaḥ sa tayā tatra gadayā bhīmarūpayā |
sa kapir mārutabalas taṁ prahāram acintayan |
dhūmrākṣasya śiro madhye giriśr̥ṅgam apātayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
sa vihvalitasarvāṅgo giriśr̥ṅgeṇa tāḍitaḥ |
papāta sahasā bhūmau vikīrṇa iva parvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
dhūmrākṣaṁ nihataṁ dr̥ṣṭvā hataśeṣā niśācarāḥ |
trastāḥ praviviśur laṅkāṁ vadhyamānāḥ plavaṁgamaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
sa tu pavanasuto nihatya śatruṁ; kṣatajavahāḥ saritaś ca saṁvikīrya |
ripuvadhajanitaśramo mahātmā; mudam agamat kapibhiś ca pūjyamānaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
