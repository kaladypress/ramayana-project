=== Sarga 89 ===

---
1.
sa dattvā tumulaṁ yuddhaṁ rāvaṇasya durātmanaḥ |
visr̥jann eva bāṇaughān suṣeṇaṁ vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
eṣa rāvaṇavegena lakṣmaṇaḥ patitaḥ kṣitau |
sarpavad veṣṭate vīro mama śokam udīrayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
śoṇitārdram imaṁ vīraṁ prāṇair iṣṭataraṁ mama |
paśyato mama kā śaktir yoddhuṁ paryākulātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
ayaṁ sa samaraślāghī bhrātā me śubhalakṣaṇaḥ |
yadi pañcatvam āpannaḥ prāṇair me kiṁ sukhena vā ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
lajjatīva hi me vīryaṁ bhraśyatīva karād dhanuḥ |
sāyakā vyavasīdanti dr̥ṣṭir bāṣpavaśaṁ gatā |
cintā me vardhate tīvrā mumūrṣā copajāyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
bhrātaraṁ nihataṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rāvaṇena durātmanā |
paraṁ viṣādam āpanno vilalāpākulendriyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
na hi yuddhena me kāryaṁ naiva prāṇair na sītayā |
bhrātaraṁ nihataṁ dr̥ṣṭvā lakṣmaṇaṁ raṇapāṁsuṣu ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
kiṁ me rājyena kiṁ prāṇair yuddhe kāryaṁ na vidyate |
yatrāyaṁ nihataḥ śete raṇamūrdhani lakṣmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
rāmam āśvāsayan vīraḥ suṣeṇo vākyam abravīt |
na mr̥to ’yaṁ mahābāhur lakṣmaṇo lakṣmivardhanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
na cāsya vikr̥taṁ vaktraṁ nāpi śyāmaṁ na niṣprabham |
suprabhaṁ ca prasannaṁ ca mukham asyābhilakṣyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
padmaraktatalau hastau suprasanne ca locane |
evaṁ na vidyate rūpaṁ gatāsūnāṁ viśāṁ pate |
māṁ viṣādaṁ kr̥thā vīra saprāṇo ’yam ariṁdama ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
ākhyāsyate prasuptasya srastagātrasya bhūtale |
socchvāsaṁ hr̥dayaṁ vīra kampamānaṁ muhur muhuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
evam uktvā tu vākyajñaḥ suṣeṇo rāghavaṁ vacaḥ |
samīpastham uvācedaṁ hanūmantam abhitvaran ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
saumya śīghram ito gatvā śailam oṣadhiparvatam |
pūrvaṁ hi kathito yo ’sau vīra jāmbavatā śubhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
dakṣiṇe śikhare tasya jātām oṣadhim ānaya |
viśalyakaraṇī nāma viśalyakaraṇīṁ śubhām ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sauvarṇakaraṇīṁ cāpi tathā saṁjīvanīm api |
saṁdhānakaraṇīṁ cāpi gatvā śīghram ihānaya |
saṁjīvanārthaṁ vīrasya lakṣmaṇasya mahātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
ity evam ukto hanumān gatvā cauṣadhiparvatam |
cintām abhyagamac chrīmān ajānaṁs tā mahauṣadhīḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tasya buddhiḥ samutpannā māruter amitaujasaḥ |
idam eva gamiṣyāmi gr̥hītvā śikharaṁ gireḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
agr̥hya yadi gacchāmi viśalyakaraṇīm aham |
kālātyayena doṣaḥ syād vaiklavyaṁ ca mahad bhavet ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
iti saṁcintya hanumān gatvā kṣipraṁ mahābalaḥ |
utpapāta gr̥hītvā tu hanūmāñ śikharaṁ gireḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
oṣadhīr nāvagachāmi tā ahaṁ haripuṁgava |
tad idaṁ śikharaṁ kr̥tsnaṁ gires tasyāhr̥taṁ mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
evaṁ kathayamānaṁ taṁ praśasya pavanātmajam |
suṣeṇo vānaraśreṣṭho jagrāhotpāṭya cauṣadhīḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tataḥ saṁkṣodayitvā tām oṣadhiṁ vānarottamaḥ |
lakṣmaṇasya dadau nastaḥ suṣeṇaḥ sumahādyutiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
saśalyaḥ sa samāghrāya lakṣmaṇaḥ paravīrahā |
viśalyo virujaḥ śīghram udatiṣṭhan mahītalāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
samutthitaṁ te harayo bhūtalāt prekṣya lakṣmaṇam |
sādhu sādhv iti suprītāḥ suṣeṇaṁ pratyapūjayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
ehy ehīty abravīd rāmo lakṣmaṇaṁ paravīrahā |
sasvaje snehagāḍhaṁ ca bāṣpaparyākulekṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
abravīc ca pariṣvajya saumitriṁ rāghavas tadā |
diṣṭyā tvāṁ vīra paśyāmi maraṇāt punar āgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
na hi me jīvitenārthaḥ sītayā ca jayena vā |
ko hi me jīvitenārthas tvayi pañcatvam āgate ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
ity evaṁ vadatas tasya rāghavasya mahātmanaḥ |
khinnaḥ śithilayā vācā lakṣmaṇo vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
tāṁ pratijñāṁ pratijñāya purā satyaparākrama |
laghuḥ kaś cid ivāsattvo naivaṁ vaktum ihārhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
na pratijñāṁ hi kurvanti vitathāṁ sādhavo ’nagha |
lakṣaṇaṁ hi mahat tv asya pratijñāparipālanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
nairāśyam upagantuṁ te tad alaṁ matkr̥te ’nagha |
vadhena rāvaṇasyādya pratijñām anupālaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
na jīvan yāsyate śatrus tava bāṇapathaṁ gataḥ |
nardatas tīkṣṇadaṁṣṭrasya siṁhasyeva mahāgajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
ahaṁ tu vadham icchāmi śīghram asya durātmanaḥ |
yāvad astaṁ na yāty eṣa kr̥takarmā divākaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
