=== Sarga 1 ===

---
1.
śrutvā hanumato vākyaṁ yathāvad abhibhāṣitam |
rāmaḥ prītisamāyukto vākyam uttaram abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
kr̥taṁ hanumatā kāryaṁ sumahad bhuvi duṣkaram |
manasāpi yad anyena na śakyaṁ dharaṇītale ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
na hi taṁ paripaśyāmi yas tareta mahārṇavam |
anyatra garuṇād vāyor anyatra ca hanūmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
devadānavayakṣāṇāṁ gandharvoragarakṣasām |
apradhr̥ṣyāṁ purīṁ laṅkāṁ rāvaṇena surakṣitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
praviṣṭaḥ sattvam āśritya jīvan ko nāma niṣkramet |
ko viśet sudurādharṣāṁ rākṣasaiś ca surakṣitām |
yo vīryabalasaṁpanno na samaḥ syād dhanūmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
bhr̥tyakāryaṁ hanumatā sugrīvasya kr̥taṁ mahat |
evaṁ vidhāya svabalaṁ sadr̥śaṁ vikramasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
yo hi bhr̥tyo niyuktaḥ san bhartrā karmaṇi duṣkare |
kuryāt tadanurāgeṇa tam āhuḥ puruṣottamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
niyukto nr̥pateḥ kāryaṁ na kuryād yaḥ samāhitaḥ |
bhr̥tyo yuktaḥ samarthaś ca tam āhuḥ puruṣādhamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tanniyoge niyuktena kr̥taṁ kr̥tyaṁ hanūmatā |
na cātmā laghutāṁ nītaḥ sugrīvaś cāpi toṣitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
ahaṁ ca raghuvaṁśaś ca lakṣmaṇaś ca mahābalaḥ |
vaidehyā darśanenādya dharmataḥ parirakṣitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
idaṁ tu mama dīnasyā mano bhūyaḥ prakarṣati |
yad ihāsya priyākhyātur na kurmi sadr̥śaṁ priyam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
eṣa sarvasvabhūtas tu pariṣvaṅgo hanūmataḥ |
mayā kālam imaṁ prāpya dattas tasya mahātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sarvathā sukr̥taṁ tāvat sītāyāḥ parimārgaṇam |
sāgaraṁ tu samāsādya punar naṣṭaṁ mano mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
kathaṁ nāma samudrasya duṣpārasya mahāmbhasaḥ |
harayo dakṣiṇaṁ pāraṁ gamiṣyanti samāhitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
yady apy eṣa tu vr̥ttānto vaidehyā gadito mama |
samudrapāragamane harīṇāṁ kim ivottaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
ity uktvā śokasaṁbhrānto rāmaḥ śatrunibarhaṇaḥ |
hanūmantaṁ mahābāhus tato dhyānam upāgamat ||

=== meaning ===

---
