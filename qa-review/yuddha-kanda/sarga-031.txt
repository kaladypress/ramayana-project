=== Sarga 31 ===

---
1.
atha tasmin nimittāni dr̥ṣṭvā lakṣmaṇapūrvajaḥ |
lakṣmaṇaṁ lakṣmisaṁpannam idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
parigr̥hyodakaṁ śītaṁ vanāni phalavanti ca |
balaughaṁ saṁvibhajyemaṁ vyūhya tiṣṭhema lakṣmaṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
lokakṣayakaraṁ bhīmaṁ bhayaṁ paśyāmy upasthitam |
nibarhaṇaṁ pravīrāṇām r̥kṣavānararakṣasām ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
vātāś ca paruṣaṁ vānti kampate ca vasuṁdharā |
parvatāgrāṇi vepante patanti dharaṇīdharāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
meghāḥ kravyādasaṁkāśāḥ paruṣāḥ paruṣasvanāḥ |
krūrāḥ krūraṁ pravarṣanti miśraṁ śoṇitabindubhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
raktacandanasaṁkāśā saṁdhyāparamadāruṇā |
jvalac ca nipataty etad ādityād agnimaṇḍalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
ādityam abhivāśyante janayanto mahad bhayam |
dīnā dīnasvarā ghorā apraśastā mr̥gadvijāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
rajanyām aprakāśaś ca saṁtāpayati candramāḥ |
kr̥ṣṇaraktāṁśuparyanto yathā lokasya saṁkṣaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
hrasvo rūkṣo ’praśastaś ca pariveṣaḥ sulohitaḥ |
ādityamaṇḍale nīlaṁ lakṣma lakṣmaṇa dr̥śyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
dr̥śyante na yathāvac ca nakṣatrāṇy abhivartate |
yugāntam iva lokasya paśya lakṣmaṇa śaṁsati ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
kākāḥ śyenās tathā gr̥dhrā nīcaiḥ paripatanti ca |
śivāś cāpy aśivā vācaḥ pravadanti mahāsvanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
kṣipram adya durādharṣāṁ purīṁ rāvaṇapālitām |
abhiyāma javenaiva sarvato haribhir vr̥tāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
ity evaṁ tu vadan vīro lakṣmaṇaṁ lakṣmaṇāgrajaḥ |
tasmād avātarac chīghraṁ parvatāgrān mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
avatīrya tu dharmātmā tasmāc chailāt sa rāghavaḥ |
paraiḥ paramadurdharṣaṁ dadarśa balam ātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
saṁnahya tu sasugrīvaḥ kapirājabalaṁ mahat |
kālajño rāghavaḥ kāle saṁyugāyābhyacodayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tataḥ kāle mahābāhur balena mahatā vr̥taḥ |
prasthitaḥ purato dhanvī laṅkām abhimukhaḥ purīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
taṁ vibhīṣaṇa sugrīvau hanūmāñ jāmbavān nalaḥ |
r̥kṣarājas tathā nīlo lakṣmaṇaś cānyayus tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tataḥ paścāt sumahatī pr̥tanarkṣavanaukasām |
pracchādya mahatīṁ bhūmim anuyāti sma rāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
śailaśr̥ṅgāṇi śataśaḥ pravr̥ddhāṁś ca mahīruhām |
jagr̥huḥ kuñjaraprakhyā vānarāḥ paravāraṇāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tau tv adīrgheṇa kālena bhrātarau rāmalakṣmaṇau |
rāvaṇasya purīṁ laṅkām āsedatur ariṁdamau ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
patākāmālinīṁ ramyām udyānavanaśobhitām |
citravaprāṁ suduṣprāpām uccaprākāratoraṇām ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tāṁ surair api durdharṣāṁ rāmavākyapracoditāḥ |
yathānideśaṁ saṁpīḍya nyaviśanta vanaukasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
laṅkāyās tūttaradvāraṁ śailaśr̥ṅgam ivonnatam |
rāmaḥ sahānujo dhanvī jugopa ca rurodha ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
laṅkām upaniviṣṭaś ca rāmo daśarathātmajaḥ |
lakṣmaṇānucaro vīraḥ purīṁ rāvaṇapālitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
uttaradvāram āsādya yatra tiṣṭhati rāvaṇaḥ |
nānyo rāmād dhi tad dvāraṁ samarthaḥ parirakṣitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
rāvaṇādhiṣṭhitaṁ bhīmaṁ varuṇeneva sāgaram |
sāyudhau rākṣasair bhīmair abhiguptaṁ samantataḥ |
laghūnāṁ trāsajananaṁ pātālam iva dānavaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
vinyastāni ca yodhānāṁ bahūni vividhāni ca |
dadarśāyudhajālāni tathaiva kavacāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
pūrvaṁ tu dvāram āsādya nīlo haricamūpatiḥ |
atiṣṭhat saha maindena dvividena ca vīryavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
aṅgado dakṣiṇadvāraṁ jagrāha sumahābalaḥ |
r̥ṣabheṇa gavākṣeṇa gajena gavayena ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
hanūmān paścimadvāraṁ rarakṣa balavān kapiḥ |
pramāthi praghasābhyāṁ ca vīrair anyaiś ca saṁgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
madhyame ca svayaṁ gulme sugrīvaḥ samatiṣṭhata |
saha sarvair hariśreṣṭhaiḥ suparṇaśvasanopamaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
vānarāṇāṁ tu ṣaṭtriṁśat koṭyaḥ prakhyātayūthapāḥ |
nipīḍyopaniviṣṭāś ca sugrīvo yatra vānaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
śāsanena tu rāmasya lakṣmaṇaḥ savibhīṣaṇaḥ |
dvāre dvāre harīṇāṁ tu koṭiṁ koṭiṁ nyaveśayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
paścimena tu rāmasya sugrīvaḥ saha jāmbavān |
adūrān madhyame gulme tasthau bahubalānugaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
te tu vānaraśārdūlāḥ śārdūlā iva daṁṣṭriṇaḥ |
gr̥hītvā drumaśailāgrān hr̥ṣṭā yuddhāya tasthire ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
sarve vikr̥talāṅgūlāḥ sarve daṁṣṭrānakhāyudhāḥ |
sarve vikr̥tacitrāṅgāḥ sarve ca vikr̥tānanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
daśanāgabalāḥ ke cit ke cid daśaguṇottarāḥ |
ke cin nāgasahasrasya babhūvus tulyavikramāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
santi caughā balāḥ ke cit ke cic chataguṇottarāḥ |
aprameyabalāś cānye tatrāsan hariyūthapāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
adbhutaś ca vicitraś ca teṣām āsīt samāgamaḥ |
tatra vānarasainyānāṁ śalabhānām ivodgamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
paripūrṇam ivākāśaṁ saṁchanneva ca medinī |
laṅkām upaniviṣṭaiś ca saṁpatadbhiś ca vānaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
śataṁ śatasahasrāṇāṁ pr̥thag r̥kṣavanaukasām |
laṅkā dvārāṇy upājagmur anye yoddhuṁ samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
āvr̥taḥ sa giriḥ sarvais taiḥ samantāt plavaṁgamaiḥ |
ayutānāṁ sahasraṁ ca purīṁ tām abhyavartata ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
vānarair balavadbhiś ca babhūva drumapāṇibhiḥ |
sarvataḥ saṁvr̥tā laṅkā duṣpraveśāpi vāyunā ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
rākṣasā vismayaṁ jagmuḥ sahasābhinipīḍitāḥ |
vānarair meghasaṁkāśaiḥ śakratulyaparākramaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
mahāñ śabdo ’bhavat tatra balaughasyābhivartataḥ |
sāgarasyeva bhinnasya yathā syāt salilasvanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
tena śabdena mahatā saprākārā satoraṇā |
laṅkā pracalitā sarvā saśailavanakānanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
rāmalakṣmaṇaguptā sā sugrīveṇa ca vāhinī |
babhūva durdharṣatarā sarvair api surāsuraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
rāghavaḥ saṁniveśyaivaṁ sainyaṁ svaṁ rakṣasāṁ vadhe |
saṁmantrya mantribhiḥ sārdhaṁ niścitya ca punaḥ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
ānantaryam abhiprepsuḥ kramayogārthatattvavit |
vibhīṣaṇasyānumate rājadharmam anusmaran |
aṅgadaṁ vālitanayaṁ samāhūyedam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
gatvā saumya daśagrīvaṁ brūhi madvacanāt kape |
laṅghayitvā purīṁ laṅkāṁ bhayaṁ tyaktvā gatavyathaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
bhraṣṭaśrīkagataiśvaryamumūrṣo naṣṭacetanaḥ |
r̥ṣīṇāṁ devatānāṁ ca gandharvāpsarasāṁ tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
52.
nāgānām atha yakṣāṇāṁ rājñāṁ ca rajanīcara |
yac ca pāpaṁ kr̥taṁ mohād avaliptena rākṣasa ||

=== meaning ===

---
53.
nūnam adya gato darpaḥ svayambhū varadānajaḥ |
yasya daṇḍadharas te ’haṁ dārāharaṇakarśitaḥ |
daṇḍaṁ dhārayamāṇas tu laṅkādvare vyavasthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
54.
padavīṁ devatānāṁ ca maharṣīṇāṁ ca rākṣasa |
rājarṣīṇāṁ ca sarveṣāṁ gamiṣyasi mayā hataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
55.
balena yena vai sītāṁ māyayā rākṣasādhama |
mām atikrāmayitvā tvaṁ hr̥tavāṁs tad vidarśaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
56.
arākṣasam imaṁ lokaṁ kartāsmi niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ |
na cec charaṇam abhyeṣi mām upādāya maithilīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
57.
dharmātmā rakṣasāṁ śreṣṭhaḥ saṁprāpto ’yaṁ vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
laṅkaiśvaryaṁ dhruvaṁ śrīmān ayaṁ prāpnoty akaṇṭakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
58.
na hi rājyam adharmeṇa bhoktuṁ kṣaṇam api tvayā |
śakyaṁ mūrkhasahāyena pāpenāvijitātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
59.
yudhyasva vā dhr̥tiṁ kr̥tvā śauryam ālambya rākṣasa |
maccharais tvaṁ raṇe śāntas tataḥ pūto bhaviṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
60.
yady āviśasi lokāṁs trīn pakṣibhūto manojavaḥ |
mama cakṣuṣpathaṁ prāpya na jīvan pratiyāsyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
61.
bravīmi tvāṁ hitaṁ vākyaṁ kriyatām aurdhvadekikam |
sudr̥ṣṭā kriyatāṁ laṅkā jīvitaṁ te mayi sthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
62.
ity uktaḥ sa tu tāreyo rāmeṇākliṣṭakarmaṇā |
jagāmākāśam āviśya mūrtimān iva havyavāṭ ||

=== meaning ===

---
63.
so ’tipatya muhūrtena śrīmān rāvaṇamandiram |
dadarśāsīnam avyagraṁ rāvaṇaṁ sacivaiḥ saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
64.
tatas tasyāvidūreṇa nipatya haripuṁgavaḥ |
dīptāgnisadr̥śas tasthāv aṅgadaḥ kanakāṅgadaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
65.
tad rāmavacanaṁ sarvam anyūnādhikam uttamam |
sāmātyaṁ śrāvayām āsa nivedyātmānam ātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
66.
dūto ’haṁ kosalendrasya rāmasyākliṣṭakarmaṇaḥ |
vāliputro ’ṅgado nāma yadi te śrotram āgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
67.
āha tvāṁ rāghavo rāmaḥ kausalyānandavardhanaḥ |
niṣpatya pratiyudhyasva nr̥śaṁsaṁ puruṣādhama ||

=== meaning ===

---
68.
hantāsmi tvāṁ sahāmātyaṁ saputrajñātibāndhavam |
nirudvignās trayo lokā bhaviṣyanti hate tvayi ||

=== meaning ===

---
69.
devadānavayakṣāṇāṁ gandharvoragarakṣasām |
śatrum adyoddhariṣyāmi tvām r̥ṣīṇāṁ ca kaṇṭakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
70.
vibhīṣaṇasya caiśvaryaṁ bhaviṣyati hate tvayi |
na cet satkr̥tya vaidehīṁ praṇipatya pradāsyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
71.
ity evaṁ paruṣaṁ vākyaṁ bruvāṇe haripuṁgave |
amarṣavaśam āpanno niśācaragaṇeśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
72.
tataḥ sa roṣatāmrākṣaḥ śaśāsa sacivāṁs tadā |
gr̥hyatām eṣa durmedhā vadhyatām iti cāsakr̥t ||

=== meaning ===

---
73.
rāvaṇasya vacaḥ śrutvā dīptāgnisamatejasaḥ |
jagr̥hus taṁ tato ghorāś catvāro rajanīcarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
74.
grāhayām āsa tāreyaḥ svayam ātmānam ātmanā |
balaṁ darśayituṁ vīro yātudhānagaṇe tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
75.
sa tān bāhudvaye saktān ādāya patagān iva |
prāsādaṁ śailasaṁkāśam utpāpātāṅgadas tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
76.
te ’ntarikṣād vinirdhūtās tasya vegena rākṣasāḥ |
bhumau nipatitāḥ sarve rākṣasendrasya paśyataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
77.
tataḥ prāsādaśikharaṁ śailaśr̥ṅgam ivonnatam |
tat paphāla tadākrāntaṁ daśagrīvasya paśyataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
78.
bhaṅktvā prāsādaśikharaṁ nāma viśrāvya cātmanaḥ |
vinadya sumahānādam utpapāta vihāyasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
79.
rāvaṇas tu paraṁ cakre krodhaṁ prāsādadharṣaṇāt |
vināśaṁ cātmanaḥ paśyan niḥśvāsaparamo ’bhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
80.
rāmas tu bahubhir hr̥ṣṭair ninadadbhiḥ plavaṁgamaiḥ |
vr̥to ripuvadhākāṅkṣī yuddhāyaivābhyavartata ||

=== meaning ===

---
81.
suṣeṇas tu mahāvīryo girikūṭopamo hariḥ |
bahubhiḥ saṁvr̥tas tatra vānaraiḥ kāmarūpibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
82.
caturdvārāṇi sarvāṇi sugrīvavacanāt kapiḥ |
paryākramata durdharṣo nakṣatrāṇīva candramāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
83.
teṣām akṣauhiṇiśataṁ samavekṣya vanaukasām |
laṅkām upaniviṣṭānāṁ sāgaraṁ cātivartatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
84.
rākṣasā vismayaṁ jagmus trāsaṁ jagmus tathāpare |
apare samaroddharṣād dharṣam evopapedire ||

=== meaning ===

---
85.
kr̥tsnaṁ hi kapibhir vyāptaṁ prākāraparikhāntaram |
dadr̥śū rākṣasā dīnāḥ prākāraṁ vānarīkr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
86.
tasmin mahābhīṣaṇake pravr̥tte; kolāhale rākṣasarājadhānyām |
pragr̥hya rakṣāṁsi mahāyudhāni; yugāntavātā iva saṁviceruḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
