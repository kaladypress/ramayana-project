=== Sarga 109 ===

---
1.
tāṁ rātrim uṣitaṁ rāmaṁ sukhotthitam ariṁdamam |
abravīt prāñjalir vākyaṁ jayaṁ pr̥ṣṭvā vibhīṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
snānāni cāṅgarāgāṇi vastrāṇy ābharaṇāni ca |
candanāni ca divyāni mālyāni vividhāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
alaṁkāravidaś cemā nāryaḥ padmanibhekṣaṇāḥ |
upasthitās tvāṁ vidhivat snāpayiṣyanti rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
evam uktas tu kākutsthaḥ pratyuvāca vibhīṣaṇam |
harīn sugrīvamukhyāṁs tvaṁ snānenopanimantraya ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
sa tu tāmyati dharmātmā mamahetoḥ sukhocitaḥ |
sukumāro mahābāhuḥ kumāraḥ satyasaṁśravaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
taṁ vinā kaikeyīputraṁ bharataṁ dharmacāriṇam |
na me snānaṁ bahumataṁ vastrāṇy ābharaṇāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
ita eva pathā kṣipraṁ pratigacchāma tāṁ purīm |
ayodhyām āyato hy eṣa panthāḥ paramadurgamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
evam uktas tu kākutsthaṁ pratyuvāca vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
ahnā tvāṁ prāpayiṣyāmi tāṁ purīṁ pārthivātmaja ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
puṣpakaṁ nāma bhadraṁ te vimānaṁ sūryasaṁnibham |
mama bhrātuḥ kuberasya rāvaṇenāhr̥taṁ balāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tad idaṁ meghasaṁkāśaṁ vimānam iha tiṣṭhati |
tena yāsyasi yānena tvam ayodhyāṁ gajajvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
ahaṁ te yady anugrāhyo yadi smarasi me guṇān |
vasa tāvad iha prājña yady asti mayi sauhr̥dam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
lakṣmaṇena saha bhrātrā vaidehyā cāpi bhāryayā |
arcitaḥ sarvakāmais tvaṁ tato rāma gamiṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
prītiyuktas tu me rāma sasainyaḥ sasuhr̥dgaṇaḥ |
satkriyāṁ vihitāṁ tāvad gr̥hāṇa tvaṁ mayodyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
praṇayād bahumānāc ca sauhr̥dena ca rāghava |
prasādayāmi preṣyo ’haṁ na khalv ājñāpayāmi te ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
evam uktas tato rāmaḥ pratyuvāca vibhīṣaṇam |
rakṣasāṁ vānarāṇāṁ ca sarveṣāṁ copaśr̥ṇvatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
pūjito ’haṁ tvayā vīra sācivyena paraṁtapa |
sarvātmanā ca ceṣṭibhiḥ sauhr̥denottamena ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
na khalv etan na kuryāṁ te vacanaṁ rākṣaseśvara |
taṁ tu me bhrātaraṁ draṣṭuṁ bharataṁ tvarate manaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
māṁ nivartayituṁ yo ’sau citrakūṭam upāgataḥ |
śirasā yācato yasya vacanaṁ na kr̥taṁ mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
kausalyāṁ ca sumitrāṁ ca kaikeyīṁ ca yaśasvinīm |
gurūṁś ca suhr̥daś caiva paurāṁś ca tanayaiḥ saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
upasthāpaya me kṣipraṁ vimānaṁ rākṣaseśvara |
kr̥takāryasya me vāsaḥ kathaṁ cid iha saṁmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
anujānīhi māṁ saumya pūjito ’smi vibhīṣaṇa |
manyur na khalu kartavyas tvaritas tvānumānaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tataḥ kāñcanacitrāṅgaṁ vaidūryamaṇivedikam |
kūṭāgāraiḥ parikṣiptaṁ sarvato rajataprabham ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
pāṇḍurābhiḥ patākābhir dhvajaiś ca samalaṁkr̥tam |
śobhitaṁ kāñcanair harmyair hemapadmavibhūṣitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
prakīrṇaṁ kiṅkiṇījālair muktāmaṇigavākṣitam |
ghaṇṭājālaiḥ parikṣiptaṁ sarvato madhurasvanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tan meruśikharākāraṁ nirmitaṁ viśvakarmaṇā |
bahubhir bhūṣitaṁ harmyair muktārajatasaṁnibhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
talaiḥ sphaṭikacitrāṅgair vaidūryaiś ca varāsanaiḥ |
mahārhāstaraṇopetair upapannaṁ mahādhanaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
upasthitam anādhr̥ṣyaṁ tad vimānaṁ manojavam |
nivedayitvā rāmāya tasthau tatra vibhīṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
