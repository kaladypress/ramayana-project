=== Sarga 107 ===

---
1.
etac chrutvā śubhaṁ vākyaṁ rāghaveṇa subhāṣitam |
idaṁ śubhataraṁ vākyaṁ vyājahāra maheśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
puṣkarākṣa mahābāho mahāvakṣaḥ paraṁtapa |
diṣṭyā kr̥tam idaṁ karma tvayā śastrabhr̥tāṁ vara ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
diṣṭyā sarvasya lokasya pravr̥ddhaṁ dāruṇaṁ tamaḥ |
apāvr̥ttaṁ tvayā saṁkhye rāma rāvaṇajaṁ bhayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
āśvāsya bharataṁ dīnaṁ kausalyāṁ ca yaśasvinīm |
kaikeyīṁ ca sumitrāṁ ca dr̥ṣṭvā lakṣmaṇamātaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
prāpya rājyam ayodhyāyāṁ nandayitvā suhr̥jjanam |
ikṣvākūṇāṁ kule vaṁśaṁ sthāpayitvā mahābala ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
iṣṭvā turagamedhena prāpya cānuttamaṁ yaśaḥ |
brāhmaṇebhyo dhanaṁ dattvā tridivaṁ gantum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
eṣa rājā vimānasthaḥ pitā daśarathas tava |
kākutstha mānuṣe loke gurus tava mahāyaśāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
indralokaṁ gataḥ śrīmāṁs tvayā putreṇa tāritaḥ |
lakṣmaṇena saha bhrātrā tvam enam abhivādaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
mahādevavacaḥ śrutvā kākutsthaḥ sahalakṣmaṇaḥ |
vimānaśikharasthasya praṇāmam akarot pituḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
dīpyamānaṁ svayāṁ lakṣmyā virajo’mbaradhāriṇam |
lakṣmaṇena saha bhrātrā dadarśa pitaraṁ prabhuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
harṣeṇa mahatāviṣṭo vimānastho mahīpatiḥ |
prāṇaiḥ priyataraṁ dr̥ṣṭvā putraṁ daśarathas tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
āropyāṅkaṁ mahābāhur varāsanagataḥ prabhuḥ |
bāhubhyāṁ saṁpariṣvajya tato vākyaṁ samādade ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
na me svargo bahumataḥ saṁmānaś ca surarṣibhiḥ |
tvayā rāma vihīnasya satyaṁ pratiśr̥ṇomi te ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
kaikeyyā yāni coktāni vākyāni vadatāṁ vara |
tava pravrājanārthāni sthitāni hr̥daye mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tvāṁ tu dr̥ṣṭvā kuśalinaṁ pariṣvajya salakṣmaṇam |
adya duḥkhād vimukto ’smi nīhārād iva bhāskaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tārito ’haṁ tvayā putra suputreṇa mahātmanā |
aṣṭāvakreṇa dharmātmā tārito brāhmaṇo yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
idānīṁ ca vijānāmi yathā saumya sureśvaraiḥ |
vadhārthaṁ rāvaṇasyeha vihitaṁ puruṣottamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
siddhārthā khalu kausalyā yā tvāṁ rāma gr̥haṁ gatam |
vanān nivr̥ttaṁ saṁhr̥ṣṭā drakṣyate śatrusūdana ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
siddhārthāḥ khalu te rāma narā ye tvāṁ purīṁ gatam |
jalārdram abhiṣiktaṁ ca drakṣyanti vasudhādhipam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
anuraktena balinā śucinā dharmacāriṇā |
iccheyaṁ tvām ahaṁ draṣṭuṁ bharatena samāgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
caturdaśasamāḥ saumya vane niryāpitās tvayā |
vasatā sītayā sārdhaṁ lakṣmaṇena ca dhīmatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
nivr̥ttavanavāso ’si pratijñā saphalā kr̥tā |
rāvaṇaṁ ca raṇe hatvā devās te paritoṣitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
kr̥taṁ karma yaśaḥ ślāghyaṁ prāptaṁ te śatrusūdana |
bhrātr̥bhiḥ saha rājyastho dīrgham āyur avāpnuhi ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
iti bruvāṇaṁ rājānaṁ rāmaḥ prāñjalir abravīt |
kuru prasādaṁ dharmajña kaikeyyā bharatasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
saputrāṁ tvāṁ tyajāmīti yad uktā kaikayī tvayā |
sa śāpaḥ kaikayīṁ ghoraḥ saputrāṁ na spr̥śet prabho ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
sa tatheti mahārājo rāmam uktvā kr̥tāñjalim |
lakṣmaṇaṁ ca pariṣvajya punar vākyam uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
rāmaṁ śuśrūṣatā bhaktyā vaidehyā saha sītayā |
kr̥tā mama mahāprītiḥ prāptaṁ dharmaphalaṁ ca te ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
dharmaṁ prāpsyasi dharmajña yaśaś ca vipulaṁ bhuvi |
rāme prasanne svargaṁ ca mahimānaṁ tathaiva ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
rāmaṁ śuśrūṣa bhadraṁ te sumitrānandavardhana |
rāmaḥ sarvasya lokasya śubheṣv abhirataḥ sadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
ete sendrās trayo lokāḥ siddhāś ca paramarṣayaḥ |
abhigamya mahātmānam arcanti puruṣottamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
etat tad uktam avyaktam akṣaraṁ brahmanirmitam |
devānāṁ hr̥dayaṁ saumya guhyaṁ rāmaḥ paraṁtapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
avāptaṁ dharmacaraṇaṁ yaśaś ca vipulaṁ tvayā |
rāmaṁ śuśrūṣatā bhaktyā vaidehyā saha sītayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
sa tathoktvā mahābāhur lakṣmaṇaṁ prāñjaliṁ sthitam |
uvāca rājā dharmātmā vaidehīṁ vacanaṁ śubham ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
kartavyo na tu vaidehi manyus tyāgam imaṁ prati |
rāmeṇa tvadviśuddhyarthaṁ kr̥tam etad dhitaiṣiṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
na tvaṁ subhru samādheyā patiśuśrūvaṇaṁ prati |
avaśyaṁ tu mayā vācyam eṣa te daivataṁ param ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
iti pratisamādiśya putrau sītāṁ tathā snuṣām |
indralokaṁ vimānena yayau daśaratho jvalan ||

=== meaning ===

---
