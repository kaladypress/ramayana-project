=== Sarga 77 ===

---
1.
yudhyamānau tu tau dr̥ṣṭvā prasaktau nararākṣasau |
śūraḥ sa rāvaṇabhrātā tasthau saṁgrāmamūrdhani ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tato visphārayām āsa mahad dhanur avasthitaḥ |
utsasarja ca tīkṣṇāgrān rākṣaseṣu mahāśarān ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
te śarāḥ śikhisaṁkāśā nipatantaḥ samāhitāḥ |
rākṣasān dārayām āsur vajrā iva mahāgirīn ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
vibhīṣaṇasyānucarās te ’pi śūlāsipaṭṭasaiḥ |
ciccheduḥ samare vīrān rākṣasān rākṣasottamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
rākṣasais taiḥ parivr̥taḥ sa tadā tu vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
babhau madhye prahr̥ṣṭānāṁ kalabhānām iva dvipaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tataḥ saṁcodayāno vai harīn rakṣoraṇapriyān |
uvāca vacanaṁ kāle kālajño rakṣasāṁ varaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
eko ’yaṁ rākṣasendrasya parāyaṇam iva sthitaḥ |
etac cheṣaṁ balaṁ tasya kiṁ tiṣṭhata harīśvarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
asmin vinihate pāpe rākṣase raṇamūrdhani |
rāvaṇaṁ varjayitvā tu śeṣam asya balaṁ hatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
prahasto nihato vīro nikumbhaś ca mahābalaḥ |
kumbhakarṇaś ca kumbhaś ca dhūmrākṣaś ca niśācaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
akampanaḥ supārśvaś ca cakramālī ca rākṣasaḥ |
kampanaḥ sattvavantaś ca devāntakanarāntakau ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
etān nihatyātibalān bahūn rākṣasasattamān |
bāhubhyāṁ sāgaraṁ tīrtvā laṅghyatāṁ goṣpadaṁ laghu ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
etāvad iha śeṣaṁ vo jetavyam iha vānarāḥ |
hatāḥ sarve samāgamya rākṣasā baladarpitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
ayuktaṁ nidhanaṁ kartuṁ putrasya janitur mama |
ghr̥ṇām apāsya rāmārthe nihanyāṁ bhrātur ātmajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
hantukāmasya me bāṣpaṁ cakśuś caiva nirudhyate |
tad evaiṣa mahābāhur lakṣmaṇaḥ śamayiṣyati |
vānarā ghnantuṁ saṁbhūya bhr̥tyān asya samīpagān ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
iti tenātiyaśasā rākṣasenābhicoditāḥ |
vānarendrā jahr̥ṣire lāṅgalāni ca vivyadhuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tatas te kapiśārdūlāḥ kṣveḍantaś ca muhur muhuḥ |
mumucur vividhān nādān meghān dr̥ṣṭveva barhiṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
jāmbavān api taiḥ sarvaiḥ svayūthair abhisaṁvr̥taḥ |
aśmabhis tāḍayām āsa nakhair dantaiś ca rākṣasān ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
nighnantam r̥kṣādhipatiṁ rākṣasās te mahābalāḥ |
parivavrur bhayaṁ tyaktvā tam anekavidhāyudhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
śaraiḥ paraśubhis tīkṣṇaiḥ paṭṭasair yaṣṭitomaraiḥ |
jāmbavantaṁ mr̥dhe jaghnur nighnantaṁ rākṣasīṁ camūm ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
sa saṁprahāras tumulaḥ saṁjajñe kapirākṣasām |
devāsurāṇāṁ kruddhānāṁ yathā bhīmo mahāsvanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
hanūmān api saṁkruddhaḥ sālam utpāṭya parvatāt |
rakṣasāṁ kadanaṁ cakre samāsādya sahasraśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
sa dattvā tumulaṁ yuddhaṁ pitr̥vyasyendrajid yudhi |
lakṣmaṇaṁ paravīraghnaṁ punar evābhyadhāvata ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tau prayuddhau tadā vīrau mr̥dhe lakṣmaṇarākṣasau |
śaraughān abhivarṣantau jaghnatus tau parasparam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
abhīkṣṇam antardadhatuḥ śarajālair mahābalau |
candrādityāv ivoṣṇānte yathā meghais tarasvinau ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
na hy ādānaṁ na saṁdhānaṁ dhanuṣo vā parigrahaḥ |
na vipramokṣo bāṇānāṁ na vikarṣo na vigrahaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
na muṣṭipratisaṁdhānaṁ na lakṣyapratipādanam |
adr̥śyata tayos tatra yudhyatoḥ pāṇilāghavāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
cāpavegapramuktaiś ca bāṇajālaiḥ samantataḥ |
antarikṣe ’bhisaṁchanne na rūpāṇi cakāśire |
tamasā pihitaṁ sarvam āsīd bhīmataraṁ mahat ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
na tadānīṁ vavau vāyur na jajvāla ca pāvakaḥ |
svastyas tu lokebhya iti jajalpaś ca maharṣayaḥ |
saṁpetuś cātra saṁprāptā gandharvāḥ saha cāraṇaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
atha rākṣasasiṁhasya kr̥ṣṇān kanakabhūṣaṇān |
śaraiś caturbhiḥ saumitrir vivyādha caturo hayān ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
tato ’pareṇa bhallena sūtasya vicariṣyataḥ |
lāghavād rāghavaḥ śrīmāñ śiraḥ kāyād apāharat ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
nihataṁ sārathiṁ dr̥ṣṭvā samare rāvaṇātmajaḥ |
prajahau samaroddharṣaṁ viṣaṇṇaḥ sa babhūva ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
viṣaṇṇavadanaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rākṣasaṁ hariyūthapāḥ |
tataḥ paramasaṁhr̥ṣṭo lakṣmaṇaṁ cābhyapūjayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
tataḥ pramāthī śarabho rabhaso gandhamādanaḥ |
amr̥ṣyamāṇāś catvāraś cakrur vegaṁ harīśvarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
te cāsya hayamukhyeṣu tūrṇam utpatya vānarāḥ |
caturṣu sumahāvīryā nipetur bhīmavikramāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
teṣām adhiṣṭhitānāṁ tair vānaraiḥ parvatopamaiḥ |
mukhebhyo rudhiraṁ vyaktaṁ hayānāṁ samavartata ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
te nihatya hayāṁs tasya pramathya ca mahāratham |
punar utpatya vegena tasthur lakṣmaṇapārśvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
sa hatāśvād avaplutya rathān mathitasāratheḥ |
śaravarṣeṇa saumitrim abhyadhāvata rāvaṇiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
tato mahendrapratimaḥ sa lakṣmaṇaḥ; padātinaṁ taṁ niśitaiḥ śarottamaiḥ |
sr̥jantam ādau niśitāñ śarottamān; bhr̥śaṁ tadā bāṇagaṇair nyavārayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
