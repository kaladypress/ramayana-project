=== Sarga 34 ===

---
1.
yudhyatām eva teṣāṁ tu tadā vānararakṣasām |
ravir astaṁ gato rātriḥ pravr̥ttā prāṇahāriṇī ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
anyonyaṁ baddhavairāṇāṁ ghorāṇāṁ jayam icchatām |
saṁpravr̥ttaṁ niśāyuddhaṁ tadā vāraṇarakṣasām ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
rākṣaso ’sīti harayo hariś cāsīti rākṣasāḥ |
anyonyaṁ samare jaghnus tasmiṁs tamasi dāruṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
jahi dāraya caitīti kathaṁ vidravasīti ca |
evaṁ sutumulaḥ śabdas tasmiṁs tamasi śuśruve ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
kālāḥ kāñcanasaṁnāhās tasmiṁs tamasi rākṣasāḥ |
saṁprādr̥śyanta śailendrā dīptauṣadhivanā iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tasmiṁs tamasi duṣpāre rākṣasāḥ krodhamūrchitāḥ |
paripetur mahāvegā bhakṣayantaḥ plavaṁgamān ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
te hayān kāñcanāpīḍan dhvajāṁś cāgniśikhopamān |
āplutya daśanais tīkṣṇair bhīmakopā vyadārayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
kuñjarān kuñjarārohān patākādhvajino rathān |
cakarṣuś ca dadaṁśuś ca daśanaiḥ krodhamūrchitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
lakṣmaṇaś cāpi rāmaś ca śarair āśīviṣomapaiḥ |
dr̥śyādr̥śyāni rakṣāṁsi pravarāṇi nijaghnatuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
turaṁgakhuravidhvastaṁ rathanemisamuddhatam |
rurodha karṇanetrāṇiṇyudhyatāṁ dharaṇīrajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
vartamāne tathā ghore saṁgrāme lomaharṣaṇe |
rudhirodā mahāvegā nadyas tatra prasusruvuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tato bherīmr̥daṅgānāṁ paṇavānāṁ ca nisvanaḥ |
śaṅkhaveṇusvanonmiśraḥ saṁbabhūvādbhutopamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
hatānāṁ stanamānānāṁ rākṣasānāṁ ca nisvanaḥ |
śastrāṇāṁ vānarāṇāṁ ca saṁbabhūvātidāruṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
śastrapuṣpopahārā ca tatrāsīd yuddhamedinī |
durjñeyā durniveśā ca śoṇitāsravakardamā ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sā babhūva niśā ghorā harirākṣasahāriṇī |
kālarātrīva bhūtānāṁ sarveṣāṁ duratikramā ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tatas te rākṣasās tatra tasmiṁs tamasi dāruṇe |
rāmam evābhyadhāvanta saṁhr̥ṣṭā śaravr̥ṣṭibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
teṣām āpatatāṁ śabdaḥ kruddhānām abhigarjatām |
udvarta iva saptānāṁ samudrāṇām abhūt svanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
teṣāṁ rāmaḥ śaraiḥ ṣaḍbhiḥ ṣaḍ jaghāna niśācarān |
nimeṣāntaramātreṇa śitair agniśikhopamaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
yajñaśatruś ca durdharṣo mahāpārśvamahodarau |
vajradaṁṣṭro mahākāyas tau cobhau śukasāraṇau ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
te tu rāmeṇa bāṇaughaḥ sarvamarmasu tāḍitāḥ |
yuddhād apasr̥tās tatra sāvaśeṣāyuṣo ’bhavan ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tataḥ kāñcanacitrāṅgaiḥ śarair agniśikhopamaiḥ |
diśaś cakāra vimalāḥ pradiśaś ca mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
ye tv anye rākṣasā vīrā rāmasyābhimukhe sthitāḥ |
te ’pi naṣṭāḥ samāsādya pataṁgā iva pāvakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
suvarṇapuṅkhair viśikhaiḥ saṁpatadbhiḥ sahasraśaḥ |
babhūva rajanī citrā khadyotair iva śāradī ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
rākṣasānāṁ ca ninadair harīṇāṁ cāpi garjitaiḥ |
sā babhūva niśā ghorā bhūyo ghoratarā tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tena śabdena mahatā pravr̥ddhena samantataḥ |
trikūṭaḥ kandarākīrṇaḥ pravyāharad ivācalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
golāṅgūlā mahākāyās tamasā tulyavarcasaḥ |
saṁpariṣvajya bāhubhyāṁ bhakṣayan rajanīcarān ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
aṅgadas tu raṇe śatruṁ nihantuṁ samupasthitaḥ |
rāvaṇer nijaghānāśu sārathiṁ ca hayān api ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
indrajit tu rathaṁ tyaktvā hatāśvo hatasārathiḥ |
aṅgadena mahāmāyas tatraivāntaradhīyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
so ’ntardhāna gataḥ pāpo rāvaṇī raṇakarkaśaḥ |
brahmadattavaro vīro rāvaṇiḥ krodhamūrchitaḥ |
adr̥śyo niśitān bāṇān mumocāśanivarcasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
sa rāmaṁ lakṣmaṇaṁ caiva ghorair nāgamayaiḥ śaraiḥ |
bibheda samare kruddhaḥ sarvagātreṣu rākṣasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
