=== Sarga 51 ===

---
1.
tasya rākṣasarājasya niśamya paridevitam |
kumbhakarṇo babhāṣe ’tha vacanaṁ prajahāsa ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
dr̥ṣṭo doṣo hi yo ’smābhiḥ purā mantravinirṇaye |
hiteṣv anabhiyuktena so ’yam āsāditas tvayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
śīghraṁ khalv abhyupetaṁ tvāṁ phalaṁ pāpasya karmaṇaḥ |
nirayeṣv eva patanaṁ yathā duṣkr̥takarmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
prathamaṁ vai mahārāja kr̥tyam etad acintitam |
kevalaṁ vīryadarpeṇa nānubandho vicāritaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
yaḥ paścāt pūrvakāryāṇi kuryād aiśvaryam āsthitaḥ |
pūrvaṁ cottarakāryāṇi na sa veda nayānayau ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
deśakālavihīnāni karmāṇi viparītavat |
kriyamāṇāni duṣyanti havīṁṣy aprayateṣv iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
trayāṇāṁ pañcadhā yogaṁ karmaṇāṁ yaḥ prapaśyati |
sacivaiḥ samayaṁ kr̥tvā sa sabhye vartate pathi ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
yathāgamaṁ ca yo rājā samayaṁ vicikīrṣati |
budhyate sacivān buddhyā suhr̥daś cānupaśyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
dharmam arthaṁ ca kāmaṁ ca sarvān vā rakṣasāṁ pate |
bhajate puruṣaḥ kāle trīṇi dvandvāni vā punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
triṣu caiteṣu yac chreṣṭhaṁ śrutvā tan nāvabudhyate |
rājā vā rājamātro vā vyarthaṁ tasya bahuśrutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
upapradānaṁ sāntvaṁ vā bhedaṁ kāle ca vikramam |
yogaṁ ca rakṣasāṁ śreṣṭha tāv ubhau ca nayānayau ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
kāle dharmārthakāmān yaḥ saṁmantrya sacivaiḥ saha |
niṣevetātmavām̐l loke na sa vyasanam āpnuyāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
hitānubandham ālokya kāryākāryam ihātmanaḥ |
rājā sahārthatattvajñaiḥ sacivaiḥ saha jīvati ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
anabhijñāya śāstrārthān puruṣāḥ paśubuddhayaḥ |
prāgalbhyād vaktum icchanti mantreṣv abhyantarīkr̥tāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
aśāstraviduṣāṁ teṣāṁ na kāryam ahitaṁ vacaḥ |
arthaśāstrānabhijñānāṁ vipulāṁ śriyam icchatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
ahitaṁ ca hitākāraṁ dhārṣṭyāj jalpanti ye narāḥ |
avekṣya mantrabāhyās te kartavyāḥ kr̥tyadūṣaṇāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
vināśayanto bhartāraṁ sahitāḥ śatrubhir budhaiḥ |
viparītāni kr̥tyāni kārayantīha mantriṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tān bhartā mitrasaṁkāśān amitrān mantranirṇaye |
vyavahāreṇa jānīyāt sacivān upasaṁhitān ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
capalasyeha kr̥tyāni sahasānupradhāvataḥ |
chidram anye prapadyante krauñcasya kham iva dvijāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
yo hi śatrum avajñāya nātmānam abhirakṣati |
avāpnoti hi so ’narthān sthānāc ca vyavaropyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tat tu śrutvā daśagrīvaḥ kumbhakarṇasya bhāṣitam |
bhrukuṭiṁ caiva saṁcakre kruddhaś cainam uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
mānyo gurur ivācāryaḥ kiṁ māṁ tvam anuśāsati |
kim evaṁ vākśramaṁ kr̥tvā kāle yuktaṁ vidhīyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
vibhramāc cittamohād vā balavīryāśrayeṇa vā |
nābhipannam idānīṁ yad vyarthās tasya punaḥ kr̥thāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
asmin kāle tu yad yuktaṁ tad idānīṁ vidhīyatām |
mamāpanayajaṁ doṣaṁ vikrameṇa samīkuru ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
yadi khalv asti me sneho bhrātr̥tvaṁ vāvagacchasi |
yadi vā kāryam etat te hr̥di kāryatamaṁ matam ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
sa suhr̥dyo vipannārthaṁ dīnam abhyavapadyate |
sa bandhur yo ’panīteṣu sāhāyyāyopakalpate ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tam athaivaṁ bruvāṇaṁ tu vacanaṁ dhīradāruṇam |
ruṣṭo ’yam iti vijñāya śanaiḥ ślakṣṇam uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
atīva hi samālakṣya bhrātaraṁ kṣubhitendriyam |
kumbhakarṇaḥ śanair vākyaṁ babhāṣe parisāntvayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
alaṁ rākṣasarājendra saṁtāpam upapadya te |
roṣaṁ ca saṁparityajya svastho bhavitum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
naitan manasi kartavvyaṁ mayi jīvati pārthiva |
tam ahaṁ nāśayiṣyāmi yatkr̥te paritapyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
avaśyaṁ tu hitaṁ vācyaṁ sarvāvasthaṁ mayā tava |
bandhubhāvād abhihitaṁ bhrātr̥snehāc ca pārthiva ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
sadr̥śaṁ yat tu kāle ’smin kartuṁ snigdhena bandhunā |
śatrūṇāṁ kadanaṁ paśya kriyamāṇaṁ mayā raṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
adya paśya mahābāho mayā samaramūrdhani |
hate rāme saha bhrātrā dravantīṁ harivāhinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
adya rāmasya tad dr̥ṣṭvā mayānītaṁ raṇāc chiraḥ |
sukhībhava mahābāho sītā bhavatu duḥkhitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
adya rāmasya paśyantu nidhanaṁ sumahat priyam |
laṅkāyāṁ rākṣasāḥ sarve ye te nihatabāndhavāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
adya śokaparītānāṁ svabandhuvadhakāraṇāt |
śatror yudhi vināśena karomy asrapramārjanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
adya parvatasaṁkāśaṁ sasūryam iva toyadam |
vikīrṇaṁ paśya samare sugrīvaṁ plavageśvaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
na paraḥ preṣaṇīyas te yuddhāyātula vikrama |
aham utsādayiṣyāmi śatrūṁs tava mahābala ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
yadi śakro yadi yamo yadi pāvakamārutau |
tān ahaṁ yodhayiṣyāmi kubera varuṇāv api ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
girimātraśarīrasya śitaśūladharasya me |
nardatas tīkṣṇadaṁṣṭrasya bibhīyāc ca puraṁdaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
atha vā tyaktaśastrasya mr̥dgatas tarasā ripūn |
na me pratimukhe kaś cic chaktaḥ sthātuṁ jijīviṣuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
naiva śaktyā na gadayā nāsinā na śitaiḥ śaraiḥ |
hastābhyām eva saṁrabdho haniṣyāmy api vajriṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
yadi me muṣṭivegaṁ sa rāghavo ’dya sahiṣyati |
tataḥ pāsyanti bāṇaughā rudhiraṁ rāghavasya te ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
cintayā bādhyase rājan kimarthaṁ mayi tiṣṭhati |
so ’haṁ śatruvināśāya tava niryātum udyataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
muñca rāmād bhayaṁ rājan haniṣyāmīha saṁyuge |
rāghavaṁ lakṣmaṇaṁ caiva sugrīvaṁ ca mahābalam |
asādhāraṇam icchāmi tava dātuṁ mahad yaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
vadhena te dāśaratheḥ sukhāvahaṁ; sukhaṁ samāhartum ahaṁ vrajāmi |
nihatya rāmaṁ sahalakṣmaṇena; khādāmi sarvān hariyūthamukhyān ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
ramasva kāmaṁ piba cāgryavāruṇīṁ; kuruṣva kr̥tyāni vinīyatāṁ jvaraḥ |
mayādya rāme gamite yamakṣayaṁ; cirāya sītā vaśagā bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
