=== Sarga 43 ===

---
1.
dhūmrākṣaṁ nihataṁ śrutvā rāvaṇo rākṣaseśvaraḥ |
balādhyakṣam uvācedaṁ kr̥tāñjalim upasthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
śīghraṁ niryāntu durdharṣā rākṣasā bhīmavikramāḥ |
akampanaṁ puraskr̥tya sarvaśastraprakovidam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tato nānāpraharaṇā bhīmākṣā bhīmadarśanāḥ |
niṣpetū rākṣasā mukhyā balādhyakṣapracoditāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
ratham āsthāya vipulaṁ taptakāñcanakuṇḍalaḥ |
rākasaiḥ saṁvr̥to ghorais tadā niryāty akampanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
na hi kampayituṁ śakyaḥ surair api mahāmr̥dhe |
akampanas tatas teṣām āditya iva tejasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tasya nidhāvamānasya saṁrabdhasya yuyutsayā |
akasmād dainyam āgacchad dhayānāṁ rathavāhinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
vyasphuran nayanaṁ cāsya savyaṁ yuddhābhinandinaḥ |
vivarṇo mukhavarṇaś ca gadgadaś cābhavat svaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
abhavat sudine cāpi durdine rūkṣamārutam |
ūcuḥ khagā mr̥gāḥ sarve vācaḥ krūrā bhayāvahāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sa siṁhopacitaskandhaḥ śārdūlasamavikramaḥ |
tān utpātān acintyaiva nirjagāma raṇājiram ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tadā nirgacchatas tasya rakṣasaḥ saha rākṣasaiḥ |
babhūva sumahān nādaḥ kṣobhayann iva sāgaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tena śabdena vitrastā vānarāṇāṁ mahācamūḥ |
drumaśailapraharaṇā yoddhuṁ samavatiṣṭhata ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
teṣāṁ yuddhaṁ mahāraudraṁ saṁjajñe kapirakṣasām |
rāmarāvaṇayor arthe samabhityaktajīvinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sarve hy atibalāḥ śūrāḥ sarve parvatasaṁnibhāḥ |
harayo rākṣasāś caiva parasparajighaṁsavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
teṣāṁ vinardātāṁ śabdaḥ saṁyuge ’titarasvinām |
śuśruve sumahān krodhād anyonyam abhigarjatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
rajaś cāruṇavarṇābhaṁ subhīmam abhavad bhr̥śam |
uddhūtaṁ harirakṣobhiḥ saṁrurodha diśo daśa ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
anyonyaṁ rajasā tena kauśeyoddhūtapāṇḍunā |
saṁvr̥tāni ca bhūtāni dadr̥śur na raṇājire ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
na dhvajo na patākāvā varma vā turago ’pi vā |
āyudhaṁ syandanaṁ vāpi dadr̥śe tena reṇunā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
śabdaś ca sumahāṁs teṣāṁ nardatām abhidhāvatām |
śrūyate tumule yuddhe na rūpāṇi cakāśire ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
harīn eva susaṁkruddhā harayo jaghnur āhave |
rākṣasāś cāpi rakṣāṁsi nijaghnus timire tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
parāṁś caiva vinighnantaḥ svāṁś ca vānararākṣasāḥ |
rudhirārdraṁ tadā cakrur mahīṁ paṅkānulepanām ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tatas tu rudhiraugheṇa siktaṁ vyapagataṁ rajaḥ |
śarīraśavasaṁkīrṇā babhūva ca vasuṁdharā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
drumaśaktiśilāprāsair gadāparighatomaraiḥ |
harayo rākṣasās tūrṇaṁ jaghnur anyonyam ojasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
bāhubhiḥ parighākārair yudhyantaḥ parvatopamāḥ |
harayo bhīmakarmāṇo rākṣasāñ jaghnur āhave ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
rākṣasāś cāpi saṁkruddhāḥ prāsatomarapāṇayaḥ |
kapīn nijaghnire tatra śastraiḥ paramadāruṇaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
harayas tv api rakṣāṁsi mahādrumamahāśmabhiḥ |
vidārayanty abhikramya śastrāṇy ācchidya vīryataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
etasminn antare vīrā harayaḥ kumudo nalaḥ |
maindaś ca paramakruddhaś cakrur vegam anuttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
te tu vr̥kṣair mahāvegā rākṣasānāṁ camūmukhe |
kadanaṁ sumaha cakrur līlayā hariyūthapāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
