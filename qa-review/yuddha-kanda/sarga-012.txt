=== Sarga 12 ===

---
1.
atha rāmaḥ prasannātmā śrutvā vāyusutasya ha |
pratyabhāṣata durdharṣaḥ śrutavān ātmani sthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
mamāpi tu vivakṣāsti kā cit prati vibhīṣaṇam |
śrutam icchāmi tat sarvaṁ bhavadbhiḥ śreyasi sthitaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
mitrabhāvena saṁprāptaṁ na tyajeyaṁ kathaṁ cana |
doṣo yady api tasya syāt satām etad agarhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
rāmasya vacanaṁ śrutvā sugrīvaḥ plavageśvaraḥ |
pratyabhāṣata kākutsthaṁ sauhārdenābhicoditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
kim atra citraṁ dharmajña lokanāthaśikhāmaṇe |
yat tvam āryaṁ prabhāṣethāḥ sattvavān sapathe sthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
mama cāpy antarātmāyaṁ śuddhiṁ vetti vibhīṣaṇam |
anumanāc ca bhāvāc ca sarvataḥ suparīkṣitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tasmāt kṣipraṁ sahāsmābhis tulyo bhavatu rāghava |
vibhīṣaṇo mahāprājñaḥ sakhitvaṁ cābhyupaitu naḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sa sugrīvasya tad vākyaṁ rāmaḥ śrutvā vimr̥śya ca |
tataḥ śubhataraṁ vākyam uvāca haripuṁgavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
suduṣṭo vāpy aduṣṭo vā kim eṣa rajanīcaraḥ |
sūkṣmam apy ahitaṁ kartuṁ mamāśaktaḥ kathaṁ cana ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
piśācān dānavān yakṣān pr̥thivyāṁ caiva rākṣasān |
aṅgulyagreṇa tān hanyām icchan harigaṇeśvara ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
śrūyate hi kapotena śatruḥ śaraṇam āgataḥ |
arcitaś ca yathānyāyaṁ svaiś ca māṁsair nimantritaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sa hi taṁ pratijagrāha bhāryā hartāram āgatam |
kapoto vānaraśreṣṭha kiṁ punar madvidho janaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
r̥ṣeḥ kaṇvasya putreṇa kaṇḍunā paramarṣiṇā |
śr̥ṇu gāthāṁ purā gītāṁ dharmiṣṭhāṁ satyavādinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
baddhāñjalipuṭaṁ dīnaṁ yācantaṁ śaraṇāgatam |
na hanyād ānr̥śaṁsyārtham api śatruṁ paraṁ pata ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
ārto vā yadi vā dr̥ptaḥ pareṣāṁ śaraṇaṁ gataḥ |
ariḥ prāṇān parityajya rakṣitavyaḥ kr̥tātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sa ced bhayād vā mohād vā kāmād vāpi na rakṣati |
svayā śaktyā yathātattvaṁ tat pāpaṁ lokagarhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
vinaṣṭaḥ paśyatas tasya rakṣiṇaḥ śaraṇāgataḥ |
ādāya sukr̥taṁ tasya sarvaṁ gacched arakṣitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
evaṁ doṣo mahān atra prapannānām arakṣaṇe |
asvargyaṁ cāyaśasyaṁ ca balavīryavināśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
kariṣyāmi yathārthaṁ tu kaṇḍor vacanam uttamam |
dharmiṣṭhaṁ ca yaśasyaṁ ca svargyaṁ syāt tu phalodaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
sakr̥d eva prapannāya tavāsmīti ca yācate |
abhayaṁ sarvabhūtebhyo dadāmy etad vrataṁ mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
ānayainaṁ hariśreṣṭha dattam asyābhayaṁ mayā |
vibhīṣaṇo vā sugrīva yadi vā rāvaṇaḥ svayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tatas tu sugrīvavaco niśamya tad; dharīśvareṇābhihitaṁ nareśvaraḥ |
vibhīṣaṇenāśu jagāma saṁgamaṁ; patatrirājena yathā puraṁdaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
