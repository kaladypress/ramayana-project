=== Sarga 68 ===

---
1.
vijñāya tu manas tasya rāghavasya mahātmanaḥ |
saṁnivr̥tyāhavāt tasmāt praviveśa puraṁ tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
so ’nusmr̥tya vadhaṁ teṣāṁ rākṣasānāṁ tarasvinām |
krodhatāmrekṣaṇaḥ śūro nirjagāma mahādyutiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sa paścimena dvāreṇa niryayau rākṣasair vr̥taḥ |
indrajit tu mahāvīryaḥ paulastyo devakaṇṭakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
indrajit tu tato dr̥ṣṭvā bhrātarau rāmalakṣmaṇau |
raṇāyābhyudyatau vīrau māyāṁ prāduṣkarot tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
indrajit tu rathe sthāpya sītāṁ māyāmayīṁ tadā |
balena mahatāvr̥tya tasyā vadham arocayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
mohanārthaṁ tu sarveṣāṁ buddhiṁ kr̥tvā sudurmatiḥ |
hantuṁ sītāṁ vyavasito vānarābhimukho yayau ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā tv abhiniryāntaṁ nagaryāḥ kānanaukasaḥ |
utpetur abhisaṁkruddhāḥ śilāhastā yuyutsavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
hanūmān puratas teṣāṁ jagāma kapikuñjaraḥ |
pragr̥hya sumahac chr̥ṅgaṁ parvatasya durāsadam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sa dadarśa hatānandāṁ sītām indrajito rathe |
ekaveṇīdharāṁ dīnām upavāsakr̥śānanām ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
parikliṣṭaikavasanām amr̥jāṁ rāghavapriyām |
rajomalābhyām āliptaiḥ sarvagātrair varastriyam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tāṁ nirīkṣya muhūrtaṁ tu maithilīm adhyavasya ca |
bāṣpaparyākulamukho hanūmān vyathito ’bhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
abravīt tāṁ tu śokārtāṁ nirānandāṁ tapasvinām |
dr̥ṣṭvā rathe stitāṁ sītāṁ rākṣasendrasutāśritām ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
kiṁ samarthitam asyeti cintayan sa mahākapiḥ |
saha tair vānaraśreṣṭhair abhyadhāvata rāvaṇim ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tad vānarabalaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rāvaṇiḥ krodhamūrchitaḥ |
kr̥tvā viśokaṁ nistriṁśaṁ mūrdhni sītāṁ parāmr̥śat ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
taṁ striyaṁ paśyatāṁ teṣāṁ tāḍayām āsa rāvaṇiḥ |
krośantīṁ rāma rāmeti māyayā yojitāṁ rathe ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
gr̥hītamūrdhajāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā hanūmān dainyam āgataḥ |
duḥkhajaṁ vārinetrābhyām utsr̥jan mārutātmajaḥ |
abravīt paruṣaṁ vākyaṁ krodhād rakṣo’dhipātmajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
durātmann ātmanāśāya keśapakṣe parāmr̥śaḥ |
brahmarṣīṇāṁ kule jāto rākṣasīṁ yonim āśritaḥ |
dhik tvāṁ pāpasamācāraṁ yasya te matir īdr̥śī ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
nr̥śaṁsānārya durvr̥tta kṣudra pāpaparākrama |
anāryasyedr̥śaṁ karma ghr̥ṇā te nāsti nirghr̥ṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
cyutā gr̥hāc ca rājyāc ca rāmahastāc ca maithilī |
kiṁ tavaiṣāparāddhā hi yad enāṁ hantum icchasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
sītāṁ ca hatvā na ciraṁ jīviṣyasi kathaṁ cana |
vadhārhakarmaṇānena mama hastagato hy asi ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
ye ca strīghātināṁ lokā lokavadhyaiś ca kutsitāḥ |
iha jīvitam utsr̥jya pretya tān pratilapsyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
iti bruvāṇo hanumān sāyudhair haribhir vr̥taḥ |
abhyadhāvata saṁkruddho rākṣasendrasutaṁ prati ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
āpatantaṁ mahāvīryaṁ tad anīkaṁ vanaukasām |
rakṣasāṁ bhīmavegānām anīkena nyavārayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
sa tāṁ bāṇasahasreṇa vikṣobhya harivāhinīm |
hariśreṣṭhaṁ hanūmantam indrajit pratyuvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
sugrīvas tvaṁ ca rāmaś ca yannimittam ihāgatāḥ |
tāṁ haniṣyāmi vaidehīm adyaiva tava paśyataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
imāṁ hatvā tato rāmaṁ lakṣmaṇaṁ tvāṁ ca vānara |
sugrīvaṁ ca vadhiṣyāmi taṁ cānāryaṁ vibhīṣaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
na hantavyāḥ striyaś ceti yad bravīṣi plavaṁgama |
pīḍā karam amitrāṇāṁ yat syāt kartavyam eta tat ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tam evam uktvā rudatīṁ sītāṁ māyāmayīṁ tataḥ |
śitadhāreṇa khaḍgena nijaghānendrajit svayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
yajñopavītamārgeṇa chinnā tena tapasvinī |
sā pr̥thivyāṁ pr̥thuśroṇī papāta priyadarśanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
tām indrajitstriyaṁ hatvā hanūmantam uvāca ha |
mayā rāmasya paśyemāṁ kopena ca niṣūditām ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
tataḥ khaḍgena mahatā hatvā tām indrajit svayam |
hr̥ṣṭaḥ sa ratham āsthāya vinanāda mahāsvanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
vānarāḥ śuśruvuḥ śabdam adūre pratyavasthitāḥ |
vyāditāsyasya nadatas tad durgaṁ saṁśritasya tu ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
tathā tu sītāṁ vinihatya durmatiḥ; prahr̥ṣṭacetāḥ sa babhūva rāvaṇiḥ |
taṁ hr̥ṣṭarūpaṁ samudīkṣya vānarā; viṣaṇṇarūpāḥ samabhipradudruvuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
