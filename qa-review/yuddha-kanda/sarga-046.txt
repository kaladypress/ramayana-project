=== Sarga 46 ===

---
1.
tataḥ prahastaṁ niryāntaṁ bhīmaṁ bhīmaparākramam |
garjantaṁ sumahākāyaṁ rākṣasair abhisaṁvr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
dadarśa mahatī senā vānarāṇāṁ balīyasām |
atisaṁjātaroṣāṇāṁ prahastam abhigarjatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
khaḍgaśaktyaṣṭibāṇāś ca śūlāni musalāni ca |
gadāś ca parighāḥ prāsā vividhāś ca paraśvadhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
dhanūṁṣi ca vicitrāṇi rākṣasānāṁ jayaiṣiṇām |
pragr̥hītāny aśobhanta vānarān abhidhāvatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
jagr̥huḥ pādapāṁś cāpi puṣpitān vānararṣabhāḥ |
śilāś ca vipulā dīrghā yoddhukāmāḥ plavaṁgamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
teṣām anyonyam āsādya saṁgrāmaḥ sumahān abhūt |
bahūnām aśmavr̥ṣṭiṁ ca śaravr̥ṣṭiṁ ca varṣatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
bahavo rākṣasā yuddhe bahūn vānarayūthapān |
vānarā rākṣasāṁś cāpi nijaghnur bahavo bahūn ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
śūlaiḥ pramathitāḥ ke cit ke cit tu paramāyudhaiḥ |
parighair āhatāḥ ke cit ke cic chinnāḥ paraśvadhaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
nirucchvāsāḥ punaḥ ke cit patitā dharaṇītale |
vibhinnahr̥dayāḥ ke cid iṣusaṁtānasaṁditāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
ke cid dvidhākr̥tāḥ khaḍgaiḥ sphurantaḥ patitā bhuvi |
vānarā rākṣasaiḥ śūlaiḥ pārśvataś ca vidāritāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
vānaraiś cāpi saṁkruddhai rākṣasaughāḥ samantataḥ |
pādapair giriśr̥ṅgaiś ca saṁpiṣṭā vasudhātale ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
vajrasparśatalair hastair muṣṭibhiś ca hatā bhr̥śam |
vemuḥ śoṇitam āsyebhyo viśīrṇadaśanekṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
ārtasvaraṁ ca svanatāṁ siṁhanādaṁ ca nardatām |
babhūva tumulaḥ śabdo harīṇāṁ rakṣasāṁ yudhi ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
vānarā rākṣasāḥ kruddhā vīramārgam anuvratāḥ |
vivr̥ttanayanāḥ krūrāś cakruḥ karmāṇy abhītavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
narāntakaḥ kumbhahanur mahānādaḥ samunnataḥ |
ete prahastasacivāḥ sarve jaghnur vanaukasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
teṣām āpatatāṁ śīghraṁ nighnatāṁ cāpi vānarān |
dvivido giriśr̥ṅgeṇa jaghānaikaṁ narāntakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
durmukhaḥ punar utpāṭya kapiḥ sa vipuladrumam |
rākṣasaṁ kṣiprahastas tu samunnatam apothayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
jāmbavāṁs tu susaṁkruddhaḥ pragr̥hya mahatīṁ śilām |
pātayām āsa tejasvī mahānādasya vakṣasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
atha kumbhahanus tatra tāreṇāsādya vīryavān |
vr̥kṣeṇābhihato mūrdhni prāṇāṁs tatyāja rākṣasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
amr̥ṣyamāṇas tat karma prahasto ratham āsthitaḥ |
cakāra kadanaṁ ghoraṁ dhanuṣpāṇir vanaukasām ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
āvarta iva saṁjajñe ubhayoḥ senayos tadā |
kṣubhitasyāprameyasya sāgarasyeva nisvanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
mahatā hi śaraugheṇa prahasto yuddhakovidaḥ |
ardayām āsa saṁkruddho vānarān paramāhave ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
vānarāṇāṁ śarīrais tu rākṣasānāṁ ca medinī |
babhūva nicitā ghorā patitair iva parvataiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
sā mahīrudhiraugheṇa pracchannā saṁprakāśate |
saṁchannā mādhave māsi palāśair iva puṣpitaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
hatavīraughavaprāṁ tu bhagnāyudhamahādrumām |
śoṇitaughamahātoyāṁ yamasāgaragāminīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
yakr̥tplīhamahāpaṅkāṁ vinikīrṇāntraśaivalām |
bhinnakāyaśiromīnām aṅgāvayavaśāḍvalām ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
gr̥dhrahaṁsagaṇākīrṇāṁ kaṅkasārasasevitām |
medhaḥphenasamākīrṇām ārtastanitanisvanām ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tāṁ kāpuruṣadustārāṁ yuddhabhūmimayīṁ nadīm |
nadīm iva ghanāpāye haṁsasārasasevitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
rākṣasāḥ kapimukhyāś ca terus tāṁ dustarāṁ nadīm |
yathā padmarajodhvastāṁ nalinīṁ gajayūthapāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
tataḥ sr̥jantaṁ bāṇaughān prahastaṁ syandane sthitam |
dadarśa tarasā nīlo vinighnantaṁ plavaṁgamān ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
sa taṁ paramadurdharṣam āpatantaṁ mahākapiḥ |
prahastaṁ tāḍayām āsa vr̥kṣam utpāṭya vīryavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
sa tenābhihataḥ kruddho nadan rākṣasapuṁgavaḥ |
vavarṣa śaravarṣāṇi plavagānāṁ camūpatau ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
apārayan vārayituṁ pratyagr̥hṇān nimīlitaḥ |
yathaiva govr̥ṣo varṣaṁ śāradaṁ śīghram āgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
evam eva prahastasya śaravarṣaṁ durāsadam |
nimīlitākṣaḥ sahasā nīlaḥ sehe sudāruṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
roṣitaḥ śaravarṣeṇa sālena mahatā mahān |
prajaghāna hayān nīlaḥ prahastasya manojavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
vidhanus tu kr̥tas tena prahasto vāhinīpatiḥ |
pragr̥hya musalaṁ ghoraṁ syandanād avapupluve ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
tāv ubhau vāhinīmukhyau jātaroṣau tarasvinau |
sthitau kṣatajadigdhāṅgau prabhinnāv iva kuñjarau ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
ullikhantau sutīkṣṇābhir daṁṣṭrābhir itaretaram |
siṁhaśārdūlasadr̥śau siṁhaśārdūlaceṣṭitau ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
vikrāntavijayau vīrau samareṣv anivartinau |
kāṅkṣamāṇau yaśaḥ prāptuṁ vr̥travāsavayoḥ samau ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
ājaghāna tadā nīlaṁ lalāṭe musalena saḥ |
prahastaḥ paramāyastas tasya susrāva śoṇitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
tataḥ śoṇitadigdhāṅgaḥ pragr̥hya sumahātarum |
prahastasyorasi kruddho visasarja mahākapiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
tam acintyaprahāraṁ sa pragr̥hya musalaṁ mahat |
abhidudrāva balinaṁ balī nīlaṁ plavaṁgamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
tam ugravegaṁ saṁrabdham āpatantaṁ mahākapiḥ |
tataḥ saṁprekṣya jagrāha mahāvego mahāśilām ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
tasya yuddhābhikāmasya mr̥dhe musalayodhinaḥ |
prahastasya śilāṁ nīlo mūrdhni tūrṇam apātayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
sā tena kapimukhyena vimuktā mahatī śilā |
bibheda bahudhā ghorā prahastasya śiras tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
sa gatāsur gataśrīko gatasattvo gatendriyaḥ |
papāta sahasā bhūmau chinnamūla iva drumaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
vibhinnaśirasas tasya bahu susrāvaśoṇitam |
śarīrād api susrāva gireḥ prasravaṇaṁ yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
hate prahaste nīlena tad akampyaṁ mahad balam |
rakṣasām aprahr̥ṣṭānāṁ laṅkām abhijagāma ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
na śekuḥ samavasthātuṁ nihate vāhinīpatau |
setubandhaṁ samāsādya viśīrṇaṁ salilaṁ yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
hate tasmiṁś camūmukhye rākṣasas te nirudyamāḥ |
rakṣaḥpatigr̥haṁ gatvā dhyānamūkatvam āgatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
tatas tu nīlo vijayī mahābalaḥ; praśasyamānaḥ svakr̥tena karmaṇā |
sametya rāmeṇa salakṣmaṇena; prahr̥ṣṭarūpas tu babhūva yūthapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
