=== Sarga 62 ===

---
1.
tato ’bravīn mahātejāḥ sugrīvo vānarādhipaḥ |
arthyaṁ vijāpayaṁś cāpi hanūmantaṁ mahābalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
yato hataḥ kumbhakarṇaḥ kumārāś ca niṣūditāḥ |
nedānīm upanirhāraṁ rāvaṇo dātum arhati ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
ye ye mahābalāḥ santi laghavaś ca plavaṁgamāḥ |
laṅkām abhyutpatantv āśu gr̥hyolkāḥ plavagarṣabhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tato ’staṁ gata āditye raudre tasmin niśāmukhe |
laṅkām abhimukhāḥ solkā jagmus te plavagarṣabhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
ulkāhastair harigaṇaiḥ sarvataḥ samabhidrutāḥ |
ārakṣasthā virūpākṣāḥ sahasā vipradudruvuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
gopurāṭṭa pratolīṣu caryāsu vividhāsu ca |
prāsādeṣu ca saṁhr̥ṣṭāḥ sasr̥jus te hutāśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
teṣāṁ gr̥hasahasrāṇi dadāha hutabhuk tadā |
āvāsān rākṣasānāṁ ca sarveṣāṁ gr̥hamedhinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
hemacitratanutrāṇāṁ sragdāmāmbaradhāriṇām |
sīdhupānacalākṣāṇāṁ madavihvalagāminām ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
kāntālambitavastrāṇāṁ śatrusaṁjātamanyunām |
gadāśūlāsi hastānāṁ khādatāṁ pibatām api ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
śayaneṣu mahārheṣu prasuptānāṁ priyaiḥ saha |
trastānāṁ gacchatāṁ tūrṇaṁ putrān ādāya sarvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
teṣāṁ gr̥hasahasrāṇi tadā laṅkānivāsinām |
adahat pāvakas tatra jajvāla ca punaḥ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sāravanti mahārhāṇi gambhīraguṇavanti ca |
hemacandrārdhacandrāṇi candraśālonnatāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
ratnacitragavākṣāṇi sādhiṣṭhānāni sarvaśaḥ |
maṇividrumacitrāṇi spr̥śantīva ca bhāskaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
krauñcabarhiṇavīṇānāṁ bhūṣaṇānāṁ ca nisvanaiḥ |
nāditāny acalābhāni veśmāny agnir dadāha saḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
jvalanena parītāni toraṇāni cakāśire |
vidyudbhir iva naddhāni meghajālāni gharmage ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
vimāneṣu prasuptāś ca dahyamānā varāṅganāḥ |
tyaktābharaṇasaṁyogā hāhety uccair vicukruśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tatra cāgniparītāni nipetur bhavanāny api |
vajrivajrahatānīva śikharāṇi mahāgireḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tāni nirdahyamānāni dūrataḥ pracakāśire |
himavacchikharāṇīva dīptauṣadhivanāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
harmyāgrair dahyamānaiś ca jvālāprajvalitair api |
rātrau sā dr̥śyate laṅkā puṣpitair iva kiṁśukaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
hastyadhyakṣair gajair muktair muktaiś ca turagair api |
babhūva laṅkā lokānte bhrāntagrāha ivārṇavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
aśvaṁ muktaṁ gajo dr̥ṣṭvā kaccid bhīto ’pasarpati |
bhīto bhītaṁ gajaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā kva cid aśvo nivartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
sā babhūva muhūrtena haribhir dīpitā purī |
lokasyāsya kṣaye ghore pradīpteva vasuṁdharā ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
nārī janasya dhūmena vyāptasyoccair vineduṣaḥ |
svano jvalanataptasya śuśruve daśayojanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
pradagdhakāyān aparān rākṣasān nirgatān bahiḥ |
sahasābhyutpatanti sma harayo ’tha yuyutsavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
udghuṣṭaṁ vānarāṇāṁ ca rākṣasānāṁ ca nisvanaḥ |
diśo daśa samudraṁ ca pr̥thivīṁ cānvanādayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
viśalyau tu mahātmānau tāv ubhau rāmalakṣmaṇau |
asaṁbhrāntau jagr̥hatus tāv ubhau dhanuṣī vare ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tato visphārayāṇasya rāmasya dhanur uttamam |
babhūva tumulaḥ śabdo rākṣasānāṁ bhayāvahaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
aśobhata tadā rāmo dhanur visphārayan mahat |
bhagavān iva saṁkruddho bhavo vedamayaṁ dhanuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
vānarodghuṣṭaghoṣaś ca rākṣasānāṁ ca nisvanaḥ |
jyāśabdaś cāpi rāmasya trayaṁ vyāpa diśo daśa ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
tasya kārmukamuktaiś ca śarais tatpuragopuram |
kailāsaśr̥ṅgapratimaṁ vikīrṇam apatad bhuvi ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
tato rāmaśarān dr̥ṣṭvā vimāneṣu gr̥heṣu ca |
saṁnāho rākṣasendrāṇāṁ tumulaḥ samapadyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
teṣāṁ saṁnahyamānānāṁ siṁhanādaṁ ca kurvatām |
śarvarī rākṣasendrāṇāṁ raudrīva samapadyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
ādiṣṭā vānarendrās te sugrīveṇa mahātmanā |
āsannā dvāram āsādya yudhyadhvaṁ plavagarṣabhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
yaś ca vo vitathaṁ kuryāt tatra tatra vyavasthitaḥ |
sa hantavyo ’bhisaṁplutya rājaśāsanadūṣakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
teṣu vānaramukhyeṣu dīptolkojjvalapāṇiṣu |
sthiteṣu dvāram āsādya rāvaṇaṁ manyur āviśat ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
tasya jr̥mbhitavikṣepād vyāmiśrā vai diśo daśa |
rūpavān iva rudrasya manyur gātreṣv adr̥śyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
sa nikumbhaṁ ca kumbhaṁ ca kumbhakarṇātmajāv ubhau |
preṣayām āsa saṁkruddho rākṣasair bahubhiḥ saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
śaśāsa caiva tān sarvān rākṣasān rākṣaseśvaraḥ |
rākṣasā gacchatātraiva siṁhanādaṁ ca nādayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
tatas tu coditās tena rākṣasā jvalitāyudhāḥ |
laṅkāyā niryayur vīrāḥ praṇadantaḥ punaḥ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
bhīmāśvarathamātaṁgaṁ nānāpatti samākulam |
dīptaśūlagadākhaḍgaprāsatomarakārmukam ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
tad rākṣasabalaṁ ghoraṁ bhīmavikramapauruṣam |
dadr̥śe jvalitaprāsaṁ kiṅkiṇīśatanāditam ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
hemajālācitabhujaṁ vyāveṣṭitaparaśvadham |
vyāghūrṇitamahāśastraṁ bāṇasaṁsaktakārmukam ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
gandhamālyamadhūtsekasaṁmodita mahānilam |
ghoraṁ śūrajanākīrṇaṁ mahāmbudharanisvanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā balam āyāntaṁ rākṣasānāṁ sudāruṇam |
saṁcacāla plavaṁgānāṁ balam uccair nanāda ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
javenāplutya ca punas tad rākṣasabalaṁ mahat |
abhyayāt pratyaribalaṁ pataṁga iva pāvakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
teṣāṁ bhujaparāmarśavyāmr̥ṣṭaparighāśani |
rākṣasānāṁ balaṁ śreṣṭhaṁ bhūyastaram aśobhata ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
tathaivāpy apare teṣāṁ kapīnām asibhiḥ śitaiḥ |
pravīrān abhito jaghnur ghorarūpā niśācarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
ghnantam anyaṁ jaghānānyaḥ pātayantam apātayat |
garhamāṇaṁ jagarhānye daśantam apare ’daśat ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
dehīty anye dadāty anyo dadāmīty aparaḥ punaḥ |
kiṁ kleśayasi tiṣṭheti tatrānyonyaṁ babhāṣire ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
samudyatamahāprāsaṁ muṣṭiśūlāsisaṁkulam |
prāvartata mahāraudraṁ yuddhaṁ vānararakṣasām ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
vānarān daśa sapteti rākṣasā abhyapātayan |
rākṣasān daśasapteti vānarā jaghnur āhave ||

=== meaning ===

---
52.
visrastakeśarasanaṁ vimuktakavacadhvajam |
balaṁ rākṣasam ālambya vānarāḥ paryavārayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
