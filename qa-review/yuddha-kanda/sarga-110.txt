=== Sarga 110 ===

---
1.
upasthitaṁ tu taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā puṣpakaṁ puṣpabhūṣitam |
avidūre sthitaṁ rāmaṁ pratyuvāca vibhīṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sa tu baddhāñjaliḥ prahvo vinīto rākṣaseśvaraḥ |
abravīt tvarayopetaḥ kiṁ karomīti rāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tam abravīn mahātejā lakṣmaṇasyopaśr̥ṇvataḥ |
vimr̥śya rāghavo vākyam idaṁ snehapuraskr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
kr̥taprayatnakarmāṇo vibhīṣaṇa vanaukasaḥ |
ratnair arthaiś ca vivibhair bhūṣaṇaiś cābhipūjaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
sahaibhir arditā laṅkā nirjitā rākṣaseśvara |
hr̥ṣṭaiḥ prāṇabhayaṁ tyaktvā saṁgrāmeṣv anivartibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
evaṁ saṁmānitāś ceme mānārhā mānada tvayā |
bhaviṣyanti kr̥tajñena nirvr̥tā hariyūthapāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tyāginaṁ saṁgrahītāraṁ sānukrośaṁ yaśasvinam |
yatas tvām avagacchanti tataḥ saṁbodhayāmi te ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
evam uktas tu rāmeṇa vānarāṁs tān vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
ratnārthaiḥ saṁvibhāgena sarvān evānvapūjayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tatas tān pūjitān dr̥ṣṭvā ratnair arthaiś ca yūthapān |
āruroha tato rāmas tad vimānam anuttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
aṅkenādāya vaidehīṁ lajjamānāṁ yaśasvinīm |
lakṣmaṇena saha bhrātrā vikrāntena dhanuṣmatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
abravīc ca vimānasthaḥ kākutsthaḥ sarvavānarān |
sugrīvaṁ ca mahāvīryaṁ rākṣasaṁ ca vibhīṣaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
mitrakāryaṁ kr̥tam idaṁ bhavadbhir vānarottamāḥ |
anujñātā mayā sarve yatheṣṭaṁ pratigacchata ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
yat tu kāryaṁ vayasyena suhr̥dā vā paraṁtapa |
kr̥taṁ sugrīva tat sarvaṁ bhavatā dharmabhīruṇā |
kiṣkindhāṁ pratiyāhy āśu svasainyenābhisaṁvr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
svarājye vasa laṅkāyāṁ mayā datte vibhīṣaṇa |
na tvāṁ dharṣayituṁ śaktāḥ sendrā api divaukasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
ayodhyāṁ pratiyāsyāmi rājadhānīṁ pitur mama |
abhyanujñātum icchāmi sarvān āmantrayāmi vaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
evam uktās tu rāmeṇa vānarās te mahābalāḥ |
ūcuḥ prāñjalayo rāmaṁ rākṣasaś ca vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
ayodhyāṁ gantum icchāmaḥ sarvān nayatu no bhavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
dr̥ṣṭvā tvām abhiṣekārdraṁ kausalyām abhivādya ca |
acireṇāgamiṣyāmaḥ svān gr̥hān nr̥pateḥ suta ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
evam uktas tu dharmātmā vānaraiḥ savibhīṣaṇaiḥ |
abravīd rāghavaḥ śrīmān sasugrīvavibhīṣaṇān ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
priyāt priyataraṁ labdhaṁ yad ahaṁ sasuhr̥jjanaḥ |
sarvair bhavadbhiḥ sahitaḥ prītiṁ lapsye purīṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
kṣipram āroha sugrīva vimānaṁ vānaraiḥ saha |
tvam adhyāroha sāmātyo rākṣasendravibhīṣaṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tatas tat puṣpakaṁ divyaṁ sugrīvaḥ saha senayā |
adhyārohat tvarañ śīghraṁ sāmātyaś ca vibhīṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
teṣv ārūḍheṣu sarveṣu kauberaṁ paramāsanam |
rāghaveṇābhyanujñātam utpapāta vihāyasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
yayau tena vimānena haṁsayuktena bhāsvatā |
prahr̥ṣṭaś ca pratītaś ca babhau rāmaḥ kuberavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
