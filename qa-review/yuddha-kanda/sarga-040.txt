=== Sarga 40 ===

---
1.
athovāca mahātejā harirājo mahābalaḥ |
kim iyaṁ vyathitā senā mūḍhavāteva naur jale ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sugrīvasya vacaḥ śrutvā vāliputro ’ṅgado ’bravīt |
na tvaṁ paśyasi rāmaṁ ca lakṣmaṇaṁ ca mahābalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
śarajālācitau vīrāv ubhau daśarathātmajau |
śaratalpe mahātmānau śayānau rudhirokṣitau ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
athābravīd vānarendraḥ sugrīvaḥ putram aṅgadam |
nānimittam idaṁ manye bhavitavyaṁ bhayena tu ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
viṣaṇṇavadanā hy ete tyaktapraharaṇā diśaḥ |
prapalāyanti harayas trāsād utphullalocanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
anyonyasya na lajjante na nirīkṣanti pr̥ṣṭhataḥ |
viprakarṣanti cānyonyaṁ patitaṁ laṅghayanti ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
etasminn antare vīro gadāpāṇir vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
sugrīvaṁ vardhayām āsa rāghavaṁ ca niraikṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
vibhīṣaṇaṁ taṁ sugrīvo dr̥ṣṭvā vānarabhīṣaṇam |
r̥kṣarājaṁ samīpasthaṁ jāmbavantam uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
vibhīṣaṇo ’yaṁ saṁprāpto yaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā vānararṣabhāḥ |
vidravanti paritrastā rāvaṇātmajaśaṅkayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
śīghram etān suvitrastān bahudhā vipradhāvitān |
paryavasthāpayākhyāhi vibhīṣaṇam upasthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sugrīveṇaivam uktas tu jāmbavān r̥kṣapārthivaḥ |
vānarān sāntvayām āsa saṁnivartya prahāvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
te nivr̥ttāḥ punaḥ sarve vānarās tyaktasaṁbhramāḥ |
r̥kṣarājavacaḥ śrutvā taṁ ca dr̥ṣṭvā vibhīṣaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
vibhīṣaṇas tu rāmasya dr̥ṣṭvā gātraṁ śaraiś citam |
lakṣmaṇasya ca dharmātmā babhūva vyathitendriyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
jalaklinnena hastena tayor netre pramr̥jya ca |
śokasaṁpīḍitamanā ruroda vilalāpa ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
imau tau sattvasaṁpannau vikrāntau priyasaṁyugau |
imām avasthāṁ gamitau rākasaiḥ kūṭayodhibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
bhrātuḥ putreṇa me tena duṣputreṇa durātmanā |
rākṣasyā jihmayā buddhyā chalitāv r̥juvikramau ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
śarair imāv alaṁ viddhau rudhireṇa samukṣitau |
vasudhāyām ima suptau dr̥śyete śalyakāv iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
yayor vīryam upāśritya pratiṣṭhā kāṅkṣitā mayā |
tāv ubhau dehanāśāya prasuptau puruṣarṣabhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
jīvann adya vipanno ’smi naṣṭarājyamanorathaḥ |
prāptapratijñaś ca ripuḥ sakāmo rāvaṇaḥ kr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
evaṁ vilapamānaṁ taṁ pariṣvajya vibhīṣaṇam |
sugrīvaḥ sattvasaṁpanno harirājo ’bravīd idam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
rājyaṁ prāpsyasi dharmajña laṅkāyāṁ nātra saṁśayaḥ |
rāvaṇaḥ saha putreṇa sa rājyaṁ neha lapsyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
śarasaṁpīḍitāv etāv ubhau rāghavalakṣmaṇau |
tyaktvā mohaṁ vadhiṣyete sagaṇaṁ rāvaṇaṁ raṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tam evaṁ sāntvayitvā tu samāśvāsya ca rākṣasaṁ |
suṣeṇaṁ śvaśuraṁ pārśve sugrīvas tam uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
saha śūrair harigaṇair labdhasaṁjñāv ariṁdamau |
gaccha tvaṁ bhrātarau gr̥hya kiṣkindhāṁ rāmalakṣmaṇau ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
ahaṁ tu rāvaṇaṁ hatvā saputraṁ sahabāndhavam |
maithilīm ānayiṣyāmi śakro naṣṭām iva śriyam ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
śrutvaitad vānarendrasya suṣeṇo vākyam abravīt |
devāsuraṁ mahāyuddham anubhūtaṁ sudāruṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tadā sma dānavā devāñ śarasaṁsparśakovidāḥ |
nijaghnuḥ śastraviduṣaś chādayanto muhur muhuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tān ārtān naṣṭasaṁjñāṁś ca parāsūṁś ca br̥haspatiḥ |
vidhyābhir mantrayuktābhir oṣadhībhiś cikitsati ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
tāny auṣadhāny ānayituṁ kṣīrodaṁ yāntu sāgaram |
javena vānarāḥ śīghraṁ saṁpāti panasādayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
harayas tu vijānanti pārvatī te mahauṣadhī |
saṁjīvakaraṇīṁ divyāṁ viśalyāṁ devanirmitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
candraś ca nāma droṇaś ca parvatau sāgarottame |
amr̥taṁ yatra mathitaṁ tatra te paramauṣadhī ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
te tatra nihite devaiḥ parvate paramauṣadhī |
ayaṁ vāyusuto rājan hanūmāṁs tatra gacchatu ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
etasminn antare vāyur meghāṁś cāpi savidyutaḥ |
paryasyan sāgare toyaṁ kampayann iva parvatān ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
mahatā pakṣavātena sarve dvīpamahādrumāḥ |
nipetur bhagnaviṭapāḥ samūlā lavaṇāmbhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
abhavan pannagās trastā bhoginas tatravāsinaḥ |
śīghraṁ sarvāṇi yādāṁsi jagmuś ca lavaṇārṇavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
tato muhūrtad garuḍaṁ vainateyaṁ mahābalam |
vānarā dadr̥śuḥ sarve jvalantam iva pāvakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
tam āgatam abhiprekṣya nāgās te vipradudruvuḥ |
yais tau satpuruṣau baddhau śarabhūtair mahābalau ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
tataḥ suparṇaḥ kākutsthau dr̥ṣṭvā pratyabhinandya ca |
vimamarśa ca pāṇibhyāṁ mukhe candrasamaprabhe ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
vainateyena saṁspr̥ṣṭās tayoḥ saṁruruhur vraṇāḥ |
suvarṇe ca tanū snigdhe tayor āśu babhūvatuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
tejo vīryaṁ balaṁ cauja utsāhaś ca mahāguṇāḥ |
pradarśanaṁ ca buddhiś ca smr̥tiś ca dviguṇaṁ tayoḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
tāv utthāpya mahāvīryau garuḍo vāsavopamau |
ubhau tau sasvaje hr̥ṣṭau rāmaś cainam uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
bhavatprasādād vyasanaṁ rāvaṇiprabhavaṁ mahat |
āvām iha vyatikrāntau śīghraṁ ca balinau kr̥tau ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
yathā tātaṁ daśarathaṁ yathājaṁ ca pitāmaham |
tathā bhavantam āsādya hr̥ṣayaṁ me prasīdati ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
ko bhavān rūpasaṁpanno divyasraganulepanaḥ |
vasāno viraje vastre divyābharaṇabhūṣitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
tam uvāca mahātejā vainateyo mahābalaḥ |
patatrirājaḥ prītātmā harṣaparyākulekṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
ahaṁ sakhā te kākutstha priyaḥ prāṇo bahiścaraḥ |
garutmān iha saṁprāpto yuvayoḥ sāhyakāraṇāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
asurā vā mahāvīryā dānavā vā mahābalāḥ |
surāś cāpi sagandharvāḥ puraskr̥tya śatakratum ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
nemaṁ mokṣayituṁ śaktāḥ śarabandhaṁ sudāruṇam |
māyā balād indrajitā nirmitaṁ krūrakarmaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
ete nāgāḥ kādraveyās tīkṣṇadaṁṣṭrāviṣolbaṇāḥ |
rakṣomāyā prabhāvena śarā bhūtvā tvadāśritāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
sabhāgyaś cāsi dharmajña rāma satyaparākrama |
lakṣmaṇena saha bhrātrā samare ripughātinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
imaṁ śrutvā tu vr̥ttāntaṁ tvaramāṇo ’ham āgataḥ |
sahasā yuvayoḥ snehāt sakhitvam anupālayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
52.
mokṣitau ca mahāghorād asmāt sāyakabandhanāt |
apramādaś ca kartavyo yuvābhyāṁ nityam eva hi ||

=== meaning ===

---
53.
prakr̥tyā rākṣasāḥ sarve saṁgrāme kūṭayodhinaḥ |
śūrāṇāṁ śuddhabhāvānāṁ bhavatām ārjavaṁ balam ||

=== meaning ===

---
54.
tan na viśvasitavyaṁ vo rākṣasānāṁ raṇājire |
etenaivopamānena nityajihmā hi rākṣasāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
55.
evam uktvā tato rāmaṁ suparṇaḥ sumahābalaḥ |
pariṣvajya suhr̥tsnigdham āpraṣṭum upacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
56.
sakhe rāghava dharmajña ripūṇām api vatsala |
abhyanujñātum icchāmi gamiṣyāmi yathāgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
57.
bālavr̥ddhāvaśeṣāṁ tu laṅkāṁ kr̥tvā śarormibhiḥ |
rāvaṇaṁ ca ripuṁ hatvā sītāṁ samupalapsyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
58.
ity evam uktvā vacanaṁ suparṇaḥ śīghravikramaḥ |
rāmaṁ ca virujaṁ kr̥tvā madhye teṣāṁ vanaukasām ||

=== meaning ===

---
59.
pradakṣiṇaṁ tataḥ kr̥tvā pariṣvajya ca vīryavān |
jagāmākāśam āviśya suparṇaḥ pavano yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
60.
virujau rāghavau dr̥ṣṭvā tato vānarayūthapāḥ |
siṁhanādāṁs tadā nedur lāṅgūlaṁ dudhuvuś ca te ||

=== meaning ===

---
61.
tato bherīḥ samājaghnur mr̥daṅgāṁś ca vyanādayan |
dadhmuḥ śaṅkhān saṁprahr̥ṣṭāḥ kṣvelanty api yathāpuram ||

=== meaning ===

---
62.
āsphoṭyāsphoṭya vikrāntā vānarā nagayodhinaḥ |
drumān utpāṭya vividhāṁs tasthuḥ śatasahasraśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
63.
visr̥janto mahānādāṁs trāsayanto niśācarān |
laṅkādvārāṇy upājagmur yoddhukāmāḥ plavaṁgamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
64.
tatas tu bhīmas tumulo ninādo; babhūva śākhāmr̥gayūthapānām |
kṣaye nidāghasya yathā ghanānāṁ; nādaḥ subhīmo nadatāṁ niśīthe ||

=== meaning ===

---
