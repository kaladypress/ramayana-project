=== Sarga 20 ===

---
1.
śukena tu samākhyātāṁs tān dr̥ṣṭvā hariyūthapān |
samīpasthaṁ ca rāmasya bhrātaraṁ svaṁ vibhīṣaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
lakṣmaṇaṁ ca mahāvīryaṁ bhujaṁ rāmasya dakṣiṇam |
sarvavānararājaṁ ca sugrīvaṁ bhīmavikramam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
kiṁ cid āvignahr̥dayo jātakrodhaś ca rāvaṇaḥ |
bhartsayām āsa tau vīrau kathānte śukasāraṇau ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
adhomukhau tau praṇatāv abravīc chukasāraṇau |
roṣagadgadayā vācā saṁrabdhaḥ paruṣaṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
na tāvat sadr̥śaṁ nāma sacivair upajīvibhiḥ |
vipriyaṁ nr̥pater vaktuṁ nigrahapragrahe vibhoḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
ripūṇāṁ pratikūlānāṁ yuddhārtham abhivartatām |
ubhābhyāṁ sadr̥śaṁ nāma vaktum aprastave stavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
ācāryā guravo vr̥ddhā vr̥thā vāṁ paryupāsitāḥ |
sāraṁ yad rājaśāstrāṇām anujīvyaṁ na gr̥hyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
gr̥hīto vā na vijñāto bhāro jñānasya vochyate |
īdr̥śaiḥ sacivair yukto mūrkhair diṣṭyā dharāmy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
kiṁ nu mr̥tyor bhayaṁ nāsti māṁ vaktuṁ paruṣaṁ vacaḥ |
yasya me śāsato jihvā prayacchati śubhāśubham ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
apy eva dahanaṁ spr̥ṣṭvā vane tiṣṭhanti pādapāḥ |
rājadoṣaparāmr̥ṣṭās tiṣṭhante nāparādhinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
hanyām aham imau pāpau śatrupakṣapraśaṁsakau |
yadi pūrvopakārair me na krodho mr̥dutāṁ vrajet ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
apadhvaṁsata gacchadhvaṁ saṁnikarṣād ito mama |
na hi vāṁ hantum icchāmi smarann upakr̥tāni vām |
hatāv eva kr̥taghnau tau mayi snehaparāṅmukhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
evam uktau tu savrīḍau tāv ubhau śukasāraṇau |
rāvaṇaṁ jayaśabdena pratinandyābhiniḥsr̥tau ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
abravīt sa daśagrīvaḥ samīpasthaṁ mahodaram |
upasthāpaya śīghraṁ me cārān nītiviśāradān ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tataś carāḥ saṁtvaritāḥ prāptāḥ pārthivaśāsanāt |
upasthitāḥ prāñjalayo vardhayitvā jayāśiṣā ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tān abravīt tato vākyaṁ rāvaṇo rākṣasādhipaḥ |
cārān pratyayikāñ śūrān bhaktān vigatasādhvasān ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
ito gacchata rāmasya vyavasāyaṁ parīkṣatha |
mantreṣv abhyantarā ye ’sya prītyā tena samāgatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
kathaṁ svapiti jāgarti kim anyac ca kariṣyati |
vijñāya nipuṇaṁ sarvam āgantavyam aśeṣataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
cāreṇa viditaḥ śatruḥ paṇḍitair vasudhādhipaiḥ |
yuddhe svalpena yatnena samāsādya nirasyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
cārās tu te tathety uktvā prahr̥ṣṭā rākṣaseśvaram |
kr̥tvā pradakṣiṇaṁ jagmur yatra rāmaḥ salakṣmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
te suvelasya śailasya samīpe rāmalakṣmaṇau |
pracchannā dadr̥śur gatvā sasugrīvavibhīṣaṇau ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
te tu dharmātmanā dr̥ṣṭā rākṣasendreṇa rākṣasāḥ |
vibhīṣaṇena tatrasthā nigr̥hītā yadr̥cchayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
vānarair arditās te tu vikrāntair laghuvikramaiḥ |
punar laṅkām anuprāptāḥ śvasanto naṣṭacetasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tato daśagrīvam upasthitās te; cārā bahirnityacarā niśācarāḥ |
gireḥ suvelasya samīpavāsinaṁ; nyavedayan bhīmabalaṁ mahābalāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
