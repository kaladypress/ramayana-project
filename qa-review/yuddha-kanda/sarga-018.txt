=== Sarga 18 ===

---
1.
tāṁs tu te ’haṁ pravakṣyāmi prekṣamāṇasya yūthapān |
rāghavārthe parākrāntā ye na rakṣanti jīvitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
snigdhā yasya bahuśyāmā bālā lāṅgūlam āśritāḥ |
tāmrāḥ pītāḥ sitāḥ śvetāḥ prakīrṇā ghorakarmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
pragr̥hītāḥ prakāśante sūryasyeva marīcayaḥ |
pr̥thivyāṁ cānukr̥ṣyante haro nāmaiṣa yūthapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
yaṁ pr̥ṣṭhato ’nugacchanti śataśo ’tha sahasraśaḥ |
drumān udyamya sahitā laṅkārohaṇatatparāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
eṣa koṭīsahasreṇa vānarāṇāṁ mahaujasām |
ākāṅkṣate tvāṁ saṁgrāme jetuṁ parapuraṁjaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
nīlān iva mahāmeghāṁs tiṣṭhato yāṁs tu paśyasi |
asitāñ janasaṁkāśān yuddhe satyaparākramān ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
nakhadaṁṣṭrāyudhān vīrāṁs tīkṣṇakopān bhayāvahān |
asaṁkhyeyān anirdeśyān paraṁ pāram ivodadheḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
parvateṣu ca ye ke cid viṣameṣu nadīṣu ca |
ete tvām abhivartante rājann r̥ṣkāḥ sudāruṇāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
eṣāṁ madhye sthito rājan bhīmākṣo bhīmadarśanaḥ |
parjanya iva jīmūtaiḥ samantāt parivāritaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
r̥kṣavantaṁ giriśreṣṭham adhyāste narmadāṁ piban |
sarvarkṣāṇām adhipatir dhūmro nāmaiṣa yūthapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
yavīyān asya tu bhrātā paśyainaṁ parvatopamam |
bhrātrā samāno rūpeṇa viśiṣṭas tu parākrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sa eṣa jāmbavān nāma mahāyūthapayūthapaḥ |
praśānto guruvartī ca saṁprahāreṣv amarṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
etena sāhyaṁ sumahat kr̥taṁ śakrasya dhīmatā |
devāsure jāmbavatā labdhāś ca bahavo varāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
āruhya parvatāgrebhyo mahābhravipulāḥ śilāḥ |
muñcanti vipulākārā na mr̥tyor udvijanti ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
rākṣasānāṁ ca sadr̥śāḥ piśācānāṁ ca romaśāḥ |
etasya sainye bahavo vicaranty agnitejasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
yaṁ tv enam abhisaṁrabdhaṁ plavamānam iva sthitam |
prekṣante vānarāḥ sarve sthitaṁ yūthapayūthapam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
eṣa rājan sahasrākṣaṁ paryupāste harīśvaraḥ |
balena balasaṁpanno rambho nāmaiṣa yūthapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
yaḥ sthitaṁ yojane śailaṁ gacchan pārśvena sevate |
ūrdhvaṁ tathaiva kāyena gataḥ prāpnoti yojanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
yasmān na paramaṁ rūpaṁ catuṣpādeṣu vidyate |
śrutaḥ saṁnādano nāma vānarāṇāṁ pitāmahaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
yena yuddhaṁ tadā dattaṁ raṇe śakrasya dhīmatā |
parājayaś ca na prāptaḥ so ’yaṁ yūthapayūthapaḥ |
yasya vikramamāṇasya śakrasyeva parākramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
eṣa gandharvakanyāyām utpannaḥ kr̥ṣṇavartmanā |
purā devāsure yuddhe sāhyārthaṁ tridivaukasām ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
yasya vaiśravaṇo rājā jambūm upaniṣevate |
yo rājā parvatendrāṇāṁ bahukiṁnarasevinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
vihārasukhado nityaṁ bhrātus te rākṣasādhipa |
tatraiṣa vasati śrīmān balavān vānararṣabhaḥ |
yuddheṣv akatthano nityaṁ krathano nāma yūthapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
vr̥taḥ koṭisahasreṇa harīṇāṁ samupasthitaḥ |
eṣaivāśaṁsate laṅkāṁ svenānīkena marditum ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
yo gaṅgām anu paryeti trāsayan hastiyūthapān |
hastināṁ vānarāṇāṁ ca pūrvavairam anusmaran ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
eṣa yūthapatir netā gacchan giriguhāśayaḥ |
harīṇāṁ vāhinī mukhyo nadīṁ haimavatīm anu ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
uśīra bījam āśritya parvataṁ mandaropamam |
ramate vānaraśreṣṭho divi śakra iva svayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
enaṁ śatasahasrāṇāṁ sahasram abhivartate |
eṣa durmarṣaṇo rājan pramāthī nāma yūthapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
vātenevoddhataṁ meghaṁ yam enam anupaśyasi |
vivartamānaṁ bahuśo yatraitad bahulaṁ rajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
ete ’sitamukhā ghorā golāṅgūlā mahābalāḥ |
śataṁ śatasahasrāṇi dr̥ṣṭvā vai setubandhanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
golāṅgūlaṁ mahāvegaṁ gavākṣaṁ nāma yūthapam |
parivāryābhivartante laṅkāṁ marditum ojasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
bhramarācaritā yatra sarvakāmaphaladrumāḥ |
yaṁ sūryatulyavarṇābham anuparyeti parvatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
yasya bhāsā sadā bhānti tadvarṇā mr̥gapakṣiṇaḥ |
yasya prasthaṁ mahātmāno na tyajanti maharṣayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
tatraiṣa ramate rājan ramye kāñcanaparvate |
mukhyo vānaramukhyānāṁ kesarī nāma yūthapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
ṣaṣṭir girisahasrāṇāṁ ramyāḥ kāñcanaparvatāḥ |
teṣāṁ madhye girivaras tvam ivānagha rakṣasām ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
tatraite kapilāḥ śvetās tāmrāsyā madhupiṅgalāḥ |
nivasanty uttamagirau tīkṣṇadaṁṣṭrānakhāyudhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
siṁha iva caturdaṁṣṭrā vyāghrā iva durāsadāḥ |
sarve vaiśvanarasamā jvalitāśīviṣopamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
sudīrghāñcitalāṅgūlā mattamātaṁgasaṁnibhāḥ |
mahāparvatasaṁkāśā mahājīmūtanisvanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
eṣa caiṣām adhipatir madhye tiṣṭhati vīryavān |
nāmnā pr̥thivyāṁ vikhyāto rājañ śatabalīti yaḥ |
eṣaivāśaṁsate laṅkāṁ svenānīkena marditum ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
gajo gavākṣo gavayo nalo nīlaś ca vānaraḥ |
ekaika eva yūthānāṁ koṭibhir daśabhir vr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
tathānye vānaraśreṣṭhā vindhyaparvatavāsinaḥ |
na śakyante bahutvāt tu saṁkhyātuṁ laghuvikramāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
sarve mahārāja mahāprabhāvāḥ; sarve mahāśailanikāśakāyāḥ |
sarve samarthāḥ pr̥thivīṁ kṣaṇena; kartuṁ pravidhvastavikīrṇaśailām ||

=== meaning ===

---
