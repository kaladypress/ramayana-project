=== Sarga 17 ===

---
1.
tad vacaḥ pathyam aklībaṁ sāraṇenābhibhāṣitam |
niśamya rāvaṇo rājā pratyabhāṣata sāraṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
yadi mām abhiyuñjīran devagandharvadānavāḥ |
naiva sītāṁ pradāsyāmi sarvalokabhayād api ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tvaṁ tu saumya paritrasto haribhir nirjito bhr̥śam |
pratipradānam adyaiva sītāyāḥ sādhu manyase |
ko hi nāma sapatno māṁ samare jetum arhati ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
ity uktvā paruṣaṁ vākyaṁ rāvaṇo rākṣasādhipaḥ |
āruroha tataḥ śrīmān prāsādaṁ himapāṇḍuram |
bahutālasamutsedhaṁ rāvaṇo ’tha didr̥kṣayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tābhyāṁ carābhyāṁ sahito rāvaṇaḥ krodhamūrchitaḥ |
paśyamānaḥ samudraṁ ca parvatāṁś ca vanāni ca |
dadarśa pr̥thivīdeśaṁ susaṁpūrṇaṁ plavaṁgamaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tad apāram asaṁkhyeyaṁ vānarāṇāṁ mahad balam |
ālokya rāvaṇo rājā paripapraccha sāraṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
eṣāṁ vānaramukhyānāṁ ke śūrāḥ ke mahābalāḥ |
ke pūrvam abhivartante mahotsāhāḥ samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
keṣāṁ śr̥ṇoti sugrīvaḥ ke vā yūthapayūthapāḥ |
sāraṇācakṣva me sarvaṁ ke pradhānāḥ plavaṁgamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sāraṇo rākṣasendrasya vacanaṁ paripr̥cchataḥ |
ācacakṣe ’tha mukhyajño mukhyāṁs tāṁs tu vanaukasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
eṣa yo ’bhimukho laṅkāṁ nardaṁs tiṣṭhati vānaraḥ |
yūthapānāṁ sahasrāṇāṁ śatena parivāritaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
yasya ghoṣeṇa mahatā saprākārā satoraṇā |
laṅkā pravepate sarvā saśailavanakānanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sarvaśākhāmr̥gendrasya sugrīvasya mahātmanaḥ |
balāgre tiṣṭhate vīro nīlo nāmaiṣa yūthapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
bāhū pragr̥hya yaḥ padbhyāṁ mahīṁ gacchati vīryavān |
laṅkām abhimukhaḥ kopād abhīkṣṇaṁ ca vijr̥mbhate ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
giriśr̥ṅgapratīkāśaḥ padmakiñjalkasaṁnibhaḥ |
sphoṭayaty abhisaṁrabdho lāṅgūlaṁ ca punaḥ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
yasya lāṅgūlaśabdena svanantīva diśo daśa |
eṣa vānararājena surgrīveṇābhiṣecitaḥ |
yauvarājye ’ṅgado nāma tvām āhvayati saṁyuge ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
ye tu viṣṭabhya gātrāṇi kṣveḍayanti nadanti ca |
utthāya ca vijr̥mbhante krodhena haripuṁgavāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
ete duṣprasahā ghorāś caṇḍāś caṇḍaparākramāḥ |
aṣṭau śatasahasrāṇi daśakoṭiśatāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
ya enam anugacchanti vīrāś candanavāsinaḥ |
eṣa āśaṁsate laṅkāṁ svenānīkena marditum ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
śveto rajatasaṁkāśaḥ sabalo bhīmavikramaḥ |
buddhimān vānaraḥ śūras triṣu lokeṣu viśrutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tūrṇaṁ sugrīvam āgamya punar gacchati vānaraḥ |
vibhajan vānarīṁ senām anīkāni praharṣayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
yaḥ purā gomatītīre ramyaṁ paryeti parvatam |
nāmnā saṁkocano nāma nānānagayuto giriḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tatra rājyaṁ praśāsty eṣa kumudo nāma yūthapaḥ |
yo ’sau śatasahasrāṇāṁ sahasraṁ parikarṣati ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
yasya vālā bahuvyāmā dīrghalāṅgūlam āśritāḥ |
tāmrāḥ pītāḥ sitāḥ śvetāḥ prakīrṇā ghorakarmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
adīno roṣaṇaś caṇḍaḥ saṁgrāmam abhikāṅkṣati |
eṣaivāśaṁsate laṅkāṁ svenānīkena marditum ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
yas tv eṣa siṁhasaṁkāśaḥ kapilo dīrghakesaraḥ |
nibhr̥taḥ prekṣate laṅkāṁ didhakṣann iva cakṣuṣā ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
vindhyaṁ kr̥ṣṇagiriṁ sahyaṁ parvataṁ ca sudarśanam |
rājan satatam adhyāste rambho nāmaiṣa yūthapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
śataṁ śatasahasrāṇāṁ triṁśac ca hariyūthapāḥ |
parivāryānugacchanti laṅkāṁ marditum ojasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
yas tu karṇau vivr̥ṇute jr̥mbhate ca punaḥ punaḥ |
na ca saṁvijate mr̥tyor na ca yūthād vidhāvati ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
mahābalo vītabhayo ramyaṁ sālveya parvatam |
rājan satatam adhyāste śarabho nāma yūthapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
etasya balinaḥ sarve vihārā nāma yūthapāḥ |
rājañ śatasahasrāṇi catvāriṁśat tathaiva ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
yas tu megha ivākāśaṁ mahān āvr̥tya tiṣṭhati |
madhye vānaravīrāṇāṁ surāṇām iva vāsavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
bherīṇām iva saṁnādo yasyaiṣa śrūyate mahān |
ghoraḥ śākhāmr̥gendrāṇāṁ saṁgrāmam abhikāṅkṣatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
eṣa parvatam adhyāste pāriyātram anuttamam |
yuddhe duṣprasaho nityaṁ panaso nāma yūthapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
enaṁ śatasahasrāṇāṁ śatārdhaṁ paryupāsate |
yūthapā yūthapaśreṣṭhaṁ yeṣāṁ yūthāni bhāgaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
yas tu bhīmāṁ pravalgantīṁ camūṁ tiṣṭhati śobhayan |
sthitāṁ tīre samudrasya dvitīya iva sāgaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
eṣa dardarasaṁkāśo vinato nāma yūthapaḥ |
pibaṁś carati parṇāśāṁ nadīnām uttamāṁ nadīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
ṣaṣṭiḥ śatasahasrāṇi balam asya plavaṁgamāḥ |
tvām āhvayati yuddhāya krathano nāma yūthapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
yas tu gairikavarṇābhaṁ vapuḥ puṣyati vānaraḥ |
gavayo nāma tejasvī tvāṁ krodhād abhivartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
enaṁ śatasahasrāṇi saptatiḥ paryupāsate |
eṣa āśaṁsate laṅkāṁ svenānīkena marditum ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
ete duṣprasahā ghorā balinaḥ kāmarūpiṇaḥ |
yūthapā yūthapaśreṣṭhā yeṣāṁ saṁkhyā na vidyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
