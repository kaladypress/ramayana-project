=== Sarga 15 ===

---
1.
tato madhyāt samudrasya sāgaraḥ svayam utthitaḥ |
udayan hi mahāśailān meror iva divākaraḥ |
pannagaiḥ saha dīptāsyaiḥ samudraḥ pratyadr̥śyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
snigdhavaidūryasaṁkāśo jāmbūnadavibhūṣitaḥ |
raktamālyāmbaradharaḥ padmapatranibhekṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sāgaraḥ samatikramya pūrvam āmantrya vīryavān |
abravīt prāñjalir vākyaṁ rāghavaṁ śarapāṇinam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
pr̥thivī vāyur ākāśam āpo jyotiś ca rāghavaḥ |
svabhāve saumya tiṣṭhanti śāśvataṁ mārgam āśritāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tat svabhāvo mamāpy eṣa yad agādho ’ham aplavaḥ |
vikāras tu bhaved rādha etat te pravadāmy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
na kāmān na ca lobhād vā na bhayāt pārthivātmaja |
grāhanakrākulajalaṁ stambhayeyaṁ kathaṁ cana ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
vidhāsye rāma yenāpi viṣahiṣye hy ahaṁ tathā |
grāhā na prahariṣyanti yāvat senā tariṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
ayaṁ saumya nalo nāma tanujo viśvakarmaṇaḥ |
pitrā dattavaraḥ śrīmān pratimo viśvakarmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
eṣa setuṁ mahotsāhaḥ karotu mayi vānaraḥ |
tam ahaṁ dhārayiṣyāmi tathā hy eṣa yathā pitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
evam uktvodadhir naṣṭaḥ samutthāya nalas tataḥ |
abravīd vānaraśreṣṭho vākyaṁ rāmaṁ mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
ahaṁ setuṁ kariṣyāmi vistīrṇe varuṇālaye |
pituḥ sāmarthyam āsthāya tattvam āha mahodadhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
mama mātur varo datto mandare viśvakarmaṇā |
aurasas tasya putro ’haṁ sadr̥śo viśvakarmaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
na cāpy aham anukto vai prabrūyām ātmano guṇān |
kāmam adyaiva badhnantu setuṁ vānarapuṁgavāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tato nisr̥ṣṭarāmeṇa sarvato hariyūthapāḥ |
abhipetur mahāraṇyaṁ hr̥ṣṭāḥ śatasahasraśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
te nagān nagasaṁkāśāḥ śākhāmr̥gagaṇarṣabhāḥ |
babhañjur vānarās tatra pracakarṣuś ca sāgaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
te sālaiś cāśvakarṇaiś ca dhavair vaṁśaiś ca vānarāḥ |
kuṭajair arjunais tālais tikalais timiśair api ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
bilvakaiḥ saptaparṇaiś ca karṇikāraiś ca puṣpitaiḥ |
cūtaiś cāśokavr̥kṣaiś ca sāgaraṁ samapūrayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
samūlāṁś ca vimūlāṁś ca pādapān harisattamāḥ |
indraketūn ivodyamya prajahrur harayas tarūn ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
prakṣipyamāṇair acalaiḥ sahasā jalam uddhatam |
samutpatitam ākāśam apāsarpat tatas tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
daśayojanavistīrṇaṁ śatayojanam āyatam |
nalaś cakre mahāsetuṁ madhye nadanadīpateḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
śilānāṁ kṣipyamāṇānāṁ śailānāṁ tatra pātyatām |
babhūva tumulaḥ śabdas tadā tasmin mahodadhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
sa nalena kr̥taḥ setuḥ sāgare makarālaye |
śuśubhe subhagaḥ śrīmān svātīpatha ivāmbare ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tato devāḥ sagandharvāḥ siddhāś ca paramarṣayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
āplavantaḥ plavantaś ca garjantaś ca plavaṁgamāḥ |
tam acintyam asahyaṁ ca adbhutaṁ lomaharṣaṇam |
dadr̥śuḥ sarvabhūtāni sāgare setubandhanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tāni koṭisahasrāṇi vānarāṇāṁ mahaujasām |
badhnantaḥ sāgare setuṁ jagmuḥ pāraṁ mahodadheḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
viśālaḥ sukr̥taḥ śrīmān subhūmiḥ susamāhitaḥ |
aśobhata mahāsetuḥ sīmanta iva sāgare ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tataḥ pare samudrasya gadāpāṇir vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
pareṣām abhighatārtham atiṣṭhat sacivaiḥ saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
agratas tasya sainyasya śrīmān rāmaḥ salakṣmaṇaḥ |
jagāma dhanvī dharmātmā sugrīveṇa samanvitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
anye madhyena gacchanti pārśvato ’nye plavaṁgamāḥ |
salile prapatanty anye mārgam anye na lebhire |
ke cid vaihāyasa gatāḥ suparṇā iva pupluvuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
ghoṣeṇa mahatā ghoṣaṁ sāgarasya samucchritam |
bhīmam antardadhe bhīmā tarantī harivāhinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
vānarāṇāṁ hi sā tīrṇā vāhinī nala setunā |
tīre niviviśe rājñā bahumūlaphalodake ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
tad adbhutaṁ rāghava karma duṣkaraṁ; samīkṣya devāḥ saha siddhacāraṇaiḥ |
upetya rāmaṁ sahitā maharṣibhiḥ; samabhyaṣiñcan suśubhair jalaiḥ pr̥thak ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
jayasva śatrūn naradeva medinīṁ; sasāgarāṁ pālaya śāśvatīḥ samāḥ |
itīva rāmaṁ naradevasatkr̥taṁ; śubhair vacobhir vividhair apūjayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
