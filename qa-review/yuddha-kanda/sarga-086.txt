=== Sarga 86 ===

---
1.
mahodare tu nihate mahāpārśvo mahābalaḥ |
aṅgadasya camūṁ bhīmāṁ kṣobhayām āsa sāyakaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sa vānarāṇāṁ mukhyānām uttamāṅgāni sarvaśaḥ |
pātayām āsa kāyebhyaḥ phalaṁ vr̥ntād ivānilaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
keṣāṁ cid iṣubhir bāhūn skandhāṁś cicheda rākṣasaḥ |
vānarāṇāṁ susaṁkruddhaḥ pārśvaṁ keṣāṁ vyadārayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
te ’rditā bāṇavarṣeṇa mahāpārśvena vānarāḥ |
viṣādavimukhāḥ sarve babhūvur gatacetasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
nirīkṣya balam udvignam aṅgado rākṣasārditam |
vegaṁ cakre mahābāhuḥ samudra iva parvaṇi ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
āyasaṁ parighaṁ gr̥hya sūryaraśmisamaprabham |
samare vānaraśreṣṭho mahāpārśve nyapātayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sa tu tena prahāreṇa mahāpārśvo vicetanaḥ |
sasūtaḥ syandanāt tasmād visaṁjñaḥ prāpatad bhuvi ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sarkṣarājas tu tejasvī nīlāñjanacayopamaḥ |
niṣpatya sumahāvīryaḥ svād yūthān meghasaṁnibhāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
pragr̥hya giriśr̥ṅgābhāṁ kruddhaḥ sa vipulāṁ śilām |
aśvāñ jaghāna tarasā syandanaṁ ca babhañja tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
muhūrtāl labdhasaṁjñas tu mahāpārśvo mahābalaḥ |
aṅgadaṁ bahubhir bāṇair bhūyas taṁ pratyavidhyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
jāmbavantaṁ tribhir bāṇair ājaghāna stanāntare |
r̥kṣarājaṁ gavākṣaṁ ca jaghāna bahubhiḥ śaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
gavākṣaṁ jāmbavantaṁ ca sa dr̥ṣṭvā śarapīḍitau |
jagrāha parighaṁ ghoram aṅgadaḥ krodhamūrchitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tasyāṅgadaḥ prakupito rākṣasasya tam āyasaṁ |
dūrasthitasya parighaṁ raviraśmisamaprabham ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
dvābhyāṁ bhujābhyāṁ saṁgr̥hya bhrāmayitvā ca vegavān |
mahāpārśvāya cikṣepa vadhārthaṁ vālinaḥ sutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa tu kṣipto balavatā parighas tasya rakṣasaḥ |
dhanuś ca saśaraṁ hastāc chirastraṁ cāpy apātayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
taṁ samāsādya vegena vāliputraḥ pratāpavān |
talenābhyahanat kruddhaḥ karṇamūle sakuṇḍale ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
sa tu kruddho mahāvego mahāpārśvo mahādyutiḥ |
kareṇaikena jagrāha sumahāntaṁ paraśvadham ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
taṁ tailadhautaṁ vimalaṁ śailasāramayaṁ dr̥ḍham |
rākṣasaḥ paramakruddho vāliputre nyapātayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tena vāmāṁsaphalake bhr̥śaṁ pratyavapātitam |
aṅgado mokṣayām āsa saroṣaḥ sa paraśvadham ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
sa vīro vajrasaṁkāśam aṅgado muṣṭim ātmanaḥ |
saṁvartayan susaṁkruddhaḥ pitus tulyaparākramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
rākṣasasya stanābhyāśe marmajño hr̥dayaṁ prati |
indrāśanisamasparśaṁ sa muṣṭiṁ vinyapātayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tena tasya nipātena rākṣasasya mahāmr̥dhe |
paphāla hr̥dayaṁ cāśu sa papāta hato bhuvi ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tasmin nipatite bhūmau tat sainyaṁ saṁpracukṣubhe |
abhavac ca mahān krodhaḥ samare rāvaṇasya tu ||

=== meaning ===

---
