=== Sarga 70 ===

---
1.
rāghavaś cāpi vipulaṁ taṁ rākṣasavanaukasām |
śrutvā saṁgrāmanirghoṣaṁ jāmbavantam uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
saumya nūnaṁ hanumatā kr̥taṁ karma suduṣkaram |
śrūyate hi yathā bhīmaḥ sumahān āyudhasvanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tad gaccha kuru sāhāyyaṁ svabalenābhisaṁvr̥taḥ |
kṣipram r̥ṣkapate tasya kapiśreṣṭhasya yudhyataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
r̥kṣarājas tathety uktvā svenānīkena saṁvr̥taḥ |
āgacchat paścimadvāraṁ hanūmān yatra vānaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
athāyāntaṁ hanūmantaṁ dadarśarkṣapatiḥ pathi |
vānaraiḥ kr̥tasaṁgrāmaiḥ śvasadbhir abhisaṁvr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
dr̥ṣṭvā pathi hanūmāṁś ca tad r̥ṣkabalam udyatam |
nīlameghanibhaṁ bhīmaṁ saṁnivārya nyavartata ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sa tena harisainyena saṁnikarṣaṁ mahāyaśāḥ |
śīghram āgamya rāmāya duḥkhito vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
samare yudhyamānānām asmākaṁ prekṣatāṁ ca saḥ |
jaghāna rudatīṁ sītām indrajid rāvaṇātmajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
udbhrāntacittas tāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā viṣaṇṇo ’ham ariṁdama |
tad ahaṁ bhavato vr̥ttaṁ vijñāpayitum āgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā rāghavaḥ śokamūrchitaḥ |
nipapāta tadā bhūmau chinnamūla iva drumaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
taṁ bhūmau devasaṁkāśaṁ patitaṁ dr̥śya rāghavam |
abhipetuḥ samutpatya sarvataḥ kapisattamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
asiñcan salilaiś cainaṁ padmotpalasugandhibhiḥ |
pradahantam asahyaṁ ca sahasāgnim ivotthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
taṁ lakṣmaṇo ’tha bāhubhyāṁ pariṣvajya suduḥkhitaḥ |
uvāca rāmam asvasthaṁ vākyaṁ hetvarthasaṁhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
śubhe vartmani tiṣṭhantaṁ tvām āryavijitendriyam |
anarthebhyo na śaknoti trātuṁ dharmo nirarthakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
bhūtānāṁ sthāvarāṇāṁ ca jaṅgamānāṁ ca darśanam |
yathāsti na tathā dharmas tena nāstīti me matiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
yathaiva sthāvaraṁ vyaktaṁ jaṅgamaṁ ca tathāvidham |
nāyam arthas tathā yuktas tvadvidho na vipadyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
yady adharmo bhaved bhūto rāvaṇo narakaṁ vrajet |
bhavāṁś ca dharmasaṁyukto naivaṁ vyasanam āpnuyāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tasya ca vyasanābhāvād vyasanaṁ ca gate tvayi |
dharmeṇopalabhed dharmam adharmaṁ cāpy adharmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
yadi dharmeṇa yujyeran nādharmarucayo janāḥ |
dharmeṇa caratāṁ dharmas tathā caiṣāṁ phalaṁ bhavet ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
yasmād arthā vivardhante yeṣv adharmaḥ pratiṣṭhitaḥ |
kliśyante dharmaśīlāś ca tasmād etau nirarthakau ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
vadhyante pāpakarmāṇo yady adharmeṇa rāghava |
vadhakarmahato dharmaḥ sa hataḥ kaṁ vadhiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
atha vā vihitenāyaṁ hanyate hanti vā param |
vidhir ālipyate tena na sa pāpena karmaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
adr̥ṣṭapratikāreṇa avyaktenāsatā satā |
kathaṁ śakyaṁ paraṁ prāptuṁ dharmeṇārivikarśana ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
yadi sat syāt satāṁ mukhya nāsat syāt tava kiṁ cana |
tvayā yadīdr̥śaṁ prāptaṁ tasmāt san nopapadyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
atha vā durbalaḥ klībo balaṁ dharmo ’nuvartate |
durbalo hr̥tamaryādo na sevya iti me matiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
balasya yadi ced dharmo guṇabhūtaḥ parākrame |
dharmam utsr̥jya vartasva yathā dharme tathā bale ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
atha cet satyavacanaṁ dharmaḥ kila paraṁtapa |
anr̥tas tvayy akaruṇaḥ kiṁ na baddhas tvayā pitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
yadi dharmo bhaved bhūta adharmo vā paraṁtapa |
na sma hatvā muniṁ vajrī kuryād ijyāṁ śatakratuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
adharmasaṁśrito dharmo vināśayati rāghava |
sarvam etad yathākāmaṁ kākutstha kurute naraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
mama cedaṁ mataṁ tāta dharmo ’yam iti rāghava |
dharmamūlaṁ tvayā chinnaṁ rājyam utsr̥jatā tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
arthebhyo hi vivr̥ddhebhyaḥ saṁvr̥ddhebhyas tatas tataḥ |
kriyāḥ sarvāḥ pravartante parvatebhya ivāpagāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
arthena hi viyuktasya puruṣasyālpatejasaḥ |
vyucchidyante kriyāḥ sarvā grīṣme kusarito yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
so ’yam arthaṁ parityajya sukhakāmaḥ sukhaidhitaḥ |
pāpam ārabhate kartuṁ tathā doṣaḥ pravartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
yasyārthās tasya mitrāṇi yasyārthās tasya bāndhavaḥ |
yasyārthāḥ sa pumām̐l loke yasyārthāḥ sa ca paṇḍitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
yasyārthāḥ sa ca vikrānto yasyārthāḥ sa ca buddhimān |
yasyārthāḥ sa mahābhāgo yasyārthāḥ sa mahāguṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
arthasyaite parityāge doṣāḥ pravyāhr̥tā mayā |
rājyam utsr̥jatā vīra yena buddhis tvayā kr̥tā ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
yasyārthā dharmakāmārthās tasya sarvaṁ pradakṣiṇam |
adhanenārthakāmena nārthaḥ śakyo vicinvatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
harṣaḥ kāmaś ca darpaś ca dharmaḥ krodhaḥ śamo damaḥ |
arthād etāni sarvāṇi pravartante narādhipa ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
yeṣāṁ naśyaty ayaṁ lokaś caratāṁ dharmacāriṇām |
te ’rthās tvayi na dr̥śyante durdineṣu yathā grahāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
tvayi pravrajite vīra guroś ca vacane sthite |
rakṣasāpahr̥tā bhāryā prāṇaiḥ priyatarā tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
tad adya vipulaṁ vīra duḥkham indrajitā kr̥tam |
karmaṇā vyapaneṣyāmi tasmād uttiṣṭha rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
ayam anagha tavoditaḥ priyārthaṁ; janakasutā nidhanaṁ nirīkṣya ruṣṭaḥ |
sahayagajarathāṁ sarākṣasendrāṁ; bhr̥śam iṣubhir vinipātayāmi laṅkām ||

=== meaning ===

---
