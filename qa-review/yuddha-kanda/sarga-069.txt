=== Sarga 69 ===

---
1.
śrutvā taṁ bhīmanirhrādaṁ śakrāśanisamasvanam |
vīkṣamāṇā diśaḥ sarvā dudruvur vānararṣabhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tān uvāca tataḥ sarvān hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ |
viṣaṇṇavadanān dīnāṁs trastān vidravataḥ pr̥thak ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
kasmād viṣaṇṇavadanā vidravadhvaṁ plavaṁgamāḥ |
tyaktayuddhasamutsāhāḥ śūratvaṁ kva nu vo gatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
pr̥ṣṭhato ’nuvrajadhvaṁ mām agrato yāntam āhave |
śūrair abhijanopetair ayuktaṁ hi nivartitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
evam uktāḥ susaṁkruddhā vāyuputreṇa dhīmatā |
śailaśr̥ṅgān drumāṁś caiva jagr̥hur hr̥ṣṭamānasāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
abhipetuś ca garjanto rākṣasān vānararṣabhāḥ |
parivārya hanūmantam anvayuś ca mahāhave ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sa tair vānaramukhyais tu hanūmān sarvato vr̥taḥ |
hutāśana ivārciṣmān adahac chatruvāhinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sa rākṣasānāṁ kadanaṁ cakāra sumahākapiḥ |
vr̥to vānarasainyena kālāntakayamopamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sa tu śokena cāviṣṭaḥ krodhena ca mahākapiḥ |
hanūmān rāvaṇi rathe mahatīṁ pātayac chilām ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tām āpatantīṁ dr̥ṣṭvaiva rathaḥ sārathinā tadā |
vidheyāśva samāyuktaḥ sudūram apavāhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tam indrajitam aprāpya rathathaṁ sahasārathim |
viveśa dharaṇīṁ bhittvā sā śilāvyartham udyatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
patitāyāṁ śilāyāṁ tu rakṣasāṁ vyathitā camūḥ |
tam abhyadhāvañ śataśo nadantaḥ kānanaukasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
te drumāṁś ca mahākāyā giriśr̥ṅgāṇi codyatāḥ |
cikṣipur dviṣatāṁ madhye vānarā bhīmavikramāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
vānarair tair mahāvīryair ghorarūpā niśācarāḥ |
vīryād abhihatā vr̥kṣair vyaveṣṭanta raṇakṣitau ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
svasainyam abhivīkṣyātha vānarārditam indrajit |
pragr̥hītāyudhaḥ kruddhaḥ parān abhimukho yayau ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sa śaraughān avasr̥jan svasainyenābhisaṁvr̥taḥ |
jaghāna kapiśārdūlān subahūn dr̥ṣṭavikramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
śūlair aśanibhiḥ khaḍgaiḥ paṭṭasaiḥ kūṭamudgaraiḥ |
te cāpy anucarāṁs tasya vānarā jaghnur āhave ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
saskandhaviṭapaiḥ sālaiḥ śilābhiś ca mahābalaiḥ |
hanūmān kadanaṁ cakre rakṣasāṁ bhīmakarmaṇām ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
sa nivārya parānīkam abravīt tān vanaukasaḥ |
hanūmān saṁnivartadhvaṁ na naḥ sādhyam idaṁ balam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tyaktvā prāṇān viceṣṭanto rāma priyacikīrṣavaḥ |
yannimittaṁ hi yudhyāmo hatā sā janakātmajā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
imam arthaṁ hi vijñāpya rāmaṁ sugrīvam eva ca |
tau yat pratividhāsyete tat kariṣyāmahe vayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
ity uktvā vānaraśreṣṭho vārayan sarvavānarān |
śanaiḥ śanair asaṁtrastaḥ sabalaḥ sa nyavartata ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
sa tu prekṣya hanūmantaṁ vrajantaṁ yatra rāghavaḥ |
nikumbhilām adhiṣṭhāya pāvakaṁ juhuve ndrajit ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
yajñabhūmyāṁ tu vidhivat pāvakas tena rakṣasā |
hūyamānaḥ prajajvāla homaśoṇitabhuk tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
so ’rciḥ pinaddho dadr̥śe homaśoṇitatarpitaḥ |
saṁdhyāgata ivādityaḥ sa tīvrāgniḥ samutthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
athendrajid rākṣasabhūtaye tu; juhāva havyaṁ vidhinā vidhānavat |
dr̥ṣṭvā vyatiṣṭhanta ca rākṣasās te; mahāsamūheṣu nayānayajñāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
