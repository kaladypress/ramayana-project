=== Sarga 105 ===

---
1.
tato vaiśravaṇo rājā yamaś cāmitrakarśanaḥ |
sahasrākṣo mahendraś ca varuṇaś ca paraṁtapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
ṣaḍardhanayanaḥ śrīmān mahādevo vr̥ṣadhvajaḥ |
kartā sarvasya lokasya brahmā brahmavidāṁ varaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
ete sarve samāgamya vimānaiḥ sūryasaṁnibhaiḥ |
āgamya nagarīṁ laṅkām abhijagmuś ca rāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tataḥ sahastābharaṇān pragr̥hya vipulān bhujān |
abruvaṁs tridaśaśreṣṭhāḥ prāñjaliṁ rāghavaṁ sthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
kartā sarvasya lokasya śreṣṭho jñānavatāṁ varaḥ |
upekṣase kathaṁ sītāṁ patantīṁ havyavāhane |
kathaṁ devagaṇaśreṣṭham ātmānaṁ nāvabudhyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
r̥tadhāmā vasuḥ pūrvaṁ vasūnāṁ ca prajāpatiḥ |
tvaṁ trayāṇāṁ hi lokānām ādikartā svayaṁprabhuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
rudrāṇām aṣṭamo rudraḥ sādhyānām api pañcamaḥ |
aśvinau cāpi te karṇau candrasūryau ca cakṣuṣī ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
ante cādau ca lokānāṁ dr̥śyase tvaṁ paraṁtapa |
upekṣase ca vaidehīṁ mānuṣaḥ prākr̥to yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
ity ukto lokapālais taiḥ svāmī lokasya rāghavaḥ |
abravīt tridaśaśreṣṭhān rāmo dharmabhr̥tāṁ varaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
ātmānaṁ mānuṣaṁ manye rāmaṁ daśarathātmajam |
yo ’haṁ yasya yataś cāhaṁ bhagavāṁs tad bravītu me ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
iti bruvāṇaṁ kākutsthaṁ brahmā brahmavidāṁ varaḥ |
abravīc chr̥ṇu me rāma satyaṁ satyaparākrama ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
bhavān nārāyaṇo devaḥ śrīmāṁś cakrāyudho vibhuḥ |
ekaśr̥ṅgo varāhas tvaṁ bhūtabhavyasapatnajit ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
akṣaraṁ brahmasatyaṁ ca madhye cānte ca rāghava |
lokānāṁ tvaṁ paro dharmo viṣvaksenaś caturbhujaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
śārṅgadhanvā hr̥ṣīkeśaḥ puruṣaḥ puruṣottamaḥ |
ajitaḥ khaḍgadhr̥g viṣṇuḥ kr̥ṣṇaś caiva br̥hadbalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
senānīr grāmaṇīś ca tvaṁ buddhiḥ sattvaṁ kṣamā damaḥ |
prabhavaś cāpyayaś ca tvam upendro madhusūdanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
indrakarmā mahendras tvaṁ padmanābho raṇāntakr̥t |
śaraṇyaṁ śaraṇaṁ ca tvām āhur divyā maharṣayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
sahasraśr̥ṅgo vedātmā śatajihvo maharṣabhaḥ |
tvaṁ yajñas tvaṁ vaṣaṭkāras tvam oṁkāraḥ paraṁtapa ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
prabhavaṁ nidhanaṁ vā te na viduḥ ko bhavān iti |
dr̥śyase sarvabhūteṣu brāhmaṇeṣu ca goṣu ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
dikṣu sarvāsu gagane parvateṣu vaneṣu ca |
sahasracaraṇaḥ śrīmāñ śataśīrṣaḥ sahasradhr̥k ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tvaṁ dhārayasi bhūtāni vasudhāṁ ca saparvatām |
ante pr̥thivyāḥ salile dr̥śyase tvaṁ mahoragaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
trīm̐l lokān dhārayan rāma devagandharvadānavān |
ahaṁ te hr̥dayaṁ rāma jihvā devī sarasvatī ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
devā gātreṣu lomāni nirmitā brahmaṇā prabho |
nimeṣas te ’bhavad rātrir unmeṣas te ’bhavad divā ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
saṁskārās te ’bhavan vedā na tad asti tvayā vinā |
jagat sarvaṁ śarīraṁ te sthairyaṁ te vasudhātalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
agniḥ kopaḥ prasādas te somaḥ śrīvatsalakṣaṇa |
tvayā lokās trayaḥ krāntāḥ purāṇe vikramais tribhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
mahendraś ca kr̥to rājā baliṁ baddhvā mahāsuram |
sītā lakṣmīr bhavān viṣṇur devaḥ kr̥ṣṇaḥ prajāpatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
vadhārthaṁ rāvaṇasyeha praviṣṭo mānuṣīṁ tanum |
tad idaṁ naḥ kr̥taṁ kāryaṁ tvayā dharmabhr̥tāṁ vara ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
nihato rāvaṇo rāma prahr̥ṣṭo divam ākrama |
amoghaṁ balavīryaṁ te amoghas te parākramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
amoghās te bhaviṣyanti bhaktimantaś ca ye narāḥ |
ye tvāṁ devaṁ dhruvaṁ bhaktāḥ purāṇaṁ puruṣottamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
ye narāḥ kīrtayiṣyanti nāsti teṣāṁ parābhavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===
