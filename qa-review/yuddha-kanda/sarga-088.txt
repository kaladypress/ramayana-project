=== Sarga 88 ===

---
1.
tasmin pratihate ’stre tu rāvaṇo rākṣasādhipaḥ |
krodhaṁ ca dviguṇaṁ cakre krodhāc cāstram anantaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
mayena vihitaṁ raudram anyad astraṁ mahādyutiḥ |
utsraṣṭuṁ rāvaṇo ghoraṁ rāghavāya pracakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tataḥ śūlāni niścerur gadāś ca musalāni ca |
kārmukād dīpyamānāni vajrasārāṇi sarvaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
kūṭamudgarapāśāś ca dīptāś cāśanayas tathā |
niṣpetur vividhās tīkṣṇā vātā iva yugakṣaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tad astraṁ rāghavaḥ śrīmān uttamāstravidāṁ varaḥ |
jaghāna paramāstreṇa gandharveṇa mahādyutiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tasmin pratihate ’stre tu rāghaveṇa mahātmanā |
rāvaṇaḥ krodhatāmrākṣaḥ sauram astram udīrayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tataś cakrāṇi niṣpetur bhāsvarāṇi mahānti ca |
kārmukād bhīmavegasya daśagrīvasya dhīmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tair āsīd gaganaṁ dīptaṁ saṁpatadbhir itas tataḥ |
patadbhiś ca diśo dīptaiś candrasūryagrahair iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tāni ciccheda bāṇaughaiś cakrāṇi tu sa rāghavaḥ |
āyudhāni vicitrāṇi rāvaṇasya camūmukhe ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tad astraṁ tu hataṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rāvaṇo rākṣasādhipaḥ |
vivyādha daśabhir bāṇai rāmaṁ sarveṣu marmasu ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sa viddho daśabhir bāṇair mahākārmukaniḥsr̥taiḥ |
rāvaṇena mahātejā na prākampata rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tato vivyādha gātreṣu sarveṣu samitiṁjayaḥ |
rāghavas tu susaṁkruddho rāvaṇaṁ bahubhiḥ śaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
etasminn antare kruddho rāghavasyānujo balī |
lakṣmaṇaḥ sāyakān sapta jagrāha paravīrahā ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
taiḥ sāyakair mahāvegai rāvaṇasya mahādyutiḥ |
dhvajaṁ manuṣyaśīrṣaṁ tu tasya ciccheda naikadhā ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sāratheś cāpi bāṇena śiro jvalitakuṇḍalam |
jahāra lakṣmaṇaḥ śrīmān nairr̥tasya mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tasya bāṇaiś ca ciccheda dhanur gajakaropamam |
lakṣmaṇo rākṣasendrasya pañcabhir niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
nīlameghanibhāṁś cāsya sadaśvān parvatopamān |
jaghānāplutya gadayā rāvaṇasya vibhīṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
hatāśvād vegavān vegād avaplutya mahārathāt |
krodham āhārayat tīvraṁ bhrātaraṁ prati rāvaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tataḥ śaktiṁ mahāśaktir dīptāṁ dīptāśanīm iva |
vibhīṣaṇāya cikṣepa rākṣasendraḥ pratāpavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
aprāptām eva tāṁ bāṇais tribhiś ciccheda lakṣmaṇaḥ |
athodatiṣṭhat saṁnādo vānarāṇāṁ tadā raṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
sā papāta tridhā chinnā śaktiḥ kāñcanamālinī |
savisphuliṅgā jvalitā maholkeva divaś cyutā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tataḥ saṁbhāvitatarāṁ kālenāpi durāsadām |
jagrāha vipulāṁ śaktiṁ dīpyamānāṁ svatejasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
sā veginā balavatā rāvaṇena durātmanā |
jajvāla sumahāghorā śakrāśanisamaprabhā ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
etasminn antare vīro lakṣmaṇas taṁ vibhīṣaṇam |
prāṇasaṁśayam āpannaṁ tūrṇam evābhyapadyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
taṁ vimokṣayituṁ vīraś cāpam āyamya lakṣmaṇaḥ |
rāvaṇaṁ śaktihastaṁ taṁ śaravarṣair avākirat ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
kīryamāṇaḥ śaraugheṇa visr̥ṣṭena mahātmanā |
na prahartuṁ manaś cakre vimukhīkr̥tavikramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
mokṣitaṁ bhrātaraṁ dr̥ṣṭvā lakṣmaṇena sa rāvaṇaḥ |
lakṣmaṇābhimukhas tiṣṭhann idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
mokṣitas te balaślāghin yasmād evaṁ vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
vimucya rākṣasaṁ śaktis tvayīyaṁ vinipātyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
eṣā te hr̥dayaṁ bhittvā śaktir lohitalakṣaṇā |
madbāhuparighotsr̥ṣṭā prāṇān ādāya yāsyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
ity evam uktvā tāṁ śaktim aṣṭaghaṇṭāṁ mahāsvanām |
mayena māyāvihitām amoghāṁ śatrughātinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
lakṣmaṇāya samuddiśya jvalantīm iva tejasā |
rāvaṇaḥ paramakruddhaś cikṣepa ca nanāda ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
sā kṣiptā bhīmavegena śakrāśanisamasvanā |
śaktir abhyapatad vegāl lakṣmaṇaṁ raṇamūrdhani ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
tām anuvyāharac chaktim āpatantīṁ sa rāghavaḥ |
svastyas tu lakṣmaṇāyeti moghā bhava hatodyamā ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
nyapatat sā mahāvegā lakṣmaṇasya mahorasi |
jihvevoragarājasya dīpyamānā mahādyutiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
tato rāvaṇavegena sudūram avagāḍhayā |
śaktyā nirbhinnahr̥dayaḥ papāta bhuvi lakṣmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
tadavasthaṁ samīpastho lakṣmaṇaṁ prekṣya rāghavaḥ |
bhrātr̥snehān mahātejā viṣaṇṇahr̥dayo ’bhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
sa muhūrtam anudhyāya bāṣpavyākulalocanaḥ |
babhūva saṁrabdhataro yugānta iva pāvakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
na viṣādasya kālo ’yam iti saṁcintya rāghavaḥ |
cakre sutumulaṁ yuddhaṁ rāvaṇasya vadhe dhr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
sa dadarśa tato rāmaḥ śaktyā bhinnaṁ mahāhave |
lakṣmaṇaṁ rudhirādigdhaṁ sapannagam ivācalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
tām api prahitāṁ śaktiṁ rāvaṇena balīyasā |
yatnatas te hariśreṣṭhā na śekur avamarditum |
arditāś caiva bāṇaughaiḥ kṣiprahastena rakṣasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
saumitriṁ sā vinirbhidya praviṣṭā dharaṇītalam |
tāṁ karābhyāṁ parāmr̥śya rāmaḥ śaktiṁ bhayāvahām |
babhañja samare kruddho balavad vicakarṣa ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
tasya niṣkarṣataḥ śaktiṁ rāvaṇena balīyasā |
śarāḥ sarveṣu gātreṣu pātitā marmabhedinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
acintayitvā tān bāṇān samāśliṣya ca lakṣmaṇam |
abravīc ca hanūmantaṁ sugrīvaṁ caiva rāghavaḥ |
lakṣmaṇaṁ parivāryeha tiṣṭhadhvaṁ vānarottamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
parākramasya kālo ’yaṁ saṁprāpto me cirepsitaḥ |
pāpātmāyaṁ daśagrīvo vadhyatāṁ pāpaniścayaḥ |
kāṅkṣitaḥ stokakasyeva gharmānte meghadarśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
asmin muhūrte nacirāt satyaṁ pratiśr̥ṇomi vaḥ |
arāvaṇam arāmaṁ vā jagad drakṣyatha vānarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
rājyanāśaṁ vane vāsaṁ daṇḍake paridhāvanam |
vaidehyāś ca parāmarśaṁ rakṣobhiś ca samāgamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
prāptaṁ duḥkhaṁ mahad ghoraṁ kleśaṁ ca nirayopamam |
adya sarvam ahaṁ tyakṣye hatvā taṁ rāvaṇaṁ raṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
yadarthaṁ vānaraṁ sainyaṁ samānītam idaṁ mayā |
sugrīvaś ca kr̥to rājye nihatvā vālinaṁ raṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
yadarthaṁ sāgaraḥ krāntaḥ setur baddhaś ca sāgare |
so ’yam adya raṇe pāpaś cakṣurviṣayam āgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
cakṣurviṣayam āgamya nāyaṁ jīvitum arhati |
dr̥ṣṭiṁ dr̥ṣṭiviṣasyeva sarpasya mama rāvaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
svasthāḥ paśyata durdharṣā yuddhaṁ vānarapuṁgavāḥ |
āsīnāḥ parvatāgreṣu mamedaṁ rāvaṇasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
52.
adya rāmasya rāmatvaṁ paśyantu mama saṁyuge |
trayo lokāḥ sagandharvāḥ sadevāḥ sarṣicāraṇāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
53.
adya karma kariṣyāmi yal lokāḥ sacarācarāḥ |
sadevāḥ kathayiṣyanti yāvad bhūmir dhariṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
54.
evam uktvā śitair bāṇais taptakāñcanabhūṣaṇaiḥ |
ājaghāna daśagrīvaṁ raṇe rāmaḥ samāhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
55.
atha pradīptair nārācair musalaiś cāpi rāvaṇaḥ |
abhyavarṣat tadā rāmaṁ dhārābhir iva toyadaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
56.
rāmarāvaṇamuktānām anyonyam abhinighnatām |
śarāṇāṁ ca śarāṇāṁ ca babhūva tumulaḥ svanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
57.
te bhinnāś ca vikīrṇāś ca rāmarāvaṇayoḥ śarāḥ |
antarikṣāt pradīptāgrā nipetur dharaṇītale ||

=== meaning ===

---
58.
tayor jyātalanirghoṣo rāmarāvaṇayor mahān |
trāsanaḥ sarvabūtānāṁ sa babhūvādbhutopamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
59.
sa kīryamāṇaḥ śarajālavr̥ṣṭibhir; mahātmanā dīptadhanuṣmatārditaḥ |
bhayāt pradudrāva sametya rāvaṇo; yathānilenābhihato balāhakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
