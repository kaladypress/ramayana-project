=== Sarga 102 ===

---
1.
sa uvāca mahāprajñam abhigamya plavaṁgamaḥ |
rāmaṁ vacanam arthajño varaṁ sarvadhanuṣmatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
yannimitto ’yam ārambhaḥ karmaṇāṁ ca phalodayaḥ |
tāṁ devīṁ śokasaṁtaptāṁ maithilīṁ draṣṭum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sā hi śokasamāviṣṭā bāṣpaparyākulekṣaṇā |
maithilī vijayaṁ śrutvā tava harṣam upāgamat ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
pūrvakāt pratyayāc cāham ukto viśvastayā tayā |
bhartāraṁ draṣṭum icchāmi kr̥tārthaṁ sahalakṣmaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
evam ukto hanumatā rāmo dharmabhr̥tāṁ varaḥ |
agacchat sahasā dhyānam āsīd bāṣpapariplutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
dīrgham uṣṇaṁ ca niśvasya medinīm avalokayan |
uvāca meghasaṁkāśaṁ vibhīṣaṇam upasthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
divyāṅgarāgāṁ vaidehīṁ divyābharaṇabhūṣitām |
iha sītāṁ śiraḥsnātām upasthāpaya māciram ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
evam uktas tu rāmeṇa tvaramāṇo vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
praviśyāntaḥpuraṁ sītāṁ strībhiḥ svābhir acodayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
divyāṅgarāgā vaidehī divyābharaṇabhūṣitā |
yānam āroha bhadraṁ te bhartā tvāṁ draṣṭum icchati ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
evam uktā tu vaidehī pratyuvāca vibhīṣaṇam |
asnātā draṣṭum icchāmi bhartāraṁ rākṣasādhipa ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tasyās tad vacanaṁ śrutvā pratyuvāca vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
yathāha rāmo bhartā te tat tathā kartum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā maithilī bhartr̥devatā |
bhartr̥bhaktivratā sādhvī tatheti pratyabhāṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tataḥ sītāṁ śiraḥsnātāṁ yuvatībhir alaṁkr̥tām |
mahārhābharaṇopetāṁ mahārhāmbaradhāriṇīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
āropya śibikāṁ dīptāṁ parārdhyāmbarasaṁvr̥tām |
rakṣobhir bahubhir guptām ājahāra vibhīṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
so ’bhigamya mahātmānaṁ jñātvābhidhyānam āsthitam |
praṇataś ca prahr̥ṣṭaś ca prāptāṁ sītāṁ nyavedayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tām āgatām upaśrutya rakṣogr̥haciroṣitām |
harṣo dainyaṁ ca roṣaś ca trayaṁ rāghavam āviśat ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tataḥ pārśvagataṁ dr̥ṣṭvā savimarśaṁ vicārayan |
vibhīṣaṇam idaṁ vākyam ahr̥ṣṭo rāghavo ’bravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
rākṣasādhipate saumya nityaṁ madvijaye rata |
vaidehī saṁnikarṣaṁ me śīghraṁ samupagacchatu ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
sa tad vacanam ājñāya rāghavasya vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
tūrṇam utsāraṇe yatnaṁ kārayām āsa sarvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
kañcukoṣṇīṣiṇas tatra vetrajharjharapāṇayaḥ |
utsārayantaḥ puruṣāḥ samantāt paricakramuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
r̥kṣāṇāṁ vānarāṇāṁ ca rākṣasānāṁ ca sarvataḥ |
vr̥ndāny utsāryamāṇāni dūram utsasr̥jus tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
teṣām utsāryamāṇānāṁ sarveṣāṁ dhvanir utthitaḥ |
vāyunodvartamānasya sāgarasyeva nisvanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
utsāryamāṇāṁs tān dr̥ṣṭvā samantāj jātasaṁbhramān |
dākṣiṇyāt tadamarṣāc ca vārayām āsa rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
saṁrabdhaś cābravīd rāmaś cakṣuṣā pradahann iva |
vibhīṣaṇaṁ mahāprājñaṁ sopālambham idaṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
kimarthaṁ mām anādr̥tya kr̥śyate ’yaṁ tvayā janaḥ |
nivartayainam udyogaṁ jano ’yaṁ svajano mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
na gr̥hāṇi na vastrāṇi na prākārās tiraskriyāḥ |
nedr̥śā rājasatkārā vr̥ttam āvaraṇaṁ striyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
vyasaneṣu na kr̥cchreṣu na yuddhe na svayaṁvare |
na kratau no vivāhe ca darśanaṁ duṣyate striyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
saiṣā yuddhagatā caiva kr̥cchre mahati ca sthitā |
darśane ’syā na doṣaḥ syān matsamīpe viśeṣataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
tad ānaya samīpaṁ me śīghram enāṁ vibhīṣaṇa |
sītā paśyatu mām eṣā suhr̥dgaṇavr̥taṁ sthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
evam uktas tu rāmeṇa savimarśo vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
rāmasyopānayat sītāṁ saṁnikarṣaṁ vinītavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
tato lakṣmaṇasugrīvau hanūmāṁś ca plavaṁgamaḥ |
niśamya vākyaṁ rāmasya babhūvur vyathitā bhr̥śam ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
kalatranirapekṣaiś ca iṅgitair asya dāruṇaiḥ |
aprītam iva sītāyāṁ tarkayanti sma rāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
lajjayā tv avalīyantī sveṣu gātreṣu maithilī |
vibhīṣaṇenānugatā bhartāraṁ sābhyavartata ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
sā vastrasaṁruddhamukhī lajjayā janasaṁsadi |
rurodāsādya bhartāram āryaputreti bhāṣiṇī ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
vismayāc ca praharṣāc ca snehāc ca paridevatā |
udaikṣata mukhaṁ bhartuḥ saumyaṁ saumyatarānanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
atha samapanudan manaḥklamaṁ sā; suciram adr̥ṣṭam udīkṣya vai priyasya |
vadanam uditapūrṇacandrakāntaṁ; vimalaśaśāṅkanibhānanā tadāsīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
