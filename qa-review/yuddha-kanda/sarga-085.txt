=== Sarga 85 ===

---
1.
hanyamāne bale tūrṇam anyonyaṁ te mahāmr̥dhe |
sarasīva mahāgharme sūpakṣīṇe babhūvatuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
svabalasya vighātena virūpākṣavadhena ca |
babhūva dviguṇaṁ kruddho rāvaṇo rākṣasādhipaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
prakṣīṇaṁ tu balaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā vadhyamānaṁ valīmukhaiḥ |
babhūvāsya vyathā yuddhe prekṣya daivaviparyayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
uvāca ca samīpasthaṁ mahodaram ariṁdamam |
asmin kāle mahābāho jayāśā tvayi me sthitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
jahi śatrucamūṁ vīra darśayādya parākramam |
bhartr̥piṇḍasya kālo ’yaṁ nirveṣṭuṁ sādhu yudhyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
evam uktas tathety uktvā rākṣasendraṁ mahodaraḥ |
praviveśārisenāṁ sa pataṁga iva pāvakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tataḥ sa kadanaṁ cakre vānarāṇāṁ mahābalaḥ |
bhartr̥vākyena tejasvī svena vīryeṇa coditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
prabhagnāṁ samare dr̥ṣṭvā vānarāṇāṁ mahācamūm |
abhidudrāva sugrīvo mahodaram anantaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
pragr̥hya vipulāṁ ghorāṁ mahīdharasamāṁ śilām |
cikṣepa ca mahātejās tad vadhāya harīśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tām āpatantīṁ sahasā śilāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā mahodaraḥ |
asaṁbhrāntas tato bāṇair nirbibheda durāsadām ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
rakṣasā tena bāṇaughair nikr̥ttā sā sahasradhā |
nipapāta śilā bhūmau gr̥dhracakram ivākulam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tāṁ tu bhinnāṁ śilāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā sugrīvaḥ krodhamūrchitaḥ |
sālam utpāṭya cikṣepa rakṣase raṇamūrdhani |
śaraiś ca vidadārainaṁ śūraḥ parapuraṁjayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sa dadarśa tataḥ kruddhaḥ parighaṁ patitaṁ bhuvi |
āvidhya tu sa taṁ dīptaṁ parighaṁ tasya darśayan |
parighāgreṇa vegena jaghānāsya hayottamān ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tasmād dhatahayād vīraḥ so ’vaplutya mahārathāt |
gadāṁ jagrāha saṁkruddho rākṣaso ’tha mahodaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
gadāparighahastau tau yudhi vīrau samīyatuḥ |
nardantau govr̥ṣaprakhyau ghanāv iva savidyutau ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
ājaghāna gadāṁ tasya parigheṇa harīśvaraḥ |
papāta sa gadodbhinnaḥ parighas tasya bhūtale ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tato jagrāha tejasvī sugrīvo vasudhātalāt |
āyasaṁ musalaṁ ghoraṁ sarvato hemabhūṣitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
taṁ samudyamya cikṣepa so ’py anyāṁ vyākṣipad gadām |
bhinnāv anyonyam āsādya petatur dharaṇītale ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tato bhagnapraharaṇau muṣṭibhyāṁ tau samīyatuḥ |
tejobalasamāviṣṭau dīptāv iva hutāśanau ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
jaghnatus tau tadānyonyaṁ nedatuś ca punaḥ punaḥ |
talaiś cānyonyam āhatya petatur dharaṇītale ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
utpetatus tatas tūrṇaṁ jaghnatuś ca parasparam |
bhujaiś cikṣepatur vīrāv anyonyam aparājitau ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
ājahāra tadā khaḍgam adūraparivartinam |
rākṣasaś carmaṇā sārdhaṁ mahāvego mahodaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tathaiva ca mahākhaḍgaṁ carmaṇā patitaṁ saha |
jagrāha vānaraśreṣṭhaḥ sugrīvo vegavattaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tau tu roṣaparītāṅgau nardantāv abhyadhāvatām |
udyatāsī raṇe hr̥ṣṭau yudhi śastraviśāradau ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
dakṣiṇaṁ maṇḍalaṁ cobhau tau tūrṇaṁ saṁparīyatuḥ |
anyonyam abhisaṁkruddhau jaye praṇihitāv ubhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
sa tu śūro mahāvego vīryaślāghī mahodaraḥ |
mahācarmaṇi taṁ khaḍgaṁ pātayām āsa durmatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
lagnam utkarṣataḥ khaḍgaṁ khaḍgena kapikuñjaraḥ |
jahāra saśiras trāṇaṁ kuṇḍalopahitaṁ śiraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
nikr̥ttaśirasas tasya patitasya mahītale |
tad balaṁ rākṣasendrasya dr̥ṣṭvā tatra na tiṣṭhati ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
hatvā taṁ vānaraiḥ sārdhaṁ nanāda mudito hariḥ |
cukrodha ca daśagrīvo babhau hr̥ṣṭaś ca rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
