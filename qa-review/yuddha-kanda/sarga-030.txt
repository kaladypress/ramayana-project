=== Sarga 30 ===

---
1.
tāṁ rātrim uṣitās tatra suvele haripuṁgavāḥ |
laṅkāyāṁ dadr̥śur vīrā vanāny upavanāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
samasaumyāni ramyāṇi viśālāny āyatāni ca |
dr̥ṣṭiramyāṇi te dr̥ṣṭvā babhūvur jātavismayāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
campakāśokapuṁnāgasālatālasamākulā |
tamālavanasaṁchannā nāgamālāsamāvr̥tā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
hintālair arjunair nīpaiḥ saptaparṇaiś ca puṣpitaiḥ |
tilakaiḥ karṇikāraiś ca paṭālaiś ca samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
śuśubhe puṣpitāgraiś ca latāparigatair drumaiḥ |
laṅkā bahuvidhair divyair yathendrasyāmarāvatī ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
vicitrakusumopetai raktakomalapallavaiḥ |
śādvalaiś ca tathā nīlaiś citrābhir vanarājibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
gandhāḍhyāny abhiramyāṇi puṣpāṇi ca phalāni ca |
dhārayanty agamās tatra bhūṣaṇānīva mānavāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tac caitrarathasaṁkāśaṁ manojñaṁ nandanopamam |
vanaṁ sarvartukaṁ ramyaṁ śuśubhe ṣaṭpadāyutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
natyūhakoyaṣṭibhakair nr̥tyamānaiś ca barhibhiḥ |
rutaṁ parabhr̥tānāṁ ca śuśruve vananirjhare ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
nityamattavihaṁgāni bhramarācaritāni ca |
kokilākulaṣaṇḍāni vihagābhirutāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
bhr̥ṅgarājābhigītāni bhramaraiḥ sevitāni ca |
koṇālakavighuṣṭāni sārasābhirutāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
viviśus te tatas tāni vanāny upavanāni ca |
hr̥ṣṭāḥ pramuditā vīrā harayaḥ kāmarūpiṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
teṣāṁ praviśatāṁ tatra vānarāṇāṁ mahaujasām |
puṣpasaṁsargasurabhir vavau ghrāṇasukho ’nilaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
anye tu harivīrāṇāṁ yūthān niṣkramya yūthapāḥ |
sugrīveṇābhyanujñātā laṅkāṁ jagmuḥ patākinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
vitrāsayanto vihagāṁs trāsayanto mr̥gadvipān |
kampayantaś ca tāṁ laṅkāṁ nādaiḥ svair nadatāṁ varāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
kurvantas te mahāvegā mahīṁ cāraṇapīḍitām |
rajaś ca sahasaivordhvaṁ jagāma caraṇoddhatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
r̥kṣāḥ siṁhā varāhāś ca mahiṣā vāraṇā mr̥gāḥ |
tena śabdena vitrastā jagmur bhītā diśo daśa ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
śikharaṁ tu trikūṭasya prāṁśu caikaṁ divispr̥śam |
samantāt puṣpasaṁchannaṁ mahārajatasaṁnibham ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
śatayojanavistīrṇaṁ vimalaṁ cārudarśanam |
ślakṣṇaṁ śrīman mahac caiva duṣprāpaṁ śakunair api ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
manasāpi durārohaṁ kiṁ punaḥ karmaṇā janaiḥ |
niviṣṭā tatra śikhare laṅkā rāvaṇapālitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
sā purī gopurair uccaiḥ pāṇḍurāmbudasaṁnibhaiḥ |
kāñcanena ca sālena rājatena ca śobhitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
prāsādaiś ca vimānaiś ca laṅkā paramabhūṣitā |
ghanair ivātapāpāye madhyamaṁ vaiṣṇavaṁ padam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
yasyāṁ stambhasahasreṇa prāsādaḥ samalaṁkr̥taḥ |
kailāsaśikharākāro dr̥śyate kham ivollikhan ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
caityaḥ sa rākṣasendrasya babhūva purabhūṣaṇam |
śatena rakṣasāṁ nityaṁ yaḥ samagreṇa rakṣyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tāṁ samr̥ddhāṁ samr̥ddhārtho lakṣmīvām̐l lakṣmaṇāgrajaḥ |
rāvaṇasya purīṁ rāmo dadarśa saha vānaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tāṁ ratnapūrṇāṁ bahusaṁvidhānāṁ; prāsādamālābhir alaṁkr̥tāṁ ca |
purīṁ mahāyantrakavāṭamukhyāṁ; dadarśa rāmo mahatā balena ||

=== meaning ===

---
