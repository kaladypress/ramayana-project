=== Sarga 37 ===

---
1.
pratipraviṣṭe laṅkāṁ tu kr̥tārthe rāvaṇātmaje |
rāghavaṁ parivāryārtā rarakṣur vānararṣabhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
hanūmān aṅgado nīlaḥ suṣeṇaḥ kumudo nalaḥ |
gajo gavākṣo gavayaḥ śarabho gandhamādanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
jāmbavān r̥ṣabhaḥ sundo rambhaḥ śatabaliḥ pr̥thuḥ |
vyūḍhānīkāś ca yattāś ca drumān ādāya sarvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
vīkṣamāṇā diśaḥ sarvās tiryag ūrdhvaṁ ca vānarāḥ |
tr̥ṇeṣv api ca ceṣṭatsu rākṣasā iti menire ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
rāvaṇaś cāpi saṁhr̥ṣṭo visr̥jyendrajitaṁ sutam |
ājuhāva tataḥ sītā rakṣaṇī rākṣasīs tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
rākṣasyas trijaṭā cāpi śāsanāt tam upasthitāḥ |
tā uvāca tato hr̥ṣṭo rākṣasī rākṣaseśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
hatāv indrajitākhyāta vaidehyā rāmalakṣmaṇau |
puṣpakaṁ ca samāropya darśayadhvaṁ hatau raṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
yad āśrayād avaṣṭabdho neyaṁ mām upatiṣṭhati |
so ’syā bhartā saha bhrātrā nirasto raṇamūrdhani ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
nirviśaṅkā nirudvignā nirapekṣā ca maithilī |
mām upasthāsyate sītā sarvābharaṇabhūṣitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
adya kālavaśaṁ prāptaṁ raṇe rāmaṁ salakṣmaṇam |
avekṣya vinivr̥ttāśā nānyāṁ gatim apaśyatī ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā rāvaṇasya durātmanaḥ |
rākṣasyas tās tathety uktvā prajagmur yatra puṣpakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tataḥ puṣpakam ādaya rākṣasyo rāvaṇājñayā |
aśokavanikāsthāṁ tāṁ maithilīṁ samupānayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tām ādāya tu rākṣasyo bhartr̥śokaparāyaṇām |
sītām āropayām āsur vimānaṁ puṣpakaṁ tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tataḥ puṣpakam āropya sītāṁ trijaṭayā saha |
rāvaṇo ’kārayal laṅkāṁ patākādhvajamālinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
prāghoṣayata hr̥ṣṭaś ca laṅkāyāṁ rākṣaseśvaraḥ |
rāghavo lakṣmaṇaś caiva hatāv indrajitā raṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
vimānenāpi sītā tu gatvā trijaṭayā saha |
dadarśa vānarāṇāṁ tu sarvaṁ sinyaṁ nipātitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
prahr̥ṣṭamanasaś cāpi dadarśa piśitāśanān |
vānarāṁś cāpi duḥkhārtān rāmalakṣmaṇapārśvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tataḥ sītā dadarśobhau śayānau śatatalpayoḥ |
lakṣmaṇaṁ caiva rāmaṁ ca visaṁjñau śarapīḍitau ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
vidhvastakavacau vīrau vipraviddhaśarāsanau |
sāyakaiś chinnasarvāṅgau śarastambhamayau kṣitau ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tau dr̥ṣṭvā bhrātarau tatra vīrau sā puruṣarṣabhau |
duḥkhārtā subhr̥śaṁ sītā karuṇaṁ vilalāpa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
sā bāṣpaśokābhihatā samīkṣya; tau bhrātarau devasamaprabhāvau |
vitarkayantī nidhanaṁ tayoḥ sā; duḥkhānvitā vākyam idaṁ jagāda ||

=== meaning ===

---
