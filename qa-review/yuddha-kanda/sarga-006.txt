=== Sarga 6 ===

---
1.
laṅkāyāṁ tu kr̥taṁ karma ghoraṁ dr̥ṣṭvā bhavāvaham |
rākṣasendro hanumatā śakreṇeva mahātmanā |
abravīd rākṣasān sarvān hriyā kiṁ cid avāṅmukhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
dharṣitā ca praviṣṭā ca laṅkā duṣprasahā purī |
tena vānaramātreṇa dr̥ṣṭā sītā ca jānakī ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
prasādo dharṣitaś caityaḥ pravarā rākṣasā hatāḥ |
āvilā ca purī laṅkā sarvā hanumatā kr̥tā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
kiṁ kariṣyāmi bhadraṁ vaḥ kiṁ vā yuktam anantaram |
ucyatāṁ naḥ samarthaṁ yat kr̥taṁ ca sukr̥taṁ bhavet ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
mantramūlaṁ hi vijayaṁ prāhur āryā manasvinaḥ |
tasmād vai rocaye mantraṁ rāmaṁ prati mahābalāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
trividhāḥ puruṣā loke uttamādhamamadhyamāḥ |
teṣāṁ tu samavetānāṁ guṇadoṣaṁ vadāmy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
mantribhir hitasaṁyuktaiḥ samarthair mantranirṇaye |
mitrair vāpi samānārthair bāndhavair api vā hitaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sahito mantrayitvā yaḥ karmārambhān pravartayet |
daive ca kurute yatnaṁ tam āhuḥ puruṣottamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
eko ’rthaṁ vimr̥śed eko dharme prakurute manaḥ |
ekaḥ kāryāṇi kurute tam āhur madhyamaṁ naram ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
guṇadoṣāv aniścitya tyaktvā daivavyapāśrayam |
kariṣyāmīti yaḥ kāryam upekṣet sa narādhamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
yatheme puruṣā nityam uttamādhamamadhyamāḥ |
evaṁ mantro ’pi vijñeya uttamādhamamadhyamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
aikamatyam upāgamya śāstradr̥ṣṭena cakṣuṣā |
mantriṇo yatra nirastās tam āhur mantram uttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
bahvyo ’pi matayo gatvā mantriṇo hy arthanirṇaye |
punar yatraikatāṁ prāptaḥ sa mantro madhyamaḥ smr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
anyonyamatim āsthāya yatra saṁpratibhāṣyate |
na caikamatye śreyo ’sti mantraḥ so ’dhama ucyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tasmāt sumantritaṁ sādhu bhavanto mantrisattamāḥ |
kāryaṁ saṁpratipadyantām etat kr̥tyatamaṁ mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
vānarāṇāṁ hi vīrāṇāṁ sahasraiḥ parivāritaḥ |
rāmo ’bhyeti purīṁ laṅkām asmākam uparodhakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tariṣyati ca suvyaktaṁ rāghavaḥ sāgaraṁ sukham |
tarasā yuktarūpeṇa sānujaḥ sabalānugaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
asminn evaṁgate kārye viruddhe vānaraiḥ saha |
hitaṁ pure ca sainye ca sarvaṁ saṁmantryatāṁ mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
