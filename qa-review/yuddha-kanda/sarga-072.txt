=== Sarga 72 ===

---
1.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā rāghavaḥ śokakarśitaḥ |
nopadhārayate vyaktaṁ yad uktaṁ tena rakṣasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tato dhairyam avaṣṭabhya rāmaḥ parapuraṁjayaḥ |
vibhīṣaṇam upāsīnam uvāca kapisaṁnidhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
nairr̥tādhipate vākyaṁ yad uktaṁ te vibhīṣaṇa |
bhūyas tac chrotum icchāmi brūhi yat te vivakṣitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
rāghavasya vacaḥ śrutvā vākyaṁ vākyaviśāradaḥ |
yat tat punar idaṁ vākyaṁ babhāṣe sa vibhīṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
yathājñaptaṁ mahābāho tvayā gulmaniveśanam |
tat tathānuṣṭhitaṁ vīra tvadvākyasamanantaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tāny anīkāni sarvāṇi vibhaktāni samantataḥ |
vinyastā yūthapāś caiva yathānyāyaṁ vibhāgaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
bhūyas tu mama vijāpyaṁ tac chr̥ṇuṣva mahāyaśaḥ |
tvayy akāraṇasaṁtapte saṁtaptahr̥dayā vayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tyaja rājann imaṁ śokaṁ mithyā saṁtāpam āgatam |
tad iyaṁ tyajyatāṁ cintā śatruharṣavivardhanī ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
udyamaḥ kriyatāṁ vīra harṣaḥ samupasevyatām |
prāptavyā yadi te sītā hantavyaś ca niśācarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
raghunandana vakṣyāmi śrūyatāṁ me hitaṁ vacaḥ |
sādhv ayaṁ yātu saumitrir balena mahatā vr̥taḥ |
nikumbhilāyāṁ saṁprāpya hantuṁ rāvaṇim āhave ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
dhanurmaṇḍalanirmuktair āśīviṣaviṣopamaiḥ |
śarair hantuṁ maheṣvāso rāvaṇiṁ samitiṁjayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tena vīreṇa tapasā varadānāt svayambhutaḥ |
astraṁ brahmaśiraḥ prāptaṁ kāmagāś ca turaṁgamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
nikumbhilām asaṁprāptam ahutāgniṁ ca yo ripuḥ |
tvām ātatāyinaṁ hanyād indraśatro sa te vadhaḥ |
ity evaṁ vihito rājan vadhas tasyaiva dhīmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
vadhāyendrajito rāma taṁ diśasva mahābalam |
hate tasmin hataṁ viddhi rāvaṇaṁ sasuhr̥jjanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
vibhīṣaṇavacaḥ śrutva rāmo vākyam athābravīt |
jānāmi tasya raudrasya māyāṁ satyaparākrama ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sa hi brahmāstravit prājño mahāmāyo mahābalaḥ |
karoty asaṁjñān saṁgrāme devān savaruṇān api ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tasyāntarikṣe carato rathasthasya mahāyaśaḥ |
na gatir jñāyate vīrasūryasyevābhrasaṁplave ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
rāghavas tu ripor jñātvā māyāvīryaṁ durātmanaḥ |
lakṣmaṇaṁ kīrtisaṁpannam idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
yad vānarendrasya balaṁ tena sarveṇa saṁvr̥taḥ |
hanūmatpramukhaiś caiva yūthapaiḥ sahalakṣmaṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
jāmbavenarkṣapatinā saha sainyena saṁvr̥taḥ |
jahi taṁ rākṣasasutaṁ māyābalaviśāradam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
ayaṁ tvāṁ sacivaiḥ sārdhaṁ mahātmā rajanīcaraḥ |
abhijñas tasya deśasya pr̥ṣṭhato ’nugamiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
rāghavasya vacaḥ śrutvā lakṣmaṇaḥ savibhīṣaṇaḥ |
jagrāha kārmukaṁ śreṣṭham anyad bhīmaparākramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
saṁnaddhaḥ kavacī khaḍgī sa śarī hemacāpadhr̥k |
rāmapādāv upaspr̥śya hr̥ṣṭaḥ saumitrir abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
adya matkārmukonmukhāḥ śarā nirbhidya rāvaṇim |
laṅkām abhipatiṣyanti haṁsāḥ puṣkariṇīm iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
adyaiva tasya raudrasya śarīraṁ māmakāḥ śarāḥ |
vidhamiṣyanti hatvā taṁ mahācāpaguṇacyutāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
sa evam uktvā dyutimān vacanaṁ bhrātur agrataḥ |
sa rāvaṇivadhākāṅkṣī lakṣmaṇas tvarito yayau ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
so ’bhivādya guroḥ pādau kr̥tvā cāpi pradakṣiṇam |
nikumbhilām abhiyayau caityaṁ rāvaṇipālitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
vibhīṣaṇena sahito rājaputraḥ pratāpavān |
kr̥tasvastyayano bhrātrā lakṣmaṇas tvarito yayau ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
vānarāṇāṁ sahasrais tu hanūmān bahubhir vr̥taḥ |
vibhīṣaṇaḥ sahāmātyas tadā lakṣmaṇam anvagāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
mahatā harisainyena savegam abhisaṁvr̥taḥ |
r̥kṣarājabalaṁ caiva dadarśa pathi viṣṭhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
sa gatvā dūram adhvānaṁ saumitrir mitranandanaḥ |
rākṣasendrabalaṁ dūrād apaśyad vyūham āsthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
sa saṁprāpya dhanuṣpāṇir māyāyogam ariṁdama |
tasthau brahmavidhānena vijetuṁ raghunandanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
vividham amalaśastrabhāsvaraṁ tad; dhvajagahanaṁ vipulaṁ mahārathaiś ca |
pratibhayatamam aprameyavegaṁ; timiram iva dviṣatāṁ balaṁ viveśa ||

=== meaning ===

---
