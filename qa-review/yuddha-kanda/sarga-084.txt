=== Sarga 84 ===

---
1.
tathā taiḥ kr̥ttagātrais tu daśagrīveṇa mārgaṇaiḥ |
babhūva vasudhā tatra prakīrṇā haribhir vr̥tā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
rāvaṇasyāprasahyaṁ taṁ śarasaṁpātam ekataḥ |
na śekuḥ sahituṁ dīptaṁ pataṁgā iva pāvakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
te ’rditā niśitair bāṇaiḥ krośanto vipradudruvuḥ |
pāvakārciḥsamāviṣṭā dahyamānā yathā gajāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
plavaṁgānām anīkāni mahābhrāṇīva mārutaḥ |
sa yayau samare tasmin vidhaman rāvaṇaḥ śaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
kadanaṁ tarasā kr̥tvā rākṣasendro vanaukasām |
āsasāda tato yuddhe rāghavaṁ tvaritas tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
sugrīvas tān kapīn dr̥ṣṭvā bhagnān vidravato raṇe |
gulme suṣeṇaṁ nikṣipya cakre yuddhe drutaṁ manaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
ātmanaḥ sadr̥śaṁ vīraṁ sa taṁ nikṣipya vānaram |
sugrīvo ’bhimukhaḥ śatruṁ pratasthe pādapāyudhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
pārśvataḥ pr̥ṣṭhataś cāsya sarve yūthādhipāḥ svayam |
anujahrur mahāśailān vividhāṁś ca mahādrumān ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sa nadan yudhi sugrīvaḥ svareṇa mahatā mahān |
pātayan vividhāṁś cānyāñ jaghānottamarākṣasān ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
mamarda ca mahākāyo rākṣasān vānareśvaraḥ |
yugāntasamaye vāyuḥ pravr̥ddhān agamān iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
rākṣasānām anīkeṣu śailavarṣaṁ vavarṣa ha |
aśmavarṣaṁ yathā meghaḥ pakṣisaṁgheṣu kānane ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
kapirājavimuktais taiḥ śailavarṣais tu rākṣasāḥ |
vikīrṇaśirasaḥ petur nikr̥ttā iva parvatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
atha saṁkṣīyamāṇeṣu rākṣaseṣu samantataḥ |
sugrīveṇa prabhagneṣu patatsu vinadatsu ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
virūpākṣaḥ svakaṁ nāma dhanvī viśrāvya rākṣasaḥ |
rathād āplutya durdharṣo gajaskandham upāruhat ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa taṁ dviradam āruhya virūpākṣo mahārathaḥ |
vinadan bhīmanirhrālaṁ vānarān abhyadhāvata ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sugrīve sa śarān ghorān visasarja camūmukhe |
sthāpayām āsā codvignān rākṣasān saṁpraharṣayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
so ’tividdhaḥ śitair bāṇaiḥ kapīndras tena rakṣasā |
cukrodha ca mahākrodho vadhe cāsya mano dadhe ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tataḥ pādapam uddhr̥tya śūraḥ saṁpradhane hariḥ |
abhipatya jaghānāsya pramukhe taṁ mahāgajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
sa tu prahārābhihataḥ sugrīveṇa mahāgajaḥ |
apāsarpad dhanurmātraṁ niṣasāda nanāda ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
gajāt tu mathitāt tūrṇam apakramya sa vīryavān |
rākṣaso ’bhimukhaḥ śatruṁ pratyudgamya tataḥ kapim ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
ārṣabhaṁ carmakhaḍgaṁ ca pragr̥hya laghuvikramaḥ |
bhartsayann iva sugrīvam āsasāda vyavasthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
sa hi tasyābhisaṁkruddhaḥ pragr̥hya mahatīṁ śilām |
virūpākṣāya cikṣepa sugrīvo jaladopamām ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
sa tāṁ śilām āpatantīṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rākṣasapuṁgavaḥ |
apakramya suvikrāntaḥ khaḍgena prāharat tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tena khaḍgena saṁkruddhaḥ sugrīvasya camūmukhe |
kavacaṁ pātayām āsa sa khaḍgābhihato ’patat ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
sa samutthāya patitaḥ kapis tasya vyasarjayat |
talaprahāram aśaneḥ samānaṁ bhīmanisvanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
talaprahāraṁ tad rakṣaḥ sugrīveṇa samudyatam |
naipuṇyān mocayitvainaṁ muṣṭinorasy atāḍayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tatas tu saṁkruddhataraḥ sugrīvo vānareśvaraḥ |
mokṣitaṁ cātmano dr̥ṣṭvā prahāraṁ tena rakṣasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
sa dadarśāntaraṁ tasya virūpākṣasya vānaraḥ |
tato nyapātayat krodhāc chaṅkhadeśe mahātalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
mahendrāśanikalpena talenābhihataḥ kṣitau |
papāta rudhiraklinnaḥ śoṇitaṁ sa samudvaman ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
vivr̥ttanayanaṁ krodhāt saphenarudhirāplutam |
dadr̥śus te virūpākṣaṁ virūpākṣataraṁ kr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
sphurantaṁ parivarjantaṁ pārśvena rudhirokṣitam |
karuṇaṁ ca vinardāntaṁ dadr̥śuḥ kapayo ripum ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
tathā tu tau saṁyati saṁprayuktau; tarasvinau vānararākṣasānām |
balārṇavau sasvanatuḥ sabhīmaṁ; mahārṇavau dvāv iva bhinnavelau ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
vināśitaṁ prekṣya virūpanetraṁ; mahābalaṁ taṁ haripārthivena |
balaṁ samastaṁ kapirākṣasānām; unmattagaṅgāpratimaṁ babhūva ||

=== meaning ===

---
