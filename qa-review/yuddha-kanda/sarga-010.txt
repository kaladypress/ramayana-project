=== Sarga 10 ===

---
1.
suniviṣṭaṁ hitaṁ vākyam uktavantaṁ vibhīṣaṇam |
abravīt paruṣaṁ vākyaṁ rāvaṇaḥ kālacoditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
vaset saha sapatnena kruddhenāśīviṣeṇa vā |
na tu mitrapravādena saṁvasec chatrusevinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
jānāmi śīlaṁ jñātīnāṁ sarvalokeṣu rākṣasa |
hr̥ṣyanti vyasaneṣv ete jñātīnāṁ jñātayaḥ sadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
pradhānaṁ sādhakaṁ vaidyaṁ dharmaśīlaṁ ca rākṣasa |
jñātayo hy avamanyante śūraṁ paribhavanti ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
nityam anyonyasaṁhr̥ṣṭā vyasaneṣv ātatāyinaḥ |
pracchannahr̥dayā ghorā jñātayas tu bhayāvahāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
śrūyante hastibhir gītāḥ ślokāḥ padmavane kva cit |
pāśahastān narān dr̥ṣṭvā śr̥ṇu tān gadato mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
nāgnir nānyāni śastrāṇi na naḥ pāśā bhayāvahāḥ |
ghorāḥ svārthaprayuktās tu jñātayo no bhayāvahāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
upāyam ete vakṣyanti grahaṇe nātra saṁśayaḥ |
kr̥tsnād bhayāj jñātibhayaṁ sukaṣṭaṁ viditaṁ ca naḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
vidyate goṣu saṁpannaṁ vidyate brāhmaṇe damaḥ |
vidyate strīṣu cāpalyaṁ vidyate jñātito bhayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tato neṣṭam idaṁ saumya yad ahaṁ lokasatkr̥taḥ |
aiśvaryam abhijātaś ca ripūṇāṁ mūrdhni ca sthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
anyas tv evaṁvidhaṁ brūyād vākyam etan niśācara |
asmin muhūrte na bhavet tvāṁ tu dhik kulapāṁsanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
ity uktaḥ paruṣaṁ vākyaṁ nyāyavādī vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
utpapāta gadāpāṇiś caturbhiḥ saha rākṣasaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
abravīc ca tadā vākyaṁ jātakrodho vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
antarikṣagataḥ śrīmān bhrātaraṁ rākṣasādhipam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sa tvaṁ bhrātāsi me rājan brūhi māṁ yad yad icchasi |
idaṁ tu paruṣaṁ vākyaṁ na kṣamāmy anr̥taṁ tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sunītaṁ hitakāmena vākyam uktaṁ daśānana |
na gr̥hṇanty akr̥tātmānaḥ kālasya vaśam āgatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sulabhāḥ puruṣā rājan satataṁ priyavādinaḥ |
apriyasya tu pathyasya vaktā śrotā ca durlabhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
baddhaṁ kālasya pāśena sarvabhūtāpahāriṇā |
na naśyantam upekṣeyaṁ pradīptaṁ śaraṇaṁ yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
dīptapāvakasaṁkāśaiḥ śitaiḥ kāñcanabhūṣaṇaiḥ |
na tvām icchāmy ahaṁ draṣṭuṁ rāmeṇa nihataṁ śaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
śūrāś ca balavantaś ca kr̥tāstrāś ca raṇājire |
kālābhipannā sīdanti yathā vālukasetavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
ātmānaṁ sarvathā rakṣa purīṁ cemāṁ sarākṣasām |
svasti te ’stu gamiṣyāmi sukhī bhava mayā vinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
nivāryamāṇasya mayā hitaiṣiṇā; na rocate te vacanaṁ niśācara |
parītakālā hi gatāyuṣo narā; hitaṁ na gr̥hṇanti suhr̥dbhir īritam ||

=== meaning ===

---
