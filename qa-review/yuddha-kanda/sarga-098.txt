=== Sarga 98 ===

---
1.
rāvaṇaṁ nihataṁ śrutvā rāghaveṇa mahātmanā |
antaḥpurād viniṣpetū rākṣasyaḥ śokakarśitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
vāryamāṇāḥ subahuśo vr̥ṣṭantyaḥ kṣitipāṁsuṣu |
vimuktakeśyo duḥkhārtā gāvo vatsahatā yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
uttareṇa viniṣkramya dvāreṇa saha rākṣasaiḥ |
praviśyāyodhanaṁ ghoraṁ vicinvantyo hataṁ patim ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
āryaputreti vādinyo hā nātheti ca sarvaśaḥ |
paripetuḥ kabandhāṅkāṁ mahīṁ śoṇitakardamām ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tā bāṣpaparipūrṇākṣyo bhartr̥śokaparājitāḥ |
kareṇva iva nardantyo vinedur hatayūthapāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
dadr̥śus tā mahākāyaṁ mahāvīryaṁ mahādyutim |
rāvaṇaṁ nihataṁ bhūmau nīlāñjanacayopamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tāḥ patiṁ sahasā dr̥ṣṭvā śayānaṁ raṇapāṁsuṣu |
nipetus tasya gātreṣu chinnā vanalatā iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
bahumānāt pariṣvajya kā cid enaṁ ruroda ha |
caraṇau kā cid āliṅgya kā cit kaṇṭhe ’valambya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
uddhr̥tya ca bhujau kā cid bhūmau sma parivartate |
hatasya vadanaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā kā cin moham upāgamat ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
kā cid aṅke śiraḥ kr̥tvā ruroda mukham īkṣatī |
snāpayantī mukhaṁ bāṣpais tuṣārair iva paṅkajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
evam ārtāḥ patiṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rāvaṇaṁ nihataṁ bhuvi |
cukruśur bahudhā śokād bhūyas tāḥ paryadevayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
yena vitrāsitaḥ śakro yena vitrāsito yamaḥ |
yena vaiśravaṇo rājā puṣpakeṇa viyojitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
gandharvāṇām r̥ṣīṇāṁ ca surāṇāṁ ca mahātmanām |
bhayaṁ yena mahad dattaṁ so ’yaṁ śete raṇe hataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
asurebhyaḥ surebhyo vā pannagebhyo ’pi vā tathā |
na bhayaṁ yo vijānāti tasyedaṁ mānuṣād bhayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
avadhyo devatānāṁ yas tathā dānavarakṣasām |
hataḥ so ’yaṁ raṇe śete mānuṣeṇa padātinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
yo na śakyaḥ surair hantuṁ na yakṣair nāsurais tathā |
so ’yaṁ kaś cid ivāsattvo mr̥tyuṁ martyena lambhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
evaṁ vadantyo bahudhā rurudus tasya tāḥ striyaḥ |
bhūya eva ca duḥkhārtā vilepuś ca punaḥ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
aśr̥ṇvatā tu suhr̥dāṁ satataṁ hitavādinām |
etāḥ samam idānīṁ te vayam ātmā ca pātitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
bruvāṇo ’pi hitaṁ vākyam iṣṭo bhrātā vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
dhr̥ṣṭaṁ paruṣito mohāt tvayātmavadhakāṅkṣiṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
yadi niryātitā te syāt sītā rāmāya maithilī |
na naḥ syād vyasanaṁ ghoram idaṁ mūlaharaṁ mahat ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
vr̥ttakāmo bhaved bhrātā rāmo mitrakulaṁ bhavet |
vayaṁ cāvidhavāḥ sarvāḥ sakāmā na ca śatravaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tvayā punar nr̥śaṁsena sītāṁ saṁrundhatā balāt |
rākṣasā vayam ātmā ca trayaṁ tulaṁ nipātitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
na kāmakāraḥ kāmaṁ vā tava rākṣasapuṁgava |
daivaṁ ceṣṭayate sarvaṁ hataṁ daivena hanyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
vānarāṇāṁ vināśo ’yaṁ rākṣasānāṁ ca te raṇe |
tava caiva mahābāho daivayogād upāgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
naivārthena na kāmena vikrameṇa na cājñayā |
śakyā daivagatir loke nivartayitum udyatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
vilepur evaṁ dīnās tā rākṣasādhipayoṣitaḥ |
kurarya iva duḥkhārtā bāṣpaparyākulekṣaṇāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
