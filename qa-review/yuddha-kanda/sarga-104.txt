=== Sarga 104 ===

---
1.
evam uktā tu vaidehī paruṣaṁ lomaharṣaṇam |
rāghaveṇa saroṣeṇa bhr̥śaṁ pravyathitābhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sā tad aśrutapūrvaṁ hi jane mahati maithilī |
śrutvā bhartr̥vaco rūkṣaṁ lajjayā vrīḍitābhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
praviśantīva gātrāṇi svāny eva janakātmajā |
vākśalyais taiḥ saśalyeva bhr̥śam aśrūṇy avartayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tato bāṣpaparikliṣṭaṁ pramārjantī svam ānanam |
śanair gadgadayā vācā bhartāram idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
kiṁ mām asadr̥śaṁ vākyam īdr̥śaṁ śrotradāruṇam |
rūkṣaṁ śrāvayase vīra prākr̥taḥ prākr̥tām iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
na tathāsmi mahābāho yathā tvam avagacchasi |
pratyayaṁ gaccha me svena cāritreṇaiva te śape ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
pr̥thak strīṇāṁ pracāreṇa jātiṁ tvaṁ pariśaṅkase |
parityajemāṁ śaṅkāṁ tu yadi te ’haṁ parīkṣitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
yady ahaṁ gātrasaṁsparśaṁ gatāsmi vivaśā prabho |
kāmakāro na me tatra daivaṁ tatrāparādhyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
madadhīnaṁ tu yat tan me hr̥dayaṁ tvayi vartate |
parādhīneṣu gātreṣu kiṁ kariṣyāmy anīśvarā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
sahasaṁvr̥ddhabhāvāc ca saṁsargeṇa ca mānada |
yady ahaṁ te na vijñātā hatā tenāsmi śāśvatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
preṣitas te yadā vīro hanūmān avalokakaḥ |
laṅkāsthāhaṁ tvayā vīra kiṁ tadā na visarjitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
pratyakṣaṁ vānarendrasya tvadvākyasamanantaram |
tvayā saṁtyaktayā vīra tyaktaṁ syāj jīvitaṁ mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
na vr̥thā te śramo ’yaṁ syāt saṁśaye nyasya jīvitam |
suhr̥jjanaparikleśo na cāyaṁ niṣphalas tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tvayā tu naraśārdūla krodham evānuvartatā |
laghuneva manuṣyeṇa strītvam eva puraskr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
apadeśena janakān notpattir vasudhātalāt |
mama vr̥ttaṁ ca vr̥ttajña bahu te na puraskr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
na pramāṇīkr̥taḥ pāṇir bālye bālena pīḍitaḥ |
mama bhaktiś ca śīlaṁ ca sarvaṁ te pr̥ṣṭhataḥ kr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
evaṁ bruvāṇā rudatī bāṣpagadgadabhāṣiṇī |
abravīl lakṣmaṇaṁ sītā dīnaṁ dhyānaparaṁ sthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
citāṁ me kuru saumitre vyasanasyāsya bheṣajam |
mithyāpavādopahatā nāhaṁ jīvitum utsahe ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
aprītasya guṇair bhartus tyaktayā janasaṁsadi |
yā kṣamā me gatir gantuṁ pravekṣye havyavāhanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
evam uktas tu vaidehyā lakṣmaṇaḥ paravīrahā |
amarṣavaśam āpanno rāghavānanam aikṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
sa vijñāya manaśchandaṁ rāmasyākārasūcitam |
citāṁ cakāra saumitrir mate rāmasya vīryavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
adhomukhaṁ tato rāmaṁ śanaiḥ kr̥tvā pradakṣiṇam |
upāsarpata vaidehī dīpyamānaṁ hutāśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
praṇamya devatābhyaś ca brāhmaṇebhyaś ca maithilī |
baddhāñjalipuṭā cedam uvācāgnisamīpataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
yathā me hr̥dayaṁ nityaṁ nāpasarpati rāghavāt |
tathā lokasya sākṣī māṁ sarvataḥ pātu pāvakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
evam uktvā tu vaidehī parikramya hutāśanam |
viveśa jvalanaṁ dīptaṁ niḥsaṅgenāntarātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
janaḥ sa sumahāṁs tatra bālavr̥ddhasamākulaḥ |
dadarśa maithilīṁ tatra praviśantīṁ hutāśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tasyām agniṁ viśantyāṁ tu hāheti vipulaḥ svanaḥ |
rakṣasāṁ vānarāṇāṁ ca saṁbabhūvādbhutopamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
