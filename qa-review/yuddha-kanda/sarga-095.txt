=== Sarga 95 ===

---
1.
tataḥ pravr̥ttaṁ sukrūraṁ rāmarāvaṇayos tadā |
sumahad dvairathaṁ yuddhaṁ sarvalokabhayāvaham ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tato rākṣasasainyaṁ ca harīṇāṁ ca mahad balam |
pragr̥hītapraharaṇaṁ niśceṣṭaṁ samatiṣṭhata ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
saṁprayuddhau tato dr̥ṣṭvā balavan nararākṣasau |
vyākṣiptahr̥dayāḥ sarve paraṁ vismayam āgatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
nānāpraharaṇair vyagrair bhujair vismitabuddhayaḥ |
tasthuḥ prekṣya ca saṁgrāmaṁ nābhijaghnuḥ parasparam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
rakṣasāṁ rāvaṇaṁ cāpi vānarāṇāṁ ca rāghavam |
paśyatāṁ vismitākṣāṇāṁ sainyaṁ citram ivābabhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tau tu tatra nimittāni dr̥ṣṭvā rāghavarāvaṇau |
kr̥tabuddhī sthirāmarṣau yuyudhāte abhītavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
jetavyam iti kākutstho martavyam iti rāvaṇaḥ |
dhr̥tau svavīryasarvasvaṁ yuddhe ’darśayatāṁ tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tataḥ krodhād daśagrīvaḥ śarān saṁdhāya vīryavān |
mumoca dhvajam uddiśya rāghavasya rathe sthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
te śarās tam anāsādya puraṁdararathadhvajam |
raktaśaktiṁ parāmr̥śya nipetur dharaṇītale ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tato rāmo ’bhisaṁkruddhaś cāpam āyamya vīryavān |
kr̥tapratikr̥taṁ kartuṁ manasā saṁpracakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
rāvaṇadhvajam uddiśya mumoca niśitaṁ śaram |
mahāsarpam ivāsahyaṁ jvalantaṁ svena tejasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
jagāma sa mahīṁ bhittvā daśagrīvadhvajaṁ śaraḥ |
sa nikr̥tto ’patad bhūmau rāvaṇasya rathadhvajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
dhvajasyonmathanaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rāvaṇaḥ sumahābalaḥ |
krodhajenāgninā saṁkhye pradīpta iva cābhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sa roṣavaśam āpannaḥ śaravarṣaṁ mahad vaman |
rāmasya turagān divyāñ śarair vivyādha rāvaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
te viddhā harayas tasya nāskhalan nāpi babhramuḥ |
babhūvuḥ svasthahr̥dayāḥ padmanālair ivāhatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
teṣām asaṁbhramaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā vājināṁ rāvaṇas tadā |
bhūya eva susaṁkruddhaḥ śaravarṣaṁ mumoca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
gadāś ca parighāṁś caiva cakrāṇi musalāni ca |
giriśr̥ṅgāṇi vr̥kṣāṁś ca tathā śūlaparaśvadhān ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
māyāvihitam etat tu śastravarṣam apātayat |
sahasraśas tato bāṇān aśrāntahr̥dayodyamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tumulaṁ trāsajananaṁ bhīmaṁ bhīmapratisvanam |
durdharṣam abhavad yuddhe naikaśastramayaṁ mahat ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
vimucya rāghavarathaṁ samantād vānare bale |
sāyakair antarikṣaṁ ca cakārāśu nirantaram |
mumoca ca daśagrīvo niḥsaṅgenāntarātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
vyāyacchamānaṁ taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā tatparaṁ rāvaṇaṁ raṇe |
prahasann iva kākutsthaḥ saṁdadhe sāyakāñ śitān ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
sa mumoca tato bāṇān raṇe śatasahasraśaḥ |
tān dr̥ṣṭvā rāvaṇaś cakre svaśaraiḥ khaṁ nirantaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tatas tābhyāṁ prayuktena śaravarṣeṇa bhāsvatā |
śarabaddham ivābhāti dvitīyaṁ bhāsvad ambaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
nānimitto ’bhavad bāṇo nātibhettā na niṣphalaḥ |
tathā visr̥jator bāṇān rāmarāvaṇayor mr̥dhe ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
prāyudhyetām avicchinnam asyantau savyadakṣiṇam |
cakratus tau śaraughais tu nirucchvāsam ivāmbaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
rāvaṇasya hayān rāmo hayān rāmasya rāvaṇaḥ |
jaghnatus tau tadānyonyaṁ kr̥tānukr̥takāriṇau ||

=== meaning ===

---
