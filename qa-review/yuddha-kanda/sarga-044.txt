=== Sarga 44 ===

---
1.
tad dr̥ṣṭvā sumahat karma kr̥taṁ vānarasattamaiḥ |
krodham āhārayām āsa yudhi tīvram akampanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
krodhamūrchitarūpas tu dhnuvan paramakārmukam |
dr̥ṣṭvā tu karma śatrūṇāṁ sārathiṁ vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tatraiva tāvat tvaritaṁ rathaṁ prāpaya sārathe |
ete ’tra bahavo ghnanti subahūn rākṣasān raṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
ete ’tra balavanto hi bhīmakāyāś ca vānarāḥ |
drumaśailapraharaṇās tiṣṭhanti pramukhe mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
etān nihantum icchāmi samaraślāghino hy aham |
etaiḥ pramathitaṁ sarvaṁ dr̥śyate rākṣasaṁ balam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tataḥ prajavitāśvena rathena rathināṁ varaḥ |
harīn abhyahanat krodhāc charajālair akampanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
na sthātuṁ vānarāḥ śekuḥ kiṁ punar yoddhum āhave |
akampanaśarair bhagnāḥ sarva eva pradudruvuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tān mr̥tyuvaśam āpannān akampanavaśaṁ gatān |
samīkṣya hanumāñ jñātīn upatasthe mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
taṁ mahāplavagaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā sarve plavagayūthapāḥ |
sametya samare vīrāḥ sahitāḥ paryavārayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
vyavasthitaṁ hanūmantaṁ te dr̥ṣṭvā hariyūthapāḥ |
babhūvur balavanto hi balavantam upāśritāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
akampanas tu śailābhaṁ hanūmantam avasthitam |
mahendra iva dhārābhiḥ śarair abhivavarṣa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
acintayitvā bāṇaughāñ śarīre patitāñ śitān |
akampanavadhārthāya mano dadhre mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sa prahasya mahātejā hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ |
abhidudrāva tad rakṣaḥ kampayann iva medinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tasyābhinardamānasya dīpyamānasya tejasā |
babhūva rūpaṁ durdharṣaṁ dīptasyeva vibhāvasoḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
ātmānaṁ tv apraharaṇaṁ jñātvā krodhasamanvitaḥ |
śailam utpāṭayām āsa vegena haripuṁgavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
taṁ gr̥hītvā mahāśailaṁ pāṇinaikena mārutiḥ |
vinadya sumahānādaṁ bhrāmayām āsa vīryavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tatas tam abhidudrāva rākṣasendram akampanam |
yathā hi namuciṁ saṁkhye vajreṇeva puraṁdaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
akampanas tu tad dr̥ṣṭvā giriśr̥ṅgaṁ samudyatam |
dūrād eva mahābāṇair ardhacandrair vyadārayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tat parvatāgram ākāśe rakṣobāṇavidāritam |
vikīrṇaṁ patitaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā hanūmān krodhamūrchitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
so ’śvakarṇaṁ samāsādya roṣadarpānvito hariḥ |
tūrṇam utpāṭayām āsa mahāgirim ivocchritam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
taṁ gr̥hītvā mahāskandhaṁ so ’śvakarṇaṁ mahādyutiḥ |
prahasya parayā prītyā bhrāmayām āsa saṁyuge ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
pradhāvann uruvegena prabhañjaṁs tarasā drumān |
hanūmān paramakruddhaś caraṇair dārayat kṣitim ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
gajāṁś ca sagajārohān sarathān rathinas tathā |
jaghāna hanumān dhīmān rākṣasāṁś ca padātikān ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tam antakam iva kruddhaṁ samare prāṇahāriṇam |
hanūmantam abhiprekṣya rākṣasā vipradudruvuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tam āpatantaṁ saṁkruddhaṁ rākṣasānāṁ bhayāvaham |
dadarśākampano vīraś cukrodha ca nanāda ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
sa caturdaśabhir bāṇaiḥ śitair dehavidāraṇaiḥ |
nirbibheda hanūmantaṁ mahāvīryam akampanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
sa tathā pratividdhas tu bahvībhiḥ śaravr̥ṣṭibhiḥ |
hanūmān dadr̥śe vīraḥ prarūḍha iva sānumān ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tato ’nyaṁ vr̥kṣam utpāṭya kr̥tvā vegam anuttamam |
śirasy abhijaghānāśu rākṣasendram akampanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
sa vr̥kṣeṇa hatas tena sakrodhena mahātmanā |
rākṣaso vānarendreṇa papāta sa mamāra ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā nihataṁ bhūmau rākṣasendram akampanam |
vyathitā rākṣasāḥ sarve kṣitikampa iva drumāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
tyaktapraharaṇāḥ sarve rākṣasās te parājitāḥ |
laṅkām abhiyayus trastā vānarais tair abhidrutāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
te muktakeśāḥ saṁbhrāntā bhagnamānāḥ parājitāḥ |
sravacchramajalair aṅgaiḥ śvasanto vipradudruvuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
anyonyaṁ pramamantus te viviśur nagaraṁ bhayāt |
pr̥ṣṭhatas te susaṁmūḍhāḥ prekṣamāṇā muhur muhuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
teṣu laṅkāṁ praviṣṭeṣu rākṣaseṣu mahābalāḥ |
sametya harayaḥ sarve hanūmantam apūjayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
so ’pi prahr̥ṣṭas tān sarvān harīn saṁpratyapūjayat |
hanūmān sattvasaṁpanno yathārham anukūlataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
vineduś ca yathā prāṇaṁ harayo jitakāśinaḥ |
cakarṣuś ca punas tatra saprāṇān eva rākṣasān ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
sa vīraśobhām abhajan mahākapiḥ; sametya rakṣāṁsi nihatya mārutiḥ |
mahāsuraṁ bhīmam amitranāśanaṁ; yathaiva viṣṇur balinaṁ camūmukhe ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
apūjayan devagaṇās tadā kapiṁ; svayaṁ ca rāmo ’tibalaś ca lakṣmaṇaḥ |
tathaiva sugrīvamukhāḥ plavaṁgamā; vibhīṣaṇaś caiva mahābalas tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
