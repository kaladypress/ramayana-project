=== Sarga 67 ===

---
1.
makarākṣaṁ hataṁ śrutvā rāvaṇaḥ samitiṁjayaḥ |
ādideśātha saṁkruddho raṇāyendrajitaṁ sutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
jahi vīra mahāvīryau bhrātarau rāmalakṣmaṇau |
adr̥śyo dr̥śyamāno vā sarvathā tvaṁ balādhikaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tvam apratimakarmāṇam indraṁ jayasi saṁyuge |
kiṁ punar mānuṣau dr̥ṣṭvā na vadhiṣyasi saṁyuge ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tathokto rākṣasendreṇa pratigr̥hya pitur vacaḥ |
yajñabhūmau sa vidhivat pāvakaṁ juhuve ndrajit ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
juhvataś cāpi tatrāgniṁ raktoṣṇīṣadharāḥ striyaḥ |
ājagmus tatra saṁbhrāntā rākṣasyo yatra rāvaṇiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
śastrāṇi śarapatrāṇi samidho ’tha vibhītakāḥ |
lohitāni ca vāsāṁsi sruvaṁ kārṣṇāyasaṁ tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sarvato ’gniṁ samāstīrya śarapatraiḥ samantataḥ |
chāgasya sarvakr̥ṣṇasya galaṁ jagrāha jīvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
caruhomasamiddhasya vidhūmasya mahārciṣaḥ |
babhūvus tāni liṅgāni vijayaṁ darśayanti ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
pradakṣiṇāvartaśikhas taptahāṭakasaṁnibhaḥ |
havis tat pratijagrāha pāvakaḥ svayam utthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
hutvāgniṁ tarpayitvātha devadānavarākṣasān |
āruroha rathaśreṣṭham antardhānagataṁ śubham ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sa vājibhiś caturbhis tu bāṇaiś ca niśitair yutaḥ |
āropitamahācāpaḥ śuśubhe syandanottame ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
jājvalyamāno vapuṣā tapanīyaparicchadaḥ |
śaraiś candrārdhacandraiś ca sa rathaḥ samalaṁkr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
jāmbūnadamahākambur dīptapāvakasaṁnibhaḥ |
babhūvendrajitaḥ ketur vaidūryasamalaṁkr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tena cādityakalpena brahmāstreṇa ca pālitaḥ |
sa babhūva durādharṣo rāvaṇiḥ sumahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
so ’bhiniryāya nagarād indrajit samitiṁjayaḥ |
hutvāgniṁ rākṣasair mantrair antardhānagato ’bravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
adya hatvāhave yau tau mithyā pravrajitau vane |
jayaṁ pitre pradāsyāmi rāvaṇāya raṇādhikam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
kr̥tvā nirvānarām urvīṁ hatvā rāmaṁ salakṣmaṇam |
kariṣye paramāṁ prītim ity uktvāntaradhīyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
āpapātātha saṁkruddho daśagrīveṇa coditaḥ |
tīkṣṇakārmukanārācais tīkṣṇas tv indraripū raṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
sa dadarśa mahāvīryau nāgau triśirasāv iva |
sr̥jantāv iṣujālāni vīrau vānaramadhyagau ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
imau tāv iti saṁcintya sajyaṁ kr̥tvā ca kārmukam |
saṁtatāneṣudhārābhiḥ parjanya iva vr̥ṣṭimān ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
sa tu vaihāyasaṁ prāpya saratho rāmalakṣmaṇau |
acakṣur viṣaye tiṣṭhan vivyādha niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tau tasya śaravegena parītau rāmalakṣmaṇau |
dhanuṣī saśare kr̥tvā divyam astraṁ pracakratuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
pracchādayantau gaganaṁ śarajālair mahābalau |
tam astraiḥ surasaṁkāśau naiva pasparśatuḥ śaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
sa hi dhūmāndhakāraṁ ca cakre pracchādayan nabhaḥ |
diśaś cāntardadhe śrīmān nīhāratamasāvr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
naiva jyātalanirghoṣo na ca nemikhurasvanaḥ |
śuśruve caratas tasya na ca rūpaṁ prakāśate ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
ghanāndhakāre timire śaravarṣam ivādbhutam |
sa vavarṣa mahābāhur nārācaśaravr̥ṣṭibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
sa rāmaṁ sūryasaṁkāśaiḥ śarair dattavaro bhr̥śam |
vivyādha samare kruddhaḥ sarvagātreṣu rāvaṇiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tau hanyamānau nārācair dhārābhir iva parvatau |
hemapuṅkhān naravyāghrau tigmān mumucatuḥ śarān ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
antarikṣaṁ samāsādya rāvaṇiṁ kaṅkapatriṇaḥ |
nikr̥tya patagā bhūmau petus te śoṇitokṣitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
atimātraṁ śaraugheṇa pīḍyamānau narottamau |
tān iṣūn patato bhallair anekair nicakartatuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
yato hi dadr̥śāte tau śarān nipatitāñ śitān |
tatas tato dāśarathī sasr̥jāte ’stram uttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
rāvaṇis tu diśaḥ sarvā rathenātirathaḥ patan |
vivyādha tau dāśarathī laghv astro niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
tenātividdhau tau vīrau rukmapuṅkhaiḥ susaṁhataiḥ |
babhūvatur dāśarathī puṣpitāv iva kiṁśukau ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
nāsya veda gatiṁ kaś cin na ca rūpaṁ dhanuḥ śarān |
na cānyad viditaṁ kiṁ cit sūryasyevābhrasaṁplave ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
tena viddhāś ca harayo nihatāś ca gatāsavaḥ |
babhūvuḥ śataśas tatra patitā dharaṇītale ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
lakṣmaṇas tu susaṁkruddho bhrātaraṁ vākyam abravīt |
brāhmam astraṁ prayokṣyāmi vadhārthaṁ sarvarakṣasām ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
tam uvāca tato rāmo lakṣmaṇaṁ śubhalakṣaṇam |
naikasya heto rakṣāṁsi pr̥thivyāṁ hantum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
ayudhyamānaṁ pracchannaṁ prāñjaliṁ śaraṇāgatam |
palāyantaṁ pramattaṁ vā na tvaṁ hantum ihārhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
asyaiva tu vadhe yatnaṁ kariṣyāvo mahābala |
ādekṣyāvo mahāvegān astrān āśīviṣopamān ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
tam enaṁ māyinaṁ kṣudram antarhitarathaṁ balāt |
rākṣasaṁ nihaniṣyanti dr̥ṣṭvā vānarayūthapāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
yady eṣa bhūmiṁ viśate divaṁ vā; rasātalaṁ vāpi nabhastalaṁ vā |
evaṁ nigūḍho ’pi mamāstradagdhaḥ; patiṣyate bhūmitale gatāsuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
ity evam uktvā vacanaṁ mahātmā; raghupravīraḥ plavagarṣabhair vr̥taḥ |
vadhāya raudrasya nr̥śaṁsakarmaṇas; tadā mahātmā tvaritaṁ nirīkṣate ||

=== meaning ===

---
