=== Sarga 38 ===

---
1.
bhartāraṁ nihataṁ dr̥ṣṭvā lakṣmaṇaṁ ca mahābalam |
vilalāpa bhr̥śaṁ sītā karuṇaṁ śokakarśitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
ūcur lakṣaṇikā ye māṁ putriṇy avidhaveti ca |
te ’sya sarve hate rāme ’jñānino ’nr̥tavādinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
yajvano mahiṣīṁ ye mām ūcuḥ patnīṁ ca satriṇaḥ |
te ’dya sarve hate rāme ’jñānino ’nr̥tavādinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
vīrapārthivapatnī tvaṁ ye dhanyeti ca māṁ viduḥ |
te ’dya sarve hate rāme ’jñānino ’nr̥tavādinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
ūcuḥ saṁśravaṇe ye māṁ dvijāḥ kārtāntikāḥ śubhām |
te ’dya sarve hate rāme ’jñānino ’nr̥tavādinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
imāni khalu padmāni pādayor yaiḥ kila striyaḥ |
adhirājye ’bhiṣicyante narendraiḥ patibhiḥ saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
vaidhavyaṁ yānti yair nāryo ’lakṣaṇair bhāgyadurlabhāḥ |
nātmanas tāni paśyāmi paśyantī hatalakṣaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
satyānīmāni padmāni strīṇām uktvāni lakṣaṇe |
tāny adya nihate rāme vitathāni bhavanti me ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
keśāḥ sūkṣmāḥ samā nīlā bhruvau cāsaṁgate mama |
vr̥tte cālomaśe jaṅghe dantāś cāviralā mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
śaṅkhe netre karau pādau gulphāv ūrū ca me citau |
anuvr̥ttā nakhāḥ snigdhāḥ samāś cāṅgulayo mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
stanau cāviralau pīnau mamemau magnacūcukau |
magnā cotsaṅginī nābhiḥ pārśvoraskaṁ ca me citam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
mama varṇo maṇinibho mr̥dūny aṅgaruhāṇi ca |
pratiṣṭhitāṁ dvadaśabhir mām ūcuḥ śubhalakṣaṇām ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
samagrayavam acchidraṁ pāṇipādaṁ ca varṇavat |
mandasmitety eva ca māṁ kanyālakṣaṇikā viduḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
adhirājye ’bhiṣeko me brāhmaṇaiḥ patinā saha |
kr̥tāntakuśalair uktaṁ tat sarvaṁ vitathīkr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
śodhayitvā janasthānaṁ pravr̥ttim upalabhya ca |
tīrtvā sāgaram akṣobhyaṁ bhrātarau goṣpade hatau ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
nanu vāruṇam āgneyam aindraṁ vāyavyam eva ca |
astraṁ brahmaśiraś caiva rāghavau pratyapadyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
adr̥śyamānena raṇe māyayā vāsavopamau |
mama nāthāv anāthāyā nihatau rāmalakṣmaṇau ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
na hi dr̥ṣṭipathaṁ prāpya rāghavasya raṇe ripuḥ |
jīvan pratinivarteta yady api syān manojavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
na kālasyātibhāro ’sti kr̥tāntaś ca sudurjayaḥ |
yatra rāmaḥ saha bhrātrā śete yudhi nipāthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
nāhaṁ śocāmi bhartāraṁ nihataṁ na ca lakṣmaṇam |
nātmānaṁ jananī cāpi yathā śvaśrūṁ tapasvinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
sā hi cintayate nityaṁ samāptavratam āgatam |
kadā drakṣyāmi sītāṁ ca rāmaṁ ca sahalakṣmaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
paridevayamānāṁ tāṁ rākṣasī trijaṭābravīt |
mā viṣādaṁ kr̥thā devi bhartāyaṁ tava jīvati ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
kāraṇāni ca vakṣyāmi mahānti sadr̥śāni ca |
yathemau jīvato devi bhrātarau rāmalakṣmaṇau ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
na hi kopaparītāni harṣaparyutsukāni ca |
bhavanti yudhi yodhānāṁ mukhāni nihate patau ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
idaṁ vimānaṁ vaidehi puṣpakaṁ nāma nāmataḥ |
divyaṁ tvāṁ dhārayen nedaṁ yady etau gajajīvitau ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
hatavīrapradhānā hi hatotsāhā nirudyamā |
senā bhramati saṁkhyeṣu hatakarṇeva naur jale ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
iyaṁ punar asaṁbhrāntā nirudvignā tarasvinī |
senā rakṣati kākutsthau māyayā nirjitau raṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
sā tvaṁ bhava suvisrabdhā anumānaiḥ sukhodayaiḥ |
ahatau paśya kākutsthau snehād etad bravīmi te ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
anr̥taṁ noktapūrvaṁ me na ca vakṣye kadā cana |
cāritrasukhaśīlatvāt praviṣṭāsi mano mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
nemau śakyau raṇe jetuṁ sendrair api surāsuraiḥ |
etayor ānanaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā mayā cāveditaṁ tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
idaṁ ca sumahac cihnaṁ śanaiḥ paśyasva maithili |
niḥsaṁjñāv apy ubhāv etau naiva lakṣmīr viyujyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
prāyeṇa gatasattvānāṁ puruṣāṇāṁ gatāyuṣām |
dr̥śyamāneṣu vaktreṣu paraṁ bhavati vaikr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
tyaja śokaṁ ca duḥkhaṁ ca mohaṁ ca janakātmaje |
rāmalakṣmaṇayor arthe nādya śakyam ajīvitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
śrutvā tu vacanaṁ tasyāḥ sītā surasutopamā |
kr̥tāñjalir uvācedam evam astv iti maithilī ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
vimānaṁ puṣpakaṁ tat tu samivartya manojavam |
dīnā trijaṭayā sītā laṅkām eva praveśitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
tatas trijaṭayā sārdhaṁ puṣpakād avaruhya sā |
aśokavanikām eva rakṣasībhiḥ praveśitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
praviśya sītā bahuvr̥kṣaṣaṇḍāṁ; tāṁ rākṣasendrasya vihārabhūmim |
saṁprekṣya saṁcintya ca rājaputrau; paraṁ viṣādaṁ samupājagāma ||

=== meaning ===

---
