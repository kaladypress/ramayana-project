=== Sarga 53 ===

---
1.
sa tathoktas tu nirbhartsya kumbhakarṇo mahodaram |
abravīd rākṣasaśreṣṭhaṁ bhrātaraṁ rāvaṇaṁ tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
so ’haṁ tava bhayaṁ ghoraṁ vadhāt tasya durātmanaḥ |
rāmasyādya pramārjāmi nirvairas tvaṁ sukhībhava ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
garjanti na vr̥thā śūra nirjalā iva toyadāḥ |
paśya saṁpādyamānaṁ tu garjitaṁ yudhi karmaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
na marṣayati cātmānaṁ saṁbhāvayati nātmanā |
adarśayitvā śūrās tu karma kurvanti duṣkaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
viklavānām abuddhīnāṁ rājñāṁ paṇḍitamāninām |
śr̥ṇvatām ādita idaṁ tvadvidhānāṁ mahodara ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
yuddhe kāpuruṣair nityaṁ bhavadbhiḥ priyavādibhiḥ |
rājānam anugacchadbhiḥ kr̥tyam etad vināśitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
rājaśeṣā kr̥tā laṅkā kṣīṇaḥ kośo balaṁ hatam |
rājānam imam āsādya suhr̥ccihnam amitrakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
eṣa niryāmy ahaṁ yuddham udyataḥ śatrunirjaye |
durnayaṁ bhavatām adya samīkartuṁ mahāhave ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
evam uktavato vākyaṁ kumbhakarṇasya dhīmataḥ |
pratyuvāca tato vākyaṁ prahasan rākṣasādhipaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
mahodaro ’yaṁ rāmāt tu paritrasto na saṁśayaḥ |
na hi rocayate tāta yuddhaṁ yuddhaviśārada ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
kaś cin me tvatsamo nāsti sauhr̥dena balena ca |
gaccha śatruvadhāya tvaṁ kumbhakarṇajayāya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
ādade niśitaṁ śūlaṁ vegāc chatrunibarhaṇaḥ |
sarvakālāyasaṁ dīptaṁ taptakāñcanabhūṣaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
indrāśanisamaṁ bhīmaṁ vajrapratimagauravam |
devadānavagandharvayakṣakiṁnarasūdanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
raktamālya mahādāma svataś codgatapāvakam |
ādāya niśitaṁ śūlaṁ śatruśoṇitarañjitam |
kumbhakarṇo mahātejā rāvaṇaṁ vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
gamiṣyāmy aham ekākī tiṣṭhatv iha balaṁ mahat |
adya tān kṣudhitaḥ kruddho bhakṣayiṣyāmi vānarān ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
kumbhakarṇavacaḥ śrutvā rāvaṇo vākyam abravīt |
sainyaiḥ parivr̥to gaccha śūlamudgalapāṇibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
vānarā hi mahātmānaḥ śīghrāś ca vyavasāyinaḥ |
ekākinaṁ pramattaṁ vā nayeyur daśanaiḥ kṣayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tasmāt paramadurdharṣaiḥ sainyaiḥ parivr̥to vraja |
rakṣasām ahitaṁ sarvaṁ śatrupakṣaṁ nisūdaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
athāsanāt samutpatya srajaṁ maṇikr̥tāntarām |
ābabandha mahātejāḥ kumbhakarṇasya rāvaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
aṅgadān aṅgulīveṣṭān varāṇy ābharaṇāni ca |
hāraṁ ca śaśisaṁkāśam ābabandha mahātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
divyāni ca sugandhīni mālyadāmāni rāvaṇaḥ |
śrotre cāsajjayām āsa śrīmatī cāsya kuṇḍale ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
kāñcanāṅgadakeyūro niṣkābharaṇabhūṣitaḥ |
kumbhakarṇo br̥hatkarṇaḥ suhuto ’gnir ivābabhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
śroṇīsūtreṇa mahatā mecakena virājitaḥ |
amr̥totpādane naddho bhujaṁgeneva mandaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
sa kāñcanaṁ bhārasahaṁ nivātaṁ; vidyutprabhaṁ dīptam ivātmabhāsā |
ābadhyamānaḥ kavacaṁ rarāja; saṁdhyābhrasaṁvīta ivādrirājaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
sarvābharaṇanaddhāṅgaḥ śūlapāṇiḥ sa rākṣasaḥ |
trivikramakr̥totsāho nārāyaṇa ivābabhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
bhrātaraṁ saṁpariṣvajya kr̥tvā cāpi pradakṣiṇam |
praṇamya śirasā tasmai saṁpratasthe mahābaliḥ |
tam āśīrbhiḥ praśastābhiḥ preṣayām āsa rāvaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
śaṅkhadundubhinirghoṣaiḥ sainyaiś cāpi varāyudhaiḥ |
taṁ gajaiś ca turaṁgaiś ca syandanaiś cāmbudasvanaiḥ |
anujagmur mahātmānaṁ rathino rathināṁ varam ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
sarpair uṣṭraiḥ kharair aśvaiḥ siṁhadvipamr̥gadvijaiḥ |
anujagmuś ca taṁ ghoraṁ kumbhakarṇaṁ mahābalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
sa puṣpavarṇair avakīryamāṇo; dhr̥tātapatraḥ śitaśūlapāṇiḥ |
madotkaṭaḥ śoṇitagandhamatto; viniryayau dānavadevaśatruḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
padātayaś a bahavo mahānādā mahābalāḥ |
anvayū rākṣasā bhīmā bhīmākṣāḥ śastrapāṇayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
raktākṣāḥ sumahākāyā nīlāñjanacayopamāḥ |
śūrān udyamya khaḍgāṁś ca niśitāṁś ca paraśvadhān ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
bahuvyāmāṁś ca vipulān kṣepaṇīyān durāsadān |
tālaskandhāṁś ca vipulān kṣepaṇīyān durāsadān ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
athānyad vapur ādāya dāruṇaṁ lomaharṣaṇam |
niṣpapāta mahātejāḥ kumbhakarṇo mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
dhanuḥśataparīṇāhaḥ sa ṣaṭśatasamucchitaḥ |
raudraḥ śakaṭacakrākṣo mahāparvatasaṁnibhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
saṁnipatya ca rakṣāṁsi dagdhaśailopamo mahān |
kumbhakarṇo mahāvaktraḥ prahasann idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
adya vānaramukhyānāṁ tāni yūthāni bhāgaśaḥ |
nirdahiṣyāmi saṁkruddhaḥ śalabhān iva pāvakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
nāparādhyanti me kāmaṁ vānarā vanacāriṇaḥ |
jātir asmadvidhānāṁ sā purodyānavibhūṣaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
purarodhasya mūlaṁ tu rāghavaḥ sahalakṣmaṇaḥ |
hate tasmin hataṁ sarvaṁ taṁ vadhiṣyāmi saṁyuge ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
evaṁ tasya bruvāṇasya kumbhakarṇasya rākṣasāḥ |
nādaṁ cakrur mahāghoraṁ kampayanta ivārṇavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
tasya niṣpatatas tūrṇaṁ kumbhakarṇasya dhīmataḥ |
babhūvur ghorarūpāṇi nimittāni samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
ulkāśaniyutā meghā vineduś ca sudāruṇāḥ |
sasāgaravanā caiva vasudhā samakampata ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
ghorarūpāḥ śivā neduḥ sajvālakavalair mukhaiḥ |
maṇḍalāny apasavyāni babandhuś ca vihaṁgamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
niṣpapāta ca gr̥dhre ’sya śūle vai pathi gacchataḥ |
prāsphuran nayanaṁ cāsya savyo bāhur akampata ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
niṣpapāta tadā coklā jvalantī bhīmanisvanā |
ādityo niṣprabhaś cāsīn na pravāti sukho ’nilaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
acintayan mahotpātān utthitām̐l lomaharṣaṇān |
niryayau kumbhakarṇas tu kr̥tāntabalacoditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
sa laṅghayitvā prākāraṁ padbhyāṁ parvatasaṁnibhaḥ |
dadarśābhraghanaprakhyaṁ vānarānīkam adbhutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
te dr̥ṣṭvā rākṣasaśreṣṭhaṁ vānarāḥ parvatopamam |
vāyununnā iva ghanā yayuḥ sarvā diśas tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
tad vānarānīkam atipracaṇḍaṁ; diśo dravad bhinnam ivābhrajālam |
sa kumbhakarṇaḥ samavekṣya harṣān; nanāda bhūyo ghanavad ghanābhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
te tasya ghoraṁ ninadaṁ niśamya; yathā ninādaṁ divi vāridasya |
petur dharaṇyāṁ bahavaḥ plavaṁgā; nikr̥ttamūlā iva sālavr̥kṣāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
vipulaparighavān sa kumbhakarṇo; ripunidhanāya viniḥsr̥to mahātmā |
kapi gaṇabhayam ādadat subhīmaṁ; prabhur iva kiṁkaradaṇḍavān yugānte ||

=== meaning ===

---
