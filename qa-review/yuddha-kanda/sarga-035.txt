=== Sarga 35 ===

---
1.
sa tasya gatim anvicchan rājaputraḥ pratāpavān |
dideśātibalo rāmo daśavānarayūthapān ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
dvau suṣeṇasya dāyādau nīlaṁ ca plavagarṣabham |
aṅgadaṁ vāliputraṁ ca śarabhaṁ ca tarasvinam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
vinataṁ jāmbavantaṁ ca sānuprasthaṁ mahābalam |
r̥ṣabhaṁ carṣabhaskandham ādideśa paraṁtapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
te saṁprahr̥ṣṭā harayo bhīmān udyamya pādapān |
ākāśaṁ viviśuḥ sarve mārgāmāṇā diśo daśa ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
teṣāṁ vegavatāṁ vegam iṣubhir vegavattaraiḥ |
astravit paramāstreṇa vārayām āsa rāvaṇiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
taṁ bhīmavegā harayo nārācaiḥ kṣatavikṣatāḥ |
andhakāre na dadr̥śur meghaiḥ sūryam ivāvr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
rāmalakṣmaṇayor eva sarvamarmabhidaḥ śarān |
bhr̥śam āveśayām āsa rāvaṇiḥ samitiṁjayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
nirantaraśarīrau tu bhrātarau rāmalakṣmaṇau |
kruddhenendrajitā vīrau pannagaiḥ śaratāṁ gataiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tayoḥ kṣatajamārgeṇa susrāva rudhiraṁ bahu |
tāv ubhau ca prakāśete puṣpitāv iva kiṁśukau ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tataḥ paryantaraktākṣo bhinnāñjanacayopamaḥ |
rāvaṇir bhrātarau vākyam antardhānagato ’bravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
yudhyamānam anālakṣyaṁ śakro ’pi tridaśeśvaraḥ |
draṣṭum āsādituṁ vāpi na śaktaḥ kiṁ punar yuvām ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
prāvr̥tāv iṣujālena rāghavau kaṅkapatriṇā |
eṣa roṣaparītātmā nayāmi yamasādanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
evam uktvā tu dharmajñau bhrātarau rāmalakṣmaṇau |
nirbibheda śitair bāṇaiḥ prajaharṣa nanāda ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
bhinnāñjanacayaśyāmo visphārya vipulaṁ dhanuḥ |
bhūyo bhūyaḥ śarān ghorān visasarja mahāmr̥dhe ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tato marmasu marmajño majjayan niśitāñ śarān |
rāmalakṣmaṇayor vīro nanāda ca muhur muhuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
baddhau tu śarabandhena tāv ubhau raṇamūrdhani |
nimeṣāntaramātreṇa na śekatur udīkṣitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tato vibhinnasarvāṅgau śaraśalyācitāv ubhau |
dhvajāv iva mahendrasya rajjumuktau prakampitau ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tau saṁpracalitau vīrau marmabhedena karśitau |
nipetatur maheṣvāsau jagatyāṁ jagatīpatī ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tau vīraśayane vīrau śayānau rudhirokṣitau |
śaraveṣṭitasarvāṅgāv ārtau paramapīḍitau ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
na hy aviddhaṁ tayor gātraṁ babhūvāṅgulam antaram |
nānirbhinnaṁ na cāstabdham ā karāgrād ajihmagaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tau tu krūreṇa nihatau rakṣasā kāmarūpiṇā |
asr̥ksusruvatus tīvraṁ jalaṁ prasravaṇāv iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
papāta prathamaṁ rāmo viddho marmasu mārgaṇaiḥ |
krodhād indrajitā yena purā śakro vinirjitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
nāracair ardhanārācair bhallair añjalikair api |
vivyādha vatsadantaiś ca siṁhadaṁṣṭraiḥ kṣurais tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
sa vīraśayane śiśye vijyam ādāya kārmukam |
bhinnamuṣṭiparīṇāhaṁ triṇataṁ rukmabhūṣitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
bāṇapātāntare rāmaṁ patitaṁ puruṣarṣabham |
sa tatra lakṣmaṇo dr̥ṣṭvā nirāśo jīvite ’bhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
baddhau tu vīrau patitau śayānau; tau vānarāḥ saṁparivārya tasthuḥ |
samāgatā vāyusutapramukhyā; viṣadam ārtāḥ paramaṁ ca jagmuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
