=== Sarga 14 ===

---
1.
tasya rāmasya suptasya kuśāstīrṇe mahītale |
niyamād apramattasya niśās tisro ’ticakramuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
na ca darśayate mandas tadā rāmasya sāgaraḥ |
prayatenāpi rāmeṇa yathārham abhipūjitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
samudrasya tataḥ kruddho rāmo raktāntalocanaḥ |
samīpastham uvācedaṁ lakṣmaṇaṁ śubhalakṣmaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
paśya tāvad anāryasya pūjyamānasya lakṣmaṇa |
avalepaṁ samudrasya na darśayati yat svayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
praśamaś ca kṣamā caiva ārjavaṁ priyavāditā |
asāmarthyaṁ phalanty ete nirguṇeṣu satāṁ guṇāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
ātmapraśaṁsinaṁ duṣṭaṁ dhr̥ṣṭaṁ viparidhāvakam |
sarvatrotsr̥ṣṭadaṇḍaṁ ca lokaḥ satkurute naram ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
na sāmnā śakyate kīrtir na sāmnā śakyate yaśaḥ |
prāptuṁ lakṣmaṇa loke ’smiñ jayo vā raṇamūdhani ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
adya madbāṇanirbhinnair makarair makarālayam |
niruddhatoyaṁ saumitre plavadbhiḥ paśya sarvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
mahābhogāni matsyānāṁ kariṇāṁ ca karān iha |
bhogāṁś ca paśya nāgānāṁ mayā bhinnāni lakṣmaṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
saśaṅkhaśuktikā jālaṁ samīnamakaraṁ śaraiḥ |
adya yuddhena mahatā samudraṁ pariśoṣaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
kṣamayā hi samāyuktaṁ mām ayaṁ makarālayaḥ |
asamarthaṁ vijānāti dhik kṣamām īdr̥śe jane ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
cāpam ānaya saumitre śarāṁś cāśīviṣopamān |
adyākṣobhyam api kruddhaḥ kṣobhayiṣyāmi sāgaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
velāsu kr̥tamaryādaṁ sahasormisamākulam |
nirmaryādaṁ kariṣyāmi sāyakair varuṇālayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
evam uktvā dhanuṣpāṇiḥ krodhavisphāritekṣaṇaḥ |
babhūva rāmo durdharṣo yugāntāgnir iva jvalan ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
saṁpīḍya ca dhanur ghoraṁ kampayitvā śarair jagat |
mumoca viśikhān ugrān vajrāṇīva śatakratuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
te jvalanto mahāvegās tejasā sāyakottamāḥ |
praviśanti samudrasya salilaṁ trastapannagam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tato vegaḥ samudrasya sanakramakaro mahān |
saṁbabhūva mahāghoraḥ samārutaravas tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
mahormimālāvitataḥ śaṅkhaśuktisamākulaḥ |
sadhūmaparivr̥ttormiḥ sahasābhūn mahodadhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
vyathitāḥ pannagāś cāsan dīptāsyā dīptalocanāḥ |
dānavāś ca mahāvīryāḥ pātālatalavāsinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
ūrmayaḥ sindhurājasya sanakramakarās tadā |
vindhyamandarasaṁkāśāḥ samutpetuḥ sahasraśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
āghūrṇitataraṅgaughaḥ saṁbhrāntoragarākṣasaḥ |
udvartita mahāgrāhaḥ saṁvr̥ttaḥ salilāśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
