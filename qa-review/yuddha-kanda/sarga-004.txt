=== Sarga 4 ===

---
1.
śrutvā hanūmato vākyaṁ yathāvad anupūrvaśaḥ |
tato ’bravīn mahātejā rāmaḥ satyaparākramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
yāṁ nivedayase laṅkāṁ purīṁ bhīmasya rakṣasaḥ |
kṣipram enāṁ vadhiṣyāmi satyam etad bravīmi te ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
asmin muhūrte sugrīva prayāṇam abhirocaye |
yukto muhūrto vijayaḥ prāpto madhyaṁ divākaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
uttarā phalgunī hy adya śvas tu hastena yokṣyate |
abhiprayāma sugrīva sarvānīkasamāvr̥tāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
nimittāni ca dhanyāni yāni prādurbhavanti me |
nihatya rāvaṇaṁ sītām ānayiṣyāmi jānakīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
upariṣṭād dhi nayanaṁ sphuramāṇam idaṁ mama |
vijayaṁ samanuprāptaṁ śaṁsatīva manoratham ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
agre yātu balasyāsya nīlo mārgam avekṣitum |
vr̥taḥ śatasahasreṇa vānarāṇāṁ tarasvinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
phalamūlavatā nīla śītakānanavāriṇā |
pathā madhumatā cāśu senāṁ senāpate naya ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
dūṣayeyur durātmānaḥ pathi mūlaphalodakam |
rākṣasāḥ parirakṣethās tebhyas tvaṁ nityam udyataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
nimneṣu vanadurgeṣu vaneṣu ca vanaukasaḥ |
abhiplutyābhipaśyeyuḥ pareṣāṁ nihataṁ balam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sāgaraughanibhaṁ bhīmam agrānīkaṁ mahābalāḥ |
kapisiṁhā prakarṣantu śataśo ’tha sahasraśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
gajaś ca girisaṁkāśo gavayaś ca mahābalaḥ |
gavākṣaś cāgrato yāntu gavāṁ dr̥ptā ivarṣabhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
yātu vānaravāhinyā vānaraḥ plavatāṁ patiḥ |
pālayan dakṣiṇaṁ pārśvam r̥ṣabho vānararṣabhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
gandhahastīva durdharṣas tarasvī gandhamādanaḥ |
yātu vānaravāhinyāḥ savyaṁ pārśvam adhiṣṭhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
yāsyāmi balamadhye ’haṁ balaugham abhiharṣayan |
adhiruhya hanūmantam airāvatam iveśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
aṅgadenaiṣa saṁyātu lakṣmaṇaś cāntakopamaḥ |
sārvabhaumeṇa bhūteśo draviṇādhipatir yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
jāmbavāṁś ca suṣeṇaś ca vegadarśī ca vānaraḥ |
r̥kṣarājo mahāsattvaḥ kukṣiṁ rakṣantu te trayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
rāghavasya vacaḥ śrutvā sugrīvo vāhinīpatiḥ |
vyādideśa mahāvīryān vānarān vānararṣabhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
te vānaragaṇāḥ sarve samutpatya yuyutsavaḥ |
guhābhyaḥ śikharebhyaś ca āśu pupluvire tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tato vānararājena lakṣmaṇena ca pūjitaḥ |
jagāma rāmo dharmātmā sasainyo dakṣiṇāṁ diśam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
śataiḥ śatasahasraiś ca koṭībhir ayutair api |
vāraṇābhiś ca haribhir yayau parivr̥tas tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
taṁ yāntam anuyāti sma mahatī harivāhinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
hr̥ṣṭāḥ pramuditāḥ sarve sugrīveṇābhipālitāḥ |
āplavantaḥ plavantaś ca garjantaś ca plavaṁgamāḥ |
kṣvelanto ninadantaś ca jagmur vai dakṣiṇāṁ diśam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
bhakṣayantaḥ sugandhīni madhūni ca phalāni ca |
udvahanto mahāvr̥kṣān mañjarīpuñjadhāriṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
anyonyaṁ sahasā dr̥ṣṭā nirvahanti kṣipanti ca |
patantaś cotpatanty anye pātayanty apare parān ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
rāvaṇo no nihantavyaḥ sarve ca rajanīcarāḥ |
iti garjanti harayo rāghavasya samīpataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
purastād r̥ṣabho vīro nīlaḥ kumuda eva ca |
pathānaṁ śodhayanti sma vānarair bahubhiḥ saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
madhye tu rājā sugrīvo rāmo lakṣmaṇa eva ca |
bahubhir balibhir bhīmair vr̥tāḥ śatrunibarhaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
hariḥ śatabalir vīraḥ koṭībhir daśabhir vr̥taḥ |
sarvām eko hy avaṣṭabhya rarakṣa harivāhinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
koṭīśataparīvāraḥ kesarī panaso gajaḥ |
arkaś cātibalaḥ pārśvam ekaṁ tasyābhirakṣati ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
suṣeṇo jāmbavāṁś caiva r̥kṣair bahubhir āvr̥taḥ |
sugrīvaṁ purataḥ kr̥tvā jaghanaṁ saṁrarakṣatuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
teṣāṁ senāpatir vīro nīlo vānarapuṁgavaḥ |
saṁpatan patatāṁ śreṣṭhas tad balaṁ paryapālayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
darīmikhaḥ prajaṅghaś ca jambho ’tha rabhasaḥ kapiḥ |
sarvataś ca yayur vīrās tvarayantaḥ plavaṁgamān ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
evaṁ te hariśārdūlā gacchanto baladarpitāḥ |
apaśyaṁs te giriśreṣṭhaṁ sahyaṁ drumalatāyutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
sāgaraughanibhaṁ bhīmaṁ tad vānarabalaṁ mahat |
niḥsasarpa mahāghoṣaṁ bhīmavega ivārṇavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
tasya dāśaratheḥ pārśve śūrās te kapikuñjarāḥ |
tūrṇam āpupluvuḥ sarve sadaśvā iva coditāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
kapibhyām uhyamānau tau śuśubhate nararṣabhau |
mahadbhyām iva saṁspr̥ṣṭau grāhābhyāṁ candrabhāskarau ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
tam aṅgadagato rāmaṁ lakṣmaṇaḥ śubhayā girā |
uvāca pratipūrṇārthaḥ smr̥timān pratibhānavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
hr̥tām avāpya vaidehīṁ kṣipraṁ hatvā ca rāvaṇam |
samr̥ddhārthaḥ samr̥ddhārthām ayodhyāṁ pratiyāsyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
mahānti ca nimittāni divi bhūmau ca rāghava |
śubhānti tava paśyāmi sarvāṇy evārthasiddhaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
anu vāti śubho vāyuḥ senāṁ mr̥duhitaḥ sukhaḥ |
pūrṇavalgusvarāś ceme pravadanti mr̥gadvijāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
prasannāś ca diśaḥ sarvā vimalaś ca divākaraḥ |
uśanā ca prasannārcir anu tvāṁ bhārgavo gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
brahmarāśir viśuddhaś ca śuddhāś ca paramarṣayaḥ |
arciṣmantaḥ prakāśante dhruvaṁ sarve pradakṣiṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
triśaṅkur vimalo bhāti rājarṣiḥ sapurohitaḥ |
pitāmahavaro ’smākam iṣkvākūṇāṁ mahātmanām ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
vimale ca prakāśete viśākhe nirupadrave |
nakṣatraṁ param asmākam ikṣvākūṇāṁ mahātmanām ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
nairr̥taṁ nairr̥tānāṁ ca nakṣatram abhipīḍyate |
mūlaṁ mūlavatā spr̥ṣṭaṁ dhūpyate dhūmaketunā ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
saraṁ caitad vināśāya rākṣasānām upasthitam |
kāle kālagr̥hītānāṁ nakatraṁ grahapīḍitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
prasannāḥ surasāś cāpo vanāni phalavanti ca |
pravānty abhyadhikaṁ gandhā yathartukusumā drumāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
vyūḍhāni kapisainyāni prakāśante ’dhikaṁ prabho |
devānām iva sainyāni saṁgrāme tārakāmaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
evam ārya samīkṣyaitān prīto bhavitum arhasi |
iti bhrātaram āśvāsya hr̥ṣṭaḥ saumitrir abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
athāvr̥tya mahīṁ kr̥tsnāṁ jagāma mahatī camūḥ |
r̥kṣavānaraśārdūlair nakhadaṁṣṭrāyudhair vr̥tā ||

=== meaning ===

---
52.
karāgraiś caraṇāgraiś ca vānarair uddhataṁ rajaḥ |
bhaumam antardadhe lokaṁ nivārya savituḥ prabhām ||

=== meaning ===

---
53.
sā sma yāti divārātraṁ mahatī harivāhinī |
hr̥ṣṭapramuditā senā sugrīveṇābhirakṣitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
54.
vanarās tvaritaṁ yānti sarve yuddhābhinandanaḥ |
mumokṣayiṣavaḥ sītāṁ muhūrtaṁ kvāpi nāsata ||

=== meaning ===

---
55.
tataḥ pādapasaṁbādhaṁ nānāmr̥gasamākulam |
sahyaparvatam āsedur malayaṁ ca mahī dharam ||

=== meaning ===

---
56.
kānanāni vicitrāṇi nadīprasravaṇāni ca |
paśyann api yayau rāmaḥ sahyasya malayasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
57.
campakāṁs tilakāṁś cūtān aśokān sinduvārakān |
karavīrāṁś ca timiśān bhañjanti sma plavaṁgamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
58.
phalāny amr̥tagandhīni mūlāni kusumāni ca |
bubhujur vānarās tatra pādapānāṁ balotkaṭāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
59.
droṇamātrapramāṇāni lambamānāni vānarāḥ |
yayuḥ pibanto hr̥ṣṭās te madhūni madhupiṅgalāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
60.
pādapān avabhañjanto vikarṣantas tathā latāḥ |
vidhamanto girivarān prayayuḥ plavagarṣabhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
61.
vr̥kṣebhyo ’nye tu kapayo nardanto madhudarpitāḥ |
anye vr̥kṣān prapadyante prapatanty api cāpare ||

=== meaning ===

---
62.
babhūva vasudhā tais tu saṁpūrṇā haripuṁgavaiḥ |
yathā kamalakedāraiḥ pakvair iva vasuṁdharā ||

=== meaning ===

---
63.
mahendram atha saṁprāpya rāmo rājīvalocanaḥ |
adhyārohan mahābāhuḥ śikharaṁ drumabhūṣitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
64.
tataḥ śikharam āruhya rāmo daśarathātmajaḥ |
kūrmamīnasamākīrṇam apaśyat salilāśayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
65.
te sahyaṁ samatikramya malayaṁ ca mahāgirim |
āsedur ānupūrvyeṇa samudraṁ bhīmaniḥsvanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
66.
avaruhya jagāmāśu velāvanam anuttamam |
rāmo ramayatāṁ śreṣṭhaḥ sasugrīvaḥ salakṣmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
67.
atha dhautopalatalāṁ toyaughaiḥ sahasotthitaiḥ |
velām āsādya vipulāṁ rāmo vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
68.
ete vayam anuprāptāḥ sugrīva varuṇālayam |
ihedānīṁ vicintā sā yā na pūrvaṁ samutthitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
69.
ataḥ paramatīro ’yaṁ sāgaraḥ saritāṁ pati |
na cāyam anupāyena śakyas taritum arṇavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
70.
tad ihaiva niveśo ’stu mantraḥ prastūyatām iha |
yathedaṁ vānarabalaṁ paraṁ pāram avāpnuyāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
71.
itīva sa mahābāhuḥ sītāharaṇakarśitaḥ |
rāmaḥ sāgaram āsādya vāsam ājñāpayat tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
72.
saṁprāpto mantrakālo naḥ sāgarasyeha laṅghane |
svāṁ svāṁ senāṁ samutsr̥jya mā ca kaś cit kuto vrajet |
gacchantu vānarāḥ śūrā jñeyaṁ channaṁ bhayaṁ ca naḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
73.
rāmasya vacanaṁ śrutvā sugrīvaḥ sahalakṣmaṇaḥ |
senāṁ nyaveśayat tīre sāgarasya drumāyute ||

=== meaning ===

---
74.
virarāja samīpasthaṁ sāgarasya tu tad balam |
madhupāṇḍujalaḥ śrīmān dvitīya iva sāgaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
75.
velāvanam upāgamya tatas te haripuṁgavāḥ |
viniviṣṭāḥ paraṁ pāraṁ kāṅkṣamāṇā mahodadheḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
76.
sā mahārṇavam āsādya hr̥ṣṭā vānaravāhinī |
vāyuvegasamādhūtaṁ paśyamānā mahārṇavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
77.
dūrapāram asaṁbādhaṁ rakṣogaṇaniṣevitam |
paśyanto varuṇāvāsaṁ niṣedur hariyūthapāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
78.
caṇḍanakragrahaṁ ghoraṁ kṣapādau divasakṣaye |
candrodaye samādhūtaṁ praticandrasamākulam ||

=== meaning ===

---
79.
caṇḍānilamahāgrāhaiḥ kīrṇaṁ timitimiṁgilaiḥ |
dīptabhogair ivākrīrṇaṁ bhujaṁgair varuṇālayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
80.
avagāḍhaṁ mahāsattair nānāśailasamākulam |
durgaṁ drugam amārgaṁ tam agādham asurālayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
81.
makarair nāgabhogaiś ca vigāḍhā vātalohitāḥ |
utpetuś ca nipetuś ca pravr̥ddhā jalarāśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
82.
agnicūrṇam ivāviddhaṁ bhāskarāmbumanoragam |
surāriviṣayaṁ ghoraṁ pātālaviṣamaṁ sadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
83.
sāgaraṁ cāmbaraprakhyam ambaraṁ sāgaropamam |
sāgaraṁ cāmbaraṁ ceti nirviśeṣam adr̥śyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
84.
saṁpr̥ktaṁ nabhasā hy ambhaḥ saṁpr̥ktaṁ ca nabho ’mbhasā |
tādr̥grūpe sma dr̥śyete tārā ratnasamākule ||

=== meaning ===

---
85.
samutpatitameghasya vīcci mālākulasya ca |
viśeṣo na dvayor āsīt sāgarasyāmbarasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
86.
anyonyair āhatāḥ saktāḥ sasvanur bhīmaniḥsvanāḥ |
ūrmayaḥ sindhurājasya mahābherya ivāhave ||

=== meaning ===

---
87.
ratnaughajalasaṁnādaṁ viṣaktam iva vāyunā |
utpatantam iva kruddhaṁ yādogaṇasamākulam ||

=== meaning ===

---
88.
dadr̥śus te mahātmāno vātāhatajalāśayam |
aniloddhūtam ākāśe pravalgatam ivormibhiḥ |
bhrāntormijalasaṁnādaṁ pralolam iva sāgaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
