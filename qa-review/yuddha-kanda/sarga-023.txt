=== Sarga 23 ===

---
1.
sā sītā tac chiro dr̥ṣṭvā tac ca kārmukam uttamam |
sugrīvapratisaṁsargam ākhyātaṁ ca hanūmatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
nayane mukhavarṇaṁ ca bhartus tat sadr̥śaṁ mukham |
keśān keśāntadeśaṁ ca taṁ ca cūḍāmaṇiṁ śubham ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
etaiḥ sarvair abhijñānair abhijñāya suduḥkhitā |
vijagarhe ’tha kaikeyīṁ krośantī kurarī yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
sakāmā bhava kaikeyi hato ’yaṁ kulanandanaḥ |
kulam utsāditaṁ sarvaṁ tvayā kalahaśīlayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
āryeṇa kiṁ nu kaikeyyāḥ kr̥taṁ rāmeṇa vipriyam |
yad gr̥hāc cīravasanas tayā prasthāpito vanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
evam uktvā tu vaidehī vepamānā tapasvinī |
jagāma jagatīṁ bālā chinnā tu kadalī yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sā muhūrtāt samāśvasya pratilabhya ca cetanām |
tac chiraḥ samupāghrāya vilalāpāyatekṣaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
hā hatāsmi mahābāho vīravratam anuvratā |
imāṁ te paścimāvasthāṁ gatāsmi vidhavā kr̥tā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
prathamaṁ maraṇaṁ nāryā bhartur vaiguṇyam ucyate |
suvr̥ttaḥ sādhuvr̥ttāyāḥ saṁvr̥ttas tvaṁ mamāgrataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
duḥkhād duḥkhaṁ prapannāyā magnāyāḥ śokasāgare |
yo hi mām udyatas trātuṁ so ’pi tvaṁ vinipātitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sā śvaśrūr mama kausalyā tvayā putreṇa rāghava |
vatseneva yathā dhenur vivatsā vatsalā kr̥tā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
ādiṣṭaṁ dīrgham āyus te yair acintyaparākrama |
anr̥taṁ vacanaṁ teṣām alpāyur asi rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
atha vā naśyati prajñā prājñasyāpi satas tava |
pacaty enaṁ tathā kālo bhūtānāṁ prabhavo hy ayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
adr̥ṣṭaṁ mr̥tyum āpannaḥ kasmāt tvaṁ nayaśāstravit |
vyasanānām upāyajñaḥ kuśalo hy asi varjane ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tathā tvaṁ saṁpariṣvajya raudrayātinr̥śaṁsayā |
kālarātryā mayācchidya hr̥taḥ kamalalocanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
upaśeṣe mahābāho māṁ vihāya tapasvinīm |
priyām iva śubhāṁ nārīṁ pr̥thivīṁ puruṣarṣabha ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
arcitaṁ satataṁ yatnād gandhamālyair mayā tava |
idaṁ te matpriyaṁ vīra dhanuḥ kāñcanabhūṣitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
pitrā daśarathena tvaṁ śvaśureṇa mamānagha |
pūrvaiś ca pitr̥bhiḥ sārdhaṁ nūnaṁ svarge samāgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
divi nakṣatrabhūtas tvaṁ mahat karma kr̥taṁ priyam |
puṇyaṁ rājarṣivaṁśaṁ tvam ātmanaḥ samupekṣase ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
kiṁ mān na prekṣase rājan kiṁ māṁ na pratibhāṣase |
bālāṁ bālena saṁprāptāṁ bhāryāṁ māṁ sahacāriṇīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
saṁśrutaṁ gr̥hṇatā pāṇiṁ cariṣyāmīti yat tvayā |
smara tan mama kākutstha naya mām api duḥkhitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
kasmān mām apahāya tvaṁ gato gatimatāṁ vara |
asmāl lokād amuṁ lokaṁ tyaktvā mām iha duḥkhitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
kalyāṇair ucitaṁ yat tat pariṣvaktaṁ mayaiva tu |
kravyādais tac charīraṁ te nūnaṁ viparikr̥ṣyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
agniṣṭomādibhir yajñair iṣṭavān āptadakṣiṇaiḥ |
agnihotreṇa saṁskāraṁ kena tvaṁ tu na lapsyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
pravrajyām upapannānāṁ trayāṇām ekam āgatam |
pariprakṣyati kausalyā lakṣmaṇaṁ śokalālasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
sa tasyāḥ paripr̥cchantyā vadhaṁ mitrabalasya te |
tava cākhyāsyate nūnaṁ niśāyāṁ rākṣasair vadham ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
sā tvāṁ suptaṁ hataṁ śrutvā māṁ ca rakṣogr̥haṁ gatām |
hr̥dayena vidīrṇena na bhaviṣyati rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
sādhu pātaya māṁ kṣipraṁ rāmasyopari rāvaṇaḥ |
samānaya patiṁ patnyā kuru kalyāṇam uttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
śirasā me śiraś cāsya kāyaṁ kāyena yojaya |
rāvaṇānugamiṣyāmi gatiṁ bhartur mahātmanaḥ |
muhūrtam api necchāmi jīvituṁ pāpajīvinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
śrutaṁ mayā vedavidāṁ brāhmaṇānāṁ pitur gr̥he |
yāsāṁ strīṇāṁ priyo bhartā tāsāṁ lokā mahodayāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
kṣamā yasmin damas tyāgaḥ satyaṁ dharmaḥ kr̥tajñatā |
ahiṁsā caiva bhūtānāṁ tam r̥te kā gatir mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
iti sā duḥkhasaṁtaptā vilalāpāyatekṣaṇā |
bhartuḥ śiro dhanus tatra samīkṣya janakātmajā ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
evaṁ lālapyamānāyāṁ sītāyāṁ tatra rākṣasaḥ |
abhicakrāma bhartāram anīkasthaḥ kr̥tāñjaliḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
vijayasvāryaputreti so ’bhivādya prasādya ca |
nyavedayad anuprāptaṁ prahastaṁ vāhinīpatim ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
amātyaiḥ sahitaḥ sarvaiḥ prahastaḥ samupasthitaḥ |
kiṁ cid ātyayikaṁ kāryaṁ teṣāṁ tvaṁ darśanaṁ kuru ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
etac chrutvā daśagrīvo rākṣasaprativeditam |
aśokavanikāṁ tyaktvā mantriṇāṁ darśanaṁ yayau ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
sa tu sarvaṁ samarthyaiva mantribhiḥ kr̥tyam ātmanaḥ |
sabhāṁ praviśya vidadhe viditvā rāmavikramam ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
antardhānaṁ tu tac chīrṣaṁ tac ca kārmukam uttamam |
jagāma rāvaṇasyaiva niryāṇasamanantaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
rākṣasendras tu taiḥ sārdhaṁ mantribhir bhīmavikramaiḥ |
samarthayām āsa tadā rāmakāryaviniścayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
avidūrasthitān sarvān balādhyakṣān hitaiṣiṇaḥ |
abravīt kālasadr̥śo rāvaṇo rākṣasādhipaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
śīghraṁ bherīninādena sphuṭakoṇāhatena me |
samānayadhvaṁ sainyāni vaktavyaṁ ca na kāraṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
tatas tatheti pratigr̥hya tad vaco; balādhipās te mahad ātmano balam |
samānayaṁś caiva samāgataṁ ca te; nyavedayan bhartari yuddhakāṅkṣiṇi ||

=== meaning ===

---
