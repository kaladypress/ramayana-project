=== Sarga 97 ===

---
1.
atha saṁsmārayām āsa rāghavaṁ mātalis tadā |
ajānann iva kiṁ vīra tvam enam anuvartase ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
visr̥jāsmai vadhāya tvam astraṁ paitāmahaṁ prabho |
vināśakālaḥ kathito yaḥ suraiḥ so ’dya vartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tataḥ saṁsmārito rāmas tena vākyena mātaleḥ |
jagrāha sa śaraṁ dīptaṁ niśvasantam ivoragam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
yam asmai prathamaṁ prādād agastyo bhagavān r̥ṣiḥ |
brahmadattaṁ mahad bāṇam amoghaṁ yudhi vīryavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
brahmaṇā nirmitaṁ pūrvam indrārtham amitaujasā |
dattaṁ surapateḥ pūrvaṁ trilokajayakāṅkṣiṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
yasya vājeṣu pavanaḥ phale pāvakabhāskarau |
śarīram ākāśamayaṁ gaurave merumandarau ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
jājvalyamānaṁ vapuṣā supuṅkhaṁ hemabhūṣitam |
tejasā sarvabhūtānāṁ kr̥taṁ bhāskaravarcasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sadhūmam iva kālāgniṁ dīptam āśīviṣaṁ yathā |
rathanāgāśvavr̥ndānāṁ bhedanaṁ kṣiprakāriṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
dvārāṇāṁ parighāṇāṁ ca girīṇām api bhedanam |
nānārudhirasiktāṅgaṁ medodigdhaṁ sudāruṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
vajrasāraṁ mahānādaṁ nānāsamitidāruṇam |
sarvavitrāsanaṁ bhīmaṁ śvasantam iva pannagam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
kaṅkagr̥dhrabalānāṁ ca gomāyugaṇarakṣasām |
nityaṁ bhakṣapradaṁ yuddhe yamarūpaṁ bhayāvaham ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
nandanaṁ vānarendrāṇāṁ rakṣasām avasādanam |
vājitaṁ vividhair vājaiś cārucitrair garutmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tam uttameṣuṁ lokānām ikṣvākubhayanāśanam |
dviṣatāṁ kīrtiharaṇaṁ praharṣakaram ātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
abhimantrya tato rāmas taṁ maheṣuṁ mahābalaḥ |
vedaproktena vidhinā saṁdadhe kārmuke balī ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa rāvaṇāya saṁkruddho bhr̥śam āyamya kārmukam |
cikṣepa param āyattas taṁ śaraṁ marmaghātinam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sa vajra iva durdharṣo vajrabāhuvisarjitaḥ |
kr̥tānta iva cāvāryo nyapatad rāvaṇorasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
sa visr̥ṣṭo mahāvegaḥ śarīrāntakaraḥ śaraḥ |
bibheda hr̥dayaṁ tasya rāvaṇasya durātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
rudhirāktaḥ sa vegena jīvitāntakaraḥ śaraḥ |
rāvaṇasya haran prāṇān viveśa dharaṇītalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
sa śaro rāvaṇaṁ hatvā rudhirārdrakr̥tacchaviḥ |
kr̥takarmā nibhr̥tavat svatūṇīṁ punar āviśat ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tasya hastād dhatasyāśu kārmukaṁ tat sasāyakam |
nipapāta saha prāṇair bhraśyamānasya jīvitāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
gatāsur bhīmavegas tu nairr̥tendro mahādyutiḥ |
papāta syandanād bhūmau vr̥tro vajrahato yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā patitaṁ bhūmau hataśeṣā niśācarāḥ |
hatanāthā bhayatrastāḥ sarvataḥ saṁpradudruvuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
nardantaś cābhipetus tān vānarā drumayodhinaḥ |
daśagrīvavadhaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā vijayaṁ rāghavasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
arditā vānarair hr̥ṣṭair laṅkām abhyapatan bhayāt |
hatāśrayatvāt karuṇair bāṣpaprasravaṇair mukhaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tato vineduḥ saṁhr̥ṣṭā vānarā jitakāśinaḥ |
vadanto rāghavajayaṁ rāvaṇasya ca taṁ vadham ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
athāntarikṣe vyanadat saumyas tridaśadundubhiḥ |
divyagandhavahas tatra mārutaḥ susukho vavau ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
nipapātāntarikṣāc ca puṣpavr̥ṣṭis tadā bhuvi |
kirantī rāghavarathaṁ duravāpā manoharāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
rāghavastavasaṁyuktā gagane ca viśuśruve |
sādhu sādhv iti vāg agryā devatānāṁ mahātmanām ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
āviveśa mahān harṣo devānāṁ cāraṇaiḥ saha |
rāvaṇe nihate raudre sarvalokabhayaṁkare ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
tataḥ sakāmaṁ sugrīvam aṅgadaṁ ca mahābalam |
cakāra rāghavaḥ prīto hatvā rākṣasapuṁgavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
tataḥ prajagmuḥ praśamaṁ marudgaṇā; diśaḥ prasedur vimalaṁ nabho ’bhavat |
mahī cakampe na ca mārutā vavuḥ; sthiraprabhaś cāpy abhavad divākaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
tatas tu sugrīvavibhīṣaṇādayaḥ; suhr̥dviśeṣāḥ sahalakṣmaṇās tadā |
sametya hr̥ṣṭā vijayena rāghavaṁ; raṇe ’bhirāmaṁ vidhinābhyapūjayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
sa tu nihataripuḥ sthirapratijñaḥ; svajanabalābhivr̥to raṇe rarāja |
raghukulanr̥panandano mahaujās; tridaśagaṇair abhisaṁvr̥to yathendraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
