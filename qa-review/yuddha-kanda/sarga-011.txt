=== Sarga 11 ===

---
1.
ity uktvā paruṣaṁ vākyaṁ rāvaṇaṁ rāvaṇānujaḥ |
ājagāma muhūrtena yatra rāmaḥ salakṣmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
taṁ meruśikharākāraṁ dīptām iva śatahradām |
gaganasthaṁ mahīsthās te dadr̥śur vānarādhipāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tam ātmapañcamaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā sugrīvo vānarādhipaḥ |
vānaraiḥ saha durdharṣaś cintayām āsa buddhimān ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
cintayitvā muhūrtaṁ tu vānarāṁs tān uvāca ha |
hanūmatpramukhān sarvān idaṁ vacanam uttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
eṣa sarvāyudhopetaś caturbhiḥ saha rākṣasaiḥ |
rākṣaso ’bhyeti paśyadhvam asmān hantuṁ na saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
sugrīvasya vacaḥ śrutvā sarve te vānarottamāḥ |
sālān udyamya śailāṁś ca idaṁ vacanam abruvan ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
śīghraṁ vyādiśa no rājan vadhāyaiṣāṁ durātmanām |
nipatantu hatāś caite dharaṇyām alpajīvitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
teṣāṁ saṁbhāṣamāṇānām anyonyaṁ sa vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
uttaraṁ tīram āsādya khastha eva vyatiṣṭhata ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
uvāca ca mahāprājñaḥ svareṇa mahatā mahān |
sugrīvaṁ tāṁś ca saṁprekṣya khastha eva vibhīṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
rāvaṇo nāma durvr̥tto rākṣaso rākṣaseśvaraḥ |
tasyāham anujo bhrātā vibhīṣaṇa iti śrutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tena sītā janasthānād dhr̥tā hatvā jaṭāyuṣam |
ruddhvā ca vivaśā dīnā rākṣasībhiḥ surakṣitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tam ahaṁ hetubhir vākyair vividhaiś ca nyadarśayam |
sādhu niryātyatāṁ sītā rāmāyeti punaḥ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sa ca na pratijagrāha rāvaṇaḥ kālacoditaḥ |
ucyamāno hitaṁ vākyaṁ viparīta ivauṣadham ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
so ’haṁ paruṣitas tena dāsavac cāvamānitaḥ |
tyaktvā putrāṁś ca dārāṁś ca rāghavaṁ śaraṇaṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sarvalokaśaraṇyāya rāghavāya mahātmane |
nivedayata māṁ kṣipraṁ vibhīṣaṇam upasthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
etat tu vacanaṁ śrutvā sugrīvo laghuvikramaḥ |
lakṣmaṇasyāgrato rāmaṁ saṁrabdham idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
rāvaṇasyānujo bhrātā vibhīṣaṇa iti śrutaḥ |
caturbhiḥ saha rakṣobhir bhavantaṁ śaraṇaṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
rāvaṇena praṇihitaṁ tam avehi vibhīṣaṇam |
tasyāhaṁ nigrahaṁ manye kṣamaṁ kṣamavatāṁ vara ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
rākṣaso jihmayā buddhyā saṁdiṣṭo ’yam upasthitaḥ |
prahartuṁ māyayā channo viśvaste tvayi rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
badhyatām eṣa tīvreṇa daṇḍena sacivaiḥ saha |
rāvaṇasya nr̥śaṁsasya bhrātā hy eṣa vibhīṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
evam uktvā tu taṁ rāmaṁ saṁrabdho vāhinīpatiḥ |
vākyajño vākyakuśalaṁ tato maunam upāgamat ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
sugrīvasya tu tad vākyaṁ śrutvā rāmo mahābalaḥ |
samīpasthān uvācedaṁ hanūmatpramukhān harīn ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
yad uktaṁ kapirājena rāvaṇāvarajaṁ prati |
vākyaṁ hetumad atyarthaṁ bhavadbhir api tac chrutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
suhr̥dā hy arthakr̥ccheṣu yuktaṁ buddhimatā satā |
samarthenāpi saṁdeṣṭuṁ śāśvatīṁ bhūtim icchatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
ity evaṁ paripr̥ṣṭās te svaṁ svaṁ matam atandritāḥ |
sopacāraṁ tadā rāmam ūcur hitacikīrṣavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
ajñātaṁ nāsti te kiṁ cit triṣu lokeṣu rāghava |
ātmānaṁ pūjayan rāma pr̥cchasy asmān suhr̥ttayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tvaṁ hi satyavrataḥ śūro dhārmiko dr̥ḍhavikramaḥ |
parīkṣya kārā smr̥timān nisr̥ṣṭātmā suhr̥tsu ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tasmād ekaikaśas tāvad bruvantu sacivās tava |
hetuto matisaṁpannāḥ samarthāś ca punaḥ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
ity ukte rāghavāyātha matimān aṅgado ’grataḥ |
vibhīṣaṇaparīkṣārtham uvāca vacanaṁ hariḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
śatroḥ sakāśāt saṁprāptaḥ sarvathā śaṅkya eva hi |
viśvāsayogyaḥ sahasā na kartavyo vibhīṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
chādayitvātmabhāvaṁ hi caranti śaṭhabuddhayaḥ |
praharanti ca randhreṣu so ’narthaḥ sumahān bhavet ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
arthānarthau viniścitya vyavasāyaṁ bhajeta ha |
guṇataḥ saṁgrahaṁ kuryād doṣatas tu visarjayet ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
yadi doṣo mahāṁs tasmiṁs tyajyatām aviśaṅkitam |
guṇān vāpi bahūñ jñātvā saṁgrahaḥ kriyatāṁ nr̥pa ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
śarabhas tv atha niścitya sārthaṁ vacanam abravīt |
kṣipram asmin naravyāghra cāraḥ pratividhīyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
praṇidhāya hi cāreṇa yathāvat sūkṣmabuddhinā |
parīkṣya ca tataḥ kāryo yathānyāyaṁ parigrahaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
jāmbavāṁs tv atha saṁprekṣya śāstrabuddhyā vicakṣaṇaḥ |
vākyaṁ vijñāpayām āsa guṇavad doṣavarjitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
baddhavairāc ca pāpāc ca rākṣasendrād vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
adeśa kāle saṁprāptaḥ sarvathā śaṅkyatām ayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
tato maindas tu saṁprekṣya nayāpanayakovidaḥ |
vākyaṁ vacanasaṁpanno babhāṣe hetumattaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
vacanaṁ nāma tasyaiṣa rāvaṇasya vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
pr̥cchyatāṁ madhureṇāyaṁ śanair naravareśvara ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
bhāvam asya tu vijñāya tatas tattvaṁ kariṣyasi |
yadi dr̥ṣṭo na duṣṭo vā buddhipūrvaṁ nararṣabha ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
atha saṁskārasaṁpanno hanūmān sacivottamaḥ |
uvāca vacanaṁ ślakṣṇam arthavan madhuraṁ laghu ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
na bhavantaṁ matiśreṣṭhaṁ samarthaṁ vadatāṁ varam |
atiśāyayituṁ śakto br̥haspatir api bruvan ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
na vādān nāpi saṁgharṣān nādhikyān na ca kāmataḥ |
vakṣyāmi vacanaṁ rājan yathārthaṁ rāmagauravāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
arthānarthanimittaṁ hi yad uktaṁ sacivais tava |
tatra doṣaṁ prapaśyāmi kriyā na hy upapadyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
r̥te niyogāt sāmarthyam avaboddhuṁ na śakyate |
sahasā viniyogo hi doṣavān pratibhāti me ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
cārapraṇihitaṁ yuktaṁ yad uktaṁ sacivais tava |
arthasyāsaṁbhavāt tatra kāraṇaṁ nopapadyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
adeśa kāle saṁprāpta ity ayaṁ yad vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
vivakṣā cātra me ’stīyaṁ tāṁ nibodha yathā mati ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
sa eṣa deśaḥ kālaś ca bhavatīha yathā tathā |
puruṣāt puruṣaṁ prāpya tathā doṣaguṇāv api ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
daurātmyaṁ rāvaṇe dr̥ṣṭvā vikramaṁ ca tathā tvayi |
yuktam āgamanaṁ tasya sadr̥śaṁ tasya buddhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
ajñātarūpaiḥ puruṣaiḥ sa rājan pr̥cchyatām iti |
yad uktam atra me prekṣā kā cid asti samīkṣitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
pr̥cchyamāno viśaṅketa sahasā buddhimān vacaḥ |
tatra mitraṁ praduṣyeta mithyapr̥ṣṭaṁ sukhāgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
52.
aśakyaḥ sahasā rājan bhāvo vettuṁ parasya vai |
antaḥ svabhāvair gītais tair naipuṇyaṁ paśyatā bhr̥śam ||

=== meaning ===

---
53.
na tv asya bruvato jātu lakṣyate duṣṭabhāvatā |
prasannaṁ vadanaṁ cāpi tasmān me nāsti saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
54.
aśaṅkitamatiḥ svastho na śaṭhaḥ parisarpati |
na cāsya duṣṭā vāk cāpi tasmān nāstīha saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
55.
ākāraś chādyamāno ’pi na śakyo vinigūhitum |
balād dhi vivr̥ṇoty eva bhāvam antargataṁ nr̥ṇām ||

=== meaning ===

---
56.
deśakālopapannaṁ ca kāryaṁ kāryavidāṁ vara |
saphalaṁ kurute kṣipraṁ prayogeṇābhisaṁhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
57.
udyogaṁ tava saṁprekṣya mithyāvr̥ttaṁ ca rāvaṇam |
vālinaś ca vadhaṁ śrutvā sugrīvaṁ cābhiṣecitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
58.
rājyaṁ prārthayamānaś ca buddhipūrvam ihāgataḥ |
etāvat tu puraskr̥tya yujyate tv asya saṁgrahaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
59.
yathāśakti mayoktaṁ tu rākṣasasyārjavaṁ prati |
tvaṁ pramāṇaṁ tu śeṣasya śrutvā buddhimatāṁ vara ||

=== meaning ===

---
