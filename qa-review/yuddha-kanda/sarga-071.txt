=== Sarga 71 ===

---
1.
rāmam āśvāsayāne tu lakṣmaṇe bhrātr̥vatsale |
nikṣipya gulmān svasthāne tatrāgacchad vibhīṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
nānāpraharaṇair vīraiś caturbhiḥ sacivair vr̥taḥ |
nīlāñjanacayākārair mātaṁgair iva yūthapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
so ’bhigamya mahātmānaṁ rāghavaṁ śokalālasaṁ |
vānarāṁś caiva dadr̥śe bāṣpaparyākulekṣaṇān ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
rāghavaṁ ca mahātmānam ikṣvākukulanandanam |
dadarśa moham āpannaṁ lakṣmaṇasyāṅkam āśritam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
vrīḍitaṁ śokasaṁtaptaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rāmaṁ vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
antarduḥkhena dīnātmā kim etad iti so ’bravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
vibhīṣaṇa mukhaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā sugrīvaṁ tāṁś ca vānarān |
uvāca lakṣmaṇo vākyam idaṁ bāṣpapariplutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
hatām indrajitā sītām iha śrutvaiva rāghavaḥ |
hanūmad vacanāt saumya tato moham upāgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
kathayantaṁ tu saumitriṁ saṁnivārya vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
puṣkalārtham idaṁ vākyaṁ visaṁjñaṁ rāmam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
manujendrārtarūpeṇa yad uktas tvaṁ hanūmatā |
tad ayuktam ahaṁ manye sāgarasyeva śoṣaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
abhiprāyaṁ tu jānāmi rāvaṇasya durātmanaḥ |
sītāṁ prati mahābāho na ca ghātaṁ kariṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
yācyamānaḥ subahuśo mayā hitacikīrṣuṇā |
vaidehīm utsr̥jasveti na ca tat kr̥tavān vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
naiva sāmnā na bhedena na dānena kuto yudhā |
sā draṣṭum api śakyeta naiva cānyena kena cit ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
vānarān mohayitvā tu pratiyātaḥ sa rākṣasaḥ |
caityaṁ nikumbhilāṁ nāma yatra homaṁ kariṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
hutavān upayāto hi devair api savāsavaiḥ |
durādharṣo bhavaty eṣa saṁgrāme rāvaṇātmajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tena mohayatā nūnam eṣā māyā prayojitā |
vighnam anvicchatā tāta vānarāṇāṁ parākrame |
sasainyās tatra gacchāmo yāvat tan na samāpyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tyajemaṁ naraśārdūlamithyā saṁtāpam āgatam |
sīdate hi balaṁ sarvaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā tvāṁ śokakarśitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
iha tvaṁ svastha hr̥dayas tiṣṭha sattvasamucchritaḥ |
lakṣmaṇaṁ preṣayāsmābhiḥ saha sainyānukarṣibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
eṣa taṁ naraśārdūlo rāvaṇiṁ niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ |
tyājayiṣyati tat karma tato vadhyo bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tasyaite niśitās tīkṣṇāḥ patripatrāṅgavājinaḥ |
patatriṇa ivāsaumyāḥ śarāḥ pāsyanti śoṇitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tat saṁdiśa mahābāho lakṣmaṇaṁ śubhalakṣaṇam |
rākṣasasya vināśāya vajraṁ vajradharo yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
manujavara na kālaviprakarṣo; ripunidhanaṁ prati yat kṣamo ’dya kartum |
tvam atisr̥ja ripor vadhāya bāṇīm; asurapuronmathane yathā mahendraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
samāptakarmā hi sa rākṣasendro; bhavaty adr̥śyaḥ samare surāsuraiḥ |
yuyutsatā tena samāptakarmaṇā; bhavet surāṇām api saṁśayo mahān ||

=== meaning ===

---
