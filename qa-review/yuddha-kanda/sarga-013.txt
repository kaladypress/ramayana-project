=== Sarga 13 ===

---
1.
rāghaveṇābhaye datte saṁnato rāvaṇānujaḥ |
khāt papātāvaniṁ hr̥ṣṭo bhaktair anucaraiḥ saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sa tu rāmasya dharmātmā nipapāta vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
pādayoḥ śaraṇānveṣī caturbhiḥ saha rākṣasaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
abravīc ca tadā rāmaṁ vākyaṁ tatra vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
dharmayuktaṁ ca yuktaṁ ca sāmprataṁ saṁpraharṣaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
anujo rāvaṇasyāhaṁ tena cāsmy avamānitaḥ |
bhavantaṁ sarvabhūtānāṁ śaraṇyaṁ śaraṇaṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
parityaktā mayā laṅkā mitrāṇi ca dhanāni ca |
bhavadgataṁ me rājyaṁ ca jīvitaṁ ca sukhāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
rākṣasānāṁ vadhe sāhyaṁ laṅkāyāś ca pradharṣaṇe |
kariṣyāmi yathāprāṇaṁ pravekṣyāmi ca vāhinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
iti bruvāṇaṁ rāmas tu pariṣvajya vibhīṣaṇam |
abravīl lakṣmaṇaṁ prītaḥ samudrāj jalam ānaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tena cemaṁ mahāprājñam abhiṣiñca vibhīṣaṇam |
rājānaṁ rakṣasāṁ kṣipraṁ prasanne mayi mānada ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
evam uktas tu saumitrir abhyaṣiñcad vibhīṣaṇam |
madhye vānaramukhyānāṁ rājānaṁ rāmaśāsanāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
taṁ prasādaṁ tu rāmasya dr̥ṣṭvā sadyaḥ plavaṁgamāḥ |
pracukruśur mahānādān sādhu sādhv iti cābruvan ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
abravīc ca hanūmāṁś ca sugrīvaś ca vibhīṣaṇam |
kathaṁ sāgaram akṣobhyaṁ tarāma varuṇālayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
upāyair abhigacchāmo yathā nadanadīpatim |
tarāma tarasā sarve sasainyā varuṇālayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
evam uktas tu dharmajñaḥ pratyuvāca vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
samudraṁ rāghavo rājā śaraṇaṁ gantum arhati ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
khānitaḥ sagareṇāyam aprameyo mahodadhiḥ |
kartum arhati rāmasya jñāteḥ kāryaṁ mahodadhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
evaṁ vibhīṣaṇenokte rākṣasena vipaścitā |
prakr̥tyā dharmaśīlasya rāghavasyāpy arocata ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sa lakṣmaṇaṁ mahātejāḥ sugrīvaṁ ca harīśvaram |
satkriyārthaṁ kriyādakṣaḥ smitapūrvam uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
vibhīṣaṇasya mantro ’yaṁ mama lakṣmaṇa rocate |
brūhi tvaṁ sahasugrīvas tavāpi yadi rocate ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
sugrīvaḥ paṇḍito nityaṁ bhavān mantravicakṣaṇaḥ |
ubhābhyāṁ saṁpradhāryāryaṁ rocate yat tad ucyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
evam uktau tu tau vīrāv ubhau sugrīvalakṣmaṇau |
samudācāra saṁyuktam idaṁ vacanam ūcatuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
kimarthaṁ no naravyāghra na rociṣyati rāghava |
vibhīṣaṇena yat tūktam asmin kāle sukhāvaham ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
abaddhvā sāgare setuṁ ghore ’smin varuṇālaye |
laṅkā nāsādituṁ śakyā sendrair api surāsuraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
vibhīṣaṇasya śūrasya yathārthaṁ kriyatāṁ vacaḥ |
alaṁ kālātyayaṁ kr̥tvā samudro ’yaṁ niyujyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
evam uktaḥ kuśāstīrṇe tīre nadanadīpateḥ |
saṁviveśa tadā rāmo vedyām iva hutāśanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
