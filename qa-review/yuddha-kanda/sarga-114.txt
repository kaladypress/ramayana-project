=== Sarga 114 ===

---
1.
bahūni nāma varṣāṇi gatasya sumahad vanam |
śr̥ṇomy ahaṁ prītikaraṁ mama nāthasya kīrtanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
kalyāṇī bata gātheyaṁ laukikī pratibhāti me |
eti jīvantam ānando naraṁ varṣaśatād api ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
rāghavasya harīṇāṁ ca katham āsīt samāgamaḥ |
kasmin deśe kim āśritya tat tvam ākhyāhi pr̥cchataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
sa pr̥ṣṭo rājaputreṇa br̥syāṁ samupaveśitaḥ |
ācacakṣe tataḥ sarvaṁ rāmasya caritaṁ vane ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
yathā pravrajito rāmo mātur datte vare tava |
yathā ca putraśokena rājā daśaratho mr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
yathā dūtais tvam ānītas tūrṇaṁ rājagr̥hāt prabho |
tvayāyodhyāṁ praviṣṭena yathā rājyaṁ na cepsitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
citrakūṭaṁ giriṁ gatvā rājyenāmitrakarśanaḥ |
nimantritas tvayā bhrātā dharmam ācaritā satām ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sthitena rājño vacane yathā rājyaṁ visarjitam |
āryasya pāduke gr̥hya yathāsi punar āgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sarvam etan mahābāho yathāvad viditaṁ tava |
tvayi pratiprayāte tu yad vr̥ttaṁ tan nibodha me ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
apayāte tvayi tadā samudbhrāntamr̥gadvijam |
praviveśātha vijanaṁ sumahad daṇḍakāvanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
teṣāṁ purastād balavān gacchatāṁ gahane vane |
vinadan sumahānādaṁ virādhaḥ pratyadr̥śyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tam utkṣipya mahānādam ūrdhvabāhum adhomukham |
nikhāte prakṣipanti sma nadantam iva kuñjaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tat kr̥tvā duṣkaraṁ karma bhrātarau rāmalakṣmaṇau |
sāyāhne śarabhaṅgasya ramyam āśramam īyatuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
śarabhaṅge divaṁ prāpte rāmaḥ satyaparākramaḥ |
abhivādya munīn sarvāñ janasthānam upāgamat ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
caturdaśasahasrāṇi rakṣasāṁ bhīmakarmaṇām |
hatāni vasatā tatra rāghaveṇa mahātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tataḥ paścāc chūrpaṇakhā rāmapārśvam upāgatā |
tato rāmeṇa saṁdiṣṭo lakṣmaṇaḥ sahasotthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
pragr̥hya khaḍgaṁ ciccheda karṇanāse mahābalaḥ |
tatas tenārditā bālā rāvaṇaṁ samupāgatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
rāvaṇānucaro ghoro mārīco nāma rākṣasaḥ |
lobhayām āsa vaidehīṁ bhūtvā ratnamayo mr̥gaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
sā rāmam abravīd dr̥ṣṭvā vaidehī gr̥hyatām iti |
aho manoharaḥ kānta āśrame no bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tato rāmo dhanuṣpāṇir dhāvantam anudhāvati |
sa taṁ jaghāna dhāvantaṁ śareṇānataparvaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
atha saumyā daśagrīvo mr̥gaṁ yāte tu rāghave |
lakṣmaṇe cāpi niṣkrānte praviveśāśramaṁ tadā |
jagrāha tarasā sītāṁ grahaḥ khe rohiṇīm iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
trātukāmaṁ tato yuddhe hatvā gr̥dhraṁ jaṭāyuṣam |
pragr̥hya sītāṁ sahasā jagāmāśu sa rāvaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tatas tv adbhutasaṁkāśāḥ sthitāḥ parvatamūrdhani |
sītāṁ gr̥hītvā gacchantaṁ vānarāḥ parvatopamāḥ |
dadr̥śur vismitās tatra rāvaṇaṁ rākṣasādhipam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
praviverśa tadā laṅkāṁ rāvaṇo lokarāvaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tāṁ suvarṇaparikrānte śubhe mahati veśmani |
praveśya maithilīṁ vākyaiḥ sāntvayām āsa rāvaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
nivartamānaḥ kākutstho dr̥ṣṭvā gr̥dhraṁ pravivyathe ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
gr̥dhraṁ hataṁ tadā dagdhvā rāmaḥ priyasakhaṁ pituḥ |
godāvarīm anucaran vanoddeśāṁś ca puṣpitān |
āsedatur mahāraṇye kabandhaṁ nāma rākṣasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tataḥ kabandhavacanād rāmaḥ satyaparākramaḥ |
r̥śyamūkaṁ giriṁ gatvā sugrīveṇa samāgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
tayoḥ samāgamaḥ pūrvaṁ prītyā hārdo vyajāyata |
itaretara saṁvādāt pragāḍhaḥ praṇayas tayoḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
rāmaḥ svabāhuvīryeṇa svarājyaṁ pratyapādayat |
vālinaṁ samare hatvā mahākāyaṁ mahābalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
sugrīvaḥ sthāpito rājye sahitaḥ sarvavānaraiḥ |
rāmāya pratijānīte rājaputryās tu mārgaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
ādiṣṭā vānarendreṇa sugrīveṇa mahātmanā |
daśakoṭyaḥ plavaṁgānāṁ sarvāḥ prasthāpitā diśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
teṣāṁ no vipranaṣṭānāṁ vindhye parvatasattame |
bhr̥śaṁ śokābhitaptānāṁ mahān kālo ’tyavartata ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
bhrātā tu gr̥dhrarājasya saṁpātir nāma vīryavān |
samākhyāti sma vasatiṁ sītāyā rāvaṇālaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
so ’haṁ duḥkhaparītānāṁ duḥkhaṁ tajjñātināṁ nudan |
ātmavīryaṁ samāsthāya yojanānāṁ śataṁ plutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
tatrāham ekām adrākṣam aśokavanikāṁ gatām |
kauśeyavastrāṁ malināṁ nirānandāṁ dr̥ḍhavratām ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
tayā sametya vidhivat pr̥ṣṭvā sarvam aninditām |
abhijñānaṁ maṇiṁ labdhvā caritārtho ’ham āgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
mayā ca punar āgamya rāmasyākliṣṭakarmaṇaḥ |
abhijñānaṁ mayā dattam arciṣmān sa mahāmaṇiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
śrutvā tāṁ maithilīṁ hr̥ṣṭas tv āśaśaṁse sa jīvitam |
jīvitāntam anuprāptaḥ pītvāmr̥tam ivāturaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
udyojayiṣyann udyogaṁ dadhre laṅkāvadhe manaḥ |
jighāṁsur iva lokāṁs te sarvām̐l lokān vibhāvasuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
tataḥ samudram āsādya nalaṁ setum akārayat |
atarat kapivīrāṇāṁ vāhinī tena setunā ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
prahastam avadhīn nīlaḥ kumbhakarṇaṁ tu rāghavaḥ |
lakṣmaṇo rāvaṇasutaṁ svayaṁ rāmas tu rāvaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
sa śakreṇa samāgamya yamena varuṇena ca |
surarṣibhiś ca kākutstho varām̐l lebhe paraṁtapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
sa tu dattavaraḥ prītyā vānaraiś ca samāgataḥ |
puṣpakeṇa vimānena kiṣkindhām abhyupāgamat ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
taṁ gaṅgāṁ punar āsādya vasantaṁ munisaṁnidhau |
avighnaṁ puṣyayogena śvo rāmaṁ draṣṭum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
tataḥ sa satyaṁ hanumadvaco mahan; niśamya hr̥ṣṭo bharataḥ kr̥tāñjaliḥ |
uvāca vāṇīṁ manasaḥ praharṣiṇī; cirasya pūrṇaḥ khalu me manorathaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
