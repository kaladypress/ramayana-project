=== Sarga 27 ===

---
1.
tat tu mālyavato vākyaṁ hitam uktaṁ daśānanaḥ |
na marṣayati duṣṭātmā kālasya vaśam āgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sa baddhvā bhrukuṭiṁ vaktre krodhasya vaśam āgataḥ |
amarṣāt parivr̥ttākṣo mālyavantam athābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
hitabuddhyā yad ahitaṁ vacaḥ paruṣam ucyate |
parapakṣaṁ praviśyaiva naitac chrotragataṁ mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
mānuṣaṁ kr̥paṇaṁ rāmam ekaṁ śākhāmr̥gāśrayam |
samarthaṁ manyase kena tyaktaṁ pitrā vanālayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
rakṣasām īśvaraṁ māṁ ca devatānāṁ bhayaṁkaram |
hīnaṁ māṁ manyase kena ahīnaṁ sarvavikramaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
vīradveṣeṇa vā śaṅke pakṣapātena vā ripoḥ |
tvayāhaṁ paruṣāṇy uktaḥ paraprotsāhanena vā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
prabhavantaṁ padasthaṁ hi paruṣaṁ ko ’bhidhāsyati |
paṇḍitaḥ śāstratattvajño vinā protsāhanād ripoḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
ānīya ca vanāt sītāṁ padmahīnām iva śriyam |
kimarthaṁ pratidāsyāmi rāghavasya bhayād aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
vr̥taṁ vānarakoṭībhiḥ sasugrīvaṁ salakṣmaṇam |
paśya kaiś cid ahobhis tvaṁ rāghavaṁ nihataṁ mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
dvandve yasya na tiṣṭhanti daivatāny api saṁyuge |
sa kasmād rāvaṇo yuddhe bhayam āhārayiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
dvidhā bhajyeyam apy evaṁ na nameyaṁ tu kasya cit |
eṣa me sahajo doṣaḥ svabhāvo duratikramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
yadi tāvat samudre tu setur baddho yadr̥cchayā |
rāmeṇa vismayaḥ ko ’tra yena te bhayam āgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sa tu tīrtvārṇavaṁ rāmaḥ saha vānarasenayā |
pratijānāmi te satyaṁ na jīvan pratiyāsyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
evaṁ bruvāṇaṁ saṁrabdhaṁ ruṣṭaṁ vijñāya rāvaṇam |
vrīḍito mālyavān vākyaṁ nottaraṁ pratyapadyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
jayāśiṣā ca rājānaṁ vardhayitvā yathocitam |
mālyavān abhyanujñāto jagāma svaṁ niveśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
rāvaṇas tu sahāmātyo mantrayitvā vimr̥śya ca |
laṅkāyām atulāṁ guptiṁ kārayām āsa rākṣasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
vyādideśa ca pūrvasyāṁ prahastaṁ dvāri rākṣasaṁ |
dakṣiṇasyāṁ mahāvīryau mahāpārśva mahodarau ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
paścimāyām atho dvāri putram indrajitaṁ tathā |
vyādideśa mahāmāyaṁ rākṣasair bahubhir vr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
uttarasyāṁ puradvāri vyādiśya śukasāraṇau |
svayaṁ cātra bhaviṣyāmi mantriṇas tān uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
rākṣasaṁ tu virūpākṣaṁ mahāvīryaparākramam |
madhyame ’sthāpayad gulme bahubhiḥ saha rākṣasaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
evaṁvidhānaṁ laṅkāyāṁ kr̥tvā rākṣasapuṁgavaḥ |
mene kr̥tārtham ātmānaṁ kr̥tāntavaśam āgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
visarjayām āsa tataḥ sa mantriṇo; vidhānam ājñāpya purasya puṣkalam |
jayāśiṣā mantragaṇena pūjito; viveśa so ’ntaḥpuram r̥ddhiman mahat ||

=== meaning ===

---
