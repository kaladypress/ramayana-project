=== Sarga 5 ===

---
1.
sā tu nīlena vidhivat svārakṣā susamāhitā |
sāgarasyottare tīre sādhu senā niveśitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
maindaś ca dvividhaś cobhau tatra vānarapuṁgavau |
viceratuś ca tāṁ senāṁ rakṣārthaṁ sarvato diśam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
niviṣṭāyāṁ tu senāyāṁ tīre nadanadīpateḥ |
pārśvasthaṁ lakṣmaṇaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rāmo vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
śokaś ca kila kālena gacchatā hy apagacchati |
mama cāpaśyataḥ kāntām ahany ahani vardhate ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
na me duḥkhaṁ priyā dūre na me duḥkhaṁ hr̥teti ca |
etad evānuśocāmi vayo ’syā hy ativartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
vāhi vāta yataḥ kanyā tāṁ spr̥ṣṭvā mām api spr̥śa |
tvayi me gātrasaṁsparśaś candre dr̥ṣṭisamāgamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tan me dahati gātrāṇi viṣaṁ pītam ivāśaye |
hā nātheti priyā sā māṁ hriyamāṇā yad abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tadviyogendhanavatā taccintāvipulārciṣā |
rātriṁ divaṁ śarīraṁ me dahyate madanāgninā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
avagāhyārṇavaṁ svapsye saumitre bhavatā vinā |
kathaṁ cit prajvalan kāmaḥ samāsuptaṁ jale dahet ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
bahv etat kāmayānasya śakyam etena jīvitum |
yad ahaṁ sā ca vāmorur ekāṁ dharaṇim āśritau ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
kedārasyeva kedāraḥ sodakasya nirūdakaḥ |
upasnehena jīvāmi jīvantīṁ yac chr̥ṇomi tām ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
kadā tu khalu susśoṇīṁ śatapatrāyatekṣaṇām |
vijitya śatrūn drakṣyāmi sītāṁ sphītām iva śriyam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
kadā nu cārubimbauṣṭhaṁ tasyāḥ padmam ivānanam |
īṣadunnamya pāsyāmi rasāyanam ivāturaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tau tasyāḥ saṁhatau pīnau stanau tālaphalopamau |
kadā nu khalu sotkampau hasantyā māṁ bhajiṣyataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sā nūnam asitāpāṅgī rakṣomadhyagatā satī |
mannāthā nāthahīneva trātāraṁ nādhigacchati ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
kadā vikṣobhya rakṣāṁsi sā vidhūyotpatiṣyati |
vidhūya jaladān nīlāñ śaśilekhā śaratsv iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
svabhāvatanukā nūnaṁ śokenānaśanena ca |
bhūyas tanutarā sītā deśakālaviparyayāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
kadā nu rākṣasendrasya nidhāyorasi sāyakān |
sītāṁ pratyāhariṣyāmi śokam utsr̥jya mānasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
kadā nu khalu māṁ sādhvī sītāmarasutopamā |
sotkaṇṭhā kaṇṭham ālambya mokṣyaty ānandajaṁ jalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
kadā śokam imaṁ ghoraṁ maithilī viprayogajam |
sahasā vipramokṣyāmi vāsaḥ śukletaraṁ yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
evaṁ vilapatas tasya tatra rāmasya dhīmataḥ |
dinakṣayān mandavapur bhāskaro ’stam upāgamat ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
āśvāsito lakṣmaṇena rāmaḥ saṁdhyām upāsata |
smaran kamalapatrākṣīṁ sītāṁ śokākulīkr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
