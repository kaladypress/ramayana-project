=== Sarga 33 ===

---
1.
yudhyatāṁ tu tatas teṣāṁ vānarāṇāṁ mahātmanām |
rakṣasāṁ saṁbabhūvātha balakopaḥ sudāruṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
te hayaiḥ kāñcanāpīḍair dhvajaiś cāgniśikhopamaiḥ |
rathaiś cādityasaṁkāśaiḥ kavacaiś ca manoramaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
niryayū rākṣasavyāghrā nādayanto diśo daśa |
rākṣasā bhīmakarmāṇo rāvaṇasya jayaiṣiṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
vānarāṇām api camūr mahatī jayam iccatām |
abhyadhāvata tāṁ senāṁ rakṣasāṁ kāmarūpiṇām ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
etasminn antare teṣām anyonyam abhidhāvatām |
rakṣasāṁ vānarāṇāṁ ca dvandvayuddham avartata ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
aṅgadenendrajit sārdhaṁ vāliputreṇa rākṣasaḥ |
ayudhyata mahātejās tryambakeṇa yathāndhakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
prajaṅghena ca saṁpātir nityaṁ durmarṣaṇo raṇe |
jambūmālinam ārabdho hanūmān api vānaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
saṁgataḥ sumahākrodho rākṣaso rāvaṇānujaḥ |
samare tīkṣṇavegena mitraghnena vibhīṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tapanena gajaḥ sārdhaṁ rākṣasena mahābalaḥ |
nikumbhena mahātejā nīlo ’pi samayudhyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
vānarendras tu sugrīvaḥ praghasena samāgataḥ |
saṁgataḥ samare śrīmān virūpākṣeṇa lakṣmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
agniketuś ca durdharṣo raśmiketuś ca rākṣasaḥ |
suptaghno yajñakopaś ca rāmeṇa saha saṁgatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
vajramuṣṭis tu maindena dvividenāśaniprabhaḥ |
rākṣasābhyāṁ sughorābhyāṁ kapimukhyau samāgatau ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
vīraḥ pratapano ghoro rākṣaso raṇadurdharaḥ |
samare tīkṣṇavegena nalena samayudhyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
dharmasya putro balavān suṣeṇa iti viśrutaḥ |
sa vidyunmālinā sārdham ayudhyata mahākapiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
vānarāś cāpare bhīmā rākṣasair aparaiḥ saha |
dvandvaṁ samīyur bahudhā yuddhāya bahubhiḥ saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tatrāsīt sumahad yuddhaṁ tumulaṁ lomaharṣaṇam |
rakṣasāṁ vānarāṇāṁ ca vīrāṇāṁ jayam icchatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
harirākṣasadehebhyaḥ prasr̥tāḥ keśaśāḍvalāḥ |
śarīrasaṁghāṭavahāḥ prasusruḥ śoṇitāpagāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
ājaghānendrajit kruddho vajreṇeva śatakratuḥ |
aṅgadaṁ gadayā vīraṁ śatrusainyavidāraṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tasya kāñcanacitrāṅgaṁ rathaṁ sāśvaṁ sasārathim |
jaghāna samare śrīmān aṅgado vegavān kapiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
saṁpātis tu tribhir bāṇaiḥ prajaṅghena samāhataḥ |
nijaghānāśvakarṇena prajaṅghaṁ raṇamūrdhani ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
jambūmālī rathasthas tu rathaśaktyā mahābalaḥ |
bibheda samare kruddho hanūmantaṁ stanāntare ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tasya taṁ ratham āsthāya hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ |
pramamātha talenāśu saha tenaiva rakṣasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
bhinnagātraḥ śarais tīkṣṇaiḥ kṣiprahastena rakṣasā |
prajaghānādriśr̥ṅgeṇa tapanaṁ muṣṭinā gajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
grasantam iva sainyāni praghasaṁ vānarādhipaḥ |
sugrīvaḥ saptaparṇena nirbibheda jaghāna ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
prapīḍya śaravarṣeṇa rākṣasaṁ bhīmadarśanam |
nijaghāna virūpākṣaṁ śareṇaikena lakṣmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
agniketuś ca durdharṣo raśmiketuś ca rākṣasaḥ |
suptighno yajñakopaś ca rāmaṁ nirbibhiduḥ śaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
teṣāṁ caturṇāṁ rāmas tu śirāṁsi samare śaraiḥ |
kruddhaś caturbhiś ciccheda ghorair agniśikhopamaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
vajramuṣṭis tu maindena muṣṭinā nihato raṇe |
papāta sarathaḥ sāśvaḥ purāṭṭa iva bhūtale ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
vajrāśanisamasparśo dvivido ’py aśaniprabham |
jaghāna giriśr̥ṅgeṇa miṣatāṁ sarvarakṣasām ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
dvividaṁ vānarendraṁ tu drumayodhinam āhave |
śarair aśanisaṁkāśaiḥ sa vivyādhāśaniprabhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
sa śarair atividdhāṅgo dvividaḥ krodhamūrchitaḥ |
sālena sarathaṁ sāśvaṁ nijaghānāśaniprabham ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
nikumbhas tu raṇe nīlaṁ nīlāñjanacayaprabham |
nirbibheda śarais tīkṣṇaiḥ karair megham ivāṁśumān ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
punaḥ śaraśatenātha kṣiprahasto niśācaraḥ |
bibheda samare nīlaṁ nikumbhaḥ prajahāsa ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
tasyaiva rathacakreṇa nīlo viṣṇur ivāhave |
śiraś ciccheda samare nikumbhasya ca sāratheḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
vidyunmālī rathasthas tu śaraiḥ kāñcanabhūṣaṇaiḥ |
suṣeṇaṁ tāḍayām āsa nanāda ca muhur muhuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
taṁ rathastham atho dr̥ṣṭvā suṣeṇo vānarottamaḥ |
giriśr̥ṅgeṇa mahatā ratham āśu nyapātayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
lāghavena tu saṁyukto vidyunmālī niśācaraḥ |
apakramya rathāt tūrṇaṁ gadāpāṇiḥ kṣitau sthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
tataḥ krodhasamāviṣṭaḥ suṣeṇo haripuṁgavaḥ |
śilāṁ sumahatīṁ gr̥hya niśācaram abhidravat ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
tam āpatantaṁ gadayā vidyunmālī niśācaraḥ |
vakṣasy abhijagnānāśu suṣeṇaṁ harisattamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
gadāprahāraṁ taṁ ghoram acintyaplavagottamaḥ |
tāṁ śilāṁ pātayām āsa tasyorasi mahāmr̥dhe ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
śilāprahārābhihato vidyunmālī niśācaraḥ |
niṣpiṣṭahr̥dayo bhūmau gatāsur nipapāta ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
evaṁ tair vānaraiḥ śūraiḥ śūrās te rajanīcarāḥ |
dvandve vimr̥ditās tatra daityā iva divaukasaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
bhallaiḥ khaḍgair gadābhiś ca śaktitomara paṭṭasaiḥ |
apaviddhaś ca bhinnaś ca rathaiḥ sāṁgrāmikair hayaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
nihataiḥ kuñjarair mattais tathā vānararākṣasaiḥ |
cakrākṣayugadaṇḍaiś ca bhagnair dharaṇisaṁśritaiḥ |
babhūvāyodhanaṁ ghoraṁ gomāyugaṇasevitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
kabandhāni samutpetur dikṣu vānararakṣasām |
vimarde tumule tasmin devāsuraraṇopame ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
vidāryamāṇā haripuṁgavais tadā; niśācarāḥ śoṇitadigdhagātrāḥ |
punaḥ suyuddhaṁ tarasā samāśritā; divākarasyāstamayābhikāṅkṣiṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
