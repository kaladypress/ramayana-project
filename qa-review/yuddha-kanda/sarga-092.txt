=== Sarga 92 ===

---
1.
sa tu tena tadā krodhāt kākutsthenārdito raṇe |
rāvaṇaḥ samaraślāghī mahākrodham upāgamat ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sa dīptanayano roṣāc cāpam āyamya vīryavān |
abhyardayat susaṁkruddho rāghavaṁ paramāhave ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
bāṇadhārāsahasrais tu sa toyada ivāmbarāt |
rāghavaṁ rāvaṇo bāṇais taṭākam iva pūrayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
pūritaḥ śarajālena dhanurmuktena saṁyuge |
mahāgirir ivākampyaḥ kākustho na prakampate ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
sa śaraiḥ śarajālāni vārayan samare sthitaḥ |
gabhastīn iva sūryasya pratijagrāha vīryavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tataḥ śarasahasrāṇi kṣiprahasto niśācaraḥ |
nijaghānorasi kruddho rāghavasya mahātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sa śoṇitasamādigdhaḥ samare lakṣmaṇāgrajaḥ |
dr̥ṣṭaḥ phulla ivāraṇye sumahān kiṁśukadrumaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
śarābhighātasaṁrabdhaḥ so ’pi jagrāha sāyakān |
kākutsthaḥ sumahātejā yugāntādityavarcasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tato ’nyonyaṁ susaṁrabdhāv ubhau tau rāmarāvaṇau |
śarāndhakāre samare nopālakṣayatāṁ tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tataḥ krodhasamāviṣṭo rāmo daśarathātmajaḥ |
uvāca rāvaṇaṁ vīraḥ prahasya paruṣaṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
mama bhāryā janasthānād ajñānād rākṣasādhama |
hr̥tā te vivaśā yasmāt tasmāt tvaṁ nāsi vīryavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
mayā virahitāṁ dīnāṁ vartamānāṁ mahāvane |
vaidehīṁ prasabhaṁ hr̥tvā śūro ’ham iti manyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
strīṣu śūra vināthāsu paradārābhimarśake |
kr̥tvā kāpuruṣaṁ karma śūro ’ham iti manyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
bhinnamaryāda nirlajja cāritreṣv anavasthita |
darpān mr̥tyum upādāya śūro ’ham iti manyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
śūreṇa dhanadabhrātrā balaiḥ samuditena ca |
ślāghanīyaṁ yaśasyaṁ ca kr̥taṁ karma mahat tvayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
utsekenābhipannasya garhitasyāhitasya ca |
karmaṇaḥ prāpnuhīdānīṁ tasyādya sumahat phalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
śūro ’ham iti cātmānam avagacchasi durmate |
naiva lajjāsti te sītāṁ coravad vyapakarṣataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
yadi matsaṁnidhau sītā dharṣitā syāt tvayā balāt |
bhrātaraṁ tu kharaṁ paśyes tadā matsāyakair hataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
diṣṭyāsi mama duṣṭātmaṁś cakṣurviṣayam āgataḥ |
adya tvāṁ sāyakais tīkṣṇair nayāmi yamasādanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
adya te maccharaiś chinnaṁ śiro jvalitakuṇḍalam |
kravyādā vyapakarṣantu vikīrṇaṁ raṇapāṁsuṣu ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
nipatyorasi gr̥dhrās te kṣitau kṣiptasya rāvaṇa |
pibantu rudhiraṁ tarṣād bāṇaśalyāntarothitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
adya madbāṇābhinnasya gatāsoḥ patitasya te |
karṣantv antrāṇi patagā garutmanta ivoragān ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
ity evaṁ sa vadan vīro rāmaḥ śatrunibarhaṇaḥ |
rākṣasendraṁ samīpasthaṁ śaravarṣair avākirat ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
babhūva dviguṇaṁ vīryaṁ balaṁ harṣaś ca saṁyuge |
rāmasyāstrabalaṁ caiva śatror nidhanakāṅkṣiṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
prādurbabhūvur astrāṇi sarvāṇi viditātmanaḥ |
praharṣāc ca mahātejāḥ śīghrahastataro ’bhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
śubhāny etāni cihnāni vijñāyātmagatāni saḥ |
bhūya evārdayad rāmo rāvaṇaṁ rākṣasāntakr̥t ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
harīṇāṁ cāśmanikaraiḥ śaravarṣaiś ca rāghavāt |
hanyamāno daśagrīvo vighūrṇahr̥dayo ’bhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
yadā ca śastraṁ nārebhe na vyakarṣac charāsanam |
nāsya pratyakarod vīryaṁ viklavenāntarātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
kṣiptāś cāpi śarās tena śastrāṇi vividhāni ca |
na raṇārthāya vartante mr̥tyukāle ’bhivartataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
sūtas tu rathanetāsya tadavasthaṁ nirīkṣya tam |
śanair yuddhād asaṁbhānto rathaṁ tasyāpavāhayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
