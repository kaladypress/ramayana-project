=== Sarga 49 ===

---
1.
tato rāmo mahātejā dhanur ādāya vīryavān |
kirīṭinaṁ mahākāyaṁ kumbhakarṇaṁ dadarśa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rākṣasaśreṣṭhaṁ parvatākāradarśanam |
kramamāṇam ivākāśaṁ purā nārāyaṇaṁ prabhum ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
satoyāmbudasaṁkāśaṁ kāñcanāṅgadabhūṣaṇam |
dr̥ṣṭvā punaḥ pradudrāva vānarāṇāṁ mahācamūḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
vidrutāṁ vāhinīṁ dr̥ṣṭvā vardhamānaṁ ca rākṣasaṁ |
savismayam idaṁ rāmo vibhīṣaṇam uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
ko ’sau parvatasaṁkaśaḥ kirīṭī harilocanaḥ |
laṅkāyāṁ dr̥śyate vīraḥ savidyud iva toyadaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
pr̥thivyāḥ ketubhūto ’sau mahān eko ’tra dr̥śyate |
yaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā vānarāḥ sarve vidravanti tatas tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
ācakṣva me mahān ko ’sau rakṣo vā yadi vāsuraḥ |
na mayaivaṁvidhaṁ bhūtaṁ dr̥ṣṭapūrvaṁ kadā cana ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sa pr̥ṣṭo rājaputreṇa rāmeṇākliṣṭakāriṇā |
vibhīṣaṇo mahāprājñaḥ kākutstham idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
yena vaivasvato yuddhe vāsavaś ca parājitaḥ |
saiṣa viśravasaḥ putraḥ kumbhakarṇaḥ pratāpavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
etena devā yudhi dānavāś ca; yakṣā bhujaṁgāḥ piśitāśanāś ca |
gandharvavidyādharakiṁnarāś ca; sahasraśo rāghava saṁprabhagnāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
śūlapāṇiṁ virūpākṣaṁ kumbhakarṇaṁ mahābalam |
hantuṁ na śekus tridaśāḥ kālo ’yam iti mohitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
prakr̥tyā hy eṣa tejasvī kumbhakarṇo mahābalaḥ |
anyeṣāṁ rākṣasendrāṇāṁ varadānakr̥taṁ balam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
etena jātamātreṇa kṣudhārtena mahātmanā |
bhakṣitāni sahasrāṇi sattvānāṁ subahūny api ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
teṣu saṁbhakṣyamāṇeṣu prajā bhayanipīḍitāḥ |
yānti sma śaraṇaṁ śakraṁ tam apy arthaṁ nyavedayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa kumbhakarṇaṁ kupito mahendro; jaghāna vajreṇa śitena vajrī |
sa śakravajrābhihato mahātmā; cacāla kopāc ca bhr̥śaṁ nanāda ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tasya nānadyamānasya kumbhakarṇasya dhīmataḥ |
śrutvā ninādaṁ vitrastā bhūyo bhūmir vitatrase ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tataḥ kopān mahendrasya kumbhakarṇo mahābalaḥ |
vikr̥ṣyairāvatād dantaṁ jaghānorasi vāsavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
kumbhakarṇaprahārārto vicacāla sa vāsavaḥ |
tato viṣeduḥ sahasā devabrahmarṣidānavāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
prajābhiḥ saha śakraś ca yayau sthānaṁ svayambhuvaḥ |
kumbhakarṇasya daurātmyaṁ śaśaṁsus te prajāpateḥ |
prajānāṁ bhakṣaṇaṁ cāpi devānāṁ cāpi dharṣaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
evaṁ prajā yadi tv eṣa bhakṣayiṣyati nityaśaḥ |
acireṇaiva kālena śūnyo loko bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
vāsavasya vacaḥ śrutvā sarvalokapitāmahaḥ |
rakṣāṁsy āvāhayām āsa kumbhakarṇaṁ dadarśa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
kumbhakarṇaṁ samīkṣyaiva vitatrāsa prajāpatiḥ |
dr̥ṣṭvā niśvasya caivedaṁ svayambhūr idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
dhruvaṁ lokavināśāya paurastyenāsi nirmitaḥ |
tasmāt tvam adya prabhr̥ti mr̥takalpaḥ śayiṣyasi |
brahmaśāpābhibhūto ’tha nipapātāgrataḥ prabhoḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tataḥ paramasaṁbhrānto rāvaṇo vākyam abravīt |
vivr̥ddhaḥ kāñcano vr̥kṣaḥ phalakāle nikr̥tyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
na naptāraṁ svakaṁ nyāyyaṁ śaptum evaṁ prajāpate |
na mithyāvacanaś ca tvaṁ svapsyaty eṣa na saṁśayaḥ |
kālas tu kriyatām asya śayane jāgare tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
rāvaṇasya vacaḥ śrutvā svayambhūr idam abravīt |
śayitā hy eṣa ṣaṇ māsān ekāhaṁ jāgariṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
ekenāhnā tv asau vīraś caran bhūmiṁ bubhukṣitaḥ |
vyāttāsyo bhakṣayel lokān saṁkruddha iva pāvakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
so ’sau vyasanam āpannaḥ kumbhakarṇam abodhayat |
tvatparākramabhītaś ca rājā saṁprati rāvaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
sa eṣa nirgato vīraḥ śibirād bhīmavikramaḥ |
vānarān bhr̥śasaṁkruddho bhakṣayan paridhāvati ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
kumbhakarṇaṁ samīkṣyaiva harayo vipradudruvuḥ |
katham enaṁ raṇe kruddhaṁ vārayiṣyanti vānarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
ucyantāṁ vānarāḥ sarve yantram etat samucchritam |
iti vijñāya harayo bhaviṣyantīha nirbhayāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
vibhīṣaṇavacaḥ śrutvā hetumat sumukhodgatam |
uvāca rāghavo vākyaṁ nīlaṁ senāpatiṁ tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
gaccha sainyāni sarvāṇi vyūhya tiṣṭhasva pāvake |
dvārāṇy ādāya laṅkāyāś caryāś cāpy atha saṁkramān ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
śailaśr̥ṅgāṇi vr̥kṣāṁś ca śilāś cāpy upasaṁharan |
tiṣṭhantu vānarāḥ sarve sāyudhāḥ śailapāṇayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
rāghaveṇa samādiṣṭo nīlo haricamūpatiḥ |
śaśāsa vānarānīkaṁ yathāvat kapikuñjaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
tato gavākṣaḥ śarabho hanumān aṅgado nalaḥ |
śailaśr̥ṅgāṇi śailābhā gr̥hītvā dvāram abhyayuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
tato harīṇāṁ tad anīkam ugraṁ; rarāja śailodyatavr̥kṣahastam |
gireḥ samīpānugataṁ yathaiva; mahan mahāmbhodharajālam ugram ||

=== meaning ===

---
