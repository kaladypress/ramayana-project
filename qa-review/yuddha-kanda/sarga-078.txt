=== Sarga 78 ===

---
1.
sa hatāśvo mahātejā bhūmau tiṣṭhan niśācaraḥ |
indrajit paramakruddhaḥ saṁprajajvāla tejasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tau dhanvinau jighāṁsantāv anyonyam iṣubhir bhr̥śam |
vijayenābhiniṣkrāntau vane gajavr̥ṣāv iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
nibarhayantaś cānyonyaṁ te rākṣasavanaukasaḥ |
bhartāraṁ na jahur yuddhe saṁpatantas tatas tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
sa lakṣmaṇaṁ samuddiśya paraṁ lāghavam āsthitaḥ |
vavarṣa śaravarṣāṇi varṣāṇīva puraṁdaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
muktam indrajitā tat tu śaravarṣam ariṁdamaḥ |
avārayad asaṁbhrānto lakṣmaṇaḥ sudurāsadam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
abhedyakacanaṁ matvā lakṣmaṇaṁ rāvaṇātmajaḥ |
lalāṭe lakṣmaṇaṁ bāṇaiḥ supuṅkhais tribhir indrajit |
avidhyat paramakruddhaḥ śīghram astraṁ pradarśayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
taiḥ pr̥ṣatkair lalāṭasthaiḥ śuśubhe raghunandanaḥ |
raṇāgre samaraślāghī triśr̥ṅga iva parvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sa tathāpy ardito bāṇai rākṣasena mahāmr̥dhe |
tam āśu prativivyādha lakṣmaṇaḥ panabhiḥ śaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
lakṣmaṇendrajitau vīrau mahābalaśarāsanau |
anyonyaṁ jaghnatur bāṇair viśikhair bhīmavikramau ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tau parasparam abhyetya sarvagātreṣu dhanvinau |
ghorair vivyadhatur bāṇaiḥ kr̥tabhāvāv ubhau jaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tasmai dr̥ḍhataraṁ kruddho hatāśvāya vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
vajrasparśasamān pañca sasarjorasi mārgaṇān ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
te tasya kāyaṁ nirbhidya rukmapuṅkhā nimittagāḥ |
babhūvur lohitādigdhā raktā iva mahoragāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sa pitr̥vyasya saṁkruddha indrajic charam ādade |
uttamaṁ rakṣasāṁ madhye yamadattaṁ mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
taṁ samīkṣya mahātejā maheṣuṁ tena saṁhitam |
lakṣmaṇo ’py ādade bāṇam anyaṁ bhīmaparākramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
kubereṇa svayaṁ svapne yad dattam amitātmanā |
durjayaṁ durviṣahyaṁ ca sendrair api surāsuraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tābhyāṁ tau dhanuṣi śreṣṭhe saṁhitau sāyakottamau |
vikr̥ṣyamāṇau vīrābhyāṁ bhr̥śaṁ jajvalatuḥ śriyā ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tau bhāsayantāv ākāśaṁ dhanurbhyāṁ viśikhau cyutau |
mukhena mukham āhatya saṁnipetatur ojasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tau mahāgrahasaṁkāśāv anyonyaṁ saṁnipatya ca |
saṁgrāme śatadhā yātau medinyāṁ vinipetatuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
śarau pratihatau dr̥ṣṭvā tāv ubhau raṇamūrdhani |
vrīḍito jātaroṣau ca lakṣmaṇendrajitāv ubhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
susaṁrabdhas tu saumitrir astraṁ vāruṇam ādade |
raudraṁ mahedrajid yuddhe vyasr̥jad yudhi viṣṭhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tayoḥ sutumulaṁ yuddhaṁ saṁbabhūvādbhutopamam |
gaganasthāni bhūtāni lakṣmaṇaṁ paryavārayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
bhairavābhirute bhīme yuddhe vānararākṣasām |
bhūtair bahubhir ākāśaṁ vismitair āvr̥taṁ babhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
r̥ṣayaḥ pitaro devā gandharvā garuṇoragāḥ |
śatakratuṁ puraskr̥tya rarakṣur lakṣmaṇaṁ raṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
athānyaṁ mārgaṇaśreṣṭhaṁ saṁdadhe rāvaṇānujaḥ |
hutāśanasamasparśaṁ rāvaṇātmajadāruṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
supatram anuvr̥ttāṅgaṁ suparvāṇaṁ susaṁsthitam |
suvarṇavikr̥taṁ vīraḥ śarīrāntakaraṁ śaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
durāvāraṁ durviṣahaṁ rākṣasānāṁ bhayāvaham |
āśīviṣaviṣaprakhyaṁ devasaṁghaiḥ samarcitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
yena śakro mahātejā dānavān ajayat prabhuḥ |
purā devāsure yuddhe vīryavān harivāhanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tad aindram astraṁ saumitriḥ saṁyugeṣv aparājitam |
śaraśreṣṭhaṁ dhanuḥ śreṣṭhe naraśreṣṭho ’bhisaṁdadhe ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
saṁdhāyāmitradalanaṁ vicakarṣa śarāsanam |
sajyam āyamya durdharśaḥ kālo lokakṣaye yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
saṁdhāya dhanuṣi śreṣṭhe vikarṣann idam abravīt |
lakṣmīvām̐l lakṣmaṇo vākyam arthasādhakam ātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
dharmātmā satyasaṁdhaś ca rāmo dāśarathir yadi |
pauruṣe cāpratidvandvas tad enaṁ jahi rāvaṇim ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
ity uktvā bāṇam ākarṇaṁ vikr̥ṣya tam ajihmagam |
lakṣmaṇaḥ samare vīraḥ sasarjendrajitaṁ prati |
aindrāstreṇa samāyujya lakṣmaṇaḥ paravīrahā ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
tac chiraḥ saśiras trāṇaṁ śrīmaj jvalitakuṇḍalam |
pramathyendrajitaḥ kāyāt papāta dharaṇītale ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
tad rākṣasatanūjasya chinnaskandhaṁ śiro mahat |
tapanīyanibhaṁ bhūmau dadr̥śe rudhirokṣitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
hatas tu nipapātāśu dharaṇyāṁ rāvaṇātmajaḥ |
kavacī saśirastrāṇo vidhvastaḥ saśarāsanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
cukruśus te tataḥ sarve vānarāḥ savibhīṣaṇāḥ |
hr̥ṣyanto nihate tasmin devā vr̥travadhe yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
athāntarikṣe bhūtānām r̥ṣīṇāṁ ca mahātmanām |
abhijajñe ca saṁnādo gandharvāpsarasām api ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
patitaṁ samabhijñāya rākṣasī sā mahācamūḥ |
vadhyamānā diśo bheje haribhir jitakāśibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
vanarair vadhyamānās te śastrāṇy utsr̥jya rākṣasāḥ |
laṅkām abhimukhāḥ sarve naṣṭasaṁjñāḥ pradhāvitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
dudruvur bahudhā bhītā rākṣasāḥ śataśo diśaḥ |
tyaktvā praharaṇān sarve paṭṭasāsiparaśvadhān ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
ke cil laṅkāṁ paritrastāḥ praviṣṭā vānarārditāḥ |
samudre patitāḥ ke cit ke cit parvatam āśritāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
hatam indrajitaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā śayānaṁ samarakṣitau |
rākṣasānāṁ sahasreṣu na kaś cit pratyadr̥śyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
yathāstaṁ gata āditye nāvatiṣṭhanti raśmayaḥ |
tathā tasmin nipatite rākṣasās te gatā diśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
śāntarakśmir ivādityo nirvāṇa iva pāvakaḥ |
sa babhūva mahātejā vyapāsta gatajīvitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
praśāntapīḍā bahulo vinaṣṭāriḥ praharṣavān |
babhūva lokaḥ patite rākṣasendrasute tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
harṣaṁ ca śakro bhagavān saha sarvaiḥ surarṣabhaiḥ |
jagāma nihate tasmin rākṣase pāpakarmaṇi ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
śuddhā āpo nabhaś caiva jahr̥ṣur daityadānavāḥ |
ājagmuḥ patite tasmin sarvalokabhayāvahe ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
ūcuś ca sahitāḥ sarve devagandharvadānavāḥ |
vijvarāḥ śāntakaluṣā brāhmaṇā vicarantv iti ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
tato ’bhyanandan saṁhr̥ṣṭāḥ samare hariyūthapāḥ |
tam apratibalaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā hataṁ nairr̥tapuṁgavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
vibhīṣaṇo hanūmāṁś ca jāmbavāṁś carkṣayūthapaḥ |
vijayenābhinandantas tuṣṭuvuś cāpi lakṣmaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
kṣveḍantaś ca nadantaś ca garjantaś ca plavaṁgamāḥ |
labdhalakṣā raghusutaṁ parivāryopatasthire ||

=== meaning ===

---
52.
lāṅgūlāni pravidhyantaḥ sphoṭayantaś ca vānarāḥ |
lakṣmaṇo jayatīty evaṁ vākyaṁ vyaśrāvayaṁs tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
53.
anyonyaṁ ca samāśliṣya kapayo hr̥ṣṭamānasāḥ |
cakrur uccāvacaguṇā rāghavāśrayajāḥ kathāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
54.
tad asukaram athābhivīkṣya hr̥ṣṭāḥ; priyasuhr̥do yudhi lakṣmaṇasya karma |
paramam upalabhan manaḥpraharṣaṁ; vinihatam indraripuṁ niśamya devāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
