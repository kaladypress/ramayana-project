=== Sarga 63 ===

---
1.
pravr̥tte saṁkule tasmin ghore vīrajanakṣaye |
aṅgadaḥ kampanaṁ vīram āsasāda raṇotsukaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
āhūya so ’ṅgadaṁ kopāt tāḍayām āsa vegitaḥ |
gadayā kampanaḥ pūrvaṁ sa cacāla bhr̥śāhataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sa saṁjñāṁ prāpya tejasvī cikṣepa śikharaṁ gireḥ |
arditaś ca prahāreṇa kampanaḥ patito bhuvi ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
hatapravīrā vyathitā rākṣasendracamūs tadā |
jagāmābhimukhī sā tu kumbhakarṇasuto yataḥ |
āpatantīṁ ca vegena kumbhas tāṁ sāntvayac camūm ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
sa dhanur dhanvināṁ śreṣṭhaḥ pragr̥hya susamāhitaḥ |
mumocāśīviṣaprakhyāñ śarān dehavidāraṇān ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tasya tac chuśubhe bhūyaḥ saśaraṁ dhanur uttamam |
vidyudairāvatārciṣmad dvitīyendradhanur yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
ākarṇakr̥ṣṭamuktena jaghāna dvividaṁ tadā |
tena hāṭakapuṅkhena patriṇā patravāsasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sahasābhihatas tena vipramuktapadaḥ sphuran |
nipapātādrikūṭābho vihvalaḥ plavagottamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
maindas tu bhrātaraṁ dr̥ṣṭvā bhagnaṁ tatra mahāhave |
abhidudrāva vegena pragr̥hya mahatīṁ śilām ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tāṁ śilāṁ tu pracikṣepa rākṣasāya mahābalaḥ |
bibheda tāṁ śilāṁ kumbhaḥ prasannaiḥ pañcabhiḥ śaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
saṁdhāya cānyaṁ sumukhaṁ śaram āśīviṣopamam |
ājaghāna mahātejā vakṣasi dvividāgrajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sa tu tena prahāreṇa maindo vānarayūthapaḥ |
marmaṇy abhihatas tena papāta bhuvi mūrchitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
aṅgado mātulau dr̥ṣṭvā patitau tau mahābalau |
abhidudrāva vegena kumbham udyatakārmukam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tam āpatantaṁ vivyādha kumbhaḥ pañcabhir āyasaiḥ |
tribhiś cānyaiḥ śitair bāṇair mātaṁgam iva tomaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
so ’ṅgadaṁ vividhair bāṇaiḥ kumbho vivyādha vīryavān |
akuṇṭhadhārair niśitais tīkṣṇaiḥ kanakabhūṣaṇaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
aṅgadaḥ pratividdhāṅgo vāliputro na kampate |
śilāpādapavarṣāṇi tasya mūrdhni vavarṣa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
sa praciccheda tān sarvān bibheda ca punaḥ śilāḥ |
kumbhakarṇātmajaḥ śrīmān vāliputrasamīritān ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
āpatantaṁ ca saṁprekṣya kumbho vānarayūthapam |
bhruvor vivyādha bāṇābhyām ulkābhyām iva kuñjaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
aṅgadaḥ pāṇinā netre pidhāya rudhirokṣite |
sālam āsannam ekena parijagrāha pāṇinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tam indraketupratimaṁ vr̥kṣaṁ mandarasaṁnibham |
samutsr̥jantaṁ vegena paśyatāṁ sarvarakṣasām ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
sa ciccheda śitair bāṇaiḥ saptabhiḥ kāyabhedanaiḥ |
aṅgado vivyathe ’bhīkṣṇaṁ sasāda ca mumoha ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
aṅgadaṁ vyathitaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā sīdantam iva sāgare |
durāsadaṁ hariśreṣṭhā rāghavāya nyavedayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
rāmas tu vyathitaṁ śrutvā vāliputraṁ mahāhave |
vyādideśa hariśreṣṭhāñ jāmbavatpramukhāṁs tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
te tu vānaraśārdūlāḥ śrutvā rāmasya śāsanam |
abhipetuḥ susaṁkruddhāḥ kumbham udyatakārmukam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tato drumaśilāhastāḥ kopasaṁraktalocanāḥ |
rirakṣiṣanto ’bhyapatann aṅgadaṁ vānararṣabhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
jāmbavāṁś ca suṣeṇaś ca vegadarśī ca vānaraḥ |
kumbhakarṇātmajaṁ vīraṁ kruddhāḥ samabhidudruvuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
samīkṣyātatatas tāṁs tu vānarendrān mahābalān |
āvavāra śaraugheṇa nageneva jalāśayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tasya bāṇacayaṁ prāpya na śoker ativartitum |
vānarendrā mahātmāno velām iva mahodadhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
tāṁs tu dr̥ṣṭvā harigaṇāñ śaravr̥ṣṭibhir arditān |
aṅgadaṁ pr̥ṣṭhataḥ kr̥tvā bhrātr̥jaṁ plavageśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
abhidudrāva vegena sugrīvaḥ kumbham āhave |
śailasānu caraṁ nāgaṁ vegavān iva kesarī ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
utpāṭya ca mahāśailān aśvakarṇān dhavān bahūn |
anyāṁś ca vividhān vr̥kṣāṁś cikṣepa ca mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
tāṁ chādayantīm ākāśaṁ vr̥kṣavr̥ṣṭiṁ durāsadām |
kumbhakarṇātmajaḥ śrīmāṁś ciccheda niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
abhilakṣyeṇa tīvreṇa kumbhena niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ |
ācitās te drumā rejur yathā ghorāḥ śataghnayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
drumavarṣaṁ tu tac chinnaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā kumbhena vīryavān |
vānarādhipatiḥ śrīmān mahāsattvo na vivyathe ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
nirbhidyamānaḥ sahasā sahamānaś ca tāñ śarān |
kumbhasya dhanur ākṣipya babhañjendradhanuḥprabham ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
avaplutya tataḥ śīghraṁ kr̥tvā karma suduṣkaram |
abravīt kupitaḥ kumbhaṁ bhagnaśr̥ṅgam iva dvipam ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
nikumbhāgraja vīryaṁ te bāṇavegaṁ tad adbhutam |
saṁnatiś ca prabhāvaś ca tava vā rāvaṇasya vā ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
prahrādabalivr̥traghnakuberavaruṇopama |
ekas tvam anujāto ’si pitaraṁ balavattaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
tvām evaikaṁ mahābāhuṁ śūlahastam ariṁdamam |
tridaśā nātivartante jitendriyam ivādhayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
varadānāt pitr̥vyas te sahate devadānavān |
kumbhakarṇas tu vīryeṇa sahate ca surāsurān ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
dhanuṣīndrajitas tulyaḥ pratāpe rāvaṇasya ca |
tvam adya rakṣasāṁ loke śreṣṭho ’si balavīryataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
mahāvimardaṁ samare mayā saha tavādbhutam |
adya bhūtāni paśyantu śakraśambarayor iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
kr̥tam apratimaṁ karma darśitaṁ cāstrakauśalam |
pātitā harivīrāś ca tvayaite bhīmavikramāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
upālambhabhayāc cāpi nāsi vīra mayā hataḥ |
kr̥takarmā pariśrānto viśrāntaḥ paśya me balam ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
tena sugrīvavākyena sāvamānena mānitaḥ |
agner ājyahutasyeva tejas tasyābhyavardhata ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
tataḥ kumbhaḥ samutpatya sugrīvam abhipadya ca |
ājaghānorasi kruddho vajravegena muṣṭinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
tasya carma ca pusphoṭa saṁjajñe cāsya śoṇitam |
sa ca muṣṭir mahāvegaḥ pratijaghne ’sthimaṇḍale ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
tadā vegena tatrāsīt tejaḥ prajvālitaṁ muhuḥ |
vajraniṣpeṣasaṁjātajvālā merau yathā girau ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
sa tatrābhihatas tena sugrīvo vānararṣabhaḥ |
muṣṭiṁ saṁvartayām āsa vajrakalpaṁ mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
arciḥsahasravikacaṁ ravimaṇḍalasaprabham |
sa muṣṭiṁ pātayām āsa kumbhasyorasi vīryavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
muṣṭinābhihatas tena nipapātāśu rākṣasaḥ |
lohitāṅga ivākāśād dīptaraśmir yadr̥cchayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
52.
kumbhasya patato rūpaṁ bhagnasyorasi muṣṭinā |
babhau rudrābhipannasya yathārūpaṁ gavāṁ pateḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
53.
tasmin hate bhīmaparākrameṇa; plavaṁgamānām r̥ṣabheṇa yuddhe |
mahī saśailā savanā cacāla; bhayaṁ ca rakṣāṁsy adhikaṁ viveśa ||

=== meaning ===

---
