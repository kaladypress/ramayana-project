=== Sarga 116 ===

---
1.
śirasy añjalim ādāya kaikeyīnandivardhanaḥ |
babhāṣe bharato jyeṣṭhaṁ rāmaṁ satyaparākramam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
pūjitā māmikā mātā dattaṁ rājyam idaṁ mama |
tad dadāmi punas tubhyaṁ yathā tvam adadā mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
dhuram ekākinā nyastām r̥ṣabheṇa balīyasā |
kiśoravad guruṁ bhāraṁ na voḍhum aham utsahe ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
vārivegena mahatā bhinnaḥ setur iva kṣaran |
durbandhanam idaṁ manye rājyacchidram asaṁvr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
gatiṁ khara ivāśvasya haṁsasyeva ca vāyasaḥ |
nānvetum utsahe deva tava mārgam ariṁdama ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
yathā ca ropito vr̥kṣo jātaś cāntarniveśane |
mahāṁś ca sudurāroho mahāskandhaḥ praśākhavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
śīryeta puṣpito bhūtvā na phalāni pradarśayet |
tasya nānubhaved arthaṁ yasya hetoḥ sa ropyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
eṣopamā mahābāho tvam arthaṁ vettum arhasi |
yady asmān manujendra tvaṁ bhaktān bhr̥tyān na śādhi hi ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
jagad adyābhiṣiktaṁ tvām anupaśyatu sarvataḥ |
pratapantam ivādityaṁ madhyāhne dīptatejasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tūryasaṁghātanirghoṣaiḥ kāñcīnūpuranisvanaiḥ |
madhurair gītaśabdaiś ca pratibudhyasva śeṣva ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
yāvad āvartate cakraṁ yāvatī ca vasuṁdharā |
tāvat tvam iha sarvasya svāmitvam abhivartaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
bharatasya vacaḥ śrutvā rāmaḥ parapuraṁjayaḥ |
tatheti pratijagrāha niṣasādāsane śubhe ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tataḥ śatrughnavacanān nipuṇāḥ śmaśruvardhakāḥ |
sukhahastāḥ suśīghrāś ca rāghavaṁ paryupāsata ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
pūrvaṁ tu bharate snāte lakṣmaṇe ca mahābale |
sugrīve vānarendre ca rākṣasendre vibhīṣaṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
viśodhitajaṭaḥ snātaś citramālyānulepanaḥ |
mahārhavasanopetas tasthau tatra śriyā jvalan ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
pratikarma ca rāmasya kārayām āsa vīryavān |
lakṣmaṇasya ca lakṣmīvān ikṣvākukulavardhanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
pratikarma ca sītāyāḥ sarvā daśarathastriyaḥ |
ātmanaiva tadā cakrur manasvinyo manoharam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tato rāghavapatnīnāṁ sarvāsām eva śobhanam |
cakāra yatnāt kausalyā prahr̥ṣṭā putravatsalā ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tataḥ śatrughnavacanāt sumantro nāma sārathiḥ |
yojayitvābhicakrāma rathaṁ sarvāṅgaśobhanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
arkamaṇḍalasaṁkāśaṁ divyaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rathaṁ sthitam |
āruroha mahābāhū rāmaḥ satyaparākramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
ayodhyāyāṁ tu sacivā rājño daśarathasya ye |
purohitaṁ puraskr̥tya mantrayām āsur arthavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
mantrayan rāmavr̥ddhyarthaṁ vr̥ttyarthaṁ nagarasya ca |
sarvam evābhiṣekārthaṁ jayārhasya mahātmanaḥ |
kartum arhatha rāmasya yad yan maṅgalapūrvakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
iti te mantriṇaḥ sarve saṁdiśya tu purohitam |
nagarān niryayus tūrṇaṁ rāmadarśanabuddhayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
hariyuktaṁ sahasrākṣo ratham indra ivānaghaḥ |
prayayau ratham āsthāya rāmo nagaram uttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
jagrāha bharato raśmīñ śatrughnaś chatram ādade |
lakṣmaṇo vyajanaṁ tasya mūrdhni saṁparyavījayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
śvetaṁ ca vālavyajanaṁ sugrīvo vānareśvaraḥ |
aparaṁ candrasaṁkāśaṁ rākṣasendro vibhīṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
r̥ṣisaṁghair tadākāśe devaiś ca samarudgaṇaiḥ |
stūyamānasya rāmasya śuśruve madhuradhvaniḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tataḥ śatruṁjayaṁ nāma kuñjaraṁ parvatopamam |
āruroha mahātejāḥ sugrīvo vānareśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
navanāgasahasrāṇi yayur āsthāya vānarāḥ |
mānuṣaṁ vigrahaṁ kr̥tvā sarvābharaṇabhūṣitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
śaṅkhaśabdapraṇādaiś ca dundubhīnāṁ ca nisvanaiḥ |
prayayū puruṣavyāghras tāṁ purīṁ harmyamālinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
dadr̥śus te samāyāntaṁ rāghavaṁ sapuraḥsaram |
virājamānaṁ vapuṣā rathenātirathaṁ tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
te vardhayitvā kākutsthaṁ rāmeṇa pratinanditāḥ |
anujagmur mahātmānaṁ bhrātr̥bhiḥ parivāritam ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
amātyair brāhmaṇaiś caiva tathā prakr̥tibhir vr̥taḥ |
śriyā viruruce rāmo nakṣatrair iva candramāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
sa purogāmibhis tūryais tālasvastikapāṇibhiḥ |
pravyāharadbhir muditair maṅgalāni yayau vr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
akṣataṁ jātarūpaṁ ca gāvaḥ kanyās tathā dvijāḥ |
narā modakahastāś ca rāmasya purato yayuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
sakhyaṁ ca rāmaḥ sugrīve prabhāvaṁ cānilātmaje |
vānarāṇāṁ ca tat karma vyācacakṣe ’tha mantriṇām |
śrutvā ca vismayaṁ jagmur ayodhyāpuravāsinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
dyutimān etad ākhyāya rāmo vānarasaṁvr̥taḥ |
hr̥ṣṭapuṣṭajanākīrṇām ayodhyāṁ praviveśa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
tato hy abhyucchrayan paurāḥ patākās te gr̥he gr̥he |
aikṣvākādhyuṣitaṁ ramyam āsasāda pitur gr̥ham ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
pitur bhavanam āsādya praviśya ca mahātmanaḥ |
kausalyāṁ ca sumitrāṁ ca kaikeyīṁ cābhyavādayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
athābravīd rājaputro bharataṁ dharmiṇāṁ varam |
athopahitayā vācā madhuraṁ raghunandanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
yac ca madbhavanaṁ śreṣṭhaṁ sāśokavanikaṁ mahat |
muktāvaidūryasaṁkīrṇaṁ sugrīvasya nivedaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā bharataḥ satyavikramaḥ |
pāṇau gr̥hītvā sugrīvaṁ praviveśa tam ālayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
tatas tailapradīpāṁś ca paryaṅkāstaraṇāni ca |
gr̥hītvā viviśuḥ kṣipraṁ śatrughnena pracoditāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
uvāca ca mahātejāḥ sugrīvaṁ rāghavānujaḥ |
abhiṣekāya rāmasya dūtān ājñāpaya prabho ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
sauvarṇān vānarendrāṇāṁ caturṇāṁ caturo ghaṭān |
dadau kṣipraṁ sa sugrīvaḥ sarvaratnavibhūṣitān ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
yathā pratyūṣasamaye caturṇāṁ sāgarāmbhasām |
pūrṇair ghaṭaiḥ pratīkṣadhvaṁ tathā kuruta vānarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
evam uktā mahātmāno vānarā vāraṇopamāḥ |
utpetur gaganaṁ śīghraṁ garuḍā iva śīghragāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
jāmbavāṁś ca hanūmāṁś ca vegadarśī ca vānaraḥ |
r̥ṣabhaś caiva kalaśāñ jalapūrṇān athānayan |
nadīśatānāṁ pañcānāṁ jale kumbhair upāharan ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
pūrvāt samudrāt kalaśaṁ jalapūrṇam athānayat |
suṣeṇaḥ sattvasaṁpannaḥ sarvaratnavibhūṣitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
r̥ṣabho dakṣiṇāt tūrṇaṁ samudrāj jalam āharat ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
raktacandanakarpūraiḥ saṁvr̥taṁ kāñcanaṁ ghaṭam |
gavayaḥ paścimāt toyam ājahāra mahārṇavāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
52.
ratnakumbhena mahatā śītaṁ mārutavikramaḥ |
uttarāc ca jalaṁ śīghraṁ garuḍānilavikramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
53.
abhiṣekāya rāmasya śatrughnaḥ sacivaiḥ saha |
purohitāya śreṣṭhāya suhr̥dbhyaś ca nyavedayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
54.
tataḥ sa prayato vr̥ddho vasiṣṭho brāhmaṇaiḥ saha |
rāmaṁ ratnamayo pīṭhe sahasītaṁ nyaveśayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
55.
vasiṣṭho vāmadevaś ca jābālir atha kāśyapaḥ |
kātyāyanaḥ suyajñaś ca gautamo vijayas tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
56.
abhyaṣiñcan naravyāghraṁ prasannena sugandhinā |
salilena sahasrākṣaṁ vasavo vāsavaṁ yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
57.
r̥tvigbhir brāhmaṇaiḥ pūrvaṁ kanyābhir mantribhis tathā |
yodhaiś caivābhyaṣiñcaṁs te saṁprahr̥ṣṭāḥ sanaigamaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
58.
sarvauṣadhirasaiś cāpi daivatair nabhasi sthitaiḥ |
caturhir lokapālaiś ca sarvair devaiś ca saṁgataiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
59.
chatraṁ tasya ca jagrāha śatrughnaḥ pāṇḍuraṁ śubham |
śvetaṁ ca vālavyajanaṁ sugrīvo vānareśvaraḥ |
aparaṁ candrasaṁkāśaṁ rākṣasendro vibhīṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
60.
mālāṁ jvalantīṁ vapuṣā kāñcanīṁ śatapuṣkarām |
rāghavāya dadau vāyur vāsavena pracoditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
61.
sarvaratnasamāyuktaṁ maṇiratnavibhūṣitam |
muktāhāraṁ narendrāya dadau śakrapracoditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
62.
prajagur devagandharvā nanr̥tuś cāpsaro gaṇāḥ |
abhiṣeke tad arhasya tadā rāmasya dhīmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
63.
bhūmiḥ sasyavatī caiva phalavantaś ca pādapāḥ |
gandhavanti ca puṣpāṇi babhūvū rāghavotsave ||

=== meaning ===

---
64.
sahasraśatam aśvānāṁ dhenūnāṁ ca gavāṁ tathā |
dadau śataṁ vr̥ṣān pūrvaṁ dvijebhyo manujarṣabhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
65.
triṁśatkoṭīr hiraṇyasya brāhmaṇebhyo dadau punaḥ |
nānābharaṇavastrāṇi mahārhāṇi ca rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
66.
arkaraśmipratīkāśāṁ kāñcanīṁ maṇivigrahām |
sugrīvāya srajaṁ divyāṁ prāyacchan manujarṣabhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
67.
vaidūryamaṇicitre ca vajraratnavibhūṣite |
vāliputrāya dhr̥timān aṅgadāyāṅgade dadau ||

=== meaning ===

---
68.
maṇipravarajuṣṭaṁ ca muktāhāram anuttamam |
sītāyai pradadau rāmaś candraraśmisamaprabham ||

=== meaning ===

---
69.
araje vāsasī divye śubhāny ābharaṇāni ca |
avekṣamāṇā vaidehī pradadau vāyusūnave ||

=== meaning ===

---
70.
avamucyātmanaḥ kaṇṭhād dhāraṁ janakanandinī |
avaikṣata harīn sarvān bhartāraṁ ca muhur muhuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
71.
tām iṅgitajñaḥ saṁprekṣya babhāṣe janakātmajām |
pradehi subhage hāraṁ yasya tuṣṭāsi bhāmini ||

=== meaning ===

---
72.
pauruṣaṁ vikramo buddhir yasminn etāni nityadā |
dadau sā vāyuputrāya taṁ hāram asitekṣaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
73.
hanūmāṁs tena hāreṇa śuśubhe vānararṣabhaḥ |
candrāṁśucayagaureṇa śvetābhreṇa yathācalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
74.
tato dvivida maindābhyāṁ nīlāya ca paraṁtapaḥ |
sarvān kāmaguṇān vīkṣya pradadau vasudhādhipaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
75.
sarvavānaravr̥ddhāś ca ye cānye vānareśvarāḥ |
vāsobhir bhūṣaṇaiś caiva yathārhaṁ pratipūjitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
76.
yathārhaṁ pūjitāḥ sarve kāmai ratnaiś ca puṣkalair |
prahr̥ṣṭamanasaḥ sarve jagmur eva yathāgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
77.
rāghavaḥ paramodāraḥ śaśāsa parayā mudā |
uvāca lakṣmaṇaṁ rāmo dharmajñaṁ dharmavatsalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
78.
ātiṣṭha dharmajña mayā sahemāṁ; gāṁ pūrvarājādhyuṣitāṁ balena |
tulyaṁ mayā tvaṁ pitr̥bhir dhr̥tā yā; tāṁ yauvarājye dhuram udvahasva ||

=== meaning ===

---
79.
sarvātmanā paryanunīyamāno; yadā na saumitrir upaiti yogam |
niyujyamāno bhuvi yauvarājye; tato ’bhyaṣiñcad bharataṁ mahātmā ||

=== meaning ===

---
80.
rāghavaś cāpi dharmātmā prāpya rājyam anuttamam |
īje bahuvidhair yajñaiḥ sasuhr̥dbhrātr̥bāndhavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
81.
pauṇḍarīkāśvamedhābhyāṁ vājapeyena cāsakr̥t |
anyaiś ca vividhair yajñair ayajat pārthivarṣabhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
82.
rājyaṁ daśasahasrāṇi prāpya varṣāṇi rāghavaḥ |
śatāśvamedhān ājahre sadaśvān bhūridakṣiṇān ||

=== meaning ===

---
83.
ājānulambibāhuś ca mahāskandhaḥ pratāpavān |
lakṣmaṇānucaro rāmaḥ pr̥thivīm anvapālayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
84.
na paryadevan vidhavā na ca vyālakr̥taṁ bhayam |
na vyādhijaṁ bhayaṁ vāpi rāme rājyaṁ praśāsati ||

=== meaning ===

---
85.
nirdasyur abhaval loko nānarthaḥ kaṁ cid aspr̥śat |
na ca sma vr̥ddhā bālānāṁ pretakāryāṇi kurvate ||

=== meaning ===

---
86.
sarvaṁ muditam evāsīt sarvo dharmaparo ’bhavat |
rāmam evānupaśyanto nābhyahiṁsan parasparam ||

=== meaning ===

---
87.
āsan varṣasahasrāṇi tathā putrasahasriṇaḥ |
nirāmayā viśokāś ca rāme rājyaṁ praśāsati ||

=== meaning ===

---
88.
nityapuṣpā nityaphalās taravaḥ skandhavistr̥tāḥ |
kālavarṣī ca parjanyaḥ sukhasparśaś ca mārutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
89.
svakarmasu pravartante tuṣṭhāḥ svair eva karmabhiḥ |
āsan prajā dharmaparā rāme śāsati nānr̥tāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
90.
sarve lakṣaṇasaṁpannāḥ sarve dharmaparāyaṇāḥ |
daśavarṣasahasrāṇi rāmo rājyam akārayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
