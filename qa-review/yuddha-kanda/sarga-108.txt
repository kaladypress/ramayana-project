=== Sarga 108 ===

---
1.
pratiprayāte kākutsthe mahendraḥ pākaśāsanaḥ |
abravīt paramaprīto rāghavaṁ prāñjaliṁ sthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
amoghaṁ darśanaṁ rāma tavāsmākaṁ paraṁtapa |
prītiyukto ’smi tena tvaṁ brūhi yan manasecchasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
evam uktas tu kākutsthaḥ pratyuvāca kr̥tāñjaliḥ |
lakṣmaṇena saha bhrātrā sītayā cāpi bhāryayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
yadi prītiḥ samutpannā mayi sarvasureśvara |
vakṣyāmi kuru me satyaṁ vacanaṁ vadatāṁ vara ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
mama hetoḥ parākrāntā ye gatā yamasādanam |
te sarve jīvitaṁ prāpya samuttiṣṭhantu vānarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
matpriyeṣv abhiraktāś ca na mr̥tyuṁ gaṇayanti ca |
tvatprasādāt sameyus te varam etad ahaṁ vr̥ṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
nīrujān nirvraṇāṁś caiva saṁpannabalapauruṣān |
golāṅgūlāṁs tathaivarkṣān draṣṭum icchāmi mānada ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
akāle cāpi mukhyāni mūlāni ca phalāni ca |
nadyaś ca vimalās tatra tiṣṭheyur yatra vānarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
śrutvā tu vacanaṁ tasya rāghavasya mahātmanaḥ |
mahendraḥ pratyuvācedaṁ vacanaṁ prītilakṣaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
mahān ayaṁ varas tāta tvayokto raghunandana |
samutthāsyanti harayaḥ suptā nidrākṣaye yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
suhr̥dbhir bāndhavaiś caiva jñātibhiḥ svajanena ca |
sarva eva sameṣyanti saṁyuktāḥ parayā mudā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
akāle puṣpaśabalāḥ phalavantaś ca pādapāḥ |
bhaviṣyanti maheṣvāsa nadyaś ca salilāyutāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
savraṇaiḥ prathamaṁ gātraiḥ saṁvr̥tair nivraṇaiḥ punaḥ |
babhūvur vānarāḥ sarve kim etad iti vismitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
kākutsthaṁ paripūrṇārthaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā sarve surottamāḥ |
ūcus te prathamaṁ stutvā stavārhaṁ sahalakṣmaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
gacchāyodhyām ito vīra visarjaya ca vānarān |
maithilīṁ sāntvayasvainām anuraktāṁ tapasvinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
bhrātaraṁ paśya bharataṁ tvacchokād vratacāriṇam |
abhiṣecaya cātmānaṁ paurān gatvā praharṣaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
evam uktvā tam āmantrya rāmaṁ saumitriṇā saha |
vimānaiḥ sūryasaṁkāśair hr̥ṣṭā jagmuḥ surā divam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
abhivādya ca kākutsthaḥ sarvāṁs tāṁs tridaśottamān |
lakṣmaṇena saha bhrātrā vāsam ājñāpayat tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tatas tu sā lakṣmaṇarāmapālitā; mahācamūr hr̥ṣṭajanā yaśasvinī |
śriyā jvalantī virarāja sarvato; niśāpraṇīteva hi śītaraśminā ||

=== meaning ===

---
