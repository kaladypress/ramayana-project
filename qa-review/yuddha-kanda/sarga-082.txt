=== Sarga 82 ===

---
1.
tāni nāgasahasrāṇi sārohāṇāṁ ca vājinām |
rathānāṁ cāgnivarṇānāṁ sadhvajānāṁ sahasraśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
rākṣasānāṁ sahasrāṇi gadāparighayodhinām |
kāñcanadhvajacitrāṇāṁ śūrāṇāṁ kāmarūpiṇām ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
nihatāni śarais tīkṣṇais taptakāñcanabhūṣaṇaiḥ |
rāvaṇena prayuktāni rāmeṇākliṣṭakarmaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
dr̥ṣṭvā śrutvā ca saṁbhrāntā hataśeṣā niśācarāḥ |
rākṣasyaś ca samāgamya dīnāś cintāpariplutāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
vidhavā hataputrāś ca krośantyo hatabāndhavāḥ |
rākṣasyaḥ saha saṁgamya duḥkhārtāḥ paryadevayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
kathaṁ śūrpaṇakhā vr̥ddhā karālā nirṇatodarī |
āsasāda vane rāmaṁ kandarpam iva rūpiṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sukumāraṁ mahāsattvaṁ sarvabhūtahite ratam |
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā lokavadhyā sā hīnarūpā prakāmitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
kathaṁ sarvaguṇair hīnā guṇavantaṁ mahaujasaṁ |
sumukhaṁ durmukhī rāmaṁ kāmayām āsa rākṣasī ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
janasyāsyālpabhāgyatvāt palinī śvetamūrdhajā |
akāryam apahāsyaṁ ca sarvalokavigarhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
rākṣasānāṁ vināśāya dūṣaṇasya kharasya ca |
cakārāpratirūpā sā rāghavasya pradharṣaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tan nimittam idaṁ vairaṁ rāvaṇena kr̥taṁ mahat |
vadhāya nītā sā sītā daśagrīveṇa rakṣasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
na ca sītāṁ daśagrīvaḥ prāpnoti janakātmajām |
baddhaṁ balavatā vairam akṣayaṁ rāghaveṇa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
vaidehīṁ prārthayānaṁ taṁ virādhaṁ prekṣya rākṣasaṁ |
hatam ekena rāmeṇa paryāptaṁ tannidarśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
caturdaśasahasrāṇi rakṣasāṁ bhīmakarmaṇām |
nihatāni janasthāne śarair agniśikhopamaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
kharaś ca nihataḥ saṁkhye dūṣaṇas triśirās tathā |
śarair ādityasaṁkāśaiḥ paryāptaṁ tannidarśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
hato yojanabāhuś ca kabandho rudhirāśanaḥ |
krodhārto vinadan so ’tha paryāptaṁ tannidarśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
jaghāna balinaṁ rāmaḥ sahasranayanātmajam |
bālinaṁ meghasaṁkāśaṁ paryāptaṁ tannidarśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
r̥śyamūke vasañ śaile dīno bhagnamanorathaḥ |
sugrīvaḥ sthāpito rājye paryāptaṁ tannidarśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
dharmārthasahitaṁ vākyaṁ sarveṣāṁ rakṣasāṁ hitam |
yuktaṁ vibhīṣaṇenoktaṁ mohāt tasya na rocate ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
vibhīṣaṇavacaḥ kuryād yadi sma dhanadānujaḥ |
śmaśānabhūtā duḥkhārtā neyaṁ laṅkā purī bhavet ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
kumbhakarṇaṁ hataṁ śrutvā rāghaveṇa mahābalam |
priyaṁ cendrajitaṁ putraṁ rāvaṇo nāvabudhyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
mama putro mama bhrātā mama bhartā raṇe hataḥ |
ity evaṁ śrūyate śabdo rākṣasānāṁ kule kule ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
rathāś cāśvāś ca nāgāś ca hatāḥ śatasahasraśaḥ |
raṇe rāmeṇa śūreṇa rākṣasāś ca padātayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
rudro vā yadi vā viṣṇur mahendro vā śatakratuḥ |
hanti no rāmarūpeṇa yadi vā svayam antakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
hatapravīrā rāmeṇa nirāśā jīvite vayam |
apaśyantyo bhayasyāntam anāthā vilapāmahe ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
rāmahastād daśagrīvaḥ śūro dattavaro yudhi |
idaṁ bhayaṁ mahāghoram utpannaṁ nāvabudhyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
na devā na ca gandharvā na piśācā na rākṣasāḥ |
upasr̥ṣṭaṁ paritrātuṁ śaktā rāmeṇa saṁyuge ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
utpātāś cāpi dr̥śyante rāvaṇasya raṇe raṇe |
kathayiṣyanti rāmeṇa rāvaṇasya nibarhaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
pitāmahena prītena devadānavarākṣasaiḥ |
rāvaṇasyābhayaṁ dattaṁ mānuṣebhyo na yācitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
tad idaṁ mānuṣān manye prāptaṁ niḥsaṁśayaṁ bhayam |
jīvitāntakaraṁ ghoraṁ rakṣasāṁ rāvaṇasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
pīḍyamānās tu balinā varadānena rakṣasā |
dīptais tapobhir vibudhāḥ pitāmaham apūjayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
devatānāṁ hitārthāya mahātmā vai pitāmahaḥ |
uvāca devatāḥ sarvā idaṁ tuṣṭo mahad vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
adya prabhr̥ti lokāṁs trīn sarve dānavarākṣasāḥ |
bhayena prāvr̥tā nityaṁ vicariṣyanti śāśvatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
daivatais tu samāgamya sarvaiś cendrapurogamaiḥ |
vr̥ṣadhvajas tripurahā mahādevaḥ prasāditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
prasannas tu mahādevo devān etad vaco ’bravīt |
utpatsyati hitārthaṁ vo nārī rakṣaḥkṣayāvahā ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
eṣā devaiḥ prayuktā tu kṣud yathā dānavān purā |
bhakṣayiṣyati naḥ sītā rākṣasaghnī sarāvaṇān ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
rāvaṇasyāpanītena durvinītasya durmateḥ |
ayaṁ niṣṭānako ghoraḥ śokena samabhiplutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
taṁ na paśyāmahe loke yo naḥ śaraṇado bhavet |
rāghaveṇopasr̥ṣṭānāṁ kāleneva yugakṣaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
itīva sarvā rajanīcarastriyaḥ; parasparaṁ saṁparirabhya bāhubhiḥ |
viṣedur ārtātibhayābhipīḍitā; vinedur uccaiś ca tadā sudāruṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
