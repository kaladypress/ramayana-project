=== Sarga 80 ===

---
1.
tataḥ paulastya sacivāḥ śrutvā cendrajitaṁ hatam |
ācacakṣur abhijñāya daśagrīvāya savyathāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
yuddhe hato mahārāja lakṣmaṇena tavātmajaḥ |
vibhīṣaṇasahāyena miṣatāṁ no mahādyute ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
śūraḥ śūreṇa saṁgamya saṁyugeṣv aparājitaḥ |
lakṣṇanena hataḥ śūraḥ putras te vibudhendrajit ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
sa taṁ pratibhayaṁ śrutvā vadhaṁ putrasya dāruṇam |
ghoram indrajitaḥ saṁkhye kaśmalaṁ prāviśan mahat ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
upalabhya cirāt saṁjñāṁ rājā rākṣasapuṁgavaḥ |
putraśokārdito dīno vilalāpākulendriyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
hā rākṣasacamūmukhya mama vatsa mahāratha |
jitvendraṁ katham adya tvaṁ lakṣmaṇasya vaśaṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
nanu tvam iṣubhiḥ kruddho bhindyāḥ kālāntakāv api |
mandarasyāpi śr̥ṅgāṇi kiṁ punar lakṣmaṇaṁ raṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
adya vaivasvato rājā bhūyo bahumato mama |
yenādya tvaṁ mahābāho saṁyuktaḥ kāladharmaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
eṣa panthāḥ suyodhānāṁ sarvāmaragaṇeṣv api |
yaḥ kr̥te hanyate bhartuḥ sa pumān svargam r̥cchati ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
adya devagaṇāḥ sarve lokapālās tatharṣayaḥ |
hatam indrajitaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā sukhaṁ svapsyanti nirbhayāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
adya lokās trayaḥ kr̥tsnāḥ pr̥thivī ca sakānanā |
ekenendrajitā hīnā śūṇyeva pratibhāti me ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
adya nairr̥takanyāyāṁ śroṣyāmy antaḥpure ravam |
kareṇusaṁghasya yathā ninādaṁ girigahvare ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
yauvarājyaṁ ca laṅkāṁ ca rakṣāṁsi ca paraṁtapa |
mātaraṁ māṁ ca bhāryāṁ ca kva gato ’si vihāya naḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
mama nāma tvayā vīra gatasya yamasādanam |
pretakāryāṇi kāryāṇi viparīte hi vartase ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa tvaṁ jīvati sugrīve rāghave ca salakṣmaṇe |
mama śalyam anuddhr̥tya kva gato ’si vihāya naḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
evamādivilāpārtaṁ rāvaṇaṁ rākṣasādhipam |
āviveśa mahān kopaḥ putravyasanasaṁbhavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
ghoraṁ prakr̥tyā rūpaṁ tat tasya krodhāgnimūrchitam |
babhūva rūpaṁ rudrasya kruddhasyeva durāsadam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tasya kruddhasya netrābhyāṁ prāpatann asrabindavaḥ |
dīptābhyām iva dīpābhyāṁ sārciṣaḥ snehabindavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
dantān vidaśatas tasya śrūyate daśanasvanaḥ |
yantrasyāveṣṭyamānasya mahato dānavair iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
kālāgnir iva saṁkruddho yāṁ yāṁ diśam avaikṣata |
tasyāṁ tasyāṁ bhayatrastā rākṣasāḥ saṁnililyire ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tam antakam iva kruddhaṁ carācaracikhādiṣum |
vīkṣamāṇaṁ diśaḥ sarvā rākṣasā nopacakramuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tataḥ paramasaṁkruddho rāvaṇo rākṣasādhipaḥ |
abravīd rakṣasāṁ madhye saṁstambhayiṣur āhave ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
mayā varṣasahasrāṇi caritvā duścaraṁ tapaḥ |
teṣu teṣv avakāśeṣu svayambhūḥ paritoṣitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tasyaiva tapaso vyuṣṭyā prasādāc ca svayambhuvaḥ |
nāsurebhyo na devebhyo bhayaṁ mama kadā cana ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
kavacaṁ brahmadattaṁ me yad ādityasamaprabham |
devāsuravimardeṣu na bhinnaṁ vajraśaktibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tena mām adya saṁyuktaṁ rathastham iha saṁyuge |
pratīyāt ko ’dya mām ājau sākṣād api puraṁdaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
yat tadābhiprasannena saśaraṁ kārmukaṁ mahat |
devāsuravimardeṣu mama dattaṁ svayambhuvā ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
adya tūryaśatair bhīmaṁ dhanur utthāpyatāṁ mahat |
rāmalakṣmaṇayor eva vadhāya paramāhave ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
sa putravadhasaṁtaptaḥ śūraḥ krodhavaśaṁ gataḥ |
samīkṣya rāvaṇo buddhyā sītāṁ hantuṁ vyavasyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
pratyavekṣya tu tāmrākṣaḥ sughoro ghoradarśanān |
dīno dīnasvarān sarvāṁs tān uvāca niśācarān ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
māyayā mama vatsena vañcanārthaṁ vanaukasām |
kiṁ cid eva hataṁ tatra sīteyam iti darśitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
tad idaṁ satyam evāhaṁ kariṣye priyam ātmanaḥ |
vaidehīṁ nāśayiṣyāmi kṣatrabandhum anuvratām |
ity evam uktvā sacivān khaḍgam āśu parāmr̥śat ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
uddhr̥tya guṇasaṁpannaṁ vimalāmbaravarcasaṁ |
niṣpapāta sa vegena sabhāyāḥ sacivair vr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
rāvaṇaḥ putraśokena bhr̥śam ākulacetanaḥ |
saṁkruddhaḥ khaḍgam ādāya sahasā yatra maithilī ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
vrajantaṁ rākṣasaṁ prekṣya siṁhanādaṁ pracukruśuḥ |
ūcuś cānyonyam āśliṣya saṁkruddhaṁ prekṣya rākṣasāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
adyainaṁ tāv ubhau dr̥ṣṭvā bhrātarau pravyathiṣyataḥ |
lokapālā hi catvāraḥ kruddhenānena nirjitāḥ |
bahavaḥ śatravaś cānye saṁyugeṣv abhipātitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
teṣāṁ saṁjalpamānānām aśokavanikāṁ gatām |
abhidudrāva vaidehīṁ rāvaṇaḥ krodhamūrchitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
vāryamāṇaḥ susaṁkruddhaḥ suhr̥dbhir hitabuddhibhiḥ |
abhyadhāvata saṁkruddhaḥ khe graho rohiṇīm iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
maithilī rakṣyamāṇā tu rākṣasībhir aninditā |
dadarśa rākṣasaṁ kruddhaṁ nistriṁśavaradhāriṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
taṁ niśāmya sanistriṁśaṁ vyathitā janakātmajā |
nivāryamāṇaṁ bahuśaḥ suhr̥dbhir anivartinam ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
yathāyaṁ mām abhikruddhaḥ samabhidravati svayam |
vadhiṣyati sanāthāṁ mām anāthām iva durmatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
bahuśaś codayām āsa bhartāraṁ mām anuvratām |
bhāryā bhava ramasyeti pratyākhyāto ’bhavan mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
so ’yaṁ mām anupasthānād vyaktaṁ nairāśyam āgataḥ |
krodhamohasamāviṣṭo nihantuṁ māṁ samudyataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
atha vā tau naravyāghrau bhrātarau rāmalakṣmaṇau |
mannimittam anāryeṇa samare ’dya nipātitau |
aho dhin mannimitto ’yaṁ vināśo rājaputrayoḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
hanūmato hi tadvākyaṁ na kr̥taṁ kṣudrayā mayā |
yady ahaṁ tasya pr̥ṣṭhena tadāyāsam aninditā |
nādyaivam anuśoceyaṁ bhartur aṅkagatā satī ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
manye tu hr̥dayaṁ tasyāḥ kausalyāyāḥ phaliṣyati |
ekaputrā yadā putraṁ vinaṣṭaṁ śroṣyate yudhi ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
sā hi janma ca bālyaṁ ca yauvanaṁ ca mahātmanaḥ |
dharmakāryāṇi rūpaṁ ca rudatī saṁsramiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
nirāśā nihate putre dattvā śrāddham acetanā |
agnim ārokṣyate nūnam apo vāpi pravekṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
dhig astu kubjām asatīṁ mantharāṁ pāpaniścayām |
yannimittam idaṁ duḥkhaṁ kausalyā pratipatsyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
ity evaṁ maithilīṁ dr̥ṣṭvā vilapantīṁ tapasvinīm |
rohiṇīm iva candreṇa vinā grahavaśaṁ gatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
supārśvo nāma medhāvī rāvaṇaṁ rākṣaseśvaram |
nivāryamāṇaṁ sacivair idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
52.
kathaṁ nāma daśagrīva sākṣād vaiśravaṇānuja |
hantum icchasi vaidehīṁ krodhād dharmam apāsya hi ||

=== meaning ===

---
53.
veda vidyāvrata snātaḥ svadharmanirataḥ sadā |
striyāḥ kasmād vadhaṁ vīra manyase rākṣaseśvara ||

=== meaning ===

---
54.
maithilīṁ rūpasaṁpannāṁ pratyavekṣasva pārthiva |
tvam eva tu sahāsmābhī rāghave krodham utsr̥ja ||

=== meaning ===

---
55.
abhyutthānaṁ tvam adyaiva kr̥ṣṇapakṣacaturdaśīm |
kr̥tvā niryāhy amāvāsyāṁ vijayāya balair vr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
56.
śūro dhīmān rathī khaḍgī rathapravaram āsthitaḥ |
hatvā dāśarathiṁ rāmaṁ bhavān prāpsyati maithilīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
57.
sa tad durātmā suhr̥dā niveditaṁ; vacaḥ sudharmyaṁ pratigr̥hya rāvaṇaḥ |
gr̥haṁ jagāmātha tataś ca vīryavān; punaḥ sabhāṁ ca prayayau suhr̥dvr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
