=== Sarga 9 ===

---
1.
tato nikumbho rabhasaḥ sūryaśatrur mahābalaḥ |
suptaghno yajñakopaś ca mahāpārśvo mahodaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
agniketuś ca durdharṣo raśmiketuś ca rākṣasaḥ |
indrajic ca mahātejā balavān rāvaṇātmajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
prahasto ’tha virūpākṣo vajradaṁṣṭro mahābalaḥ |
dhūmrākṣaś cātikāyaś ca durmukhaś caiva rākṣasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
parighān paṭṭasān prāsāñ śaktiśūlaparaśvadhān |
cāpāni ca sabāṇāni khaḍgāṁś ca vipulāñ śitān ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
pragr̥hya paramakruddhāḥ samutpatya ca rākṣasāḥ |
abruvan rāvaṇaṁ sarve pradīptā iva tejasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
adya rāmaṁ vadhiṣyāmaḥ sugrīvaṁ ca salakṣmaṇam |
kr̥paṇaṁ ca hanūmantaṁ laṅkā yena pradharṣitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tān gr̥hītāyudhān sarvān vārayitvā vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
abravīt prāñjalir vākyaṁ punaḥ pratyupaveśya tān ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
apy upāyais tribhis tāta yo ’rthaḥ prāptuṁ na śakyate |
tasya vikramakālāṁs tān yuktān āhur manīṣiṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
pramatteṣv abhiyukteṣu daivena prahateṣu ca |
vikramās tāta sidhyanti parīkṣya vidhinā kr̥tāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
apramattaṁ kathaṁ taṁ tu vijigīṣuṁ bale sthitam |
jitaroṣaṁ durādharṣaṁ pradharṣayitum icchatha ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
samudraṁ laṅghayitvā tu ghoraṁ nadanadīpatim |
kr̥taṁ hanumatā karma duṣkaraṁ tarkayeta kaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
balāny aparimeyāni vīryāṇi ca niśācarāḥ |
pareṣāṁ sahasāvajñā na kartavyā kathaṁ cana ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
kiṁ ca rākṣasarājasya rāmeṇāpakr̥taṁ purā |
ājahāra janasthānād yasya bhāryāṁ yaśasvinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
kharo yady ativr̥ttas tu rāmeṇa nihato raṇe |
avaśyaṁ prāṇināṁ prāṇā rakṣitavyā yathā balam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
etannimittaṁ vaidehī bhayaṁ naḥ sumahad bhavet |
āhr̥tā sā parityājyā kalahārthe kr̥te na kim ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
na naḥ kṣamaṁ vīryavatā tena dharmānuvartinā |
vairaṁ nirarthakaṁ kartuṁ dīyatām asya maithilī ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
yāvan na sagajāṁ sāśvāṁ bahuratnasamākulām |
purīṁ dārayate bāṇair dīyatām asya maithilī ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
yāvat sughorā mahatī durdharṣā harivāhinī |
nāvaskandati no laṅkāṁ tāvat sītā pradīyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
vinaśyed dhi purī laṅkā śūrāḥ sarve ca rākṣasāḥ |
rāmasya dayitā patnī na svayaṁ yadi dīyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
prasādaye tvāṁ bandhutvāt kuruṣva vacanaṁ mama |
hitaṁ pathyaṁ tv ahaṁ brūmi dīyatām asya maithilī ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
purā śaratsūryamarīcisaṁnibhān; navāgrapuṅkhān sudr̥ḍhān nr̥pātmajaḥ |
sr̥jaty amoghān viśikhān vadhāya te; pradīyatāṁ dāśarathāya maithilī ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tyajasva kopaṁ sukhadharmanāśanaṁ; bhajasva dharmaṁ ratikīrtivardhanam |
prasīda jīvema saputrabāndhavāḥ; pradīyatāṁ dāśarathāya maithilī ||

=== meaning ===

---
