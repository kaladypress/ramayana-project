=== Sarga 36 ===

---
1.
tato dyāṁ pr̥thivīṁ caiva vīkṣamāṇā vanaukasaḥ |
dadr̥śuḥ saṁtatau bāṇair bhrātarau rāmalakṣmaṇau ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
vr̥ṣṭvevoparate deve kr̥takarmaṇi rākṣase |
ājagāmātha taṁ deśaṁ sasugrīvo vibhīṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
nīladvividamaindāś ca suṣeṇasumukhāṅgadāḥ |
tūrṇaṁ hanumatā sārdham anvaśocanta rāghavau ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
niśceṣṭau mandaniḥśvāsau śoṇitaughapariplutau |
śarajālācitau stabdhau śayānau śaratalpayoḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
niḥśvasantau yathā sarpau niśceṣṭau mandavikramau |
rudhirasrāvadigdhāṅgau tāpanīyāv iva dhvajau ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tau vīraśayane vīrau śayānau mandaceṣṭitau |
yūthapais taiḥ parivr̥tau bāṣpavyākulalocanaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
rāghavau patitau dr̥ṣṭvā śarajālasamāvr̥tau |
babhūvur vyathitāḥ sarve vānarāḥ savibhīṣaṇāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
antarikṣaṁ nirīkṣanto diśaḥ sarvāś ca vānarāḥ |
na cainaṁ māyayā channaṁ dadr̥śū rāvaṇiṁ raṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
taṁ tu māyāpraticchinnaṁ māyayaiva vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
vīkṣamāṇo dadarśātha bhrātuḥ putram avasthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tam apratima karmāṇam apratidvandvam āhave |
dadarśāntarhitaṁ vīraṁ varadānād vibhīṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
indrajit tv ātmanaḥ karma tau śayānau samīkṣya ca |
uvāca paramaprīto harṣayan sarvanairr̥tān ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
dūṣaṇasya ca hantārau kharasya ca mahābalau |
sāditau māmakair bāṇair bhrātarau rāmalakṣmaṇau ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
nemau mokṣayituṁ śakyāv etasmād iṣubandhanāt |
sarvair api samāgamya sarṣisaṅghaiḥ surāsuraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
yatkr̥te cintayānasya śokārtasya pitur mama |
aspr̥ṣṭvā śayanaṁ gātrais triyāmā yāti śarvatī ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
kr̥tsneyaṁ yatkr̥te laṅkā nadī varṣāsv ivākulā |
so ’yaṁ mūlaharo ’narthaḥ sarveṣāṁ nihato mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
rāmasya lakṣmaṇasyaiva sarveṣāṁ ca vanaukasām |
vikramā niṣphalāḥ sarve yathā śaradi toyadāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
evam uktvā tu tān sarvān rākṣasān paripārśvagān |
yūthapān api tān sarvāṁs tāḍayām āsa rāvaṇiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tān ardayitvā bāṇaughais trāsayitvā ca vānarān |
prajahāsa mahābāhur vacanaṁ cedam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
śarabandhena ghoreṇa mayā baddhau camūmukhe |
sahitau bhrātarāv etau niśāmayata rākṣasāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
evam uktās tu te sarve rākṣasāḥ kūṭayodhinaḥ |
paraṁ vismayam ājagmuḥ karmaṇā tena toṣitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
vineduś ca mahānādān sarve te jaladopamāḥ |
hato rāma iti jñātvā rāvaṇiṁ samapūjayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
niṣpandau tu tadā dr̥ṣṭvā tāv ubhau rāmalakṣmaṇau |
vasudhāyāṁ nirucchvāsau hatāv ity anvamanyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
harṣeṇa tu samāviṣṭa indrajit samitiṁjayaḥ |
praviveśa purīṁ laṅkāṁ harṣayan sarvanairr̥tān ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
rāmalakṣmaṇayor dr̥ṣṭvā śarīre sāyakaiś cite |
sarvāṇi cāṅgopāṅgāni sugrīvaṁ bhayam āviśat ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tam uvāca paritrastaṁ vānarendraṁ vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
sabāṣpavadanaṁ dīnaṁ śokavyākulalocanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
alaṁ trāsena sugrīva bāṣpavego nigr̥hyatām |
evaṁ prāyāṇi yuddhāni vijayo nāsti naiṣṭhikaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
saśeṣabhāgyatāsmākaṁ yadi vīra bhaviṣyati |
moham etau prahāsyete bhrātarau rāmalakṣmaṇau ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
paryavasthāpayātmānam anāthaṁ māṁ ca vānara |
satyadharmānuraktānāṁ nāsti mr̥tyukr̥taṁ bhayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
evam uktvā tatas tasya jalaklinnena pāṇinā |
sugrīvasya śubhe netre pramamārja vibhīṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
pramr̥jya vadanaṁ tasya kapirājasya dhīmataḥ |
abravīt kālasaṁprātam asaṁbhrāntam idaṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
na kālaḥ kapirājendra vaiklavyam anuvartitum |
atisneho ’py akāle ’smin maraṇāyopapadyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
tasmād utsr̥jya vaiklavyaṁ sarvakāryavināśanam |
hitaṁ rāmapurogāṇāṁ sainyānām anucintyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
atha vā rakṣyatāṁ rāmo yāvat saṁjñā viparyayaḥ |
labdhasaṁjñau tu kākutsthau bhayaṁ no vyapaneṣyataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
naitat kiṁ cana rāmasya na ca rāmo mumūrṣati |
na hy enaṁ hāsyate lakṣmīr durlabhā yā gatāyuṣām ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
tasmād āśvāsayātmānaṁ balaṁ cāśvāsaya svakam |
yāvat sarvāṇi sainyāni punaḥ saṁsthāpayāmy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
ete hy utphullanayanās trāsād āgatasādhvasāḥ |
karṇe karṇe prakathitā harayo haripuṁgava ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
māṁ tu dr̥ṣṭvā pradhāvantam anīkaṁ saṁpraharṣitum |
tyajantu harayas trāsaṁ bhuktapūrvām iva srajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
samāśvāsya tu sugrīvaṁ rākṣasendro vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
vidrutaṁ vānarānīkaṁ tat samāśvāsayat punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
indrajit tu mahāmāyaḥ sarvasainyasamāvr̥taḥ |
viveśa nagarīṁ laṅkāṁ pitaraṁ cābhyupāgamat ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
tatra rāvaṇam āsīnam abhivādya kr̥tāñjaliḥ |
ācacakṣe priyaṁ pitre nihatau rāmalakṣmaṇau ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
utpapāta tato hr̥ṣṭaḥ putraṁ ca pariṣasvaje |
rāvaṇo rakṣasāṁ madhye śrutvā śatrū nipātitau ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
upāghrāya sa mūrdhny enaṁ papraccha prītamānasaḥ |
pr̥cchate ca yathāvr̥ttaṁ pitre sarvaṁ nyavedayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
sa harṣavegānugatāntarātmā; śrutvā vacas tasya mahārathasya |
jahau jvaraṁ dāśaratheḥ samutthitaṁ; prahr̥ṣya vācābhinananda putram ||

=== meaning ===

---
