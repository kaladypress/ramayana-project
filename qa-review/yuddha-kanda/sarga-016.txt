=== Sarga 16 ===

---
1.
sabale sāgaraṁ tīrṇe rāme daśarathātmaje |
amātyau rāvaṇaḥ śrīmān abravīc chukasāraṇau ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
samagraṁ sāgaraṁ tīrṇaṁ dustaraṁ vānaraṁ balam |
abhūtapūrvaṁ rāmeṇa sāgare setubandhanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sāgare setubandhaṁ tu na śraddadhyāṁ kathaṁ cana |
avaśyaṁ cāpi saṁkhyeyaṁ tan mayā vānaraṁ balam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
bhavantau vānaraṁ sainyaṁ praviśyānupalakṣitau |
parimāṇaṁ ca vīryaṁ ca ye ca mukhyāḥ plavaṁgamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
mantriṇo ye ca rāmasya sugrīvasya ca saṁmatāḥ |
ye pūrvam abhivartante ye ca śūrāḥ plavaṁgamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
sa ca setur yathā baddhaḥ sāgare salilārṇave |
niveśaś ca yathā teṣāṁ vānarāṇāṁ mahātmanām ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
rāmasya vyavasāyaṁ ca vīryaṁ praharaṇāni ca |
lakṣmaṇasya ca vīrasya tattvato jñātum arhatha ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
kaś ca senāpatis teṣāṁ vānarāṇāṁ mahaujasām |
etaj jñātvā yathātattvaṁ śīghram agantum arhathaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
iti pratisamādiṣṭau rākṣasau śukasāraṇau |
harirūpadharau vīrau praviṣṭau vānaraṁ balam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tatas tad vānaraṁ sainyam acintyaṁ lomaharṣaṇam |
saṁkhyātuṁ nādhyagacchetāṁ tadā tau śukasāraṇau ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tat sthitaṁ parvatāgreṣu nirdareṣu guhāsu ca |
samudrasya ca tīreṣu vaneṣūpavaneṣu ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
taramāṇaṁ ca tīrṇaṁ ca tartukāmaṁ ca sarvaśaḥ |
niviṣṭaṁ niviśac caiva bhīmanādaṁ mahābalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tau dadarśa mahātejāḥ pracchannau ca vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
ācacakṣe ’tha rāmāya gr̥hītvā śukasāraṇau |
laṅkāyāḥ samanuprāptau cārau parapuraṁjayau ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tau dr̥ṣṭvā vyathitau rāmaṁ nirāśau jīvite tadā |
kr̥tāñjalipuṭau bhītau vacanaṁ cedam ūcatuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
āvām ihāgatau saumya rāvaṇaprahitāv ubhau |
parijñātuṁ balaṁ kr̥tsnaṁ tavedaṁ raghunandana ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tayos tad vacanaṁ śrutvā rāmo daśarathātmajaḥ |
abravīt prahasan vākyaṁ sarvabhūtahite rataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
yadi dr̥ṣṭaṁ balaṁ kr̥tsnaṁ vayaṁ vā susamīkṣitāḥ |
yathoktaṁ vā kr̥taṁ kāryaṁ chandataḥ pratigamyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
praviśya nagarīṁ laṅkāṁ bhavadbhyāṁ dhanadānujaḥ |
vaktavyo rakṣasāṁ rājā yathoktaṁ vacanaṁ mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
yad balaṁ ca samāśritya sītāṁ me hr̥tavān asi |
tad darśaya yathākāmaṁ sasainyaḥ sahabāndhavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
śvaḥkāle nagarīṁ laṅkāṁ saprākārāṁ satoraṇām |
rākṣasaṁ ca balaṁ paśya śarair vidhvaṁsitaṁ mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
ghoraṁ roṣam ahaṁ mokṣye balaṁ dhāraya rāvaṇa |
śvaḥkāle vajravān vajraṁ dānaveṣv iva vāsavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
iti pratisamādiṣṭau rākṣasau śukasāraṇau |
āgamya nagarīṁ laṅkām abrūtāṁ rākṣasādhipam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
vibhīṣaṇagr̥hītau tu vadhārhau rākṣaseśvara |
dr̥ṣṭvā dharmātmanā muktau rāmeṇāmitatejasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
ekasthānagatā yatra catvāraḥ puruṣarṣabhāḥ |
lokapālopamāḥ śūrāḥ kr̥tāstrā dr̥ḍhavikramāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
rāmo dāśarathiḥ śrīmām̐l lakṣmaṇaś ca vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
sugrīvaś ca mahātejā mahendrasamavikramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
ete śaktāḥ purīṁ laṅkāṁ saprākārāṁ satoraṇām |
utpāṭya saṁkrāmayituṁ sarve tiṣṭhantu vānarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
yādr̥śaṁ tasya rāmasya rūpaṁ praharaṇāni ca |
vadhiṣyati purīṁ laṅkām ekas tiṣṭhantu te trayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
rāmalakṣmaṇaguptā sā sugrīveṇa ca vāhinī |
babhūva durdharṣatarā sarvair api surāsuraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
prahr̥ṣṭarūpā dhvajinī vanaukasāṁ; mahātmanāṁ saṁprati yoddhum icchatām |
alaṁ virodhena śamo vidhīyatāṁ; pradīyatāṁ dāśarathāya maithilī ||

=== meaning ===

---
