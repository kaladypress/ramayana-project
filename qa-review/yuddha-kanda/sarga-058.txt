=== Sarga 58 ===

---
1.
narāntakaṁ hataṁ dr̥ṣṭvā cukruśur nairr̥tarṣabhāḥ |
devāntakas trimūrdhā ca paulastyaś ca mahodaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
ārūḍho meghasaṁkāśaṁ vāraṇendraṁ mahodaraḥ |
vāliputraṁ mahāvīryam abhidudrāva vīryavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
bhrātr̥vyasanasaṁtaptas tadā devāntako balī |
ādāya parighaṁ dīptam aṅgadaṁ samabhidravat ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
ratham ādityasaṁkāśaṁ yuktaṁ paramavājibhiḥ |
āsthāya triśirā vīro vāliputram athābhyayāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
sa tribhir devadarpaghnair nairr̥tendrair abhidrutaḥ |
vr̥kṣam utpāṭayām āsa mahāviṭapam aṅgadaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
devāntakāya taṁ vīraś cikṣepa sahasāṅgadaḥ |
mahāvr̥kṣaṁ mahāśākhaṁ śakro dīptam ivāśanim ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
triśirās taṁ praciccheda śarair āśīviṣopamaiḥ |
sa vr̥kṣaṁ kr̥ttam ālokya utpapāta tato ’ṅgadaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sa vavarṣa tato vr̥kṣāñ śilāś ca kapikuñjaraḥ |
tān praciccheda saṁkruddhas triśirā niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
parighāgreṇa tān vr̥kṣān babhañja ca surāntakaḥ |
triśirāś cāṅgadaṁ vīram abhidudrāva sāyakaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
gajena samabhidrutya vāliputraṁ mahodaraḥ |
jaghānorasi saṁkruddhas tomarair vajrasaṁnibhaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
devāntakaś ca saṁkruddhaḥ parigheṇa tadāṅgadam |
upagamyābhihatyāśu vyapacakrāma vegavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sa tribhir nairr̥taśreṣṭhair yugapat samabhidrutaḥ |
na vivyathe mahātejā vāliputraḥ pratāpavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
talena bhr̥śam utpatya jaghānāsya mahāgajam |
petatur locane tasya vinanāda sa vāraṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
viṣāṇaṁ cāsya niṣkr̥ṣya vāliputro mahābalaḥ |
devāntakam abhidrutya tāḍayām āsa saṁyuge ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa vihvalitasarvāṅgo vātoddhata iva drumaḥ |
lākṣārasasavarṇaṁ ca susrāva rudhiraṁ mukhāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
athāśvāsya mahātejāḥ kr̥cchrād devāntako balī |
āvidhya parighaṁ ghoram ājaghāna tadāṅgadam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
parighābhihataś cāpi vānarendrātmajas tadā |
jānubhyāṁ patito bhūmau punar evotpapāta ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
samutpatantaṁ triśirās tribhir āśīviṣopamaiḥ |
ghorair haripateḥ putraṁ lalāṭe ’bhijaghāna ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tato ’ṅgadaṁ parikṣiptaṁ tribhir nairr̥tapuṁgavaiḥ |
hanūmān api vijñāya nīlaś cāpi pratasthatuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tataś cikṣepa śailāgraṁ nīlas triśirase tadā |
tad rāvaṇasuto dhīmān bibheda niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tad bāṇaśatanirbhinnaṁ vidāritaśilātalam |
savisphuliṅgaṁ sajvālaṁ nipapāta gireḥ śiraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tato jr̥mbhitam ālokya harṣād devāntakas tadā |
parigheṇābhidudrāva mārutātmajam āhave ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tam āpatantam utpatya hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ |
ājaghāna tadā mūrdhni vajravegena muṣṭinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
sa muṣṭiniṣpiṣṭavikīrṇamūrdhā; nirvāntadantākṣivilambijihvaḥ |
devāntako rākṣasarājasūnur; gatāsur urvyāṁ sahasā papāta ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tasmin hate rākṣasayodhamukhye; mahābale saṁyati devaśatrau |
kruddhas trimūrdhā niśitāgram ugraṁ; vavarṣa nīlorasi bāṇavarṣam ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
sa taiḥ śaraughair abhivarṣyamāṇo; vibhinnagātraḥ kapisainyapālaḥ |
nīlo babhūvātha visr̥ṣṭagātro; viṣṭambhitas tena mahābalena ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tatas tu nīlaḥ pratilabhya saṁjñāṁ; śailaṁ samutpāṭya savr̥kṣaṣaṇḍam |
tataḥ samutpatya bhr̥śogravego; mahodaraṁ tena jaghāna mūrdhni ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tataḥ sa śailābhinipātabhagno; mahodaras tena saha dvipena |
vipothito bhūmitale gatāsuḥ; papāta varjābhihato yathādriḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
pitr̥vyaṁ nihataṁ dr̥ṣṭvā triśirāś cāpam ādade |
hanūmantaṁ ca saṁkruddho vivyādha niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
hanūmāṁs tu samutpatya hayāṁs triśirasas tadā |
vidadāra nakhaiḥ kruddho gajendraṁ mr̥garāḍ iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
atha śaktiṁ samādāya kālarātrim ivāntakaḥ |
cikṣepānilaputrāya triśirā rāvaṇātmajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
divi kṣiptām ivolkāṁ tāṁ śaktiṁ kṣiptām asaṁgatām |
gr̥hītvā hariśārdūlo babhañja ca nanāda ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
tāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā ghorasaṁkāśāṁ śaktiṁ bhagnāṁ hanūmatā |
prahr̥ṣṭā vānaragaṇā vinedur jaladā iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
tataḥ khaḍgaṁ samudyamya triśirā rākṣasottamaḥ |
nicakhāna tadā roṣād vānarendrasya vakṣasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
khaḍgaprahārābhihato hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ |
ājaghāna trimūrdhānaṁ talenorasi vīryavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
sa talabhihatas tena srastahastāmbaro bhuvi |
nipapāta mahātejās triśirās tyaktacetanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
sa tasya patataḥ khaḍgaṁ samācchidya mahākapiḥ |
nanāda girisaṁkāśas trāsayan sarvanairr̥tān ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
amr̥ṣyamāṇas taṁ ghoṣam utpapāta niśācaraḥ |
utpatya ca hanūmantaṁ tāḍayām āsa muṣṭinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
tena muṣṭiprahāreṇa saṁcukopa mahākapiḥ |
kupitaś ca nijagrāha kirīṭe rākṣasarṣabham ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
sa tasya śīrṣāṇy asinā śitena; kirīṭajuṣṭāni sakuṇḍalāni |
kruddhaḥ praciccheda suto ’nilasya; tvaṣṭuḥ sutasyeva śirāṁsi śakraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
tāny āyatākṣāṇy agasaṁnibhāni; pradīptavaiśvānaralocanāni |
petuḥ śirāṁsīndraripor dharaṇyāṁ; jyotīṁṣi muktāni yathārkamārgāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
tasmin hate devaripau triśīrṣe; hanūmata śakraparākrameṇa |
neduḥ plavaṁgāḥ pracacāla bhūmī; rakṣāṁsy atho dudruvire samantāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
hataṁ triśirasaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā tathaiva ca mahodaram |
hatau prekṣya durādharṣau devāntakanarāntakau ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
cukopa paramāmarṣī mahāpārśvo mahābalaḥ |
jagrāhārciṣmatīṁ cāpi gadāṁ sarvāyasīṁ śubhām ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
hemapaṭṭaparikṣiptāṁ māṁsaśoṇitalepanām |
virājamānāṁ vapuṣā śatruśoṇitarañjitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
tejasā saṁpradīptāgrāṁ raktamālyavibhūṣitām |
airāvatamahāpadmasārvabhauma bhayāvahām ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
gadām ādāya saṁkruddho mahāpārśvo mahābalaḥ |
harīn samabhidudrāva yugāntāgnir iva jvalan ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
atharṣayaḥ samutpatya vānaro ravaṇānujam |
mahāpārśvam upāgamya tasthau tasyāgrato balī ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
taṁ purastāt sthitaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā vānaraṁ parvatopamam |
ājaghānorasi kruddho gadayā vajrakalpayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
sa tayābhihatas tena gadayā vānararṣabhaḥ |
bhinnavakṣāḥ samādhūtaḥ susrāva rudhiraṁ bahu ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
sa saṁprāpya cirāt saṁjñām r̥ṣabho vānararṣabhaḥ |
kruddho visphuramāṇauṣṭho mahāpārśvam udaikṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
52.
tāṁ gr̥hītvā gadāṁ bhīmām āvidhya ca punaḥ punaḥ |
mattānīkaṁ mahāpārśvaṁ jaghāna raṇamūrdhani ||

=== meaning ===

---
53.
sa svayā gadayā bhinno vikīrṇadaśanekṣaṇaḥ |
nipapāta mahāpārśvo vajrāhata ivācalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
54.
tasmin hate bhrātari rāvaṇasya; tan nairr̥tānāṁ balam arṇavābham |
tyaktāyudhaṁ kevalajīvitārthaṁ; dudrāva bhinnārṇavasaṁnikāśam ||

=== meaning ===

---
