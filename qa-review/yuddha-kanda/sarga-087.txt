=== Sarga 87 ===

---
1.
mahodaramahāpārśvau hatau dr̥ṣṭvā tu rākṣasau |
tasmiṁś ca nihate vīre virūpākṣe mahābale ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
āviveśa mahān krodho rāvaṇaṁ tu mahāmr̥dhe |
sūtaṁ saṁcodayām āsa vākyaṁ cedam uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
nihatānām amātyānāṁ ruddhasya nagarasya ca |
duḥkham eṣo ’paneṣyāmi hatvā tau rāmalakṣmaṇau ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
rāmavr̥kṣaṁ raṇe hanmi sītāpuṣpaphalapradam |
praśākhā yasya sugrīvo jāmbavān kumudo nalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
sa diśo daśa ghoṣeṇa rathasyātiratho mahān |
nādayan prayayau tūrṇaṁ rāghavaṁ cābhyavartata ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
pūritā tena śabdena sanadīgirikānanā |
saṁcacāla mahī sarvā savarāhamr̥gadvipā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tāmasaṁ sumahāghoraṁ cakārāstraṁ sudāruṇam |
nirdadāha kapīn sarvāṁs te prapetuḥ samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tāny anīkāny anekāni rāvaṇasya śarottamaiḥ |
dr̥ṣṭvā bhagnāni śataśo rāghavaḥ paryavasthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sa dadarśa tato rāmaṁ tiṣṭhantam aparājitam |
lakṣmaṇena saha bhrātrā viṣṇunā vāsavaṁ yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
ālikhantam ivākāśam avaṣṭabhya mahad dhanuḥ |
padmapatraviśālākṣaṁ dīrghabāhum ariṁdamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
vānarāṁś ca raṇe bhagnān āpatantaṁ ca rāvaṇam |
samīkṣya rāghavo hr̥ṣṭo madhye jagrāha kārmukam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
visphārayitum ārebhe tataḥ sa dhanur uttamam |
mahāvegaṁ mahānādaṁ nirbhindann iva medinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tayoḥ śarapathaṁ prāpya rāvaṇo rājaputrayoḥ |
sa babhūva yathā rāhuḥ samīpe śaśisūryayoḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
rāvaṇasya ca bāṇaughai rāmavispharitena ca |
śabdena rākṣasās tena petuś ca śataśas tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tam icchan prathamaṁ yoddhuṁ lakṣmaṇo niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ |
mumoca dhanur āyamya śarān agniśikhopamān ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tān muktamātrān ākāśe lakṣmaṇena dhanuṣmatā |
bāṇān bāṇair mahātejā rāvaṇaḥ pratyavārayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
ekam ekena bāṇena tribhis trīn daśabhir daśa |
lakṣmaṇasya praciccheda darśayan pāṇilāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
abhyatikramya saumitriṁ rāvaṇaḥ samitiṁjayaḥ |
āsasāda tato rāmaṁ sthitaṁ śailam ivācalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
sa saṁkhye rāmam āsādya krodhasaṁraktalocanaḥ |
vyasr̥jac charavarṣāni rāvaṇo rāghavopari ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
śaradhārās tato rāmo rāvaṇasya dhanuścyutāḥ |
dr̥ṣṭvaivāpatitāḥ śīghraṁ bhallāñ jagrāha satvaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tāñ śaraughāṁs tato bhallais tīkṣṇaiś ciccheda rāghavaḥ |
dīpyamānān mahāvegān kruddhān āśīviṣān iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
rāghavo rāvaṇaṁ tūrṇaṁ rāvaṇo rāghavaṁ tathā |
anyonyaṁ vividhais tīkṣṇaiḥ śarair abhivavarṣatuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
ceratuś ca ciraṁ citraṁ maṇḍalaṁ savyadakṣiṇam |
bāṇavegān samudīkṣya samareṣv aparājitau ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tayor bhūtāni vitreṣur yugapat saṁprayudhyatoḥ |
raudrayoḥ sāyakamucor yamāntakanikāśayoḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
saṁtataṁ vividhair bāṇair babhūva gaganaṁ tadā |
ghanair ivātapāpāye vidyunmālāsamākulaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
gavākṣitam ivākāśaṁ babhūva śūravr̥ṣṭibhiḥ |
mahāvegaiḥ sutīkṣṇāgrair gr̥dhrapatraiḥ suvājitaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
śarāndhakāraṁ tau bhīmaṁ cakratuḥ paramaṁ tadā |
gate ’staṁ tapane cāpi mahāmeghāv ivotthitau ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
babhūva tumulaṁ yuddham anyonyavadhakāṅkṣiṇoḥ |
anāsādyam acintyaṁ ca vr̥travāsavayor iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
ubhau hi parameṣvāsāv ubhau śastraviśāradau |
ubhau cāstravidāṁ mukhyāv ubhau yuddhe viceratuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
ubhau hi yena vrajatas tena tena śarormayaḥ |
ūrmayo vāyunā viddhā jagmuḥ sāgarayor iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
tataḥ saṁsaktahastas tu rāvaṇo lokarāvaṇaḥ |
nārācamālāṁ rāmasya lalāṭe pratyamuñcata ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
raudracāpaprayuktāṁ tāṁ nīlotpaladalaprabhām |
śirasā dhārayan rāmo na vyathāṁ pratyapadyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
atha mantrān api japan raudram astram udīrayan |
śarān bhūyaḥ samādāya rāmaḥ krodhasamanvitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
mumoca ca mahātejāś cāpam āyamya vīryavān |
tāñ śarān rākṣasendrāya cikṣepācchinnasāyakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
te mahāmeghasaṁkāśe kavace patitāḥ śarāḥ |
avadhye rākṣasendrasya na vyathāṁ janayaṁs tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
punar evātha taṁ rāmo rathasthaṁ rākṣasādhipam |
lalāṭe paramāstreṇa sarvāstrakuśalo ’bhinat ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
te bhittvā bāṇarūpāṇi pañcaśīrṣā ivoragāḥ |
śvasanto viviśur bhūmiṁ rāvaṇapratikūlatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
nihatya rāghavasyāstraṁ rāvaṇaḥ krodhamūrchitaḥ |
āsuraṁ sumahāghoram anyad astraṁ samādade ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
siṁhavyāghramukhāṁś cānyān kaṅkakākamukhān api |
gr̥dhraśyenamukhāṁś cāpi sr̥gālavadanāṁs tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
īhāmr̥gamukhāṁś cānyān vyāditāsyān bhayāvahān |
pañcāsyām̐l lelihānāṁś ca sasarja niśitāñ śarān ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
śarān kharamukhāṁś cānyān varāhamukhasaṁsthitān |
śvānakukkuṭavaktrāṁś ca makarāśīviṣānanān ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
etāṁś cānyāṁś ca māyābhiḥ sasarja niśitāñ śarān |
rāmaṁ prati mahātejāḥ kruddhaḥ sarpa iva śvasan ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
āsureṇa samāviṣṭaḥ so ’streṇa raghunandanaḥ |
sasarjāstraṁ mahotsāhaḥ pāvakaṁ pāvakopamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
agnidīptamukhān bāṇāṁs tathā sūryamukhān api |
candrārdhacandravaktrāṁś ca dhūmaketumukhān api ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
grahanakṣatravarṇāṁś ca maholkāmukhasaṁsthitān |
vidyujjihvopamāṁś cānyān sasarja niśitāñ śarān ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
te rāvaṇaśarā ghorā rāghavāstrasamāhatāḥ |
vilayaṁ jagmur ākāśe jagmuś caiva sahasraśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
tad astraṁ nihataṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rāmeṇākliṣṭakarmaṇā |
hr̥ṣṭā nedus tataḥ sarve kapayaḥ kāmarūpiṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
