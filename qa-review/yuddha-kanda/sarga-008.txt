=== Sarga 8 ===

---
1.
tato nīlāmbudanibhaḥ prahasto nāma rākṣasaḥ |
abravīt prāñjalir vākyaṁ śūraḥ senāpatis tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
devadānavagandharvāḥ piśācapatagoragāḥ |
na tvāṁ dharṣayituṁ śaktāḥ kiṁ punar vānarā raṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sarve pramattā viśvastā vañcitāḥ sma hanūmatā |
na hi me jīvato gacchej jīvan sa vanagocaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
sarvāṁ sāgaraparyantāṁ saśailavanakānanām |
karomy avānarāṁ bhūmim ājñāpayatu māṁ bhavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
rakṣāṁ caiva vidhāsyāmi vānarād rajanīcara |
nāgamiṣyati te duḥkhaṁ kiṁ cid ātmāparādhajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
abravīc ca susaṁkruddho durmukho nāma rākṣasaḥ |
idaṁ na kṣamaṇīyaṁ hi sarveṣāṁ naḥ pradharṣaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
ayaṁ paribhavo bhūyaḥ purasyāntaḥpurasya ca |
śrīmato rākṣasendrasya vānarendrapradharṣaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
asmin muhūrte hatvaiko nivartiṣyāmi vānarān |
praviṣṭān sāgaraṁ bhīmam ambaraṁ vā rasātalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tato ’bravīt susaṁkruddho vajradaṁṣṭro mahābalaḥ |
pragr̥hya parighaṁ ghoraṁ māṁsaśoṇitarūpitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
kiṁ vo hanumatā kāryaṁ kr̥paṇena tapasvinā |
rāme tiṣṭhati durdharṣe sugrīve sahalakṣmaṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
adya rāmaṁ sasugrīvaṁ parigheṇa salakṣmaṇam |
āgamiṣyāmi hatvaiko vikṣobhya harivāhinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
kaumbhakarṇis tato vīro nikumbho nāma vīryavān |
abravīt paramakurddho rāvaṇaṁ lokarāvaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sarve bhavantas tiṣṭhantu mahārājena saṁgatāḥ |
aham eko haniṣyāmi rāghavaṁ sahalakṣmaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tato vajrahanur nāma rākṣasaḥ parvatopamaḥ |
kruddhaḥ parilihan vaktraṁ jihvayā vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
svairaṁ kurvantu kāryāṇi bhavanto vigatajvarāḥ |
eko ’haṁ bhakṣayiṣyāmi tān sarvān hariyūthapān ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
svasthāḥ krīḍantu niścintāḥ pibantu madhuvāruṇīm |
aham eko haniṣyāmi sugrīvaṁ sahalakṣmaṇam |
sāṅgadaṁ ca hanūmantaṁ rāmaṁ ca raṇakuñjaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
