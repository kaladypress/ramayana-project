=== Sarga 25 ===

---
1.
atha tāṁ jātasaṁtāpāṁ tena vākyena mohitām |
saramā hlādayām āsa pr̥tivīṁ dyaur ivāmbhasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tatas tasyā hitaṁ sakhyāś cikīrṣantī sakhī vacaḥ |
uvāca kāle kālajñā smitapūrvābhibhāṣiṇī ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
utsaheyam ahaṁ gatvā tvadvākyam asitekṣaṇe |
nivedya kuśalaṁ rāme praticchannā nivartitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
na hi me kramamāṇāyā nirālambe vihāyasi |
samartho gatim anvetuṁ pavano garuḍo ’pi vā ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
evaṁ bruvāṇāṁ tāṁ sītā saramāṁ punar abravīt |
madhuraṁ ślakṣṇayā vācā pūrvaśokābhipannayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
samarthā gaganaṁ gantum api vā tvaṁ rasātalam |
avagacchāmy akartavyaṁ kartavyaṁ te madantare ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
matpriyaṁ yadi kartavyaṁ yadi buddhiḥ sthirā tava |
jñātum icchāmi taṁ gatvā kiṁ karotīti rāvaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sa hi māyābalaḥ krūro rāvaṇaḥ śatrurāvaṇaḥ |
māṁ mohayati duṣṭātmā pītamātreva vāruṇī ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tarjāpayati māṁ nityaṁ bhartsāpayati cāsakr̥t |
rākṣasībhiḥ sughorābhir yā māṁ rakṣanti nityaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
udvignā śaṅkitā cāsmi na ca svasthaṁ mano mama |
tad bhayāc cāham udvignā aśokavanikāṁ gatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
yadi nāma kathā tasya niścitaṁ vāpi yad bhavet |
nivedayethāḥ sarvaṁ tat paro me syād anugrahaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sā tv evaṁ bruvatīṁ sītāṁ saramā valgubhāṣiṇī |
uvāca vacanaṁ tasyāḥ spr̥śantī bāṣpaviklavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
eṣa te yady abhiprāyas tasmād gacchāmi jānaki |
gr̥hya śatror abhiprāyam upāvr̥ttāṁ ca paśya mām ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
evam uktvā tato gatvā samīpaṁ tasya rakṣasaḥ |
śuśrāva kathitaṁ tasya rāvaṇasya samantriṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sā śrutvā niścayaṁ tasya niścayajñā durātmanaḥ |
punar evāgamat kṣipram aśokavanikāṁ tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sā praviṣṭā punas tatra dadarśa janakātmajām |
pratīkṣamāṇāṁ svām eva bhraṣṭapadmām iva śriyam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tāṁ tu sītā punaḥ prāptāṁ saramāṁ valgubhāṣiṇīm |
pariṣvajya ca susnigdhaṁ dadau ca svayam āsanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
ihāsīnā sukhaṁ sarvam ākhyāhi mama tattvataḥ |
krūrasya niścayaṁ tasya rāvaṇasya durātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
evam uktā tu saramā sītayā vepamānayā |
kathitaṁ sarvam ācaṣṭa rāvaṇasya samantriṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
jananyā rākṣasendro vai tvanmokṣārthaṁ br̥hadvacaḥ |
aviddhena ca vaidehi mantrivr̥ddhena bodhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
dīyatām abhisatkr̥tya manujendrāya maithilī |
nidarśanaṁ te paryāptaṁ janasthāne yad adbhutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
laṅghanaṁ ca samudrasya darśanaṁ ca hanūmataḥ |
vadhaṁ ca rakṣasāṁ yuddhe kaḥ kuryān mānuṣo bhuvi ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
evaṁ sa mantrivr̥ddhaiś ca mātrā ca bahu bhāṣitaḥ |
na tvām utsahate moktum artahm arthaparo yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
notsahaty amr̥to moktuṁ yuddhe tvām iti maithili |
sāmātyasya nr̥śaṁsasya niścayo hy eṣa vartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tad eṣā susthirā buddhir mr̥tyulobhād upasthitā |
bhayān na śaktas tvāṁ moktum anirastas tu saṁyuge |
rākṣasānāṁ ca sarveṣām ātmanaś ca vadhena hi ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
nihatya rāvaṇaṁ saṁkhye sarvathā niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ |
pratineṣyati rāmas tvām ayodhyām asitekṣaṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
etasminn antare śabdo bherīśaṅkhasamākulaḥ |
śruto vai sarvasainyānāṁ kampayan dharaṇītalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
śrutvā tu taṁ vānarasainyaśabdaṁ; laṅkāgatā rākṣasarājabhr̥tyāḥ |
naṣṭaujaso dainyaparītaceṣṭāḥ; śreyo na paśyanti nr̥pasya doṣaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
