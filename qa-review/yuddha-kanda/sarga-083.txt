=== Sarga 83 ===

---
1.
ārtānāṁ rākṣasīnāṁ tu laṅkāyāṁ vai kule kule |
rāvaṇaḥ karuṇaṁ śabdaṁ śuśrāva pariveditam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sa tu dīrghaṁ viniśvasya muhūrtaṁ dhyānam āsthitaḥ |
babhūva paramakruddho rāvaṇo bhīmadarśanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
saṁdaśya daśanair oṣṭhaṁ krodhasaṁraktalocanaḥ |
rākṣasair api durdarśaḥ kālāgnir iva mūrchitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
uvāca ca samīpasthān rākṣasān rākṣaseśvaraḥ |
bhayāvyaktakathāṁs tatra nirdahann iva cakṣuṣā ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
mahodaraṁ mahāpārśvaṁ virūpākṣaṁ ca rākṣasaṁ |
śīghraṁ vadata sainyāni niryāteti mamājñayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā rākṣasās te bhayārditāḥ |
codayām āsur avyagrān rākṣasāṁs tān nr̥pājñayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
te tu sarve tathety uktvā rākṣasā ghoradarśanāḥ |
kr̥tasvastyayanāḥ sarve rāvaṇābhimukhā yayuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
pratipūjya yathānyāyaṁ rāvaṇaṁ te mahārathāḥ |
tasthuḥ prāñjalayaḥ sarve bhartur vijayakāṅkṣiṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
athovāca prahasyaitān rāvaṇaḥ krodhamūrchitaḥ |
mahodaramahāpārśvau virūpākṣaṁ ca rākṣasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
adya bāṇair dhanurmuktair yugāntādityasaṁnibhaiḥ |
rāghavaṁ lakṣmaṇaṁ caiva neṣyāmi yamasādhanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
kharasya kumbhakarṇasya prahastendrajitos tathā |
kariṣyāmi pratīkāram adya śatruvadhād aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
naivāntarikṣaṁ na diśo na nadyo nāpi sāgaraḥ |
prakāśatvaṁ gamiṣyanti madbāṇajaladāvr̥tāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
adya vānarayūthānāṁ tāni yūthāni bhāgaśaḥ |
dhanuḥsamudrād udbhūtair mathiṣyāmi śarormibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
vyākośapadmacakrāṇi padmakesaravarcasām |
adya yūthataṭākāni gajavat pramathāmy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
saśarair adya vadanaiḥ saṁkhye vānarayūthapāḥ |
maṇḍayiṣyanti vasudhāṁ sanālair iva paṅkalaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
adya yuddhapracaṇḍānāṁ harīṇāṁ drumayodhinām |
muktenaikeṣuṇā yuddhe bhetsyāmi ca śataṁśatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
hato bhartā hato bhrātā yāsāṁ ca tanayā hatāḥ |
vadhenādya ripos tāsāṁ karmomy asrapramārjanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
adya madbāṇanirbhinnaiḥ prakīrṇair gatacetanaiḥ |
karomi vānarair yuddhe yatnāvekṣyatalāṁ mahīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
adya gomāyavo gr̥dhrā ye ca māṁsāśino ’pare |
sarvāṁs tāṁs tarpayiṣyāmi śatrumāṁsaiḥ śarārditaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
kalpyatāṁ me rathaśīghraṁ kṣipram ānīyatāṁ dhanuḥ |
anuprayāntu māṁ yuddhe ye ’vaśiṣṭā niśācarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā mahāpārśvo ’bravīd vacaḥ |
balādhyakṣān sthitāṁs tatra balaṁ saṁtvaryatām iti ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
balādhyakṣās tu saṁrabdhā rākṣasāṁs tān gr̥hād gr̥hāt |
codayantaḥ pariyayur laṅkāṁ laghuparākramāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tato muhūrtān niṣpetū rākṣasā bhīmavikramāḥ |
nardanto bhīmavadanā nānāpraharaṇair bhujaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
asibhiḥ paṭṭasaiḥ śūlair galābhir musalair halaiḥ |
śaktibhis tīkṣṇadhārābhir mahadbhiḥ kūṭamudgaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
yaṣṭibhir vimalaiś cakrair niśitaiś ca paraśvadhaiḥ |
bhiṇḍipālaiḥ śataghnībhir anyaiś cāpi varāyudhaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
athānayan balādhyakṣāś catvāro rāvaṇājñayā |
drutaṁ sūtasamāyuktaṁ yuktāṣṭaturagaṁ ratham ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
āruroha rathaṁ divyaṁ dīpyamānaṁ svatejasā |
rāvaṇaḥ sattvagāmbhīryād dārayann iva medinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
rāvaṇenābhyanujñātau mahāpārśvamahodarau |
virūpākṣaś ca durdharṣo rathān āruruhus tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
te tu hr̥ṣṭā vinardanto bhindanta iva medinīm |
nādaṁ ghoraṁ vimuñcanto niryayur jayakāṅkṣiṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
tato yuddhāya tejasvī rakṣogaṇabalair vr̥taḥ |
niryayāv udyatadhanuḥ kālāntakayamomapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
tataḥ prajavanāśvena rathena sa mahārathaḥ |
dvāreṇa niryayau tena yatra tau rāmalakṣmaṇau ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
tato naṣṭaprabhaḥ sūryo diśaś ca timirāvr̥tāḥ |
dvijāś ca nedur ghorāś ca saṁcacāla ca medinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
vavarṣa rudhiraṁ devaś caskhaluś ca turaṁgamāḥ |
dhvajāgre nyapatad gr̥dhro vineduś cāśivaṁ śivāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
nayanaṁ cāsphurad vāmaṁ savyo bāhur akampata |
vivarṇavadanaś cāsīt kiṁ cid abhraśyata svaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
tato niṣpatato yuddhe daśagrīvasya rakṣasaḥ |
raṇe nidhanaśaṁsīni rūpāṇy etāni jajñire ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
antarikṣāt papātolkā nirghātasamanisvanā |
vinedur aśivaṁ gr̥dhrā vāyasair anunāditāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
etān acintayan ghorān utpātān samupasthitān |
niryayau rāvaṇo mohād vadhārthī kālacoditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
teṣāṁ tu rathaghoṣeṇa rākṣasānāṁ mahātmanām |
vānarāṇām api camūr yuddhāyaivābhyavartata ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
teṣāṁ sutumulaṁ yuddhaṁ babhūva kapirakṣasām |
anyonyam āhvayānānāṁ kruddhānāṁ jayam icchatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
tataḥ kruddho daśagrīvaḥ śaraiḥ kāñcanabhūṣaṇaiḥ |
vānarāṇām anīkeṣu cakāra kadanaṁ mahat ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
nikr̥ttaśirasaḥ ke cid rāvaṇena valīmukhāḥ |
nirucchvāsā hatāḥ ke cit ke cit pārśveṣu dāritāḥ |
ke cid vibhinnaśirasaḥ ke cic cakṣurvivarjitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
daśānanaḥ krodhavivr̥ttanetro; yato yato ’bhyeti rathena saṁkhye |
tatas tatas tasya śarapravegaṁ; soḍhuṁ na śekur hariyūthapās te ||

=== meaning ===

---
