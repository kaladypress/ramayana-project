=== Sarga 52 ===

---
1.
tad uktam atikāyasya balino bāhuśālinaḥ |
kumbhakarṇasya vacanaṁ śrutvovāca mahodaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
kumbhakarṇakule jāto dhr̥ṣṭaḥ prākr̥tadarśanaḥ |
avalipto na śaknoṣi kr̥tyaṁ sarvatra veditum ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
na hi rājā na jānīte kumbhakarṇa nayānayau |
tvaṁ tu kaiśorakād dhr̥ṣṭaḥ kevalaṁ vaktum icchasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
sthānaṁ vr̥ddhiṁ ca hāniṁ ca deśakālavibhāgavit |
ātmanaś ca pareṣāṁ ca budhyate rākṣasarṣabha ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
yat tu śakyaṁ balavatā kartuṁ prākr̥tabuddhinā |
anupāsitavr̥ddhena kaḥ kuryāt tādr̥śaṁ budhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
yāṁs tu dharmārthakāmāṁs tvaṁ bravīṣi pr̥thag āśrayān |
anuboddhuṁ svabhāvena na hi lakṣaṇam asti te ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
karma caiva hi sarveṣāṁ kāraṇānāṁ prayojanam |
śreyaḥ pāpīyasāṁ cātra phalaṁ bhavati karmaṇām ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
niḥśreyasa phalāv eva dharmārthāv itarāv api |
adharmānarthayoḥ prāptiḥ phalaṁ ca pratyavāyikam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
aihalaukikapāratryaṁ karma pumbhir niṣevyate |
karmāṇy api tu kalpyāni labhate kāmam āsthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tatra kl̥ptam idaṁ rājñā hr̥di kāryaṁ mataṁ ca naḥ |
śatrau hi sāhasaṁ yat syāt kim ivātrāpanīyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
ekasyaivābhiyāne tu hetur yaḥ prakr̥tas tvayā |
tatrāpy anupapannaṁ te vakṣyāmi yad asādhu ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
yena pūrvaṁ janasthāne bahavo ’tibalā hatāḥ |
rākṣasā rāghavaṁ taṁ tvaṁ katham eko jayiṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
ye purā nirjitās tena janasthāne mahaujasaḥ |
rākṣasāṁs tān pure sarvān bhītān adyāpi paśyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
taṁ siṁham iva saṁkruddhaṁ rāmaṁ daśarathātmajam |
sarpaṁ suptam ivābuddhyā prabodhayitum icchasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
jvalantaṁ tejasā nityaṁ krodhena ca durāsadam |
kas taṁ mr̥tyum ivāsahyam āsādayitum arhati ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
saṁśayastham idaṁ sarvaṁ śatroḥ pratisamāsane |
ekasya gamanaṁ tatra na hi me rocate tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
hīnārthas tu samr̥ddhārthaṁ ko ripuṁ prākr̥to yathā |
niścitaṁ jīvitatyāge vaśam ānetum icchati ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
yasya nāsti manuṣyeṣu sadr̥śo rākṣasottama |
katham āśaṁsase yoddhuṁ tulyenendravivasvatoḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
evam uktvā tu saṁrabdhaṁ kumbhakarṇaṁ mahodaraḥ |
uvāca rakṣasāṁ madhye rāvaṇo lokarāvaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
labdhvā punas tāṁ vaidehīṁ kimarthaṁ tvaṁ prajalpasi |
yadecchasi tadā sītā vaśagā te bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
dr̥ṣṭaḥ kaś cid upāyo me sītopasthānakārakaḥ |
rucitaś cet svayā buddhyā rākṣaseśvara taṁ śr̥ṇu ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
ahaṁ dvijihvaḥ saṁhrādī kumbhakarṇo vitardanaḥ |
pañcarāmavadhāyaite niryāntīty avaghoṣaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tato gatvā vayaṁ yuddhaṁ dāsyāmas tasya yatnataḥ |
jeṣyāmo yadi te śatrūn nopāyaiḥ kr̥tyam asti naḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
atha jīvati naḥ śatrur vayaṁ ca kr̥tasaṁyugāḥ |
tataḥ samabhipatsyāmo manasā yat samīkṣitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
vayaṁ yuddhād ihaiṣyāmo rudhireṇa samukṣitāḥ |
vidārya svatanuṁ bāṇai rāmanāmāṅkitaiḥ śitaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
bhakṣito rāghavo ’smābhir lakṣmaṇaś ceti vādinaḥ |
tava pādau grahīṣyāmas tvaṁ naḥ kāma prapūraya ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tato ’vaghoṣaya pure gajaskandhena pārthiva |
hato rāmaḥ saha bhrātrā sasainya iti sarvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
prīto nāma tato bhūtvā bhr̥tyānāṁ tvam ariṁdama |
bhogāṁś ca parivārāṁś ca kāmāṁś ca vasudāpaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
tato mālyāni vāsāṁsi vīrāṇām anulepanam |
peyaṁ ca bahu yodhebhyaḥ svayaṁ ca muditaḥ piba ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
tato ’smin bahulībhūte kaulīne sarvato gate |
praviśyāśvāsya cāpi tvaṁ sītāṁ rahasi sāntvaya |
dhanadhānyaiś ca kāmaiś ca ratnaiś caināṁ pralobhaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
anayopadhayā rājan bhayaśokānubandhayā |
akāmā tvadvaśaṁ sītā naṣṭanāthā gamiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
rañjanīyaṁ hi bhartāraṁ vinaṣṭam avagamya sā |
nairāśyāt strīlaghutvāc ca tvadvaśaṁ pratipatsyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
sā purā sukhasaṁvr̥ddhā sukhārhā duḥkhakarṣitā |
tvayy adhīnaḥ sukhaṁ jñātvā sarvathopagamiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
etat sunītaṁ mama darśanena; rāmaṁ hi dr̥ṣṭvaiva bhaved anarthaḥ |
ihaiva te setsyati motsuko bhūr; mahān ayuddhena sukhasya lābhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
anaṣṭasainyo hy anavāptasaṁśayo; ripūn ayuddhena jayañ janādhipa |
yaśaś ca puṇyaṁ ca mahan mahīpate; śriyaṁ ca kīrtiṁ ca ciraṁ samaśnute ||

=== meaning ===

---
