=== Sarga 101 ===

---
1.
iti pratisamādiṣṭo hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ |
praviveśa purīṁ laṅkāṁ pūjyamāno niśācaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
praviśya tu mahātejā rāvaṇasya niveśanam |
dadarśa śaśinā hīnāṁ sātaṅkām iva rohiṇīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
nibhr̥taḥ praṇataḥ prahvaḥ so ’bhigamyābhivādya ca |
rāmasya vacanaṁ sarvam ākhyātum upacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
vaidehi kuśalī rāmaḥ sasugrīvaḥ salakṣmaṇaḥ |
kuśalaṁ cāha siddhārtho hataśatrur ariṁdamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
vibhīṣaṇasahāyena rāmeṇa haribhiḥ saha |
nihato rāvaṇo devi lakṣmaṇasya nayena ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
pr̥ṣṭvā ca kuśalaṁ rāmo vīras tvāṁ raghunandanaḥ |
abravīt paramaprītaḥ kr̥tārthenāntarātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
priyam ākhyāmi te devi tvāṁ tu bhūyaḥ sabhājaye |
diṣṭyā jīvasi dharmajñe jayena mama saṁyuge ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
labdho no vijayaḥ sīte svasthā bhava gatavyathā |
rāvaṇaḥ sa hataḥ śatrur laṅkā ceyaṁ vaśe sthitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
mayā hy alabdhanidreṇa dhr̥tena tava nirjaye |
pratijñaiṣā vinistīrṇā baddhvā setuṁ mahodadhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
saṁbhramaś ca na kartavyo vartantyā rāvaṇālaye |
vibhīṣaṇavidheyaṁ hi laṅkaiśvaryam idaṁ kr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tad āśvasihi viśvastā svagr̥he parivartase |
ayaṁ cābhyeti saṁhr̥ṣṭas tvaddarśanasamutsukaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
evam uktā samutpatya sītā śaśinibhānanā |
praharṣeṇāvaruddhā sā vyājahāra na kiṁ cana ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
abravīc ca hariśreṣṭhaḥ sītām apratijalpatīm |
kiṁ tvaṁ cintayase devi kiṁ ca māṁ nābhibhāṣase ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
evam uktā hanumatā sītā dharme vyavasthitā |
abravīt paramaprītā harṣagadgadayā girā ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
priyam etad upaśrutya bhartur vijayasaṁśritam |
praharṣavaśam āpannā nirvākyāsmi kṣaṇāntaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
na hi paśyāmi sadr̥śaṁ cintayantī plavaṁgama |
matpriyākhyānakasyeha tava pratyabhinandanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
na ca paśyāmi tat saumya pr̥thivyām api vānara |
sadr̥śaṁ matpriyākhyāne tava dātuṁ bhavet samam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
hiraṇyaṁ vā suvarṇaṁ vā ratnāni vividhāni ca |
rājyaṁ vā triṣu lokeṣu naitad arhati bhāṣitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
evam uktas tu vaidehyā pratyuvāca plavaṁgamaḥ |
pragr̥hītāñjalir vākyaṁ sītāyāḥ pramukhe sthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
bhartuḥ priyahite yukte bhartur vijayakāṅkṣiṇi |
snigdham evaṁvidhaṁ vākyaṁ tvam evārhasi bhāṣitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tavaitad vacanaṁ saumye sāravat snigdham eva ca |
ratnaughād vividhāc cāpi devarājyād viśiṣyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
arthataś ca mayā prāptā devarājyādayo guṇāḥ |
hataśatruṁ vijayinaṁ rāmaṁ paśyāmi yat sthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
imās tu khalu rākṣasyo yadi tvam anumanyase |
hantum icchāmy ahaṁ sarvā yābhis tvaṁ tarjitā purā ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
kliśyantīṁ patidevāṁ tvām aśokavanikāṁ gatām |
ghorarūpasamācārāḥ krūrāḥ krūratarekṣaṇāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
rākṣasyo dāruṇakathā varam etaṁ prayaccha me |
icchāmi vividhair ghātair hantum etāḥ sudāruṇāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
muṣṭibhiḥ pāṇibhiś caiva caraṇaiś caiva śobhane |
ghorair jānuprahāraiś ca daśanānāṁ ca pātanaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
bhakṣaṇaiḥ karṇanāsānāṁ keśānāṁ luñcanais tathā |
bhr̥śaṁ śuṣkamukhībhiś ca dāruṇair laṅghanair hataiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
evaṁprakārair bahubhir viprakārair yaśasvini |
hantum icchāmy ahaṁ devi tavemāḥ kr̥takilbiṣāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
evam uktā mahumatā vaidehī janakātmajā |
uvāca dharmasahitaṁ hanūmantaṁ yaśasvinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
rājasaṁśrayavaśyānāṁ kurvatīnāṁ parājñayā |
vidheyānāṁ ca dāsīnāṁ kaḥ kupyed vānarottama ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
bhāgyavaiṣamyayogena purā duścaritena ca |
mayaitat prāpyate sarvaṁ svakr̥taṁ hy upabhujyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
prāptavyaṁ tu daśāyogān mayaitad iti niścitam |
dāsīnāṁ rāvaṇasyāhaṁ marṣayāmīha durbalā ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
ājñaptā rāvaṇenaitā rākṣasyo mām atarjayan |
hate tasmin na kuryur hi tarjanaṁ vānarottama ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
ayaṁ vyāghrasamīpe tu purāṇo dharmasaṁhitaḥ |
r̥kṣeṇa gītaḥ śloko me taṁ nibodha plavaṁgama ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
na paraḥ pāpam ādatte pareṣāṁ pāpakarmaṇām |
samayo rakṣitavyas tu santaś cāritrabhūṣaṇāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
pāpānāṁ vā śubhānāṁ vā vadhārhāṇāṁ plavaṁgama |
kāryaṁ kāruṇyam āryeṇa na kaś cin nāparādhyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
lokahiṁsāvihārāṇāṁ rakṣasāṁ kāmarūpiṇam |
kurvatām api pāpāni naiva kāryam aśobhanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
evam uktas tu hanumān sītayā vākyakovidaḥ |
pratyuvāca tataḥ sītāṁ rāmapatnīṁ yaśasvinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
yuktā rāmasya bhavatī dharmapatnī yaśasvinī |
pratisaṁdiśa māṁ devi gamiṣye yatra rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
evam uktā hanumatā vaidehī janakātmajā |
abravīd draṣṭum icchāmi bhartāraṁ vānarottama ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
tasyās tad vacanaṁ śrutvā hanumān pavanātmajaḥ |
harṣayan maithilīṁ vākyam uvācedaṁ mahādyutiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
pūrṇacandrānanaṁ rāmaṁ drakṣyasy ārye salakṣmaṇam |
sthiramitraṁ hatāmitraṁ śacīva tridaśeśvaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
tām evam uktvā rājantīṁ sītāṁ sākṣād iva śriyam |
ājagāma mahāvego hanūmān yatra rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
