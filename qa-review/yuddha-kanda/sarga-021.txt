=== Sarga 21 ===

---
1.
tatas tam akṣobhya balaṁ laṅkādhipataye carāḥ |
suvele rāghavaṁ śaile niviṣṭaṁ pratyavedayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
cārāṇāṁ rāvaṇaḥ śrutvā prāptaṁ rāmaṁ mahābalam |
jātodvego ’bhavat kiṁ cic chārdūlaṁ vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
ayathāvac ca te varṇo dīnaś cāsi niśācara |
nāsi kaccid amitrāṇāṁ kruddhānāṁ vaśam āgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
iti tenānuśiṣṭas tu vācaṁ mandam udīrayat |
tadā rākṣasaśārdūlaṁ śārdūlo bhayavihvalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
na te cārayituṁ śakyā rājan vānarapuṁgavāḥ |
vikrāntā balavantaś ca rāghaveṇa ca rakṣitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
nāpi saṁbhāṣituṁ śakyāḥ saṁpraśno ’tra na labhyate |
sarvato rakṣyate panthā vānaraiḥ parvatopamaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
praviṣṭamātre jñāto ’haṁ bale tasminn acārite |
balād gr̥hīto bahubhir bahudhāsmi vidāritaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
jānubhir muṣṭibhir dantais talaiś cābhihato bhr̥śam |
pariṇīto ’smi haribhir balavadbhir amarṣaṇaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
pariṇīya ca sarvatra nīto ’haṁ rāmasaṁsadam |
rudhirādigdhasarvāṅgo vihvalaś calitendriyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
haribhir vadhyamānaś ca yācamānaḥ kr̥tāñjaliḥ |
rāghaveṇa paritrāto jīvāmi ha yadr̥cchayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
eṣa śailaiḥ śilābhiś ca pūrayitvā mahārṇavam |
dvāram āśritya laṅkāyā rāmas tiṣṭhati sāyudhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
garuḍavyūham āsthāya sarvato haribhir vr̥taḥ |
māṁ visr̥jya mahātejā laṅkām evābhivartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
purā prākāram āyāti kṣipram ekataraṁ kuru |
sītāṁ cāsmai prayacchāśu suyuddhaṁ vā pradīyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
manasā saṁtatāpātha tac chrutvā rākṣasādhipaḥ |
śārdūlasya mahad vākyam athovāca sa rāvaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
yadi māṁ pratiyudhyeran devagandharvadānavāḥ |
naiva sītāṁ pradāsyāmi sarvalokabhayād api ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
evam uktvā mahātejā rāvaṇaḥ punar abravīt |
cāritā bhavatā senā ke ’tra śūrāḥ plavaṁgamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
kīdr̥śāḥ kiṁprabhāvāś ca vānarā ye durāsadāḥ |
kasya putrāś ca pautrāś ca tattvam ākhyāhi rākṣasa ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tatr atra pratipatsyāmi jñātvā teṣāṁ balābalam |
avaśyaṁ balasaṁkhyānaṁ kartavyaṁ yuddham icchatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
athaivam uktaḥ śārdūlo rāvaṇenottamaś caraḥ |
idaṁ vacanam ārebhe vaktuṁ rāvaṇasaṁnidhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
atharkṣarajasaḥ putro yudhi rājan sudurjayaḥ |
gadgadasyātha putro ’tra jāmbavān iti viśrutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
gadgadasyaiva putro ’nyo guruputraḥ śatakratoḥ |
kadanaṁ yasya putreṇa kr̥tam ekena rakṣasām ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
suṣeṇaś cāpi dharmātmā putro dharmasya vīryavān |
saumyaḥ somātmajaś cātra rājan dadhimukhaḥ kapiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
sumukho durmukhaś cātra vegadarśī ca vānaraḥ |
mr̥tyur vānararūpeṇa nūnaṁ sr̥ṣṭaḥ svayambhuvā ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
putro hutavahasyātha nīlaḥ senāpatiḥ svayam |
anilasya ca putro ’tra hanūmān iti viśrutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
naptā śakrasya durdharṣo balavān aṅgado yuvā |
maindaś ca dvividaś cobhau balināv aśvisaṁbhavau ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
putrā vaivasvatasyātra pañcakālāntakopamāḥ |
gajo gavākṣo gavayaḥ śarabho gandhamādanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
śveto jyotirmukhaś cātra bhāskarasyātmasaṁbhavau |
varuṇasya ca putro ’tha hemakūṭaḥ plavaṁgamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
viśvakarmasuto vīro nalaḥ plavagasattamaḥ |
vikrānto vegavān atra vasuputraḥ sudurdharaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
daśavānarakoṭyaś ca śūrāṇāṁ yuddhakāṅkṣiṇām |
śrīmatāṁ devaputrāṇāṁ śeṣān nākhyātum utsahe ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
putro daśarathasyaiṣa siṁhasaṁhanano yuvā |
dūṣaṇo nihato yena kharaś ca triśirās tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
nāsti rāmasya sadr̥śo vikrame bhuvi kaś cana |
virādho nihato yena kabandhaś cāntakopamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
vaktuṁ na śakto rāmasya naraḥ kaś cid guṇān kṣitau |
janasthānagatā yena tāvanto rākṣasā hatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
lakṣmaṇaś cātra dharmātmā mātaṁgānām ivarṣabhaḥ |
yasya bāṇapathaṁ prāpya na jīved api vāsavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
rākṣasānāṁ variṣṭhaś ca tava bhrātā vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
parigr̥hya purīṁ laṅkāṁ rāghavasya hite rataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
iti sarvaṁ samākhyātaṁ tavedaṁ vānaraṁ balam |
suvele ’dhiṣṭhitaṁ śaile śeṣakārye bhavān gatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
