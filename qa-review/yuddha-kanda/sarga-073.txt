=== Sarga 73 ===

---
1.
atha tasyām avasthāyāṁ lakṣmaṇaṁ rāvaṇānujaḥ |
pareṣām ahitaṁ vākyam arthasādhakam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
asyānīkasya mahato bhedane yatalakṣmaṇa |
rākṣasendrasuto ’py atra bhinne dr̥śyo bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sa tvam indrāśaniprakhyaiḥ śarair avakiran parān |
abhidravāśu yāvad vai naitat karma samāpyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
jahi vīradurātmānaṁ māyāparam adhārmikam |
rāvaṇiṁ krūrakarmāṇaṁ sarvalokabhayāvaham ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
vibhīṣaṇavacaḥ śrutvā lakṣmaṇaḥ śubhalakṣaṇaḥ |
vavarṣa śaravarṣāṇi rākṣasendrasutaṁ prati ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
r̥kṣāḥ śākhāmr̥gāś caiva drumādrivarayodhinaḥ |
abhyadhāvanta sahitās tad anīkam avasthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
rākṣasāś ca śitair bāṇair asibhiḥ śaktitomaraiḥ |
udyataiḥ samavartanta kapisainyajighāṁsavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sa saṁprahāras tumulaḥ saṁjajñe kapirakṣasām |
śabdena mahatā laṅkāṁ nādayan vai samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
śastrair bahuvidhākāraiḥ śitair bāṇaiś ca pādapaiḥ |
udyatair giriśr̥ṅgaiś ca ghorair ākāśam āvr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
te rākṣasā vānareṣu vikr̥tānanabāhavaḥ |
niveśayantaḥ śastrāṇi cakrus te sumahad bhayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tathaiva sakalair vr̥kṣair giriśr̥ṅgaiś ca vānarāḥ |
abhijaghnur nijaghnuś ca samare rākṣasarṣabhān ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
r̥kṣavānaramukhyaiś ca mahākāyair mahābalaiḥ |
rakṣasāṁ vadhyamānānāṁ mahad bhayam ajāyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
svam anīkaṁ viṣaṇṇaṁ tu śrutvā śatrubhir arditam |
udatiṣṭhata durdharṣas tat karmaṇy ananuṣṭhite ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
vr̥kṣāndhakārān niṣkramya jātakrodhaḥ sa rāvaṇiḥ |
āruroha rathaṁ sajjaṁ pūrvayuktaṁ sa rākṣasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa bhīmakārmukaśaraḥ kr̥ṣṇāñjanacayopamaḥ |
raktāsyanayanaḥ krūro babhau mr̥tyur ivāntakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
dr̥ṣṭvaiva tu rathasthaṁ taṁ paryavartata tad balam |
rakṣasāṁ bhīmavegānāṁ lakṣmaṇena yuyutsatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tasmin kāle tu hanumān udyamya sudurāsadam |
dharaṇīdharasaṁkāśī mahāvr̥kṣam ariṁdamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
sa rākṣasānāṁ tat sainyaṁ kālāgnir iva nirdahan |
cakāra bahubhir vr̥kṣair niḥsaṁjñaṁ yudhi vānaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
vidhvaṁsayantaṁ tarasā dr̥ṣṭvaiva pavanātmajam |
rākṣasānāṁ sahasrāṇi hanūmantam avākiran ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
śitaśūladharāḥ śūlair asibhiś cāsipāṇayaḥ |
śaktibhiḥ śaktihastāś ca paṭṭasaiḥ paṭṭasāyudhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
parighaiś ca gadābhiś ca kuntaiś ca śubhadarśanaiḥ |
śataśaś ca śataghnībhir āyasair api mudgaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
ghoraiḥ paraśubhiś caiva bhiṇḍipālaiś ca rākṣasāḥ |
muṣṭibhir vajravegaiś ca talair aśanisaṁnibhaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
abhijaghnuḥ samāsādya samantāt parvatopamam |
teṣām api ca saṁkruddhaś cakāra kadanaṁ mahat ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
sa dadarśa kapiśreṣṭham acalopamam indrajit |
sūdayānam amitraghnam amitrān pavanātmajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
sa sārathim uvācedaṁ yāhi yatraiṣa vānaraḥ |
kṣayam eva hi naḥ kuryād rākṣasānām upekṣitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
ity uktaḥ sārathis tena yayau yatra sa mārutiḥ |
vahan paramadurdharṣaṁ sthitam indrajitaṁ rathe ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
so ’bhyupetya śarān khaḍgān paṭṭasāsiparaśvadhān |
abhyavarṣata durdharṣaḥ kapimūrdhni sa rākṣasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tāni śastrāṇi ghorāṇi pratigr̥hya sa mārutiḥ |
roṣeṇa mahatāviṣo vākyaṁ cedam uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
yudhyasva yadi śūro ’si rāvaṇātmaja durmate |
vāyuputraṁ samāsādya na jīvan pratiyāsyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
bāhubhyāṁ saṁprayudhyasva yadi me dvandvam āhave |
vegaṁ sahasva durbuddhe tatas tvaṁ rakṣasāṁ varaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
hanūmantaṁ jighāṁsantaṁ samudyataśarāsanam |
rāvaṇātmajam ācaṣṭe lakṣmaṇāya vibhīṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
yas tu vāsavanirjetā rāvaṇasyātmasaṁbhavaḥ |
sa eṣa ratham āsthāya hanūmantaṁ jighāṁsati ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
tam apratimasaṁsthānaiḥ śaraiḥ śatruvidāraṇaiḥ |
jīvitāntakarair ghoraiḥ saumitre rāvaṇiṁ jahi ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
ity evam uktas tu tadā mahātmā; vibhīṣaṇenārivibhīṣaṇena |
dadarśa taṁ parvatasaṁnikāśaṁ; rathasthitaṁ bhīmabalaṁ durāsadam ||

=== meaning ===

---
