=== Sarga 96 ===

---
1.
tau tathā yudhyamānau tu samare rāmarāvaṇau |
dadr̥śuḥ sarvabhūtāni vismitenāntarātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
ardayantau tu samare tayos tau syandanottamau |
parasparavadhe yuktau ghorarūpau babhūvatuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
maṇḍalāni ca vīthīś ca gatapratyāgatāni ca |
darśayantau bahuvidhāṁ sūtau sārathyajāṁ gatim ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
ardayan rāvaṇaṁ rāmo rāghavaṁ cāpi rāvaṇaḥ |
gativegaṁ samāpannau pravartana nivartane ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
kṣipatoḥ śarajālāni tayos tau syandanottamau |
ceratuḥ saṁyugamahīṁ sāsārau jaladāv iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
darśayitvā tadā tau tu gatiṁ bahuvidhāṁ raṇe |
parasparasyābhimukhau punar eva ca tasthatuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
dhuraṁ dhureṇa rathayor vaktraṁ vaktreṇa vājinām |
patākāś ca patākābhiḥ sameyuḥ sthitayos tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
rāvaṇasya tato rāmo dhanurmuktaiḥ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ |
caturbhiś caturo dīptān hayān pratyapasarpayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sa krodhavaśam āpanno hayānām apasarpaṇe |
mumoca niśitān bāṇān rāghavāya niśācaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
so ’tividdho balavatā daśagrīveṇa rāghavaḥ |
jagāma na vikāraṁ ca na cāpi vyathito ’bhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
cikṣepa ca punar bāṇān vajrapātasamasvanān |
sārathiṁ vajrahastasya samuddiśya niśācaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
mātales tu mahāvegāḥ śarīre patitāḥ śarāḥ |
na sūkṣmam api saṁmohaṁ vyathāṁ vā pradadur yudhi ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tayā dharṣaṇayā kroddho mātaler na tathātmanaḥ |
cakāra śarajālena rāghavo vimukhaṁ ripum ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
viṁśatiṁ triṁśataṁ ṣaṣṭiṁ śataśo ’tha sahasraśaḥ |
mumoca rāghavo vīraḥ sāyakān syandane ripoḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
gadānāṁ musalānāṁ ca parighāṇāṁ ca nisvanaiḥ |
śarāṇāṁ puṅkhavātaiś ca kṣubhitāḥ saptasāgarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
kṣubdhānāṁ sāgarāṇāṁ ca pātālatalavāsinaḥ |
vyathitāḥ pannagāḥ sarve dānavāś ca sahasraśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
cakampe medinī kr̥tsnā saśailavanakānanā |
bhāskaro niṣprabhaś cābhūn na vavau cāpi mārutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tato devāḥ sagandharvāḥ siddhāś ca paramarṣayaḥ |
cintām āpedire sarve sakiṁnaramahoragāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
svasti gobrāhmaṇebhyo ’stu lokās tiṣṭhantu śāśvatāḥ |
jayatāṁ rāghavaḥ saṁkhye rāvaṇaṁ rākṣaseśvaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tataḥ kruddho mahābāhū raghūṇāṁ kīrtivardhanaḥ |
saṁdhāya dhanuṣā rāmaḥ kṣuram āśīviṣopamam |
rāvaṇasya śiro ’cchindac chrīmaj jvalitakuṇḍalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tac chiraḥ patitaṁ bhūmau dr̥ṣṭaṁ lokais tribhis tadā |
tasyaiva sadr̥śaṁ cānyad rāvaṇasyotthitaṁ śiraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tat kṣipraṁ kṣiprahastena rāmeṇa kṣiprakāriṇā |
dvitīyaṁ rāvaṇaśiraś chinnaṁ saṁyati sāyakaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
chinnamātraṁ ca tac chīrṣaṁ punar anyat sma dr̥śyate |
tad apy aśanisaṁkāśaiś chinnaṁ rāmeṇa sāyakaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
evam eva śataṁ chinnaṁ śirasāṁ tulyavarcasām |
na caiva rāvaṇasyānto dr̥śyate jīvitakṣaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tataḥ sarvāstravid vīraḥ kausalyānandivardhanaḥ |
mārgaṇair bahubhir yuktaś cintayām āsa rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
mārīco nihato yais tu kharo yais tu sudūṣaṇaḥ |
krañcāraṇye virādhas tu kabandho daṇḍakā vane ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
ta ime sāyakāḥ sarve yuddhe pratyayikā mama |
kiṁ nu tat kāraṇaṁ yena rāvaṇe mandatejasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
iti cintāparaś cāsīd apramattaś ca saṁyuge |
vavarṣa śaravarṣāṇi rāghavo rāvaṇorasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
rāvaṇo ’pi tataḥ kruddho rathastho rākṣaseśvaraḥ |
gadāmusalavarṣeṇa rāmaṁ pratyardayad raṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
devadānavayakṣāṇāṁ piśācoragarakṣasām |
paśyatāṁ tan mahad yuddhaṁ sarvarātram avartata ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
naiva ratriṁ na divasaṁ na muhūrtaṁ na cakṣaṇam |
rāmarāvaṇayor yuddhaṁ virāmam upagacchati ||

=== meaning ===

---
