=== Sarga 66 ===

---
1.
nirgataṁ makarākṣaṁ te dr̥ṣṭvā vānarapuṁgavāḥ |
āplutya sahasā sarve yoddhukāmā vyavasthitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tataḥ pravr̥ttaṁ sumahat tad yuddhaṁ lomaharṣaṇam |
niśācaraiḥ plavaṁgānāṁ devānāṁ dānavair iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
vr̥kṣaśūlanipātaiś ca śilāparighapātanaiḥ |
anyonyaṁ mardayanti sma tadā kapiniśācarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
śaktiśūlagadākhaḍgais tomaraiś ca niśācarāḥ |
paṭṭasair bhindipālaiś ca bāṇapātaiḥ samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
pāśamudgaradaṇḍaiś ca nirghātaiś cāparais tathā |
kadanaṁ kapisiṁhānāṁ cakrus te rajanīcarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
bāṇaughair arditāś cāpi kharaputreṇa vānarāḥ |
saṁbhrāntamanasaḥ sarve dudruvur bhayapīḍitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tān dr̥ṣṭvā rākṣasāḥ sarve dravamāṇān vanaukasaḥ |
nedus te siṁhavad dhr̥ṣṭā rākṣasā jitakāśinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
vidravatsu tadā teṣu vānareṣu samantataḥ |
rāmas tān vārayām āsa śaravarṣeṇa rākṣasān ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
vāritān rākṣasān dr̥ṣṭvā makarākṣo niśācaraḥ |
krodhān alasam āviṣṭo vacanaṁ cedam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tiṣṭha rāma mayā sārdhaṁ dvandvayuddhaṁ dadāmi te |
tyājayiṣyāmi te prāṇān dhanurmuktaiḥ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
yat tadā daṇḍakāraṇye pitaraṁ hatavān mama |
madagrataḥ svakarmasthaṁ smr̥tvā roṣo ’bhivardhate ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
dahyante bhr̥śam aṅgāni durātman mama rāghava |
yan mayāsi na dr̥ṣṭas tvaṁ tasmin kāle mahāvane ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
diṣṭyāsi darśanaṁ rāma mama tvaṁ prāptavān iha |
kāṅkṣito ’si kṣudhārtasya siṁhasyevetaro mr̥gaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
adya madbāṇavegena pretarāḍ viṣayaṁ gataḥ |
ye tvayā nihatāḥ śūrāḥ saha tais tvaṁ sameṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
bahunātra kim uktena śr̥ṇu rāma vaco mama |
paśyantu sakalā lokās tvāṁ māṁ caiva raṇājire ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
astrair vā gadayā vāpi bāhubhyāṁ vā mahāhave |
abhyastaṁ yena vā rāma tena vā vartatāṁ yudhi ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
makarākṣavacaḥ śrutvā rāmo daśarathātmajaḥ |
abravīt prahasan vākyam uttarottaravādinam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
caturdaśasahasrāṇi rakṣasāṁ tvatpitā ca yaḥ |
triśirā dūṣaṇaś cāpi daṇḍake nihatā mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
svāśitās tava māṁsena gr̥dhragomāyuvāyasāḥ |
bhaviṣyanty adya vai pāpa tīkṣṇatuṇḍanakhāṅkuśāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
evam uktas tu rāmeṇa kharaputro niśācaraḥ |
bāṇaughān asr̥jat tasmai rāghavāya raṇājire ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tāñ śarāñ śaravarṣeṇa rāmaś ciccheda naikadhā |
nipetur bhuvi te chinnā rukmapuṅkhāḥ sahasraśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tad yuddham abhavat tatra sametyānyonyam ojasā |
khara rākṣasaputrasya sūnor daśarathasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
jīmūtayor ivākāśe śabdo jyātalayos tadā |
dhanur muktaḥ svanotkr̥ṣṭaḥ śrūyate ca raṇājire ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
devadānavagandharvāḥ kiṁnarāś ca mahoragāḥ |
antarikṣagatāḥ sarve draṣṭukāmās tad adbhutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
viddham anyonyagātreṣu dviguṇaṁ vardhate balam |
kr̥tapratikr̥tānyonyaṁ kurvāte tau raṇājire ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
rāmam uktās tu bāṇaughān rākṣasas tv acchinad raṇe |
rakṣomuktāṁs tu rāmo vai naikadhā prācchinac charaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
bāṇaughavitatāḥ sarvā diśaś ca vidiśas tathā |
saṁchannā vasudhā caiva samantān na prakāśate ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tataḥ kruddho mahābāhur dhanuś ciccheda rakṣasaḥ |
aṣṭābhir atha nārācaiḥ sūtaṁ vivyādha rāghavaḥ |
bhittvā śarai rathaṁ rāmo rathāśvān samapātayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
viratho vasudhāṁ tiṣṭhan makarākṣo niśācaraḥ |
atiṣṭhad vasudhāṁ rakṣaḥ śūlaṁ jagrāha pāṇinā |
trāsanaṁ sarvabhūtānāṁ yugāntāgnisamaprabham ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
vibhrāmya ca mahac chūlaṁ prajvalantaṁ niśācaraḥ |
sa krodhāt prāhiṇot tasmai rāghavāya mahāhave ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
tam āpatantaṁ jvalitaṁ kharaputrakarāc cyutam |
bāṇais tu tribhir ākāśe śūlaṁ ciccheda rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
sacchinno naikadhā śūlo divyahāṭakamaṇḍitaḥ |
vyaśīryata mahokleva rāmabāṇārdito bhuvi ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
tac chūlaṁ nihataṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rāmeṇādbhutakarmaṇā |
sādhu sādhv iti bhūtāni vyāharanti nabhogatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
tad dr̥ṣṭvā nihataṁ śūlaṁ makarākṣo niśācaraḥ |
muṣṭim udyamya kākutsthaṁ tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti cābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
sa taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā patantaṁ vai prahasya raghunandanaḥ |
pāvakāstraṁ tato rāmaḥ saṁdadhe svaśarāsane ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
tenāstreṇa hataṁ rakṣaḥ kākutsthena tadā raṇe |
saṁchinnahr̥dayaṁ tatra papāta ca mamāra ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
dr̥ṣṭvā te rākṣasāḥ sarve makarākṣasya pātanam |
laṅkām eva pradhāvanta rāmabālārditās tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
daśarathanr̥paputrabāṇavegai; rajanicaraṁ nihataṁ kharātmajaṁ tam |
dadr̥śur atha ca devatāḥ prahr̥ṣṭā; girim iva vajrahataṁ yathā viśīrṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
