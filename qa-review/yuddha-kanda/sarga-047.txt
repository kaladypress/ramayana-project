=== Sarga 47 ===

---
1.
tasmin hate rākṣasasainyapāle; plavaṁgamānām r̥ṣabheṇa yuddhe |
bhīmāyudhaṁ sāgaratulyavegaṁ; pradudruve rākṣasarājasainyam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
gatvā tu rakṣo’dhipateḥ śaśaṁsuḥ; senāpatiṁ pāvakasūnuśastam |
tac cāpi teṣāṁ vacanaṁ niśamya; rakṣo’dhipaḥ krodhavaśaṁ jagāma ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
saṁkhye prahastaṁ nihataṁ niśamya; śokārditaḥ krodhaparītacetāḥ |
uvāca tān nairr̥tayodhamukhyān; indro yathā cāmarayodhamukhyān ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
nāvajñā ripave kāryā yair indrabalasūdanaḥ |
sūditaḥ sainyapālo me sānuyātraḥ sakuñjaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
so ’haṁ ripuvināśāya vijayāyāvicārayan |
svayam eva gamiṣyāmi raṇaśīrṣaṁ tad adbhutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
adya tad vānarānīkaṁ rāmaṁ ca sahalakṣmaṇam |
nirdahiṣyāmi bāṇaughair vanaṁ dīptair ivāgnibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sa evam uktvā jvalanaprakāśaṁ; rathaṁ turaṁgottamarājiyuktam |
prakāśamānaṁ vapuṣā jvalantaṁ; samārurohāmararājaśatruḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sa śaṅkhabherīpaṭaha praṇādair; āsphoṭitakṣveḍitasiṁhanādaiḥ |
puṇyaiḥ stavaiś cāpy abhipūjyamānas; tadā yayau rākṣasarājamukhyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sa śailajīmūtanikāśa rūpair; māṁsāśanaiḥ pāvakadīptanetraiḥ |
babhau vr̥to rākṣasarājamukhyair; bhūtair vr̥to rudra ivāmareśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tato nagaryāḥ sahasā mahaujā; niṣkramya tad vānarasainyam ugram |
mahārṇavābhrastanitaṁ dadarśa; samudyataṁ pādapaśailahastam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tad rākṣasānīkam atipracaṇḍam; ālokya rāmo bhujagendrabāhuḥ |
vibhīṣaṇaṁ śastrabhr̥tāṁ variṣṭham; uvāca senānugataḥ pr̥thuśrīḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
nānāpatākādhvajaśastrajuṣṭaṁ; prāsāsiśūlāyudhacakrajuṣṭam |
sainyaṁ nagendropamanāgajuṣṭaṁ; kasyedam akṣobhyam abhīrujuṣṭam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tatas tu rāmasya niśamya vākyaṁ; vibhīṣaṇaḥ śakrasamānavīryaḥ |
śaśaṁsa rāmasya balapravekaṁ; mahātmanāṁ rākṣasapuṁgavānām ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
yo ’sau gajaskandhagato mahātmā; navoditārkopamatāmravaktraḥ |
prakampayan nāgaśiro ’bhyupaiti; hy akampanaṁ tv enam avehi rājan ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
yo ’sau rathastho mr̥garājaketur; dhūnvan dhanuḥ śakradhanuḥprakāśam |
karīva bhāty ugravivr̥ttadaṁṣṭraḥ; sa indrajin nāma varapradhānaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
yaś caiṣa vindhyāstamahendrakalpo; dhanvī rathastho ’tiratho ’tivīryaḥ |
visphārayaṁś cāpam atulyamānaṁ; nāmnātikāyo ’tivivr̥ddhakāyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
yo ’sau navārkoditatāmracakṣur; āruhya ghaṇṭāninadapraṇādam |
gajaṁ kharaṁ garjati vai mahātmā; mahodaro nāma sa eṣa vīraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
yo ’sau hayaṁ kāñcanacitrabhāṇḍam; āruhya saṁdhyābhragiriprakāśam |
prāsaṁ samudyamya marīcinaddhaṁ; piśāca eṣāśanitulyavegaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
yaś caiṣa śūlaṁ niśitaṁ pragr̥hya; vidyutprabhaṁ kiṁkaravajravegam |
vr̥ṣendram āsthāya giriprakāśam; āyāti so ’sau triśirā yaśasvī ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
asau ca jīmūtanikāśa rūpaḥ; kumbhaḥ pr̥thuvyūḍhasujātavakṣāḥ |
samāhitaḥ pannagarājaketur; visphārayan bhāti dhanur vidhūnvan ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
yaś caiṣa jāmbūnadavajrajuṣṭaṁ; dīptaṁ sadhūmaṁ parighaṁ pragr̥hya |
āyāti rakṣobalaketubhūtaḥ; so ’sau nikumbho ’dbhutaghorakarmā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
yaś caiṣa cāpāsiśaraughajuṣṭaṁ; patākinaṁ pāvakadīptarūpam |
rathaṁ samāsthāya vibhāty udagro; narāntako ’sau nagaśr̥ṅgayodhī ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
yaś caiṣa nānāvidhaghorarūpair; vyāghroṣṭranāgendramr̥gendravaktraiḥ |
bhūtair vr̥to bhāti vivr̥ttanetraiḥ; so ’sau surāṇām api darpahantā ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
yatraitad indupratimaṁ vibhātic; chattraṁ sitaṁ sūkṣmaśalākam agryam |
atraiṣa rakṣo’dhipatir mahātmā; bhūtair vr̥to rudra ivāvabhāti ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
asau kirīṭī calakuṇḍalāsyo; nāgendravindhyopamabhīmakāyaḥ |
mahendravaivasvatadarpahantā; rakṣo’dhipaḥ sūrya ivāvabhāti ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
pratyuvāca tato rāmo vibhīṣaṇam ariṁdamam |
aho dīpto mahātejā rāvaṇo rākṣaseśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
āditya iva duṣprekṣyo raśmibhir bhāti rāvaṇaḥ |
suvyaktaṁ lakṣaye hy asya rūpaṁ tejaḥsamāvr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
devadānavavīrāṇāṁ vapur naivaṁvidhaṁ bhavet |
yādr̥śaṁ rākṣasendrasya vapur etat prakāśate ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
sarve parvatasaṁkāśāḥ sarve parvatayodhinaḥ |
sarve dīptāyudhadharā yodhaś cāsya mahaujasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
bhāti rākṣasarājo ’sau pradīptair bhīmavikramaiḥ |
bhūtaiḥ parivr̥tas tīkṣṇair dehavadbhir ivāntakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
evam uktvā tato rāmo dhanur ādāya vīryavān |
lakṣmaṇānucaras tasthau samuddhr̥tya śarottamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
tataḥ sa rakṣo’dhipatir mahātmā; rakṣāṁsi tāny āha mahābalāni |
dvāreṣu caryāgr̥hagopureṣu; sunirvr̥tās tiṣṭhata nirviśaṅkāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
visarjayitvā sahasā tatas tān; gateṣu rakṣaḥsu yathāniyogam |
vyadārayad vānarasāgaraughaṁ; mahājhaṣaḥ pūrmam ivārṇavaugham ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
tam āpatantaṁ sahasā samīkṣya; dīpteṣucāpaṁ yudhi rākṣasendram |
mahat samutpāṭya mahīdharāgraṁ; dudrāva rakṣo’dhipatiṁ harīśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
tac chailaśr̥ṅgaṁ bahuvr̥kṣasānuṁ; pragr̥hya cikṣepa niśācarāya |
tam āpatantaṁ sahasā samīkṣya; bibheda bāṇais tapanīyapuṅkhaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
tasmin pravr̥ddhottamasānuvr̥kṣe; śr̥ṅge vikīrṇe patite pr̥thivyām |
mahāhikalpaṁ śaram antakābhaṁ; samādade rākṣasalokanāthaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
sa taṁ gr̥hītvānilatulyavegaṁ; savisphuliṅgajvalanaprakāśam |
bāṇaṁ mahendrāśanitulyavegaṁ; cikṣepa sugrīvavadhāya ruṣṭaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
sa sāyako rāvaṇabāhumuktaḥ; śakrāśaniprakhyavapuḥ śitāgraḥ |
sugrīvam āsādya bibheda vegād; guheritā kraucam ivograśaktiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
sa sāyakārto viparītacetāḥ; kūjan pr̥thivyāṁ nipapāta vīraḥ |
taṁ prekṣya bhūmau patitaṁ visaṁjmaṁ; neduḥ prahr̥ṣṭā yudhi yātudhānāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
tato gavākṣo gavayaḥ sudaṁṣṭras; tatharṣabho jyotimukho nalaś ca |
śailān samudyamya vivr̥ddhakāyāḥ; pradudruvus taṁ prati rākṣasendram ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
teṣāṁ prahārān sa cakāra meghān; rakṣo’dhipo bāṇagaṇaiḥ śitāgraiḥ |
tān vānarendrān api bāṇajālair; bibheda jāmbūnadacitrapuṅkhaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
te vānarendrās tridaśāribāṇair; bhinnā nipetur bhuvi bhīmarūpāḥ |
tatas tu tad vānarasainyam ugraṁ; pracchādayām āsa sa bāṇajālaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
te vadhyamānāḥ patitāgryavīrā; nānadyamānā bhayaśalyaviddhāḥ |
śākhāmr̥gā rāvaṇasāyakārtā; jagmuḥ śaraṇyaṁ śaraṇaṁ sma rāmam ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
tato mahātmā sa dhanur dhanuṣmān; ādāya rāmaḥ saharā jagāma |
taṁ lakṣmaṇaḥ prāñjalir abhyupetya; uvāca vākyaṁ paramārthayuktam ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
kāmam āryaḥ suparyāpto vadhāyāsya durātmanaḥ |
vidhamiṣyāmy ahaṁ nīcam anujānīhi māṁ vibho ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
tam abravīn mahātejā rāmaḥ satyaparākramaḥ |
gaccha yatnaparaś cāpi bhava lakṣmaṇa saṁyuge ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
rāvaṇo hi mahāvīryo raṇe ’dbhutaparākramaḥ |
trailokyenāpi saṁkruddho duṣprasahyo na saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
tasya cchidrāṇi mārgasva svacchidrāṇi ca gopaya |
cakṣuṣā dhanuṣā yatnād rakṣātmānaṁ samāhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
rāghavasya vacaḥ śrutvā saṁpariṣvajya pūjya ca |
abhivādya tato rāmaṁ yayau saumitrir āhavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
sa rāvaṇaṁ vāraṇahastabāhur; dadarśa dīptodyatabhīmacāpam |
pracchādayantaṁ śaravr̥ṣṭijālais; tān vānarān bhinnavikīrṇadehān ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
tam ālokya mahātejā hanūmān mārutātmajā |
nivārya śarajālāni pradudrāva sa rāvaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
52.
rathaṁ tasya samāsādya bhujam udyamya dakṣiṇam |
trāsayan rāvaṇaṁ dhīmān hanūmān vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
53.
devadānavagandharvā yakṣāś ca saha rākṣasaiḥ |
avadhyatvāt tvayā bhagnā vānarebhyas tu te bhayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
54.
eṣa me dakṣiṇo bāhuḥ pañcaśākhaḥ samudyataḥ |
vidhamiṣyati te dehād bhūtātmānaṁ ciroṣitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
55.
śrutvā hanūmato vākyaṁ rāvaṇo bhīmavikramaḥ |
saṁraktanayanaḥ krodhād idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
56.
kṣipraṁ prahara niḥśaṅkaṁ sthirāṁ kīrtim avāpnuhi |
tatas tvāṁ jñātivikrāntaṁ nāśayiṣyāmi vānara ||

=== meaning ===

---
57.
rāvaṇasya vacaḥ śrutvā vāyusūnur vaco ’bravīt |
prahr̥taṁ hi mayā pūrvam akṣaṁ smara sutaṁ tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
58.
evam ukto mahātejā rāvaṇo rākṣaseśvaraḥ |
ājaghānānilasutaṁ talenorasi vīryavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
59.
sa talābhihatas tena cacāla ca muhur muhuḥ |
ājaghānābhisaṁkruddhas talenaivāmaradviṣam ||

=== meaning ===

---
60.
tatas talenābhihato vānareṇa mahātmanā |
daśagrīvaḥ samādhūto yathā bhūmicale ’calaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
61.
saṁgrāme taṁ tathā dr̥ṣṭva rāvaṇaṁ talatāḍitam |
r̥ṣayo vānarāḥ siddhā nedur devāḥ sahāsurāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
62.
athāśvasya mahātejā rāvaṇo vākyam abravīt |
sādhu vānaravīryeṇa ślāghanīyo ’si me ripuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
63.
rāvaṇenaivam uktas tu mārutir vākyam abravīt |
dhig astu mama vīryaṁ tu yat tvaṁ jīvasi rāvaṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
64.
sakr̥t tu praharedānīṁ durbuddhe kiṁ vikatthase |
tatas tvāṁ māmako muṣṭir nayiṣyāmi yathākṣayam |
tato mārutivākyena krodhas tasya tadājvalat ||

=== meaning ===

---
65.
saṁraktanayano yatnān muṣṭim udyamya dakṣiṇam |
pātayām āsa vegena vānarorasi vīryavān |
hanūmān vakṣasi vyūdhe saṁcacāla hataḥ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
66.
vihvalaṁ taṁ tadā dr̥ṣṭvā hanūmantaṁ mahābalam |
rathenātirathaḥ śīghraṁ nīlaṁ prati samabhyagāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
67.
pannagapratimair bhīmaiḥ paramarmātibhedibhiḥ |
śarair ādīpayām āsa nīlaṁ haricamūpatim ||

=== meaning ===

---
68.
sa śaraughasamāyasto nīlaḥ kapicamūpatiḥ |
kareṇaikena śailāgraṁ rakṣo’dhipataye ’sr̥jat ||

=== meaning ===

---
69.
hanūmān api tejasvī samāśvasto mahāmanāḥ |
viprekṣamāṇo yuddhepsuḥ saroṣam idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
70.
nīlena saha saṁyuktaṁ rāvaṇaṁ rākṣaseśvaram |
anyena yudhyamānasya na yuktam abhidhāvanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
71.
rāvaṇo ’pi mahātejās tac chr̥ṅgaṁ saptabhiḥ śaraiḥ |
ājaghāna sutīkṣṇāgrais tad vikīrṇaṁ papāta ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
72.
tad vikīrṇaṁ gireḥ śr̥ṅgaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā haricamūpatiḥ |
kālāgnir iva jajvāla krodhena paravīrahā ||

=== meaning ===

---
73.
so ’śvakarṇān dhavān sālāṁś cūtāṁś cāpi supuṣpitān |
anyāṁś ca vividhān vr̥kṣān nīlaś cikṣepa saṁyuge ||

=== meaning ===

---
74.
sa tān vr̥kṣān samāsādya praticiccheda rāvaṇaḥ |
abhyavarṣat sughoreṇa śaravarṣeṇa pāvakim ||

=== meaning ===

---
75.
abhivr̥ṣṭaḥ śaraugheṇa megheneva mahācalaḥ |
hrasvaṁ kr̥tvā tadā rūpaṁ dhvajāgre nipapāta ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
76.
pāvakātmajam ālokya dhvajāgre samavasthitam |
jajvāla rāvaṇaḥ krodhāt tato nīlo nanāda ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
77.
dhvajāgre dhanuṣaś cāgre kirīṭāgre ca taṁ harim |
lakṣmaṇo ’tha hanūmāṁś ca dr̥ṣṭvā rāmaś ca vismitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
78.
rāvaṇo ’pi mahātejāḥ kapilāghavavismitaḥ |
astram āhārayām āsa dīptam āgneyam adbhutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
79.
tatas te cukruśur hr̥ṣṭā labdhalakṣyāḥ plavaṁgamāḥ |
nīlalāghavasaṁbhrāntaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rāvaṇam āhave ||

=== meaning ===

---
80.
vānarāṇāṁ ca nādena saṁrabdho rāvaṇas tadā |
saṁbhramāviṣṭahr̥dayo na kiṁ cit pratyapadyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
81.
āgneyenātha saṁyuktaṁ gr̥hītvā rāvaṇaḥ śaram |
dhvajaśīrṣasthitaṁ nīlam udaikṣata niśācaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
82.
tato ’bravīn mahātejā rāvaṇo rākṣaseśvaraḥ |
kape lāghavayukto ’si māyayā parayānayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
83.
jīvitaṁ khalu rakṣasva yadi śaknoṣi vānara |
tāni tāny ātmarūpāṇi sr̥jase tvam anekaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
84.
tathāpi tvāṁ mayā muktaḥ sāyako ’straprayojitaḥ |
jīvitaṁ parirakṣantaṁ jīvitād bhraṁśayiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
85.
evam uktvā mahābāhū rāvaṇo rākṣaseśvaraḥ |
saṁdhāya bāṇam astreṇa camūpatim atāḍayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
86.
so ’strayuktena bāṇena nīlo vakṣasi tāḍitaḥ |
nirdahyamānaḥ sahasā nipapāta mahītale ||

=== meaning ===

---
87.
pitr̥māhātmya saṁyogād ātmanaś cāpi tejasā |
jānubhyām apatad bhūmau na ca prāṇair vyayujyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
88.
visaṁjñaṁ vānaraṁ dr̥ṣṭvā daśagrīvo raṇotsukaḥ |
rathenāmbudanādena saumitrim abhidudruve ||

=== meaning ===

---
89.
tam āha saumitrir adīnasattvo; visphārayantaṁ dhanur aprameyam |
anvehi mām eva niśācarendra; na vānarāṁs tvaṁ prati yoddhum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
90.
sa tasya vākyaṁ paripūrṇaghoṣaṁ; jyāśabdam ugraṁ ca niśamya rājā |
āsādya saumitrim avasthitaṁ taṁ; kopānvitaṁ vākyam uvāca rakṣaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
91.
diṣṭyāsi me rāghava dr̥ṣṭimārgaṁ; prāpto ’ntagāmī viparītabuddhiḥ |
asmin kṣaṇe yāsyasi mr̥tyudeśaṁ; saṁsādyamāno mama bāṇajālaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
92.
tam āha saumitrir avismayāno; garjantam udvr̥ttasitāgradaṁṣṭram |
rājan na garjanti mahāprabhāvā; vikatthase pāpakr̥tāṁ variṣṭha ||

=== meaning ===

---
93.
jānāmi vīryaṁ tava rākṣasendra; balaṁ pratāpaṁ ca parākramaṁ ca |
avasthito ’haṁ śaracāpapāṇir; āgaccha kiṁ moghavikatthanena ||

=== meaning ===

---
94.
sa evam uktaḥ kupitaḥ sasarja; rakṣo’dhipaḥ saptaśarān supuṅkhān |
tām̐l lakṣmaṇaḥ kāñcanacitrapuṅkhaiś; ciccheda bāṇair niśitāgradhāraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
95.
tān prekṣamāṇaḥ sahasā nikr̥ttān; nikr̥ttabhogān iva pannagendrān |
laṅkeśvaraḥ krodhavaśaṁ jagāma; sasarja cānyān niśitān pr̥ṣatkān ||

=== meaning ===

---
96.
sa bāṇavarṣaṁ tu vavarṣa tīvraṁ; rāmānujaḥ kārmukasaṁprayuktam |
kṣurārdhacandrottamakarṇibhallaiḥ; śarāṁś ca ciccheda na cukṣubhe ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
97.
sa lakṣmaṇaś cāśu śarāñ śitāgrān; mahendravajrāśanitulyavegān |
saṁdhāya cāpe jvalanaprakāśān; sasarja rakṣo’dhipater vadhāya ||

=== meaning ===

98.
