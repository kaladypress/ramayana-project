=== Sarga 19 ===

---
1.
sāraṇasya vacaḥ śrutvā rāvaṇaṁ rākṣasādhipam |
balam ālokayan sarvaṁ śuko vākyam athābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sthitān paśyasi yān etān mattān iva mahādvipān |
nyagrodhān iva gāṅgeyān sālān haimavatīn iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
ete duṣprasahā rājan balinaḥ kāmarūpiṇaḥ |
daityadānavasaṁkāśā yuddhe devaparākramāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
eṣāṁ koṭisahasrāṇi nava pañcaca sapta ca |
tathā śaṅkhasahasrāṇi tathā vr̥ndaśatāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
ete sugrīvasacivāḥ kiṣkindhānilayāḥ sadā |
harayo devagandharvair utpannāḥ kāmarūpiṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
yau tau paśyasi tiṣṭhantau kumārau devarūpiṇau |
maindaś ca dvividaś cobhau tābhyāṁ nāsti samo yudhi ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
brahmaṇā samanujñātāv amr̥taprāśināv ubhau |
āśaṁsete yudhā laṅkām etau marditum ojasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
yāv etāv etayoḥ pārśve sthitau parvatasaṁnibhau |
sumukho vimukhaś caiva mr̥tyuputrau pituḥ samau ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
yaṁ tu paśyasi tiṣṭhantaṁ prabhinnam iva kuñjaram |
yo balāt kṣobhayet kruddhaḥ samudram api vānaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
eṣo ’bhigantā laṅkāyā vaidehyās tava ca prabho |
enaṁ paśya purā dr̥ṣṭaṁ vānaraṁ punar āgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
jyeṣṭhaḥ kesariṇaḥ putro vātātmaja iti śrutaḥ |
hanūmān iti vikhyāto laṅghito yena sāgaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
kāmarūpī hariśreṣṭho balarūpasamanvitaḥ |
anivāryagatiś caiva yathā satatagaḥ prabhuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
udyantaṁ bhāskaraṁ dr̥ṣṭvā bālaḥ kila pipāsitaḥ |
triyojanasahasraṁ tu adhvānam avatīrya hi ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
ādityam āhariṣyāmi na me kṣut pratiyāsyati |
iti saṁcintya manasā puraiṣa baladarpitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
anādhr̥ṣyatamaṁ devam api devarṣidānavaiḥ |
anāsādyaiva patito bhāskarodayane girau ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
patitasya kaper asya hanur ekā śilātale |
kiṁ cid bhinnā dr̥ḍhahanor hanūmān eṣa tena vai ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
satyam āgamayogena mamaiṣa vidito hariḥ |
nāsya śakyaṁ balaṁ rūpaṁ prabhāvo vānubhāṣitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
eṣa āśaṁsate laṅkām eko marditum ojasā |
yaś caiṣo ’nantaraḥ śūraḥ śyāmaḥ padmanibhekṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
ikṣvākūṇām atiratho loke vikhyāta pauruṣaḥ |
yasmin na calate dharmo yo dharmaṁ nātivartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
yo brāhmam astraṁ vedāṁś ca veda vedavidāṁ varaḥ |
yo bhindyād gaganaṁ bāṇaiḥ parvatāṁś cāpi dārayet ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
yasya mr̥tyor iva krodhaḥ śakrasyeva parākramaḥ |
sa eṣa rāmas tvāṁ yoddhuṁ rājan samabhivartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
yaś caiṣa dakṣiṇe pārśve śuddhajāmbūnadaprabhaḥ |
viśālavakṣās tāmrākṣo nīlakuñcitamūrdhajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
eṣo ’sya lakṣmaṇo nāma bhrātā prāṇasamaḥ priyaḥ |
naye yuddhe ca kuśalaḥ sarvaśāstraviśāradaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
amarṣī durjayo jetā vikrānto buddhimān balī |
rāmasya dakṣiṇo bāhur nityaṁ prāṇo bahiścaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
na hy eṣa rāghavasyārthe jīvitaṁ parirakṣati |
eṣaivāśaṁsate yuddhe nihantuṁ sarvarākṣasān ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
yas tu savyam asau pakṣaṁ rāmasyāśritya tiṣṭhati |
rakṣogaṇaparikṣipto rājā hy eṣa vibhīṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
śrīmatā rājarājena laṅkāyām abhiṣecitaḥ |
tvām eva pratisaṁrabdho yuddhāyaiṣo ’bhivartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
yaṁ tu paśyasi tiṣṭhantaṁ madhye girim ivācalam |
sarvaśākhāmr̥gendrāṇāṁ bhartāram aparājitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
tejasā yaśasā buddhyā jñānenābhijanena ca |
yaḥ kapīn ati babhrāja himavān iva parvatān ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
kiṣkindhāṁ yaḥ samadhyāste guhāṁ sagahanadrumām |
durgāṁ parvatadurgasthāṁ pradhānaiḥ saha yūthapaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
yasyaiṣā kāñcanī mālā śobhate śatapuṣkarā |
kāntā devamanuṣyāṇāṁ yasyāṁ lakṣmīḥ pratiṣṭhitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
etāṁ ca mālāṁ tārāṁ ca kapirājyaṁ ca śāśvatam |
sugrīvo vālinaṁ hatvā rāmeṇa pratipāditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
evaṁ koṭisahasreṇa śaṅkūnāṁ ca śatena ca |
sugrīvo vānarendras tvāṁ yuddhārtham abhivartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
imāṁ mahārājasamīkṣya vāhinīm; upasthitāṁ prajvalitagrahopamām |
tataḥ prayatnaḥ paramo vidhīyatāṁ; yathā jayaḥ syān na paraiḥ parājayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
