=== Sarga 113 ===

---
1.
ayodhyāṁ tu samālokya cintayām āsa rāghavaḥ |
cintayitvā tato dr̥ṣṭiṁ vānareṣu nyapātayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
priyakāmaḥ priyaṁ rāmas tatas tvaritavikramam |
uvāca dhīmāṁs tejasvī hanūmantaṁ plavaṁgamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
ayodhyāṁ tvarito gaccha kṣipraṁ tvaṁ plavagottama |
jānīhi kaccit kuśalī jano nr̥patimandire ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
śr̥ṅgaverapuraṁ prāpya guhaṁ gahanagocaram |
niṣādādhipatiṁ brūhi kuśalaṁ vacanān mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
śrutvā tu māṁ kuśalinam arogaṁ vigatajvaram |
bhaviṣyati guhaḥ prītaḥ sa mamātmasamaḥ sakhā ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
ayodhyāyāś ca te mārgaṁ pravr̥ttiṁ bharatasya ca |
nivedayiṣyati prīto niṣādādhipatir guhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
bharatas tu tvayā vācyaḥ kuśalaṁ vacanān mama |
siddhārthaṁ śaṁsa māṁ tasmai sabhāryaṁ sahalakṣmaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
haraṇaṁ cāpi vaidehyā rāvaṇena balīyasā |
sugrīveṇa ca saṁvādaṁ vālinaś ca vadhaṁ raṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
maithilyanveṣaṇaṁ caiva yathā cādhigatā tvayā |
laṅghayitvā mahātoyam āpagāpatim avyayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
upayānaṁ samudrasya sāgarasya ca darśanam |
yathā ca kāritaḥ setū rāvaṇaś ca yathā hataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
varadānaṁ mahendreṇa brahmaṇā varuṇena ca |
mahādevaprasādāc ca pitrā mama samāgamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
jitvā śatrugaṇān rāmaḥ prāpya cānuttamaṁ yaśaḥ |
upayāti samr̥ddhārthaḥ saha mitrair mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
etac chrutvā yamākāraṁ bhajate bharatas tataḥ |
sa ca te veditavyaḥ syāt sarvaṁ yac cāpi māṁ prati ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
jñeyāḥ sarve ca vr̥ttāntā bharatasyeṅgitāni ca |
tattvena mukhavarṇena dr̥ṣṭyā vyābhāṣaṇena ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sarvakāmasamr̥ddhaṁ hi hastyaśvarathasaṁkulam |
pitr̥paitāmahaṁ rājyaṁ kasya nāvartayen manaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
saṁgatyā bharataḥ śrīmān rājyenārthī svayaṁ bhavet |
praśāstu vasudhāṁ sarvām akhilāṁ raghunandanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tasya buddhiṁ ca vijñāya vyavasāyaṁ ca vānara |
yāvan na dūraṁ yātāḥ smaḥ kṣipram āgantum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
iti pratisamādiṣṭo hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ |
mānuṣaṁ dhārayan rūpam ayodhyāṁ tvarito yayau ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
laṅghayitvā pitr̥pathaṁ bhujagendrālayaṁ śubham |
gaṅgāyamunayor bhīmaṁ saṁnipātam atītya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
śr̥ṅgaverapuraṁ prāpya guham āsādya vīryavān |
sa vācā śubhayā hr̥ṣṭo hanūmān idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
sakhā tu tava kākutstho rāmaḥ satyaparākramaḥ |
sasītaḥ saha saumitriḥ sa tvāṁ kuśalam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
pañcamīm adya rajanīm uṣitvā vacanān muneḥ |
bharadvājābhyanujñātaṁ drakṣyasy adyaiva rāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
evam uktvā mahātejāḥ saṁprahr̥ṣṭatanūruhaḥ |
utpapāta mahāvego vegavān avicārayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
so ’paśyad rāmatīrthaṁ ca nadīṁ vālukinīṁ tathā |
gomatīṁ tāṁ ca so ’paśyad bhīmaṁ sālavanaṁ tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
sa gatvā dūram adhvānaṁ tvaritaḥ kapikuñjaraḥ |
āsasāda drumān phullān nandigrāmasamīpajān ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
krośamātre tv ayodhyāyāś cīrakr̥ṣṇājināmbaram |
dadarśa bharataṁ dīnaṁ kr̥śam āśramavāsinam ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
jaṭilaṁ maladigdhāṅgaṁ bhrātr̥vyasanakarśitam |
phalamūlāśinaṁ dāntaṁ tāpasaṁ dharmacāriṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
samunnatajaṭābhāraṁ valkalājinavāsasaṁ |
niyataṁ bhāvitātmānaṁ brahmarṣisamatejasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
pāduke te puraskr̥tya śāsantaṁ vai vasuṁdharām |
caturvarṇyasya lokasya trātāraṁ sarvato bhayāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
upasthitam amātyaiś ca śucibhiś ca purohitaiḥ |
balamukhyaiś ca yuktaiś ca kāṣāyāmbaradhāribhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
na hi te rājaputraṁ taṁ cīrakr̥ṣṇājināmbaram |
parimoktuṁ vyavasyanti paurā vai dharmavatsalāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
taṁ dharmam iva dharmajñaṁ devavantam ivāparam |
uvāca prāñjalir vākyaṁ hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
vasantaṁ daṇḍakāraṇye yaṁ tvaṁ cīrajaṭādharam |
anuśocasi kākutsthaṁ sa tvā kuśalam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
priyam ākhyāmi te deva śokaṁ tyakṣyasi dāruṇam |
asmin muhūrte bhrātrā tvaṁ rāmeṇa saha saṁgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
nihatya rāvaṇaṁ rāmaḥ pratilabhya ca maithilīm |
upayāti samr̥ddhārthaḥ saha mitrair mahābalaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
lakṣmaṇaś ca mahātejā vaidehī ca yaśasvinī |
sītā samagrā rāmeṇa mahendreṇa śacī yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
evam ukto hanumatā bharataḥ kaikayīsutaḥ |
papāta sahasā hr̥ṣṭo harṣān mohaṁ jagāma ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
tato muhūrtād utthāya pratyāśvasya ca rāghavaḥ |
hanūmantam uvācedaṁ bharataḥ priyavādinam ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
aśokajaiḥ prītimayaiḥ kapim āliṅgya saṁbhramāt |
siṣeca bharataḥ śrīmān vipulair aśrubindubhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
devo vā mānuṣo vā tvam anukrośād ihāgataḥ |
priyākhyānasya te saumya dadāmi bruvataḥ priyam ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
gavāṁ śatasahasraṁ ca grāmāṇāṁ ca śataṁ param |
sakuṇḍalāḥ śubhācārā bhāryāḥ kanyāś ca ṣoḍaśa ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
hemavarṇāḥ sunāsorūḥ śaśisaumyānanāḥ striyaḥ |
sarvābharaṇasaṁpannā saṁpannāḥ kulajātibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
niśamya rāmāgamanaṁ nr̥pātmajaḥ; kapipravīrasya tadādbhutopamam |
praharṣito rāmadidr̥kṣayābhavat; punaś ca harṣād idam abravīd vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
