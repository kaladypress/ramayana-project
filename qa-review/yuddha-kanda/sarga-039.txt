=== Sarga 39 ===

---
1.
ghoreṇa śarabandhena baddhau daśarathātmajau |
niśvasantau yathā nāgau śayānau rudhirokṣitau ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sarve te vānaraśreṣṭhāḥ sasugrīvā mahābalāḥ |
parivārya mahātmānau tasthuḥ śokapariplutāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
etasminn antere rāmaḥ pratyabudhyata vīryavān |
sthiratvāt sattvayogāc ca śaraiḥ saṁdānito ’pi san ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tato dr̥ṣṭvā sarudhiraṁ viṣaṇṇaṁ gāḍham arpitam |
bhrātaraṁ dīnavadanaṁ paryadevayad āturaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
kiṁ nu me sītayā kāryaṁ kiṁ kāryaṁ jīvitena vā |
śayānaṁ yo ’dya paśyāmi bhrātaraṁ yudhi nirjitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
śakyā sītā samā nārī prāptuṁ loke vicinvatā |
na lakṣmaṇasamo bhrātā sacivaḥ sāmparāyikaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
parityakṣyāmy ahaṁ prāṇān vānarāṇāṁ tu paśyatām |
yadi pañcatvam āpannaḥ sumitrānandavardhanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
kiṁ nu vakṣyāmi kausalyāṁ mātaraṁ kiṁ nu kaikayīm |
katham ambāṁ sumitrāṁca putradarśanalālasām ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
vivatsāṁ vepamānāṁ ca krośantīṁ kurarīm iva |
katham āśvāsayiṣyāmi yadi yāsyāmi taṁ vinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
kathaṁ vakṣyāmi śatrughnaṁ bharataṁ ca yaśasvinam |
mayā saha vanaṁ yāto vinā tenāgataḥ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
upālambhaṁ na śakṣyāmi soḍhuṁ bata sumitrayā |
ihaiva dehaṁ tyakṣyāmi na hi jīvitum utsahe ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
dhiṅ māṁ duṣkr̥takarmāṇam anāryaṁ yatkr̥te hy asau |
lakṣmaṇaḥ patitaḥ śete śaratalpe gatāsuvat ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tvaṁ nityaṁ suviṣaṇṇaṁ mām āśvāsayasi lakṣmaṇa |
gatāsur nādya śaknoṣi mām ārtam abhibhāṣitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
yenādya bahavo yuddhe rākṣasā nihatāḥ kṣitau |
tasyām eva kṣitau vīraḥ sa śete nihataḥ paraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
śayānaḥ śaratalpe ’smin svaśoṇitapariplutaḥ |
śarajālaiś cito bhāti bhāskaro ’stam iva vrajan ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
bāṇābhihatamarmatvān na śaknoty abhivīkṣitum |
rujā cābruvato hy asya dr̥ṣṭirāgeṇa sūcyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
yathaiva māṁ vanaṁ yāntam anuyāto mahādyutiḥ |
aham apy anuyāsyāmi tathaivainaṁ yamakṣayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
iṣṭabandhujano nityaṁ māṁ ca nityam anuvrataḥ |
imām adya gato ’vasthāṁ mamānāryasya durnayaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
suruṣṭenāpi vīreṇa lakṣmaṇenā na saṁsmare |
paruṣaṁ vipriyaṁ vāpi śrāvitaṁ na kadā cana ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
visasarjaikavegena pañcabāṇaśatāni yaḥ |
iṣvastreṣv adhikas tasmāt kārtavīryāc ca lakṣmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
astrair astrāṇi yo hanyāc chakrasyāpi mahātmanaḥ |
so ’yam urvyāṁhataḥ śete mahārhaśayanocitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tac ca mithyā pralaptaṁ māṁ pradhakṣyati na saṁśayaḥ |
yan mayā na kr̥to rājā rākṣasānāṁ vibhīṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
asmin muhūrte sugrīva pratiyātum ito ’rhasi |
matvā hīnaṁ mayā rājan rāvaṇo ’bhidraved balī ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
aṅgadaṁ tu puraskr̥tya sasainyaḥ sasuhr̥jjanaḥ |
sāgaraṁ tara sugrīva punas tenaiva setunā ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
kr̥taṁ hanumatā kāryaṁ yad anyair duṣkaraṁ raṇe |
r̥kṣarājena tuṣyāmi golāṅgūlādhipena ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
aṅgadena kr̥taṁ karma maindena dvividena ca |
yuddhaṁ kesariṇā saṁkhye ghoraṁ saṁpātinā kr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
gavayena gavākṣeṇa śarabheṇa gajena ca |
anyaiś ca haribhir yuddhaṁ madārthe tyaktajīvitaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
na cātikramituṁ śakyaṁ daivaṁ sugrīva mānuṣaiḥ |
yat tu śakyaṁ vayasyena suhr̥dā vā paraṁtapa |
kr̥taṁ sugrīva tat sarvaṁ bhavatādharmabhīruṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
mitrakāryaṁ kr̥tam idaṁ bhavadbhir vānararṣabhāḥ |
anujñātā mayā sarve yatheṣṭaṁ gantum arhatha ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
śuśruvus tasya te sarve vānarāḥ paridevitam |
vartayāṁ cakrur aśrūṇi netraiḥ kr̥ṣṇetarekṣaṇāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
tataḥ sarvāṇy anīkāni sthāpayitvā vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
ājagāma gadāpāṇis tvarito yatra rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā tvaritaṁ yāntaṁ nīlāñjanacayopamam |
vānarā dudruvuḥ sarve manyamānās tu rāvaṇim ||

=== meaning ===

---
