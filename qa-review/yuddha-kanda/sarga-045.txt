=== Sarga 45 ===

---
1.
akampanavadhaṁ śrutvā kruddho vai rākṣaseśvaraḥ |
kiṁ cid dīnamukhaś cāpi sacivāṁs tān udaikṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sa tu dhyātvā muhūrtaṁ tu mantribhiḥ saṁvicārya ca |
purīṁ pariyayau laṅkāṁ sarvān gulmān avekṣitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tāṁ rākṣasagaṇair guptāṁ gulmair bahubhir āvr̥tām |
dadarśa nagarīṁ laṅkāṁ patākādhvajamālinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
ruddhāṁ tu nagarīṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rāvaṇo rākṣaseśvaraḥ |
uvācāmarṣitaḥ kāle prahastaṁ yuddhakovidam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
purasyopaniviṣṭasya sahasā pīḍitasya ca |
nānyaṁ yuddhāt prapaśyāmi mokṣaṁ yuddhaviśārada ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
ahaṁ vā kumbhakarṇo vā tvaṁ vā senāpatir mama |
indrajid vā nikumbho vā vaheyur bhāram īdr̥śam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sa tvaṁ balam itaḥ śīghram ādāya parigr̥hya ca |
vijayāyābhiniryāhi yatra sarve vanaukasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
niryāṇād eva te nūnaṁ capalā harivāhinī |
nardatāṁ rākṣasendrāṇāṁ śrutvā nādaṁ draviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
capalā hy avinītāś ca calacittāś ca vānarāḥ |
na sahiṣyanti te nādaṁ siṁhanādam iva dvipāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
vidrute ca bale tasmin rāmaḥ saumitriṇā saha |
avaśaste nirālambaḥ prahastavaśam eṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
āpatsaṁśayitā śreyo nātra niḥsaṁśayīkr̥tā |
pratilomānulomaṁ vā yad vā no manyase hitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
rāvaṇenaivam uktas tu prahasto vāhinīpatiḥ |
rākṣasendram uvācedam asurendram ivośanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
rājan mantritapūrvaṁ naḥ kuśalaiḥ saha mantribhiḥ |
vivādaś cāpi no vr̥ttaḥ samavekṣya parasparam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
pradānena tu sītāyāḥ śreyo vyavasitaṁ mayā |
apradāne punar yuddhaṁ dr̥ṣṭam etat tathaiva naḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
so ’haṁ dānaiś ca mānaiś ca satataṁ pūjitas tvayā |
sāntvaiś ca vividhaiḥ kāle kiṁ na kuryāṁ priyaṁ tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
na hi me jīvitaṁ rakṣyaṁ putradāradhanāni vā |
tvaṁ paśya māṁ juhūṣantaṁ tvadarthe jīvitaṁ yudhi ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
evam uktvā tu bhartāraṁ rāvaṇaṁ vāhinīpatiḥ |
samānayata me śīghraṁ rākṣasānāṁ mahad balam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
madbāṇāśanivegena hatānāṁ tu raṇājire |
adya tr̥pyantu māṁsena pakṣiṇaḥ kānanaukasām ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
ity uktās te prahastena balādhyakṣāḥ kr̥tatvarāḥ |
balam udyojayām āsus tasmin rākṣasamandire ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
sā babhūva muhūrtena tigmanānāvidhāyudhaiḥ |
laṅkā rākṣasavīrais tair gajair iva samākulā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
hutāśanaṁ tarpayatāṁ brāhmaṇāṁś ca namasyatām |
ājyagandhaprativahaḥ surabhir māruto vavau ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
srajaś ca vividhākārā jagr̥hus tv abhimantritāḥ |
saṁgrāmasajjāḥ saṁhr̥ṣṭā dhārayan rākṣasās tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
sadhanuṣkāḥ kavacino vegād āplutya rākṣasāḥ |
rāvaṇaṁ prekṣya rājānaṁ prahastaṁ paryavārayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
athāmantrya ca rājānaṁ bherīm āhatya bhairavām |
āruroha rathaṁ divyaṁ prahastaḥ sajjakalpitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
hayair mahājavair yuktaṁ samyak sūtasusaṁyutam |
mahājaladanirghoṣaṁ sākṣāc candrārkabhāsvaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
uragadhvajadurdharṣaṁ suvarūthaṁ svapaskaram |
suvarṇajālasaṁyuktaṁ prahasantam iva śriyā ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tatas taṁ ratham āsthāya rāvaṇārpitaśāsanaḥ |
laṅkāyā niryayau tūrṇaṁ balena mahatā vr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tato duṁdubhinirghoṣaḥ parjanyaninadopamaḥ |
śuśruve śaṅkhaśabdaś ca prayāte vāhinīpatau ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
ninadantaḥ svarān ghorān rākṣasā jagmur agrataḥ |
bhīmarūpā mahākāyāḥ prahastasya puraḥsarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
vyūḍhenaiva sughoreṇa pūrvadvārāt sa niryayau |
gajayūthanikāśena balena mahatā vr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
sāgarapratimaughena vr̥tas tena balena saḥ |
prahasto niryayau tūrṇaṁ kruddhaḥ kālāntakopamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
tasya niryāṇa ghoṣeṇa rākṣasānāṁ ca nardatām |
laṅkāyāṁ sarvabhūtāni vinedur vikr̥taiḥ svaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
vyabhram ākāśam āviśya māṁsaśoṇitabhojanāḥ |
maṇḍalāny apasavyāni khagāś cakrū rathaṁ prati ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
vamantyaḥ pāvakajvālāḥ śivā ghorā vavāśire ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
antarikṣāt papātolkā vāyuś ca paruṣo vavau |
anyonyam abhisaṁrabdhā grahāś ca na cakāśire ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
vavarṣū rudhiraṁ cāsya siṣicuś ca puraḥsarān |
ketumūrdhani gr̥dhro ’sya vilīno dakṣiṇāmukhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
sārather bahuśaś cāsya saṁgrāmam avagāhataḥ |
pratodo nyapatad dhastāt sūtasya hayasādinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
niryāṇa śrīś ca yāsyāsīd bhāsvarā ca sudurlabhā |
sā nanāśa muhūrtena same ca skhalitā hayāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
prahastaṁ tv abhiniryāntaṁ prakhyāta balapauruṣam |
yudhi nānāpraharaṇā kapisenābhyavartata ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
atha ghoṣaḥ sutumulo harīṇāṁ samajāyata |
vr̥kṣān ārujatāṁ caiva gurvīś cāgr̥hṇatāṁ śilāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
ubhe pramudite sainye rakṣogaṇavanaukasām |
vegitānāṁ samarthānām anyonyavadhakāṅkṣiṇām |
parasparaṁ cāhvayatāṁ ninādaḥ śrūyate mahān ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
tataḥ prahastaḥ kapirājavāhinīm; abhipratasthe vijayāya durmatiḥ |
vivr̥ddhavegāṁ ca viveśa tāṁ camūṁ; yathā mumūrṣuḥ śalabho vibhāvasum ||

=== meaning ===

---
