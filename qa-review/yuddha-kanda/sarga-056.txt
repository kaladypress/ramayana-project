=== Sarga 56 ===

---
1.
kumbhakarṇaṁ hataṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rāghaveṇa mahātmanā |
rākṣasā rākṣasendrāya rāvaṇāya nyavedayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
śrutvā vinihataṁ saṁkhye kumbhakarṇaṁ mahābalam |
rāvaṇaḥ śokasaṁtapto mumoha ca papāta ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
pitr̥vyaṁ nihataṁ śrutvā devāntakanarāntakau |
triśirāś cātikāyaś ca ruruduḥ śokapīḍitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
bhrātaraṁ nihataṁ śrutvā rāmeṇākliṣṭakarmaṇā |
mahodaramahāpārśvau śokākrāntau babhūvatuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tataḥ kr̥cchrāt samāsādya saṁjñāṁ rākṣasapuṁgavaḥ |
kumbhakarṇavadhād dīno vilalāpa sa rāvaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
hā vīra ripudarpaghna kumbhakarṇa mahābala |
śatrusainyaṁ pratāpyaikaḥ kva māṁ saṁtyajya gacchasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
idānīṁ khalv ahaṁ nāsmi yasya me patito bhujaḥ |
dakṣiṇo yaṁ samāśritya na bibhemi surāsurān ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
katham evaṁvidho vīro devadānavadarpahā |
kālāgnipratimo hy adya rāghaveṇa raṇe hataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
yasya te vajraniṣpeṣo na kuryād vyasanaṁ sadā |
sa kathaṁ rāmabāṇārtaḥ prasupto ’si mahītale ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
ete devagaṇāḥ sārdham r̥ṣibhir gagane sthitāḥ |
nihataṁ tvāṁ raṇe dr̥ṣṭvā ninadanti praharṣitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
dhruvam adyaiva saṁhr̥ṣṭā labdhalakṣyāḥ plavaṁgamāḥ |
ārokṣyantīha durgāṇi laṅkādvārāṇi sarvaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
rājyena nāsti me kāryaṁ kiṁ kariṣyāmi sītayā |
kumbhakarṇavihīnasya jīvite nāsti me ratiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
yady ahaṁ bhrātr̥hantāraṁ na hanmi yudhi rāghavam |
nanu me maraṇaṁ śreyo na cedaṁ vyarthajīvitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
adyaiva taṁ gamiṣyāmi deśaṁ yatrānujo mama |
na hi bhrātr̥̄n samutsr̥jya kṣaṇaṁ jīvitum utsahe ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
devā hi māṁ hasiṣyanti dr̥ṣṭvā pūrvāpakāriṇam |
katham indraṁ jayiṣyāmi kumbhakarṇahate tvayi ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tad idaṁ mām anuprāptaṁ vibhīṣaṇavacaḥ śubham |
yad ajñānān mayā tasya na gr̥hītaṁ mahātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
vibhīṣaṇavaco yāvat kumbhakarṇaprahastayoḥ |
vināśo ’yaṁ samutpanno māṁ vrīḍayati dāruṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tasyāyaṁ karmaṇaḥ prāto vipāko mama śokadaḥ |
yan mayā dhārmikaḥ śrīmān sa nirasto vibhīṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
iti bahuvidham ākulāntarātmā; kr̥paṇam atīva vilapya kumbhakarṇam |
nyapatad atha daśānano bhr̥śārtas; tam anujam indraripuṁ hataṁ viditvā ||

=== meaning ===

---
