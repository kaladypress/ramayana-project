=== Sarga 26 ===

---
1.
tena śaṅkhavimiśreṇa bherīśabdena rāghavaḥ |
upayato mahābāhū rāmaḥ parapuraṁjayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
taṁ ninādaṁ niśamyātha rāvaṇo rākṣaseśvaraḥ |
muhūrtaṁ dhyānam āsthāya sacivān abhyudaikṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
atha tān sacivāṁs tatra sarvān ābhāṣya rāvaṇaḥ |
sabhāṁ saṁnādayan sarvām ity uvāca mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
taraṇaṁ sāgarasyāpi vikramaṁ balasaṁcayam |
yad uktavanto rāmasya bhavantas tan mayā śrutam |
bhavataś cāpy ahaṁ vedmi yuddhe satyaparākramān ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tatas tu sumahāprājño mālyavān nāma rākṣasaḥ |
rāvaṇasya vacaḥ śrutvā mātuḥ paitāmaho ’bravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
vidyāsv abhivinīto yo rājā rājan nayānugaḥ |
sa śāsti ciram aiśvaryam arīṁś ca kurute vaśe ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
saṁdadhāno hi kālena vigr̥hṇaṁś cāribhiḥ saha |
svapakṣavardhanaṁ kurvan mahad aiśvaryam aśnute ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
hīyamānena kartavyo rājñā saṁdhiḥ samena ca |
na śatrum avamanyeta jyāyān kurvīta vigraham ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tan mahyaṁ rocate saṁdhiḥ saha rāmeṇa rāvaṇa |
yadartham abhiyuktāḥ sma sītā tasmai pradīyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tasya devarṣayaḥ sarve gandharvāś ca jayaiṣiṇaḥ |
virodhaṁ mā gamas tena saṁdhis te tena rocatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
asr̥jad bhagavān pakṣau dvāv eva hi pitāmahaḥ |
surāṇām asurāṇāṁ ca dharmādharmau tadāśrayau ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
dharmo hi śrūyate pakṣaḥ surāṇāṁ ca mahātmanām |
adharmo rakṣasaṁ pakṣo hy asurāṇāṁ ca rāvaṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
dharmo vai grasate ’dharmaṁ tataḥ kr̥tam abhūd yugam |
adharmo grasate dharmaṁ tatas tiṣyaḥ pravartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tat tvayā caratā lokān dharmo vinihato mahān |
adharmaḥ pragr̥hītaś ca tenāsmadbalinaḥ pare ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa pramādād vivr̥ddhas te ’dharmo ’hir grasate hi naḥ |
vivardhayati pakṣaṁ ca surāṇāṁ surabhāvanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
viṣayeṣu prasaktena yatkiṁcitkāriṇā tvayā |
r̥ṣīṇām agnikalpānām udvego janito mahān |
teṣāṁ prabhāvo durdharṣaḥ pradīpta iva pāvakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tapasā bhāvitātmāno dharmasyānugrahe ratāḥ |
mukhyair yajñair yajanty ete nityaṁ tais tair dvijātayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
juhvaty agnīṁś ca vidhivad vedāṁś coccair adhīyate |
abhibhūya ca rakṣāṁsi brahmaghoṣān udairayan |
diśo vipradrutāḥ sarve stanayitnur ivoṣṇage ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
r̥ṣīṇām agnikalpānām agnihotrasamutthitaḥ |
ādatte rakṣasāṁ tejo dhūmo vyāpya diśo daśa ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
teṣu teṣu ca deśeṣu puṇyeṣu ca dr̥ḍhavrataiḥ |
caryamāṇaṁ tapas tīvraṁ saṁtāpayati rākṣasān ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
utpātān vividhān dr̥ṣṭvā ghorān bahuvidhāṁs tathā |
vināśam anupaśyāmi sarveṣāṁ rakṣasām aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
kharābhis tanitā ghorā meghāḥ pratibhayaṁkaraḥ |
śoṇitenābhivarṣanti laṅkām uṣṇena sarvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
rudatāṁ vāhanānāṁ ca prapatanty asrabindavaḥ |
dhvajā dhvastā vivarṇāś ca na prabhānti yathāpuram ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
vyālā gomāyavo gr̥dhrā vāśanti ca subhairavam |
praviśya laṅkām aniśaṁ samavāyāṁś ca kurvate ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
kālikāḥ pāṇḍurair dantaiḥ prahasanty agrataḥ sthitāḥ |
striyaḥ svapneṣu muṣṇantyo gr̥hāṇi pratibhāṣya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
gr̥hāṇāṁ balikarmāṇi śvānaḥ paryupabhuñjate |
kharā goṣu prajāyante mūṣikā nakulaiḥ saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
mārjārā dvīpibhiḥ sārdhaṁ sūkarāḥ śunakaiḥ saha |
kiṁnarā rākṣasaiś cāpi sameyur mānuṣaiḥ saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
pāṇḍurā raktapādāś ca vihagāḥ kālacoditāḥ |
rākṣasānāṁ vināśāya kapotā vicaranti ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
cīkī kūcīti vāśantyaḥ śārikā veśmasu sthitāḥ |
patanti grathitāś cāpi nirjitāḥ kalahaiṣiṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
karālo vikaṭo muṇḍaḥ puruṣaḥ kr̥ṣṇapiṅgalaḥ |
kālo gr̥hāṇi sarveṣāṁ kāle kāle ’nvavekṣate |
etāny anyāni duṣṭāni nimittāny utpatanti ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
viṣṇuṁ manyāmahe rāmaṁ mānuṣaṁ deham āsthitam |
na hi mānuṣamātro ’sau rāghavo dr̥ḍhavikramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
yena baddhaḥ samudrasya sa setuḥ paramādbhutaḥ |
kuruṣva nararājena saṁdhiṁ rāmeṇa rāvaṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
idaṁ vacas tatra nigadya mālyavan; parīkṣya rakṣo’dhipater manaḥ punaḥ |
anuttameṣūttamapauruṣo balī; babhūva tūṣṇīṁ samavekṣya rāvaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
