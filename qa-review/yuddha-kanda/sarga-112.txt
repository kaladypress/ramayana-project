=== Sarga 112 ===

---
1.
pūrṇe caturdaśe varṣe pañcabhyāṁ lakṣmaṇāgrajaḥ |
bharadvājāśramaṁ prāpya vavande niyato munim ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
so ’pr̥cchad abhivādyainaṁ bharadvājaṁ tapodhanam |
śr̥ṇoṣi ka cid bhagavan subhikṣānāmayaṁ pure |
kaccic ca yukto bharato jīvanty api ca mātaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
evam uktas tu rāmeṇa bharadvājo mahāmuniḥ |
pratyuvāca raghuśreṣṭhaṁ smitapūrvaṁ prahr̥ṣṭavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
paṅkadigdhas tu bharato jaṭilas tvāṁ pratīkṣate |
pāduke te puraskr̥tya sarvaṁ ca kuśalaṁ gr̥he ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tvāṁ purā cīravasanaṁ praviśantaṁ mahāvanam |
strītr̥tīyaṁ cyutaṁ rājyād dharmakāmaṁ ca kevalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
padātiṁ tyaktasarvasvaṁ pitur vacanakāriṇam |
svargabhogaiḥ parityaktaṁ svargacyutam ivāmaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
dr̥ṣṭvā tu karuṇā pūrvaṁ mamāsīt samitiṁjaya |
kaikeyīvacane yuktaṁ vanyamūlaphalāśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sāmprataṁ susamr̥ddhārthaṁ samitragaṇabāndhavam |
samīkṣya vijitāriṁ tvāṁ mama prītir anuttamā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sarvaṁ ca sukhaduḥkhaṁ te viditaṁ mama rāghava |
yat tvayā vipulaṁ prāptaṁ janasthānavadhādikam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
brāhmaṇārthe niyuktasya rakṣataḥ sarvatāpasān |
mārīcadarśanaṁ caiva sītonmathanam eva ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
kabandhadarśanaṁ caiva pampābhigamanaṁ tathā |
sugrīveṇa ca te sakhyaṁ yac ca vālī hatas tvayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
mārgaṇaṁ caiva vaidehyāḥ karma vātātmajasya ca |
viditāyāṁ ca vaidehyāṁ nalasetur yathā kr̥taḥ |
yathā ca dīpitā laṅkā prahr̥ṣṭair hariyūthapaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
saputrabāndhavāmātyaḥ sabalaḥ saha vāhanaḥ |
yathā ca nihataḥ saṁkhye rāvaṇo devakaṇṭakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
samāgamaś ca tridaśair yathādattaś ca te varaḥ |
sarvaṁ mamaitad viditaṁ tapasā dharmavatsala ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
aham apy atra te dadmi varaṁ śastrabhr̥tāṁ vara |
arghyaṁ pratigr̥hāṇedam ayodhyāṁ śvo gamiṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tasya tac chirasā vākyaṁ pratigr̥hya nr̥pātmajaḥ |
bāḍham ity eva saṁhr̥ṣṭaḥ śrīmān varam ayācata ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
akālaphalino vr̥kṣāḥ sarve cāpi madhusravāḥ |
bhavantu mārge bhagavann ayodhyāṁ prati gacchataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
niṣphalāḥ phalinaś cāsan vipuṣpāḥ puṣpaśālinaḥ |
śuṣkāḥ samagrapatrās te nagāś caiva madhusravāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
