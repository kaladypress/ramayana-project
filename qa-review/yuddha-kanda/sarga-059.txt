=== Sarga 59 ===

---
1.
svabalaṁ vyathitaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā tumulaṁ lomaharṣaṇam |
bhrātr̥̄ṁś ca nihatān dr̥ṣṭvā śakratulyaparākramān ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
pitr̥vyau cāpi saṁdr̥śya samare saṁniṣūditau |
mahodaramahāpārśvau bhrātarau rākṣasarṣabhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
cukopa ca mahātejā brahmadattavaro yudhi |
atikāyo ’drisaṁkāśo devadānavadarpahā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
sa bhāskarasahasrasya saṁghātam iva bhāsvaram |
ratham āsthāya śakrārir abhidudrāva vānarān ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
sa visphārya mahac cāpaṁ kirīṭī mr̥ṣṭakuṇḍalaḥ |
nāma viśrāvayām āsa nanāda ca mahāsvanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tena siṁhapraṇādena nāmaviśrāvaṇena ca |
jyāśabdena ca bhīmena trāsayām āsa vānarān ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
te tasya rūpam ālokya yathā viṣṇos trivikrame |
bhayārtā vānarāḥ sarve vidravanti diśo daśa ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
te ’tikāyaṁ samāsādya vānarā mūḍhacetasaḥ |
śaraṇyaṁ śaraṇaṁ jagmur lakṣmaṇāgrajam āhave ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tato ’tikāyaṁ kākutstho rathasthaṁ parvatopamam |
dadarśa dhanvinaṁ dūrād garjantaṁ kālameghavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
sa taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā mahātmānaṁ rāghavas tu suvismitaḥ |
vānarān sāntvayitvā tu vibhīṣaṇam uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
ko ’sau parvatasaṁkāśo dhanuṣmān harilocanaḥ |
yukte hayasahasreṇa viśāle syandane sthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
ya eṣa niśitaiḥ śūlaiḥ sutīkṣṇaiḥ prāsatomaraiḥ |
arciṣmadbhir vr̥to bhāti bhūtair iva maheśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
kālajihvāprakāśābhir ya eṣo ’bhivirājate |
āvr̥to rathaśaktībhir vidyudbhir iva toyadaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
dhanūṁsi cāsya sajyāni hemapr̥ṣṭhāni sarvaśaḥ |
śobhayanti rathaśreṣṭhaṁ śakrapātam ivāmbaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
ka eṣa rakṣaḥ śārdūlo raṇabhūmiṁ virājayan |
abhyeti rathināṁ śreṣṭho rathenādityatejasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
dhvajaśr̥ṅgapratiṣṭhena rāhuṇābhivirājate |
sūryaraśmiprabhair bāṇair diśo daśa virājayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
triṇataṁ meghanirhrādaṁ hemapr̥ṣṭham alaṁkr̥tam |
śatakratudhanuḥprakhyaṁ dhanuś cāsya virājate ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
sadhvajaḥ sapatākaś ca sānukarṣo mahārathaḥ |
catuḥsādisamāyukto meghastanitanisvanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
viṁśatir daśa cāṣṭau ca tūṇīraratham āsthitāḥ |
kārmukāṇi ca bhīmāni jyāś ca kāñcanapiṅgalāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
dvau ca khaḍgau rathagatau pārśvasthau pārśvaśobhinau |
caturhastatsarucitau vyaktahastadaśāyatau ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
raktakaṇṭhaguṇo dhīro mahāparvatasaṁnibhaḥ |
kālaḥ kālamahāvaktro meghastha iva bhāskaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
kāñcanāṅgadanaddhābhyāṁ bhujābhyām eṣa śobhate |
śr̥ṅgābhyām iva tuṅgābhyāṁ himavān parvatottamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
kuṇḍalābhyāṁ tu yasyaitad bhāti vaktraṁ śubhekṣaṇam |
punarvasvantaragataṁ pūrṇabimbam ivaindavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
ācakṣva me mahābāho tvam enaṁ rākṣasottamam |
yaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā vānarāḥ sarve bhayārtā vidrutā diśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
sa pr̥ṣṭho rājaputreṇa rāmeṇāmitatejasā |
ācacakṣe mahātejā rāghavāya vibhīṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
daśagrīvo mahātejā rājā vaiśravaṇānujaḥ |
bhīmakarmā mahotsāho rāvaṇo rākṣasādhipaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tasyāsīd vīryavān putro rāvaṇapratimo raṇe |
vr̥ddhasevī śrutadharaḥ sarvāstraviduṣāṁ varaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
aśvapr̥ṣṭhe rathe nāge khaḍge dhanuṣi karṣaṇe |
bhede sāntve ca dāne ca naye mantre ca saṁmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
yasya bāhuṁ samāśritya laṅkā bhavati nirbhayā |
tanayaṁ dhānyamālinyā atikāyam imaṁ viduḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
etenārādhito brahmā tapasā bhāvitātmanā |
astrāṇi cāpy avāptāni ripavaś ca parājitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
surāsurair avadhyatvaṁ dattam asmai svayambhuvā |
etac ca kavacaṁ divyaṁ rathaś caiṣo ’rkabhāskaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
etena śataśo devā dānavāś ca parājitāḥ |
rakṣitāni ca rakṣāmi yakṣāś cāpi niṣūditāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
vajraṁ viṣṭambhitaṁ yena bāṇair indrasya dhīmataḥ |
pāśaḥ salilarājasya yuddhe pratihatas tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
eṣo ’tikāyo balavān rākṣasānām atharṣabhaḥ |
rāvaṇasya suto dhīmān devadanava darpahā ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
tad asmin kriyatāṁ yatnaḥ kṣipraṁ puruṣapuṁgava |
purā vānarasainyāni kṣayaṁ nayati sāyakaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
tato ’tikāyo balavān praviśya harivāhinīm |
visphārayām āsa dhanur nanāda ca punaḥ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
taṁ bhīmavapuṣaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rathasthaṁ rathināṁ varam |
abhipetur mahātmāno ye pradhānāḥ plavaṁgamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
kumudo dvivido maindo nīlaḥ śarabha eva ca |
pādapair giriśr̥ṅgaiś ca yugapat samabhidravan ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
teṣāṁ vr̥kṣāṁś ca śailāṁś ca śaraiḥ kāñcanabhūṣaṇaiḥ |
atikāyo mahātejāś cicchedāstravidāṁ varaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
tāṁś caiva sarān sa harīñ śaraiḥ sarvāyasair balī |
vivyādhābhimukhaḥ saṁkhye bhīmakāyo niśācaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
te ’rditā bāṇabarṣeṇa bhinnagātrāḥ plavaṁgamāḥ |
na śekur atikāyasya pratikartuṁ mahāraṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
tat sainyaṁ harivīrāṇāṁ trāsayām āsa rākṣasaḥ |
mr̥gayūtham iva kruddho harir yauvanam āsthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
sa rāṣasendro harisainyamadhye; nāyudhyamānaṁ nijaghāna kaṁ cit |
upetya rāmaṁ sadhanuḥ kalāpī; sagarvitaṁ vākyam idaṁ babhāṣe ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
rathe sthito ’haṁ śaracāpapāṇir; na prākr̥taṁ kaṁ cana yodhayāmi |
yasyāsti śaktir vyavasāya yuktā; dadātuṁ me kṣipram ihādya yuddham ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
tat tasya vākyaṁ bruvato niśamya; cukopa saumitrir amitrahantā |
amr̥ṣyamāṇaś ca samutpapāta; jagrāha cāpaṁ ca tataḥ smayitvā ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
kruddhaḥ saumitrir utpatya tūṇād ākṣipya sāyakam |
purastād atikāyasya vicakarṣa mahad dhanuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
pūrayan sa mahīṁ śailān ākāśaṁ sāgaraṁ diśaḥ |
jyāśabdo lakṣmaṇasyogras trāsayan rajanīcarān ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
saumitreś cāpanirghoṣaṁ śrutvā pratibhayaṁ tadā |
visiṣmiye mahātejā rākṣasendrātmajo balī ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
athātikāyaḥ kupito dr̥ṣṭvā lakṣmaṇam utthitam |
ādāya niśitaṁ bāṇam idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
bālas tvam asi saumitre vikrameṣv avicakṣaṇaḥ |
gaccha kiṁ kālasadr̥śaṁ māṁ yodhayitum icchasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
na hi madbāhusr̥ṣṭānām astrāṇāṁ himavān api |
soḍhum utsahate vegam antarikṣam atho mahī ||

=== meaning ===

---
52.
sukhaprasuptaṁ kālāgniṁ prabodhayitum icchasi |
nyasya cāpaṁ nivartasva mā prāṇāñ jahi madgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
53.
atha vā tvaṁ pratiṣṭabdho na nivartitum icchasi |
tiṣṭha prāṇān parityajya gamiṣyasi yamakṣayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
54.
paśya me niśitān bāṇān aridarpaniṣūdanān |
īśvarāyudhasaṁkāśāṁs taptakāñcanabhūṣaṇān ||

=== meaning ===

---
55.
eṣa te sarpasaṁkāśo bāṇaḥ pāsyati śoṇitam |
mr̥garāja iva kruddho nāgarājasya śoṇitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
56.
śrutvātikāyasya vacaḥ saroṣaṁ; sagarvitaṁ saṁyati rājaputraḥ |
sa saṁcukopātibalo br̥hacchrīr; uvāca vākyaṁ ca tato mahārtham ||

=== meaning ===

---
57.
na vākyamātreṇa bhavān pradhāno; na katthanāt satpuruṣā bhavanti |
mayi sthite dhanvini bāṇapāṇau; vidarśayasvātmabalaṁ durātman ||

=== meaning ===

---
58.
karmaṇā sūcayātmānaṁ na vikatthitum arhasi |
pauruṣeṇa tu yo yuktaḥ sa tu śūra iti smr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
59.
sarvāyudhasamāyukto dhanvī tvaṁ ratham āsthitaḥ |
śarair vā yadi vāpy astrair darśayasva parākramam ||

=== meaning ===

---
60.
tataḥ śiras te niśitaiḥ pātayiṣyāmy ahaṁ śaraiḥ |
mārutaḥ kālasaṁpakvaṁ vr̥ntāt tālaphalaṁ yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
61.
adya te māmakā bāṇās taptakāñcanabhūṣaṇāḥ |
pāsyanti rudhiraṁ gātrād bāṇaśalyāntarotthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
62.
bālo ’yam iti vijñāya na māvajñātum arhasi |
bālo vā yadi vā vr̥ddho mr̥tyuṁ jānīhi saṁyuge ||

=== meaning ===

---
63.
lakṣmaṇasya vacaḥ śrutvā hetumat paramārthavat |
atikāyaḥ pracukrodha bāṇaṁ cottamam ādade ||

=== meaning ===

---
64.
tato vidyādharā bhūtā devā daityā maharṣayaḥ |
guhyakāś ca mahātmānas tad yuddhaṁ dadr̥śus tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
65.
tato ’tikāyaḥ kupitaś cāpam āropya sāyakam |
lakṣmaṇasya pracikṣepa saṁkṣipann iva cāmbaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
66.
tam āpatantaṁ niśitaṁ śaram āśīviṣopamam |
ardhacandreṇa ciccheda lakṣmaṇaḥ paravīrahā ||

=== meaning ===

---
67.
taṁ nikr̥ttaṁ śaraṁ dr̥ṣṭvā kr̥ttabhogam ivoragam |
atikāyo bhr̥śaṁ kruddhaḥ pañcabāṇān samādade ||

=== meaning ===

---
68.
tāñ śarān saṁpracikṣepa lakṣmaṇāya niśācaraḥ |
tān aprāptāñ śarais tīkṣṇaiś ciccheda bharatānujaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
69.
sa tāṁś chittvā śarais tīkṣṇair lakṣmaṇaḥ paravīrahā |
ādade niśitaṁ bāṇaṁ jvalantam iva tejasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
70.
tam ādāya dhanuḥ śreṣṭhe yojayām āsa lakṣmaṇaḥ |
vicakarṣa ca vegena visasarja ca sāyakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
71.
pūrṇāyatavisr̥ṣṭena śareṇānata parvaṇā |
lalāṭe rākṣasaśreṣṭham ājaghāna sa vīryavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
72.
sa lalāṭe śaro magnas tasya bhīmasya rakṣasaḥ |
dadr̥śe śoṇitenāktaḥ pannagendra ivāhave ||

=== meaning ===

---
73.
rākṣasaḥ pracakampe ca lakṣmaṇeṣu prakampitaḥ |
rudrabāṇahataṁ bhīmaṁ yathā tripuragopuram ||

=== meaning ===

---
74.
cintayām āsa cāśvasya vimr̥śya ca mahābalaḥ |
sādhu bāṇanipātena śvāghanīyo ’si me ripuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
75.
vicāryaivaṁ vinamyāsyaṁ vinamya ca bhujāv ubhau |
sa rathopastham āsthāya rathena pracacāra ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
76.
ekaṁ trīn pañca sapteti sāyakān rākṣasarṣabhaḥ |
ādade saṁdadhe cāpi vicakarṣotsasarja ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
77.
te bāṇāḥ kālasaṁkāśā rākṣasendradhanuś cyutāḥ |
hemapuṅkhā raviprakhyāś cakrur dīptam ivāmbaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
78.
tatas tān rākṣasotsr̥ṣṭāñ śaraughān rāvaṇānujaḥ |
asaṁbhrāntaḥ praciccheda niśitair bahubhiḥ śaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
79.
tāñ śarān yudhi saṁprekṣya nikr̥ttān rāvaṇātmajaḥ |
cukopa tridaśendrārir jagrāha niśitaṁ śaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
80.
sa saṁdhāya mahātejās taṁ bāṇaṁ sahasotsr̥jat |
tataḥ saumitrim āyāntam ājaghāna stanāntare ||

=== meaning ===

---
81.
atikāyena saumitris tāḍito yudhi vakṣasi |
susrāva rudhiraṁ tīvraṁ madaṁ matta iva dvipaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
82.
sa cakāra tadātmānaṁ viśalyaṁ sahasā vibhuḥ |
jagrāha ca śaraṁ tīṣṇam astreṇāpi samādadhe ||

=== meaning ===

---
83.
āgneyena tadāstreṇa yojayām āsa sāyakam |
sa jajvāla tadā bāṇo dhanuś cāsya mahātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
84.
atikāyo ’titejasvī sauram astraṁ samādade |
tena bāṇaṁ bhujaṁgābhaṁ hemapuṅkham ayojayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
85.
tatas taṁ jvalitaṁ ghoraṁ lakṣmaṇaḥ śaram āhitam |
atikāyāya cikṣepa kāladaṇḍam ivāntakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
86.
āgneyenābhisaṁyuktaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā bāṇaṁ niśācaraḥ |
utsasarja tadā bāṇaṁ dīptaṁ sūryāstrayojitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
87.
tāv ubhāv ambare bāṇāv anyonyam abhijaghnatuḥ |
tejasā saṁpradīptāgrau kruddhāv iva bhujaṁ gamau ||

=== meaning ===

---
88.
tāv anyonyaṁ vinirdahya petatur dharaṇītale |
nirarciṣau bhasmakr̥tau na bhrājete śarottamau ||

=== meaning ===

---
89.
tato ’tikāyaḥ saṁkruddhas tv astram aiṣīkam utsr̥jat |
tat praciccheda saumitrir astram aindreṇa vīryavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
90.
aiṣīkaṁ nihataṁ dr̥ṣṭvā kumāro rāvaṇātmajaḥ |
yāmyenāstreṇa saṁkruddho yojayām āsa sāyakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
91.
tatas tad astraṁ cikṣepa lakṣmaṇāya niśācaraḥ |
vāyavyena tad astraṁ tu nijaghāna sa lakṣmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
92.
athainaṁ śaradhārābhir dhārābhir iva toyadaḥ |
abhyavarṣata saṁkruddho lakṣmaṇo rāvaṇātmajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
93.
te ’tikāyaṁ samāsādya kavace vajrabhūṣite |
bhagnāgraśalyāḥ sahasā petur bāṇā mahītale ||

=== meaning ===

---
94.
tān moghān abhisaṁprekṣya lakṣmaṇaḥ paravīrahā |
abhyavarṣata bāṇānāṁ sahasreṇa mahāyaśāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
95.
sa varṣyamāṇo bāṇaughair atikāyo mahābalaḥ |
avadhyakavacaḥ saṁkhye rākṣaso naiva vivyathe ||

=== meaning ===

---
96.
na śaśāka rujaṁ kartuṁ yudhi tasya narottamaḥ |
athainam abhyupāgamya vāyur vākyam uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
97.
brahmadattavaro hy eṣa avadhya kavacāvr̥taḥ |
brāhmeṇāstreṇa bhindhy enam eṣa vadhyo hi nānyathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
98.
tataḥ sa vāyor vacanaṁ niśamya; saumitrir indrapratimānavīryaḥ |
samādade bāṇam amoghavegaṁ; tad brāhmam astraṁ sahasā niyojya ||

=== meaning ===

---
99.
tasmin varāstre tu niyujyamāne; saumitriṇā bāṇavare śitāgre |
diśaḥ sacandrārkamahāgrahāś ca; nabhaś ca tatrāsa rarāsa corvī ||

=== meaning ===

---
100.
taṁ brahmaṇo ’streṇa niyujya cāpe; śaraṁ supuṅkhaṁ yamadūtakalpam |
saumitrir indrārisutasya tasya; sasarja bāṇaṁ yudhi vajrakalpam ||

=== meaning ===

---
101.
taṁ lakṣmaṇotsr̥ṣṭam amoghavegaṁ; samāpatantaṁ jvalanaprakāśam |
suvarṇavajrottamacitrapuṅkhaṁ; tadātikāyaḥ samare dadarśa ||

=== meaning ===

---
102.
taṁ prekṣamāṇaḥ sahasātikāyo; jaghāna bāṇair niśitair anekaiḥ |
sa sāyakas tasya suparṇavegas; tadātivegena jagāma pārśvam ||

=== meaning ===

---
103.
tam āgataṁ prekṣya tadātikāyo; bāṇaṁ pradīptāntakakālakalpam |
jaghāna śaktyr̥ṣṭigadākuṭhāraiḥ; śūlair halaiś cāpy avipannaceṣṭaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
104.
tāny āyudhāny adbhutavigrahāṇi; moghāni kr̥tvā sa śaro ’gnidīptaḥ |
prasahya tasyaiva kirīṭajuṣṭaṁ; tadātikāyasya śiro jahāra ||

=== meaning ===

---
105.
tac chiraḥ saśiras trāṇaṁ lakṣmaṇeṣuprapīḍitam |
papāta sahasā bhūmau śr̥ṅgaṁ himavato yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
106.
praharṣayuktā bahavas tu vānarā; prabuddhapadmapratimānanās tadā |
apūjayam̐l lakṣmaṇam iṣṭabhāginaṁ; hate ripau bhīmabale durāsade ||

=== meaning ===

---
