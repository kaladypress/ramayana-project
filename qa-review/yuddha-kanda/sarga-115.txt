=== Sarga 115 ===

---
1.
śrutvā tu param ānandaṁ bharataḥ satyavikramaḥ |
hr̥ṣṭam ājñāpayām āsa śatrughnaṁ paravīrahā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
daivatāni ca sarvāṇi caityāni nagarasya ca |
sugandhamālyair vāditrair arcantu śucayo narāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
rājadārās tathāmātyāḥ sainyāḥ senāgaṇāṅganāḥ |
abhiniryāntu rāmasya draṣṭuṁ śaśinibhaṁ mukham ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
bharatasya vacaḥ śrutvā śatrughnaḥ paravīrahā |
viṣṭīr anekasāhasrīś codayām āsa vīryavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
samīkuruta nimnāni viṣamāṇi samāni ca |
sthānāni ca nirasyantāṁ nandigrāmād itaḥ param ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
siñcantu pr̥thivīṁ kr̥tsnāṁ himaśītena vāriṇā |
tato ’bhyavakiraṁs tv anye lājaiḥ puṣpaiś ca sarvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
samucchritapatākās tu rathyāḥ puravarottame |
śobhayantu ca veśmāni sūryasyodayanaṁ prati ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sragdāmamuktapuṣpaiś ca sugandhaiḥ pañcavarṇakaiḥ |
rājamārgam asaṁbādhaṁ kirantu śataśo narāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
mattair nāgasahasraiś ca śātakumbhavibhūṣitaḥ |
apare hemakakṣyābhiḥ sagajābhiḥ kareṇubhiḥ |
niryayus tvarayā yuktā rathaiś ca sumahārathāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tato yānāny upārūḍhāḥ sarvā daśarathastriyaḥ |
kausalyāṁ pramukhe kr̥tvā sumitrāṁ cāpi niryayuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
aśvānāṁ khuraśabdena rathanemisvanena ca |
śaṅkhadundubhinādena saṁcacāleva medinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
kr̥tsnaṁ ca nagaraṁ tat tu nandigrāmam upāgamat |
dvijātimukhyair dharmātmā śreṇīmukhyaiḥ sanaigamaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
mālyamodaka hastaiś ca mantribhir bharato vr̥taḥ |
śaṅkhabherīninādaiś ca bandibhiś cābhivanditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
āryapādau gr̥hītvā tu śirasā dharmakovidaḥ |
pāṇḍuraṁ chatram ādāya śuklamālyopaśobhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
śukle ca vālavyajane rājārhe hemabhūṣite |
upavāsakr̥śo dīnaś cīrakr̥ṣṇājināmbaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
bhrātur āgamanaṁ śrutvā tat pūrvaṁ harṣam āgataḥ |
pratyudyayau tadā rāmaṁ mahātmā sacivaiḥ saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
samīkṣya bharato vākyam uvāca pavanātmajam |
kaccin na khalu kāpeyī sevyate calacittatā |
na hi paśyāmi kākutsthaṁ rāmam āryaṁ paraṁtapam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
athaivam ukte vacane hanūmān idam abravīt |
arthaṁ vijñāpayann eva bharataṁ satyavikramam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
sadā phalān kusumitān vr̥kṣān prāpya madhusravān |
bharadvājaprasādena mattabhramaranāditān ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tasya caiṣa varo datto vāsavena paraṁtapa |
sasainyasya tadātithyaṁ kr̥taṁ sarvaguṇānvitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
nisvanaḥ śrūyate bhīmaḥ prahr̥ṣṭānāṁ vanaukasām |
manye vānarasenā sā nadīṁ tarati gomatīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
rajovarṣaṁ samudbhūtaṁ paśya vālukinīṁ prati |
manye sālavanaṁ ramyaṁ lolayanti plavaṁgamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tad etad dr̥śyate dūrād vimalaṁ candrasaṁnibham |
vimānaṁ puṣpakaṁ divyaṁ manasā brahmanirmitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
rāvaṇaṁ bāndhavaiḥ sārdhaṁ hatvā labdhaṁ mahātmanā |
dhanadasya prasādena divyam etan manojavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
etasmin bhrātarau vīrau vaidehyā saha rāghavau |
sugrīvaś ca mahātejā rākṣasendro vibhīṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tato harṣasamudbhūto nisvano divam aspr̥śat |
strībālayuvavr̥ddhānāṁ rāmo ’yam iti kīrtitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
rathakuñjaravājibhyas te ’vatīrya mahīṁ gatāḥ |
dadr̥śus taṁ vimānasthaṁ narāḥ somam ivāmbare ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
prāñjalir bharato bhūtvā prahr̥ṣṭo rāghavonmukhaḥ |
svāgatena yathārthena tato rāmam apūjayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
manasā brahmaṇā sr̥ṣṭe vimāne lakṣmaṇāgrajaḥ |
rarāja pr̥thudīrghākṣo vajrapāṇir ivāparaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
tato vimānāgragataṁ bharato bhrātaraṁ tadā |
vavande praṇato rāmaṁ merustham iva bhāskaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
āropito vimānaṁ tad bharataḥ satyavikramaḥ |
rāmam āsādya muditaḥ punar evābhyavādayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
taṁ samutthāpya kākutsthaś cirasyākṣipathaṁ gatam |
aṅke bharatam āropya muditaḥ pariṣaṣvaje ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
tato lakṣmaṇam āsādya vaidehīṁ ca paraṁtapaḥ |
abhyavādayata prīto bharato nāma cābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
sugrīvaṁ kaikayī putro jāmbavantaṁ tathāṅgadam |
maindaṁ ca dvividaṁ nīlam r̥ṣabhaṁ caiva sasvaje ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
te kr̥tvā mānuṣaṁ rūpaṁ vānarāḥ kāmarūpiṇaḥ |
kuśalaṁ paryapr̥cchanta prahr̥ṣṭā bharataṁ tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
vibhīṣaṇaṁ ca bharataḥ sāntvayan vākyam abravīt |
diṣṭyā tvayā sahāyena kr̥taṁ karma suduṣkaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
śatrughnaś ca tadā rāmam abhivādya salakṣmaṇam |
sītāyāś caraṇau paścād vavande vinayānvitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
rāmo mātaram āsādya viṣaṇṇaṁ śokakarśitām |
jagrāha praṇataḥ pādau mano mātuḥ prasādayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
abhivādya sumitrāṁ ca kaikeyīṁ ca yaśasvinīm |
sa mātr̥̄ś ca tadā sarvāḥ purohitam upāgamat ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
svāgataṁ te mahābāho kausalyānandavardhana |
iti prāñjalayaḥ sarve nāgarā rāmam abruvan ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
tany añjalisahasrāṇi pragr̥hītāni nāgaraiḥ |
ākośānīva padmāni dadarśa bharatāgrajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
pāduke te tu rāmasya gr̥hītvā bharataḥ svayam |
caraṇābhyāṁ narendrasya yojayām āsa dharmavit ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
abravīc ca tadā rāmaṁ bharataḥ sa kr̥tāñjaliḥ |
etat te rakṣitaṁ rājan rājyaṁ niryātitaṁ mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
adya janma kr̥tārthaṁ me saṁvr̥ttaś ca manorathaḥ |
yas tvāṁ paśyāmi rājānam ayodhyāṁ punar āgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
avekṣatāṁ bhavān kośaṁ koṣṭhāgāraṁ puraṁ balam |
bhavatas tejasā sarvaṁ kr̥taṁ daśaguṇaṁ mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
tathā bruvāṇaṁ bharataṁ dr̥ṣṭvā taṁ bhrātr̥vatsalam |
mumucur vānarā bāṣpaṁ rākṣasaś ca vibhīṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
tataḥ praharṣād bharatam aṅkam āropya rāghavaḥ |
yayau tena vimānena sasainyo bharatāśramam ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
bharatāśramam āsādya sasainyo rāghavas tadā |
avatīrya vimānāgrād avatasthe mahītale ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
abravīc ca tadā rāmas tadvimānam anuttamam |
vaha vaiśravaṇaṁ devam anujānāmi gamyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
tato rāmābhyanujñātaṁ tadvimānam anuttamam |
uttarāṁ diśam uddiśya jagāma dhanadālayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
purohitasyātmasamasya rāghavo; br̥haspateḥ śakra ivāmarādhipaḥ |
nipīḍya pādau pr̥thag āsane śubhe; sahaiva tenopaviveśa vīryavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
