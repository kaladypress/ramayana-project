=== Sarga 32 ===

---
1.
tatas te rākṣasās tatra gatvā rāvaṇamandiram |
nyavedayan purīṁ ruddhāṁ rāmeṇa saha vānaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
ruddhāṁ tu nagarīṁ śrutvā jātakrodho niśācaraḥ |
vidhānaṁ dviguṇaṁ śrutvā prāsādaṁ so ’dhyarohata ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sa dadarśāvr̥tāṁ laṅkāṁ saśailavanakānanām |
asaṁkhyeyair harigaṇaiḥ sarvato yuddhakāṅkṣibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
sa dr̥ṣṭvā vānaraiḥ sarvāṁ vasudhāṁ kavalīkr̥tām |
kathaṁ kṣapayitavyāḥ syur iti cintāparo ’bhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
sa cintayitvā suciraṁ dhairyam ālambya rāvaṇaḥ |
rāghavaṁ hariyūthāṁś ca dadarśāyatalocanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
prekṣato rākṣasendrasya tāny anīkāni bhāgaśaḥ |
rāghavapriyakāmārthaṁ laṅkām āruruhus tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
te tāmravaktrā hemābhā rāmārthe tyaktajīvitāḥ |
laṅkām evāhyavartanta sālatālaśilāyudhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
te drumaiḥ parvatāgraiś ca muṣṭibhiś ca plavaṁgamāḥ |
prāsādāgrāṇi coccāni mamantus toraṇāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
pārikhāḥ pūrayanti sma prasannasalilāyutāḥ |
pāṁsubhiḥ parvatāgraiś ca tr̥ṇaiḥ kāṣṭhaiś ca vānarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tataḥ sahasrayūthāś ca koṭiyūthāś ca yūthapāḥ |
koṭīśatayutāś cānye laṅkām āruruhus tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
kāñcanāni pramr̥dnantas toraṇāni plavaṁgamāḥ |
kailāsaśikharābhāni gopurāṇi pramathya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
āplavantaḥ plavantaś ca garjantaś ca plavaṁgamāḥ |
laṅkāṁ tām abhyavartanta mahāvāraṇasaṁnibhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
jayaty atibalo rāmo lakṣmaṇaś ca mahābalaḥ |
rājā jayati sugrīvo rāghaveṇābhipālitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
ity evaṁ ghoṣayantaś ca garjantaś ca plavaṁgamāḥ |
abhyadhāvanta laṅkāyāḥ prākāraṁ kāmarūpiṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
vīrabāhuḥ subāhuś ca nalaś ca vanagocaraḥ |
nipīḍyopaniviṣṭās te prākāraṁ hariyūthapāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
etasminn antare cakruḥ skandhāvāraniveśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
pūrvadvāraṁ tu kumudaḥ koṭibhir daśabhir vr̥taḥ |
āvr̥tya balavāṁs tasthau haribhir jitakāśibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
dakṣiṇadvāram āgamya vīraḥ śatabaliḥ kapiḥ |
āvr̥tya balavāṁs tasthau viṁśatyā koṭibhir vr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
suṣeṇaḥ paścimadvāraṁ gatas tārā pitā hariḥ |
āvr̥tya balavāṁs tasthau ṣaṣṭi koṭibhir āvr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
uttaradvāram āsādya rāmaḥ saumitriṇā saha |
āvr̥tya balavāṁs tasthau sugrīvaś ca harīśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
golāṅgūlo mahākāyo gavākṣo bhīmadarśanaḥ |
vr̥taḥ koṭyā mahāvīryas tasthau rāmasya pārvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
r̥ṣkāṇāṁ bhīmavegānāṁ dhūmraḥ śatrunibarhaṇaḥ |
vr̥taḥ koṭyā mahāvīryas tasthau rāmasya pārśvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
saṁnaddhas tu mahāvīryo gadāpāṇir vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
vr̥to yas tais tu sacivais tasthau tatra mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
gajo gavākṣo gavayaḥ śarabho gandhamādanaḥ |
samantāt parighāvanto rarakṣur harivāhinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tataḥ kopaparītātmā rāvaṇo rākṣaseśvaraḥ |
niryāṇaṁ sarvasainyānāṁ drutam ājñāpayat tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
niṣpatanti tataḥ sainyā hr̥ṣṭā rāvaṇacoditāḥ |
samaye pūryamāṇasya vegā iva mahodadheḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
etasminn antare ghoraḥ saṁgrāmaḥ samapadyata |
rakṣasāṁ vānarāṇāṁ ca yathā devāsure purā ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
te gadābhiḥ pradīptābhiḥ śaktiśūlaparaśvadhaiḥ |
nijaghnur vānarān ghorāḥ kathayantaḥ svavikramān ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
tathā vr̥kṣair mahākāyāḥ parvatāgraiś ca vānarāḥ |
rākṣasās tāni rakṣāṁsi nakhair dantaiś ca vegitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
rākṣasās tv apare bhīmāḥ prākārasthā mahīgatān |
bhiṇḍipālaiś ca khaḍgaiś ca śūlaiś caiva vyadārayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
vānarāś cāpi saṁkruddhāḥ prākārasthān mahīgatāḥ |
rākṣasān pātayām āsuḥ samāplutya plavaṁgamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
sa saṁprahāras tumulo māṁsaśoṇitakardamaḥ |
rakṣasāṁ vānarāṇāṁ ca saṁbabhūvādbhutopamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
