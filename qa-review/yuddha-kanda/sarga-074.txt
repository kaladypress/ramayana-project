=== Sarga 74 ===

---
1.
evam uktvā tu saumitriṁ jātaharṣo vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
dhanuṣpāṇinam ādāya tvaramāṇo jagāma saḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
avidūraṁ tato gatvā praviśya ca mahad vanam |
darśayām āsa tat karma lakṣmaṇāya vibhīṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
nīlajīmūtasaṁkāśaṁ nyagrodhaṁ bhīmadarśanam |
tejasvī rāvaṇabhrātā lakṣmaṇāya nyavedayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
ihopahāraṁ bhūtānāṁ balavān rāvaṇātajaḥ |
upahr̥tya tataḥ paścāt saṁgrāmam abhivartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
adr̥śyaḥ sarvabhūtānāṁ tato bhavati rākṣasaḥ |
nihanti samare śatrūn badhnāti ca śarottamaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tam apraviṣṭaṁ nyagrodhaṁ balinaṁ rāvaṇātmajam |
vidhvaṁsaya śarais tīkṣṇaiḥ sarathaṁ sāśvasārathim ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tathety uktvā mahātejāḥ saumitrir mitranandanaḥ |
babhūvāvasthitas tatra citraṁ visphārayan dhanuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sa rathenāgnivarṇena balavān rāvaṇātmajaḥ |
indrajit kavacī khaḍgī sadhvajaḥ pratyadr̥śyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tam uvāca mahātejāḥ paulastyam aparājitam |
samāhvaye tvāṁ samare samyag yuddhaṁ prayaccha me ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
evam ukto mahātejā manasvī rāvaṇātmajaḥ |
abravīt paruṣaṁ vākyaṁ tatra dr̥ṣṭvā vibhīṣaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
iha tvaṁ jātasaṁvr̥ddhaḥ sākṣād bhrātā pitur mama |
kathaṁ druhyasi putrasya pitr̥vyo mama rākṣasa ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
na jñātitvaṁ na sauhārdaṁ na jātis tava durmate |
pramāṇaṁ na ca sodaryaṁ na dharmo dharmadūṣaṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
śocyas tvam asi durbuddhe nindanīyaś ca sādhubhiḥ |
yas tvaṁ svajanam utsr̥jya parabhr̥tyatvam āgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
naitac chithilayā buddhyā tvaṁ vetsi mahad antaram |
kva ca svajanasaṁvāsaḥ kva ca nīcaparāśrayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
guṇavān vā parajanaḥ svajano nirguṇo ’pi vā |
nirguṇaḥ svajanaḥ śreyān yaḥ paraḥ para eva saḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
niranukrośatā ceyaṁ yādr̥śī te niśācara |
svajanena tvayā śakyaṁ paruṣaṁ rāvaṇānuja ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
ity ukto bhrātr̥putreṇa pratyuvāca vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
ajānann iva macchīlaṁ kiṁ rākṣasa vikatthase ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
rākṣasendrasutāsādho pāruṣyaṁ tyaja gauravāt |
kule yady apy ahaṁ jāto rakṣasāṁ krūrakarmaṇām |
guṇo ’yaṁ prathamo nr̥̄ṇāṁ tan me śīlam arākṣasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
na rame dāruṇenāhaṁ na cādharmeṇa vai rame |
bhrātrā viṣamaśīlena kathaṁ bhrātā nirasyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
parasvānāṁ ca haraṇaṁ paradārābhimarśanam |
suhr̥dām atiśaṅkāṁ ca trayo doṣāḥ kṣayāvahāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
maharṣīṇāṁ vadho ghoraḥ sarvadevaiś ca vigrahaḥ |
abhimānaś ca kopaś ca vairitvaṁ pratikūlatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
ete doṣā mama bhrātur jīvitaiśvaryanāśanāḥ |
guṇān pracchādayām āsuḥ parvatān iva toyadāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
doṣair etaiḥ parityakto mayā bhrātā pitā tava |
neyam asti purī laṅkā na ca tvaṁ na ca te pitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
atimānī ca bālaś ca durvinītaś ca rākṣasa |
baddhas tvaṁ kālapāśena brūhi māṁ yad yad icchasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
adya te vyasanaṁ prāptaṁ kim iha tvaṁ tu vakṣyasi |
praveṣṭuṁ na tvayā śakyo nyagrodho rākṣasādhama ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
dharṣayitvā tu kākutsthau na śakyaṁ jīvituṁ tvayā |
yudhyasva naradevena lakṣmaṇena raṇe saha |
hatas tvaṁ devatā kāryaṁ kariṣyasi yamakṣaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
nidarśayasvātmabalaṁ samudyataṁ; kuruṣva sarvāyudhasāyakavyayam |
na lakṣmaṇasyaitya hi bāṇagocaraṁ; tvam adya jīvan sabalo gamiṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
