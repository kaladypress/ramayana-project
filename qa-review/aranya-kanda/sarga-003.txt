=== Sarga 3 ===

---
1.
athovāca punar vākyaṁ virādhaḥ pūrayan vanam |
ātmānaṁ pr̥cchate brūtaṁ kau yuvāṁ kva gamiṣyathaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tam uvāca tato rāmo rākṣasaṁ jvalitānanam |
pr̥cchantaṁ sumahātejā ikṣvākukulam ātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
kṣatriyo vr̥ttasaṁpannau viddhi nau vanagocarau |
tvāṁ tu veditum icchāvaḥ kas tvaṁ carasi daṇḍakān ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tam uvāca virādhas tu rāmaṁ satyaparākramam |
hanta vakṣyāmi te rājan nibodha mama rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
putraḥ kila jayasyāhaṁ mātā mama śatahradā |
virādha iti mām āhuḥ pr̥thivyāṁ sarvarākṣasāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tapasā cāpi me prāptā brahmaṇo hi prasādajā |
śastreṇāvadhyatā loke ’cchedyābhedyatvam eva ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
utsr̥jya pramadām enām anapekṣau yathāgatam |
tvaramāṇau pālayethāṁ na vāṁ jīvitam ādade ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
taṁ rāmaḥ pratyuvācedaṁ kopasaṁraktalocanaḥ |
rākṣasaṁ vikr̥tākāraṁ virādhaṁ pāpacetasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
kṣudra dhik tvāṁ tu hīnārthaṁ mr̥tyum anveṣase dhruvam |
raṇe saṁprāpsyase tiṣṭha na me jīvan gamiṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tataḥ sajyaṁ dhanuḥ kr̥tvā rāmaḥ suniśitāñ śarān |
suśīghram abhisaṁdhāya rākṣasaṁ nijaghāna ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
dhanuṣā jyāguṇavatā saptabāṇān mumoca ha |
rukmapuṅkhān mahāvegān suparṇānilatulyagān ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
te śarīraṁ virādhasya bhittvā barhiṇavāsasaḥ |
nipetuḥ śoṇitādigdhā dharaṇyāṁ pāvakopamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sa vinadya mahānādaṁ śūlaṁ śakradhvajopamam |
pragr̥hyāśobhata tadā vyāttānana ivāntakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tac chūlaṁ vajrasaṁkāśaṁ gagane jvalanopamam |
dvābhyāṁ śarābhyāṁ ciccheda rāmaḥ śastrabhr̥tāṁ varaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tasya raudrasya saumitrir bāhuṁ savyaṁ babhañja ha |
rāmas tu dakṣiṇaṁ bāhuṁ tarasā tasya rakṣasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sa bhagnabāhuḥ saṁvigno nipapātāśu rākṣasaḥ |
dharaṇyāṁ meghasaṁkāśo vajrabhinna ivācalaḥ |
idaṁ provāca kākutsthaṁ virādhaḥ puruṣarṣabham ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
kausalyā suprajās tāta rāmas tvaṁ vidito mayā |
vaidehī ca mahābhāgā lakṣmaṇaś ca mahāyaśāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
abhiśāpād ahaṁ ghorāṁ praviṣṭo rākṣasīṁ tanum |
tumburur nāma gandharvaḥ śapto vaiśvaraṇena hi ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
prasādyamānaś ca mayā so ’bravīn māṁ mahāyaśāḥ |
yadā dāśarathī rāmas tvāṁ vadhiṣyati saṁyuge ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tadā prakr̥tim āpanno bhavān svargaṁ gamiṣyati |
iti vaiśravaṇo rājā rambhāsaktam uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
anupasthīyamāno māṁ saṁkruddho vyajahāra ha |
tava prasādān mukto ’ham abhiśāpāt sudāruṇāt |
bhavanaṁ svaṁ gamiṣyāmi svasti vo ’stu paraṁtapa ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
ito vasati dharmātmā śarabhaṅgaḥ pratāpavān |
adhyardhayojane tāta maharṣiḥ sūryasaṁnibhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
taṁ kṣipram abhigaccha tvaṁ sa te śreyo vidhāsyati |
avaṭe cāpi māṁ rāma nikṣipya kuśalī vraja ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
rakṣasāṁ gatasattvānām eṣa dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ |
avaṭe ye nidhīyante teṣāṁ lokāḥ sanātanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
evam uktvā tu kākutsthaṁ virādhaḥ śarapīḍitaḥ |
babhūva svargasaṁprāpto nyastadeho mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
taṁ muktakaṇṭham utkṣipya śaṅkukarṇaṁ mahāsvanam |
virādhaṁ prākṣipac chvabhre nadantaṁ bhairavasvanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tatas tu tau kāñcanacitrakārmukau; nihatya rakṣaḥ parigr̥hya maithilīm |
vijahratus tau muditau mahāvane; divi sthitau candradivākarāv iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
