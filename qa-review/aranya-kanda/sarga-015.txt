=== Sarga 15 ===

---
1.
vasatas tasya tu sukhaṁ rāghavasya mahātmanaḥ |
śaradvyapāye hemanta r̥tur iṣṭaḥ pravartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sa kadā cit prabhātāyāṁ śarvaryāṁ raghunandanaḥ |
prayayāv abhiṣekārthaṁ ramyāṁ godāvarīṁ nadīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
prahvaḥ kalaśahastas taṁ sītayā saha vīryavān |
pr̥ṣṭhato ’nuvrajan bhrātā saumitrir idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
ayaṁ sa kālaḥ saṁprāptaḥ priyo yas te priyaṁvada |
alaṁkr̥ta ivābhāti yena saṁvatsaraḥ śubhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
nīhāraparuṣo lokaḥ pr̥thivī sasyamālinī |
jalāny anupabhogyāni subhago havyavāhanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
navāgrayaṇapūjābhir abhyarcya pitr̥devatāḥ |
kr̥tāgrayaṇakāḥ kāle santo vigatakalmaṣāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
prājyakāmā janapadāḥ saṁpannataragorasāḥ |
vicaranti mahīpālā yātrārthaṁ vijigīṣavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sevamāne dr̥ḍhaṁ sūrye diśam antakasevitām |
vihīnatilakeva strī nottarā dik prakāśate ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
prakr̥tyā himakośāḍhyo dūrasūryaś ca sāmpratam |
yathārthanāmā suvyaktaṁ himavān himavān giriḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
atyantasukhasaṁcārā madhyāhne sparśataḥ sukhāḥ |
divasāḥ subhagādityāś chāyāsaliladurbhagāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
mr̥dusūryāḥ sanīhārāḥ paṭuśītāḥ samārutāḥ |
śūnyāraṇyā himadhvastā divasā bhānti sāmpratam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
nivr̥ttākāśaśayanāḥ puṣyanītā himāruṇāḥ |
śītā vr̥ddhatarāyāmās triyāmā yānti sāmpratam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
ravisaṁkrāntasaubhāgyas tuṣārāruṇamaṇḍalaḥ |
niḥśvāsāndha ivādarśaś candramā na prakāśate ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
jyotsnā tuṣāramalinā paurṇamāsyāṁ na rājate |
sīteva cātapaśyāmā lakṣyate na tu śobhate ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
prakr̥tyā śītalasparśo himaviddhaś ca sāmpratam |
pravāti paścimo vāyuḥ kāle dviguṇaśītalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
bāṣpacchannāny araṇyāni yavagodhūmavanti ca |
śobhante ’bhyudite sūrye nadadbhiḥ krauñcasārasaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
kharjūrapuṣpākr̥tibhiḥ śirobhiḥ pūrṇataṇḍulaiḥ |
śobhante kiṁ cidālambāḥ śālayaḥ kanakaprabhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
mayūkhair upasarpadbhir himanīhārasaṁvr̥taiḥ |
dūram abhyuditaḥ sūryaḥ śaśāṅka iva lakṣyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
agrāhyavīryaḥ pūrvāhṇe madhyāhne sparśataḥ sukhaḥ |
saṁraktaḥ kiṁ cid āpāṇḍur ātapaḥ śobhate kṣitau ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
avaśyāyanipātena kiṁ cit praklinnaśādvalā |
vanānāṁ śobhate bhūmir niviṣṭataruṇātapā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
avaśyāyatamonaddhā nīhāratamasāvr̥tāḥ |
prasuptā iva lakṣyante vipuṣpā vanarājayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
bāṣpasaṁchannasalilā rutavijñeyasārasāḥ |
himārdravālukais tīraiḥ sarito bhānti sāmpratam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tuṣārapatanāc caiva mr̥dutvād bhāskarasya ca |
śaityād agāgrastham api prāyeṇa rasavaj jalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
jarājarjaritaiḥ parṇaiḥ śīrṇakesarakarṇikaiḥ |
nālaśeṣā himadhvastā na bhānti kamalākarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
asmiṁs tu puruṣavyāghra kāle duḥkhasamanvitaḥ |
tapaś carati dharmātmā tvadbhaktyā bharataḥ pure ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tyaktvā rājyaṁ ca mānaṁ ca bhogāṁś ca vividhān bahūn |
tapasvī niyatāhāraḥ śete śīte mahītale ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
so ’pi velām imāṁ nūnam abhiṣekārtham udyataḥ |
vr̥taḥ prakr̥tibhir nityaṁ prayāti sarayūṁ nadīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
atyantasukhasaṁvr̥ddhaḥ sukumāro himārditaḥ |
kathaṁ tv apararātreṣu sarayūm avagāhate ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
padmapatrekṣaṇaḥ śyāmaḥ śrīmān nirudaro mahān |
dharmajñaḥ satyavādī ca hrīniṣedho jitendriyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
priyābhibhāṣī madhuro dīrghabāhur ariṁdamaḥ |
saṁtyajya vividhān saukhyān āryaṁ sarvātmanāśritaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
jitaḥ svargas tava bhrātrā bharatena mahātmanā |
vanastham api tāpasye yas tvām anuvidhīyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
na pitryam anuvarntante mātr̥kaṁ dvipadā iti |
khyāto lokapravādo ’yaṁ bharatenānyathākr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
bhartā daśaratho yasyāḥ sādhuś ca bharataḥ sutaḥ |
kathaṁ nu sāmbā kaikeyī tādr̥śī krūradarśinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
ity evaṁ lakṣmaṇe vākyaṁ snehād bruvati dhārmike |
parivādaṁ jananyās tam asahan rāghavo ’bravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
na te ’mbā madhyamā tāta garhitavyā kathaṁ cana |
tām evekṣvākunāthasya bharatasya kathāṁ kuru ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
niścitāpi hi me buddhir vanavāse dr̥ḍhavratā |
bharatasnehasaṁtaptā bāliśīkriyate punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
ity evaṁ vilapaṁs tatra prāpya godāvarīṁ nadīm |
cakre ’bhiṣekaṁ kākutsthaḥ sānujaḥ saha sītayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
tarpayitvātha salilais te pitr̥̄n daivatāni ca |
stuvanti smoditaṁ sūryaṁ devatāś ca samāhitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
kr̥tābhiṣekaḥ sa rarāja rāmaḥ; sītādvitīyaḥ saha lakṣmaṇena |
kr̥tābhiṣekas tv agarājaputryā; rudraḥ sanandir bhagavān iveśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
