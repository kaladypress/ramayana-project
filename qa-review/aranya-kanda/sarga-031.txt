=== Sarga 31 ===

---
1.
tataḥ śūrpaṇakhā dīnā rāvaṇaṁ lokarāvaṇam |
amātyamadhye saṁkruddhā paruṣaṁ vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
pramattaḥ kāmabhogeṣu svairavr̥tto niraṅkuśaḥ |
samutpannaṁ bhayaṁ ghoraṁ boddhavyaṁ nāvabudhyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
saktaṁ grāmyeṣu bhogeṣu kāmavr̥ttaṁ mahīpatim |
lubdhaṁ na bahu manyante śmaśānāgnim iva prajāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
svayaṁ kāryāṇi yaḥ kāle nānutiṣṭhati pārthivaḥ |
sa tu vai saha rājyena taiś ca kāryair vinaśyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
ayuktacāraṁ durdarśam asvādhīnaṁ narādhipam |
varjayanti narā dūrān nadīpaṅkam iva dvipāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
ye na rakṣanti viṣayam asvādhīnā narādhipaḥ |
te na vr̥ddhyā prakāśante girayaḥ sāgare yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
ātmavadbhir vigr̥hya tvaṁ devagandharvadānavaiḥ |
ayuktacāraś capalaḥ kathaṁ rājā bhaviṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
yeṣāṁ cāraś ca kośaś ca nayaś ca jayatāṁ vara |
asvādhīnā narendrāṇāṁ prākr̥tais te janaiḥ samāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
yasmāt paśyanti dūrasthān sarvān arthān narādhipāḥ |
cāreṇa tasmād ucyante rājāno dīrghacakṣuṣaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
ayuktacāraṁ manye tvāṁ prākr̥taiḥ sacivair vr̥tam |
svajanaṁ ca janasthānaṁ hataṁ yo nāvabudhyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
caturdaśa sahasrāṇi rakṣasāṁ bhīmakarmaṇām |
hatāny ekena rāmeṇa kharaś ca sahadūṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
r̥ṣīṇām abhayaṁ dattaṁ kr̥takṣemāś ca daṇḍakāḥ |
dharṣitaṁ ca janasthānaṁ rāmeṇākliṣṭakarmaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tvaṁ tu lubdhaḥ pramattaś ca parādhīnaś ca rāvaṇa |
viṣaye sve samutpannaṁ bhayaṁ yo nāvabudhyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tīkṣṇam alpapradātāraṁ pramattaṁ garvitaṁ śaṭham |
vyasane sarvabhūtāni nābhidhāvanti pārthivam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
atimāninam agrāhyam ātmasaṁbhāvitaṁ naram |
krodhanaṁ vyasane hanti svajano ’pi narādhipam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
nānutiṣṭhati kāryāṇi bhayeṣu na bibheti ca |
kṣipraṁ rājyāc cyuto dīnas tr̥ṇais tulyo bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
śuṣkakāṣṭhair bhavet kāryaṁ loṣṭair api ca pāṁsubhiḥ |
na tu sthānāt paribhraṣṭaiḥ kāryaṁ syād vasudhādhipaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
upabhuktaṁ yathā vāsaḥ srajo vā mr̥ditā yathā |
evaṁ rājyāt paribhraṣṭaḥ samartho ’pi nirarthakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
apramattaś ca yo rājā sarvajño vijitendriyaḥ |
kr̥tajño dharmaśīlaś ca sa rājā tiṣṭhate ciram ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
nayanābhyāṁ prasupto ’pi jāgarti nayacakṣuṣā |
vyaktakrodhaprasādaś ca sa rājā pūjyate janaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tvaṁ tu rāvaṇadurbuddhir guṇair etair vivarjitaḥ |
yasya te ’viditaś cārai rakṣasāṁ sumahān vadhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
parāvamantā viṣayeṣu saṁgato; nadeśa kālapravibhāga tattvavit |
ayuktabuddhir guṇadoṣaniścaye; vipannarājyo na cirād vipatsyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
iti svadoṣān parikīrtitāṁs tayā; samīkṣya buddhyā kṣaṇadācareśvaraḥ |
dhanena darpeṇa balena cānvito; vicintayām āsa ciraṁ sa rāvaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
