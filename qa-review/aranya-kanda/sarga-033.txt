=== Sarga 33 ===

---
1.
tataḥ śūrpaṇakhā vākyaṁ tac chrutvā romaharṣaṇam |
sacivān abhyanujñāya kāryaṁ buddhvā jagāma ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tat kāryam anugamyātha yathāvad upalabhya ca |
doṣāṇāṁ ca guṇānāṁ ca saṁpradhārya balābalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
iti kartavyam ity eva kr̥tvā niścayam ātmanaḥ |
sthirabuddhis tato ramyāṁ yānaśālāṁ jagāma ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
yānaśālāṁ tato gatvā pracchannaṁ rākṣasādhipaḥ |
sūtaṁ saṁcodayām āsa rathaḥ saṁyujyatām iti ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
evam uktaḥ kṣaṇenaiva sārathir laghuvikramaḥ |
rathaṁ saṁyojayām āsa tasyābhimatam uttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
kāñcanaṁ ratham āsthāya kāmagaṁ ratnabhūṣitam |
piśācavadanair yuktaṁ kharaiḥ kanakabhūṣaṇaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
meghapratimanādena sa tena dhanadānujaḥ |
rākṣasādhipatiḥ śrīmān yayau nadanadīpatim ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sa śvetabālavyasanaḥ śvetacchatro daśānanaḥ |
snigdhavaidūryasaṁkāśas taptakāñcanabhūṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
daśāsyo viṁśatibhujo darśanīya paricchadaḥ |
tridaśārir munīndraghno daśaśīrṣa ivādrirāṭ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
kāmagaṁ ratham āsthāya śuśubhe rākṣasādhipaḥ |
vidyunmaṇḍalavān meghaḥ sabalāka ivāmbare ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
saśailaṁ sāgarānūpaṁ vīryavān avalokayan |
nānāpuṣpaphalair vr̥kṣair anukīrṇaṁ sahasraśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
śītamaṅgalatoyābhiḥ padminībhiḥ samantataḥ |
viśālair āśramapadair vedimadbhiḥ samāvr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
kadaly āḍhakisaṁbādhaṁ nālikeropaśobhitam |
sālais tālais tamālaiś ca tarubhiś ca supuṣpitaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
atyantaniyatāhāraiḥ śobhitaṁ paramarṣibhiḥ |
nāgaiḥ suparṇair gandharvaiḥ kiṁnaraiś ca sahasraśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
jitakāmaiś ca siddhaiś ca cāmaṇaiś copaśobhitam |
ājair vaikhānasair māṣair vālakhilyair marīcipaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
divyābharaṇamālyābhir divyarūpābhir āvr̥tam |
krīḍā ratividhijñābhir apsarobhiḥ sahasraśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
sevitaṁ devapatnībhiḥ śrīmatībhiḥ śriyā vr̥tam |
devadānavasaṁghaiś ca caritaṁ tv amr̥tāśibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
haṁsakrauñcaplavākīrṇaṁ sārasaiḥ saṁpraṇāditam |
vaidūryaprastaraṁ ramyaṁ snigdhaṁ sāgaratejasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
pāṇḍurāṇi viśālāni divyamālyayutāni ca |
tūryagītābhijuṣṭāni vimānāni samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tapasā jitalokānāṁ kāmagāny abhisaṁpatan |
gandharvāpsarasaś caiva dadarśa dhanadānujaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
niryāsarasamūlānāṁ candanānāṁ sahasraśaḥ |
vanāni paśyan saumyāni ghrāṇatr̥ptikarāṇi ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
agarūṇāṁ ca mukhyānāṁ vanāny upavanāni ca |
takkolānāṁ ca jātyānāṁ phalānāṁ ca sugandhinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
puṣpāṇi ca tamālasya gulmāni maricasya ca |
muktānāṁ ca samūhāni śuṣyamāṇāni tīrataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
śaṅkhānāṁ prastaraṁ caiva pravālanicayaṁ tathā |
kāñcanāni ca śailāni rājatāni ca sarvaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
prasravāṇi manojñāni prasannāni hradāni ca |
dhanadhānyopapannāni strīratnair āvr̥tāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
hastyaśvarathagāḍhāni nagarāṇy avalokayan |
taṁ samaṁ sarvataḥ snigdhaṁ mr̥dusaṁsparśamārutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
anūpaṁ sindhurājasya dadarśa tridivopamam |
tatrāpaśyat sa meghābhaṁ nyagrodham r̥ṣibhir vr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
samantād yasya tāḥ śākhāḥ śatayojanam āyatāḥ |
yasya hastinam ādāya mahākāyaṁ ca kaccapam |
bhakṣārthaṁ garuḍaḥ śākhām ājagāma mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
tasya tāṁ sahasā śākhāṁ bhāreṇa patagottamaḥ |
suparṇaḥ parṇabahulāṁ babhañjātha mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
tatra vaikhānasā māṣā vālakhilyā marīcipāḥ |
ajā babhūvur dhūmrāś ca saṁgatāḥ paramarṣayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
teṣāṁ dayārthaṁ garuḍas tāṁ śākhāṁ śatayojanām |
jagāmādāya vegena tau cobhau gajakacchapau ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
ekapādena dharmātmā bhakṣayitvā tad āmiṣam |
niṣādaviṣayaṁ hatvā śākhayā patagottamaḥ |
praharṣam atulaṁ lebhe mokṣayitvā mahāmunīn ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
sa tenaiva praharṣeṇa dviguṇīkr̥tavikramaḥ |
amr̥tānayanārthaṁ vai cakāra matimān matim ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
ayojālāni nirmathya bhittvā ratnagr̥haṁ varam |
mahendrabhavanād guptam ājahārāmr̥taṁ tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
taṁ maharṣigaṇair juṣṭaṁ suparṇakr̥talakṣaṇam |
nāmnā subhadraṁ nyagrodhaṁ dadarśa dhanadānujaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
taṁ tu gatvā paraṁ pāraṁ samudrasya nadīpateḥ |
dadarśāśramam ekānte puṇye ramye vanāntare ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
tatra kr̥ṣṇājinadharaṁ jaṭāvalkaladhāriṇam |
dadarśa niyatāhāraṁ mārīcaṁ nāma rākṣasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
sa rāvaṇaḥ samāgamya vidhivat tena rakṣasā |
tataḥ paścād idaṁ vākyam abravīd vākyakovidaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
