=== Sarga 61 ===

---
1.
tapyamānaṁ tathā rāmaṁ sītāharaṇakarśitam |
lokānām abhave yuktaṁ sāmvartakam ivānalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
vīkṣamāṇaṁ dhanuḥ sajyaṁ niḥśvasantaṁ muhur muhuḥ |
hantukāmaṁ paśuṁ rudraṁ kruddhaṁ dakṣakratau yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
adr̥ṣṭapūrvaṁ saṁkruddhaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rāmaṁ sa lakṣmaṇaḥ |
abravīt prāñjalir vākyaṁ mukhena pariśuṣyatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
purā bhūtvā mr̥dur dāntaḥ sarvabhūtahite rataḥ |
na krodhavaśam āpannaḥ prakr̥tiṁ hātum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
candre lakṣṇīḥ prabhā sūrye gatir vāyau bhuvi kṣamā |
etac ca niyataṁ sarvaṁ tvayi cānuttamaṁ yaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
na tu jānāmi kasyāyaṁ bhagnaḥ sāṁgrāmiko rathaḥ |
kena vā kasya vā hetoḥ sāyudhaḥ saparicchadaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
khuranemikṣataś cāyaṁ sikto rudhirabindubhiḥ |
deśo nivr̥ttasaṁgrāmaḥ sughoraḥ pārthivātmaja ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
ekasya tu vimardo ’yaṁ na dvayor vadatāṁ vara |
na hi vr̥ttaṁ hi paśyāmi balasya mahataḥ padam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
naikasya tu kr̥te lokān vināśayitum arhasi |
yuktadaṇḍā hi mr̥davaḥ praśāntā vasudhādhipāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
sadā tvaṁ sarvabhūtānāṁ śaraṇyaḥ paramā gatiḥ |
ko nu dārapraṇāśaṁ te sādhu manyeta rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
saritaḥ sāgarāḥ śailā devagandharvadānavāḥ |
nālaṁ te vipriyaṁ kartuṁ dīkṣitasyeva sādhavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
yena rājan hr̥tā sītā tam anveṣitum arhasi |
maddvitīyo dhanuṣpāṇiḥ sahāyaiḥ paramarṣibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
samudraṁ ca viceṣyāmaḥ parvatāṁś ca vanāni ca |
guhāś ca vividhā ghorā nalinīḥ pārvatīś ca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
devagandharvalokāṁś ca viceṣyāmaḥ samāhitāḥ |
yāvan nādhigamiṣyāmas tava bhāryāpahāriṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
na cet sāmnā pradāsyanti patnīṁ te tridaśeśvarāḥ |
kosalendra tataḥ paścāt prāptakālaṁ kariṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
śīlena sāmnā vinayena sītāṁ; nayena na prāpsyasi cen narendra |
tataḥ samutsādaya hemapuṅkhair; mahendravajrapratimaiḥ śaraughaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
