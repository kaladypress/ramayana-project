=== Sarga 68 ===

---
1.
evam uktau tu tau vīrau kabandhena nareśvarau |
giripradaram āsādya pāvakaṁ visasarjatuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
lakṣmaṇas tu maholkābhir jvalitābhiḥ samantataḥ |
citām ādīpayām āsa sā prajajvāla sarvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tac charīraṁ kabandhasya ghr̥tapiṇḍopamaṁ mahat |
medasā pacyamānasya mandaṁ dahati pāvaka ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
sa vidhūya citām āśu vidhūmo ’gnir ivotthitaḥ |
araje vāsasī vibhran mālāṁ divyāṁ mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tataś citāyā vegena bhāsvaro virajāmbaraḥ |
utpapātāśu saṁhr̥ṣṭaḥ sarvapratyaṅgabhūṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
vimāne bhāsvare tiṣṭhan haṁsayukte yaśaskare |
prabhayā ca mahātejā diśo daśa virājayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
so ’ntarikṣagato rāmaṁ kabandho vākyam abravīt |
śr̥ṇu rāghava tattvena yathā sīmām avāpsyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
rāma ṣaḍ yuktayo loke yābhiḥ sarvaṁ vimr̥śyate |
parimr̥ṣṭo daśāntena daśābhāgena sevyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
daśābhāgagato hīnas tvaṁ rāma sahalakṣmaṇaḥ |
yat kr̥te vyasanaṁ prāptaṁ tvayā dārapradharṣaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tad avaśyaṁ tvayā kāryaḥ sa suhr̥t suhr̥dāṁ vara |
akr̥tvā na hi te siddhim ahaṁ paśyāmi cintayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
śrūyatāṁ rāma vakṣyāmi sugrīvo nāma vānaraḥ |
bhrātrā nirastaḥ kruddhena vālinā śakrasūnunā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
r̥ṣyamūke girivare pampāparyantaśobhite |
nivasaty ātmavān vīraś caturbhiḥ saha vānaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
vayasyaṁ taṁ kuru kṣipram ito gatvādya rāghava |
adrohāya samāgamya dīpyamāne vibhāvasau ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
na ca te so ’vamantavyaḥ sugrīvo vānarādhipaḥ |
kr̥tajñaḥ kāmarūpī ca sahāyārthī ca vīryavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
śaktau hy adya yuvāṁ kartuṁ kāryaṁ tasya cikīrṣitam |
kr̥tārtho vākr̥tārtho vā kr̥tyaṁ tava kariṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sa r̥kṣarajasaḥ putraḥ pampām aṭati śaṅkitaḥ |
bhāskarasyaurasaḥ putro vālinā kr̥takilbiṣaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
saṁnidhāyāyudhaṁ kṣipram r̥ṣyamūkālayaṁ kapim |
kuru rāghava satyena vayasyaṁ vanacāriṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
sa hi sthānāni sarvāṇi kārtsnyena kapikuñjaraḥ |
naramāṁsāśināṁ loke naipuṇyād adhigacchati ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
na tasyāviditaṁ loke kiṁ cid asti hi rāghava |
yāvat sūryaḥ pratapati sahasrāṁśur ariṁdama ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
sa nadīr vipulāñ śailān giridurgāṇi kandarān |
anviṣya vānaraiḥ sārdhaṁ patnīṁ te ’dhigamiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
vānarāṁś ca mahākāyān preṣayiṣyati rāghava |
diśo vicetuṁ tāṁ sītāṁ tvadviyogena śocatīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
sa meruśr̥ṅgāgragatām aninditāṁ; praviśya pātālatale ’pi vāśritām |
plavaṁgamānāṁ pravaras tava priyāṁ; nihatya rakṣāṁsi punaḥ pradāsyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
