=== Sarga 11 ===

---
1.
sa praviśyāśramapadaṁ lakṣmaṇo rāghavānujaḥ |
agastyaśiṣyam āsādya vākyam etad uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
rājā daśaratho nāma jyeṣṭhas tasya suto balī |
rāmaḥ prāpto muniṁ draṣṭuṁ bhāryayā saha sītayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
lakṣmaṇo nāma tasyāhaṁ bhrātā tv avarajo hitaḥ |
anukūlaś ca bhaktaś ca yadi te śrotram āgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
te vayaṁ vanam atyugraṁ praviṣṭāḥ pitr̥śāsanāt |
draṣṭum icchāmahe sarve bhagavantaṁ nivedyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā lakṣmaṇasya tapodhanaḥ |
tathety uktvāgniśaraṇaṁ praviveśa niveditum ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
sa praviśya muniśreṣṭhaṁ tapasā duṣpradharṣaṇam |
kr̥tāñjalir uvācedaṁ rāmāgamanam añjasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
putrau daśarathasyemau rāmo lakṣmaṇa eva ca |
praviṣṭāv āśramapadaṁ sītayā saha bhāryayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
draṣṭuṁ bhavantam āyātau śuśrūṣārtham ariṁdamau |
yad atrānantaraṁ tattvam ājñāpayitum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tataḥ śiṣyād upaśrutya prāptaṁ rāmaṁ salakṣmaṇam |
vaidehīṁ ca mahābhāgām idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
diṣṭyā rāmaś cirasyādya draṣṭuṁ māṁ samupāgataḥ |
manasā kāṅkṣitaṁ hy asya mayāpy āgamanaṁ prati ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
gamyatāṁ satkr̥to rāmaḥ sabhāryaḥ sahalakṣmaṇaḥ |
praveśyatāṁ samīpaṁ me kiṁ cāsau na praveśitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
evam uktas tu muninā dharmajñena mahātmanā |
abhivādyābravīc chiṣyas tatheti niyatāñjaliḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tato niṣkramya saṁbhrāntaḥ śiṣyo lakṣmaṇam abravīt |
kvāsau rāmo muniṁ draṣṭum etu praviśatu svayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tato gatvāśramapadaṁ śiṣyeṇa saha lakṣmaṇaḥ |
darśayām āsa kākutsthaṁ sītāṁ ca janakātmajām ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
taṁ śiṣyaḥ praśritaṁ vākyam agastyavacanaṁ bruvan |
prāveśayad yathānyāyaṁ satkārārthaṁ susatkr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
praviveśa tato rāmaḥ sītayā sahalakṣmaṇaḥ |
praśāntahariṇākīrṇam āśramaṁ hy avalokayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
sa tatra brahmaṇaḥ sthānam agneḥ sthānaṁ tathaiva ca |
viṣṇoḥ sthānaṁ mahendrasya sthānaṁ caiva vivasvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
somasthānaṁ bhagasthānaṁ sthānaṁ kauberam eva ca |
dhātur vidhātuḥ sthānaṁ ca vāyoḥ sthānaṁ tathaiva ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tataḥ śiṣyaiḥ parivr̥to munir apy abhiniṣpatat |
taṁ dadarśāgrato rāmo munīnāṁ dīptatejasaṁ |
abravīd vacanaṁ vīro lakṣmaṇaṁ lakṣmivardhanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
eṣa lakṣmaṇa niṣkrāmaty agastyo bhagavān r̥ṣiḥ |
audāryeṇāvagacchāmi nidhānaṁ tapasām imam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
evam uktvā mahābāhur agastyaṁ sūryavarcasaṁ |
jagrāha paramaprītas tasya pādau paraṁtapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
abhivādya tu dharmātmā tasthau rāmaḥ kr̥tāñjaliḥ |
sītayā saha vaidehyā tadā rāma salakṣmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
pratigr̥hya ca kākutstham arcayitvāsanodakaiḥ |
kuśalapraśnam uktvā ca āsyatām iti so ’bravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
agniṁ hutvā pradāyārghyam atithiṁ pratipūjya ca |
vānaprasthena dharmeṇa sa teṣāṁ bhojanaṁ dadau ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
prathamaṁ copaviśyātha dharmajño munipuṁgavaḥ |
uvāca rāmam āsīnaṁ prāñjaliṁ dharmakovidam ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
anyathā khalu kākutstha tapasvī samudācaran |
duḥsākṣīva pare loke svāni māṁsāni bhakṣayet ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
rājā sarvasya lokasya dharmacārī mahārathaḥ |
pūjanīyaś ca mānyaś ca bhavān prāptaḥ priyātithiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
evam uktvā phalair mūlaiḥ puṣpaiś cānyaiś ca rāghavam |
pūjayitvā yathākāmaṁ punar eva tato ’bravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
idaṁ divyaṁ mahac cāpaṁ hemavajravibhūṣitam |
vaiṣṇavaṁ puruṣavyāghra nirmitaṁ viśvakarmaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
amoghaḥ sūryasaṁkāśo brahmadattaḥ śarottamaḥ |
datto mama mahendreṇa tūṇī cākṣayasāyakau ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
saṁpūrṇau niśitair bāṇair jvaladbhir iva pāvakaiḥ |
mahārājata kośo ’yam asir hemavibhūṣitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
anena dhanuṣā rāma hatvā saṁkhye mahāsurān |
ājahāra śriyaṁ dīptāṁ purā viṣṇur divaukasām ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
tad dhanus tau ca tūṇīrau śaraṁ khaḍgaṁ ca mānada |
jayāya pratigr̥hṇīṣva vajraṁ vajradharo yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
evam uktvā mahātejāḥ samastaṁ tad varāyudham |
dattvā rāmāya bhagavān agastyaḥ punar abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
