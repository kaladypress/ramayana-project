=== Sarga 27 ===

---
1.
nihataṁ dūṣaṇaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā raṇe triśirasā saha |
kharasyāpy abhavat trāso dr̥ṣṭvā rāmasya vikramam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sa dr̥ṣṭvā rākṣasaṁ sainyam aviṣahyaṁ mahābalam |
hatam ekena rāmeṇa dūṣaṇas triśirā api ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tad balaṁ hatabhūyiṣṭhaṁ vimanāḥ prekṣya rākṣasaḥ |
āsasāda kharo rāmaṁ namucir vāsavaṁ yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
vikr̥ṣya balavac cāpaṁ nārācān raktabhojanān |
kharaś cikṣepa rāmāya kruddhān āśīviṣān iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
jyāṁ vidhunvan subahuśaḥ śikṣayāstrāṇi darśayan |
cacāra samare mārgāñ śarai rathagataḥ kharaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
sa sarvāś ca diśo bāṇaiḥ pradiśaś ca mahārathaḥ |
pūrayām āsa taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rāmo ’pi sumahad dhanuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sa sāyakair durviṣahaiḥ sasphuliṅgair ivāgnibhiḥ |
nabhaś cakārāvivaraṁ parjanya iva vr̥ṣṭibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tad babhūva śitair bāṇaiḥ khararāmavisarjitaiḥ |
paryākāśam anākāśaṁ sarvataḥ śarasaṁkulam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
śarajālāvr̥taḥ sūryo na tadā sma prakāśate |
anyonyavadhasaṁrambhād ubhayoḥ saṁprayudhyatoḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tato nālīkanārācais tīkṣṇāgraiś ca vikarṇibhiḥ |
ājaghāna raṇe rāmaṁ totrair iva mahādvipam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
taṁ rathasthaṁ dhanuṣpāṇiṁ rākṣasaṁ paryavasthitam |
dadr̥śuḥ sarvabhūtāni pāśahastam ivāntakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
taṁ siṁham iva vikrāntaṁ siṁhavikrāntagāminam |
dr̥ṣṭvā nodvijate rāmaḥ siṁhaḥ kṣudramr̥gaṁ yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tataḥ sūryanikāśena rathena mahatā kharaḥ |
āsasāda raṇe rāmaṁ pataṅga iva pāvakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tato ’sya saśaraṁ cāpaṁ muṣṭideśe mahātmanaḥ |
kharaś ciccheda rāmasya darśayan pāṇilāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa punas tv aparān sapta śarān ādāya varmaṇi |
nijaghāna raṇe kruddhaḥ śakrāśanisamaprabhān ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tatas tat prahataṁ bāṇaiḥ kharamuktaiḥ suparvabhiḥ |
papāta kavacaṁ bhūmau rāmasyādityavarcasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
sa śarair arpitaḥ kruddhaḥ sarvagātreṣu rāghavaḥ |
rarāja samare rāmo vidhūmo ’gnir iva jvalan ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tato gambhīranirhrādaṁ rāmaḥ śatrunibarhaṇaḥ |
cakārāntāya sa ripoḥ sajyam anyan mahad dhanuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
sumahad vaiṣṇavaṁ yat tad atisr̥ṣṭaṁ maharṣiṇā |
varaṁ tad dhanur udyamya kharaṁ samabhidhāvata ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tataḥ kanakapuṅkhais tu śaraiḥ saṁnataparvabhiḥ |
ciccheda rāmaḥ saṁkruddhaḥ kharasya samare dhvajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
sa darśanīyo bahudhā vicchinnaḥ kāñcano dhvajaḥ |
jagāma dharaṇīṁ sūryo devatānām ivājñayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
taṁ caturbhiḥ kharaḥ kruddho rāmaṁ gātreṣu mārgaṇaiḥ |
vivyādha hr̥di marmajño mātaṅgam iva tomaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
sa rāmo bahubhir bāṇaiḥ kharakārmukaniḥsr̥taiḥ |
viddho rudhirasiktāṅgo babhūva ruṣito bhr̥śam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
sa dhanur dhanvināṁ śreṣṭhaḥ pragr̥hya paramāhave |
mumoca parameṣvāsaḥ ṣaṭ śarān abhilakṣitān ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
śirasy ekena bāṇena dvābhyāṁ bāhvor athārpayat |
tribhiś candrārdhavaktraiś ca vakṣasy abhijaghāna ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tataḥ paścān mahātejā nārācān bhāskaropamān |
jighāṁsū rākṣasaṁ kruddhas trayodaśa śilāśitān ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tato ’sya yugam ekena caturbhiś caturo hayān |
ṣaṣṭhena ca śiraḥ saṁkhye ciccheda kharasāratheḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tribhis triveṇuṁ balavān dvābhyām akṣaṁ mahābalaḥ |
dvādaśena tu bāṇena kharasya saśaraṁ dhanuḥ |
chittvā vajranikāśena rāghavaḥ prahasann iva |
trayodaśenendrasamo bibheda samare kharam ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
prabhagnadhanvā viratho hatāśvo hatasārathiḥ |
gadāpāṇir avaplutya tasthau bhūmau kharas tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
tat karma rāmasya mahārathasya; sametya devāś ca maharṣayaś ca |
apūjayan prāñjalayaḥ prahr̥ṣṭās; tadā vimānāgragatāḥ sametāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
