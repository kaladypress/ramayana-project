=== Sarga 56 ===

---
1.
sa dr̥ṣṭvā lakṣmaṇaṁ dīnaṁ śūnye daśarathātmajaḥ |
paryapr̥cchata dharmātmā vaidehīm āgataṁ vinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
prasthitaṁ daṇḍakāraṇyaṁ yā mām anujagāma ha |
kva sā lakṣmaṇa vaidehī yāṁ hitvā tvam ihāgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
rājyabhraṣṭasya dīnasya daṇḍakān paridhāvataḥ |
kva sā duḥkhasahāyā me vaidehī tanumadhyamā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
yāṁ vinā notsahe vīra muhūrtam api jīvitum |
kva sā prāṇasahāyā me sītā surasutopamā ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
patitvam amarāṇāṁ vā pr̥thivyāś cāpi lakṣmaṇa |
vinā tāṁ tapanīyābhāṁ neccheyaṁ janakātmajām ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
kaccij jīvati vaidehī prāṇaiḥ priyatarā mama |
kaccit pravrājanaṁ saumya na me mithyā bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sītānimittaṁ saumitre mr̥te mayi gate tvayi |
kaccit sakāmā sukhitā kaikeyī sā bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
saputrarājyāṁ siddhārthāṁ mr̥taputrā tapasvinī |
upasthāsyati kausalyā kaccin saumya na kaikayīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
yadi jīvati vaidehī gamiṣyāmy āśramaṁ punaḥ |
suvr̥ttā yadi vr̥ttā sā prāṇāṁs tyakṣyāmi lakṣmaṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
yadi mām āśramagataṁ vaidehī nābhibhāṣate |
punaḥ prahasitā sītā vinaśiṣyāmi lakṣmaṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
brūhi lakṣmaṇa vaidehī yadi jīvati vā na vā |
tvayi pramatte rakṣobhir bhakṣitā vā tapasvinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sukumārī ca bālā ca nityaṁ cāduḥkhadarśinī |
madviyogena vaidehī vyaktaṁ śocati durmanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sarvathā rakṣasā tena jihmena sudurātmanā |
vadatā lakṣmaṇety uccais tavāpi janitaṁ bhayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
śrutaś ca śaṅke vaidehyā sa svaraḥ sadr̥śo mama |
trastayā preṣitas tvaṁ ca draṣṭuṁ māṁ śīghram āgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sarvathā tu kr̥taṁ kaṣṭaṁ sītām utsr̥jatā vane |
pratikartuṁ nr̥śaṁsānāṁ rakṣasāṁ dattam antaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
duḥkhitāḥ kharaghātena rākṣasāḥ piśitāśanāḥ |
taiḥ sītā nihatā ghorair bhaviṣyati na saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
aho ’smi vyasane magnaḥ sarvathā ripunāśana |
kiṁ tv idānīṁ kariṣyāmi śaṅke prāptavyam īdr̥śam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
iti sītāṁ varārohāṁ cintayann eva rāghavaḥ |
ājagāma janasthānaṁ tvarayā sahalakṣmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
vigarhamāṇo ’nujam ārtarūpaṁ; kṣudhā śramāc caiva pipāsayā ca |
viniḥśvasañ śuṣkamukho viṣaṇṇaḥ; pratiśrayaṁ prāpya samīkṣya śūnyam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
svam āśramaṁ saṁpravigāhya vīro; vihāradeśān anusr̥tya kāṁś cit |
etat tad ity eva nivāsabhūmau; prahr̥ṣṭaromā vyathito babhūva ||

=== meaning ===

---
