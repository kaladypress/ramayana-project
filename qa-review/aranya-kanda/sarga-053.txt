=== Sarga 53 ===

---
1.
saṁdiśya rākṣasān ghorān rāvaṇo ’ṣṭau mahābalān |
ātmānaṁ buddhivaiklavyāt kr̥takr̥tyam amanyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sa cintayāno vaidehīṁ kāmabāṇasamarpitaḥ |
praviveśa gr̥haṁ ramyaṁ sītāṁ draṣṭum abhitvaran ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sa praviśya tu tadveśma rāvaṇo rākṣasādhipaḥ |
apaśyad rākṣasīmadhye sītāṁ śokaparāyaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
aśrupūrṇamukhīṁ dīnāṁ śokabhārāvapīḍitām |
vāyuvegair ivākrāntāṁ majjantīṁ nāvam arṇave ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
mr̥gayūthaparibhraṣṭāṁ mr̥gīṁ śvabhir ivāvr̥tām |
adhomukhamukhīṁ dīnām abhyetya ca niśācaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tāṁ tu śokavaśāṁ dīnām avaśāṁ rākṣasādhipaḥ |
sa balād darśayām āsa gr̥haṁ devagr̥hopamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
harmyaprāsādasaṁbadhaṁ strīsahasraniṣevitam |
nānāpakṣigaṇair juṣṭaṁ nānāratnasamanvitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
kāñcanais tāpanīyaiś ca sphāṭikai rājatais tathā |
vajravaidūryacitraiś ca stambhair dr̥ṣṭimanoharaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
divyadundubhinirhrādaṁ taptakāñcanatoraṇam |
sopānaṁ kāñcanaṁ citram āruroha tayā saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
dāntakā rājatāś caiva gavākṣāḥ priyadarśanāḥ |
hemajālāvr̥tāś cāsaṁs tatra prāsādapaṅktayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sudhāmaṇivicitrāṇi bhūmibhāgāni sarvaśaḥ |
daśagrīvaḥ svabhavane prādarśayata maithilīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
dīrghikāḥ puṣkariṇyaś ca nānāpuṣpasamāvr̥tāḥ |
rāvaṇo darśayām āsa sītāṁ śokaparāyaṇām ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
darśayitvā tu vaidehīṁ kr̥tsnaṁ tad bhavanottamam |
uvāca vākyaṁ pāpātmā rāvaṇo janakātmajām ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
daśarākṣasakoṭyaś ca dvāviṁśatir athāparāḥ |
varjayitvā jarā vr̥ddhān bālāṁś ca rajanīcarān ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
teṣāṁ prabhur ahaṁ sīte sarveṣāṁ bhīmakarmaṇām |
sahasram ekam ekasya mama kāryapuraḥsaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
yad idaṁ rājyatantraṁ me tvayi sarvaṁ pratiṣṭhitam |
jīvitaṁ ca viśālākṣi tvaṁ me prāṇair garīyasī ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
bahūnāṁ strīsahasrāṇāṁ mama yo ’sau parigrahaḥ |
tāsāṁ tvam īśvarī sīte mama bhāryā bhava priye ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
sādhu kiṁ te ’nyayā buddhyā rocayasva vaco mama |
bhajasva mābhitaptasya prasādaṁ kartum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
parikṣiptā samudreṇa laṅkeyaṁ śatayojanā |
neyaṁ dharṣayituṁ śakyā sendrair api surāsuraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
na deveṣu na yakṣeṣu na gandharveṣu narṣiṣu |
ahaṁ paśyāmi lokeṣu yo me vīryasamo bhavet ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
rājyabhraṣṭena dīnena tāpasena gatāyuṣā |
kiṁ kariṣyasi rāmeṇa mānuṣeṇālpatejasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
bhajasva sīte mām eva bhartāhaṁ sadr̥śas tava |
yauvanaṁ hy adhruvaṁ bhīru ramasveha mayā saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
darśane mā kr̥thā buddhiṁ rāghavasya varānane |
kāsya śaktir ihāgantum api sīte manorathaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
na śakyo vāyur ākāśe pāśair baddhaṁ mahājavaḥ |
dīpyamānasya vāpy agner grahītuṁ vimalāṁ śikhām ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
trayāṇām api lokānāṁ na taṁ paśyāmi śobhane |
vikrameṇa nayed yas tvāṁ madbāhuparipālitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
laṅkāyāṁ sumahad rājyam idaṁ tvam anupālaya |
abhiṣekodakaklinnā tuṣṭā ca ramayasva mām ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
duṣkr̥taṁ yat purā karma vanavāsena tad gatam |
yaś ca te sukr̥to dharmas tasyeha phalam āpnuhi ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
iha sarvāṇi mālyāni divyagandhāni maithili |
bhūṣaṇāni ca mukhyāni tāni seva mayā saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
puṣpakaṁ nāma suśroṇi bhrātur vaiśravaṇasya me |
vimānaṁ ramaṇīyaṁ ca tad vimānaṁ manojavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
tatra sīte mayā sārdhaṁ viharasva yathāsukham |
vadanaṁ padmasaṁkāśaṁ vimalaṁ cārudarśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
śokārtaṁ tu varārohe na bhrājati varānane |
alaṁ vrīḍena vaidehi dharmalopa kr̥tena te ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
ārṣo ’yaṁ daivaniṣyando yas tvām abhigamiṣyati |
etau pādau mayā snigdhau śirobhiḥ paripīḍitau ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
prasādaṁ kuru me kṣipraṁ vaśyo dāso ’ham asmi te |
nemāḥ śūnyā mayā vācaḥ śuṣyamāṇena bhāṣitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
na cāpi rāvaṇaḥ kāṁ cin mūrdhnā strīṁ praṇameta ha |
evam uktvā daśagrīvo maithilīṁ janakātmajām ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
kr̥tāntavaśam āpanno mameyam iti manyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
