=== Sarga 42 ===

---
1.
tathā tu taṁ samādiśya bhrātaraṁ raghunandanaḥ |
babandhāsiṁ mahātejā jāmbūnadamayatsarum ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tatas triviṇataṁ cāpam ādāyātmavibhūṣaṇam |
ābadhya ca kalāpau dvau jagāmodagravikramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
taṁ vañcayāno rājendram āpatantaṁ nirīkṣya vai |
babhūvāntarhitas trāsāt punaḥ saṁdarśane ’bhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
baddhāsir dhanur ādāya pradudrāva yato mr̥gaḥ |
taṁ sa paśyati rūpeṇa dyotamānam ivāgrataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
avekṣyāvekṣya dhāvantaṁ dhanuṣpāṇir mahāvane |
ativr̥ttam iṣoḥ pātāl lobhayānaṁ kadā cana ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
śaṅkitaṁ tu samudbhrāntam utpatantam ivāmbare |
daśyamānam adr̥śyaṁ ca navoddeśeṣu keṣu cit ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
chinnābhrair iva saṁvītaṁ śāradaṁ candramaṇḍalam |
muhūrtād eva dadr̥śe muhur dūrāt prakāśate ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
darśanādarśanenaiva so ’pākarṣata rāghavam |
āsīt kruddhas tu kākutstho vivaśas tena mohitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
athāvatasthe suśrāntaś chāyām āśritya śādvale |
mr̥gaiḥ parivr̥to vanyair adūrāt pratyadr̥śyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
dr̥ṣṭvā rāmo mahātejās taṁ hantuṁ kr̥taniścayaḥ |
saṁdhāya sudr̥ḍhe cāpe vikr̥ṣya balavad balī ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tam eva mr̥gam uddiśya jvalantam iva pannagam |
mumoca jvalitaṁ dīptam astrabrahmavinirmitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sa bhr̥śaṁ mr̥garūpasya vinirbhidya śarottamaḥ |
mārīcasyaiva hr̥dayaṁ vibhedāśanisaṁnibhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tālamātram athotpatya nyapatat sa śarāturaḥ |
vyanadad bhairavaṁ nādaṁ dharaṇyām alpajīvitaḥ |
mriyamāṇas tu mārīco jahau tāṁ kr̥trimāṁ tanum ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
saṁprāptakālam ājñāya cakāra ca tataḥ svaram |
sadr̥śaṁ rāghavasyaiva hā sīte lakṣmaṇeti ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tena marmaṇi nirviddhaḥ śareṇānupamena hi |
mr̥garūpaṁ tu tat tyaktvā rākṣasaṁ rūpam ātmanaḥ |
cakre sa sumahākāyo mārīco jīvitaṁ tyajan ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tato vicitrakeyūraḥ sarvābharaṇabhūṣitaḥ |
hemamālī mahādaṁṣṭro rākṣaso ’bhūc charāhataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā patitaṁ bhūmau rākṣasaṁ ghoradarśanam |
jagāma manasā sītāṁ lakṣmaṇasya vacaḥ smaran ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
hā sīte lakṣmaṇety evam ākruśya tu mahāsvaram |
mamāra rākṣasaḥ so ’yaṁ śrutvā sītā kathaṁ bhavet ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
lakṣmaṇaś ca mahābāhuḥ kām avasthāṁ gamiṣyati |
iti saṁcintya dharmātmā rāmo hr̥ṣṭatanūruhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tatra rāmaṁ bhayaṁ tīvram āviveśa viṣādajam |
rākṣasaṁ mr̥garūpaṁ taṁ hatvā śrutvā ca tat svaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
nihatya pr̥ṣataṁ cānyaṁ māṁsam ādāya rāghavaḥ |
tvaramāṇo janasthānaṁ sasārābhimukhas tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
