=== Sarga 34 ===

---
1.
mārīca śrūyatāṁ tāta vacanaṁ mama bhāṣataḥ |
ārto ’smi mama cārtasya bhavān hi paramā gatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
jānīṣe tvaṁ janasthānaṁ bhrātā yatra kharo mama |
dūṣaṇaś ca mahābāhuḥ svasā śūrpaṇakhā ca me ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
triśirāś ca mahātejā rākṣasaḥ piśitāśanaḥ |
anye ca bahavaḥ śūrā labdhalakṣā niśācarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
vasanti manniyogena adhivāsaṁ ca rākṣasaḥ |
bādhamānā mahāraṇye munīn ye dharmacāriṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
caturdaśa sahasrāṇi rakṣasāṁ bhīmakarmaṇām |
śūrāṇāṁ labdhalakṣāṇāṁ kharacittānuvartinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
te tv idānīṁ janasthāne vasamānā mahābalāḥ |
saṁgatāḥ param āyattā rāmeṇa saha saṁyuge ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tena saṁjātaroṣeṇa rāmeṇa raṇamūrdhani |
anuktvā paruṣaṁ kiṁ cic charair vyāpāritaṁ dhanuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
caturdaśa sahasrāṇi rakṣasāṁ bhīmakarmaṇām |
nihatāni śarais tīkṣṇair mānuṣeṇa padātinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
kharaś ca nihataḥ saṁkhye dūṣaṇaś ca nipātitaḥ |
hatvā triśirasaṁ cāpi nirbhayā daṇḍakāḥ kr̥tāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
pitrā nirastaḥ kruddhena sabhāryaḥ kṣīṇajīvitaḥ |
sa hantā tasya sainyasya rāmaḥ kṣatriyapāṁsanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
aśīlaḥ karkaśas tīkṣṇo mūrkho lubdho ’jitendriyaḥ |
tyaktadharmas tv adharmātmā bhūtānām ahite rataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
yena vairaṁ vināraṇye sattvam āśritya kevalam |
karṇanāsāpahāreṇa bhaginī me virūpitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tasya bhāryāṁ janasthānāt sītāṁ surasutopamām |
ānayiṣyāmi vikramya sahāyas tatra me bhava ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tvayā hy ahaṁ sahāyena pārśvasthena mahābala |
bhrātr̥bhiś ca surān yuddhe samagrān nābhicintaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tat sahāyo bhava tvaṁ me samartho hy asi rākṣasa |
vīrye yuddhe ca darpe ca na hy asti sadr̥śas tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
etadartham ahaṁ prāptas tvatsamīpaṁ niśācara |
śr̥ṇu tat karma sāhāyye yat kāryaṁ vacanān mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
sauvarṇas tvaṁ mr̥go bhūtvā citro rajatabindubhiḥ |
āśrame tasya rāmasya sītāyāḥ pramukhe cara ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tvāṁ tu niḥsaṁśayaṁ sītā dr̥ṣṭvā tu mr̥garūpiṇam |
gr̥hyatām iti bhartāraṁ lakṣmaṇaṁ cābhidhāsyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tatas tayor apāye tu śūnye sītāṁ yathāsukham |
nirābādho hariṣyāmi rāhuś candraprabhām iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tataḥ paścāt sukhaṁ rāme bhāryāharaṇakarśite |
visrabdhaṁ prahariṣyāmi kr̥tārthenāntarātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tasya rāmakathāṁ śrutvā mārīcasya mahātmanaḥ |
śuṣkaṁ samabhavad vaktraṁ paritrasto babhūva ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
sa rāvaṇaṁ trastaviṣaṇṇacetā; mahāvane rāmaparākramajñaḥ |
kr̥tāñjalis tattvam uvāca vākyaṁ; hitaṁ ca tasmai hitam ātmanaś ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
