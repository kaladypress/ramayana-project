=== Sarga 13 ===

---
1.
atha pañcavaṭīṁ gacchann antarā raghunandanaḥ |
āsasāda mahākāyaṁ gr̥dhraṁ bhīmaparākramam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā tau mahābhāgau vanasthaṁ rāmalakṣmaṇau |
menāte rākṣasaṁ pakṣiṁ bruvāṇau ko bhavān iti ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sa tau madhurayā vācā saumyayā prīṇayann iva |
uvāca vatsa māṁ viddhi vayasyaṁ pitur ātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
sa taṁ pitr̥sakhaṁ buddhvā pūjayām āsa rāghavaḥ |
sa tasya kulam avyagram atha papraccha nāma ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
rāmasya vacanaṁ śrutvā kulam ātmānam eva ca |
ācacakṣe dvijas tasmai sarvabhūtasamudbhavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
pūrvakāle mahābāho ye prajāpatayo ’bhavan |
tān me nigadataḥ sarvān āditaḥ śr̥ṇu rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
kardamaḥ prathamas teṣāṁ vikr̥tas tadanantaram |
śeṣaś ca saṁśrayaś caiva bahuputraś ca vīryavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sthāṇur marīcir atriś ca kratuś caiva mahābalaḥ |
pulastyaś cāṅgirāś caiva pracetāḥ pulahas tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
dakṣo vivasvān aparo ’riṣṭanemiś ca rāghava |
kaśyapaś ca mahātejās teṣām āsīc ca paścimaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
prajāpates tu dakṣasya babhūvur iti naḥ śrutam |
ṣaṣṭir duhitaro rāma yaśasvinyo mahāyaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
kaśyapaḥ pratijagrāha tāsām aṣṭau sumadhyamāḥ |
aditiṁ ca ditiṁ caiva danūm api ca kālakām ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tāmrāṁ krodhavaśāṁ caiva manuṁ cāpy analām api |
tās tu kanyās tataḥ prītaḥ kaśyapaḥ punar abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
putrāṁs trailokyabhartr̥̄n vai janayiṣyatha mat samān |
aditis tan manā rāma ditiś ca danur eva ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
kālakā ca mahābāho śeṣās tv amanaso ’bhavan |
adityāṁ jajñire devās trayastriṁśad ariṁdama ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
ādityā vasavo rudrā aśvinau ca paraṁtapa |
ditis tv ajanayat putrān daityāṁs tāta yaśasvinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
teṣām iyaṁ vasumatī purāsīt savanārṇavā |
danus tv ajanayat putram aśvagrīvam ariṁdama ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
narakaṁ kālakaṁ caiva kālakāpi vyajāyata |
krauñcīṁ bhāsīṁ tathā śyenīṁ dhr̥tarāṣṭrīṁ tathā śukīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tāmrāpi suṣuve kanyāḥ pañcaitā lokaviśrutāḥ |
ulūkāñ janayat krauñcī bhāsī bhāsān vyajāyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
śyenī śyenāṁś ca gr̥dhrāṁś ca vyajāyata sutejasaḥ |
dhr̥tarāṣṭrī tu haṁsāṁś ca kalahaṁsāṁś ca sarvaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
cakravākāṁś ca bhadraṁ te vijajñe sāpi bhāminī |
śukī natāṁ vijajñe tu natāyā vinatā sutā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
daśakrodhavaśā rāma vijajñe ’py ātmasaṁbhavāḥ |
mr̥gīṁ ca mr̥gamandāṁ ca harīṁ bhadramadām api ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
mātaṅgīm atha śārdūlīṁ śvetāṁ ca surabhīṁ tathā |
sarvalakṣaṇasaṁpannāṁ surasāṁ kadrukām api ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
apatyaṁ tu mr̥gāḥ sarve mr̥gyā naravarottama |
r̥ṣkāś ca mr̥gamandāyāḥ sr̥marāś camarās tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tatas tv irāvatīṁ nāma jajñe bhadramadā sutām |
tasyās tv airāvataḥ putro lokanātho mahāgajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
haryāś ca harayo ’patyaṁ vānarāś ca tapasvinaḥ |
golāṅgūlāṁś ca śārdūlī vyāghrāṁś cājanayat sutān ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
mātaṅgyās tv atha mātaṅgā apatyaṁ manujarṣabha |
diśāgajaṁ tu śvetākṣaṁ śvetā vyajanayat sutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tato duhitarau rāma surabhir devy ajāyata |
rohiṇīṁ nāma bhadraṁ te gandharvīṁ ca yaśasvinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
rohiṇy ajanayad gā vai gandharvī vājinaḥ sutān |
surasājanayan nāgān rāma kadrūś ca pannagān ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
manur manuṣyāñ janayat kaśyapasya mahātmanaḥ |
brāhmaṇān kṣatriyān vaiśyāñ śūdrāṁś ca manujarṣabha ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
mukhato brāhmaṇā jātā urasaḥ kṣatriyās tathā |
ūrubhyāṁ jajñire vaiśyāḥ padbhyāṁ śūdrā iti śrutiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
sarvān puṇyaphalān vr̥kṣān analāpi vyajāyata |
vinatā ca śukī pautrī kadrūś ca surasā svasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
kadrūr nāgasahaskraṁ tu vijajñe dharaṇīdharam |
dvau putrau vinatāyās tu garuḍo ’ruṇa eva ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
tasmāj jāto ’ham aruṇāt saṁpātiś ca mamāgrajaḥ |
jaṭāyur iti māṁ viddhi śyenīputram ariṁdama ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
so ’haṁ vāsasahāyas te bhaviṣyāmi yadīcchasi |
sītāṁ ca tāta rakṣiṣye tvayi yāte salakṣmaṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
jaṭāyuṣaṁ tu pratipūjya rāghavo; mudā pariṣvajya ca saṁnato ’bhavat |
pitur hi śuśrāva sakhitvam ātmavāñ; jaṭāyuṣā saṁkathitaṁ punaḥ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
sa tatra sītāṁ paridāya maithilīṁ; sahaiva tenātibalena pakṣiṇā |
jagāma tāṁ pañcavaṭīṁ salakṣmaṇo; ripūn didhakṣañ śalabhān ivānalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
