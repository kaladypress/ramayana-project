=== Sarga 43 ===

---
1.
ārtasvaraṁ tu taṁ bhartur vijñāya sadr̥śaṁ vane |
uvāca lakṣmaṇaṁ sītā gaccha jānīhi rāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
na hi me jīvitaṁ sthāne hr̥dayaṁ vāvatiṣṭhate |
krośataḥ paramārtasya śrutaḥ śabdo mayā bhr̥śam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
ākrandamānaṁ tu vane bhrātaraṁ trātum arhasi |
taṁ kṣipram abhidhāva tvaṁ bhrātaraṁ śaraṇaiṣiṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
rakṣasāṁ vaśam āpannaṁ siṁhānām iva govr̥ṣam |
na jagāma tathoktas tu bhrātur ājñāya śāsanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tam uvāca tatas tatra kupitā janakātmajā |
saumitre mitrarūpeṇa bhrātus tvam asi śatruvat ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
yas tvam asyām avasthāyāṁ bhrātaraṁ nābhipadyase |
icchasi tvaṁ vinaśyantaṁ rāmaṁ lakṣmaṇa matkr̥te ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
vyasanaṁ te priyaṁ manye sneho bhrātari nāsti te |
tena tiṣṭhasi visrabdhas tam apaśyan mahādyutim ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
kiṁ hi saṁśayam āpanne tasminn iha mayā bhavet |
kartavyam iha tiṣṭhantyā yat pradhānas tvam āgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
iti bruvāṇaṁ vaidehīṁ bāṣpaśokapariplutām |
abravīl lakṣmaṇas trastāṁ sītāṁ mr̥gavadhūm iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
devi devamanuṣyeṣu gandharveṣu patatriṣu |
rākṣaseṣu piśāceṣu kiṁnareṣu mr̥geṣu ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
dānaveṣu ca ghoreṣu na sa vidyeta śobhane |
yo rāmaṁ pratiyudhyeta samare vāsavopamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
avadhyaḥ samare rāmo naivaṁ tvaṁ vaktum arhasi |
na tvām asmin vane hātum utsahe rāghavaṁ vinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
anivāryaṁ balaṁ tasya balair balavatām api |
tribhir lokaiḥ samudyuktaiḥ seśvaraiḥ sāmarair api ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
hr̥dayaṁ nirvr̥taṁ te ’stu saṁtāpas tyajyatām ayam |
āgamiṣyati te bhartā śīghraṁ hatvā mr̥gottamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
na sa tasya svaro vyaktaṁ na kaś cid api daivataḥ |
gandharvanagaraprakhyā māyā sā tasya rakṣasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
nyāsabhūtāsi vaidehi nyastā mayi mahātmanā |
rāmeṇa tvaṁ varārohe na tvāṁ tyaktum ihotsahe ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
kr̥tavairāś ca kalyāṇi vayam etair niśācaraiḥ |
kharasya nidhane devi janasthānavadhaṁ prati ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
rākṣasā vidhinā vāco visr̥janti mahāvane |
hiṁsāvihārā vaidehi na cintayitum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
lakṣmaṇenaivam uktā tu kruddhā saṁraktalocanā |
abravīt paruṣaṁ vākyaṁ lakṣmaṇaṁ satyavādinam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
anārya karuṇārambha nr̥śaṁsa kulapāṁsana |
ahaṁ tava priyaṁ manye tenaitāni prabhāṣase ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
naitac citraṁ sapatneṣu pāpaṁ lakṣmaṇa yad bhavet |
tvadvidheṣu nr̥śaṁseṣu nityaṁ pracchannacāriṣu ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
suduṣṭas tvaṁ vane rāmam ekam eko ’nugacchasi |
mama hetoḥ praticchannaḥ prayukto bharatena vā ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
katham indīvaraśyāmaṁ rāmaṁ padmanibhekṣaṇam |
upasaṁśritya bhartāraṁ kāmayeyaṁ pr̥thag janam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
samakṣaṁ tava saumitre prāṇāṁs tyakṣye na saṁśayaḥ |
rāmaṁ vinā kṣaṇam api na hi jīvāmi bhūtale ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
ity uktaḥ paruṣaṁ vākyaṁ sītayā somaharṣaṇam |
abravīl lakṣmaṇaḥ sītāṁ prāñjalir vijitendriyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
uttaraṁ notsahe vaktuṁ daivataṁ bhavatī mama |
vākyam apratirūpaṁ tu na citraṁ strīṣu maithili ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
svabhāvas tv eṣa nārīṇām eṣu lokeṣu dr̥śyate |
vimuktadharmāś capalās tīkṣṇā bhedakarāḥ striyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
upaśr̥ṇvantu me sarve sākṣibhūtā vanecarāḥ |
nyāyavādī yathā vākyam ukto ’haṁ paruṣaṁ tvayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
dhik tvām adya praṇaśya tvaṁ yan mām evaṁ viśaṅkase |
strītvād duṣṭasvabhāvena guruvākye vyavasthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
gamiṣye yatra kākutsthaḥ svasti te ’stu varānane |
rakṣantu tvāṁ viśālākṣi samagrā vanadevatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
nimittāni hi ghorāṇi yāni prādurbhavanti me |
api tvāṁ saha rāmeṇa paśyeyaṁ punar āgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
lakṣmaṇenaivam uktā tu rudatī janakātmajā |
pratyuvāca tato vākyaṁ tīvraṁ bāṣpapariplutā ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
godāvarīṁ pravekṣyāmi vinā rāmeṇa lakṣmaṇa |
ābandhiṣye ’tha vā tyakṣye viṣame deham ātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
pibāmi vā viṣaṁ tīkṣṇaṁ pravekṣyāmi hutāśanam |
na tv ahaṁ rāghavād anyaṁ padāpi puruṣaṁ spr̥śe ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
iti lakṣmaṇam ākruśya sītā duḥkhasamanvitā |
pāṇibhyāṁ rudatī duḥkhād udaraṁ prajaghāna ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
tām ārtarūpāṁ vimanā rudantīṁ; saumitrir ālokya viśālanetrām |
āśvāsayām āsa na caiva bhartus; taṁ bhrātaraṁ kiṁ cid uvāca sītā ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
tatas tu sītām abhivādya lakṣmaṇaḥ; kr̥tāñjaliḥ kiṁ cid abhipraṇamya |
avekṣamāṇo bahuśaś ca maithilīṁ; jagāma rāmasya samīpam ātmavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
