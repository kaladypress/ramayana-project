=== Sarga 26 ===

---
1.
kharaṁ tu rāmābhimukhaṁ prayāntaṁ vāhinīpatiḥ |
rākṣasas triśirā nāma saṁnipatyedam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
māṁ niyojaya vikrānta saṁnivartasva sāhasāt |
paśya rāmaṁ mahābāhuṁ saṁyuge vinipātitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
pratijānāmi te satyam āyudhaṁ cāham ālabhe |
yathā rāmaṁ vadhiṣyāmi vadhārhaṁ sarvarakṣasām ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
ahaṁ vāsya raṇe mr̥tyur eṣa vā samare mama |
vinivartya raṇotsāhaṁ muhūrtaṁ prāśniko bhava ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
prahr̥ṣṭo vā hate rāme janasthānaṁ prayāsyasi |
mayi vā nihate rāmaṁ saṁyugāyopayāsyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
kharas triśirasā tena mr̥tyulobhāt prasāditaḥ |
gaccha yudhyety anujñāto rāghavābhimukho yayau ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
triśirāś ca rathenaiva vājiyuktena bhāsvatā |
abhyadravad raṇe rāmaṁ triśr̥ṅga iva parvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
śaradhārā samūhān sa mahāmegha ivotsr̥jan |
vyasr̥jat sadr̥śaṁ nādaṁ jalārdrasyeva dundubheḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
āgacchantaṁ triśirasaṁ rākṣasaṁ prekṣya rāghavaḥ |
dhanuṣā pratijagrāha vidhunvan sāyakāñ śitān ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
sa saṁprahāras tumulo rāma triśirasor mahān |
babhūvātīva balinoḥ siṁhakuñjarayor iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tatas triśirasā bāṇair lalāṭe tāḍitas tribhiḥ |
amarṣī kupito rāmaḥ saṁrabdham idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
aho vikramaśūrasya rākṣasasyedr̥śaṁ balam |
puṣpair iva śarair yasya lalāṭe ’smi parikṣataḥ |
mamāpi pratigr̥hṇīṣva śarāṁś cāpaguṇacyutān ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
evam uktvā tu saṁrabdhaḥ śarān āśīviṣopamān |
triśiro vakṣasi kruddho nijaghāna caturdaśa ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
caturbhis turagān asya śaraiḥ saṁnataparvabhiḥ |
nyapātayata tejasvī caturas tasya vājinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
aṣṭabhiḥ sāyakaiḥ sūtaṁ rathopasthe nyapātayat |
rāmaś ciccheda bāṇena dhvajaṁ cāsya samucchritam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tato hatarathāt tasmād utpatantaṁ niśācaram |
bibheda rāmas taṁ bāṇair hr̥daye so ’bhavaj jaḍaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
sāyakaiś cāprameyātmā sāmarṣas tasya rakṣasaḥ |
śirāṁsy apātayat trīṇi vegavadbhis tribhiḥ śataiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
sa bhūmau śoṇitodgārī rāmabāṇābhipīḍitaḥ |
nyapatat patitaiḥ pūrvaṁ svaśirobhir niśācaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
hataśeṣās tato bhagnā rākṣasāḥ kharasaṁśrayāḥ |
dravanti sma na tiṣṭhanti vyāghratrastā mr̥gā iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tān kharo dravato dr̥ṣṭvā nivartya ruṣitaḥ svayam |
rāmam evābhidudrāva rāhuś candramasaṁ yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
