=== Sarga 23 ===

---
1.
āśramaṁ prati yāte tu khare kharaparākrame |
tān evautpātikān rāmaḥ saha bhrātrā dadarśa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tān utpātān mahāghorān utthitān romaharṣaṇān |
prajānām ahitān dr̥ṣṭvā vākyaṁ lakṣmaṇam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
imān paśya mahābāho sarvabhūtāpahāriṇaḥ |
samutthitān mahotpātān saṁhartuṁ sarvarākṣasān ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
amī rudhiradhārās tu visr̥jantaḥ kharasvanān |
vyomni meghā vivartante paruṣā gardabhāruṇāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
sadhūmāś ca śarāḥ sarve mama yuddhābhinandinaḥ |
rukmapr̥ṣṭhāni cāpāni viveṣṭante ca lakṣmaṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
yādr̥śā iha kūjanti pakṣiṇo vanacāriṇaḥ |
agrato no bhayaṁ prāptaṁ saṁśayo jīvitasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
saṁprahāras tu sumahān bhaviṣyati na saṁśayaḥ |
ayam ākhyāti me bāhuḥ sphuramāṇo muhur muhuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
saṁnikarṣe tu naḥ śūra jayaṁ śatroḥ parājayam |
suprabhaṁ ca prasannaṁ ca tava vaktraṁ hi lakṣyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
udyatānāṁ hi yuddhārthaṁ yeṣāṁ bhavati lakṣmaṇaḥ |
niṣprabhaṁ vadanaṁ teṣāṁ bhavaty āyuḥ parikṣayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
anāgatavidhānaṁ tu kartavyaṁ śubham icchatā |
āpadaṁ śaṅkamānena puruṣeṇa vipaścitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tasmād gr̥hītvā vaidehīṁ śarapāṇir dhanurdharaḥ |
guhām āśrayaśailasya durgāṁ pādapasaṁkulām ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
pratikūlitum icchāmi na hi vākyam idaṁ tvayā |
śāpito mama pādābhyāṁ gamyatāṁ vatsa māciram ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
evam uktas tu rāmeṇa lakṣmaṇaḥ saha sītayā |
śarān ādāya cāpaṁ ca guhāṁ durgāṁ samāśrayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tasmin praviṣṭe tu guhāṁ lakṣmaṇe saha sītayā |
hanta niryuktam ity uktvā rāmaḥ kavacam āviśat ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sā tenāgninikāśena kavacena vibhūṣitaḥ |
babhūva rāmas timire vidhūmo ’gnir ivotthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sa cāpam udyamya mahac charān ādāya vīryavān |
babhūvāvasthitas tatra jyāsvanaiḥ pūrayan diśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tato devāḥ sagandharvāḥ siddhāś ca saha cāraṇaiḥ |
ūcuḥ paramasaṁtrastā guhyakāś ca parasparam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
caturdaśa sahasrāṇi rakṣasāṁ bhīmakarmaṇām |
ekaś ca rāmo dharmātmā kathaṁ yuddhaṁ bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tato gambhīranirhrādaṁ ghoravarmāyudhadhvajam |
anīkaṁ yātudhānānāṁ samantāt pratyadr̥śyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
siṁhanādaṁ visr̥jatām anyonyam abhigarjatām |
cāpāni vispharayatāṁ jr̥mbhatāṁ cāpy abhīkṣṇaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
vipraghuṣṭasvanānāṁ ca dundubhīṁś cāpi nighnatām |
teṣāṁ sutumulaḥ śabdaḥ pūrayām āsa tad vanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tena śabdena vitrastāḥ śvāpadā vanacāriṇaḥ |
dudruvur yatra niḥśabdaṁ pr̥ṣṭhato nāvalokayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tat tv anīkaṁ mahāvegaṁ rāmaṁ samupasarpata |
ghr̥tanānāpraharaṇaṁ gambhīraṁ sāgaropamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
rāmo ’pi cārayaṁś cakṣuḥ sarvato raṇapaṇḍitaḥ |
dadarśa kharasainyaṁ tad yuddhābhimukham udyatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
vitatya ca dhanur bhīmaṁ tūṇyāś coddhr̥tya sāyakān |
krodham āhārayat tīvraṁ vadhārthaṁ sarvarakṣasām ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
duṣprekṣyaḥ so ’bhavat kruddho yugāntāgnir iva jvalan |
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā tejasāviṣṭaṁ prāvyathan vanadevatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tasya kruddhasya rūpaṁ tu rāmasya dadr̥śe tadā |
dakṣasyeva kratuṁ hantum udyatasya pinākinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
