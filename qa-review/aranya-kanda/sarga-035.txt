=== Sarga 35 ===

---
1.
tac chrutvā rākṣasendrasya vākyaṁ vākyaviśāradaḥ |
pratyuvāca mahāprājño mārīco rākṣaseśvaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sulabhāḥ puruṣā rājan satataṁ priyavādinaḥ |
apriyasya ca pathyasya vaktā śrotā ca durlabhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
na nūnaṁ budhyase rāmaṁ mahāvīryaṁ guṇonnatam |
ayuktacāraś capalo mahendravaruṇopamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
api svasti bhavet tāta sarveṣāṁ bhuvi rakṣasām |
api rāmo na saṁkruddhaḥ kuryāl lokam arākṣasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
api te jīvitāntāya notpannā janakātmajā |
api sītā nimittaṁ ca na bhaved vyasanaṁ mahat ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
api tvām īśvaraṁ prāpya kāmavr̥ttaṁ niraṅkuśam |
na vinaśyet purī laṅkā tvayā saha sarākṣasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tvadvidhaḥ kāmavr̥tto hi duḥśīlaḥ pāpamantritaḥ |
ātmānaṁ svajanaṁ rāṣṭraṁ sa rājā hanti durmatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
na ca pitrā parityakto nāmaryādaḥ kathaṁ cana |
na lubdho na ca duḥśīlo na ca kṣatriyapāṁsanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
na ca dharmaguṇair hīnaiḥ kausalyānandavardhanaḥ |
na ca tīkṣṇo hi bhūtānāṁ sarveṣāṁ ca hite rataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
vañcitaṁ pitaraṁ dr̥ṣṭvā kaikeyyā satyavādinam |
kariṣyāmīti dharmātmā tataḥ pravrajito vanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
kaikeyyāḥ priyakāmārthaṁ pitur daśarathasya ca |
hitvā rājyaṁ ca bhogāṁś ca praviṣṭo daṇḍakāvanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
na rāmaḥ karkaśas tāta nāvidvān nājitendriyaḥ |
anr̥taṁ na śrutaṁ caiva naiva tvaṁ vaktum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
rāmo vigrahavān dharmaḥ sādhuḥ satyaparākramaḥ |
rājā sarvasya lokasya devānām iva vāsavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
kathaṁ tvaṁ tasya vaidehīṁ rakṣitāṁ svena tejasā |
icchasi prasabhaṁ hartuṁ prabhām iva vivasvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
śarārciṣam anādhr̥ṣyaṁ cāpakhaḍgendhanaṁ raṇe |
rāmāgniṁ sahasā dīptaṁ na praveṣṭuṁ tvam arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
dhanurvyāditadīptāsyaṁ śarārciṣam amarṣaṇam |
cāpabāṇadharaṁ vīraṁ śatrusenāpahāriṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
rājyaṁ sukhaṁ ca saṁtyajya jīvitaṁ ceṣṭam ātmanaḥ |
nātyāsādayituṁ tāta rāmāntakam ihārhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
aprameyaṁ hi tat tejo yasya sā janakātmajā |
na tvaṁ samarthas tāṁ hartuṁ rāmacāpāśrayāṁ vane ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
prāṇebhyo ’pi priyatarā bhāryā nityam anuvratā |
dīptasyeva hutāśasya śikhā sītā sumadhyamā ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
kim udyamaṁ vyartham imaṁ kr̥tvā te rākṣasādhipa |
dr̥ṣṭaś cet tvaṁ raṇe tena tad antaṁ tava jīvitam |
jīvitaṁ ca sukhaṁ caiva rājyaṁ caiva sudurlabham ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
sa sarvaiḥ sacivaiḥ sārdhaṁ vibhīṣaṇapuraskr̥taiḥ |
mantrayitvā tu dharmiṣṭhaiḥ kr̥tvā niścayam ātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
doṣāṇāṁ ca guṇānāṁ ca saṁpradhārya balābalam |
ātmanaś ca balaṁ jñātvā rāghavasya ca tattvataḥ |
hitaṁ hi tava niścitya kṣamaṁ tvaṁ kartum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
ahaṁ tu manye tava na kṣamaṁ raṇe; samāgamaṁ kosalarājasūnunā |
idaṁ hi bhūyaḥ śr̥ṇu vākyam uttamaṁ; kṣamaṁ ca yuktaṁ ca niśācarādhipa ||

=== meaning ===

---
