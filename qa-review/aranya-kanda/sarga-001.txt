=== Sarga 1 ===

---
1.
praviśya tu mahāraṇyaṁ daṇḍakāraṇyam ātmavān |
dadarśa rāmo durdharṣas tāpasāśramamaṇḍalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
kuśacīraparikṣiptaṁ brāhmyā lakṣmyā samāvr̥tam |
yathā pradīptaṁ durdharśaṁ gagane sūryamaṇḍalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
śaraṇyaṁ sarvabhūtānāṁ susamr̥ṣṭājiraṁ sadā |
pūjitaṁ copanr̥ttaṁ ca nityam apsarasāṁ gaṇaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
viśālair agniśaraṇaiḥ srugbhāṇḍair ajinaiḥ kuśaiḥ |
samidbhis toyakalaśaiḥ phalamūlaiś ca śobhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
āraṇyaiś ca mahāvr̥kṣaiḥ puṇyaiḥ svāduphalair vr̥tam |
balihomārcitaṁ puṇyaṁ brahmaghoṣanināditam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
puṣpair vanyaiḥ parikṣiptaṁ padminyā ca sapadmayā |
phalamūlāśanair dāntaiś cīrakr̥ṣṇājināmbaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sūryavaiśvānarābhaiś ca purāṇair munibhir vr̥tam |
puṇyaiś a niyatāhāraiḥ śobhitaṁ paramarṣibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tad brahmabhavanaprakhyaṁ brahmaghoṣanināditam |
brahmavidbhir mahābhāgair brāhmaṇair upaśobhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tad dr̥ṣṭvā rāghavaḥ śrīmāṁs tāpasāśramamaṇḍalam |
abhyagacchan mahātejā vijyaṁ kr̥tvā mahad dhanuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
divyajñānopapannās te rāmaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā maharṣayaḥ |
abhyagacchaṁs tadā prītā vaidehīṁ ca yaśasvinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
te taṁ somam ivodyantaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā vai dharmacāriṇaḥ |
maṅgalāni prayuñjānāḥ pratyagr̥hṇan dr̥ḍhavratāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
rūpasaṁhananaṁ lakṣmīṁ saukumāryaṁ suveṣatām |
dadr̥śur vismitākārā rāmasya vanavāsinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
vaidehīṁ lakṣmaṇaṁ rāmaṁ netrair animiṣair iva |
āścaryabhūtān dadr̥śuḥ sarve te vanacāriṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
atrainaṁ hi mahābhāgāḥ sarvabhūtahite ratāḥ |
atithiṁ parṇaśālāyāṁ rāghavaṁ saṁnyaveśayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tato rāmasya satkr̥tya vidhinā pāvakopamāḥ |
ājahrus te mahābhāgāḥ salilaṁ dharmacāriṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
mūlaṁ puṣpaṁ phalaṁ vanyam āśramaṁ ca mahātmanaḥ |
nivedayītvā dharmajñās tataḥ prāñjalayo ’bruvan ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
dharmapālo janasyāsya śaraṇyaś ca mahāyaśāḥ |
pūjanīyaś ca mānyaś ca rājā daṇḍadharo guruḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
indrasyaiva caturbhāgaḥ prajā rakṣati rāghava |
rājā tasmād vanān bhogān bhuṅkte lokanamaskr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
te vayaṁ bhavatā rakṣyā bhavadviṣayavāsinaḥ |
nagarastho vanastho vā tvaṁ no rājā janeśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
nyastadaṇḍā vayaṁ rājañ jitakrodhā jitendriyāḥ |
rakṣitavyās tvayā śaśvad garbhabhūtās tapodhanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
evam uktvā phalair mūlaiḥ puṣpair vanyaiś ca rāghavam |
anyaiś ca vividhāhāraiḥ salakṣmaṇam apūjayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tathānye tāpasāḥ siddhā rāmaṁ vaiśvānaropamāḥ |
nyāyavr̥ttā yathānyāyaṁ tarpayām āsur īśvaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
