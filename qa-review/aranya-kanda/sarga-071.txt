=== Sarga 71 ===

---
1.
divaṁ tu tasyāṁ yātāyāṁ śabaryāṁ svena karmaṇā |
lakṣmaṇena saha bhrātrā cintayām āsa rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
cintayitvā tu dharmātmā prabhāvaṁ taṁ mahātmanām |
hitakāriṇam ekāgraṁ lakṣmaṇaṁ rāghavo ’bravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
dr̥ṣṭo ’yam āśramaḥ saumya bahvāścaryaḥ kr̥tātmanām |
viśvastamr̥gaśārdūlo nānāvihagasevitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
saptānāṁ ca samudrāṇām eṣu tīrtheṣu lakṣmaṇa |
upaspr̥ṣṭaṁ ca vidhivat pitaraś cāpi tarpitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
pranaṣṭam aśubhaṁ yat tat kalyāṇaṁ samupasthitam |
tena tv etat prahr̥ṣṭaṁ me mano lakṣmaṇa saṁprati ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
hr̥daye hi naravyāghra śubham āvirbhaviṣyati |
tad āgaccha gamiṣyāvaḥ pampāṁ tāṁ priyadarśanām ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
r̥śyamūko girir yatra nātidūre prakāśate |
yasmin vasati dharmātmā sugrīvo ’ṁśumataḥ sutaḥ |
nityaṁ vālibhayāt trastaś caturbhiḥ saha vānaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
abhitvare ca taṁ draṣṭuṁ sugrīvaṁ vānararṣabham |
tadadhīnaṁ hi me saumya sītāyāḥ parimārgaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
iti bruvāṇaṁ taṁ rāmaṁ saumitrir idam abravīt |
gacchāvas tvaritaṁ tatra mamāpi tvarate manaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
āśramāt tu tatas tasmān niṣkramya sa viśāṁ patiḥ |
ājagāma tataḥ pampāṁ lakṣmaṇena sahābhibhūḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
samīkṣamāṇaḥ puṣpāḍhyaṁ sarvato vipuladrumam |
koyaṣṭibhiś cārjunakaiḥ śatapatraiś ca kīcakaiḥ |
etaiś cānyaiś ca vividhair nāditaṁ tad vanaṁ mahat ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sa rāmo vidhivān vr̥kṣān sarāṁsi vividhāni ca |
paśyan kāmābhisaṁtapto jagāma paramaṁ hradam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sa tām āsādya vai rāmo dūrād udakavāhinīm |
mataṅgasarasaṁ nāma hradaṁ samavagāhata ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sa tu śokasamāviṣṭo rāmo daśarathātmajaḥ |
viveśa nalinīṁ pampāṁ paṅkajaiś ca samāvr̥tām ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tilakāśokapuṁnāgabakuloddāla kāśinīm |
ramyopavanasaṁbādhāṁ padmasaṁpīḍitodakām ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sphaṭikopamatoyāḍhyāṁ ślakṣṇavālukasaṁtatām |
matsyakacchapasaṁbādhāṁ tīrasthadrumaśobhitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
sakhībhir iva yuktābhir latābhir anuveṣṭitām |
kiṁnaroragagandharvayakṣarākṣasasevitām |
nānādrumalatākīrṇāṁ śītavārinidhiṁ śubhām ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
padmaiḥ saugandhikais tāmrāṁ śuklāṁ kumudamaṇḍalaiḥ |
nīlāṁ kuvalayoddhātair bahuvarṇāṁ kuthām iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
aravindotpalavatīṁ padmasaugandhikāyutām |
puṣpitāmravaṇopetāṁ barhiṇodghuṣṭanāditām ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
sa tāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā tataḥ pampāṁ rāmaḥ saumitriṇā saha |
vilalāpa ca tejasvī kāmād daśarathātmajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tilakair bījapūraiś ca vaṭaiḥ śukladrumais tathā |
puṣpitaiḥ karavīraiś ca puṁnāgaiś ca supuṣpitaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
mālatīkundagulmaiś ca bhaṇḍīrair niculais tathā |
aśokaiḥ saptaparṇaiś ca ketakair atimuktakaiḥ |
anyaiś ca vividhair vr̥kṣaiḥ pramadevopaśobhitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
asyās tīre tu pūrvoktaḥ parvato dhātumaṇḍitaḥ |
r̥śyamūka iti khyātaś citrapuṣpitakānanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
harir r̥kṣarajo nāmnaḥ putras tasya mahātmanaḥ |
adhyāste taṁ mahāvīryaḥ sugrīva iti viśrutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
sugrīvam abhigaccha tvaṁ vānarendraṁ nararṣabha |
ity uvāca punar vākyaṁ lakṣmaṇaṁ satyavikramam ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tato mahad vartma ca dūrasaṁkramaṁ; krameṇa gatvā pravilokayan vanam |
dadarśa pampāṁ śubhadarśa kānanām; anekanānāvidhapakṣisaṁkulām ||

=== meaning ===

---
