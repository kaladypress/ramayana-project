=== Sarga 63 ===

---
1.
pūrvajo ’py uktamātras tu lakṣmaṇena subhāṣitam |
sāragrāhī mahāsāraṁ pratijagrāha rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
saṁnigr̥hya mahābāhuḥ pravr̥ddhaṁ kopam ātmanaḥ |
avaṣṭabhya dhanuś citraṁ rāmo lakṣmaṇam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
kiṁ kariṣyāvahe vatsa kva vā gacchāva lakṣmaṇa |
kenopāyena paśyeyaṁ sītām iti vicintaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
taṁ tathā paritāpārtaṁ lakṣmaṇo rāmam abravīt |
idam eva janasthānaṁ tvam anveṣitum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
rākṣasair bahubhiḥ kīrṇaṁ nānādrumalatāyutam |
santīha giridurgāṇi nirdarāḥ kandarāṇi ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
guhāś ca vividhā ghorā nānāmr̥gagaṇākulāḥ |
āvāsāḥ kiṁnarāṇāṁ ca gandharvabhavanāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tāni yukto mayā sārdhaṁ tvam anveṣitum arhasi |
tvadvidho buddhisaṁpannā māhātmāno nararṣabha ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
āpatsu na prakampante vāyuvegair ivācalāḥ |
ity uktas tad vanaṁ sarvaṁ vicacāra salakṣmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
kruddho rāmaḥ śaraṁ ghoraṁ saṁdhāya dhanuṣi kṣuram |
tataḥ parvatakūṭābhaṁ mahābhāgaṁ dvijottamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
dadarśa patitaṁ bhūmau kṣatajārdraṁ jaṭāyuṣam |
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā giriśr̥ṅgābhaṁ rāmo lakṣmaṇam abravīt |
anena sītā vaidehī bhakṣitā nātra saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
gr̥dhrarūpam idaṁ vyaktaṁ rakṣo bhramati kānanam |
bhakṣayitvā viśālākṣīm āste sītāṁ yathāsukham |
enaṁ vadhiṣye dīptāgrair ghorair bāṇair ajihmagaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
ity uktvābhyapatad gr̥dhraṁ saṁdhāya dhanuṣi kṣuram |
kruddho rāmaḥ samudrāntāṁ cālayann iva medinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
taṁ dīnadīnayā vācā saphenaṁ rudhiraṁ vaman |
abhyabhāṣata pakṣī tu rāmaṁ daśarathātmajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
yām oṣadhim ivāyuṣmann anveṣasi mahāvane |
sā devī mama ca prāṇā rāvaṇenobhayaṁ hr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tvayā virahitā devī lakṣmaṇena ca rāghava |
hriyamāṇā mayā dr̥ṣṭā rāvaṇena balīyasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sītām abhyavapan no ’haṁ rāvaṇaś ca raṇe mayā |
vidhvaṁsitarathacchatraḥ pātito dharaṇītale ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
etad asya dhanur bhagnam etad asya śarāvaram |
ayam asya raṇe rāma bhagnaḥ sāṁgrāmiko rathaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
pariśrāntasya me pakṣau chittvā khaḍgena rāvaṇaḥ |
sītām ādāya vaidehīm utpapāta vihāyasaṁ |
rakṣasā nihataṁ pūrvma na māṁ hantuṁ tvam arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
rāmas tasya tu vijñāya sītāsaktāṁ priyāṁ kathām |
gr̥dhrarājaṁ pariṣvajya ruroda sahalakṣmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
ekam ekāyane durge niḥśvasantaṁ kathaṁ cana |
samīkṣya duḥkhito rāmaḥ saumitrim idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
rājyād bhraṁśo vane vāsaḥ sītā naṣṭā hato dvijaḥ |
īdr̥śīyaṁ mamālakṣmīr nirdahed api pāvakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
saṁpūrṇam api ced adya pratareyaṁ mahodadhim |
so ’pi nūnaṁ mamālakṣmyā viśuṣyet saritāṁ patiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
nāsty abhāgyataro loke matto ’smin sacarācare |
yeneyaṁ mahatī prāptā mayā vyasanavāgurā ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
ayaṁ pitr̥vayasyo me gr̥dhrarājo jarānvitaḥ |
śete vinihato bhūmau mama bhāgyaviparyayāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
ity evam uktvā bahuśo rāghavaḥ sahalakṣmaṇaḥ |
jaṭāyuṣaṁ ca pasparśa pitr̥snehaṁ nidarśayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
nikr̥ttapakṣaṁ rudhirāvasiktaṁ; taṁ gr̥dhrarājaṁ parirabhya rāmaḥ |
kva maithili prāṇasamā mameti; vimucya vācaṁ nipapāta bhūmau ||

=== meaning ===

---
