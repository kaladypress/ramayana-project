=== Sarga 67 ===

---
1.
purā rāma mahābāho mahābalaparākrama |
rūpam āsīn mamācintyaṁ triṣu lokeṣu viśrutam |
yathā somasya śakrasya sūryasya ca yathā vapuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
so ’haṁ rūpam idaṁ kr̥tvā lokavitrāsanaṁ mahat |
r̥ṣīn vanagatān rāma trāsayāmi tatas tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tataḥ sthūlaśirā nāma maharṣiḥ kopito mayā |
saṁcinvan vividhaṁ vanyaṁ rūpeṇānena dharṣitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tenāham uktaḥ prekṣyaivaṁ ghoraśāpābhidhāyinā |
etad eva nr̥śaṁsaṁ te rūpam astu vigarhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
sa mayā yācitaḥ kruddhaḥ śāpasyānto bhaved iti |
abhiśāpakr̥tasyeti tenedaṁ bhāṣitaṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
yadā chittvā bhujau rāmas tvāṁ dahed vijane vane |
tadā tvaṁ prāpsyase rūpaṁ svam eva vipulaṁ śubham ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
śriyā virājitaṁ putraṁ danos tvaṁ viddhi lakṣmaṇa |
indrakopād idaṁ rūpaṁ prāptam evaṁ raṇājire ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
ahaṁ hi tapasogreṇa pitāmaham atoṣayam |
dīrgham āyuḥ sa me prādāt tato māṁ vibhramo ’spr̥śat ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
dīrgham āyur mayā prāptaṁ kiṁ me śakraḥ kariṣyati |
ity evaṁ buddhim āsthāya raṇe śakram adharṣayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tasya bāhupramuktena vajreṇa śataparvaṇā |
sakthinī ca śiraś caiva śarīre saṁpraveśitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sa mayā yācyamānaḥ sann ānayad yamasādanam |
pitāmahavacaḥ satyaṁ tad astv iti mamābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
anāhāraḥ kathaṁ śakto bhagnasakthiśiromukhaḥ |
vajreṇābhihataḥ kālaṁ sudīrgham api jīvitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
evam uktas tu me śakro bāhū yojanam āyatau |
prādād āsyaṁ ca me kukṣau tīkṣṇadaṁṣṭram akalpayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
so ’haṁ bhujābhyāṁ dīrghābhyāṁ samākr̥ṣya vanecarān |
siṁhadvipamr̥gavyāghrān bhakṣayāmi samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa tu mām abravīd indro yadā rāmaḥ salakṣmaṇaḥ |
chetsyate samare bāhū tadā svargaṁ gamiṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sa tvaṁ rāmo ’si bhadraṁ te nāham anyena rāghava |
śakyo hantuṁ yathātattvam evam uktaṁ maharṣiṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
ahaṁ hi matisācivyaṁ kariṣyāmi nararṣabha |
mitraṁ caivopadekṣyāmi yuvābhyāṁ saṁskr̥to ’gninā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
evam uktas tu dharmātmā danunā tena rāghavaḥ |
idaṁ jagāda vacanaṁ lakṣmaṇasyopaśr̥ṇvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
rāvaṇena hr̥tā sītā mama bhāryā yaśasvinī |
niṣkrāntasya janasthānāt saha bhrātrā yathāsukham ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
nāmamātraṁ tu jānāmi na rūpaṁ tasya rakṣasaḥ |
nivāsaṁ vā prabhāvaṁ vā vayaṁ tasya na vidmahe ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
śokārtānām anāthānām evaṁ viparidhāvatām |
kāruṇyaṁ sadr̥śaṁ kartum upakāre ca vartatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
kāṣṭhāny ānīya śuṣkāṇi kāle bhagnāni kuñjaraiḥ |
bhakṣyāmas tvāṁ vayaṁ vīra śvabhre mahati kalpite ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
sa tvaṁ sītāṁ samācakṣva yena vā yatra vā hr̥tā |
kuru kalyāṇam atyarthaṁ yadi jānāsi tattvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
evam uktas tu rāmeṇa vākyaṁ danur anuttamam |
provāca kuśalo vaktuṁ vaktāram api rāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
divyam asti na me jñānaṁ nābhijānāmi maithilīm |
yas tāṁ jñāsyati taṁ vakṣye dagdhaḥ svaṁ rūpam āsthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
adagdhasya hi vijñātuṁ śaktir asti na me prabho |
rākṣasaṁ taṁ mahāvīryaṁ sītā yena hr̥tā tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
vijñānaṁ hi mahad bhraṣṭaṁ śāpadoṣeṇa rāghava |
svakr̥tena mayā prāptaṁ rūpaṁ lokavigarhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
kiṁ tu yāvan na yāty astaṁ savitā śrāntavāhanaḥ |
tāvan mām avaṭe kṣiptvā daha rāma yathāvidhi ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
dagdhas tvayāham avaṭe nyāyena raghunandana |
vakṣyāmi tam ahaṁ vīra yas taṁ jñāsyati rākṣasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
tena sakhyaṁ ca kartavyaṁ nyāyyavr̥ttena rāghava |
kalpayiṣyati te prītaḥ sāhāyyaṁ laghuvikramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
na hi tasyāsty avijñātaṁ triṣu lokeṣu rāghava |
sarvān parisr̥to lokān purā vai kāraṇāntare ||

=== meaning ===

---
