=== Sarga 9 ===

---
1.
vākyam etat tu vaidehyā vyāhr̥taṁ bhartr̥bhaktayā |
śrutvā dharme sthito rāmaḥ pratyuvācātha maithilīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
hitam uktaṁ tvayā devi snigdhayā sadr̥śaṁ vacaḥ |
kulaṁ vyapadiśantyā ca dharmajñe janakātmaje ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
kiṁ tu vakṣyāmy ahaṁ devi tvayaivoktam idaṁ vacaḥ |
kṣatriyair dhāryate cāpo nārtaśabdo bhaved iti ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
te cārtā daṇḍakāraṇye munayaḥ saṁśitavratāḥ |
māṁ sīte svayam āgamya śaraṇyāḥ śaraṇaṁ gatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
vasanto dharmaniratā vane mūlaphalāśanāḥ |
na labhante sukhaṁ bhītā rākṣasaiḥ krūrakarmabhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
kāle kāle ca niratā niyamair vividhair vane |
bhakṣyante rākṣasair bhīmair naramāṁsopajīvibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
te bhakṣyamāṇā munayo daṇḍakāraṇyavāsinaḥ |
asmān abhyavapadyeti mām ūcur dvijasattamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
mayā tu vacanaṁ śrutvā teṣām evaṁ mukhāc cyutam |
kr̥tvā caraṇaśuśrūṣāṁ vākyam etad udāhr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
prasīdantu bhavanto me hrīr eṣā hi mamātulā |
yadīdr̥śair ahaṁ viprair upastheyair upasthitaḥ |
kiṁ karomīti ca mayā vyāhr̥taṁ dvijasaṁnidhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
sarvair eva samāgamya vāg iyaṁ samudāhr̥tā |
rākṣasair daṇḍakāraṇye bahubhiḥ kāmarūpibhiḥ |
arditāḥ sma bhr̥śaṁ rāma bhavān nas trātum arhati ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
homakāle tu saṁprāpte parvakāleṣu cānagha |
dharṣayanti sma durdharṣā rākṣasāḥ piśitāśanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
rākṣasair dharṣitānāṁ ca tāpasānāṁ tapasvinām |
gatiṁ mr̥gayamāṇānāṁ bhavān naḥ paramā gatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
kāmaṁ tapaḥ prabhāvena śaktā hantuṁ niśācarān |
cirārjitaṁ tu necchāmas tapaḥ khaṇḍayituṁ vayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
bahuvighnaṁ taponityaṁ duścaraṁ caiva rāghava |
tena śāpaṁ na muñcāmo bhakṣyamāṇāś ca rākṣasaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tad ardyamānān rakṣobhir daṇḍakāraṇyavāsibhiḥ |
rakṣanas tvaṁ saha bhrātrā tvannāthā hi vayaṁ vane ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
mayā caitad vacaḥ śrutvā kārtsnyena paripālanam |
r̥ṣīṇāṁ daṇḍakāraṇye saṁśrutaṁ janakātmaje ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
saṁśrutya ca na śakṣyāmi jīvamānaḥ pratiśravam |
munīnām anyathā kartuṁ satyam iṣṭaṁ hi me sadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
apy ahaṁ jīvitaṁ jahyāṁ tvāṁ vā sīte salakṣmaṇām |
na tu pratijñāṁ saṁśrutya brāhmaṇebhyo viśeṣataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tad avaśyaṁ mayā kāryam r̥ṣīṇāṁ paripālanam |
anuktenāpi vaidehi pratijñāya tu kiṁ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
mama snehāc ca sauhārdād idam uktaṁ tvayā vacaḥ |
parituṣṭo ’smy ahaṁ sīte na hy aniṣṭo ’nuśiṣyate |
sadr̥śaṁ cānurūpaṁ ca kulasya tava śobhane ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
ity evam uktvā vacanaṁ mahātmā; sītāṁ priyāṁ maithila rājaputrīm |
rāmo dhanuṣmān sahalakṣmaṇena; jagāma ramyāṇi tapovanāni ||

=== meaning ===

---
