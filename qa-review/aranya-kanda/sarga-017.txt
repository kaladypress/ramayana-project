=== Sarga 17 ===

---
1.
tāṁ tu śūrpaṇakhāṁ rāmaḥ kāmapāśāvapāśitām |
svecchayā ślakṣṇayā vācā smitapūrvam athābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
kr̥tadāro ’smi bhavati bhāryeyaṁ dayitā mama |
tvadvidhānāṁ tu nārīṇāṁ suduḥkhā sasapatnatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
anujas tv eṣa me bhrātā śīlavān priyadarśanaḥ |
śrīmān akr̥tadāraś ca lakṣmaṇo nāma vīryavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
apūrvī bhāryayā cārthī taruṇaḥ priyadarśanaḥ |
anurūpaś ca te bhartā rūpasyāsya bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
enaṁ bhaja viśālākṣi bhartāraṁ bhrātaraṁ mama |
asapatnā varārohe merum arkaprabhā yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
iti rāmeṇa sā proktā rākṣasī kāmamohitā |
visr̥jya rāmaṁ sahasā tato lakṣmaṇam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
asya rūpasya te yuktā bhāryāhaṁ varavarṇinī |
mayā saha sukhaṁ sarvān daṇḍakān vicariṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
evam uktas tu saumitrī rākṣasyā vākyakovidaḥ |
tataḥ śūrpaṇakhīṁ smitvā lakṣmaṇo yuktam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
kathaṁ dāsasya me dāsī bhāryā bhavitum icchasi |
so ’ham āryeṇa paravān bhrātrā kamalavarṇini ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
samr̥ddhārthasya siddhārthā muditāmalavarṇinī |
āryasya tvaṁ viśālākṣi bhāryā bhava yavīyasī ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
etāṁ virūpām asatīṁ karālāṁ nirṇatodarīm |
bhāryāṁ vr̥ddhāṁ parityajya tvām evaiṣa bhajiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
ko hi rūpam idaṁ śreṣṭhaṁ saṁtyajya varavarṇini |
mānuṣeṣu varārohe kuryād bhāvaṁ vicakṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
iti sā lakṣmaṇenoktā karālā nirṇatodarī |
manyate tad vacaḥ satyaṁ parihāsāvicakṣaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sā rāmaṁ parṇaśālāyām upaviṣṭaṁ paraṁtapam |
sītayā saha durdharṣam abravīt kāmamohitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
imāṁ virūpām asatīṁ karālāṁ nirṇatodarīm |
vr̥ddhāṁ bhāryām avaṣṭabhya na māṁ tvaṁ bahu manyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
adyemāṁ bhakṣayiṣyāmi paśyatas tava mānuṣīm |
tvayā saha cariṣyāmi niḥsapatnā yathāsukham ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
ity uktvā mr̥gaśāvākṣīm alātasadr̥śekṣaṇā |
abhyadhāvat susaṁkruddhā maholkā rohiṇīm iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tāṁ mr̥tyupāśapratimām āpatantīṁ mahābalaḥ |
nigr̥hya rāmaḥ kupitas tato lakṣmaṇam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
krūrair anāryaiḥ saumitre parihāsaḥ kathaṁ cana |
na kāryaḥ paśya vaidehīṁ kathaṁ cit saumya jīvatīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
imāṁ virūpām asatīm atimattāṁ mahodarīm |
rākṣasīṁ puruṣavyāghra virūpayitum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
ity ukto lakṣmaṇas tasyāḥ kruddho rāmasya paśyataḥ |
uddhr̥tya khaḍgaṁ ciccheda karṇanāsaṁ mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
nikr̥ttakarṇanāsā tu visvaraṁ sā vinadya ca |
yathāgataṁ pradudrāva ghorā śūrpaṇakhā vanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
sā virūpā mahāghorā rākṣasī śoṇitokṣitā |
nanāda vividhān nādān yathā prāvr̥ṣi toyadaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
sā vikṣarantī rudhiraṁ bahudhā ghoradarśanā |
pragr̥hya bāhū garjantī praviveśa mahāvanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tatas tu sā rākṣasasaṁghasaṁvr̥taṁ; kharaṁ janasthānagataṁ virūpitā |
upetya taṁ bhrātaram ugratejasaṁ; papāta bhūmau gaganād yathāśaniḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tataḥ sabhāryaṁ bhayamohamūrchitā; salakṣmaṇaṁ rāghavam āgataṁ vanam |
virūpaṇaṁ cātmani śoṇitokṣitā; śaśaṁsa sarvaṁ bhaginī kharasya sā ||

=== meaning ===

---
