=== Sarga 38 ===

---
1.
mārīcena tu tad vākyaṁ kṣamaṁ yuktaṁ ca rāvaṇaḥ |
ukto na pratijagrāha martukāma ivauṣadham ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
taṁ pathyahitavaktāraṁ mārīcaṁ rākṣasādhipaḥ |
abravīt paruṣaṁ vākyam ayuktaṁ kālacoditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
yat kilaitad ayuktārthaṁ mārīca mayi kathyate |
vākyaṁ niṣphalam atyarthaṁ bījam uptam ivoṣare ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tvadvākyair na tu māṁ śakyaṁ bhettuṁ rāmasya saṁyuge |
pāpaśīlasya mūrkhasya mānuṣasya viśeṣataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
yas tyaktvā suhr̥do rājyaṁ mātaraṁ pitaraṁ tathā |
strīvākyaṁ prākr̥taṁ śrutvā vanam ekapade gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
avaśyaṁ tu mayā tasya saṁyuge kharaghātinaḥ |
prāṇaiḥ priyatarā sītā hartavyā tava saṁnidhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
evaṁ me niścitā buddhir hr̥di mārīca vartate |
na vyāvartayituṁ śakyā sendrair api surāsuraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
doṣaṁ guṇaṁ vā saṁpr̥ṣṭas tvam evaṁ vaktum arhasi |
apāyaṁ vāpy upāyaṁ vā kāryasyāsya viniścaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
saṁpr̥ṣṭena tu vaktavyaṁ sacivena vipaścitā |
udyatāñjalinā rājño ya icched bhūtim ātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
vākyam apratikūlaṁ tu mr̥dupūrvaṁ śubhaṁ hitam |
upacāreṇa yuktaṁ ca vaktavyo vasudhādhipaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sāvamardaṁ tu yad vākyaṁ mārīca hitam ucyate |
nābhinandati tad rājā mānārho mānavarjitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
pañcarūpāṇi rājāno dhārayanty amitaujasaḥ |
agner indrasya somasya yamasya varuṇasya ca |
auṣṇyaṁ tathā vikramaṁ ca saumyaṁ daṇḍaṁ prasannatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tasmāt sarvāsv avasthāsu mānyāḥ pūjyāś ca pārthivāḥ |
tvaṁ tu dharmam avijñāya kevalaṁ moham āsthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
abhyāgataṁ māṁ daurātmyāt paruṣaṁ vadasīdr̥śam |
guṇadoṣau na pr̥cchāmi kṣamaṁ cātmani rākṣasa |
asmiṁs tu sa bhavān kr̥tye sāhāyyaṁ kartum arhati ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sauvarṇas tvaṁ mr̥go bhūtvā citro rajatabindubhiḥ |
pralobhayitvā vaidehīṁ yatheṣṭaṁ gantum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tvāṁ tu māyāmr̥gaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā kāñcanaṁ jātavismayā |
ānayainam iti kṣipraṁ rāmaṁ vakṣyati maithilī ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
apakrānte ca kākutsthe lakṣmaṇe ca yathāsukham |
ānayiṣyāmi vaidehīṁ sahasrākṣaḥ śacīm iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
evaṁ kr̥tvā tv idaṁ kāryaṁ yatheṣṭaṁ gaccha rākṣasa |
rājyasyārdhaṁ pradāsyāmi mārīca tava suvrata ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
gaccha saumya śivaṁ mārgaṁ kāryasyāsya vivr̥ddhaye |
prāpya sītām ayuddhena vañcayitvā tu rāghavam |
laṅkāṁ prati gamiṣyāmi kr̥takāryaḥ saha tvayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
etat kāryam avaśyaṁ me balād api kariṣyasi |
rājño hi pratikūlastho na jātu sukham edhate ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
āsādya taṁ jīvitasaṁśayas te; mr̥tyur dhruvo hy adya mayā virudhya |
etad yathāvat parigr̥hya buddhyā; yad atra pathyaṁ kuru tat tathā tvam ||

=== meaning ===

---
