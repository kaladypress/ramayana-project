=== Sarga 60 ===

---
1.
sa dīno dīnayā vācā lakṣmaṇaṁ vākyam abravīt |
śīghraṁ lakṣmaṇa jānīhi gatvā godāvarīṁ nadīm |
api godāvarīṁ sītā padmāny ānayituṁ gatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
evam uktas tu rāmeṇa lakṣmaṇaḥ punar eva hi |
nadīṁ godāvarīṁ ramyāṁ jagāma laghuvikramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tāṁ lakṣmaṇas tīrthavatīṁ vicitvā rāmam abravīt |
naināṁ paśyāmi tīrtheṣu krośato na śr̥ṇoti me ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
kaṁ nu sā deśam āpannā vaidehī kleśanāśinī |
na hi taṁ vedmi vai rāma yatra sā tanumadhyamā ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
lakṣmaṇasya vacaḥ śrutvā dīnaḥ saṁtāpa mohitaḥ |
rāmaḥ samabhicakrāma svayaṁ godāvarīṁ nadīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
sa tām upasthito rāmaḥ kva sītety evam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
bhūtāni rākṣasendreṇa vadhārheṇa hr̥tām api |
na tāṁ śaśaṁsū rāmāya tathā godāvarī nadī ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tataḥ pracoditā bhūtaiḥ śaṁsāsmai tāṁ priyām iti |
na ca sābhyavadat sītāṁ pr̥ṣṭā rāmeṇa śocitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
rāvaṇasya ca tad rūpaṁ karmāṇi ca durātmanaḥ |
dhyātvā bhayāt tu vaidehīṁ sā nadī na śaśaṁsa tām ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
nirāśas tu tayā nadyā sītāyā darśane kr̥taḥ |
uvāca rāmaḥ saumitriṁ sītādarśanakarśitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
kiṁ nu lakṣmaṇa vakṣyāmi sametya janakaṁ vacaḥ |
mātaraṁ caiva vaidehyā vinā tām aham apriyam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
yā me rājyavihīnasya vane vanyena jīvataḥ |
sarvaṁ vyapanayac chokaṁ vaidehī kva nu sā gatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
jñātipakṣavihīnasya rājaputrīm apaśyataḥ |
manye dīrghā bhaviṣyanti rātrayo mama jāgrataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
godāvarīṁ janasthānam imaṁ prasravaṇaṁ girim |
sarvāṇy anucariṣyāmi yadi sītā hi dr̥śyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
evaṁ saṁbhāṣamāṇau tāv anyonyaṁ bhrātarāv ubhau |
vasuṁdharāyāṁ patitaṁ puṣpamārgam apaśyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tāṁ puṣpavr̥ṣṭiṁ patitāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rāmo mahītale |
uvāca lakṣmaṇaṁ vīro duḥkhito duḥkhitaṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
abhijānāmi puṣpāṇi tānīmāmīha lakṣmaṇa |
apinaddhāni vaidehyā mayā dattāni kānane ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
evam uktvā mahābāhur lakṣmaṇaṁ puruṣarṣabham |
kruddho ’bravīd giriṁ tatra siṁhaḥ kṣudramr̥gaṁ yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tāṁ hemavarṇāṁ hemābhāṁ sītāṁ darśaya parvata |
yāvat sānūni sarvāṇi na te vidhvaṁsayāmy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
mama bāṇāgninirdagdho bhasmībhūto bhaviṣyasi |
asevyaḥ satataṁ caiva nistr̥ṇadrumapallavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
imāṁ vā saritaṁ cādya śoṣayiṣyāmi lakṣmaṇa |
yadi nākhyāti me sītām adya candranibhānanām ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
evaṁ sa ruṣito rāmo didhakṣann iva cakṣuṣā |
dadarśa bhūmau niṣkrāntaṁ rākṣasasya padaṁ mahat ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
sa samīkṣya parikrāntaṁ sītāyā rākṣasasya ca |
saṁbhrāntahr̥dayo rāmaḥ śaśaṁsa bhrātaraṁ priyam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
paśya lakṣmaṇa vaidehyāḥ śīrṇāḥ kanakabindavaḥ |
bhūṣaṇānāṁ hi saumitre mālyāni vividhāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
taptabindunikāśaiś ca citraiḥ kṣatajabindubhiḥ |
āvr̥taṁ paśya saumitre sarvato dharaṇītalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
manye lakṣmaṇa vaidehī rākṣasaiḥ kāmarūpibhiḥ |
bhittvā bhittvā vibhaktā vā bhakṣitā vā bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tasya nimittaṁ vaidehyā dvayor vivadamānayoḥ |
babhūva yuddhaṁ saumitre ghoraṁ rākṣasayor iha ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
muktāmaṇicitaṁ cedaṁ tapanīyavibhūṣitam |
dharaṇyāṁ patitaṁ saumya kasya bhagnaṁ mahad dhanuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
taruṇādityasaṁkāśaṁ vaidūryagulikācitam |
viśīrṇaṁ patitaṁ bhūmau kavacaṁ kasya kāñcanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
chatraṁ śataśalākaṁ ca divyamālyopaśobhitam |
bhagnadaṇḍam idaṁ kasya bhūmau saumya nipātitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
kāñcanoraśchadāś ceme piśācavadanāḥ kharāḥ |
bhīmarūpā mahākāyāḥ kasya vā nihatā raṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
dīptapāvakasaṁkāśo dyutimān samaradhvajaḥ |
apaviddhaś ca bhagnaś ca kasya sāṁgrāmiko rathaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
rathākṣamātrā viśikhās tapanīyavibhūṣaṇāḥ |
kasyeme ’bhihatā bāṇāḥ prakīrṇā ghorakarmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
vairaṁ śataguṇaṁ paśya mamedaṁ jīvitāntakam |
sughorahr̥dayaiḥ saumya rākṣasaiḥ kāmarūpibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
hr̥tā mr̥tā vā sītā hi bhakṣitā vā tapasvinī |
na dharmas trāyate sītāṁ hriyamāṇāṁ mahāvane ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
bhakṣitāyāṁ hi vaidehyāṁ hr̥tāyām api lakṣmaṇa |
ke hi loke priyaṁ kartuṁ śaktāḥ saumya mameśvarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
kartāram api lokānāṁ śūraṁ karuṇavedinam |
ajñānād avamanyeran sarvabhūtāni lakṣmaṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
mr̥duṁ lokahite yuktaṁ dāntaṁ karuṇavedinam |
nirvīrya iti manyante nūnaṁ māṁ tridaśeśvarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
māṁ prāpya hi guṇo doṣaḥ saṁvr̥ttaḥ paśya lakṣmaṇa |
adyaiva sarvabhūtānāṁ rakṣasām abhavāya ca |
saṁhr̥tyaiva śaśijyotsnāṁ mahān sūrya ivoditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
naiva yakṣā na gandharvā na piśācā na rākṣasāḥ |
kiṁnarā vā manuṣyā vā sukhaṁ prāpsyanti lakṣmaṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
mamāstrabāṇasaṁpūrṇam ākāśaṁ paśya lakṣmaṇa |
niḥsaṁpātaṁ kariṣyāmi hy adya trailokyacāriṇām ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
saṁniruddhagrahagaṇam āvāritaniśākaram |
vipranaṣṭānalamarudbhāskaradyutisaṁvr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
vinirmathitaśailāgraṁ śuṣyamāṇajalāśayam |
dhvastadrumalatāgulmaṁ vipraṇāśitasāgaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
na tāṁ kuśalinīṁ sītāṁ pradāsyanti mameśvarāḥ |
asmin muhūrte saumitre mama drakṣyanti vikramam ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
nākāśam utpatiṣyanti sarvabhūtāni lakṣmaṇa |
mama cāpaguṇān muktair bāṇajālair nirantaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
arditaṁ mama nārācair dhvastabhrāntamr̥gadvijam |
samākulam amaryādaṁ jagat paśyādya lakṣmaṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
ākarṇapūrṇair iṣubhir jīvalokaṁ durāvaraiḥ |
kariṣye maithilīhetor apiśācam arākṣasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
mama roṣaprayuktānāṁ sāyakānāṁ balaṁ surāḥ |
drakṣyanty adya vimuktānām amarṣād dūragāminām ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
naiva devā na daiteyā na piśācā na rākṣasāḥ |
bhaviṣyanti mama krodhāt trailokye vipraṇāśite ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
devadānavayakṣāṇāṁ lokā ye rakṣasām api |
bahudhā nipatiṣyanti bāṇaughaiḥ śakulīkr̥tāḥ |
nirmaryādān imām̐l lokān kariṣyāmy adya sāyakaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
yathā jarā yathā mr̥tyur yathākālo yathāvidhiḥ |
nityaṁ na pratihanyante sarvabhūteṣu lakṣmaṇa |
tathāhaṁ krodhasaṁyukto na nivāryo ’smy asaṁśayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
52.
pureva me cārudatīm aninditāṁ; diśanti sītāṁ yadi nādya maithilīm |
sadevagandharvamanuṣya pannagaṁ; jagat saśailaṁ parivartayāmy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
