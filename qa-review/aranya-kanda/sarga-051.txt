=== Sarga 51 ===

---
1.
kham utpatantaṁ taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā maithilī janakātmajā |
duḥkhitā paramodvignā bhaye mahati vartinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
roṣarodanatāmrākṣī bhīmākṣaṁ rākṣasādhipam |
rudatī karuṇaṁ sītā hriyamāṇedam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
na vyapatrapase nīca karmaṇānena rāvaṇa |
jñātvā virahitāṁ yo māṁ corayitvā palāyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tvayaiva nūnaṁ duṣṭātman bhīruṇā hartum icchatā |
mamāpavāhito bhartā mr̥garūpeṇa māyayā |
yo hi mām udyatas trātuṁ so ’py ayaṁ vinipātitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
paramaṁ khalu te vīryaṁ dr̥śyate rākṣasādhama |
viśrāvya nāmadheyaṁ hi yuddhe nāsti jitā tvayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
īdr̥śaṁ garhitaṁ karma kathaṁ kr̥tvā na lajjase |
striyāś ca haraṇaṁ nīca rahite ca parasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
kathayiṣyanti lokeṣu puruṣāḥ karma kutsitam |
sunr̥śaṁsam adharmiṣṭhaṁ tava śauṇḍīryamāninaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
dhik te śauryaṁ ca sattvaṁ ca yat tvayā kathitaṁ tadā |
kulākrośakaraṁ loke dhik te cāritram īdr̥śam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
kiṁ śakyaṁ kartum evaṁ hi yaj javenaiva dhāvasi |
muhūrtam api tiṣṭhasva na jīvan pratiyāsyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
na hi cakṣuḥpathaṁ prāpya tayoḥ pārthivaputrayoḥ |
sasainyo ’pi samartaḥs tvaṁ muhūrtam api jīvitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
na tvaṁ tayoḥ śarasparśaṁ soḍhuṁ śaktaḥ kathaṁ cana |
vane prajvalitasyeva sparśam agner vihaṁgamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sādhu kr̥tvātmanaḥ pathyaṁ sādhu māṁ muñca rāvaṇa |
matpradharṣaṇaruṣṭo hi bhrātrā saha patir mama |
vidhāsyati vināśāya tvaṁ māṁ yadi na muñcasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
yena tvaṁ vyavasāyena balān māṁ hartum icchasi |
vyavasāyaḥ sa te nīca bhaviṣyati nirarthakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
na hy ahaṁ tam apaśyantī bhartāraṁ vibudhopamam |
utsahe śatruvaśagā prāṇān dhārayituṁ ciram ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
na nūnaṁ cātmanaḥ śreyaḥ pathyaṁ vā samavekṣase |
mr̥tyukāle yathā martyo viparītāni sevate ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
mumūrṣūṇāṁ hi sarveṣāṁ yat pathyaṁ tan na rocate |
paśyāmīva hi kaṇṭhe tvāṁ kālapāśāvapāśitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
yathā cāsmin bhayasthāne na bibheṣe daśānana |
vyaktaṁ hiraṇmayān hi tvaṁ saṁpaśyasi mahīruhān ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
nadīṁ vairataṇīṁ ghorāṁ rudhiraughanivāhinīm |
khaḍgapatravanaṁ caiva bhīmaṁ paśyasi rāvaṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
taptakāñcanapuṣpāṁ ca vaidūryapravaracchadām |
drakṣyase śālmalīṁ tīkṣṇām āyasaiḥ kaṇṭakaiś citām ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
na hi tvam īdr̥śaṁ kr̥tvā tasyālīkaṁ mahātmanaḥ |
dhārituṁ śakṣyasi ciraṁ viṣaṁ pītveva nirghr̥ṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
baddhas tvaṁ kālapāśena durnivāreṇa rāvaṇa |
kva gato lapsyase śarma bhartur mama mahātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
nimeṣāntaramātreṇa vinā bhrātaram āhave |
rākṣasā nihatā yena sahasrāṇi caturdaśa ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
sa kathaṁ rāghavo vīraḥ sarvāstrakuśalo balī |
na tvāṁ hanyāc charais tīkṣṇair iṣṭabhāryāpahāriṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
etac cānyac ca paruṣaṁ vaidehī rāvaṇāṅkagā |
bhayaśokasamāviṣṭā karuṇaṁ vilalāpa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tathā bhr̥śārtāṁ bahu caiva bhāṣiṇīṁ; vilalāpa pūrvaṁ karuṇaṁ ca bhāminīm |
jahāra pāpas taruṇīṁ viveṣṭatīṁ; nr̥pātmajām āgatagātravepathum ||

=== meaning ===

---
