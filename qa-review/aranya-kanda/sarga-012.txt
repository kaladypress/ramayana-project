=== Sarga 12 ===

---
1.
rāma prīto ’smi bhadraṁ te parituṣṭo ’smi lakṣmaṇa |
abhivādayituṁ yan māṁ prāptau sthaḥ saha sītayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
adhvaśrameṇa vāṁ khedo bādhate pracuraśramaḥ |
vyaktam utkaṇṭhate cāpi maithilī janakātmajā ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
eṣā hi sukumārī ca duḥkhaiś ca na vimānitā |
prājyadoṣaṁ vanaṁ praptā bhartr̥snehapracoditā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
yathaiṣā ramate rāma iha sītā tathā kuru |
duṣkaraṁ kr̥tavaty eṣā vane tvām anugacchatī ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
eṣā hi prakr̥tiḥ strīṇām āsr̥ṣṭe raghunandana |
samastham anurajyante viṣamasthaṁ tyajanti ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
śatahradānāṁ lolatvaṁ śastrāṇāṁ tīkṣṇatāṁ tathā |
garuḍānilayoḥ śaighryam anugacchanti yoṣitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
iyaṁ tu bhavato bhāryā doṣair etair vivarjitāḥ |
ślāghyā ca vyapadeśyā ca yathā devī hy arundhatī ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
alaṁkr̥to ’yaṁ deśaś ca yatra saumitriṇā saha |
vaidehyā cānayā rāma vatsyasi tvam ariṁdama ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
evam uktas tu muninā rāghavaḥ saṁyatāñjaliḥ |
uvāca praśritaṁ vākyam r̥ṣiṁ dīptam ivānalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
dhanyo ’smy anugr̥hīto ’smi yasya me munipuṁgavaḥ |
guṇaiḥ sabhrātr̥bhāryasya varadaḥ parituṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
kiṁ tu vyādiśa me deśaṁ sodakaṁ bahukānanam |
yatrāśramapadaṁ kr̥tvā vaseyaṁ nirataḥ sukham ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tato ’bravīn muni śreṣṭhaḥ śrutvā rāmasya bhāṣitam |
dhyātvā muhūrtaṁ dharmātmā dhīro dhīrataraṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
ito dviyojane tāta bahumūlaphalodakaḥ |
deśo bahumr̥gaḥ śrīmān pañcavaṭy abhiviśrutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tatra gatvāśramapadaṁ kr̥tvā saumitriṇā saha |
ramasva tvaṁ pitur vākyaṁ yathoktam anupālayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
vidito hy eṣa vr̥ttānto mama sarvas tavānagha |
tapasaś ca prabhāvena snehād daśarathasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
hr̥dayasthaś ca te chando vijñātas tapasā mayā |
iha vāsaṁ pratijñāya mayā saha tapovane ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
ataś ca tvām ahaṁ brūmi gaccha pañcavaṭīm iti |
sa hi ramyo vanoddeśo maithilī tatra raṁsyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
sa deśaḥ ślāghanīyaś ca nātidūre ca rāghava |
godāvaryāḥ samīpe ca maithilī tatra raṁsyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
prājyamūlaphalaiś caiva nānādvija gaṇair yutaḥ |
viviktaś ca mahābāho puṇyo ramyas tathaiva ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
bhavān api sadāraś ca śaktaś ca parirakṣaṇe |
api cātra vasan rāmas tāpasān pālayiṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
etad ālakṣyate vīra madhukānāṁ mahad vanam |
uttareṇāsya gantavyaṁ nyagrodham abhigacchatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tataḥ sthalam upāruhya parvatasyāvidūrataḥ |
khyātaḥ pañcavaṭīty eva nityapuṣpitakānanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
agastyenaivam uktas tu rāmaḥ saumitriṇā saha |
sātkr̥tyāmantrayām āsa tam r̥ṣiṁ satyavādinam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tau tu tenābhyanujñātau kr̥tapādābhivandanau |
tadāśramāt pañcavaṭīṁ jagmatuḥ saha sītayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
gr̥hītacāpau tu narādhipātmajau; viṣaktatūṇī samareṣv akātarau |
yathopadiṣṭena pathā maharṣiṇā; prajagmatuḥ pañcavaṭīṁ samāhitau ||

=== meaning ===

---
