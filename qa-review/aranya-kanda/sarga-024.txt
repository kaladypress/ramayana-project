=== Sarga 24 ===

---
1.
avaṣṭabdhadhanuṁ rāmaṁ kruddhaṁ ca ripughātinam |
dadarśāśramam āgamya kharaḥ saha puraḥsaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā saguṇaṁ cāpam udyamya kharaniḥsvanam |
rāmasyābhimukhaṁ sūtaṁ codyatām ity acodayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sa kharasyājñayā sūtas turagān samacodayat |
yatra rāmo mahābāhur eko dhunvan dhanuḥ sthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
taṁ tu niṣpatitaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā sarve te rajanīcarāḥ |
nardamānā mahānādaṁ sacivāḥ paryavārayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
sa teṣāṁ yātudhānānāṁ madhye rato gataḥ kharaḥ |
babhūva madhye tārāṇāṁ lohitāṅga ivoditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tatas taṁ bhīmadhanvānaṁ kruddhāḥ sarve niśācarāḥ |
rāmaṁ nānāvidhaiḥ śastrair abhyavarṣanta durjayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
mudgarair āyasaiḥ śūlaiḥ prāsaiḥ khaḍgaiḥ paraśvadhaiḥ |
rākṣasāḥ samare rāmaṁ nijaghnū roṣatatparāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
te balāhakasaṁkāśā mahānādā mahābalāḥ |
abhyadhāvanta kākutsthaṁ rāmaṁ yuddhe jighāṁsavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
te rāme śaravarṣāṇi vyasr̥jan rakṣasāṁ guṇāḥ |
śailendram iva dhārābhir varṣamāṇā mahādhanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
sa taiḥ parivr̥to ghorai rāghavo rakṣasāṁ gaṇaiḥ |
tithiṣv iva mahādevo vr̥taḥ pāriṣadāṁ gaṇaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tāni muktāni śastrāṇi yātudhānaiḥ sa rāghavaḥ |
pratijagrāha viśikhair nadyoghān iva sāgaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sa taiḥ praharaṇair ghorair bhinnagātro na vivyathe |
rāmaḥ pradīptair bahubhir vajrair iva mahācalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sa viddhaḥ kṣatajādigdhaḥ sarvagātreṣu rāghavaḥ |
babhūva rāmaḥ saṁdhyābhrair divākara ivāvr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
viṣedur devagandharvāḥ siddhāś ca paramarṣayaḥ |
ekaṁ sahastrair bahubhis tadā dr̥ṣṭvā samāvr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tato rāmaḥ susaṁkruddho maṇḍalīkr̥takārmukaḥ |
sasarja niśitān bāṇāñ śataśo ’tha sahasraśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
durāvārān durviṣahān kālapāśopamān raṇe |
mumoca līlayā rāmaḥ kaṅkapatrān ajihmagān ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
te śarāḥ śatrusainyeṣu muktā rāmeṇa līlayā |
ādadū rakṣasāṁ prāṇān pāśāḥ kālakr̥tā iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
bhittvā rākṣasadehāṁs tāṁs te śarā rudhirāplutāḥ |
antarikṣagatā rejur dīptāgnisamatejasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
asaṁkhyeyās tu rāmasya sāyakāś cāpamaṇḍalāt |
viniṣpetur atīvogrā rakṣaḥ prāṇāpahāriṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tair dhanūṁṣi dhvajāgrāṇi varmāṇi ca śirāṁsi ca |
bahūn sahastābharaṇān ūrūn karikaropamān ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tato nālīkanārācais tīkṣṇāgraiś ca vikarṇibhiḥ |
bhīmam ārtasvaraṁ cakrur bhidyamānā niśācarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tat sainyaṁ niśitair bāṇair arditaṁ marmabhedibhiḥ |
rāmeṇa na sukhaṁ lebhe śuṣkaṁ vanam ivāgninā ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
ke cid bhīmabalāḥ śūrāḥ śūlān khaḍgān paraśvadhān |
cikṣipuḥ paramakruddhā rāmāya rajanīcarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tāni bāṇair mahābāhuḥ śastrāṇy āvārya rāghavaḥ |
jahāra samare prāṇāṁś ciccheda ca śirodharān ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
avaśiṣṭāś ca ye tatra viṣaṇṇāś ca niśācarāḥ |
kharam evābhyadhāvanta śaraṇārthaṁ śarārditāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tān sarvān punar ādāya samāśvāsya ca dūṣaṇaḥ |
abhyadhāvata kākutsthaṁ kruddho rudram ivāntakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
nivr̥ttās tu punaḥ sarve dūṣaṇāśrayanirbhayāḥ |
rāmam evābhyadhāvanta sālatālaśilāyudhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tad babhūvādbhutaṁ yuddhaṁ tumulaṁ romaharṣaṇam |
rāmasyāsya mahāghoraṁ punas teṣāṁ ca rakṣasām ||

=== meaning ===

---
