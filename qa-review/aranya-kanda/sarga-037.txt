=== Sarga 37 ===

---
1.
evam asmi tadā muktaḥ kathaṁ cit tena saṁyuge |
idānīm api yad vr̥ttaṁ tac chr̥ṇuṣva yad uttaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
rākṣasābhyām ahaṁ dvābhyām anirviṇṇas tathā kr̥taḥ |
sahito mr̥garūpābhyāṁ praviṣṭo daṇḍakāvanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
dīptajihvo mahākāyas tīkṣṇaśr̥ṇgo mahābalaḥ |
vyacaran daṇḍakāraṇyaṁ māṁsabhakṣo mahāmr̥gaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
agnihotreṣu tīrtheṣu caityavr̥kṣeṣu rāvaṇa |
atyantaghoro vyacaraṁs tāpasāṁs tān pradharṣayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
nihatya daṇḍakāraṇye tāpasān dharmacāriṇaḥ |
rudhirāṇi pibaṁs teṣāṁ tathā māṁsāni bhakṣayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
r̥ṣimāṁsāśanaḥ krūras trāsayan vanagocarān |
tadā rudhiramatto ’haṁ vyacaraṁ daṇḍakāvanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tadāhaṁ daṇḍakāraṇye vicaran dharmadūṣakaḥ |
āsādayaṁ tadā rāmaṁ tāpasaṁ dharmam āśritam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
vaidehīṁ ca mahābhāgāṁ lakṣmaṇaṁ ca mahāratham |
tāpasaṁ niyatāhāraṁ sarvabhūtahite ratam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
so ’haṁ vanagataṁ rāmaṁ paribhūya mahābalam |
tāpaso ’yam iti jñātvā pūrvavairam anusmaran ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
abhyadhāvaṁ susaṁkruddhas tīkṣṇaśr̥ṅgo mr̥gākr̥tiḥ |
jighāṁsur akr̥taprajñas taṁ prahāram anusmaran ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tena muktās trayo bāṇāḥ śitāḥ śatrunibarhaṇāḥ |
vikr̥ṣya balavac cāpaṁ suparṇānilatulyagāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
te bāṇā vajrasaṁkāśāḥ sughorā raktabhojanāḥ |
ājagmuḥ sahitāḥ sarve trayaḥ saṁnataparvaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
parākramajño rāmasya śaṭho dr̥ṣṭabhayaḥ purā |
samutkrāntas tato muktas tāv ubhau rākṣasau hatau ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
śareṇa mukto rāmasya kathaṁ cit prāpya jīvitam |
iha pravrājito yuktas tāpaso ’haṁ samāhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
vr̥kṣe vr̥kṣe hi paśyāmi cīrakr̥ṣṇājināmbaram |
gr̥hītadhanuṣaṁ rāmaṁ pāśahastam ivāntakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
api rāmasahasrāṇi bhītaḥ paśyāmi rāvaṇa |
rāmabhūtam idaṁ sarvam araṇyaṁ pratibhāti me ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
rāmam eva hi paśyāmi rahite rākṣaseśvara |
dr̥ṣṭvā svapnagataṁ rāmam udbhramāmi vicetanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
rakārādīni nāmāni rāmatrastasya rāvaṇa |
ratnāni ca rathāś caiva trāsaṁ saṁjanayanti me ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
ahaṁ tasya prabhāvajño na yuddhaṁ tena te kṣamam |
raṇe rāmeṇa yudhyasva kṣamāṁ vā kuru rākṣasa |
na te rāmakathā kāryā yadi māṁ draṣṭum icchasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
idaṁ vaco bandhuhitārthinā mayā; yathocyamānaṁ yadi nābhipatsyase |
sabāndhavas tyakṣyasi jīvitaṁ raṇe; hato ’dya rāmeṇa śarair ajihmagaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
