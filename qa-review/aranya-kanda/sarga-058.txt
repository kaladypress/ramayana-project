=== Sarga 58 ===

---
1.
bhr̥śam āvrajamānasya tasyādhovāmalocanam |
prāsphurac cāskhalad rāmo vepathuś cāsya jāyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
upālakṣya nimittāni so ’śubhāni muhur muhuḥ |
api kṣemaṁ tu sītāyā iti vai vyājahāra ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tvaramāṇo jagāmātha sītādarśanalālasaḥ |
śūnyam āvasathaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā babhūvodvignamānasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
udbhramann iva vegena vikṣipan raghunandanaḥ |
tatra tatroṭajasthānam abhivīkṣya samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
dadarśa parṇaśālāṁ ca rahitāṁ sītayā tadā |
śriyā virahitāṁ dhvastāṁ hemante padminīm iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
rudantam iva vr̥kṣaiś ca mlānapuṣpamr̥gadvijam |
śriyā vihīnaṁ vidhvastaṁ saṁtyaktavanadaivatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
viprakīrṇājinakuśaṁ vipraviddhabr̥sīkaṭam |
dr̥ṣṭvā śūnyoṭajasthānaṁ vilalāpa punaḥ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
hr̥tā mr̥tā vā naṣṭā vā bhakṣitā vā bhaviṣyati |
nilīnāpy atha vā bhīrur atha vā vanam āśritā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
gatā vicetuṁ puṣpāṇi phalāny api ca vā punaḥ |
atha vā padminīṁ yātā jalārthaṁ vā nadīṁ gatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
yatnān mr̥gayamāṇas tu nāsasāda vane priyām |
śokaraktekṣaṇaḥ śokād unmatta iva lakṣyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
vr̥kṣād vr̥kṣaṁ pradhāvan sa girīṁś cāpi nadīn nadīm |
babhūva vilapan rāmaḥ śokapaṅkārṇavaplutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
asti kaccit tvayā dr̥ṣṭā sā kadambapriyā priyā |
kadamba yadi jānīṣe śaṁsa sītāṁ śubhānanām ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
snigdhapallavasaṁkāśāṁ pītakauśeyavāsinīm |
śaṁsasva yadi vā dr̥ṣṭā bilva bilvopamastanī ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
atha vārjuna śaṁsa tvaṁ priyāṁ tām arjunapriyām |
janakasya sutā bhīrur yadi jīvati vā na vā ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
kakubhaḥ kakubhoruṁ tāṁ vyaktaṁ jānāti maithilīm |
latāpallavapuṣpāḍhyo bhāti hy eṣa vanaspatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
bhramarair upagītaś ca yathā drumavaro hy ayam |
eṣa vyaktaṁ vijānāti tilakas tilakapriyām ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
aśokaśokāpanuda śokopahatacetasaṁ |
tvannāmānaṁ kuru kṣipraṁ priyāsaṁdarśanena mām ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
yadi tāla tvayā dr̥ṣṭā pakvatālaphalastanī |
kathayasva varārohāṁ kāruṣyaṁ yadi te mayi ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
yadi dr̥ṣṭā tvayā sītā jambujāmbūnadaprabhā |
priyāṁ yadi vijānīṣe niḥśaṅkaṁ kathayasva me ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
atha vā mr̥gaśāvākṣīṁ mr̥ga jānāsi maithilīm |
mr̥gaviprekṣaṇī kāntā mr̥gībhiḥ sahitā bhavet ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
gaja sā gajanāsorur yadi dr̥ṣṭā tvayā bhavet |
tāṁ manye viditāṁ tubhyam ākhyāhi varavāraṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
śārdūla yadi sā dr̥ṣṭā priyā candranibhānanā |
maithilī mama visrabdhaḥ kathayasva na te bhayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
kiṁ dhāvasi priye nūnaṁ dr̥ṣṭāsi kamalekṣaṇe |
vr̥kṣeṇācchādya cātmānaṁ kiṁ māṁ na pratibhāṣase ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tiṣṭha tiṣṭha varārohe na te ’sti karuṇā mayi |
nātyarthaṁ hāsyaśīlāsi kimarthaṁ mām upekṣase ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
pītakauśeyakenāsi sūcitā varavarṇini |
dhāvanty api mayā dr̥ṣṭā tiṣṭha yady asti sauhr̥dam ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
naiva sā nūnam atha vā hiṁsitā cāruhāsinī |
kr̥cchraṁ prāptaṁ hi māṁ nūnaṁ yathopekṣitum arhati ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
vyaktaṁ sā bhakṣitā bālā rākṣasaiḥ piśitāśanaiḥ |
vibhajyāṅgāni sarvāṇi mayā virahitā priyā ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
nūnaṁ tac chubhadantauṣṭhaṁ mukhaṁ niṣprabhatāṁ gatam |
sā hi campakavarṇābhā grīvā graiveya śobhitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
komalā vilapantyās tu kāntāyā bhakṣitā śubhā |
nūnaṁ vikṣipyamāṇau tau bāhū pallavakomalau ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
bhakṣitau vepamānāgrau sahastābharaṇāṅgadau |
mayā virahitā bālā rakṣasāṁ bhakṣaṇāya vai ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
sārtheneva parityaktā bhakṣitā bahubāndhavā |
hā lakṣmaṇa mahābāho paśyasi tvaṁ priyāṁ kva cit ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
hā priye kva gatā bhadre hā sīteti punaḥ punaḥ |
ity evaṁ vilapan rāmaḥ paridhāvan vanād vanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
kva cid udbhramate vegāt kva cid vibhramate balāt |
kva cin matta ivābhāti kāntān veṣaṇatatparaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
sa vanāni nadīḥ śailān giriprasravaṇāni ca |
kānanāni ca vegena bhramaty aparisaṁsthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
tathā sa gatvā vipulaṁ mahad vanaṁ; parītya sarvaṁ tv atha maithilīṁ prati |
aniṣṭhitāśaḥ sa cakāra mārgaṇe; punaḥ priyāyāḥ paramaṁ pariśramam ||

=== meaning ===

---
