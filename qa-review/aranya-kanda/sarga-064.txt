=== Sarga 64 ===

---
1.
rāmaḥ prekṣya tu taṁ gr̥dhraṁ bhuvi raudreṇa pātitam |
saumitriṁ mitrasaṁpannam idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
mamāyaṁ nūnam artheṣu yatamāno vihaṁgamaḥ |
rākṣasena hataḥ saṁkhye prāṇāṁs tyajati dustyajān ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
ayam asya śarīre ’smin prāṇo lakṣmaṇa vidyate |
tathā svaravihīno ’yaṁ viklavaṁ samudīkṣate ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
jaṭāyo yadi śaknoṣi vākyaṁ vyāharituṁ punaḥ |
sītām ākhyāhi bhadraṁ te vadham ākhyāhi cātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
kiṁnimitto ’harat sītāṁ rāvaṇas tasya kiṁ mayā |
aparāddhaṁ tu yaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rāvaṇena hr̥tā priyā ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
kathaṁ tac candrasaṁkāśaṁ mukham āsīn manoharam |
sītayā kāni coktāni tasmin kāle dvijottama ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
kathaṁvīryaḥ kathaṁrūpaḥ kiṁkarmā sa ca rākṣasaḥ |
kva cāsya bhavanaṁ tāta brūhi me paripr̥cchataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tam udvīkṣyātha dīnātmā vilapantam anantaram |
vācātisannayā rāmaṁ jaṭāyur idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sā hr̥tā rākṣasendreṇa rāvaṇena vihāyasā |
māyām āsthāya vipulāṁ vātadurdinasaṁkulām ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
pariśrāntasya me tāta pakṣau chittvā niśācaraḥ |
sītām ādāya vaidehīṁ prayāto dakṣiṇā mukhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
uparudhyanti me prāṇā dr̥ṣṭir bhramati rāghava |
paśyāmi vr̥kṣān sauvarṇān uśīrakr̥tamūrdhajān ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
yena yāti muhūrtena sītām ādāya rāvaṇaḥ |
vipranaṣṭaṁ dhanaṁ kṣipraṁ tat svāmipratipadyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
vindo nāma muhūrto ’sau sa ca kākutstha nābudhat |
jhaṣavad baḍiśaṁ gr̥hya kṣipram eva vinaśyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
na ca tvayā vyathā kāryā janakasya sutāṁ prati |
vaidehyā raṁsyase kṣipraṁ hatvā taṁ rākṣasaṁ raṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
asaṁmūḍhasya gr̥dhrasya rāmaṁ pratyanubhāṣataḥ |
āsyāt susrāva rudhiraṁ mriyamāṇasya sāmiṣam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
putro viśravasaḥ sākṣād bhrātā vaiśravaṇasya ca |
ity uktvā durlabhān prāṇān mumoca patageśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
brūhi brūhīti rāmasya bruvāṇasya kr̥tāñjaleḥ |
tyaktvā śarīraṁ gr̥dhrasya jagmuḥ prāṇā vihāyasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
sa nikṣipya śiro bhūmau prasārya caraṇau tadā |
vikṣipya ca śarīraṁ svaṁ papāta dharaṇītale ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
taṁ gr̥dhraṁ prekṣya tāmrākṣaṁ gatāsum acalopamam |
rāmaḥ subahubhir duḥkhair dīnaḥ saumitrim abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
bahūni rakṣasāṁ vāse varṣāṇi vasatā sukham |
anena daṇḍakāraṇye vicīrṇam iha pakṣiṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
anekavārṣiko yas tu cirakālaṁ samutthitaḥ |
so ’yam adya hataḥ śete kālo hi duratikramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
paśya lakṣmaṇa gr̥dhro ’yam upakārī hataś ca me |
sītām abhyavapan no vai rāvaṇena balīyasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
gr̥dhrarājyaṁ parityajya pitr̥paitāmahaṁ mahat |
mama hetor ayaṁ prāṇān mumoca patageśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
sarvatra khalu dr̥śyante sādhavo dharmacāriṇaḥ |
śūrāḥ śaraṇyāḥ saumitre tiryagyonigateṣv api ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
sītāharaṇajaṁ duḥkhaṁ na me saumya tathāgatam |
yathā vināśo gr̥dhrasya matkr̥te ca paraṁtapa ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
rājā daśarathaḥ śrīmān yathā mama mayā yaśāḥ |
pūjanīyaś ca mānyaś ca tathāyaṁ patageśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
saumitre hara kāṣṭhāni nirmathiṣyāmi pāvakam |
gr̥dhrarājaṁ didhakṣāmi matkr̥te nidhanaṁ gatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
nāthaṁ patagalokasya citām āropayāmy aham |
imaṁ dhakṣyāmi saumitre hataṁ raudreṇa rakṣasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
yā gatir yajñaśīlānām āhitāgneś ca yā gatiḥ |
aparāvartināṁ yā ca yā ca bhūmipradāyinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
mayā tvaṁ samanujñāto gaccha lokān anuttamān |
gr̥dhrarāja mahāsattva saṁskr̥taś ca mayā vraja ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
evam uktvā citāṁ dīptām āropya patageśvaram |
dadāha rāmo dharmātmā svabandhum iva duḥkhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
rāmo ’tha sahasaumitrir vanaṁ yātvā sa vīryavān |
sthūlān hatvā mahārohīn anu tastāra taṁ dvijam ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
rohimāṁsāni coddhr̥tya peśīkr̥tvā mahāyaśāḥ |
śakunāya dadau rāmo ramye haritaśādvale ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
yat tat pretasya martyasya kathayanti dvijātayaḥ |
tat svargagamanaṁ tasya kṣipraṁ rāmo jajāpa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
tato godāvarīṁ gatvā nadīṁ naravarātmajau |
udakaṁ cakratus tasmai gr̥dhrarājāya tāv ubhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
sa gr̥dhrarājaḥ kr̥tavān yaśaskaraṁ; suduṣkaraṁ karma raṇe nipātitaḥ |
maharṣikalpena ca saṁskr̥tas tadā; jagāma puṇyāṁ gatim ātmanaḥ śubhām ||

=== meaning ===

---
