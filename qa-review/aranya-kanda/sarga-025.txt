=== Sarga 25 ===

---
1.
tad drumāṇāṁ śilānāṁ ca varṣaṁ prāṇaharaṁ mahat |
pratijagrāha dharmātmā rāghavas tīkṣṇasāyakaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
pratigr̥hya ca tad varaṁ nimīlita ivarṣabhaḥ |
rāmaḥ krodhaṁ paraṁ bheje vadhārthaṁ sarvarakṣasām ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tataḥ krodhasamāviṣṭaḥ pradīpta iva tejasā |
śarair abhyakirat sainyaṁ sarvataḥ sahadūṣaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tataḥ senāpatiḥ kruddho dūṣaṇaḥ śatrudūṣaṇaḥ |
jagrāha giriśr̥ṅgābhaṁ parighaṁ romaharṣaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
veṣṭitaṁ kāñcanaiḥ paṭṭair devasainyābhimardanam |
āyasaiḥ śaṅkubhis tīkṣṇaiḥ kīrṇaṁ paravasokṣitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
vajrāśanisamasparśaṁ paragopuradāraṇam |
taṁ mahoragasaṁkāśaṁ pragr̥hya parighaṁ raṇe |
dūṣaṇo ’bhyapatad rāmaṁ krūrakarmā niśācaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tasyābhipatamānasya dūṣaṇasya sa rāghavaḥ |
dvābhyāṁ śarābhyāṁ ciccheda sahastābharaṇau bhujau ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
bhraṣṭas tasya mahākāyaḥ papāta raṇamūrdhani |
parighaś chinnahastasya śakradhvaja ivāgrataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sa karābhyāṁ vikīrṇābhyāṁ papāta bhuvi dūṣaṇaḥ |
viṣāṇābhyāṁ viśīrṇābhyāṁ manasvīva mahāgajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
dr̥ṣṭvā taṁ patitaṁ bhūmau dūṣaṇaṁ nihataṁ raṇe |
sādhu sādhv iti kākutsthaṁ sarvabhūtāny apūjayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
etasminn antare kruddhās trayaḥ senāgrayāyinaḥ |
saṁhatyābhyadravan rāmaṁ mr̥tyupāśāvapāśitāḥ |
mahākapālaḥ sthūlākṣaḥ pramāthī ca mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
mahākapālo vipulaṁ śūlam udyamya rākṣasaḥ |
sthūlākṣaḥ paṭṭiśaṁ gr̥hya pramāthī ca paraśvadham ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
dr̥ṣṭvaivāpatatas tāṁs tu rāghavaḥ sāyakaiḥ śitaiḥ |
tīkṣṇāgraiḥ pratijagrāha saṁprāptān atithīn iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
mahākapālasya śiraś ciccheda raghunaṅganaḥ |
asaṁkhyeyais tu bāṇaughaiḥ pramamātha pramāthinam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sthūlākṣasyākṣiṇī tīkṣṇaiḥ pūrayām āsa sāyakaiḥ |
sa papāta hato bhūmau viṭapīva mahādrumaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tataḥ pāvakasaṁkāśair hemavajravibhūṣitaiḥ |
jaghanaśeṣaṁ tejasvī tasya sainyasya sāyakaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
te rukmapuṅkhā viśikhāḥ sadhūmā iva pāvakāḥ |
nijaghnus tāni rakṣāṁsi vajrā iva mahādrumān ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
rakṣasāṁ tu śataṁ rāmaḥ śatenaikena karṇinā |
sahasraṁ ca sahasreṇa jaghāna raṇamūrdhani ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tair bhinnavarmābharaṇāś chinnabhinnaśarāsanāḥ |
nipetuḥ śoṇitādigdhā dharaṇyāṁ rajanīcarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tair muktakeśaiḥ samare patitaiḥ śoṇitokṣitaiḥ |
āstīrṇā vasudhā kr̥tsnā mahāvediḥ kuśair iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
kṣaṇena tu mahāghoraṁ vanaṁ nihatarākṣasaṁ |
babhūva niraya prakhyaṁ māṁsaśoṇitakardamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
caturdaśa sahasrāṇi rakṣasāṁ bhīmakarmaṇām |
hatāny ekena rāmeṇa mānuṣeṇa padātinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tasya sainyasya sarvasya kharaḥ śeṣo mahārathaḥ |
rākṣasas triśirāś caiva rāmaś ca ripusūdanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tatas tu tad bhīmabalaṁ mahāhave; samīkṣya rāmeṇa hataṁ balīyasā |
rathena rāmaṁ mahatā kharas tataḥ; samāsasādendra ivodyatāśaniḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
