=== Sarga 4 ===

---
1.
hatvā tu taṁ bhīmabalaṁ virādhaṁ rākṣasaṁ vane |
tataḥ sītāṁ pariṣvajya samāśvāsya ca vīryavān |
abravīl lakṣmaṇāṁ rāmo bhrātaraṁ dīptatejasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
kaṣṭaṁ vanam idaṁ durgaṁ na ca smo vanagocarāḥ |
abhigacchāmahe śīghraṁ śarabhaṅgaṁ tapodhanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
āśramaṁ śarabhaṅgasya rāghavo ’bhijagāma ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tasya devaprabhāvasya tapasā bhāvitātmanaḥ |
samīpe śarabhaṅgasya dadarśa mahad adbhutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
vibhrājamānaṁ vapuṣā sūryavaiśvānaropamam |
asaṁspr̥śantaṁ vasudhāṁ dadarśa vibudheśvaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
suprabhābharaṇaṁ devaṁ virajo ’mbaradhāriṇam |
tadvidhair eva bahubhiḥ pūjyamānaṁ mahātmabhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
haribhir vājibhir yuktam antarikṣagataṁ ratham |
dadarśādūratas tasya taruṇādityasaṁnibham ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
pāṇḍurābhraghanaprakhyaṁ candramaṇḍalasaṁnibham |
apaśyad vimalaṁ chatraṁ citramālyopaśobhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
cāmaravyajane cāgrye rukmadaṇḍe mahādhane |
gr̥hīte vananārībhyāṁ dhūyamāne ca mūrdhani ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
gandharvāmarasiddhāś ca bahavaḥ paramarṣayaḥ |
antarikṣagataṁ devaṁ vāgbhir agryābhir īḍire ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
dr̥ṣṭvā śatakratuṁ tatra rāmo lakṣmaṇam abravīt |
ye hayāḥ puruhūtasya purā śakrasya naḥ śrutāḥ |
antarikṣagatā divyās ta ime harayo dhruvam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
ime ca puruṣavyāghra ye tiṣṭhanty abhito ratham |
śataṁ śataṁ kuṇḍalino yuvānaḥ khaḍgapāṇayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
urodeśeṣu sarveṣāṁ hārā jvalanasaṁnibhāḥ |
rūpaṁ bibhrati saumitre pañcaviṁśativārṣikam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
etad dhi kila devānāṁ vayo bhavati nityadā |
yatheme puruṣavyāghrā dr̥śyante priyadarśanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
ihaiva saha vaidehyā muhūrtaṁ tiṣṭha lakṣmaṇa |
yāvaj janāmy ahaṁ vyaktaṁ ka eṣa dyutimān rathe ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tam evam uktvā saumitrim ihaiva sthīyatām iti |
abhicakrāma kākutsthaḥ śarabhaṅgāśramaṁ prati ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tataḥ samabhigacchantaṁ prekṣya rāmaṁ śacīpatiḥ |
śarabhaṅgam anujñāpya vibudhān idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
ihopayāty asau rāmo yāvan māṁ nābhibhāṣate |
niṣṭhāṁ nayata tāvat tu tato māṁ draṣṭum arhati ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
jitavantaṁ kr̥tārthaṁ ca draṣṭāham acirād imam |
karma hy anena kartavyaṁ mahad anyaiḥ suduṣkaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
iti vajrī tam āmantrya mānayitvā ca tāpasaṁ |
rathena hariyuktena yayau divam ariṁdamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
prayāte tu sahasrākṣe rāghavaḥ saparicchadaḥ |
agnihotram upāsīnaṁ śarabhaṅgam upāgamat ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tasya pādau ca saṁgr̥hya rāmaḥ sītā ca lakṣmaṇaḥ |
niṣedus tadanujñātā labdhavāsā nimantritāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tataḥ śakropayānaṁ tu paryapr̥cchat sa rāghavaḥ |
śarabhaṅgaś ca tat sarvaṁ rāghavāya nyavedayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
mām eṣa varado rāma brahmalokaṁ ninīṣati |
jitam ugreṇa tapasā duṣprāpam akr̥tātmabhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
ahaṁ jñātvā naravyāghra vartamānam adūrataḥ |
brahmalokaṁ na gacchāmi tvām adr̥ṣṭvā priyātithim ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
samāgamya gamiṣyāmi tridivaṁ devasevitam |
akṣayā naraśārdūla jitā lokā mayā śubhāḥ |
brāhmyāś ca nākapr̥ṣṭhyāś ca pratigr̥hṇīṣva māmakān ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
evam ukto naravyāghraḥ sarvaśāstraviśāradaḥ |
r̥ṣiṇā śarabhaṅgena rāghavo vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
aham evāhariṣyāmi sarvām̐l lokān mahāmune |
āvāsaṁ tv aham icchāmi pradiṣṭam iha kānane ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
rāghaveṇaivam uktas tu śakratulyabalena vai |
śarabhaṅgo mahāprājñaḥ punar evābravīd vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
sutīkṣṇam abhigaccha tvaṁ śucau deśe tapasvinam |
ramaṇīye vanoddeśe sa te vāsaṁ vidhāsyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
eṣa panthā naravyāghra muhūrtaṁ paśya tāta mām |
yāvaj jahāmi gātrāṇi jīrṇaṁ tvacam ivoragaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
tato ’gniṁ sa samādhāya hutvā cājyena mantravit |
śarabhaṅgo mahātejāḥ praviveśa hutāśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
tasya romāṇi keśāṁś ca dadāhāgnir mahātmanaḥ |
jīrṇaṁ tvacaṁ tathāsthīni yac ca māṁsaṁ ca śoṇitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
sa ca pāvakasaṁkāśaḥ kumāraḥ samapadyata |
utthāyāgnicayāt tasmāc charabhaṅgo vyarocata ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
sa lokān āhitāgnīnām r̥ṣīṇāṁ ca mahātmanām |
devānāṁ ca vyatikramya brahmalokaṁ vyarohata ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
sa puṇyakarmā bhuvane dvijarṣabhaḥ; pitāmahaṁ sānucaraṁ dadarśa ha |
pitāmahaś cāpi samīkṣya taṁ dvijaṁ; nananda susvāgatam ity uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
