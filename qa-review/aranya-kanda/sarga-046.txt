=== Sarga 46 ===

---
1.
evaṁ bruvatyāṁ sītāyāṁ saṁrabdhaḥ paruṣākṣaram |
lalāṭe bhrukuṭīṁ kr̥tvā rāvaṇaḥ pratyuvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
bhrātā vaiśravaṇasyāhaṁ sāpatnyo varavarṇini |
rāvaṇo nāma bhadraṁ te daśagrīvaḥ pratāpavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
yasya devāḥ sagandharvāḥ piśācapatagoragāḥ |
vidravanti bhayād bhītā mr̥tyor iva sadā prajāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
yena vaiśravaṇo bhrātā vaimātraḥ kāraṇāntare |
dvandvam āsāditaḥ krodhād raṇe vikramya nirjitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
madbhayārtaḥ parityajya svam adhiṣṭhānam r̥ddhimat |
kailāsaṁ parvataśreṣṭham adhyāste naravāhanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
yasya tat puṣpakaṁ nāma vimānaṁ kāmagaṁ śubham |
vīryād āvarjitaṁ bhadre yena yāmi vihāyasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
mama saṁjātaroṣasya mukhaṁ dr̥ṣṭvaiva maithili |
vidravanti paritrastāḥ surāḥ śakrapurogamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
yatra tiṣṭhāmy ahaṁ tatra māruto vāti śaṅkitaḥ |
tīvrāṁśuḥ śiśirāṁśuś ca bhayāt saṁpadyate raviḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
niṣkampapatrās taravo nadyaś ca stimitodakāḥ |
bhavanti yatra yatrāhaṁ tiṣṭhāmi ca carāmi ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
mama pāre samudrasya laṅkā nāma purī śubhā |
saṁpūrṇā rākṣasair ghorair yathendrasyāmarāvatī ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
prākāreṇa parikṣiptā pāṇḍureṇa virājitā |
hemakakṣyā purī ramyā vaidūryamaya toraṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
hastyaśvarathasaṁbhādhā tūryanādavināditā |
sarvakāmaphalair vr̥kṣaiḥ saṁkulodyānaśobhitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tatra tvaṁ vasatī sīte rājaputri mayā saha |
na sramiṣyasi nārīṇāṁ mānuṣīṇāṁ manasvini ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
bhuñjānā mānuṣān bhogān divyāṁś ca varavarṇini |
na smariṣyasi rāmasya mānuṣasya gatāyuṣaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sthāpayitvā priyaṁ putraṁ rājñā daśarathena yaḥ |
mandavīryaḥ suto jyeṣṭhas tataḥ prasthāpito vanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tena kiṁ bhraṣṭarājyena rāmeṇa gatacetasā |
kariṣyasi viśālākṣi tāpasena tapasvinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
sarvarākṣasabhartāraṁ kāmāt svayam ihāgatam |
na manmathaśarāviṣṭaṁ pratyākhyātuṁ tvam arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
pratyākhyāya hi māṁ bhīru paritāpaṁ gamiṣyasi |
caraṇenābhihatyeva purūravasam urvaśī ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
evam uktā tu vaidehī kruddhā saṁraktalocanā |
abravīt paruṣaṁ vākyaṁ rahite rākṣasādhipam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
kathaṁ vaiśravaṇaṁ devaṁ sarvabhūtanamaskr̥tam |
bhrātaraṁ vyapadiśya tvam aśubhaṁ kartum icchasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
avaśyaṁ vinaśiṣyanti sarve rāvaṇa rākṣasāḥ |
yeṣāṁ tvaṁ karkaśo rājā durbuddhir ajitendriyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
apahr̥tya śacīṁ bhāryāṁ śakyam indrasya jīvitum |
na tu rāmasya bhāryāṁ mām apanīyāsti jīvitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
jīvec ciraṁ vajradharasya hastāc; chacīṁ pradhr̥ṣyāpratirūparūpām |
na mādr̥śīṁ rākṣasadharṣayitvā; pītāmr̥tasyāpi tavāsti mokṣaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
