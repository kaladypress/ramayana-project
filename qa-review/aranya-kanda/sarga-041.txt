=== Sarga 41 ===

---
1.
sā taṁ saṁprekṣya suśroṇī kusumāni vicinvatī |
hemarājatavarṇābhyāṁ pārśvābhyām upaśobhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
prahr̥ṣṭā cānavadyāṅgī mr̥ṣṭahāṭakavarṇinī |
bhartāram api cākrandal lakṣmaṇaṁ caiva sāyudham ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tayāhūtau naravyāghrau vaidehyā rāmalakṣmaṇau |
vīkṣamāṇau tu taṁ deśaṁ tadā dadr̥śatur mr̥gam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
śaṅkamānas tu taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā lakṣmaṇo rāmam abravīt |
tam evainam ahaṁ manye mārīcaṁ rākṣasaṁ mr̥gam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
caranto mr̥gayāṁ hr̥ṣṭāḥ pāpenopādhinā vane |
anena nihatā rāma rājānaḥ kāmarūpiṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
asya māyāvido māyāmr̥garūpam idaṁ kr̥tam |
bhānumatpuruṣavyāghra gandharvapurasaṁnibham ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
mr̥go hy evaṁvidho ratnavicitro nāsti rāghava |
jagatyāṁ jagatīnātha māyaiṣā hi na saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
evaṁ bruvāṇaṁ kākutsthaṁ prativārya śucismitā |
uvāca sītā saṁhr̥ṣṭā chadmanā hr̥tacetanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
āryaputrābhirāmo ’sau mr̥go harati me manaḥ |
ānayainaṁ mahābāho krīḍārthaṁ no bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
ihāśramapade ’smākaṁ bahavaḥ puṇyadarśanāḥ |
mr̥gāś caranti sahitāś camarāḥ sr̥marās tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
r̥kṣāḥ pr̥ṣatasaṁghāś ca vānarāḥ kiṁnarās tathā |
vicaranti mahābāho rūpaśreṣṭhā mahābalāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
na cāsya sadr̥śo rājan dr̥ṣṭapūrvo mr̥gaḥ purā |
tejasā kṣamayā dīptyā yathāyaṁ mr̥gasattamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
nānāvarṇavicitrāṅgo ratnabindusamācitaḥ |
dyotayan vanam avyagraṁ śobhate śaśisaṁnibhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
aho rūpam aho lakṣmīḥ svarasaṁpac ca śobhanā |
mr̥go ’dbhuto vicitro ’sau hr̥dayaṁ haratīva me ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
yadi grahaṇam abhyeti jīvann eva mr̥gas tava |
āścaryabhūtaṁ bhavati vismayaṁ janayiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
samāptavanavāsānāṁ rājyasthānāṁ ca naḥ punaḥ |
antaḥpuravibhūṣārtho mr̥ga eṣa bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
bharatasyāryaputrasya śvaśrūṇāṁ mama ca prabho |
mr̥garūpam idaṁ divyaṁ vismayaṁ janayiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
jīvan na yadi te ’bhyeti grahaṇaṁ mr̥gasattamaḥ |
ajinaṁ naraśārdūla ruciraṁ me bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
nihatasyāsya sattvasya jāmbūnadamayatvaci |
śaṣpabr̥syāṁ vinītāyām icchāmy aham upāsitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
kāmavr̥ttam idaṁ raudraṁ strīṇām asadr̥śaṁ matam |
vapuṣā tv asya sattvasya vismayo janito mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tena kāñcanaromṇā tu maṇipravaraśr̥ṅgiṇā |
taruṇādityavarṇena nakṣatrapathavarcasā |
babhūva rāghavasyāpi mano vismayam āgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
evaṁ sītāvacaḥ śrutvā dr̥ṣṭvā ca mr̥gam adbhutam |
uvāca rāghavo hr̥ṣṭo bhrātaraṁ lakṣmaṇaṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
paśya lakṣmaṇa vaidehyāḥ spr̥hāṁ mr̥gagatām imām |
rūpaśreṣṭhatayā hy eṣa mr̥go ’dya na bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
na vane nandanoddeśe na caitrarathasaṁśraye |
kutaḥ pr̥thivyāṁ saumitre yo ’sya kaś cit samo mr̥gaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
pratilomānulomāś ca rucirā romarājayaḥ |
śobhante mr̥gam āśritya citrāḥ kanakabindubhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
paśyāsya jr̥mbhamāṇasya dīptām agniśikhopamām |
jihvāṁ mukhān niḥsarantīṁ meghād iva śatahradām ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
masāragalvarkamukhaḥ śaṅkhamuktānibhodaraḥ |
kasya nāmānirūpyo ’sau na mano lobhayen mr̥gaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
kasya rūpam idaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā jāmbūnadamayaprabham |
nānāratnamayaṁ divyaṁ na mano vismayaṁ vrajet ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
māṁsahetor api mr̥gān vihārārthaṁ ca dhanvinaḥ |
ghnanti lakṣmaṇa rājāno mr̥gayāyāṁ mahāvane ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
dhanāni vyavasāyena vicīyante mahāvane |
dhātavo vividhāś cāpi maṇiratnasuvarṇinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
tat sāram akhilaṁ nr̥̄ṇāṁ dhanaṁ nicayavardhanam |
manasā cintitaṁ sarvaṁ yathā śukrasya lakṣmaṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
arthī yenārthakr̥tyena saṁvrajaty avicārayan |
tam artham arthaśāstrajñaḥ prāhur arthyāś ca lakṣmaṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
etasya mr̥garatnasya parārdhye kāñcanatvaci |
upavekṣyati vaidehī mayā saha sumadhyamā ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
na kādalī na priyakī na praveṇī na cāvikī |
bhaved etasya sadr̥śī sparśaneneti me matiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
eṣa caiva mr̥gaḥ śrīmān yaś ca divyo nabhaścaraḥ |
ubhāv etau mr̥gau divyau tārāmr̥gamahīmr̥gau ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
yadi vāyaṁ tathā yan māṁ bhaved vadasi lakṣmaṇa |
māyaiṣā rākṣasasyeti kartavyo ’sya vadho mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
etena hi nr̥śaṁsena mārīcenākr̥tātmanā |
vane vicaratā pūrvaṁ hiṁsitā munipuṁgavāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
utthāya bahavo yena mr̥gayāyāṁ janādhipāḥ |
nihatāḥ parameṣvāsās tasmād vadhyas tv ayaṁ mr̥gaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
purastād iha vātāpiḥ paribhūya tapasvinaḥ |
udarastho dvijān hanti svagarbho ’śvatarīm iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
sa kadā cic cirāl loke āsasāda mahāmunim |
agastyaṁ tejasā yuktaṁ bhakṣyas tasya babhūva ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
samutthāne ca tad rūpaṁ kartukāmaṁ samīkṣya tam |
utsmayitvā tu bhagavān vātāpim idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
tvayāvigaṇya vātāpe paribhūtāś ca tejasā |
jīvaloke dvijaśreṣṭhās tasmād asi jarāṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
evaṁ tan na bhaved rakṣo vātāpir iva lakṣmaṇa |
madvidhaṁ yo ’timanyeta dharmanityaṁ jitendriyam ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
bhaved dhato ’yaṁ vātāpir agastyeneva mā gatiḥ |
iha tvaṁ bhava saṁnaddho yantrito rakṣa maithilīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
asyām āyattam asmākaṁ yat kr̥tyaṁ raghunandana |
aham enaṁ vadhiṣyāmi grahīṣyāmy atha vā mr̥gam ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
yāvad gacchāmi saumitre mr̥gam ānayituṁ drutam |
paśya lakṣmaṇa vaidehīṁ mr̥gatvaci gataspr̥hām ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
tvacā pradhānayā hy eṣa mr̥go ’dya na bhaviṣyati |
apramattena te bhāvyam āśramasthena sītayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
yāvat pr̥ṣatam ekena sāyakena nihanmy aham |
hatvaitac carma ādāya śīghram eṣyāmi lakṣmaṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
pradakṣiṇenātibalena pakṣiṇā; jaṭāyuṣā buddhimatā ca lakṣmaṇa |
bhavāpramattaḥ pratigr̥hya maithilīṁ; pratikṣaṇaṁ sarvata eva śaṅkitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
