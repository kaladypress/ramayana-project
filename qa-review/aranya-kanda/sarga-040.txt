=== Sarga 40 ===

---
1.
evam uktvā tu paruṣaṁ mārīco rāvaṇaṁ tataḥ |
gacchāvety abravīd dīno bhayād rātriṁcaraprabhoḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
dr̥ṣṭaś cāhaṁ punas tena śaracāpāsidhāriṇā |
madvadhodyataśastreṇa vinaṣṭaṁ jīvitaṁ ca me ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
kiṁ tu kartuṁ mayā śakyam evaṁ tvayi durātmani |
eṣa gacchāmy ahaṁ tāta svasti te ’stu niśācara ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
prahr̥ṣṭas tv abhavat tena vacanena sa rākṣasaḥ |
pariṣvajya susaṁśliṣṭam idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
etac chauṇḍīryayuktaṁ te macchandād iva bhāṣitam |
idānīm asi mārīcaḥ pūrvam anyo niśācaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
āruhyatām ayaṁ śīghraṁ khago ratnavibhūṣitaḥ |
mayā saha ratho yuktaḥ piśācavadanaiḥ kharaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tato rāvaṇamārīcau vimānam iva taṁ ratham |
āruhya yayatuḥ śīghraṁ tasmād āśramamaṇḍalāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tathaiva tatra paśyantau pattanāni vanāni ca |
girīṁś ca saritaḥ sarvā rāṣṭrāṇi nagarāṇi ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sametya daṇḍakāraṇyaṁ rāghavasyāśramaṁ tataḥ |
dadarśa sahamarīco rāvaṇo rākṣasādhipaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
avatīrya rathāt tasmāt tataḥ kāñcanabhūṣaṇāt |
haste gr̥hītvā mārīcaṁ rāvaṇo vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
etad rāmāśramapadaṁ dr̥śyate kadalīvr̥tam |
kriyatāṁ tat sakhe śīghraṁ yadarthaṁ vayam āgatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sa rāvaṇavacaḥ śrutvā mārīco rākṣasas tadā |
mr̥go bhūtvāśramadvāri rāmasya vicacāra ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
maṇipravaraśr̥ṅgāgraḥ sitāsitamukhākr̥tiḥ |
raktapadmotpalamukha indranīlotpalaśravāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
kiṁ cid abhyunnata grīva indranīlanibhodaraḥ |
madhūkanibhapārśvaś ca kañjakiñjalkasaṁnibhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
vaidūryasaṁkāśakhuras tanujaṅghaḥ susaṁhataḥ |
indrāyudhasavarṇena pucchenordhvaṁ virājitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
manoharasnigdhavarṇo ratnair nānāvidhair vr̥taḥ |
kṣaṇena rākṣaso jāto mr̥gaḥ paramaśobhanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
vanaṁ prajvalayan ramyaṁ rāmāśramapadaṁ ca tat |
manoharaṁ darśanīyaṁ rūpaṁ kr̥tvā sa rākṣasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
pralobhanārthaṁ vaidehyā nānādhātuvicitritam |
vicaran gacchate samyak śādvalāni samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
rūpyabinduśataiś citro bhūtvā ca priyadarśanaḥ |
viṭapīnāṁ kisalayān bhaṅktvādan vicacāra ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
kadalīgr̥hakaṁ gatvā karṇikārān itas tataḥ |
samāśrayan mandagatiḥ sītāsaṁdarśanaṁ tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
rājīvacitrapr̥ṣṭhaḥ sa virarāja mahāmr̥gaḥ |
rāmāśramapadābhyāśe vicacāra yathāsukham ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
punar gatvā nivr̥ttaś ca vicacāra mr̥gottamaḥ |
gatvā muhūrtaṁ tvarayā punaḥ pratinivartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
vikrīḍaṁś ca punar bhūmau punar eva niṣīdati |
āśramadvāram āgamya mr̥gayūthāni gacchati ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
mr̥gayūthair anugataḥ punar eva nivartate |
sītādarśanam ākāṅkṣan rākṣaso mr̥gatāṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
paribhramati citrāṇi maṇḍalāni viniṣpatan |
samudvīkṣya ca sarve taṁ mr̥gā ye ’nye vanecarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
upagamya samāghrāya vidravanti diśo daśa |
rākṣasaḥ so ’pi tān vanyān mr̥gān mr̥gavadhe rataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
pracchādanārthaṁ bhāvasya na bhakṣayati saṁspr̥śan |
tasminn eva tataḥ kāle vaidehī śubhalocanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
kusumāpacaye vyagrā pādapān atyavartata |
karṇikārān aśokāṁś ca cūṭāṁś ca madirekṣaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
kusumāny apacinvantī cacāra rucirānanā |
anarhāraṇyavāsasya sā taṁ ratnamayaṁ mr̥gam |
muktāmaṇivicitrāṅgaṁ dadarśa paramāṅganā ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
taṁ vai ruciradaṇtauṣṭhaṁ rūpyadhātutanūruham |
vismayotphullanayanā sasnehaṁ samudaikṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
sa ca tāṁ rāmadayitāṁ paśyan māyāmayo mr̥gaḥ |
vicacāra tatas tatra dīpayann iva tad vanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
adr̥ṣṭapūrvaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā taṁ nānāratnamayaṁ mr̥gam |
vismayaṁ paramaṁ sītā jagāma janakātmajā ||

=== meaning ===

---
