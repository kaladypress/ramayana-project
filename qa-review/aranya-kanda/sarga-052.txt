=== Sarga 52 ===

---
1.
hriyamāṇā tu vaidehī kaṁ cin nātham apaśyatī |
dadarśa giriśr̥ṅgasthān pañcavānarapuṁgavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
teṣāṁ madhye viśālākṣī kauśeyaṁ kanakaprabham |
uttarīyaṁ varārohā śubhāny ābharaṇāni ca |
mumoca yadi rāmāya śaṁseyur iti maithilī ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
vastram utsr̥jya tan madhye vinikṣiptaṁ sabhūṣaṇam |
saṁbhramāt tu daśagrīvas tat karma na ca buddhivān ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
piṅgākṣās tāṁ viśālākṣīṁ netrair animiṣair iva |
vikrośantīṁ tadā sītāṁ dadr̥śur vānararṣabhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
sa ca pampām atikramya laṅkām abhimukhaḥ purīm |
jagāma rudatīṁ gr̥hya maithilīṁ rākṣaseśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tāṁ jahāra susaṁhr̥ṣṭo rāvaṇo mr̥tyum ātmanaḥ |
utsaṅgenaiva bhujagīṁ tīkṣṇadaṁṣṭrāṁ mahāviṣām ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
vanāni saritaḥ śailān sarāṁsi ca vihāyasā |
sa kṣipraṁ samatīyāya śaraś cāpād iva cyutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
timinakraniketaṁ tu varuṇālayam akṣayam |
saritāṁ śaraṇaṁ gatvā samatīyāya sāgaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
saṁbhramāt parivr̥ttormī ruddhamīnamahoragaḥ |
vaidehyāṁ hriyamāṇāyāṁ babhūva varuṇālayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
antarikṣagatā vācaḥ sasr̥juś cāraṇās tadā |
etad anto daśagrīva iti siddhās tadābruvan ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sa tu sītāṁ viveṣṭantīm aṅkenādāya rāvaṇaḥ |
praviveśa purīṁ laṅkāṁ rūpiṇīṁ mr̥tyum ātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
so ’bhigamya purīṁ laṅkāṁ suvibhaktamahāpathām |
saṁrūḍhakakṣyā bahulaṁ svam antaḥpuram āviśat ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tatra tām asitāpāṅgīṁ śokamohaparāyaṇām |
nidadhe rāvaṇaḥ sītāṁ mayo māyām ivāsurīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
abravīc ca daśagrīvaḥ piśācīr ghoradarśanāḥ |
yathā naināṁ pumān strī vā sītāṁ paśyaty asaṁmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
muktāmaṇisuvarṇāni vastrāṇy ābharaṇāni ca |
yad yad icchet tad evāsyā deyaṁ macchandato yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
yā ca vakṣyati vaidehīṁ vacanaṁ kiṁ cid apriyam |
ajñānād yadi vā jñānān na tasyā jīvitaṁ priyam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tathoktvā rākṣasīs tās tu rākṣasendraḥ pratāpavān |
niṣkramyāntaḥpurāt tasmāt kiṁ kr̥tyam iti cintayan |
dadarśāṣṭau mahāvīryān rākṣasān piśitāśanān ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
sa tān dr̥ṣṭvā mahāvīryo varadānena mohitaḥ |
uvācaitān idaṁ vākyaṁ praśasya balavīryataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
nānāpraharaṇāḥ kṣipram ito gacchata satvarāḥ |
janasthānaṁ hatasthānaṁ bhūtapūrvaṁ kharālayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tatroṣyatāṁ janasthāne śūnye nihatarākṣase |
pauruṣaṁ balam āśritya trāsam utsr̥jya dūrataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
balaṁ hi sumahad yan me janasthāne niveśitam |
sadūṣaṇakharaṁ yuddhe hataṁ tad rāmasāyakaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tataḥ krodho mamāpūrvo dhairyasyopari vardhate |
vairaṁ ca sumahaj jātaṁ rāmaṁ prati sudāruṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
niryātayitum icchāmi tac ca vairam ahaṁ ripoḥ |
na hi lapsyāmy ahaṁ nidrām ahatvā saṁyuge ripum ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
taṁ tv idānīm ahaṁ hatvā kharadūṣaṇaghātinam |
rāmaṁ śarmopalapsyāmi dhanaṁ labdhveva nirdhanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
janasthāne vasadbhis tu bhavadbhī rāmam āśritā |
pravr̥ttir upanetavyā kiṁ karotīti tattvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
apramādāc ca gantavyaṁ sarvair eva niśācaraiḥ |
kartavyaś ca sadā yatno rāghavasya vadhaṁ prati ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
yuṣmākaṁ hi balajño ’haṁ bahuśo raṇamūrdhani |
ataś cāsmiñ janasthāne mayā yūyaṁ niyojitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tataḥ priyaṁ vākyam upetya rākṣasā; mahārtham aṣṭāv abhivādya rāvaṇam |
vihāya laṅkāṁ sahitāḥ pratasthire; yato janasthānam alakṣyadarśanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
tatas tu sītām upalabhya rāvaṇaḥ; susaṁprahr̥ṣṭaḥ parigr̥hya maithilīm |
prasajya rāmeṇa ca vairam uttamaṁ; babhūva mohān muditaḥ sa rākṣasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
