=== Sarga 22 ===

---
1.
tat prayātaṁ balaṁ ghoram aśivaṁ śoṇitodakam |
abhyavarṣan mahāmeghas tumulo gardabhāruṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
nipetus turagās tasya rathayuktā mahājavāḥ |
same puṣpacite deśe rājamārge yadr̥cchayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
śyāmaṁ rudhiraparyantaṁ babhūva pariveṣaṇam |
alātacakrapratimaṁ pratigr̥hya divākaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tato dhvajam upāgamya hemadaṇḍaṁ samucchritam |
samākramya mahākāyas tasthau gr̥dhraḥ sudāruṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
janasthānasamīpe ca samākramya kharasvanāḥ |
visvarān vividhāṁś cakrur māṁsādā mr̥gapakṣiṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
vyājahruś ca padīptāyāṁ diśi vai bhairavasvanam |
aśivā yātu dāhānāṁ śivā ghorā mahāsvanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
prabhinnagirisaṁkāśās toyaśoṣitadhāriṇaḥ |
ākāśaṁ tad anākāśaṁ cakrur bhīmā balāhakāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
babhūva timiraṁ ghoram uddhataṁ romaharṣaṇam |
diśo vā vidiśo vāpi suvyaktaṁ na cakāśire ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
kṣatajārdrasavarṇābhā saṁdhyākālaṁ vinā babhau |
kharasyābhimukhaṁ nedus tadā ghorā mr̥gāḥ khagāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
nityāśivakarā yuddhe śivā ghoranidarśanāḥ |
nedur balasyābhimukhaṁ jvālodgāribhir ānanaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
kabandhaḥ parighābhāso dr̥śyate bhāskarāntike |
jagrāha sūryaṁ svarbhānur aparvaṇi mahāgrahaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
pravāti mārutaḥ śīghraṁ niṣprabho ’bhūd divākaraḥ |
utpetuś ca vinā rātriṁ tārāḥ khadyotasaprabhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
saṁlīnamīnavihagā nalinyaḥ puṣpapaṅkajāḥ |
tasmin kṣaṇe babhūvuś ca vinā puṣpaphalair drumāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
uddhūtaś ca vinā vātaṁ reṇur jaladharāruṇaḥ |
vīcīkūcīti vāśyanto babhūvus tatra sārikāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
ulkāś cāpi sanirghoṣā nipetur ghoradarśanāḥ |
pracacāla mahī cāpi saśailavanakānanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
kharasya ca rathasthasya nardamānasya dhīmataḥ |
prākampata bhujaḥ savyaḥ kharaś cāsyāvasajjata ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
sāsrā saṁpadyate dr̥ṣṭiḥ paśyamānasya sarvataḥ |
lalāṭe ca rujā jātā na ca mohān nyavartata ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tān samīkṣya mahotpātān utthitān romaharṣaṇān |
abravīd rākṣasān sarvān prahasan sa kharas tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
mahotpātān imān sarvān utthitān ghoradarśanān |
na cintayāmy ahaṁ vīryād balavān durbalān iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tārā api śarais tīkṣṇaiḥ pātayeyaṁ nabhastalāt |
mr̥tyuṁ maraṇadharmeṇa saṁkruddho yojayāmy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
rāghavaṁ taṁ balotsiktaṁ bhrātaraṁ cāpi lakṣmaṇam |
ahatvā sāyakais tīkṣṇair nopāvartitum utsahe ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
sakāmā bhaginī me ’stu pītvā tu rudhiraṁ tayoḥ |
yannimittaṁ tu rāmasya lakṣmaṇasya viparyayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
na kva cit prāptapūrvo me saṁyugeṣu parājayaḥ |
yuṣmākam etat pratyakṣaṁ nānr̥taṁ kathayāmy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
devarājam api kruddho mattairāvatayāyinam |
vajrahastaṁ raṇe hanyāṁ kiṁ punas tau ca mānuṣau ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
sā tasya garjitaṁ śrutvā rākṣasasya mahācamūḥ |
praharṣam atulaṁ lebhe mr̥tyupāśāvapāśitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
sameyuś ca mahātmāno yuddhadarśanakāṅkṣiṇaḥ |
r̥ṣayo devagandharvāḥ siddhāś ca saha cāraṇaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
sametya coruḥ sahitās te ’nyāyaṁ puṇyakarmaṇaḥ |
svasti gobrāhmaṇebhyo ’stu lokānāṁ ye ca saṁmatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
jayatāṁ rāghavo yuddhe paulastyān rajanīcarān |
cakrā hasto yathā yuddhe sarvān asurapuṁgavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
etac cānyac ca bahuśo bruvāṇāḥ paramarṣayaḥ |
dadr̥śur vāhinīṁ teṣāṁ rākṣasānāṁ gatāyuṣām ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
rathena tu kharo vegāt sainyasyāgrād viniḥsr̥taḥ |
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rākṣasaṁ bhūyo rākṣasāś ca viniḥsr̥tāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
śyena gāmī pr̥thugrīvo yajñaśatrur vihaṁgamaḥ |
durjayaḥ karavīrākṣaḥ paruṣaḥ kālakārmukaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
meghamālī mahāmālī sarpāsyo rudhirāśanaḥ |
dvādaśaite mahāvīryāḥ pratasthur abhitaḥ kharam ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
mahākapālaḥ sthūlākṣaḥ pramāthī triśirās tathā |
catvāra ete senāgryā dūṣaṇaṁ pr̥ṣṭhato ’nvayuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
sā bhīmavegā samarābhikāmā; sudāruṇā rākṣasavīra senā |
tau rājaputrau sahasābhyupetā; mālāgrahāṇām iva candrasūryau ||

=== meaning ===

---
