=== Sarga 54 ===

---
1.
sā tathoktā tu vaidehī nirbhayā śokakarṣitā |
tr̥ṇam antarataḥ kr̥tvā rāvaṇaṁ pratyabhāṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
rājā daśaratho nāma dharmasetur ivācalaḥ |
satyasandhaḥ parijñāto yasya putraḥ sa rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
rāmo nāma sa dharmātmā triṣu lokeṣu viśrutaḥ |
dīrghabāhur viśālākṣo daivataṁ sa patir mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
ikṣvākūṇāṁ kule jātaḥ siṁhaskandho mahādyutiḥ |
lakṣmaṇena saha bhrātrā yas te prāṇāṁ hariṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
pratyakṣaṁ yady ahaṁ tasya tvayā syāṁ dharṣitā balāt |
śayitā tvaṁ hataḥ saṁkhye janasthāne yathā kharaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
ya ete rākṣasāḥ proktā ghorarūpā mahābalāḥ |
rāghave nirviṣāḥ sarve suparṇe pannagā yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tasya jyāvipramuktās te śarāḥ kāñcanabhūṣaṇāḥ |
śarīraṁ vidhamiṣyanti gaṅgākūlam ivormayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
asurair vā surair vā tvaṁ yady avadho ’si rāvaṇa |
utpādya sumahad vairaṁ jīvaṁs tasya na mokṣyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sa te jīvitaśeṣasya rāghavo ’ntakaro balī |
paśor yūpagatasyeva jīvitaṁ tava durlabham ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
yadi paśyet sa rāmas tvāṁ roṣadīptena cakṣuṣā |
rakṣas tvam adya nirdagdho gaccheḥ sadyaḥ parābhavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
yaś candraṁ nabhaso bhūmau pātayen nāśayeta vā |
sāgaraṁ śoṣayed vāpi sa sītāṁ mocayed iha ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
gatāyus tvaṁ gataśrīko gatasattvo gatendriyaḥ |
laṅkā vaidhavyasaṁyuktā tvatkr̥tena bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
na te pāpam idaṁ karma sukhodarkaṁ bhaviṣyati |
yāhaṁ nītā vinā bhāvaṁ patipārśvāt tvayā vanāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sa hi daivatasaṁyukto mama bhartā mahādyutiḥ |
nirbhayo vīryam āśritya śūnye vasati daṇḍake ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa te darpaṁ balaṁ vīryam utsekaṁ ca tathāvidham |
apaneṣyati gātrebhyaḥ śaravarṣeṇa saṁyuge ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
yadā vināśo bhūtānāṁ dr̥śyate kālacoditaḥ |
tadā kārye pramādyanti narāḥ kālavaśaṁ gatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
māṁ pradhr̥ṣya sa te kālaḥ prāpto ’yaṁ rakṣasādhama |
ātmano rākṣasānāṁ ca vadhāyāntaḥpurasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
na śakyā yajñamadhyasthā vediḥ srugbhāṇḍa maṇḍitā |
dvijātimantrasaṁpūtā caṇḍālenāvamarditum ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
idaṁ śarīraṁ niḥsaṁjñaṁ bandha vā ghātayasva vā |
nedaṁ śarīraṁ rakṣyaṁ me jīvitaṁ vāpi rākṣasa |
na hi śakṣyāmy upakrośaṁ pr̥thivyāṁ dātum ātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
evam uktvā tu vaidehī kroddhāt suparuṣaṁ vacaḥ |
rāvaṇaṁ maithilī tatra punar novāca kiṁ cana ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
sītāyā vacanaṁ śrutvā paruṣaṁ romaharṣaṇam |
pratyuvāca tataḥ sītāṁ bhayasaṁdarśanaṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
śr̥ṇu maithili madvākyaṁ māsān dvādaśa bhāmini |
kālenānena nābhyeṣi yadi māṁ cāruhāsini |
tatas tvāṁ prātarāśārthaṁ sūdāś chetsyanti leśaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
ity uktvā paruṣaṁ vākyaṁ rāvaṇaḥ śatrurāvaṇaḥ |
rākṣasīś ca tataḥ kruddha idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
śīghram evaṁ hi rākṣasyo vikr̥tā ghoradarśanāḥ |
darpam asyā vineṣyantu māṁsaśoṇitabhojanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
vacanād eva tās tasya vikr̥tā ghoradarśanāḥ |
kr̥taprāñjalayo bhūtvā maithilīṁ paryavārayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
sa tāḥ provāca rājā tu rāvaṇo ghoradarśanaḥ |
pracālya caraṇotkarṣair dārayann iva medinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
aśokavanikāmadhye maithilī nīyatām iti |
tatreyaṁ rakṣyatāṁ gūḍham uṣmābhiḥ parivāritā ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tatraināṁ tarjanair ghoraiḥ punaḥ sāntvaiś ca maithilīm |
ānayadhvaṁ vaśaṁ sarvā vanyāṁ gajavadhūm iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
iti pratisamādiṣṭā rākṣasyo rāvaṇena tāḥ |
aśokavanikāṁ jagmur maithilīṁ parigr̥hya tām ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
sarvakāmaphalair vr̥kṣair nānāpuṣpaphalair vr̥tām |
sarvakālamadaiś cāpi dvijaiḥ samupasevitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
sā tu śokaparītāṅgī maithilī janakātmajā |
rākṣasī vaśam āpannā vyāghrīṇāṁ hariṇī yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
na vindate tatra tu śarma maithilī; virūpanetrābhir atīva tarjitā |
patiṁ smarantī dayitaṁ ca devaraṁ; vicetanābhūd bhayaśokapīḍitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
