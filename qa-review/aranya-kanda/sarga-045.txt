=== Sarga 45 ===

---
1.
rāvaṇena tu vaidehī tadā pr̥ṣṭā jihīrṣuṇā |
parivrājakarūpeṇa śaśaṁsātmānam ātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
brāhmaṇaś cātithiś caiṣa anukto hi śapeta mām |
iti dhyātvā muhūrtaṁ tu sītā vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
duhitā janakasyāhaṁ maithilasya mahātmanaḥ |
sītā nāmnāsmi bhadraṁ te rāmabhāryā dvijottama ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
saṁvatsaraṁ cādhyuṣitā rāghavasya niveśane |
bhuñjānā mānuṣān bhogān sarvakāmasamr̥ddhinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tataḥ saṁvatsarād ūrdhvaṁ samamanyata me patim |
abhiṣecayituṁ rāmaṁ sameto rājamantribhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tasmin saṁbhriyamāṇe tu rāghavasyābhiṣecane |
kaikeyī nāma bhartāraṁ mamāryā yācate varam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
pratigr̥hya tu kaikeyī śvaśuraṁ sukr̥tena me |
mama pravrājanaṁ bhartur bharatasyābhiṣecanam |
dvāv ayācata bhartāraṁ satyasaṁdhaṁ nr̥pottamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
nādya bhokṣye na ca svapsye na pāsye ’haṁ kadā cana |
eṣa me jīvitasyānto rāmo yady abhiṣicyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
iti bruvāṇāṁ kaikeyīṁ śvaśuro me sa mānadaḥ |
ayācatārthair anvarthair na ca yācñāṁ cakāra sā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
mama bhartā mahātejā vayasā pañcaviṁśakaḥ |
rāmeti prathito loke guṇavān satyavāk śuciḥ |
viśālākṣo mahābāhuḥ sarvabhūtahite rataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
abhiṣekāya tu pituḥ samīpaṁ rāmam āgatam |
kaikeyī mama bhartāram ity uvāca drutaṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tava pitrā samājñaptaṁ mamedaṁ śr̥ṇu rāghava |
bharatāya pradātavyam idaṁ rājyam akaṇṭakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tvayā tu khalu vastavyaṁ nava varṣāṇi pañca ca |
vane pravraja kākutstha pitaraṁ mocayānr̥tāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tathety uvāca tāṁ rāmaḥ kaikeyīm akutobhayaḥ |
cakāra tad vacas tasyā mama bhartā dr̥ḍhavrataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
dadyān na pratigr̥hṇīyāt satyabrūyān na cānr̥tam |
etad brāhmaṇa rāmasya vrataṁ dhruvam anuttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tasya bhrātā tu vaimātro lakṣmaṇo nāma vīryavān |
rāmasya puruṣavyāghraḥ sahāyaḥ samare ’rihā ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
sa bhrātā lakṣmaṇo nāma dharmacārī dr̥ḍhavrataḥ |
anvagacchad dhanuṣpāṇiḥ pravrajantaṁ mayā saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
te vayaṁ pracyutā rājyāt kaileyyās tu kr̥te trayaḥ |
vicarāma dvijaśreṣṭha vanaṁ gambhīram ojasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
samāśvasa muhūrtaṁ tu śakyaṁ vastum iha tvayā |
āgamiṣyati me bhartā vanyam ādāya puṣkalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
sa tvaṁ nāma ca gotraṁ ca kulam ācakṣva tattvataḥ |
ekaś ca daṇḍakāraṇye kimarthaṁ carasi dvija ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
evaṁ bruvatyāṁ sītāyāṁ rāmapatnyāṁ mahābalaḥ |
pratyuvācottaraṁ tīvraṁ rāvaṇo rākṣasādhipaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
yena vitrāsitā lokāḥ sadevāsurapannagāḥ |
ahaṁ sa rāvaṇo nāma sīte rakṣogaṇeśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tvāṁ tu kāñcanavarṇābhāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā kauśeyavāsinīm |
ratiṁ svakeṣu dāreṣu nādhigacchāmy anindite ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
bahvīnām uttamastrīṇām āhr̥tānām itas tataḥ |
sarvāsām eva bhadraṁ te mamāgramahiṣī bhava ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
laṅkā nāma samudrasya madhye mama mahāpurī |
sāgareṇa parikṣiptā niviṣṭā girimūrdhani ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tatra sīte mayā sārdhaṁ vaneṣu vicariṣyasi |
na cāsyāraṇyavāsasya spr̥hayiṣyasi bhāmini ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
pañcadāsyaḥ sahasrāṇi sarvābharaṇabhūṣitāḥ |
sīte paricariṣyanti bhāryā bhavasi me yadi ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
rāvaṇenaivam uktā tu kupitā janakātmajā |
pratyuvācānavadyāṅgī tam anādr̥tya rākṣasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
mahāgirim ivākampyaṁ mahendrasadr̥śaṁ patim |
mahodadhim ivākṣobhyam ahaṁ rāmam anuvratā ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
mahābāhuṁ mahoraskaṁ siṁhavikrāntagāminam |
nr̥siṁhaṁ siṁhasaṁkāśam ahaṁ rāmam anuvratā ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
pūrṇacandrānanaṁ vīraṁ rājavatsaṁ jitendriyam |
pr̥thukīrtiṁ mahābāhum ahaṁ rāmam anuvratā ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
tvaṁ punar jambukaḥ siṁhīṁ mām ihecchasi durlabhām |
nāhaṁ śakyā tvayā spraṣṭum ādityasya prabhā yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
pādapān kāñcanān nūnaṁ bahūn paśyasi mandabhāk |
rāghavasya priyāṁ bhāryāṁ yas tvam icchasi rāvaṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
kṣudhitasya ca siṁhasya mr̥gaśatros tarasvinaḥ |
āśīviṣasya vadanād daṁṣṭrām ādātum icchasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
mandaraṁ parvataśreṣṭhaṁ pāṇinā hartum icchasi |
kālakūṭaṁ viṣaṁ pītvā svastimān gantum icchasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
akṣisūcyā pramr̥jasi jihvayā leḍhi ca kṣuram |
rāghavasya priyāṁ bhāryām adhigantuṁ tvam icchasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
avasajya śilāṁ kaṇṭhe samudraṁ tartum icchasi |
sūryā candramasau cobhau prāṇibhyāṁ hartum icchasi |
yo rāmasya priyāṁ bhāryāṁ pradharṣayitum icchasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
agniṁ prajvalitaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā vastreṇāhartum icchasi |
kalyāṇa vr̥ttāṁ rāmasya yo bhāryāṁ hartum icchasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
ayomukhānāṁ śūlānām agre caritum icchasi |
rāmasya sadr̥śīṁ bhāryāṁ yo ’dhigantuṁ tvam icchasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
yad antaraṁ siṁhaśr̥gālayor vane; yad antaraṁ syandanikāsamudrayoḥ |
surāgryasauvīrakayor yad antaraṁ; tad antaraṁ dāśarathes tavaiva ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
yad antaraṁ kāñcanasīsalohayor; yad antaraṁ candanavāripaṅkayoḥ |
yad antaraṁ hastibiḍālayor vane; tad antaraṁ daśarathes tavaiva ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
yad antaraṁ vāyasavainateyayor; yad antaraṁ madgumayūrayor api |
yad antaraṁ sārasagr̥dhrayor vane; tad antaraṁ dāśarathes tavaiva ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
tasmin sahasrākṣasamaprabhāve; rāme sthite kārmukabāṇapāṇau |
hr̥tāpi te ’haṁ na jarāṁ gamiṣye; vajraṁ yathā makṣikayāvagīrṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
itīva tad vākyam aduṣṭabhāvā; sudr̥ṣṭam uktvā rajanīcaraṁ tam |
gātraprakampād vyathitā babhūva; vātoddhatā sā kadalīva tanvī ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
tāṁ vepamānām upalakṣya sītāṁ; sa rāvaṇo mr̥tyusamaprabhāvaḥ |
kulaṁ balaṁ nāma ca karma cātmanaḥ; samācacakṣe bhayakāraṇārtham ||

=== meaning ===

---
