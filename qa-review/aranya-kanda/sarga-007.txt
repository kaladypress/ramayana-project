=== Sarga 7 ===

---
1.
rāmas tu sahasaumitriḥ sutīkṣṇenābhipūjitaḥ |
pariṇamya niśāṁ tatra prabhāte pratyabudhyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
utthāya tu yathākālaṁ rāghavaḥ saha sītayā |
upāspr̥śat suśītena jalenotpalagandhinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
atha te ’gniṁ surāṁś caiva vaidehī rāmalakṣmaṇau |
kālyaṁ vidhivad abhyarcya tapasviśaraṇe vane ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
udayanntaṁ dinakaraṁ dr̥ṣṭvā vigatakalmaṣāḥ |
sutīkṣṇam abhigamyedaṁ ślakṣṇaṁ vacanam abruvan ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
sukhoṣitāḥ sma bhagavaṁs tvayā pūjyena pūjitāḥ |
āpr̥cchāmaḥ prayāsyāmo munayas tvarayanti naḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tvarāmahe vayaṁ draṣṭuṁ kr̥tsnam āśramamaṇḍalam |
r̥ṣīṇāṁ puṇyaśīlānāṁ daṇḍakāraṇyavāsinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
abhyanujñātum icchāmaḥ sahaibhir munipuṅgavaiḥ |
dharmanityais tapodāntair viśikhair iva pāvakaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
aviṣahyātapo yāvat sūryo nātivirājite |
amārgeṇāgatāṁ lakṣmīṁ prāpyevānvayavarjitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tāvad icchāmahe gantum ity uktvā caraṇau muneḥ |
vavande sahasaumitriḥ sītayā saha rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tau saṁspr̥śantau caraṇāv utthāpya munipuṁgavaḥ |
gāḍham āliṅgya sasneham idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
ariṣṭaṁ gaccha panthānaṁ rāma saumitriṇā saha |
sītayā cānayā sārdhaṁ chāyayevānuvr̥ttayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
paśyāśramapadaṁ ramyaṁ daṇḍakāraṇyavāsinām |
eṣāṁ tapasvināṁ vīra tapasā bhāvitātmanām ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
suprājyaphalamūlāni puṣpitāni vanāni ca |
praśāntamr̥gayūthāni śāntapakṣigaṇāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
phullapaṅkajaṣaḍāni prasannasalilāni ca |
kāraṇḍavavikīrṇāni taṭākāni sarāṁsi ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
drakṣyase dr̥ṣṭiramyāṇi giriprasravaṇāni ca |
ramaṇīyāny araṇyāni mayūrābhirutāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
gamyatāṁ vatsa saumitre bhavān api ca gacchatu |
āgantavyaṁ ca te dr̥ṣṭvā punar evāśramaṁ mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
evam uktas tathety uktvā kākutsthaḥ sahalakṣmaṇaḥ |
pradakṣiṇaṁ muniṁ kr̥tā prasthātum upacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tataḥ śubhatare tūṇī dhanuṣī cāyatekṣaṇā |
dadau sītā tayor bhrātroḥ khaḍgau ca vimalau tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
ābadhya ca śubhe tūṇī cāpe cādāya sasvane |
niṣkrāntāv āśramād gantum ubhau tau rāmalakṣmaṇau ||

=== meaning ===

---
