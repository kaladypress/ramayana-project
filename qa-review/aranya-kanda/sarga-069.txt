=== Sarga 69 ===

---
1.
nidarśayitvā rāmāya sītāyāḥ pratipādane |
vākyam anvartham arthajñaḥ kabandhaḥ punar abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
eṣa rāma śivaḥ panthā yatraite puṣpitā drumāḥ |
pratīcīṁ diśam āśritya prakāśante manoramāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
jambūpriyālapanasāḥ plakṣanyagrodhatindukāḥ |
aśvatthāḥ karṇikārāś ca cūtāś cānye ca pādapāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tān āruhyātha vā bhūmau pātayitvā ca tān balāt |
phalāny amr̥takalpāni bhakṣayantau gamiṣyathaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
caṅkramantau varān deśāñ śailāc chailaṁ vanād vanam |
tataḥ puṣkariṇīṁ vīrau pampāṁ nāma gamiṣyathaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
aśarkarām avibhraṁśāṁ samatīrtham aśaivalām |
rāma saṁjātavālūkāṁ kamalotpalaśobhitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tatra haṁsāḥ plavāḥ krauñcāḥ kurarāś caiva rāghava |
valgusvarā nikūjanti pampāsalilagocarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
nodvijante narān dr̥ṣṭvā vadhasyākovidāḥ śubhāḥ |
ghr̥tapiṇḍopamān sthūlāṁs tān dvijān bhakṣayiṣyathaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
rohitān vakratuṇḍāṁś ca nalamīnāṁś ca rāghava |
pampāyām iṣubhir matsyāṁs tatra rāma varān hatān ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
nistvakpakṣān ayastaptān akr̥śān ekakaṇṭakān |
tava bhaktyā samāyukto lakṣmaṇaḥ saṁpradāsyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
bhr̥śaṁ te khādato matsyān pampāyāḥ puṣpasaṁcaye |
padmagandhi śivaṁ vāri sukhaśītam anāmayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
uddhr̥tya sa tadākliṣṭaṁ rūpyasphaṭikasaṁnibham |
atha puṣkaraparṇena lakṣmaṇaḥ pāyayiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sthūlān giriguhāśayyān varāhān vanacāriṇaḥ |
apāṁ lobhād upāvr̥ttān vr̥ṣabhān iva nardataḥ |
rūpānvitāṁś ca pampāyāṁ drakṣyasi tvaṁ narottama ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sāyāhne vicaran rāma viṭapī mālyadhāriṇaḥ |
śītodakaṁ ca pampāyāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā śokaṁ vihāsyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sumanobhiś citāṁs tatra tilakān naktamālakān |
utpalāni ca phullāni paṅkajāni ca rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
na tāni kaś cin mālyāni tatrāropayitā naraḥ |
mataṅgaśiṣyās tatrāsann r̥ṣayaḥ susamāhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
teṣāṁ bhārābhitaptānāṁ vanyam āharatāṁ guroḥ |
ye prapetur mahīṁ tūrṇaṁ śarīrāt svedabindavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tāni mālyāni jātāni munīnāṁ tapasā tadā |
svedabindusamutthāni na vinaśyanti rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
teṣām adyāpi tatraiva dr̥śyate paricāriṇī |
śramaṇī śabarī nāma kākutstha cirajīvinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tvāṁ tu dharme sthitā nityaṁ sarvabhūtanamaskr̥tam |
dr̥ṣṭvā devopamaṁ rāma svargalokaṁ gamiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tatas tad rāma pampāyās tīram āśritya paścimam |
āśramasthānam atulaṁ guhyaṁ kākutstha paśyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
na tatrākramituṁ nāgāḥ śaknuvanti tam āśramam |
r̥ṣes tasya mataṅgasya vidhānāt tac ca kānanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tasmin nandanasaṁkāśe devāraṇyopame vane |
nānāvihagasaṁkīrṇe raṁsyase rāma nirvr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
r̥ṣyamūkas tu pampāyāḥ purastāt puṣpitadrumaḥ |
suduḥkhārohaṇo nāma śiśunāgābhirakṣitaḥ |
udāro brahmaṇā caiva pūrvakāle vinirmitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
śayānaḥ puruṣo rāma tasya śailasya mūrdhani |
yat svapne labhate vittaṁ tat prabuddho ’dhigacchati ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
na tv enaṁ viṣamācāraḥ pāpakarmādhirohati |
tatraiva praharanty enaṁ suptam ādāya rākṣasāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tato ’pi śiśunāgānām ākrandaḥ śrūyate mahān |
krīḍatāṁ rāma pampāyāṁ mataṅgāraṇyavāsinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
siktā rudhiradhārābhiḥ saṁhatya paramadvipāḥ |
pracaranti pr̥thak kīrṇā meghavarṇās tarasvinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
te tatra pītvā pānīyaṁ vimalaṁ śītam avyayam |
nivr̥ttāḥ saṁvigāhante vanāni vanagocarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
rāma tasya tu śailasya mahatī śobhate guhā |
śilāpidhānā kākutstha duḥkhaṁ cāsyāḥ praveśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
tasyā guhāyāḥ prāgdvāre mahāñ śītodako hradaḥ |
bahumūlaphalo ramyo nānānagasamāvr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
tasyāṁ vasati sugrīvaś caturbhiḥ saha vānaraiḥ |
kadā cic chikhare tasya parvatasyāvatiṣṭhate ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
kabandhas tv anuśāsyaivaṁ tāv ubhau rāmalakṣmaṇau |
sragvī bhāskaravarṇābhaḥ khe vyarocata vīryavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
taṁ tu khasthaṁ mahābhāgaṁ kabandhaṁ rāmalakṣmaṇau |
prasthitau tvaṁ vrajasveti vākyam ūcatur antikāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
gamyatāṁ kāryasiddhyartham iti tāv abravīc ca saḥ |
suprītau tāv anujñāpya kabandhaḥ prasthitas tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
sa tat kabandhaḥ pratipadya rūpaṁ; vr̥taḥ śriyā bhāskaratulyadehaḥ |
nidarśayan rāmam avekṣya khasthaḥ; sakhyaṁ kuruṣveti tadābhyuvāca ||

=== meaning ===

---
