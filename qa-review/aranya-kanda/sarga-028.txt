=== Sarga 28 ===

---
1.
kharaṁ tu virathaṁ rāmo gadāpāṇim avasthitam |
mr̥dupūrvaṁ mahātejāḥ paruṣaṁ vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
gajāśvarathasaṁbādhe bale mahati tiṣṭhatā |
kr̥taṁ sudāruṇaṁ karma sarvalokajugupsitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
udvejanīyo bhūtānāṁ nr̥śaṁsaḥ pāpakarmakr̥t |
trayāṇām api lokānām īśvaro ’pi na tiṣṭhati ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
karma lokaviruddhaṁ tu kurvāṇaṁ kṣaṇadācara |
tīkṣṇaṁ sarvajano hanti sarpaṁ duṣṭam ivāgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
lobhāt pāpāni kurvāṇaḥ kāmād vā yo na budhyate |
bhraṣṭaḥ paśyati tasyāntaṁ brāhmaṇī karakād iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
vasato daṇḍakāraṇye tāpasān dharmacāriṇaḥ |
kiṁ nu hatvā mahābhāgān phalaṁ prāpsyasi rākṣasa ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
na ciraṁ pāpakarmāṇaḥ krūrā lokajugupsitāḥ |
aiśvaryaṁ prāpya tiṣṭhanti śīrṇamūlā iva drumāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
avaśyaṁ labhate kartā phalaṁ pāpasya karmaṇaḥ |
ghoraṁ paryāgate kāle drumaḥ puṣpam ivārtavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
nacirāt prāpyate loke pāpānāṁ karmaṇāṁ phalam |
saviṣāṇām ivānnānāṁ bhuktānāṁ kṣaṇadācara ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
pāpam āccaratāṁ ghoraṁ lokasyāpriyam icchatām |
aham āsādito rājā prāṇān hantuṁ niśācara ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
adya hi tvāṁ mayā muktāḥ śarāḥ kāñcanabhūṣaṇāḥ |
vidārya nipatiṣyanti valmīkam iva pannagāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
ye tvayā daṇḍakāraṇye bhakṣitā dharmacāriṇaḥ |
tān adya nihataḥ saṁkhye sasainyo ’nugamiṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
adya tvāṁ nihataṁ bāṇaiḥ paśyantu paramarṣayaḥ |
nirayasthaṁ vimānasthā ye tvayā hiṁsitāḥ purā ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
prahara tvaṁ yathākāmaṁ kuru yatnaṁ kulādhama |
adya te pātayiṣyāmi śiras tālaphalaṁ yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
evam uktas tu rāmeṇa kruddhaḥ saṁraktalocanaḥ |
pratyuvāca tato rāmaṁ prahasan krodhamūrchitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
prākr̥tān rākṣasān hatvā yuddhe daśarathātmaja |
ātmanā katham ātmānam apraśasyaṁ praśaṁsasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
vikrāntā balavanto vā ye bhavanti nararṣabhāḥ |
kathayanti na te kiṁ cit tejasā svena garvitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
prākr̥tās tv akr̥tātmāno loke kṣatriyapāṁsanāḥ |
nirarthakaṁ vikatthante yathā rāma vikatthase ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
kulaṁ vyapadiśan vīraḥ samare ko ’bhidhāsyati |
mr̥tyukāle hi saṁprāpte svayam aprastave stavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
sarvathā tu laghutvaṁ te katthanena vidarśitam |
suvarṇapratirūpeṇa tapteneva kuśāgninā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
na tu mām iha tiṣṭhantaṁ paśyasi tvaṁ gadādharam |
dharādharam ivākampyaṁ parvataṁ dhātubhiś citam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
paryāpto ’haṁ gadāpāṇir hantuṁ prāṇān raṇe tava |
trayāṇām api lokānāṁ pāśahasta ivāntakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
kāmaṁ bahv api vaktavyaṁ tvayi vakṣyāmi na tv aham |
astaṁ gacched dhi savitā yuddhavighras tato bhavet ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
caturdaśa sahasrāṇi rākṣasānāṁ hatāni te |
tvadvināśāt karomy adya teṣām aśrupramārjanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
ity uktvā paramakruddhas tāṁ gadāṁ paramāṅgadām |
kharaś cikṣepa rāmāya pradīptām aśaniṁ yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
kharabāhupramuktā sā pradīptā mahatī gadā |
bhasmavr̥kṣāṁś ca gulmāṁś ca kr̥tvāgāt tatsamīpataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tām āpatantīṁ jvalitāṁ mr̥tyupāśopamāṁ gadā |
antarikṣagatāṁ rāmaś ciccheda bahudhā śaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
sā viśīrṇā śarair bhinnā papāta dharaṇītale |
gadāmantrauṣadhibalair vyālīva vinipātitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
