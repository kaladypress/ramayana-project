=== Sarga 2 ===

---
1.
kr̥tātithyo ’tha rāmas tu sūryasyodayanaṁ prati |
āmantrya sa munīn sarvān vanam evānvagāhata ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
nānāmr̥gagaṇākīrṇaṁ śārdūlavr̥kasevitam |
dhvastavr̥kṣalatāgulmaṁ durdarśa salilāśayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
niṣkūjanānāśakuni jhillikā gaṇanāditam |
lakṣmaṇānugato rāmo vanamadhyaṁ dadarśa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
vanamadhye tu kākutsthas tasmin ghoramr̥gāyute |
dadarśa giriśr̥ṅgābhaṁ puruṣādaṁ mahāsvanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
gabhīrākṣaṁ mahāvaktraṁ vikaṭaṁ viṣamodaram |
bībhatsaṁ viṣamaṁ dīrghaṁ vikr̥taṁ ghoradarśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
vasānaṁ carmavaiyāghraṁ vasārdraṁ rudhirokṣitam |
trāsanaṁ sarvabhūtānāṁ vyāditāsyam ivāntakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
trīn siṁhāṁś caturo vyāghrān dvau vr̥kau pr̥ṣatān daśa |
saviṣāṇaṁ vasādigdhaṁ gajasya ca śiro mahat ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
avasajyāyase śūle vinadantaṁ mahāsvanam |
sa rāmaṁ lakṣmaṇaṁ caiva sītāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā ca maithilīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
abhyadhāvat susaṁkruddhaḥ prajāḥ kāla ivāntakaḥ |
sa kr̥tvā bhairavaṁ nādaṁ cālayann iva medinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
aṅgenādāya vaidehīm apakramya tato ’bravīt |
yuvāṁ jaṭācīradharau sabhāryau kṣīṇajīvitau ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
praviṣṭau daṇḍakāraṇyaṁ śaracāpāsidhāriṇau |
kathaṁ tāpasayor vāṁ ca vāsaḥ pramadayā saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
adharmacāriṇau pāpau kau yuvāṁ munidūṣakau |
ahaṁ vanam idaṁ durgaṁ virāgho nāma rākṣasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
carāmi sāyudho nityam r̥ṣimāṁsāni bhakṣayan |
iyaṁ nārī varārohā mama bharyā bhaviṣyati |
yuvayoḥ pāpayoś cāhaṁ pāsyāmi rudhiraṁ mr̥dhe ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tasyaivaṁ bruvato dhr̥ṣṭaṁ virādhasya durātmanaḥ |
śrutvā sagarvitaṁ vākyaṁ saṁbhrāntā janakātmajā |
sītā prāvepatodvegāt pravāte kadalī yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rāghavaḥ sītāṁ virādhāṅkagatāṁ śubhām |
abravīl lakṣmaṇaṁ vākyaṁ mukhena pariśuṣyatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
paśya saumya narendrasya janakasyātmasaṁbhavām |
mama bhāryāṁ śubhācārāṁ virādhāṅke praveśitām |
atyanta sukhasaṁvr̥ddhāṁ rājaputrīṁ yaśasvinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
yad abhipretam asmāsu priyaṁ vara vr̥taṁ ca yat |
kaikeyyās tu susaṁvr̥ttaṁ kṣipram adyaiva lakṣmaṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
yā na tuṣyati rājyena putrārthe dīrghadarśinī |
yayāhaṁ sarvabhūtānāṁ hitaḥ prasthāpito vanam |
adyedānīṁ sakāmā sā yā mātā mama madhyamā ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
parasparśāt tu vaidehyā na duḥkhataram asti me |
pitur vināśāt saumitre svarājyaharaṇāt tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
iti bruvati kākutsthe bāṣpaśokapariplute |
abravīl lakṣmaṇaḥ kruddho ruddho nāga iva śvasan ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
anātha iva bhūtānāṁ nāthas tvaṁ vāsavopamaḥ |
mayā preṣyeṇa kākutstha kimarthaṁ paritapsyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
śareṇa nihatasyādya mayā kruddhena rakṣasaḥ |
virādhasya gatāsor hi mahī pāsyati śoṇitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
rājyakāme mama krodho bharate yo babhūva ha |
taṁ virādhe vimokṣyāmi vajrī vajram ivācale ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
mama bhujabalavegavegitaḥ; patatu śaro ’sya mahān mahorasi |
vyapanayatu tanoś ca jīvitaṁ; patatu tataś ca mahīṁ vighūrṇitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
