=== Sarga 49 ===

---
1.
ity uktasya yathānyāyaṁ rāvaṇasya jaṭāyuṣā |
kruddhasyāgninibhāḥ sarvā rejur viṁśatidr̥ṣṭayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
saṁraktanayanaḥ kopāt taptakāñcanakuṇḍalaḥ |
rākṣasendro ’bhidudrāva patagendram amarṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sa saṁprahāras tumulas tayos tasmin mahāvane |
babhūva vātoddhatayor meghayor gagane yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tad babhūvādbhutaṁ yuddhaṁ gr̥dhrarākṣasayos tadā |
sapakṣayor mālyavator mahāparvatayor iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tato nālīkanārācais tīkṣṇāgraiś ca vikarṇibhiḥ |
abhyavarṣan mahāghorair gr̥dhrarājaṁ mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
sa tāni śarajālāni gr̥dhraḥ patraratheśvaraḥ |
jaṭāyuḥ pratijagrāha rāvaṇāstrāṇi saṁyuge ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tasya tīkṣṇanakhābhyāṁ tu caraṇābhyāṁ mahābalaḥ |
cakāra bahudhā gātre vraṇān patagasattamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
atha krodhād daśagrīvo jagrāha daśamārgaṇān |
mr̥tyudaṇḍanibhān ghorāñ śatrumardanakāṅkṣayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sa tair bāṇair mahāvīryaḥ pūrṇamuktair ajihmagaiḥ |
bibheda niśitais tīkṣṇair gr̥dhraṁ ghoraiḥ śilīmukhaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
sa rākṣasarathe paśyañ jānakīṁ bāṣpalocanām |
acintayitvā bāṇāṁs tān rākṣasaṁ samabhidravat ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tato ’sya saśaraṁ cāpaṁ muktāmaṇivibhūṣitam |
caraṇābhyāṁ mahātejā babhañja patageśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tac cāgnisadr̥śaṁ dīptaṁ rāvaṇasya śarāvaram |
pakṣābhyāṁ ca mahātejā vyadhunot patageśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
kāñcanoraśchadān divyān piśācavadanān kharān |
tāṁś cāsya javasaṁpannāñ jaghāna samare balī ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
varaṁ triveṇusaṁpannaṁ kāmagaṁ pāvakārciṣam |
maṇihemavicitrāṅgaṁ babhañja ca mahāratham |
pūrṇacandrapratīkāśaṁ chatraṁ ca vyajanaiḥ saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa bhagnadhanvā viratho hatāśvo hatasārathiḥ |
aṅkenādāya vaidehīṁ papāta bhuvi rāvaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
dr̥ṣṭvā nipatitaṁ bhūmau rāvaṇaṁ bhagnavāhanam |
sādhu sādhv iti bhūtāni gr̥dhrarājam apūjayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
pariśrāntaṁ tu taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā jarayā pakṣiyūthapam |
utpapāta punar hr̥ṣṭo maithilīṁ gr̥hya rāvaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
taṁ prahr̥ṣṭaṁ nidhāyāṅke gacchantaṁ janakātmajām |
gr̥dhrarājaḥ samutpatya jaṭāyur idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
vajrasaṁsparśabāṇasya bhāryāṁ rāmasya rāvaṇa |
alpabuddhe harasy enāṁ vadhāya khalu rakṣasām ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
samitrabandhuḥ sāmātyaḥ sabalaḥ saparicchadaḥ |
viṣapānaṁ pibasy etat pipāsita ivodakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
anubandham ajānantaḥ karmaṇām avicakṣaṇāḥ |
śīghram eva vinaśyanti yathā tvaṁ vinaśiṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
baddhas tvaṁ kālapāśena kva gatas tasya mokṣyase |
vadhāya baḍiśaṁ gr̥hya sāmiṣaṁ jalajo yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
na hi jātu durādharṣau kākutsthau tava rāvaṇa |
dharṣaṇaṁ cāśramasyāsya kṣamiṣyete tu rāghavau ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
yathā tvayā kr̥taṁ karma bhīruṇā lokagarhitam |
taskarācarito mārgo naiṣa vīraniṣevitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
yudhyasva yadi śūro ’si muhūrtaṁ tiṣṭha rāvaṇa |
śayiṣyase hato bhūmau yathā bhrātā kharas tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
paretakāle puruṣo yat karma pratipadyate |
vināśāyātmano ’dharmyaṁ pratipanno ’si karma tat ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
pāpānubandho vai yasya karmaṇaḥ ko nu tat pumān |
kurvīta lokādhipatiḥ svayambhūr bhagavān api ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
evam uktvā śubhaṁ vākyaṁ jaṭāyus tasya rakṣasaḥ |
nipapāta bhr̥śaṁ pr̥ṣṭhe daśagrīvasya vīryavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
taṁ gr̥hītvā nakhais tīkṣṇair virarāda samantataḥ |
adhirūḍho gajārohi yathā syād duṣṭavāraṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
virarāda nakhair asya tuṇḍaṁ pr̥ṣṭhe samarpayan |
keśāṁś cotpāṭayām āsa nakhapakṣamukhāyudhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
sa tathā gr̥dhrarājena kliśyamāno muhur muhuḥ |
amarṣasphuritauṣṭhaḥ san prākampata sa rākṣasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
saṁpariṣvajya vaidehīṁ vāmenāṅkena rāvaṇaḥ |
talenābhijaghānārto jaṭāyuṁ krodhamūrchitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
jaṭāyus tam atikramya tuṇḍenāsya kharādhipaḥ |
vāmabāhūn daśa tadā vyapāharad ariṁdamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
tataḥ kruddho daśakrīvaḥ sītām utsr̥jya vīryavān |
muṣṭibhyāṁ caraṇābhyāṁ ca gr̥dhrarājam apothayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
tato muhūrtaṁ saṁgrāmo babhūvātulavīryayoḥ |
rākṣasānāṁ ca mukhyasya pakṣiṇāṁ pravarasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
tasya vyāyacchamānasya rāmasyārthe ’tha rāvaṇaḥ |
pakṣau pādau ca pārśvau ca khaḍgam uddhr̥tya so ’cchinat ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
sa chinnapakṣaḥ sahasā rakṣasā raudrakarmaṇā |
nipapāta hato gr̥dhro dharaṇyām alpajīvitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā patitaṁ bhūmau kṣatajārdraṁ jaṭāyuṣam |
abhyadhāvata vaidehī svabandhum iva duḥkhitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
taṁ nīlajīmūtanikāśakalpaṁ; supāṇḍuroraskam udāravīryam |
dadarśa laṅkādhipatiḥ pr̥thivyāṁ; jaṭāyuṣaṁ śāntam ivāgnidāvam ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
tatas tu taṁ patrarathaṁ mahītale; nipātitaṁ rāvaṇavegamarditam |
punaḥ pariṣvajya śaśiprabhānanā; ruroda sītā janakātmajā tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
