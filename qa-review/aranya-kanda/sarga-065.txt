=== Sarga 65 ===

---
1.
kr̥tvaivam udakaṁ tasmai prasthitau rāghavau tadā |
avekṣantau vane sītāṁ paścimāṁ jagmatur diśam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tāṁ diśaṁ dakṣiṇāṁ gatvā śaracāpāsidhāriṇau |
aviprahatam aikṣvākau panthānaṁ pratipedatuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
gulmair vr̥kṣaiś ca bahubhir latābhiś ca praveṣṭitam |
āvr̥taṁ sarvato durgaṁ gahanaṁ ghoradarśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
vyatikramya tu vegena gr̥hītvā dakṣiṇāṁ diśam |
subhīmaṁ tan mahāraṇyaṁ vyatiyātau mahābalau ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tataḥ paraṁ janasthānāt trikrośaṁ gamya rāghavau |
krauñcāraṇyaṁ viviśatur gahanaṁ tau mahaujasau ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
nānāmeghaghanaprakhyaṁ prahr̥ṣṭam iva sarvataḥ |
nānāvarṇaiḥ śubhaiḥ puṣpair mr̥gapakṣigaṇair yutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
didr̥kṣamāṇau vaidehīṁ tad vanaṁ tau vicikyatuḥ |
tatra tatrāvatiṣṭhantau sītāharaṇakarśitau ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
lakṣmaṇas tu mahātejāḥ sattvavāñ śīlavāñ śuciḥ |
abravīt prāñjalir vākyaṁ bhrātaraṁ dīptatejasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
spandate me dr̥ḍhaṁ bāhur udvignam iva me manaḥ |
prāyaśaś cāpy aniṣṭāni nimittāny upalakṣaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tasmāt sajjībhavārya tvaṁ kuruṣva vacanaṁ hitam |
mamaiva hi nimittāni sadyaḥ śaṁsanti saṁbhramam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
eṣa vañculako nāma pakṣī paramadāruṇaḥ |
āvayor vijayaṁ yuddhe śaṁsann iva vinardati ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tayor anveṣator evaṁ sarvaṁ tad vanam ojasā |
saṁjajñe vipulaḥ śabdaḥ prabhañjann iva tad vanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
saṁveṣṭitam ivātyarthaṁ gahanaṁ mātariśvanā |
vanasya tasya śabdo ’bhūd divam āpūrayann iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
taṁ śabdaṁ kāṅkṣamāṇas tu rāmaḥ kakṣe sahānujaḥ |
dadarśa sumahākāyaṁ rākṣasaṁ vipulorasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
āsedatus tatas tatra tāv ubhau pramukhe sthitam |
vivr̥ddham aśirogrīvaṁ kabandham udare mukham ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
romabhir nicitais tīkṣṇair mahāgirim ivocchritam |
nīlameghanibhaṁ raudraṁ meghastanitaniḥsvanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
mahāpakṣmeṇa piṅgena vipulenāyatena ca |
ekenorasi ghoreṇa nayanenāśudarśinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
mahādaṁṣṭropapannaṁ taṁ lelihānaṁ mahāmukham |
bhakṣayantaṁ mahāghorān r̥kṣasiṁhamr̥gadvipān ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
ghorau bhujau vikurvāṇam ubhau yojanam āyatau |
karābhyāṁ vividhān gr̥hya r̥ṣkān pakṣigaṇān mr̥gān ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
ākarṣantaṁ vikarṣantam anekān mr̥gayūthapān |
sthitam āvr̥tya panthānaṁ tayor bhrātroḥ prapannayoḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
atha tau samatikramya krośamātre dadarśatuḥ |
mahāntaṁ dāruṇaṁ bhīmaṁ kabandhaṁ bhujasaṁvr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
sa mahābāhur atyarthaṁ prasārya vipulau bhujau |
jagrāha sahitāv eva rāghavau pīḍayan balāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
khaḍginau dr̥ḍhadhanvānau tigmatejau mahābhujau |
bhrātarau vivaśaṁ prāptau kr̥ṣyamāṇau mahābalau ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tāv uvāca mahābāhuḥ kabandho dānavottamaḥ |
kau yuvāṁ vr̥ṣabhaskandhau mahākhaḍgadhanurdharau ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
ghoraṁ deśam imaṁ prāptau mama bhakṣāv upasthitau |
vadataṁ kāryam iha vāṁ kimarthaṁ cāgatau yuvām ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
imaṁ deśam anuprāptau kṣudhārtasyeha tiṣṭhataḥ |
sabāṇacāpakhaḍgau ca tīkṣṇaśr̥ṅgāv ivarṣabhau |
mamāsyam anusaṁprāptau durlabhaṁ jīvitaṁ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā kabandhasya durātmanaḥ |
uvāca lakṣmaṇaṁ rāmo mukhena pariśuṣyatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
kr̥cchrāt kr̥cchrataraṁ prāpya dāruṇaṁ satyavikrama |
vyasanaṁ jīvitāntāya prāptam aprāpya tāṁ priyām ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
kālasya sumahad vīryaṁ sarvabhūteṣu lakṣmaṇa |
tvāṁ ca māṁ ca naravyāghra vyasanaiḥ paśya mohitau |
nātibhāro ’sti daivasya sarvabhuteṣu lakṣmaṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
śūrāś ca balavantaś ca kr̥tāstrāś ca raṇājire |
kālābhipannāḥ sīdanti yathā vālukasetavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
iti bruvāṇo dr̥ḍhasatyavikramo; mahāyaśā dāśarathiḥ pratāpavān |
avekṣya saumitrim udagravikramaṁ; sthirāṁ tadā svāṁ matim ātmanākarot ||

=== meaning ===

---
