=== Sarga 29 ===

---
1.
bhittvā tu tāṁ gadāṁ bāṇai rāghavo dharmavatsalaḥ |
smayamānaḥ kharaṁ vākyaṁ saṁrabdham idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
etat te balasarvasvaṁ darśitaṁ rākṣasādhama |
śaktihīnataro matto vr̥thā tvam upagarjitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
eṣā bāṇavinirbhinnā gadā bhūmitalaṁ gatā |
abhidhānapragalbhasya tava pratyayaghātinī ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
yat tvayoktaṁ vinaṣṭānām idam aśrupramārjanam |
rākṣasānāṁ karomīti mithyā tad api te vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
nīcasya kṣudraśīlasya mithyāvr̥ttasya rakṣasaḥ |
prāṇān apahariṣyāmi garutmān amr̥taṁ yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
adya te bhinnakaṇṭhasya phenabudbudabhūṣitam |
vidāritasya madbāṇair mahī pāsyati śoṇitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
pāṁsurūṣitasarvāṅgaḥ srastanyastabhujadvayaḥ |
svapsyase gāṁ samāśliṣya durlabhāṁ pramadām iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
pravr̥ddhanidre śayite tvayi rākṣasapāṁsane |
bhaviṣyanty aśaraṇyānāṁ śaraṇyā daṇḍakā ime ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
janasthāne hatasthāne tava rākṣasamaccharaiḥ |
nirbhayā vicariṣyanti sarvato munayo vane ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
adya viprasariṣyanti rākṣasyo hatabāndhavāḥ |
bāṣpārdravadanā dīnā bhayād anyabhayāvahāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
adya śokarasajñās tā bhaviṣyanti niśācara |
anurūpakulāḥ patnyo yāsāṁ tvaṁ patir īdr̥śaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
nr̥śaṁsaśīla kṣudrātman nityaṁ brāhmaṇakaṇṭaka |
tvatkr̥te śaṅkitair agnau munibhiḥ pātyate haviḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tam evam abhisaṁrabdhaṁ bruvāṇaṁ rāghavaṁ raṇe |
kharo nirbhartsayām āsa roṣāt kharatara svanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
dr̥ḍhaṁ khalv avalipto ’si bhayeṣv api ca nirbhayaḥ |
vācyāvācyaṁ tato hi tvaṁ mr̥tyuvaśyo na budhyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
kālapāśaparikṣiptā bhavanti puruṣā hi ye |
kāryākāryaṁ na jānanti te nirastaṣaḍindriyāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
evam uktvā tato rāmaṁ saṁrudhya bhr̥kuṭiṁ tataḥ |
sa dadarśa mahāsālam avidūre niśācaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
raṇe praharaṇasyārthe sarvato hy avalokayan |
sa tam utpāṭayām āsa saṁdr̥śya daśanacchadam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
taṁ samutkṣipya bāhubhyāṁ vinarditvā mahābalaḥ |
rāmam uddiśya cikṣepa hatas tvam iti cābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tam āpatantaṁ bāṇaughaiś chittvā rāmaḥ pratāpavān |
roṣam āhārayat tīvraṁ nihantuṁ samare kharam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
jātasvedas tato rāmo roṣād raktāntalocanaḥ |
nirbibheda sahasreṇa bāṇānāṁ samare kharam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tasya bāṇāntarād raktaṁ bahu susrāva phenilam |
gireḥ prasravaṇasyeva toyadhārāparisravaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
vihvalaḥ sa kr̥to bāṇaiḥ kharo rāmeṇa saṁyuge |
matto rudhiragandhena tam evābhyadravad drutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tam āpatantaṁ saṁrabdhaṁ kr̥tāstro rudhirāplutam |
apasarpat pratipadaṁ kiṁ cit tvaritavikramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tataḥ pāvakasaṁkāśaṁ badhāya samare śaram |
kharasya rāmo jagrāha brahmadaṇḍam ivāparam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
sa tad dattaṁ maghavatā surarājena dhīmatā |
saṁdadhe ca sa dharmātmā mumoca ca kharaṁ prati ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
sa vimukto mahābāṇo nirghātasamaniḥsvanaḥ |
rāmeṇa dhanur udyamya kharasyorasi cāpatat ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
sa papāta kharo bhūmau dahyamānaḥ śarāgninā |
rudreṇaiva vinirdagdhaḥ śvetāraṇye yathāndhakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
sa vr̥tra iva vajreṇa phenena namucir yathā |
balo vendrāśanihato nipapāta hataḥ kharaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
tato rājarṣayaḥ sarve saṁgatāḥ paramarṣayaḥ |
sabhājya muditā rāmam idaṁ vacanam abruvan ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
etadarthaṁ mahātejā mahendraḥ pākaśāsanaḥ |
śarabhaṅgāśramaṁ puṇyam ājagāma puraṁdaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
ānītas tvam imaṁ deśam upāyena maharṣibhiḥ |
eṣāṁ vadhārthaṁ krūrāṇāṁ rakṣasāṁ pāpakarmaṇām ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
tad idaṁ naḥ kr̥taṁ kāryaṁ tvayā daśarathātmaja |
sukhaṁ dharmaṁ cariṣyanti daṇḍakeṣu maharṣayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
etasminn antare vīro lakṣmaṇaḥ saha sītayā |
giridurgād viniṣkramya saṁviveśāśramaṁ sukhī ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
tato rāmas tu vijayī pūjyamāno maharṣibhiḥ |
praviveśāśramaṁ vīro lakṣmaṇenābhivāditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā śatruhantāraṁ maharṣīṇāṁ sukhāvaham |
babhūva hr̥ṣṭā vaidehī bhartāraṁ pariṣasvaje ||

=== meaning ===

---
