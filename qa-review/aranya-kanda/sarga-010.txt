=== Sarga 10 ===

---
1.
agrataḥ prayayau rāmaḥ sītā madhye sumadhyamā |
pr̥ṣṭhatas tu dhanuṣpāṇir lakṣmaṇo ’nujagāma ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tau paśyamānau vividhāñ śailaprasthān vanāni ca |
nadīś ca vividhā ramyā jagmatuḥ saha sītayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sārasāṁś cakravākāṁś ca nadīpulinacāriṇaḥ |
sarāṁsi ca sapadmāni yutāni jalajaiḥ khagaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
yūthabaddhāṁś ca pr̥ṣatān madonmattān viṣāṇinaḥ |
mahiṣāṁś ca varāhāṁś ca gajāṁś ca drumavairiṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
te gatvā dūram adhvānaṁ lambamāne divākare |
dadr̥śuḥ sahitā ramyaṁ taṭākaṁ yojanāyatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
padmapuṣkarasaṁbādhaṁ gajayūthair alaṁkr̥tam |
sārasair haṁsakādambaiḥ saṁkulaṁ jalacāribhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
prasannasalile ramyatasmin sarasi śuśruve |
gītavāditranirghoṣo na tu kaś cana dr̥śyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tataḥ kautūhalād rāmo lakṣmaṇaś ca mahārathaḥ |
muniṁ dharmabhr̥taṁ nāma praṣṭuṁ samupacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
idam atyadbhutaṁ śrutvā sarveṣāṁ no mahāmune |
kautūhalaṁ mahaj jātaṁ kim idaṁ sādhu kathyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tenaivam ukto dharmātmā rāghaveṇa munis tadā |
prabhāvaṁ sarasaḥ kr̥tsnam ākhyātum upacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
idaṁ pañcāpsaro nāma taṭākaṁ sārvakālikam |
nirmitaṁ tapasā rāma muninā māṇḍakarṇinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sa hi tepe tapas tīvraṁ māṇḍakarṇir mahāmuniḥ |
daśavarṣasahasrāṇi vāyubhakṣo jalāśraya ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tataḥ pravyathitāḥ sarve devāḥ sāgnipurogamāḥ |
abruvan vacanaṁ sarve paraspara samāgatāḥ |
asmakaṁ kasya cit sthānam eṣa prārthayate muniḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tataḥ kartuṁ tapovighnaṁ sarvair devair niyojitāḥ |
pradhānāpsarasaḥ pañcavidyuccalitavarcasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
apsarobhis tatas tābhir munir dr̥ṣṭaparāvaraḥ |
nīto madanavaśyatvaṁ surāṇāṁ kāryasiddhaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tāś caivāpsarasaḥ pañcamuneḥ patnītvam āgatāḥ |
taṭāke nirmitaṁ tāsām asminn antarhitaṁ gr̥ham ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tatraivāpsarasaḥ pañcanivasantyo yathāsukham |
ramayanti tapoyogān muniṁ yauvanam āsthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tāsāṁ saṁkrīḍamānānām eṣa vāditraniḥsvanaḥ |
śrūyate bhūṣaṇonmiśro gītaśabdo manoharaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
āścaryam iti tasyaitad vacanaṁ bhāvitātmanaḥ |
rāghavaḥ pratijagrāha saha bhrātrā mahāyaśāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
evaṁ kathayamānasya dadarśāśramamaṇḍalam |
kuśacīraparikṣiptaṁ nānāvr̥kṣasamāvr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
praviśya saha vaidehyā lakṣmaṇena ca rāghavaḥ |
tadā tasmin sa kākutsthaḥ śrīmaty āśramamaṇḍale ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
uṣitvā susukhaṁ tatra pūrjyamāno maharṣibhiḥ |
jagāma cāśramāṁs teṣāṁ paryāyeṇa tapasvinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
yeṣām uṣitavān pūrvaṁ sakāśe sa mahāstravit |
kva cit paridaśān māsān ekaṁ saṁvatsaraṁ kva cit ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
kva cic ca caturo māsān pañcaṣaṭ cāparān kva cit |
aparatrādhikān māsān adhyardham adhikaṁ kva cit ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
trīn māsān aṣṭamāsāṁś ca rāghavo nyavasat sukham |
tathā saṁvasatas tasya munīnām āśrameṣu vai |
ramataś cānukulyena yayuḥ saṁvatsarā daśa ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
parisr̥tya ca dharmajño rāghavaḥ saha sītayā |
sutīkṣṇasyāśramaṁ śrīmān punar evājagāma ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
sa tam āśramam āgamya munibhiḥ pratipūjitaḥ |
tatrāpi nyavasad rāmaḥ kaṁ cit kālam ariṁdamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
athāśramastho vinayāt kadā cit taṁ mahāmunim |
upāsīnaḥ sa kākutsthaḥ sutīkṣṇam idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
asminn araṇye bhagavann agastyo munisattamaḥ |
vasatīti mayā nityaṁ kathāḥ kathayatāṁ śrutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
na tu jānāmi taṁ deśaṁ vanasyāsya mahattayā |
kutrāśramapadaṁ puṇyaṁ maharṣes tasya dhīmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
prasādāt tatra bhavataḥ sānujaḥ saha sītayā |
agastyam abhigaccheyam abhivādayituṁ munim ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
manoratho mahān eṣa hr̥di saṁparivartate |
yad ahaṁ taṁ munivaraṁ śuśrūṣeyam api svayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
iti rāmasya sa muniḥ śrutvā dharmātmano vacaḥ |
sutīkṣṇaḥ pratyuvācedaṁ prīto daśarathātmajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
aham apy etad eva tvāṁ vaktukāmaḥ salakṣmaṇam |
agastyam abhigaccheti sītayā saha rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
diṣṭyā tv idānīm arthe ’smin svayam eva bravīṣi mām |
aham ākhyāmi te vatsa yatrāgastyo mahāmuniḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
yojanāny āśramāt tāta yāhi catvāri vai tataḥ |
dakṣiṇena mahāñ śrīmān agastyabhrātur āśramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
sthalaprāye vanoddeśe pippalīvanaśobhite |
bahupuṣpaphale ramye nānāśakuninādite ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
padminyo vividhās tatra prasannasalilāḥ śivāḥ |
haṁsakāraṇḍavākīrṇāś cakravākopaśobhitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
tatraikāṁ rajanīm uṣya prabhāte rāma gamyatām |
dakṣiṇāṁ diśam āsthāya vanakhaṇḍasya pārśvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
tatrāgastyāśramapadaṁ gatvā yojanam antaram |
ramaṇīye vanoddeśe bahupādapa saṁvr̥te |
raṁsyate tatra vaidehī lakṣmaṇaś ca tvayā saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
sa hi ramyo vanoddeśo bahupādapasaṁkulaḥ |
yadi buddhiḥ kr̥tā draṣṭum agastyaṁ taṁ mahāmunim |
adyaiva gamane buddhiṁ rocayasva mahāyaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
iti rāmo muneḥ śrutvā saha bhrātrābhivādya ca |
pratasthe ’gastyam uddiśya sānujaḥ saha sītayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
paśyan vanāni citrāṇi parvapāṁś cābhrasaṁnibhān |
sarāṁsi saritaś caiva pathi mārgavaśānugāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
sutīkṣṇenopadiṣṭena gatvā tena pathā sukham |
idaṁ paramasaṁhr̥ṣṭo vākyaṁ lakṣmaṇam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
etad evāśramapadaṁ nūnaṁ tasya mahātmanaḥ |
agastyasya muner bhrātur dr̥śyate puṇyakarmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
yathā hīme vanasyāsya jñātāḥ pathi sahasraśaḥ |
saṁnatāḥ phalabhareṇa puṣpabhāreṇa ca drumāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
pippalīnāṁ ca pakvānāṁ vanād asmād upāgataḥ |
gandho ’yaṁ pavanotkṣiptaḥ sahasā kaṭukodayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
tatra tatra ca dr̥śyante saṁkṣiptāḥ kāṣṭhasaṁcayāḥ |
lūnāś ca pathi dr̥śyante darbhā vaidūryavarcasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
etac ca vanamadhyasthaṁ kr̥ṣṇābhraśikharopamam |
pāvakasyāśramasthasya dhūmāgraṁ saṁpradr̥śyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
vivikteṣu ca tīrtheṣu kr̥tasnānā dvijātayaḥ |
puṣpopahāraṁ kurvanti kusumaiḥ svayam ārjitaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
tat sutīkṣṇasya vacanaṁ yathā saumya mayā śrutam |
agastyasyāśramo bhrātur nūnam eṣa bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
52.
nigr̥hya tarasā mr̥tyuṁ lokānāṁ hitakāmyayā |
yasya bhrātrā kr̥teyaṁ dik śaraṇyā puṇyakarmaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
53.
ihaikadā kila krūro vātāpir api celvalaḥ |
bhrātarau sahitāv āstāṁ brāhmaṇaghnau mahāsurau ||

=== meaning ===

---
54.
dhārayan brāhmaṇaṁ rūpam ilvalaḥ saṁskr̥taṁ vadan |
āmantrayati viprān sa śrāddham uddiśya nirghr̥ṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
55.
bhrātaraṁ saṁskr̥taṁ bhrātā tatas taṁ meṣarūpiṇam |
tān dvijān bhojayām āsa śrāddhadr̥ṣṭena karmaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
56.
tato bhuktavatāṁ teṣāṁ viprāṇām ilvalo ’bravīt |
vātāpe niṣkramasveti svareṇa mahatā vadan ||

=== meaning ===

---
57.
tato bhrātur vacaḥ śrutvā vātāpir meṣavan nadan |
bhittvā bhitvā śarīrāṇi brāhmaṇānāṁ viniṣpatat ||

=== meaning ===

---
58.
brāhmaṇānāṁ sahasrāṇi tair evaṁ kāmarūpibhiḥ |
vināśitāni saṁhatya nityaśaḥ piśitāśanaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
59.
agastyena tadā devaiḥ prārthitena maharṣiṇā |
anubhūya kila śrāddhe bhakṣitaḥ sa mahāsuraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
60.
tataḥ saṁpannam ity uktvā dattvā hastāvasecanam |
bhrātaraṁ niṣkramasveti ilvalaḥ so ’bhyabhāṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
61.
taṁ tathā bhāṣamāṇaṁ tu bhrātaraṁ vipraghātinam |
abravīt prahasan dhīmān agastyo munisattamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
62.
kuto niṣkramituṁ śaktir mayā jīrṇasya rakṣasaḥ |
bhrātus te meṣa rūpasya gatasya yamasādanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
63.
atha tasya vacaḥ śrutvā bhrātur nidhanasaṁśritam |
pradharṣayitum ārebhe muniṁ krodhān niśācaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
64.
so ’bhyadravad dvijendraṁ taṁ muninā dīptatejasā |
cakṣuṣānalakalpena nirdagdho nidhanaṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
65.
tasyāyam āśramo bhrātus taṭākavanaśobhitaḥ |
viprānukampayā yena karmedaṁ duṣkaraṁ kr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
66.
evaṁ kathayamānasya tasya saumitriṇā saha |
rāmasyāstaṁ gataḥ sūryaḥ saṁdhyākālo ’bhyavartata ||

=== meaning ===

---
67.
upāsya paścimāṁ saṁdhyāṁ saha bhrātrā yathāvidhi |
praviveśāśramapadaṁ tam r̥ṣiṁ cābhyavādayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
68.
samyak pratigr̥hītas tu muninā tena rāghavaḥ |
nyavasat tāṁ niśām ekāṁ prāśya mūlaphalāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
69.
tasyāṁ rātryāṁ vyatītāyāṁ vimale sūryamaṇḍale |
bhrātaraṁ tam agastyasya āmantrayata rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
70.
abhivādaye tvā bhagavan sukham adhyuṣito niśām |
āmantraye tvāṁ gacchāmi guruṁ te draṣṭum agrajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
71.
gamyatām iti tenokto jagāma raghunandanaḥ |
yathoddiṣṭena mārgeṇa vanaṁ tac cāvalokayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
72.
nīvārān panasāṁs tālāṁs timiśān vañjulān dhavān |
ciribilvān madhūkāṁś ca bilvān api ca tindukān ||

=== meaning ===

---
73.
puṣpitān puṣpitāgrābhir latābhir anuveṣṭitān |
dadarśa rāmaḥ śataśas tatra kāntārapādapān ||

=== meaning ===

---
74.
hastihastair vimr̥ditān vānarair upaśobhitān |
mattaiḥ śakunisaṁghaiś ca śataśaḥ pratināditān ||

=== meaning ===

---
75.
tato ’bravīt samīpasthaṁ rāmo rājīvalocanaḥ |
pr̥ṣṭhato ’nugataṁ vīraṁ lakṣmaṇaṁ lakṣmivardhanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
76.
snigdhapatrā yathā vr̥kṣā yathā kṣāntā mr̥gadvijāḥ |
āśramo nātidūrastho maharṣer bhāvitātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
77.
agastya iti vikhyāto loke svenaiva karmaṇā |
āśramo dr̥śyate tasya pariśrānta śramāpahaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
78.
prājyadhūmākulavanaś cīramālāpariṣkr̥taḥ |
praśāntamr̥gayūthaś ca nānāśakunināditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
79.
nigr̥hya tarasā mr̥tyuṁ lokānāṁ hitakāmyayā |
dakṣiṇā dik kr̥tā yena śaraṇyā puṇyakarmaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
80.
tasyedam āśramapadaṁ prabhāvād yasya rākṣasaiḥ |
dig iyaṁ dakṣiṇā trāsād dr̥śyate nopabhujyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
81.
yadā prabhr̥ti cākrāntā dig iyaṁ puṇyakarmaṇā |
tadā prabhr̥ti nirvairāḥ praśāntā rajanīcarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
82.
nāmnā ceyaṁ bhagavato dakṣiṇā dik pradakṣiṇā |
prathitā triṣu lokeṣu durdharṣā krūrakarmabhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
83.
mārgaṁ niroddhuṁ satataṁ bhāskarasyācalottamaḥ |
saṁdeśaṁ pālayaṁs tasya vindhyaśaulo na vardhate ||

=== meaning ===

---
84.
ayaṁ dīrghāyuṣas tasya loke viśrutakarmaṇaḥ |
agastyasyāśramaḥ śrīmān vinītamr̥gasevitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
85.
eṣa lokārcitaḥ sādhur hite nityaṁ rataḥ satām |
asmān adhigatān eṣa śreyasā yojayiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
86.
ārādhayiṣyāmy atrāham agastyaṁ taṁ mahāmunim |
śeṣaṁ ca vanavāsasya saumya vatsyāmy ahaṁ prabho ||

=== meaning ===

---
87.
atra devāḥ sagandharvāḥ siddhāś ca paramarṣayaḥ |
agastyaṁ niyatāhāraṁ satataṁ paryupāsate ||

=== meaning ===

---
88.
nātra jīven mr̥ṣāvādī krūro vā yadi vā śaṭhaḥ |
nr̥śaṁsaḥ kāmavr̥tto vā munir eṣa tathāvidhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
89.
atra devāś ca yakṣāś ca nāgāś ca patagaiḥ saha |
vasanti niyatāhārā dharmam ārādhayiṣṇavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
90.
atra siddhā mahātmāno vimānaiḥ sūryasaṁnibhaiḥ |
tyaktvā dehān navair dehaiḥ svaryātāḥ paramarṣayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
91.
yakṣatvam amaratvaṁ ca rājyāni vividhāni ca |
atra devāḥ prayacchanti bhūtair ārādhitāḥ śubhaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
92.
āgatāḥ smāśramapadaṁ saumitre praviśāgrataḥ |
nivedayeha māṁ prāptam r̥ṣaye saha sītayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
