=== Sarga 19 ===

---
1.
tataḥ śūrpaṇakhā ghorā rāghavāśramam āgatā |
rakṣasām ācacakṣe tau bhrātarau saha sītayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
te rāmaṁ parṇaśālāyām upaviṣṭaṁ mahābalam |
dadr̥śuḥ sītayā sārdhaṁ vaidehyā lakṣmaṇena ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tān dr̥ṣṭvā rāghavaḥ śrīmān āgatāṁ tāṁ ca rākṣasīm |
abravīd bhrātaraṁ rāmo lakṣmaṇaṁ dīptatejasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
muhūrtaṁ bhava saumitre sītāyāḥ pratyanantaraḥ |
imān asyā vadhiṣyāmi padavīm āgatān iha ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
vākyam etat tataḥ śrutvā rāmasya viditātmanaḥ |
tatheti lakṣmaṇo vākyaṁ rāmasya pratyapūjayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
rāghavo ’pi mahac cāpaṁ cāmīkaravibhūṣitam |
cakāra sajyaṁ dharmātmā tāni rakṣāṁsi cābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
putrau daśarathasyāvāṁ bhrātarau rāmalakṣmaṇau |
praviṣṭau sītayā sārdhaṁ duścaraṁ daṇḍakāvanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
phalamūlāśanau dāntau tāpasau dharmacāriṇau |
vasantau daṇḍakāraṇye kimartham upahiṁsatha ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
yuṣmān pāpātmakān hantuṁ viprakārān mahāvane |
r̥ṣīṇāṁ tu niyogena prāpto ’haṁ saśarāsanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tiṣṭhataivātra saṁtuṣṭā nopasarpitum arhatha |
yadi prāṇair ihārtho vo nivartadhvaṁ niśācarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā rākṣasās te caturdaśa |
ūcur vācaṁ susaṁkruddhā brahmaghnaḥ śūlapāṇayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
saṁraktanayanā ghorā rāmaṁ raktāntalocanam |
paruṣā madhurābhāṣaṁ hr̥ṣṭādr̥ṣṭaparākramam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
krodham utpādya no bhartuḥ kharasya sumahātmanaḥ |
tvam eva hāsyase prāṇān adyāsmābhir hato yudhi ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
kā hi te śaktir ekasya bahūnāṁ raṇamūrdhani |
asmākam agrataḥ sthātuṁ kiṁ punar yoddhum āhave ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
ebhir bāhuprayuktair naḥ parighaiḥ śūlapaṭṭiśaiḥ |
prāṇāṁs tyakṣyasi vīryaṁ ca dhanuś ca karapīḍitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
ity evam uktvā saṁrabdhā rākṣasās te caturdaśa |
udyatāyudhanistriṁśā rāmam evābhidudruvuḥ |
cikṣipus tāni śūlāni rāghavaṁ prati durjayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tāni śūlāni kākutsthaḥ samastāni caturdaśa |
tāvadbhir eva ciccheda śaraiḥ kāñcanabhūṣaṇaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tataḥ paścān mahātejā nārācān sūryasaṁnibhān |
jagrāha paramakruddhaś caturdaśa śilāśitān ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
gr̥hītvā dhanur āyamya lakṣyān uddiśya rākṣasān |
mumoca rāghavo bāṇān vajrān iva śatakratuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
rukmapuṅkhāś ca viśikhāḥ pradīptā hemabhūṣaṇāḥ |
antarikṣe maholkānāṁ babhūvus tulyavarcasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
te bhittvā rakṣasāṁ vegād vakṣāṁsi rudhirāplutāḥ |
viniṣpetus tadā bhūmau nyamajjantāśanisvanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
te bhinnahr̥dayā bhūmau chinnamūlā iva drumāḥ |
nipetuḥ śoṇitārdrāṅgā vikr̥tā vigatāsavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tān bhūmau patitān dr̥ṣṭvā rākṣasī krodhamūrchitā |
paritrastā punas tatra vyasr̥jad bhairavaṁ ravam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
sā nadantī mahānādaṁ javāc chūrpaṇakhā punaḥ |
upagamya kharaṁ sā tu kiṁ cit saṁśuṣka śoṇitā |
papāta punar evārtā saniryāseva vallarī ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
nipātitān prekṣya raṇe tu rākṣasān; pradhāvitā śūrpaṇakhā punas tataḥ |
vadhaṁ ca teṣāṁ nikhilena rakṣasāṁ; śaśaṁsa sarvaṁ bhaginī kharasya sā ||

=== meaning ===

---
