=== Sarga 47 ===

---
1.
sītāyā vacanaṁ śrutvā daśagrīvaḥ pratāpavān |
haste hastaṁ samāhatya cakāra sumahad vapuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sa maithilīṁ punar vākyaṁ babhāṣe ca tato bhr̥śam |
nonmattayā śrutau manye mama vīryaparākramau ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
udvaheyaṁ bhujābhyāṁ tu medinīm ambare sthitaḥ |
āpibeyaṁ samudraṁ ca mr̥tyuṁ hanyāṁ raṇe sthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
arkaṁ rundhyāṁ śarais tīkṣṇair vibhindyāṁ hi mahītalam |
kāmarūpiṇam unmatte paśya māṁ kāmadaṁ patim ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
evam uktavatas tasya rāvaṇasya śikhiprabhe |
kruddhasya hariparyante rakte netre babhūvatuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
sadyaḥ saumyaṁ parityajya bhikṣurūpaṁ sa rāvaṇaḥ |
svaṁ rūpaṁ kālarūpābhaṁ bheje vaiśravaṇānujaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
saṁraktanayanaḥ śrīmāṁs taptakāñcanakuṇḍalaḥ |
daśāsyaḥ kārmukī bāṇī babhūva kṣaṇadācaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sa parivrājakacchadma mahākāyo vihāya tat |
pratipede svakaṁ rūpaṁ rāvaṇo rākṣasādhipaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
saṁraktanayanaḥ krodhāj jīmūtanicayaprabhaḥ |
raktāmbaradharas tasthau strīratnaṁ prekṣya maithilīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
sa tām asitakeśāntāṁ bhāskarasya prabhām iva |
vasanābharaṇopetāṁ maithilīṁ rāvaṇo ’bravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
triṣu lokeṣu vikhyātaṁ yadi bhartāram icchasi |
mām āśraya varārohe tavāhaṁ sadr̥śaḥ patiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
māṁ bhajasva cirāya tvam ahaṁ ślāghyas tava priyaḥ |
naiva cāhaṁ kva cid bhadre kariṣye tava vipriyam |
tyajyatāṁ mānuṣo bhāvo mayi bhāvaḥ praṇīyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
rājyāc cyutam asiddhārthaṁ rāmaṁ parimitāyuṣam |
kair guṇair anuraktāsi mūḍhe paṇḍitamānini ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
yaḥ striyā vacanād rājyaṁ vihāya sasuhr̥jjanam |
asmin vyālānucarite vane vasati durmatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
ity uktvā maithilīṁ vākyaṁ priyārhāṁ priyavādinīm |
jagrāha rāvaṇaḥ sītāṁ budhaḥ khe rohiṇīm iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
vāmena sītāṁ padmākṣīṁ mūrdhajeṣu kareṇa saḥ |
ūrvos tu dakṣiṇenaiva parijagrāha pāṇinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā giriśr̥ṅgābhaṁ tīkṣṇadaṁṣṭraṁ mahābhujam |
prādravan mr̥tyusaṁkāśaṁ bhayārtā vanadevatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
sa ca māyāmayo divyaḥ kharayuktaḥ kharasvanaḥ |
pratyadr̥śyata hemāṅgo rāvaṇasya mahārathaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tatas tāṁ paruṣair vākyair abhitarjya mahāsvanaḥ |
aṅkenādāya vaidehīṁ ratham āropayat tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
sā gr̥hītāticukrośa rāvaṇena yaśasvinī |
rāmeti sītā duḥkhārtā rāmaṁ dūragataṁ vane ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tām akāmāṁ sa kāmārtaḥ pannagendravadhūm iva |
viveṣṭamānām ādāya utpapāthātha rāvaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tataḥ sā rākṣasendreṇa hriyamāṇā vihāyasā |
bhr̥śaṁ cukrośa matteva bhrāntacittā yathāturā ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
hā lakṣmaṇa mahābāho gurucittaprasādaka |
hriyamāṇāṁ na jānīṣe rakṣasā kāmarūpiṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
jīvitaṁ sukham arthāṁś ca dharmahetoḥ parityajan |
hriyamāṇām adharmeṇa māṁ rāghava na paśyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
nanu nāmāvinītānāṁ vinetāsi paraṁtapa |
katham evaṁvidhaṁ pāpaṁ na tvaṁ śādhi hi rāvaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
nanu sadyo ’vinītasya dr̥śyate karmaṇaḥ phalam |
kālo ’py aṅgī bhavaty atra sasyānām iva paktaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
sa karma kr̥tavān etat kālopahatacetanaḥ |
jīvitāntakaraṁ ghoraṁ rāmād vyasanam āpnuhi ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
hantedānīṁ sakāmā tu kaikeyī bāndhavaiḥ saha |
hriyeyaṁ dharmakāmasya dharmapatnī yaśasvinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
āmantraye janasthānaṁ karṇikārāṁś ca puṣpitān |
kṣipraṁ rāmāya śaṁsadhvaṁ sītāṁ harati rāvaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
mālyavantaṁ śikhariṇaṁ vande prasravaṇaṁ girim |
kṣipraṁ rāmāya śaṁsadhvaṁ sītāṁ harati rāvaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
haṁsasārasasaṁghuṣṭāṁ vande godāvarīṁ nadīm |
kṣipraṁ rāmāya śaṁsadhvaṁ sītāṁ harati rāvaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
daivatāni ca yānty asmin vane vividhapādape |
namaskaromy ahaṁ tebhyo bhartuḥ śaṁsata māṁ hr̥tām ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
yāni kāni cid apy atra sattvāni nivasanty uta |
sarvāṇi śaraṇaṁ yāmi mr̥gapakṣigaṇān api ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
hriyamāṇāṁ priyāṁ bhartuḥ prāṇebhyo ’pi garīyasīm |
vivaśāpahr̥tā sītā rāvaṇeneti śaṁsata ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
viditvā māṁ mahābāhur amutrāpi mahābalaḥ |
āneṣyati parākramya vaivasvatahr̥tām api ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
rāmāya tu yathātattvaṁ jaṭāyo haraṇaṁ mama |
lakṣmaṇāya ca tat sarvam ākhyātavyam aśeṣataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
