=== Sarga 57 ===

---
1.
athāśramād upāvr̥ttam antarā raghunandanaḥ |
paripapraccha saumitriṁ rāmo duḥkhārditaḥ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tam uvāca kimarthaṁ tvam āgato ’pāsya maithilīm |
yadā sā tava viśvāsād vane viharitā mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
dr̥ṣṭvaivābhyāgataṁ tvāṁ me maithilīṁ tyajya lakṣmaṇa |
śaṅkamānaṁ mahat pāpaṁ yat satyaṁ vyathitaṁ manaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
sphurate nayanaṁ savyaṁ bāhuś ca hr̥dayaṁ ca me |
dr̥ṣṭvā lakṣmaṇa dūre tvāṁ sītāvirahitaṁ pathi ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
evam uktas tu saumitrir lakṣmaṇaḥ śubhalakṣaṇaḥ |
bhūyo duḥkhasamāviṣṭo duḥkhitaṁ rāmam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
na svayaṁ kāmakāreṇa tāṁ tyaktvāham ihāgataḥ |
pracoditas tayaivograis tvatsakāśam ihāgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
āryeṇeva parikruṣṭaṁ hā sīte lakṣmaṇeti ca |
paritrāhīti yad vākyaṁ maithilyās tac chrutiṁ gatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sā tam ārtasvaraṁ śrutvā tava snehena maithilī |
gaccha gaccheti mām āha rudantī bhayavihvalā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
pracodyamānena mayā gaccheti bahuśas tayā |
pratyuktā maithilī vākyam idaṁ tvatpratyayānvitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
na tat paśyāmy ahaṁ rakṣo yad asya bhayam āvahet |
nirvr̥tā bhava nāsty etat kenāpy evam udāhr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
vigarhitaṁ ca nīcaṁ ca katham āryo ’bhidhāsyati |
trāhīti vacanaṁ sīte yas trāyet tridaśān api ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
kiṁnimittaṁ tu kenāpi bhrātur ālambya me svaram |
visvaraṁ vyāhr̥taṁ vākyaṁ lakṣmaṇa trāhi mām iti |
na bhavatyā vyathā kāryā kunārījanasevitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
alaṁ vaiklavyam ālambya svasthā bhava nirutsukā |
na cāsti triṣu lokeṣu pumān yo rāghavaṁ raṇe |
jāto vā jāyamāno vā saṁyuge yaḥ parājayet ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
evam uktā tu vaidehī parimohitacetanā |
uvācāśrūṇi muñcantī dāruṇaṁ mām idaṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
bhāvo mayi tavātyarthaṁ pāpa eva niveśitaḥ |
vinaṣṭe bhrātari prāpte na ca tvaṁ mām avāpsyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
saṁketād bharatena tvaṁ rāmaṁ samanugacchasi |
krośantaṁ hi yathātyarthaṁ nainam abhyavapadyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
ripuḥ pracchannacārī tvaṁ madartham anugacchasi |
rāghavasyāntaraprepsus tathainaṁ nābhipadyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
evam ukto hi vaidehyā saṁrabdho raktalocanaḥ |
krodhāt prasphuramāṇauṣṭha āśramād abhinirgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
evaṁ bruvāṇaṁ saumitriṁ rāmaḥ saṁtāpamohitaḥ |
abravīd duṣkr̥taṁ saumya tāṁ vinā yat tvam āgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
jānann api samarthaṁ māṁ rakṣasāṁ vinivāraṇe |
anena krodhavākyena maithilyā niḥsr̥to bhavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
na hi te parituṣyāmi tyaktvā yad yāsi maithilīm |
kruddhāyāḥ paruṣaṁ śrutvā striyā yat tvam ihāgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
sarvathā tv apanītaṁ te sītayā yat pracoditaḥ |
krodhasya vaśam āgamya nākaroḥ śāsanaṁ mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
asau hi rākṣasaḥ śete śareṇābhihato mayā |
mr̥garūpeṇa yenāham āśramād apavāditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
vikr̥ṣya cāpaṁ paridhāya sāyakaṁ; salīla bāṇena ca tāḍito mayā |
mārgīṁ tanuṁ tyajya ca viklavasvaro; babhūva keyūradharaḥ sa rākṣasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
śarāhatenaiva tadārtayā girā; svaraṁ mamālambya sudūrasaṁśravam |
udāhr̥taṁ tad vacanaṁ sudāruṇaṁ; tvam āgato yena vihāya maithilīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
