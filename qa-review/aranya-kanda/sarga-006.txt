=== Sarga 6 ===

---
1.
rāmas tu sahito bhrātrā sītayā ca paraṁtapaḥ |
sutīkṣṇasyāśramapadaṁ jagāma saha tair dvijaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sa gatvā dūram adhvānaṁ nadīs tīrtva bahūdakāḥ |
dadarśa vipulaṁ śailaṁ mahāmegham ivonnatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tatas tad ikṣvākuvarau satataṁ vividhair drumaiḥ |
kānanaṁ tau viviśatuḥ sītayā saha rāghavau ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
praviṣṭas tu vanaṁ ghoraṁ bahupuṣpaphaladrumam |
dadarśāśramam ekānte cīramālāpariṣkr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tatra tāpasam āsīnaṁ malapaṅkajaṭādharam |
rāmaḥ sutīkṣṇaṁ vidhivat tapovr̥ddham abhāṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
rāmo ’ham asmi bhagavan bhavantaṁ draṣṭum āgataḥ |
tan mābhivada dharmajña maharṣe satyavikrama ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sa nirīkṣya tato vīraṁ rāmaṁ dharmabhr̥tāṁ varam |
samāśliṣya ca bāhubhyām idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
svāgataṁ khalu te vīra rāma dharmabhr̥tāṁ vara |
āśramo ’yaṁ tvayākrāntaḥ sanātha iva sāmpratam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
pratīkṣamāṇas tvām eva nārohe ’haṁ mahāyaśaḥ |
devalokam ito vīra dehaṁ tyaktvā mahītale ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
citrakūṭam upādāya rājyabhraṣṭo ’si me śrutaḥ |
ihopayātaḥ kākutstho devarājaḥ śatakratuḥ |
sarvām̐l lokāñ jitān āha mama puṇyena karmaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
teṣu devarṣijuṣṭeṣu jiteṣu tapasā mayā |
matprasādāt sabhāryas tvaṁ viharasva salakṣmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tam ugratapasaṁ dīptaṁ maharṣiṁ satyavādinam |
pratyuvācātmavān rāmo brahmāṇam iva vāsavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
aham evāhariṣyāmi svayaṁ lokān mahāmune |
āvāsaṁ tv aham icchāmi pradiṣṭam iha kānane ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
bhavān sarvatra kuśalaḥ sarvabhūtahite rataḥ |
ākhyātaḥ śarabhaṅgena gautamena mahātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
evam uktas tu rāmeṇa maharṣir lokaviśrutaḥ |
abravīn madhuraṁ vākyaṁ harṣeṇa mahatāplutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
ayam evāśramo rāma guṇavān ramyatām iha |
r̥ṣisaṁghānucaritaḥ sadā mūlaphalair yutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
imam āśramam āgamya mr̥gasaṁghā mahāyaśāḥ |
aṭitvā pratigacchanti lobhayitvākutobhayāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tac chrutvā vacanaṁ tasya maharṣer lakṣmaṇāgrajaḥ |
uvāca vacanaṁ dhīro vikr̥ṣya saśaraṁ dhanuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tān ahaṁ sumahābhāga mr̥gasaṁghān samāgatān |
hanyāṁ niśitadhāreṇa śareṇāśanivarcasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
bhavāṁs tatrābhiṣajyeta kiṁ syāt kr̥cchrataraṁ tataḥ |
etasminn āśrame vāsaṁ ciraṁ tu na samarthaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tam evam uktvā varadaṁ rāmaḥ saṁdhyām upāgamat |
anvāsya paścimāṁ saṁdhyāṁ tatra vāsam akalpayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tataḥ śubhaṁ tāpasabhojyam annaṁ; svayaṁ sutīkṣṇaḥ puruṣarṣabhābhyām |
tābhyāṁ susatkr̥tya dadau mahātmā; saṁdhyānivr̥ttau rajanīṁ samīkṣya ||

=== meaning ===

---
