=== Sarga 5 ===

---
1.
śarabhaṅge divaṁ prāpte munisaṁghāḥ samāgatāḥ |
abhyagacchanta kākutsthaṁ rāmaṁ jvalitatejasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
vaikhānasā vālakhilyāḥ saṁprakṣālā marīcipāḥ |
aśmakuṭṭāś ca bahavaḥ patrāhārāś ca tāpasāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
dantolūkhalinaś caiva tathaivonmajjakāḥ pare |
munayaḥ salilāhārā vāyubhakṣās tathāpare ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
ākāśanilayāś caiva tathā sthaṇḍilaśāyinaḥ |
tathordhvavāsino dāntās tathārdrapaṭavāsasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
sajapāś ca taponityās tathā pañcatapo’nvitāḥ |
sarve brāhmyā śriyā juṣṭā dr̥ḍhayogasamāhitāḥ |
śarabhaṅgāśrame rāmam abhijagmuś ca tāpasāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
abhigamya ca dharmajñā rāmaṁ dharmabhr̥tāṁ varam |
ūcuḥ paramadharmajñam r̥ṣisaṁghāḥ samāhitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tvam ikṣvākukulasyāsya pr̥thivyāś ca mahārathaḥ |
pradhānaś cāsi nāthaś ca devānāṁ maghavān iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
viśrutas triṣu lokeṣu yaśasā vikrameṇa ca |
pitr̥vratatvaṁ satyaṁ ca tvayi dharmaś ca puṣkalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tvām āsādya mahātmānaṁ dharmajñaṁ dharmavatsalam |
arthitvān nātha vakṣyāmas tac ca naḥ kṣantum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
adhārmas tu mahāṁs tāta bhavet tasya mahīpateḥ |
yo hared baliṣaḍbhāgaṁ na ca rakṣati putravat ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
yuñjānaḥ svān iva prāṇān prāṇair iṣṭān sutān iva |
nityayuktaḥ sadā rakṣan sarvān viṣayavāsinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
prāpnoti śāśvatīṁ rāma kīrtiṁ sa bahuvārṣikīm |
brahmaṇaḥ sthānam āsādya tatra cāpi mahīyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
yat karoti paraṁ dharmaṁ munir mūlaphalāśanaḥ |
tatra rājñaś caturbhāgaḥ prajā dharmeṇa rakṣataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
so ’yaṁ brāhmaṇabhūyiṣṭho vānaprasthagaṇo mahān |
tvan nātho ’nāthavad rāma rākṣasair vadhyate bhr̥śam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
ehi paśya śarīrāṇi munīnāṁ bhāvitātmanām |
hatānāṁ rākṣasair ghorair bahūnāṁ bahudhā vane ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
pampānadīnivāsānām anumandākinīm api |
citrakūṭālayānāṁ ca kriyate kadanaṁ mahat ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
evaṁ vayaṁ na mr̥ṣyāmo viprakāraṁ tapasvinam |
kriyamāṇaṁ vane ghoraṁ rakṣobhir bhīmakarmabhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tatas tvāṁ śaraṇārthaṁ ca śaraṇyaṁ samupasthitāḥ |
paripālaya no rāma vadhyamānān niśācaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
etac chrutvā tu kākutsthas tāpasānāṁ tapasvinām |
idaṁ provāca dharmātmā sarvān eva tapasvinaḥ |
naivam arhatha māṁ vaktum ājñāpyo ’haṁ tapasvinam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
bhavatām arthasiddhyartham āgato ’haṁ yadr̥cchayā |
tasya me ’yaṁ vane vāso bhaviṣyati mahāphalaḥ |
tapasvināṁ raṇe śatrūn hantum icchāmi rākṣasān ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
dattvā varaṁ cāpi tapodhanānāṁ; dharme dhr̥tātmā sahalakṣmaṇena |
tapodhanaiś cāpi sahārya vr̥ttaḥ; sutīṣkṇam evābhijagāma vīraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
