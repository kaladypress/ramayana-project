=== Sarga 62 ===

---
1.
taṁ tathā śokasaṁtaptaṁ vilapantam anāthavat |
mohena mahatāviṣṭaṁ paridyūnam acetanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tataḥ saumitrir āśvāsya muhūrtād iva lakṣmaṇaḥ |
rāmaṁ saṁbodhayām āsa caraṇau cābhipīḍayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
mahatā tapasā rāma mahatā cāpi karmaṇā |
rājñā daśarathenāsīl labdho ’mr̥tam ivāmaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tava caiva guṇair baddhas tvadviyogān mahīpatiḥ |
rājā devatvam āpanno bharatasya yathā śrutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
yadi duḥkham idaṁ prāptaṁ kākutstha na sahiṣyase |
prākr̥taś cālpasattvaś ca itaraḥ kaḥ sahiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
duḥkhito hi bhavām̐l lokāṁs tejasā yadi dhakṣyate |
ārtāḥ prajā naravyāghra kva nu yāsyanti nirvr̥tim ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
lokasvabhāva evaiṣa yayātir nahuṣātmajaḥ |
gataḥ śakreṇa sālokyam anayas taṁ samaspr̥śat ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
maharṣayo vasiṣṭhas tu yaḥ pitur naḥ purohitaḥ |
ahnā putraśataṁ jajñe tathaivāsya punar hatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
yā ceyaṁ jagato mātā devī lokanamaskr̥tā |
asyāś ca calanaṁ bhūmer dr̥śyate satyasaṁśrava ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
yau cemau jagatāṁ netre yatra sarvaṁ pratiṣṭhitam |
ādityacandrau grahaṇam abhyupetau mahābalau ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sumahānty api bhūtāni devāś ca puruṣarṣabha |
na daivasya pramuñcanti sarvabhūtāni dehinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
śakrādiṣv api deveṣu vartamānau nayānayau |
śrūyete naraśārdūla na tvaṁ vyathitum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
naṣṭāyām api vaidehyāṁ hr̥tāyām api cānagha |
śocituṁ nārhase vīra yathānyaḥ prākr̥tas tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tvadvidhā hi na śocanti satataṁ satyadarśinaḥ |
sumahatsv api kr̥cchreṣu rāmānirviṇṇadarśaṇāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tattvato hi naraśreṣṭha buddhyā samanucintaya |
buddhyā yuktā mahāprājñā vijānanti śubhāśubhe ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
adr̥ṣṭaguṇadoṣāṇām adhr̥tānāṁ ca karmaṇām |
nāntareṇa kriyāṁ teṣāṁ phalam iṣṭaṁ pravartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
mām eva hi purā vīra tvam eva bahuṣo ’nvaśāḥ |
anuśiṣyād dhi ko nu tvām api sākṣād br̥haspatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
buddhiś ca te mahāprājña devair api duranvayā |
śokenābhiprasuptaṁ te jñānaṁ saṁbodhayāmy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
divyaṁ ca mānuṣaṁ caivam ātmanaś ca parākramam |
ikṣvākuvr̥ṣabhāvekṣya yatasva dviṣatāṁ badhe ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
kiṁ te sarvavināśena kr̥tena puruṣarṣabha |
tam eva tu ripuṁ pāpaṁ vijñāyoddhartum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
