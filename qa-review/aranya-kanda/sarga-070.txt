=== Sarga 70 ===

---
1.
tau kabandhena taṁ mārgaṁ pampāyā darśitaṁ vane |
ātasthatur diśaṁ gr̥hya pratīcīṁ nr̥varātmajau ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tau śaileṣv ācitānekān kṣaudrakalpaphaladrumān |
vīkṣantau jagmatur draṣṭuṁ sugrīvaṁ rāmalakṣmaṇau ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
kr̥tvā ca śailapr̥ṣṭhe tu tau vāsaṁ raghunandanau |
pampāyāḥ paścimaṁ tīraṁ rāghavāv upatasthatuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tau puṣkariṇyāḥ pampāyās tīram āsādya paścimam |
apaśyatāṁ tatas tatra śabaryā ramyam āśramam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tau tam āśramam āsādya drumair bahubhir āvr̥tam |
suramyam abhivīkṣantau śabarīm abhyupeyatuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tau tu dr̥ṣṭvā tadā siddhā samutthāya kr̥tāñjaliḥ |
pādau jagrāha rāmasya lakṣmaṇasya ca dhīmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tām uvāca tato rāmaḥ śramaṇīṁ saṁśitavratām |
kaccit te nirjitā vighnāḥ kaccit te vardhate tapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
kaccit te niyataḥ kopa āhāraś ca tapodhane |
kaccit te niyamāḥ prāptāḥ kaccit te manasaḥ sukham |
kaccit te guruśuśrūṣā saphalā cārubhāṣiṇi ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
rāmeṇa tāpasī pr̥ṣṭhā sā siddhā siddhasaṁmatā |
śaśaṁsa śabarī vr̥ddhā rāmāya pratyupasthitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
citrakūṭaṁ tvayi prāpte vimānair atulaprabhaiḥ |
itas te divam ārūḍhā yān ahaṁ paryacāriṣam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
taiś cāham uktā dharmajñair mahābhāgair maharṣibhiḥ |
āgamiṣyati te rāmaḥ supuṇyam imam āśramam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sa te pratigrahītavyaḥ saumitrisahito ’tithiḥ |
taṁ ca dr̥ṣṭvā varām̐l lokān akṣayāṁs tvaṁ gamiṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
mayā tu vividhaṁ vanyaṁ saṁcitaṁ puruṣarṣabha |
tavārthe puruṣavyāghra pampāyās tīrasaṁbhavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
evam uktaḥ sa dharmātmā śabaryā śabarīm idam |
rāghavaḥ prāha vijñāne tāṁ nityam abahiṣkr̥tām ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
danoḥ sakāśāt tattvena prabhāvaṁ te mahātmanaḥ |
śrutaṁ pratyakṣam icchāmi saṁdraṣṭuṁ yadi manyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
etat tu vacanaṁ śrutvā rāmavaktrād viniḥsr̥tam |
śabarī darśayām āsa tāv ubhau tad vanaṁ mahat ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
paśya meghaghanaprakhyaṁ mr̥gapakṣisamākulam |
mataṅgavanam ity eva viśrutaṁ raghunandana ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
iha te bhāvitātmāno guravo me mahādyute |
juhavāṁś cakrire tīrthaṁ mantravan mantrapūjitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
iyaṁ pratyak sthalī vedī yatra te me susatkr̥tāḥ |
puṣpopahāraṁ kurvanti śramād udvepibhiḥ karaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
teṣāṁ tapaḥ prabhāvena paśyādyāpi raghūttama |
dyotayanti diśaḥ sarvāḥ śriyā vedyo ’tulaprabhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
aśaknuvadbhis tair gantum upavāsaśramālasaiḥ |
cintite ’bhyāgatān paśya sametān sapta sāgarān ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
kr̥tābhiṣekais tair nyastā valkalāḥ pādapeṣv iha |
adyāpi na viśuṣyanti pradeśe raghunandana ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
kr̥tsnaṁ vanam idaṁ dr̥ṣṭaṁ śrotavyaṁ ca śrutaṁ tvayā |
tad icchāmy abhyanujñātā tyaktum etat kalevaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
teṣām icchāmy ahaṁ gantuṁ samīpaṁ bhāvitātmanām |
munīnām āśraṁmo yeṣām ahaṁ ca paricāriṇī ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
dharmiṣṭhaṁ tu vacaḥ śrutvā rāghavaḥ sahalakṣmaṇaḥ |
anujānāmi gaccheti prahr̥ṣṭavadano ’bravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
anujñātā tu rāmeṇa hutvātmānaṁ hutāśane |
jvalatpāvakasaṁkāśā svargam eva jagāma sā ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
yatra te sukr̥tātmāno viharanti maharṣayaḥ |
tat puṇyaṁ śabarīsthānaṁ jagāmātmasamādhinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
