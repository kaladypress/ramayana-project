=== Sarga 8 ===

---
1.
sutīkṣṇenābhyanujñātaṁ prasthitaṁ raghunandanam |
vaidehī snigdhayā vācā bhartāram idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
ayaṁ dharmaḥ susūkṣmeṇa vidhinā prāpyate mahān |
nivr̥ttena ca śakyo ’yaṁ vyasanāt kāmajād iha ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
trīṇy eva vyasanāny atra kāmajāni bhavanty uta |
mithyā vākyaṁ paramakaṁ tasmād gurutarāv ubhau |
paradārābhigamanaṁ vinā vairaṁ ca raudratā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
mithyāvākyaṁ na te bhūtaṁ na bhaviṣyati rāghava |
kuto ’bhilaṣaṇaṁ strīṇāṁ pareṣāṁ dharmanāśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tac ca sarvaṁ mahābāho śakyaṁ voḍhuṁ jitendriyaiḥ |
tava vaśyendriyatvaṁ ca jānāmi śubhadarśana ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tr̥tīyaṁ yad idaṁ raudraṁ paraprāṇābhihiṁsanam |
nirvairaṁ kriyate mohāt tac ca te samupasthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
pratijñātas tvayā vīra daṇḍakāraṇyavāsinām |
r̥ṣīṇāṁ rakṣaṇārthāya vadhaḥ saṁyati rakṣasām ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
etannimittaṁ ca vanaṁ daṇḍakā iti viśrutam |
prasthitas tvaṁ saha bhrātrā dhr̥tabāṇaśarāsanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tatas tvāṁ prasthitaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā mama cintākulaṁ manaḥ |
tvad vr̥ttaṁ cintayantyā vai bhaven niḥśreyasaṁ hitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
na hi me rocate vīra gamanaṁ daṇḍakān prati |
kāraṇaṁ tatra vakṣyāmi vadantyāḥ śrūyatāṁ mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tvaṁ hi bāṇadhanuṣpāṇir bhrātrā saha vanaṁ gataḥ |
dr̥ṣṭvā vanacarān sarvān kaccit kuryāḥ śaravyayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
kṣatriyāṇām iha dhanur hutāśasyendhanāni ca |
samīpataḥ sthitaṁ tejobalam ucchrayate bhr̥śam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
purā kila mahābāho tapasvī satyavāk śuciḥ |
kasmiṁś cid abhavat puṇye vane ratamr̥gadvije ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tasyaiva tapaso vighnaṁ kartum indraḥ śacīpatiḥ |
khaḍgapāṇir athāgacchad āśramaṁ bhaṭa rūpadhr̥k ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tasmiṁs tad āśramapade nihitaḥ khaḍga uttamaḥ |
sa nyāsavidhinā dattaḥ puṇye tapasi tiṣṭhataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sa tac chastram anuprāpya nyāsarakṣaṇatatparaḥ |
vane tu vicaraty eva rakṣan pratyayam ātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
yatra gacchaty upādātuṁ mūlāni ca phalāni ca |
na vinā yāti taṁ khaḍgaṁ nyāsarakṣaṇatatparaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
nityaṁ śastraṁ parivahan krameṇa sa tapodhanaḥ |
cakāra raudrīṁ svāṁ buddhiṁ tyaktvā tapasi niścayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tataḥ sa raudrābhirataḥ pramatto ’dharmakarṣitaḥ |
tasya śastrasya saṁvāsāj jagāma narakaṁ muniḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
snehāc ca bahumānāc ca smāraye tvāṁ na śikṣaye |
na kathaṁ cana sā kāryā hr̥hītadhanuṣā tvayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
buddhir vairaṁ vinā hantuṁ rākṣasān daṇḍakāśritān |
aparādhaṁ vinā hantuṁ lokān vīra na kāmaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
kṣatriyāṇāṁ tu vīrāṇāṁ vaneṣu niyatātmanām |
dhanuṣā kāryam etāvad ārtānām abhirakṣaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
kva ca śastraṁ kva ca vanaṁ kva ca kṣātraṁ tapaḥ kva ca |
vyāviddham idam asmābhir deśadharmas tu pūjyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tad āryakaluṣā buddhir jāyate śastrasevanāt |
punar gatvā tv ayodhyāyāṁ kṣatradharmaṁ cariṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
akṣayā tu bhavet prītiḥ śvaśrū śvaśurayor mama |
yadi rājyaṁ hi saṁnyasya bhaves tvaṁ nirato muniḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
dharmād arthaḥ prabhavati dharmāt prabhavate sukham |
dharmeṇa labhate sarvaṁ dharmasāram idaṁ jagat ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
ātmānaṁ niyamais tais taiḥ karṣayitvā prayatnataḥ |
prāpyate nipuṇair dharmo na sukhāl labhyate sukham ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
nityaṁ śucimatiḥ saumya cara dharmaṁ tapovane |
sarvaṁ hi viditaṁ tubhyaṁ trailokyam api tattvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
strīcāpalād etad udāhr̥taṁ me; dharmaṁ ca vaktuṁ tava kaḥ samarthaḥ |
vicārya buddhyā tu sahānujena; yad rocate tat kuru mācireṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
