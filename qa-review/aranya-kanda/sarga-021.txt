=== Sarga 21 ===

---
1.
evam ādharṣitaḥ śūraḥ śūrpaṇakhyā kharas tadā |
uvāca rakṣasāṁ madhye kharaḥ kharataraṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tavāpamānaprabhavaḥ krodho ’yam atulo mama |
na śakyate dhārayituṁ lavaṇāmbha ivotthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
na rāmaṁ gaṇaye vīryān mānuṣaṁ kṣīṇajīvitam |
ātmā duścaritaiḥ prāṇān hato yo ’dya vimokṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
bāṣpaḥ saṁhriyatām eṣa saṁbhramaś ca vimucyatām |
ahaṁ rāmaḥ saha bhrātrā nayāmi yamasādanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
paraśvadhahatasyādya mandaprāṇasya bhūtale |
rāmasya rudhiraṁ raktam uṣṇaṁ pāsyasi rākṣasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
sā prahr̥ṣṭvā vacaḥ śrutvā kharasya vadanāc cyutam |
praśaśaṁsa punar maurkhyād bhrātaraṁ rakṣasāṁ varam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tayā paruṣitaḥ pūrvaṁ punar eva praśaṁsitaḥ |
abravīd dūṣaṇaṁ nāma kharaḥ senāpatiṁ tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
caturdaśa sahasrāṇi mama cittānuvartinām |
rakṣasīṁ bhīmavegānāṁ samareṣv anivartinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
nīlajīmūtavarṇānāṁ ghorāṇāṁ krūrakarmaṇām |
lokasiṁhāvihārāṇāṁ balinām ugratejasām ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
teṣāṁ śārdūladarpāṇāṁ mahāsyānāṁ mahaujasām |
sarvodyogam udīrṇānāṁ rakṣasāṁ saumya kāraya ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
upasthāpaya me kṣipraṁ rathaṁ saumya dhanūṁṣi ca |
śarāṁś ca citrān khaḍgāṁś ca śaktīś ca vividhāḥ śitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
agre niryātum icchāmi paulastyānāṁ mahātmanām |
vadhārthaṁ durvinītasya rāmasya raṇakovidaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
iti tasya bruvāṇasya sūryavarṇaṁ mahāratham |
sadaśvaiḥ śabalair yuktam ācacakṣe ’tha dūṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
taṁ meruśikharākāraṁ taptakāñcanabhūṣaṇam |
hemacakram asaṁbādhaṁ vaidūryamaya kūbaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
matsyaiḥ puṣpair drumaiḥ śailaiś candrasūryaiś ca kāñcanaiḥ |
māṅgalyaiḥ pakṣisaṁghaiś ca tārābhiś ca samāvr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
dhvajanistriṁśasaṁpannaṁ kiṅkiṇīkavibhūṣitam |
sadaśvayuktaṁ so ’marṣād āruroha rathaṁ kharaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
niśāmya taṁ rathagataṁ rākṣasā bhīmavikramāḥ |
tasthuḥ saṁparivāryainaṁ dūṣaṇaṁ ca mahābalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
kharas tu tān maheṣvāsān ghoracarmāyudhadhvajān |
niryātety abravīd dr̥ṣṭvā rathasthaḥ sarvarākṣasān ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tatas tad rākṣasaṁ sainyaṁ ghoracarmāyudhadhvajam |
nirjagāma janasthānān mahānādaṁ mahājavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
mudgaraiḥ paṭṭiśaiḥ śūlaiḥ sutīkṣṇaiś ca paraśvadhaiḥ |
khaḍgaiś cakraiś ca hastasthair bhrājamānaiś ca tomaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
śaktibhiḥ patighair ghorair atimātraiś ca kārmukaiḥ |
gadāsimusalair vajrair gr̥hītair bhīmadarśanaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
rākṣasānāṁ sughorāṇāṁ sahasrāṇi caturdaśa |
niryātāni janasthānāt kharacittānuvartinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tāṁs tv abhidravato dr̥ṣṭvā rākṣasān bhīmavikramān |
kharasyāpi rathaḥ kiṁ cij jagāma tadanantaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tatas tāñ śabalān aśvāṁs taptakāñcanabhūṣitān |
kharasya matam ājñāya sārathiḥ samacodayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
sa codito rathaḥ śīghraṁ kharasya ripughātinaḥ |
śabdenāpūrayām āsa diśaś ca pratiśas tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
pravr̥ddhamanyus tu kharaḥ kharasvano; ripor vadhārthaṁ tvarito yathāntakaḥ |
acūcudat sārathim unnadan punar; mahābalo megha ivāśmavarṣavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
