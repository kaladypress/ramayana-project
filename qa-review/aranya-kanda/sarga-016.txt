=== Sarga 16 ===

---
1.
kr̥tābhiṣeko rāmas tu sītā saumitrir eva ca |
tasmād godāvarītīrāt tato jagmuḥ svam āśramam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
āśramaṁ tam upāgamya rāghavaḥ sahalakṣmaṇaḥ |
kr̥tvā paurvāhṇikaṁ karma parṇaśālām upāgamat ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sa rāmaḥ parṇaśālāyām āsīnaḥ saha sītayā |
virarāja mahābāhuś citrayā candramā iva |
lakṣmaṇena saha bhrātrā cakāra vividhāḥ kathāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tadāsīnasya rāmasya kathāsaṁsaktacetasaḥ |
taṁ deśaṁ rākṣasī kā cid ājagāma yadr̥cchayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
sā tu śūrpaṇakhā nāma daśagrīvasya rakṣasaḥ |
bhaginī rāmam āsādya dadarśa tridaśopamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
siṁhoraskaṁ mahābāhuṁ padmapatranibhekṣaṇam |
sukumāraṁ mahāsattvaṁ pārthivavyañjanānvitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
rāmam indīvaraśyāmaṁ kandarpasadr̥śaprabham |
babhūvendropamaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rākṣasī kāmamohitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sumukhaṁ durmukhī rāmaṁ vr̥ttamadhyaṁ mahodarī |
viśālākṣaṁ virūpākṣī sukeśaṁ tāmramūrdhajā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
priyarūpaṁ virūpā sā susvaraṁ bhairavasvanā |
taruṇaṁ dāruṇā vr̥ddhā dakṣiṇaṁ vāmabhāṣiṇī ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
nyāyavr̥ttaṁ sudurvr̥ttā priyam apriyadarśanā |
śarīrajasamāviṣṭā rākṣasī rāmam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
jaṭī tāpasarūpeṇa sabhāryaḥ śaracāpadhr̥k |
āgatas tvam imaṁ deśaṁ kathaṁ rākṣasasevitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
evam uktas tu rākṣasyā śūrpaṇakhyā paraṁtapaḥ |
r̥jubuddhitayā sarvam ākhyātum upacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
āsīd daśaratho nāma rājā tridaśavikramaḥ |
tasyāham agrajaḥ putro rāmo nāma janaiḥ śrutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
bhrātāyaṁ lakṣmaṇo nāma yavīyān mām anuvrataḥ |
iyaṁ bhāryā ca vaidehī mama sīteti viśrutā ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
niyogāt tu narendrasya pitur mātuś ca yantritaḥ |
dharmārthaṁ dharmakāṅkṣī ca vanaṁ vastum ihāgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tvāṁ tu veditum icchāmi kathyatāṁ kāsi kasya vā |
iha vā kiṁnimittaṁ tvam āgatā brūhi tattvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
sābravīd vacanaṁ śrutvā rākṣasī madanārditā |
śrūyatāṁ rāma vakṣyāmi tattvārthaṁ vacanaṁ mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
ahaṁ śūrpaṇakhā nāma rākṣasī kāmarūpiṇī |
araṇyaṁ vicarāmīdam ekā sarvabhayaṁkarā ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
rāvaṇo nāma me bhrātā rākṣaso rākṣaseśvaraḥ |
pravr̥ddhanidraś ca sadā kumbhakarṇo mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
vibhīṣaṇas tu dharmātmā na tu rākṣasaceṣṭitaḥ |
prakhyātavīryau ca raṇe bhrātarau kharadūṣaṇau ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tān ahaṁ samatikrāntā rāma tvāpūrvadarśanāt |
samupetāsmi bhāvena bhartāraṁ puruṣottamam |
cirāya bhava bhartā me sītayā kiṁ kariṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
vikr̥tā ca virūpā ca na seyaṁ sadr̥śī tava |
aham evānurūpā te bhāryārūpeṇa paśya mām ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
imāṁ virūpām asatīṁ karālāṁ nirṇatodarīm |
anena saha te bhrātrā bhakṣayiṣyāmi mānuṣīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tataḥ parvataśr̥ṅgāṇi vanāni vividhāni ca |
paśyan saha mayā kānta daṇḍakān vicariṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
ity evam uktaḥ kākutsthaḥ prahasya madirekṣaṇām |
idaṁ vacanam ārebhe vaktuṁ vākyaviśāradaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
