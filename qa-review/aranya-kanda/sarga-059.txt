=== Sarga 59 ===

---
1.
dr̥ṣṭāśramapadaṁ śūnyaṁ rāmo daśarathātmajaḥ |
rahitāṁ parṇaśālāṁ ca vidhvastāny āsanāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
adr̥ṣṭvā tatra vaidehīṁ saṁnirīkṣya ca sarvaśaḥ |
uvāca rāmaḥ prākruśya pragr̥hya rucirau bhujau ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
kva nu lakṣmaṇa vaidehī kaṁ vā deśam ito gatā |
kenāhr̥tā vā saumitre bhakṣitā kena vā priyā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
vr̥ṣkeṇāvārya yadi māṁ sīte hasitum icchasi |
alaṁ te hasitenādya māṁ bhajasva suduḥkhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
yaiḥ saha krīḍase sīte viśvastair mr̥gapotakaiḥ |
ete hīnās tvayā saumye dhyāyanty asrāvilekṣaṇāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
mr̥taṁ śokena mahatā sītāharaṇajena mām |
paraloke mahārājo nūnaṁ drakṣyati me pitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
kathaṁ pratijñāṁ saṁśrutya mayā tvam abhiyojitaḥ |
apūrayitvā taṁ kālaṁ matsakāśam ihāgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
kāmavr̥ttam anāryaṁ māṁ mr̥ṣāvādinam eva ca |
dhik tvām iti pare loke vyaktaṁ vakṣyati me pitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
vivaśaṁ śokasaṁtaptaṁ dīnaṁ bhagnamanoratham |
mām ihotsr̥jya karuṇaṁ kīrtir naram ivānr̥jum ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
kva gacchasi varārohe mām utsr̥jya sumadhyame |
tvayā virahitaś cāhaṁ mokṣye jīvitam ātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
itīva vilapan rāmaḥ sītādarśanalālasaḥ |
na dadarśa suduḥkhārto rāghavo janakātmajām ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
anāsādayamānaṁ taṁ sītāṁ daśarathātmajam |
paṅkam āsādya vipulaṁ sīdantam iva kuñjaram |
lakṣmaṇo rāmam atyartham uvāca hitakāmyayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
mā viṣādaṁ mahābāho kuru yatnaṁ mayā saha |
idaṁ ca hi vanaṁ śūra bahukandaraśobhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
priyakānanasaṁcārā vanonmattā ca maithilī |
sā vanaṁ vā praviṣṭā syān nalinīṁ vā supuṣpitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
saritaṁ vāpi saṁprāptā mīnavañjurasevitām |
vitrāsayitukāmā vā līnā syāt kānane kva cit |
jijñāsamānā vaidehī tvāṁ māṁ ca puruṣarṣabha ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tasyā hy anveṣaṇe śrīman kṣipram eva yatāvahe |
vanaṁ sarvaṁ vicinuvo yatra sā janakātmajā |
manyase yadi kākutstha mā sma śoke manaḥ kr̥thāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
evam uktas tu sauhārdāl lakṣmaṇena samāhitaḥ |
saha saumitriṇā rāmo vicetum upacakrame |
tau vanāni girīṁś caiva saritaś ca sarāṁsi ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
nikhilena vicinvantau sītāṁ daśarathātmajau |
tasya śailasya sānūni guhāś ca śikharāṇi ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
nikhilena vicinvantau naiva tām abhijagmatuḥ |
vicitya sarvataḥ śailaṁ rāmo lakṣmaṇam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
neha paśyāmi saumitre vaidehīṁ parvate śubhe |
tato duḥkhābhisaṁtapto lakṣmaṇo vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
vicaran daṇḍakāraṇyaṁ bhrātaraṁ dīptatejasaṁ |
prāpsyasi tvaṁ mahāprājña maithilīṁ janakātmajām ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
yathā viṣṇur mahābāhur baliṁ baddhvā mahīm imām |
evam uktas tu vīreṇa lakṣmaṇena sa rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
uvāca dīnayā vācā duḥkhābhihatacetanaḥ |
vanaṁ sarvaṁ suvicitaṁ padminyaḥ phullapaṅkajāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
giriś cāyaṁ mahāprājña bahukandaranirjharaḥ |
na hi paśyāmi vaidehīṁ prāṇebhyo ’pi garīyasīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
evaṁ sa vilapan rāmaḥ sītāharaṇakarśitaḥ |
dīnaḥ śokasamāviṣṭo muhūrtaṁ vihvalo ’bhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
sa vihvalitasarvāṅgo gatabuddhir vicetanaḥ |
viṣasādāturo dīno niḥśvasyāśītam āyatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
bahuśaḥ sa tu niḥśvasya rāmo rājīvalocanaḥ |
hā priyeti vicukrośa bahuśo bāṣpagadgadaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
taṁ sāntvayām āsa tato lakṣmaṇaḥ priyabāndhavaḥ |
bahuprakāraṁ dharmajñaḥ praśritaḥ praśritāñjaliḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
anādr̥tya tu tad vākyaṁ lakṣmaṇauṣṭhapuṭacyutam |
apaśyaṁs tāṁ priyāṁ sītāṁ prākrośat sa punaḥ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
