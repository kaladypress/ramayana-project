=== Sarga 44 ===

---
1.
tayā paruṣam uktas tu kupito rāghavānujaḥ |
sa vikāṅkṣan bhr̥śaṁ rāmaṁ pratasthe nacirād iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tadāsādya daśagrīvaḥ kṣipram antaram āsthitaḥ |
abhicakrāma vaidehīṁ parivrājakarūpadhr̥k ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
ślakṣṇakāṣāyasaṁvītaḥ śikhī chatrī upānahī |
vāme cāṁse ’vasajyātha śubhe yaṣṭikamaṇḍalū |
parivrājakarūpeṇa vaidehīṁ samupāgamat ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tām āsasādātibalo bhrātr̥bhyāṁ rahitāṁ vane |
rahitāṁ sūryacandrābhyāṁ saṁdhyām iva mahattamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tām apaśyat tato bālāṁ rājaputrīṁ yaśasvinīm |
rohiṇīṁ śaśinā hīnāṁ grahavad bhr̥śadāruṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tam ugraṁ pāpakarmāṇaṁ janasthānaruhā drumāḥ |
samīkṣya na prakampante na pravāti ca mārutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
śīghrasrotāś ca taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā vīkṣantaṁ raktalocanam |
stimitaṁ gantum ārebhe bhayād godāvarī nadī ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
rāmasya tv antaraṁ prepsur daśagrīvas tadantare |
upatasthe ca vaidehīṁ bhikṣurūpeṇa rāvaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
abhavyo bhavyarūpeṇa bhartāram anuśocatīm |
abhyavartata vaidehīṁ citrām iva śanaiścaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
sa pāpo bhavyarūpeṇa tr̥ṇaiḥ kūpa ivāvr̥taḥ |
atiṣṭhat prekṣya vaidehīṁ rāmapatnīṁ yaśasvinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
śubhāṁ ruciradantauṣṭhīṁ pūrṇacandranibhānanām |
āsīnāṁ parṇaśālāyāṁ bāṣpaśokābhipīḍitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sa tāṁ padmapalāśākṣīṁ pītakauśeyavāsinīm |
abhyagacchata vaidehīṁ duṣṭacetā niśācaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sa manmathaśarāviṣṭo brahmaghoṣam udīrayan |
abravīt praśritaṁ vākyaṁ rahite rākṣasādhipaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tām uttamāṁ trilokānāṁ padmahīnām iva śriyam |
vibhrājamānāṁ vapuṣā rāvaṇaḥ praśaśaṁsa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
kā tvaṁ kāñcanavarṇābhe pītakauśeyavāsini |
kamalānāṁ śubhāṁ mālāṁ padminīva ca bibhratī ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
hrīḥ śrīḥ kīrtiḥ śubhā lakṣmīr apsarā vā śubhānane |
bhūtir vā tvaṁ varārohe ratir vā svairacāriṇī ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
samāḥ śikhariṇaḥ snigdhāḥ pāṇḍurā daśanās tava |
viśāle vimale netre raktānte kr̥ṣṇatārake ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
viśālaṁ jaghanaṁ pīnam ūrū karikaropamau |
etāv upacitau vr̥ttau sahitau saṁpragalbhitau ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
pīnonnatamukhau kāntau snigdhatālaphalopamau |
maṇipravekābharaṇau rucirau te payodharau ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
cārusmite cārudati cārunetre vilāsini |
mano harasi me rāme nadīkūlam ivāmbhasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
karāntamitamadhyāsi sukeśī saṁhatastanī |
naiva devī na gandharvī na yakṣī na ca kiṁnarī ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
naivaṁrūpā mayā nārī dr̥ṣṭapūrvā mahītale |
iha vāsaś ca kāntāre cittam unmāthayanti me ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
sā pratikrāma bhadraṁ te na tvaṁ vastum ihārhasi |
rākṣasānām ayaṁ vāso ghorāṇāṁ kāmarūpiṇām ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
prāsādāgryāṇi ramyāṇi nagaropavanāni ca |
saṁpannāni sugandhīni yuktāny ācarituṁ tvayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
varaṁ mālyaṁ varaṁ pānaṁ varaṁ vastraṁ ca śobhane |
bhartāraṁ ca varaṁ manye tvadyuktam asitekṣaṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
kā tvaṁ bhavasi rudrāṇāṁ marutāṁ vā śucismite |
vasūnāṁ vā varārohe devatā pratibhāsi me ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
neha gacchantī gandharvā na devā na ca kiṁnarāḥ |
rākṣasānām ayaṁ vāsaḥ kathaṁ nu tvam ihāgatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
iha śākhāmr̥gāḥ siṁhā dvīpivyāghramr̥gās tathā |
r̥kṣās tarakṣavaḥ kaṅkāḥ kathaṁ tebhyo na bibhyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
madānvitānāṁ ghorāṇāṁ kuñjarāṇāṁ tarasvinām |
katham ekā mahāraṇye na bibheṣi vanānane ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
kāsi kasya kutaś ca tvaṁ kiṁnimittaṁ ca daṇḍakān |
ekā carasi kalyāṇi ghorān rākṣasasevitān ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
iti praśastā vaidehī rāvaṇena durātmanā |
dvijātiveṣeṇa hi taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rāvaṇam āgatam |
sarvair atithisatkāraiḥ pūjayām āsa maithilī ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
upānīyāsanaṁ pūrvaṁ pādyenābhinimantrya ca |
abravīt siddham ity eva tadā taṁ saumyadarśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
dvijātiveṣeṇa samīkṣya maithilī; tam āgataṁ pātrakusumbhadhāriṇam |
aśakyam uddveṣṭum upāyadarśanān; nyamantrayad brāhmaṇavad yathāgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
iyaṁ br̥sī brāhmaṇa kāmam āsyatām; idaṁ ca pādyaṁ pratigr̥hyatām iti |
idaṁ ca siddhaṁ vanajātam uttamaṁ; tvadartham avyagram ihopabhujyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
nimantryamāṇaḥ pratipūrṇabhāṣiṇīṁ; narendrapatnīṁ prasamīkṣya maithilīm |
prahasya tasyā haraṇe dhr̥taṁ manaḥ; samarpayām āsa vadhāya rāvaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
tataḥ suveṣaṁ mr̥gayā gataṁ patiṁ; pratīkṣamāṇā sahalakṣmaṇaṁ tadā |
nirīkṣamāṇā haritaṁ dadarśa tan; mahad vanaṁ naiva tu rāmalakṣmaṇau ||

=== meaning ===

---
