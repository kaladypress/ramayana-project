=== Sarga 52 ===

---
1.
tataḥ sumantras tv āgamya rāghavaṁ vākyam abravīt |
ete nivāritā rājan dvāri tiṣṭhanti tāpasāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
bhārgavaṁ cyavanaṁ nāma puraskr̥tya maharṣayaḥ |
darśanaṁ te mahārāja codayanti kr̥tatvarāḥ |
prīyamāṇā naravyāghra yamunātīravāsinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā rāmaḥ provāca dharmavit |
praveśyantāṁ mahātmāno bhārgavapramukhā dvijāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
rājñas tv ājñāṁ puraskr̥tya dvāḥstho mūrdhni kr̥tāñjaliḥ |
praveśayām āsa tatas tāpasān saṁmatān bahūn ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
śataṁ samadhikaṁ tatra dīpyamānaṁ svatejasā |
praviṣṭaṁ rājabhavanaṁ tāpasānāṁ mahātmanām ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
te dvijāḥ pūrṇakalaśaiḥ sarvatīrthāmbu satkr̥tam |
gr̥hītvā phalamūlaṁ ca rāmasyābhyāharan bahu ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
pratigr̥hya tu tat sarvaṁ rāmaḥ prītipuraskr̥taḥ |
tīrthodakāni sarvāṇi phalāni vividhāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
uvāca ca mahābāhuḥ sarvān eva mahāmunīn |
imāny āsanamukhyāni yathārham upaviśyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
rāmasya bhāṣitaṁ śrutvā sarva eva maharṣayaḥ |
br̥sīṣu rucirākhyāsu niṣeduḥ kāñcanīṣu te ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
upaviṣṭān r̥ṣīṁs tatra dr̥ṣṭvā parapuraṁjayaḥ |
prayataḥ prāñjalir bhūtvā rāghavo vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
kim āgamanakāryaṁ vaḥ kiṁ karomi tapodhanāḥ |
ājñāpyo ’haṁ maharṣīṇāṁ sarvakāmakaraḥ sukham ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
idaṁ rājyaṁ ca sakalaṁ jīvitaṁ ca hr̥di sthitam |
sarvam etad dvijārthaṁ me satyam etad bravīmi vaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā sādhuvādo mahān abhūt |
r̥ṣīṇām ugratapasāṁ yamunātīravāsinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
ūcuś ca te mahātmāno harṣeṇa mahatānvitāḥ |
upapannaṁ naraśreṣṭha tavaiva bhuvi nānyataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
bahavaḥ pārthivā rājann atikrāntā mahābalāḥ |
kāryagauravam aśrutvā pratijñāṁ nābhyarocayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tvayā punar brāhmaṇagauravād iyaṁ; kr̥tā pratijñā hy anavekṣya kāraṇam |
kuruṣva kartā hy asi nātra saṁśayo; mahābhayāt trātum r̥ṣīṁs tvam arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
