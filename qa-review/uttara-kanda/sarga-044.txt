=== Sarga 44 ===

---
1.
teṣāṁ samupaviṣṭānāṁ sarveṣāṁ dīnacetasām |
uvāca vākyaṁ kākutstho mukhena pariśuṣyatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sarve śr̥ṇuta bhadraṁ vo mā kurudhvaṁ mano ’nyathā |
paurāṇāṁ mama sītāyāṁ yādr̥śī vartate kathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
paurāpavādaḥ sumahāṁs tathā janapadasya ca |
vartate mayi bībhatsaḥ sa me marmāṇi kr̥ntati ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
ahaṁ kila kule jāta ikṣvākūṇāṁ mahātmanām |
sītāṁ pāpasamācārām ānayeyaṁ kathaṁ pure ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
jānāsi hi yathā saumya daṇḍake vijane vane |
rāvaṇena hr̥tā sītā sa ca vidhvaṁsito mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
pratyakṣaṁ tava saumitre devānāṁ havyavāhanaḥ |
apāpāṁ maithilīm āha vāyuś cākāśagocaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
candrādityau ca śaṁsete surāṇāṁ saṁnidhau purā |
r̥ṣīṇāṁ caiva sarveṣām apāpāṁ janakātmajām ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
evaṁ śuddhasamācārā devagandharvasaṁnidhau |
laṅkādvīpe mahendreṇa mama haste niveśitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
antarātmā ca me vetti sītāṁ śuddhāṁ yaśasvinīm |
tato gr̥hītvā vaidehīm ayodhyām aham āgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
ayaṁ tu me mahān vādaḥ śokaś ca hr̥di vartate |
paurāpavādaḥ sumahāṁs tathā janapadasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
akīrtir yasya gīyeta loke bhūtasya kasya cit |
pataty evādhamām̐l lokān yāvac chabdaḥ sa kīrtyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
akīrtir nindyate daivaiḥ kīrtir deveṣu pūjyate |
kīrtyarthaṁ ca samārambhaḥ sarva eva mahātmanām ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
apy ahaṁ jīvitaṁ jahyāṁ yuṣmān vā puruṣarṣabhāḥ |
apavādabhayād bhītaḥ kiṁ punar janakātmajām ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tasmād bhavantaḥ paśyantu patitaṁ śokasāgare |
na hi paśyāmy ahaṁ bhūyaḥ kiṁ cid duḥkham ato ’dhikam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
śvas tvaṁ prabhāte saumitre sumantrādhiṣṭhitaṁ ratham |
āruhya sītām āropya viṣayānte samutsr̥ja ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
gaṅgāyās tu pare pāre vālmīkeḥ sumahātmanaḥ |
āśramo divyasaṁkāśas tamasātīram āśritaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tatraināṁ vijane kakṣe visr̥jya raghunandana |
śīghram āgaccha saumitre kuruṣva vacanaṁ mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
na cāsmi prativaktavyaḥ sītāṁ prati kathaṁ cana |
aprītiḥ paramā mahyaṁ bhavet tu prativārite ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
śāpitāś ca mayā yūyaṁ bhujābhyāṁ jīvitena ca |
ye māṁ vākyāntare brūyur anunetuṁ kathaṁ cana ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
mānayantu bhavanto māṁ yadi macchāsane sthitāḥ |
ito ’dya nīyatāṁ sītā kuruṣva vacanaṁ mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
pūrvam ukto ’ham anayā gaṅgātīre mahāśramān |
paśyeyam iti tasyāś ca kāmaḥ saṁvartyatām ayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
evam uktvā tu kākutstho bāṣpeṇa pihitekṣaṇaḥ |
praviveśa sa dharmātmā bhrātr̥bhiḥ parivāritaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
