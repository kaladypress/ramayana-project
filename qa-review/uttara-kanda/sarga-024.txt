=== Sarga 24 ===

---
1.
nivartamānaḥ saṁhr̥ṣṭo rāvaṇaḥ sa durātmavān |
jahre pathi narendrarṣidevagandharvakanyakāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
darśanīyāṁ hi yāṁ rakṣaḥ kanyāṁ strīṁ vātha paśyati |
hatvā bandhujanaṁ tasyā vimāne saṁnyaveśayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tatra pannagayakṣāṇāṁ mānuṣāṇāṁ ca rakṣasām |
daityānāṁ dānavānāṁ ca kanyā jagrāha rāvaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
dīrghakeśyaḥ sucārvaṅgyaḥ pūrṇacandranibhānanāḥ |
śokāyattās taruṇyaś ca samastā stananamritāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tulyam agnyarciṣāṁ tatra śokāgnibhayasaṁbhavam |
pravepamānā duḥkhārtā mumucur bāṣpajaṁ jalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tāsāṁ niśvasamānānāṁ niśvasaiḥ saṁpradīpitam |
agnihotram ivābhāti saṁniruddhāgnipuṣpakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
kā cid dadhyau suduḥkhārtā hanyād api hi mām ayam |
smr̥tvā mātr̥̄ḥ pitr̥̄n bhrātr̥̄n putrān vai śvaśurān api |
duḥkhaśokasamāviṣṭā vilepuḥ sahitāḥ striyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
kathaṁ nu khalu me putraḥ kariṣyati mayā vinā |
kathaṁ mātā kathaṁ bhrātā nimagnāḥ śokasāgare ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
hā kathaṁ nu kariṣyāmi bhartāraṁ daivataṁ vinā |
mr̥tyo prasīda yāce tvāṁ naya māṁ yamasādanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
kiṁ nu me duṣkr̥taṁ karma kr̥taṁ dehāntare purā |
tato ’smi dharṣitānena patitā śokasāgare ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
na khalv idānīṁ paśyāmi duḥkhasyāntam ihātmanaḥ |
aho dhin mānuṣām̐l lokān nāsti khalv adhamaḥ paraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
yad durbalā balavatā bāndhavā rāvaṇena me |
uditenaiva sūryeṇa tārakā iva nāśitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
aho subalavad rakṣo vadhopāyeṣu rajyate |
aho durvr̥ttam ātmānaṁ svayam eva na budhyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sarvathā sadr̥śas tāvad vikramo ’sya durātmanaḥ |
idaṁ tv asadr̥śaṁ karma paradārābhimarśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
yasmād eṣa parakhyāsu strīṣu rajyati durmatiḥ |
tasmād dhi strīkr̥tenaiva vadhaṁ prāpsyati rāvaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
śaptaḥ strībhiḥ sa tu tadā hatatejāḥ suniṣprabha |
pativratābhiḥ sādhvībhiḥ sthitābhiḥ sādhuvartmani ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
evaṁ vilapamānāsu rāvaṇo rākṣasādhipaḥ |
praviveśa purīṁ laṅkāṁ pūjyamāno niśācaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tato rākṣasarājasya svasā paramaduḥkhitā |
pādayoḥ patitā tasya vaktum evopacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tataḥ svasāram utthāpya rāvaṇaḥ parisāntvayan |
abravīt kim idaṁ bhadre vaktum arhasi me drutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
sā bāṣpapariruddhākṣī rākṣasī vākyam abravīt |
hatāsmi vidhavā rājaṁs tvayā balavatā kr̥tā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
ete vīryāt tvayā rājan daityā vinihatā raṇe |
kālakeyā iti khyātā mahābalaparākramāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tatra me nihato bhartā garīyāñ jīvitād api |
sa tvayā dayitas tatra bhrātrā śatrusamena vai ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
yā tvayāsmi hatā rājan svayam eveha bandhunā |
duḥkhaṁ vaidhavyaśabdaṁ ca dattaṁ bhokṣyāmy ahaṁ tvayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
nanu nāma tvayā rakṣyo jāmātā samareṣv api |
taṁ nihatya raṇe rājan svayam eva na lajjase ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
evam uktas tayā rakṣo bhaginyā krośamānayā |
abravīt sāntvayitvā tāṁ sāmapūrvam idaṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
alaṁ vatse viṣādena na bhetavyaṁ ca sarvaśaḥ |
mānadānaviśeṣais tvāṁ toṣayiṣyāmi nityaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
yuddhe pramatto vyākṣipto jayakāṅkṣī kṣipañ śarān |
nāvagacchāmi yuddheṣu svān parān vāpy ahaṁ śubhe |
tenāsau nihataḥ saṁkhye mayā bhartā tava svasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
asmin kāle tu yat prāptaṁ tat kariṣyāmi te hitam |
bhrātur aiśvaryasaṁsthasya kharasya bhava pārśvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
caturdaśānāṁ bhrātā te sahasrāṇāṁ bhaviṣyati |
prabhuḥ prayāṇe dāne ca rākṣasānāṁ mahaujasām ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
tatra mātr̥ṣvasuḥ putro bhrātā tava kharaḥ prabhuḥ |
bhaviṣyati sadā kurvan yad vakṣyasi vacaḥ svayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
śīghraṁ gacchatv ayaṁ śūro daṇḍakān parirakṣitum |
dūṣaṇo ’sya balādhyakṣo bhaviṣyati mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
sa hi śapto vanoddeśaḥ kruddhenośanasā purā |
rākṣasānām ayaṁ vāso bhaviṣyati na saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
evam uktvā daśagrīvaḥ sainyaṁ tasyādideśa ha |
caturdaśa sahasrāṇi rakṣasāṁ kāmarūpiṇām ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
sa taiḥ sarvaiḥ parivr̥to rākṣasair ghoradarśanaiḥ |
kharaḥ saṁprayayau śīghraṁ daṇḍakān akutobhayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
sa tatra kārayām āsa rājyaṁ nihatakaṇṭakam |
sā ca śūrpaṇakhā prītā nyavasad daṇḍakāvane ||

=== meaning ===

---
