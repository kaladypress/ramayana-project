=== Sarga 39 ===

---
1.
tathā sma teṣāṁ vasatām r̥kṣavānararakṣasām |
rāghavas tu mahātejāḥ sugrīvam idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
gamyatāṁ saumya kiṣkindhāṁ durādharṣāṁ surāsuraiḥ |
pālayasva sahāmātyai rājyaṁ nihatakaṇṭakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
aṅgadaṁ ca mahābāho prītyā paramayānvitaḥ |
paśya tvaṁ hanumantaṁ ca nalaṁ ca sumahābalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
suṣeṇaṁ śvaśuraṁ śūraṁ tāraṁ ca balināṁ varam |
kumudaṁ caiva durdharṣaṁ nīlaṁ ca sumahābalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
vīraṁ śatabaliṁ caiva maindaṁ dvividam eva ca |
gajaṁ gavākṣaṁ gavayaṁ śarabhaṁ ca mahābalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
r̥kṣarājaṁ ca durdharṣaṁ jāmbavantaṁ mahābalam |
paśya prītisamāyukto gandhamādanam eva ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
ye cānye sumahātmāno madarthe tyaktajīvitāḥ |
paśya tvaṁ prītisaṁyukto mā caiṣāṁ vipriyaṁ kr̥thāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
evam uktvā ca sugrīvaṁ praśasya ca punaḥ punaḥ |
vibhīṣaṇam athovāca rāmo madhurayā girā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
laṅkāṁ praśādhi dharmeṇa saṁmato hy asi pārthiva |
purasya rākṣasānāṁ ca bhrātur vaiśvaraṇasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
mā ca buddhim adharme tvaṁ kuryā rājan kathaṁ cana |
buddhimanto hi rājāno dhruvam aśnanti medinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
ahaṁ ca nityaśo rājan sugrīvasahitas tvayā |
smartavyaḥ parayā prītyā gaccha tvaṁ vigatajvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
rāmasya bhāṣitaṁ śrutvā r̥kṣavānararākṣasāḥ |
sādhu sādhv iti kākutsthaṁ praśaśaṁsuḥ punaḥ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tava buddhir mahābāho vīryam adbhutam eva ca |
mādhuryaṁ paramaṁ rāma svayambhor iva nityadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
teṣām evaṁ bruvāṇānāṁ vānarāṇāṁ ca rakṣasām |
hanūmān praṇato bhūtvā rāghavaṁ vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sneho me paramo rājaṁs tvayi nityaṁ pratiṣṭhitaḥ |
bhaktiś ca niyatā vīra bhāvo nānyatra gacchati ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
yāvad rāmakathāṁ vīra śroṣye ’haṁ pr̥thivītale |
tāvac charīre vatsyantu mama prāṇā na saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
evaṁ bruvāṇaṁ rājendro hanūmantam athāsanāt |
utthāya ca pariṣvajya vākyam etad uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
evam etat kapiśreṣṭha bhavitā nātra saṁśayaḥ |
lokā hi yāvat sthāsyanti tāvat sthāsyati me kathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
cariṣyati kathā yāval lokān eṣā hi māmikā |
tāvac charīre vatsyanti prāṇās tava na saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tato ’sya hāraṁ candrābhaṁ mucya kaṇṭhāt sa rāghavaḥ |
vaidūryataralaṁ snehād ābabandhe hanūmati ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tenorasi nibaddhena hāreṇa sa mahākapiḥ |
rarāja hemaśailendraś candreṇākrāntamastakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
śrutvā tu rāghavasyaitad utthāyotthāya vānarāḥ |
praṇamya śirasā pādau prajagmus te mahābalāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
sugrīvaś caiva rāmeṇa pariṣvakto mahābhujaḥ |
vibhīṣaṇaś ca dharmātmā nirantaram urogataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
sarve ca te bāṣpagalāḥ sāśrunetrā vicetasaḥ |
saṁmūḍhā iva duḥkhena tyajante rāghavaṁ tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
