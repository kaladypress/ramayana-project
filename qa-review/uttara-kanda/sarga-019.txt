=== Sarga 19 ===

---
1.
atha jitvā maruttaṁ sa prayayau rākṣasādhipaḥ |
nagarāṇi narendrāṇāṁ yuddhakāṅkṣī daśānanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sa samāsādya rājendrān mahendravaruṇopamān |
abravīd rākṣasendras tu yuddhaṁ me dīyatām iti ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
nirjitāḥ smeti vā brūta eṣo hi mama niścayaḥ |
anyathā kurvatām evaṁ mokṣo vo nopapadyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tatas tu bahavaḥ prājñāḥ pārthivā dharmaṇiścayāḥ |
nirjitāḥ smety abhāṣanta jñātvā varabalaṁ ripoḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
duṣyantaḥ suratho gādhir gayo rājā purūravāḥ |
ete sarve ’bruvaṁs tāta nirjitāḥ smeti pārthivāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
athāyodhyāṁ samāsādya rāvaṇo rākṣasādhipaḥ |
suguptām anaraṇyena śakreṇevāmarāvatīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
prāha rājānam āsādya yuddhaṁ me saṁpradīyatām |
nirjito ’smīti vā brūhi mamaitad iha śāsanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
anaraṇyaḥ susaṁkruddho rākṣasendram athābravīt |
dīyate dvandvayuddhaṁ te rākṣasādhipate mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
atha pūrvaṁ śrutārthena sajjitaṁ sumahad dhi yat |
niṣkrāmat tan narendrasya balaṁ rakṣovadhodyatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
nāgānāṁ bahusāhasraṁ vājinām ayutaṁ tathā |
mahīṁ saṁchādya niṣkrāntaṁ sapadātirathaṁ kṣaṇāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tad rāvaṇabalaṁ prāpya balaṁ tasya mahīpateḥ |
prāṇaśyata tadā rājan havyaṁ hutam ivānale ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
so ’paśyata narendras tu naśyamānaṁ mahad balam |
mahārṇavaṁ samāsādya yathā pañcāpagā jalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tataḥ śakradhanuḥprakhyaṁ dhanur visphārayan svayam |
āsadāda narendrās taṁ rāvaṇaṁ krodhamūrchitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tato bāṇaśatāny aṣṭau pātayām āsa mūrdhani |
tasya rākṣasarājasya ikṣvākukulanandanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tasya bāṇāḥ patantas te cakrire na kṣataṁ kva cit |
vāridhārā ivābhrebhyaḥ patantyo nagamūrdhani ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tato rākṣasarājena kruddhena nr̥patis tadā |
talena bhihato mūrdhni sa rathān nipapāta ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
sa rājā patito bhūmau vihvalāṅgaḥ pravepitaḥ |
vajradagdha ivāraṇye sālo nipatito mahān ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
taṁ prahasyābravīd rakṣa ikṣvākuṁ pr̥thivīpatim |
kim idānīṁ tvayā prāptaṁ phalaṁ māṁ prati yudhyatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
trailokye nāsti yo dvandvaṁ mama dadyān narādhipa |
śaṅke pramatto bhogeṣu na śr̥ṇoṣi balaṁ mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tasyaivaṁ bruvato rājā mandāsur vākyam abravīt |
kiṁ śakyam iha kartuṁ vai yat kālo duratikramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
na hy ahaṁ nirjito rakṣas tvayā cātmapraśaṁsinā |
kāleneha vipanno ’haṁ hetubhūtas tu me bhavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
kiṁ tv idānīṁ mayā śakyaṁ kartuṁ prāṇaparikṣaye |
ikṣvākuparibhāvitvād vaco vakṣyāmi rākṣasa ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
yadi dattaṁ yadi hutaṁ yadi me sukr̥taṁ tapaḥ |
yadi guptāḥ prajāḥ samyak tathā satyaṁ vaco ’stu me ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
utpatsyate kule hy asminn ikṣvākūṇāṁ mahātmanām |
rājā paramatejasvī yas te prāṇān hariṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tato jaladharodagras tāḍito devadundubhiḥ |
tasminn udāhr̥te śāpe puṣpavr̥ṣṭiś ca khāc cyutā ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tataḥ sa rājā rājendra gataḥ sthānaṁ triviṣṭapam |
svargate ca nr̥pe rāma rākṣasaḥ sa nyavartata ||

=== meaning ===

---
