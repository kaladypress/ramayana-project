=== Sarga 73 ===

---
1.
r̥ṣer vacanam ājñāya rāmaḥ saṁdhyām upāsitum |
upākrāmat saraḥ puṇyam apsarobhir niṣevitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tatrodakam upaspr̥śya saṁdhyām anvāsya paścimām |
āśramaṁ prāviśad rāmaḥ kumbhayoner mahātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
asyāgastyo bahuguṇaṁ phalamūlaṁ tathauṣadhīḥ |
śākāni ca pavitrāṇi bhojanārtham akalpayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
sa bhuktavān naraśreṣṭhas tad annam amr̥topamam |
prītaś ca parituṣṭaś ca tāṁ rātriṁ samupāvasat ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
prabhāte kālyam utthāya kr̥tvāhnikam ariṁdamaḥ |
r̥ṣiṁ samabhicakrāma gamanāya raghūttamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
abhivādyābravīd rāmo maharṣiṁ kumbhasaṁbhavam |
āpr̥cche tvāṁ gamiṣyāmi mām anujñātum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
dhanyo ’smy anugr̥hīto ’smi darśanena mahātmanaḥ |
draṣṭuṁ caivāgamiṣyāmi pāvanārtham ihātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tathā vadati kākutsthe vākyam adbhutadarśanam |
uvāca paramaprīto dharmanetras tapodhanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
atyadbhutam idaṁ vākyaṁ tava rāma śubhākṣaram |
pāvanaḥ sarvalokānāṁ tvam eva raghunandana ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
muhūrtam api rāma tvāṁ ye nu paśyanti ke cana |
pāvitāḥ svargabhūtās te pūjyante divi daivataiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
ye ca tvāṁ ghoracakṣurbhir īkṣante prāṇino bhuvi |
hatās te yamadaṇḍena sadyo nirayagāminaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
gaccha cāriṣṭam avyagraḥ panthānam akutobhayam |
praśādhi rājyaṁ dharmeṇa gatir hi jagato bhavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
evam uktas tu muninā prāñjaliḥ pragraho nr̥paḥ |
abhyavādayata prājñas tam r̥ṣiṁ puṇyaśīlinam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
abhivādya muniśreṣṭhaṁ tāṁś ca sarvāṁs tapodhanān |
adhyārohat tad avyagraḥ puṣpakaṁ hemabhūṣitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
taṁ prayāntaṁ munigaṇā āśīrvādaiḥ samantataḥ |
apūjayan mahendrābhaṁ sahasrākṣam ivāmarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
svasthaḥ sa dadr̥śe rāmaḥ puṣpake hemabhūṣite |
śaśī meghasamīpastho yathā jaladharāgame ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tato ’rdhadivase prāpte pūjyamānas tatas tataḥ |
ayodhyāṁ prāpya kākutstho vimānād avarohata ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tato visr̥jya ruciraṁ puṣpakaṁ kāmagāminam |
kakṣyāntaravinikṣiptaṁ dvāḥsthaṁ rāmo ’bravīd vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
lakṣmaṇaṁ bharataṁ caiva gatvā tau laghuvikramau |
mamāgamanam ākhyāya śabdāpaya ca mā ciram ||

=== meaning ===

---
