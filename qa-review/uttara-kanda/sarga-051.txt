=== Sarga 51 ===

---
1.
tatra tāṁ rajanīm uṣya gomatyāṁ raghunandanaḥ |
prabhāte punar utthāya lakṣmaṇaḥ prayayau tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tato ’rdhadivase prāpte praviveśa mahārathaḥ |
ayodhyāṁ ratnasaṁpūrṇāṁ hr̥ṣṭapuṣṭajanāvr̥tām ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
saumitris tu paraṁ dainyaṁ jagāma sumahāmatiḥ |
rāmapādau samāsādya vakṣyāmi kim ahaṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tasyaivaṁ cintayānasya bhavanaṁ śaśisaṁnibham |
rāmasya paramodāraṁ purastāt samadr̥śyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
rājñas tu bhavanadvāri so ’vatīrya narottamaḥ |
avāṅmukho dīnamanāḥ praviveśānivāritaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
sa dr̥ṣṭvā rāghavaṁ dīnam āsīnaṁ paramāsane |
netrābhyām aśrupūrṇābhyāṁ dadarśāgrajam agrataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
jagrāha caraṇau tasya lakṣmaṇo dīnacetanaḥ |
uvāca dīnayā vācā prāñjaliḥ susamāhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
āryasyājñāṁ puraskr̥tya visr̥jya janakātmajām |
gaṅgātīre yathoddiṣṭe vālmīker āśrame śubhe |
punar asmy āgato vīra pādamūlam upāsitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
mā śucaḥ puruṣavyāghra kālasya gatir īdr̥śī |
tvadvidhā na hi śocanti sattvavanto manasvinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
sarve kṣayāntā nicayāḥ patanāntāḥ samucchrayāḥ |
saṁyogā viprayogāntā maraṇāntaṁ ca jīvitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
śaktas tvam ātmanātmānaṁ vijetuṁ manasaiva hi |
lokān sarvāṁś ca kākutstha kiṁ punar duḥkham īdr̥śam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
nedr̥śeṣu vimuhyanti tvadvidhāḥ puruṣarṣabhāḥ |
yadarthaṁ maithilī tyaktā apavādabhayān nr̥pa ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sa tvaṁ puruṣaśārdūla dhairyeṇa susamāhitaḥ |
tyajemāṁ durbalāṁ buddhiṁ saṁtāpaṁ mā kuruṣva ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
evam uktas tu kākutstho lakṣmaṇena mahātmanā |
uvāca parayā prītyā saumitriṁ mitravatsalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
evam etan naraśreṣṭha yathā vadasi lakṣmaṇa |
paritoṣaś ca me vīra mama kāryānuśāsane ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
nirvr̥tiś ca kr̥tā saumya saṁtāpaś ca nirākr̥taḥ |
bhavadvākyaiḥ sumadhurair anunīto ’smi lakṣmaṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
