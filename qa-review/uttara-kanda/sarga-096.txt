=== Sarga 96 ===

---
1.
avāṅmukham atho dīnaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā somam ivāplutam |
rāghavaṁ lakṣmaṇo vākyaṁ hr̥ṣṭo madhuram abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
na saṁtāpaṁ mahābāho madarthaṁ kartum arhasi |
pūrvanirmāṇabaddhā hi kālasya gatir īdr̥śī ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
jahi māṁ saumya visrabdhaḥ pratijñāṁ paripālaya |
hīnapratijñāḥ kākutstha prayānti narakaṁ narāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
yadi prītir mahārāja yady anugrāhyatā mayi |
jahi māṁ nirviśaṅkas tvaṁ dharmaṁ vardhaya rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
lakṣmaṇena tathoktas tu rāmaḥ pracalitendriyaḥ |
mantriṇaḥ samupānīya tathaiva ca purodhasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
abravīc ca yathāvr̥ttaṁ teṣāṁ madhye narādhipaḥ |
durvāso’bhigamaṁ caiva pratijñāṁ tāpasasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tac chrutvā mantriṇaḥ sarve sopādhyāyāḥ samāsata |
vasiṣṭhas tu mahātejā vākyam etad uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
dr̥ṣṭam etan mahābāho kṣayaṁ te lomaharṣaṇam |
lakṣmaṇena viyogaś ca tava rāma mahāyaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tyajainaṁ balavān kālo mā pratijñāṁ vr̥thā kr̥thāḥ |
vinaṣṭāyāṁ pratijñāyāṁ dharmo hi vilayaṁ vrajet ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tato dharme vinaṣṭe tu trailokye sacarācaram |
sadevarṣigaṇaṁ sarvaṁ vinaśyeta na saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sa tvaṁ puruṣaśārdūla trailokyasyābhipālanam |
lakṣmaṇasya vadhenādya jagat svasthaṁ kuruṣva ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
teṣāṁ tat samavetānāṁ vākyaṁ dharmārthasaṁhitam |
śrutvā pariṣado madhye rāmo lakṣmaṇam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
visarjaye tvāṁ saumitre mā bhūd dharmaviparyayaḥ |
tyāgo vadho vā vihitaḥ sādhūnām ubhayaṁ samam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
rāmeṇa bhāṣite vākye bāṣpavyākulitekṣaṇaḥ |
lakṣmaṇas tvaritaḥ prāyāt svagr̥haṁ na viveśa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa gatvā sarayūtīram upaspr̥śya kr̥tāñjaliḥ |
nigr̥hya sarvasrotāṁsi niḥśvāsaṁ na mumoca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
anucchvasantaṁ yuktaṁ taṁ saśakrāḥ sāpsarogaṇāḥ |
devāḥ sarṣigaṇāḥ sarve puṣpair avakiraṁs tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
adr̥śyaṁ sarvam anujaiḥ saśarīraṁ mahābalam |
pragr̥hya lakṣmaṇaṁ śakro divaṁ saṁpraviveśa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tato viṣṇoś caturbhāgam āgataṁ surasattamāḥ |
hr̥ṣṭāḥ pramuditāḥ sarve ’pūjayan r̥ṣibhiḥ saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
