=== Sarga 2 ===

---
1.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā rāghavasya mahātmanaḥ |
kumbhayonir mahātejā vākyam etad uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
śr̥ṇu rājan yathāvr̥ttaṁ yasya tejobalaṁ mahat |
jaghāna ca ripūn yuddhe yathāvadhyaś ca śatrubhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
ahaṁ te rāvaṇasyedaṁ kulaṁ janma ca rāghava |
varapradānaṁ ca tathā tasmai dattaṁ bravīmi te ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
purā kr̥tayuge rāma prajāpatisutaḥ prabhuḥ |
pulastyo nāma brahmarṣiḥ sākṣād iva pitāmahaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
nānukīrtyā guṇās tasya dharmataḥ śīlatas tathā |
prajāpateḥ putra iti vaktuṁ śakyaṁ hi nāmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
sa tu dharmaprasaṅgena meroḥ pārśve mahāgireḥ |
tr̥ṇabindvāśramaṁ gatvā nyavasan munipuṁgavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tapas tepe sa dharmātmā svādhyāyaniyatendriyaḥ |
gatvāśramapadaṁ tasya vighnaṁ kurvanti kanyakāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
devapannagakanyāś ca rājarṣitanayāś ca yāḥ |
krīḍantyo ’psarasaś caiva taṁ deśam upapedire ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sarvartuṣūpabhogyatvād ramyatvāt kānanasya ca |
nityaśas tās tu taṁ deśaṁ gatvā krīḍanti kanyakāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
atha ruṣṭo mahātejā vyājahāra mahāmuniḥ |
yā me darśanam āgacchet sā garbhaṁ dhārayiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tās tu sarvāḥ pratigatāḥ śrutvā vākyaṁ mahātmanaḥ |
brahmaśāpabhayād bhītās taṁ deśaṁ nopacakramuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tr̥ṇabindos tu rājarṣes tanayā na śr̥ṇoti tat |
gatvāśramapadaṁ tasya vicacāra sunirbhayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tasminn eva tu kāle sa prājāpatyo mahān r̥ṣiḥ |
svādhyāyam akarot tatra tapasā dyotitaprabhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sā tu vedadhvaniṁ śrutvā dr̥ṣṭvā caiva tapodhanam |
abhavat pāṇḍudehā sā suvyañjitaśarīrajā ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
dr̥ṣṭvā paramasaṁvignā sā tu tadrūpam ātmanaḥ |
idaṁ me kiṁ nv iti jñātvā pitur gatvāgrataḥ sthitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tāṁ tu dr̥ṣṭvā tathā bhūtāṁ tr̥ṇabindur athābravīt |
kiṁ tvam etat tv asadr̥śaṁ dhārayasy ātmano vapuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
sā tu kr̥tvāñjaliṁ dīnā kanyovāca tapodhanam |
na jāne kāraṇaṁ tāta yena me rūpam īdr̥śam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
kiṁ tu pūrvaṁ gatāsmy ekā maharṣer bhāvitātmanaḥ |
pulastyasyāśramaṁ divyam anveṣṭuṁ svasakhījanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
na ca paśyāmy ahaṁ tatra kāṁ cid apy āgatāṁ sakhīm |
rūpasya tu viparyāsaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā cāham ihāgatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tr̥ṇabindus tu rājarṣis tapasā dyotitaprabhaḥ |
dhyānaṁ viveśa tac cāpi apaśyad r̥ṣikarmajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
sa tu vijñāya taṁ śāpaṁ maharṣer bhāvitātmanaḥ |
gr̥hītvā tanayāṁ gatvā pulastyam idam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
bhagavaṁs tanayāṁ me tvaṁ guṇaiḥ svair eva bhūṣitām |
bhikṣāṁ pratigr̥hāṇemāṁ maharṣe svayam udyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tapaścaraṇayuktasya śrāmyamāṇendriyasya te |
śuśrūṣātatparā nityaṁ bhaviṣyati na saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
taṁ bruvāṇaṁ tu tadvākyaṁ rājarṣiṁ dhārmikaṁ tadā |
jighr̥kṣur abravīt kanyāṁ bāḍham ity eva sa dvijaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
dattvā tu sa gato rājā svam āśramapadaṁ tadā |
sāpi tatrāvasat kanyā toṣayantī patiṁ guṇaiḥ |
prītaḥ sa tu mahātejā vākyam etad uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
parituṣṭo ’smi bhadraṁ te guṇānāṁ saṁpadā bhr̥śam |
tasmāt te viramāmy adya putram ātmasamaṁ guṇaiḥ |
ubhayor vaṁśakartāraṁ paulastya iti viśrutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
yasmāt tu viśruto vedas tvayehābhyasyato mama |
tasmāt sa viśravā nāma bhaviṣyati na saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
evam uktā tu sā kanyā prahr̥ṣṭenāntarātmanā |
acireṇaiva kālena sūtā viśravasaṁ sutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
sa tu lokatraye khyātaḥ śaucadharmasamanvitaḥ |
piteva tapasā yukto viśravā munipuṁgavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
