=== Sarga 60 ===

---
1.
kathāṁ kathayatāṁ teṣāṁ jayaṁ cākāṅkṣatāṁ śubham |
vyatītā rajanī śīghraṁ śatrughnasya mahātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tataḥ prabhāte vimale tasmin kāle sa rākṣasaḥ |
nirgatas tu purād vīro bhakṣāhārapracoditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
etasminn antare śūraḥ śatrughno yamunāṁ nadīm |
tīrtvā madhupuradvāri dhanuṣpāṇir atiṣṭhata ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tato ’rdhadivase prāpte krūrakarmā sa rākṣasaḥ |
āgacchad bahusāhasraṁ prāṇinām udvahan bharam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tato dadarśa śatrughnaṁ sthitaṁ dvāri dhr̥tāyudham |
tam uvāca tato rakṣaḥ kim anena kariṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
īdr̥śānāṁ sahasrāṇi sāyudhānāṁ narādhama |
bhakṣitāni mayā roṣāt kālam ākāṅkṣase nu kim ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
āhāraś cāpy asaṁpūrṇo mamāyaṁ puruṣādhama |
svayaṁ praviṣṭo nu mukhaṁ katham āsādya durmate ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tasyaivaṁ bhāṣamāṇasya hasataś ca muhur muhuḥ |
śatrughno vīryasaṁpanno roṣād aśrūṇy avartayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tasya roṣābhibhūtasya śatrughnasya mahātmanaḥ |
tejomayā marīcyas tu sarvagātrair viniṣpatan ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
uvāca ca susaṁkruddhaḥ śatrughnas taṁ niśācaram |
yoddhum icchāmi durbuddhe dvandvayuddhaṁ tvayā saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
putro daśarathasyāhaṁ bhrātā rāmasya dhīmataḥ |
śatrughno nāma śatrughno vadhākāṅkṣī tavāgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tasya me yuddhakāmasya dvandvayuddhaṁ pradīyatām |
śatrus tvaṁ sarvajīvānāṁ na me jīvan gamiṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tasmiṁs tathā bruvāṇe tu rākṣasaḥ prahasann iva |
pratyuvāca naraśreṣṭhaṁ diṣṭyā prāpto ’si durmate ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
mama mātr̥ṣvasur bhrātā rāvaṇo nāma rākṣasaḥ |
hato rāmeṇa durbuddhe strīhetoḥ puruṣādhama ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tac ca sarvaṁ mayā kṣāntaṁ rāvaṇasya kulakṣayam |
avajñāṁ purataḥ kr̥tvā mayā yūyaṁ viśeṣataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
na hatāś ca hi me sarve paribhūtās tr̥ṇaṁ yathā |
bhūtāś caiva bhaviṣyāś ca yūyaṁ ca puruṣādhamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tasya te yuddhakāmasya yuddhaṁ dāsyāmi durmate |
īpsitaṁ yādr̥śaṁ tubhyaṁ sajjaye yāvad āyudham ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tam uvācātha śatrughnaḥ kva me jīvan gamiṣyasi |
durbalo ’py āgataḥ śatrur na moktavyaḥ kr̥tātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
yo hi viklavayā buddhyā prasaraṁ śatrave dadau |
sa hato mandabuddhitvād yathā kāpuruṣas tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
