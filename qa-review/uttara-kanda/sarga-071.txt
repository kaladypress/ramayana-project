=== Sarga 71 ===

---
1.
etad ākhyāya rāmāya maharṣiḥ kumbhasaṁbhavaḥ |
asyām evāparaṁ vākyaṁ kathāyām upacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tataḥ sa daṇḍaḥ kākutstha bahuvarṣagaṇāyutam |
akarot tatra mandātmā rājyaṁ nihatakaṇṭakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
atha kāle tu kasmiṁś cid rājā bhārgavam āśramam |
ramaṇīyam upākrāmac caitre māsi manorame ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tatra bhārgavakanyāṁ sa rūpeṇāpratimāṁ bhuvi |
vicarantīṁ vanoddeśe daṇḍo ’paśyad anuttamām ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
sa dr̥ṣṭvā tāṁ sudurmedhā anaṅgaśarapīḍitaḥ |
abhigamya susaṁvignaḥ kanyāṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
kutas tvam asi suśroṇi kasya vāsi sutā śubhe |
pīḍito ’ham anaṅgena pr̥cchāmi tvāṁ sumadhyame ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tasya tv evaṁ bruvāṇasya mohonmattasya kāminaḥ |
bhārgavī pratyuvācedaṁ vacaḥ sānunayaṁ nr̥pam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
bhārgavasya sutāṁ viddhi devasyākliṣṭakarmaṇaḥ |
arajāṁ nāma rājendra jyeṣṭhām āśramavāsinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
guruḥ pitā me rājendra tvaṁ ca śiṣyo mahātmanaḥ |
vyasanaṁ sumahat kruddhaḥ sa te dadyān mahātapāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
yadi vātra mayā kāryaṁ dharmadr̥ṣṭena satpathā |
varayasva nr̥pa śreṣṭha pitaraṁ me mahādyutim ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
anyathā tu phalaṁ tubhyaṁ bhaved ghorābhisaṁhitam |
krodhena hi pitā me ’sau trailokyam api nirdahet ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
evaṁ bruvāṇām arajāṁ daṇḍaḥ kāmaśarārditaḥ |
pratyuvāca madonmattaḥ śirasy ādhāya so ’ñjalim ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
prasādaṁ kuru suśroṇi na kālaṁ kṣeptum arhasi |
tvatkr̥te hi mama prāṇā vidīryante śubhānane ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tvāṁ prāpya hi vadho vāpi pāpaṁ vāpi sudāruṇam |
bhaktaṁ bhajasva māṁ bhīru bhajamānaṁ suvihvalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
evam uktvā tu tāṁ kanyāṁ dorbhyāṁ gr̥hya balād balī |
visphurantīṁ yathākāmaṁ maithunāyopacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tam anarthaṁ mahāghoraṁ daṇḍaḥ kr̥tvā sudāruṇam |
nagaraṁ prayayau cāśu madhumantam anuttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
arajāpi rudantī sā āśramasyāvidūrataḥ |
pratīkṣate susaṁtrastā pitaraṁ devasaṁnibham ||

=== meaning ===

---
