=== Sarga 28 ===

---
1.
sumālinaṁ hataṁ dr̥ṣṭvā vasunā bhasmasātkr̥tam |
vidrutaṁ cāpi svaṁ sainyaṁ lakṣayitvārditaṁ śaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tataḥ sa balavān kruddho rāvaṇasya suto yudhi |
nivartya rākṣasān sarvān meghanādo vyatiṣṭhata ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sa rathenāgnivarṇena kāmagena mahārathaḥ |
abhidudrāva senāṁ tāṁ vanāny agnir iva jvalan ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tataḥ praviśatas tasya vividhāyudhadhāriṇaḥ |
vidudruvur diśaḥ sarvā devās tasya ca darśanāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
na tatrāvasthitaḥ kaś cid raṇe tasya yuyutsataḥ |
sarvān āvidhya vitrastān dr̥ṣṭvā śakro ’bhyabhāṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
na bhetavyaṁ na gantavyaṁ nivartadhvaṁ raṇaṁ prati |
eṣa gacchati me putro yuddhārtham aparājitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tataḥ śakrasuto devo jayanta iti viśrutaḥ |
rathenādbhutakalpena saṁgrāmam abhivartata ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tatas te tridaśāḥ sarve parivārya śacīsutam |
rāvaṇasya sutaṁ yuddhe samāsādya vyavasthitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
teṣāṁ yuddhaṁ mahad abhūt sadr̥śaṁ devarakṣasām |
kr̥te mahendraputrasya rākṣasendrasutasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tato mātaliputre tu gomukhe rākṣasātmajaḥ |
sārathau pātayām āsa śarān kāñcanabhūṣaṇān ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
śacīsutas tv api tathā jayantas tasya sārathim |
taṁ caiva rāvaṇiṁ kruddhaḥ pratyavidhyad raṇājire ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tataḥ kruddho mahātejā rakṣo visphāritekṣaṇaḥ |
rāvaṇiḥ śakraputraṁ taṁ śaravarṣair avākirat ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tataḥ pragr̥hya śastrāṇi sāravanti mahānti ca |
śataghnīs tomarān prāsān gadākhaḍgaparaśvadhān |
sumahānty adriśr̥ṅgāṇi pātayām āsa rāvaṇiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tataḥ pravyathitā lokāḥ saṁjajñe ca tamo mahat |
tasya rāvaṇaputrasya tadā śatrūn abhighnataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tatas tad daivatabalaṁ samantāt taṁ śacīsutam |
bahuprakāram asvasthaṁ tatra tatra sma dhāvati ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
nābhyajānaṁs tadānyonyaṁ śatrūn vā daivatāni vā |
tatra tatra viparyastaṁ samantāt paridhāvitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
etasminn antare śūraḥ pulomā nāma vīryavān |
daiteyas tena saṁgr̥hya śacīputro ’pavāhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
gr̥hītvā taṁ tu naptāraṁ praviṣṭaḥ sa mahodadhim |
mātāmaho ’ryakas tasya paulomī yena sā śacī ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
praṇāśaṁ dr̥śya tu surā jayantasyātidāruṇam |
vyathitāś cāprahr̥ṣṭāś ca samantād vipradudruvuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
rāvaṇis tv atha saṁhr̥ṣṭo balaiḥ parivr̥taḥ svakaiḥ |
abhyadhāvata devāṁs tān mumoca ca mahāsvanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
dr̥ṣṭvā praṇāśaṁ putrasya rāvaṇeś cāpi vikramam |
mātaliṁ prāha devendro rathaḥ samupanīyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
sa tu divyo mahābhīmaḥ sajja eva mahārathaḥ |
upasthito mātalinā vāhyamāno manojavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tato meghā rathe tasmiṁs taḍidvanto mahāsvanāḥ |
agrato vāyucapalā gacchanto vyanadaṁs tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
nānāvādyāni vādyanta stutayaś ca samāhitāḥ |
nanr̥tuś cāpsaraḥsaṁghāḥ prayāte vāsave raṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
rudrair vasubhir ādityaiḥ sādhyaiś ca samarudgaṇaiḥ |
vr̥to nānāpraharaṇair niryayau tridaśādhipaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
nirgacchatas tu śakrasya paruṣaṁ pavano vavau |
bhāskaro niṣprabhaś cāsīn maholkāś ca prapedire ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
etasminn antare śūro daśagrīvaḥ pratāpavān |
āruroha rathaṁ divyaṁ nirmitaṁ viśvakarmaṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
pannagaiḥ sumahākāyair veṣṭitaṁ lomaharṣaṇaiḥ |
yeṣāṁ niśvāsavātena pradīptam iva saṁyugam ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
daityair niśācaraiḥ śūrai rathaḥ saṁparivāritaḥ |
samarābhimukho divyo mahendram abhivartata ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
putraṁ taṁ vārayitvāsau svayam eva vyavasthitaḥ |
so ’pi yuddhād viniṣkramya rāvaṇiḥ samupāviśat ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
tato yuddhaṁ pravr̥ttaṁ tu surāṇāṁ rākṣasaiḥ saha |
śastrābhivarṣaṇaṁ ghoraṁ meghānām iva saṁyuge ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
kumbhakarṇas tu duṣṭātmā nānāpraharaṇodyataḥ |
nājñāyata tadā yuddhe saha kenāpy ayudhyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
dantair bhujābhyāṁ padbhyāṁ ca śaktitomarasāyakaiḥ |
yena kenaiva saṁrabdhas tāḍayām āsa vai surān ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
tato rudrair mahābhāgaiḥ sahādityair niśācaraḥ |
prayuddhas taiś ca saṁgrāme kr̥ttaḥ śastrair nirantaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
tatas tad rākṣasaṁ sainyaṁ tridaśaiḥ samarudgaṇaiḥ |
raṇe vidrāvitaṁ sarvaṁ nānāpraharaṇaiḥ śitaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
ke cid vinihatāḥ śastrair veṣṭanti sma mahītale |
vāhaneṣv avasaktāś ca sthitā evāpare raṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
rathān nāgān kharān uṣṭrān pannagāṁs turagāṁs tathā |
śiṁśumārān varāhāṁś ca piśācavadanāṁs tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
tān samāliṅgya bāhubhyāṁ viṣṭabdhāḥ ke cid ucchritāḥ |
devais tu śastrasaṁviddhā mamrire ca niśācarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
citrakarma ivābhāti sa teṣāṁ raṇasaṁplavaḥ |
nihatānāṁ pramattānāṁ rākṣasānāṁ mahītale ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
śoṇitodaka niṣyandākaṅkagr̥dhrasamākulā |
pravr̥ttā saṁyugamukhe śastragrāhavatī nadī ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
etasminn antare kruddho daśagrīvaḥ pratāpavān |
nirīkṣya tad balaṁ sarvaṁ daivatair vinipātitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
sa taṁ prativigāhyāśu pravr̥ddhaṁ sainyasāgaram |
tridaśān samare nighnañ śakram evābhyavartata ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
tataḥ śakro mahac cāpaṁ visphārya sumahāsvanam |
yasya visphāraghoṣeṇa svananti sma diśo daśa ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
tad vikr̥ṣya mahac cāpam indro rāvaṇamūrdhani |
nipātayām āsa śarān pāvakādityavarcasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
tathaiva ca mahābāhur daśagrīvo vyavasthitaḥ |
śakraṁ kārmukavibhraṣṭaiḥ śaravarṣair avākirat ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
prayudhyator atha tayor bāṇavarṣaiḥ samantataḥ |
nājñāyata tadā kiṁ cit sarvaṁ hi tamasā vr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
