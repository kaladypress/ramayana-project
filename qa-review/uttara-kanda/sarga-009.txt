=== Sarga 9 ===

---
1.
kasya cit tv atha kālasya sumālī nāma rākṣasaḥ |
rasātalān martyalokaṁ sarvaṁ vai vicacāra ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
nīlajīmūtasaṁkāśas taptakāñcanakuṇḍalaḥ |
kanyāṁ duhitaraṁ gr̥hya vinā padmam iva śriyam |
athāpaśyat sa gacchantaṁ puṣpakeṇa dhaneśvaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvāmarasaṁkāśaṁ gacchantaṁ pāvakopamam |
athābravīt sutāṁ rakṣaḥ kaikasīṁ nāma nāmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
putri pradānakālo ’yaṁ yauvanaṁ te ’tivartate |
tvatkr̥te ca vayaṁ sarve yantritā dharmabuddhayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tvaṁ hi sarvaguṇopetā śrīḥ sapadmeva putrike |
pratyākhyānāc ca bhītais tvaṁ na varaiḥ pratigr̥hyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
kanyāpitr̥tvaṁ duḥkhaṁ hi sarveṣāṁ mānakāṅkṣiṇām |
na jñāyate ca kaḥ kanyāṁ varayed iti putrike ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
mātuḥ kulaṁ pitr̥kulaṁ yatra caiva pradīyate |
kulatrayaṁ sadā kanyā saṁśaye sthāpya tiṣṭhati ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sā tvaṁ munivaraśreṣṭhaṁ prajāpatikulodbhavam |
gaccha viśravasaṁ putri paulastyaṁ varaya svayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
īdr̥śās te bhaviṣyanti putrāḥ putri na saṁśayaḥ |
tejasā bhāskarasamā yādr̥śo ’yaṁ dhaneśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
etasminn antare rāma pulastyatanayo dvijaḥ |
agnihotram upātiṣṭhac caturtha iva pāvakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sā tu tāṁ dāruṇāṁ velām acintya pitr̥gauravāt |
upasr̥tyāgratas tasya caraṇādhomukhī sthitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sa tu tāṁ vīkṣya suśroṇīṁ pūrṇacandranibhānanām |
abravīt paramodāro dīpyamāna ivaujasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
bhadre kasyāsi duhitā kuto vā tvam ihāgatā |
kiṁ kāryaṁ kasya vā hetos tattvato brūhi śobhane ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
evam uktā tu sā kanyā kr̥tāñjalir athābravīt |
ātmaprabhāvena mune jñātum arhasi me matam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
kiṁ tu viddhi hi māṁ brahmañ śāsanāt pitur āgatām |
kaikasī nāma nāmnāhaṁ śeṣaṁ tvaṁ jñātum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sa tu gatvā munir dhyānaṁ vākyam etad uvāca ha |
vijñātaṁ te mayā bhadre kāraṇaṁ yan manogatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
dāruṇāyāṁ tu velāyāṁ yasmāt tvaṁ mām upasthitā |
śr̥ṇu tasmāt sutān bhadre yādr̥śāñ janayiṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
dāruṇān dāruṇākārān dāruṇābhijanapriyān |
prasaviṣyasi suśroṇi rākṣasān krūrakarmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
sā tu tad vacanaṁ śrutvā praṇipatyābravīd vacaḥ |
bhagavan nedr̥śāḥ putrās tvatto ’rhā brahmayonitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
athābravīn munis tatra paścimo yas tavātmajaḥ |
mama vaṁśānurūpaś ca dharmātmā ca bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
evam uktā tu sā kanyā rāma kālena kena cit |
janayām āsa bībhatsaṁ rakṣorūpaṁ sudāruṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
daśaśīrṣaṁ mahādaṁṣṭraṁ nīlāñjanacayopamam |
tāmrauṣṭhaṁ viṁśatibhujaṁ mahāsyaṁ dīptamūrdhajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
jātamātre tatas tasmin sajvālakavalāḥ śivāḥ |
kravyādāś cāpasavyāni maṇḍalāni pracakrire ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
vavarṣa rudhiraṁ devo meghāś ca kharanisvanāḥ |
prababhau na ca khe sūryo maholkāś cāpatan bhuvi ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
atha nāmākarot tasya pitāmahasamaḥ pitā |
daśaśīrṣaḥ prasūto ’yaṁ daśagrīvo bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tasya tv anantaraṁ jātaḥ kumbhakarṇo mahābalaḥ |
pramāṇād yasya vipulaṁ pramāṇaṁ neha vidyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tataḥ śūrpaṇakhā nāma saṁjajñe vikr̥tānanā |
vibhīṣaṇaś ca dharmātmā kaikasyāḥ paścimaḥ sutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
te tu tatra mahāraṇye vavr̥dhuḥ sumahaujasaḥ |
teṣāṁ krūro daśagrīvo lokodvegakaro ’bhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
kumbhakarṇaḥ pramattas tu maharṣīn dharmasaṁśritān |
trailokyaṁ trāsayan duṣṭo bhakṣayan vicacāra ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
vibhīṣaṇas tu dharmātmā nityaṁ dharmapathe sthitaḥ |
svādhyāyaniyatāhāra uvāsa niyatendriyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
atha vitteśvaro devas tatra kālena kena cit |
āgacchat pitaraṁ draṣṭuṁ puṣpakeṇa mahaujasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā kaikasī tatra jvalantam iva tejasā |
āsthāya rākṣasīṁ buddhiṁ daśagrīvam uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
putra vaiśravaṇaṁ paśya bhrātaraṁ tejasā vr̥tam |
bhrātr̥bhāve same cāpi paśyātmānaṁ tvam īdr̥śam ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
daśagrīva tathā yatnaṁ kuruṣvāmitavikrama |
yathā bhavasi me putra śīghraṁ vaiśvaraṇopamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
mātus tad vacanaṁ śrutvā daśagrīvaḥ pratāpavān |
amarṣam atulaṁ lebhe pratijñāṁ cākarot tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
satyaṁ te pratijānāmi tulyo bhrātrādhiko ’pi vā |
bhaviṣyāmy acirān mātaḥ saṁtāpaṁ tyaja hr̥dgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
tataḥ krodhena tenaiva daśagrīvaḥ sahānujaḥ |
prāpsyāmi tapasā kāmam iti kr̥tvādhyavasya ca |
āgacchad ātmasiddhyarthaṁ gokarṇasyāśramaṁ śubham ||

=== meaning ===

---
