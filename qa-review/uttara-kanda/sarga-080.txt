=== Sarga 80 ===

---
1.
śrutvā kiṁpuruṣotpattiṁ lakṣmaṇo bharatas tadā |
āścaryam iti cābrūtām ubhau rāmaṁ janeśvaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
atha rāmaḥ kathām etāṁ bhūya eva mahāyaśāḥ |
kathayām āsa dharmātmā prajāpatisutasya vai ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sarvās tā vidrutā dr̥ṣṭvā kiṁnarīr r̥ṣisattamaḥ |
uvāca rūpasaṁpannāṁ tāṁ striyaṁ prahasann iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
somasyāhaṁ sudayitaḥ sutaḥ surucirānane |
bhajasva māṁ varārohe bhaktyā snigdhena cakṣuṣā ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā śūnye svajanavarjitā |
ilā suruciraprakhyaṁ pratyuvāca mahāgraham ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
ahaṁ kāmakarī saumya tavāsmi vaśavartinī |
praśādhi māṁ somasuta yathecchasi tathā kuru ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tasyās tad adbhutaprakhyaṁ śrutvā harṣasamanvitaḥ |
sa vai kāmī saha tayā reme candramasaḥ sutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
budhasya mādhavo māsas tām ilāṁ rucirānanām |
gato ramayato ’tyarthaṁ kṣaṇavat tasya kāminaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
atha māse tu saṁpūrṇe pūrṇendusadr̥śānanaḥ |
prajāpatisutaḥ śrīmāñ śayane pratyabudhyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
so ’paśyat somajaṁ tatra tapyantaṁ salilāśaye |
ūrdhvabāhuṁ nirālambaṁ taṁ rājā pratyabhāṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
bhagavan parvataṁ durgaṁ praviṣṭo ’smi sahānugaḥ |
na ca paśyāmi tat sainyaṁ kva nu te māmakā gatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tac chrutvā tasya rājarṣer naṣṭasaṁjñasya bhāṣitam |
pratyuvāca śubhaṁ vākyaṁ sāntvayan parayā girā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
aśmavarṣeṇa mahatā bhr̥tyās te vinipātitāḥ |
tvaṁ cāśramapade supto vātavarṣabhayārditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
samāśvasihi bhadraṁ te nirbhayo vigatajvaraḥ |
phalamūlāśano vīra vasa ceha yathāsukham ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa rājā tena vākyena pratyāśvasto mahāyaśāḥ |
pratyuvāca śubhaṁ vākyaṁ dīno bhr̥tyajanakṣayāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tyakṣyāmy ahaṁ svakaṁ rājyaṁ nāhaṁ bhr̥tyair vinā kr̥taḥ |
vartayeyaṁ kṣaṇaṁ brahman samanujñātum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
suto dharmaparo brahmañ jyeṣṭho mama mahāyaśāḥ |
śaśabindur iti khyātaḥ sa me rājyaṁ prapatsyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
na hi śakṣyāmy ahaṁ gatvā bhr̥tyadārān sukhānvitān |
prativaktuṁ mahātejaḥ kiṁ cid apy aśubhaṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tathā bruvati rājendre budhaḥ paramam adbhutam |
sāntvapūrvam athovāca vāsas ta iha rocatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
na saṁtāpas tvayā kāryaḥ kārdameya mahābala |
saṁvatsaroṣitasyeha kārayiṣyāmi te hitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā budhasyākliṣṭakarmaṇaḥ |
vāsāya vidadhe buddhiṁ yad uktaṁ brahmavādinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
māsaṁ sa strī tadā bhūtvā ramayaty aniśaṁ śubhā |
māsaṁ puruṣabhāvena dharmabuddhiṁ cakāra saḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tataḥ sa navame māsi ilā somasutātmajam |
janayām āsa suśroṇī purūravasam ātmajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
jātamātraṁ tu suśroṇī pitur haste nyaveśayat |
budhasya samavarṇābham ilāputraṁ mahābalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
budho ’pi puruṣībhūtaṁ samāśvāsya narādhipam |
kathābhī ramayām āsa dharmayuktābhir ātmavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
