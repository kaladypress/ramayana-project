=== Sarga 14 ===

---
1.
tataḥ sa sacivaiḥ sārdhaṁ ṣaḍbhir nityaṁ balotkaṭaiḥ |
mahodaraprahastābhyāṁ mārīcaśukasāraṇaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
dhūmrākṣeṇa ca vīreṇa nityaṁ samaragr̥dhnunā |
vr̥taḥ saṁprayayau śrīmān krodhāl lokān dahann iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
purāṇi sa nadīḥ śailān vanāny upavanāni ca |
atikramya muhūrtena kailāsaṁ girim āviśat ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
taṁ niviṣṭaṁ girau tasmin rākṣasendraṁ niśamya tu |
rājño bhrātāyam ity uktvā gatā yatra dhaneśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
gatvā tu sarvam ācakhyur bhrātus tasya viniścayam |
anujñātā yayuś caiva yuddhāya dhanadena te ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tato balasya saṁkṣobhaḥ sāgarasyeva vardhataḥ |
abhūn nairr̥tarājasya giriṁ saṁcālayann iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tato yuddhaṁ samabhavad yakṣarākṣasasaṁkulam |
vyathitāś cābhavaṁs tatra sacivās tasya rakṣasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā tādr̥śaṁ sainyaṁ daśagrīvo niśācaraḥ |
harṣān nādaṁ tataḥ kr̥tvā roṣāt samabhivartata ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
ye tu te rākṣasendrasya sacivā ghoravikramaḥ |
te sahasraṁ sahasrāṇām ekaikaṁ samayodhayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tato gadābhiḥ parighair asibhiḥ śaktitomaraiḥ |
vadhyamāno daśagrīvas tat sainyaṁ samagāhata ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tair nirucchvāsavat tatra vadhyamāno daśānanaḥ |
varṣamāṇair iva ghanair yakṣendraiḥ saṁnirudhyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sa durātmā samudyamya kāladaṇḍopamāṁ gadām |
praviveśa tataḥ sainyaṁ nayan yakṣān yamakṣayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sa kakṣam iva vistīrṇaṁ śuṣkendhanasamākulam |
vātenāgnir ivāyatto ’dahat sainyaṁ sudāruṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tais tu tasya mr̥dhe ’mātyair mahodaraśukādibhiḥ |
alpāvaśiṣṭās te yakṣāḥ kr̥tā vātair ivāmbudāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
ke cit tv āyudhabhagnāṅgāḥ patitāḥ samarakṣitau |
oṣṭhān svadaśanais tīkṣṇair daṁśanto bhuvi pātitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
bhayād anyonyam āliṅgya bhraṣṭaśastrā raṇājire |
niṣedus te tadā yakṣāḥ kūlā jalahatā iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
hatānāṁ svargasaṁsthānāṁ yudhyatāṁ pr̥thivītale |
prekṣatām r̥ṣisaṁghānāṁ na babhūvāntaraṁ divi ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
etasminn antare rāma vistīrṇabalavāhanaḥ |
agamat sumahān yakṣo nāmnā saṁyodhakaṇṭakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tena yakṣeṇa mārīco viṣṇuneva samāhataḥ |
patitaḥ pr̥thivīṁ bheje kṣīṇapuṇya ivāmbarāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
prāptasaṁjño muhūrtena viśramya ca niśācaraḥ |
taṁ yakṣaṁ yodhayām āsa sa ca bhagnaḥ pradudruve ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tataḥ kāñcanacitrāṅgaṁ vaidūryarajatokṣitam |
maryādāṁ dvārapālānāṁ toraṇaṁ tat samāviśat ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tato rāma daśagrīvaṁ praviśantaṁ niśācaram |
sūryabhānur iti khyāto dvārapālo nyavārayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tatas toraṇam utpāṭya tena yakṣeṇa tāḍitaḥ |
rākṣaso yakṣasr̥ṣṭena toraṇena samāhataḥ |
na kṣitiṁ prayayau rāma varāt salilayoninaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
sa tu tenaiva taṁ yakṣaṁ toraṇena samāhanat |
nādr̥śyata tadā yakṣo bhasma tena kr̥tas tu saḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tataḥ pradudruvuḥ sarve yakṣā dr̥ṣṭvā parākramam |
tato nadīr guhāś caiva viviśur bhayapīḍitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
