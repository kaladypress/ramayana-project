=== Sarga 43 ===

---
1.
visr̥jya tu suhr̥dvargaṁ buddhyā niścitya rāghavaḥ |
samīpe dvāḥstham āsīnam idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
śīghram ānaya saumitriṁ lakṣmaṇaṁ śubhalakṣaṇam |
bharataṁ ca mahābāhuṁ śatrughnaṁ cāparājitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
rāmasya bhāṣitaṁ śrutvā dvāḥstho mūrdhni kr̥tāñjaliḥ |
lakṣmaṇasya gr̥haṁ gatvā praviveśānivāritaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
uvāca ca tadā vākyaṁ vardhayitvā kr̥tāñjaliḥ |
draṣṭum icchati rājā tvāṁ gamyatāṁ tatra mā ciram ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
bāḍham ity eva saumitriḥ śrutvā rāghavaśāsanam |
prādravad ratham āruhya rāghavasya niveśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
prayāntaṁ lakṣmaṇaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā dvāḥstho bharatam antikāt |
uvāca prāñjalir vākyaṁ rājā tvāṁ draṣṭum icchati ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
bharatas tu vacaḥ śrutvā dvāḥsthād rāmasamīritam |
utpapātāsanāt tūrṇaṁ padbhyām eva tato ’gamat ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
dr̥ṣṭvā prayāntaṁ bharataṁ tvaramāṇaḥ kr̥tāñjaliḥ |
śatrughnabhavanaṁ gatvā tato vākyaṁ jagāda ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
ehy āgaccha raghuśreṣṭha rājā tvāṁ draṣṭum icchati |
gato hi lakṣmaṇaḥ pūrvaṁ bharataś ca mahāyaśāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
śrutvā tu vacanaṁ tasya śatrughno rāmaśāsanam |
śirasā vandya dharaṇīṁ prayayau yatra rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
kumārān āgatāñ śrutvā cintāvyākulitendriyaḥ |
avākśirā dīnamanā dvāḥsthaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
praveśaya kumārāṁs tvaṁ matsamīpaṁ tvarānvitaḥ |
eteṣu jīvitaṁ mahyam ete prāṇā bahiścarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
ājñaptās tu narendreṇa kumārāḥ śuklavāsasaḥ |
prahvāḥ prāñjalayo bhūtvā viviśus te samāhitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
te tu dr̥ṣṭvā mukhaṁ tasya sagrahaṁ śaśinaṁ yathā |
saṁdhyāgatam ivādityaṁ prabhayā parivarjitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
bāṣpapūrṇe ca nayane dr̥ṣṭvā rāmasya dhīmataḥ |
hataśobhaṁ yathā padmaṁ mukhaṁ vīkṣya ca tasya te ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tato ’bhivādya tvaritāḥ pādau rāmasya mūrdhabhiḥ |
tasthuḥ samāhitāḥ sarve rāmaś cāśrūṇy avartayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tān pariṣvajya bāhubhyām utthāpya ca mahābhujaḥ |
āsaneṣv ādhvam ity uktvā tato vākyaṁ jagāda ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
bhavanto mama sarvasvaṁ bhavanto mama jīvitam |
bhavadbhiś ca kr̥taṁ rājyaṁ pālayāmi nareśvarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
bhavantaḥ kr̥taśāstrārthā buddhau ca pariniṣṭhitāḥ |
saṁbhūya ca madartho ’yam anveṣṭavyo nareśvarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
