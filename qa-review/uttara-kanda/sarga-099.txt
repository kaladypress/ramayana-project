=== Sarga 99 ===

---
1.
prabhātāyāṁ tu śarvaryāṁ pr̥thuvakṣā mahāyaśāḥ |
rāmaḥ kamalapatrākṣaḥ purodhasam athābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
agnihotraṁ vrajatv agre sarpir jvalitapāvakam |
vājapeyātapatraṁ ca śobhayānaṁ mahāpatham ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tato vasiṣṭhas tejasvī sarvaṁ niravaśeṣataḥ |
cakāra vidhivad dharmyaṁ mahāprāsthānikaṁ vidhim ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tataḥ kṣaumāmbaradharo brahma cāvartayan param |
kuśān gr̥hītvā pāṇibhyāṁ prasajya prayayāv atha ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
avyāharan kva cit kiṁ cin niśceṣṭo niḥsukhaḥ pathi |
nirjagāma gr̥hāt tasmād dīpyamāno yathāṁśumān ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
rāmasya pārśve savye tu padmā śrīḥ susamāhitā |
dakṣiṇe hrīr viśālākṣī vyavasāyas tathāgrataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
śarā nānāvidhāś cāpi dhanur āyatavigraham |
anuvrajanti kākutsthaṁ sarve puruṣavigrahāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
vedā brāhmaṇarūpeṇa sāvitrī sarvarakṣiṇī |
oṁkāro ’tha vaṣaṭkāraḥ sarve rāmam anuvratāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
r̥ṣayaś ca mahātmānaḥ sarva eva mahīsurāḥ |
anvagacchanta kākutsthaṁ svargadvāram upāgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
taṁ yāntam anuyānti sma antaḥpuracarāḥ striyaḥ |
savr̥ddhabāladāsīkāḥ savarṣavarakiṁkarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sāntaḥpuraś ca bharataḥ śatrughnasahito yayau ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
rāmavratam upāgamya rāghavaṁ samanuvratāḥ |
tato viprā mahātmānaḥ sāgnihotrāḥ samāhitāḥ |
saputradārāḥ kākutstham anvagacchan mahāmatim ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
mantriṇo bhr̥tyavargāś ca saputrāḥ sahabāndhavāḥ |
sānugā rāghavaṁ sarve anvagacchan prahr̥ṣṭavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tataḥ sarvāḥ prakr̥tayo hr̥ṣṭapuṣṭajanāvr̥tāḥ |
anujagmuḥ pragacchantaṁ rāghavaṁ guṇarañjitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
snātaṁ pramuditaṁ sarvaṁ hr̥ṣṭapuṣṭam anuttamam |
dr̥ptaṁ kilikilāśabdaiḥ sarvaṁ rāmam anuvratam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
na tatra kaś cid dīno ’bhūd vrīḍito vāpi duḥkhitaḥ |
hr̥ṣṭaṁ pramuditaṁ sarvaṁ babhūva paramādbhutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
draṣṭukāmo ’tha niryāṇaṁ rājño jānapado janaḥ |
saṁprāptaḥ so ’pi dr̥ṣṭvaiva saha sarvair anuvrataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
r̥kṣavānararakṣāṁsi janāś ca puravāsinaḥ |
agachan parayā bhaktyā pr̥ṣṭhataḥ susamāhitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
