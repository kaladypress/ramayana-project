=== Sarga 95 ===

---
1.
tathā tayoḥ kathayator durvāsā bhagavān r̥ṣiḥ |
rāmasya darśanākāṅkṣī rājadvāram upāgamat ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
so ’bhigamya ca saumitrim uvāca r̥ṣisattamaḥ |
rāmaṁ darśaya me śīghraṁ purā me ’rtho ’tivartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
munes tu bhāṣitaṁ śrutvā lakṣmaṇaḥ paravīrahā |
abhivādya mahātmānaṁ vākyam etad uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
kiṁ kāryaṁ brūhi bhagavan ko vārthaḥ kiṁ karomy aham |
vyagro hi rāghavo brahman muhūrtaṁ vā pratīkṣatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tac chrutvā r̥ṣiśārdūlaḥ krodhena kaluṣīkr̥taḥ |
uvāca lakṣmaṇaṁ vākyaṁ nirdahann iva cakṣuṣā ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
asmin kṣaṇe māṁ saumitre rāmāya prativedaya |
viṣayaṁ tvāṁ puraṁ caiva śapiṣye rāghavaṁ tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
bharataṁ caiva saumitre yuṣmākaṁ yā ca saṁtatiḥ |
na hi śakṣyāmy ahaṁ bhūyo manyuṁ dhārayituṁ hr̥di ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tac chrutvā ghorasaṁkāśaṁ vākyaṁ tasya mahātmanaḥ |
cintayām āsa manasā tasya vākyasya niścayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
ekasya maraṇaṁ me ’stu mā bhūt sarvavināśanam |
iti buddhyā viniścitya rāghavāya nyavedayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
lakṣmaṇasya vacaḥ śrutvā rāmaḥ kālaṁ visr̥jya ca |
niṣpatya tvaritaṁ rājā atreḥ putraṁ dadarśa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
so ’bhivādya mahātmānaṁ jvalantam iva tejasā |
kiṁ kāryam iti kākutsthaḥ kr̥tāñjalir abhāṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tad vākyaṁ rāghaveṇoktaṁ śrutvā munivaraḥ prabhuḥ |
pratyāha rāmaṁ durvāsāḥ śrūyatāṁ dharmavatsala ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
adya varṣasahasrasya samāptir mama rāghava |
so ’haṁ bhojanam icchāmi yathāsiddhaṁ tavānagha ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tac chrutvā vacanaṁ rāmo harṣeṇa mahatānvitaḥ |
bhojanaṁ munimukhyāya yathāsiddham upāharat ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa tu bhuktvā muniśreṣṭhas tad annam amr̥topamam |
sādhu rāmeti saṁbhāṣya svam āśramam upāgamat ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tasmin gate mahātejā rāghavaḥ prītamānasaḥ |
saṁsmr̥tya kālavākyāni tato duḥkham upeyivān ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
duḥkhena ca susaṁtaptaḥ smr̥tvā tad ghoradarśanam |
avānmukho dīnamanā vyāhartuṁ na śaśāka ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tato buddhyā viniścitya kālavākyāni rāghavaḥ |
naitad astīti coktvā sa tūṣṇīm āsīn mahāyaśāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
