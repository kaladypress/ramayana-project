=== Sarga 27 ===

---
1.
kailāsaṁ laṅghayitvātha daśagrīvaḥ sarākṣasaḥ |
āsasāda mahātejā indralokaṁ niśācaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tasya rākṣasasainyasya samantād upayāsyataḥ |
devalokaṁ yayau śabdo bhidyamānārṇavopamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
śrutvā tu rāvaṇaṁ prāptam indraḥ saṁcalitāsanaḥ |
abravīt tatra tān devān sarvān eva samāgatān ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
ādityān savasūn rudrān viśvān sādhyān marudgaṇān |
sajjībhavata yuddhārthaṁ rāvaṇasya durātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
evam uktās tu śakreṇa devāḥ śakrasamā yudhi |
saṁnahyanta mahāsattvā yuddhaśraddhāsamanvitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
sa tu dīnaḥ paritrasto mahendro rāvaṇaṁ prati |
viṣṇoḥ samīpam āgatya vākyam etad uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
viṣṇo kathaṁ kariṣyāmo mahāvīryaparākrama |
asau hi balavān rakṣo yuddhārtham abhivartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
varapradānād balavān na khalv anyena hetunā |
tac ca satyaṁ hi kartavyaṁ vākyaṁ deva prajāpateḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tad yathā namucir vr̥tro balir narakaśambarau |
tvan mataṁ samavaṣṭabhya yathā dagdhās tathā kuru ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
na hy anyo deva devānām āpatsu sumahābala |
gatiḥ parāyaṇaṁ vāsti tvām r̥te puruṣottama ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tvaṁ hi nārāyaṇaḥ śrīmān padmanābhaḥ sanātanaḥ |
tvayāhaṁ sthāpitaś caiva devarājye sanātane ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tad ākhyāhi yathātattvaṁ devadeva mama svayam |
asicakrasahāyas tvaṁ yudhyase saṁyuge ripum ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
evam uktaḥ sa śakreṇa devo nārāyaṇaḥ prabhuḥ |
abravīn na paritrāsaḥ kāryas te śrūyatāṁ ca me ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
na tāvad eṣa durvr̥ttaḥ śakyo daivatadānavaiḥ |
hantuṁ yudhi samāsādya varadānena durjayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sarvathā tu mahat karma kariṣyati balotkaṭaḥ |
rakṣaḥ putrasahāyo ’sau dr̥ṣṭam etan nisargataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
bravīṣi yat tu māṁ śakra saṁyuge yotsyasīti ha |
naivāhaṁ pratiyotsye taṁ rāvaṇaṁ rākṣasādhipam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
anihatya ripuṁ viṣṇur na hi pratinivartate |
durlabhaś caiṣa kāmo ’dya varam āsādya rākṣase ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
pratijānāmi devendra tvatsamīpaṁ śatakrato |
rākṣasasyāham evāsya bhavitā mr̥tyukāraṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
aham enaṁ vadhiṣyāmi rāvaṇaṁ sasutaṁ yudhi |
devatās toṣayiṣyāmi jñātvā kālam upasthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
etasminn antare nādaḥ śuśruve rajanīkṣaye |
tasya rāvaṇasainyasya prayuddhasya samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
atha yuddhaṁ samabhavad devarākṣasayos tadā |
ghoraṁ tumulanirhrādaṁ nānāpraharaṇāyudham ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
etasminn antare śūrā rākṣasā ghoradarśanāḥ |
yuddhārtham abhyadhāvanta sacivā rāvaṇājñayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
mārīcaś ca prahastaś ca mahāpārśvamahodarau |
akampano nikumbhaś ca śukaḥ sāraṇa eva ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
saṁhrādir dhūmaketuś ca mahādaṁṣṭro mahāmukhaḥ |
jambumālī mahāmālī virūpākṣaś ca rākṣasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
etaiḥ sarvair mahāvīryair vr̥to rākṣasapuṁgavaḥ |
rāvaṇasyāryakaḥ sainyaṁ sumālī praviveśa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
sa hi devagaṇān sarvān nānāpraharaṇaiḥ śitaiḥ |
vidhvaṁsayati saṁkruddhaḥ saha taiḥ kṣaṇadācaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
etasminn antare śūro vasūnām aṣṭamo vasuḥ |
sāvitra iti vikhyātaḥ praviveśa mahāraṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tato yuddhaṁ samabhavat surāṇāṁ rākṣasaiḥ saha |
kruddhānāṁ rakṣasāṁ kīrtiṁ samareṣv anivartinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
tatas te rākṣasāḥ śūrā devāṁs tān samare sthitān |
nānāpraharaṇair ghorair jaghnuḥ śatasahasraśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
surās tu rākṣasān ghorān mahāvīryān svatejasā |
samare vividhaiḥ śastrair anayan yamasādanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
etasminn antare śūraḥ sumālī nāma rākṣasaḥ |
nānāpraharaṇaiḥ kruddho raṇam evābhyavartata ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
devānāṁ tad balaṁ sarvaṁ nānāpraharaṇaiḥ śitaiḥ |
vidhvaṁsayati saṁkruddho vāyur jaladharān iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
te mahābāṇavarṣaiś ca śūlaiḥ prāsaiś ca dāruṇaiḥ |
pīḍyamānāḥ surāḥ sarve na vyatiṣṭhan samāhitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
tato vidrāvyamāṇeṣu tridaśeṣu sumālinā |
vasūnām aṣṭamo devaḥ sāvitro vyavatiṣṭhata ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
saṁvr̥taḥ svair anīkais tu praharantaṁ niśācaram |
vikrameṇa mahātejā vārayām āsa saṁyuge ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
sumattayos tayor āsīd yuddhaṁ loke sudāruṇam |
sumālino vasoś caiva samareṣv anivartinoḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
tatas tasya mahābāṇair vasunā sumahātmanā |
mahān sa pannagarathaḥ kṣaṇena vinipātitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
hatvā tu saṁyuge tasya rathaṁ bāṇaśataiḥ śitaiḥ |
gadāṁ tasya vadhārthāya vasur jagrāha pāṇinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
tāṁ pradīptāṁ pragr̥hyāśu kāladaṇḍanibhāṁ śubhām |
tasya mūrdhani sāvitraḥ sumāler vinipātayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
tasya mūrdhani solkābhā patantī ca tadā babhau |
sahasrākṣasamutsr̥ṣṭā girāv iva mahāśaniḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
tasya naivāsthi kāyo vā na māṁsaṁ dadr̥śe tadā |
gadayā bhasmasādbhūto raṇe tasmin nipātitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā nihataṁ saṁkhye rākṣasās te samantataḥ |
dudruvuḥ sahitāḥ sarve krośamānā mahāsvanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
