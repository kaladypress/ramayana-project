=== Sarga 91 ===

---
1.
śrutvā senāpatiṁ prāptaṁ bharataṁ kekayādhipaḥ |
yudhājid gārgyasahitaṁ parāṁ prītim upāgamat ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sa niryayau janaughena mahatā kekayādhipaḥ |
tvaramāṇo ’bhicakrāma gandharvān devarūpiṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
bharataś ca yudhājic ca sametau laghuvikramau |
gandharvanagaraṁ prāptau sabalau sapadānugau ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
śrutvā tu bharataṁ prāptaṁ gandharvās te samāgatāḥ |
yoddhukāmā mahāvīryā vinadantaḥ samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tataḥ samabhavad yuddhaṁ tumulaṁ lomaharṣaṇam |
saptarātraṁ mahābhīmaṁ na cānyatarayor jayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tato rāmānujaḥ kruddhaḥ kālasyāstraṁ sudāruṇam |
saṁvartaṁ nāma bharato gandharveṣv abhyayojayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
te baddhāḥ kālapāśena saṁvartena vidāritāḥ |
kṣaṇenābhihatās tisras tatra koṭyo mahātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
taṁ ghātaṁ ghorasaṁkāśaṁ na smaranti divaukasaḥ |
nimeṣāntaramātreṇa tādr̥śānāṁ mahātmanām ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
hateṣu teṣu vīreṣu bharataḥ kaikayīsutaḥ |
niveśayām āsa tadā samr̥ddhe dve purottame |
takṣaṁ takṣaśilāyāṁ tu puṣkaraṁ puṣkarāvatau ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
gandharvadeśo ruciro gāndhāraviṣayaś ca saḥ |
varṣaiḥ pañcabhir ākīrṇo viṣayair nāgarais tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
dhanaratnaughasaṁpūrṇo kānanair upaśobhite |
anyonyasaṁgharṣakr̥te spardhayā guṇavistare ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
ubhe suruciraprakhye vyavahārair akalmaṣaiḥ |
udyānayānaughavr̥te suvibhaktāntarāpaṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
ubhe puravare ramye vistarair upaśobhite |
gr̥hamukhyaiḥ surucirair vimānaiḥ samavarṇibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
śobhite śobhanīyaiś ca devāyatanavistaraiḥ |
niveśya pañcabhir varṣair bharato rāghavānujaḥ |
punar āyān mahābāhur ayodhyāṁ kaikayīsutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
so ’bhivādya mahātmānaṁ sākṣād dharmam ivāparam |
rāghavaṁ bharataḥ śrīmān brahmāṇam iva vāsavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
śaśaṁsa ca yathāvr̥ttaṁ gandharvavadham uttamam |
niveśanaṁ ca deśasya śrutvā prīto ’sya rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
