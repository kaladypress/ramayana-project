=== Sarga 88 ===

---
1.
vālmīkinaivam uktas tu rāghavaḥ pratyabhāṣata |
prāñjalir jagato madhye dr̥ṣṭvā tāṁ devavarṇinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
evam etan mahābhāga yathā vadasi dharmavit |
pratyayo hi mama brahmaṁs tava vākyair akalmaṣaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
pratyayo hi purā datto vaidehyā surasaṁnidhau |
seyaṁ lokabhayād brahmann apāpety abhijānatā |
parityaktā mayā sītā tad bhavān kṣantum arhati ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
jānāmi cemau putrau me yamajātau kuśīlavau |
śuddhāyāṁ jagato madhye maithilyāṁ prītir astu me ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
abhiprāyaṁ tu vijñāya rāmasya surasattamāḥ |
pitāmahaṁ puraskr̥tya sarva eva samāgatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
ādityā vasavo rudrā viśvedevā marudgaṇāḥ |
aśvināv r̥ṣigandharvā apsarāṇāṁ gaṇās tathā |
sādhyāś ca devāḥ sarve te sarve ca paramarṣayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tato vāyuḥ śubhaḥ puṇyo divyagandho manoramaḥ |
taṁ janaughaṁ suraśreṣṭho hlādayām āsa sarvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tad adbhutam ivācintyaṁ nirīkṣante samāhitāḥ |
mānavāḥ sarvarāṣṭrebhyaḥ pūrvaṁ kr̥tayuge yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sarvān samāgatān dr̥ṣṭvā sītā kāṣāyavāsinī |
abravīt prāñjalir vākyam adhodr̥ṣṭir avāṅmukhī ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
yathāhaṁ rāghavād anyaṁ manasāpi na cintaye |
tathā me mādhavī devī vivaraṁ dātum arhati ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tathā śapantyāṁ vaidehyāṁ prādurāsīt tad adbhutam |
bhūtalād utthitaṁ divyaṁ siṁhāsanam anuttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
dhriyamāṇaṁ śirobhis tan nāgair amitavikramaiḥ |
divyaṁ divyena vapuṣā sarvaratnavibhūṣitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tasmiṁs tu dharaṇī devī bāhubhyāṁ gr̥hya maithilīm |
svāgatenābhinandyainām āsane copaveṣayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tām āsanagatāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā praviśantīṁ rasātalam |
puṣpavr̥ṣṭir avicchinnā divyā sītām avākirat ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sādhukāraś ca sumahān devānāṁ sahasotthitaḥ |
sādhu sādhv iti vai sīte yasyās te śīlam īdr̥śam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
evaṁ bahuvidhā vāco hy antarikṣagatāḥ surāḥ |
vyājahrur hr̥ṣṭamanaso dr̥ṣṭvā sītāpraveśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
yajñavāṭagatāś cāpi munayaḥ sarva eva te |
rājānaś ca naravyāghrā vismayān noparemire ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
antarikṣe ca bhūmau ca sarve sthāvarajaṅgamāḥ |
dānavāś ca mahākāyāḥ pātāle pannagādhipāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
ke cid vineduḥ saṁhr̥ṣṭāḥ ke cid dhyānaparāyaṇāḥ |
ke cid rāmaṁ nirīkṣante ke cit sītām acetanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
sītāpraveśanaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā teṣām āsīt samāgamaḥ |
taṁ muhūrtam ivātyarthaṁ sarvaṁ saṁmohitaṁ jagat ||

=== meaning ===

---
