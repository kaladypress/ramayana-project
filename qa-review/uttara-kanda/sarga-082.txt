=== Sarga 82 ===

---
1.
etad ākhyāya kākutstho bhrātr̥bhyām amitaprabhaḥ |
lakṣmaṇaṁ punar evāha dharmayuktam idaṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
vasiṣṭhaṁ vāmadevaṁ ca jābālim atha kaśyapam |
dvijāṁś ca sarvapravarān aśvamedhapuraskr̥tān ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
etān sarvān samāhūya mantrayitvā ca lakṣmaṇa |
hayaṁ lakṣmaṇasaṁpannaṁ vimokṣyāmi samādhinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tad vākyaṁ rāghaveṇoktaṁ śrutvā tvaritavikramaḥ |
dvijān sarvān samāhūya darśayām āsa rāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
te dr̥ṣṭvā devasaṁkāśaṁ kr̥tapādābhivandanam |
rāghavaṁ sudurādharṣam āśīrbhiḥ samapūjayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
prāñjalis tu tato bhūtvā rāghavo dvijasattamān |
uvāca dharmasaṁyuktam aśvamedhāśritaṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sa teṣāṁ dvijamukhyānāṁ vākyam adbhutadarśanam |
aśvamedhāśritaṁ śrutvā bhr̥śaṁ prīto ’bhavat tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
vijñāya tu mataṁ teṣāṁ rāmo lakṣmaṇam abravīt |
preṣayasva mahābāho sugrīvāya mahātmane ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
śīghraṁ mahadbhir haribhir bahubhiś ca tadāśrayaiḥ |
sārdham āgaccha bhadraṁ te anubhoktuṁ makhottamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
vibhīṣaṇaś ca rakṣobhiḥ kāmagair bahubhir vr̥taḥ |
aśvamedhaṁ mahābāhuḥ prāpnotu laghuvikramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
rājānaś ca naravyāghra ye me priyacikīrṣavaḥ |
sānugāḥ kṣipram āyāntu yajñabhūmim anuttamām ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
deśāntaragatā ye ca dvijā dharmaparāyaṇāḥ |
nimantrayasva tān sarvān aśvamedhāya lakṣmaṇa ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
r̥ṣayaś ca mahābāho āhūyantāṁ tapodhanāḥ |
deśāntaragatā ye ca sadārāś ca maharṣayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
yajñavāṭaś ca sumahān gomatyā naimiṣe vane |
ājñāpyatāṁ mahābāho tad dhi puṇyam anuttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
śataṁ vāhasahasrāṇāṁ taṇḍulānāṁ vapuṣmatām |
ayutaṁ tilamudgasya prayātv agre mahābala ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
suvarṇakoṭyo bahulā hiraṇyasya śatottarāḥ |
agrato bharataḥ kr̥tvā gacchatv agre mahāmatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
antarāpaṇavīthyaś ca sarvāṁś ca naṭanartakān |
naigamān bālavr̥ddhāṁś ca dvijāṁś ca susamāhitān ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
karmāntikāṁś ca kuśalāñ śilpinaś ca supaṇḍitān |
mātaraś caiva me sarvāḥ kumārāntaḥpurāṇi ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
kāñcanīṁ mama patnīṁ ca dīkṣārhāṁ yajñakarmaṇi |
agrato bharataḥ kr̥tvā gacchatv agre mahāmatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
