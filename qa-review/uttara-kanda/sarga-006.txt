=== Sarga 6 ===

---
1.
tair vadhyamānā devāś ca r̥ṣayaś ca tapodhanāḥ |
bhayārtāḥ śaraṇaṁ jagmur devadevaṁ maheśvaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
te sametya tu kāmāriṁ tripurāriṁ trilocanam |
ūcuḥ prāñjalayo devā bhayagadgadabhāṣiṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sukeśaputrair bhagavan pitāmahavaroddhataiḥ |
prajādhyakṣa prajāḥ sarvā bādhyante ripubādhana ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
śaraṇyāny aśaraṇyāni āśramāṇi kr̥tāni naḥ |
svargāc ca cyāvitaḥ śakraḥ svarge krīḍanti śakravat ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
ahaṁ viṣṇur ahaṁ rudro brahmāhaṁ devarāḍ aham |
ahaṁ yamo ’haṁ varuṇaś candro ’haṁ ravir apy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
iti te rākṣasā deva varadānena darpitāḥ |
bādhante samaroddharṣā ye ca teṣāṁ puraḥsarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tan no devabhayārtānām abhayaṁ dātum arhasi |
aśivaṁ vapur āsthāya jahi daivatakaṇṭakān ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
ity uktas tu suraiḥ sarvaiḥ kapardī nīlalohitaḥ |
sukeśaṁ prati sāpekṣa āha devagaṇān prabhuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
nāhaṁ tān nihaniṣyāmi avadhyā mama te ’surāḥ |
kiṁ tu mantraṁ pradāsyāmi yo vai tān nihaniṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
evam eva samudyogaṁ puraskr̥tya surarṣabhāḥ |
gacchantu śaraṇaṁ viṣṇuṁ haniṣyati sa tān prabhuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tatas te jayaśabdena pratinandya maheśvaram |
viṣṇoḥ samīpam ājagmur niśācarabhayārditāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
śaṅkhacakradharaṁ devaṁ praṇamya bahumānya ca |
ūcuḥ saṁbhrāntavad vākyaṁ sukeśatanayārditāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sukeśatanayair deva tribhis tretāgnisaṁnibhaiḥ |
ākramya varadānena sthānāny apahr̥tāni naḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
laṅkā nāma purī durgā trikūṭaśikhare sthitā |
tatra sthitāḥ prabādhante sarvān naḥ kṣaṇadācarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa tvam asmatpriyārthaṁ tu jahi tān madhusūdana |
cakrakr̥ttāsyakamalān nivedaya yamāya vai ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
bhayeṣv abhayado ’smākaṁ nānyo ’sti bhavatā samaḥ |
nuda tvaṁ no bhayaṁ deva nīhāram iva bhāskaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
ity evaṁ daivatair ukto devadevo janārdanaḥ |
abhayaṁ bhayado ’rīṇāṁ dattvā devān uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
sukeśaṁ rākṣasaṁ jāne īśānavaradarpitam |
tāṁś cāsya tanayāñ jāne yeṣāṁ jyeṣṭhaḥ sa mālyavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tān ahaṁ samatikrāntamaryādān rākṣasādhamān |
sūdayiṣyāmi saṁgrāme surā bhavata vijvarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
ity uktās te surāḥ sarve viṣṇunā prabhaviṣṇunā |
yathā vāsaṁ yayur hr̥ṣṭāḥ praśamanto janārdanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
vibudhānāṁ samudyogaṁ mālyavān sa niśācaraḥ |
śrutvā tau bhrātarau vīrāv idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
amarā r̥ṣayaś caiva saṁhatya kila śaṁkaram |
asmadvadhaṁ parīpsanta idam ūcus trilocanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
sukeśatanayā deva varadānabaloddhatāḥ |
bādhante ’smān samudyuktā ghorarūpāḥ pade pade ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
rākṣasair abhibhūtāḥ sma na śaktāḥ sma umāpate |
sveṣu veśmasu saṁsthātuṁ bhayāt teṣāṁ durātmanām ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tad asmākaṁ hitārthe tvaṁ jahi tāṁs tāṁs trilocana |
rākṣasān huṁkr̥tenaiva daha pradahatāṁ vara ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
ity evaṁ tridaśair ukto niśamyāndhakasūdanaḥ |
śiraḥ karaṁ ca dhunvāna idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
avadhyā mama te devāḥ sukeśatanayā raṇe |
mantraṁ tu vaḥ pradāsyāmi yo vai tān nihaniṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
yaḥ sa cakragadāpāṇiḥ pītavāsā janārdanaḥ |
haniṣyati sa tān yuddhe śaraṇaṁ taṁ prapadyatha ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
harān nāvāpya te kāmaṁ kāmārim abhivādya ca |
nārāyaṇālayaṁ prāptās tasmai sarvaṁ nyavedayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
tato nārāyaṇenoktā devā indrapurogamāḥ |
surārīn sūdayiṣyāmi surā bhavata vijvarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
devānāṁ bhayabhītānāṁ hariṇā rākṣasarṣabhau |
pratijñāto vadho ’smākaṁ tac cintayatha yat kṣamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
hiraṇyakaśipor mr̥tyur anyeṣāṁ ca suradviṣām |
duḥkhaṁ nārāyaṇaṁ jetuṁ yo no hantum abhīpsati ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
tataḥ sumālī mālī ca śrutvā mālyavato vacaḥ |
ūcatur bhrātaraṁ jyeṣṭhaṁ bhagāṁśāv iva vāsavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
svadhītaṁ dattam iṣṭaṁ ca aiśvaryaṁ paripālitam |
āyur nirāmayaṁ prāptaṁ svadharmaḥ sthāpitaś ca naḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
devasāgaram akṣobhyaṁ śastraughaiḥ pravigāhya ca |
jitā devā raṇe nityaṁ na no mr̥tyukr̥taṁ bhayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
nārāyaṇaś ca rudraś ca śakraś cāpi yamas tathā |
asmākaṁ pramukhe sthātuṁ sarva eva hi bibhyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
viṣṇor doṣaś ca nāsty atra kāraṇaṁ rākṣaseśvara |
devānām eva doṣeṇa viṣṇoḥ pracalitaṁ manaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
tasmād adya samudyuktāḥ sarvasainyasamāvr̥tāḥ |
devān eva jighāṁsāmo yebhyo doṣaḥ samutthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
iti mālī sumālī ca mālyavān agrajaḥ prabhuḥ |
udyogaṁ ghoṣayitvātha rākṣasāḥ sarva eva te |
yuddhāya niryayuḥ kruddhā jambhavr̥trabalā iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
syandanair vāraṇendraiś ca hayaiś ca girisaṁnibhaiḥ |
kharair gobhir athoṣṭraiś ca śiṁśumārair bhujaṁgamaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
makaraiḥ kacchapair mīnair vihaṁgair garuḍopamaiḥ |
siṁhair vyāghrair varāhaiś ca sr̥maraiś camarair api ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
tyaktvā laṅkāṁ tataḥ sarve rākṣasā balagarvitāḥ |
prayātā devalokāya yoddhuṁ daivataśatravaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
laṅkāviparyayaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā yāni laṅkālayāny atha |
bhūtāni bhayadarśīni vimanaskāni sarvaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
bhaumās tathāntarikṣāś ca kālājñaptā bhayāvahāḥ |
utpātā rākṣasendrāṇām abhāvāyotthitā drutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
asthīni meghā varṣanti uṣṇaṁ śoṇitam eva ca |
velāṁ samudro ’py utkrāntaś calante cācalottamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
aṭṭahāsān vimuñcanto ghananādasamasvanān |
bhūtāḥ paripatanti sma nr̥tyamānāḥ sahasraśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
gr̥dhracakraṁ mahac cāpi jvalanodgāribhir mukhaiḥ |
rākṣasānām upari vai bhramate kālacakravat ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
tān acintya mahotpātān rākṣasā balagarvitāḥ |
yanty eva na nivartante mr̥tyupāśāvapāśitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
mālyavāṁś ca sumālī ca mālī ca rajanīcarāḥ |
āsan puraḥsarās teṣāṁ kratūnām iva pāvakāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
mālyavantaṁ tu te sarve mālyavantam ivācalam |
niśācarā āśrayante dhātāram iva dehinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
tad balaṁ rākṣasendrāṇāṁ mahābhraghananāditam |
jayepsayā devalokaṁ yayau mālī vaśe sthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
52.
rākṣasānāṁ samudyogaṁ taṁ tu nārāyaṇaḥ prabhuḥ |
devadūtād upaśrutya dadhre yuddhe tato manaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
53.
sa devasiddharṣimahoragaiś ca; gandharvamukhyāpsarasopagītaḥ |
samāsasādāmaraśatrusainyaṁ; cakrāsisīrapravarādidhārī ||

=== meaning ===

---
54.
suparṇapakṣānilanunnapakṣaṁ; bhramatpatākaṁ pravikīrṇaśastram |
cacāla tad rākṣasarājasainyaṁ; calopalo nīla ivācalendraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
55.
tataḥ śitaiḥ śoṇitamāṁsarūṣitair; yugāntavaiśvānaratulyavigrahaiḥ |
niśācarāḥ saṁparivārya mādhavaṁ; varāyudhair nirbibhiduḥ sahasraśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
