=== Sarga 3 ===

---
1.
atha putraḥ pulastyasya viśravā munipuṁgavaḥ |
acireṇaiva kālena piteva tapasi sthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
satyavāñ śīlavān dakṣaḥ svādhyāyanirataḥ śuciḥ |
sarvabhogeṣv asaṁsakto nityaṁ dharmaparāyaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
jñātvā tasya tu tadvr̥ttaṁ bharadvājo mahān r̥ṣiḥ |
dadau viśravase bhāryāṁ svāṁ sutāṁ devavarṇinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
pratigr̥hya tu dharmeṇa bharadvājasutāṁ tadā |
mudā paramayā yukto viśravā munipuṁgavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
sa tasyāṁ vīryasaṁpannam apatyaṁ paramādbhutam |
janayām āsa dharmātmā sarvair brahmaguṇair yutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tasmiñ jāte tu saṁhr̥ṣṭaḥ sa babhūva pitāmahaḥ |
nāma cāsyākarot prītaḥ sārdhaṁ devarṣibhis tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
yasmād viśravaso ’patyaṁ sādr̥śyād viśravā iva |
tasmād vaiśravaṇo nāma bhaviṣyaty eṣa viśrutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sa tu vaiśravaṇas tatra tapovanagatas tadā |
avardhata mahātejā hutāhutir ivānalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tasyāśramapadasthasya buddhir jajñe mahātmanaḥ |
cariṣye niyato dharmaṁ dharmo hi paramā gatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
sa tu varṣasahasrāṇi tapas taptvā mahāvane |
pūrṇe varṣasahasre tu taṁ taṁ vidhim avartata ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
jalāśī mārutāhāro nirāhāras tathaiva ca |
evaṁ varṣasahasrāṇi jagmus tāny eva varṣavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
atha prīto mahātejāḥ sendraiḥ suragaṇaiḥ saha |
gatvā tasyāśramapadaṁ brahmedaṁ vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
parituṣṭo ’smi te vatsa karmaṇānena suvrata |
varaṁ vr̥ṇīṣva bhadraṁ te varārhas tvaṁ hi me mataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
athābravīd vaiśravaṇaḥ pitāmaham upasthitam |
bhagavam̐l lokapālatvam iccheyaṁ vittarakṣaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tato ’bravīd vaiśravaṇaṁ parituṣṭena cetasā |
brahmā suragaṇaiḥ sārdhaṁ bāḍham ity eva hr̥ṣṭavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
ahaṁ hi lokapālānāṁ caturthaṁ sraṣṭum udyataḥ |
yamendravaruṇānāṁ hi padaṁ yat tava cepsitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tatkr̥taṁ gaccha dharmajña dhaneśatvam avāpnuhi |
yamendravaruṇānāṁ hi caturtho ’dya bhaviṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
etac ca puṣpakaṁ nāma vimānaṁ sūryasaṁnibham |
pratigr̥hṇīṣva yānārthaṁ tridaśaiḥ samatāṁ vraja ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
svasti te ’stu gamiṣyāmaḥ sarva eva yathāgatam |
kr̥takr̥tyā vayaṁ tāta dattvā tava mahāvaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
gateṣu brahmapūrveṣu deveṣv atha nabhastalam |
dhaneśaḥ pitaraṁ prāha vinayāt praṇato vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
bhagavam̐l labdhavān asmi varaṁ kamalayonitaḥ |
nivāsaṁ na tu me devo vidadhe sa prajāpatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tat paśya bhagavan kaṁ cid deśaṁ vāsāya naḥ prabho |
na ca pīḍā bhaved yatra prāṇino yasya kasya cit ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
evam uktas tu putreṇa viśravā munipuṁgavaḥ |
vacanaṁ prāha dharmajña śrūyatām iti dharmavit ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
laṅkā nāma purī ramyā nirmitā viśvakarmaṇā |
rākṣasānāṁ nivāsārthaṁ yathendrasyāmarāvatī ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
ramaṇīyā purī sā hi rukmavaidūryatoraṇā |
rākṣasaiḥ sā parityaktā purā viṣṇubhayārditaiḥ |
śūnyā rakṣogaṇaiḥ sarvai rasātalatalaṁ gataiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
sa tvaṁ tatra nivāsāya rocayasva matiṁ svakām |
nirdoṣas tatra te vāso na ca bādhāsti kasya cit ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
etac chrutvā tu dharmātmā dharmiṣṭhaṁ vacanaṁ pituḥ |
niveśayām āsa tadā laṅkāṁ parvatamūrdhani ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
nairr̥tānāṁ sahasrais tu hr̥ṣṭaiḥ pramuditaiḥ sadā |
acireṇaikakālena saṁpūrṇā tasya śāsanāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
atha tatrāvasat prīto dharmātmā nairr̥tādhipaḥ |
samudraparidhānāyāṁ laṅkāyāṁ viśravātmajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
kāle kāle vinītātmā puṣpakeṇa dhaneśvaraḥ |
abhyagacchat susaṁhr̥ṣṭaḥ pitaraṁ mātaraṁ ca saḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
sa devagandharvagaṇair abhiṣṭutas; tathaiva siddhaiḥ saha cāraṇair api |
gabhastibhiḥ sūrya ivaujasā vr̥taḥ; pituḥ samīpaṁ prayayau śriyā vr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
