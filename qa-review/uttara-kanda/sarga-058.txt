=== Sarga 58 ===

---
1.
yām eva rātriṁ śatrughnaḥ parṇaśālāṁ samāviśat |
tām eva rātriṁ sītāpi prasūtā dārakadvayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tato ’rdharātrasamaye bālakā munidārakāḥ |
vālmīkeḥ priyam ācakhyuḥ sītāyāḥ prasavaṁ śubham |
tasya rakṣāṁ mahātejaḥ kuru bhūtavināśinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
teṣāṁ tad vacanaṁ śrutvā munir harṣam upāgamat |
bhūtaghnīṁ cākarot tābhyāṁ rakṣāṁ rakṣovināśinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
kuśamuṣṭim upādāya lavaṁ caiva tu sa dvijaḥ |
vālmīkiḥ pradadau tābhyāṁ rakṣāṁ bhūtavināśinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
yas tayoḥ pūrvajo jātaḥ sa kuśair mantrasaṁskr̥taiḥ |
nirmārjanīyas tu bhavet kuśa ity asya nāmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
yaś cāparo bhavet tābhyāṁ lavena susamāhitaḥ |
nirmārjanīyo vr̥ddhābhir lavaś ceti sa nāmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
evaṁ kuśalavau nāmnā tāv ubhau yamajātakau |
matkr̥tābhyāṁ ca nāmabhyāṁ khyātiyuktau bhaviṣyataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
te rakṣāṁ jagr̥hus tāṁ ca munihastāt samāhitāḥ |
akurvaṁś ca tato rakṣāṁ tayor vigatakalmaṣāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tathā tāṁ kriyamāṇāṁ tu rakṣāṁ gotraṁ ca nāma ca |
saṁkīrtanaṁ ca rāmasya sītāyāḥ prasavau śubhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
ardhārātre tu śatrughnaḥ śuśrāva sumahat priyam |
parṇaśālāṁ gato rātrau diṣṭyā diṣṭyeti cābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tathā tasya prahr̥ṣṭasya śatrughnasya mahātmanaḥ |
vyatītā vārṣikī rātriḥ śrāvaṇī laghuvikramā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
prabhāte tu mahāvīryaḥ kr̥tvā paurvāhṇikaṁ kramam |
muniṁ prāñjalir āmantrya prāyāt paścānmukhaḥ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sa gatvā yamunātīraṁ saptarātroṣitaḥ pathi |
r̥ṣīṇāṁ puṇyakīrtīnām āśrame vāsam abhyayāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sa tatra munibhiḥ sārdhaṁ bhārgavapramukhair nr̥paḥ |
kathābhir bahurūpābhir vāsaṁ cakre mahāyaśāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
