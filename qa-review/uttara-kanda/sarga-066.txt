=== Sarga 66 ===

---
1.
nāradasya tu tad vākyaṁ śrutvāmr̥tamayaṁ yathā |
praharṣam atulaṁ lebhe lakṣmaṇaṁ cedam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
gaccha saumya dvijaśreṣṭhaṁ samāśvāsaya lakṣmaṇa |
bālasya ca śarīraṁ tat tailadroṇyāṁ nidhāpaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
gandhaiś ca paramodārais tailaiś ca susugandhibhiḥ |
yathā na kṣīyate bālas tathā saumya vidhīyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
yathā śarīre bālasya guptasyākliṣṭakarmaṇaḥ |
vipattiḥ paribhedo vā bhaven na ca tathā kuru ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tathā saṁdiśya kākutstho lakṣmaṇaṁ śubhalakṣaṇam |
manasā puṣpakaṁ dadhyāv āgaccheti mahāyaśāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
iṅgitaṁ sa tu vijñāya puṣpako hemabhūṣitaḥ |
ājagāma muhūrtena samīpaṁ rāghavasya vai ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
so ’bravīt praṇato bhūtvā ayam asmi narādhipa |
vaśyas tava mahābāho kiṁkaraḥ samupasthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
bhāṣitaṁ ruciraṁ śrutvā puṣpakasya narādhipaḥ |
abhivādya maharṣīs tān vimānaṁ so ’dhyarohata ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
dhanur gr̥hītvā tūṇīṁ ca khaḍgaṁ ca ruciraprabham |
nikṣipya nagare vīrau saumitribharatāv ubhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
prāyāt pratīcīṁ sa marūn vicinvaṁś ca samantataḥ |
uttarām agamac chrīmān diśaṁ himavadāvr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
apaśyamānas tatrāpi svalpam apy atha duṣkr̥tam |
pūrvām api diśaṁ sarvām athāpaśyan narādhipaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
dakṣiṇāṁ diśam ākrāmat tato rājarṣinandanaḥ |
śaivalasyottare pārśve dadarśa sumahat saraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tasmin sarasi tapyantaṁ tāpasaṁ sumahat tapaḥ |
dadarśa rāghavaḥ śrīmām̐l lambamānam adhomukham ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
athainaṁ samupāgamya tapyantaṁ tapa uttamam |
uvāca rāghavo vākyaṁ dhanyas tvam asi suvrata ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
kasyāṁ yonyāṁ tapovr̥ddha vartase dr̥ḍhavikrama |
kautūhalāt tvāṁ pr̥cchāmi rāmo dāśarathir hy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
manīṣitas te ko nv arthaḥ svargalābho varāśrayaḥ |
yam aśritya tapas taptaṁ śrotum icchāmi tāpasa ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
brāhmaṇo vāsi bhadraṁ te kṣatriyo vāsi durjayaḥ |
vaiśyo vā yadi vā śūdraḥ satyam etad bravīhi me ||

=== meaning ===

---
