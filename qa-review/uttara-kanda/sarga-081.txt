=== Sarga 81 ===

---
1.
tathoktavati rāme tu tasya janma tad adbhutam |
uvāca lakṣmaṇo bhūyo bharataś ca mahāyaśāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sā priyā somaputrasya saṁvatsaram athoṣitā |
akarot kiṁ naraśreṣṭha tat tvaṁ śaṁsitum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tayos tad vākyamādhuryaṁ niśamya paripr̥cchatoḥ |
rāmaḥ punar uvācemāṁ prajāpatisute kathām ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
puruṣatvaṁ gate śūre budhaḥ paramabuddhimān |
saṁvartaṁ paramodāram ājuhāva mahāyaśāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
cyavanaṁ bhr̥guputraṁ ca muniṁ cāriṣṭaneminam |
pramodanaṁ modakaraṁ tato durvāsasaṁ munim ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
etān sarvān samānīya vākyajñas tattvadarśinaḥ |
uvāca sarvān suhr̥do dhairyeṇa susamāhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
ayaṁ rājā mahābāhuḥ kardamasya ilaḥ sutaḥ |
jānītainaṁ yathā bhūtaṁ śreyo hy asya vidhīyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
teṣāṁ saṁvadatām eva tam āśramam upāgamat |
kardamaḥ sumahātejā dvijaiḥ saha mahātmabhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
pulastyaś ca kratuś caiva vaṣaṭkāras tathaiva ca |
oṁkāraś ca mahātejās tam āśramam upāgaman ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
te sarve hr̥ṣṭamanasaḥ parasparasamāgame |
hitaiṣiṇo bāhli pateḥ pr̥thag vākyam athābruvan ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
kardamas tv abravīd vākyaṁ sutārthaṁ paramaṁ hitam |
dvijāḥ śr̥ṇuta madvākyaṁ yac chreyaḥ pārthivasya hi ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
nānyaṁ paśyāmi bhaiṣajyam antareṇa vr̥ṣadhvajam |
nāśvamedhāt paro yajñaḥ priyaś caiva mahātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tasmād yajāmahe sarve pārthivārthe durāsadam |
kardamenaivam uktās tu sarva eva dvijarṣabhāḥ |
rocayanti sma taṁ yajñaṁ rudrasyārādhanaṁ prati ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
saṁvartasya tu rājarṣiḥ śiṣyaḥ parapuraṁjayaḥ |
marutta iti vikhyātas taṁ yajñaṁ samupāharat ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tato yajño mahān āsīd budhāśramasamīpataḥ |
rudraś ca paramaṁ toṣam ājagāma mahāyaśāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
atha yajñasamāptau tu prītaḥ paramayā mudā |
umāpatir dvijān sarvān uvācedam ilāṁ prati ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
prīto ’smi hayamedhena bhaktyā ca dvijasattamāḥ |
asya bāhlipateś caiva kiṁ karomi priyaṁ śubham ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tathā vadati deveśe dvijās te susamāhitāḥ |
prasādayanti deveśaṁ yathā syāt puruṣas tv ilā ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tataḥ prītamanā rudraḥ puruṣatvaṁ dadau punaḥ |
ilāyai sumahātejā dattvā cāntaradhīyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
nivr̥tte hayamedhe tu gate cādarśanaṁ hare |
yathāgataṁ dvijāḥ sarve agacchan dīrghadarśinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
rājā tu bāhlim utsr̥jya madhyadeśe hy anuttamam |
niveśayām āsa puraṁ pratiṣṭhānaṁ yaśaskaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
śaśabindus tu rājāsīd bāhlyāṁ parapuraṁjayaḥ |
pratiṣṭhāna ilo rājā prajāpatisuto balī ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
sa kāle prāptavām̐l lokam ilo brāhmam anuttamam |
ailaḥ purūravā rājā pratiṣṭhānam avāptavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
īdr̥śo hy aśvamedhasya prabhāvaḥ puruṣarṣabhau |
strībhūtaḥ pauruṣaṁ lebhe yac cānyad api durlabham ||

=== meaning ===

---
