=== Sarga 61 ===

---
1.
tac chrutvā bhāṣitaṁ tasya śatrughnasya mahātmanaḥ |
krodham āhārayat tīvraṁ tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti cābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
pāṇau pāṇiṁ viniṣpiṣya dantān kaṭakaṭāyya ca |
lavaṇo raghuśārdūlam āhvayām āsa cāsakr̥t ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
taṁ bruvāṇaṁ tathā vākyaṁ lavaṇaṁ ghoravikramam |
śatrughno deva śatrughna idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
śatrughno na tadā jāto yadānye nirjitās tvayā |
tad adya bāṇābhihato vraja taṁ yamasādanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
r̥ṣayo ’py adya pāpātman mayā tvāṁ nihataṁ raṇe |
paśyantu viprā vidvāṁsas tridaśā iva rāvaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tvayi madbāṇanirdagdhe patite ’dya niśācara |
puraṁ janapadaṁ cāpi kṣemam etad bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
adya madbāhuniṣkrāntaḥ śaro vajranibhānanaḥ |
pravekṣyate te hr̥dayaṁ padmam aṁśur ivārkajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
evam ukto mahāvr̥kṣaṁ lavaṇaḥ krodhamūrchitaḥ |
śatrughnorasi cikṣepa taṁ śūraḥ śatadhācchinat ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tad dr̥ṣṭvā viphalaṁ karma rākṣasaḥ punar eva tu |
pādapān subahūn gr̥hya śatrughne vyasr̥jad balī ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
śatrughnaś cāpi tejasvī vr̥kṣān āpatato bahūn |
tribhiś caturbhir ekaikaṁ ciccheda nataparvabhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tato bāṇamayaṁ varṣaṁ vyasr̥jad rākṣasor asi |
śatrughno vīryasaṁpanno vivyathe na ca rākṣasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tataḥ prahasya lavaṇo vr̥kṣam utpāṭya līlayā |
śirasy abhyahanac chūraṁ srastāṅgaḥ sa mumoha vai ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tasmin nipatite vīre hāhākāro mahān abhūt |
r̥ṣīṇāṁ deva saṁghānāṁ gandharvāpsarasām api ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tam avajñāya tu hataṁ śatrughnaṁ bhuvi pātitam |
rakṣo labdhāntaram api na viveśa svam ālayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
nāpi śūlaṁ prajagrāha taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā bhuvi pātitam |
tato hata iti jñātvā tān bhakṣān samudāvahat ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
muhūrtāl labdhasaṁjñas tu punas tasthau dhr̥tāyudhaḥ |
śatrughno rākṣasadvāri r̥ṣibhiḥ saṁprapūjitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tato divyam amoghaṁ taṁ jagrāha śaram uttamam |
jvalantaṁ tejasā ghoraṁ pūrayantaṁ diśo daśa ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
vajrānanaṁ vajravegaṁ merumandara gauravam |
nataṁ parvasu sarveṣu saṁyugeṣv aparājitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
asr̥kcandanadigdhāṅgaṁ cārupatraṁ patatriṇam |
dānavendrācalendrāṇām asurāṇāṁ ca dāruṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
taṁ dīptam iva kālāgniṁ yugānte samupasthite |
dr̥ṣṭvā sarvāṇi bhūtāni paritrāsam upāgaman ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
sadevāsuragandharvaṁ samuniṁ sāpsarogaṇam |
jagad dhi sarvam asvasthaṁ pitāmaham upasthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
ūcuś ca devadeveśaṁ varadaṁ prapitāmaham |
kaccil lokakṣayo deva prāpto vā yugasaṁkayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
nedr̥śaṁ dr̥ṣṭapūrvaṁ na śrutaṁ vā prapitāmaha |
devānāṁ bhayasaṁmoho lokānāṁ saṁkṣayaḥ prabho ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
teṣāṁ tad vacanaṁ śrutvā brahmā lokapitāmanaḥ |
bhayakāraṇam ācaṣṭe devānām abhayaṁkaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
vadhāya lavaṇasyājau śaraḥ śatrughnadhāritaḥ |
tejasā yasya sarve sma saṁmūḍhāḥ surasattamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
eṣo hi pūrvaṁ devasya lokakartuḥ sanātanaḥ |
śaras tejomayo vatsā yena vai bhayam āgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
eṣa vai kaiṭabhasyārthe madhunaś ca mahāśaraḥ |
sr̥ṣṭo mahātmanā tena vadhārthaṁ daityayos tayoḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
evam etaṁ prajānīdhvaṁ viṣṇos tejomayaṁ śaram |
eṣā caiva tanuḥ pūrvā viṣṇos tasya mahātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
ito gacchatā paśyadhvaṁ vadhyamānaṁ mahātmanā |
rāmānujena vīreṇa lavaṇaṁ rākṣasottamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
tasya te devadevasya niśamya madhurāṁ giram |
ājagmur yatra yudhyete śatrughnalavaṇāv ubhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
taṁ śaraṁ divyasaṁkāśaṁ śatrughnakaradhāritam |
dadr̥śuḥ sarvabhūtāni yugāntāgnim ivotthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
ākāśam āvr̥taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā devair hi raghunandanaḥ |
siṁhanādaṁ muhuḥ kr̥tvā dadarśa lavaṇaṁ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
āhūtaś ca tatas tena śatrughnena mahātmanā |
lavaṇaḥ krodhasaṁyukto yuddhāya samupasthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
ākarṇāt sa vikr̥ṣyātha tad dhanur dhanvināṁ varaḥ |
sa mumoca mahābāṇaṁ lavaṇasya mahorasi |
uras tasya vidāryāśu praviveśa rasātalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
gatvā rasātalaṁ divyaṁ śaro vibudhapūjitaḥ |
punar evāgamat tūrṇam ikṣvākukulanandanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
śatrughnaśaranirbhinno lavaṇaḥ sa niśācaraḥ |
papāta sahasā bhūmau vajrāhata ivācalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
tac ca divyaṁ mahac chūlaṁ hate lavaṇarākṣase |
paśyatāṁ sarvabhūtānāṁ rudrasya vaśam anvagāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
ekeṣupātena bhayaṁ nihatya; lokatrayasyāsya raghupravīraḥ |
vinirbabhāv udyatacāpabāṇas; tamaḥ praṇudyeva sahasraraśmiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
