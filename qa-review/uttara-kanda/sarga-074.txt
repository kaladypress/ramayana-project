=== Sarga 74 ===

---
1.
tac chrutvā bhāṣitaṁ tasya rāmasyākliṣṭakarmaṇaḥ |
dvāḥsthaḥ kumārāv āhūya rāghavāya nyavedayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
dr̥ṣṭvā tu rāghavaḥ prāptau priyau bharatalakṣmaṇau |
pariṣvajya tato rāmo vākyam etad uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
kr̥taṁ mayā yathātathyaṁ dvijakāryam anuttamam |
dharmasetumato bhūyaḥ kartum icchāmi rāghavau ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
yuvābhyām ātmabhūtābhyāṁ rājasūyam anuttamam |
sahito yaṣṭum icchāmi tatra dharmo hi śāśvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
iṣṭvā tu rājasūyena mitraḥ śatrunibarhaṇaḥ |
suhutena suyajñena varuṇatvam upāgamat ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
somaś ca rājasūyena iṣṭvā dharmeṇa dharmavit |
prāptaś ca sarvalokānāṁ kīrtiṁ sthānaṁ ca śāśvatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
asminn ahani yac chreyaś cintyatāṁ tan mayā saha |
hitaṁ cāyati yuktaṁ ca prayatau vaktum arhatha ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
śrutvā tu rāghavasyaitad vākyaṁ vākyaviśāradaḥ |
bharataḥ prāñjalir bhūtvā vākyam etad uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tvayi dharmaḥ paraḥ sādho tvayi sarvā vasuṁdharā |
pratiṣṭhitā mahābāho yaśaś cāmitavikrama ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
mahīpālāś ca sarve tvāṁ prajāpatim ivāmarāḥ |
nirīkṣante mahātmāno lokanāthaṁ yathā vayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
prajāś ca pitr̥vad rājan paśyanti tvāṁ mahābala |
pr̥thivyāṁ gatibhūto ’si prāṇinām api rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sa tvam evaṁvidhaṁ yajñam āhartāsi kathaṁ nr̥pa |
pr̥thivyāṁ rājavaṁśānāṁ vināśo yatra dr̥śyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
pr̥thivyāṁ ye ca puruṣā rājan pauruṣam āgatāḥ |
sarveṣāṁ bhavitā tatra kṣayaḥ sarvāntakopamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sa tvaṁ puruṣaśārdūla guṇair atulavikrama |
pr̥thivīṁ nārhase hantuṁ vaśe hi tava vartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
bharatasya tu tad vākyaṁ śrutvāmr̥tamayaṁ yathā |
praharṣam atulaṁ lebhe rāmaḥ satyaparākramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
uvāca ca śubhāṁ vāṇīṁ kaikeyyā nandivardhanam |
prīto ’smi parituṣṭo ’smi tavādya vacanena hi ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
idaṁ vacanam aklībaṁ tvayā dharmasamāhitam |
vyāhr̥taṁ puruṣavyāghra pr̥thivyāḥ paripālanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
eṣa tasmād abhiprāyād rājasūyāt kratūttamāt |
nivartayāmi dharmajña tava suvyāhr̥tena vai ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
prajānāṁ pālanaṁ dharmo rājñāṁ yajñena saṁmitaḥ |
tasmāc chr̥ṇomi te vākyaṁ sādhūktaṁ susamāhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
