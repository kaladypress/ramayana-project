=== Sarga 84 ===

---
1.
vartamāne tathābhūte yajñe paramake ’dbhute |
saśiṣya ājagāmāśu vālmīkir munipuṁgavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sa dr̥ṣṭvā divyasaṁkāśaṁ yajñam adbhutadarśanam |
ekānte r̥ṣivāṭānāṁ cakāra uṭajāñ śubhān ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sa śiṣyāv abravīd dhr̥ṣṭo yuvāṁ gatvā samāhitau |
kr̥tsnaṁ rāmāyaṇaṁ kāvyaṁ gāyatāṁ parayā mudā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
r̥ṣivāṭeṣu puṇyeṣu brāhmaṇāvasatheṣu ca |
rathyāsu rājamārgeṣu pārthivānāṁ gr̥heṣu ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
rāmasya bhavanadvāri yatra karma ca vartate |
r̥tvijām agrataś caiva tatra geyaṁ viśeṣataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
imāni ca phalāny atra svādūni vividhāni ca |
jātāni parvatāgreṣu āsvādyāsvādya gīyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
na yāsyathaḥ śramaṁ vatsau bhakṣayitvā phalāni vai |
mūlāni ca sumr̥ṣṭāni nagarāt parihāsyatha ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
yadi śabdāpayed rāmaḥ śravaṇāya mahīpatiḥ |
r̥ṣīṇām upaviṣṭānāṁ tato geyaṁ pravartatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
divase viṁśatiḥ sargā geyā vai parayā mudā |
pramāṇair bahubhis tatra yathoddiṣṭaṁ mayā purā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
lobhaś cāpi na kartavyaḥ svalpo ’pi dhanakāṅkṣayā |
kiṁ dhanenāśramasthānāṁ phalamūlopabhoginām ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
yadi pr̥cchet sa kākutstho yuvāṁ kasyeti dārakau |
vālmīker atha śiṣyau hi brūtām evaṁ narādhipam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
imās tantrīḥ sumadhurāḥ sthānaṁ vā pūrvadarśitam |
mūrchayitvā sumadhuraṁ gāyetāṁ vigatajvarau ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
ādiprabhr̥ti geyaṁ syān na cāvajñāya pārthivam |
pitā hi sarvabhūtānāṁ rājā bhavati dharmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tad yuvāṁ hr̥ṣṭamanasau śvaḥ prabhāte samādhinā |
gāyetāṁ madhuraṁ geyaṁ tantrīlayasamanvitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
iti saṁdiśya bahuśo muniḥ prācetasas tadā |
vālmīkiḥ paramodāras tūṣṇīm āsīn mahāyaśāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tām adbhutāṁ tau hr̥daye kumārau; niveśya vāṇīm r̥ṣibhāṣitāṁ śubhām |
samutsukau tau sukham ūṣatur niśāṁ; yathāśvinau bhārgavanītisaṁskr̥tau ||

=== meaning ===

---
