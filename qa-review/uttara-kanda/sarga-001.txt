=== Sarga 1 ===

---
1.
prāptarājyasya rāmasya rākṣasānāṁ vadhe kr̥te |
ājagmur r̥ṣayaḥ sarve rāghavaṁ pratinanditum ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
kauśiko ’tha yavakrīto raibhyaś cyavana eva ca |
kaṇvo medhātitheḥ putraḥ pūrvasyāṁ diśi ye śritāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
svastyātreyaś ca bhagavān namuciḥ pramucus tathā |
ājagmus te sahāgastyā ye śritā dakṣiṇāṁ diśam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
pr̥ṣadguḥ kavaṣo dhaumyo raudreyaś ca mahān r̥ṣiḥ |
te ’py ājagmuḥ saśiṣyā vai ye śritāḥ paścimāṁ diśam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
vasiṣṭhaḥ kaśyapo ’thātrir viśvāmitro ’tha gautamaḥ |
jamadagnir bharadvājas te ’pi saptamaharṣayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
saṁprāpyaite mahātmāno rāghavasya niveśanam |
viṣṭhitāḥ pratihārārthaṁ hutāśanasamaprabhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
pratihāras tatas tūrṇam agastyavacanād atha |
samīpaṁ rāghavasyāśu praviveśa mahātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sa rāmaṁ dr̥śya sahasā pūrṇacandrasamadyutim |
agastyaṁ kathayām āsa saṁprāptam r̥ṣibhiḥ saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
śrutvā prāptān munīṁs tāṁs tu bālasūryasamaprabhān |
tadovāca nr̥po dvāḥsthaṁ praveśaya yathāsukham ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
dr̥ṣṭvā prāptān munīṁs tāṁs tu pratyutthāya kr̥tāñjaliḥ |
rāmo ’bhivādya prayata āsanāny ādideśa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
teṣu kāñcanacitreṣu svāstīrṇeṣu sukheṣu ca |
yathārham upaviṣṭās te āsaneṣv r̥ṣipuṁgavāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
rāmeṇa kuśalaṁ pr̥ṣṭāḥ saśiṣyāḥ sapurogamāḥ |
maharṣayo vedavido rāmaṁ vacanam abruvan ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
kuśalaṁ no mahābāho sarvatra raghunandana |
tvāṁ tu diṣṭyā kuśalinaṁ paśyāmo hataśātravam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
na hi bhāraḥ sa te rāma rāvaṇo rākṣaseśvaraḥ |
sadhanus tvaṁ hi lokāṁs trīn vijayethā na saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
diṣṭyā tvayā hato rāma rāvaṇaḥ putrapautravān |
diṣṭyā vijayinaṁ tvādya paśyāmaḥ saha bhāryayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
diṣṭyā prahasto vikaṭo virūpākṣo mahodaraḥ |
akampanaś ca durdharṣo nihatās te niśācarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
yasya pramāṇād vipulaṁ pramāṇaṁ neha vidyate |
diṣṭyā te samare rāma kumbhakarṇo nipātitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
diṣṭyā tvaṁ rākṣasendreṇa dvandvayuddham upāgataḥ |
devatānām avadhyena vijayaṁ prāptavān asi ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
saṁkhye tasya na kiṁ cit tu rāvaṇasya parābhavaḥ |
dvandvayuddham anuprāpto diṣṭyā te rāvaṇir hataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
diṣṭyā tasya mahābāho kālasyevābhidhāvataḥ |
muktaḥ suraripor vīra prāptaś ca vijayas tvayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
vismayas tv eṣa naḥ saumya saṁśrutyendrajitaṁ hatam |
avadhyaḥ sarvabhūtānāṁ mahāmāyādharo yudhi ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
dattvā puṇyām imāṁ vīra saumyām abhayadakṣiṇām |
diṣṭyā vardhasi kākutstha jayenāmitrakarśana ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
śrutvā tu vacanaṁ teṣām r̥ṣīṇāṁ bhāvitātmanām |
vismayaṁ paramaṁ gatvā rāmaḥ prāñjalir abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
bhavantaḥ kumbhakarṇaṁ ca rāvaṇaṁ ca niśācaram |
atikramya mahāvīryau kiṁ praśaṁsatha rāvaṇim ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
mahodaraṁ prahastaṁ ca virūpākṣaṁ ca rākṣasaṁ |
atikramya mahāvīryān kiṁ praśaṁsatha rāvaṇim ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
kīdr̥śo vai prabhāvo ’sya kiṁ balaṁ kaḥ parākramaḥ |
kena vā kāraṇenaiṣa rāvaṇād atiricyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
śakyaṁ yadi mayā śrotuṁ na khalv ājñāpayāmi vaḥ |
yadi guhyaṁ na ced vaktuṁ śrotum icchāmi kathyatām |
kathaṁ śakro jitas tena kathaṁ labdhavaraś ca saḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
