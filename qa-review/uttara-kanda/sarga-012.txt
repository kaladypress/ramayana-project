=== Sarga 12 ===

---
1.
rākṣasendro ’bhiṣiktas tu bhrātr̥bhyāṁ sahitas tadā |
tataḥ pradānaṁ rākṣasyā bhaginyāḥ samacintayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
dadau tāṁ kālakeyāya dānavendrāya rākṣasīm |
svasāṁ śūrpaṇakhāṁ nāma vidyujjihvāya nāmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
atha dattvā svasāraṁ sa mr̥gayāṁ paryaṭan nr̥paḥ |
tatrāpaśyat tato rāma mayaṁ nāma diteḥ sutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
kanyāsahāyaṁ taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā daśagrīvo niśācaraḥ |
apr̥cchat ko bhavān eko nirmanuṣya mr̥ge vane ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
mayas tv athābravīd rāma pr̥cchantaṁ taṁ niśācaram |
śrūyatāṁ sarvam ākhyāsye yathāvr̥ttam idaṁ mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
hemā nāmāpsarās tāta śrutapūrvā yadi tvayā |
daivatair mama sā dattā paulomīva śatakratoḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tasyāṁ saktamanās tāta pañcavarṣaśatāny aham |
sā ca daivatakāryeṇa gatā varṣaṁ caturdaśam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tasyāḥ kr̥te ca hemāyāḥ sarvaṁ hemapuraṁ mayā |
vajravaidūryacitraṁ ca māyayā nirmitaṁ tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tatrāham aratiṁ vindaṁs tayā hīnaḥ suduḥkhitaḥ |
tasmāt purād duhitaraṁ gr̥hītvā vanam āgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
iyaṁ mamātmajā rājaṁs tasyāḥ kukṣau vivardhitā |
bhartāram anayā sārdham asyāḥ prāpto ’smi mārgitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
kanyāpitr̥tvaṁ duḥkhaṁ hi narāṇāṁ mānakāṅkṣiṇām |
kanyā hi dve kule nityaṁ saṁśaye sthāpya tiṣṭhati ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
dvau sutau tu mama tv asyāṁ bhāryāyāṁ saṁbabhūvatuḥ |
māyāvī prathamas tāta dundubhis tadanantaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
etat te sarvam ākhyātaṁ yāthātathyena pr̥cchataḥ |
tvām idānīṁ kathaṁ tāta jānīyāṁ ko bhavān iti ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
evam ukto rākṣasendro vinītam idam abravīt |
ahaṁ paulastyatanayo daśagrīvaś ca nāmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
brahmarṣes taṁ sutaṁ jñātvā mayo harṣam upāgataḥ |
dātuṁ duhitaraṁ tasya rocayām āsa tatra vai ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
prahasan prāha daityendro rākṣasendram idaṁ vacaḥ |
iyaṁ mamātmajā rājan hemayāpsarasā dhr̥tā |
kanyā mandodarī nāma patnyarthaṁ pratigr̥hyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
bāḍham ity eva taṁ rāma daśagrīvo ’bhyabhāṣata |
prajvālya tatra caivāgnim akarot pāṇisaṁgraham ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
na hi tasya mayo rāma śāpābhijñas tapodhanāt |
viditvā tena sā dattā tasya paitāmahaṁ kulam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
amoghāṁ tasya śaktiṁ ca pradadau paramādbhutām |
pareṇa tapasā labdhāṁ jaghnivām̐l lakṣmaṇaṁ yayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
evaṁ sa kr̥tadāro vai laṅkāyām īśvaraḥ prabhuḥ |
gatvā tu nagaraṁ bhārye bhrātr̥bhyāṁ samudāvahat ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
vairocanasya dauhitrīṁ vajrajvāleti nāmataḥ |
tāṁ bhāryāṁ kumbhakarṇasya rāvaṇaḥ samudāvahat ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
gandharvarājasya sutāṁ śailūṣasya mahātmanaḥ |
saramāṁ nāma dharmajño lebhe bhāryāṁ vibhīṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tīre tu sarasaḥ sā vai saṁjajñe mānasasya ca |
mānasaṁ ca saras tāta vavr̥dhe jaladāgame ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
mātrā tu tasyāḥ kanyāyāḥ snehenākranditaṁ vacaḥ |
saro mā vardhatety uktaṁ tataḥ sā saramābhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
evaṁ te kr̥tadārā vai remire tatra rākṣasāḥ |
svāṁ svāṁ bhāryām upādāya gandharvā iva nandane ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tato mandodarī putraṁ meghanādam asūyata |
sa eṣa indrajin nāma yuṣmābhir abhidhīyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
jātamātreṇa hi purā tena rākṣasasūnunā |
rudatā sumahān mukto nādo jaladharopamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
jaḍīkr̥tāyāṁ laṅkāyāṁ tena nādena tasya vai |
pitā tasyākaron nāma meghanāda iti svayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
so ’vardhata tadā rāma rāvaṇāntaḥpure śubhe |
rakṣyamāṇo varastrībhiś channaḥ kāṣṭhair ivānalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
