=== Sarga 98 ===

---
1.
te dūtā rāmavākyena coditā laghuvikramāḥ |
prajagmur madhurāṁ śīghraṁ cakrur vāsaṁ na cādhvani ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tatas tribhir ahorātraiḥ saṁprāpya madhurām atha |
śatrughnāya yathāvr̥ttam ācakhyuḥ sarvam eva tat ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
lakṣmaṇasya parityāgaṁ pratijñāṁ rāghavasya ca |
putrayor abhiṣekaṁ ca paurānugamanaṁ tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
kuśasya nagarī ramyā vindhyaparvatarodhasi |
kuśāvatīti nāmnā sā kr̥tā rāmeṇa dhīmatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
śrāvitā ca purī ramyā śrāvatīti lavasya ca |
ayodhyāṁ vijanāṁ caiva bharataṁ rāghavānugam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
evaṁ sarvaṁ nivedyāśu śatrughnāya mahātmane |
viremus te tato dūtās tvara rājann iti bruvan ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
śrutvā taṁ ghorasaṁkāśaṁ kulakṣayam upasthitam |
prakr̥tīs tu samānīya kāñcanaṁ ca purohitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
teṣāṁ sarvaṁ yathāvr̥ttam ākhyāya raghunandanaḥ |
ātmanaś ca viparyāsaṁ bhaviṣyaṁ bhrātr̥bhiḥ saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tataḥ putradvayaṁ vīraḥ so ’bhyaṣiñcan narādhipaḥ |
subāhur madhurāṁ lebhe śatrughātī ca vaidiśam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
dvidhākr̥tvā tu tāṁ senāṁ mādhurīṁ putrayor dvayoḥ |
dhanadhānyasamāyuktau sthāpayām āsa pārthivau ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tato visr̥jya rājānaṁ vaidiśe śatrughātinam |
jagāma tvarito ’yodhyāṁ rathenaikena rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sa dadarśa mahātmānaṁ jvalantam iva pāvakam |
kṣaumasūkṣmāmbaradharaṁ munibhiḥ sārdham akṣayaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
so ’bhivādya tato rāmaṁ prāñjaliḥ prayatendriyaḥ |
uvāca vākyaṁ dharmajño dharmam evānucintayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
kr̥tvābhiṣekaṁ sutayor yuktaṁ rāghavayor dhanaiḥ |
tavānugamane rājan viddhi māṁ kr̥taniścayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
na cānyad atra vaktavyaṁ dustaraṁ tava śāsanam |
tyaktuṁ nārhasi māṁ vīra bhaktimantaṁ viśeṣataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tasya tāṁ buddhim aklībāṁ vijñāya raghunandanaḥ |
bāḍham ity eva śatrughnaṁ rāmo vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tasya vākyasya vākyānte vānarāḥ kāmarūpiṇaḥ |
r̥kṣarākṣasasaṁghāś ca samāpetur anekaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
devaputrā r̥ṣisutā gandharvāṇāṁ sutās tathā |
rāmakṣayaṁ viditvā te sarva eva samāgatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
te rāmam abhivādyāhuḥ sarva eva samāgatāḥ |
tavānugamane rājan saṁprāptāḥ sma mahāyaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
yadi rāma vināsmābhir gacches tvaṁ puruṣarṣabha |
yamadaṇḍam ivodyamya tvayā sma vinipātitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
evaṁ teṣāṁ vacaḥ śrutvā r̥kṣavānararakṣasām |
vibhīṣaṇam athovāca madhuraṁ ślakṣṇayā girā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
yāvat prajā dhariṣyanti tāvat tvaṁ vai vibhīṣaṇa |
rākṣasendra mahāvīrya laṅkāsthaḥ svaṁ dhariṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
prajāḥ saṁrakṣa dharmeṇa nottaraṁ vaktum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tam evam uktvā kākutstho hanūmantam athābravīt |
jīvite kr̥tabuddhis tvaṁ mā pratijñāṁ vilopaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
matkathāḥ pracariṣyanti yāval loke harīśvara |
tāvat tvaṁ dhārayan prāṇān pratijñām anupālaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tathaivam uktvā kākutsthaḥ sarvāṁs tān r̥kṣavānarān |
mayā sārdhaṁ prayāteti tadā tān rāghavo ’bravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
