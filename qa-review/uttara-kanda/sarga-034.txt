=== Sarga 34 ===

---
1.
arjunena vimuktas tu rāvaṇo rākṣasādhipaḥ |
cacāra pr̥thivīṁ sarvām anirviṇṇas tathā kr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
rākṣasaṁ vā manuṣyaṁ vā śr̥ṇute yaṁ balādhikam |
rāvaṇas taṁ samāsādya yuddhe hvayati darpitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tataḥ kadā cit kiṣkindhāṁ nagarīṁ vālipālitām |
gatvāhvayati yuddhāya vālinaṁ hemamālinam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tatas taṁ vānarāmātyas tāras tārāpitā prabhuḥ |
uvāca rāvaṇaṁ vākyaṁ yuddhaprepsum upāgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
rākṣasendra gato vālī yas te pratibalo bhavet |
nānyaḥ pramukhataḥ sthātuṁ tava śaktaḥ plavaṁgamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
caturbhyo ’pi samudrebhyaḥ saṁdhyām anvāsya rāvaṇa |
imaṁ muhūrtam āyāti vālī tiṣṭha muhūrtakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
etān asthicayān paśya ya ete śaṅkhapāṇḍurāḥ |
yuddhārthinām ime rājan vānarādhipatejasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
yad vāmr̥tarasaḥ pītas tvayā rāvaṇarākṣasa |
tathā vālinam āsādya tadantaṁ tava jīvitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
atha vā tvarase martuṁ gaccha dakṣiṇasāgaram |
vālinaṁ drakṣyase tatra bhūmiṣṭham iva bhāskaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
sa tu tāraṁ vinirbhartsya rāvaṇo rākṣaseśvaraḥ |
puṣpakaṁ tat samāruhya prayayau dakṣiṇārṇavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tatra hemagiriprakhyaṁ taruṇārkanibhānanam |
rāvaṇo vālinaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā saṁdhyopāsanatatparam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
puṣpakād avaruhyātha rāvaṇo ’ñjanasaṁnibhaḥ |
grahītuṁ vālinaṁ tūrṇaṁ niḥśabdapadam ādravat ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
yadr̥cchayonmīlayatā vālināpi sa rāvaṇaḥ |
pāpābhiprāyavān dr̥ṣṭaś cakāra na ca saṁbhramam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
śaśam ālakṣya siṁho vā pannagaṁ garuḍo yathā |
na cintayati taṁ vālī rāvaṇaṁ pāpaniścayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
jighr̥kṣamāṇam adyainaṁ rāvaṇaṁ pāpabuddhinam |
kakṣāvalambinaṁ kr̥tvā gamiṣyāmi mahārṇavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
drakṣyanty ariṁ mamāṅkasthaṁ sraṁsitorukarāmbaram |
lambamānaṁ daśagrīvaṁ garuḍasyeva pannagam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
ity evaṁ matim āsthāya vālī karṇam upāśritaḥ |
japan vai naigamān mantrāṁs tasthau parvatarāḍ iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tāv anyonyaṁ jighr̥kṣantau harirākṣasapārthivau |
prayatnavantau tat karma īhatur baladarpitau ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
hastagrāhyaṁ tu taṁ matvā pādaśabdena rāvaṇam |
parāṅmukho ’pi jagrāha vālī sarpam ivāṇḍajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
grahītukāmaṁ taṁ gr̥hya rakṣasām īśvaraṁ hariḥ |
kham utpapāta vegena kr̥tvā kakṣāvalambinam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
sa taṁ pīḍdayamānas tu vitudantaṁ nakhair muhuḥ |
jahāra rāvaṇaṁ vālī pavanas toyadaṁ yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
atha te rākṣasāmātyā hriyamāṇe daśānane |
mumokṣayiṣavo ghorā ravamāṇā hy abhidravan ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
anvīyamānas tair vālī bhrājate ’mbaramadhyagaḥ |
anvīyamāno meghaughair ambarastha ivāṁśumān ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
te ’śaknuvantaḥ saṁprāptaṁ vālinaṁ rākṣasottamāḥ |
tasya bāhūruvegena pariśrāntaḥ patanti ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
vālimārgād apākrāman parvatendrā hi gacchataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
apakṣigaṇasaṁpāto vānarendro mahājavaḥ |
kramaśaḥ sāgarān sarvān saṁdhyākālam avandata ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
sabhājyamāno bhūtais tu khecaraiḥ khecaro hariḥ |
paścimaṁ sāgaraṁ vālī ājagāma sarāvaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tatra saṁdhyām upāsitvā snātvā japtvā ca vānaraḥ |
uttaraṁ sāgaraṁ prāyād vahamāno daśānanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
uttare sāgare saṁdhyām upāsitvā daśānanam |
vahamāno ’gamad vālī pūrvam ambumahānidhim ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
tatrāpi saṁdhyām anvāsya vāsaviḥ sa harīśvaraḥ |
kiṣkindhābhimukho gr̥hya rāvaṇaṁ punar āgamat ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
caturṣv api samudreṣu saṁdhyām anvāsya vānaraḥ |
rāvaṇodvahanaśrāntaḥ kiṣkindhopavane ’patat ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
rāvaṇaṁ tu mumocātha svakakṣāt kapisattamaḥ |
kutas tvam iti covāca prahasan rāvaṇaṁ prati ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
vismayaṁ tu mahad gatvā śramalokanirīkṣaṇaḥ |
rākṣaseśo harīśaṁ tam idaṁ vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
vānarendra mahendrābha rākṣasendro ’smi rāvaṇaḥ |
yuddhepsur ahaṁ saṁprāptaḥ sa cādyāsāditas tvayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
aho balam aho vīryam aho gambhīratā ca te |
yenāhaṁ paśuvad gr̥hya bhrāmitaś caturo ’rṇavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
evam aśrāntavad vīra śīghram eva ca vānara |
māṁ caivodvahamānas tu ko ’nyo vīraḥ kramiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
trayāṇām eva bhūtānāṁ gatir eṣā plavaṁgama |
mano’nilasuparṇānāṁ tava vā nātra saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
so ’haṁ dr̥ṣṭabalas tubhyam icchāmi haripuṁgava |
tvayā saha ciraṁ sakhyaṁ susnigdhaṁ pāvakāgrataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
dārāḥ putrāḥ puraṁ rāṣṭraṁ bhogācchādanabhojanam |
sarvam evāvibhaktaṁ nau bhaviṣyati harīśvara ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
tataḥ prajvālayitvāgniṁ tāv ubhau harirākṣasau |
bhrātr̥tvam upasaṁpannau pariṣvajya parasparam ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
anyonyaṁ lambitakarau tatas tau harirākṣasau |
kiṣkindhāṁ viśatur hr̥ṣṭau siṁhau giriguhām iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
sa tatra māsam uṣitaḥ sugrīva iva rāvaṇaḥ |
amātyair āgatair nīcas trailokyotsādanārthibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
evam etat purāvr̥ttaṁ vālinā rāvaṇaḥ prabho |
dharṣitaś ca kr̥taś cāpi bhrātā pāvakasaṁnidhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
balam apratimaṁ rāma vālino ’bhavad uttamam |
so ’pi tayā vinirdagdhaḥ śalabho vahninā yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
