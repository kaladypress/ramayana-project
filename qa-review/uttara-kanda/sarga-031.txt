=== Sarga 31 ===

---
1.
tato rāmo mahātejā vismayāt punar eva hi |
uvāca praṇato vākyam agastyam r̥ṣisattamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
bhagavan kiṁ tadā lokāḥ śūnyā āsan dvijottama |
dharṣaṇāṁ yatra na prāpto rāvaṇo rākṣaseśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
utāho hīnavīryās te babhuvuḥ pr̥thivīkṣitaḥ |
bahiṣkr̥tā varāstraiś ca bahavo nirjitā nr̥pāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
rāghavasya vacaḥ śrutvā agastyo bhagavān r̥ṣiḥ |
uvāca rāmaṁ prahasan pitāmaha iveśvaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
sa evaṁ bādhamānas tu pārthivān pārthivarṣabha |
cacāra rāvaṇo rāma pr̥thivyāṁ pr̥thivīpate ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tato māhiṣmatīṁ nāma purīṁ svargapurīprabhām |
saṁprāpto yatra sāmnidhyaṁ paramaṁ vasuretasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tulya āsīn nr̥pas tasya pratāpād vasuretasaḥ |
arjuno nāma yasyāgniḥ śarakuṇḍe śayaḥ sadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tam eva divasaṁ so ’tha haihayādhipatir balī |
arjuno narmadāṁ rantuṁ gataḥ strībhiḥ saheśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
rāvaṇo rākṣasendras tu tasyāmātyān apr̥cchata |
kvārjuno vo nr̥paḥ so ’dya śīghram ākhyātum arhatha ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
rāvaṇo ’ham anuprāpto yuddhepsur nr̥vareṇa tu |
mamāgamanam avyagrair yuṣmābhiḥ saṁnivedyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
ity evaṁ rāvaṇenoktās te ’mātyāḥ suvipaścitaḥ |
abruvan rākṣasapatim asāmnidhyaṁ mahīpateḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
śrutvā viśravasaḥ putraḥ paurāṇām arjunaṁ gatam |
apasr̥tyāgato vindhyaṁ himavatsaṁnibhaṁ girim ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sa tam abhram ivāviṣṭam udbhrāntam iva medinīm |
apaśyad rāvaṇo vindhyam ālikhantam ivāmbaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sahasraśikharopetaṁ siṁhādhyuṣitakandaram |
prapāta patitaiḥ śītaiḥ sāṭṭahāsam ivāmbubhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
devadānavagandharvaiḥ sāpsarogaṇakiṁnaraiḥ |
sāha strībhiḥ krīḍamānaiḥ svargabhūtaṁ mahocchrayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
nadībhiḥ syandamānābhir agatipratimaṁ jalam |
sphuṭībhiś calajihvābhir vamantam iva viṣṭhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
ulkāvantaṁ darīvantaṁ himavatsaṁnibhaṁ girim |
paśyamānas tato vindhyaṁ rāvaṇo narmadāṁ yayau ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
calopalajalāṁ puṇyāṁ paścimodadhigāminīm |
mahiṣaiḥ sr̥maraiḥ siṁhaiḥ śārdūlarkṣagajottamaiḥ |
uṣṇābhitaptais tr̥ṣitaiḥ saṁkṣobhitajalāśayām ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
cakravākaiḥ sakāraṇḍaiḥ sahaṁsajalakukkuṭaiḥ |
sārasaiś ca sadāmattaiḥ kokūjadbhiḥ samāvr̥tām ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
phulladrumakr̥tottaṁsāṁ cakravākayugastanīm |
vistīrṇapulinaśroṇīṁ haṁsāvalisumekhalām ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
puṣpareṇvanuliptāṅgīṁ jalaphenāmalāṁśukām |
jalāvagāhasaṁsparśāṁ phullotpalaśubhekṣaṇām ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
puṣpakād avaruhyāśu narmadāṁ saritāṁ varām |
iṣṭām iva varāṁ nārīm avagāhya daśānanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
sa tasyāḥ puline ramye nānākusumaśobhite |
upopaviṣṭaḥ sacivaiḥ sārdhaṁ rākṣasapuṁgavaḥ |
narmadā darśajaṁ harṣam āptavān rākṣaseśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tataḥ salīlaṁ prahasān rāvaṇo rākṣasādhipaḥ |
uvāca sacivāṁs tatra mārīcaśukasāraṇān ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
eṣa raśmisahasreṇa jagat kr̥tveva kāñcanam |
tīkṣṇatāpakaraḥ sūryo nabhaso madhyam āsthitaḥ |
mām āsīnaṁ viditveha candrāyāti divākaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
narmadā jalaśītaś ca sugandhiḥ śramanāśanaḥ |
madbhayād anilo hy eṣa vāty asau susamāhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
iyaṁ cāpi saricchreṣṭhā narmadā narma vardhinī |
līnamīnavihaṁgormiḥ sabhayevāṅganā sthitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tad bhavantaḥ kṣatāḥ śastrair nr̥pair indrasamair yudhi |
candanasya raseneva rudhireṇa samukṣitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
te yūyam avagāhadhvaṁ narmadāṁ śarmadāṁ nr̥ṇām |
mahāpadmamukhā mattā gaṅgām iva mahāgajāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
asyāṁ snātvā mahānadyāṁ pāpmānaṁ vipramokṣyatha ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
aham apy atra puline śaradindusamaprabhe |
puṣpopaharaṁ śanakaiḥ kariṣyāmi umāpateḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
rāvaṇenaivam uktās tu mārīcaśukasāraṇāḥ |
samahodaradhūmrākṣā narmadām avagāhire ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
rākṣasendragajais tais tu kṣobhyate narmadā nadī |
vāmanāñjanapadmādyair gaṅgā iva mahāgajaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
tatas te rākṣasāḥ snātvā narmadāyā varāmbhasi |
uttīrya puṣpāṇy ājahrur balyarthaṁ rāvaṇasya tu ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
narmadā puline ramye śubhrābhrasadr̥śaprabhe |
rākṣasendrair muhūrtena kr̥taḥ puṣpamayo giriḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
puṣpeṣūpahr̥teṣv eva rāvaṇo rākṣaseśvaraḥ |
avatīrṇo nadīṁ snātuṁ gaṅgām iva mahāgajaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
tatra snātvā ca vidhivaj japtvā japyam anuttamam |
narmadā salilāt tasmād uttatāra sa rāvaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
rāvaṇaṁ prāñjaliṁ yāntam anvayuḥ saptarākṣasāḥ |
yatra yatra sa yāti sma rāvaṇo rākṣasādhipaḥ |
jāmbūnadamayaṁ liṅgaṁ tatra tatra sma nīyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
vālukavedimadhye tu tal liṅgaṁ sthāpya rāvaṇaḥ |
arcayām āsa gandhaiś ca puṣpaiś cāmr̥tagandhibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
tataḥ satām ārtiharaṁ haraṁ paraṁ; varapradaṁ candramayūkhabhūṣaṇam |
samarcayitvā sa niśācaro jagau; prasārya hastān praṇanarta cāyatān ||

=== meaning ===

---
