=== Sarga 97 ===

---
1.
visr̥jya lakṣmaṇaṁ rāmo duḥkhaśokasamanvitaḥ |
purodhasaṁ mantriṇaś ca naigamāṁś cedam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
adya rājye ’bhiṣekṣyāmi bharataṁ dharmavatsalam |
ayodhyāyāṁ patiṁ vīraṁ tato yāsyāmy ahaṁ vanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
praveśayata saṁbhārān mā bhūt kālātyayo yathā |
adyaivāhaṁ gamiṣyāmi lakṣmaṇena gatāṁ gatim ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tac chrutvā rāghaveṇoktaṁ sarvāḥ prakr̥tayo bhr̥śam |
mūrdhabhiḥ praṇatā bhūmau gatasattvā ivābhavan ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
bharataś ca visaṁjño ’bhūc chrutvā rāmasya bhāṣitam |
rājyaṁ vigarhayām āsa rāghavaṁ cedam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
satyena hi śape rājan svargaloke na caiva hi |
na kāmaye yathā rājyaṁ tvāṁ vinā raghunandana ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
imau kuśīlavau rājann abhiṣiñca narādhipa |
kosaleṣu kuśaṁ vīram uttareṣu tathā lavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
śatrughnasya tu gacchantu dūtās tvaritavikramāḥ |
idaṁ gamanam asmākaṁ svargāyākhyāntu māciram ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tac chrutvā bharatenoktaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā cāpi hy adhomukhān |
paurān duḥkhena saṁtaptān vasiṣṭho vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
vatsa rāma imāḥ paśya dharaṇīṁ prakr̥tīr gatāḥ |
jñātvaiṣām īpsitaṁ kāryaṁ mā caiṣāṁ vipriyaṁ kr̥thāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
vasiṣṭhasya tu vākyena utthāpya prakr̥tījanam |
kiṁ karomīti kākutsthaḥ sarvān vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tataḥ sarvāḥ prakr̥tayo rāmaṁ vacanam abruvan |
gacchantam anugacchāmo yato rāma gamiṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
eṣā naḥ paramā prītir eṣa dharmaḥ paro mataḥ |
hr̥dgatā naḥ sadā tuṣṭis tavānugamane dr̥ḍhā ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
paureṣu yadi te prītir yadi sneho hy anuttamaḥ |
saputradārāḥ kākutstha samaṁ gacchāma satpatham ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tapovanaṁ vā durgaṁ vā nadīm ambhonidhiṁ tathā |
vayaṁ te yadi na tyājyāḥ sarvān no naya īśvara ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sa teṣāṁ niścayaṁ jñātvā kr̥tāntaṁ ca nirīkṣya ca |
paurāṇāṁ dr̥ḍhabhaktiṁ ca bāḍham ity eva so ’bravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
evaṁ viniścayaṁ kr̥tvā tasminn ahani rāghavaḥ |
kosaleṣu kuśaṁ vīram uttareṣu tathā lavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
abhiṣiñcan mahātmānāv ubhāv eva kuśīlavau |
rathānāṁ tu sahasrāṇi trīṇi nāgāyutāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
daśa cāśvasahasrāṇi ekaikasya dhanaṁ dadau |
bahuratnau bahudhanau hr̥ṣṭapuṣṭajanāvr̥tau ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
abhiṣicya tu tau vīrau prasthāpya svapure tathā |
dūtān saṁpreṣayām āsa śatrughnāya mahātmane ||

=== meaning ===

---
