=== Sarga 48 ===

---
1.
sītāṁ tu rudatīṁ dr̥ṣṭvā ye tatra munidārakāḥ |
prādravan yatra bhagavān āste vālmīkir agryadhīḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
abhivādya muneḥ pādau muniputrā maharṣaye |
sarve nivedayām āsus tasyās tu ruditasvanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
adr̥ṣṭapūrvā bhagavan kasyāpy eṣā mahātmanaḥ |
patnī śrīr iva saṁmohād virauti vikr̥tasvarā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
bhagavan sādhu paśyemāṁ devatām iva khāc cyutām |
na hy enāṁ mānuṣīṁ vidmaḥ satkriyāsyāḥ prayujyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
teṣāṁ tad vacanaṁ śrutvā buddhyā niścitya dharmavit |
tapasā labdhacakṣuṣmān prādravad yatra maithilī ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
taṁ tu deśam abhipretya kiṁ cit padbhyāṁ mahāmuniḥ |
arghyam ādāya ruciraṁ jāhnavītīram āśritaḥ |
dadarśa rāghavasyeṣṭāṁ patnīṁ sītām anāthavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tāṁ sītāṁ śokabhārārtāṁ vālmīkir munipuṁgavaḥ |
uvāca madhurāṁ vāṇīṁ hlādayann iva tejasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
snuṣā daśarathasya tvaṁ rāmasya mahiṣī satī |
janakasya sutā rājñaḥ svāgataṁ te pativrate ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
āyānty evāsi vijñātā mayā dharmasamādhinā |
kāraṇaṁ caiva sarvaṁ me hr̥dayenopalakṣitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
apāpāṁ vedmi sīte tvāṁ tapolabdhena cakṣuṣā |
viśuddhabhāvā vaidehi sāmprataṁ mayi vartase ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
āśramasyāvidūre me tāpasyas tapasi sthitāḥ |
tās tvāṁ vatse yathā vatsaṁ pālayiṣyanti nityaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
idam arghyaṁ pratīccha tvaṁ visrabdhā vigatajvarā |
yathā svagr̥ham abhyetya viṣādaṁ caiva mā kr̥thāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
śrutvā tu bhāṣitaṁ sītā muneḥ paramam adbhutam |
śirasā vandya caraṇau tathety āha kr̥tāñjaliḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
taṁ prayāntaṁ muniṁ sītā prāñjaliḥ pr̥ṣṭhato ’nvagāt |
anvayād yatra tāpasyo dharmanityāḥ samāhitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā munim āyāntaṁ vaidehyānugataṁ tadā |
upājagmur mudā yuktā vacanaṁ cedam abruvan ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
svāgataṁ te muniśreṣṭha cirasyāgamanaṁ prabho |
abhivādayāmaḥ sarvās tvām ucyatāṁ kiṁ ca kurmahe ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tāsāṁ tad vacanaṁ śrutvā vālmīkir idam abravīt |
sīteyaṁ samanuprāptā patnī rāmasya dhīmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
snuṣā daśarathasyaiṣā janakasya sutā satī |
apāpā patinā tyaktā paripālyā mayā sadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
imāṁ bhavatyaḥ paśyantu snehena parameṇa ha |
gauravān mama vākyasya pūjyā vo ’stu viśeṣataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
muhur muhuś ca vaidehīṁ parisāntvya mahāyaśāḥ |
svam āśramaṁ śiṣyavr̥taḥ punar āyān mahātapāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
