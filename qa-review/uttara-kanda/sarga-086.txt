=== Sarga 86 ===

---
1.
rāmo bahūny ahāny eva tad gītaṁ paramādbhutam |
śuśrāva munibhiḥ sārdhaṁ rājabhiḥ saha vānaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tasmin gīte tu vijñāya sītāputrau kuśīlavau |
tasyāḥ pariṣado madhye rāmo vacanam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
madvaco brūta gacchadhvam iti bhagavato ’ntikam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
yadi śuddhasamācārā yadi vā vītakalmaṣā |
karotv ihātmanaḥ śuddhim anumānya mahāmunim ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
chandaṁ munes tu vijñāya sītāyāś ca manogatam |
pratyayaṁ dātukāmāyās tataḥ śaṁsata me laghu ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
śvaḥ prabhāte tu śapathaṁ maithilī janakātmajā |
karotu pariṣanmadhye śodhanārthaṁ mameha ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
śrutvā tu rāghavasyaitad vacaḥ paramam adbhutam |
dūtāḥ saṁprayayur vāṭaṁ yatrāste munipuṁgavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
te praṇamya mahātmānaṁ jvalantam amitaprabham |
ūcus te rāmavākyāni mr̥dūni madhurāṇi ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
teṣāṁ tad bhāṣitaṁ śrutvā rāmasya ca manogatam |
vijñāya sumahātejā munir vākyam athābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
evaṁ bhavatu bhadraṁ vo yathā tuṣyati rāghavaḥ |
tathā kariṣyate sītā daivataṁ hi patiḥ striyāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tathoktā muninā sarve rāmadūtā mahaujasaḥ |
pratyetya rāghavaṁ sarve munivākyaṁ babhāṣire ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tataḥ prahr̥ṣṭaḥ kākutsthaḥ śrutvā vākyaṁ mahātmanaḥ |
r̥ṣīṁs tatra sametāṁś ca rājñaś caivābhyabhāṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
bhagavantaḥ saśiṣyā vai sānugāś ca narādhipāḥ |
paśyantu sītāśapathaṁ yaś caivānyo ’bhikāṅkṣate ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā rāghavasya mahātmanaḥ |
sarveṣām r̥ṣimukhyānāṁ sādhuvādo mahān abhūt ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
rājānaś ca mahātmānaḥ praśaṁsanti sma rāghavam |
upapannaṁ naraśreṣṭha tvayy eva bhuvi nānyataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
evaṁ viniścayaṁ kr̥tvā śvobhūta iti rāghavaḥ |
visarjayām āsa tadā sarvāṁs tāñ śatrusūdanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
