=== Sarga 63 ===

---
1.
tato dvādaśame varṣe śatrughno rāmapālitām |
ayodhyāṁ cakame gantum alpabhr̥tyabalānugaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
mantriṇo balamukhyāṁś ca nivartya ca purodhasaṁ |
jagāma rathamukhyena hayayuktena bhāsvatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sa gatvā gaṇitān vāsān saptāṣṭau raghunandanaḥ |
ayodhyām agamat tūrṇaṁ rāghavotsukadarśanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
sa praviśya purīṁ ramyāṁ śrīmān ikṣvākunandanaḥ |
praviveśa mahābāhur yatra rāmo mahādyutiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
so ’bhivādya mahātmānaṁ jvalantam iva tejasā |
uvāca prāñjalir bhūtvā rāmaṁ satyaparākramam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
yad ājñaptaṁ mahārāja sarvaṁ tat kr̥tavān aham |
hataḥ sa lavaṇaḥ pāpaḥ purī sā ca niveśitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
dvādaśaṁ ca gataṁ varṣaṁ tvāṁ vinā raghunandana |
notsaheyam ahaṁ vastuṁ tvayā virahito nr̥pa ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
sa me prasādaṁ kākutstha kuruṣvāmitavikrama |
mātr̥hīno yathā vatsas tvāṁ vinā pravasāmy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
evaṁ bruvāṇaṁ śatrughnaṁ pariṣvajyedam abravīt |
mā viṣādaṁ kr̥thā vīra naitat kṣatriyaceṣṭitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
nāvasīdanti rājāno vipravāseṣu rāghava |
prajāś ca paripālyā hi kṣatradharmeṇa rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
kāle kāle ca māṁ vīra ayodhyām avalokitum |
āgaccha tvaṁ naraśreṣṭha gantāsi ca puraṁ tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
mamāpi tvaṁ sudayitaḥ prāṇair api na saṁśayaḥ |
avaśyaṁ karaṇīyaṁ ca rājyasya paripālanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tasmāt tvaṁ vasa kākutstha pañcarātraṁ mayā saha |
ūrdhvaṁ gantāsi madhurāṁ sabhr̥tyabalavāhanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
rāmasyaitad vacaḥ śrutvā dharmayuktaṁ mano’nugam |
śatrughno dīnayā vācā bāḍham ity eva cābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa pañcarātraṁ kākutstho rāghavasya yathājñayā |
uṣya tatra maheṣvāso gamanāyopacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
āmantrya tu mahātmānaṁ rāmaṁ satyaparākramam |
bharataṁ lakṣmaṇaṁ caiva mahāratham upāruhat ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
dūraṁ tābhyām anugato lakṣmaṇena mahātmanā |
bharatena ca śatrughno jagāmāśu purīṁ tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
