=== Sarga 38 ===

---
1.
te prayātā mahātmānaḥ pārthivāḥ sarvato diśam |
kampayanto mahīṁ vīrāḥ svapurāṇi prahr̥ṣṭavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
akṣauhiṇīsahasrais te samavetās tv anekaśaḥ |
hr̥ṣṭāḥ pratigatāḥ sarve rāghavārthe samāgatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
ūcuś caiva mahīpālā baladarpasamanvitāḥ |
na nāma rāvaṇaṁ yuddhe paśyāmaḥ purataḥ sthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
bharatena vayaṁ paścāt samānītā nirarthakam |
hatā hi rākṣasās tatra pārthivaiḥ syur na saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
rāmasya bāhuvīryeṇa pālitā lakṣmaṇasya ca |
sukhaṁ pāre samudrasya yudhyema vigatajvarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
etāś cānyāś ca rājānaḥ kathās tatra sahasraśaḥ |
kathayantaḥ svarāṣṭrāṇi viviśus te mahārathāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
yathāpurāṇi te gatvā ratnāni vividhāni ca |
rāmāya priyakāmārtham upahārān nr̥pā daduḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
aśvān ratnāni vastrāṇi hastinaś ca madotkaṭān |
candanāni ca divyāni divyāny ābharaṇāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
bharato lakṣmaṇaś caiva śatrughnaś ca mahārathaḥ |
ādāya tāni ratnāni ayodhyām agaman punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
āgatāś ca purīṁ ramyām ayodhyāṁ puruṣarṣabhāḥ |
daduḥ sarvāṇi ratnāni rāghavāya mahātmane ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
pratigr̥hya ca tat sarvaṁ prītiyuktaḥ sa rāghavaḥ |
sarvāṇi tāni pradadau sugrīvāya mahātmane ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
vibhīṣaṇāya ca dadau ye cānye r̥kṣavānarāḥ |
hanūmatpramukhā vīrā rākṣasāś ca mahābalāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
te sarve hr̥ṣṭamanaso rāmadattāni tāny atha |
śirobhir dhārayām āsur bāhubhiś ca mahābalāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
papuś caiva sugandhīni madhūni vividhāni ca |
māṁsāni ca sumr̥ṣṭāni phalāny āsvādayanti ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
evaṁ teṣāṁ nivasatāṁ māsaḥ sāgro gatas tadā |
muhūrtam iva tat sarvaṁ rāmabhaktyā samarthayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
reme rāmaḥ sa taiḥ sārdhaṁ vānaraiḥ kāmarūpibhiḥ |
rājabhiś ca mahāvīryai rākṣasaiś ca mahābalaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
evaṁ teṣāṁ yayau māso dvitīyaḥ śaiśiraḥ sukham |
vānarāṇāṁ prahr̥ṣṭānāṁ rākṣasānāṁ ca sarvaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
