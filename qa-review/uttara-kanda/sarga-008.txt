=== Sarga 8 ===

---
1.
hanyamāne bale tasmin padmanābhena pr̥ṣṭhataḥ |
mālyavān saṁnivr̥tto ’tha velātiga ivārṇavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
saṁraktanayanaḥ kopāc calan maulir niśācaraḥ |
padmanābham idaṁ prāha vacanaṁ paruṣaṁ tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
nārāyaṇa na jānīṣe kṣatradharmaṁ sanātanam |
ayuddhamanaso bhagnān yo ’smān haṁsi yathetaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
parāṅmukhavadhaṁ pāpaṁ yaḥ karoti sureśvara |
sa hantā na gataḥ svargaṁ labhate puṇyakarmaṇām ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
yuddhaśraddhātha vā te ’sti śaṅkhacakragadādhara |
ahaṁ sthito ’smi paśyāmi balaṁ darśaya yat tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
uvāca rākṣasendraṁ taṁ devarājānujo balī |
yuṣmatto bhayabhītānāṁ devānāṁ vai mayābhayam |
rākṣasotsādanaṁ dattaṁ tad etad anupālyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
prāṇair api priyaṁ kāryaṁ devānāṁ hi sadā mayā |
so ’haṁ vo nihaniṣyāmi rasātalagatān api ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
devam evaṁ bruvāṇaṁ tu raktāmburuhalocanam |
śaktyā bibheda saṁkruddho rākṣasendro rarāsa ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
mālyavad bhujanirmuktā śaktir ghaṇṭākr̥tasvanā |
harer urasi babhrāja meghastheva śatahradā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tatas tām eva cotkr̥ṣya śaktiṁ śaktidharapriyaḥ |
mālyavantaṁ samuddiśya cikṣepāmburuhekṣaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
skandotsr̥ṣṭeva sā śaktir govindakaraniḥsr̥tā |
kāṅkṣantī rākṣasaṁ prāyān maholkevāñjanācalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sā tasyorasi vistīrṇe hārabhāsāvabhāsite |
apatad rākṣasendrasya girikūṭa ivāśaniḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tayā bhinnatanutrāṇāḥ prāviśad vipulaṁ tamaḥ |
mālyavān punar āśvastas tasthau girir ivācalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tataḥ kārṣṇāyasaṁ śūlaṁ kaṇṭakair bahubhiś citam |
pragr̥hyābhyahanad devaṁ stanayor antare dr̥ḍham ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tathaiva raṇaraktas tu muṣṭinā vāsavānujam |
tāḍayitvā dhanurmātram apakrānto niśācaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tato ’mbare mahāñ śabdaḥ sādhu sādhv iti cotthitaḥ |
āhatya rākṣaso viṣṇuṁ garuḍaṁ cāpy atāḍayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
vainateyas tataḥ kruddhaḥ pakṣavātena rākṣasaṁ |
vyapohad balavān vāyuḥ śuṣkaparṇacayaṁ yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
dvijendrapakṣavātena drāvitaṁ dr̥śya pūrvajam |
sumālī svabalaiḥ sārdhaṁ laṅkām abhimukho yayau ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
pakṣavātabaloddhūto mālyavān api rākṣasaḥ |
svabalena samāgamya yayau laṅkāṁ hriyā vr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
evaṁ te rākṣasā rāma hariṇā kamalekṣaṇa |
bahuśaḥ saṁyuge bhagnā hatapravaranāyakāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
aśaknuvantas te viṣṇuṁ pratiyoddhuṁ bhayārditāḥ |
tyaktvā laṅkāṁ gatā vastuṁ pātālaṁ sahapatnayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
sumālinaṁ samāsādya rākṣasaṁ raghunandana |
sthitāḥ prakhyātavīryās te vaṁśe sālakaṭaṅkaṭe ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
ye tvayā nihatās te vai paulastyā nāma rākṣasāḥ |
sumālī mālyavān mālī ye ca teṣāṁ puraḥsarāḥ |
sarva ete mahābhāga rāvaṇād balavattarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
na cānyo rakṣasāṁ hantā sureṣv api puraṁjaya |
r̥te nārāyaṇaṁ devaṁ śaṅkhacakragadādharam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
bhavān nārāyaṇo devaś caturbāhuḥ sanātanaḥ |
rākṣasān hantum utpanno ajeyaḥ prabhur avyayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
