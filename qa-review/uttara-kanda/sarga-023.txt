=== Sarga 23 ===

---
1.
sa tu jitvā daśagrīvo yamaṁ tridaśapuṁgavam |
rāvaṇas tu jayaślāghī svasahāyān dadarśa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
jayena vardhayitvā ca mārīcapramukhās tataḥ |
puṣpakaṁ bhejire sarve sāntvitā ravaṇena ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tato rasātalaṁ hr̥ṣṭaḥ praviṣṭaḥ payaso nidhim |
daityoraga gaṇādhyuṣṭaṁ varuṇena surakṣitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
sa tu bhogavatīṁ gatvā purīṁ vāsukipālitām |
sthāpya nāgān vaśe kr̥tvā yayau maṇimatīṁ purīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
nivātakavacās tatra daityā labdhavarā vasan |
rākṣasas tān samāsādya yuddhena samupāhvayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
te tu sarve suvikrāntā daiteyā balaśālinaḥ |
nānāpraharaṇās tatra prayuddhā yuddhadurmadāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
teṣāṁ tu yudhyamānānāṁ sāgraḥ saṁvatsaro gataḥ |
na cānyatarayos tatra vijayo vā kṣayo ’pi vā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tataḥ pitāmahas tatra trailokyagatir avyayaḥ |
ājagāma drutaṁ devo vimānavaram āsthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
nivātakavacānāṁ tu nivārya raṇakarma tat |
vr̥ddhaḥ pitāmaho vākyam uvāca viditārthavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
na hy ayaṁ rāvaṇo yuddhe śakyo jetuṁ surāsuraiḥ |
na bhavantaḥ kṣayaṁ netuṁ śakyāḥ sendraiḥ surāsuraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
rākṣasasya sakhitvaṁ vai bhavadbhiḥ saha rocate |
avibhaktā hi sarvārthāḥ suhr̥dāṁ nātra saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tato ’gnisākṣikaṁ sakhyaṁ kr̥tavāṁs tatra rāvaṇaḥ |
nivātakavacaiḥ sārdhaṁ prītimān abhavat tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
arcitas tair yathānyāyaṁ saṁvatsarasukhoṣitaḥ |
svapurān nirviśeṣaṁ ca pūjāṁ prāpto daśānanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
sa tūpadhārya māyānāṁ śatam ekonam ātmavān |
salilendrapurānveṣī sa babhrāma rasātalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tato ’śmanagaraṁ nāma kālakeyābhirakṣitam |
taṁ vijitya muhūrtena jaghne daityāṁś catuḥśatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tataḥ pāṇḍurameghābhaṁ kailāsam iva saṁsthitam |
varuṇasyālayaṁ divyam apaśyad rākṣasādhipaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
kṣarantīṁ ca payo nityaṁ surabhiṁ gām avasthitām |
yasyāḥ payoviniṣyandāt kṣīrodo nāma sāgaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
yasmāc candraḥ prabhavati śītaraśmiḥ prajāhitaḥ |
yaṁ samāsādya jīvanti phenapāḥ paramarṣayaḥ |
amr̥taṁ yatra cotpannaṁ surā cāpi surāśinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
yāṁ bruvanti narā loke surabhiṁ nāma nāmataḥ |
pradakṣiṇaṁ tu tāṁ kr̥tvā rāvaṇaḥ paramādbhutām |
praviveśa mahāghoraṁ guptaṁ bahuvidhair balaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tato dhārāśatākīrṇaṁ śāradābhranibhaṁ tadā |
nityaprahr̥ṣṭaṁ dadr̥śe varuṇasya gr̥hottamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tato hatvā balādhyakṣān samare taiś ca tāḍitaḥ |
abravīt kva gato yo vo rājā śīghraṁ nivedyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
yuddhārthī rāvaṇaḥ prāptas tasya yuddhaṁ pradīyatām |
vada vā na bhayaṁ te ’sti nirjito ’smīti sāñjaliḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
etasminn antare kruddhā varuṇasya mahātmanaḥ |
putrāḥ pautrāś ca niṣkrāman gauś ca puṣkara eva ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
te tu vīryaguṇopetā balaiḥ parivr̥tāḥ svakaiḥ |
yuktvā rathān kāmagamān udyadbhāskaravarcasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tato yuddhaṁ samabhavad dāruṇaṁ lomaharṣaṇam |
salilendrasya putrāṇāṁ rāvaṇasya ca rakṣasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
amātyais tu mahāvīryair daśagrīvasya rakṣasaḥ |
vāruṇaṁ tad balaṁ kr̥tsnaṁ kṣaṇena vinipātitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
samīkṣya svabalaṁ saṁkhye varuṇasyā sutās tadā |
arditāḥ śarajālena nivr̥ttā raṇakarmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
mahītalagatās te tu rāvaṇaṁ dr̥śya puṣpake |
ākāśam āśu viviśuḥ syandanaiḥ śīghragāmibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
mahad āsīt tatas teṣāṁ tulyaṁ sthānam avāpya tat |
ākāśayuddhaṁ tumulaṁ devadānavayor iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
tatas te rāvaṇaṁ yuddhe śaraiḥ pāvakasaṁnibhaiḥ |
vimukhīkr̥tya saṁhr̥ṣṭā vinedur vividhān ravān ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
tato mahodaraḥ kruddho rājānaṁ dr̥śya dharṣitam |
tyaktvā mr̥tyubhayaṁ vīro yuddhakāṅkṣī vyalokayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
tena teṣāṁ hayā ye ca kāmagāḥ pavanopamāḥ |
mahodareṇa gadayā hatās te prayayuḥ kṣitim ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
teṣāṁ varuṇasūnūnāṁ hatvā yodhān hayāṁś ca tān |
mumocāśu mahānādaṁ virathān prekṣya tān sthitān ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
te tu teṣāṁ rathāḥ sāśvāḥ saha sārathibhir varaiḥ |
mahodareṇa nihatāḥ patitāḥ pr̥thivītale ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
te tu tyaktvā rathān putrā varuṇasya mahātmanaḥ |
ākāśe viṣṭhitāḥ śūrāḥ svaprabhāvān na vivyathuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
dhanūṁṣi kr̥tvā sajyāni vinirbhidya mahodaram |
rāvaṇaṁ samare kruddhāḥ sahitāḥ samabhidravan ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
tataḥ kruddho daśagrīvaḥ kālāgnir iva viṣṭhitaḥ |
śaravarṣaṁ mahāvegaṁ teṣāṁ marmasv apātayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
musalāni vicitrāṇi tato bhallaśatāni ca |
paṭṭasāṁś caiva śaktīś ca śataghnīs tomarāṁs tathā |
pātayām āsa durdharṣas teṣām upari viṣṭhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
atha viddhās tu te vīrā viniṣpetuḥ padātayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
tato rakṣo mahānādaṁ muktvā hanti sma vāruṇān |
nānāpraharaṇair ghorair dhārāpātair ivāmbudaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
tatas te vimukhāḥ sarve patitā dharaṇītale |
raṇāt svapuruṣaiḥ śīghraṁ gr̥hāṇy eva praveśitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
tān abravīt tato rakṣo varuṇāya nivedyatām |
rāvaṇaṁ cābravīn mantrī prabhāso nāma vāruṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
gataḥ khalu mahātejā brahmalokaṁ jaleśvaraḥ |
gāndharvaṁ varuṇaḥ śrotuṁ yaṁ tvam āhvayase yudhi ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
tat kiṁ tava vr̥thā vīra pariśrāmya gate nr̥pe |
ye tu saṁnihitā vīrāḥ kumārās te parājitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
rākṣasendras tu tac chrutvā nāma viśrāvya cātmanaḥ |
harṣān nādaṁ vimuñcan vai niṣkrānto varuṇālayāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
āgatas tu pathā yena tenaiva vinivr̥tya saḥ |
laṅkām abhimukho rakṣo nabhastalagato yayau ||

=== meaning ===

---
