=== Sarga 13 ===

---
1.
atha lokeśvarotsr̥ṣṭā tatra kālena kena cit |
nidrā samabhavat tīvrā kumbhakarṇasya rūpiṇī ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tato bhrātaram āsīnaṁ kumbhakarṇo ’bravīd vacaḥ |
nidrā māṁ bādhate rājan kārayasva mamālayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
viniyuktās tato rājñā śilpino viśvakarmavat |
akurvan kumbhakarṇasya kailāsasamam ālayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
vistīrṇaṁ yojanaṁ śubhraṁ tato dviguṇam āyatam |
darśanīyaṁ nirābādhaṁ kumbhakarṇasya cakrire ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
sphāṭikaiḥ kāñcanaiś citraiḥ stambhaiḥ sarvatra śobhitam |
vaidūryakr̥taśobhaṁ ca kiṅkiṇījālakaṁ tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
dantatoraṇavinyastaṁ vajrasphaṭikavedikam |
sarvartusukhadaṁ nityaṁ meroḥ puṇyāṁ guhām iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tatra nidrāṁ samāviṣṭaḥ kumbhakarṇo niśācaraḥ |
bahūny abdasahasrāṇi śayāno nāvabudhyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
nidrābhibhūte tu tadā kumbhakarṇe daśānanaḥ |
devarṣiyakṣagandharvān bādhate sma sa nityaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
udyānāni vicitrāṇi nandanādīni yāni ca |
tāni gatvā susaṁkruddho bhinatti sma daśānanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
nadīṁ gaja iva krīḍan vr̥kṣān vāyur iva kṣipan |
nagān vajra iva sr̥ṣṭo vidhvaṁsayati nityaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tathā vr̥ttaṁ tu vijñāya daśagrīvaṁ dhaneśvaraḥ |
kulānurūpaṁ dharmajña vr̥ttaṁ saṁsmr̥tya cātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
saubhrātradarśanārthaṁ tu dūtaṁ vaiśvaraṇas tadā |
laṅkāṁ saṁpreṣayām āsa daśagrīvasya vai hitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sa gatvā nagarīṁ laṅkām āsasāda vibhīṣaṇam |
mānitas tena dharmeṇa pr̥ṣṭaś cāgamanaṁ prati ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
pr̥ṣṭvā ca kuśalaṁ rājño jñātīn api ca bāndhavān |
sabhāyāṁ darśayām āsa tam āsīnaṁ daśānanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa dr̥ṣṭvā tatra rājānaṁ dīpyamānaṁ svatejasā |
jayena cābhisaṁpūjya tūṣṇīm āsīn muhūrtakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tasyopanīte paryaṅke varāstaraṇasaṁvr̥te |
upaviśya daśagrīvaṁ dūto vākyam athābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
rājan vadāmi te sarvaṁ bhrātā tava yad abravīt |
ubhayoḥ sadr̥śaṁ saumya vr̥ttasya ca kulasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
sādhu paryāptam etāvat kr̥taś cāritrasaṁgrahaḥ |
sādhu dharme vyavasthānaṁ kriyatāṁ yadi śakyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
dr̥ṣṭaṁ me nandanaṁ bhagnam r̥ṣayo nihatāḥ śrutāḥ |
devānāṁ tu samudyogas tvatto rājañ śrutaś ca me ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
nirākr̥taś ca bahuśas tvayāhaṁ rākṣasādhipa |
aparāddhā hi bālyāc ca rakṣaṇīyāḥ svabāndhavāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
ahaṁ tu himavatpr̥ṣṭhaṁ gato dharmam upāsitum |
raudraṁ vrataṁ samāsthāya niyato niyatendriyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tatra devo mayā dr̥ṣṭaḥ saha devyomayā prabhuḥ |
savyaṁ cakṣur mayā caiva tatra devyāṁ nipātitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
kā nv iyaṁ syād iti śubhā na khalv anyena hetunā |
rūpaṁ hy anupamaṁ kr̥tvā tatra krīḍati pārvatī ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tato devyāḥ prabhāvena dagdhaṁ savyaṁ mamekṣaṇam |
reṇudhvastam iva jyotiḥ piṅgalatvam upāgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tato ’ham anyad vistīrṇaṁ gatvā tasya gires taṭam |
pūrṇaṁ varṣaśatāny aṣṭau samavāpa mahāvratam ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
samāpte niyame tasmiṁs tatra devo maheśvaraḥ |
prītaḥ prītena manasā prāha vākyam idaṁ prabhuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
prīto ’smi tava dharmajña tapasānena suvrata |
mayā caitad vrataṁ cīrṇaṁ tvayā caiva dhanādhipa ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tr̥tīyaḥ puruṣo nāsti yaś cared vratam īdr̥śam |
vrataṁ suduścaraṁ hy etan mayaivotpāditaṁ purā ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
tat sakhitvaṁ mayā sārdhaṁ rocayasva dhaneśvara |
tapasā nirjitatvād dhi sakhā bhava mamānagha ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
devyā dagdhaṁ prabhāvena yac ca sāvyaṁ tavekṣaṇam |
ekākṣi piṅgalety eva nāma sthāsyati śāśvatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
evaṁ tena sakhitvaṁ ca prāpyānujñāṁ ca śaṁkarāt |
āgamya ca śruto ’yaṁ me tava pāpaviniścayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
tadadharmiṣṭhasaṁyogān nivarta kuladūṣaṇa |
cintyate hi vadhopāyaḥ sarṣisaṁghaiḥ surais tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
evam ukto daśagrīvaḥ kruddhaḥ saṁraktalocanaḥ |
hastān dantāṁś a saṁpīḍya vākyam etad uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
vijñātaṁ te mayā dūta vākyaṁ yat tvaṁ prabhāṣase |
naiva tvam asi naivāsau bhrātrā yenāsi preṣitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
hitaṁ na sa mamaitad dhi bravīti dhanarakṣakaḥ |
maheśvarasakhitvaṁ tu mūḍha śrāvayase kila ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
na hantavyo gurur jyeṣṭho mamāyam iti manyate |
tasya tv idānīṁ śrutvā me vākyam eṣā kr̥tā matiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
trīm̐l lokān api jeṣyāmi bāhuvīryam upāśritaḥ |
etan muhūrtam eṣo ’haṁ tasyaikasya kr̥te ca vai |
caturo lokapālāṁs tān nayiṣyāmi yamakṣayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
evam uktvā tu laṅkeśo dūtaṁ khaḍgena jaghnivān |
dadau bhakṣayituṁ hy enaṁ rākṣasānāṁ durātmanām ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
tataḥ kr̥tasvastyayano ratham āruhya rāvaṇaḥ |
trailokyavijayākāṅkṣī yayau tatra dhaneśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
