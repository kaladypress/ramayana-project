=== Sarga 18 ===

---
1.
praviṣṭāyāṁ hutāśaṁ tu vedavatyāṁ sa rāvaṇaḥ |
puṣpakaṁ tat samāruhya paricakrāma medinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tato maruttaṁ nr̥patiṁ yajantaṁ saha daivataiḥ |
uśīrabījam āsādya dadarśa sa tu rākṣasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
saṁvarto nāma brahmarṣir bhrātā sākṣād br̥haspateḥ |
yājayām āsa dharmajñaḥ sarvair brahmagaṇair vr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
dr̥ṣṭvā devās tu tad rakṣo varadānena durjayam |
tāṁ tāṁ yoniṁ samāpannās tasya dharṣaṇabhīravaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
indro mayūraḥ saṁvr̥tto dharmarājas tu vāyasaḥ |
kr̥kalāso dhanādhyakṣo haṁso vai varuṇo ’bhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
taṁ ca rājānam āsādya rāvaṇo rākṣasādhipaḥ |
prāha yuddhaṁ prayacceti nirjito ’smīti vā vada ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tato marutto nr̥patiḥ ko bhavān ity uvāca tam |
avahāsaṁ tato muktvā rākṣaso vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
akutūhalabhāvena prīto ’smi tava pārthiva |
dhanadasyānujaṁ yo māṁ nāvagacchasi rāvaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
triṣu lokeṣu kaḥ so ’sti yo na jānāti me balam |
bhrātaraṁ yena nirjitya vimānam idam āhr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tato marutto nr̥patis taṁ rākṣasam athābravīt |
dhanyaḥ khalu bhavān yena jyeṣṭho bhrātā raṇe jitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
nādharmasahitaṁ ślāghyaṁ na lokapratisaṁhitam |
karma daurātmyakaṁ kr̥tvā ślāghase bhrātr̥nirjayāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
kiṁ tvaṁ prāk kevalaṁ dharmaṁ caritvā labdhavān varam |
śrutapūrvaṁ hi na mayā yādr̥śaṁ bhāṣase svayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tataḥ śarāsanaṁ gr̥hya sāyakāṁś ca sa pārthivaḥ |
raṇāya niryayau kruddhaḥ saṁvarto mārgam āvr̥ṇot ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
so ’bravīt snehasaṁyuktaṁ maruttaṁ taṁ mahān r̥ṣiḥ |
śrotavyaṁ yadi madvākyaṁ saṁprahāro na te kṣamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
māheśvaram idaṁ satram asamāptaṁ kulaṁ dahet |
dīkṣitasya kuto yuddhaṁ krūratvaṁ dīkṣite kutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
saṁśayaś ca raṇe nityaṁ rākṣasaś caiṣa durjayaḥ |
sa nivr̥tto guror vākyān maruttaḥ pr̥thivīpatiḥ |
visr̥jya saśaraṁ cāpaṁ svastho makhamukho ’bhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tatas taṁ nirjitaṁ matvā ghoṣayām āsa vai śukaḥ |
rāvaṇo jitavāṁś ceti harṣān nādaṁ ca muktavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tān bhakṣayitvā tatrasthān maharṣīn yajñam āgatān |
vitr̥pto rudhirais teṣāṁ punaḥ saṁprayayau mahīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
rāvaṇe tu gate devāḥ sendrāś caiva divaukasaḥ |
tataḥ svāṁ yonim āsādya tāni sattvāny athābruvan ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
harṣāt tadābravīd indro mayūraṁ nīlabarhiṇam |
prīto ’smi tava dharmajña upakārād vihaṁgama ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
mama netrasahasraṁ yat tat te barhe bhaviṣyati |
varṣamāṇe mayi mudaṁ prāpsyase prītilakṣaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
nīlāḥ kila purā barhā mayūrāṇāṁ narādhipa |
surādhipād varaṁ prāpya gatāḥ sarve vicitratām ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
dharmarājo ’bravīd rāma prāgvaṁśe vāyasaṁ sthitam |
pakṣiṁs tavāsmi suprītaḥ prītasya ca vacaḥ śr̥ṇu ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
yathānye vividhai rogaiḥ pīḍyante prāṇino mayā |
te na te prabhaviṣyanti mayi prīte na saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
mr̥tyutas te bhayaṁ nāsti varān mama vihaṁgama |
yāvat tvāṁ na vadhiṣyanti narās tāvad bhaviṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
ye ca madviṣayasthās tu mānavāḥ kṣudhayārditāḥ |
tvayi bhukte tu tr̥ptās te bhaviṣyanti sabāndhavāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
varuṇas tv abravīd dhaṁsaṁ gaṅgātoyavicāriṇam |
śrūyatāṁ prītisaṁyuktaṁ vacaḥ patraratheśvara ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
varṇo manoharaḥ saumyaś candramaṇḍalasaṁnibhaḥ |
bhaviṣyati tavodagraḥ śuklaphenasamaprabhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
maccharīraṁ samāsādya kānto nityaṁ bhaviṣyasi |
prāpsyase cātulāṁ prītim etan me prītilakṣaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
haṁsānāṁ hi purā rāma na varṇaḥ sarvapāṇḍuraḥ |
pakṣā nīlāgrasaṁvītāḥ kroḍāḥ śaṣpāgranirmalāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
athābravīd vaiśravaṇaḥ kr̥kalāsaṁ girau sthitam |
hairaṇyaṁ saṁprayacchāmi varṇaṁ prītis tavāpy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
sadravyaṁ ca śiro nityaṁ bhaviṣyati tavākṣayam |
eṣa kāñcanako varṇo matprītyā te bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
evaṁ dattvā varāṁs tebhyas tasmin yajñotsave surāḥ |
nivr̥tte saha rājñā vai punaḥ svabhavanaṁ gatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
