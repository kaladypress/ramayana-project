=== Sarga 33 ===

---
1.
rāvaṇagrahaṇaṁ tat tu vāyugrahaṇasaṁnibham |
r̥ṣiḥ pulastyaḥ śuśrāva kathitaṁ divi daivataiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tataḥ putrasutasnehāt kampyamāno mahādhr̥tiḥ |
māhiṣmatīpatiṁ draṣṭum ājagāma mahān r̥ṣiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sa vāyumārgam āsthāya vāyutulyagatir dvijaḥ |
purīṁ māhiṣmatīṁ prāpto manaḥsaṁtāpavikramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
so ’marāvatisaṁkāśāṁ hr̥ṣṭapuṣṭajanāvr̥tām |
praviveśa purīṁ brahmā indrasyevāmarāvatīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
pādacāram ivādityaṁ niṣpatantaṁ sudurdr̥śam |
tatas te pratyabhijñāya arjunāya nyavedayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
pulastya iti taṁ śrutvā vacanaṁ haihayādhipaḥ |
śirasy añjalim uddhr̥tya pratyudgacchad dvijottamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
purohito ’sya gr̥hyārghyaṁ madhuparkaṁ tathaiva ca |
purastāt prayayau rājña indrasyeva br̥haspatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tatas tam r̥ṣim āyāntam udyantam iva bhāskaram |
arjuno dr̥śya saṁprāptaṁ vavandendra iveśvaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sa tasya madhuparkaṁ ca pādyam arghyaṁ ca dāpayan |
pulastyam āha rājendro harṣagadgadayā girā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
adyeyam amarāvatyā tulyā māhiṣmatī kr̥tā |
adyāhaṁ tu dvijendrendra yasmāt paśyāmi durdr̥śam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
adya me kuśalaṁ deva adya me kulam uddhr̥tam |
yat te devagaṇair vandyau vande ’haṁ caraṇāv imau ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
idaṁ rājyam ime putrā ime dārā ime vayam |
brahman kiṁ kurma kiṁ kāryam ājñāpayatu no bhavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
taṁ dharme ’gniṣu bhr̥tyeṣu śivaṁ pr̥ṣṭvātha pārthivam |
pulastyovāca rājānaṁ haihayānāṁ tadārjunam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
rājendrāmalapadmākṣapūrṇacandranibhānana |
atulaṁ te balaṁ yena daśagrīvas tvayā jitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
bhayād yasyāvatiṣṭhetāṁ niṣpandau sāgarānilau |
so ’yam adya tvayā baddhaḥ pautro me ’tīvadurjayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tat putraka yaśaḥ sphītaṁ nāma viśrāvitaṁ tvayā |
madvākyād yācyamāno ’dya muñca vatsa daśānanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
pulastyājñāṁ sa gr̥hyātha akiṁcanavaco ’rjunaḥ |
mumoca pārthivendrendro rākṣasendraṁ prahr̥ṣṭavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
sa taṁ pramuktvā tridaśārim arjunaḥ; prapūjya divyābharaṇasragambaraiḥ |
ahiṁsākaṁ sakhyam upetya sāgnikaṁ; praṇamya sa brahmasutaṁ gr̥haṁ yayau ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
pulastyenāpi saṁgamya rākṣasendraḥ pratāpavān |
pariṣvaṅgakr̥tātithyo lajjamāno visarjitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
pitāmahasutaś cāpi pulastyo munisattamaḥ |
mocayitvā daśagrīvaṁ brahmalokaṁ jagāma saḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
evaṁ sa rāvaṇaḥ prāptaḥ kārtavīryāt tu dharṣaṇāt |
pulastyavacanāc cāpi punar mokṣam avāptavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
evaṁ balibhyo balinaḥ santi rāghavanandana |
nāvajñā parataḥ kāryā ya icchec chreya ātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tataḥ sa rājā piśitāśanānāṁ; sahasrabāhor upalabhya maitrīm |
punar narāṇāṁ kadanaṁ cakāra; cacāra sarvāṁ pr̥thivīṁ ca darpāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
