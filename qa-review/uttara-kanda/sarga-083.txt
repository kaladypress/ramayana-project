=== Sarga 83 ===

---
1.
tat sarvam akhilenāśu prasthāpya bharatāgrajaḥ |
hayaṁ lakṣmaṇasaṁpannaṁ kr̥ṣṇasāraṁ mumoca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
r̥tvigbhir lakṣmaṇaṁ sārdham aśve ca viniyujya saḥ |
tato ’bhyagacchat kākutsthaḥ saha sainyena naimiṣam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
yajñavāṭaṁ mahābāhur dr̥ṣṭvā paramam adbhutam |
praharṣam atulaṁ lebhe śrīmān iti ca so ’bravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
naimiṣe vasatas tasya sarva eva narādhipāḥ |
ājagmuḥ sarvarāṣṭrebhyas tān rāmaḥ pratyapūjayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
upakāryān mahārhāṁś ca pārthivānāṁ mahātmanām |
sānugānāṁ naraśreṣṭho vyādideśa mahādyutiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
annapānāni vastrāṇi sānugānāṁ mahātmanām |
bharataḥ saṁdadāv āśu śatrughnasahitas tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
vānarāś ca mahātmānaḥ sugrīvasahitās tadā |
viprāṇāṁ praṇatāḥ sarve cakrire pariveṣaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
vibhīṣaṇaś ca rakṣobhiḥ sragvibhir bahubhir vr̥taḥ |
r̥ṣīṇām ugratapasāṁ kiṁkaraḥ paryupasthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
evaṁ suvihito yajño hayamedho ’bhyavartata |
lakṣmaṇenābhiguptā ca hayacaryā pravartitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
nānyaḥ śabdo ’bhavat tatra hayamedhe mahātmanaḥ |
chandato dehi visrabdho yāvat tuṣyanti yācakāḥ |
tāvad vānararakṣobhir dattam evābhyadr̥śyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
na kaś cin malinas tatra dīno vāpy atha vā kr̥śaḥ |
tasmin yajñavare rājño hr̥ṣṭapuṣṭajanāvr̥te ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
ye ca tatra mahātmāno munayaś cirajīvinaḥ |
nāsmaraṁs tādr̥śaṁ yajñaṁ dānaughasamalaṁkr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
rajatānāṁ suvarṇānāṁ ratnānām atha vāsasām |
aniśaṁ dīyamānānāṁ nāntaḥ samupadr̥śyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
na śakrasya na somasya yamasya varuṇasya vā |
īdr̥śo dr̥ṣṭapūrvo na evam ūcus tapodhanāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sarvatra vānarās tasthuḥ sarvatraiva ca rākṣasāḥ |
vāso dhanāni kāmibhyaḥ pūrṇahastā dadur bhr̥śam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
īdr̥śo rājasiṁhasya yajñaḥ sarvaguṇānvitaḥ |
saṁvatsaram atho sāgraṁ vartate na ca hīyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
