=== Sarga 11 ===

---
1.
sumālī varalabdhāṁs tu jñātvā tān vai niśācarān |
udatiṣṭhad bhayaṁ tyaktvā sānugaḥ sa rasātalāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
mārīcaś ca prahastaś ca virūpākṣo mahodaraḥ |
udatiṣṭhan susaṁrabdhāḥ sacivās tasya rakṣasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sumālī caiva taiḥ sarvair vr̥to rākṣasapuṁgavaiḥ |
abhigamya daśagrīvaṁ pariṣvajyedam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
diṣṭyā te putra saṁprāptaś cintito ’yaṁ manorathaḥ |
yas tvaṁ tribhuvaṇaśreṣṭhāl labdhavān varam īdr̥śam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
yatkr̥te ca vayaṁ laṅkāṁ tyaktvā yātā rasātalam |
tad gataṁ no mahābāho mahad viṣṇukr̥taṁ bhayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
asakr̥t tena bhagnā hi parityajya svam ālayam |
vidrutāḥ sahitāḥ sarve praviṣṭāḥ sma rasātalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
asmadīyā ca laṅkeyaṁ nagarī rākṣasoṣitā |
niveśitā tava bhrātrā dhanādhyakṣeṇa dhīmatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
yadi nāmātra śakyaṁ syāt sāmnā dānena vānagha |
tarasā vā mahābāho pratyānetuṁ kr̥taṁ bhavet ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tvaṁ ca laṅkeśvaras tāta bhaviṣyasi na saṁśayaḥ |
sarveṣāṁ naḥ prabhuś caiva bhaviṣyasi mahābala ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
athābravīd daśagrīvo mātāmaham upasthitam |
vitteśo gurur asmākaṁ nārhasy evaṁ prabhāṣitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
uktavantaṁ tathā vākyaṁ daśagrīvaṁ niśācaraḥ |
prahastaḥ praśritaṁ vākyam idam āha sakāraṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
daśagrīva mahābāho nārhas tvaṁ vaktum īdr̥śam |
saubhrātraṁ nāsti śūrāṇāṁ śr̥ṇu cedaṁ vaco mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
aditiś ca ditiś caiva bhaginyau sahite kila |
bhārye paramarūpiṇyau kaśyapasya prajāpateḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
aditir janayām āsa devāṁs tribhuvaṇeśvarān |
ditis tv ajanayad daityān kaśyapasyātmasaṁbhavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
daityānāṁ kila dharmajña pureyaṁ savanārṇavā |
saparvatā mahī vīra te ’bhavan prabhaviṣṇavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
nihatya tāṁs tu samare viṣṇunā prabhaviṣṇunā |
devānāṁ vaśam ānītaṁ trailokyam idam avyayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
naitad eko bhavān eva kariṣyati viparyayam |
surair ācaritaṁ pūrvaṁ kuruṣvaitad vaco mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
evam ukto daśagrīvaḥ prahastena durātmanā |
cintayitvā muhūrtaṁ vai bāḍham ity eva so ’bravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
sa tu tenaiva harṣeṇa tasminn ahani vīryavān |
vanaṁ gato daśagrīvaḥ saha taiḥ kṣaṇadācaraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
trikūṭasthaḥ sa tu tadā daśagrīvo niśācaraḥ |
preṣayām āsa dautyena prahastaṁ vākyakovidam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
prahasta śīghraṁ gatvā tvaṁ brūhi nairr̥tapuṁgavam |
vacanān mama vitteśaṁ sāmapūrvam idaṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
iyaṁ laṅkā purī rājan rākṣasānāṁ mahātmanām |
tvayā niveśitā saumya naitad yuktaṁ tavānagha ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tad bhavān yadi sāmnaitāṁ dadyād atulavikrama |
kr̥tā bhaven mama prītir dharmaś caivānupālitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
ity uktaḥ sa tadā gatvā prahasto vākyakovidaḥ |
daśagrīvavacaḥ sarvaṁ vitteśāya nyavedayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
prahastād api saṁśrutya devo vaiśravaṇo vacaḥ |
pratyuvāca prahastaṁ taṁ vākyaṁ vākyaviśāradaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
brūhi gaccha daśagrīvaṁ purī rājyaṁ ca yan mama |
tavāpy etan mahābāho bhuṅkṣvaitad dhatakaṇṭakam ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
sarvaṁ kartāsmi bhadraṁ te rākṣaseśa vaco ’cirāt |
kiṁ tu tāvat pratīkṣasva pitur yāvan nivedaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
evam uktvā dhanādhyakṣo jagāma pitur antikam |
abhivādya guruṁ prāha rāvaṇasya yadīpsitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
eṣa tāta daśagrīvo dūtaṁ preṣitavān mama |
dīyatāṁ nagarī laṅkā pūrvaṁ rakṣogaṇoṣitā |
mayātra yad anuṣṭheyaṁ tan mamācakṣva suvrata ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
brahmarṣis tv evam ukto ’sau viśravā munipuṁgavaḥ |
uvāca dhanadaṁ vākyaṁ śr̥ṇu putra vaco mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
daśagrīvo mahābāhur uktavān mama saṁnidhau |
mayā nirbhartsitaś cāsīd bahudhoktaḥ sudurmatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
sa krodhena mayā cokto dhvaṁsasveti punaḥ punaḥ |
śreyo’bhiyuktaṁ dharmyaṁ ca śr̥ṇu putra vaco mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
varapradānasaṁmūḍho mānyāmānyaṁ sudurmatiḥ |
na vetti mama śāpāc ca prakr̥tiṁ dāruṇāṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
tasmād gaccha mahābāho kailāsaṁ dharaṇīdharam |
niveśaya nivāsārthaṁ tyaja laṅkāṁ sahānugaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
tatra mandākinī ramyā nadīnāṁ pravarā nadī |
kāñcanaiḥ sūryasaṁkāśaiḥ paṅkajaiḥ saṁvr̥todakā ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
na hi kṣamaṁ tvayā tena vairaṁ dhanada rakṣasā |
jānīṣe hi yathānena labdhaḥ paramako varaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
evam ukto gr̥hītvā tu tad vacaḥ pitr̥gauravāt |
sadārapauraḥ sāmātyaḥ savāhanadhano gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
prahastas tu daśagrīvaṁ gatvā sarvaṁ nyavedayat |
śūnyā sā nagarī laṅkā triṁśadyojanam āyatā |
praviśya tāṁ sahāsmābhiḥ svadharmaṁ tatra pālaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
evam uktaḥ prahastena rāvaṇo rākṣasas tadā |
viveśa nagarīṁ laṅkāṁ sabhrātā sabalānugaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
sa cābhiṣiktaḥ kṣaṇadācarais tadā; niveśayām āsa purīṁ daśānanaḥ |
nikāmapūrṇā ca babhūva sā purī; niśācarair nīlabalāhakopamaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
dhaneśvaras tv atha pitr̥vākyagauravān; nyaveśayac chaśivimale girau purīm |
svalaṁkr̥tair bhavanavarair vibhūṣitāṁ; puraṁdarasyeva tadāmarāvatīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
