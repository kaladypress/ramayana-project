=== Sarga 30 ===

---
1.
jite mahendre ’tibale rāvaṇasya sutena vai |
prajāpatiṁ puraskr̥tya gatā laṅkāṁ surās tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
taṁ rāvaṇaṁ samāsādya putrabhrātr̥bhir āvr̥tam |
abravīd gagane tiṣṭhan sāntvapūrvaṁ prajāpatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
vatsa rāvaṇa tuṣṭo ’smi tava putrasya saṁyuge |
aho ’sya vikramaudāryaṁ tava tulyo ’dhiko ’pi vā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
jitaṁ hi bhavatā sarvaṁ trailokyaṁ svena tejasā |
kr̥tā pratijñā saphalā prīto ’smi svasutena vai ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
ayaṁ ca putro ’tibalas tava rāvaṇa rāvaṇiḥ |
indrajit tv iti vikhyāto jagaty eṣa bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
balavāñ śatrunirjetā bhaviṣyaty eṣa rākṣasaḥ |
yam āśritya tvayā rājan sthāpitās tridaśā vaśe ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tan mucyatāṁ mahābāho mahendraḥ pākaśāsanaḥ |
kiṁ cāsya mokṣaṇārthāya prayacchanti divaukasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
athābravīn mahātejā indrajit samitiṁjayaḥ |
amaratvam ahaṁ deva vr̥ṇomīhāsya mokṣaṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
abravīt tu tadā devo rāvaṇiṁ kamalodbhavaḥ |
nāsti sarvāmaratvaṁ hi keṣāṁ cit prāṇināṁ bhuvi ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
athābravīt sa tatrastham indrajit padmasaṁbhavam |
śrūyatāṁ yā bhavet siddhiḥ śatakratuvimokṣaṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
mameṣṭaṁ nityaśo deva havyaiḥ saṁpūjya pāvakam |
saṁgrāmam avatartuṁ vai śatrunirjayakāṅkṣiṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tasmiṁś ced asamāpte tu japyahome vibhāvasoḥ |
yudhyeyaṁ deva saṁgrāme tadā me syād vināśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sarvo hi tapasā caiva vr̥ṇoty amaratāṁ pumān |
vikrameṇa mayā tv etad amaratvaṁ pravartitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
evam astv iti taṁ prāha vākyaṁ devaḥ prajāpatiḥ |
muktaś cendrajitā śakro gatāś ca tridivaṁ surāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
etasminn antare śakro dīno bhraṣṭāmbarasrajaḥ |
rāma cintāparītātmā dhyānatatparatāṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
taṁ tu dr̥ṣṭvā tathābhūtaṁ prāha devaḥ prajāpatiḥ |
śatakrato kim utkaṇṭhāṁ karoṣi smara duṣkr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
amarendra mayā bahvyaḥ prajāḥ sr̥ṣṭāḥ purā prabho |
ekavarṇāḥ samābhāṣā ekarūpāś ca sarvaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tāsāṁ nāsti viśeṣo hi darśane lakṣaṇe ’pi vā |
tato ’ham ekāgramanās tāḥ prajāḥ paryacintayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
so ’haṁ tāsāṁ viśeṣārthaṁ striyam ekāṁ vinirmame |
yad yat prajānāṁ pratyaṅgaṁ viśiṣṭaṁ tat tad uddhr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tato mayā rūpaguṇair ahalyā strī vinirmitā |
ahalyety eva ca mayā tasyā nāma pravartitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
nirmitāyāṁ tu devendra tasyāṁ nāryāṁ surarṣabha |
bhaviṣyatīti kasyaiṣā mama cintā tato ’bhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tvaṁ tu śakra tadā nārīṁ jānīṣe manasā prabho |
sthānādhikatayā patnī mamaiṣeti puraṁdara ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
sā mayā nyāsabhūtā tu gautamasya mahātmanaḥ |
nyastā bahūni varṣāṇi tena niryātitā ca sā ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tatas tasya parijñāya mayā sthairyaṁ mahāmuneḥ |
jñātvā tapasi siddhiṁ ca patnyarthaṁ sparśitā tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
sa tayā saha dharmātmā ramate sma mahāmuniḥ |
āsan nirāśā devās tu gautame dattayā tayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tvaṁ kruddhas tv iha kāmātmā gatvā tasyāśramaṁ muneḥ |
dr̥ṣṭavāṁś ca tadā tāṁ strīṁ dīptām agniśikhām iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
sā tvayā dharṣitā śakra kāmārtena samanyunā |
dr̥ṣṭas tvaṁ ca tadā tena āśrame paramarṣiṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tataḥ kruddhena tenāsi śaptaḥ paramatejasā |
gato ’si yena devendra daśābhāgaviparyayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
yasmān me dharṣitā patnī tvayā vāsava nirbhayam |
tasmāt tvaṁ samare rājañ śatruhastaṁ gamiṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
ayaṁ tu bhāvo durbuddhe yas tvayeha pravartitaḥ |
mānuṣeṣv api sarveṣu bhaviṣyati na saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
tatrādharmaḥ subalavān samutthāsyati yo mahān |
tatrārdhaṁ tasya yaḥ kartā tvayy ardhaṁ nipatiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
na ca te sthāvaraṁ sthānaṁ bhaviṣyati puraṁdara |
etenādharmayogena yas tvayeha pravartitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
yaś ca yaś ca surendraḥ syād dhruvaḥ sa na bhaviṣyati |
eṣa śāpo mayā mukta ity asau tvāṁ tadābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
tāṁ tu bhāryāṁ vinirbhartsya so ’bravīt sumahātapāḥ |
durvinīte vinidhvaṁsa mamāśramasamīpataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
rūpayauvanasaṁpannā yasmāt tvam anavasthitā |
tasmād rūpavatī loke na tvam ekā bhaviṣyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
rūpaṁ ca tat prajāḥ sarvā gamiṣyanti sudurlabham |
yat tavedaṁ samāśritya vibhrame ’yam upasthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
tadā prabhr̥ti bhūyiṣṭhaṁ prajā rūpasamanvitāḥ |
śāpotsargād dhi tasyedaṁ muneḥ sarvam upāgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
tat smara tvaṁ mahābāho duṣkr̥taṁ yat tvayā kr̥tam |
yena tvaṁ grahaṇaṁ śatror gato nānyena vāsava ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
śīghraṁ yajasva yajñaṁ tvaṁ vaiṣṇavaṁ susamāhitaḥ |
pāvitas tena yajñena yāsyasi tridivaṁ tataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
putraś ca tava devendra na vinaṣṭo mahāraṇe |
nītaḥ saṁnihitaś caiva aryakeṇa mahodadhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
etac chrutvā mahendras tu yajñam iṣṭvā ca vaiṣṇavam |
punas tridivam ākrāmad anvaśāsac ca devatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
etad indrajito rāma balaṁ yat kīrtitaṁ mayā |
nirjitas tena devendraḥ prāṇino ’nye ca kiṁ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
