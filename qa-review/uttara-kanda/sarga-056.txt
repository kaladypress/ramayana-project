=== Sarga 56 ===

---
1.
evam uktvā tu kākutsthaṁ praśasya ca punaḥ punaḥ |
punar evāparaṁ vākyam uvāca raghunandanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
imāny aśvasahasrāṇi catvāri puruṣarṣabha |
rathānāṁ ca sahasre dve gajānāṁ śatam eva ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
antarāpaṇavīthyaś ca nānāpaṇyopaśobhitāḥ |
anugacchantu śatrughna tathaiva naṭanartakāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
hiraṇyasya suvarṇasya ayutaṁ puruṣarṣabha |
gr̥hītvā gaccha śatrughna paryāptadhanavāhanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
balaṁ ca subhr̥taṁ vīra hr̥ṣṭapuṣṭam anuttamam |
saṁbhāṣya saṁpradānena rañjayasva narottama ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
na hy arthās tatra tiṣṭhanti na dārā na ca bāndhavāḥ |
suprīto bhr̥tyavargas tu yatra tiṣṭhati rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
ato hr̥ṣṭajanākīrṇāṁ prasthāpya mahatīṁ camūm |
eka eva dhanuṣpānis tad gaccha tvaṁ madhor vanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
yathā tvāṁ na prajānāti gacchantaṁ yuddhakāṅkṣiṇam |
lavaṇas tu madhoḥ putras tathā gaccher aśaṅkitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
na tasya mr̥tyur anyo ’sti kaś cid dhi puruṣarṣabha |
darśanaṁ yo ’bhigaccheta sa vadhyo lavaṇena hi ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
sa grīṣme vyapayāte tu varṣarātra upasthite |
hanyās tvaṁ lavaṇaṁ saumya sa hi kālo ’sya durmateḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
maharṣīṁs tu puraskr̥tya prayāntu tava sainikāḥ |
yathā grīṣmāvaśeṣeṇa tareyur jāhnavījalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tataḥ sthāpya balaṁ sarvaṁ nadītīre samāhitaḥ |
agrato dhanuṣā sārdhaṁ gaccha tvaṁ laghuvikrama ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
evam uktas tu rāmeṇa śatrughnas tān mahābalān |
senāmukhyān samānīya tato vākyam uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
ete vo gaṇitā vāsā yatra yatra nivatsyatha |
sthātavyaṁ cāvirodhena yathā bādhā na kasya cit ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tathā tāṁs tu samājñāpya niryāpya ca mahad balam |
kausalyāṁ ca sumitrāṁ ca kaikeyīṁ cābhyavādayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
rāmaṁ pradakṣiṇaṁ kr̥tvā śirasābhipraṇamya ca |
rāmeṇa cābhyanujñātaḥ śatrughnaḥ śatrutāpanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
lakṣmaṇaṁ bharataṁ caiva praṇipatya kr̥tāñjaliḥ |
purodhasaṁ vasiṣṭhaṁ ca śatrughnaḥ prayatātmavān |
pradakṣiṇam atho kr̥tvā nirjagāma mahābalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
