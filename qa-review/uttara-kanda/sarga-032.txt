=== Sarga 32 ===

---
1.
narmadā puline yatra rākṣasendraḥ sa rāvaṇaḥ |
puṣpopahāraṁ kurute tasmād deśād adūrataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
arjuno jayatāṁ śreṣṭho māhiṣmatyāḥ patiḥ prabhuḥ |
krīḍite saha nārībhir narmadātoyam āśritaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tāsāṁ madhyagato rāja rarāja sa tato ’rjunaḥ |
kareṇūnāṁ sahasrasya madhyastha iva kuñjaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
jijñāsuḥ sa tu bāhūnāṁ sahasrasyottamaṁ balam |
rurodha narmadā vegaṁ bāhubhiḥ sa tadārjunaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
kārtavīryabhujāsetuṁ taj jalaṁ prāpya nirmalam |
kūlāpahāraṁ kurvāṇaṁ pratisrotaḥ pradhāvati ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
samīnanakramakaraḥ sapuṣpakuśasaṁstaraḥ |
sa narmadāmbhaso vegaḥ prāvr̥ṭkāla ivābabhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sa vegaḥ kārtavīryeṇa saṁpreṣiṭa ivāmbhasaḥ |
puṣpopahāraṁ tat sarvaṁ rāvaṇasya jahāra ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
rāvaṇo ’rdhasamāptaṁ tu utsr̥jya niyamaṁ tadā |
narmadāṁ paśyate kāntāṁ pratikūlāṁ yathā priyām ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
paścimena tu taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā sāgarodgārasaṁnibham |
vardhantam ambhaso vegaṁ pūrvām āśāṁ praviśya tu ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tato ’nudbhrāntaśakunāṁ svābhāvye parame sthitām |
nirvikārāṅganābhāsāṁ paśyate rāvaṇo nadīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
savyetarakarāṅgulyā saśabdaṁ ca daśānanaḥ |
vegaprabhavam anveṣṭuṁ so ’diśac chukasāraṇau ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tau tu rāvaṇasaṁdiṣṭau bhrātarau śukasāraṇau |
vyomāntaracarau vīrau prasthitau paścimonmukhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
ardhayojanamātraṁ tu gatvā tau tu niśācarau |
paśyetāṁ puruṣaṁ toye krīḍantaṁ sahayoṣitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
br̥hatsālapratīkaśaṁ toyavyākulamūrdhajam |
madaraktāntanayanaṁ madanākāravarcasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
nadīṁ bāhusahasreṇa rundhantam arimardanam |
giriṁ pādasahasreṇa rundhantam iva medinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
bālānāṁ varanārīṇāṁ sahasreṇābhisaṁvr̥tam |
samadānāṁ kareṇūnāṁ sahasreṇeva kuñjaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tam adbhutatamaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rākṣasau śukasāraṇau |
saṁnivr̥ttāv upāgamya rāvaṇaṁ tam athocatuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
br̥hatsālapratīkāśaḥ ko ’py asau rākṣaseśvara |
narmadāṁ rodhavad ruddhvā krīḍāpayati yoṣitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tena bāhusahasreṇa saṁniruddhajalā nadī |
sāgarodgārasaṁkāśān udgārān sr̥jate muhuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
ity evaṁ bhāṣamāṇau tau niśamya śukasāraṇau |
rāvaṇo ’rjuna ity uktvā uttasthau yuddhalālasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
arjunābhimukhe tasmin prasthite rākṣaseśvare |
sakr̥d eva kr̥to rāvaḥ saraktaḥ preṣito ghanaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
mahodaramahāpārśvadhūmrākṣaśukasāraṇaiḥ |
saṁvr̥to rākṣasendras tu tatrāgād yatra so ’rjunaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
nātidīrgheṇa kālena sa tato rākṣaso balī |
taṁ narmadā hradaṁ bhīmam ājagāmāñjanaprabhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
sa tatra strīparivr̥taṁ vāśitābhir iva dvipam |
narendraṁ paśyate rājā rākṣasānāṁ tadārjunam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
sa roṣād raktanayano rākṣasendro baloddhataḥ |
ity evam arjunāmātyān āha gambhīrayā girā ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
amātyāḥ kṣipram ākhyāta haihayasya nr̥pasya vai |
yuddhārthaṁ samanuprāpto rāvaṇo nāma nāmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
rāvaṇasya vacaḥ śrutvā mantriṇo ’thārjunasya te |
uttasthuḥ sāyudhās taṁ ca rāvaṇaṁ vākyam abruvan ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
yuddhasya kālo vijñātaḥ sādhu bhoḥ sādhu rāvaṇa |
yaḥ kṣībaṁ strīvr̥taṁ caiva yoddhum icchasi no nr̥pam |
vāśitāmadhyagaṁ mattaṁ śārdūla iva kuñjaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
kṣamasvādya daśagrīva uṣyatāṁ rajanī tvayā |
yuddhaśraddhā tu yady asti śvas tāta samare ’rjunam ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
yadi vāpi tvarā tubhyaṁ yuddhatr̥ṣṇāsamāvr̥tā |
nihatyāsmāṁs tato yuddham arjunenopayāsyasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
tatas te rāvaṇāmātyair amātyāḥ pārthivasya tu |
sūditāś cāpi te yuddhe bhakṣitāś ca bubhukṣitaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
tato halahalāśabdo narmadā tira ābabhau |
arjunasyānuyātrāṇāṁ rāvaṇasya ca mantriṇām ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
iṣubhis tomaraiḥ śūlair vajrakalpaiḥ sakarṣaṇaiḥ |
sarāvaṇān ardayantaḥ samantāt samabhidrutāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
haihayādhipayodhānāṁ vega āsīt sudāruṇaḥ |
sanakramīnamakarasamudrasyeva nisvanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
rāvaṇasya tu te ’mātyāḥ prahastaśukasāraṇāḥ |
kārtavīryabalaṁ kruddhā nirdahanty agnitejasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
arjunāya tu tat karma rāvaṇasya samantriṇaḥ |
krīḍamānāya kathitaṁ puruṣair dvārarakṣibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
uktvā na bhetavyam iti strījanaṁ sa tato ’rjunaḥ |
uttatāra jalāt tasmād gaṅgātoyād ivāñjanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
krodhadūṣitanetras tu sa tato ’rjuna pāvakaḥ |
prajajvāla mahāghoro yugānta iva pāvakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
sa tūrṇataram ādāya varahemāṅgado gadām |
abhidravati rakṣāṁsi tamāṁsīva divākaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
bāhuvikṣepakaraṇāṁ samudyamya mahāgadām |
gāruḍaṁ vegam āsthāya āpapātaiva so ’rjunaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
tasya margaṁ samāvr̥tya vindhyo ’rkasyeva parvataḥ |
sthito vindhya ivākampyaḥ prahasto musalāyudhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
tato ’sya musalaṁ ghoraṁ lohabaddhaṁ madoddhataḥ |
prahastaḥ preṣayan kruddho rarāsa ca yathāmbudaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
tasyāgre musalasyāgnir aśokāpīḍasaṁnibhaḥ |
prahastakaramuktasya babhūva pradahann iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
ādhāvamānaṁ musalaṁ kārtavīryas tadārjunaḥ |
nipuṇaṁ vañcayām āsa sagado gajavikramaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
tatas tam abhidudrāva prahastaṁ haihayādhipaḥ |
bhrāmayāṇo gadāṁ gurvīṁ pañcabāhuśatocchrayām ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
tenāhato ’tivegena prahasto gadayā tadā |
nipapāta sthitaḥ śailo vajrivajrahato yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
prahastaṁ patitaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā mārīcaśukasāraṇāḥ |
samahodaradhūmrākṣā apasr̥ptā raṇājirāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
apakrānteṣv amātyeṣu prahaste ca nipātite |
rāvaṇo ’bhyadravat tūrṇam arjunaṁ nr̥pasattamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
sahasrabāhos tad yuddhaṁ viṁśadbāhoś ca dāruṇam |
nr̥parākṣasayos tatra ārabdhaṁ lomaharṣaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
sāgarāv iva saṁkṣubdhau calamūlāv ivācalau |
tejoyuktāv ivādityau pradahantāv ivānalau ||

=== meaning ===

---
51.
baloddhatau yathā nāgau vāśitārthe yathā vr̥ṣau |
meghāv iva vinardantau siṁhāv iva balotkaṭau ||

=== meaning ===

---
52.
rudrakālāv iva kruddhau tau tathā rākṣasārjunau |
parasparaṁ gadābhyāṁ tau tāḍayām āsatur bhr̥śam ||

=== meaning ===

---
53.
vajraprahārān acalā yathā ghorān viṣehire |
gadāprahārāṁs tadvat tau sahete nararākṣasau ||

=== meaning ===

---
54.
yathāśaniravebhyas tu jāyate vai pratiśrutiḥ |
tathā tābhyāṁ gadāpātair diśaḥ sarvāḥ pratiśrutāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
55.
arjunasya gadā sā tu pātyamānāhitorasi |
kāñcanābhaṁ nabhaś cakre vidyutsaudāmanī yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
56.
tathaiva rāvaṇenāpi pātyamānā muhur muhuḥ |
arjunorasi nirbhāti gadolkeva mahāgirau ||

=== meaning ===

---
57.
nārjunaḥ khedam āpnoti na rākṣasagaṇeśvaraḥ |
samam āsīt tayor yuddhaṁ yathā pūrvaṁ balīndrayoḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
58.
śr̥ṅgair maharṣabhau yadvad dantāgrair iva kuñjarau |
parasparaṁ vinighnantau nararākṣasasattamau ||

=== meaning ===

---
59.
tato ’rjunena kruddhena sarvaprāṇena sā gadā |
stanayor antare muktā rāvaṇasya mahāhave ||

=== meaning ===

---
60.
varadānakr̥tatrāṇe sā gadā rāvaṇorasi |
durbaleva yathā senā dvidhābhūtāpatat kṣitau ||

=== meaning ===

---
61.
sa tv arjunapramuktena gadāpātena rāvaṇaḥ |
apāsarpad dhanurmātraṁ niṣasāda ca niṣṭanan ||

=== meaning ===

---
62.
sa vihvalaṁ tad ālakṣya daśagrīvaṁ tato ’rjunaḥ |
sahasā pratijagrāha garutmān iva pannagam ||

=== meaning ===

---
63.
sa taṁ bāhusahasreṇa balād gr̥hya daśānanam |
babandha balavān rājā baliṁ nārāyaṇo yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
64.
badhyamāne daśagrīve siddhacāraṇadevatāḥ |
sādhvīti vādinaḥ puṣpaiḥ kiranty arjunamūrdhani ||

=== meaning ===

---
65.
vyāghro mr̥gam ivādāya siṁharāḍ iva dantinam |
rarāsa haihayo rājā harṣād ambudavan muhuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
66.
prahastas tu samāśvasto dr̥ṣṭvā baddhaṁ daśānanam |
saha tai rākasaiḥ kruddha abhidudrāva pārthivam ||

=== meaning ===

---
67.
naktaṁcarāṇāṁ vegas tu teṣām āpatatāṁ babhau |
uddhr̥ta ātapāpāye samudrāṇām ivādbhutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
68.
muñca muñceti bhāṣantas tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti cāsakr̥t |
musalāni ca śūlāni utsasarjus tadārjune ||

=== meaning ===

---
69.
aprāptāny eva tāny āśu asaṁbhrāntas tadārjunaḥ |
āyudhāny amarārīṇāṁ jagrāha ripusūdanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
70.
tatas tair eva rakṣāṁsi durdharaiḥ pravarāyudhaiḥ |
bhittvā vidrāvayām āsa vāyur ambudharān iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
71.
rākṣasāṁs trāsayitvā tu kārtavīryārjunas tadā |
rāvaṇaṁ gr̥hya nagaraṁ praviveśa suhr̥dvr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
72.
sa kīryamāṇaḥ kusumākṣatotkarair; dvijaiḥ sapauraiḥ puruhūtasaṁnibhaḥ |
tadārjunaḥ saṁpraviveśa tāṁ purīṁ; baliṁ nigr̥hyaiva sahasralocanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
