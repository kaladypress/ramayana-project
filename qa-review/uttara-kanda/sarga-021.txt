=== Sarga 21 ===

---
1.
evaṁ saṁcintya viprendro jagāma laghuvikramaḥ |
ākhyātuṁ tad yathāvr̥ttaṁ yamasya sadanaṁ prati ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
apaśyat sa yamaṁ tatra devam agnipuraskr̥tam |
vidhānam upatiṣṭhantaṁ prāṇino yasya yādr̥śam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sa tu dr̥ṣṭvā yamaḥ prāptaṁ maharṣiṁ tatra nāradam |
abravīt sukham āsīnam arghyam āvedya dharmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
kaccit kṣemaṁ nu devarṣe kaccid dharmo na naśyati |
kim āgamanakr̥tyaṁ te devagandharvasevita ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
abravīt tu tadā vākyaṁ nārado bhagavān r̥ṣiḥ |
śrūyatām abhidhāsyāmi vidhānaṁ ca vidhīyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
eṣa nāmnā daśagrīvaḥ pitr̥rāja niśācaraḥ |
upayāti vaśaṁ netuṁ vikramais tvāṁ sudurjayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
etena kāraṇenāhaṁ tvarito ’smy āgataḥ prabho |
daṇḍapraharaṇasyādya tava kiṁ nu kariṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
etasminn antare dūrād aṁśumantam ivoditam |
dadr̥śe divyam āyāntaṁ vimānaṁ tasya rakṣasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
taṁ deśaṁ prabhayā tasya puṣpakasya mahābalaḥ |
kr̥tvā vitimiraṁ sarvaṁ samīpaṁ samavartata ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
sa tv apaśyan mahābāhur daśagrīvas tatas tataḥ |
prāṇinaḥ sukr̥taṁ karma bhuñjānāṁś caiva duṣkr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tatas tān vadhyamānāṁs tu karmabhir duṣkr̥taiḥ svakaiḥ |
rāvaṇo mocayām āsa vikrameṇa balād balī ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
preteṣu mucyamāneṣu rākṣasena balīyasā |
pretagopāḥ susaṁrabdhā rākṣasendram abhidravan ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
te prāsaiḥ parighaiḥ śūlair mudgaraiḥ śaktitomaraiḥ |
puṣpakaṁ samavarṣanta śūrāḥ śatasahasraśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tasyāsanāni prāsādān vedikāstaraṇāni ca |
puṣpakasya babhañjus te śīghraṁ madhukarā iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
devaniṣṭhānabhūtaṁ tad vimānaṁ puṣpakaṁ mr̥dhe |
bhajyamānaṁ tathaivāsīd akṣayaṁ brahmatejasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tatas te rāvaṇāmātyā yathākāmaṁ yathābalam |
ayudhyanta mahāvīryāḥ sa ca rājā daśānanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
te tu śoṇitadigdhāṅgāḥ sarvaśastrasamāhatāḥ |
amātyā rākṣasendrasya cakrur āyodhanaṁ mahat ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
anyonyaṁ ca mahābhāgā jaghnuḥ praharaṇair yudhi |
yamasya ca mahat sainyaṁ rākṣasasya ca mantriṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
amātyāṁs tāṁs tu saṁtyajya rākṣasasya mahaujasaḥ |
tam eva samadhāvanta śūlavarṣair daśānanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tataḥ śoṇitadigdhāṅgaḥ prahārair jarjarīkr̥taḥ |
vimāne rākṣasaśreṣṭhaḥ phullāśoka ivābabhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
sa śūlāni gadāḥ prāsāñ śaktitomarasāyakān |
musalāni śilāvr̥kṣān mumocāstrabalād balī ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tāṁs tu sarvān samākṣipya tad astram apahatya ca |
jaghnus te rākṣasaṁ ghoram ekaṁ śatasahasrakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
parivārya ca taṁ sarve śailaṁ meghotkarā iva |
bhindipālaiś ca śūlaiś ca nirucchvāsam akārayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
vimuktakavacaḥ kruddho siktaḥ śoṇitavisravaiḥ |
sa puṣpakaṁ parityajya pr̥thivyām avatiṣṭhata ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tataḥ sa kārmukī bāṇī pr̥thivyāṁ rākṣasādhipaḥ |
labdhasaṁjño muhūrtena kruddhas tasthau yathāntakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tataḥ pāśupataṁ divyam astraṁ saṁdhāya kārmuke |
tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti tān uktvā tac cāpaṁ vyapakarṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
jvālāmālī sa tu śaraḥ kravyādānugato raṇe |
mukto gulmān drumāṁś caiva bhasmakr̥tvā pradhāvati ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
te tasya tejasā dagdhāḥ sainyā vaivasvatasya tu |
raṇe tasmin nipatitā dāvadagdhā nagā iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
tataḥ sa sacivaiḥ sārdhaṁ rākṣaso bhīmavikramaḥ |
nanāda sumahānādaṁ kampayann iva medinīm ||

=== meaning ===

---
