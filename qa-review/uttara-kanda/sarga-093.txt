=== Sarga 93 ===

---
1.
kasya cit tv atha kālasya rāme dharmapathe sthite |
kālas tāpasarūpeṇa rājadvāram upāgamat ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
so ’bravīl lakṣmaṇaṁ vākyaṁ dhr̥timantaṁ yaśasvinam |
māṁ nivedaya rāmāya saṁprāptaṁ kāryagauravāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
dūto hy atibalasyāhaṁ maharṣer amitaujasaḥ |
rāmaṁ didr̥kṣur āyātaḥ kāryeṇa hi mahābala ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā saumitris tvarayānvitaḥ |
nyavedayata rāmāya tāpasasya vivakṣitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
jayasva rājan dharmeṇa ubhau lokau mahādyute |
dūtas tvāṁ draṣṭum āyātas tapasvī bhāskaraprabhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tad vākyaṁ lakṣmaṇenoktaṁ śrutvā rāma uvāca ha |
praveśyatāṁ munis tāta mahaujās tasya vākyadhr̥k ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
saumitris tu tathety uktvā prāveśayata taṁ munim |
jvalantam iva tejobhiḥ pradahantam ivāṁśubhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
so ’bhigamya raghuśreṣṭhaṁ dīpyamānaṁ svatejasā |
r̥ṣir madhurayā vācā vardhasvety āha rāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tasmai rāmo mahātejāḥ pūjām arghyapurogamām |
dadau kuśalam avyagraṁ praṣṭuṁ caivopacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
pr̥ṣṭaś ca kuśalaṁ tena rāmeṇa vadatāṁ varaḥ |
āsane kāñcane divye niṣasāda mahāyaśāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tam uvāca tato rāmaḥ svāgataṁ te mahāmune |
prāpayasva ca vākyāni yato dūtas tvam āgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
codito rājasiṁhena munir vākyam udīrayat |
dvandvam etat pravaktavyaṁ na ca cakṣur hataṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
yaḥ śr̥ṇoti nirīkṣed vā sa vadhyas tava rāghava |
bhaved vai munimukhyasya vacanaṁ yady avekṣase ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tatheti ca pratijñāya rāmo lakṣmaṇam abravīt |
dvāri tiṣṭha mahābāho pratihāraṁ visarjaya ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
sa me vadhyaḥ khalu bhavet kathāṁ dvandvasamīritām |
r̥ṣer mama ca saumitre paśyed vā śr̥ṇuyāc ca yaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tato nikṣipya kākutstho lakṣmaṇaṁ dvārasaṁgrahe |
tam uvāca muniṁ vākyaṁ kathayasveti rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
yat te manīṣitaṁ vākyaṁ yena vāsi samāhitaḥ |
kathayasva viśaṅkas tvaṁ mamāpi hr̥di vartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
