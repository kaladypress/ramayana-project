=== Sarga 87 ===

---
1.
tasyāṁ rajanyāṁ vyuṣṭāyāṁ yajñavāṭagato nr̥paḥ |
r̥ṣīn sarvān mahātejāḥ śabdāpayati rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
vasiṣṭho vāmadevaś ca jābālir atha kāśyapaḥ |
viśvāmitro dīrghatapā durvāsāś ca mahātapāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
agastyo ’tha tathāśaktir bhārgavaś caiva vāmanaḥ |
mārkaṇḍeyaś ca dīrghāyur maudgalyaś ca mahātapāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
bhārgavaś cyavanaś caiva śatānandaś ca dharmavit |
bharadvājaś ca tejasvī agniputraś ca suprabhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
ete cānye ca munayo bahavaḥ saṁśitavratāḥ |
rājānaś ca naravyāghrāḥ sarva eva samāgatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
rākṣasāś ca mahāvīryā vānarāś ca mahābalāḥ |
samājagmur mahātmānaḥ sarva eva kutūhalāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
kṣatriyāś caiva vaiśyāś ca śūdrāś caiva sahasraśaḥ |
sītāśapathavīkṣārthaṁ sarva eva samāgatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tathā samāgataṁ sarvam aśvabhūtam ivācalam |
śrutvā munivaras tūrṇaṁ sasītaḥ samupāgamat ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tam r̥ṣiṁ pr̥ṣṭhataḥ sītā sānvagacchad avāṅmukhī |
kr̥tāñjalir bāṣpagalā kr̥tvā rāmaṁ manogatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā śrīm ivāyāntīṁ brahmāṇam anugāminīm |
vālmīkeḥ pr̥ṣṭhataḥ sītāṁ sādhukāro mahān abhūt ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tato halahalāśabdaḥ sarveṣām evam ābabhau |
duḥkhajena viśālena śokenākulitātmanām ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sādhu sīteti ke cit tu sādhu rāmeti cāpare |
ubhāv eva tu tatrānye sādhu sādhv iti cābruvan ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tato madhyaṁ janaughānāṁ praviśya munipuṁgavaḥ |
sītāsahāyo vālmīkir iti hovāca rāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
iyaṁ dāśarathe sītā suvratā dharmacāriṇī |
apāpā te parityaktā mamāśramasamīpataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
lokāpavādabhītasya tava rāma mahāvrata |
pratyayaṁ dāsyate sītā tām anujñātum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
imau ca jānakīputrāv ubhau ca yamajātakau |
sutau tavaiva durdharṣau satyam etad bravīmi te ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
pracetaso ’haṁ daśamaḥ putro rāghavanandana |
na smarāmy anr̥taṁ vākyaṁ tathemau tava putrakau ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
bahuvarṣasahasrāṇi tapaścaryā mayā kr̥tā |
tasyāḥ phalam upāśnīyām apāpā maithilī yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
ahaṁ pañcasu bhūteṣu manaḥṣaṣṭheṣu rāghava |
vicintya sītāṁ śuddheti nyagr̥hṇāṁ vananirjhare ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
iyaṁ śuddhasamācārā apāpā patidevatā |
lokāpavādabhītasya dāsyati pratyayaṁ tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
