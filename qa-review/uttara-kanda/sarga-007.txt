=== Sarga 7 ===

---
1.
nārāyaṇagiriṁ te tu garjanto rākṣasāmbudāḥ |
avarṣann iṣuvarṣeṇa varṣeṇādrim ivāmbudāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
śyāmāvadātas tair viṣṇur nīlair naktaṁcarottamaiḥ |
vr̥to ’ñjanagirīvāsīd varṣamāṇaiḥ payodharaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
śalabhā iva kedāraṁ maśakā iva parvatam |
yathāmr̥taghaṭaṁ jīvā makarā iva cārṇavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tathā rakṣodhanur muktā vajrānilamanojavāḥ |
hariṁ viśanti sma śarā lokāstam iva paryaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
syandanaiḥ syandanagatā gajaiś ca gajadhūr gatāḥ |
aśvārohāḥ sadaśvaiś ca pādātāś cāmbare carāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
rākṣasendrā girinibhāḥ śaraśaktyr̥ṣṭitomaraiḥ |
nirucchvāsaṁ hariṁ cakruḥ prāṇāyāma iva dvijam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
niśācarais tudyamāno mīnair iva mahātimiḥ |
śārṅgam āyamya gātrāṇi rākṣasānāṁ mahāhave ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
śaraiḥ pūrṇāyatotsr̥ṣṭair vajravaktrair manojavaiḥ |
ciccheda tilaśo viṣṇuḥ śataśo ’tha sahasraśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
vidrāvya śaravarṣaṁ taṁ varṣaṁ vāyur ivotthitam |
pāñcajanyaṁ mahāśaṅkhaṁ pradadhmau puruṣottamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
so ’mbujo hariṇā dhmātaḥ sarvaprāṇena śaṅkharāṭ |
rarāsa bhīmanihrādo yugānte jalado yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
śaṅkharājaravaḥ so ’tha trāsayām āsa rākṣasān |
mr̥garāja ivāraṇye samadān iva kuñjarān ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
na śekur aśvāḥ saṁsthātuṁ vimadāḥ kuñjarābhavan |
syandanebhyaś cyutā yodhāḥ śaṅkharāvitadurbalāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
śārṅgacāpavinirmuktā vajratulyānanāḥ śarāḥ |
vidārya tāni rakṣāṁsi supuṅkhā viviśuḥ kṣitim ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
bhidyamānāḥ śaraiś cānye nārāyaṇadhanuścyutaiḥ |
nipetū rākṣasā bhīmāḥ śailā vajrahatā iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
vraṇair vraṇakarārīṇām adhokṣajaśarodbhavaiḥ |
asr̥k kṣaranti dhārābhiḥ svarṇadhārām ivācalāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
śaṅkharājaravaś cāpi śārṅgacāparavas tathā |
rākṣasānāṁ ravāṁś cāpi grasate vaiṣṇavo ravaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
sūryād iva karā ghorā ūrmayaḥ sāgarād iva |
parvatād iva nāgendrā vāryoghā iva cāmbudāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tathā bāṇā vinirmuktāḥ śārṅgān narāyaṇeritāḥ |
nirdhāvantīṣavas tūrṇaṁ śataśo ’tha sahasraśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
śarabheṇa yathā siṁhāḥ siṁhena dviradā yathā |
dviradena yathā vyāghrā vyāghreṇa dvīpino yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
dvīpinā ca yathā śvānaḥ śunā mārjārakā yathā |
mārjāreṇa yathā sarpāḥ sarpeṇa ca yathākhavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tathā te rākṣasā yuddhe viṣṇunā prabhaviṣṇunā |
dravanti drāvitāś caiva śāyitāś ca mahītale ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
rākṣasānāṁ sahasrāṇi nihatya madhusūdanaḥ |
vārijaṁ nādayām āsa toyadaṁ surarāḍ iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
nārāyaṇaśaragrastaṁ śaṅkhanādasuvihvalam |
yayau laṅkām abhimukhaṁ prabhagnaṁ rākṣasaṁ balam ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
prabhagne rākṣasabale nārāyaṇaśarāhate |
sumālī śaravarṣeṇa āvavāra raṇe harim ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
utkṣipya hemābharaṇaṁ karaṁ karam iva dvipaḥ |
rarāsa rākṣaso harṣāt sataḍit toyado yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
sumāler nardatas tasya śiro jvalitakuṇḍalam |
ciccheda yantur aśvāś ca bhrāntās tasya tu rakṣasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tair aśvair bhrāmyate bhrāntaiḥ sumālī rākṣaseśvaraḥ |
indriyāśvair yathā bhrāntair dhr̥tihīno yathā naraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
mālī cābhyadravad yuddhe pragr̥hya saśaraṁ dhanuḥ |
māler dhanuścyutā bāṇāḥ kārtasvaravibhūṣitāḥ |
viviśur harim āsādya krauñcaṁ patrarathā iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
ardyamānaḥ śaraiḥ so ’tha mālimuktaiḥ sahasraśaḥ |
cukṣubhe na raṇe viṣṇur jitendriya ivādhibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
atha maurvī svanaṁ kr̥tvā bhagavān bhūtabhāvanaḥ |
mālinaṁ prati bāṇaughān sasarjāsigadādharaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
te mālideham āsādya vajravidyutprabhāḥ śarāḥ |
pibanti rudhiraṁ tasya nāgā iva purāmr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
mālinaṁ vimukhaṁ kr̥tvā mālimauliṁ harir balāt |
rathaṁ ca sadhvajaṁ cāpaṁ vājinaś ca nyapātayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
virathas tu gadāṁ gr̥hya mālī naktaṁcarottamaḥ |
āpupluve gadāpāṇir giryagrād iva keṣarī ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
sa tayā garuḍaṁ saṁkhye īśānam iva cāntakaḥ |
lalāṭadeśe ’bhyahanad vajreṇendro yathācalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
gadayābhihatas tena mālinā garuḍo bhr̥śam |
raṇāt parāṅmukhaṁ devaṁ kr̥tavān vedanāturaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
parāṅmukhe kr̥te deve mālinā garuḍena vai |
udatiṣṭhan mahānādo rakṣasām abhinardatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
rakṣasāṁ nadatāṁ nādaṁ śrutvā harihayānujaḥ |
parāṅmukho ’py utsasarja cakraṁ mālijighāṁsayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
tat sūryamaṇḍalābhāsaṁ svabhāsā bhāsayan nabhaḥ |
kālacakranibhaṁ cakraṁ māleḥ śīrṣam apātayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
tacchiro rākṣasendrasya cakrotkr̥ttaṁ vibhīṣaṇam |
papāta rudhirodgāri purā rāhuśiro yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
tataḥ suraiḥ susaṁhr̥ṣṭaiḥ sarvaprāṇasamīritaḥ |
siṁhanādaravo muktaḥ sādhu deveti vādibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
mālinaṁ nihataṁ dr̥ṣṭvā sumālī malyavān api |
sabalau śokasaṁtaptau laṅkāṁ prati vidhāvitau ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
garuḍas tu samāśvastaḥ saṁnivr̥tya mahāmanāḥ |
rākṣasān drāvayām āsa pakṣavātena kopitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
nārāyaṇo ’pīṣuvarāśanībhir; vidārayām āsa dhanuḥpramuktaiḥ |
naktaṁcarān muktavidhūtakeśān; yathāśanībhiḥ sataḍinmahendraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
bhinnātapatraṁ patamānaśastraṁ; śarair apadhvastaviśīrṇadeham |
viniḥsr̥tāntraṁ bhayalolanetraṁ; balaṁ tad unmattanibhaṁ babhūva ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
siṁhārditānām iva kuñjarāṇāṁ; niśācarāṇāṁ saha kuñjarāṇām |
ravāś ca vegāś ca samaṁ babhūvuḥ; purāṇasiṁhena vimarditānām ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
saṁchādyamānā haribāṇajālaiḥ; svabāṇajālāni samutsr̥jantaḥ |
dhāvanti naktaṁcarakālameghā; vāyupraṇunnā iva kālameghāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
cakraprahārair vinikr̥ttaśīrṣāḥ; saṁcūrṇitāṅgāś ca gadāprahāraiḥ |
asiprahārair bahudhā vibhaktāḥ; patanti śailā iva rākṣasendrāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
cakrakr̥ttāsyakamalā gadāsaṁcūrṇitorasaḥ |
lāṅgalaglapitagrīvā musalair bhinnamastakāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
ke cic caivāsinā chinnās tathānye śaratāḍitāḥ |
nipetur ambarāt tūrṇaṁ rākṣasāḥ sāgarāmbhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
tadāmbaraṁ vigalitahārakuṇḍalair; niśācarair nīlabalāhakopamaiḥ |
nipātyamānair dadr̥śe nirantaraṁ; nipātyamānair iva nīlaparvataiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
