=== Sarga 4 ===

---
1.
śrutvāgastyeritaṁ vākyaṁ rāmo vismayam āgataḥ |
pūrvam āsīt tu laṅkāyāṁ rakṣasām iti saṁbhavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tataḥ śiraḥ kampayitvā tretāgnisamavigraham |
agastyaṁ taṁ muhur dr̥ṣṭvā smayamāno ’bhyabhāṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
bhagavan pūrvam apy eṣā laṅkāsīt piśitāśinām |
itīdaṁ bhavataḥ śrutvā vismayo janito mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
pulastyavaṁśād udbhūtā rākṣasā iti naḥ śrutam |
idānīm anyataś cāpi saṁbhavaḥ kīrtitas tvayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
rāvaṇāt kumbhakarṇāc ca prahastād vikaṭād api |
rāvaṇasya ca putrebhyaḥ kiṁ nu te balavattarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
ka eṣāṁ pūrvako brahman kiṁnāmā kiṁtapobalaḥ |
aparādhaṁ ca kaṁ prāpya viṣṇunā drāvitāḥ purā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
etad vistarataḥ sarvaṁ kathayasva mamānagha |
kautūhalaṁ kr̥taṁ mahyaṁ nuda bhānur yathā tamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
rāghavasya tu tac chrutvā saṁskārālaṁkr̥taṁ vacaḥ |
īṣadvismayamānas tam agastyaḥ prāha rāghavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
prajāpatiḥ purā sr̥ṣṭvā apaḥ salilasaṁbhavaḥ |
tāsāṁ gopāyane sattvān asr̥jat padmasaṁbhavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
te sattvāḥ sattvakartāraṁ vinītavad upasthitāḥ |
kiṁ kurma iti bhāṣantaḥ kṣutpipāsābhayārditāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
prajāpatis tu tāny āha sattvāni prahasann iva |
ābhāṣya vācā yatnena rakṣadhvam iti mānadaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
rakṣāma iti tatrānyair yakṣāmeti tathāparaiḥ |
bhuṅkṣitābhuṅkṣitair uktas tatas tān āha bhūtakr̥t ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
rakṣāma iti yair uktaṁ rākṣasās te bhavantu vaḥ |
yakṣāma iti yair uktaṁ te vai yakṣā bhavantu vaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tatra hetiḥ prahetiś ca bhrātarau rākṣasarṣabhau |
madhukaiṭabhasaṁkāśau babhūvatur ariṁdamau ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
prahetir dhārmikas tatra na dārān so ’bhikāṅkṣati |
hetir dārakriyārthaṁ tu yatnaṁ param athākarot ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sa kālabhaginīṁ kanyāṁ bhayāṁ nāma bhayāvahām |
udāvahad ameyātmā svayam eva mahāmatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
sa tasyāṁ janayām āsa hetī rākṣasapuṁgavaḥ |
putraṁ putravatāṁ śreṣṭho vidyutkeśa iti śrutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
vidyutkeśo hetiputraḥ pradīptāgnisamaprabhaḥ |
vyavardhata mahātejās toyamadhya ivāmbujam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
sa yadā yauvanaṁ bhadram anuprāpto niśācaraḥ |
tato dārakriyāṁ tasya kartuṁ vyavasitaḥ pitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
saṁdhyāduhitaraṁ so ’tha saṁdhyātulyāṁ prabhāvataḥ |
varayām āsa putrārthaṁ hetī rākṣasapuṁgavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
avaśyam eva dātavyā parasmai seti saṁdhyayā |
cintayitvā sutā dattā vidyutkeśāya rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
saṁdhyāyās tanayāṁ labdhvā vidyutkeśo niśācaraḥ |
ramate sa tayā sārdhaṁ paulomyā maghavān iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
kena cit tv atha kālena rāma sālakaṭaṁkaṭā |
vidyutkeśād garbham āpa ghanarājir ivārṇavāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tataḥ sā rākṣasī garbhaṁ ghanagarbhasamaprabham |
prasūtā mandaraṁ gatvā gaṅgā garbham ivāgnijam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
tam utsr̥jya tu sā garbhaṁ vidyutkeśād ratārthinī |
reme sā patinā sārdhaṁ vismr̥tya sutam ātmajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tayotsr̥ṣṭaḥ sa tu śiśuḥ śaradarkasamadyutiḥ |
pāṇim āsye samādhāya ruroda ghanarāḍ iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
athopariṣṭād gacchan vai vr̥ṣabhastho haraḥ prabhuḥ |
apaśyad umayā sārdhaṁ rudantaṁ rākṣasātmajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
kāruṇyabhāvāt pārvatyā bhavas tripurahā tataḥ |
taṁ rākṣasātmajaṁ cakre mātur eva vayaḥsamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
amaraṁ caiva taṁ kr̥tvā mahādevo ’kṣayo ’vyayaḥ |
puram ākāśagaṁ prādāt pārvatyāḥ priyakāmyayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
umayāpi varo datto rākṣasīnāṁ nr̥pātmaja |
sadyopalabdhir garbhasya prasūtiḥ sadya eva ca |
sadya eva vayaḥprāptir mātur eva vayaḥ samam ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
tataḥ sukeśo varadānagarvitaḥ; śriyaṁ prabhoḥ prāpya harasya pārśvataḥ |
cacāra sarvatra mahāmatiḥ khagaḥ; khagaṁ puraṁ prāpya puraṁdaro yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
