=== Sarga 67 ===

---
1.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā rāmasyākliṣṭakarmaṇaḥ |
avākśirās tathābhūto vākyam etad uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
śūdrayonyāṁ prasūto ’smi tapa ugraṁ samāsthitaḥ |
devatvaṁ prārthaye rāma saśarīro mahāyaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
na mithyāhaṁ vade rājan devalokajigīṣayā |
śūdraṁ māṁ viddhi kākutstha śambūkaṁ nāma nāmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
bhāṣatas tasya śūdrasya khaḍgaṁ suruciraprabham |
niṣkr̥ṣya kośād vimalaṁ śiraś ciccheda rāghavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tasmin muhūrte bālo ’sau jīvena samayujyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tato ’gastyāśramapadaṁ rāmaḥ kamalalocanaḥ |
sa gatvā vinayenaiva taṁ natvā mumude sukhī ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
so ’bhivādya mahātmānaṁ jvalantam iva tejasā |
ātithyaṁ paramaṁ prāpya niṣasāda narādhipaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tam uvāca mahātejāḥ kumbhayonir mahātapāḥ |
svāgataṁ te naraśreṣṭha diṣṭyā prāpto ’si rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tvaṁ me bahumato rāma guṇair bahubhir uttamaiḥ |
atithiḥ pūjanīyaś ca mama rājan hr̥di sthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
surā hi kathayanti tvām āgataṁ śūdraghātinam |
brāhmaṇasya tu dharmeṇa tvayā jīvāpitaḥ sutaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
uṣyatāṁ ceha rajanīṁ sakāśe mama rāghava |
prabhāte puṣpakeṇa tvaṁ gantā svapuram eva hi ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
idaṁ cābharaṇaṁ saumya nirmitaṁ viśvakarmaṇā |
divyaṁ divyena vapuṣā dīpyamānaṁ svatejasā |
pratigr̥hṇīṣva kākutstha matpriyaṁ kuru rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
dattasya hi punar dānaṁ sumahat phalam ucyate |
tasmāt pradāsye vidhivat tat pratīccha nararṣabha ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tad rāmaḥ pratijagrāha munes tasya mahātmanaḥ |
divyam ābharaṇaṁ citraṁ pradīptam iva bhāskaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
pratigr̥hya tato rāmas tad ābharaṇam uttamam |
āgamaṁ tasya divyasya praṣṭum evopacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
atyadbhutam idaṁ brahman vapuṣā yuktam uttamam |
kathaṁ bhagavatā prāptaṁ kuto vā kena vāhr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
kutūhalatayā brahman pr̥cchāmi tvāṁ mahāyaśaḥ |
āścaryāṇāṁ bahūnāṁ hi nidhiḥ paramako bhavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
evaṁ bruvati kākutsthe munir vākyam athābravīt |
śr̥ṇu rāma yathāvr̥ttaṁ purā tretāyuge gate ||

=== meaning ===

---
