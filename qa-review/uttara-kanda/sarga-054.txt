=== Sarga 54 ===

---
1.
tathokte tān r̥ṣīn rāmaḥ pratyuvāca kr̥tāñjaliḥ |
kimāhāraḥ kimācāro lavaṇaḥ kva ca vartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
rāghavasya vacaḥ śrutvā r̥ṣayaḥ sarva eva te |
tato nivedayām āsur lavaṇo vavr̥dhe yathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
āhāraḥ sarvasattvāni viśeṣeṇa ca tāpasāḥ |
ācāro raudratā nityaṁ vāso madhuvane sadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
hatvā daśasahasrāṇi siṁhavyāghramr̥gadvipān |
mānuṣāṁś caiva kurute nityam āhāram āhnikam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tato ’parāṇi sattvāni khādate sa mahābalaḥ |
saṁhāre samanuprāpte vyāditāsya ivāntakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tac chrutvā rāghavo vākyam uvāca sa mahāmunīn |
ghātayiṣyāmi tad rakṣo vyapagacchatu vo bhayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tathā teṣāṁ pratijñāya munīnām ugratejasām |
sa bhrātr̥̄n sahitān sarvān uvāca raghunandanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
ko hantā lavaṇaṁ vīrāḥ kasyāṁśaḥ sa vidhīyatām |
bharatasya mahābāhoḥ śatrughnasyātha vā punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
rāghaveṇaivam uktas tu bharato vākyam abravīt |
aham enaṁ vadhiṣyāmi mamāṁśaḥ sa vidhīyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
bharatasya vacaḥ śrutvā śauryavīryasamanvitam |
lakṣmaṇāvarajas tasthau hitvā sauvarṇam āsanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
śatrughnas tv abravīd vākyaṁ praṇipatya narādhipam |
kr̥takarmā mahābāhur madhyamo raghunandanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
āryeṇa hi purā śūnyā ayodhyā rakṣitā purī |
saṁtāpaṁ hr̥daye kr̥tvā āryasyāgamanaṁ prati ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
duḥkhāni ca bahūnīha anubhūtāni pārthiva |
śayāno duḥkhaśayyāsu nandigrāme mahātmanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
phalamūlāśano bhūtvā jaṭācīradharas tathā |
anubhūyedr̥śaṁ duḥkham eṣa rāghavanandanaḥ |
preṣye mayi sthite rājan na bhūyaḥ kleśam āpnuyāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tathā bruvati śatrughne rāghavaḥ punar abravīt |
evaṁ bhavatu kākutstha kriyatāṁ mama śāsanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
rājye tvām abhiṣekṣyāmi madhos tu nagare śubhe |
niveśaya mahābāho bharataṁ yady avekṣase ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
śūras tvaṁ kr̥tavidyaś ca samarthaḥ saṁniveśane |
nagaraṁ madhunā juṣṭaṁ tathā janapadāñ śubhān ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
yo hi vaṁśaṁ samutpāṭya pārthivasya punaḥ kṣaye |
na vidhatte nr̥paṁ tatra narakaṁ sa nigacchati ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
sa tvaṁ hatvā madhusutaṁ lavaṇaṁ pāpaniścayam |
rājyaṁ praśādhi dharmeṇa vākyaṁ me yady avekṣase ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
uttaraṁ ca na vaktavyaṁ śūra vākyāntare mama |
bālena pūrvajasyājñā kartavyā nātra saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
abhiṣekaṁ ca kākutstha pratīcchasva mayodyatam |
vasiṣṭhapramukhair viprair vidhimantrapuraskr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
