=== Sarga 29 ===

---
1.
tatas tamasi saṁjāte rākṣasā daivataiḥ saha |
ayudhyanta balonmattāḥ sūdayantaḥ parasparam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tatas tu devasainyena rākṣasānāṁ mahad balam |
daśāṁśaṁ sthāpitaṁ yuddhe śeṣaṁ nītaṁ yamakṣayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tasmiṁs tu tamasā naddhe sarve te devarākṣasāḥ |
anyonyaṁ nābhyajānanta yudhyamānāḥ parasparam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
indraś ca rāvaṇaś caiva rāvaṇiś ca mahābalaḥ |
tasmiṁs tamojālavr̥te moham īyur na te trayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
sa tu dr̥ṣṭvā balaṁ sarvaṁ nihataṁ rāvaṇo raṇe |
krodham abhyāgamat tīvraṁ mahānādaṁ ca muktavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
krodhāt sūtaṁ ca durdharṣaḥ syandanastham uvāca ha |
parasainyasya madhyena yāvadantaṁ nayasva mām ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
adyaitāṁs tridaśān sarvān vikramaiḥ samare svayam |
nānāśastrair mahāsārair nāśayāmi nabhastalāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
aham indraṁ vadhiṣyāmi varuṇaṁ dhanadaṁ yamam |
tridaśān vinihatyāśu svayaṁ sthāsyāmy athopari ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
viṣādo na ca kartavyaḥ śīghraṁ vāhaya me ratham |
dviḥ khalu tvāṁ bravīmy adya yāvadantaṁ nayasva mām ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
ayaṁ sa nandanoddeśo yatra vartāmahe vayam |
naya mām adya tatra tvam udayo yatra parvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā turagān sa manojavān |
ādideśātha śatrūṇāṁ madhyenaiva ca sārathiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tasya taṁ niścayaṁ jñātvā śakro deveśvaras tadā |
rathasthaḥ samarasthāṁs tān devān vākyam athābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
surāḥ śr̥ṇuta madvākyaṁ yat tāvan mama rocate |
jīvann eva daśagrīvaḥ sādhu rakṣo nigr̥hyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
eṣa hy atibalaḥ sainye rathena pavanaujasā |
gamiṣyati pravr̥ddhormiḥ samudra iva parvaṇi ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
na hy eṣa hantuṁ śakyo ’dya varadānāt sunirbhayaḥ |
tad grahīṣyāmahe rakṣo yattā bhavata saṁyuge ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
yathā baliṁ nigr̥hyaitat trailokyaṁ bhujyate mayā |
evam etasya pāpasya nigraho mama rocate ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tato ’nyaṁ deśam āsthāya śakraḥ saṁtyajya rāvaṇam |
ayudhyata mahātejā rākṣasān nāśayan raṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
uttareṇa daśagrīvaḥ praviveśānivartitaḥ |
dakṣiṇena tu pārśvena praviveśa śatakratuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tataḥ sa yojanaśataṁ praviṣṭo rākṣasādhipaḥ |
devatānāṁ balaṁ kr̥tsnaṁ śaravarṣair avākirat ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tataḥ śakro nirīkṣyātha praviṣṭaṁ taṁ balaṁ svakam |
nyavartayad asaṁbhrāntaḥ samāvr̥tya daśānanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
etasminn antare nādo mukto dānavarākṣasaiḥ |
hā hatāḥ smeti taṁ dr̥ṣṭvā grastaṁ śakreṇa rāvaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tato rathaṁ samāruhya rāvaṇiḥ krodhamūrchitaḥ |
tat sainyam atisaṁkruddhaḥ praviveśa sudāruṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
sa tāṁ praviśya māyāṁ tu dattāṁ gopatinā purā |
adr̥śyaḥ sarvabhūtānāṁ tat sainyaṁ samavākirat ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tataḥ sa devān saṁtyajya śakram evābhyayād drutam |
mahendraś ca mahātejā na dadarśa sutaṁ ripoḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
sa mātaliṁ hayāṁś caiva tāḍayitvā śarottamaiḥ |
mahendraṁ bāṇavarṣeṇa śīghrahasto hy avākirat ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tataḥ śakro rathaṁ tyaktva visr̥jya ca sa mātalim |
airāvataṁ samāruhya mr̥gayām āsa rāvaṇim ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
sa tu māyā balād rakṣaḥ saṁgrāme nābhyadr̥śyata |
kiramāṇaḥ śaraughena mahendram amitaujasaṁ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
sa taṁ yadā pariśrāntam indraṁ mene ’tha rāvaṇiḥ |
tadainaṁ māyayā baddhvā svasainyam abhito ’nayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
taṁ dr̥ṣṭvātha balāt tasmin māyayāpahr̥taṁ raṇe |
mahendram amarāḥ sarve kiṁ nv etad iti cukruśuḥ |
na hi dr̥śyati vidyāvān māyayā yena nīyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
etasminn antare cāpi sarve suragaṇās tadā |
abhyadravan susaṁkruddhā rāvaṇaṁ śastravr̥ṣṭibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
rāvaṇas tu samāsādya vasvādityamarudgaṇān |
na śaśāka raṇe sthātuṁ na yoddhuṁ śastrapīḍitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
taṁ tu dr̥ṣṭvā pariśrāntaṁ prahārair jarjaracchavim |
rāvaṇiḥ pitaraṁ yuddhe ’darśanastho ’bravīd idam ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
āgaccha tāta gacchāvo nivr̥ttaṁ raṇakarma tat |
jitaṁ te viditaṁ bho ’stu svastho bhava gatajvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
ayaṁ hi surasainyasya trailokyasya ca yaḥ prabhuḥ |
sa gr̥hīto mayā śakro bhagnamānāḥ surāḥ kr̥tāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
yatheṣṭaṁ bhuṅkṣva trailokyaṁ nigr̥hya ripum ojasā |
vr̥thā te kiṁ śramaṁ kr̥tvā yuddhaṁ hi tava niṣphalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
sa daivatabalāt tasmān nivr̥tto raṇakarmaṇaḥ |
tac chrutvā rāvaṇer vākyaṁ svasthacetā daśānanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
atha raṇavigatajvaraḥ prabhur; vijayam avāpya niśācarādhipaḥ |
bhavanam abhi tato jagāma hr̥ṣṭaḥ; svasutam avāpya ca vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
atibalasadr̥śaiḥ parākramais tair; mama kulamānavivardhanaṁ kr̥tam |
yad amarasamavikrama tvayā; tridaśapatis tridaśāś ca nirjitāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
tvaritam upanayasva vāsavaṁ; nagaram ito vraja sainyasaṁvr̥taḥ |
aham api tava gacchato drutaṁ; saha sacivair anuyāmi pr̥ṣṭhataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
atha sa balavr̥taḥ savāhanas; tridaśapatiṁ parigr̥hya rāvaṇiḥ |
svabhavanam upagamya rākṣaso; muditamanā visasarja rākṣasān ||

=== meaning ===

---
