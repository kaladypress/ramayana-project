=== Sarga 68 ===

---
1.
purā tretāyuge hy āsīd araṇyaṁ bahuvistaram |
samantād yojanaśataṁ nirmr̥gaṁ pakṣivarjitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tasmin nirmānuṣe ’raṇye kurvāṇas tapa uttamam |
aham ākramituṁ saumya tad araṇyam upāgamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tasya rūpam araṇyasya nirdeṣṭuṁ na śaśāka ha |
phalamūlaiḥ sukhāsvādair bahurūpaiś ca pādapaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tasyāraṇyasya madhye tu saro yojanam āyatam |
padmotpalasamākīrṇaṁ samatikrāntaśaivalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
tad āścaryam ivātyarthaṁ sukhāsvādam anuttamam |
arajaskaṁ tathākṣobhyaṁ śrīmatpakṣigaṇāyutam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tasmin saraḥsamīpe tu mahad adbhutam āśramam |
purāṇaṁ puṇyam atyarthaṁ tapasvijanavarjitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tatrāham avasaṁ rātriṁ naidāghīṁ puruṣarṣabha |
prabhāte kālyam utthāya saras tad upacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
athāpaśyaṁ śavaṁ tatra supuṣṭam ajaraṁ kva cit |
tiṣṭhantaṁ parayā lakṣmyā tasmiṁs toyāśaye nr̥pa ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tam arthaṁ cintayāno ’haṁ muhūrtaṁ tatra rāghava |
viṣṭhito ’smi sarastīre kiṁ nv idaṁ syād iti prabho ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
athāpaśyaṁ muhūrtāt tu divyam adbhutadarśanam |
vimānaṁ paramodāraṁ haṁsayuktaṁ manojavam ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
atyarthaṁ svargiṇaṁ tatra vimāne raghunandana |
upāste ’psarasāṁ vīra sahasraṁ divyabhūṣaṇam |
gānti geyāni ramyāṇi vādayanti tathāparāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
paśyato me tadā rāma vimānād avaruhya ca |
taṁ śavaṁ bhakṣayām āsa sa svargī raghunandana ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tato bhuktvā yathākāmaṁ māṁsaṁ bahu ca suṣṭhu ca |
avatīrya saraḥ svargī saṁspraṣṭum upacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
upaspr̥śya yathānyāyaṁ sa svargī puruṣarṣabha |
āroḍhum upacakrāma vimānavaram uttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tam ahaṁ devasaṁkāśam ārohantam udīkṣya vai |
athāham abruvaṁ vākyaṁ tam eva puruṣarṣabha ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
ko bhavān devasaṁkāśa āhāraś ca vigarhitaḥ |
tvayāyaṁ bhujyate saumya kiṁ karthaṁ vaktum arhasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
āścaryam īdr̥śo bhāvo bhāsvaro devasaṁmataḥ |
āhāro garhitaḥ saumya śrotum icchāmi tattvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
