=== Sarga 49 ===

---
1.
dr̥ṣṭvā tu maithilīṁ sītām āśramaṁ saṁpraveśitām |
saṁtāpam akarod ghoraṁ lakṣmaṇo dīnacetanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
abravīc ca mahātejāḥ sumantraṁ mantrasārathim |
sītāsaṁtāpajaṁ duḥkhaṁ paśya rāmasya dhīmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
ato duḥkhataraṁ kiṁ nu rāghavasya bhaviṣyati |
patnīṁ śuddhasamācārāṁ visr̥jya janakātmajām ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
vyaktaṁ daivād ahaṁ manye rāghavasya vinābhavam |
vaidehyā sārathe sārdhaṁ daivaṁ hi duratikramam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
yo hi devān sagandharvān asurān saha rākṣasaiḥ |
nihanyād rāghavaḥ kruddhaḥ sa daivam anuvartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
purā mama pitur vākyair daṇḍake vijane vane |
uṣito nava varṣāṇi pañca caiva sudāruṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tato duḥkhataraṁ bhūyaḥ sītāyā vipravāsanam |
paurāṇāṁ vacanaṁ śrutvā nr̥śaṁsaṁ pratibhāti me ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
ko nu dharmāśrayaḥ sūta karmaṇy asmin yaśohare |
maithilīṁ prati saṁprāptaḥ paurair hīnārthavādibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
etā bahuvidhā vācaḥ śrutvā lakṣmaṇabhāṣitāḥ |
sumantraḥ prāñjalir bhūtvā vākyam etad uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
na saṁtāpas tvayā kāryaḥ saumitre maithilīṁ prati |
dr̥ṣṭam etat purā vipraiḥ pitus te lakṣmaṇāgrataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
bhaviṣyati dr̥ḍhaṁ rāmo duḥkhaprāyo ’lpasaukhyavān |
tvāṁ caiva maithilīṁ caiva śatrughnabharatau tathā |
saṁtyajiṣyati dharmātmā kālena mahatā mahān ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
na tv idaṁ tvayi vaktavyaṁ saumitre bharate ’pi vā |
rājñā vo ’vyāhr̥taṁ vākyaṁ durvāsā yad uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
mahārājasamīpe ca mama caiva nararṣabha |
r̥ṣiṇā vyāhr̥taṁ vākyaṁ vasiṣṭhasya ca saṁnidhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
r̥ṣes tu vacanaṁ śrutvā mām āha puruṣarṣabhaḥ |
sūta na kva cid evaṁ te vaktavyaṁ janasaṁnidhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tasyāhaṁ lokapālasya vākyaṁ tat susamāhitaḥ |
naiva jātv anr̥taṁ kuryām iti me saumya darśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sarvathā nāsty avaktavyaṁ mayā saumya tavāgrataḥ |
yadi te śravaṇe śraddhā śrūyatāṁ raghunandana ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
yady apy ahaṁ narendreṇa rahasyaṁ śrāvitaḥ purā |
tac cāpy udāhariṣyāmi daivaṁ hi duratikramam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tac chrutvā bhāṣitaṁ tasya gambhīrārthapadaṁ mahat |
tathyaṁ brūhīti saumitriḥ sūtaṁ vākyam athābravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
