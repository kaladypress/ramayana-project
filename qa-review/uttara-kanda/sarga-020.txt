=== Sarga 20 ===

---
1.
tato vitrāsayan martyān pr̥thivyāṁ rākṣasādhipaḥ |
āsasāda ghane tasmin nāradaṁ munisattamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
nāradas tu mahātejā devarṣir amitaprabhaḥ |
abravīn meghapr̥ṣṭhastho rāvaṇaṁ puṣpake sthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
rākṣasādhipate saumya tiṣṭha viśravasaḥ suta |
prīto ’smy abhijanopeta vikramair ūrjitais tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
viṣṇunā daityaghātaiś ca tārkṣyasyoragadharṣaṇaiḥ |
tvayā samaramardaiś ca bhr̥śaṁ hi paritoṣitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
kiṁ cid vakṣyāmi tāvat te śrotavyaṁ śroṣyase yadi |
śrutvā cānantaraṁ kāryaṁ tvayā rākṣasapuṁgava ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
kim ayaṁ vadhyate lokas tvayāvadhyena daivataiḥ |
hata eva hy ayaṁ loko yadā mr̥tyuvaśaṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
paśya tāvan mahābāho rākṣaseśvaramānuṣam |
lokam enaṁ vicitrārthaṁ yasya na jñāyate gatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
kva cid vāditranr̥ttāni sevyante muditair janaiḥ |
rudyate cāparair ārtair dhārāśrunayanānanaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
mātā pitr̥sutasnehair bhāryā bandhumanoramaiḥ |
mohenāyaṁ jano dhvastaḥ kleśaṁ svaṁ nāvabudhyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tat kim evaṁ parikliśya lokaṁ mohanirākr̥tam |
jita eva tvayā saumya martyaloko na saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
evam uktas tu laṅkeśo dīpyamāna ivaujasā |
abravīn nāradaṁ tatra saṁprahasyābhivādya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
maharṣe devagandharvavihāra samarapriya |
ahaṁ khalūdyato gantuṁ vijayārthī rasātalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tato lokatrayaṁ jitvā sthāpya nāgān surān vaśe |
samudram amr̥tārthaṁ vai mathiṣyāmi rasālayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
athābravīd daśagrīvaṁ nārado bhagavān r̥ṣiḥ |
kva khalv idānīṁ mārgeṇa tvayānena gamiṣyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
ayaṁ khalu sudurgamyaḥ pitr̥rājñaḥ puraṁ prati |
mārgo gacchati durdharṣo yamasyāmitrakarśana ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sa tu śāradameghābhaṁ muktvā hāsaṁ daśānanaḥ |
uvāca kr̥tam ity eva vacanaṁ cedam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
tasmād eṣa mahābrahman vaivasvatavadhodyataḥ |
gacchāmi dakṣiṇām āśāṁ yatra sūryātmajo nr̥paḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
mayā hi bhagavan krodhāt pratijñātaṁ raṇārthinā |
avajeṣyāmi caturo lokapālān iti prabho ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tenaiṣa prasthito ’haṁ vai pitr̥rājapuraṁ prati |
prāṇisaṁkleśakartāraṁ yojayiṣyāmi mr̥tyunā ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
evam uktvā daśagrīvo muniṁ tam abhivādya ca |
prayayau dakṣiṇām āśāṁ prahr̥ṣṭaiḥ saha mantribhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
nāradas tu mahātejā muhūrtaṁ dhyānam āsthitaḥ |
cintayām āsa viprendro vidhūma iva pāvakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
yena lokās trayaḥ sendrāḥ kliśyante sacarācarāḥ |
kṣīṇe cāyuṣi dharme ca sa kālo hiṁsyate katham ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
yasya nityaṁ trayo lokā vidravanti bhayārditāḥ |
taṁ kathaṁ rākṣasendro ’sau svayam evābhigacchati ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
yo vidhātā ca dhātā ca sukr̥te duṣkr̥te yathā |
trailokyaṁ vijitaṁ yena taṁ kathaṁ nu vijeṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
aparaṁ kiṁ nu kr̥tvaivaṁ vidhānaṁ saṁvidhāsyati |
kautūhalasamutpanno yāsyāmi yamasādanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
