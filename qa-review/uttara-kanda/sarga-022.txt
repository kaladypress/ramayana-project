=== Sarga 22 ===

---
1.
sa tu tasya mahānādaṁ śrutvā vaivasvato yamaḥ |
śatruṁ vijayinaṁ mene svabalasya ca saṁkṣayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
sa tu yodhān hatān matvā krodhaparyākulekṣaṇaḥ |
abravīt tvaritaṁ sūtaṁ rathaḥ samupanīyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tasya sūto rathaṁ divyam upasthāpya mahāsvanam |
sthitaḥ sa ca mahātejā āruroha mahāratham ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
pāśamudgarahastaś ca mr̥tyus tasyāgrato sthitaḥ |
yena saṁkṣipyate sarvaṁ trailokyaṁ sacarācaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
kāladaṇḍaś ca pārśvastho mūrtimān syandane sthitaḥ |
yamapraharaṇaṁ divyaṁ prajvalann iva tejasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tato lokās trayas trastāḥ kampante ca divaukasaḥ |
kālaṁ kruddhaṁ tadā dr̥ṣṭvā lokatrayabhayāvaham ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
dr̥ṣṭvā tu te taṁ vikr̥taṁ rathaṁ mr̥tyusamanvitam |
sacivā rākṣasendrasya sarvalokabhayāvaham ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
laghusattvatayā sarve naṣṭasaṁjñā bhayārditāḥ |
nātra yoddhuṁ samarthāḥ sma ity uktvā vipradudruvuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
sa tu taṁ tādr̥śaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā rathaṁ lokabhayāvaham |
nākṣubhyata tadā rakṣo vyathā caivāsya nābhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
sa tu rāvaṇam āsādya visr̥jañ śaktitomarān |
yamo marmāṇi saṁkruddho rākṣasasya nyakr̥ntata ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
rāvaṇas tu sthitaḥ svasthaḥ śaravarṣaṁ mumoca ha |
tasmin vaivasvatarathe toyavarṣam ivāmbudaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tato mahāśaktiśataiḥ pātyamānair mahorasi |
pratikartuṁ sa nāśaknod rākṣasaḥ śalyapīḍitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
nānāpraharaṇair evaṁ yamenāmitrakarśinā |
saptarātraṁ kr̥te saṁkhye na bhagno vijito ’pi vā ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tato ’bhavat punar yuddhaṁ yamarākṣasayos tadā |
vijayākāṅkṣiṇos tatra samareṣv anivartinoḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tato devāḥ sagandharvāḥ siddhāś ca paramarṣayaḥ |
prajāpatiṁ puraskr̥tya dadr̥śus tad raṇājiram ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
saṁvarta iva lokānām abhavad yudhyatos tayoḥ |
rākṣasānāṁ ca mukhyasya pretānām īśvarasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
rākṣasendras tataḥ kruddhaś cāpam āyamya saṁyuge |
nirantaram ivākāśaṁ kurvan bāṇān mumoca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
mr̥tyuṁ caturbhir viśikhaiḥ sūtaṁ saptabhir ardayat |
yamaṁ śarasahasreṇa śīghraṁ marmasv atāḍayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tataḥ kruddhasya sahasā yamasyābhiviniḥsr̥taḥ |
jvālāmālo viniśvāso vadanāt krodhapāvakaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tato ’paśyaṁs tadāścaryaṁ devadānavarākṣasāḥ |
krodhajaṁ pāvakaṁ dīptaṁ didhakṣantaṁ ripor balam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
mr̥tyus tu paramakruddho vaivasvatam athābravīt |
muñca māṁ deva śīghraṁ tvaṁ nihanmi samare ripum ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
narakaḥ śambaro vr̥traḥ śambhuḥ kārtasvaro balī |
namucir virocanaś caiva tāv ubhau madhukaiṭabhau ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
ete cānye ca bahavo balavanto durāsadāḥ |
vinipannā mayā dr̥ṣṭāḥ kā cintāsmin niśācare ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
muñca māṁ sādhu dharmajña yāvad enaṁ nihanmy aham |
na hi kaś cin mayā dr̥ṣṭo muhūrtam api jīvati ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
balaṁ mama na khalv etan maryādaiṣā nisargataḥ |
saṁspr̥ṣṭo hi mayā kaś cin na jīved iti niścayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
etat tu vacanaṁ śrutvā dharmarājaḥ pratāpavān |
abravīt tatra taṁ mr̥tyumayam enaṁ nihanmy aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
tataḥ saṁraktanayanaḥ kruddho vaivasvataḥ prabhuḥ |
kāladaṇḍam amoghaṁ taṁ tolayām āsa pāṇinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
yasya pārśveṣu niśchidrāḥ kālapāśāḥ pratiṣṭhitāḥ |
pāvakasparśasaṁkāśo mudgaro mūrtimān sthitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
darśanād eva yaḥ prāṇān prāṇinām uparudhyati |
kiṁ punas tāḍanād vāpi pīḍanād vāpi dehinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
sa jvālāparivāras tu pibann iva niśācaram |
karaspr̥ṣṭo balavatā daṇḍaḥ kruddhaḥ sudāruṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
tato vidudruvuḥ sarve sattvās tasmād raṇājirāt |
surāś ca kṣubhitā dr̥ṣṭvā kāladaṇḍodyataṁ yamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
tasmin prahartukāme tu daṇḍam udyamya rāvaṇam |
yamaṁ pitāmahaḥ sākṣād darśayitvedam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
vaivasvata mahābāho na khalv atulavikrama |
prahartavyaṁ tvayaitena daṇḍenāsmin niśācare ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
varaḥ khalu mayā dattas tasya tridaśapuṁgava |
tat tvayā nānr̥taṁ kāryaṁ yan mayā vyāhr̥taṁ vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
amogho hy eṣa sarvāsāṁ prajānāṁ vinipātane |
kāladaṇḍo mayā sr̥ṣṭaḥ pūrvaṁ mr̥tyupuraskr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
tan na khalv eṣa te saumya pātyo rākṣasamūrdhani |
na hy asmin patite kaś cin muhūrtam api jīvati ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
yadi hy asmin nipatite na mriyetaiṣa rākṣasaḥ |
mriyeta vā daśagrīvas tathāpy ubhayato ’nr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
rākṣasendrān niyacchādya daṇḍam enaṁ vadhodyatam |
satyaṁ mama kuruṣvedaṁ lokāṁs tvaṁ samavekṣya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
evam uktas tu dharmātmā pratyuvāca yamas tadā |
eṣa vyāvartito daṇḍaḥ prabhaviṣṇur bhavān hi naḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
kiṁ tv idānīṁ mayā śakyaṁ kartuṁ raṇagatena hi |
yan mayā yan na hantavyo rākṣaso varadarpitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
eṣa tasmāt praṇaśyāmi darśanād asya rakṣasaḥ |
ity uktvā sarathaḥ sāśvas tatraivāntaradhīyata ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
daśagrīvas tu taṁ jitvā nāma viśrāvya cātmanaḥ |
puṣpakeṇa tu saṁhr̥ṣṭo niṣkrānto yamasādanāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
tato vaivasvato devaiḥ saha brahmapurogamaiḥ |
jagāma tridivaṁ hr̥ṣṭo nāradaś ca mahāmuniḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
