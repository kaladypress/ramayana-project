=== Sarga 25 ===

---
1.
sa tu dattvā daśagrīvo vanaṁ ghoraṁ kharasya tat |
bhaginīṁ ca samāśvāsya hr̥ṣṭaḥ svasthataro ’bhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tato nikumbhilā nāma laṅkāyāḥ kānanaṁ mahat |
mahātmā rākṣasendras tat praviveśa sahānugaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tatra yūpaśatākīrṇaṁ saumyacaityopaśobhitam |
dadarśa viṣṭhitaṁ yajñaṁ saṁpradīptam iva śriyā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tataḥ kr̥ṣṇājinadharaṁ kamaṇḍaluśikhādhvajam |
dadarśa svasutaṁ tatra meghanādam ariṁdamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
rakṣaḥpatiḥ samāsādya samāśliṣya ca bāhubhiḥ |
abravīt kim idaṁ vatsa vartate tad bravīhi me ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
uśanā tv abravīt tatra gurur yajñasamr̥ddhaye |
rāvaṇaṁ rākṣasaśreṣṭhaṁ dvijaśreṣṭho mahātapāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
aham ākhyāmi te rājañ śrūyatāṁ sarvam eva ca |
yajñās te sapta putreṇa prāptāḥ subahuvistarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
agniṣṭomo ’śvamedhaś ca yajño bahusuvarṇakaḥ |
rājasūyas tathā yajño gomedho vaiṣṇavas tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
māheśvare pravr̥tte tu yajñe pumbhiḥ sudurlabhe |
varāṁs te labdhavān putraḥ sākṣāt paśupater iha ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
kāmagaṁ syandanaṁ divyam antarikṣacaraṁ dhruvam |
māyāṁ ca tāmasīṁ nāma yayā saṁpadyate tamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
etayā kila saṁgrāme māyayā rākṣaseśvara |
prayuddhasya gatiḥ śakyā na hi jñātuṁ surāsuraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
akṣayāv iṣudhī bāṇaiś cāpaṁ cāpi sudurjayam |
astraṁ ca balavat saumya śatruvidhvaṁsanaṁ raṇe ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
etān sarvān varām̐l labdhvā putras te ’yaṁ daśānana |
adya yajñasamāptau ca tvatpratīkṣaḥ sthito aham ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tato ’bravīd daśagrīvo na śobhanam idaṁ kr̥tam |
pūjitāḥ śatravo yasmād dravyair indrapurogamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
ehīdānīṁ kr̥taṁ yad dhi tad akartuṁ na śakyate |
āgaccha saumya gacchāmaḥ svam eva bhavanaṁ prati ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tato gatvā daśagrīvaḥ saputraḥ savibhīṣaṇaḥ |
striyo ’vatārayām āsa sarvās tā bāṣpaviklavāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
lakṣiṇyo ratnabūtāś ca devadānavarakṣasām |
nānābhūṣaṇasaṁpannā jvalantyaḥ svena tejasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
vibhīṣaṇas tu tā nārīr dr̥ṣṭvā śokasamākulāḥ |
tasya tāṁ ca matiṁ jñātvā dharmātmā vākyam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
īdr̥śais taiḥ samācārair yaśo’rthakulanāśanaiḥ |
dharṣaṇaṁ prāṇināṁ dattvā svamatena viceṣṭase ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
jñātīn vai dharṣayitvemās tvayānītā varāṅganāḥ |
tvām atikramya madhunā rājan kumbhīnasī hr̥tā ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
rāvaṇas tv abravīd vākyaṁ nāvagacchāmi kiṁ tv idam |
ko vāyaṁ yas tvayākhyāto madhur ity eva nāmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
vibhīṣaṇas tu saṁkruddho bhrātaraṁ vākyam abravīt |
śrūyatām asya pāpasya karmaṇaḥ phalam āgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
mātāmahasya yo ’smākaṁ jyeṣṭho bhrātā sumālinaḥ |
mālyavān iti vikhyāto vr̥ddhaprājño niśācaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
pitur jyeṣṭho jananyāś ca asmākaṁ tv āryako ’bhavat |
tasya kumbhīnasī nāma duhitur duhitābhavat ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
mātr̥ṣvasur athāsmākaṁ sā kanyā cānalodbhavā |
bhavaty asmākam eṣā vai bhrātr̥̄ṇāṁ dharmataḥ svasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
sā hr̥tā madhunā rājan rākṣasena balīyasā |
yajñapravr̥tte putre te mayi cāntarjaloṣite ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
nihatya rākṣasaśreṣṭhān amātyāṁs tava saṁmatān |
dharṣayitvā hr̥tā rājan guptā hy antaḥpure tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
śrutvā tv etan mahārāja kṣāntam eva hato na saḥ |
yasmād avaśyaṁ dātavyā kanyā bhartre hi dātr̥bhiḥ |
asminn evābhisaṁprāptaṁ loke viditam astu te ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
tato ’bravīd daśagrīvaḥ kruddhaḥ saṁraktalocanaḥ |
kalpyatāṁ me rathaḥ śīghraṁ śūrāḥ sajjībhavantu ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
bhrātā me kumbhakarṇaś ca ye ca mukhyā niśācarāḥ |
vāhanāny adhirohantu nānāpraharaṇāyudhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
adya taṁ samare hatvā madhuṁ rāvaṇanirbhayam |
indralokaṁ gamiṣyāmi yuddhakāṅkṣī suhr̥dvr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
tato vijitya tridivaṁ vaśe sthāpya puraṁdaram |
nirvr̥to vihariṣyāmi trailokyaiśvaryaśobhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
akṣauhiṇīsahasrāṇi catvāry ugrāṇi rakṣasām |
nānāpraharaṇāny āśu niryayur yuddhakāṅkṣiṇām ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
indrajit tv agrataḥ sainyaṁ sainikān parigr̥hya ca |
rāvaṇo madhyataḥ śūraḥ kumbhakarṇaś ca pr̥ṣṭhataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
vibhīṣaṇas tu dharmātmā laṅkāyāṁ dharmam ācarat |
te tu sarve mahābhāgā yayur madhupuraṁ prati ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
rathair nāgaiḥ kharair uṣṭrair hayair dīptair mahoragaiḥ |
rākṣasāḥ prayayuḥ sarve kr̥tvākāśaṁ nirantaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
daityāś ca śataśas tatra kr̥tavairāḥ suraiḥ saha |
rāvaṇaṁ prekṣya gacchantam anvagacchanta pr̥ṣṭhataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
sa tu gatvā madhupuraṁ praviśya ca daśānanaḥ |
na dadarśa madhuṁ tatra bhaginīṁ tatra dr̥ṣṭavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
sā prahvā prāñjalir bhūtvā śirasā pādayor gatā |
tasya rākṣasarājasya trastā kumbhīnasī svasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
tāṁ samutthāpayām āsa na bhetavyam iti bruvan |
rāvaṇo rākṣasaśreṣṭhaḥ kiṁ cāpi karavāṇi te ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
sābravīd yadi me rājan prasannas tvaṁ mahābala |
bhartāraṁ na mamehādya hantum arhasi mānada ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
satyavāg bhava rājendra mām avekṣasva yācatīm |
tvayā hy uktaṁ mahābāho na bhetavyam iti svayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
rāvaṇas tv abravīd dhr̥ṣṭaḥ svasāraṁ tatra saṁsthitām |
kva cāsau tava bhartā vai mama śīghraṁ nivedyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
saha tena gamiṣyāmi suralokaṁ jayāya vai |
tava kāruṇyasauhārdān nivr̥tto ’smi madhor vadhāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
ity uktā sā prasuptaṁ taṁ samutthāpya niśācaram |
abravīt saṁprahr̥ṣṭeva rākṣasī suvipaścitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
eṣa prāpto daśagrīvo mama bhrātā niśācaraḥ |
suralokajayākāṅkṣī sāhāyye tvāṁ vr̥ṇoti ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
tad asya tvaṁ sahāyārthaṁ sabandhur gaccha rākṣasa |
snigdhasya bhajamānasya yuktam arthāya kalpitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
48.
tasyās tad vacanaṁ śrutvā tathety āha madhur vacaḥ |
dadarśa rākṣasaśreṣṭhaṁ yathānyāyam upetya saḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
49.
pūjayām āsa dharmeṇa rāvaṇaṁ rākṣasādhipam |
prāptapūjo daśagrīvo madhuveśmani vīryavān |
tatra caikāṁ niśām uṣya gamanāyopacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
50.
tataḥ kailāsam āsādya śailaṁ vaiśravaṇālayam |
rākṣasendro mahendrābhaḥ senām upaniveśayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
