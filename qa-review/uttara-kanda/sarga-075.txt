=== Sarga 75 ===

---
1.
tathoktavati rāme tu bharate ca mahātmani |
lakṣmaṇo ’pi śubhaṁ vākyam uvāca raghunandanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
aśvamedho mahāyajñaḥ pāvanaḥ sarvapāpmanām |
pāvanas tava durdharṣo rocatāṁ kratupuṁgavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
śrūyate hi purāvr̥ttaṁ vāsave sumahātmani |
brahmahatyāvr̥taḥ śakro hayamedhena pāvitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
purā kila mahābāho devāsurasamāgame |
vr̥tro nāma mahān āsīd daiteyo lokasaṁmataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
vistīrṇā yojanaśatam ucchritas triguṇaṁ tataḥ |
anurāgeṇa lokāṁs trīn snehāt paśyati sarvataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
dharmajñaś ca kr̥tajñaś ca buddhyā ca pariniṣṭhitaḥ |
śaśāsa pr̥thivīṁ sarvāṁ dharmeṇa susamāhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tasmin praśāsati tadā sarvakāmadughā mahī |
rasavanti prasūtāni mūlāni ca phalāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
akr̥ṣṭapacyā pr̥thivī susaṁpannā mahātmanaḥ |
sa rājyaṁ tādr̥śaṁ bhuṅkte sphītam adbhutadarśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tasya buddhiḥ samutpannā tapaḥ kuryām anuttamam |
tapo hi paramaṁ śreyas tapo hi paramaṁ sukham ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
sa nikṣipya sutaṁ jyeṣṭhaṁ paureṣu parameśvaram |
tapa ugram upātiṣṭhat tāpayan sarvadevatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tapas tapyati vr̥tre tu vāsavaḥ paramārtavat |
viṣṇuṁ samupasaṁkramya vākyam etad uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tapasyatā mahābāho lokā vr̥treṇa nirjitāḥ |
balavān sa hi dharmātmā nainaṁ śakṣyāmi bādhitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
yady asau tapa ātiṣṭhed bhūya eva sureśvara |
yāval lokā dhariṣyanti tāvad asya vaśānugāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tvaṁ cainaṁ paramodāram upekṣasi mahābala |
kṣaṇaṁ hi na bhaved vr̥traḥ kruddhe tvayi sureśvara ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
yadā hi prītisaṁyogaṁ tvayā viṣṇo samāgataḥ |
tadā prabhr̥ti lokānāṁ nāthatvam upalabdhavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sa tvaṁ prasādaṁ lokānāṁ kuruṣva sumahāyaśaḥ |
tvatkr̥tena hi sarvaṁ syāt praśāntam ajaraṁ jagat ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
ime hi sarve viṣṇo tvāṁ nirīkṣante divaukasaḥ |
vr̥traghatena mahatā eṣāṁ sāhyaṁ kuruṣva ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tvayā hi nityaśaḥ sāhyaṁ kr̥tam eṣāṁ mahātmanām |
asahyam idam anyeṣām agatīnāṁ gatir bhavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
