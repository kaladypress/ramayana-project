=== Sarga 90 ===

---
1.
kasya cit tv atha kālasya yudhājit kekayo nr̥paḥ |
svaguruṁ preṣayām āsa rāghavāya mahātmane ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
gārgyam aṅgirasaḥ putraṁ brahmarṣim amitaprabham |
daśa cāśvasahasrāṇi prītidānam anuttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
kambalāni ca ratnāni citravastram athottamam |
rāmāya pradadau rājā bahūny ābharaṇāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
śrutvā tu rāghavo gārgyaṁ maharṣiṁ samupāgatam |
mātulasyāśvapatinaḥ priyaṁ dūtam upāgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
pratyudgamya ca kākutsthaḥ krośamātraṁ sahānugaḥ |
gārgyaṁ saṁpūjayām āsa dhanaṁ tat pratigr̥hya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
pr̥ṣṭvā ca prītidaṁ sarvaṁ kuśalaṁ mātulasya ca |
upaviṣṭaṁ mahābhāgaṁ rāmaḥ praṣṭuṁ pracakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
kim āha mātulo vākyaṁ yadarthaṁ bhagavān iha |
prāpto vākyavidāṁ śreṣṭha sākṣād iva br̥haspatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
rāmasya bhāṣitaṁ śrutvā brahmarṣiḥ kāryavistaram |
vaktum adbhutasaṁkāśaṁ rāghavāyopacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
mātulas te mahābāho vākyam āha nararṣabha |
yudhājit prītisaṁyuktaṁ śrūyatāṁ yadi rocate ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
ayaṁ gandharvaviṣayaḥ phalamūlopaśobhitaḥ |
sindhor ubhayataḥ pārśve deśaḥ paramaśobhanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
taṁ ca rakṣanti gandharvāḥ sāyudhā yuddhakovidāḥ |
śailūṣasya sutā vīrās tisraḥ koṭyo mahābalāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tān vinirjitya kākutstha gandharvaviṣayaṁ śubham |
niveśaya mahābāho dve pure susamāhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
anyasya na gatis tatra deśaś cāyaṁ suśobhanaḥ |
rocatāṁ te mahābāho nāhaṁ tvām anr̥taṁ vade ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tac chrutvā rāghavaḥ prīto maharṣer mātulasya ca |
uvāca bāḍham ity evaṁ bharataṁ cānvavaikṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
so ’bravīd rāghavaḥ prītaḥ prāñjalipragraho dvijam |
imau kumārau taṁ deśaṁ brahmarṣe vijayiṣyataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
bharatasyātmajau vīrau takṣaḥ puṣkala eva ca |
mātulena suguptau tau dharmeṇa ca samāhitau ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
bharataṁ cāgrataḥ kr̥tvā kumārau sabalānugau |
nihatya gandharvasutān dve pure vibhajiṣyataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
niveśya te puravare ātmājau saṁniveśya ca |
āgamiṣyati me bhūyaḥ sakāśam atidhārmikaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
brahmarṣim evam uktvā tu bharataṁ sabalānugam |
ājñāpayām āsa tadā kumārau cābhyaṣecayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
nakṣatreṇa ca saumyena puraskr̥tyāṅgiraḥ sutam |
bharataḥ saha sainyena kumārābhyāṁ ca niryayau ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
sā senā śakrayukteva naragān niryayāv atha |
rāghavānugatā dūraṁ durādharṣā surāsuraiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
māṁsāśīni ca sattvāni rakṣāṁsi sumahānti ca |
anujagmuś ca bharataṁ rudhirasya pipāsayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
bhūtagrāmāś ca bahavo māṁsabhakṣāḥ sudāruṇāḥ |
gandharvaputramāṁsāni bhoktukāmāḥ sahasraśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
siṁhavyāghrasr̥gālānāṁ khecarāṇāṁ ca pakṣiṇām |
bahūni vai sahasrāṇi senāyā yayur agrataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
adhyardhamāsam uṣitā pathi senā nirāmayā |
hr̥ṣṭapuṣṭajanākīrṇā kekayaṁ samupāgamat ||

=== meaning ===

---
