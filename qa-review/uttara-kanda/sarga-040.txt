=== Sarga 40 ===

---
1.
visr̥jya ca mahābāhur r̥kṣavānararākṣasān |
bhrātr̥bhiḥ sahito rāmaḥ pramumoda sukhī sukham ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
athāparāhṇasamaye bhrātr̥bhiḥ saha rāghavaḥ |
śuśrāva madhurāṁ vāṇīm antarikṣāt prabhāṣitām ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
saumya rāma nirīkṣasva saumyena vadanena mām |
kailāsaśikharāt prāptaṁ viddhi māṁ puṣkaraṁ prabho ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tava śāsanam ājñāya gato ’smi dhanadaṁ prati |
upasthātuṁ naraśreṣṭha sa ca māṁ pratyabhāṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
nirjitas tvaṁ narendreṇa rāghaveṇa mahātmanā |
nihatya yudhi durdharṣaṁ rāvaṇaṁ rākṣasādhipam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
mamāpi paramā prītir hate tasmin durātmani |
rāvaṇe sagaṇe saumya saputrāmātyabāndhave ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
sa tvaṁ rāmeṇa laṅkāyāṁ nirjitaḥ paramātmanā |
vaha saumya tam eva tvam aham ājñāpayāmi te ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
eṣa me paramaḥ kāmo yat tvaṁ rāghavanandanam |
vaher lokasya saṁyānaṁ gacchasva vigatajvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tacchāsanam ahaṁ jñātvā dhanadasya mahātmanaḥ |
tvatsakāśaṁ punaḥ prāptaḥ sa evaṁ pratigr̥hṇa mām ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
bāḍham ity eva kākutsthaḥ puṣpakaṁ samapūjayat |
lājākṣataiś ca puṣpaiś ca gandhaiś ca susugandhibhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
gamyatāṁ ca yathākāmam āgacches tvaṁ yadā smare |
evam astv iti rāmeṇa visr̥ṣṭaḥ puṣpakaḥ punaḥ |
abhipretāṁ diśaṁ prāyāt puṣpakaḥ puṣpabhūṣitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
evam antarhite tasmin puṣpake vividhātmani |
bharataḥ prāñjalir vākyam uvāca raghunandanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
atyadbhutāni dr̥śyante tvayi rājyaṁ praśāsati |
amānuṣāṇāṁ sattvānāṁ vyāhr̥tāni muhur muhuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
anāmayāc ca martyānāṁ sāgro māso gato hy ayam |
jīrṇānām api sattvānāṁ mr̥tyur nāyāti rāghava ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
putrān nāryaḥ prasūyante vapuṣmantaś ca mānavāḥ |
harṣaś cābhyadhiko rājañ janasya puravāsinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
kāle ca vāsavo varṣaṁ pātayaty amr̥topamam |
vāyavaś cāpi vāyante sparśavantaḥ sukhapradāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
īdr̥śo naś ciraṁ rājā bhavatv iti nareśvara |
kathayanti pure paurā janā janapadeṣu ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
etā vācaḥ sumadhurā bharatena samīritāḥ |
śrutvā rāmo mudā yuktaḥ pramumoda sukhī sukham ||

=== meaning ===

---
