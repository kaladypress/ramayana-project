=== Sarga 15 ===

---
1.
tatas tān vidrutān dr̥ṣṭvā yakṣāñ śatasahasraśaḥ |
svayam eva dhanādhyakṣo nirjagāma raṇaṁ prati ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tatra māṇicāro nāma yakṣaḥ paramadurjayaḥ |
vr̥to yakṣasahasraiḥ sa caturbhiḥ samayodhayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
te gadāmusalaprāsaśaktitomaramudgaraiḥ |
abhighnanto raṇe yakṣā rākṣasān abhidudruvuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tataḥ prahastena tadā sahasraṁ nihataṁ raṇe |
mahodareṇa gadayā sahasram aparaṁ hatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
kruddhena ca tadā rāma mārīcena durātmanā |
nimeṣāntaramātreṇa dve sahasre nipātite ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
dhūmrākṣeṇa samāgamya māṇibhadro mahāraṇe |
musalenorasi krodhāt tāḍito na ca kampitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tato gadāṁ samāvidhya māṇibhadreṇa rākṣasaḥ |
dhūmrākṣas tāḍito mūrdhni vihvalo nipapāta ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
dhūmrākṣaṁ tāḍitaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā patitaṁ śoṇitokṣitam |
abhyadhāvat susaṁkruddho māṇibhadraṁ daśānanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
taṁ kruddham abhidhāvantaṁ yugāntāgnim ivotthitam |
śaktibhis tāḍayām āsa tisr̥bhir yakṣapuṁgavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tato rākṣasarājena tāḍito gadayā raṇe |
tasya tena prahāreṇa mukuṭaḥ pārśvam āgataḥ |
tadā prabhr̥ti yakṣo ’sau pārśvamaulir iti smr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
tasmiṁs tu vimukhe yakṣe māṇibhadre mahātmani |
saṁnādaḥ sumahān rāma tasmiñ śaile vyavardhata ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tato dūrāt pradadr̥śe dhanādhyakṣo gadādharaḥ |
śukraproṣṭaḥpadābhyāṁ ca śaṅkhapadmasamāvr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sa dr̥ṣṭvā bhrātaraṁ saṁkhye śāpād vibhraṣṭagauravam |
uvāca vacanaṁ dhīmān yuktaṁ paitāmahe kule ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
mayā tvaṁ vāryamāṇo ’pi nāvagacchasi durmate |
paścād asya phalaṁ prāpya jñāsyase nirayaṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
yo hi mohād viṣaṁ pītvā nāvagacchati mānavaḥ |
pariṇāme sa vi mūḍho jānīte karmaṇaḥ phalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
daivatāni hi nandanti dharmayuktena kena cit |
yena tvam īdr̥śaṁ bhāvaṁ nītas tac ca na budhyase ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
yo hi mātr̥̄ḥ pitr̥̄n bhrātr̥̄n ācaryāṁś cāvamanyate |
sa paśyati phalaṁ tasya pretarājavaśaṁ gataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
adhruve hi śarīre yo na karoti tapo ’rjanam |
sa paścāt tapyate mūḍho mr̥to dr̥ṣṭvātmano gatim ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
kasya cin na hi durbudheś chandato jāyate matiḥ |
yādr̥śaṁ kurute karma tādr̥śaṁ phalam aśnute ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
buddhiṁ rūpaṁ balaṁ vittaṁ putrān māhātmyam eva ca |
prapnuvanti narāḥ sarvaṁ svakr̥taiḥ pūrvakarmabhiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
evaṁ nirayagāmī tvaṁ yasya te matir īdr̥śī |
na tvāṁ samabhibhāṣiṣye durvr̥ttasyaiṣa nirṇayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
evam uktvā tatas tena tasyāmātyāḥ samāhatāḥ |
mārīcapramukhāḥ sarve vimukhā vipradudruvuḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tatas tena daśagrīvo yakṣendreṇa mahātmanā |
gadayābhihato mūrdhni na ca sthānād vyakampata ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
tatas tau rāma nighnantāv anyonyaṁ paramāhave |
na vihvalau na ca śrāntau babhūvatur amarṣaṇaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
āgneyam astraṁ sa tato mumoca dhanado raṇe |
vāruṇena daśagrīvas tad astraṁ pratyavārayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tato māyāṁ praviṣṭaḥ sa rākṣasīṁ rākṣaseśvaraḥ |
jaghāna mūrdhni dhanadaṁ vyāvidhya mahatīṁ gadām ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
evaṁ sa tenābhihato vihvalaḥ śoṇitokṣitaḥ |
kr̥ttamūla ivāśoko nipapāta dhanādhipaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
tataḥ padmādibhis tatra nidhibhiḥ sa dhanādhipaḥ |
nandanaṁ vanam ānīya dhanado śvāsitas tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
tato nirjitya taṁ rāma dhanadaṁ rākṣasādhipaḥ |
puṣpakaṁ tasya jagrāha vimānaṁ jayalakṣaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
kāñcanastambhasaṁvītaṁ vaidūryamaṇitoraṇam |
muktājālapraticchannaṁ sarvakāmaphaladrumam ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
tat tu rājā samāruhya kāmagaṁ vīryanirjitam |
jitvā vaiśravaṇaṁ devaṁ kailāsād avarohata ||

=== meaning ===

---
