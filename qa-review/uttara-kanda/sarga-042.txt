=== Sarga 42 ===

---
1.
tatropaviṣṭaṁ rājānam upāsante vicakṣaṇāḥ |
kathānāṁ bahurūpāṇāṁ hāsyakārāḥ samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
vijayo madhumattaś ca kāśyapaḥ piṅgalaḥ kuśaḥ |
surājiḥ kāliyo bhadro dantavakraḥ samāgadhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
ete kathā bahuvidhā parihāsasamanvitāḥ |
kathayanti sma saṁhr̥ṣṭā rāghavasya mahātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
tataḥ kathāyāṁ kasyāṁ cid rāghavaḥ samabhāṣata |
kāḥ kathā nagare bhadra vartante viṣayeṣu ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
mām āśritāni kāny āhuḥ paurajānapadā janāḥ |
kiṁ ca sītāṁ samāśritya bharataṁ kiṁ nu lakṣmaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
kiṁ nu śatrughnam āśritya kaikeyīṁ mātaraṁ ca me |
vaktavyatāṁ ca rājāno nave rājye vrajanti hi ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
evam ukte tu rāmeṇa bhadraḥ prāñjalir abravīt |
sthitāḥ kathāḥ śubhā rājan vartante puravāsinām ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
ayaṁ tu vijayaḥ saumya daśagrīvavadhāśritaḥ |
bhūyiṣṭhaṁ svapure pauraiḥ kathyate puruṣarṣabha ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
evam uktas tu bhadreṇa rāghavo vākyam abravīt |
kathayasva yathātathyaṁ sarvaṁ niravaśeṣataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
śubhāśubhāni vākyāni yāny āhuḥ puravāsinaḥ |
śrutvedānīṁ śubhaṁ kuryāṁ na kuryām aśubhāni ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
kathayasva ca visrabdho nirbhayo vigatajvaraḥ |
kathayante yathā paurā janā janapadeṣu ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
rāghaveṇaivam uktas tu bhadraḥ suruciraṁ vacaḥ |
pratyuvāca mahābāhuṁ prāñjaliḥ susamāhitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
śr̥ṇu rājan yathā paurāḥ kathayanti śubhāśubham |
catvarāpaṇarathyāsu vaneṣūpavaneṣu ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
duṣkaraṁ kr̥tavān rāmaḥ samudre setubandhanam |
akr̥taṁ pūrvakaiḥ kaiś cid devair api sadānavaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
rāvaṇaś ca durādharṣo hataḥ sabalavāhanaḥ |
vānarāś ca vaśaṁ nītā r̥kṣāś ca saha rākṣasaiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
hatvā ca rāvaṇaṁ yuddhe sītām āhr̥tya rāghavaḥ |
amarṣaṁ pr̥ṣṭhataḥ kr̥tvā svaveśma punar ānayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
kīdr̥śaṁ hr̥daye tasya sītāsaṁbhogajaṁ sukham |
aṅkam āropya hi purā rāvaṇena balād dhr̥tām ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
laṅkām api punar nītām aśokavanikāṁ gatām |
rakṣasāṁ vaśam āpannāṁ kathaṁ rāmo na kutsate ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
asmākam api dāreṣu sahanīyaṁ bhaviṣyati |
yathā hi kurute rājā prajā tam anuvartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
evaṁ bahuvidhā vāco vadanti puravāsinaḥ |
nagareṣu ca sarveṣu rājañ janapadeṣu ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tasyaitad bhāṣitaṁ śrutvā rāghavaḥ paramārtavat |
uvāca sarvān suhr̥daḥ katham etan nivedyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
sarve tu śirasā bhūmāv abhivādya praṇamya ca |
pratyūcū rāghavaṁ dīnam evam etan na saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
śrutvā tu vākyaṁ kākutsthaḥ sarveṣāṁ samudīritam |
visarjayām āsa tadā sarvāṁs tāñ śatrutāpanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
