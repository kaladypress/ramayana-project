=== Sarga 36 ===

---
1.
tataḥ pitāmahaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā vāyuḥ putravadhārditaḥ |
śiśukaṁ taṁ samādāya uttasthau dhātur agrataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
calatkuṇḍalamaulisraktapanīyavibhūṣaṇaḥ |
pādayor nyapatad vāyus tisro ’vasthāya vedhase ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
taṁ tu vedavidādyas tu lambābharaṇaśobhinā |
vāyum utthāpya hastena śiśuṁ taṁ parimr̥ṣṭavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
spr̥ṣṭamātras tataḥ so ’tha salīlaṁ padmajanmanā |
jalasiktaṁ yathā sasyaṁ punar jīvitam āptavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
prāṇavantam imaṁ dr̥ṣṭvā prāṇo gandhavaho mudā |
cacāra sarvabhūteṣu saṁniruddhaṁ yathāpurā ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
marudrogavinirmuktāḥ prajā vai muditābhavan |
śītavātavinirmuktāḥ padminya iva sāmbujāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tatas triyugmas trikakut tridhāmā tridaśārcitaḥ |
uvāca devatā brahmā mārutapriyakāmyayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
bho mahendrāgnivaruṇadhaneśvaramaheśvarāḥ |
jānatām api tat sarvaṁ hitaṁ vakṣyāmi śrūyatām ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
anena śiśunā kāryaṁ kartavyaṁ vo bhaviṣyati |
dadatāsya varān sarve mārutasyāsya tuṣṭidān ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tataḥ sahasranayanaḥ prītiraktaḥ śubhānanaḥ |
kuśe śayamayīṁ mālāṁ samutkṣipyedam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
matkarotsr̥ṣṭavajreṇa hanur asya yathā kṣataḥ |
nāmnaiṣa kapiśārdūlo bhavitā hanumān iti ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
aham evāsya dāsyāmi paramaṁ varam uttamam |
ataḥ prabhr̥ti vajrasya mamāvadhyo bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
mārtāṇḍas tv abravīt tatra bhagavāṁs timirāpahaḥ |
tejaso ’sya madīyasya dadāmi śatikāṁ kalām ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
yadā tu śāstrāṇy adhyetuṁ śaktir asya bhaviṣyati |
tadāsya śāstraṁ dāsyāmi yena vāgmī bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
varuṇaś ca varaṁ prādān nāsya mr̥tyur bhaviṣyati |
varṣāyutaśatenāpi matpāśād udakād api ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
yamo ’pi daṇḍāvadhyatvam arogatvaṁ ca nityaśaḥ |
diśate ’sya varaṁ tuṣṭa aviṣādaṁ ca saṁyuge ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
gadeyaṁ māmikā nainaṁ saṁyugeṣu vadhiṣyati |
ity evaṁ varadaḥ prāha tadā hy ekākṣipiṅgalaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
matto madāyudhānāṁ ca na vadhyo ’yaṁ bhaviṣyati |
ity evaṁ śaṁkareṇāpi datto ’sya paramo varaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
sarveṣāṁ brahmadaṇḍānām avadhyo ’yaṁ bhaviṣyati |
dīrghāyuś ca mahātmā ca iti brahmābravīd vacaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
viśvakarmā tu dr̥ṣṭvainaṁ bālasūryopamaṁ śiśum |
śilpināṁ pravaraḥ prāha varam asya mahāmatiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
vinirmitāni devānām āyudhānīha yāni tu |
teṣāṁ saṁgrāmakāle tu avadhyo ’yaṁ bhaviṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
tataḥ surāṇāṁ tu varair dr̥ṣṭvā hy enam alaṁkr̥tam |
caturmukhas tuṣṭamukho vāyum āha jagadguruḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
amitrāṇāṁ bhayakaro mitrāṇām abhayaṁkaraḥ |
ajeyo bhavitā te ’tra putro mārutamārutiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
rāvaṇotsādanārthāni rāmaprītikarāṇi ca |
romaharṣakarāṇy eṣa kartā karmāṇi saṁyuge ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
evam uktvā tam āmantrya mārutaṁ te ’maraiḥ saha |
yathāgataṁ yayuḥ sarve pitāmahapurogamāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
so ’pi gandhavahaḥ putraṁ pragr̥hya gr̥ham ānayat |
añjanāyāstam ākhyāya varaṁ dattaṁ viniḥsr̥taḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
prāpya rāma varān eṣa varadānabalānvitaḥ |
balenātmani saṁsthena so ’pūryata yathārṇavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
balenāpūryamāṇo hi eṣa vānarapuṁgavaḥ |
āśrameṣu maharṣīṇām aparādhyati nirbhayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
srugbhāṇḍān agnihotraṁ ca valkalānāṁ ca saṁcayān |
bhagnavicchinnavidhvastān suśāntānāṁ karoty ayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
sarveṣāṁ brahmadaṇḍānām avadhyaṁ brahmaṇā kr̥tam |
jānanta r̥ṣayas taṁ vai kṣamante tasya nityaśaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
yadā keṣariṇā tv eṣa vāyunā sāñjanena ca |
pratiṣiddho ’pi maryādāṁ laṅghayaty eva vānaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
tato maharṣayaḥ kruddhā bhr̥gvaṅgirasavaṁśajāḥ |
śepur enaṁ raghuśreṣṭha nātikruddhātimanyavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
bādhase yat samāśritya balam asmān plavaṁgama |
tad dīrghakālaṁ vettāsi nāsmākaṁ śāpamohitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
tatas tu hr̥tatejaujā maharṣivacanaujasā |
eṣo śramāṇi nātyeti mr̥dubhāvagataś caran ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
atha r̥kṣarajā nāma vālisugrīvayoḥ pitā |
sarvavānararājāsīt tejasā iva bhāskaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
sa tu rājyaṁ ciraṁ kr̥tvā vānarāṇāṁ harīśvaraḥ |
tatas tvarkṣarajā nāma kāladharmeṇa saṁgataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
tasminn astamite vālī mantribhir mantrakovidaiḥ |
pitrye pade kr̥to rājā sugrīvo vālinaḥ pade ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
sugrīveṇa samaṁ tv asya advaidhaṁ chidravarjitam |
ahāryaṁ sakhyam abhavad anilasya yathāgninā ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
eṣa śāpavaśād eva na vedabalam ātmanaḥ |
vālisugrīvayor vairaṁ yadā rāma samutthitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
na hy eṣa rāma sugrīvo bhrāmyamāṇo ’pi vālinā |
vedayāno na ca hy eṣa balam ātmani mārutiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
parākramotsāhamatipratāpaiḥ; sauśīlyamādhuryanayānayaiś ca |
gāmbhīryacāturyasuvīryadhairyair; hanūmataḥ ko ’py adhiko ’sti loke ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
asau purā vyākaraṇaṁ grahīṣyan; sūryonmukhaḥ pr̥ṣṭhagamaḥ kapīndraḥ |
udyadgirer astagiriṁ jagāma; granthaṁ mahad dhārayad aprameyaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
pravīvivikṣor iva sāgarasya; lokān didhakṣor iva pāvakasya |
lokakṣayeṣv eva yathāntakasya; hanūmataḥ sthāsyati kaḥ purastāt ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
eṣo ’pi cānye ca mahākapīndrāḥ; sugrīvamaindadvividāḥ sanīlāḥ |
satāratāreyanalāḥ sarambhās; tvatkāraṇād rāma surair hi sr̥ṣṭāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
tad etat kathitaṁ sarvaṁ yan māṁ tvaṁ paripr̥cchasi |
hanūmato bālabhāve karmaitat kathitaṁ mayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
dr̥ṣṭaḥ saṁbhāṣitaś cāsi rāma gacchamahe vayam |
evam uktvā gatāḥ sarve r̥ṣayas te yathāgatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
