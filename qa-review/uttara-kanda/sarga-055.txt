=== Sarga 55 ===

---
1.
evam uktas tu rāmeṇa parāṁ vrīḍām upāgataḥ |
śatrughno vīryasaṁpanno mandaṁ mandam uvāca ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
avaśyaṁ karaṇīyaṁ ca śāsanaṁ puruṣarṣabha |
tava caiva mahābhāga śāsanaṁ duratikramam |
ayaṁ kāmakaro rājaṁs tavāsmi puruṣarṣabha ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
evam ukte tu śūreṇa śatrughnena mahātmanā |
uvāca rāmaḥ saṁhr̥ṣṭo lakṣmaṇaṁ bharataṁ tathā ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
saṁbhārān abhiṣekasya ānayadhvaṁ samāhitāḥ |
adyaiva puruṣavyāghram abhiṣekṣyāmi durjayam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
purodhasaṁ ca kākutsthau naigamān r̥tvijas tathā |
mantriṇaś caiva me sarvān ānayadhvaṁ mamājñayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
rājñaḥ śāsanam ājñāya tathākurvan mahārathāḥ |
abhiṣekasamārambhaṁ puraskr̥tya purodhasaṁ |
praviṣṭā rājabhavanaṁ puraṁdaragr̥hopamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
tato ’bhiṣeko vavr̥dhe śatrughnasya mahātmanaḥ |
saṁpraharṣakaraḥ śrīmān rāghavasya purasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tato ’bhiṣiktaṁ śatrughnam aṅkam āropya rāghavaḥ |
uvāca madhurāṁ vāṇīṁ tejas tasyābhipūrayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
ayaṁ śaras tv amoghas te divyaḥ parapuraṁjayaḥ |
anena lavaṇaṁ saumya hantāsi raghunandana ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
sr̥ṣṭaḥ śaro ’yaṁ kākutstha yadā śete mahārṇave |
svayambhūr ajito devo yaṁ nāpaśyan surāsurāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
adr̥śyaḥ sarvabhūtānāṁ tenāyaṁ hi śarottamaḥ |
sr̥ṣṭaḥ krodhābhibhūtena vināśārthaṁ durātmanoḥ |
madhukaiṭabhayor vīra vighāte vartamānayoḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
sraṣṭukāmena lokāṁs trīṁs tau cānena hatau yudhi |
anena śaramukhyena tato lokāṁś cakāra saḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
nāyaṁ mayā śaraḥ pūrvaṁ rāvaṇasya vadhārthinā |
muktaḥ śatrughna bhūtānāṁ mahāṁs trāso bhaved iti ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
yac ca tasya mahac chūlaṁ tryambakeṇa mahātmanā |
dattaṁ śatruvināśāya madhor āyudham uttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
tat saṁnikṣipya bhavane pūjyamānaṁ punaḥ punaḥ |
diśaḥ sarvāḥ samālokya prāpnoty āhāram ātmanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
yadā tu yuddham ākāṅkṣan kaś cid enaṁ samāhvayet |
tadā śūlaṁ gr̥hītvā tad bhasma rakṣaḥ karoti tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
sa tvaṁ puruṣaśārdūla tam āyudhavivarjitam |
apraviṣṭapuraṁ pūrvaṁ dvāri tiṣṭha dhr̥tāyudhaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
apraviṣṭaṁ ca bhavanaṁ yuddhāya puruṣarṣabha |
āhvayethā mahābāho tato hantāsi rākṣasam ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
anyathā kriyamāṇe tu avadhyaḥ sa bhaviṣyati |
yadi tv evaṁ kr̥te vīra vināśam upayāsyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
etat te sarvam ākhyātaṁ śūlasya ca viparyayam |
śrīmataḥ śitikaṇṭhasya kr̥tyaṁ hi duratikramam ||

=== meaning ===

---
