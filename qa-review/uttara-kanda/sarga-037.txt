=== Sarga 37 ===

---
1.
vimr̥śya ca tato rāmo vayasyam akutobhayam |
pratardanaṁ kāśipatiṁ pariṣvajyedam abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
darśitā bhavatā prītir darśitaṁ sauhr̥daṁ param |
udyogaś ca kr̥to rājan bharatena tvayā saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tad bhavān adya kāśeyīṁ purīṁ vārāṇasīṁ vraja |
ramaṇīyāṁ tvayā guptāṁ suprākārāṁ sutoraṇām ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
etāvad uktvā utthāya kākutsthaḥ paramāsanāt |
paryaṣvajata dharmātmā nirantaram urogatam ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
visr̥jya taṁ vayasyaṁ sa svāgatān pr̥thivīpatīn |
prahasan rāghavo vākyam uvāca madhurākṣaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
bhavatāṁ prītir avyagrā tejasā parirakṣitā |
dharmaś ca niyato nityaṁ satyaṁ ca bhavatāṁ sadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
yuṣmākaṁ ca prabhāvena tejasā ca mahātmanām |
hato durātmā durbuddhī rāvaṇo rākṣasādhipaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
hetumātram ahaṁ tatra bhavatāṁ tejasā hataḥ |
rāvaṇaḥ sagaṇo yuddhe saputraḥ sahabāndhavaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
bhavantaś ca samānītā bharatena mahātmanā |
śrutvā janakarājasya kānane tanayāṁ hr̥tām ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
udyuktānāṁ ca sarveṣāṁ pārthivānāṁ mahātmanām |
kālo hy atītaḥ sumahān gamane rocatāṁ matiḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
pratyūcus taṁ ca rājāno harṣeṇa mahatānvitāḥ |
diṣṭyā tvaṁ vijayī rāma rājyaṁ cāpi pratiṣṭhitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
diṣṭyā pratyāhr̥tā sītā diṣṭyā śatruḥ parājitaḥ |
eṣa naḥ paramaḥ kāma eṣā naḥ kīrtir uttamā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
yat tvāṁ vijayinaṁ rāma paśyāmo hataśātravam |
upapannaṁ ca kākutstha yat tvam asmān praśaṁsasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
praśaṁsārhā hi jānanti praśaṁsāṁ vaktum īdr̥śīm |
āpr̥cchāmo gamiṣyāmo hr̥distho naḥ sadā bhavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
bhavec ca te mahārāja prītir asmāsu nityadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
