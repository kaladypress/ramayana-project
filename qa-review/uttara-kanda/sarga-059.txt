=== Sarga 59 ===

---
1.
atha rātryāṁ pravr̥ttāyāṁ śatrughno bhr̥gunandanam |
papraccha cyavanaṁ vipraṁ lavaṇasya balābalam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
śūlasya ca balaṁ brahman ke ca pūrvaṁ nipātitāḥ |
anena śūlamukhena dvandvayuddham upāgatāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
tasya tadbhāṣitaṁ śrutvā śatrughnasya mahātmanaḥ |
pratyuvāca mahātejāś cyavano raghunandanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
asaṁkhyeyāni karmāṇi yāny asya puruṣarṣabha |
ikṣvākuvaṁśaprabhave yad vr̥ttaṁ tac chr̥ṇuṣva me ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
ayodhyāyāṁ purā rājā yuvanāśvasuto balī |
māndhātā iti vikhyātas triṣu lokeṣu vīryavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
sa kr̥tvā pr̥thivīṁ kr̥tsnāṁ śāsane pr̥thivīpatiḥ |
suralokam atho jetum udyogam akaron nr̥paḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
indrasya tu bhayaṁ tīvraṁ surāṇāṁ ca mahātmanām |
māndhātari kr̥todyoge devalokajigīṣayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
ardhāsanena śakrasya rājyārdhena ca pārthivaḥ |
vandyamānaḥ suragaṇaiḥ pratijñām adhyarohata ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tasya pāpam abhiprāyaṁ viditvā pākaśāsanaḥ |
sāntvapūrvam idaṁ vākyam uvāca yuvanāśvajam ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
rājā tvaṁ mānuṣe loke na tāvat puruṣarṣabha |
akr̥tvā pr̥thivīṁ vaśyāṁ devarājyam ihecchasi ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
yadi vīra samagrā te medinī nikhilā vaśe |
devarājyaṁ kuruṣveha sabhr̥tyabalavāhanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
indram evaṁ bruvāṇaṁ tu māndhātā vākyam abravīt |
kva me śakra pratihataṁ śāsanaṁ pr̥thivītale ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tam uvāca sahasrākṣo lavaṇo nāma rākṣasaḥ |
madhuputro madhuvane nājñāṁ te kurute ’nagha ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tac chrutvā vipriyaṁ ghoraṁ sahasrākṣeṇa bhāṣitam |
vrīḍito ’vāṅmukho rājā vyāhartuṁ na śaśāka ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
āmantrya tu sahasrākṣaṁ hriyā kiṁ cid avāṅmukhaḥ |
punar evāgamac chrīmān imaṁ lokaṁ nareśvaraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
sa kr̥tvā hr̥daye ’marṣaṁ sabhr̥tyabalavāhanaḥ |
ājagāma madhoḥ putraṁ vaśe kartum aninditaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
sa kāṅkṣamāṇo lavaṇaṁ yuddhāya puruṣarṣabhaḥ |
dūtaṁ saṁpreṣayām āsa sakāśaṁ lavaṇasya saḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
sa gatvā vipriyāṇy āha bahūni madhunaḥ sutam |
vadantam evaṁ taṁ dūtaṁ bhakṣayām āsa rākṣasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
cirāyamāṇe dūte tu rājā krodhasamanvitaḥ |
ardayām āsa tad rakṣaḥ śaravr̥ṣṭyā samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tataḥ prahasya lavaṇaḥ śūlaṁ jagrāha pāṇinā |
vadhāya sānubandhasya mumocāyudham uttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
tac chūlaṁ dīpyamānaṁ tu sabhr̥tyabalavāhanam |
bhasmīkr̥tya nr̥paṁ bhūyo lavaṇasyāgamat karam ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
evaṁ sa rājā sumahān hataḥ sabalavāhanaḥ |
śūlasya ca balaṁ vīra aprameyam anuttamam ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
śvaḥ prabhāte tu lavaṇaṁ vadhiṣyasi na saṁśayaḥ |
agr̥hītāyudhaṁ kṣipraṁ dhruvo hi vijayas tava ||

=== meaning ===

---
