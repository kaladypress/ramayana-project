=== Sarga 76 ===

---
1.
lakṣmaṇasya tu tad vākyaṁ śrutvā śatrunibarhaṇaḥ |
vr̥traghātam aśeṣeṇa kathayety āha lakṣmaṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
rāghaveṇaivam uktas tu sumitrānandavardhanaḥ |
bhūya eva kathāṁ divyāṁ kathayām āsa lakṣmaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sahasrākṣavacaḥ śrutvā sarveṣāṁ ca divaukasām |
viṣṇur devān uvācedaṁ sarvān indrapurogamān ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
pūrvaṁ sauhr̥dabaddho ’smi vr̥trasya sumahātmanaḥ |
tena yuṣmat priyārthaṁ vai nāhaṁ hanmi mahāsuram ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
avaśyaṁ karaṇīyaṁ ca bhavatāṁ sukham uttamam |
tasmād upāyam ākhyāsye yena vr̥traṁ haniṣyatha ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
tridhā bhūtaṁ kariṣye ’ham ātmānaṁ surasattamāḥ |
tena vr̥traṁ sahasrākṣo haniṣyati na saṁśayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
eko ’ṁśo vāsavaṁ yātu dvitīyo vajram eva tu |
tr̥tīyo bhūtalaṁ śakras tato vr̥traṁ haniṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tathā bruvati deveśe devā vākyam athābruvan |
evam etan na saṁdeho yathā vadasi daityahan ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
bhadraṁ te ’stu gamiṣyāmo vr̥trāsuravadhaiṣiṇaḥ |
bhajasva paramodāravāsavaṁ svena tejasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
tataḥ sarve mahātmānaḥ sahasrākṣapurogamāḥ |
tad araṇyam upākrāman yatra vr̥tro mahāsuraḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
te ’paśyaṁs tejasā bhūtaṁ tapantam asurottamam |
pibantam iva lokāṁs trīn nirdahantam ivāmbaram ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
dr̥ṣṭvaiva cāsuraśreṣṭhaṁ devās trāsam upāgaman |
katham enaṁ vadhiṣyāmaḥ kathaṁ na syāt parājayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
teṣāṁ cintayatāṁ tatra sahasrākṣaḥ puraṁdaraḥ |
vajraṁ pragr̥hya bāhubhyāṁ prahiṇod vr̥tramūrdhani ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
kālāgnineva ghoreṇa dīpteneva mahārciṣā |
prataptaṁ vr̥traśirasi jagat trāsam upāgamat ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
asaṁbhāvyaṁ vadhaṁ tasya vr̥trasya vibudhādhipaḥ |
cintayāno jagāmāśu lokasyāntaṁ mahāyaśāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
tam indraṁ brahmahatyāśu gacchantam anugacchati |
apatac cāsya gātreṣu tam indraṁ duḥkham āviśat ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
hatārayaḥ pranaṣṭendrā devāḥ sāgnipurogamāḥ |
viṣṇuṁ tribhuvaṇaśreṣṭhaṁ muhur muhur apūjayan ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
tvaṁ gatiḥ paramā deva pūrvajo jagataḥ prabhuḥ |
rathārthaṁ sarvabhūtānāṁ viṣṇutvam upajagmivān ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
hataś cāyaṁ tvayā vr̥tro brahmahatyā ca vāsavam |
bādhate suraśārdūla mokṣaṁ tasya vinirdiśa ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
teṣāṁ tad vacanaṁ śrutvā devānāṁ viṣṇur abravīt |
mām eva yajatāṁ śakraḥ pāvayiṣyāmi vajriṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
puṇyena hayamedhena mām iṣṭvā pākaśāsanaḥ |
punar eṣyati devānām indratvam akutobhayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
evaṁ saṁdiśya devānāṁ tāṁ vāṇīm amr̥topamā |
jagāma viṣṇur deveśaḥ stūyamānas triviṣṭapam ||

=== meaning ===

---
