=== Sarga 94 ===

---
1.
śr̥ṇu rāma mahābāho yadartham aham āhataḥ |
pitāmahena devena preṣito ’smi mahābala ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tavāhaṁ pūrvake bhāve putraḥ parapuraṁjaya |
māyāsaṁbhāvito vīra kālaḥ sarvasamāharaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
pitāmahaś ca bhagavān āha lokapatiḥ prabhuḥ |
samayas te mahābāho svarlokān parirakṣitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
saṁkṣipya ca purā lokān māyayā svayam eva hi |
mahārṇave śayāno ’psu māṁ tvaṁ pūrvam ajījanaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
bhogavantaṁ tato nāgam anantam udake śayam |
māyayā janayitvā tvaṁ dvau ca sattvau mahābalau ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
madhuṁ ca kaiṭabhaṁ caiva yayor asthicayair vr̥tā |
iyaṁ parvatasaṁbādhā medinī cābhavan mahī ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
padme divyārkasaṁkāśe nābhyām utpādya mām api |
prājāpatyaṁ tvayā karma sarvaṁ mayi niveśitam ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
so ’haṁ saṁnyastabhāro hi tvām upāse jagatpatim |
rakṣāṁ vidhatsva bhūteṣu mama tejas karo bhavān ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tatas tvam api durdharṣas tasmād bhāvāt sanātanāt |
rakṣārthaṁ sarvabhūtānāṁ viṣṇutvam upajagmivān ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
adityāṁ vīryavān putro bhrātr̥̄ṇāṁ harṣavardhanaḥ |
samutpanneṣu kr̥tyeṣu lokasāhyāya kalpase ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
sa tvaṁ vitrāsyamānāsu prajāsu jagatāṁ vara |
rāvaṇasya vadhākāṅkṣī mānuṣeṣu mano ’dadhāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
daśavarṣasahasrāṇi daśavarṣaśatāni ca |
kr̥tvā vāsasya niyatiṁ svayam evātmanaḥ purā ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
sa tvaṁ manomayaḥ putraḥ pūrṇāyur mānuṣeṣv iha |
kālo naravaraśreṣṭha samīpam upavartitum ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
yadi bhūyo mahārāja prajā icchasy upāsitum |
vasa vā vīra bhadraṁ te evam āha pitāmahaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
atha vā vijigīṣā te suralokāya rāghava |
sanāthā viṣṇunā devā bhavantu vigatajvarāḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
śrutvā pitāmahenoktaṁ vākyaṁ kālasamīritam |
rāghavaḥ prahasan vākyaṁ sarvasaṁhāram abravīt ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
śrutaṁ me devadevasya vākyaṁ paramam adbhutam |
prītir hi mahatī jātā tavāgamanasaṁbhavā ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
bhadraṁ te ’stu gamiṣyāmi yata evāham āgataḥ |
hr̥d gato hy asi saṁprāpto na me ’sty atra vicāraṇā ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
mayā hi sarvakr̥tyeṣu devānāṁ vaśavartinām |
sthātavyaṁ sarvasaṁhāre yathā hy āha pitāmahaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
