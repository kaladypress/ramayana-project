=== Sarga 17 ===

---
1.
atha rājan mahābāhur vicaran sa mahītalam |
himavadvanam āsādya paricakrāma rāvaṇaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
tatrāpaśyata vai kanyāṁ kr̥ṣṭājinajaṭādharām |
ārṣeṇa vidhinā yuktāṁ tapantīṁ devatām iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
sa dr̥ṣṭvā rūpasaṁpannāṁ kanyāṁ tāṁ sumahāvratām |
kāmamohaparītātmā papraccha prahasann iva ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
kim idaṁ vartase bhadre viruddhaṁ yauvanasya te |
na hi yuktā tavaitasya rūpasyeyaṁ pratikriyā ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
kasyāsi duhitā bhadre ko vā bhartā tavānaghe |
pr̥cchataḥ śaṁsa me śīghraṁ ko vā hetus tapo’rjane ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
evam uktā tu sā kanyā tenānāryeṇa rakṣasā |
abravīd vidhivat kr̥tvā tasyātithyaṁ tapodhanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
kuśadhvajo nāma pitā brahmarṣir mama dhārmikaḥ |
br̥haspatisutaḥ śrīmān buddhyā tulyo br̥haspateḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
tasyāhaṁ kurvato nityaṁ vedābhyāsaṁ mahātmanaḥ |
saṁbhūtā vānmayī kanyā nāmnā vedavatī smr̥tā ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
tato devāḥ sagandharvā yakṣarākṣasapannagāḥ |
te cāpi gatvā pitaraṁ varaṇaṁ rocayanti me ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
na ca māṁ sa pitā tebhyo dattavān rākṣaseśvara |
kāraṇaṁ tad vadiṣyāmi niśāmaya mahābhuja ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
pitus tu mama jāmātā viṣṇuḥ kila surottamaḥ |
abhipretas trilokeśas tasmān nānyasya me pitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
dātum icchati dharmātmā tac chrutvā baladarpitaḥ |
śambhur nāma tato rājā daityānāṁ kupito ’bhavat |
tena rātrau prasupto me pitā pāpena hiṁsitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
tato me jananī dīnā tac charīraṁ pitur mama |
pariṣvajya mahābhāgā praviṣṭā dahanaṁ saha ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tato manorathaṁ satyaṁ pitur nārāyaṇaṁ prati |
karomīti mamecchā ca hr̥daye sādhu viṣṭhitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
ahaṁ pretagatasyāpi kariṣye kāṅkṣitaṁ pituḥ |
iti pratijñām āruhya carāmi vipulaṁ tapaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
etat te sarvam ākhyātaṁ mayā rākṣasapuṁgava |
āśritāṁ viddhi māṁ dharmaṁ nārāyaṇapatīcchayā ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
vijñātas tvaṁ hi me rājan gaccha paulastyanandana |
jānāmi tapasā sarvaṁ trailokye yad dhi vartate ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
so ’bravīd rāvaṇas tatra tāṁ kanyāṁ sumahāvratām |
avaruhya vimānāgrāt kandarpaśarapīḍitaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
avaliptāsi suśroṇi yasyās te matir īdr̥śī |
vr̥ddhānāṁ mr̥gaśāvākṣi bhrājate dharmasaṁcayaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
tvaṁ sarvaguṇasaṁpannā nārhase kartum īdr̥śam |
trailokyasundarī bhīru yauvane vārdhakaṁ vidhim ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
kaś ca tāvad asau yaṁ tvaṁ viṣṇur ity abhibhāṣase |
vīryeṇa tapasā caiva bhogena ca balena ca |
na mayāsau samo bhadre yaṁ tvaṁ kāmayase ’ṅgane ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
mā maivam iti sā kanyā tam uvāca niśācaram |
mūrdhajeṣu ca tāṁ rakṣaḥ karāgreṇa parāmr̥śat ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
tato vedavatī kruddhā keśān hastena sācchinat |
uvācāgniṁ samādhāya maraṇāya kr̥tatvarā ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
dharṣitāyās tvayānārya nedānīṁ mama jīvitam |
rakṣas tasmāt pravekṣyāmi paśyatas te hutāśanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
yasmāt tu dharṣitā cāham apāpā cāpy anāthavat |
tasmāt tava vadhārthaṁ vai samutpatsyāmy ahaṁ punaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
na hi śakyaḥ striyā pāpa hantuṁ tvaṁ tu viśeṣataḥ |
śāpe tvayi mayotsr̥ṣṭe tapasaś ca vyayo bhavet ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
yadi tv asti mayā kiṁ cit kr̥taṁ dattaṁ hutaṁ tathā |
tena hy ayonijā sādhvī bhaveyaṁ dharmiṇaḥ sutā ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
evam uktvā praviṣṭā sā jvalantaṁ vai hutāśanam |
papāta ca divo divyā puṣpavr̥ṣṭiḥ samantataḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
pūrvaṁ krodhahataḥ śatrur yayāsau nihatas tvayā |
samupāśritya śailābhaṁ tava vīryam amānuṣam ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
evam eṣā mahābhāgā martyeṣūtpadyate punaḥ |
kṣetre halamukhagraste vedyām agniśikhopamā ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
eṣā vedavatī nāma pūrvam āsīt kr̥te yuge |
tretāyugam anuprāpya vadhārthaṁ tasya rakṣasaḥ |
sītotpanneti sītaiṣā mānuṣaiḥ punar ucyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
