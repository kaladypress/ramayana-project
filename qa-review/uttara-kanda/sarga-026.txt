=== Sarga 26 ===

---
1.
sa tu tatra daśagrīvaḥ saha sainyena vīryavān |
astaṁ prāpte dinakare nivāsaṁ samarocayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
2.
udite vimale candre tulyaparvatavarcasi |
sa dadarśa guṇāṁs tatra candrapādopaśobhitān ||

=== meaning ===

---
3.
karṇikāravanair divyaiḥ kadambagahanais tathā |
padminībhiś ca phullābhir mandākinyā jalair api ||

=== meaning ===

---
4.
ghaṇṭānām iva saṁnādaḥ śuśruve madhurasvanaḥ |
apsarogaṇasaṁghanāṁ gāyatāṁ dhanadālaye ||

=== meaning ===

---
5.
puṣpavarṣāṇi muñcanto nagāḥ pavanatāḍitāḥ |
śailaṁ taṁ vāsayantīva madhumādhavagandhinaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
6.
madhupuṣparajaḥpr̥ktaṁ gandham ādāya puṣkalam |
pravavau vardhayan kāmaṁ rāvaṇasya sukho ’nilaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
7.
geyāt puṣpasamr̥ddhyā ca śaityād vāyor guṇair gireḥ |
pravr̥ttāyāṁ rajanyāṁ ca candrasyodayanena ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
8.
rāvaṇaḥ sumahāvīryaḥ kāmabāṇavaśaṁ gataḥ |
viniśvasya viniśvasya śaśinaṁ samavaikṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
9.
etasminn antare tatra divyapuṣpavibhūṣitā |
sarvāpsarovarā rambhā pūrṇacandranibhānanā ||

=== meaning ===

---
10.
kr̥tair viśeṣakair ārdraiḥ ṣaḍartukusumotsavaiḥ |
nīlaṁ satoyameghābhaṁ vastraṁ samavaguṇṭhitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
11.
yasya vaktraṁ śaśinibhaṁ bhruvau cāpanibhe śubhe |
ūrū karikarākārau karau pallavakomalau |
sainyamadhyena gacchantī rāvaṇenopalakṣitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
12.
tāṁ samutthāya rakṣendraḥ kāmabāṇabalārditaḥ |
kare gr̥hītvā gacchantīṁ smayamāno ’bhyabhāṣata ||

=== meaning ===

---
13.
kva gacchasi varārohe kāṁ siddhiṁ bhajase svayam |
kasyābhyudayakālo ’yaṁ yas tvāṁ samupabhokṣyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
14.
tavānanarasasyādya padmotpalasugandhinaḥ |
sudhāmr̥tarasasyeva ko ’dya tr̥ptiṁ gamiṣyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
15.
svarṇakumbhanibhau pīnau śubhau bhīru nirantarau |
kasyorasthalasaṁsparśaṁ dāsyatas te kucāv imau ||

=== meaning ===

---
16.
suvarṇacakrapratimaṁ svarṇadāmacitaṁ pr̥thu |
adhyārokṣyati kas te ’dya svargaṁ jaghanarūpiṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
17.
madviśiṣṭaḥ pumān ko ’nyaḥ śakro viṣṇur athāśvinau |
mām atītya hi yasya tvaṁ yāsi bhīru na śobhanam ||

=== meaning ===

---
18.
viśrama tvaṁ pr̥thuśroṇi śilātalam idaṁ śubham |
trailokye yaḥ prabhuś caiva tulyo mama na vidyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
19.
tad eṣa prāñjaliḥ prahvo yācate tvāṁ daśānanaḥ |
yaḥ prabhuś cāpi bhartā ca trailokyasya bhajasva mām ||

=== meaning ===

---
20.
evam uktābravīd rambhā vepamānā kr̥tāñjaliḥ |
prasīda nārhase vaktum īdr̥śaṁ tvaṁ hi me guruḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
21.
anyebhyo ’pi tvayā rakṣyā prāpnuyāṁ dharṣaṇaṁ yadi |
dharmataś ca snuṣā te ’haṁ tattvam etad bravīmi te ||

=== meaning ===

---
22.
abravīt tāṁ daśagrīvaś caraṇādhomukhīṁ sthitām |
sutasya yadi me bhāryā tatas tvaṁ me snuṣā bhaveḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
23.
bāḍham ity eva sā rambhā prāha rāvaṇam uttaram |
dharmatas te sutasyāhaṁ bhāryā rākṣasapuṁgava ||

=== meaning ===

---
24.
putraḥ priyataraḥ prāṇair bhrātur vaiśravaṇasya te |
khyāto yas triṣu lokeṣu nalakūbara ity asau ||

=== meaning ===

---
25.
dharmato yo bhaved vipraḥ kṣatriyo vīryato bhavet |
krodhād yaś ca bhaved agniḥ kṣāntyā ca vasudhāsamaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
26.
tasyāsmi kr̥tasaṁketā lokapālasutasya vai |
tam uddiśya ca me sarvaṁ vibhūṣaṇam idaṁ kr̥tam ||

=== meaning ===

---
27.
yasya tasya hi nānyasya bhāvo māṁ prati tiṣṭhati |
tena satyena māṁ rājan moktum arhasy ariṁdama ||

=== meaning ===

---
28.
sa hi tiṣṭhati dharmātmā sāmprataṁ matsamutsukaḥ |
tan na vighnaṁ sutasyeha kartum arhasi muñca mām ||

=== meaning ===

---
29.
sadbhir ācaritaṁ mārgaṁ gaccha rākṣasapuṁgava |
mānanīyo mayā hi tvaṁ lālanīyā tathāsmi te ||

=== meaning ===

---
30.
evaṁ bruvāṇāṁ rambhāṁ tāṁ dharmārthasahitaṁ vacaḥ |
nirbhartsya rākṣaso mohāt pratigr̥hya balād balī |
kāmamohābhisaṁrabdho maithunāyopacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
31.
sā vimuktā tato rambhā bhraṣṭamālyavibhūṣaṇā |
gajendrākrīḍamathitā nadīvākulatāṁ gatā ||

=== meaning ===

---
32.
sā vepamānā lajjantī bhītā karakr̥tāñjaliḥ |
nalakūbaram āsādya pādayor nipapāta ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
33.
tadavasthāṁ ca tāṁ dr̥ṣṭvā mahātmā nalakūbaraḥ |
abravīt kim idaṁ bhadre pādayoḥ patitāsi me ||

=== meaning ===

---
34.
sā tu niśvasamānā ca vepamānātha sāñjaliḥ |
tasmai sarvaṁ yathātathyam ākhyātum upacakrame ||

=== meaning ===

---
35.
eṣa deva daśagrīvaḥ prāpto gantuṁ triviṣṭapam |
tena sainyasahāyena niśeha pariṇāmyate ||

=== meaning ===

---
36.
āyāntī tena dr̥ṣṭāsmi tvatsakāśam ariṁdama |
gr̥hītvā tena pr̥ṣṭāsmi kasya tvam iti rakṣasā ||

=== meaning ===

---
37.
mayā tu sarvaṁ yat satyaṁ tad dhi tasmai niveditam |
kāmamohābhibhūtātmā nāśrauṣīt tad vaco mama ||

=== meaning ===

---
38.
yācyamāno mayā deva snuṣā te ’ham iti prabho |
tat sarvaṁ pr̥ṣṭhataḥ kr̥tvā balāt tenāsmi dharṣitā ||

=== meaning ===

---
39.
evaṁ tvam aparādhaṁ me kṣantum arhasi mānada |
na hi tulyaṁ balaṁ saumya striyāś ca puruṣasya ca ||

=== meaning ===

---
40.
evaṁ śrutvā tu saṁkruddhas tadā vaiśvaraṇātmajaḥ |
dharṣaṇāṁ tāṁ parāṁ śrutvā dhyānaṁ saṁpraviveśa ha ||

=== meaning ===

---
41.
tasya tat karma vijñāya tadā vaiśravaṇātmajaḥ |
muhūrtād roṣatāmrākṣas toyaṁ jagrāha pāṇinā ||

=== meaning ===

---
42.
gr̥hītvā salilaṁ divyam upaspr̥śya yathāvidhi |
utsasarja tadā śāpaṁ rākṣasendrāya dāruṇam ||

=== meaning ===

---
43.
akāmā tena yasmāt tvaṁ balād bhadre pradharṣitā |
tasmāt sa yuvatīm anyāṁ nākāmām upayāsyati ||

=== meaning ===

---
44.
yadā tv akāmāṁ kāmārto dharṣayiṣyati yoṣitam |
mūrdhā tu saptadhā tasya śakalībhavitā tadā ||

=== meaning ===

---
45.
tasminn udāhr̥te śāpe jvalitāgnisamaprabhe |
devadundubhayo neduḥ puṣpavr̥ṣṭiś ca khāc cyutā ||

=== meaning ===

---
46.
prajāpatimukhāś cāpi sarve devāḥ praharṣitāḥ |
jñātvā lokagatiṁ sarvāṁ tasya mr̥tyuṁ ca rakṣasaḥ ||

=== meaning ===

---
47.
śrutvā tu sa daśagrīvas taṁ śāpaṁ romaharṣaṇam |
nārīṣu maithunaṁ bhāvaṁ nākāmāsv abhyarocayat ||

=== meaning ===

---
